Simple Website Maintenance.In general, Laravel-built websites are simple to update over time. So, let’s say you want to add some new features to your site (which was already constructed using Laravel) after a few years. A new developer may easily continue where your previous developers left the site. It is incredibly simple to maintain a Laravel site over time because of its unique characteristics, such as clean code, MVC design (which separates functionality and presentation), and OOP concepts.
