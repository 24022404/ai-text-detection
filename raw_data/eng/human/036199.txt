The current state-of-the-art of the CRE task is Contrastive Replay (CRL) which is a replay-based method. Different from previous work, CRL maintains learned knowledge by introducing a contrastive replay mechanism that removes the uses of Softmax classifier and Cross-Entropy loss. It instead proposes to use Supervised Contrastive loss and Nearest Class Mean classifier to make the representation space well-separated. Although CRL outperforms older approaches that use traditional Cross-Entropy loss, it did not learn this pattern in-depth. As a result, it begs the question of why models with Cross-Entropy loss perform so poorly.
