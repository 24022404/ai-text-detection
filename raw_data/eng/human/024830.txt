Businesses must pay attention to building suitable warehouse management pro cesses, arranging and classifying items in the warehouse in a scientific manner, andusing technology applications to support management if they are to manage logis tics warehouses efficiently. In order to guarantee that the items are constantly in the finest condition, efficient warehouse management, in particular, must regularly monitor and inspect the commodities. Hence, inventory work is always crucial for all warehouses, not just logistics warehouses. Businesses may identify the precise quantity and quality of the actual items in the warehouse using the inventory, which also aids in the early identification of management process mistakes.
