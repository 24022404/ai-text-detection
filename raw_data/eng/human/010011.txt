The difference between the two methodsis that RClassMix selects classes with a probability P(c)while DACS randomly se lects half of the classes in the source images. The blue bars of Fig. 4.1 show that more pixels of the source images belong to the unusual classes are chosen and also pixels belong to common classes (road, sidewalk) are almost never selected with an extremely small probability.
