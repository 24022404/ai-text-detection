API A collection of specifications and protocols known as an API3are used to create and integrate application software. It’s also referred to as a contract between a supplier of information and a user of that information, outlining the content that the consumer (the call) and the producer (the producer) are obligated to deliver (the response). For instance, the API architecture for a weather service would need the user to provide their zip code and the producer to respond with two parts: the high temperature and the low temperature.
