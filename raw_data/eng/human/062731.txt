After rearranging the inventory, the user performs a recheck to ensure that the goods have been moved to the desired location. The inventory rearrangement screen displays the storage location before rearrangement, the desired location,and the storage location after rearrangement for each product’s RFID tag. Af ter the inventory rearrangement process is complete, the user can check the RFID management page to verify that the product’s storage location has been updated accurately according to the actual location.
