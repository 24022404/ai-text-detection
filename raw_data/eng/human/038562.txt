First, it is observed that without my proposed regularization, the model is the original CRL combined with CE loss, and its performances are poor compared to the original one, especially on TACRED. In detail, combining CE loss leads to drop 0.1%and 0.6 %on FewRel and TACRED datasets, respectively. It therefore shows the negative impacts of CE loss on continual learning. The reason why there is a large performance gap on TACRED is because the CE loss reduces the eigenvalues significantly. While on FewRel, model still performs well as CE loss does not hurt eigenvalues too much, see Figure 4.1.
