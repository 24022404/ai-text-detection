The objective of this project is to develop a multi-domain machine translation system. Notably, contemporary machine translation tools like Google Translate and Bing primarily concentrate on general domains, rendering them less effective in domains with specific or specialized vocabulary. Consequently, the translationsprovided by these tools might yield unfavorable outcomes and potential misunderst andings for users.
