In recent years, dimensional collapse ,  has attracted a surge of interest in representation learning field. Dimensional collapse happens when the projected features collapse into a low-dimensional manifold, which has been shown to harm the generalization of learned features.  verifies the connection between dimensional collapse and strong correlation, and shows that feature decorrelation can help mitigate the problem. Many works using feature decorrelation in self-supervised setting ,  has illustrated competitive performance in comparison with conventional contrastive learning methods.
