An RFID tag typically consists of a microprocessor for computation, an internal memory for storage, and an antenna for communication. The card’s memory can be read-only, write-once-multiple, or fully read-write. Basic components of an RFID tag:•Antenna Silicon Chips Chip cover material Power supplies (active and semi-passive tags only) There are three types of RFID tags in use today, active RFID tags and passive RFID tags and semi-passive RFID tags, each with their own characteristics and suitable for certain functions.
