The vocabulary of the NMT model is fixed. Any out of vocabulary (OOV) token is mapped to a special token < UNK > , which stands for unknown. The size of the vocabulary of an NMT model is chosen to balance the coverage over the processed tokens with a practical constraint on the size of the model. The vocabulary of an NMT model is usually limited to 30-40 thousand types. In the following discussion, I denote Σx,Σythe source vocabulary and the target vocabulary, respectively. .
