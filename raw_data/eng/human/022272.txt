This thesis has developed an automatic inventory procedure coupled with RFIDtags to implement automatic inventory with them. From there, companies can eas ily decide whether to perform automatic inventory based on their needs. After the inventory process is complete, the data are provided in detail so that firms may monitor, manage, and decide on the best course of action. Also, this study reuses the "Digital workspace system DXClan.com" APIs to carry out the task of storingdata and allocating tasks to the objects taking part in this inventory.
