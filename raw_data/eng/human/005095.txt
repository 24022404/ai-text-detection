The resolution is 2048 x 1024, and there are 2,975 training images, 500 validation images, and 1,525 test images in all. Each of these photographs has been meticulously annotated with pixel-level semantic labels, and each pixel is separated into 19 different categories. Since the test set of the synthetic-to-real adaptation does not contain ground truth labeling, evaluations of the validation set are performed.
