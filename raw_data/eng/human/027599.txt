By utilizing DistillBERT, I aim to reduce the computational and memory resources required for domain classification tasks, making it more efficient and practical for real-world applications. Despite its smaller size, DistillBERT maintains a consider able level of performance, making it a suitable choice for domain classification in the multi-domain machine translation system. Knowledge distillation  is primary technique used for training DistilBERT, as 3.2, hence the name "distilBert." During the training process, distilBert, or the student model, benefits from the guidance of the teacher model to enhance its performance.
