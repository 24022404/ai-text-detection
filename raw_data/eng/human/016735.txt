The testing scenario is applied to a small-scale Logistics warehouse with electronic devices such as laptops, computer monitors, and computer accessories instock. The testing data set is obtained from the inventory list at a logistics ware house of Phong Vu. The dataset includes information on the category of goods, product names, units of measurement, a list of physical storage locations in the warehouse, and the number of products that can be stored at each location. The testing equipment consists of one RFID card reader with a frequency of 13.56 MHz and 20 passive RFID tags.
