In modern logistics warehouses, there are numerous techniques to inventory the items. At warehouses where categorizing products is not too complex, the handcounting method is still frequently utilized. Also, it is customary in larger businesses to integrate scientific technologies in the management process. Each ap proach, though, has advantages and disadvantages of its own. In my thesis, I’vechosen to propose a solution based on Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) tech nology, which uses radio waves to identify objects.
