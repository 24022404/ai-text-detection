The objective of the ARNav project is to develop a user-friendly AR-based in door navigation app that can help users navigate more effectively and efficiently inside a building. The app will be designed to provide real-time, interactive, and intuitive navigation assistance, using AR technology to overlay virtual informationon the real environment. The scope of the project is to cover the design, devel opment, and testing of the ARNav app, with a focus on its key features, benefits, limitations, and areas for improvement.
