General use case diagramThe user has to have an enterprise account that has been authorized by the system administrator in order to access the application - Task Management system  DxClan. Users will be given certain access privileges that have been specified onthe system by the administrator for each function and role. The location of the em ployee, the organizational structure, and the level of the company’s power all affect the functions they may access and carry out. Users will be given permissions by the management according to their position, and they are only permitted to access and utilize the resources in the warehouse where they are employed.
