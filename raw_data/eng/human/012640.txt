The ServiceController class is responsible for handling service-related opera tions in the booking system. It operates on the Service entity, which contains details of various services offered by providers. The class includes methods for performing CRUD (Create, Read, Update, Delete) functions on services, enabling providers tomanage their service offerings effectively. Additionally, the ServiceController pro vides a viewing mechanism for customers to browse services and apply filters to find suitable options. The class contributes to maintaining a well-organized and streamlined service management environment.
