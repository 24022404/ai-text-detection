Discriminative learning of violent/non-violent behaviour’s using an SVM Classifier Two well-known datasets were used to test the proposed approach: ‘Hockey fight’ dataset  that contains 1000 video clips and the ‘Crowd Violence’ Dataset. The proposed ‘STACOG features + SVM’ model shown 91.38% accuracy in violence detection overcoming state-of-the-art methods like HOF+BoW, HNF+BoF, ViF+SVM, BiLSTM, GMOF, and others.
