Experiments Results Conclusion and Future Works2.1 Scope of Research This research focuses on Vietnamese text summarization, particularly in the context of abstractive multi-document summarization. Specifically, the author explored the use of pre-trained models and studied PRIMERA , a specialized model formulti-document summarization, due to its impressive performance. Unlike general purpose models like T5  and BART , PRIMERA is specifically designed for this task and incorporates two key components: the Gap Sentence Generation objective proposed in  and the Longformer architecture , which enables efficient processing of long sequences.
