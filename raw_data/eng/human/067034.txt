For continual learning, if the shape ( i.e.covariance) of the representation distribution of each old class does not change much after learning new tasks, the previous knowledge is well-preserved. Moreover, through the spectral analysis viewpoint, an eigenvector direction that is adjusted slightly after updating will have a small corresponding angle, and vice versa. Therefore, monitoring the corresponding angle can give helpful information about representation shift: eigenvectors that do not change much preserve knowledge well, while ones that significantly change do not carry helpful knowledge in terms of reducing catastrophic forgetting.
