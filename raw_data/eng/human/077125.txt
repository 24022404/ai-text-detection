In multiplayer action games, synchronization among players is the most sig nificant requirement. For instance, when you and your friend are playing a game together, at time t, you successfully defeat a boss. However, at time t + 30s, your friend still sees the boss alive, or at time t, you move to the second floor of a building, while your friend only sees you standing at the front door. Asynchronous or delayed updates can heavily impact the experience and are a crucial factor in determining the success of a multiplayer game.
