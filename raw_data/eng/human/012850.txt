I propose semantic-guided representation learning in the embedding space toaddress the problem of traditional self-learning neglecting explicit domain align ment, which may result in noise in pseudo labels. The approach is to immediately adopt global category prototypes computed on the source domain to direct thealignment between the source, mixed and target domains. Additionally, I devel oped a novel mixing approach that often pastes pixels from rare classes from the source images to the target images in order to address the imbalance problem in the dataset. As a result, the help model learned more data on these classes and improved their performance.
