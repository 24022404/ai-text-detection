Limited product experience.Although looking at product photographs on a website can give you a good idea of what it is like, it’s not the same as actually using the object, such as when you play a guitar, evaluate the picture quality of a television, or try on clothes. On shoppers may wind up purchasing goods that fall short of their expectations and need to be returned. In some circumstances, the cost of shipping a returned item to the merchant is borne by the customer. The capacity of customers to inspect and test e-commerce goods is anticipated to improve with augmented reality technology.
