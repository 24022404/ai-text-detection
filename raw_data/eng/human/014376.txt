RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) is a physical identification technology widely used in logistics warehouse management today. Data is transferred between RFID readers and tags affixed to products via radio waves in RFID devices. Fromthere, merchandise can be controlled and tracked more efficiently. RFID, in partic ular, helps the warehouse save a lot of time and money on inventory. Enterprises must overcome obstacles including investment costs, interoperability with current warehouse management systems, and data security in order to integrate RFID in the products management process.
