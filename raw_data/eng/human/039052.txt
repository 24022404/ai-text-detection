More specifically, a blockchain is a ar chain of connected blocks secured by cryptographic proofs. A blockchain is typically built as a distributed system that functions as a decentralized ledger. This means there are multiple copies of the ledger (distributed) and no single entity has sole control (decentralized). Simply put, each user participating in the blockchain network keeps an electronic copy of the blockchain data. Blockchain data is regularly updated with all the latest transactions and synchronized with the user’s copy.
