Applications that run on both the client-side (Angular2) and server-side can be created using TypeScript (NodeJS). TypeScript makes use of all the ECMAScript 2015 (ES6) features, including classes and modules. Prior to the creation of TypeScript, Google also created CoffeScript and Dart, libraries that supported the aforementioned ideas but made use of a whole different syntax. Thus, despite its later birth, TypeScript continues to get favorable reviews from programmers.
