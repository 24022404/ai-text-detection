To access this screen, the user selects View inventory details after performing an automatic inventory. This screen displays information including: (i) Commodity information, (ii) Shipment information - quantity of goods in the lot, (iii) list of RFID codes of that shipment and quantity of goods. on 1 RFID tag (iv) storage location of goods according to each RFID code. The red RFID code is the RFID code of the goods that are not found at the warehouse and with the warehouse location, the red shows that the goods are not in their correct location being stored on the system.
