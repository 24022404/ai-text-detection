Through extensive experimental evaluations, FedImp showcases its superior convergence rate compared to both FedAdp and FedAvg in various scenarios with non-IID data. Notably, FedImp reduces the number of communication rounds by up to 22.6%, 27.8%, and 25.4% on the EMNIST, CIFAR-10, and Large Movie Review datasets, respectively, when compared to FedAdp. Moreover, when compared to FedAvg, FedImp demonstrated even more significant reductions of up to 40.2%, 50.2%, and 16.4% on the same datasets. These outcomes underscore the impactful contributions of this thesis in recognizing and addressing the limitations of FedAdp, offering a promising solution in FedImp to enhance the effectiveness of FL in the face of non-IID data distributions.
