ResNet101-DeepLabv2 is typically used as afeature extractor segmentation head pair, or a variation of it, in segmentation adap tation techniques. The more cutting-edge DeepLabv3+ head is used in very fewnew methods. The ResNet50-DeepLabv2 was chosen to use a somewhat less com plex model (ResNet50) to speed up training. All models are pre-trained on COCOdataset. In the training phase, I chose RestNet101 as a feature extractor head. Af ter the final encoder layer, Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP) is added withdilation rates of [6,12,18,24]. The projection head His constructed by two succes sive convolutions (2048 hidden layer channels and 128 output channels) with oneintermediate ReLU layer.
