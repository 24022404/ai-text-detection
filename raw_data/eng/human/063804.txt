Evaluation on AICS - violence dataset To create a base for the AICS - violence dataset, we have evaluated the accuracies of some SOTA methods in action recognition in general and violence detection in particular namely Convolutional 3D (C3D) , Convolutional Long Short Term Memory (ConvLSTM) , 3D DenseNet , and Two-Stream Network for Violence Detection Using Separable Convolutional LSTM (TSSCL) .
