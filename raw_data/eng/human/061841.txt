Mixing has been successful in SSL for semantic segmentation when applyingstrong augmentations on unlabeled images for consistency regularization. The bi nary mixing technique CutMix , from one image, random rectangular areas are cut off and put onto another. A binary mask the same size as the images is used in this method, which is based on mask-based mixing, to combine two images. The ClassMix algorithm  took this idea a step further by replacing the mask usedfor mixing with one generated dynamically based on network predictions. The aug mentation technique takes half of the predicted classes for a given image and pastes the associated pixels onto a second image to create a significantly affected imagewhere the semantic bounds of objects are always closely followed.
