As users’ appetite for video content grew, a demand emerged for platforms that could efficiently host, stream, and share videos with a global audience. Platforms like YouTube, Vimeo, and Dailymotion were born out of this necessity, offering creators and users a space to upload, share, and discover an extensive range of videos. As users sought to connect, engage, and express themselves through video, these platforms evolved into massive digital ecosystems that revolutionized content consumption, entertainment, and on social interaction.
