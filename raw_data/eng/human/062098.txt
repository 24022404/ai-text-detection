Entropy is often employed as a tool in the evaluation of impurity or disorder inherent within the dataset linked to a specific node, particularly in structures like decision trees or complex information processing systems. The entropy of a node is higher when the data is more diverse or uncertain, and lower when the data is more homogeneous or certain.
