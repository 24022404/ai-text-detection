Motion Direction Inconsistency-Based Fight Detection for Multi view Surveillance Videos Yao et al. present a multiview fight detection technique based on optical flow statistical features and random forest . This technique may provide fast and reliable information to cyber-physical monitoring systems. Motion Direction Inconsistency (MoDI) and Weighted Motion Direction Inconsistency (WMoDI), two new descriptors, are developed to enhance the performance of current techniques for films with various filming perspectives and to address misjudgement on nonfighting activities like jogging and chatting.
