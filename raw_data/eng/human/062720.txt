The Usecase Specifications, being a detailed description of the functionality of the system, are essential for the development process. However, including them in the main report would make it too lengthy and difficult to read. Therefore, they have been placed in the Appendix section for those who require a more in-depth understanding of the system’s functions. The Appendix serves as a reference for developers and other technical staff who may need to refer to the specifications during the development and testing process.
