Mask RCNN + LSTM To identify violent behaviours of a single person, an ensemble model of the Mask RCNN and LSTM was proposed . Initially, human key points and masks were extracted, and then temporal information was captured. Experiments have been performed on datasets such as, ‘Weizmann’ , ‘KTH’ . The results demonstrated that the proposed model outperforms individual models showing a violence detection accuracy rate of 93.4% in its best result. The proposed approach is more relevant to the industry, which is beneficial to society in terms of security.
