JSON Web Tokens consist of three parts separated by dots (.), which are: header, payload, and signature. The header typically consists of two parts: the type of the token, which is JWT, and the signing algorithm being used. The payload contains the claims. Claims are statements about an entity (typically, the user) and additional data. To create the signature part, you have to take the encoded header, the encoded payload, a secret, and the algorithm specified in the header, and sign that.
