Gated Recurrent Unit Given its widespread success, numerous variants of the LSTM architecture havebeen explored. Most of these variants involve only minor modifications to the stan dard LSTM. However, the Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) represents a significantdeparture from the LSTM. Although the internal state of a GRU is somewhat com plex and less intuitive than that of an LSTM, it has fewer parameters. Consequently, GRUs are easier to train than LSTMs and require less training data.
