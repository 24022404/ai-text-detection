Proposed solution The EDF scheduling algorithmAs presented in the Chapter 4: Experiment and Evaluation, when a ticket is cre ated by a customer, the Admin will check the ticket’s information, then the Admin will approve and assign the ticket to which staff for supporting and solving this ticket. The most important point here is that the Admin needs to identify which policies from SLA criteria will be applied to this ticket. In real-time systems, due to different ticket priorities, ticket scheduling is often overloaded, which leads to a decrease in the success rate of ticket scheduling and the waste of system resources.
