Table 5.4 displays the number of communication rounds required to attain over 80.5% accuracy on the test set using a 4-layer CNN model in different testingscenarios. In the scenario with 7 balanced nodes and 3 imbalanced nodes, FedImp outperforms both FedAdp and FedAvg by achieving convergence in 258 rounds, while FedAdp and FedAvg require 272 and 291 rounds, respectively. Transitioning to the case of 5 balanced nodes and 5 imbalanced nodes, FedImp demonstrates a substantial improvement, requiring 332 rounds, as opposed to 460 for FedAdp and a significantly higher 667 for FedAvg. This signifies a decrease in the number of rounds by around 27.8% and 50.2% when comparing FedImp to FedAdp and FedAvg, respectively. In the scenario with 3 balanced nodes and 7 imbalanced nodes, while FedAvg cannot reach the target accuracy of over 80.5%, FedImp demonstrates a slower convergence, reaching convergence in 584 rounds, whereas FedAdp achieves the same in 471 rounds.
