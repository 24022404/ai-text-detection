Specifically, the Rouge scores (R-1, R-2) achieved by the PhoBERT tokenizer are significantly higher than those obtained by the other tokenizers. The average Rouge score (AVG) for the PhoBERT-large tokenizer was also the highest among all three tokenization approaches. The PhoBERT tokenizer demonstrates an impressive R-1 F1 score of 78.98 and an R-2 F1 score of 55.08 on the ViMs dataset, surpassing the performance of the 34k-vocab tokenizer, which achieves an R-1 F1 score of 76.05 and an R-2 F1 score of 52.92. The ViT5 tokenizer, on the other hand, showed the lowest Rouge scores among the three.
