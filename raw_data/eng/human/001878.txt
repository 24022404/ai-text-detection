There are many different artificial intelligence methods including machine learning, natural language processing (NLP), computer vision, robotics, and others. Machine learning is an important method in the field of artificial intelligence, in which algorithms are designed to analyze data and find patterns and rules. Natural language processing is a method that allows computers to understand and produce natural human language. Computer vision is a method that allows computers to understand and process images and videos. Robotics is a method that allows computers to control robots and automation systems. This research paper uses machine learning methods to classify security vulnerabilities present in smart contracts.
