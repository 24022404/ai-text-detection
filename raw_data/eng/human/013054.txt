Create inventory bill and perform automated inventory with RFID With the function of creating inventory checklists, there are two types: regular inventory checks and periodic inventory checks. For regular inventory checks, users are required to add each product they want to check to the list, while for periodic inventory checks, the system automatically takes all products in the warehouse for inventory checking. During the inventory check, the person in charge uses the RFID tag and performs the check by using the RFID reader to scan the RFID code at all areas and shelves in the warehouse and saves the collected data for the system to process. At the same time, during the scanning process, the quality control staff also visually inspects to ensure that all goods are still sealed and not tampered with, as well as their storage condition.
