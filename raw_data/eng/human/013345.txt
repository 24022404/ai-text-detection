Overfitting Overfitting occurs when a model learns the training data too well, to the point that it starts to memorize the noise or random fluctuations in the training datarather than capturing the underlying patterns or relationships. As a result, the model becomes too complex and specific to the training data, leading to poor performance on new, unseen data. Signs of overfitting include very high accuracy or low error on the training data, but poor performance on the test data.
