In scenarios involving non-IID data, the contributions of participating nodes to the training process are unequal. To address this, Hongda Wu et al. introduced the Federated Adaptive Weighting (FedAdp) algorithm in their work . This algorithm dynamically assigns varying weights for updating the global model based on each node’s contribution in every training round. The contribution of participatingnodes is first measured by the angle between the local gradient vector and the global gradient vector, and then, weight is quantified by a designed non-ar mapping function subsequently. The authors has shown that FL with FedAdp can reduce the number of communication rounds by up to 54.1% on MNIST dataset and up to 45.4% on FashionMNIST dataset, as compared to FedAvg algorithm.
