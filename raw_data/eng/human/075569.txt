In , the BART framework was introduced as a solution to overcome generative limitations. Thisframework follows a denoising sequence-to-sequence approach, wherein the pre training stage involves two key tasks: (1) corrupted text restoration and (2) original text reconstruction, i.e. translation. One of the strengths of BART lies in its flexibility, as both the core components, namely the BERT-based encoder and the autoregressive decoder, can be modified to suit specific needs, including pre-training schemes modification. BART has demonstrated remarkable effectiveness in various text generation tasks, particularly text summarization.
