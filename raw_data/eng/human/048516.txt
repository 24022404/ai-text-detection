We can see that the results here have improved further, but the execution time for detection has also increased. However, a drawback of the Multi-BERT model is the potential loss of token relationships at the boundaries of concatenated SecBERT blocks. This limitation may impact the model’s ability to effectively capture long-range dependencies and context across the entire input sequence.
