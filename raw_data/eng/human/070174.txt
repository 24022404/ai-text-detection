MBart use a standard sequence-to-sequence Transformer architecture , with 12 layers of encoder and 12 layers of decoder with model dimension of 1024 on 16 heads (680M parameters). MBart include an additional layer-normalization layer on top of both the encoder and decoder, which MBart found stabilized training at FP16 precision. In their work, following , the researchers utilize two types of noise in the generation process. Firstly, they remove sections of text and substitute them with a mask token. This involves masking approximately 35% of the words in each instance, with the span length randomly sampled based on a Poisson distribution each instance. The decoder input is the original text, but with a one-position offset.
