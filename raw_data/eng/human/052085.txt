This approach to working has helped practitioners of natural language processing produce exceptionally impressive text processing outcomes, such as language generation, summarization, translation, and other related tasks. Recurrent neural networks may no longer be the primary choice for developing language models because the Transformers have overcome the problems with long-term memory (by using attention) and computation speed (by getting rid of the recurrent parts).
