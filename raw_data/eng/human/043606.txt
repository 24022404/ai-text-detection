Vanilla RNN, LSTM, and GRU I have trained vanilla RNNs, LSTMs, and GRU-based models for 20 epochs each, using a learning rate of 0.001 and a batch size of 128, based on the first 500 opcodes from each malware sample. I have conducted multiple experiments with various other parameters. Additionally, I applied a dropout layer with 0.3 probabil-ity for all models with more than one layer.
