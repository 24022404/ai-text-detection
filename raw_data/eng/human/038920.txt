Unsupervised Domain Adaptation (UDA), a relatively new area of machine learning, emerged as a result of the increasing amount of data and the limited and expensive resources needed for annotation. Any labels for the target domain data are completely absent in unsupervised domain adaptation (UDA). A model that was trained on data from the source domain must independently adapt to the target domain. Style transfer, feature alignment and self-training are the three main categories of the existing methodologies for UDA of semantic segmentation.
