The application is first deployed by the google kubernetes engine. First, we cre ate a new cluster. The cluster on the engine will be the autoscaling one. GKE’s cluster autoscaler automatically resizes the number of nodes in a given node pool,based on the demands of your workloads. When demand is low, the cluster au toscaler scales back down to a minimum size that you designate. This can increase the availability of your workloads when you need it, while controlling costs. You don’t need to manually add or remove nodes or over-provision your node pools.
