RFID ( Radio Frequency Identification ) RFID  - Radio Frequency Identification, refers to identification using radiofrequencies. RFID is a system that automatically recognizes and tracks identify ing tags affixed to things using radio waves. Through the use of radio transceivertechnology, it is now feasible to monitor and control each thing while also recog nizing it as an object. Organizational managers can identify and manage assets andgadgets thanks to RFID tag technology. It applies to magnetic card locks in ho tels and resorts, item tagging in retail establishments, inventory systems and so on.
