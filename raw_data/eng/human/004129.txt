ISO 14443 includes many parts, including communication modes between cards and card readers, classes and protocols for security and anti-conflict, and technical requirements for smart card devices. and smart card reader. Currently, users need to fully support all 4 parts of ISO/IEC 14443 in a reader (called Proximity Coupling Device - PCD) and card (called Proximity integrated Circuit Cards - PICC). The PICC is used within 10cm of the reader antenna at 13.56MHz. The selection of this frequency is based on a number of technical reasons. ISO 14443 does not specify the operating system in the card and reader, because that is the copyright of each manufacturer.
