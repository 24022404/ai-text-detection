Precision: The precision for each class measures how accurate the model is when it predicts that class. For example, the precision of "zeroaccess" is thenumber of correctly classified "zeroaccess" samples divided by the total num ber of samples predicted as "zeroaccess."Recall: The recall for each class measures how well the model is able to iden tify samples belonging to that class. For example, the recall of "zeroaccess" is the number of correctly classified "zeroaccess" samples divided by the total number of actual "zeroaccess" samples.
