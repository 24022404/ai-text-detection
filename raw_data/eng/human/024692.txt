For example, simple weather forecast, people will use calculations and observations, records about the past weather to forecast the weather of the next days. How ever, what if there are extremely many observations made, maybe up to millions, billions of observations, then it is impossible for humans to perform calculations on such big data. Furthermore, calculations with such large data can be error-prone and lead to erroneous predictions. At this time, applying machine learning to let computers learn from observations recorded in the past, they can predict future weather with much higher accuracy than humans predict.
