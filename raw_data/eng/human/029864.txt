Sequence diagram of use case update product Figure 4.9: Sequence diagram of use case update productSuppliers can update product as described in figure 4.9. When they request to update a product, the system will check if any new images or videos will upload to blob storage. After that, the system will update the product information in the database and elastic search. Finally, the system updates the stock inventory of that product. If the process has any error, the system will roll back all action and notify to user.
