ISO - 14443ISO (International Organization for Standardization)  and IEC (Inter national Electrotechnical Commission) establishes an international council forglobal technical standardization. ISO is a pioneer in the development of international technical standards, to ensure that products, services and processes developed and used globally comply with common rules and standards. , thereby help ing to increase efficiency, ensure safety and improve quality. ISO has developed more than 23,000 different standards, including those in the industrial, technical, medical, environmental, information and communication sectors, and more. These standards are considered international standards and are widely adopted across the globe.
