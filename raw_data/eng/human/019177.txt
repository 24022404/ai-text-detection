Unity 3.0 (2010) In September 2010, Unity 3.0 was released, increasing the en gine’s graphical capabilities for desktop computers and gaming consoles. Alongwith Android support, Unity 3 included delayed rendering, a built-in tree edi tor, native font rendering, automatic UV mapping, audio filters, and the BeastLightmap tool from Illuminate Labs. VentureBeat noted in 2012, "Few businesseshave helped the flow of independently developed games as much as Unity Tech nologies. Its tools are used by more than 1.3 million game creators to produce cutting-edge graphics for their iOS, Android, console, PC, and web-based games.
