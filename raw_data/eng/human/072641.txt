Talking about scalability , I can confidently say that every modules in this project can be scaled horizontally with ease - which means instead of having to turn off themachine, add more or replace with better hardwares, we can increase the computa tional of the system by adding more nodes to it. This approach has zero downtime since no hardware needs to be turned off during the process and end-users will not even notice that there was an upgrade. With sufficient resources and investments, those microservices can be integrated into state-of-the-art cloud solutions such as Kubernetes in a matter of days.
