Even after spending a lot of effort, the buyer is still not satisfied with the product that has been discovered. Additionally, a significant portion of users either don’t fully and precisely specify their search criteria at the time of purchase or they do so in response to the products that the system displays (or in response to the information produced by the system). The search engines are unable to resolve the issue at that point. Furthermore, the best-selling products only make up a small portion of the whole product, and the long list of products that follow them provides very little revenue. This phenomenon is known as the "long tail" in commerce.
