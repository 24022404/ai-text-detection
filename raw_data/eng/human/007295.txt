This thesis has developed a common warehousing procedure to assign RFID numbers to items during the importation process. An RFID code will be assigned in conjunction with batching the products throughout the import process. A new consignment will be given an RFID code as soon as it enters the warehouse for management and tracking purposes. Also, each RFID tag has the ability to retrieve the information related to the goods that are attached to it. Also, to complete theprocess of storing the items, this study reuses APIs in the "Digital workspace sys tem DXClan.com" system.
