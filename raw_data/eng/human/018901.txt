Once the loading is complete, the GameManager will receive the signal and trigger all the necessary classes to start performing their tasks to start the gameplay. Triggeredclasses include SpawnerManager, BuffManager, PlayerManager, and GameMan ager also calls CanvasGameplay to appear.
