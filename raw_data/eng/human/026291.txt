Blockchain technology has emerged as a ground-breaking breakthrough that has the power to alter entire industries and the way in which digital transactions arecarried out. It offers a decentralized, open, and transparent platform for safe, per manent record-keeping, doing away with middlemen and promoting peer-to-peerinteractions. This chapter offers a succinct overview of blockchain technology, emphasizing its historical background, the issue it tries to address, and the main con tributions of its initial author.
