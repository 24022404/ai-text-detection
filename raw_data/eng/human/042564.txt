Blockchain  is a ledger that records the balance information and transaction history of all accounts participating in its transaction chain. Each block in the blockchain system contains information and is called a "block". These blocks contain data about the creation time, along with transaction data and a timecode. They are linked to the previous block, forming a chain of information. Once data is accepted by the network, it cannot be changed or altered. Therefore, blockchain was created as a solution to prevent fraud and ensure the integrity of data in transactions.
