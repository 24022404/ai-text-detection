Netcode type Unlike a local game where the inputs of all players are processed and executed instantly in the same simulation or instance of the game, an on game containsseveral parallel simulations (one for each player) where the inputs from their re spective players are received instantly, while the inputs for the same frame from other players arrive with a certain delay. The goal of video game networking is to create the illusion that two or more players are sharing a single environment . It is the job of network engineers to ensure these simulations stay in sync and create a overall responsive and crisp experience for players under various internet conditions. The extend to which desynchronize resolutions are enforced dependson genres of game. Generally, there are 2 solutions to the netcode problem: delay based and rollback.
