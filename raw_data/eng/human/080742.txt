Transformers in Hugging Face allows researchers, developers, and NLP enthusiasts to quickly and efficiently leverage state-of-the-art transformer architectures such as BERT, GPT, RoBERTa, and more. These models have achieved remarkable success in various NLP tasks, including text classification, question-answering, language translation, and sentiment analysis. The library offers a unified and consistent API, making it straightforward to use and switch between different transformer models and architectures seamlessly.
