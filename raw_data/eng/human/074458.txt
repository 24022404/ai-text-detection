However, in the dataset, we encountered certain vulnerabilities that had an insufficient number of samples, making it difficult for the model to extract meaningful patterns or insights. Consequently, these vulnerabilities were deemed unsuitable for training purposes and were subsequently excluded from the dataset. As a result, we focused our analysis on four prevalent vulnerabilities that exhibited a sufficient number of samples: timestamp dependence, outdated solidity version, frozen Ether, and delegate call injection.
