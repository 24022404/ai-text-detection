The dataset is quite large with around 15 milion documents and it takes about 2 to 3 weeks to preprocess the dataset. After that, I pretrain the model with 4 NVIDIA A100 40GB GPUs and it takes 4 days for the model to converge, however, the validation loss is quite high, at about 2.16, while the validation loss of the English PRIMERA version is around 1 when being evaluated in English pretraining dataset.
