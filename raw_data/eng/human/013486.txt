Security - combining the potential benefits of blockchain smart-contract, SSS,and Pedersen DKG protocol. The security infrastructure of the system is estab lished upon the utilization of blockchain smart contracts. Programmable contracts,operating on a decentralized network, guarantee the attributes of transparency, im mutability, and tamper-proof execution of operations. Shamir secret sharing is usedto fortify the protection of cryptographic keys and other forms of private infor mation. This method entails breaking up a secret into smaller pieces and givingthem to several parties. The Petersen DKG protocol distributes and secures cryp tographic key generation, ensuring system security. This system lets a group of people generate a shared cryptographic key without anyone having the full key.
