Pretraining step a, Pretraining with Newscorpus In the experiment section of my thesis, I conducted an analysis and evaluation of the Vietnamese text summarization task. The motivation behind this experiment stems from the scarcity of pretraining multi-document datasets in the Vietnamese language. To address this limitation, I leveraged the Newscorpus dataset, initiallydesigned as a single-document dataset, and transformed it into a pseudo multi document dataset. Each document within the dataset was divided into smaller parts, taking into consideration the length of the document. This segmentation resulted in segments ranging from 2 to 5 parts, enabling a more robust and diverse dataset for training.
