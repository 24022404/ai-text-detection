Another prominent English dataset is the Newshead dataset , which contains a vast collection of English stories with a total of 369,940 stories and 932,571 unique URLs. For my research, I utilized 359,940 stories for training and reserved 5,000 stories each for validation and testing purposes. Each news story in the dataset consists of a minimum of three and a maximum of five articles. The dataset was curated from news stories published between May 2018 and May 2019. To construct the dataset, a clustering algorithm was employed to group articles based on their content similarity within specific time windows. From each cluster, up to five representative articles were chosen, which were then used to generate concise heads for the stories. To ensure the quality and relevance of the heads,curators from a crowd-sourcing platform were engaged to provide concise summaries limited to 35 characters, effectively capturing the key information covered in each story.
