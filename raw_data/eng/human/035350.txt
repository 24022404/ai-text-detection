In the domain of blockchain-based decentralized applications (DApps), the executor plays a crucial role in facilitating the execution of blockchain network transactions. As the backend component of a DApp, the executor functions as an intermediary between the user-facing frontend and the blockchain. It is primarily re sponsible for establishing a connection to the blockchain, submitting transactionson behalf of users, and managing various interactions with the smart contract. Management of the user’s private key or mnemonic is a fundamental aspect of an ex ecutor’s functionality. A crucial piece of cryptographic information, the private key enables the authentication of transactions and verifies the identity of blockchain users. In some implementations, the private key is stored as a mnemonic, whichis a string of phrases that serves as a human-readable representation of the key.
