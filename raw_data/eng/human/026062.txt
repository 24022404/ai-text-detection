The second gate is called the input gate, which takes Xtas its input. It is dual in nature, because Xtand the hidden state from the previous cell are passed through here normalizes the data by forcing the input into the [−1,1]range. The Sigmoid function once again maps the combination to [0,1]range, indicating which parts of the input must be retained. After that, what must be maintained and what must be forgotten mix to form the result. This is passed to the third gate, the output gate.
