Object detection remains a fundamental challenge in computer vision, with increasing demands for accuracy and efficiency in real-time applications. This paper introduces HAR-Net, a novel single-stage object detection framework that leverages joint learning of hybrid attention mechanisms to enhance feature representation and detection performance. HAR-Net integrates both spatial and channel attention modules into its architecture, allowing the network to dynamically focus on relevant regions and features at multiple levels. The proposed method employs an end-to-end training strategy, effectively combining attention-driven feature refinement with robust object localization in a unified network. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that HAR-Net achieves superior detection accuracy and speed compared to existing single-stage detectors, particularly in scenarios with dense and overlapping objects. The proposed hybrid attention approach contributes to improved robustness against background noise and scale variations. Our findings suggest that HAR-Net offers a compelling solution for practical object detection tasks, combining high precision, efficiency, and adaptability. Keywords: object detection, HAR-Net, hybrid attention, single-stage detector, computer vision, attention mechanisms.
