This paper presents a novel real-time global inference network designed specifically for one-stage referring expression comprehension (REC). Referring expression comprehension is the task of localizing an object within an image described by a natural language expression. Existing approaches often rely on multi-stage architectures or local context modeling, which can limit efficiency and global reasoning capabilities, hindering real-world deployment. To address these challenges, we propose an end-to-end deep neural network that fuses global visual and linguistic features in a single-stage pipeline. Our method employs a cross-modal attention mechanism to effectively capture dependencies between language expressions and the entire visual scene, eliminating the need for region proposals or sequential refinements. Experimental evaluations on benchmark REC datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art accuracy while significantly reducing inference time compared to conventional multi-stage and region-based methods. Furthermore, ablation studies verify the benefits of global inference and real-time processing enabled by our architecture. The proposed network advances the field by enabling fast and accurate REC, facilitating applications in real-time human-computer interaction, robotics, and visual question answering.  
**Keywords:** referring expression comprehension, global inference, real-time, cross-modal attention, one-stage network, deep learning, visual grounding.
