Face image demorphing poses a significant challenge in forensic investigation, particularly when attempting to restore the accomplice's facial features from a morphed image. This paper introduces FD-GAN, a Face-Demorphing Generative Adversarial Network designed to address this challenge. FD-GAN employs a novel architecture incorporating a de-morphing network and a feature disentanglement module to separate and restore the individual identities present in a morphed face image. The de-morphing network leverages adversarial training to generate a set of plausible accomplice face images, while the feature disentanglement module ensures identity-specific features are accurately represented. Experimental results on a synthesized morphed face dataset and real-world images demonstrate that FD-GAN outperforms state-of-the-art face reconstruction and deblurring methods in terms of visual quality and identity preservation metrics. The proposed method offers a valuable tool for law enforcement and forensic analysis, enabling the recovery of crucial information about potential accomplices. Keywords: Face demorphing, generative adversarial network, forensic analysis, face reconstruction, image restoration, identity disentanglement.
