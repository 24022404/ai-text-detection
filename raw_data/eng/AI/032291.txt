In the rapidly advancing field of autonomous driving, accurate and reliable environmental perception is crucial. This paper introduces LEAD, a novel LiDAR Extender designed to enhance the current LiDAR systems used in autonomous vehicles. LEAD aims to overcome the limitations in range and resolution of existing LiDAR technologies, thereby improving vehicle perception and safety.

Methods/Approach: The proposed LEAD system integrates advanced signal processing algorithms alongside a hybrid sensor fusion approach, which synergizes data from LiDAR with complementary sensor modalities like cameras and radar. This integration enhances the spatial recognition capabilities of the vehicle's perception system. Our methodology focuses on optimizing data fusion and processing speed to ensure real-time applicability in dynamic driving environments.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that the LEAD system significantly extends the effective range and resolution of traditional LiDAR systems without elevating computation costs. Our results showcase up to a 30% improvement in object detection accuracy and a 25% increase in environment mapping fidelity compared to state-of-the-art LiDAR configurations. Performance assessments in various driving scenarios confirm the system's robustness and adaptability to diverse environmental conditions.

Conclusion/Implications: The LEAD system's enhancements to LiDAR functionality present substantial advancements in the field of autonomous driving. By improving LiDAR performance, LEAD contributes to safer, more reliable autonomous vehicle operations. Potential applications extend beyond automotive contexts, offering implications for robotics, industrial automation, and intelligent transportation systems. The introduction of LEAD marks a significant step towards achieving comprehensive and accurate environmental perception, a cornerstone for the future of fully autonomous vehicles.

Keywords: LiDAR, autonomous driving, sensor fusion, environmental perception, real-time systems, autonomous vehicles.
