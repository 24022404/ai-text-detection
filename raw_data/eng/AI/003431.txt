Accurate and interpretable sleep staging is vital for diagnosing sleep disorders and advancing sleep research. However, most state-of-the-art automated sleep staging methods rely heavily on deep learning models that, despite high accuracy, function as black boxes and lack transparency for clinical users. This paper introduces SLEEPER, a novel and interpretable sleep staging framework that leverages prototype learning guided by expert-defined rules. The proposed method first encodes polysomnographic (PSG) data into meaningful representations and identifies prototypical epochs that align with sleep stage definitions established by clinicians. By integrating expert rules directly into the prototype selection process, SLEEPER ensures that each decision made by the model is traceable to clinically validated features, enhancing both trust and usability. Experimental evaluations on publicly available sleep datasets demonstrate that SLEEPER achieves competitive accuracy compared to traditional deep learning approaches while offering clear rationales for stage assignments. Our method also enables clinicians to visualize and validate the learned prototypes, opening new pathways for collaborative model refinement and domain adaptation. The results support the feasibility of interpretable and reliable AI-assisted sleep staging, enabling broader adoption in clinical contexts. Keywords: sleep staging, interpretability, prototype learning, expert rules, machine learning, polysomnography, clinical decision support.
