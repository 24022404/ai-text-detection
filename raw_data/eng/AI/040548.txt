This paper addresses the critical challenge of optimizing cyber-physical systems (CPS) through Design Space Exploration (DSE). Cyber-physical systems, which integrate physical processes with computational components, require sophisticated design strategies to ensure high performance, reliability, and efficiency. 

Methods/Approach: We present DISPATCH, a novel framework for DSE which leverages advanced algorithms to methodically explore the design space of CPS. The approach incorporates multi-objective optimization techniques, enabling the evaluation of trade-offs among various design parameters. Our framework integrates both simulation and analytical modeling to assess system behavior under diverse conditions.

Results/Findings: Through extensive experimentation, DISPATCH demonstrated significant improvements in optimizing CPS designs, achieving up to a 30% increase in system performance and efficiency compared to traditional approaches. The framework's ability to handle complex multi-objective tasks provides a competitive edge in both academic and industrial settings, showcasing superior adaptability and scalability.

Conclusion/Implications: DISPATCH offers a substantial contribution to the field of CPS by enhancing the understanding of the design space and providing robust solutions that accommodate the dynamic requirements of modern systems. The application of DISPATCH can lead to advancements in various domains, including automotive, aerospace, and smart grid systems, where optimizing the interaction between physical and digital components is crucial. Key keywords: Cyber-Physical Systems, Design Space Exploration, Multi-Objective Optimization, System Performance, Framework, Simulation.
