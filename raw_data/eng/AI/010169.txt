Continuous blood pressure (CBP) monitoring offers significant advantages in healthcare management, but traditional methods are invasive. Photoplethysmography (PPG) provides a non-invasive alternative, yet developing robust, personalized models remains a challenge due to data heterogeneity and privacy concerns. This paper proposes a federated learning (FL) approach to estimate CBP from PPG signals. Our method enables collaborative model training across multiple decentralized datasets without sharing raw patient data, addressing privacy issues. We developed a novel FL framework incorporating personalized layers to capture individual-specific physiological characteristics. The proposed approach was evaluated on a benchmark dataset and compared to traditional centralized learning and conventional FL methods. Results demonstrate that our personalized federated learning method achieves superior accuracy in CBP estimation, as measured by mean absolute error (MAE) and root mean squared error (RMSE), while preserving data privacy. This study highlights the potential of FL in developing robust and personalized CBP estimation models, paving the way for improved non-invasive blood pressure monitoring and personalized healthcare.

**Keywords:** Continuous Blood Pressure, Photoplethysmography (PPG), Federated Learning, Personalized Modeling, Privacy-Preserving, Non-Invasive Monitoring.
