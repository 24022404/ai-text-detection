Accurate camera localization is a fundamental requirement for various applications in augmented reality, robotics, and autonomous navigation. This paper presents AtLoc, an attention-guided deep learning framework designed to enhance camera pose estimation from visual inputs. The proposed model leverages an attention mechanism integrated with convolutional neural networks to automatically focus on salient image regions that are most informative for localization tasks. Our approach processes single-view RGB images and produces precise six degrees-of-freedom (6-DoF) camera poses. Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets demonstrate that AtLoc surpasses existing state-of-the-art methods in both accuracy and robustness, especially in challenging scenes with repetitive structures or dynamic elements. Notably, the attention modules improve pose prediction by effectively filtering out irrelevant background information, leading to significant performance gains. The results highlight the potential of attention-augmented architectures in advancing the field of visual localization. AtLoc offers practical implications for real-time location-aware systems and can be integrated into a wide range of vision-based applications. Keywords: camera localization, attention mechanism, deep learning, pose estimation, computer vision, image-based localization.
