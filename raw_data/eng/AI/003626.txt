Manga colorization is a challenging task due to the diverse artistic styles and intricate line art. This paper introduces a novel painting style-aware manga colorization method based on Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs). Our approach incorporates a style embedding module to capture the unique painting styles present in different manga, guiding the colorization process to generate visually appealing and consistent results. Specifically, we utilize a conditional GAN architecture, where the generator learns to map grayscale manga images to colorized versions conditioned on the style embedding. A discriminator is employed to distinguish between real and generated colorized manga, enhancing the realism and fidelity of the output. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art approaches in terms of visual quality and style consistency, as evaluated by both quantitative metrics and subjective user studies. The proposed framework enables automated and high-quality manga colorization, offering significant potential for content creation and preservation. Keywords: Manga Colorization, Generative Adversarial Networks, Style Transfer, Image Synthesis, Deep Learning.
