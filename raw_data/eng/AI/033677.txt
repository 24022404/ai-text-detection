This paper proposes FFA-Net, a novel deep learning-based approach for single image dehazing, aiming to restore high-quality images from hazy inputs. The objective is to effectively remove haze and improve image visibility, addressing the challenges of traditional dehazing methods. Our approach employs a Feature Fusion Attention Network, which integrates multi-scale features and attention mechanisms to selectively focus on informative regions. The network is designed to learn a non-linear mapping between hazy and clear images, leveraging the strengths of both convolutional neural networks and attention-based architectures. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of FFA-Net over state-of-the-art dehazing methods, with significant improvements in image quality metrics such as PSNR and SSIM. The proposed method has potential applications in various fields, including autonomous driving, surveillance, and image processing. Key contributions of this work include the introduction of a feature fusion attention module and a comprehensive evaluation framework for single image dehazing. Relevant keywords: single image dehazing, deep learning, attention mechanisms, feature fusion, image restoration.
