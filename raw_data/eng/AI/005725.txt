Point cloud registration is a fundamental problem in 3D computer vision with applications in robotics, autonomous driving, and augmented reality. While many feature-based registration methods exist, they often struggle with noisy data, outliers, and variations in point density. This paper introduces StickyPillars, a novel approach for robust and efficient feature matching on point clouds using Graph Neural Networks (GNNs). Our method first voxelizes the point cloud into pillar-shaped structures, then constructs a sparse graph where nodes represent pillars and edges connect neighboring pillars. A GNN is trained to learn robust feature embeddings for each pillar, capturing both geometric and contextual information. Keypoint matching is performed by finding nearest neighbors in the learned feature space, followed by geometric verification to filter out false positives. We demonstrate that StickyPillars achieves state-of-the-art performance on several challenging benchmark datasets, exhibiting superior accuracy and robustness compared to existing methods, especially in the presence of noise and low overlap. Furthermore, the sparse graph representation enables efficient computation, making it suitable for real-time applications. The results highlight the effectiveness of GNNs for learning robust 3D features and improving point cloud registration accuracy and efficiency.
Keywords: Point Cloud Registration, Graph Neural Networks, Feature Matching, 3D Computer Vision, Robotics.
