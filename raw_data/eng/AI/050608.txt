This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing the efficiency and stability of reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms by developing optimal learning rate policies. Traditional RL methods often rely on fixed or manually tuned learning rates, which can hinder convergence speed and lead to suboptimal performance. To overcome these limitations, we propose a novel framework that dynamically adjusts the learning rate based on real-time feedback from the training process, leveraging reward trajectories and loss landscape analysis. Our approach incorporates adaptive policy selection, using heuristic and data-driven strategies to fine-tune the learning rate at each episode or step, thereby improving the overall learning dynamics. Experimental evaluations are conducted on benchmark RL environments, demonstrating significant improvements in convergence speed, cumulative rewards, and robustness compared to conventional fixed and scheduled learning rate schemes. The proposed method demonstrates generalizability across multiple RL architectures, including value-based and policy-based algorithms. The results highlight the potential of adaptive learning rate policies to accelerate training and achieve superior performance in complex decision-making tasks. This work contributes to the advancement of RL by providing a practical solution for automated learning rate optimization, with implications for real-world applications in robotics, autonomous systems, and intelligent control. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, learning rate optimization, adaptive policy, convergence, algorithm performance.
