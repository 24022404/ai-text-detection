In the system, the Helpdesk team and the Customer each play distinct yet interconnected roles, ensuring seamless operations and effective problem resolution. The Helpdesk team is primarily responsible for managing accounts, handling various ticketing processes, participating in chat communications, and generating or overseeing systematic reports. These responsibilities enable the Helpdesk team to maintain the system, address issues swiftly, and provide valuable insights through report analysis. On the other hand, the Customer is tasked with their own account management, submitting requests for assistance, engaging in chat conversations for real-time support, and providing feedback to improve the service.
