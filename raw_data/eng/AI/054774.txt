This paper investigates the challenge of user association in 5G and beyond networks, where optimizing network performance requires intelligent and adaptable user assignment strategies. We propose a novel framework that combines transfer learning and distributed decision-making to achieve efficient and transferable user association policies across diverse network environments. Our approach leverages a deep reinforcement learning model trained on a source network and then efficiently transferred to new target networks with different characteristics, significantly reducing the training overhead. Furthermore, we develop a distributed algorithm that enables individual base stations to make autonomous user association decisions based on local observations and limited information exchange, enhancing scalability and robustness. Simulation results demonstrate that our proposed framework achieves significant improvements in terms of network throughput, user fairness, and load balancing compared to traditional user association methods and centralized approaches. The transferable nature of the learned policies facilitates rapid deployment and adaptation in dynamic network conditions, making it a promising solution for next-generation wireless networks.

Keywords: User Association, 5G, Beyond 5G, Transfer Learning, Distributed Algorithm, Deep Reinforcement Learning, Wireless Networks.
