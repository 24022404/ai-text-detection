This paper proposes a novel Multiresolution Graph Variational Autoencoder (MGVAE) designed to learn compact and meaningful representations of graph-structured data at multiple scales. The objective is to address the challenges of graph representation learning by capturing both local and global patterns in a unified framework. Our approach combines the strengths of graph convolutional networks and variational autoencoders, enabling the model to learn multiresolution representations that preserve the structural properties of the input graphs. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of MGVAE in graph classification, clustering, and link prediction tasks, outperforming state-of-the-art methods. The key findings highlight the importance of multiresolution representations in graph learning, leading to improved performance and interpretability. This research contributes to the development of more powerful and flexible graph representation learning models, with potential applications in network analysis, recommendation systems, and computer vision. Key keywords: graph neural networks, variational autoencoders, multiresolution representation learning, graph classification, link prediction.
