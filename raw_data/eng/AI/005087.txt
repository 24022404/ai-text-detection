Reinforcement Learning (RL) systems are increasingly deployed in real-world applications, but their vulnerability to adversarial attacks, particularly reward poisoning, poses significant risks. This paper addresses the critical challenge of defending RL agents against reward poisoning attacks, where adversaries manipulate reward signals to degrade learning performance or induce harmful behaviors. We propose a novel defense mechanism that leverages anomaly detection and robust optimization techniques to identify and mitigate malicious reward manipulations. Our approach integrates a dynamic reward filtering framework that distinguishes between legitimate and poisoned rewards, ensuring the integrity of the learning process. Through extensive experiments on benchmark RL environments, we demonstrate that our method significantly reduces the impact of reward poisoning attacks while maintaining high learning efficiency. Compared to existing defenses, our solution achieves superior robustness and scalability across diverse attack scenarios. The proposed framework not only enhances the security of RL systems but also provides a foundation for developing resilient learning algorithms in adversarial settings. This research contributes to advancing the field of secure RL by offering a practical and effective defense strategy against reward poisoning, with potential applications in autonomous systems, robotics, and cybersecurity.  

**Keywords**: Reinforcement Learning, Reward Poisoning, Anomaly Detection, Robust Optimization, Adversarial Attacks, Secure RL.
