This paper presents a novel approach to real-time dense 3D reconstruction by combining Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) with multi-spectral photometric stereo. The objective is to achieve accurate and efficient 3D modeling of complex scenes, addressing the limitations of traditional SLAM methods in terms of textureless surfaces and low-light conditions. Our approach leverages the strengths of both SLAM and photometric stereo, utilizing a multi-spectral lighting system to capture detailed surface reflectance and a SLAM framework to estimate camera poses and reconstruct the scene. The results demonstrate significant improvements in reconstruction accuracy and robustness, particularly in scenes with varying albedo and specularity. Our method achieves real-time performance, making it suitable for applications such as robotics, augmented reality, and 3D scanning. The key contributions of this work include the development of a multi-spectral photometric stereo system, the integration of SLAM with photometric stereo, and the demonstration of real-time dense 3D reconstruction capabilities. This research has important implications for computer vision, robotics, and 3D reconstruction, and paves the way for future work in multi-spectral 3D modeling and scene understanding. Key keywords: SLAM, photometric stereo, multi-spectral imaging, real-time 3D reconstruction, computer vision, robotics.
