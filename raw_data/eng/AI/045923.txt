Time series data are increasingly prevalent in critical applications, making their robustness to adversarial attacks a significant concern. This paper investigates the vulnerability of time series classification models to adversarial perturbations. We introduce a novel attack framework, Temporal Gradient Iterative Attack (TGIA), which crafts adversarial examples by iteratively perturbing the time series data based on the gradient of the classification loss function. TGIA incorporates a temporal smoothing constraint to ensure the generated perturbations are realistic and difficult to detect. We evaluate TGIA against state-of-the-art time series classification models using benchmark datasets, demonstrating its effectiveness in significantly reducing classification accuracy with minimal, imperceptible perturbations. The results reveal the fragility of existing models to adversarial attacks and highlight the need for robust defense mechanisms. This work provides valuable insights into the adversarial landscape of time series data and offers a foundation for developing more resilient time series analysis techniques. Keywords: Adversarial attacks, time series, security, machine learning, robustness, classification.
