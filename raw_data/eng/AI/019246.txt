This paper introduces a novel approach for generating high-quality bokeh effects in images through depth-aware blending of smoothed images. The objective is to create visually appealing out-of-focus blur, mimicking the aesthetic qualities of professional photography. Our method utilizes a depth map, either captured or estimated, to guide the blending process. Specifically, we generate multiple smoothed versions of the input image with varying blur radii and then employ a depth-aware weighting scheme to combine these blurred images. This blending strategy allows for precise control over the blur amount at different depths, resulting in a realistic and customizable bokeh effect. We compare our method against existing bokeh simulation techniques and demonstrate improved performance in terms of visual quality and realism. Experimental results show that our approach effectively reduces artifacts, such as halos, often present in other methods, leading to a more natural and pleasing bokeh effect. This technique is applicable to both still images and video, offering a versatile solution for enhancing visual content. Keywords: Bokeh effect, Depth-aware blending, Image smoothing, Image processing, Blur simulation.
