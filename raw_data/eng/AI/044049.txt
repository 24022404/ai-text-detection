This paper introduces LIT, a novel block-wise intermediate representation training approach designed to achieve efficient model compression for deep neural networks. The objective is to reduce the computational cost and memory requirements of large models while preserving their accuracy. LIT employs a unique training strategy that focuses on intermediate representations at the block level, allowing for more effective pruning and quantization. The method utilizes a combination of knowledge distillation and sparse regularization to guide the compression process. Experimental results demonstrate that LIT outperforms existing compression techniques, achieving significant reductions in model size and computational overhead while maintaining competitive performance on various benchmark datasets. The proposed approach has important implications for deploying deep learning models on resource-constrained devices and edge computing platforms, enabling wider adoption of AI technologies in real-world applications. Key contributions include improved compression ratios, accelerated inference speeds, and enhanced model interpretability, making LIT a valuable tool for researchers and practitioners in the field of model compression, deep learning, and AI.
