This paper introduces the Face Transformer, a novel deep learning approach for face recognition tasks. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of face recognition systems by leveraging the strengths of transformer architectures. Our method utilizes a transformer-based model to learn robust and discriminative facial features, which are then used for recognition. The Face Transformer is trained on a large-scale face dataset and achieves state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark face recognition datasets. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, with significant improvements in recognition accuracy and reduced computational requirements. The Face Transformer has important implications for various applications, including security, surveillance, and social media. By combining the benefits of transformer models with the specific requirements of face recognition, our research contributes to the development of more accurate and efficient face recognition systems, with potential applications in AI, computer vision, and machine learning. Key keywords: face recognition, transformer, deep learning, facial features, AI models.
