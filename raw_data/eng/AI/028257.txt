This paper addresses the challenge of effectively optimizing policies in model-based reinforcement learning (MBRL) under environment uncertainty. We propose an uncertainty-aware model-based policy optimization algorithm that explicitly incorporates model uncertainty into the policy optimization process. Our approach leverages Bayesian neural networks to represent the environment dynamics and quantify the uncertainty associated with model predictions. This uncertainty is then incorporated into the policy optimization objective, encouraging the policy to be robust to potential model errors. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our algorithm on a range of continuous control tasks, showing significant improvements in sample efficiency and performance compared to existing MBRL methods that do not explicitly account for model uncertainty. Results indicate that the proposed approach leads to more stable and reliable policy learning, particularly in environments with high uncertainty. Keywords: Model-Based Reinforcement Learning, Policy Optimization, Uncertainty Quantification, Bayesian Neural Networks, Robust Control.
