This research aims to address the challenge of computation offloading in digital twin networks, an essential component for realizing efficient and responsive cyber-physical systems. Digital twins enable the virtual representation of physical entities and require robust mechanisms to manage computational tasks across a network of interconnected devices under uncertainty.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel deep reinforcement learning (DRL) approach to optimize stochastic computation offloading within digital twin networks. The model leverages a custom-designed neural network architecture tailored for dynamic and uncertain environments, efficiently learning optimal offloading policies. By integrating DRL, our method adapts to varying network conditions and task complexities, reducing the overhead and latency associated with traditional offloading strategies.

Results/Findings: The proposed DRL-based offloading method demonstrates superior performance in simulation scenarios, achieving significant improvements in computational efficiency and resource utilization compared to baseline methods. Our experiments indicate a reduction in latency and energy consumption while maintaining high levels of task completion accuracy. The adaptability of the model to fluctuating network conditions underlines its practicality for real-world applications.

Conclusion/Implications: This study contributes an innovative DRL framework for stochastic computation offloading, providing valuable insights into enhancing digital twin networks' efficiency and responsiveness. The results showcase the potential of DRL to optimize complex decision-making processes in cyber-physical systems. Future work will explore scaling the solution for larger networks and dynamic environments, potentially extending its application across diverse industries such as manufacturing, healthcare, and smart cities.

Keywords: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Stochastic Computation Offloading, Digital Twin Networks, Cyber-Physical Systems, Neural Network, Latency Reduction, Resource Utilization.
