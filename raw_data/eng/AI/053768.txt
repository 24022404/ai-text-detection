Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have emerged as powerful tools for representation learning on graph-structured data. However, their vulnerability to adversarial attacks, particularly injection attacks, poses a significant threat. This paper introduces TDGIA, a novel Targeted Domain-aware Graph Injection Attack, designed to effectively compromise GNN performance. TDGIA leverages domain-specific knowledge to strategically inject malicious nodes and edges into the graph, maximizing the attack's impact while maintaining stealth. Our method identifies critical structural vulnerabilities in the target graph and crafts adversarial perturbations that exploit these weaknesses. We evaluate TDGIA on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its superior attack success rate compared to existing state-of-the-art injection attack methods. Results show a significant degradation in GNN accuracy across various tasks, including node classification and graph classification. The proposed attack highlights the need for robust defense mechanisms against sophisticated injection attacks on GNNs. This work contributes to a deeper understanding of GNN vulnerabilities and provides insights for developing more resilient graph learning models.

Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Adversarial Attacks, Injection Attacks, Graph Security, Machine Learning.
