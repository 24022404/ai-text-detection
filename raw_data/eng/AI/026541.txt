This paper introduces Meta-SAC, a novel approach to automatically tune the entropy temperature of the Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) algorithm via metagradient. The objective is to improve the stability and efficiency of SAC by adaptively adjusting the entropy temperature, a crucial hyperparameter that balances exploration and exploitation. Our method employs a metagradient update rule to optimize the entropy temperature, allowing the agent to learn an effective temperature schedule during training. Experimental results demonstrate that Meta-SAC outperforms traditional SAC with manually tuned entropy temperatures, achieving improved performance and sample efficiency in a range of continuous control tasks. The key findings highlight the importance of adaptive entropy temperature tuning and the effectiveness of metagradient-based optimization. This research contributes to the development of more efficient and robust reinforcement learning algorithms, with potential applications in areas such as robotics and autonomous systems. Key keywords: Soft Actor-Critic, entropy temperature, metagradient, reinforcement learning, autonomous systems.
