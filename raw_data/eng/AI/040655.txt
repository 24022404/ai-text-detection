This paper presents PointNetLK, a novel approach to point cloud registration that leverages the strengths of deep learning and traditional registration methods. The objective is to develop a robust and efficient algorithm for aligning two point clouds, which is a fundamental task in various computer vision and robotics applications. Our approach utilizes the PointNet architecture to learn a compact and informative representation of the point clouds, and then employs the Lucas-Kanade algorithm to iteratively refine the registration. The results demonstrate that PointNetLK outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of registration accuracy and efficiency, especially in the presence of noise, outliers, and partial overlap. The key contributions of this work include the integration of PointNet with the Lucas-Kanade algorithm, and the development of a robust and efficient point cloud registration framework. This research has significant implications for various applications, including 3D reconstruction, object recognition, and robotic mapping. Key keywords: point cloud registration, PointNet, Lucas-Kanade, deep learning, 3D vision, robotic mapping.
