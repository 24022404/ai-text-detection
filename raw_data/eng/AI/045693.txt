This paper presents a novel adiabatic theorem for policy tracking with TD-learning, addressing the challenge of adapting to changing environments in reinforcement learning. The objective is to develop a theoretical framework that enables TD-learning algorithms to efficiently track changes in the optimal policy. Our approach combines TD-learning with adiabatic techniques, allowing the algorithm to adapt to shifting policies while maintaining convergence guarantees. The key findings demonstrate that our method achieves improved tracking performance and stability compared to existing TD-learning algorithms. The results have significant implications for applications in dynamic environments, such as robotics and game playing, where policies may change over time. Our contributions include a new adiabatic theorem that provides a theoretical foundation for policy tracking with TD-learning, as well as empirical evaluations that highlight the effectiveness of our approach. Key keywords: TD-learning, adiabatic theorem, policy tracking, reinforcement learning, dynamic environments.
