This paper proposes an innovative approach to classification problems by introducing an ensemble of example-dependent cost-sensitive decision trees. The objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of decision-making in scenarios where different types of misclassifications incur varying costs. Our method employs a novel approach by incorporating example-dependent costs into the decision tree construction process, allowing for more nuanced and context-aware decision-making. The ensemble is formed by combining multiple decision trees, each optimized for a specific cost distribution, thereby enhancing the overall performance and adaptability of the model. Experimental results demonstrate that our ensemble approach outperforms state-of-the-art cost-sensitive classification methods, particularly in scenarios with complex cost structures. The key contributions of this research include the development of a flexible and efficient algorithm for constructing example-dependent cost-sensitive decision trees and the demonstration of its effectiveness in real-world applications. This work has significant implications for fields such as finance, healthcare, and security, where accurate and cost-sensitive decision-making is crucial. Key keywords: cost-sensitive learning, decision trees, ensemble methods, example-dependent costs, classification.
