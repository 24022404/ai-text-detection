This paper introduces CoCoNets, a novel approach for learning continuous contrastive 3D scene representations. The primary objective is to generate robust and informative scene embeddings applicable across diverse 3D understanding tasks. Our method leverages a contrastive learning framework, training a neural network to encode 3D scenes into a continuous latent space where similar scenes are mapped close together, and dissimilar scenes are pushed apart. We utilize a point cloud-based architecture combined with a hierarchical contrastive loss that captures both global scene similarity and local geometric relationships. Experiments on standard benchmark datasets demonstrate that CoCoNets achieve state-of-the-art performance in 3D scene retrieval and consistently outperform existing methods in few-shot scene classification and semantic segmentation. These results highlight the effectiveness of our continuous contrastive learning strategy for generating generalizable 3D scene representations. The learned embeddings can be readily integrated into downstream tasks, providing a powerful tool for 3D scene understanding. Keywords: 3D scene representation, contrastive learning, point cloud, scene retrieval, semantic segmentation, deep learning.
