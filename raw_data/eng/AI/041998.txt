This paper addresses the challenge of video-based person re-identification by proposing a novel Multi-Granularity Reference-Aided Attentive Feature Aggregation (MGRAFA) approach. The objective is to improve the accuracy of person re-identification in surveillance videos by effectively aggregating features from multiple granularities. Our method employs a reference-aided attentive mechanism to selectively focus on relevant features and a multi-granularity framework to capture both local and global characteristics of individuals. Experimental results demonstrate that MGRAFA outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in re-identification accuracy. The key findings highlight the importance of attentive feature aggregation and multi-granularity representation in video-based person re-identification. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and efficient person re-identification systems, with potential applications in surveillance, security, and smart cities. The proposed MGRAFA approach offers a new perspective on feature aggregation and attention mechanisms, making it a valuable addition to the field of computer vision and person re-identification. Key keywords: person re-identification, video-based re-identification, attentive feature aggregation, multi-granularity representation, computer vision, surveillance.
