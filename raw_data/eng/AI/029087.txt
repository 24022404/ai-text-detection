This paper addresses the challenging problem of American Sign Language (ASL) fingerspelling recognition in unconstrained, real-world settings ("in the wild"). Current ASL recognition systems often struggle with variations in lighting, background clutter, hand orientation, and signer style. We propose a novel deep learning framework that combines a convolutional neural network (CNN) for feature extraction with a recurrent neural network (RNN) incorporating attention mechanisms for improved sequence modeling.
