This study aims to develop an accurate leaf recognition system using convolutional neural networks (CNNs) based features. The objective is to classify leaves into their respective species, which is crucial for botanical research and conservation efforts. A deep learning approach is employed, utilizing a CNN model to extract features from leaf images. The proposed method involves training the CNN on a large dataset of leaf images, followed by fine-tuning and validation. The results show a significant improvement in recognition accuracy, with the CNN-based model outperforming traditional feature extraction methods. The key findings indicate that the use of CNNs can effectively capture the complex patterns and textures present in leaf images, leading to a more accurate classification. This research contributes to the development of automated leaf recognition systems, with potential applications in plant taxonomy, ecology, and conservation biology. The novelty of this study lies in the application of CNNs to leaf recognition, demonstrating the effectiveness of deep learning techniques in this domain. Key keywords: leaf recognition, convolutional neural networks, deep learning, plant taxonomy, image classification.
