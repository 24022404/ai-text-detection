**Abstract:**  
Keypoint localization is a critical task in computer vision, with applications spanning human pose estimation, activity recognition, and augmented reality. This paper presents TransPose, a novel framework utilizing transformer architectures for precise keypoint localization. Unlike traditional convolutional approaches, TransPose leverages the self-attention mechanism of transformers to efficiently model long-range dependencies and spatial relationships across image regions. The proposed model processes feature embeddings extracted by a lightweight convolutional backbone, enabling robust and scalable integration of global context. Comprehensive experiments on standard benchmark datasets demonstrate that TransPose achieves superior accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods, particularly in challenging scenarios with occlusion or complex backgrounds. The results indicate marked improvements in both localization precision and inference speed. These advancements highlight the potential of transformer-based architectures to redefine keypoint detection tasks in computer vision. TransPose offers a modular and flexible solution, suitable for real-time applications where reliable human pose estimation and keypoint analysis are essential.  
**Keywords:** transformer, keypoint localization, pose estimation, self-attention, computer vision
