This paper presents an experimental analysis of reinforcement learning techniques applied to spectrum sharing radar systems. The objective is to develop adaptive and efficient methods for radar spectrum allocation, enabling coexistence with commercial wireless systems. We employ a deep reinforcement learning approach, utilizing techniques such as Q-learning and policy gradients, to optimize radar transmission parameters and minimize interference. Our results show significant improvements in spectrum utilization and interference reduction, outperforming traditional fixed allocation methods. The proposed framework demonstrates the potential for reinforcement learning to enhance radar spectrum sharing, with implications for future radar systems and wireless communication networks. Key contributions include the development of a novel reinforcement learning-based radar spectrum management algorithm and an experimental evaluation of its performance in a realistic environment. This research has important applications in areas such as radar-based sensing, wireless communication, and spectrum management, with relevant keywords including reinforcement learning, spectrum sharing, radar systems, Q-learning, and policy gradients.
