Abstract:

This paper addresses the critical challenge of enhancing the interpretability of attention mechanisms in modern deep learning models, with a focus on achieving high resolution, computational efficiency, and accuracy. We propose a novel high-resolution attention framework designed to provide fine-grained, visually comprehensible attention maps without sacrificing speed or performance. Our approach integrates an efficient hierarchical attention computation strategy that reduces complexity while preserving detailed spatial information, facilitating rapid processing and real-time applicability. Empirical evaluations across multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate that our model not only delivers accurate predictions but also generates attention maps that closely align with human intuition and ground-truth relevance regions. Comparative analysis shows significant improvements in both interpretability and inference speed compared to existing state-of-the-art attention methods. The proposed framework supports transparent decision-making in applications such as image classification, natural language processing, and medical diagnosis, enabling practitioners to better understand and trust model outputs. These results highlight the potential of our model to set new standards for interpretable and efficient attention-based systems in artificial intelligence. Key keywords: attention mechanism, interpretability, high-resolution, deep learning, transparency, model efficiency, explainability.
