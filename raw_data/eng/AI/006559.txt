This paper introduces a novel framework, Ask & Explore, designed to enhance curiosity-driven exploration in reinforcement learning agents. The primary objective is to enable agents to autonomously discover and interact with informative aspects of their environment through grounded question answering. Our method combines intrinsic motivation with a question-answering module, where the agent learns to ask questions about its observations and then actively explores to find answers. We utilize a variational information bottleneck to encourage the agent to ask grounded and relevant questions. Experimental results in a series of challenging simulated environments demonstrate that Ask & Explore outperforms existing exploration strategies, leading to more efficient discovery of environment dynamics and improved task performance. The approach facilitates learning complex behaviors by focusing exploration on areas where the agent has knowledge gaps. Key innovations include the integration of a differentiable question-answering module with intrinsic motivation and the use of information bottleneck for question grounding. Keywords: reinforcement learning, curiosity, exploration, question answering, information bottleneck, intrinsic motivation.
