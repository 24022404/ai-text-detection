Weakly supervised instance segmentation aims to train models with only image-level labels, reducing annotation costs. This paper introduces Label Propagation and Enhancement Networks (Label-PEnet), a novel framework that iteratively refines instance segmentation results. Label-PEnet leverages sequential label propagation to generate pseudo-masks from image-level labels, followed by an enhancement network to refine these masks. Specifically, our method employs a propagation module to expand initial seed regions based on pixel similarity and spatial proximity, and a subsequent enhancement module utilizes contextual information to correct and refine the propagated masks. Experiments on the PASCAL VOC 2012 and COCO datasets demonstrate that Label-PEnet achieves state-of-the-art performance compared to existing weakly supervised instance segmentation methods. The proposed framework significantly improves the quality of pseudo-masks, leading to more accurate instance segmentation. Key contributions include the sequential propagation and enhancement approach and the demonstration of its effectiveness in utilizing weak supervision for high-quality instance segmentation. Keywords: Weakly Supervised Instance Segmentation, Label Propagation, Semantic Segmentation, Deep Learning.
