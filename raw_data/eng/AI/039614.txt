Parameter selection plays a critical role in the development and deployment of information technology systems, directly influencing performance, reliability, and adaptability. This paper investigates the often-overlooked importance of systematic parameter selection across various IT domains, including machine learning, algorithm design, and system configuration. We present a comprehensive analysis of common parameter selection methods and propose an adaptive framework that integrates automated optimization and domain-specific heuristics. Using empirical studies and benchmark datasets, we demonstrate that informed parameter selection can yield significant improvements in accuracy, efficiency, and robustness compared to standard practices. The findings highlight that neglecting this key step may limit system capabilities or result in suboptimal outcomes. Our proposed framework not only simplifies the selection process but also supports reproducibility and scalability in real-world applications. The study underscores the necessity of elevating parameter selection to a central focus in IT research and practice, with potential implications for AI, data science, and software engineering. Key keywords: parameter tuning, optimization, machine learning, system configuration, performance improvement.
