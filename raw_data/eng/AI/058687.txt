Detecting objects in densely packed scenes presents a significant challenge due to occlusion, scale variation, and background clutter. This paper introduces a novel detection framework designed to improve the precision of object detection in such environments. Our approach combines a refined feature extraction module with an adaptive receptive field network, enabling the model to better distinguish between closely packed objects. We employ a context-aware attention mechanism to suppress irrelevant background noise and enhance the representation of target objects. Experimental results on challenging datasets demonstrate a significant improvement in detection accuracy compared to state-of-the-art detectors, particularly in scenarios with high object density. The proposed method achieves a substantial reduction in false positives and improved localization precision. This work contributes a robust and efficient solution for precise object detection in densely packed scenes, with potential applications in autonomous driving, surveillance, and crowd analysis.

Keywords: Object Detection, Densely Packed Scenes, Feature Extraction, Attention Mechanism, Deep Learning, Computer Vision.
