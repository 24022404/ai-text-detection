Image-to-image translation aims to convert an image from one domain to another while preserving its content. Current methods often struggle with generating diverse and high-quality outputs, especially when dealing with significant domain gaps. This paper introduces a novel Group-wise Deep Whitening-and-Coloring Transformation (GDWCT) to address these limitations. Our approach decouples the style and content features of an image by employing a whitening transformation to remove style information and a coloring transformation to inject the target style. Crucially, GDWCT operates on group-wise feature statistics, enabling more fine-grained style control and improved content preservation. We demonstrate the effectiveness of GDWCT on several challenging image translation tasks, including photo-to-painting, day-to-night, and style transfer. Experimental results show that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of visual quality, diversity, and fidelity compared to existing techniques. Furthermore, ablation studies validate the importance of group-wise processing. The proposed GDWCT offers a powerful and flexible framework for image-to-image translation, paving the way for more realistic and controllable image generation.

**Keywords:** Image-to-image translation, style transfer, whitening and coloring transform, deep learning, generative models.
