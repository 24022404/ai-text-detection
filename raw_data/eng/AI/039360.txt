This paper explores novel Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) architectures for high-quality image synthesis by integrating capsule networks. Addressing limitations in GANs related to capturing hierarchical relationships and viewpoint invariance in images, we propose a Capsule-GAN framework. This framework replaces traditional convolutional layers in both the generator and discriminator with capsule layers, enabling the network to explicitly model part-whole relationships and spatial arrangements. Several Capsule-GAN architectures, varying in capsule routing mechanisms and network depth, are evaluated. Experimental results on benchmark datasets such as CIFAR-10 and CelebA demonstrate that Capsule-GANs achieve improved image quality and stability compared to traditional CNN-based GANs, as evidenced by higher Inception Score and lower Frechet Inception Distance (FID). The study highlights the potential of capsule networks to enhance GAN performance by improving feature representation and offers new directions for generative modeling. Keywords: Generative Adversarial Networks, Capsule Networks, Image Synthesis, GAN Architectures, Deep Learning.
