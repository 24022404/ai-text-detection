Understanding the inner workings of deep neural networks (DNNs) remains a significant challenge in the field of artificial intelligence. This paper proposes a novel divide-and-conquer approach to systematically analyze and interpret the behavior of deep networks. The objective is to decompose complex DNN architectures into smaller, more interpretable components, enabling a clearer understanding of their decision-making processes. Our method involves partitioning the network into functional modules and analyzing their individual contributions to the overall performance. We employ a combination of visualization techniques, feature attribution methods, and modular performance evaluation to achieve this goal. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach provides deeper insights into network behavior, revealing patterns that are often obscured in traditional end-to-end analyses. Furthermore, the proposed method outperforms existing interpretability techniques in terms of both accuracy and computational efficiency. The findings highlight the potential of this approach to enhance the transparency and trustworthiness of deep learning systems, with implications for applications in healthcare, autonomous systems, and other critical domains. Keywords: deep neural networks, interpretability, divide-and-conquer, feature attribution, modular analysis.
