This paper proposes a novel hybrid approach for breast cancer subtype classification, combining the strengths of relation networks and localized graph convolutional filtering. The objective is to improve the accuracy of subtype classification, which is crucial for personalized treatment and patient outcomes. Our method utilizes a relation network to model complex interactions between genes and a localized graph convolutional filtering technique to capture spatial hierarchies of gene expression. The results show that our hybrid approach outperforms existing methods, achieving a significant improvement in classification accuracy. The key findings indicate that the integration of relation networks and localized graph convolutional filtering enables the capture of both global and local patterns in gene expression data, leading to more accurate subtype classification. This research contributes to the development of advanced machine learning techniques for cancer subtype classification, with potential applications in precision medicine and personalized healthcare. The proposed approach highlights the importance of combining multiple techniques to improve classification performance, particularly in complex biological systems. Key keywords: breast cancer subtype classification, relation networks, localized graph convolutional filtering, gene expression analysis, machine learning, precision medicine.
