This paper proposes a novel deep feature embedding approach, termed Local Similarity-Aware Deep Feature Embedding, which aims to improve the representation learning of complex data by incorporating local similarity information. The objective is to develop a robust and efficient method for learning compact and informative feature embeddings that capture the underlying structure of the data. Our approach employs a deep neural network architecture that integrates a local similarity-aware module, which adaptively weighs the importance of neighboring samples in the feature space. The results show that our method outperforms state-of-the-art feature embedding techniques in terms of clustering accuracy, classification performance, and visualization quality. The key findings indicate that the proposed approach can effectively preserve the local geometric structure of the data, leading to more discriminative and generalizable feature representations. This research contributes to the development of advanced feature learning techniques, with potential applications in image recognition, natural language processing, and recommender systems, and highlights the importance of local similarity awareness in deep feature embedding. Key keywords: deep feature embedding, local similarity, representation learning, neural networks, feature extraction.
