This study aims to develop a convolutional neural network (CNN)-based approach for cervical cancer classification in whole-slide histopathology images. The proposed method utilizes a deep learning architecture to automatically detect and classify cervical cancer from histopathological images. Our approach involves a transfer learning-based strategy, where a pre-trained CNN model is fine-tuned on a dataset of annotated whole-slide images. The results show that our method achieves high accuracy and sensitivity in distinguishing between normal and abnormal tissue samples, outperforming traditional machine learning-based approaches. The key findings of this study highlight the potential of CNN-based methods for automated cervical cancer diagnosis, enabling faster and more accurate detection. This research contributes to the development of computer-aided diagnosis systems for cervical cancer, with potential applications in clinical practice and medical research. The novelty of this approach lies in its ability to handle large whole-slide images and provide accurate classification results, making it a valuable tool for pathologists and medical professionals. Key keywords: CNN, deep learning, histopathology images, cervical cancer classification, computer-aided diagnosis.
