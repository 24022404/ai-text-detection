**Abstract:**

Super resolution technologies have significantly advanced the enhancement of low-resolution images, delivering higher quality visuals essential for applications in medical imaging, surveillance, and digital media. However, conventional methods typically produce a single, deterministic output, which may not fully capture the range of plausible high-resolution interpretations inherent in ambiguous or degraded input data. This paper introduces the concept of Explorable Super Resolution, a novel framework that enables users to interactively explore multiple high-resolution reconstructions for a single low-resolution input. Our approach leverages a guided generative model, integrating conditional generative adversarial networks (GANs) with user-influenced latent variable manipulation, to generate diverse and realistic image outputs. We evaluate the proposed system on standard benchmark datasets, demonstrating that Explorable Super Resolution not only achieves competitive quantitative results compared to state-of-the-art algorithms but also provides superior interpretability and user control. Qualitative assessments indicate improved user satisfaction and trust in the reconstructions. This work highlights the potential of explorable frameworks for increasing transparency and adaptability in super resolution tasks, opening new paradigms for user-driven image enhancement in artificial intelligence and computer vision. 

**Keywords:** super resolution, explorable AI, interactive image enhancement, generative models, GANs, computer vision, user-guided reconstruction
