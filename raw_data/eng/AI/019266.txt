This paper introduces GraphVAE, a novel approach to generating small graphs using variational autoencoders (VAEs). The objective is to develop a model that can effectively learn the underlying structure of small graphs and generate new graphs that are similar in structure and properties. To achieve this, we employ a VAE-based approach that combines a graph neural network (GNN) encoder with a graph generation decoder. Our method learns a probabilistic representation of the input graphs, allowing for the generation of diverse and realistic graphs. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of GraphVAE in generating small graphs that are comparable to real-world graphs in terms of structure and properties. The proposed approach has significant implications for applications such as molecular generation, social network analysis, and graph-based data augmentation. Key contributions of this work include the development of a VAE-based framework for graph generation, the use of GNNs for encoding graph structures, and the demonstration of the effectiveness of GraphVAE in generating high-quality small graphs. Relevant keywords: graph generation, variational autoencoders, graph neural networks, small graphs, molecular generation.
