This paper presents the gundapusunil approach to multimodal memotion analysis, a task that involves detecting emotions in internet memes. The objective of this research is to develop a system that can effectively analyze the sentiment and emotions conveyed by memes, which are a combination of images and text. To achieve this, we employed a multimodal approach, utilizing both visual and textual features to train a deep learning model. Our method leverages convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for image feature extraction and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) for text analysis, which are then fused to predict the emotion conveyed by the meme. The results show that our system achieves competitive performance on the SemEval-2020 Task 8 dataset, outperforming several baseline models. The key contributions of this research include the development of a novel multimodal fusion technique and the demonstration of its effectiveness in memotion analysis. This work has important implications for the development of AI models that can understand and interpret multimodal content, with potential applications in social media monitoring and sentiment analysis. Key keywords: multimodal analysis, memotion analysis, deep learning, CNN, RNN, sentiment analysis, emotion detection.
