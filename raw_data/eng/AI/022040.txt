This paper introduces LocGAN, a novel approach for precise localization in aerial imagery utilizing grid maps. The primary objective is to achieve accurate georeferencing of aerial images without relying on GPS or extensive feature engineering. LocGAN employs a generative adversarial network (GAN) framework, where the generator synthesizes grid maps from aerial images, and the discriminator distinguishes between generated and real grid maps. This adversarial training process enables the generator to learn robust feature representations for accurate localization. The method leverages a grid-based representation to capture spatial relationships within the aerial scene, enhancing localization performance in challenging environments. Experimental results demonstrate that LocGAN achieves state-of-the-art localization accuracy compared to existing methods, especially in scenarios with limited visual features or significant viewpoint variations. The proposed technique offers a practical and scalable solution for applications such as autonomous navigation, urban mapping, and disaster response. Key words: Aerial Imagery, Localization, Grid Maps, Generative Adversarial Networks, Georeferencing, Computer Vision.
