The decentralized architecture and inherent technical complexity of Web3 present considerable barriers for non-technical users striving to interact with decentralized applications (DApps), thus significantly impeding the mainstream adoption of these novel technologies. For many, understanding concepts such as decentralized identity management, complex cryptographic keys, and blockchain interactions is far from intuitive; the requirement to handle private keys, perform manual backup processes, or navigate convoluted onboarding procedures frequently deters average users. To bridge this gap and foster inclusivity, the proposed approach leverages a combination of smart contracts, the Pedersen Distributed Key Generation (DKG) protocol, and Shamir secret sharing. By integrating these cryptographic tools, the solution aims to offload technical burdens from users—secret management, key recovery, and transaction approval can be abstracted behind user-friendly interfaces. Smart contracts automate essential Web3 features while the Pedersen DKG protocol distributes trust and strengthens cryptographic resilience, and Shamir secret sharing ensures that sensitive data is never exposed or stored in a single vulnerable location. Collectively, these advancements not only simplify Web3 onboarding and ongoing management for everyday individuals but also significantly elevate the standards of privacy and security, making decentralized technologies approachable and secure for a much broader audience.
