Video super-resolution (VSR) aims to enhance the spatial resolution of low-resolution video frames while preserving temporal consistency and fine details. Despite significant progress, existing methods often struggle with complex motion patterns and temporal misalignments, leading to artifacts and reduced visual quality. This paper introduces the Temporally Deformable Alignment Network (TDAN), a novel framework designed to address these challenges. TDAN leverages deformable convolutions to dynamically align frames across time, enabling precise motion compensation and improved feature integration. The proposed network consists of a multi-scale alignment module, a feature fusion mechanism, and a reconstruction module, all optimized end-to-end for high-quality VSR. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that TDAN outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of both quantitative metrics and visual quality. Notably, TDAN achieves superior performance in handling large motions and occlusions, making it highly effective for real-world video enhancement tasks. The results highlight the potential of TDAN for applications in video streaming, surveillance, and medical imaging, where high-resolution and temporally consistent outputs are critical.  

**Keywords**: Video super-resolution, deformable convolutions, temporal alignment, motion compensation, deep learning.
