Graph exploration, a fundamental task in various domains, often requires traversing large and complex networks. Traditional graph exploration algorithms typically rely on hand-engineered heuristics, which may not generalize well across different graph structures and exploration objectives. This paper introduces a novel approach to learn transferable graph exploration strategies using reinforcement learning. We propose a graph neural network (GNN) based agent that learns to navigate graphs by predicting optimal node selection at each step. Our method focuses on training the agent on a diverse set of synthetic graphs and subsequently transferring the learned policy to unseen real-world graphs. Experimental results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms traditional graph exploration algorithms and heuristic-based methods, achieving significant improvements in terms of exploration efficiency and coverage. The learned exploration strategies exhibit strong transferability, enabling effective graph exploration even in previously unseen environments. This research contributes a practical framework for automated graph exploration with broad applicability in areas such as social network analysis, knowledge graph traversal, and drug discovery.

**Keywords:** Graph Exploration, Reinforcement Learning, Graph Neural Networks, Transfer Learning, Graph Algorithms.
