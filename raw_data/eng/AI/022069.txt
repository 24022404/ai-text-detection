This paper proposes a novel approach to conditional generative adversarial networks (CGANs), enhancing their robustness and stability. The objective is to address the challenges of mode collapse and sensitivity to hyperparameters in traditional CGANs. Our method, termed Robust Conditional GANs (RCGANs), employs a modified architecture that incorporates a regularization term to encourage diverse generations and a robust discriminator to improve training stability. We evaluate RCGANs on various benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in generation quality and diversity compared to state-of-the-art CGANs. The results show that RCGANs achieve superior performance in terms of inception score and Fréchet inception distance. Our approach has important implications for applications such as image-to-image translation, data augmentation, and generative modeling. Key contributions include the development of a robust and stable CGAN framework, which can be applied to various domains, including computer vision and machine learning. Relevant keywords: conditional generative adversarial networks, robustness, mode collapse, deep learning, computer vision.
