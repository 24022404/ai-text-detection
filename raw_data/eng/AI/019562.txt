Text-based person re-identification (Re-ID) is a critical task in computer vision, aiming to match individuals across different images based on textual descriptions. This paper introduces a novel Dual-path Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) architecture enhanced with a Max Gated block to address the challenges of aligning visual and textual features effectively. The proposed model leverages a dual-path framework to process visual and textual inputs independently, ensuring robust feature extraction for both modalities. The Max Gated block is designed to dynamically highlight the most discriminative features by selectively gating information flow, improving the alignment between visual and textual representations. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed approach achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The results highlight the effectiveness of the Max Gated block in enhancing feature representation and cross-modal matching. This research contributes to advancing text-based person Re-ID by providing a scalable and efficient solution with potential applications in surveillance, security, and human-computer interaction.  

**Keywords**: Text-based person re-identification, Dual-path CNN, Max Gated block, Cross-modal matching, Feature alignment, Computer vision.
