This paper presents an Auto-Parsing Network (APN) designed to address key challenges in image captioning and visual question answering (VQA). The objective is to enhance the capacity of deep learning systems to understand visual content and generate contextually accurate textual descriptions and answers. The proposed APN architecture integrates an automatic parsing mechanism that extracts hierarchical semantic information from images, facilitating improved visual representation. Employing a combination of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for feature extraction and attention-based modules for semantic parsing, our approach enables dynamic focus on salient image regions tailored to the given question or captioning context. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the APN achieves superior performance compared to conventional end-to-end models, yielding more precise and descriptive captions as well as higher accuracy in VQA tasks. Notably, the model exhibits robust generalization across diverse visual domains and question types. The Auto-Parsing Network advances the state-of-the-art in cross-modal understanding by introducing adaptive parsing mechanisms that bridge visual and textual semantics. This work contributes valuable insights for applications in automated image description, human-computer interaction, and multimedia retrieval systems. Key keywords: auto-parsing network, image captioning, visual question answering, deep learning, semantic parsing, attention mechanism.
