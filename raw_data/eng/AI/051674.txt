In recent years, Wasserstein distributionally robust optimization (WDRO) has emerged as a powerful framework to enhance decision-making under uncertainty. However, existing approaches often assume global perturbations within a Wasserstein ball, overlooking the practical implication of local perturbations. This paper presents a principled learning method designed to address this gap by introducing local perturbations into the WDRO framework. The proposed approach is grounded in a thorough theoretical analysis and leverages advanced machine learning techniques to construct robust solutions tailored to specific local distributional changes. Our methodology employs a novel algorithm that iteratively refines decisions by simulating local shifts within the Wasserstein metric space, ensuring adaptability and robustness. Empirical results demonstrate that our method outperforms traditional WDRO methods in scenarios characterized by local distributional variability, showcasing significant improvements in both robustness and computational efficiency. Key findings underline the superiority of localized approaches in complex environments where distributional changes are not uniformly global. This research not only contributes a cutting-edge algorithm to the field of optimization but also expands the applicability of WDRO by integrating local perturbations. Ultimately, the implications of this work are far-reaching, offering potential applications in areas such as finance, supply chain management, and any domain where uncertainty and variation are prevalent, all while advancing the frontier of distributionally robust optimization. Keywords: Wasserstein distributionally robust optimization, local perturbations, machine learning, robust decision-making, optimization algorithms.
