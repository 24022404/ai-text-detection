Adversarial attacks on 3D point clouds pose significant threats to the reliability of deep learning models in applications such as autonomous driving, robotics, and augmented reality. This paper introduces DUP-Net, a novel deep neural network architecture designed to enhance the robustness of 3D point cloud classifiers against adversarial perturbations. DUP-Net integrates two key components: a denoising module that systematically removes adversarial and noisy points, and an upsampling module that reconstructs high-quality, dense point clouds to preserve critical geometric details. The proposed framework operates as a robust preprocessing defense, seamlessly filtering corrupted input before classification. Extensive experiments demonstrate that DUP-Net significantly improves adversarial defense compared to existing methods, achieving higher classification accuracy across multiple datasets and attack types. Moreover, DUP-Net maintains the integrity and semantic features of clean point clouds, ensuring minimal loss in benign scenarios. These results emphasize the practicality and effectiveness of DUP-Net for protecting 3D deep learning systems from adversarial threats. Keywords: 3D point cloud, adversarial defense, denoising, upsampling, deep learning, robust classification.
