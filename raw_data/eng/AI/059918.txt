Sentiment analysis in low-resource settings suffers from data scarcity, limiting the performance of machine learning models. This paper explores a novel data augmentation technique leveraging Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to address this challenge. We propose a GAN-based framework that generates synthetic sentences conditioned on sentiment labels, effectively increasing the size and diversity of the training data. Our approach incorporates a sentiment classifier within the GAN's discriminator to ensure the generated samples are sentiment-consistent and of high quality. Experiments on multiple low-resource sentiment analysis datasets demonstrate that models trained with our augmented data achieve significant improvements in accuracy and F1-score compared to baseline models and traditional data augmentation methods. Specifically, we observed an average increase of 8% in F1-score across the datasets tested. This work highlights the potential of GANs for data augmentation in sentiment analysis and provides a promising direction for improving performance in low-resource scenarios. Keywords: Sentiment Analysis, Data Augmentation, Generative Adversarial Networks, GAN, Low-Resource Learning, Natural Language Processing.
