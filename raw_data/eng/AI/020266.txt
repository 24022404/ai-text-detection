Action recognition in video is a challenging task due to variations in scale, viewpoint, and execution speed. This paper introduces a novel Hierarchical Multi-scale Attention Network (HMAN) to address these challenges by effectively capturing spatio-temporal features at multiple scales. HMAN employs a hierarchical architecture to decompose the video into different temporal segments, enabling the extraction of both short-term and long-term dependencies. Multi-scale attention modules are integrated at each level of the hierarchy to selectively focus on the most relevant regions and time frames, enhancing the discriminative power of the features. Experiments on benchmark datasets, including UCF101 and HMDB51, demonstrate that HMAN achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods by a significant margin. The results highlight the effectiveness of the proposed hierarchical multi-scale attention mechanism in capturing comprehensive spatio-temporal information for robust action recognition. This approach offers a promising direction for improving video understanding and has potential applications in video surveillance, human-computer interaction, and robotics.

**Keywords:** Action Recognition, Video Understanding, Attention Mechanism, Multi-scale Learning, Hierarchical Networks, Spatio-temporal Features.
