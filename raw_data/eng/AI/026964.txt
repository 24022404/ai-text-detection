This paper presents a novel, light-weight approach to enhance the interpretability and explainability of classification networks using Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM). The objective is to provide a more transparent and trustworthy understanding of deep learning models by highlighting the most relevant features contributing to their predictions. Our method leverages a simple yet effective technique to refine the Grad-CAM visualization, resulting in more accurate and informative saliency maps. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our approach, showing improved performance in terms of explanation fidelity and model interpretability. The proposed technique has significant implications for the development of more reliable and explainable AI systems, particularly in applications where model transparency is crucial. By promoting a deeper understanding of classification networks, our work contributes to the advancement of explainable AI and has potential applications in areas such as computer vision, healthcare, and finance, with key keywords including Grad-CAM, explainability, interpretability, deep learning, and transparent AI.
