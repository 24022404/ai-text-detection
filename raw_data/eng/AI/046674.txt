This paper addresses the challenge of estimating light direction and color from a single image, a fundamental problem in computer vision. Our objective is to develop a deep learning-based approach that can accurately infer illumination properties from an image. We propose a novel deep regression model that leverages convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to learn the complex relationship between image features and light properties. Our method uses a large dataset of images with ground-truth light direction and color annotations to train the model. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving high accuracy in estimating light direction and color. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a new deep regression framework and the creation of a large-scale dataset for light estimation. Our work has significant implications for various applications, including image relighting, augmented reality, and computer-generated imagery (CGI). Key keywords: deep learning, light estimation, color estimation, single image, CNNs, computer vision, image relighting.
