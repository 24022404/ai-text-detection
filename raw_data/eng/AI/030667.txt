This paper introduces a novel approach to 3D face identification using deep learning. Current 3D face recognition systems often struggle with variations in pose, expression, and occlusion. To address these challenges, we propose a deep convolutional neural network architecture that directly learns discriminative features from raw 3D face scans. Our method incorporates a multi-view aggregation strategy to capture comprehensive facial information from different perspectives. Furthermore, we introduce a custom loss function designed to maximize inter-subject variability while minimizing intra-subject variations. Experimental results on challenging benchmark datasets, including the FRGCv2 and Bosphorus databases, demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms state-of-the-art 3D face recognition methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. Specifically, our system achieves a 15% improvement in identification rate under varying expression conditions compared to existing techniques. The proposed method offers a practical and accurate solution for 3D face identification, with potential applications in security, biometrics, and personalized services. Keywords: 3D Face Recognition, Deep Learning, Convolutional Neural Networks, Biometrics, Multi-view Learning.
