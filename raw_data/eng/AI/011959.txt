This paper addresses the challenge of developing intelligent agents capable of adapting to unforeseen tasks without requiring additional retraining, a concept known as zero-shot transfer. The research specifically focuses on leveraging unsupervised environment design to facilitate the emergence of complexity in learning systems, enabling agents to transfer learned skills across diverse and novel scenarios effectively.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel framework that employs unsupervised environment design to autonomously generate a variety of training environments. This approach encourages the development of complex behaviors by exposing agents to a wide range of challenges. Our methodology integrates advanced reinforcement learning techniques with environment adaptation strategies to foster robust and versatile policy development.

Results/Findings: The findings from extensive experimental evaluations demonstrate that agents trained under our unsupervised environment design framework exhibit superior zero-shot transfer capabilities compared to traditional baselines. The agents not only show enhanced generalization when exposed to unseen tasks but also display improved adaptability and performance in complex environments. Quantitative assessments reveal significant performance gains, evidencing the efficacy of our approach in fostering emergent complexity.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes to the field by introducing a transformative method that harnesses the potential of unsupervised environment design for zero-shot transfer learning. The results suggest that our approach can reduce the reliance on extensive task-specific training data, thus expediting the deployment of intelligent systems in real-world applications. Future work will explore further refinements to the framework and its application to other domains, setting a foundation for more resilient and adaptive AI models.

Keywords: zero-shot transfer, unsupervised environment design, reinforcement learning, emergent complexity, adaptive AI models.
