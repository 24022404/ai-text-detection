Fingerprint image segmentation is a critical preprocessing step in automated fingerprint identification systems (AFIS), aiming to accurately separate the foreground (ridge patterns) from the background. This paper presents a novel filter design approach to enhance the performance of fingerprint image segmentation. The proposed method utilizes a combination of Gabor filters and morphological operations, optimized for capturing local ridge orientation and frequency characteristics while suppressing noise. We developed an adaptive filtering strategy that adjusts filter parameters based on local image statistics, improving robustness to variations in image quality. Experimental results on benchmark fingerprint databases demonstrate that our approach achieves superior segmentation accuracy compared to traditional methods, with a significant reduction in false positive and false negative rates. Specifically, the proposed method achieves an average accuracy improvement of 5-7% over existing techniques. The enhanced segmentation results contribute to improved feature extraction and matching performance in AFIS. Keywords: Fingerprint segmentation, Gabor filter, morphological operation, image enhancement, AFIS.
