Analyzing correlated multivariate time series is crucial in various domains, from finance to climate science. This paper introduces a deep structural model designed to capture complex dependencies and temporal dynamics in such data. Our approach leverages a novel neural network architecture that combines recurrent layers for time series modeling with a graph-based attention mechanism to explicitly learn and represent inter-series correlations. The model decomposes the time series into interpretable structural components, allowing for nuanced analysis of underlying drivers and their relationships. We evaluate our model on both synthetic and real-world datasets, including financial market data and climate measurements. Results demonstrate that our model outperforms state-of-the-art methods in forecasting accuracy and correlation capture. Furthermore, the learned structural representations provide valuable insights into the underlying system dynamics. The proposed model offers a powerful tool for understanding and predicting complex multivariate time series, with potential applications in risk management, anomaly detection, and scientific discovery.

Keywords: Multivariate Time Series, Deep Learning, Structural Modeling, Correlation Analysis, Recurrent Neural Networks, Graph Attention Networks.
