In emerging memory technologies such as racetrack memories, synchronization errors like deletions and sticky insertions are a common challenge. To address this, Chee et al. have proposed a novel approach that employs locally-constrained de Bruijn sequences for constructing codes capable of better correcting these specific types of errors. Locally-constrained de Bruijn sequences not only enhance the correction rate but also contribute critical value from both practical and theoretical perspectives, making their application significant in the ongoing development of robust storage systems.
