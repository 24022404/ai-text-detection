This paper introduces ALET, a comprehensive framework for automated labeling of equipment and tools in real-world environments. The objective is to develop a robust tool detection system capable of identifying and categorizing tools in uncontrolled settings. To achieve this, we created a large-scale dataset comprising diverse tools and equipment, which serves as the foundation for our research. Our approach employs a deep learning-based baseline model, leveraging computer vision techniques to detect and classify tools. The results show significant improvements in detection accuracy and efficiency compared to existing methods. A key use case demonstrates the practical application of ALET in a construction site scenario, highlighting its potential to enhance workplace safety and productivity. The contributions of this research include the introduction of a novel dataset, a robust baseline model, and a real-world use case, paving the way for future advancements in tool detection and automated labeling. Key keywords: tool detection, automated labeling, computer vision, deep learning, equipment recognition.
