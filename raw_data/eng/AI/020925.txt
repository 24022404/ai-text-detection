Early Time Series Classification (ETSC) aims to predict the class label of a time series as early as possible, a crucial task in domains like healthcare and finance. However, existing deep learning models for ETSC often treat all time points equally, neglecting the importance of early segments for accurate prediction. This paper introduces Earliness-Aware Deep Convolutional Networks (EA-DCNN), a novel architecture designed to explicitly emphasize the predictive power of initial time steps. EA-DCNN incorporates an attention mechanism that dynamically weights convolutional filter outputs based on their temporal position, assigning higher significance to features extracted from the beginning of the time series. We evaluate EA-DCNN on a diverse set of benchmark datasets and demonstrate that it achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of both earliness and accuracy. Specifically, EA-DCNN exhibits a significant improvement in early classification accuracy compared to existing methods, while maintaining competitive overall accuracy. The proposed architecture provides a principled approach to incorporate earliness awareness into deep convolutional models, enabling more timely and reliable predictions in ETSC applications. Keywords: Time Series Classification, Early Classification, Deep Learning, Convolutional Neural Networks, Attention Mechanism.
