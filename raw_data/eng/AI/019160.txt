This paper introduces MHER, a novel model-based approach to Hindsight Experience Replay (HER), designed to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of off-policy reinforcement learning. The objective is to improve the sample complexity of HER by leveraging a learned model of the environment to generate additional experiences. MHER utilizes a probabilistic ensemble model to predict the outcomes of actions, allowing for the generation of synthetic experiences that augment the original replay buffer. Our approach combines the benefits of model-based reinforcement learning with the simplicity and flexibility of HER. Experimental results demonstrate that MHER significantly outperforms existing HER methods in several benchmark environments, achieving improved performance and sample efficiency. The key findings highlight the importance of model accuracy and the potential for MHER to be applied to complex, high-dimensional tasks. This research contributes to the development of more efficient and effective off-policy reinforcement learning algorithms, with potential applications in robotics, game playing, and other areas of artificial intelligence, including model-based reinforcement learning, offline reinforcement learning, and imitation learning.
