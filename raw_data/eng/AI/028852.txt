This paper proposes a novel approach to visual object tracking using Spatially Supervised Recurrent Convolutional Neural Networks (SSRCNNs). The objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of object tracking in complex scenes by leveraging the strengths of both convolutional and recurrent neural networks. Our approach employs a spatially supervised mechanism to guide the learning of recurrent connections, enabling the network to effectively capture temporal dependencies and spatial relationships between objects. The SSRCNN model is trained using a large-scale dataset and evaluated on several benchmark tracking datasets. Results show that our method outperforms state-of-the-art tracking algorithms, achieving significant improvements in terms of accuracy and robustness. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of spatial supervision to recurrent convolutional networks and the demonstration of its effectiveness in visual object tracking. This work has important implications for applications such as surveillance, robotics, and autonomous vehicles, where accurate and reliable object tracking is crucial. Key keywords: visual object tracking, recurrent convolutional neural networks, spatial supervision, deep learning, computer vision.
