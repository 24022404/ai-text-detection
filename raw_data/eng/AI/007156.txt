In recent years, the dissemination of misinformation through social media platforms has become a critical concern, particularly during significant events when rumors can spread rapidly. This paper investigates the effectiveness of ensemble deep learning techniques applied to time-series representations of tweets for rumor detection. Our primary objective is to enhance the accuracy and reliability of misinformation identification by leveraging the temporal dynamics inherent in social media interactions. We propose a novel framework that combines multiple deep learning models, including convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and long short-term memory (LSTM) networks, to capture both spatial and temporal features from tweet data. Through extensive experimentation on curated datasets, we demonstrate that our ensemble approach significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving a detection accuracy of over 92%. Moreover, our results indicate that incorporating temporal information leads to a marked improvement in identifying emerging rumors as they unfold. This research contributes to the development of more robust misinformation detection systems and has practical implications for social media platforms, policymakers, and researchers working to counteract the effects of false information. Key keywords: rumor detection, deep learning, time-series data, social media, misinformation, ensemble methods.
