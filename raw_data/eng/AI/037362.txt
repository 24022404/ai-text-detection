Hyperparameter optimization is a critical challenge in machine learning, significantly impacting model performance and generalization. This paper introduces a novel spectral approach to hyperparameter optimization, leveraging spectral analysis to efficiently explore and identify optimal hyperparameter configurations. The proposed method transforms the hyperparameter space into a spectral domain, enabling the identification of key patterns and relationships that guide the optimization process. By integrating spectral techniques with gradient-based optimization, our approach reduces computational complexity and improves convergence rates compared to traditional methods such as grid search and random search. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the spectral approach achieves superior performance in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency, particularly for high-dimensional hyperparameter spaces. Furthermore, the method exhibits robustness across diverse machine learning models, including deep neural networks and support vector machines. This research contributes a scalable and effective framework for hyperparameter optimization, with potential applications in large-scale machine learning systems and automated model tuning. Key innovations include the integration of spectral analysis with optimization algorithms and the development of a domain-agnostic methodology applicable to a wide range of machine learning tasks. Keywords: hyperparameter optimization, spectral analysis, machine learning, gradient-based optimization, automated tuning.
