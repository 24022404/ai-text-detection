This research addresses the challenge of accurately classifying phantom test patterns, which are crucial for quality assurance in medical imaging systems. Current methods are often time-consuming and require manual intervention. Our aim is to develop an automated classification technique to improve efficiency and accuracy.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel approach leveraging transfer learning with deep neural networks to achieve automatic classification of phantom test patterns. By utilizing pre-trained models on large-scale image datasets, we adapt these models to specialize in recognizing detailed features specific to phantom patterns. This is followed by fine-tuning the network to enhance classification performance for this specific task.

Results/Findings: Our experimental evaluation demonstrates that the proposed method significantly outperforms traditional classification techniques. The system achieved high accuracy and robustness across various phantom types, reducing the need for human intervention. Comparative analyses with existing methods highlight a notable improvement in classification speed and precision.

Conclusion/Implications: This study presents a significant advancement in the field of automatic medical imaging quality assurance. By automating the classification process through transfer learning, the approach promises enhanced reliability and efficiency in healthcare diagnostics. The implications of this work suggest potential for further applications in other domains of medical imaging where pattern recognition is critical. Key innovations include the adaptation of cutting-edge deep learning architectures for specialized medical tasks, contributing to the broader adoption of AI in healthcare.

Keywords: Transfer Learning, Deep Neural Networks, Phantom Test Patterns, Medical Imaging, Automation, Quality Assurance.
