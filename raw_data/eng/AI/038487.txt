This paper introduces the Variational Mixture of Normalizing Flows (VMNF), a novel generative modeling framework that combines the flexibility of mixture models with the expressiveness of normalizing flows. The objective is to improve density estimation and sample generation for complex, multimodal data distributions, which often challenge standard variational and flow-based approaches. Our method constructs a mixture of multiple normalizing flows, each tailored to capture distinct modes of the data, and employs variational inference to learn both the flow parameters and the mixture components effectively. Through extensive experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets, VMNF demonstrates superior performance in log-likelihood, sample quality, and mode coverage compared to traditional flow-based models and other mixture-based generative models. These results highlight VMNF’s ability to model highly complex distributions without significant computational overhead. The proposed framework advances the capabilities of deep generative models, offering promising applications in unsupervised learning, anomaly detection, and data synthesis. Keywords: variational inference, normalizing flows, mixture models, generative modeling, density estimation.
