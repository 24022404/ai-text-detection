This paper proposes a novel approach to object recognition by integrating contour sparse representation with Self-Dual Descriptor (SDD) features. The objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of object recognition systems by effectively capturing and representing the contour information of objects. Our method utilizes a sparse representation framework to select the most informative contour features, which are then combined with SDD features to form a powerful and compact descriptor. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in recognition accuracy on several benchmark datasets. The key contributions of this work include the introduction of a contour-based sparse representation framework and the integration of SDD features, which enhance the discriminative power and generalizability of the object recognition system. Our approach has important implications for various applications, including image classification, object detection, and robotics. Key keywords: object recognition, contour sparse representation, SDD features, sparse coding, computer vision.
