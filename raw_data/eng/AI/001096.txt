This paper introduces WiSoSuper, a comprehensive benchmarking framework designed to evaluate the performance of super-resolution methods on wind and solar data. The objective is to enhance the resolution of low-quality environmental data, which is crucial for accurate renewable energy forecasting and planning. We employ a range of state-of-the-art super-resolution techniques, including deep learning-based models and traditional image processing algorithms, to upscale wind speed and solar irradiance data. Our results show that the proposed framework achieves significant improvements in data resolution, with a notable increase in forecasting accuracy. The WiSoSuper benchmark provides a standardized platform for comparing different super-resolution methods, facilitating the development of more accurate and reliable renewable energy prediction systems. Key contributions include the creation of a large-scale dataset for wind and solar super-resolution, as well as the introduction of novel evaluation metrics tailored to renewable energy applications. This research has important implications for the field of renewable energy, enabling more efficient and effective integration of wind and solar power into the energy grid. Relevant keywords: super-resolution, wind energy, solar energy, renewable energy forecasting, deep learning, benchmarking.
