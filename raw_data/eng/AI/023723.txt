This paper addresses the challenge of missing data estimation in temporal multilayer position-aware graph neural networks (TMP-GNNs). The objective is to develop an effective approach for estimating missing data in dynamic graph-structured data, which is crucial for various applications such as traffic forecasting, social network analysis, and recommendation systems. We propose a novel TMP-GNN model that integrates position-aware graph attention and temporal convolutional layers to capture both spatial and temporal dependencies in the data. Our approach leverages a multi-layer architecture to learn complex patterns and relationships in the graph data. Experimental results demonstrate that our model outperforms existing methods in estimating missing data, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and reducing errors by up to 30%. The key contributions of this research include the development of a position-aware graph attention mechanism and a temporal convolutional layer, which enable the model to effectively capture dynamic changes in the graph data. This study has important implications for various applications that involve graph-structured data with missing values, and highlights the potential of TMP-GNNs in improving the accuracy and reliability of data-driven decision-making systems. Key keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Temporal Graphs, Missing Data Estimation, Position-aware Attention, Temporal Convolutional Layers.
