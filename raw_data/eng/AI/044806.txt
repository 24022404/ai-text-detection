This paper presents a novel approach to image correction using deep reciprocating High Dynamic Range (HDR) transformation. The objective is to enhance the visual quality of images by restoring lost details and correcting overexposed or underexposed regions. Our method employs a deep learning-based framework that integrates a reciprocating transformation module, allowing for efficient and effective image correction. The approach utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture to learn the complex mapping between low dynamic range (LDR) images and their corresponding HDR representations. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our method in terms of image quality metrics, such as peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM), compared to state-of-the-art image correction techniques. The proposed deep reciprocating HDR transformation offers significant improvements in image correction, making it a valuable tool for various applications, including image and video processing, computer vision, and photography. Key contributions of this research include the development of a novel reciprocating transformation module and the demonstration of its effectiveness in image correction tasks, highlighting the potential of deep learning-based methods for HDR image processing and computer vision applications, with relevant keywords including HDR, image correction, deep learning, CNN, and computer vision.
