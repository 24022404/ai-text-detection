Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) has emerged as a powerful paradigm for solving complex continuous control tasks, yet its performance varies significantly across algorithms, environments, and hyperparameters. This paper presents a comprehensive benchmarking study of state-of-the-art DRL algorithms for continuous control, aiming to provide insights into their strengths, limitations, and practical applicability. We evaluate popular algorithms, including Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), Soft Actor-Critic (SAC), and Twin Delayed Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (TD3), across a diverse set of simulated environments, such as robotic manipulation and locomotion tasks. Our methodology involves systematic hyperparameter tuning, reward analysis, and computational efficiency comparisons to ensure fair and reproducible results. The findings reveal that SAC consistently outperforms other methods in terms of sample efficiency and final performance, while PPO demonstrates robustness in high-dimensional tasks. Additionally, we identify key challenges, such as sensitivity to hyperparameters and scalability issues, that hinder the deployment of DRL in real-world applications. This study contributes a standardized framework for evaluating DRL algorithms, offering actionable recommendations for researchers and practitioners. The results underscore the potential of DRL for continuous control while highlighting areas for future improvement, particularly in terms of stability and generalization.  

**Keywords**: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Continuous Control, Benchmarking, PPO, SAC, TD3, Robotic Control.
