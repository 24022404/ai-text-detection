This paper presents a novel approach to profile face recognition, leveraging attention-guided progressive mapping to enhance the accuracy of identifying individuals from non-frontal facial images. The objective is to address the challenges posed by pose variations and occlusions in traditional face recognition systems. Our method employs a deep learning framework that integrates attention mechanisms to selectively focus on informative facial regions, while progressively refining the feature mappings to improve recognition performance. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets and outperforming existing methods by a significant margin. The key contributions of this research lie in its ability to adaptively weigh the importance of different facial features and its robustness to pose and expression variations. This work has important implications for real-world applications, such as surveillance, security, and law enforcement, where profile face recognition is a critical component. Key keywords: profile face recognition, attention mechanisms, progressive mapping, deep learning, face recognition systems.
