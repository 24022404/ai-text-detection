This paper proposes a novel deep embedded multi-view clustering approach that leverages collaborative training to improve clustering performance. The objective is to develop a robust and efficient method for clustering high-dimensional multi-view data, where each view provides complementary information. Our approach employs a deep neural network to learn compact and informative representations from multiple views, while a collaborative training mechanism is designed to promote consensus among different views. The results show that our method outperforms state-of-the-art multi-view clustering algorithms, achieving significant improvements in clustering accuracy and robustness. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of collaborative training in capturing complex relationships between views and enhancing the quality of learned representations. This research contributes to the development of advanced clustering techniques, with potential applications in data mining, computer vision, and machine learning. Key keywords: multi-view clustering, deep learning, collaborative training, representation learning, unsupervised learning.
