This paper introduces Non-Transferable Learning, a novel approach designed to enhance model verification and authorization in machine learning. The objective is to prevent unauthorized model usage while ensuring legitimate access, addressing a critical security gap in current AI systems. Our method utilizes a unique combination of watermarking and cryptographic techniques to create non-transferable models, which can only be used by authorized entities. The approach involves embedding a hidden signature into the model's parameters, allowing for verification of the model's authenticity and authorization status. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of Non-Transferable Learning in preventing model theft and misuse, while maintaining the model's original performance. This research contributes to the development of more secure and trustworthy AI systems, with potential applications in sensitive domains such as finance, healthcare, and national security. Key aspects of this work include model verification, authorization, watermarking, and cryptographic techniques, highlighting the importance of secure machine learning practices.
