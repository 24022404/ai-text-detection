**Abstract**

Efficient and accurate segmentation of individual workpieces within cluttered bins is a vital challenge in automated bin picking for industrial robotics. This paper presents a novel deep learning-based approach for workpiece region segmentation tailored specifically to bin picking scenarios. The proposed method utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture enhanced by attention mechanisms to effectively discern and segment overlapping and partially occluded objects from depth and RGB images. To address the scarcity of annotated bin picking datasets, we introduce a synthetic data generation pipeline that simulates realistic bin environments, enabling robust model training. Evaluation was conducted on both synthetic and real-world datasets, demonstrating that our approach achieves significant improvements in segmentation accuracy and processing speed compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. The results indicate a substantial reduction in object mis-segmentation and improved precision in workpiece localization, thereby enhancing pick success rates in robotic applications. This research contributes an effective, scalable solution to the bin picking segmentation problem, facilitating more reliable automation in manufacturing settings. Keywords: bin picking, deep learning, region segmentation, convolutional neural network, robotic automation, industrial vision systems.
