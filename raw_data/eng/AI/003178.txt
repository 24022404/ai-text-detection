This paper introduces Wavelet Integrated Convolutional Neural Networks (WI-CNNs) for robust image classification in noisy environments. The objective is to improve classification accuracy of CNNs when images are corrupted by significant noise. Our method integrates discrete wavelet transform (DWT) into the CNN architecture to decompose input images into different frequency sub-bands, enabling the network to learn noise-invariant features. Specifically, we modify the initial convolutional layers to operate on wavelet sub-bands instead of the raw image. We evaluate WI-CNNs on benchmark datasets such as CIFAR-10 and ImageNet with various noise levels, comparing its performance against standard CNN architectures and other noise reduction techniques. Experimental results demonstrate that WI-CNNs consistently outperform existing methods, achieving a significant improvement in classification accuracy under high noise conditions. This shows that wavelet decomposition is better at denoising then current CNN architectures. The integration of wavelet analysis provides a promising direction for developing noise-robust deep learning models.
**Keywords:** Convolutional Neural Networks, Wavelet Transform, Image Classification, Noise Robustness, Deep Learning.
