This paper introduces MAGNet, a novel multi-agent graph network designed to enhance deep multi-agent reinforcement learning. The objective is to develop an efficient framework that enables multiple agents to learn cooperative behaviors in complex, dynamic environments. MAGNet employs a graph-based approach, where each agent is represented as a node, and their interactions are modeled as edges. This allows for the capture of intricate relationships between agents and the environment. The method utilizes a combination of graph convolutional networks and attention mechanisms to facilitate effective communication and cooperation among agents. Experimental results demonstrate that MAGNet outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in several multi-agent reinforcement learning benchmarks, showcasing improved scalability and adaptability. The key findings highlight the importance of graph-based representations and attention mechanisms in multi-agent learning. This research contributes to the development of more efficient and cooperative multi-agent systems, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, smart grids, and autonomous vehicles. Key keywords: multi-agent reinforcement learning, graph neural networks, cooperative learning, attention mechanisms, deep learning.
