Representation learning is fundamental to numerous applications in machine learning, where the challenge lies in extracting informative and discriminative features from high-dimensional data. This paper introduces a novel approach termed Adversarially-Contrastive Optimal Transport (ACOT) for unsupervised representation learning. The proposed method integrates adversarial training with contrastive learning and optimal transport theory to improve the alignment and separation of learned feature distributions. By leveraging optimal transport, ACOT systematically matches the data distribution and the representation space, reducing mode collapse and ensuring meaningful pairwise relationships. The adversarial-contrastive framework enhances feature robustness by simultaneously maximizing inter-class distinction while minimizing intra-class variability. Extensive experiments are conducted on benchmark datasets, demonstrating that ACOT outperforms state-of-the-art representation learning methods in terms of both linear evaluation and transfer learning tasks. The results show superior representation quality, improved generalizability, and increased resistance to overfitting. ACOT offers a theoretically grounded and practical solution for extracting high-quality features, with strong potential for applications in computer vision, natural language processing, and other domains relying on deep representation learning. Keywords: representation learning, optimal transport, contrastive learning, adversarial training, unsupervised learning, deep learning.
