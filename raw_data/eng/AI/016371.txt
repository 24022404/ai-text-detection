This paper addresses the challenge of optimizing policies in reinforcement learning (RL) under constraints to ensure safe and efficient learning and deployment. Traditional policy optimization methods often neglect or inadequately handle constraints, potentially leading to undesirable or unsafe outcomes in real-world applications.

Methods/Approach: We present a novel approach called Constrained Policy Optimization (CPO), which integrates constraint handling directly within the policy optimization process. The method employs a Lagrangian-based framework to balance the trade-off between achieving high rewards and satisfying predefined constraints. By leveraging advanced optimization techniques and adapting constraint satisfaction measures, CPO effectively navigates the policy space while adhering to specified limitations.

Results/Findings: Our experiments demonstrate that CPO significantly outperforms existing unconstrained and constrained RL methods across various benchmark tasks, including robotics and autonomous systems. Notably, CPO consistently ensures constraint satisfaction while enhancing overall policy performance. Comparative analyses reveal improved sample efficiency and robustness in maintaining constraints, highlighting the method's potential for real-time applications.

Conclusion/Implications: The Constrained Policy Optimization framework represents a significant advancement in safe RL by seamlessly integrating constraints into policy learning. This research contributes to developing more reliable and robust AI systems, offering compelling applications in safety-critical domains such as healthcare, autonomous driving, and industrial automation. Future work could explore extending the CPO framework to handle more complex and dynamic environments or integrating it with model-based approaches.

Keywords: Constrained Policy Optimization, Reinforcement Learning, Safe AI, Lagrangian Framework, Constraint Handling, Robotics, Autonomous Systems.
