This paper introduces LioNets, a novel approach for interpreting neural networks by decoding the penultimate layer to provide local explanations. The objective is to address the lack of transparency in deep learning models by developing a method that can effectively attribute predictions to specific input features. Our approach utilizes a decoding mechanism to transform the penultimate layer's representations into feature importance scores, enabling the identification of key factors influencing model decisions. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of LioNets in providing insightful explanations for various neural network architectures and datasets. Compared to existing interpretation methods, LioNets offers improved fidelity and efficiency, making it a valuable tool for understanding and trusting neural network predictions. The contributions of this research lie in its ability to provide local, feature-level explanations, which can be applied to a wide range of applications, including computer vision, natural language processing, and decision-making systems. Key keywords: neural network interpretation, explainable AI, penultimate layer decoding, feature importance scores, transparent deep learning.
