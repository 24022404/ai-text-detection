The increasing adoption of surround-view camera systems in autonomous driving and intelligent transportation necessitates robust vehicle re-identification (Re-ID) techniques to enhance tracking and monitoring capabilities. This paper addresses the challenge of accurately identifying and re-identifying vehicles across multiple camera views in complex urban environments. We propose a novel deep learning-based framework that leverages multi-view feature fusion and spatial-temporal consistency to improve vehicle Re-ID performance. Our approach integrates a transformer-based architecture to capture long-range dependencies and a lightweight attention mechanism to focus on discriminative vehicle features, such as color, shape, and license plates. The proposed model is trained and evaluated on a large-scale dataset comprising diverse urban scenarios, demonstrating superior accuracy and robustness compared to existing methods. Experimental results show a significant improvement in mean average precision (mAP) and rank-1 accuracy, achieving state-of-the-art performance. The framework’s efficiency and scalability make it suitable for real-time deployment in surround-view camera systems. This research contributes to advancing vehicle Re-ID by addressing key challenges in multi-view data integration and feature representation, with potential applications in traffic management, surveillance, and autonomous driving systems.  

**Keywords**: Vehicle Re-Identification, Surround-View Cameras, Deep Learning, Transformer, Multi-View Fusion, Autonomous Driving.
