Early time series classification (ETSC) is a critical task in various domains, such as healthcare, finance, and industrial monitoring, where timely decision-making is essential. This paper introduces **Earliness-Aware Deep Convolutional Networks (EADCN)**, a novel framework designed to optimize the trade-off between classification accuracy and decision earliness in ETSC. The proposed approach leverages deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) enhanced with an earliness-aware mechanism that dynamically adjusts the model's decision-making process based on the confidence of predictions at each time step. Unlike traditional methods that rely on fixed thresholds or heuristics, EADCN learns to balance the urgency of decisions with the quality of predictions through end-to-end training. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that EADCN achieves superior performance compared to state-of-the-art ETSC methods, reducing decision latency while maintaining high accuracy. The framework's adaptability and efficiency make it a promising solution for real-world applications requiring early and reliable time series classification. Key contributions include the integration of earliness-awareness into deep learning architectures and a robust evaluation of the framework across diverse datasets. This work advances the field of ETSC by providing a scalable and effective approach for early decision-making in time-sensitive environments.  

**Keywords**: Early time series classification, deep convolutional networks, earliness-awareness, decision latency, real-time systems.
