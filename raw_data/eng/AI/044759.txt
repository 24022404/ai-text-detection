This paper addresses the issue of "fake background" in object tracking, where the tracker loses focus on the target object and instead follows the background. The objective is to develop a novel approach that can effectively re-engage the tracker with the target object. Our method, termed "One More Check," utilizes a dual-module framework, combining a foreground-aware module with a background-suppression module. The foreground-aware module enhances the features of the target object, while the background-suppression module reduces the impact of distracting background information. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly improves tracking performance, especially in scenarios with complex backgrounds or occlusions. Compared to state-of-the-art tracking methods, "One More Check" achieves superior accuracy and robustness. The key contributions of this research include a innovative dual-module architecture and a novel loss function that facilitates effective tracking. This work has important implications for various applications, including surveillance, autonomous driving, and robotics, where reliable object tracking is crucial. Key keywords: object tracking, fake background, dual-module framework, foreground-aware, background-suppression.
