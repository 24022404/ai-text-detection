This paper explores the intersection of neural networks and reproducing kernel Banach spaces, aiming to enhance the understanding of neural network architectures. Our objective is to investigate how reproducing kernel Banach spaces can be utilized to analyze and improve the performance of neural networks. We employ a novel approach that combines the strengths of kernel methods with the representational power of neural networks, leveraging the flexibility of Banach spaces to capture complex patterns in data. Our method involves constructing reproducing kernel Banach spaces that can effectively approximate neural network mappings, allowing for a more nuanced understanding of their behavior. The results show that this approach can lead to significant improvements in neural network performance, particularly in cases where traditional kernel methods may struggle. Our findings have important implications for the development of more efficient and effective neural network architectures, and demonstrate the potential of reproducing kernel Banach spaces as a tool for analyzing and optimizing neural networks. Key contributions include the introduction of a new framework for understanding neural networks, and the demonstration of its efficacy in improving neural network performance. Relevant keywords: neural networks, reproducing kernel Banach spaces, kernel methods, deep learning, function approximation.
