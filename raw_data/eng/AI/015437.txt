Few-shot learning (FSL) remains a significant challenge in machine learning, particularly in scenarios where limited labeled data is available. This paper introduces Sparse Spatial Transformers (SST), a novel approach designed to enhance few-shot learning by leveraging spatial attention mechanisms with sparsity constraints. The proposed SST framework dynamically focuses on the most relevant regions of input data, reducing computational overhead while improving feature extraction efficiency. By integrating sparse attention modules into a transformer-based architecture, SST achieves superior generalization capabilities with minimal labeled examples. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that SST outperforms existing FSL methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The results highlight the model's ability to adapt to new tasks with limited data, making it a promising solution for real-world applications such as medical imaging and autonomous systems. This work contributes to the advancement of few-shot learning by introducing a computationally efficient and effective spatial attention mechanism, paving the way for future research in resource-constrained environments.  

**Keywords**: Few-shot learning, spatial transformers, sparse attention, transformers, machine learning, computational efficiency.
