Accurately forecasting taxi demand is a critical challenge for urban transportation planning, impacting both operational efficiency and rider satisfaction. This paper introduces a novel tessellation strategy for taxi demand forecasting that leverages the HEDGE algorithm, an adaptive online learning technique designed to combine the strengths of multiple predictive models. The proposed approach dynamically partitions the urban area into spatial tessellations, optimizing the granularity of regions based on real-time demand patterns. By assigning model weights via HEDGE, the system adaptively selects and refines forecasts from a diverse set of base models, thereby enhancing prediction accuracy across varying spatial and temporal contexts. Experimental evaluations conducted on real-world taxi datasets demonstrate that our HEDGE-based tessellation framework outperforms conventional spatial partitioning methods and individual predictive models, yielding lower forecasting error and improved robustness to non-stationary demand fluctuations. These results suggest that integrating adaptive tessellation strategies with ensemble learning substantially advances the state of urban taxi demand forecasting, providing valuable insights for intelligent transportation systems, resource allocation, and policy development. Key contributions include the introduction of an adaptive tessellation method, effective application of the HEDGE algorithm in spatial demand prediction, and comprehensive performance analysis on large-scale taxi data. Keywords: taxi demand forecasting, HEDGE algorithm, spatial tessellation, ensemble learning, urban transportation, predictive modeling.
