This paper addresses the increasing complexity of deep neural networks, which, despite their remarkable success across various domains, remain largely inscrutable and difficult to interpret. Our objective is to enhance the understanding of these models by employing a divide-and-conquer strategy that systematically breaks down the network into more manageable components.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel methodology that dissects deep networks into independent modules, each analyzed individually and combined to provide insights into the overall architecture. This approach leverages layer-wise analysis, activation pattern exploration, and modular testing to uncover the roles and interactions of components within the network. Our framework builds upon established techniques and integrates innovative measures to ensure comprehensive analysis.

Results/Findings: The application of our divide-and-conquer methodology reveals significant patterns in the internal representation of deep networks, facilitating an understanding of how these models process input data. Comparisons with traditional holistic analysis methods showcase improved interpretability and more granular insights into model behavior. Our approach also demonstrates superior performance in identifying the contributions of individual layers and neurons to the network's decision-making process.

Conclusion/Implications: Our research contributes to the field of neural network interpretability by providing a structured approach to dissect and understand deep networks. This work not only aids in demystifying complex models but also holds potential applications in model debugging, optimization, and the development of more transparent AI systems. By offering a clearer understanding of deep networks, this study paves the way for advancing the reliability and accountability of AI-powered technologies.

Keywords: deep neural networks, interpretability, divide-and-conquer, layer-wise analysis, model transparency.
