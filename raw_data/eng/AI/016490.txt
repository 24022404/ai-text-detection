Multi-label classification remains a challenging task, particularly in few-shot and zero-shot scenarios where labeled data is scarce or nonexistent for certain classes. This paper introduces a novel approach to multi-label few-zero-shot learning that leverages knowledge aggregated from multiple label graphs. Our method constructs several label graphs, each capturing distinct semantic relationships between labels extracted from diverse knowledge sources. We then propose a knowledge aggregation module that dynamically weights and integrates information from these graphs to enhance feature representation and improve label prediction accuracy. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in both few-shot and zero-shot multi-label classification settings. The results highlight the effectiveness of aggregating knowledge from multiple label graphs for improved generalization and adaptation to novel labels. This work provides a new direction for addressing the challenges of limited supervision in multi-label learning.
Keywords: Multi-label learning, Few-shot learning, Zero-shot learning, Graph Neural Networks, Knowledge Aggregation, Semantic Relationships.
