**Abstract**

Anomaly detection in multivariate time-series data is vital for ensuring the reliability and security of complex systems such as industrial processes, sensor networks, and IT infrastructure. Existing approaches often struggle to model intricate correlations and event-driven patterns within high-dimensional datasets, leading to suboptimal performance. In this paper, we propose Event2Graph, a novel event-driven bipartite graph framework for robust anomaly detection in multivariate time-series data. The proposed method transforms raw time-series into discrete event representations, capturing significant changes and interactions between different variables. These events are then encoded as nodes in a bipartite graph, where edges reflect temporal and cross-variable dependencies. We introduce a tailored graph-based anomaly detection algorithm that leverages both event co-occurrence patterns and graph structure dynamics to identify abnormal behavior. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that Event2Graph achieves superior detection accuracy and timeliness compared to traditional statistical models and recent deep learning approaches. Our approach also exhibits enhanced interpretability, facilitating root-cause analysis by highlighting anomalous events and their relationships. Event2Graph provides an effective, scalable, and explainable solution for anomaly detection in diverse multivariate time-series applications, offering a significant advancement over existing methodologies. 

**Keywords:** anomaly detection, multivariate time-series, event-driven modeling, bipartite graph, graph algorithms, interpretability
