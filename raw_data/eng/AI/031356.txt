This paper addresses the challenge of forecasting high-dimensional dynamics, a problem that arises in various fields such as physics, biology, and finance. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that leverages suboptimal embeddings to improve predictive performance. We propose a methodology that combines dimensionality reduction techniques with advanced machine learning algorithms to identify and exploit low-dimensional patterns in high-dimensional data. Our approach utilizes a multi-step framework, involving the selection of relevant features, the construction of suboptimal embeddings, and the training of a predictive model. The results show that our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art techniques in forecasting high-dimensional dynamics, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and robustness. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of suboptimal embeddings in capturing complex patterns in high-dimensional data, and demonstrate the potential of this approach for applications such as time series forecasting, anomaly detection, and signal processing. Overall, this study contributes to the development of more accurate and efficient methods for forecasting high-dimensional dynamics, with implications for a wide range of fields. Key keywords: high-dimensional dynamics, suboptimal embeddings, dimensionality reduction, machine learning, time series forecasting.
