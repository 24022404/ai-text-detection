This paper presents an efficient model-based deep reinforcement learning approach that leverages variational state tabulation to enhance the learning process. The objective is to improve the sample efficiency and scalability of deep reinforcement learning algorithms in complex environments. Our method utilizes a probabilistic model to represent the system dynamics and a variational autoencoder to compress the state space, reducing the dimensionality and improving the generalization capabilities of the model. The results show that our approach outperforms existing model-based reinforcement learning methods in terms of sample efficiency and cumulative rewards. Key findings include a significant reduction in the number of required interactions with the environment and improved performance in high-dimensional state spaces. The proposed method has important implications for real-world applications, such as robotics and autonomous systems, where efficient exploration and adaptation are crucial. Our contributions include a novel combination of variational state tabulation and model-based deep reinforcement learning, enabling more efficient and scalable learning in complex environments. Key keywords: deep reinforcement learning, model-based reinforcement learning, variational autoencoders, state tabulation, sample efficiency.
