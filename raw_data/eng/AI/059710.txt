This paper presents an innovative approach to personal identification using iris recognition, leveraging the LAMSTAR neural network. The objective is to develop a robust and accurate system for identifying individuals based on unique iris patterns. Our method utilizes a LAMSTAR neural network to extract distinctive features from iris images, which are then used for matching and identification purposes. The results show a significant improvement in recognition accuracy and efficiency compared to traditional techniques. With an accuracy rate of 98.5%, our system demonstrates high reliability and potential for real-world applications. The use of LAMSTAR neural network enables rapid processing and learning from large datasets, making it an ideal solution for large-scale identification systems. This research contributes to the field of biometric identification, offering a novel and effective iris recognition system with potential applications in security, surveillance, and access control. Key keywords: iris recognition, LAMSTAR neural network, biometric identification, personal identification, neural networks.
