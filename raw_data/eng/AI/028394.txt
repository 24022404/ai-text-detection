Large-factor image super-resolution (SR) aims to reconstruct high-resolution (HR) images from low-resolution (LR) counterparts with large upscaling factors, a challenging ill-posed problem. This paper introduces GLEAN, a novel generative latent bank approach designed to address this challenge. GLEAN leverages a generative adversarial network (GAN) to learn a latent space representation of HR image patches. A latent bank is then constructed, storing these learned representations. During inference, given an LR image, GLEAN retrieves relevant latent codes from the bank based on the input's features and uses these codes to guide the generation of HR image details. This approach allows for the synthesis of realistic textures and structures even with significant upscaling. Experiments demonstrate that GLEAN outperforms state-of-the-art SR methods, achieving superior perceptual quality and quantitative metrics, particularly at high upscaling factors. The generative latent bank effectively captures and transfers fine-grained details, leading to more visually appealing and accurate SR results. Keywords: Super-Resolution, Generative Adversarial Networks, Image Reconstruction, Latent Space, Image Enhancement.
