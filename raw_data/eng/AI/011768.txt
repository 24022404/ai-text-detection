Curiosity-driven exploration in artificial intelligence has the potential to significantly enhance autonomous learning and decision-making in complex environments. This paper introduces Ask & Explore, a novel framework for grounded question answering (QA) that empowers AI agents to actively generate and pursue information-seeking questions as part of their exploration strategy. Our approach integrates a question-generation module with an environment-grounded QA system, enabling agents to iteratively inquire about their surroundings and acquire knowledge that guides efficient exploration. We implement the framework within simulated environments where agents interact with objects and scenes, leveraging natural language understanding to produce contextually relevant questions and interpret grounded responses. Experimental results demonstrate that agents equipped with Ask & Explore achieve superior task completion, information gain, and generalization compared to baseline methods that rely solely on passive perception or scripted querying. The framework also exhibits robustness to noisy environments and novel scenarios. Our findings highlight the advantages of integrating question-driven curiosity and grounding mechanisms in intelligent systems, paving the way for more adaptive and autonomous exploration strategies. Key contributions include the formulation of curiosity-driven, grounded QA as a unified framework, empirical validation in challenging environments, and insights into the synergy between question-asking behaviors and effective knowledge acquisition. Keywords: curiosity-driven exploration, grounded question answering, AI agents, autonomous learning, natural language understanding.
