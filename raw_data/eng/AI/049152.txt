This paper explores the integration of physical modeling techniques into active contours for enhanced image segmentation. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of segmentation by leveraging the principles of physics to guide the contour evolution. A novel approach is proposed, combining the traditional active contour model with physical modeling techniques, such as viscoelasticity and electromagnetism, to create a more robust and adaptive segmentation framework. The method is tested on various images, demonstrating improved performance in terms of precision and recall, particularly in cases with complex or noisy boundaries. The results show that the physical modeling-based active contours can effectively capture intricate details and handle topological changes, outperforming traditional methods. This research contributes to the advancement of image segmentation techniques, with potential applications in medical imaging, object detection, and computer vision. Key keywords: active contours, image segmentation, physical modeling, viscoelasticity, computer vision.
