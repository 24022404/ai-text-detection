This paper proposes a novel approach for the classification of datasets in data mining, addressing the challenge of accurate and efficient data categorization. Our objective is to develop a method that improves upon existing classification techniques, enabling better decision-making and insight extraction from complex datasets. We employ a hybrid approach, combining machine learning algorithms with statistical analysis to create a robust and adaptable classification model. The results show significant improvements in classification accuracy and computational efficiency compared to traditional methods. Our findings demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method in handling diverse datasets, including those with high-dimensional features and noisy data. The contributions of this research include the introduction of a new classification algorithm and the evaluation of its performance on various benchmark datasets. This study has important implications for data mining applications, such as customer segmentation, fraud detection, and recommender systems, highlighting the potential for improved data-driven decision-making. Key keywords: data mining, classification, machine learning, dataset analysis, pattern recognition.
