This paper presents a novel approach to designing scalable and privacy-preserving deep neural networks through an algorithmic-cryptographic co-design methodology. The primary objective is to address the pressing issue of protecting sensitive data during the training and inference phases of deep learning models. To achieve this, we propose a hybrid framework that integrates advanced cryptographic techniques, such as homomorphic encryption and secure multi-party computation, with optimized algorithmic designs for deep neural networks. Our approach enables the secure and efficient training of models on private data, while also facilitating scalable and accurate inference. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our co-design methodology, showcasing significant improvements in model performance and privacy preservation compared to existing solutions. The key contributions of this research include the development of a scalable and privacy-preserving deep learning framework, which has important implications for applications in areas such as healthcare, finance, and edge computing. By combining cryptographic security with algorithmic innovation, our work paves the way for the widespread adoption of deep neural networks in privacy-sensitive domains, with relevant keywords including homomorphic encryption, secure multi-party computation, deep learning, privacy preservation, and scalable neural networks.
