This paper introduces RotLSTM, a novel extension to the Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) architecture designed to enhance sequence modeling by incorporating rotating memory cells. The objective is to improve the network's ability to capture long-range dependencies and periodic patterns within sequential data. Our approach involves augmenting the standard LSTM cell with a rotation mechanism, enabling memory states to be periodically transformed through a learned rotation matrix. This allows the network to dynamically manage and emphasize different aspects of past information. We evaluated RotLSTM on several benchmark datasets, including synthetic tasks with long-range dependencies and real-world time series prediction challenges. Results demonstrate that RotLSTM consistently outperforms standard LSTM and other variants, exhibiting improved accuracy and faster convergence. The learned rotation matrices offer valuable insights into the temporal dynamics of the data. The proposed RotLSTM architecture provides a significant advancement in recurrent neural networks, offering a robust and interpretable method for modeling sequential data with complex temporal structures. Keywords: LSTM, Recurrent Neural Networks, Sequence Modeling, Rotating Memory, Time Series Prediction, Long-Range Dependencies.
