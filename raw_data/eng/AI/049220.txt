Abstract:

The rapid growth of digital advertising has led to an increasing demand for efficient and effective methods to analyze and structure video advertisement content. This paper presents a novel multi-modal representation learning framework designed to automatically structure video advertisements by integrating visual, auditory, and textual modalities. Our approach leverages deep neural networks to extract and fuse features from video frames, audio tracks, and embedded text, enabling a comprehensive understanding of ad content. The proposed model utilizes a unified embedding space to align information across modalities, enhancing the detection of key segments such as scene boundaries, product demonstrations, and call-to-action moments. Experiments conducted on a large-scale dataset of video advertisements demonstrate significant improvements in content segmentation accuracy and semantic event detection compared to unimodal and baseline multi-modal models. The results highlight the benefits of multi-modal fusion in capturing complex narrative and promotional cues inherent to advertisements. This research provides valuable insights for downstream applications, including targeted ad generation, content summarization, and user engagement analysis. Our contributions advance the field of video content understanding by showcasing the potential of multi-modal representation learning for structured advertisement analysis. 

Keywords: multi-modal learning, video advertisement, deep learning, representation learning, content structuring, audio-visual fusion, semantic segmentation.
