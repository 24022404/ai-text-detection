This paper introduces a large-scale multimodal knowledge base system designed to effectively answer visual queries. The system integrates diverse data modalities, including images, text, and structured knowledge graphs, to provide comprehensive answers to complex user queries involving visual content. Our approach leverages advanced techniques in computer vision, natural language processing, and knowledge representation to facilitate reasoning across modalities. Specifically, we employ a novel attention mechanism to align visual features with relevant textual descriptions and knowledge graph entities, enabling accurate and context-aware query answering. Experimental results on a newly created benchmark dataset demonstrate that our system significantly outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The proposed system showcases the potential of multimodal knowledge bases for a wide range of applications, including image retrieval, visual question answering, and intelligent assistants. Keywords: Multimodal Knowledge Base, Visual Question Answering, Knowledge Graph, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Attention Mechanism.
