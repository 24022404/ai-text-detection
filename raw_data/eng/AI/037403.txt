Sequence prediction is a fundamental challenge in various domains, including natural language processing, time-series analysis, and bioinformatics. Traditional recurrent neural networks (RNNs) often struggle with capturing long-term dependencies and suffer from vanishing gradient problems. This paper introduces Spectral RNNs, a novel approach leveraging spectral transformations to enhance sequence modeling capabilities. The proposed method integrates Fourier-based spectral analysis into the RNN architecture, enabling efficient learning of both short-term and long-term patterns in sequential data. We evaluate Spectral RNNs on benchmark datasets for time-series forecasting and language modeling tasks, comparing their performance against state-of-the-art models such as LSTMs and GRUs. Experimental results demonstrate that Spectral RNNs achieve superior accuracy and faster convergence, particularly in scenarios requiring the modeling of complex temporal dependencies. The findings highlight the potential of spectral methods to address key limitations in traditional RNNs, offering a promising direction for future research in sequence prediction. This work contributes to the advancement of deep learning architectures by introducing a computationally efficient and scalable solution for sequential data analysis.  

**Keywords**: Sequence prediction, Spectral RNNs, Fourier transform, recurrent neural networks, time-series analysis, language modeling.
