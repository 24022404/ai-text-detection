Abstract:

Medical image analysis plays a vital role in modern healthcare, facilitating accurate diagnosis and treatment planning. However, the inherent complexity and high dimensionality of medical imaging data present significant challenges for conventional processing methods. This paper investigates the application of modern convex optimization techniques to various tasks in medical image analysis, including image segmentation, reconstruction, and denoising. We propose a unified framework that leverages advanced convex optimization algorithms, such as primal-dual and accelerated gradient methods, to efficiently solve key medical imaging problems. Our approach incorporates domain-specific constraints and regularization strategies to enhance robustness and accuracy, especially in noisy or incomplete data scenarios. Extensive experiments on benchmark medical image datasets demonstrate significant improvements in segmentation accuracy and reconstruction quality compared to traditional non-convex and heuristic-based methods. The results highlight the effectiveness of convex optimization in achieving faster convergence, improved reproducibility, and scalability to large-scale imaging studies. This research showcases the potential of modern convex optimization as a foundational tool for medical image analysis, offering practical insights for future integration into clinical decision support systems and automated diagnostic workflows. 

Keywords: convex optimization, medical image analysis, image segmentation, image reconstruction, denoising, biomedical imaging, algorithm efficiency.
