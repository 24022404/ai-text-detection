Cataract, a leading cause of blindness worldwide, necessitates accurate and efficient diagnostic methods. This paper introduces CaDIS, a novel dataset designed to advance research in automated cataract segmentation in slit-lamp images. CaDIS comprises a diverse collection of meticulously annotated slit-lamp images, capturing varying degrees of cataract severity and morphological characteristics. We employ a semi-automatic annotation pipeline, leveraging expert ophthalmologist knowledge to ensure high-quality ground truth labels. We benchmark several state-of-the-art deep learning models, including U-Net and Mask R-CNN, on CaDIS, providing a comparative analysis of their segmentation performance. Experimental results demonstrate the challenges posed by cataract variability and highlight the potential for deep learning techniques in this domain. Specifically, we achieve a Dice score of 0.85 using a modified U-Net architecture. CaDIS serves as a valuable resource for developing and evaluating automated cataract diagnosis systems, paving the way for improved accessibility and efficiency in ophthalmological care. The dataset and benchmarking code are publicly available to foster further research.

Keywords: Cataract Segmentation, Slit-Lamp Imaging, Deep Learning, U-Net, Medical Image Analysis, Dataset.
