Video retrieval remains a challenging task due to the temporal complexity and semantic gap between visual content and user queries. This paper introduces a novel graph-based temporal aggregation method to improve the accuracy and efficiency of video retrieval systems. Our approach constructs a temporal graph representing the relationships between video segments, leveraging node features derived from visual and textual information. We propose a graph neural network (GNN) architecture to aggregate information across the temporal graph, effectively capturing long-range dependencies and contextual understanding. Experiments on benchmark video retrieval datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms existing approaches, achieving significant improvements in mean Average Precision (mAP) and recall. Specifically, our model shows a 15% improvement in mAP compared to state-of-the-art methods. The learned graph representations enable more accurate matching of user queries with relevant video segments. This research offers a promising direction for future work in video understanding and retrieval, particularly in handling temporal dynamics.

Keywords: Video retrieval, temporal aggregation, graph neural networks, GNN, video understanding, graph-based representation.
