Data-free compression aims to reduce the storage and transmission costs of neural networks without access to the original training data, presenting a significant challenge. This paper introduces MixMix, a novel data-free compression technique centered around feature and data mixing to synthesize informative data surrogates. MixMix leverages feature mixing within network layers to generate diverse feature maps and subsequently employs data mixing across these feature maps to construct synthetic data. These synthetic data are then used to guide network compression via techniques such as pruning or quantization. We demonstrate that MixMix effectively captures the essential characteristics of the original data distribution, allowing for substantial model compression with minimal performance degradation. Extensive experiments on various datasets and network architectures demonstrate that MixMix outperforms existing data-free compression methods, achieving state-of-the-art compression rates while preserving accuracy. The simplicity and effectiveness of MixMix make it a practical solution for deploying compressed models in resource-constrained environments. Keywords: Data-Free Compression, Model Compression, Feature Mixing, Data Mixing, Neural Networks, Pruning, Quantization.
