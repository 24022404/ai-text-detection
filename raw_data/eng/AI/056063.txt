This paper addresses the challenge of fingerprint image segmentation by proposing a novel filter design approach. The objective is to develop an efficient and accurate method for separating fingerprint patterns from background noise. A custom-designed filter, utilizing a combination of spatial and frequency domain techniques, is applied to enhance image quality and facilitate segmentation. The filter's performance is evaluated using a comprehensive dataset of fingerprint images, with results demonstrating significant improvements in segmentation accuracy and computational efficiency. Compared to existing methods, the proposed approach achieves higher precision and recall rates, making it a valuable contribution to the field of biometric image processing. The study highlights the importance of optimized filter design in fingerprint image segmentation, with potential applications in forensic analysis, security systems, and identity verification. Key contributions include the development of a robust and adaptable filter, as well as a thorough evaluation framework for assessing segmentation performance. Relevant keywords: fingerprint image segmentation, filter design, biometric image processing, spatial and frequency domain analysis, image enhancement.
