Abstract

Accurate sales demand forecasting is a critical component for optimizing inventory management and resource allocation within the fast-growing e-commerce sector. This paper presents a novel methodology leveraging Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) neural networks to improve the precision of sales demand predictions in e-commerce environments. The proposed approach utilizes historical sales data, seasonality patterns, and promotional events to train a robust LSTM model capable of capturing complex temporal dependencies and non-linear trends. Experimental evaluations were conducted on a large-scale real-world e-commerce dataset, with comparative analyses against traditional forecasting methods, including moving average and ARIMA models. Results demonstrate that the LSTM-based approach significantly enhances prediction accuracy, reducing forecasting error rates and providing more reliable demand forecasts. These findings underline the potential of deep learning techniques, particularly LSTM networks, in addressing the unique challenges of sales demand prediction in dynamic e-commerce settings. The methodology has practical implications for inventory optimization, supply chain management, and customer satisfaction. Key keywords: sales forecasting, e-commerce, LSTM, neural networks, deep learning, demand prediction, time series analysis.
