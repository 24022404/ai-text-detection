This paper introduces a novel Multi-Subspace Neural Network (MSNN) designed to enhance image recognition performance by leveraging subspace learning within a deep learning framework. Traditional convolutional neural networks (CNNs) often struggle with capturing diverse feature representations across varying image domains, leading to suboptimal generalization. To address this limitation, we propose a multi-subspace approach that partitions the feature space into multiple subspaces, each optimized to capture distinct patterns and characteristics of the input data. The MSNN architecture integrates subspace-specific feature extraction layers, followed by a fusion mechanism to combine subspace outputs for final classification. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets, including CIFAR-10 and ImageNet, demonstrate that MSNN achieves superior accuracy compared to conventional CNNs and state-of-the-art subspace-based methods. The results highlight the model's ability to effectively handle complex image recognition tasks by exploiting diverse feature representations. This work contributes to advancing image recognition by introducing a scalable and interpretable framework that bridges subspace learning and deep neural networks, with potential applications in medical imaging, autonomous systems, and beyond.  

**Keywords**: Image recognition, multi-subspace learning, neural networks, feature extraction, deep learning, computer vision.
