Processing-in-memory (PIM) architectures offer significant potential for energy-efficient neural network acceleration. However, the inherent noise characteristics of emerging memory technologies pose a significant challenge to the reliability and accuracy of PIM-based neural networks. This paper introduces a novel noise-aware normalization technique designed to mitigate the impact of noise in PIM environments. Our approach characterizes the noise distribution of the memory array and dynamically adjusts normalization parameters during training to improve robustness. We evaluate our method on several benchmark datasets and neural network architectures, demonstrating a significant improvement in accuracy compared to traditional normalization techniques under various noise conditions. Specifically, our results show up to a 5% improvement in accuracy for ResNet-50 on ImageNet when deployed on a simulated noisy PIM system. The proposed noise-aware normalization offers a practical solution for deploying robust and accurate neural networks on emerging PIM hardware, paving the way for energy-efficient AI systems. Keywords: Processing-in-memory, Neural Networks, Noise-Aware Training, Normalization, Robustness, Emerging Memory.
