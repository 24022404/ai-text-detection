**Abstract**

This paper addresses the challenge of reinforcement learning (RL) in environments featuring hybrid action spaces, where each action selection involves both discrete choices and associated continuous parameters. Traditional deep Q-networks (DQNs) excel in purely discrete action spaces, but their applicability is constrained in hybrid domains commonly found in robotics, autonomous systems, and resource allocation problems. We propose a novel Parametrized Deep Q-Networks (PDQN) framework capable of efficiently learning policies in discrete-continuous hybrid action spaces. Our approach integrates discrete action selection with dedicated parameter networks for continuous components, utilizing end-to-end training with joint loss optimization. Extensive experimental evaluations on a set of benchmark hybrid RL environments demonstrate that PDQN achieves superior performance compared to existing baselines, including parameterized policy gradients and hybrid actor-critic methods. The results indicate improved convergence rates, stable learning dynamics, and consistent policy performance across various problem scales. Our findings establish the effectiveness of the PDQN framework and open new avenues for real-world RL applications that require fine-grained action control. Key innovations include seamless parameter sharing between discrete and continuous branches and an efficient exploration strategy tailored to hybrid action spaces. This work advances the state of the art in reinforcement learning by providing a scalable and generalizable solution for environments with discrete-continuous action requirements.

**Keywords:** reinforcement learning, deep Q-network, hybrid action space, discrete-continuous actions, parametrized actions, policy optimization, deep learning
