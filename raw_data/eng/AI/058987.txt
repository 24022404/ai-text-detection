This paper presents Dam Burst, a novel region-merging-based image segmentation method designed to efficiently partition images into meaningful regions. The objective of this research is to address the challenges of traditional image segmentation techniques, which often struggle with complex scenes and noisy data. Our approach utilizes a hierarchical merging strategy, leveraging spatial and spectral information to guide the region merging process. The method is based on a robust similarity metric that assesses the homogeneity of adjacent regions, allowing for adaptive and accurate boundary detection. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of Dam Burst in segmenting diverse images, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The key contributions of this work include a flexible and scalable segmentation framework, capable of handling high-resolution images and complex scenes. The proposed method has significant implications for various applications, including object recognition, scene understanding, and medical image analysis. Key keywords: image segmentation, region merging, hierarchical clustering, computer vision, object recognition.
