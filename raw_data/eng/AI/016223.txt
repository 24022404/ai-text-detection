Autonomous aerial vehicles (AAVs) require robust and adaptive control policies to navigate complex and dynamic environments. This paper addresses the challenge of learning high-performance flight controllers by proposing a novel framework that integrates deep reinforcement learning with model predictive control (MPC)-guided policy search. The objective is to leverage the precision of MPC in generating optimal reference trajectories and safe control actions, while utilizing deep neural networks to learn generalized, real-time control policies directly from raw sensor data. In our approach, the policy network is trained using supervised learning, where training data is obtained from expert demonstrations generated by MPC under varying environmental and dynamic conditions. Experimental evaluations on both simulated and real-world aerial platforms demonstrate that the learned policies achieve superior flight stability, obstacle avoidance, and adaptation to sensor noise when compared to baseline reinforcement learning and conventional controller benchmarks. The results highlight significant improvements in data efficiency, policy robustness, and real-time performance. Our findings suggest that MPC-guided policy search offers a scalable and effective methodology for developing autonomous aerial vehicle controllers capable of handling complex tasks in challenging scenarios. The proposed framework is broadly applicable to a range of autonomous robotics domains requiring safe, efficient, and adaptive decision-making.  
Keywords: autonomous aerial vehicles, deep reinforcement learning, model predictive control, policy search, neural networks, real-time control, robotics.
