This study aims to improve the accuracy and reliability of breast ultrasound diagnosis by leveraging a multi-AI combination approach. The objective is to develop a more robust and trustworthy AI solution for detecting breast lesions and distinguishing between benign and malignant tumors. Our approach utilizes a ensemble of deep learning models, including convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and transfer learning techniques, to analyze ultrasound images and provide diagnostic predictions. The results show that the multi-AI combination approach outperforms single-model methods, achieving a significant improvement in diagnostic accuracy and reducing false positives. The proposed system has the potential to assist radiologists in making more accurate diagnoses and ultimately enhance patient outcomes. The novelty of this research lies in the combination of multiple AI models to improve the reliability and generalizability of breast ultrasound diagnosis, making it a valuable contribution to the field of medical imaging and AI-assisted diagnosis. Key keywords: breast ultrasound, multi-AI combination, deep learning, CNNs, medical imaging, AI-assisted diagnosis.
