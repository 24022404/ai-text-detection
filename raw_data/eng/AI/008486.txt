This paper explores the application of reinforcement learning (RL) techniques within the R programming environment, aiming to bridge the gap between RL research and practical implementation for data scientists and statisticians. We present a novel framework for implementing and evaluating RL algorithms directly in R, leveraging its extensive statistical computing capabilities and visualization tools. Our approach focuses on providing a user-friendly interface and efficient computational methods for both discrete and continuous action spaces. We implement several classic RL algorithms, including Q-learning, SARSA, and policy gradient methods, and benchmark their performance on a suite of benchmark environments within R. Results demonstrate the feasibility and effectiveness of performing RL tasks natively in R, achieving comparable performance to implementations in other languages while benefiting from R's strong statistical analysis and visualization capabilities. This work facilitates the use of RL for solving real-world problems in areas such as marketing, finance, and healthcare, by making RL more accessible to a broader audience of R users. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, R Programming, Statistical Computing, Machine Learning, Q-learning.
