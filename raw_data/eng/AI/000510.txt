This paper aims to explore the application of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) in uncovering hidden patterns within patient multivariate time series data to predict healthcare costs. Our approach involves utilizing CNNs to learn complex representations of patient data, including clinical, demographic, and temporal features. The proposed model is evaluated on a real-world dataset, demonstrating its ability to accurately predict healthcare costs and outperform traditional machine learning methods. Key findings indicate that the CNN-based approach can effectively capture non-linear relationships and temporal dependencies in patient data, leading to improved predictive performance. The study contributes to the existing body of research by introducing a novel application of CNNs in healthcare cost prediction, highlighting the potential of deep learning techniques in identifying high-risk patients and optimizing resource allocation. The results have significant implications for healthcare providers, policymakers, and insurers, enabling data-driven decision-making and personalized care. Relevant keywords: Convolutional Neural Networks, multivariate time series analysis, healthcare cost prediction, deep learning, patient outcomes.
