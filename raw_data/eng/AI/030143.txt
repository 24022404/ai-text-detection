This paper addresses the challenge of conservativeness in offline reinforcement learning, where learned policies often exhibit overly cautious behavior due to the limitations of offline data. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that reduces conservativeness while maintaining the reliability of offline reinforcement learning. We propose a new framework that combines offline data with a carefully designed regularization technique to encourage more exploratory policies. Our method utilizes a modified behavioral cloning algorithm, incorporating a conservativeness penalty term to mitigate the issue. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing offline reinforcement learning methods, achieving improved performance on a range of benchmark tasks. The key findings of this study highlight the importance of balancing exploration and exploitation in offline reinforcement learning, and our proposed method provides a valuable contribution to the field. By reducing conservativeness, our approach enables more effective offline reinforcement learning, with potential applications in areas such as robotics and autonomous systems. Key keywords: offline reinforcement learning, conservativeness, behavioral cloning, regularization techniques, exploration-exploitation trade-off.
