This paper presents a novel system for the automatic discovery and geotagging of objects from street view imagery. The objective is to extract structured geospatial information from ubiquitous visual data without relying on manual annotation. Our approach combines deep learning-based object detection with geometric reasoning to identify and precisely locate objects of interest. We leverage a pre-trained convolutional neural network fine-tuned for object categories relevant to urban environments. Detected objects are then geotagged using camera pose information extracted from the street view imagery metadata and refined through a novel bundle adjustment process. Experiments conducted on a large-scale dataset demonstrate high accuracy in object detection and geotagging. The system achieves a significant improvement in localization precision compared to methods that rely solely on GPS data. This research enables the creation of rich, automatically generated geospatial datasets, facilitating applications such as urban planning, autonomous navigation, and infrastructure monitoring. Keywords: street view imagery, object detection, geotagging, deep learning, computer vision, geospatial data.
