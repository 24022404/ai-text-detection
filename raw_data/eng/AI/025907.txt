This paper introduces Semi-Relaxed Quantization with DropBits, a novel approach to training low-bit neural networks. The objective is to improve the performance of quantized neural networks by mitigating the loss of precision inherent in low-bit representations. Our method employs a bit-wise regularization technique, DropBits, which randomly drops bits during training to simulate the effects of quantization noise. This approach enables the network to learn robust features and adapt to the noise introduced by quantization. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing quantization techniques, achieving state-of-the-art performance on various benchmark datasets. The key findings indicate that Semi-Relaxed Quantization with DropBits can effectively train low-bit neural networks with minimal loss in accuracy. This research contributes to the development of efficient and scalable deep learning models, with potential applications in edge AI, mobile devices, and other resource-constrained environments. Key keywords: quantized neural networks, low-bit representation, bit-wise regularization, DropBits, efficient deep learning.
