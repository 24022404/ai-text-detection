This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing security in EEG-based biometric systems through the application of adversarial deep learning techniques. EEG biometrics, known for their uniqueness and resistance to traditional spoofing attacks, face emerging threats from adversarial examples that can compromise system integrity.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel adversarial deep learning framework specifically designed for EEG biometrics. This framework employs a combination of generative adversarial networks (GANs) and convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to create and detect adversarial perturbations. The generative model is utilized to simulate adversarial examples against EEG datasets, while the detection model is trained to recognize and mitigate these threats, thereby enhancing system robustness.

Results/Findings: Our experiments demonstrate that the proposed method significantly improves the resilience of EEG biometric systems against adversarial attacks. The framework achieves a high detection accuracy of adversarial examples while maintaining the identification accuracy of legitimate EEG signals. Compared to traditional methods, our approach exhibits superior performance, particularly in the scalability across diverse EEG datasets and varying noise levels.

Conclusion/Implications: The integration of adversarial deep learning into EEG biometrics offers a promising direction for improving security in biometric authentication systems. This research contributes a unique adversarial defense mechanism, paving the way for more secure biometric applications. Our findings suggest potential applications in secure access control and personalized healthcare systems, where EEG-based user authentication is becoming increasingly prevalent.

Keywords: Adversarial Deep Learning, EEG Biometrics, Generative Adversarial Networks, Convolutional Neural Networks, Biometric Security, Adversarial Examples, Authentication.
