This paper addresses the challenge of accurately classifying filling levels in containers, a crucial task in various industrial applications. The objective is to enhance the robustness and reliability of filling level classification models by leveraging adversarial training techniques. Our approach involves training a deep neural network using adversarial examples, which are specifically designed to mislead the model, thereby improving its resilience to variations in data. The results show that our adversarial training method significantly improves the classification accuracy and reduces the error rate compared to traditional training methods. Key findings indicate that the proposed approach can effectively handle noisy and uncertain data, leading to more reliable filling level classification. The conclusion highlights the importance of adversarial training in improving the performance of classification models, with potential applications in industrial automation, quality control, and monitoring systems. Our research contributes to the development of more robust and accurate filling level classification systems, with relevant keywords including adversarial training, deep learning, classification, industrial automation, and computer vision.
