When a customer creates a ticket, the Admin closely reviews its details, approves its validity, assigns it to the appropriate staff member, and selects which SLA policies should be enforced. In real-time systems, this ticket scheduling process can become overwhelmed, primarily because tickets arrive with differing levels of priority. This variation often leads to overload, making it challenging to successfully schedule all tickets within required timeframes. As a result, the efficiency of resource usage decreases, ultimately leading to a decline in overall scheduling success and a rise in resource waste.
