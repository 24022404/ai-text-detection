This paper proposes the Teacher-Class Network, a novel neural network compression mechanism designed to reduce the computational complexity and memory requirements of large-scale deep learning models. The objective is to develop an efficient method for compressing neural networks while preserving their accuracy, thereby enabling their deployment on resource-constrained devices. Our approach involves a teacher-student framework, where a pre-trained teacher model guides the training of a smaller student model, with a focus on class-level knowledge distillation. The results show that our method achieves significant compression ratios while maintaining competitive performance on various benchmark datasets. The key findings indicate that the Teacher-Class Network outperforms existing compression techniques, such as pruning and quantization, in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The implications of this research are substantial, as it enables the widespread adoption of deep learning models in edge devices and real-time applications, contributing to the development of more efficient and scalable AI systems. Key keywords: neural network compression, knowledge distillation, teacher-student framework, deep learning, model pruning, quantization.
