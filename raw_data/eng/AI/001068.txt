The objective of this paper is to introduce PISE, an innovative framework for Person Image Synthesis and Editing utilizing a Decoupled Generative Adversarial Network (GAN). Our approach addresses challenges in generating high-quality, realistic personalized images, while enabling advanced editing capabilities. PISE employs a novel decoupling strategy that separates style and semantic feature representations, allowing for greater flexibility and precision in image synthesis and manipulation tasks. The GAN architecture is meticulously designed with two distinct modules: a style generator and a semantic transformer, which collaboratively enhance the realism and customization of person images. Our experimental results demonstrate that PISE significantly outperforms existing methods in terms of visual quality and editing accuracy, especially in scenarios requiring complex background integration and fine-grained attribute adjustments. The findings indicate that the decoupling approach effectively mitigates common artifacts associated with traditional GANs, resulting in smoother transitions and lifelike textures. Conclusively, PISE offers substantial contributions to the field of visual computing, paving the way for sophisticated applications in personalized media content creation, virtual try-on systems, and adaptive digital avatars. Key keywords include person image synthesis, image editing, GAN, decoupled GAN, style generation, and semantic transformation.
