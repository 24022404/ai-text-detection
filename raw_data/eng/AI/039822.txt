This paper introduces Smooth Grad-CAM++, an advanced inference-level visualization technique designed to enhance the interpretability of deep convolutional neural network (CNN) models. The objective is to provide a more accurate and detailed visualization of the decision-making process in CNNs, addressing the limitations of existing methods. Our approach combines the strengths of Grad-CAM++ with smoothing techniques to generate high-resolution visualizations that highlight the most relevant input features contributing to the model's predictions. The results demonstrate that Smooth Grad-CAM++ outperforms state-of-the-art visualization methods in terms of visual quality and feature relevance. Our technique has significant implications for model interpretability, explainability, and trustworthiness, particularly in applications where understanding model decisions is crucial, such as medical imaging and autonomous driving. Key contributions include improved visualization fidelity, enhanced feature localization, and increased model transparency. Relevant keywords: Grad-CAM++, deep learning, CNNs, model interpretability, explainable AI, visualization techniques.
