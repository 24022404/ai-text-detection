This paper presents a novel approach to face attribute manipulation using residual image learning. The objective is to develop an efficient and effective method for editing facial attributes, such as hair color, eye color, and facial expression, while preserving the original image's identity and realism. Our approach employs a deep learning model that learns to generate residual images, which are then combined with the original image to produce the desired attribute manipulation. The method utilizes a generative adversarial network (GAN) architecture, incorporating a residual learning module to refine the attribute editing process. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving high-quality and realistic face attribute manipulations with significant improvements over existing methods. The proposed technique has potential applications in various fields, including computer vision, graphics, and social media. Key contributions of this research include the introduction of residual image learning for face attribute manipulation and the development of a robust GAN-based framework for efficient and realistic attribute editing. Relevant keywords: face attribute manipulation, residual image learning, generative adversarial networks, deep learning, computer vision.
