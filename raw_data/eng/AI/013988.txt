This paper proposes a novel approach to anomaly detection, leveraging attention maps and hard augmentation techniques in a two-stage framework. The objective is to enhance the accuracy and robustness of anomaly detection models, particularly in scenarios where anomalies are subtle or rare. Our method employs a hard augmentation strategy to generate diverse and challenging samples, which are then used to train an attention map-guided model. This model focuses on salient regions of the input data, allowing for more effective anomaly detection. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in detection accuracy and recall. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of attention map guidance and hard augmentation, which collectively enhance the model's ability to identify complex anomalies. This work has important implications for applications such as surveillance, healthcare, and quality control, where accurate anomaly detection is critical. Key keywords: anomaly detection, attention maps, hard augmentation, deep learning, computer vision.
