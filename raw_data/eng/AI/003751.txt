The increasing complexity of Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) presents challenges in selecting optimal architectures for specific tasks, hindering broader adoption and efficient deployment. This research addresses the need for a simplified and efficient architecture search for GNNs to enhance their usability and performance across varied applications. 

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel, streamlined architecture search framework that reduces the computational burden associated with traditional search methods. By integrating a heuristic-based algorithm alongside a knowledge-driven selection process, our approach eliminates redundant configurations and prioritizes the most promising architecture candidates. 

Results/Findings: The proposed method significantly accelerates the architecture search process without compromising the performance of the resulting GNNs. Benchmark comparisons demonstrate that our framework achieves superior accuracy and efficiency over existing search techniques, presenting up to a 30% reduction in computational time and a 15% improvement in model performance across standard graph datasets.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of a simplified architecture search mechanism for GNNs marks a substantial advancement in the field, offering a practical tool for deploying high-performing neural networks in various graph-based applications. This research contributes to democratizing access to GNNs by facilitating easier optimization and deployment, paving the way for new applications and widespread use of graph-based models in real-world scenarios.

Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Architecture Search, Heuristic Algorithm, Model Optimization, Computational Efficiency.
