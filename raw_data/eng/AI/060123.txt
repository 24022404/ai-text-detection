MailLeak addresses the challenge of extracting sensitive information from email communications compromised through data breaches, where obfuscation techniques hinder accurate character recognition. This paper introduces a novel transfer learning approach to enhance the robustness of Optical Character Recognition (OCR) systems against email-specific obfuscations, such as character substitutions and font distortions. We fine-tune pre-trained OCR models using a synthetic dataset generated to mimic real-world email obfuscation patterns. Our method significantly improves character extraction accuracy on obfuscated email text compared to traditional OCR engines and other transfer learning strategies. Evaluation on a benchmark dataset of leaked emails demonstrates an average F1-score increase of 15% in character recognition. The proposed approach enables more effective identification of leaked personally identifiable information (PII) and sensitive data in compromised email archives, improving data breach analysis and incident response capabilities. Keywords: OCR, Transfer Learning, Data Breach, Email Obfuscation, Character Recognition, Information Extraction, MailLeak.
