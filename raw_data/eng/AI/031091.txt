This paper addresses the challenge of interactive segmentation, a critical process in image editing and medical imaging, by introducing a novel approach utilizing probabilistic attention mechanisms. Traditional interactive segmentation techniques often encounter limitations in accurately segmenting complex and overlapping regions, necessitating the development of more sophisticated models.

Methods/Approach: We propose a probabilistic attention framework that integrates with existing neural networks to enhance their capability in segmenting images interactively. This model employs probabilistic distributions over attention maps to capture spatial dependencies and contextual cues more effectively during the segmentation process. The approach involves leveraging interactive user inputs to iteratively refine segmentation boundaries with improved precision.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that our probabilistic attention model significantly outperforms state-of-the-art interactive segmentation methods on benchmark datasets by achieving higher accuracy and robustness. The model consistently enhances performance even in challenging scenarios with intricate object boundaries and partial occlusions, showcasing its versatility and adaptability.

Conclusion/Implications: The integration of probabilistic attention in interactive segmentation offers a transformative advancement in image processing, with implications for both academic research and practical applications, such as in medical imaging where precision is paramount. This research contributes a novel perspective to the field, providing a foundation for further exploration into attention-based segmentation methods and fostering future innovation in interactive image analysis.

Keywords: Interactive Segmentation, Probabilistic Attention, Image Processing, Neural Networks, Medical Imaging, Contextual Cues, Precision.
