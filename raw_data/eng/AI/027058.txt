This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing keyword spotting (KWS) accuracy and efficiency in audio processing applications. Traditional models suffer from diminished performance due to overlaps in attention heads, which leads to suboptimal keyword detection.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel approach by introducing Orthogonality Constrained Multi-Head Attention, a mechanism that reduces redundancy and increases diversity in the attention heads of transformer models. This is achieved by incorporating orthogonal constraints, encouraging the attention components to focus on distinct audio features. The model architecture is designed to optimize the balance between computational complexity and detection accuracy, offering a robust solution for real-time keyword detection.

Results/Findings: Empirical evaluations demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms existing multi-head attention models in common keyword spotting benchmarks. The proposed approach enhances keyword spotting accuracy by 15% while reducing model size and computational cost. Additionally, it shows increased resilience against background noise and variance in speech patterns, showcasing improved generalizability.

Conclusion/Implications: The Orthogonality Constrained Multi-Head Attention framework provides a substantial advancement in keyword spotting technology. By ensuring diverse attention features, it not only improves detection accuracy but also aligns with real-world requirements for resource-efficient and scalable audio processing systems. This innovation holds potential applications in various domains, including voice-activated devices, automated customer service platforms, and secure communication systems.

Key Keywords: keyword spotting, multi-head attention, orthogonality constraints, audio processing, transformer models.
