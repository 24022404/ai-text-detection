Abstract:

This paper addresses the challenge of bridging the gap between image processing and high-level image perception systems by proposing an end-to-end framework that jointly optimizes both stages. Traditional image processing pipelines and perceptual models are typically developed and fine-tuned independently, which can result in suboptimal system performance when exposed to real-world image artifacts such as noise, compression, and sensor defects—collectively referred to as “dirty pixels.” To overcome these limitations, we introduce a unified deep learning architecture that integrates image restoration, enhancement, and perception modules into a single trainable model. Our method leverages convolutional neural networks and task-aware loss functions to enable the seamless flow of information between low-level and high-level tasks. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach not only improves perceptual accuracy under challenging conditions but also outperforms conventional cascaded systems in terms of robustness and computational efficiency. The findings highlight the potential of end-to-end paradigms for advancing reliable computer vision, with applications in autonomous systems, surveillance, and mobile imaging. Keywords: end-to-end learning, image processing, perception, deep learning, noisy images, image enhancement, computer vision.
