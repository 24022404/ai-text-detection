Obesity is a growing global health concern, necessitating innovative solutions for effective prevention and management. This paper introduces *MOFit*, a novel framework that leverages Machine Learning (ML) and Internet of Things (IoT) technologies to address obesity by providing personalized health monitoring and intervention strategies. The objective of *MOFit* is to integrate real-time data from wearable IoT devices with advanced ML algorithms to analyze user behavior, predict health risks, and deliver tailored recommendations for physical activity and dietary habits. The framework employs a hybrid ML model combining supervised and unsupervised learning techniques to process diverse data streams, including activity levels, heart rate, and caloric intake. Results from a pilot study demonstrate that *MOFit* achieves a 15% improvement in predicting obesity-related health risks compared to existing methods, while also enhancing user engagement through adaptive feedback mechanisms. The framework’s scalability and real-time capabilities make it a promising tool for healthcare providers and individuals seeking to combat obesity. *MOFit* contributes to the field by bridging the gap between IoT-enabled health monitoring and ML-driven personalized interventions, offering a practical and innovative approach to obesity reduction.  

**Keywords**: Obesity, Machine Learning, IoT, Health Monitoring, Personalized Interventions, Wearable Devices.
