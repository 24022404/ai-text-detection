This study aims to develop an innovative approach for constructing forest biomass prediction maps using radar backscatter data. By leveraging a sequential regression framework integrated with a conditional generative adversarial network (CGAN), our method enhances the accuracy and efficiency of biomass estimation. The approach involves a two-stage process, where initial biomass predictions are refined through a CGAN-based model that incorporates radar backscatter and environmental factors. Our results show significant improvements in biomass prediction accuracy compared to traditional methods, with a reduction in mean absolute error. The proposed technique has important implications for forest management, carbon cycle modeling, and environmental monitoring. Key contributions include the integration of CGANs with sequential regression for biomass prediction and the demonstration of improved performance using radar backscatter data. This research highlights the potential of advanced machine learning techniques, such as deep learning and generative models, for enhancing forest biomass estimation and mapping. Relevant keywords: forest biomass, radar backscatter, conditional generative adversarial network, sequential regression, remote sensing, environmental monitoring.
