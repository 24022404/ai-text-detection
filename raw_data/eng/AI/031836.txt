This paper investigates the impact of tensor product representation (TPR) based feature fusion on (de)compositional neural network architectures for image caption generation. The objective is to enhance the semantic alignment between visual features and linguistic constructs to improve the quality and descriptiveness of automatically generated captions. Our approach incorporates TPR to fuse high-level visual features extracted from convolutional neural networks with compositional language representations, enabling the network to capture richer associations between objects, attributes, and their relations in images. We systematically compare the proposed TPR-based feature fusion model with traditional concatenation and attention-based fusion methods across standard image captioning benchmarks. Experimental results demonstrate that the TPR-enhanced (de)compositional network achieves superior performance, as measured by BLEU, METEOR, and CIDEr scores, particularly in generating captions that accurately reflect complex scene structures. The findings highlight the effectiveness of structured feature fusion for grounded language generation tasks. This work contributes a novel perspective on integrating tensor-based representations within captioning networks and opens avenues for more interpretable and flexible multimodal models in vision and language processing. Key keywords include tensor product representation, feature fusion, (de)compositional networks, image caption generation, and multimodal learning.
