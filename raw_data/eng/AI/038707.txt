This paper addresses the challenge of transductive inference in higher-order Markov Random Fields (MRFs) by proposing a novel Discrete-Continuous Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (ADMM) approach. The objective is to efficiently infer the labels of unseen data points by leveraging the higher-order dependencies and structural information in the data. Our method combines the strengths of discrete and continuous optimization techniques to solve the resulting complex inference problem. The proposed ADMM algorithm is designed to handle the non-submodular higher-order potentials and large-scale datasets. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, showing improved performance compared to existing methods. The key contributions of this work include the development of a scalable and efficient algorithm for transductive inference in higher-order MRFs, and the demonstration of its applicability to various real-world problems. Our approach has significant implications for applications such as image segmentation, object recognition, and network analysis, where higher-order dependencies play a crucial role. Key keywords: ADMM, transductive inference, higher-order MRFs, discrete-continuous optimization, non-submodular potentials.
