Gaussian Processes (GPs) offer a powerful non-parametric Bayesian approach for modeling complex functions. However, standard GP models struggle with dynamically changing relationships in data, particularly when represented as evolving graphs. This paper introduces Evolving-Graph Gaussian Processes (EGGP), a novel framework that extends GPs to explicitly model time-varying graph structures. EGGP integrates graph neural networks (GNNs) within a GP framework, allowing the GP to adapt its covariance function based on the evolving graph topology. Specifically, we employ a GNN to learn latent representations of nodes, which are then used to modulate the GP kernel. This allows the model to capture both the temporal dependencies in the node features and the evolving relationships between nodes represented by the graph. We evaluate EGGP on several synthetic and real-world graph datasets, demonstrating its superior performance in prediction accuracy and its ability to adapt to changing graph structures compared to static-graph GP models and other baselines. The results highlight EGGP's potential in applications such as dynamic social network analysis and time-varying sensor networks.
Keywords: Gaussian Processes, Graph Neural Networks, Evolving Graphs, Time-Series Analysis, Bayesian Models.
