**Abstract**

The rapid adoption of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in decision-critical domains has highlighted the necessity for transparent and interpretable models. This paper introduces SIDU, a novel Explainable AI (XAI) method centered on Similarity, Difference, and Uniqueness analyses to provide deeper insights into model predictions. The objective is to address the challenge of elucidating complex AI model decisions by enabling users to understand what features or data segments contribute most significantly to individual predictions. SIDU operates by systematically quantifying the similarities and differences among data instances, and isolating unique elements that drive specific outcomes. The proposed framework is evaluated using both tabular and image datasets, applying SIDU to state-of-the-art deep learning and ensemble models. Results demonstrate that SIDU generates interpretable explanations with high fidelity, outperforming existing XAI techniques in both qualitative human assessments and quantitative metrics such as explanation coherence and user trust. The explainability is further enhanced by intuitive visualizations that highlight discriminative features and unique factors in each prediction context. The study concludes that SIDU offers a robust and generalizable approach for interpretable AI, thereby facilitating model validation, debugging, and trust in a wide range of applications. Key keywords include Explainable AI, interpretability, model transparency, decision support, similarity analysis, difference analysis, uniqueness, deep learning, and visualization.
