This paper investigates the effectiveness of bonus-based exploration methods in reinforcement learning (RL) within the Arcade Learning Environment (ALE). While exploration is critical for discovering optimal policies in RL, traditional methods often struggle in complex environments with sparse rewards. To address this, we evaluate several bonus-based exploration techniques, including intrinsic motivation and curiosity-driven approaches, which incentivize agents to explore novel states. We propose a hybrid exploration strategy that combines intrinsic rewards with environment-specific heuristics to enhance exploration efficiency. Our experiments compare the performance of these methods across a diverse set of ALE games, measuring metrics such as cumulative reward, exploration coverage, and convergence speed. Results demonstrate that our hybrid approach outperforms baseline methods, achieving higher rewards and faster convergence in environments with sparse rewards. The findings highlight the importance of tailored exploration strategies in complex RL tasks and provide insights into the design of more efficient exploration mechanisms for future applications in gaming, robotics, and beyond. Key contributions include a novel exploration framework and empirical evidence of its effectiveness in the ALE. Keywords: reinforcement learning, exploration strategies, intrinsic motivation, Arcade Learning Environment, bonus-based methods.
