Snow removal from images is a critical challenge in computer vision, particularly for applications in autonomous driving, surveillance, and remote sensing. Existing methods often struggle with preserving fine details and handling diverse snow conditions. This paper introduces **DesnowNet**, a novel context-aware deep learning network designed to effectively remove snow artifacts while maintaining image quality. The proposed approach leverages a multi-scale feature extraction mechanism combined with a context aggregation module to adaptively address varying snow patterns and intensities. By integrating spatial and contextual information, DesnowNet achieves superior performance in both synthetic and real-world snow scenarios. Experimental results demonstrate that DesnowNet outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of visual quality, structural preservation, and quantitative metrics such as PSNR and SSIM. The network’s robustness across diverse datasets highlights its potential for real-world deployment. This research contributes to advancing snow removal techniques, offering a scalable solution for enhancing image clarity in adverse weather conditions.  

**Keywords**: Snow removal, deep learning, context-aware networks, image restoration, computer vision.
