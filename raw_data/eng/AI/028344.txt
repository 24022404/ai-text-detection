This paper presents GLEAN, a novel generative model designed for large-factor image super-resolution. The primary objective of this research is to address the limitations of existing super-resolution techniques that often struggle with reconstructing high-quality images from low-resolution inputs, particularly at large upscaling factors. We propose a unique latent bank approach that encodes diverse visual patterns, enabling the model to learn rich representations from a vast dataset. Utilizing a combination of advanced convolutional neural networks and generative adversarial networks, GLEAN demonstrates enhanced performance in generating high-resolution images that not only preserve fine details but also exhibit improved perceptual quality compared to traditional methods.

Our results showcase significant advancements, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets with large-factor scaling, outperforming leading super-resolution frameworks in both quantitative metrics and qualitative visual assessments. The findings underscore the effectiveness of our latent bank architecture in capturing intricate textures and promoting plausible image generation.

In conclusion, GLEAN represents a significant contribution to the field of image processing, offering innovative methodologies for large-factor super-resolution with potential applications in various domains such as digital media, biomedical imaging, and remote sensing. Future work will explore the extension of this model to other image generation tasks, further broadening its impact. 

Keywords: generative model, image super-resolution, latent bank, deep learning, convolutional neural networks.
