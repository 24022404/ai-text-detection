This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing image quality through High Dynamic Range (HDR) transformation, particularly in scenarios with uneven lighting, overexposure, or underexposure. We propose a novel deep learning-based framework, termed Deep Reciprocating HDR Transformation (DRHT), which leverages a reciprocating neural network architecture to dynamically adjust pixel intensities and restore visual details. The DRHT model integrates multi-scale feature extraction and adaptive tone mapping to ensure robust performance across diverse lighting conditions. Experimental results demonstrate that DRHT outperforms existing methods in terms of both qualitative and quantitative metrics, achieving superior image clarity, color accuracy, and detail preservation. The proposed approach is computationally efficient and scalable, making it suitable for real-time applications in photography, surveillance, and medical imaging. This research contributes a significant advancement in image correction techniques, offering a practical solution for improving visual quality in challenging environments.  

**Keywords**: High Dynamic Range (HDR), image correction, deep learning, reciprocating neural network, adaptive tone mapping, real-time processing.
