This paper addresses the challenge of extreme relative pose estimation for RGB-D scans, a critical task in 3D scene understanding and reconstruction. Traditional methods often struggle with large viewpoint changes or incomplete scene data, limiting their applicability in real-world scenarios. To overcome these limitations, we propose a novel approach that leverages scene completion techniques to enhance pose estimation accuracy. Our method integrates a deep learning-based scene completion network with a robust pose estimation framework, enabling the system to infer missing scene geometry and align scans even under extreme viewpoint variations. We evaluate our approach on benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in pose estimation accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods. The results highlight the effectiveness of scene completion in handling incomplete data and large viewpoint changes, offering a promising solution for applications in robotics, augmented reality, and 3D mapping. This work contributes a new perspective on combining scene understanding and pose estimation, paving the way for more robust and scalable 3D reconstruction systems.  

**Keywords**: RGB-D scans, relative pose estimation, scene completion, 3D reconstruction, deep learning, viewpoint invariance.
