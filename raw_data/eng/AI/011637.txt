This paper explores the integration of causality into machine learning frameworks, addressing the critical challenge of distinguishing between correlation and causation in complex datasets. Our approach leverages causal graph structures and do-calculus to develop a novel algorithm that can identify and quantify causal relationships. We apply this method to various domains, including healthcare and finance, demonstrating its ability to improve model interpretability and predictive accuracy. The results show significant enhancements in model performance, particularly in scenarios where causal insights are crucial. Our research contributes to the development of more reliable and transparent machine learning models, with potential applications in high-stakes decision-making. By emphasizing causality, we provide a foundation for more accurate and informative AI systems, highlighting the importance of causal machine learning in real-world problem-solving. Key aspects of this work include causal discovery, causal inference, and explainable AI, offering a unique perspective on the intersection of machine learning and causality.
