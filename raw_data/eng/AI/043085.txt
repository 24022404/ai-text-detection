This paper introduces HDMapGen, a novel hierarchical graph generative model designed to create high definition maps. The objective is to address the challenge of generating accurate and detailed maps, crucial for autonomous vehicles and smart city applications. Our approach combines graph theory and generative models to produce maps that capture complex road networks and geometric relationships. The HDMapGen model utilizes a hierarchical representation, allowing it to generate maps at multiple scales and resolutions. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of HDMapGen in producing high-quality maps, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The key findings highlight the importance of hierarchical graph structures in capturing the complexity of real-world maps. This research contributes to the development of advanced mapping technologies, with potential applications in autonomous driving, urban planning, and geographic information systems. The HDMapGen model showcases the power of graph generative models in creating detailed and accurate maps, paving the way for future research in this field. Key keywords: high definition maps, graph generative models, autonomous vehicles, smart city applications, geographic information systems.
