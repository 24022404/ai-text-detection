This paper introduces Language2Pose, a novel approach to pose forecasting that leverages natural language to predict future human poses. The objective is to develop a system that can accurately forecast poses based on linguistic descriptions, enabling applications in fields such as robotics, animation, and human-computer interaction. Our approach utilizes a multimodal fusion of language and vision, combining the strengths of language models and pose estimation algorithms to generate accurate pose forecasts. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of Language2Pose, outperforming existing methods in pose forecasting accuracy and robustness. The key findings highlight the importance of incorporating linguistic context in pose forecasting, leading to improved performance and generalizability. This research contributes to the development of more sophisticated human-machine interaction systems, with potential applications in areas such as virtual reality, gaming, and assistive technologies. Key keywords: pose forecasting, natural language processing, human-computer interaction, multimodal fusion, language models.
