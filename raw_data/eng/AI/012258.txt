The paper introduces RL-Duet, an innovative framework designed to generate real-time music accompaniment, addressing the challenge of creating dynamic and adaptive accompaniments that enhance live performances. Traditional methods are often limited by pre-composed solutions, which lack adaptability to variations in live settings.

Methods/Approach: RL-Duet employs deep reinforcement learning to train a model capable of producing high-quality musical accompaniments in a live context. The framework utilizes a novel reward system to evaluate harmony, rhythm, and overall musicality, training the model to not only mimic style but also complement live input dynamically. The architecture is based on a combination of neural networks that analyze input data in real time, enabling instantaneous response to changes in musical input.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate that RL-Duet outperforms existing systems in generating cohesive and artistically satisfying accompaniments. Quantitative analyses confirm its superiority in adaptability and responsiveness, while qualitative feedback from musicians highlights its potential to transform live performances. In comparison to traditional accompaniment systems, RL-Duet offers enhanced harmonic and rhythmic compatibility.

Conclusion/Implications: RL-Duet marks a significant advancement in live music technology by integrating deep reinforcement learning into music generation. This development opens new possibilities for interactive live music performances, offering musicians a versatile tool that responds intuitively to their playstyle. Future applications of RL-Duet could extend to various musical settings, including amateur and professional ensembles, underscoring the potential of AI to redefine artistic collaboration.

Keywords: Music accompaniment, deep reinforcement learning, real-time generation, adaptive music systems, AI-driven music technology.
