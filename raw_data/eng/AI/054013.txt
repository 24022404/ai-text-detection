Face alignment is a critical task in computer vision, with applications ranging from facial recognition to augmented reality. Despite significant progress, achieving both speed and accuracy remains a challenge, particularly in scenarios with varying poses, expressions, and occlusions. This paper introduces a novel Structure Coherence Component (SCC) designed to enhance the performance of face alignment models. The SCC leverages spatial relationships between facial landmarks to improve robustness and precision. We propose a lightweight yet effective architecture that integrates SCC into existing alignment frameworks, enabling real-time processing without compromising accuracy. Our approach is evaluated on multiple benchmark datasets, including 300W and AFLW, demonstrating state-of-the-art results in terms of both speed and alignment error reduction. Compared to existing methods, the proposed model achieves a 15% improvement in accuracy while maintaining a processing speed of over 30 frames per second. The findings highlight the importance of structural coherence in facial landmark detection and offer a practical solution for real-world applications. This work contributes to advancing face alignment technology by providing a fast, accurate, and scalable solution that can be readily integrated into modern computer vision systems.  

**Keywords**: Face alignment, Structure Coherence Component, facial landmarks, computer vision, real-time processing, spatial relationships.
