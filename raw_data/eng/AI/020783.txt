This paper presents an innovative approach to automatic phantom test pattern classification using transfer learning with deep neural networks. The objective is to develop a robust and efficient system for classifying phantom test patterns, which is crucial in various applications such as quality control and defect detection. Our approach leverages pre-trained deep neural networks and fine-tunes them on a dataset of phantom test patterns to achieve high classification accuracy. The method utilizes convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and transfer learning techniques to extract relevant features and classify patterns. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms traditional machine learning methods and achieves state-of-the-art performance on phantom test pattern classification tasks. The key findings include improved classification accuracy, reduced training time, and increased robustness to variations in pattern appearance. This research contributes to the development of intelligent quality control systems and has potential applications in industries such as manufacturing and healthcare. Key keywords: deep learning, transfer learning, phantom test patterns, convolutional neural networks, classification, quality control.
