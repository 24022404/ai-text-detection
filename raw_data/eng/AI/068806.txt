This paper presents a novel approach to dense video captioning, leveraging the synergy of audio-visual cues through a bi-modal transformer architecture. The objective is to improve the accuracy and comprehensiveness of video captioning by effectively integrating visual and auditory features. Our method employs a transformer-based model that jointly processes audio and visual inputs, enabling the capture of nuanced relationships between the two modalities. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements over state-of-the-art methods, with our approach achieving enhanced performance on benchmark datasets. The key findings highlight the importance of bi-modal fusion in dense video captioning, leading to more accurate and descriptive captions. This research contributes to the advancement of multimodal processing and has potential applications in areas such as video analysis, multimedia retrieval, and human-computer interaction. By exploring the complementary nature of audio-visual cues, this work paves the way for more sophisticated and effective video understanding systems, with relevant keywords including dense video captioning, bi-modal transformer, audio-visual fusion, and multimodal learning.
