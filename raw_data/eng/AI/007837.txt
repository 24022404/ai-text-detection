Understanding the decision-making processes of machine learning models, particularly on high-dimensional data, remains a critical challenge in achieving human-interpretable explainability. This paper addresses the problem of providing clear and actionable insights into complex models without sacrificing predictive performance. We propose a novel framework that combines dimensionality reduction techniques with interpretable surrogate models to bridge the gap between high-dimensional data and human-understandable explanations. Our approach leverages feature importance analysis and visualization methods to highlight key decision factors in a model's predictions. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed method achieves comparable accuracy to state-of-the-art models while significantly improving interpretability. The results show that our framework can effectively identify and communicate the most influential features, even in datasets with thousands of dimensions. This work contributes to the growing field of explainable AI by offering a scalable and practical solution for interpreting high-dimensional models, with potential applications in healthcare, finance, and other domains where transparency is crucial. Keywords: explainable AI, high-dimensional data, interpretability, dimensionality reduction, surrogate models.
