This paper addresses the inefficiencies associated with deep neural network (DNN) weight pruning, specifically targeting the lack of desirable sparsity when executing DNN models in real-time on mobile devices. The research investigates a novel methodology to enhance computational performance and energy efficiency in these resource-constrained environments.

Methods/Approach: We introduce PCONV, an innovative pruning technique that strategically induces a balanced sparsity pattern in DNN weights. Our approach combines structured pruning with layer-specific optimization, ensuring that the pruned networks maintain their predictive accuracy while being computationally efficient. Key features of PCONV include a focus on convolutional layer optimization and the incorporation of a specialized algorithm to fine-tune the sparsity levels.

Results/Findings: Experiments demonstrate that PCONV significantly enhances real-time execution capabilities of DNNs on mobile devices by reducing computational overhead without compromising accuracy. Comparative analysis with traditional pruning methods showcases PCONV's superiority in achieving a more desirable sparsity pattern, leading to improved inference speeds and lower power consumption. Benchmarks conducted on various neural network architectures validate the robustness and scalability of our approach.

Conclusion/Implications: The proposed PCONV technique offers a compelling solution for the practical deployment of high-performance DNNs on mobile platforms. By addressing the missing sparsity in weight pruning, PCONV facilitates both the reduction of computational demands and enhancement of energy efficiency, paving the way for broader adoption of sophisticated AI applications on mobile devices. This research contributes to the field by presenting a scalable and effective method for optimizing neural networks, ultimately bridging the gap between advanced AI models and their practical, real-world applications.

Key Keywords: DNN weight pruning, real-time execution, mobile devices, PCONV, sparsity pattern, computational efficiency, energy efficiency, convolutional layers, AI applications.
