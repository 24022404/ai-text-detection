This paper introduces SketchGen, an innovative approach to generating constrained Computer-Aided Design (CAD) sketches, addressing the challenges in automating the creation of detailed and precise design drafts that adhere to specific constraints. Traditional manual methods are often time-consuming and prone to errors, motivating the need for an automated solution.

Methods/Approach: SketchGen employs advanced algorithms to capture design constraints and generate CAD sketches accordingly. Utilizing a combination of constraint-solving techniques and automated sketch generation algorithms, SketchGen translates abstract design requirements into precise geometrical representations. The methodology ensures that all produced sketches adhere to predetermined constraints, thus maintaining the integrity and accuracy required in professional CAD applications.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate that SketchGen significantly improves efficiency in CAD sketch generation, reducing manual correction time and increasing overall design accuracy. Comparisons with existing CAD generation tools reveal that SketchGen not only accelerates the design process but also addresses a higher complexity of constraints with improved fidelity. User studies confirm the effectiveness of SketchGen in producing design outputs that align closely with user intentions and predefined constraints.

Conclusion/Implications: SketchGen presents a substantial advancement in the field of CAD design, offering a robust tool for engineers and designers to automate the sketch generation process while ensuring compliance with critical design constraints. This research contributes to enhanced productivity in design workflows and opens pathways for further development in automated CAD applications. Potential applications of SketchGen include architecture, mechanical design, and any domain where precision CAD sketching is required. Key innovations in constraint satisfaction and automated generation provide a foundation for future exploration and refinement in automated design tools.

Keywords: CAD, sketch generation, constraint-solving, automated design, design tools, CAD sketches, constraint satisfaction.
