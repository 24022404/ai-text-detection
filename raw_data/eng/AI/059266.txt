This paper introduces a novel, modular approach to learning from small data sets, moving beyond traditional fine-tuning methods. The objective is to improve the adaptability and performance of large language models in low-resource scenarios. Our approach involves decomposing the learning process into modular components, allowing for more efficient and targeted updates of model parameters. We employ a combination of transfer learning and meta-learning techniques to enable the model to quickly adapt to new tasks and datasets. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms fine-tuning and other state-of-the-art approaches on several benchmark datasets, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency. The key findings highlight the potential of modular learning for overcoming the limitations of fine-tuning and enabling more effective learning from small data. Our research contributes to the development of more versatile and adaptable AI models, with potential applications in areas such as natural language processing, computer vision, and edge AI. Key keywords: modular learning, small data, transfer learning, meta-learning, large language models, low-resource learning.
