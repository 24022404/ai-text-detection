This paper presents a novel approach to photorealistic style transfer using wavelet transforms, aiming to bridge the gap between artistic and realistic image synthesis. Our objective is to develop an efficient and effective method for transferring the style of one image to another while preserving the photorealism of the target image. We propose a wavelet-based framework that leverages the multiresolution properties of wavelet transforms to separate and recombine the style and content components of images. Our approach utilizes a combination of discrete wavelet transforms and a custom-designed loss function to achieve high-quality style transfer. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our method in terms of visual quality, comparing favorably to state-of-the-art style transfer techniques. The key findings of this research highlight the potential of wavelet transforms in image processing and style transfer applications, with implications for fields such as computer vision, graphics, and artificial intelligence. Our contributions include a novel wavelet-based style transfer algorithm, a comprehensive evaluation framework, and a detailed analysis of the results, showcasing the effectiveness of our approach in achieving photorealistic style transfer. Key keywords: photorealistic style transfer, wavelet transforms, image processing, computer vision, artificial intelligence.
