This paper presents a novel approach to model-based deep reinforcement learning (DRL) that leverages Variational State Tabulation (VST) to enhance sample efficiency and learning stability. The primary objective is to address the challenges posed by high-dimensional state spaces and sparse rewards in DRL environments. Our method integrates variational inference techniques to dynamically discretize the continuous state space into informative latent representations, which are then stored in a tabular structure for efficient value estimation and policy updates. Through extensive experiments on standard benchmark environments, we demonstrate that our VST-driven DRL framework outperforms traditional model-based and model-free baselines in terms of convergence speed and cumulative reward. Notably, our approach effectively reduces model bias and mitigates the issue of state aliasing, resulting in more robust policy learning. The proposed framework requires fewer environment interactions, thus lowering computational costs while maintaining high performance. These advancements have significant implications for real-world applications where data efficiency and reliable learning are crucial, such as robotics, autonomous systems, and adaptive control. Keywords: deep reinforcement learning, model-based RL, variational inference, state representation, sample efficiency, tabular methods, policy learning.
