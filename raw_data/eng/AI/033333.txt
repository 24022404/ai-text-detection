The ANSV’s current IT customer support system is missing Service Level Agreement (SLA) functionality, a critical component in defining the terms of service, responsiveness, and the nature of support provided between a service provider and a client. Although an SLA is not a contract itself, it forms a vital part of a contract by clearly outlining expectations and obligations for both parties. Without integrated SLA functionality, the existing system cannot adequately ensure accountability or guarantee performance standards, which are essential for effective support and customer satisfaction.
