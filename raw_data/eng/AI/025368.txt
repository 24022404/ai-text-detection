Video action recognition is a critical task in computer vision, with applications ranging from surveillance to human-computer interaction. Despite significant advancements, accurately capturing temporal dynamics and salient features in video sequences remains a challenge. This paper introduces **Temporal Saliency Integration (TSI)**, a novel framework designed to enhance video action recognition by effectively integrating temporal saliency information. The proposed approach leverages a multi-stream architecture that combines spatial and temporal features, with a focus on identifying and emphasizing salient temporal regions that contribute most to action recognition. A key innovation of TSI is its adaptive saliency weighting mechanism, which dynamically adjusts the importance of temporal segments based on their relevance to the action. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets, including UCF101 and HMDB51, demonstrate that TSI achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The results highlight the effectiveness of integrating temporal saliency for improving action recognition, particularly in complex and dynamic scenarios. This work contributes to the field by providing a scalable and efficient solution for video analysis, with potential applications in real-time systems and large-scale video understanding.  

**Keywords**: Video action recognition, temporal saliency, multi-stream architecture, adaptive weighting, computer vision.
