Video Story Question Answering (Video Story QA) requires comprehensive understanding of video content and accurate reasoning over multimodal information. Existing methods often struggle with complex temporal dependencies and cross-modal interactions. This paper introduces a novel Multimodal Dual Attention Memory network (MDAM) to address these challenges. MDAM employs a hierarchical memory architecture to encode visual and textual information, utilizing dual attention mechanisms to capture both intra-modal and inter-modal correlations. Specifically, temporal attention focuses on identifying salient video frames, while semantic attention highlights relevant words in the question. A cross-modal attention mechanism then dynamically fuses the attended visual and textual features to generate context-aware representations. Experimental results on the MovieQA benchmark demonstrate that MDAM outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in accuracy. The proposed model effectively leverages multimodal information and enhances reasoning capabilities for video story comprehension. Keywords: Video Story QA, Multimodal Learning, Attention Mechanism, Memory Network, Video Understanding.
