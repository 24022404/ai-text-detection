This paper presents a novel approach to fine-grained facial expression manipulation, addressing the growing demand for more detailed and nuanced modifications in digital images and videos. The objective of this research is to enhance the granularity and precision of facial expression alterations while maintaining the natural appearance and identity consistency of the original subject. Our approach employs a state-of-the-art generative adversarial network (GAN) framework combined with an innovative expression component analysis to better capture subtle expression nuances. Key methods include the integration of adaptive encoder-decoder architectures and the utilization of localized expression modification vectors for targeted adjustments. The results demonstrate significant improvements in expression manipulation fidelity and flexibility when compared to existing methods, as validated by both quantitative performance metrics and qualitative user evaluations. This research contributes critical advancements in the fields of computer vision and graphics, offering potential applications in augmented reality, virtual avatars, and emotion-driven human-computer interaction systems. The findings underscore the importance of fine-grained manipulation techniques, positioning this work as a stepping stone toward more intuitive and immersive digital expressions. Keywords: facial expression manipulation, generative adversarial networks, image processing, computer vision, adaptive encoder-decoder architectures.
