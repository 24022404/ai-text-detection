This paper proposes a novel Backtracking Spatial Pyramid Pooling (SPP)-based image classifier for weakly supervised top-down salient object detection. The objective is to accurately detect salient objects in images using weak supervision, where only image-level labels are available. Our approach employs a deep neural network that integrates SPP with a backtracking mechanism to effectively capture multi-scale features and refine object detection. The method utilizes a pyramid pooling strategy to extract features at different scales, which are then backtracked to refine the detection of salient objects. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of precision, recall, and F1-score. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a backtracking mechanism to improve SPP-based feature extraction and the application of weakly supervised learning to salient object detection. This work has significant implications for computer vision applications, such as image segmentation, object recognition, and scene understanding, with relevant keywords including salient object detection, weakly supervised learning, spatial pyramid pooling, backtracking, and deep learning.
