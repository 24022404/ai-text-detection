This paper addresses the challenge of invariant causal prediction in Block Markov Decision Processes (MDPs), a critical problem in decision-making under uncertainty. Our objective is to develop a framework that enables accurate predictions of outcomes across diverse environments, despite changes in dynamics or distributions. We propose a novel approach that leverages causal inference and representation learning to identify invariant factors that affect outcomes. Our method utilizes a combination of graphical models and deep learning techniques to learn environment-invariant representations of states and actions. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in achieving superior predictive performance and generalization across multiple Block MDPs scenarios. The key findings of this research contribute to the development of more robust and transferable decision-making systems, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, healthcare, and autonomous driving. By highlighting the importance of causal invariance in MDPs, this work provides new insights into the design of more reliable and adaptive AI systems, and paves the way for further research in invariant causal prediction and decision-making under uncertainty. Key keywords: invariant causal prediction, Block MDPs, causal inference, representation learning, decision-making under uncertainty.
