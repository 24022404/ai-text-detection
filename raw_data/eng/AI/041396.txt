This paper explores the application of the Structural Similarity Index (SSIM) in generative models and inferential autoencoders, with the objective of enhancing image generation and reconstruction quality. We propose a novel approach that incorporates SSIM as a loss function to optimize the performance of these models. Our method leverages the strengths of SSIM in capturing perceptual similarities between images, thereby improving the visual coherence and fidelity of generated samples. The results demonstrate that the SSIM-based approach outperforms traditional metrics, such as mean squared error and peak signal-to-noise ratio, in terms of visual quality and diversity. Our findings have significant implications for the development of more sophisticated generative models and autoencoders, with potential applications in image and video processing, computer vision, and artificial intelligence. The use of SSIM in this context contributes to the ongoing quest for more effective and efficient models, highlighting the importance of perceptual metrics in evaluating and optimizing generative performance. Key keywords: Structural Similarity Index, generative models, inferential autoencoders, image generation, perceptual metrics, deep learning, computer vision.
