This paper introduces Portrait Image Embedding (PIE), a novel approach for semantic control in portrait image manipulation. PIE leverages a pre-trained facial recognition model to extract a compact embedding vector that captures crucial semantic attributes from a portrait image, such as pose, expression, and lighting. These embeddings are then utilized within a generative adversarial network (GAN) framework to enable fine-grained control over image synthesis and editing. We demonstrate that PIE allows for intuitive manipulation of portrait attributes, achieving state-of-the-art performance in tasks like pose editing, expression transfer, and relighting. Experimental results, including quantitative metrics and qualitative evaluations, showcase the superiority of PIE compared to existing methods in terms of both realism and control accuracy. The proposed approach offers a significant advancement in portrait image manipulation, with potential applications in virtual avatars, personalized content creation, and image editing tools. Keywords: Portrait Image Embedding, GAN, Semantic Control, Image Manipulation, Facial Attributes.
