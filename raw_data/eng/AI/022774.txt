This paper introduces Temporally-Extended ε-Greedy Exploration, a novel approach to balance exploration and exploitation in sequential decision-making problems. The objective is to improve the efficiency of traditional ε-greedy methods by incorporating temporal considerations, allowing for more informed exploration strategies. Our method utilizes a probabilistic framework to extend the ε-greedy algorithm over time, enabling the agent to adapt its exploration rate based on the dynamics of the environment. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms standard ε-greedy and other state-of-the-art exploration methods in various environments, including those with delayed rewards and non-stationary dynamics. The key findings highlight the importance of temporal considerations in exploration and the potential for improved performance in complex decision-making tasks. Our research contributes to the development of more efficient and adaptive exploration strategies, with implications for applications in reinforcement learning, autonomous systems, and decision-making under uncertainty. Key keywords: ε-greedy exploration, temporal extension, sequential decision-making, reinforcement learning, adaptive exploration.
