Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have shown promise in various signal processing tasks, yet incorporating uncertainty and handling noisy data remain challenges. This paper introduces a novel variational framework that integrates probabilistic modeling with GNNs for robust signal processing. Our approach leverages variational inference to learn a posterior distribution over graph signals, enabling the quantification of uncertainty and improved performance in the presence of noise. We develop a specific model based on a graph convolutional network within the variational framework. The proposed model is evaluated on benchmark graph signal denoising and reconstruction tasks, demonstrating significant improvements over existing GNN-based methods and traditional signal processing techniques. Results indicate that the variational approach enhances robustness to noise and provides valuable uncertainty estimates, leading to more reliable signal processing. This work contributes a principled approach to incorporating uncertainty into GNN-based signal processing, opening new avenues for robust analysis and interpretation of graph-structured data.

Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Variational Inference, Signal Processing, Graph Signals, Uncertainty Quantification, Denoising, Reconstruction.
