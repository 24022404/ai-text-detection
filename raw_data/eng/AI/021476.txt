This paper addresses the challenge of change-point detection in time series data, a crucial task for identifying significant shifts in underlying patterns. Our objective is to develop a robust and efficient method for detecting change-points, which is essential in various applications such as finance, climate science, and healthcare. We propose a novel approach based on direct density ratio estimation, which enables the detection of change-points by estimating the ratio of probability densities between two time series segments. Our method leverages the strengths of machine learning and statistical techniques to provide a flexible and accurate framework for change-point detection. The results show that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of detection accuracy and computational efficiency, particularly in the presence of noise and non-stationarity. The key contributions of this research include the development of a scalable and robust change-point detection algorithm, the introduction of a new evaluation metric for assessing detection performance, and the demonstration of the method's effectiveness in real-world applications. Our approach has significant implications for time series analysis, anomaly detection, and predictive modeling, and can be applied to various domains where change-point detection is critical. Key keywords: change-point detection, time series analysis, direct density ratio estimation, machine learning, anomaly detection.
