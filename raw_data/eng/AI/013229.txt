Estimating scene flow in 3D point clouds, which represents the 3D motion vectors of points between consecutive scans, is a crucial task for understanding dynamic 3D environments. This paper introduces FlowNet3D, an end-to-end learning framework that directly estimates scene flow from raw point clouds. The architecture leverages point cloud feature learning and incorporates a novel flow embedding layer to match points across frames and predict their motion vectors. Unlike previous methods that rely on handcrafted features or voxelization, FlowNet3D operates directly on unstructured point clouds, preserving geometric details. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach on both synthetic and real-world datasets, showing significant improvements in accuracy and robustness compared to existing scene flow estimation techniques. The network exhibits strong generalization capabilities and provides a foundation for various applications, including autonomous navigation and robotic manipulation. Key words: scene flow, 3D point clouds, deep learning, point cloud processing, FlowNet3D.
