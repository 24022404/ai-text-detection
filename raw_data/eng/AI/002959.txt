Distribution matching aims to generate samples that closely resemble a target distribution, a crucial task in various generative modeling applications. This paper introduces a novel "Best-of-Many-Samples" (BoMS) approach to enhance distribution matching performance. Our method leverages a discriminator to select the sample from a set of candidates that best matches the target distribution, effectively refining the generator's output. We apply this technique to both implicit and explicit generative models. Experimentally, BoMS demonstrates significant improvements in image generation quality on benchmark datasets, achieving superior Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) scores compared to standard training methods. Furthermore, analysis reveals that BoMS promotes higher diversity and better coverage of the target distribution. The proposed method offers a simple yet effective strategy for boosting the performance of distribution matching algorithms, applicable across diverse generative modeling tasks. Keywords: Generative Models, Distribution Matching, GAN, Sampling, Diversity, Image Generation.
