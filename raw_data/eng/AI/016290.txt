This paper presents a novel approach to gesture recognition, focusing on identifying important actions within complex sequences. Our method utilizes a path searching algorithm in a weighted graph to accurately detect and classify gestures. By constructing a graph where nodes represent key frames and edges are weighted based on the similarity between them, we enable the efficient exploration of possible gesture paths. The proposed algorithm then searches for the most likely path, corresponding to the most significant actions, allowing for the filtering out of irrelevant or redundant movements. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving high recognition accuracy and outperforming existing methods. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a weighted graph-based representation for gesture recognition and the development of a path searching method that prioritizes important actions. This work has significant implications for applications in human-computer interaction, robotics, and surveillance, where accurate gesture recognition is crucial. Key keywords: gesture recognition, path searching, weighted graph, human-computer interaction, action detection.
