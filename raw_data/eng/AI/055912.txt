This paper explores a novel deep learning framework for extracting transferable multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) channel modes to enhance sixth-generation (6G) vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communications. The primary objective is to address the growing demands for high-reliability and ultra-low latency in highly dynamic vehicular environments under 6G requirements. We propose an end-to-end neural architecture that leverages domain adaptation and transfer learning to build robust representations of MIMO channel characteristics across diverse V2X scenarios. The approach incorporates convolutional and attention-based modules to capture spatial and temporal dependencies, enabling efficient learning of latent channel modes that generalize well between simulation environments and real-world datasets. Experimental evaluations demonstrate that the proposed model achieves superior channel estimation accuracy and improved link reliability when compared to traditional signal processing methods and baseline deep learning approaches. The findings also indicate significant gains in adaptability and performance robustness—even when transferred across geographically and spectrally distinct environments. This research provides a foundational step towards intelligent, adaptive wireless systems for connected vehicles in 6G, with broad implications for real-time resource allocation, interference mitigation, and scalable deployment in next-generation V2X communications. Key keywords: deep learning, 6G, MIMO, V2X communications, channel modeling, transfer learning, domain adaptation, intelligent wireless systems.
