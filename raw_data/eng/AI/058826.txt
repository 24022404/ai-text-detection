Autonomous aerial vehicles (AAVs) require robust and adaptive control policies to navigate complex environments effectively. This paper addresses the challenge of learning deep control policies for AAVs by integrating Model Predictive Control (MPC) with policy search techniques. The proposed approach leverages MPC as a guiding mechanism to train deep neural networks, enabling the policy to generalize across diverse scenarios while maintaining stability and efficiency. The method combines the predictive capabilities of MPC with the flexibility of deep reinforcement learning, resulting in a hybrid framework that balances optimality and adaptability. Experimental evaluations demonstrate that the MPC-guided policy search outperforms traditional control methods in terms of trajectory accuracy, energy efficiency, and robustness to environmental disturbances. The results highlight the potential of this approach for real-world applications, such as search-and-rescue missions, package delivery, and environmental monitoring. This work contributes a novel methodology for learning control policies that are both computationally efficient and scalable, paving the way for advanced autonomous systems in dynamic and uncertain environments.  

**Keywords**: Autonomous aerial vehicles, Model Predictive Control, deep reinforcement learning, policy search, adaptive control, trajectory optimization.
