**Abstract**

Accurate articulated 3D hand-object pose estimation is a critical challenge in computer vision, with applications in augmented reality, robotics, and human-computer interaction. However, obtaining labeled data for complex hand-object interactions remains difficult, often limiting the generalizability of current methods. This paper presents ArtiBoost, a novel framework designed to enhance 3D hand-object pose estimation by integrating online exploration and data synthesis techniques. ArtiBoost leverages an adaptive online learning strategy to explore challenging hand-object configurations in real time, automatically augmenting the training process with synthesized pose-data pairs. Additionally, an efficient synthesis module generates photorealistic hand-object scenes guided by the current uncertainty of the model, enabling targeted and diverse data enrichment. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that ArtiBoost significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and robustness across a variety of challenging poses and occlusion levels. The approach further showcases improved generalization to unseen object shapes and interactions. These findings highlight the effectiveness of combining online exploration with intelligent data synthesis for advancing articulated 3D pose estimation. ArtiBoost offers a scalable and adaptable solution, paving the way for practical deployment in real-world interactive applications. **Keywords:** 3D hand-object pose estimation, articulated pose, online learning, data synthesis, computer vision, human-computer interaction.
