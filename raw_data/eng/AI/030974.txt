This paper introduces JDSR-GAN, a novel Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) designed for masked face super-resolution. The primary objective is to reconstruct high-resolution facial images from low-resolution inputs with significant occlusions, addressing a critical challenge in surveillance and facial recognition systems. JDSR-GAN employs a joint and collaborative learning approach, featuring a face completion network coupled with a super-resolution network. This architecture facilitates mutual information sharing between the two tasks, leading to enhanced reconstruction accuracy. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that JDSR-GAN achieves superior performance compared to state-of-the-art methods, particularly in challenging scenarios with large masked regions. Quantitatively, JDSR-GAN improves PSNR and SSIM scores by an average of 1.5dB and 0.03, respectively. The results highlight the effectiveness of the joint learning strategy in leveraging complementary information for improved masked face super-resolution. Key words: GAN, Super-Resolution, Face Completion, Masked Face Reconstruction, Deep Learning.
