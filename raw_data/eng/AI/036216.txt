Semantic segmentation of LiDAR point clouds is a critical task for enabling safe and reliable autonomous driving systems. This paper introduces *SalsaNext*, a fast and uncertainty-aware deep learning model designed to address the challenges of real-time semantic segmentation in dynamic and complex driving environments. The proposed approach builds upon the SalsaNet architecture, incorporating novel enhancements to improve both accuracy and computational efficiency. Specifically, *SalsaNext* integrates a lightweight encoder-decoder structure with a probabilistic uncertainty estimation module, enabling the model to not only predict semantic labels but also quantify prediction confidence. This uncertainty-awareness is crucial for decision-making in safety-critical applications. The model is trained and evaluated on large-scale LiDAR datasets, demonstrating state-of-the-art performance in terms of segmentation accuracy and inference speed. Experimental results show that *SalsaNext* achieves a significant reduction in computational overhead while maintaining competitive performance compared to existing methods. The proposed framework offers a robust solution for real-time semantic segmentation, paving the way for more reliable perception systems in autonomous vehicles.  

**Keywords**: LiDAR, semantic segmentation, autonomous driving, uncertainty estimation, deep learning, real-time processing.
