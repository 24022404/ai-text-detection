This paper introduces MEVA, a novel large-scale multiview, multimodal video dataset designed for activity detection. The objective is to provide a comprehensive resource for training and evaluating machine learning models in real-world environments. MEVA features a diverse collection of videos captured from multiple viewpoints and modalities, including RGB, depth, and infrared. A multimodal fusion approach is employed to integrate features from different modalities, enhancing the accuracy of activity detection. The dataset consists of over 10,000 videos, annotated with 50 distinct activity labels. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of MEVA in training state-of-the-art models, outperforming existing datasets in terms of accuracy and robustness. The introduction of MEVA contributes significantly to the field of computer vision, enabling the development of more accurate and reliable activity detection systems. Key applications include surveillance, healthcare, and smart home technologies. Relevant keywords: multiview video dataset, multimodal fusion, activity detection, machine learning, computer vision.
