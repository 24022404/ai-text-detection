This paper explores the application of deep learning techniques for time series classification, a crucial task in various fields such as finance, healthcare, and engineering. The objective is to develop an accurate and efficient model that can classify time series data into distinct categories. A novel approach is proposed, utilizing a combination of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) to extract relevant features from time series data. The methodology involves training the model on a large dataset of labeled time series samples, followed by evaluation on a separate test set. The results show that the proposed model outperforms traditional machine learning methods, achieving a significant improvement in classification accuracy. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of deep learning in capturing complex patterns in time series data. This research contributes to the development of more accurate time series classification systems, with potential applications in anomaly detection, forecasting, and decision-making. The study demonstrates the novelty of using deep learning for time series classification, with relevant keywords including time series analysis, deep learning, CNN, RNN, and classification algorithms.
