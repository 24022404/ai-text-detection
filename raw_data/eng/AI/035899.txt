Temporal Difference (TD) learning is a fundamental approach for reinforcement learning, but its performance is highly sensitive to parameter tuning. This paper introduces a novel parameter-free Gradient Temporal Difference (GTD) learning algorithm designed to automatically adapt learning rates, eliminating the need for manual parameter selection. The proposed method leverages a meta-gradient approach to optimize the learning rate online, ensuring stable convergence and improved performance across various environments. We evaluate the algorithm on a range of benchmark reinforcement learning tasks, comparing it against conventional TD learning methods with hand-tuned parameters and existing adaptive learning rate techniques. The results demonstrate that our parameter-free GTD algorithm achieves comparable or superior performance without requiring any parameter tuning, significantly reducing the engineering effort required for applying TD learning in practice. This advancement promotes the broader applicability of TD learning in complex, real-world scenarios. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Temporal Difference Learning, Meta-Learning, Adaptive Learning Rates, Parameter-Free Algorithm.
