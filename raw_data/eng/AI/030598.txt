This paper introduces a novel approach to object detection and tracking using a fully convolutional neural network (FCNN) and heatmap regression. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of object detection and tracking in video sequences by leveraging the spatial awareness inherent in FCNNs. Our method directly predicts object locations and scales by generating heatmap representations, eliminating the need for explicit bounding box proposals. A temporal association module is then employed to link detections across frames, creating robust object tracks. We evaluate our method on standard benchmarks, demonstrating competitive performance in terms of both accuracy and computational speed compared to state-of-the-art object detection and tracking algorithms. Furthermore, our approach exhibits resilience to occlusion and variations in object scale and appearance. The results indicate that heatmap-based object detection and tracking with FCNNs offers a promising alternative to traditional methods, particularly for real-time applications. Key words: object detection, object tracking, fully convolutional network, heatmap, deep learning, computer vision.
