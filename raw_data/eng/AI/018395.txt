This paper presents PFDet, our solution for the Open Images Challenge 2018 Object Detection track, where it achieved 2nd place. Our approach leverages a novel combination of existing object detection frameworks and data augmentation techniques to enhance detection performance on a large-scale, diverse dataset. Specifically, we utilized an ensemble of Faster R-CNN and Mask R-CNN models with various backbones, including ResNet and Inception architectures, pre-trained on ImageNet and fine-tuned on the Open Images dataset. We further improved performance through a sophisticated data augmentation pipeline, incorporating techniques such as random cropping, flipping, and color jittering, as well as a novel instance-aware copy-paste strategy. The effectiveness of PFDet is demonstrated by its competitive performance in the challenge, outperforming numerous other submissions. Our analysis reveals the importance of model ensembling and robust data augmentation for achieving high accuracy in object detection on complex datasets. The code and configurations will be publicly released to facilitate future research.

Keywords: Object Detection, Open Images Challenge, Faster R-CNN, Mask R-CNN, Ensemble Learning, Data Augmentation, Deep Learning.
