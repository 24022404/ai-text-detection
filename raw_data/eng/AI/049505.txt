This paper introduces MeInGame, a novel approach to generating a 3D game character face from a single 2D portrait image. The objective is to create a realistic and personalized game character face that captures the likeness of the input portrait. Our method utilizes a deep learning-based framework, combining the strengths of generative adversarial networks (GANs) and 3D reconstruction techniques. The approach involves training a GAN model to learn the mapping between 2D portraits and 3D face models, allowing for the generation of detailed and realistic 3D faces. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of MeInGame, showing significant improvements in face reconstruction accuracy and visual fidelity compared to existing methods. The proposed system has important implications for the gaming and entertainment industries, enabling the creation of personalized and engaging game characters. Key contributions include a novel GAN-based architecture, a large-scale dataset of 2D-3D face pairs, and a user-friendly interface for game developers and players. Relevant keywords: 3D face reconstruction, GANs, game character creation, portrait-based modeling, deep learning, computer vision.
