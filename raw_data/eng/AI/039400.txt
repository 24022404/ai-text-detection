This paper introduces a novel benchmark for knowledge-augmented visual question answering, termed Select, Substitute, Search (SSS). The objective is to evaluate the ability of visual question answering models to incorporate external knowledge and reason about complex scenarios. Our approach involves designing a comprehensive dataset that requires models to select relevant knowledge, substitute it into the question context, and search for the correct answer. We propose a new evaluation metric that assesses the model's ability to effectively utilize external knowledge and demonstrate its superiority over existing metrics. Our results show that state-of-the-art models struggle to perform well on the SSS benchmark, highlighting the need for more advanced knowledge-augmentation techniques. The SSS benchmark contributes to the field by providing a challenging testbed for evaluating the limits of current visual question answering models and encouraging the development of more sophisticated knowledge-augmentation methods. Key contributions include the introduction of a new benchmark dataset, a novel evaluation metric, and insights into the challenges of knowledge-augmented visual question answering, with relevant keywords including visual question answering, knowledge augmentation, benchmarking, and multimodal reasoning.
