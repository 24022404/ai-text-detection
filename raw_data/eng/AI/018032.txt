This paper addresses the challenge of performing Bayesian inference in high-dimensional time-series data, which are prevalent in various fields such as finance, climate science, and bioinformatics. The key objective is to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of inference processes in these intricate datasets.

Methods/Approach: We introduce the Orthogonal Stochastic Linear Mixing Model (OSLMM), a novel approach that incorporates orthogonal transformations to simplify the dependence structure of time-series data. This model leverages a Bayesian framework to capture the underlying dynamics and temporal correlations effectively. The integration of orthogonal transformations aims to enhance computational efficiency without sacrificing the quality of inference.

Results/Findings: Our experimental evaluation demonstrates that the OSLMM exhibits superior performance in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency compared to existing methods. Key findings show that our model can efficiently handle high-dimensional datasets, offering accelerated convergence rates and reduced computational demands. Additionally, OSLMM's ability to uncover latent structures leads to improved prediction capabilities in diverse time-series scenarios.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of the OSLMM offers significant contributions to the domain of high-dimensional time-series analysis by addressing both computational and analytical challenges. Our approach not only advances Bayesian inference techniques but also provides a practical tool for applications requiring real-time data processing and accurate predictive modeling. Future work will explore the applicability of OSLMM in other domains such as real-time stock market analysis and complex climatological modeling.

Keywords: Bayesian inference, high-dimensional time-series, orthogonal transformations, stochastic modeling, computational efficiency, prediction accuracy.
