In 2021, Zhang, Oi, Lowndes, and colleagues used the de Bruijn sequence, modifying it into a higher-order de Bruijn (HdB) sequence, to enhance the precision of timing and synchronization for quantum communication between satellites and ground stations. This innovative approach provided a more efficient framework for aligning signal patterns, which is critical for the successful transmission of quantum information over long distances.
