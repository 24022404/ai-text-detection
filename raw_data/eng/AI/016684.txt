The "dying ReLU" problem refers to a situation in neural networks where ReLU neurons become stuck on the negative side and always output zero, rendering them inactive and useless. This issue arises because ReLU has a zero gradient for negative inputs, so once a neuron's output is zero, it rarely recovers, particularly when the learning rate is set too high or the neuron has a large negative bias. Over time, this can result in a significant portion of the network's neurons not participating in learning, which reduces the overall capacity of the model.
