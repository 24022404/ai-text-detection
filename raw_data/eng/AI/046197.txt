The accurate segmentation of lung CT images is critical for diagnosing and monitoring pulmonary diseases. This paper presents a novel Knowledge-based Fully Convolutional Network (KFCN) designed to improve segmentation accuracy by integrating domain-specific knowledge into a deep learning framework. The proposed approach leverages anatomical priors and spatial relationships to guide the network, addressing challenges such as irregular lesion shapes and low-contrast boundaries in medical imaging. The KFCN architecture combines a fully convolutional network with a knowledge module that encodes expert insights, enabling the model to learn more effectively from limited annotated datasets. Experiments were conducted on publicly available lung CT datasets, and the results demonstrate that KFCN outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of segmentation precision, recall, and Dice coefficient. The model achieves robust performance across diverse cases, including those with complex pathologies. This research contributes a new perspective on incorporating domain knowledge into deep learning models, offering potential applications in medical image analysis and beyond. The findings highlight the importance of hybrid approaches that combine data-driven learning with expert-driven insights to advance segmentation tasks in healthcare.  

**Keywords**: Lung CT segmentation, Fully Convolutional Network, Knowledge-based learning, Medical image analysis, Deep learning.
