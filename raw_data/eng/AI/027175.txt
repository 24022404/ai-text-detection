The objective of this paper is to address the challenge of vehicle re-identification, a critical task in intelligent transportation systems and security surveillance, where accurate tracking of vehicles across different cameras is required. Our approach leverages an advanced deep learning model that integrates convolutional neural networks (CNNs) with attention mechanisms to enhance feature representation and discrimination of vehicles' visual characteristics. The proposed model focuses on extracting and aligning fine-grained features from vehicle images, improving the robustness against variations in viewpoint, lighting, and occlusions. In our experiments, conducted on benchmark datasets, the model demonstrated superior performance, achieving higher re-identification accuracy and reduced error rates compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. The findings suggest significant improvements in recognizing and tracking vehicles in complex environments. Conclusively, this research introduces a novel method for vehicle re-identification that not only advances current capabilities in automated surveillance systems but also provides potential applications in traffic monitoring and smart city infrastructure. Keywords include vehicle re-identification, deep learning, CNN, attention mechanisms, and intelligent transportation systems.
