Domain knowledge plays a critical role in shaping machine learning (ML) workflows, influencing data preparation, feature selection, model design, and result interpretation. This paper provides a quantitative analysis of the tangible impacts that incorporating domain expertise has on machine learning performance across various domains and tasks. We systematically evaluate the effects of domain-driven enhancements by embedding expert-curated features, domain-specific preprocessing techniques, and tailored model constraints in comparison to generic, automated approaches. Using both publicly available and proprietary datasets spanning healthcare, finance, and natural language processing, a series of controlled experiments highlights the measurable improvements in classification accuracy, model interpretability, and training efficiency. Furthermore, we introduce a novel metric for quantifying the value added by domain knowledge, enabling objective assessments across different scenarios. Results indicate that the strategic integration of domain expertise can yield up to a 20% boost in model accuracy and significant gains in robustness and transparency. Our findings underscore the necessity of domain knowledge for creating high-performing, reliable ML solutions and offer practical guidelines for practitioners seeking to maximize the impact of their models. Key keywords: domain knowledge, machine learning, feature engineering, model interpretability, quantitative analysis, expert systems.
