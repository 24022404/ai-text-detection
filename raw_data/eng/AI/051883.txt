This paper addresses the challenge of offline reinforcement learning (RL) under partial coverage, where the agent must learn from a limited set of experiences without interacting with the environment. Our objective is to develop a pessimistic model-based approach that provides robust and efficient learning in such scenarios. We propose a novel algorithm that combines posterior sampling with pessimistic model-based RL, ensuring that the agent explores the environment while avoiding overestimation of the value function. Our method achieves PAC (Probably Approximately Correct) bounds, guaranteeing a high probability of finding a near-optimal policy. The key findings of our research include improved sample efficiency and robustness to model misspecification, compared to existing offline RL methods. Our approach has significant implications for real-world applications, such as robotics and healthcare, where exploration is costly or risky. The novelty of our work lies in the integration of pessimistic modeling with posterior sampling, enabling efficient and reliable offline RL under partial coverage. Key keywords: offline reinforcement learning, pessimistic model-based RL, posterior sampling, PAC bounds, partial coverage.
