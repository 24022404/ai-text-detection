Sentiment analysis in low-resource languages remains a significant challenge due to the scarcity of labeled datasets, which limits the performance of machine learning models. This paper addresses this issue by proposing a novel data augmentation framework leveraging Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to generate synthetic training data for low-resource sentiment analysis tasks. The proposed approach employs a conditional GAN architecture to generate high-quality, sentiment-preserving text samples that closely mimic the distribution of real-world data. The augmented dataset is then used to train a sentiment classification model, which is evaluated on benchmark datasets for low-resource languages. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method significantly improves model performance, achieving a 12% increase in accuracy compared to baseline models trained on limited data. Furthermore, the generated data exhibits high linguistic quality and sentiment consistency, as validated through human evaluation. This work highlights the potential of GAN-based data augmentation as a practical solution for enhancing sentiment analysis in low-resource settings, offering a scalable and cost-effective alternative to manual data collection. The findings have broad implications for applications in multilingual sentiment analysis, social media monitoring, and cross-cultural communication.  

**Keywords**: Sentiment analysis, low-resource languages, data augmentation, Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), text generation, machine learning.
