This paper addresses the challenge of effective off-policy reinforcement learning by introducing a novel approach that learns routines to improve the efficiency and robustness of existing methods. Our objective is to develop a framework that enables agents to learn from unstructured and diverse experience data, without requiring on-policy interactions with the environment. We propose a new algorithm that integrates routine learning with off-policy reinforcement learning, utilizing a combination of model-free and model-based techniques to discover and refine effective routines. Our approach is evaluated on a range of complex tasks, demonstrating significant improvements in learning speed and final performance compared to state-of-the-art off-policy methods. The results show that our routine-learning framework can efficiently adapt to new situations and generalize across different environments, making it a promising direction for real-world applications. Key contributions of this work include the introduction of a routine-learning mechanism, the development of a novel off-policy reinforcement learning algorithm, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in various domains, highlighting the potential of this approach for improving the efficiency and scalability of reinforcement learning systems, with relevant keywords including off-policy reinforcement learning, routine learning, model-free reinforcement learning, and model-based reinforcement learning.
