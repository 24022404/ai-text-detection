Variational inference offers a powerful framework for approximating intractable posterior distributions in Bayesian models. However, standard variational distributions often lack the flexibility to capture complex posterior structures. To address this limitation, we introduce a Variational Mixture of Normalizing Flows (VMNF), a novel approach that combines the expressiveness of normalizing flows with the diversity of mixture models within a variational framework. VMNF leverages a mixture of base distributions transformed by learnable, invertible mappings to approximate the target posterior. This allows the model to capture multi-modal and highly non-Gaussian posterior densities. We present a scalable training algorithm using stochastic variational inference. Empirical results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that VMNF significantly outperforms existing variational inference methods, including single normalizing flows and standard mixture models, in terms of approximation accuracy and posterior predictive performance. The proposed method offers a more flexible and accurate approach to Bayesian inference, enabling improved uncertainty estimation in various applications.

**Keywords:** Variational Inference, Normalizing Flows, Mixture Models, Bayesian Inference, Posterior Approximation, Deep Learning.
