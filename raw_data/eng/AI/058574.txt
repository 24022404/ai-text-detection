Class imbalance is a pervasive challenge in deep learning, often leading to biased models that favor majority classes while underperforming on minority classes. This paper introduces *ReMix*, a novel resampling technique designed to address class imbalance by calibrating the sampling process to ensure balanced representation during model training. Unlike traditional methods such as oversampling or undersampling, ReMix dynamically adjusts the sampling distribution based on class difficulty and model performance, enabling more effective learning across all classes. The proposed approach integrates a lightweight calibration mechanism into the training pipeline, which evaluates class-specific contributions to the loss and optimizes the sampling strategy accordingly. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets, including CIFAR-10-LT and ImageNet-LT, demonstrate that ReMix significantly improves model performance on imbalanced datasets, achieving state-of-the-art results in terms of accuracy and F1-score. Furthermore, the method is computationally efficient and easily integrable into existing deep learning frameworks. The findings highlight the importance of adaptive resampling strategies in mitigating class imbalance and suggest broader applications in domains such as medical imaging, fraud detection, and natural language processing. Keywords: class imbalance, deep learning, resampling, adaptive sampling, calibration, minority classes.
