The objective of this research is to address the challenge of manually tuning the entropy temperature in the Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) algorithm, a significant component in modern reinforcement learning frameworks. Traditional methods require extensive experimentation to set this hyperparameter optimally, which can hinder performance across different environments. We propose an innovative approach named Meta-SAC that utilizes metagradient methods to automatically adjust the entropy temperature. Our method employs a meta-optimization strategy that adjusts the temperature dynamically, improving the adaptability and efficiency of SAC across varying tasks. Experimental evaluations demonstrate Meta-SAC's superiority, showcasing improved performance metrics in multiple benchmark reinforcement learning environments when compared to traditional SAC implementations. The results indicate not only performance enhancement but also significant reductions in the time and computational resources needed for manual tuning. This research highlights the potential for metagradient-based optimization in reinforcement learning algorithms, paving the way for more autonomous and effective learning processes. Keywords include Soft Actor-Critic, reinforcement learning, entropy temperature, metagradient, auto-tuning, and algorithm optimization.
