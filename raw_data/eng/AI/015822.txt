This paper presents a novel approach to automatic natural image matting, leveraging deep learning techniques to achieve high-quality results. The objective is to accurately separate foreground objects from background scenes in complex, real-world images. Our method employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, incorporating a multi-scale fusion strategy and a refinement module to enhance matting accuracy. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of alpha matte quality and composition complexity. The proposed technique achieves a significant reduction in computation time while maintaining high precision, making it suitable for real-time applications. Our research contributes to the advancement of image matting, with potential applications in image editing, video production, and computer vision. Key aspects of this work include deep learning, natural image matting, alpha matting, and image segmentation, offering a valuable resource for researchers and developers in the field of computer vision and image processing.
