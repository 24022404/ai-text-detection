This paper presents an end-to-end approach for super-resolution imaging by revisiting and extending the classical Lucas-Kanade framework to operate directly on raw image bursts. The primary objective is to enhance image quality and detail extraction in scenarios where multiple low-resolution raw images are available, such as in consumer camera applications or scientific imaging. Our method integrates a modernized motion estimation technique with deep learning-based reconstruction, enabling accurate alignment and fusion of raw image sequences without the need for demosaicing as a preprocessing step. Experimental results on standard burst super-resolution benchmarks demonstrate significant improvements in both perceptual quality and quantitative metrics compared to state-of-the-art methods. The proposed system is robust to challenging real-world conditions, such as non-uniform camera motion and variable lighting, and can be efficiently deployed on edge devices. This work establishes a new paradigm for burst super-resolution by combining the strengths of classical optical flow with end-to-end learning, offering substantial benefits for computational photography, mobile imaging, and other applications requiring high-fidelity image recovery from raw data. Keywords: super-resolution, image bursts, Lucas-Kanade, deep learning, raw images, optical flow, computational photography.
