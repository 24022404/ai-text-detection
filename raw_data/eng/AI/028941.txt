This paper introduces a novel approach to language-based image editing using recurrent attentive models. Our method addresses the challenge of precisely manipulating image content based on natural language instructions by sequentially applying localized edits. The proposed model leverages a recurrent neural network to process the input text and generate a sequence of attention maps, each focusing on specific image regions relevant to the given linguistic command. These attention maps are then used to guide localized image transformations, allowing for iterative refinement based on the entire textual description. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach through quantitative evaluations and qualitative results on benchmark datasets, showing significant improvements in editing accuracy and realism compared to existing methods. Specifically, our model achieves superior performance in complex editing scenarios requiring compositional reasoning and fine-grained control. The key contribution lies in the integration of recurrent attention mechanisms with image manipulation, enabling more precise and context-aware language-based image editing. Keywords: Image Editing, Natural Language Processing, Attention Mechanisms, Recurrent Neural Networks, Image Manipulation.
