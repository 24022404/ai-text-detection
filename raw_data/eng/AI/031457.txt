This paper addresses the challenge of efficiently analyzing large time series datasets by proposing a novel method for reducing the complexity of Markov models through symbolic analysis. The research aims to improve the interpretability and computational efficiency while maintaining the predictive capabilities of the models.

Methods/Approach: The approach involves the use of symbolic analysis techniques to transform continuous time series data into discrete symbols, significantly simplifying the dataset. Subsequently, a reduced order Markov model is constructed from this symbolic representation, optimizing model complexity and preserving essential dynamics. The methodology is validated on multiple benchmark datasets, evaluating its scalability and adaptability.

Results/Findings: The proposed symbolic analysis-based reduced order Markov modeling technique demonstrates significant reductions in computational time and resource requirements without compromising accuracy in predictive tasks. Comparative analysis with traditional full-order Markov models shows a marked improvement in efficiency, making it suitable for real-time applications. The model also exhibits robustness across different types of time series data, demonstrating broad applicability.

Conclusion/Implications: This study contributes a novel approach to time series analysis by integrating symbolic data processing with Markov modeling, offering a practical solution for managing large-scale datasets. The reduced order Markov model presents clear advantages in computational efficiency, making it particularly useful for applications in fields such as finance, healthcare, and environmental monitoring. Future research directions include exploring further optimizations and extending the applicability to more complex time series phenomena.

Keywords: symbolic analysis, reduced order, Markov modeling, time series data, computational efficiency, predictive modeling.
