Dropout is a widely used regularization technique in deep learning, known for preventing overfitting and improving model generalization. However, its role as an optimization trick remains less explored. This paper investigates dropout from an optimization perspective, aiming to uncover its mechanisms beyond traditional regularization. We analyze how dropout influences the training dynamics of neural networks, particularly its impact on gradient descent optimization and loss landscape exploration. Through extensive experiments and theoretical analysis, we demonstrate that dropout acts as an implicit optimizer by introducing stochasticity, which helps escape poor local minima and accelerates convergence. Our findings reveal that dropout not only regularizes but also enhances optimization efficiency, especially in complex, non-convex loss landscapes. This dual role of dropout provides new insights into its effectiveness and suggests potential applications in designing more robust optimization strategies for deep learning models. The implications of this study extend to improving training stability and performance in various machine learning tasks, offering a fresh perspective on dropout as a versatile tool in neural network optimization.  

**Keywords**: dropout, optimization, deep learning, regularization, stochastic gradient descent, loss landscape, neural networks.
