The rapid evolution of video game complexity has introduced significant challenges in ensuring software quality through traditional testing methods. This paper explores the application of Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) to augment automated game testing, addressing the limitations of conventional approaches in handling dynamic and unpredictable game environments. We propose a novel DRL-based framework that leverages adaptive learning algorithms to simulate human-like decision-making and exploration within games. The framework is designed to autonomously identify bugs, test edge cases, and evaluate game mechanics across diverse scenarios. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms rule-based and random testing techniques, achieving higher bug detection rates and improved coverage of game states. Furthermore, the system exhibits scalability across various game genres and platforms, showcasing its potential for widespread adoption in the gaming industry. This research contributes to the advancement of automated testing by introducing a robust, adaptive solution that enhances efficiency and reliability, ultimately reducing development costs and improving player experiences.  

**Keywords**: Automated Game Testing, Deep Reinforcement Learning, Software Quality Assurance, Adaptive Learning, Bug Detection, Game Development.
