This paper introduces a novel approach for learning plannable representations using a Causal InfoGAN. The objective is to discover latent representations that facilitate effective planning in complex environments by explicitly incorporating causal relationships. We leverage the InfoGAN framework to disentangle latent variables, promoting independence and interpretability, and introduce a causal regularization term to encourage the learned representations to reflect the underlying causal structure of the environment. This causal regularization utilizes interventional data to guide the learning process. Our experiments demonstrate that the proposed Causal InfoGAN learns representations that lead to significantly improved planning performance compared to standard InfoGAN and other representation learning techniques. The learned representations enable more efficient exploration and goal achievement. Key findings show that incorporating causal information enhances the robustness and generalizability of the learned representations. The approach offers a promising direction for representation learning in robotics and reinforcement learning where planning is crucial. Keywords: representation learning, planning, causal inference, InfoGAN, reinforcement learning, disentanglement.
