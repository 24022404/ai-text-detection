The rapid growth of online fashion platforms has increased the demand for intelligent systems capable of recommending complementary items to enhance user outfits. This paper addresses the challenge of fashion outfit complementary item retrieval by proposing a novel deep learning-based framework. The objective is to accurately identify and recommend items that aesthetically and functionally complement a given outfit, improving user experience and personalization. Our approach leverages a hybrid model combining convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for visual feature extraction and graph neural networks (GNNs) to capture the relational dynamics between outfit components. The model is trained on a large-scale dataset of fashion outfits, incorporating user preferences and style compatibility metrics. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed framework outperforms existing methods in terms of retrieval accuracy and user satisfaction, achieving a 12% improvement in precision-recall metrics. Additionally, the system shows robustness across diverse fashion styles and item categories. The findings highlight the potential of integrating visual and relational data for fashion recommendation systems, offering valuable insights for future research in personalized fashion AI. This work contributes to advancing intelligent fashion systems, with applications in e-commerce, virtual styling, and wardrobe management.  

**Keywords**: fashion recommendation, complementary item retrieval, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, graph neural networks, personalized styling.
