This paper presents a novel approach to compressing neural networks using adversarial techniques, aiming to reduce model size while preserving accuracy. Our objective is to develop an efficient compression method that can be applied to various deep learning architectures. We propose a framework that utilizes an adversarial agent to identify and eliminate redundant neurons, resulting in a compact model with minimal performance degradation. Our approach employs a generative adversarial network (GAN) to learn a compressed representation of the original model, which is then fine-tuned to maintain its predictive capabilities. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing compression techniques, achieving significant reductions in model size while maintaining competitive accuracy. The key contributions of this research include a novel adversarial compression algorithm, a comprehensive evaluation of its effectiveness, and insights into the application of GANs for model compression. This work has important implications for the deployment of deep learning models in resource-constrained environments, such as edge devices and mobile applications, and highlights the potential of adversarial techniques for efficient model compression. Key keywords: neural network compression, adversarial learning, generative adversarial networks, deep learning, model pruning.
