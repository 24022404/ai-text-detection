This paper addresses the challenge of forecasting time series data by optimizing the weights and activation functions of neural networks. The primary objective is to enhance prediction accuracy in complex temporal datasets where traditional models often fall short. Our approach involves designing a novel framework that simultaneously refines the weight parameters and selects the most suitable activation functions for the given task. We utilized a combination of gradient-based optimization techniques and adaptive algorithms to dynamically adjust these components during the training phase. Our experimental results demonstrate that this method outperforms conventional neural network models, showcasing significant improvements in prediction accuracy across multiple benchmark time series datasets. The findings imply that such tailored optimizations contribute to more robust forecasting models, paving the way for enhanced decision-making processes in various applications, including finance, meteorology, and resource management. This research contributes to the field by offering a systematic approach to neural network customization, emphasizing the impact of dynamic activation function selection and weight optimization. Key keywords include neural networks, time series forecasting, weight optimization, activation functions, and prediction accuracy.
