This paper addresses the challenge of interpreting complex machine learning classifiers by leveraging StyleSpace, an innovative latent space representation. The study aims to develop a method that provides intuitive visual explanations for classifier decisions, enhancing transparency in AI systems.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel application of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) for generating explanations by transforming classifier outputs into comprehensible visualizations within StyleSpace. The GAN is trained to map classifier decisions onto stylistic modifications, creating an intuitive interface for understanding model behavior. Our approach involves fine-tuning a pre-trained StyleGAN to synthesize images that highlight the features influencing classifier outputs.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate that our method successfully generates recognizable visual explanations, allowing users to interactively explore the decision-making process of various classifiers. Comparisons with traditional explanation methods show that our StyleSpace-based explanations are more intuitive and interpretable. The approach effectively visualizes classifier biases and subtle decision factors, making AI decisions more accessible to non-specialists.

Conclusion/Implications: Our research introduces a novel paradigm for explaining classifier decisions using GANs in StyleSpace, offering significant improvements in AI interpretability. This method fosters greater trust and understanding of complex models, potentially benefiting fields such as medicine, autonomous systems, and any area reliant on deep learning models. Future work could explore extending this framework to other forms of data and classifiers, enhancing the versatility of AI explainability tools.

Keywords: GAN, classifier explanation, StyleSpace, AI transparency, visual interpretation, StyleGAN.
