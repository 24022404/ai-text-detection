Fashion outfit complementary item retrieval is a challenging task due to the complex interplay of visual features, fashion knowledge, and personal preferences. This paper addresses the problem by proposing a novel approach that combines a multi-modal embedding space with a graph-based reasoning module. Our method leverages pre-trained convolutional neural networks and transformer models to extract visual and textual features from fashion items. These features are then projected into a shared embedding space, allowing for effective comparison of different items. Furthermore, we construct a fashion graph to model relationships between items and utilize graph neural networks to infer complementary items based on outfit context. Experiments on a large-scale fashion dataset demonstrate that our proposed approach significantly outperforms existing methods in terms of retrieval accuracy and fashion compatibility. The results highlight the effectiveness of combining multi-modal embeddings with graph-based reasoning for fashion outfit completion, offering a promising direction for personalized fashion recommendation systems. Keywords: Fashion Retrieval, Outfit Completion, Multi-modal Embedding, Graph Neural Networks, Recommendation Systems.
