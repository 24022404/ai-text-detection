Reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms learn optimal policies through interaction with an environment. A critical aspect of successful RL is the exploration strategy, which balances exploiting known rewards with discovering new, potentially better actions. This survey provides a comprehensive overview of exploration methods in RL, categorizing and analyzing various approaches including epsilon-greedy, optimistic initialization, upper confidence bound (UCB), Thompson sampling, and intrinsic motivation. We examine the theoretical underpinnings of these methods, comparing their performance across different problem domains, from discrete to continuous action spaces and from model-free to model-based settings. Furthermore, the survey discusses recent advances in exploration techniques, such as exploration driven by deep neural networks and hierarchical exploration. The analysis identifies the strengths and weaknesses of each approach, highlighting their suitability for specific types of RL problems. Finally, we discuss open challenges and future research directions in the field of RL exploration, including lifelong learning and safe exploration. This survey serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners seeking to understand and apply effective exploration strategies in reinforcement learning. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Exploration, Exploitation, Epsilon-Greedy, Upper Confidence Bound, Thompson Sampling, Intrinsic Motivation.
