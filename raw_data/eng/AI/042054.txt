This paper proposes a novel framework, Joint and Progressive Subspace Analysis (JPSA), for semi-supervised hyperspectral dimensionality reduction. The objective is to effectively reduce the dimensionality of hyperspectral data while preserving the intrinsic spatial-spectral structure. JPSA integrates a spatial-spectral manifold alignment approach to jointly consider the spatial and spectral characteristics of the data. The method progressively learns a subspace that aligns with the underlying manifold, leveraging both labeled and unlabeled data. Experimental results demonstrate that JPSA outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of classification accuracy and computational efficiency. The key findings highlight the importance of spatial-spectral manifold alignment in hyperspectral dimensionality reduction. This research contributes to the development of advanced dimensionality reduction techniques for hyperspectral data analysis, with potential applications in remote sensing, environmental monitoring, and computer vision. Key keywords: hyperspectral dimensionality reduction, semi-supervised learning, spatial-spectral manifold alignment, subspace analysis.
