This paper addresses the critical challenge of object detection in autonomous driving by proposing an innovative approach to optimize anchor generation and mitigate class imbalance. The primary objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of object detection systems, which is essential for safe and reliable autonomous vehicle operation. Our method employs a novel anchor generation strategy that adaptively adjusts the size and aspect ratio of anchors based on the object's dimensions and location. Additionally, we introduce a class imbalance alleviation technique that leverages a weighted loss function and a dynamic sampling strategy to ensure that the model is equally effective in detecting objects from diverse classes. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art object detection algorithms, achieving significant improvements in detection accuracy and recall. The proposed technique has important implications for the development of autonomous driving systems, enabling more accurate and reliable object detection in complex scenarios. Key contributions include the optimization of anchor generation, effective addressing of class imbalance, and the potential to enhance the overall performance of autonomous vehicles. Relevant keywords: object detection, autonomous driving, anchor generation, class imbalance, computer vision, deep learning.
