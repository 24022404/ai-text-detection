The objective of this research is to address the challenge of accurately predicting pedestrian trajectories in dynamic environments, which is crucial for applications in autonomous driving and smart city planning. We propose a novel method leveraging Context-Augmented Transformer Networks to enhance prediction accuracy by incorporating contextual elements such as crowd behaviors and environmental factors. Our approach utilizes a Transformer model augmented with contextual embeddings to capture intricate interactions between pedestrians and their surroundings. We conducted extensive experiments using publicly available pedestrian trajectory datasets to evaluate the performance of our proposed system. The results demonstrate a significant improvement in prediction accuracy over state-of-the-art models, showcasing the efficacy of context integration in transformer networks. This research provides valuable insights into the role of contextual information, offering a robust framework for future trajectory prediction endeavors. The implications of our findings suggest potential applications in the development of more reliable autonomous navigation systems and urban planning tools. Key keywords include pedestrian trajectory prediction, context-augmented transformer, autonomous driving, and smart city planning.
