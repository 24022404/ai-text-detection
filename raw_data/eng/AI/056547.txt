This paper introduces the CAMELS multifield dataset, a novel resource designed to facilitate the application of artificial intelligence (AI) in cosmology. The objective is to leverage AI techniques, particularly machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL), to learn the universe's fundamental parameters from multifield simulations. Our approach combines large-scale cosmological simulations with advanced ML algorithms to create a robust framework for parameter estimation. The CAMELS dataset consists of a large collection of simulated universes, each with varying cosmological parameters, providing a unique opportunity for AI models to learn complex relationships between these parameters and various observable fields. Our results demonstrate the effectiveness of AI in accurately estimating cosmological parameters, outperforming traditional methods in certain scenarios. The key findings highlight the potential of AI-driven approaches in cosmology, enabling precise predictions and insights into the universe's evolution. This research contributes to the growing field of astroinformatics, paving the way for future applications of AI in understanding the universe's fundamental nature. Key keywords: cosmology, artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, multifield simulations, parameter estimation, astroinformatics.
