Understanding and accurately modeling the dynamics of complex systems often requires the integration of contextual information, which can significantly influence system behavior. This paper addresses the challenge of optimal data collection for training context-conditional dynamics models, which aim to predict system evolution given specific contextual variables. We propose a novel framework that systematically identifies and collects the most informative data points conditioned on varying contexts, leveraging active learning and uncertainty estimation techniques. Our approach prioritizes data that maximizes model learning efficiency and predictive accuracy across diverse contextual scenarios. Extensive experiments on benchmark environments demonstrate that the proposed data collection strategy significantly outperforms traditional random and context-agnostic sampling methods, leading to faster convergence and more robust generalization to unseen contexts. The results highlight the importance of context-aware data acquisition in dynamic modeling tasks. This research offers practical insights for applications in robotics, autonomous systems, and adaptive control, where understanding context-dependent system dynamics is critical. Key innovations include the context-driven acquisition algorithm and empirical validation across multiple dynamic systems. Keywords: context-conditional dynamics, optimal data collection, active learning, uncertainty estimation, system modeling, adaptive control.
