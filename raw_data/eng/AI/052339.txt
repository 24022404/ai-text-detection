This paper introduces Deep Coordination Graphs (DCG), a novel framework for addressing multi-agent coordination challenges in complex environments. DCG extends traditional coordination graphs by integrating deep neural networks to learn and represent agent interactions, enabling more sophisticated and adaptable coordination strategies. Our approach uses a message-passing architecture where agents exchange learned embeddings, allowing for decentralized decision-making while maintaining global coherence. We evaluate DCG on several benchmark multi-agent tasks, including cooperative navigation and traffic control scenarios. Results demonstrate that DCG significantly outperforms existing coordination algorithms and reinforcement learning methods in terms of both convergence speed and achieved performance. Specifically, DCG exhibits superior adaptability to dynamic environments and scales effectively to scenarios with a large number of agents. The key contribution of this work is the development of a scalable and learnable coordination framework that leverages deep learning to enhance multi-agent system performance. This work opens new avenues for designing intelligent multi-agent systems in robotics, autonomous vehicles, and distributed computing.

Keywords: Multi-Agent Systems, Coordination Graphs, Deep Learning, Reinforcement Learning, Decentralized Control.
