This paper proposes LSENet, a novel deep learning-based approach for multi-class ocean front detection, enhancing the accuracy of oceanic boundary identification. The objective is to improve the detection of diverse ocean fronts by incorporating location and seasonality information into the network architecture. LSENet utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) framework, leveraging spatial and temporal features to capture the complex dynamics of ocean fronts. The methodology involves a multi-task learning approach, where the network is trained to predict both the location and class of ocean fronts. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of LSENet, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The key findings highlight the importance of integrating location and seasonality features in ocean front detection, leading to improved performance in various environmental conditions. This research contributes to the development of advanced ocean monitoring systems, with potential applications in climate modeling, marine conservation, and weather forecasting. The proposed LSENet model showcases the power of deep learning in tackling complex geophysical problems, with relevant keywords including ocean front detection, deep learning, CNN, location awareness, seasonality, and multi-class classification.
