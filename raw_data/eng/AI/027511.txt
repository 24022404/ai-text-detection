This paper addresses the challenge of precise detection in densely packed scenes, a problem that has significant implications for various applications in computer vision. Our objective is to develop a robust and efficient detection system capable of accurately identifying and localizing objects in cluttered environments. To achieve this, we propose a novel approach that combines a state-of-the-art convolutional neural network (CNN) with a specially designed algorithm for handling dense object packing. Our method utilizes a multi-scale feature extraction technique and a non-maximum suppression strategy to effectively detect objects with high precision. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing detection methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency, particularly in scenes with high object densities. The key contributions of this research include a significant improvement in detection precision and a substantial reduction in computational complexity. Our work has important implications for applications such as autonomous driving, surveillance, and robotics, where accurate object detection in densely packed scenes is crucial. Key keywords: object detection, dense scenes, convolutional neural networks, computer vision, robotics.
