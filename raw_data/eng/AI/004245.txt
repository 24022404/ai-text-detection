This paper addresses the challenges and considerations involved in deploying clinical machine learning models in real-world healthcare settings. The objective is to identify key factors that influence the successful integration of machine learning algorithms into clinical practice. A comprehensive approach is taken, combining insights from machine learning, healthcare policy, and clinical decision-making. The methods involve a mixed-methods analysis of existing literature, expert interviews, and case studies of successful deployments. The results highlight the importance of data quality, model interpretability, and clinical validation in ensuring the safe and effective use of machine learning in healthcare. The findings also emphasize the need for multidisciplinary collaboration, robust testing, and ongoing monitoring to address potential biases and errors. The conclusion underscores the potential of clinical machine learning to improve patient outcomes, while emphasizing the need for careful consideration of the complexities and challenges involved in deployment. Key contributions include a framework for evaluating the readiness of machine learning models for clinical deployment and recommendations for healthcare organizations seeking to leverage machine learning for improved patient care. Relevant keywords: clinical machine learning, deployment, healthcare, AI, model interpretability, data quality.
