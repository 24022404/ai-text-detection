The proliferation of manipulated media, particularly "fake backgrounds" in videos, poses significant challenges for tracking systems, often leading to failures in object detection and tracking. This paper addresses the critical issue of improving the robustness of tracking algorithms against such adversarial manipulations. We propose a novel framework, *One More Check* (OMC), which integrates a lightweight verification module into existing tracking pipelines to re-evaluate and correct tracking decisions in real-time. The OMC framework leverages a combination of spatial-temporal consistency checks and deep learning-based anomaly detection to identify and mitigate the impact of fake backgrounds. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that OMC significantly enhances tracking accuracy, achieving a 15% improvement in precision and a 12% increase in recall compared to state-of-the-art methods. Furthermore, the framework maintains computational efficiency, adding minimal overhead to the tracking process. This research contributes to the advancement of robust tracking systems, offering a practical solution for real-world applications where manipulated media is prevalent.  

**Keywords**: fake background, object tracking, adversarial manipulation, anomaly detection, spatial-temporal consistency, deep learning.
