This paper presents a novel approach to face age progression, incorporating attribute manipulation to generate realistic and diverse age-progression sequences. The objective is to develop a deep learning-based model that can accurately predict and visualize the aging process of a face while allowing for controlled manipulation of attributes such as facial expression, hair color, and accessories. Our method employs a combination of generative adversarial networks (GANs) and attribute editing techniques to produce high-quality, age-progressive images. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming existing methods in terms of visual realism and attribute control. The proposed model has significant implications for applications in forensic science, entertainment, and social media, enabling the creation of realistic age-progression simulations with fine-grained control over facial attributes. Key contributions include the introduction of a novel attribute manipulation module and a comprehensive evaluation framework for face age progression. Relevant keywords: face age progression, attribute manipulation, GANs, deep learning, facial analysis.
