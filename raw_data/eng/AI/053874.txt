This paper introduces a novel Multi-Subspace Neural Network (MSNN) architecture for improved image recognition accuracy. The objective is to enhance feature representation by learning multiple subspace projections within a single neural network. Our approach utilizes a series of parallel subspace modules, each trained to capture distinct features from the input image. These subspace features are then concatenated and fed into a classifier. We demonstrate the effectiveness of MSNN on several benchmark datasets, including CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet. Experimental results show that MSNN achieves significant performance gains compared to traditional convolutional neural networks and other state-of-the-art methods, particularly in scenarios with high intra-class variability. The proposed MSNN offers a more robust and discriminative feature representation, leading to improved image recognition performance. This research contributes a new perspective on feature learning by leveraging multiple subspaces within a unified neural network framework. Keywords: Image Recognition, Neural Networks, Subspace Learning, Feature Extraction, Machine Learning.
