An authorized helpdesk team member logs in to the system and navigates to the SLA policies section to add new policy information. After entering the required details for the new policy, the member saves the changes, which updates the list of existing SLA policies in the system. However, if the provided policy information contains duplicate details—such as an already existing primary key—the system prevents the update and instead displays an error message. This mechanism ensures that all entries in the SLA policy list remain unique, helping to maintain the integrity and accuracy of the records within the helpdesk environment.
