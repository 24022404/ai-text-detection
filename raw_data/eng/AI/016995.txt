This paper introduces Luna, a novel attention mechanism designed for enhanced sequence modeling. Luna unifies the strengths of linear attention and nested attention through a hierarchical structure, achieving improved efficiency and representational power. Unlike traditional quadratic attention, Luna achieves linear complexity with respect to sequence length, making it suitable for long-range dependencies. The method employs a linear attention mechanism at each nested level, enabling efficient computation while capturing multi-scale contextual information. We evaluate Luna on a range of sequence modeling tasks, including machine translation, text summarization, and long document classification. Experimental results demonstrate that Luna outperforms existing linear attention methods and achieves comparable performance to full attention mechanisms with significantly reduced computational cost. The key innovation lies in its ability to model complex relationships through its nested structure while maintaining computational efficiency. Luna offers a practical solution for sequence modeling tasks demanding both accuracy and scalability. Keywords: attention mechanism, linear attention, nested attention, sequence modeling, long-range dependencies, deep learning.
