Deep transfer learning has emerged as a transformative approach in artificial intelligence, enabling models to leverage knowledge learned from one domain and apply it effectively to new, related tasks. This survey systematically examines the current landscape of deep transfer learning, focusing on its foundational principles, major techniques, and diverse applications across domains such as computer vision, natural language processing, and speech recognition. The paper evaluates key transfer learning strategies, including feature-based transfer, fine-tuning, domain adaptation, and adversarial approaches, highlighting their relative strengths and challenges. Methodological advances, including developments in deep neural networks, convolutional architectures, and transformer models, are discussed in relation to their impact on transfer efficiency and performance. The survey also presents comprehensive comparisons between deep transfer learning and traditional learning methods, emphasizing improvements in data efficiency, model generalization, and adaptability to limited-label scenarios. Critical challenges such as negative transfer, domain discrepancy, and transferability metrics are analyzed, along with recent solutions addressing these issues. By synthesizing recent trends and open research directions, this survey provides valuable insights for researchers and practitioners aiming to deploy deep transfer learning in real-world applications. Key keywords include deep transfer learning, domain adaptation, feature transfer, neural networks, and knowledge transfer.
