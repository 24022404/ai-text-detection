Volumetric medical image segmentation is crucial for diagnosis and treatment planning, yet remains challenging due to high computational costs and complex anatomical structures. This paper introduces a novel 3D coarse-to-fine framework designed to improve both efficiency and accuracy in segmenting medical images. Our approach initially employs a lightweight 3D convolutional neural network to generate a coarse segmentation, rapidly identifying regions of interest. Subsequently, a refined segmentation is achieved by focusing computational resources on these regions using a deeper network with high resolution. We evaluate the proposed framework on a diverse set of medical imaging datasets, including MRI and CT scans of various organs. Experimental results demonstrate that our method significantly reduces computational time while maintaining segmentation accuracy comparable to state-of-the-art methods. The coarse-to-fine strategy effectively balances computational efficiency and segmentation precision, offering a practical solution for volumetric medical image analysis. Key words: medical image segmentation, 3D convolutional neural networks, coarse-to-fine, volumetric data, deep learning.
