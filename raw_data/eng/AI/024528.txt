Video summarization is a critical task in multimedia analysis, aiming to condense lengthy videos into concise, informative summaries. Traditional methods often struggle to adapt to diverse query conditions and dynamic content, limiting their effectiveness. This paper introduces a novel Query-Conditioned Three-Player Adversarial Network (QCTPAN) for video summarization, designed to address these challenges. The proposed framework leverages a three-player adversarial architecture, consisting of a generator, a discriminator, and a query-conditioned encoder, to generate summaries tailored to specific user queries. The generator produces candidate summaries, while the discriminator evaluates their relevance to the query. The query-conditioned encoder ensures that the generated summaries align with the semantic context of the input query. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that QCTPAN outperforms existing methods in terms of summary relevance, diversity, and query alignment. Key findings reveal that the adversarial training process enhances the model's ability to capture query-specific features, resulting in more accurate and context-aware summaries. This research contributes a robust, query-driven approach to video summarization, with potential applications in video retrieval, content recommendation, and multimedia indexing. Keywords: video summarization, adversarial networks, query-conditioned learning, multimedia analysis, deep learning.
