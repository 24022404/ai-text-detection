This paper introduces a novel periodicity-based parallel algorithm for time series prediction optimized for cloud computing environments. The algorithm leverages the inherent periodic patterns often present in time series data to decompose the prediction task into smaller, independent sub-problems. These sub-problems are then processed concurrently across multiple cloud instances, significantly reducing overall prediction time. The method utilizes a dynamic period detection mechanism to automatically identify relevant periodicities, enhancing its adaptability to diverse datasets. Performance evaluations, conducted using real-world datasets and simulated cloud environments, demonstrate significant speedup compared to traditional sequential prediction methods. Specifically, the parallel algorithm achieves up to a 5x reduction in execution time for large-scale time series datasets. Furthermore, the accuracy of the proposed algorithm is comparable to, and in some cases exceeds, that of existing state-of-the-art methods. This research offers a scalable and efficient solution for time series forecasting in resource-intensive applications, such as financial modeling and weather forecasting.

Keywords: Time Series Prediction, Parallel Computing, Cloud Computing, Periodicity Detection, Distributed Algorithms, Forecasting.
