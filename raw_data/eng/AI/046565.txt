This paper introduces GridMask, a novel data augmentation technique designed to improve the robustness and generalizability of deep learning models. The objective is to address the issue of overfitting and enhance model performance on various computer vision tasks. Our approach involves applying a grid-like mask to training images, which helps to reduce the impact of overfitting by forcing the model to focus on more diverse and informative regions. The GridMask technique is simple, efficient, and can be easily integrated into existing deep learning pipelines. Experimental results demonstrate that GridMask outperforms other state-of-the-art data augmentation methods, achieving significant improvements in model accuracy and robustness. The key findings suggest that GridMask can be effectively used to regularize deep neural networks, leading to better performance on image classification, object detection, and segmentation tasks. Overall, this research contributes to the development of more robust and reliable deep learning models, with potential applications in areas such as autonomous driving, medical imaging, and surveillance. Key keywords: data augmentation, deep learning, computer vision, GridMask, robustness, generalizability.
