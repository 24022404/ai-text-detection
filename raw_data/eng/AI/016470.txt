This paper explores the application of the You Only Look Once (YOLO) object detection framework to the identification and classification of marine life. Addressing the challenges of underwater image analysis, such as varying lighting conditions and occlusions, we present a YOLO-based model tailored for real-time marine species recognition. Our approach involves fine-tuning a pre-trained YOLOv5 architecture with a custom dataset of labeled underwater images, encompassing a diverse range of fish, invertebrates, and marine mammals. The performance of the proposed model is evaluated in terms of precision, recall, and F1-score, demonstrating significant improvements over traditional image processing techniques. Results indicate high accuracy and efficiency in detecting and classifying marine organisms in complex underwater environments. This research contributes to the development of automated tools for marine biodiversity monitoring and conservation efforts, offering a robust and scalable solution for underwater image analysis. Keywords: YOLO, object detection, marine life, underwater imaging, computer vision, biodiversity monitoring.
