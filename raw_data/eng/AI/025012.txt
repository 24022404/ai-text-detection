Object detection in aerial images has become increasingly significant for various applications such as surveillance, cartography, and environmental monitoring. The availability of robust datasets is crucial for advancing object detection technology in these contexts. This paper introduces DOTA, a comprehensive dataset specifically designed to enhance object detection tasks in aerial imagery.

Methods/Approach: The DOTA dataset consists of a large-scale collection of aerial images that capture diverse scenarios and conditions. It includes a wide range of object categories, meticulously annotated to cater to the demands of modern object detection frameworks. Advanced labeling techniques are implemented to ensure precision, including polygon annotations that provide detailed object boundaries. The dataset supports fine-grained analysis and training of state-of-the-art object detection models.

Results/Findings: Evaluation of contemporary object detection algorithms on the DOTA dataset demonstrates the challenging nature of aerial image analysis, due to factors such as scale variation, occlusion, and complex backgrounds. Enhancements in model performance were observed when utilizing DOTA for training, highlighting the dataset's contribution to improving detection accuracy and robustness in aerial image contexts.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of the DOTA dataset constitutes a significant advancement in the field of aerial image object detection. Its comprehensive nature and detailed annotations enable the development and testing of more effective detection models. The dataset holds potential applications in various sectors requiring precise aerial surveillance and monitoring. This work lays foundational support for future research and development in automated aerial object detection systems.

Keywords: DOTA, object detection, aerial images, dataset, surveillance, annotation, model performance.
