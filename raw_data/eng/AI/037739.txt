The objective of this study is to address the challenges associated with data ablation during deep learning model development by introducing "AI Playground," an innovative tool built upon Unreal Engine. This tool facilitates an interactive environment for visualizing and simulating data ablation scenarios, enabling researchers to systematically understand the impact of varying data subsets on neural network performance. Utilizing Unreal Engine's advanced 3D rendering capabilities, "AI Playground" offers a flexible and dynamic platform for conducting experiments across diverse datasets and model architectures. Key findings demonstrate that this tool enhances the ability of researchers to accurately ascertain the significance of distinct data components, resulting in optimized model performances. Users of AI Playground can manipulate variables and instantly observe corresponding outcomes, providing insights into data contribution and robustness. The study's conclusions suggest that the Unreal Engine-based tool is not only practical for educational purposes in understanding deep learning mechanisms, but also invaluable for refining model training methodologies. The development of such a tool paves the way for broader applications in AI model training, offering improved model reliability and efficiency through strategic data utilization. Keywords include data ablation, deep learning, Unreal Engine, neural networks, AI Playground, and model optimization.
