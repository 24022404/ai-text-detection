This paper presents an uncertainty-aware model-based policy optimization approach, addressing the challenge of balancing exploration and exploitation in complex decision-making environments. Our objective is to develop a framework that accounts for uncertainty in model predictions, enabling more robust and efficient policy optimization. We propose a novel methodology that integrates uncertainty quantification into the model-based policy optimization process, utilizing Bayesian neural networks to estimate model uncertainty. Our approach is evaluated on a range of benchmark tasks, demonstrating improved performance and robustness compared to existing methods. The results show that our uncertainty-aware framework achieves significant reductions in cumulative regret and improved policy convergence rates. This research contributes to the development of more reliable and efficient model-based policy optimization algorithms, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, autonomous systems, and decision-making under uncertainty. Key aspects of this work include uncertainty quantification, Bayesian neural networks, model-based policy optimization, and robust decision-making, highlighting the importance of accounting for uncertainty in complex decision-making scenarios.
