This paper introduces SliderGAN, a novel generative model for synthesizing expressive face images by manipulating 3D blendshape parameters. The objective is to create a system that can generate highly realistic and diverse facial expressions. To achieve this, we employ a sliding mechanism that allows for the dynamic adjustment of 3D blendshape parameters, enabling the generation of a wide range of facial expressions. Our approach utilizes a combination of generative adversarial networks (GANs) and 3D face modeling techniques. The results show that SliderGAN outperforms existing methods in terms of expression diversity and realism, with applications in fields such as computer vision, animation, and human-computer interaction. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a sliding mechanism for 3D blendshape parameter manipulation and the demonstration of its effectiveness in generating expressive face images. This work has significant implications for the development of more realistic and engaging facial animation systems, and can be applied to various areas, including facial recognition, emotion analysis, and virtual reality. Key keywords: SliderGAN, 3D blendshape parameters, generative adversarial networks, facial expression synthesis, computer vision, facial animation.
