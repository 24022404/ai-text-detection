This paper proposes Attentive CutMix, a novel data augmentation approach designed to enhance the performance of deep learning-based image classification models. The objective is to improve model robustness and generalization by generating more diverse and informative training samples. Our approach combines the strengths of CutMix, a state-of-the-art data augmentation technique, with the attention mechanism, allowing the model to focus on the most relevant regions of the image. The Attentive CutMix method involves a dynamic attention-based masking strategy, which selectively applies CutMix to the most salient areas of the image. Experimental results demonstrate that Attentive CutMix outperforms existing data augmentation methods, achieving significant improvements in image classification accuracy on several benchmark datasets. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of Attentive CutMix in reducing overfitting, improving model adaptability, and enhancing overall performance. This research contributes to the development of more efficient and robust deep learning models for image classification, with potential applications in computer vision, healthcare, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: data augmentation, deep learning, image classification, attention mechanism, CutMix.
