**Abstract**

Image classification performance in deep learning is strongly influenced by the diversity and quality of training data. Conventional augmentation methods like CutMix have improved model generalization by mixing images and their labels, but lack of contextual awareness can sometimes lead to suboptimal feature learning. This paper introduces Attentive CutMix, a novel data augmentation technique that leverages attention maps to guide the selection and placement of image regions during the mixing process. By focusing on semantically significant areas, Attentive CutMix encourages models to learn more meaningful and discriminative representations. Experiments were conducted on benchmark datasets including CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet, using state-of-the-art convolutional neural networks. Results demonstrate that Attentive CutMix consistently outperforms traditional augmentation strategies, yielding higher accuracy and improved robustness to input variations. Ablation studies confirm the effectiveness of attention-guided region selection in enhancing feature diversity without sacrificing label consistency. The proposed approach is easily integrated into existing deep learning pipelines, requires minimal additional computation, and is compatible with various network architectures. Attentive CutMix represents a significant advancement in data augmentation for image classification, offering practical benefits for both research and deployment in real-world computer vision applications.

**Keywords:** data augmentation, Attentive CutMix, deep learning, image classification, attention mechanisms, convolutional neural networks, robustness, feature representation.
