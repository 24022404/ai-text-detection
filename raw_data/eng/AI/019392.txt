This paper addresses the challenge of accurately segmenting clouds from high-dynamic-range (HDR) images, a crucial task for various applications such as weather forecasting, climate modeling, and remote sensing. Our objective is to develop an effective cloud segmentation approach that leverages the enhanced contrast and detail of HDR images. We propose a novel method that combines a deep learning-based architecture with a histogram-based thresholding technique to separate clouds from the surrounding environment. Our approach utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) to learn features from HDR images, which are then refined using a thresholding algorithm to produce accurate cloud masks. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our method over existing cloud segmentation techniques, achieving a significant improvement in accuracy and robustness. The proposed approach has important implications for improving the accuracy of cloud cover analysis, which can lead to better weather forecasting and climate modeling. Key contributions of this research include the development of a robust HDR imaging pipeline for cloud segmentation and the demonstration of the effectiveness of deep learning techniques in this domain. Relevant keywords: HDR imaging, cloud segmentation, deep learning, CNN, thresholding, remote sensing.
