This paper introduces a novel approach to nonparametric deconvolution, addressing the challenge of recovering an unknown signal from noisy observations without assuming a specific parametric form. We propose a new deconvolution model based on kernel density estimation and regularization techniques to mitigate the ill-posed nature of the problem. Our method estimates the unknown signal and the blurring kernel simultaneously using an iterative algorithm that alternates between estimating the signal given the kernel and vice versa. The algorithm incorporates a regularization term to promote smoothness of the estimated signal, enhancing stability and reducing noise amplification. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach through simulations, comparing its performance against existing nonparametric deconvolution methods. Results show that our model achieves superior accuracy in signal recovery, particularly in scenarios with high noise levels and complex blurring kernels. The proposed method offers a robust and flexible solution for deconvolution problems in various fields, including image processing, spectral analysis, and astronomical data analysis. Keywords: Nonparametric Deconvolution, Kernel Density Estimation, Inverse Problems, Regularization, Signal Recovery.
