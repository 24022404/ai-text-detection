Causal discovery from observational data is a challenging task, especially when data is incomplete. This paper introduces a novel approach for learning causal relationships from incomplete datasets by combining the strengths of deep learning and reinforcement learning. Our method employs an encoder to learn latent representations of the observed data, effectively imputing missing values while capturing underlying data dependencies. Subsequently, a reinforcement learning agent explores the search space of possible causal structures, guided by a reward function that balances model fit and structural complexity. Experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing causal discovery algorithms in terms of accuracy, especially when dealing with high levels of missingness. The proposed method provides a robust and scalable solution for causal inference in domains where data incompleteness is a significant obstacle. Keywords: Causal Discovery, Incomplete Data, Encoder, Reinforcement Learning, Missing Data Imputation.
