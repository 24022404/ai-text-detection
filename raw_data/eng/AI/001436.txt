Explainable online validation of machine learning (ML) models is crucial for ensuring reliability and trustworthiness in real-world applications. This paper addresses the challenge of validating ML models continuously and transparently in dynamic environments where data distributions evolve over time. We introduce a novel framework that combines statistical process control with explainable AI (XAI) techniques to provide real-time performance monitoring and insightful explanations for model degradation. Our approach employs a drift detection mechanism based on statistical measures of input feature distributions, coupled with XAI methods like feature importance analysis and counterfactual explanations, to identify potential causes of performance decline. The framework not only alerts users to performance issues but also provides actionable insights into which features or data segments are contributing to the problem. Experimental results on benchmark datasets and a real-world fraud detection dataset demonstrate the effectiveness of our method in detecting and explaining model drift, enabling proactive intervention and model maintenance. The proposed framework enhances model transparency, builds user trust, and facilitates the responsible deployment of ML models in practical applications.

**Keywords:** Machine Learning, Model Validation, Explainable AI, Drift Detection, Online Monitoring, Feature Importance, Counterfactual Explanations.
