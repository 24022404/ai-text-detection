This paper introduces a novel approach for Gaussian expert selection within a Mixture of Experts (MoE) framework, leveraging graphical models to enhance performance and efficiency. The primary objective is to improve the accuracy and reduce the computational cost associated with traditional MoE methods. Our approach utilizes a graphical model to represent dependencies between experts, enabling a more informed selection process. Specifically, we employ a conditional independence structure to guide the selection of the most relevant experts for a given input, thereby reducing unnecessary computations. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our Gaussian Expert Selection using Graphical Models (GES-GM) outperforms standard MoE implementations and other expert selection strategies in terms of both prediction accuracy and computational speed. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of incorporating dependency information through graphical models for efficient expert selection. This work contributes a practical and scalable method for enhancing MoE performance in various applications, particularly in high-dimensional data settings. Keywords: Mixture of Experts, Gaussian Experts, Graphical Models, Expert Selection, Machine Learning, Conditional Independence.
