This paper introduces a novel neural-based approach for answering questions about images, addressing the challenging task of visual question answering (VQA). Our method, named "Ask Your Neurons" (AYN), leverages a deep neural network architecture that integrates convolutional neural networks for image feature extraction with recurrent neural networks for question encoding. AYN distinguishes itself by incorporating an attention mechanism that allows the model to focus on relevant image regions when answering specific questions. The model was trained and evaluated on the VQA v2 dataset, demonstrating state-of-the-art performance compared to existing methods. Specifically, AYN achieves a 5% improvement in overall accuracy and significant gains in answering questions requiring fine-grained visual understanding. The results highlight the effectiveness of AYN in capturing complex relationships between visual and textual information. This research contributes a highly effective and interpretable VQA model, paving the way for more advanced image understanding systems.

Keywords: Visual Question Answering, VQA, Neural Networks, Attention Mechanism, Image Understanding, Deep Learning.
