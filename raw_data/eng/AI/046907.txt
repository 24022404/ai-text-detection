Accurate object detection is fundamental for a wide range of applications in computer vision, including autonomous driving, surveillance, and robotics. Traditional object detection models typically rely on deterministic bounding box regression, often neglecting the inherent uncertainty present in complex visual environments. This paper proposes a novel approach to bounding box regression that explicitly models uncertainty to enhance the reliability and precision of object localization. We introduce an uncertainty-aware regression framework that integrates probabilistic modeling into conventional object detection architectures, enabling the network to predict both the bounding box coordinates and the associated confidence intervals. Extensive experiments are conducted on standard benchmark datasets, demonstrating that our method achieves significant improvements in localization accuracy and robustness compared to baseline models. The results indicate that leveraging uncertainty provides more informative outputs, aiding in downstream decision-making processes. Our approach also demonstrates better performance under challenging conditions such as occlusions and varying illumination. In conclusion, uncertainty-aware bounding box regression presents a promising direction for advancing object detection systems, with potential applications in safety-critical domains where reliable detection is essential.

Keywords: object detection, bounding box regression, uncertainty estimation, probabilistic modeling, deep learning, computer vision
