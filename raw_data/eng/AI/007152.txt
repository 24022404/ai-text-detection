This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing molecule representation learning by integrating chemical reaction awareness into the model. Traditional methods often overlook the dynamic nature and transformations of molecules during reactions, which limits their ability to predict molecular behavior accurately.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel approach that incorporates chemical reaction contexts into the molecule representation learning process. Utilizing advanced neural networks, including graph neural networks (GNNs) and attention mechanisms, our model captures the structural and reactive properties of molecules as they participate in various chemical reactions. The model is trained on a curated dataset that emphasizes reaction patterns and molecular transformations.

Results/Findings: The proposed model demonstrates superior performance in key molecular property prediction tasks compared to existing benchmarks. By focusing on chemical-reaction-aware representations, it achieves higher accuracy in predicting reaction outcomes and molecular activities. Additionally, our approach reveals deeper insights into the interactions and mechanisms driving molecular changes, highlighting its potential for designing novel compounds.

Conclusion/Implications: Our research underscores the importance of incorporating chemical reaction data into molecule representation learning frameworks. This innovative model not only contributes to the understanding of molecular reactivity but also opens new pathways for applications in drug discovery, materials science, and synthetic chemistry. The integration of reaction-aware features presents a promising direction for enhancing the predictive capabilities of computational chemistry models.

Keywords: molecule representation learning, chemical reactions, graph neural networks, attention mechanisms, molecular property prediction, computational chemistry.
