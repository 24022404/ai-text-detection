The objective of this research is to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of automated game testing by integrating Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) techniques. Traditional automated testing methods often face challenges in dealing with complex game environments and dynamic mechanics, limiting their ability to uncover deep-seated bugs and edge cases. Our approach utilizes DRL to develop intelligent testing agents capable of learning optimal testing strategies through interaction with the game environments. By applying a DRL framework, specifically leveraging techniques such as deep Q-learning and policy gradient methods, the study demonstrates improvements in the adaptability and coverage of the testing processes. The findings reveal that DRL-augmented testing can identify a broader range of defects and achieve more extensive game state exploration compared to conventional automated testing tools. This enhanced capability translates into more robust game releases and reduced post-launch maintenance costs. The research contributes valuable insights into the potential of DRL applications beyond its traditional domains, paving the way for its adoption in automated testing workflows across various game development contexts. Keywords: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Automated Game Testing, Deep Q-learning, Policy Gradient, Game Environment Exploration.
