The rapid growth of data in various domains has necessitated the development of efficient methods for dataset classification, a critical step in data mining. This paper introduces a novel method for classifying datasets, aiming to improve accuracy and scalability in data preprocessing. The proposed approach leverages a hybrid model combining unsupervised clustering techniques with supervised learning algorithms to automatically categorize datasets based on their inherent characteristics. By integrating feature extraction and dimensionality reduction, the method ensures robust classification even for high-dimensional and heterogeneous datasets. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed method achieves a 12% improvement in classification accuracy compared to existing techniques, while significantly reducing computational overhead. The findings highlight the method's potential for enhancing data mining workflows, particularly in applications requiring real-time processing and large-scale data analysis. This research contributes to the field by providing a scalable and efficient solution for dataset classification, with implications for improving decision-making processes in areas such as healthcare, finance, and e-commerce.  

**Keywords**: dataset classification, data mining, hybrid model, unsupervised clustering, supervised learning, feature extraction, scalability.
