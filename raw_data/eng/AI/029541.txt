This study aims to develop an innovative approach for classifying 3D MRI brain volumes using deep reinforcement learning (DRL) combined with automated label extraction from clinical reports. The proposed method utilizes a DRL framework to learn optimal policies for extracting relevant labels from unstructured clinical text, which are then used to train a 3D convolutional neural network (CNN) for brain volume classification. Our approach leverages natural language processing (NLP) techniques to automatically extract labels, eliminating the need for manual annotation. The results demonstrate that our method achieves high accuracy in classifying 3D MRI brain volumes, outperforming traditional machine learning approaches. The key findings of this study highlight the potential of DRL and automated label extraction to improve the efficiency and accuracy of medical image analysis. This research contributes to the development of more effective and efficient diagnostic tools, with potential applications in neurology and radiology. The use of DRL, NLP, and 3D CNNs enables the analysis of complex medical images and text data, making this study a significant advancement in the field of medical image analysis, with relevant keywords including deep learning, reinforcement learning, medical image analysis, and natural language processing.
