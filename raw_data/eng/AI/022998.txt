Generative models have shown remarkable success in capturing complex data distributions, but they often suffer from inherent biases that can lead to inaccurate or unrealistic outputs. This paper addresses the challenge of bias correction in learned generative models by proposing a novel likelihood-free importance weighting approach. The objective is to mitigate biases without requiring explicit access to the true data distribution or the model's likelihood function. Our method leverages importance weighting to adjust the model's outputs, ensuring they align more closely with the target distribution. We evaluate the proposed approach on several benchmark datasets, comparing its performance against existing bias correction techniques. Results demonstrate significant improvements in both the quality and fairness of generated samples, with reduced bias across diverse metrics. The proposed method is computationally efficient and scalable, making it suitable for real-world applications. This work contributes to the growing field of generative modeling by providing a practical and effective solution for bias correction, with potential implications for fairness-aware AI systems and data generation tasks.  

**Keywords**: generative models, bias correction, importance weighting, likelihood-free methods, fairness, AI systems.
