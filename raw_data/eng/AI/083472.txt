Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) have become a critical tool for capturing high-resolution images, enabling applications in surveillance, agriculture, and disaster management. However, object detection in UAV images remains challenging due to varying scales, orientations, and complex backgrounds. This paper introduces **SyNet**, an ensemble network designed to address these challenges by leveraging the strengths of multiple deep learning architectures. The proposed approach integrates feature extraction from state-of-the-art convolutional neural networks (CNNs) with a novel fusion mechanism to enhance detection accuracy across diverse object sizes and orientations. SyNet employs a multi-scale feature pyramid network (FPN) to capture fine-grained details and a self-attention module to prioritize relevant regions in cluttered scenes. Experiments were conducted on benchmark UAV datasets, demonstrating that SyNet achieves superior performance compared to existing methods, with significant improvements in precision and recall. The results highlight the network's robustness to scale variations and complex backgrounds, making it a promising solution for real-world UAV applications. This research contributes to advancing object detection in aerial imagery by introducing an ensemble-based framework that combines scalability, accuracy, and computational efficiency.  

**Keywords**: UAV, object detection, ensemble network, deep learning, feature pyramid network, self-attention, multi-scale analysis.
