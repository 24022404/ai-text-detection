Autonomous driving heavily relies on accurate motion forecasting of surrounding agents. This paper addresses the challenge of predicting future trajectories of multiple interacting agents in complex, real-world scenarios. We present a large-scale interactive motion forecasting model trained and evaluated on the Waymo Open Motion Dataset (WOMD), a comprehensive dataset comprising diverse driving scenarios. Our model leverages a novel graph neural network architecture to capture complex inter-agent dependencies and incorporates high-definition map information for improved prediction accuracy. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods on the WOMD benchmark, achieving substantial improvements in metrics such as displacement error and collision rate. Furthermore, we analyze the impact of different input modalities and model components on the overall performance, providing insights into the critical factors for interactive motion forecasting. The findings highlight the importance of considering agent interactions and map context for robust and reliable autonomous driving systems. Key words: Motion Forecasting, Autonomous Driving, Interactive Prediction, Graph Neural Networks, Waymo Open Motion Dataset.
