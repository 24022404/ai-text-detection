Abstract:

The rapid growth in text and multimodal data has highlighted critical limitations in the scalability and efficiency of existing attention mechanisms within transformer-based models. This paper introduces Luna: Linear Unified Nested Attention, a novel method designed to address the quadratic complexity of traditional attention while retaining or enhancing model performance across diverse sequence processing tasks. Luna leverages a linearized unified attention framework with a nested structure, enabling efficient handling of long sequences by combining global and local contextual interactions in a single pass. We detail the architectural design and theoretical underpinnings of Luna, outlining how the approach integrates both coarse-grained and fine-grained attention within a scalable linear-time algorithm. Experimental evaluations on natural language processing and sequential vision tasks demonstrate that Luna achieves competitive or superior accuracy compared to standard and linearized attention baselines, while substantially reducing memory usage and computational cost. Our results indicate that Luna enables practical deployment of large transformer models on resource-constrained devices and unlocks the ability to process longer sequences than previously feasible. The unified nested attention of Luna represents a significant advance in efficient transformer architectures, with broad implications for real-time language processing, bioinformatics, and large-scale sequence analysis. Keywords: linear attention, nested attention, transformers, sequence modeling, efficient deep learning.
