This paper presents a novel approach to modeling the human cardiovascular system by integrating Neural Networks with Functional Mockup Units (FMUs), termed NeuralFMUs. The objective is to develop a hybrid model that combines the strengths of physical modeling and machine learning to improve the accuracy and personalization of cardiovascular simulations. Our approach utilizes Neural Networks to learn complex relationships between physiological parameters, which are then integrated with FMUs to create a hybrid model that can simulate various cardiovascular scenarios. The results show that the NeuralFMU model outperforms traditional physical models in predicting blood pressure and flow rates, with a significant reduction in simulation error. The conclusion highlights the potential of NeuralFMUs to revolutionize cardiovascular modeling, enabling personalized medicine and more accurate diagnoses. Key contributions include the development of a novel hybrid modeling framework, the application of Neural Networks to cardiovascular modeling, and the demonstration of improved simulation accuracy. Relevant keywords: NeuralFMUs, cardiovascular modeling, hybrid modeling, machine learning, FMUs, personalized medicine.
