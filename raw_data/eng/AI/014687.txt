Explainable AI (XAI) aims to elucidate the decision-making processes of complex models, such as deep reinforcement learning (DRL) agents. Saliency maps, which highlight input features most relevant to a model's prediction, are a popular XAI technique. This paper focuses on benchmarking perturbation-based saliency methods for explaining Atari agents. We evaluate several perturbation techniques, including occlusion and blurring, across a suite of Atari games with varying characteristics. Our methodology assesses the fidelity and localization accuracy of these saliency maps in the context of DRL agents. Results demonstrate significant variations in the effectiveness of different perturbation methods, with occlusion-based approaches generally outperforming blurring. We further identify game-specific factors that influence saliency map quality. These findings provide valuable insights into the strengths and weaknesses of perturbation-based saliency methods for DRL and offer guidance for selecting appropriate explanation techniques in Atari environments. The benchmark and analysis contribute to the development of more reliable and informative XAI tools for understanding and improving DRL agents. Keywords: Explainable AI, Reinforcement Learning, Saliency Maps, Atari, Perturbation, Benchmarking.
