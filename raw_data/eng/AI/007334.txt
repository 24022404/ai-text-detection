This paper introduces the Interval Type-2 Correlation-Aware Fuzzy Neural Network (IT2CFNN), a novel approach for nonlinear function approximation that addresses the limitations of traditional fuzzy systems in handling uncertainty and complexity. The IT2CFNN leverages interval type-2 fuzzy sets to model and manage uncertainties inherent in data, while incorporating a correlation-aware mechanism to capture interdependencies among input variables. This allows the construction of non-separable fuzzy rules with adaptive shapes, enhancing the system's ability to approximate complex nonlinear functions. The network structure integrates fuzzy inference with neural network learning, enabling automated rule generation and parameter optimization. Experimental results demonstrate that the IT2CFNN achieves superior performance compared to existing fuzzy neural networks and other approximation techniques, particularly in scenarios involving noisy and high-dimensional data. The proposed approach offers a robust and adaptive solution for nonlinear function approximation, with potential applications in control systems, pattern recognition, and time-series prediction.

Keywords: Interval Type-2 Fuzzy Sets, Fuzzy Neural Network, Nonlinear Function Approximation, Correlation Analysis, Uncertainty Modeling, Adaptive Fuzzy Systems.
