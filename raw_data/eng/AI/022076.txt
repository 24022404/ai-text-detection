This paper presents a novel approach to ultra high-resolution image inpainting, addressing the critical challenge of restoring missing or corrupted image regions while preserving fine details and context. The proposed technique, Contextual Residual Aggregation (CRA), leverages deep learning to effectively combine local and global context, allowing for more accurate and coherent image reconstructions. Our method enhances residual representation through a multi-scale aggregation strategy, resulting in improved synthesis quality compared to existing inpainting techniques. Experimental results demonstrate that CRA significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of visual quality and quantitative metrics, even in scenarios with substantial damage to the image. Furthermore, we analyze the implications of employing multiscale contextual information and present compelling insights into its effect on texture fidelity and detail enhancement. The promising outcomes of this research indicate its potential applications in various fields, including photography restoration, digital art, and autonomous vehicle perception systems. Keywords: image inpainting, high-resolution, deep learning, contextual aggregation, residual learning.
