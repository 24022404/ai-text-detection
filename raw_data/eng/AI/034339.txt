This paper proposes a novel multi-view 3D object detection network designed to enhance the perception capabilities of autonomous driving systems. The objective is to accurately detect and localize objects in 3D space using multiple camera views, addressing the limitations of traditional single-view approaches. Our method employs a fusion of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and 3D reconstruction techniques to integrate features from different views, resulting in a more comprehensive understanding of the environment. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving significant improvements in detection accuracy and robustness compared to state-of-the-art single-view methods. The proposed network architecture enables real-time processing and is adaptable to various autonomous driving scenarios. Our research contributes to the development of more reliable and efficient 3D object detection systems, with potential applications in autonomous vehicles, robotics, and surveillance. Key aspects of this work include multi-view fusion, 3D reconstruction, and deep learning-based object detection, making it a valuable addition to the field of computer vision and autonomous driving.
