Abstract:

This paper investigates the interplay between graphs, entity representations, and step mixture strategies to enhance information extraction and reasoning in complex data environments. The primary objective is to address limitations in traditional graph-based models, which often struggle to capture dynamic relationships and multi-step interactions among diverse entities. We propose a novel framework that integrates heterogeneous entity graphs with a step mixture mechanism, allowing for adaptive and context-aware traversal of relational structures. Our method leverages hybrid node embeddings and dynamic path selection algorithms to model both static and evolving connections among entities. Experimental results on benchmark knowledge graph and event extraction datasets demonstrate significant improvements in relation discovery, inference accuracy, and scalability compared to baseline methods. The study reveals that step mixture strategies enable more flexible aggregation of multi-hop information, leading to richer entity understanding. Our approach offers potential applications in areas such as knowledge graph completion, recommendation systems, and semantic search. By demonstrating the value of integrating step mixtures in graph-based learning, this research contributes to advancing data-driven entity reasoning and representation learning. Key keywords: knowledge graphs, entity representation, step mixture, relational reasoning, graph algorithms, information extraction.
