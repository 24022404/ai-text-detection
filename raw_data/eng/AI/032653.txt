This paper addresses the critical issue of uncertainty estimation in medical tabular data classification and risk prediction. The objective is to develop a reliable framework for quantifying uncertainty in predictive models, enabling informed decision-making in healthcare. We propose a novel approach that leverages Bayesian neural networks and Monte Carlo dropout to estimate uncertainty in classification and risk prediction tasks. Our method is evaluated on several medical datasets, demonstrating improved performance and robustness compared to traditional approaches. The results show that our framework provides accurate and reliable uncertainty estimates, leading to better calibration and improved decision-making. The key contributions of this research include the development of a scalable and efficient uncertainty estimation framework for medical tabular data, and the demonstration of its potential to enhance the reliability and trustworthiness of AI-driven healthcare systems. This work has significant implications for clinical practice, highlighting the importance of uncertainty estimation in medical decision-making and paving the way for the development of more robust and reliable AI models in healthcare. Key keywords: uncertainty estimation, medical tabular data, Bayesian neural networks, Monte Carlo dropout, classification, risk prediction, healthcare, AI models.
