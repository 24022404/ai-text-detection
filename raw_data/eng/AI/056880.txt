This paper addresses the problem of probabilistic permutation synchronization, a crucial task in various computer vision and machine learning applications. The objective is to develop a novel framework that leverages the Riemannian structure of the Birkhoff polytope to efficiently synchronize permutations in a probabilistic setting. Our approach utilizes a geometric framework to model the space of permutations as a Riemannian manifold, enabling the use of intrinsic metrics and geodesics to compare and synchronize permutations. The proposed method employs a probabilistic formulation to account for uncertainties and ambiguities in the synchronization process. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in achieving accurate and robust permutation synchronization, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a Riemannian framework for permutation synchronization and the development of a probabilistic algorithm that can handle large-scale permutation synchronization problems. This work has significant implications for applications such as structure from motion, object recognition, and data mining, where permutation synchronization plays a critical role. Key keywords: permutation synchronization, Riemannian manifold, Birkhoff polytope, probabilistic formulation, computer vision, machine learning.
