This paper provides a comprehensive survey of exploration methods in reinforcement learning (RL), a key area in artificial intelligence where agents learn to make decisions through trial and error. Efficient exploration is crucial to ensure agents effectively discover optimal policies, especially in environments with sparse or deceptive rewards. We systematically review the major categories of exploration strategies, including random and systematic methods, intrinsic motivation approaches, count-based exploration, novelty-based techniques, and exploration via uncertainty estimation. The survey outlines the underlying principles, strengths, and limitations of each method and compares their empirical performance on widely used benchmark environments. Furthermore, we highlight recent advancements leveraging deep learning and generative models to enhance exploration efficiency in high-dimensional and continuous spaces. Our analysis identifies common challenges such as sample efficiency, scalability, and exploration-exploitation trade-offs, and discusses promising directions for future research. This work aims to serve as a foundational reference for researchers and practitioners, providing a structured overview of the state-of-the-art in RL exploration. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, exploration strategies, intrinsic motivation, uncertainty estimation, deep learning, decision making.
