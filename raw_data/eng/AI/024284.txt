This paper introduces MagGAN, a novel mask-guided generative adversarial network designed for high-resolution face attribute editing. The objective is to enable precise and realistic editing of facial attributes, such as hair color, eye shape, and skin tone, while preserving the original image's quality and details. MagGAN employs a mask-guided approach, allowing for targeted editing of specific facial regions. The network consists of a generator and a discriminator, trained jointly to produce photorealistic results. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of MagGAN in editing high-resolution face images with improved accuracy and realism compared to existing methods. The proposed approach achieves state-of-the-art performance in face attribute editing, with potential applications in fields such as computer vision, graphics, and social media. Key contributions include the introduction of a mask-guided mechanism, which enhances editing precision, and the ability to handle high-resolution images. Relevant keywords: face attribute editing, generative adversarial networks, mask-guided editing, high-resolution imaging, computer vision, deep learning.
