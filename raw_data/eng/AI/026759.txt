This paper addresses the challenge of noise-robust image classification by proposing a novel approach that integrates wavelet transforms with Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs). Traditional CNNs often struggle with performance degradation in the presence of noise, limiting their effectiveness in real-world applications. To overcome this limitation, we introduce a hybrid architecture that leverages the multi-resolution analysis capabilities of wavelet transforms to enhance feature extraction and noise suppression. The proposed model decomposes input images into wavelet subbands, which are then processed by a CNN to capture both spatial and frequency-domain information. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets, including CIFAR-10 and MNIST, demonstrate that the wavelet-integrated CNN achieves superior classification accuracy under various noise conditions compared to standard CNN architectures. The results highlight the model's robustness to Gaussian, salt-and-pepper, and speckle noise, with performance improvements of up to 15% in high-noise scenarios. This research contributes a scalable and efficient framework for noise-resilient image classification, with potential applications in medical imaging, autonomous vehicles, and surveillance systems. The integration of wavelet transforms with deep learning opens new avenues for enhancing the robustness of AI systems in noisy environments.  

**Keywords**: Wavelet transform, Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), noise robustness, image classification, multi-resolution analysis, deep learning.
