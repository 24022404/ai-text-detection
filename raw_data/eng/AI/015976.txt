Automatic image captioning has achieved significant progress with deep learning models; however, generating accurate descriptions for images containing novel objects unseen during training remains challenging due to limited vocabulary coverage. This paper presents a new approach for captioning images featuring novel objects by leveraging online vocabulary expansion. The proposed method integrates an adaptive mechanism that dynamically incorporates external object names and concepts into the captioning model’s vocabulary as new visual categories are detected. By employing a modular architecture that combines convolutional neural networks for image feature extraction with a sequence generation model, our system dynamically updates its word embedding and decoding layers in real time, allowing inclusion of previously unseen objects in generated captions. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach significantly improves captioning quality for novel object images compared to existing fixed-vocabulary models, as evidenced by higher scores on metrics such as BLEU, METEOR, and CIDEr. The results showcase both the flexibility and scalability of online vocabulary expansion, enabling robust adaptation to evolving visual environments. Our method advances the state-of-the-art in image captioning and holds promise for applications in autonomous agents, assistive technologies, and multimedia content analysis. Key keywords: image captioning, novel objects, online vocabulary expansion, deep learning, adaptive language modeling, computer vision.
