Predicting driver intention at intersections is critical for developing effective Advanced Driver-Assistance Systems (ADAS) and autonomous vehicles. This paper introduces a novel deep learning model incorporating an attention mechanism to enhance the accuracy and reliability of driver intention prediction. Our approach utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) to extract spatial features from the driving scene, which are then fed into a recurrent neural network (RNN) with a temporal attention mechanism to capture the dynamic evolution of driving behavior. The attention mechanism focuses on the most relevant historical features for predicting future actions. We evaluate our model on a real-world driving dataset and demonstrate significant improvements in prediction accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods, particularly in complex intersection scenarios. The results show that the attention mechanism effectively identifies crucial cues for intention recognition, leading to more reliable predictions. This research contributes to safer and more efficient autonomous driving by enabling proactive decision-making based on accurate driver intention prediction.
Keywords: Driver Intention Prediction, Deep Learning, Attention Mechanism, Autonomous Driving, Intersection Management, ADAS.
