This paper presents a novel framework, Kernel Based Progressive Distillation (KBPD), designed to enhance the performance and efficiency of Adder Neural Networks (AdderNets). AdderNets, which utilize addition operations instead of multiplications, offer significant energy efficiency and reduced computational costs, making them attractive for deployment on resource-constrained devices. However, the performance gap between AdderNets and conventional convolutional neural networks (CNNs) remains a challenge. To address this, our proposed KBPD approach progressively transfers knowledge from a high-performing teacher model to an AdderNet student by leveraging kernel-based similarity measures, thereby facilitating a more structured and informative distillation process. We systematically investigate the integration of kernel metrics into the distillation pipeline, enabling a gradual alignment of intermediate representations and output distributions. Extensive experimental results on benchmark image classification datasets demonstrate that KBPD consistently outperforms existing distillation techniques for AdderNets, achieving higher accuracy with minimal additional computational overhead. Our approach bridges the performance gap with standard CNNs and paves the way for more practical deployment of energy-efficient neural networks in real-world applications. Key contributions of the study include the novel kernel-driven progressive distillation scheme, comprehensive evaluation on multiple datasets, and demonstrated scalability to various network architectures. Keywords: Adder Neural Networks, Knowledge Distillation, Kernel Methods, Energy-Efficient Deep Learning, Model Compression.
