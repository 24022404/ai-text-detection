Explainable AI (XAI) is crucial for understanding deep learning models, especially in complex multiscale systems. This paper introduces a novel approach leveraging mutual information (MI) to enhance the interpretability of deep learning models applied to such systems. Our method, Multiscale Information Bottleneck Network (MIBNet), integrates MI estimation within a deep learning architecture to identify and extract salient features across different scales. MIBNet learns compressed representations that maximize MI with relevant output variables while minimizing MI with irrelevant input features, thereby promoting disentanglement and improving explanation quality. We demonstrate the effectiveness of MIBNet on benchmark multiscale datasets, showing improved accuracy and significantly enhanced interpretability compared to standard deep learning models and other XAI techniques. Quantitative evaluations using information-theoretic metrics and qualitative analyses through visualization highlight MIBNet's ability to reveal meaningful relationships between multiscale inputs and model predictions. This work provides a powerful tool for understanding and trusting deep learning models in complex scientific domains.

Keywords: Explainable AI, Deep Learning, Mutual Information, Multiscale Systems, Interpretability, Information Bottleneck.
