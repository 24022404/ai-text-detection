This paper explores transformations between deep neural networks (DNNs), addressing the challenge of adapting pre-trained models to new tasks or resource constraints. We introduce a novel framework for directly converting a source DNN into a target DNN with a different architecture or parameterization. Our method leverages knowledge distillation and network morphism techniques to preserve functionality during the transformation process. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach by transforming large, complex DNNs into smaller, more efficient models while maintaining comparable accuracy on image classification and natural language processing tasks. Experiments show that our transformation method achieves a 15-20% reduction in model size and inference time compared to training from scratch, with minimal performance degradation. This work provides a practical solution for deploying DNNs in resource-constrained environments and facilitates knowledge transfer across diverse network architectures. Keywords: Deep Neural Networks, Network Transformation, Model Compression, Knowledge Distillation, Network Morphism.
