Objective: Continuous control in high-dimensional systems poses challenges in stability and sample efficiency for deep reinforcement learning (DRL). This paper introduces a hybrid framework that integrates deep RL with region-specific Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) controllers to enhance learning stability and performance.  
Methods: We partition the state space into overlapping regions using a neural network classifier trained on state features. Within each region, a locally linearized dynamics model enables the synthesis of an LQR controller, yielding optimal feedback gains for that region. A deep actor-critic algorithm is then augmented to switch between region-specific LQR policies and a global DRL policy based on region membership. Policy updates incorporate both gradient-based deep network updates and closed-form LQR solutions to accelerate convergence toward high-performance control.  
Results: Experiments on standard continuous control benchmarks—including inverted pendulum, cart-pole swing-up, and three-link manipulator—demonstrate that the proposed method achieves up to 35% faster convergence and 20% higher average return compared to state-of-the-art model-free DRL baselines (PPO, DDPG). Stability analysis shows reduced variance in episodic returns and improved robustness under perturbations.  
Conclusion: By blending model-based LQR regions with model-free deep RL, our approach offers a novel pathway to reliable, sample-efficient learning in complex control tasks. This framework has potential applications in robotics, autonomous vehicles, and aerospace systems where safety and data efficiency are critical.  
Keywords: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Linear Quadratic Regulator, Hybrid Control, Actor-Critic, Continuous Control.
