This paper introduces CoSimGNN, a novel approach for large-scale graph similarity computation. The objective is to efficiently compare and analyze the structural similarities between massive graphs, a crucial task in various applications such as social network analysis, recommendation systems, and bioinformatics. To achieve this, we propose a graph neural network (GNN) based framework that leverages a contrastive learning strategy to learn effective graph representations. Our method employs a siamese GNN architecture to jointly embed pairs of graphs into a compact latent space, enabling fast and accurate similarity computation. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of CoSimGNN over state-of-the-art methods in terms of both accuracy and scalability. Notably, our approach can handle graphs with millions of nodes and edges, making it a promising solution for real-world applications. The key contributions of this work include a scalable GNN-based framework for graph similarity computation, a novel contrastive learning objective, and a comprehensive evaluation on large-scale graph datasets. Overall, CoSimGNN paves the way for efficient and accurate graph similarity analysis, with potential applications in graph-based AI models, network science, and data mining. Key keywords: graph similarity, graph neural networks, contrastive learning, large-scale graphs, network analysis.
