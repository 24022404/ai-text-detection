This paper presents a novel approach to adaptive assistive navigation systems by introducing personalized dynamics models. The objective is to enhance the navigation experience for individuals with diverse needs and abilities by developing a system that can adapt to their unique requirements. Our approach utilizes machine learning algorithms to create personalized models that capture the dynamic behavior of each user, enabling the system to provide tailored assistance and feedback. The methodology involves collecting user data, processing it through a deep learning framework, and integrating the resulting models into a navigation system. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in navigation accuracy and user satisfaction compared to traditional, one-size-fits-all approaches. The key findings highlight the importance of personalization in assistive navigation, showcasing the potential of our system to positively impact the daily lives of individuals with disabilities. This research contributes to the development of more effective and user-centric assistive technologies, with potential applications in various fields, including healthcare, transportation, and education. Key keywords: personalized dynamics models, adaptive assistive navigation, machine learning, user-centered design, accessibility.
