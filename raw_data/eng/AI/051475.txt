The paper introduces FIDNet, a novel approach to semantic segmentation of LiDAR point clouds, addressing the limitations inherent in current methods that often struggle with capturing fine-grained details due to sparse data distributions.

Methods/Approach: FIDNet leverages a fully interpolation decoding mechanism that effectively maps sparse LiDAR data to dense representations, enabling more accurate semantic labeling. The architecture combines an encoder that processes raw point cloud data with a decoder that utilizes an innovative interpolation technique to enhance feature propagation across spatially distributed points.

Results/Findings: Our experiments demonstrate that FIDNet significantly improves segmentation accuracy and detail recognition compared to state-of-the-art methods. Evaluated on multiple benchmark datasets, FIDNet achieved remarkable performance gains, with quantitative metrics showing superior precision and recall rates, while qualitative analyses confirm enhanced semantic mapping, particularly in complex urban environments.

Conclusion/Implications: FIDNet offers a robust solution for semantic segmentation in LiDAR point clouds, with potential applications in autonomous driving, robotics, and geographic information systems. The fully interpolation decoding methodology not only addresses existing challenges in point cloud data processing but also provides a scalable framework adaptable to various 3D reconstruction and scene understanding tasks. Key insights highlight the potential for integrating FIDNet into real-time systems, thereby advancing the operational capabilities of LiDAR-based technologies.

Keywords: FIDNet, LiDAR, point cloud, semantic segmentation, interpolation, 3D reconstruction, autonomous driving, robotics.
