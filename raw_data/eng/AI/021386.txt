This paper proposes a novel approach to enhance the efficiency of distributed learning systems by introducing an alternative to traditional error feedback methods. The primary objective is to reduce communication overhead while maintaining model accuracy, a crucial challenge in modern distributed machine learning. Our method leverages a decentralized, peer-to-peer communication strategy, enabling nodes to share and adapt knowledge more effectively. By employing a stochastic gradient descent algorithm with a specially designed loss function, our approach achieves significant reductions in communication costs without compromising model performance. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing error feedback techniques, particularly in scenarios with high latency or limited bandwidth. The key contributions of this research include improved communication efficiency, enhanced model accuracy, and increased scalability for distributed learning systems. This work has significant implications for the development of more efficient and robust distributed machine learning algorithms, with potential applications in edge computing, IoT, and large-scale data processing. Key keywords: distributed learning, communication efficiency, error feedback, stochastic gradient descent, decentralized algorithms.
