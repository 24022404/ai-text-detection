Interactive segmentation is a critical task in computer vision, enabling precise object delineation with minimal user input. This paper introduces **Probabilistic Attention**, a novel approach designed to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of interactive segmentation systems. The proposed method leverages probabilistic modeling to dynamically focus on regions of interest, improving the segmentation process by reducing ambiguity in user-provided cues. Unlike traditional attention mechanisms, which rely on deterministic frameworks, our probabilistic approach adapts to varying levels of uncertainty in user interactions, ensuring robust performance across diverse datasets.  

We evaluate the proposed method on benchmark datasets, including PASCAL VOC and COCO, and compare it against state-of-the-art techniques. Experimental results demonstrate that Probabilistic Attention achieves superior segmentation accuracy, particularly in scenarios with sparse or noisy user inputs. Additionally, the method exhibits faster convergence during training, making it computationally efficient.  

The key contribution of this work lies in its ability to integrate probabilistic reasoning into attention mechanisms, offering a more flexible and reliable solution for interactive segmentation tasks. This innovation has broad implications for applications in medical imaging, autonomous systems, and augmented reality, where precise and adaptive segmentation is essential.  

**Keywords**: interactive segmentation, probabilistic attention, computer vision, uncertainty modeling, user-guided segmentation.
