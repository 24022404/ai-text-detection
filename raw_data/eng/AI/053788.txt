This paper presents Tag2Pix, a novel deep learning framework designed for automatic line art colorization guided by semantic text tags. The primary objective is to bridge the gap between user intent, expressed through descriptive tags, and visual colorization of line drawings, a task that remains challenging for existing AI-based solutions. To achieve this, we introduce two key innovations: the SECat (Semantic-Enhanced Category Attention) module, which dynamically integrates textual tag information into the colorization process, and a changing loss function that adapts during training to balance content fidelity and semantic adherence. Our method leverages an encoder-decoder architecture wherein the SECat module enhances feature extraction by attending to tag-relevant semantics, significantly improving color placement and consistency. We evaluate Tag2Pix on multiple benchmark datasets, demonstrating superior results in both quantitative metrics and human preference studies compared to baseline colorization models. The framework supports diverse and controllable outputs, reflecting complex user intentions more effectively. Our findings highlight the effectiveness of combining semantic textual guidance with adaptive training objectives in line art colorization, paving the way for more interactive and artistically meaningful AI-driven digital art tools. Keywords: line art colorization, deep learning, text-guided image synthesis, attention mechanism, adaptive loss, SECat, human-AI interaction.
