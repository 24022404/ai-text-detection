This paper introduces the Generative Video Transformer, a novel approach to video generation that treats objects as fundamental units, analogous to words in natural language processing. Our objective is to explore the potential of object-centric video generation, enabling more controllable and efficient video synthesis. We propose a transformer-based architecture that leverages object detection and tracking to represent videos as sequences of object embeddings. Our method allows for flexible and conditional video generation, enabling applications such as video editing, summarization, and content creation. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving state-of-the-art performance in video generation tasks. The Generative Video Transformer contributes to the advancement of video understanding and generation, with potential implications for computer vision, robotics, and multimedia applications. Key contributions include the introduction of object-centric video representation, the development of a transformer-based video generation model, and the demonstration of its efficacy in various video processing tasks. Relevant keywords: video generation, transformer, object detection, computer vision, AI models.
