Real-world image denoising is a challenging problem due to the complexity and diversity of noise present in captured images. This paper introduces a novel feature attention-based denoising method designed to effectively remove noise while preserving image details. Our approach leverages a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture incorporating feature attention modules that selectively emphasize informative features and suppress noise-related activations across different network layers. These modules dynamically learn to weight features based on their relevance to the denoising task. We train our model on a large dataset of real noisy images captured under varying conditions. Experimental results demonstrate that our proposed method achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing denoising algorithms in terms of both quantitative metrics (PSNR and SSIM) and visual quality. The feature attention mechanism significantly improves the network's ability to distinguish between noise and fine details, leading to sharper and more visually pleasing denoised images. This work offers a practical solution for real image denoising with improved detail preservation.

Keywords: Image Denoising, Feature Attention, Convolutional Neural Networks, Real Noise, Image Processing.
