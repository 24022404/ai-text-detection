This paper presents a novel methodology for the classification of datasets within the context of data mining, aiming to enhance both accuracy and efficiency in handling diverse and complex data. The proposed method leverages a hybrid approach combining advanced feature selection algorithms with an optimized classification framework tailored for large-scale and heterogeneous datasets. Extensive experiments were conducted on a variety of benchmark datasets, encompassing both structured and unstructured data, to assess the performance of the proposed method against existing state-of-the-art classification techniques. The results demonstrate significant improvements in classification accuracy, processing time, and robustness to noisy or incomplete data. Furthermore, our approach exhibits superior adaptability to different data domains, suggesting its broad applicability across various data mining scenarios. The key contributions of this research include the integration of adaptive feature selection, a modular classification pipeline, and comprehensive empirical evaluation. The implications of this work extend to improved preprocessing and knowledge discovery in big data analytics, machine learning pipeline automation, and real-world applications such as fraud detection, medical diagnosis, and customer segmentation. Keywords: data classification, data mining, feature selection, machine learning, big data, dataset heterogeneity.
