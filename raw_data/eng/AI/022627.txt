Video super-resolution (SR) aims to enhance the resolution of low-resolution (LR) videos. However, compressed videos pose a significant challenge due to compression artifacts. This paper introduces COMISR, a novel Compression-Informed Video Super-Resolution framework designed to mitigate the impact of compression on SR performance. COMISR leverages a dual-branch network: one branch focuses on spatial SR, while the other learns compression characteristics through a dedicated compression artifact reduction module. These features are then fused to guide the SR process. We incorporate a compression quality estimator to dynamically adjust the SR network based on the degree of compression. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets with varying compression levels demonstrate that COMISR significantly outperforms state-of-the-art SR methods, particularly in handling heavily compressed videos. The proposed framework effectively reduces compression artifacts and recovers high-frequency details, resulting in visually superior SR results. Key words: Video Super-Resolution, Compression Artifact Reduction, Deep Learning, Image Enhancement, Video Quality.
