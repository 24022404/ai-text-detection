This paper presents a novel, all-in-one deep learning framework that integrates facial expression transfer, editing, and recognition into a single network. The objective is to develop a unified model that can efficiently manipulate and analyze facial expressions in images. Our approach utilizes a multi-task learning strategy, where a single network is trained to perform expression transfer, editing, and recognition simultaneously. The network architecture combines convolutional neural networks and generative adversarial networks to learn expressive and discriminative features. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our model, achieving state-of-the-art performance in facial expression recognition and transfer tasks. The proposed framework also enables intuitive editing of facial expressions, allowing for precise control over the generated expressions. Our research contributes to the development of more versatile and efficient facial analysis systems, with potential applications in human-computer interaction, social robotics, and affective computing. Key keywords: facial expression analysis, deep learning, multi-task learning, generative models, computer vision.
