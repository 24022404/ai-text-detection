This paper presents a novel approach to point cloud processing using Recurrent Set Encoding (RSE), a technique that leverages the power of recurrent neural networks to effectively encode and process 3D point cloud data. The objective is to develop an efficient and scalable method for tasks such as point cloud classification, segmentation, and feature extraction. Our approach employs a recurrent set encoding module that recursively aggregates and transforms point cloud data, capturing complex geometric and spatial relationships. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of RSE in achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The proposed method has significant implications for various applications, including robotics, computer vision, and 3D reconstruction. Key contributions of this research include the introduction of a novel recurrent set encoding architecture, the demonstration of its effectiveness in point cloud processing tasks, and the exploration of its potential applications in real-world scenarios. Relevant keywords: point cloud processing, recurrent neural networks, set encoding, 3D reconstruction, computer vision.
