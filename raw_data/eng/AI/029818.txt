This paper aims to investigate the application of deep learning techniques in X-ray security imaging for automatic threat detection. The increasing need for efficient and accurate security screening has driven research into leveraging deep learning models to enhance the detection of prohibited items in X-ray images. Our approach involves a comprehensive survey of recent advances in deep learning architectures, including convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and transfer learning, tailored for X-ray image analysis. The results show that deep learning-based methods outperform traditional computer vision techniques in detecting threats, with improved accuracy and reduced false alarm rates. Key findings highlight the effectiveness of fine-tuning pre-trained models on X-ray image datasets and the importance of data augmentation techniques to address the limited availability of annotated data. This research contributes to the development of more reliable and automated X-ray security screening systems, with potential applications in aviation security, border control, and public safety. The study's novelty lies in its thorough examination of deep learning's role in X-ray threat detection, emphasizing the benefits of AI-driven security solutions. Relevant keywords: X-ray security imaging, deep learning, threat detection, CNNs, transfer learning, computer vision, AI security.
