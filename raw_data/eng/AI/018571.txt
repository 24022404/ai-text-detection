The increasing complexity of highway traffic systems necessitates advanced solutions for real-time event detection and monitoring. This paper addresses the challenge of automating video surveillance for highway traffic events using deep learning architectures. The primary objective is to develop a robust system capable of accurately identifying and classifying traffic incidents, such as accidents, congestion, and illegal maneuvers, from video feeds. To achieve this, we propose a novel deep learning framework that integrates convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for spatial feature extraction and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) for temporal sequence analysis. The model is trained on a large-scale dataset of annotated highway traffic videos, enabling it to learn complex patterns and correlations in dynamic traffic scenarios. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed architecture achieves superior performance in terms of detection accuracy and computational efficiency compared to existing methods. Specifically, the system achieves an F1-score of 92.5% and reduces false positives by 15% over traditional approaches. The findings highlight the potential of deep learning in enhancing real-time traffic monitoring and management. This research contributes to the field by introducing a scalable and adaptive solution for highway surveillance, with implications for improving road safety and traffic flow optimization.  

**Keywords**: video surveillance, deep learning, highway traffic, event detection, convolutional neural networks, recurrent neural networks.
