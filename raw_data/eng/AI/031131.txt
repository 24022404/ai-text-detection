This paper addresses the problem of best group subset selection, a combinatorial challenge with applications in diverse fields such as feature selection, sensor placement, and personalized recommendations. We introduce a novel, certifiably polynomial-time algorithm that efficiently identifies the optimal subset of groups, maximizing a given objective function while adhering to cardinality constraints. Our approach leverages a carefully constructed relaxation coupled with a rounding scheme that guarantees near-optimal solutions with provable approximation guarantees. Specifically, the algorithm provides a certificate of optimality within a bounded factor. Empirical evaluations on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that our algorithm achieves significantly improved performance compared to existing heuristics and approximation methods, both in terms of solution quality and computational efficiency. The algorithm's polynomial-time complexity and certification properties make it a practical and reliable tool for large-scale group subset selection problems. Keywords: Group Subset Selection, Combinatorial Optimization, Polynomial-Time Algorithm, Approximation Algorithm, Certification, Feature Selection.
