Text-based person re-identification (Re-ID) is a challenging task in the field of computer vision, aiming to match textual descriptions with visual images of individuals. This study introduces a novel dual-path convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture featuring a Max Gated block, designed to enhance the performance and accuracy of text-based person Re-ID systems.

Methods/Approach: The proposed dual-path CNN framework integrates a visual path for image processing and a textual path for understanding descriptive inputs. The Max Gated block is employed within the model to dynamically filter and emphasize salient features during the matching process. This architecture leverages deep learning techniques to achieve an efficient interaction between text and image modalities, improving feature extraction and representation.

Results/Findings: The dual-path CNN with Max Gated block outperforms conventional single-path models and prior dual-path architectures on benchmark datasets. The experimental evaluations demonstrate significant improvements in accuracy and robustness, with the model achieving state-of-the-art performance in text-to-image matching precision and recall metrics.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a novel architectural framework that significantly advances the capabilities of text-based person Re-ID systems. The integration of the Max Gated block within a dual-path CNN offers a more precise and reliable matching mechanism, making it a promising solution for applications in surveillance, security, and personalized content retrieval. Future work can build upon this approach to explore broader applications and more complex scenarios in multimodal data integration.

Keywords: text-based person re-identification, dual-path CNN, Max Gated block, deep learning, multimodal integration, feature extraction, computer vision.
