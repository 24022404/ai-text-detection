This paper presents a novel approach to deep 3D face identification, addressing the challenges of traditional 2D face recognition methods. The objective is to develop a robust and accurate system for identifying individuals using 3D facial models. Our approach utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, leveraging the unique characteristics of 3D data to extract distinctive features. The proposed method involves a multi-stage process, including 3D face reconstruction, feature extraction, and matching. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our approach, achieving a significant improvement in identification accuracy compared to state-of-the-art 2D face recognition systems. The key contributions of this research include the development of a large-scale 3D face dataset and a novel 3D face representation technique, enabling efficient and accurate identification. This study has important implications for various applications, including security, surveillance, and forensic analysis, and highlights the potential of deep learning techniques in 3D face identification. Key keywords: 3D face recognition, deep learning, CNN, face identification, biometrics.
