This paper presents D3DLO, a novel Deep 3D LiDAR Odometry approach designed to estimate the pose of a LiDAR sensor in real-time. The objective is to address the challenges of traditional odometry methods, which often struggle with complex environments and dynamic scenes. Our method leverages a deep learning architecture to learn the underlying patterns in 3D point cloud data, enabling accurate and robust pose estimation. The D3DLO framework utilizes a combination of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) to process sequential 3D point cloud scans, extracting features that capture the spatial and temporal relationships between scans. Experimental results demonstrate that D3DLO outperforms state-of-the-art odometry methods, achieving higher accuracy and efficiency in various scenarios, including urban and rural environments. The key contributions of this research include the development of a novel deep learning-based odometry approach, the introduction of a new 3D point cloud feature extraction technique, and the demonstration of the method's effectiveness in real-world applications, such as autonomous vehicles and robotics. Keywords: LiDAR Odometry, Deep Learning, 3D Point Clouds, Autonomous Vehicles, Robotics.
