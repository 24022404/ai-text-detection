Video Story Question Answering (VSQA) is an emerging challenge in the intersection of computer vision and natural language processing, aiming to develop models that can understand and answer questions related to video content. This paper introduces a novel approach utilizing a Multimodal Dual Attention Memory mechanism designed to enhance the comprehension and retrieval of complementary information from both visual and textual modalities within video narratives.

Methods/Approach: Our approach integrates dual attention mechanisms with a memory-augmented neural network framework. The dual attention method focuses on simultaneously extracting and aligning features from video frames and corresponding subtitles or narration, allowing the system to dynamically attend to relevant segments across both modalities. We employ a memory component that compiles cross-modal interactions over time, improving the model's ability to recall pertinent details when generating answers.

Results/Findings: Through extensive experiments conducted on benchmark VSQA datasets, our framework demonstrates a significant improvement in accuracy and contextual understanding over existing state-of-the-art methods. The multimodal dual attention mechanism effectively captures intricate detail and context, leading to a more precise and coherent answering capability. Comparative analysis shows enhanced performance primarily in complex scenarios where video and language cues are intertwined.

Conclusion/Implications: The proposed Multimodal Dual Attention Memory model provides a robust solution for the Video Story Question Answering task, particularly excelling in scenarios that require deep integration of multimodal content. Our findings highlight the potential of dual attention models combined with memory networks in improving the interpretative abilities of AI systems in nuanced storytelling environments. This contribution opens pathways for future advancements in multimodal learning applications, offering promising applications in fields like interactive media, education, and content recommendation systems.

Keywords: Video Story Question Answering, Multimodal Learning, Dual Attention, Memory-Augmented Networks, AI, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing.
