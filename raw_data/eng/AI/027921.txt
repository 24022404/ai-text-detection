Clustering data from multiple views or modalities has become increasingly important in modern information systems, where heterogeneous sources provide complementary perspectives on complex datasets. This paper addresses the challenge of effectively integrating diverse structural information captured by multiple graphs in multi-view clustering tasks. We propose a Consistent Multiple Graph Embedding (CMGE) framework that jointly learns low-dimensional representations for each view while preserving both the local graph structure and cross-view consistency. The proposed method constructs separate graph embeddings for each view, then aligns these embeddings via a consistency regularization mechanism. This approach allows the model to exploit shared patterns across views while maintaining unique information inherent to each modality. Extensive experiments on benchmark multi-view datasets demonstrate that CMGE significantly outperforms state-of-the-art clustering methods in terms of clustering accuracy and normalized mutual information. The results validate the framework’s ability to robustly integrate multi-view information and handle discrepancies among views. Our approach provides a scalable and flexible solution for multi-view data analysis, with potential applications in image retrieval, social network analysis, and bioinformatics. Keywords: multi-view clustering, graph embedding, representation learning, consistency regularization, multi-modal data integration.
