Autonomous driving heavily relies on accurate and efficient object detection, especially in complex urban environments. This paper introduces a novel pillar-based object detection framework designed to enhance the perception capabilities of autonomous vehicles. Our method utilizes a 3D pillar representation of LiDAR point clouds, enabling faster processing and reduced memory footprint compared to traditional voxel-based approaches. We propose a new pillar feature encoding module that effectively captures contextual information within and across pillars using attention mechanisms. Furthermore, we introduce a refined detection head specifically tailored for pillar-based features, improving object localization accuracy, particularly for small and distant objects. Experimental results on the KITTI dataset demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance in 3D object detection while maintaining real-time processing speeds suitable for autonomous driving applications. The proposed framework offers a robust and efficient solution for object detection, contributing to safer and more reliable autonomous navigation. Keywords: Autonomous Driving, Object Detection, LiDAR, 3D Pillars, Deep Learning.
