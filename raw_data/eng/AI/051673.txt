Accurate filling level classification is critical for applications in industrial automation, logistics, and waste management. However, existing methods often struggle with variability in object shapes, lighting conditions, and occlusions, leading to suboptimal performance. This paper proposes a novel approach to improve filling level classification by leveraging adversarial training techniques. We introduce a deep learning framework that combines a convolutional neural network (CNN) with a generative adversarial network (GAN) to enhance the robustness and generalization capabilities of the classification model. The adversarial component is designed to generate challenging synthetic data, enabling the classifier to learn more discriminative features under diverse conditions. Experiments conducted on a benchmark dataset demonstrate that our method achieves a significant improvement in classification accuracy compared to traditional approaches, with a 12% increase in precision and a 9% reduction in error rates. Furthermore, the model exhibits superior performance in real-world scenarios with varying environmental factors. The results highlight the potential of adversarial training to address key challenges in filling level classification, offering a scalable and adaptable solution for industrial applications. This research contributes to advancing the state-of-the-art in automated monitoring systems and provides a foundation for future work in adversarial learning for classification tasks.  

**Keywords**: filling level classification, adversarial training, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, generative adversarial networks, industrial automation.
