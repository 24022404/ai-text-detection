This thesis introduces an innovative, user-friendly, and secure social login system tailored for Web3 environments, with the explicit goal of bridging the gap between complex blockchain technologies and broader user adoption. The proposed system ingeniously integrates smart contracts, the Pedersen Distributed Key Generation (DKG) protocol, and Shamir's secret sharing scheme to establish a robust authentication process that eliminates the necessity for users to possess specialized blockchain knowledge or undergo complicated onboarding procedures. By dissecting the system into three streamlined sub-processes, the architecture emphasizes both usability and security, ensuring that individuals can seamlessly access decentralized applications through familiar social login mechanisms while their credentials and cryptographic secrets remain protected within a decentralized framework. This user-centric approach significantly lowers entry barriers, reducing the learning curve for non-technical users and fostering inclusivity in the rapidly evolving Web3 ecosystem. The thesis not only details the design choices underlying this architecture but also thoroughly explains the novel mechanisms and technical innovations that differentiate it from existing solutions, particularly within the emerging decentralized oracle network ecosystem. By democratizing access to Web3 technologies and maintaining a secure authentication environment, the proposed solution addresses a critical need for usability and accessibility, setting a new standard for how users interact with decentralized platforms and ultimately contributing to the mainstream adoption of blockchain-based applications.
