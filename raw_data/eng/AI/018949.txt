This paper introduces DH3D, a novel deep learning framework for generating hierarchical 3D descriptors to address the challenge of robust large-scale 6DoF (6 Degrees of Freedom) relocalization. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of relocalization in complex, large-scale environments, which is critical for applications such as augmented reality, autonomous navigation, and robotics. The proposed approach leverages a hierarchical architecture that combines local and global feature extraction, enabling the system to capture fine-grained details while maintaining contextual awareness. A deep neural network is trained to generate discriminative 3D descriptors that are invariant to viewpoint changes and environmental variations. The method is evaluated on multiple large-scale datasets, demonstrating superior performance in terms of relocalization accuracy and robustness compared to state-of-the-art techniques. Experimental results show that DH3D achieves significant improvements in challenging scenarios with dynamic lighting, occlusions, and repetitive structures. The framework’s scalability and computational efficiency make it suitable for real-time applications. This work contributes a novel hierarchical descriptor generation approach that advances the state of the art in 6DoF relocalization, with potential applications in robotics, AR/VR, and 3D mapping.  

**Keywords**: 6DoF relocalization, 3D descriptors, hierarchical deep learning, large-scale environments, augmented reality, robotics.
