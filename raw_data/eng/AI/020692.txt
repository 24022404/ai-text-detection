Airline crew pairing optimization is a critical challenge in the aviation industry, requiring the efficient assignment of flight schedules to crew members while minimizing costs and adhering to complex regulations. Traditional approaches often struggle with scalability and computational complexity, especially for large-scale operations. This paper introduces a novel framework that leverages combinatorial pattern learning to enhance the efficiency of crew pairing optimization. By identifying and exploiting recurring patterns in flight schedules, our approach reduces the search space and accelerates the optimization process. We propose a hybrid model combining graph-based representations with machine learning techniques to predict and prioritize high-value pairing patterns. Experimental results on real-world datasets demonstrate significant improvements in solution quality and computational efficiency compared to conventional methods. Our findings highlight the potential of integrating combinatorial pattern learning into large-scale optimization problems, offering a scalable and cost-effective solution for airline operations. This research contributes to advancing optimization techniques in the aviation industry and provides a foundation for future applications in other domains requiring combinatorial optimization.  

**Keywords**: Airline crew pairing, combinatorial optimization, pattern learning, machine learning, scalability, graph-based models.
