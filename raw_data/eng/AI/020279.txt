Understanding market dynamics requires accurate identification of participants and their behaviors, a challenge often complicated by the complexity of financial time series data. This paper addresses the problem of discovering market participants and their strategies from observed time series data using a novel adversarial calibration approach for multi-agent simulators. The proposed method leverages a combination of deep reinforcement learning and generative adversarial networks (GANs) to model and calibrate the behaviors of heterogeneous agents in a simulated market environment. By iteratively refining the simulator through adversarial training, the approach identifies the most likely set of market participants and their strategies that explain the observed data. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of the method in accurately recovering participant behaviors and outperforming traditional calibration techniques in terms of predictive accuracy and robustness. The findings highlight the potential of adversarial calibration in enhancing the realism of multi-agent simulations and improving decision-making in financial markets. This research contributes to the growing field of market simulation by introducing a scalable and data-driven framework for participant discovery, with implications for algorithmic trading, risk management, and regulatory analysis.  

**Keywords**: market simulation, multi-agent systems, adversarial calibration, time series analysis, deep reinforcement learning, generative adversarial networks, financial markets.
