Time series forecasting is a critical task in numerous domains, from finance to energy management, yet accurately capturing complex temporal dependencies remains a challenge. This paper introduces *Topological Attention*, a novel neural network architecture designed to enhance time series forecasting by leveraging topological data analysis (TDA) to model intricate temporal structures. The proposed approach integrates attention mechanisms with persistent homology, a TDA technique, to identify and prioritize significant topological features in time series data. By doing so, it captures both local and global patterns, improving the model's ability to generalize across diverse datasets. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that Topological Attention outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving superior forecasting accuracy and robustness. The results highlight the effectiveness of combining topological insights with deep learning, offering a new perspective for addressing temporal dependencies in time series data. This work contributes to the growing field of hybrid AI models, showcasing the potential of integrating domain-specific mathematical tools with neural networks for improved predictive performance.  

**Keywords**: Time series forecasting, topological data analysis, attention mechanisms, persistent homology, neural networks, hybrid AI models.
