Object detection is a critical task in computer vision, with applications ranging from autonomous driving to surveillance systems. While significant progress has been made, achieving high-quality detection with precise localization and accurate classification remains challenging. This paper introduces Cascade R-CNN, a novel multi-stage object detection framework designed to address these challenges. The proposed approach builds upon the Region-based Convolutional Neural Network (R-CNN) architecture, incorporating a cascade of detectors trained with increasing Intersection over Union (IoU) thresholds. This progressive refinement enables the model to improve detection quality at each stage, effectively reducing false positives and enhancing localization accuracy.  

Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets such as COCO and PASCAL VOC demonstrate the superiority of Cascade R-CNN over existing methods. The framework achieves state-of-the-art performance, with significant improvements in both detection precision and recall. Notably, the cascade structure is shown to be robust across various backbone networks, making it a versatile solution for high-quality object detection.  

The key contribution of this work lies in its ability to systematically improve detection quality through a cascaded refinement process, offering a scalable and effective approach for real-world applications. Cascade R-CNN sets a new standard for object detection, providing valuable insights for future research in the field.  

**Keywords**: Object detection, Cascade R-CNN, IoU threshold, localization accuracy, deep learning, computer vision.
