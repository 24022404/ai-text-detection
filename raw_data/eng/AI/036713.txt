This study introduces ICADx, a novel interpretable computer-aided diagnosis system for breast masses, aiming to improve the accuracy and transparency of diagnostic decisions. By leveraging a combination of deep learning and feature attribution techniques, ICADx provides insights into the decision-making process, enabling clinicians to understand the underlying factors driving the predictions. The system utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture to analyze medical images, which is then coupled with a feature importance module to identify the most relevant image features contributing to the diagnosis. Experimental results demonstrate that ICADx achieves high diagnostic accuracy, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods, while also providing interpretable explanations of its decisions. The key contributions of this research include the development of a transparent and trustworthy diagnostic framework, which has the potential to enhance clinical decision-making and improve patient outcomes. ICADx highlights the importance of explainability in medical imaging analysis, particularly in applications such as breast cancer diagnosis, where accuracy and reliability are paramount. Relevant keywords: computer-aided diagnosis, interpretable machine learning, breast cancer, medical imaging, deep learning, explainability.
