This paper introduces a novel approach to synthesizing facial action units (AUs) using region-based adversarial learning. The objective is to generate realistic and controllable AU manipulations in facial images. Our method employs a generative adversarial network (GAN) architecture with a regional discriminator that focuses on specific facial regions associated with target AUs. This region-specific attention improves the realism and accuracy of AU synthesis compared to global discriminator approaches. We utilize a disentanglement loss to ensure independence between different AUs and an identity preservation loss to maintain subject identity. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach generates higher quality AU manipulations, achieving superior performance in visual realism and AU intensity control compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. Furthermore, we show that our synthesized data can be effectively used for training AU recognition models, improving their performance and robustness. The proposed method offers significant potential for applications in facial expression analysis, animation, and computer vision. Keywords: Facial Action Units, Adversarial Learning, GAN, Facial Synthesis, Region-Based, Computer Vision.
