This paper presents Pixelated Semantic Colorization, a novel approach to image colorization that leverages semantic information to produce vibrant and contextually accurate results. The objective is to develop an efficient and effective method for colorizing grayscale images, preserving the original pixelation and texture. Our approach utilizes a deep learning-based model that integrates semantic segmentation and colorization techniques, enabling the system to understand the scene and apply appropriate colors to each object or region. The results demonstrate significant improvements over existing colorization methods, with enhanced color accuracy, texture preservation, and visual coherence. Our contributions include a unique pixelated colorization framework, a semantic-aware color transfer mechanism, and a comprehensive evaluation protocol. The proposed method has potential applications in image and video editing, graphic design, and computer vision. Key aspects of this research include semantic image understanding, colorization, pixelation, and deep learning, making it a valuable contribution to the field of computer vision and image processing.
