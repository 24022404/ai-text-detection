This paper introduces Snapshot Distillation, a novel approach to teacher-student optimization that achieves knowledge transfer in a single generation. The objective is to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of distillation methods by leveraging the concept of snapshots, which capture the intermediate states of a teacher model during its training process. Our method employs a unique approach where a student model is trained to mimic the behavior of the teacher model at multiple snapshots, effectively distilling the knowledge acquired by the teacher over time. The results show that Snapshot Distillation outperforms traditional distillation methods, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency. Key findings include a reduction in training time and an increase in the student model's performance, making it a promising technique for applications where computational resources are limited. The proposed method has important implications for the development of more efficient and effective knowledge distillation techniques, with potential applications in areas such as computer vision, natural language processing, and edge AI. Keywords: knowledge distillation, teacher-student optimization, snapshot distillation, efficient training, edge AI.
