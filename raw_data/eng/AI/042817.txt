Graph generation is a fundamental problem with applications in drug discovery, social network analysis, and materials science. Existing graph generation methods often struggle to produce small, structurally diverse graphs with desired properties. This paper introduces GraphVAE, a novel variational autoencoder (VAE) architecture specifically designed for generating small graphs. GraphVAE employs a graph-structured encoder and decoder to learn a latent representation of graph data. The encoder maps input graphs to a latent space, while the decoder reconstructs graphs from latent vectors. A key innovation is the use of adjacency matrix and node feature decoders, allowing for precise control over graph structure and node attributes. We evaluate GraphVAE on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its superior performance in generating small graphs with high validity, uniqueness, and novelty compared to existing graph generation models. Experimental results show that GraphVAE achieves state-of-the-art performance in generating graphs with specific structural properties. The generated graphs exhibit improved chemical validity and desired structural motifs, highlighting the potential of GraphVAE for applications requiring the generation of small, high-quality graphs. Keywords: Graph Generation, Variational Autoencoder, GraphVAE, Deep Learning, Molecular Generation.
