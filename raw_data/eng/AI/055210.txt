Exploration is a critical challenge in reinforcement learning (RL), particularly in environments with sparse rewards. This paper introduces *Never Give Up* (NGU), a novel approach to learning directed exploration strategies that significantly improve agent performance in complex and sparse-reward environments. The proposed method combines intrinsic motivation with episodic memory to guide exploration, enabling agents to revisit and build upon previously discovered states effectively. NGU employs a dual-objective mechanism: one for maximizing extrinsic rewards and another for intrinsic rewards derived from novelty and curiosity. This dual focus ensures that the agent maintains a balance between exploiting known strategies and exploring new, potentially rewarding areas.  

The approach is evaluated across a range of benchmark environments, including procedurally generated mazes and high-dimensional control tasks. Results demonstrate that NGU outperforms existing exploration strategies, achieving higher cumulative rewards and faster convergence. Notably, the method exhibits robustness in environments with varying levels of reward sparsity and complexity.  

The key contribution of this work lies in its ability to integrate episodic memory with intrinsic motivation, providing a scalable and efficient solution for exploration in RL. This research has significant implications for applications requiring autonomous decision-making in uncertain environments, such as robotics, game AI, and real-world problem-solving. Keywords: reinforcement learning, exploration strategies, intrinsic motivation, episodic memory, sparse rewards, autonomous agents.
