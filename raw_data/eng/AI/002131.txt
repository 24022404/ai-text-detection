Neural network pruning is a crucial technique for deploying deep learning models on resource-constrained devices. However, existing methods often overlook the intrinsic geometric structure of neural networks, leading to suboptimal pruning decisions and performance degradation. This paper introduces RicciNets, a novel curvature-guided pruning framework that leverages discrete Ricci flow to identify and remove less significant connections in high-performance neural networks. Our approach computes a discrete Ricci curvature on the network's graph representation, using it as a proxy for connection importance. Edges with low or negative Ricci curvature are then iteratively pruned, guided by a predefined sparsity target. We evaluate RicciNets on various benchmark datasets and network architectures, demonstrating significant improvements in pruning efficiency and model accuracy compared to state-of-the-art pruning techniques. Specifically, RicciNets achieves comparable or superior accuracy with significantly higher sparsity levels. This method offers a principled and efficient approach to neural network compression, making it suitable for deployment in edge computing and mobile applications. Keywords: Neural Network Pruning, Ricci Flow, Curvature, Network Compression, Deep Learning, Edge Computing.
