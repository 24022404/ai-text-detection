This paper introduces DisCoveR, a novel approach for the accurate and efficient discovery of declarative process models. The objective is to address the challenges of traditional process discovery methods, which often result in complex and imprecise models. DisCoveR leverages a combination of machine learning and process mining techniques to discover declarative models that capture the underlying behavior of business processes. The approach utilizes a unique algorithm that efficiently explores the search space of possible models, ensuring high accuracy and scalability. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of DisCoveR, outperforming existing methods in terms of model accuracy, complexity, and discovery time. The key findings highlight the importance of declarative process models in capturing complex process behavior, and the potential applications of DisCoveR in various domains, such as business process management, workflow optimization, and decision support systems. Overall, DisCoveR contributes to the field of process mining by providing a robust and efficient method for discovering accurate declarative process models, with significant implications for improving business process intelligence and automation. Key keywords: process mining, declarative process models, machine learning, business process management, workflow optimization.
