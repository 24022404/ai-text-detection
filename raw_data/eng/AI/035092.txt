Medical image segmentation and localization are critical for computer-aided diagnosis and treatment planning. This paper introduces a novel approach for accurate and efficient medical image segmentation and localization utilizing deformable templates. Our method employs an adaptive deformable template model driven by a hybrid energy function that combines image-based features with shape priors. The template is initialized based on anatomical landmarks and iteratively deformed to match the target structure in the medical image. We incorporate a multi-resolution strategy to improve robustness and capture fine-grained details. The proposed method was evaluated on a diverse dataset of medical images, including MRI and CT scans of the brain, heart, and liver. Results demonstrate superior segmentation accuracy and localization performance compared to conventional deformable models and other state-of-the-art techniques. The developed approach offers a promising solution for automated medical image analysis, potentially reducing manual intervention and improving diagnostic precision.

Keywords: Medical Image Segmentation, Localization, Deformable Templates, Image Analysis, Computer-Aided Diagnosis.
