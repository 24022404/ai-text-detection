This paper presents a novel approach to dictionary learning, introducing Equiprobable Matching Pursuit (EMP), a method designed to improve the efficiency and accuracy of sparse signal representation. The objective is to develop a dictionary learning framework that adapts to diverse signal structures by incorporating an equiprobable selection strategy into the matching pursuit algorithm. EMP combines the benefits of traditional matching pursuit techniques with the flexibility of probabilistic selection, allowing for a more balanced and efficient exploration of the signal space. The results show that EMP outperforms existing dictionary learning methods in terms of reconstruction error and signal representation quality, particularly in scenarios with complex or mixed signal structures. The key findings highlight the potential of EMP in various applications, including image and audio processing, where accurate and efficient signal representation is crucial. By introducing a novel probabilistic approach to dictionary learning, this research contributes to the advancement of sparse signal processing and opens up new avenues for the development of more sophisticated and adaptive signal representation techniques, with relevant keywords including dictionary learning, sparse signal representation, matching pursuit, and probabilistic selection.
