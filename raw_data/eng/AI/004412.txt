This paper investigates the challenge of nonlinear disentanglement in natural data by introducing a novel framework based on temporal sparse coding. Traditional disentanglement methods often rely on linear assumptions or require supervision, limiting their efficiency in capturing the complex structures inherent in real-world signals such as images, audio, and video. Our proposed method employs a temporally-aware sparse coding model that leverages temporal continuity to encourage the separation of underlying nonlinear factors, while also promoting compact and interpretable representations. We develop an efficient algorithm to learn temporally-sparse codes and corresponding nonlinear basis functions, enabling unsupervised disentanglement in high-dimensional and sequential datasets. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including video sequences and speech signals, demonstrate that our approach achieves superior disentanglement performance compared to conventional linear methods and recent deep learning-based alternatives. The findings indicate significant improvements in interpretability and downstream task effectiveness, such as classification and data reconstruction. This research highlights the potential of temporal sparse coding as a versatile and powerful tool for nonlinear disentanglement in natural data, opening new avenues for applications in machine learning, neuroscience, and signal processing. Key keywords: nonlinear disentanglement, temporal sparse coding, natural data, unsupervised learning, representation learning.
