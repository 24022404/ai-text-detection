Bounding box regression is crucial for object detection, yet current loss functions often struggle with imprecise localization due to inconsistent handling of side and corner information. This paper introduces SCALoss, a novel Side and Corner Aligned Loss designed to improve bounding box regression accuracy. SCALoss decomposes the bounding box into its sides and corners, applying adaptive weighting based on the prediction confidence of each component. Specifically, we introduce a side alignment term that enforces consistency between predicted and ground truth sides, and a corner alignment term that focuses on accurate corner localization. By adaptively weighting these terms, SCALoss provides a more robust and precise regression objective. Experiments on benchmark datasets, including COCO and Pascal VOC, demonstrate that SCALoss significantly outperforms state-of-the-art bounding box regression losses, achieving substantial improvements in Average Precision (AP). The results highlight the effectiveness of SCALoss in enhancing object detection accuracy through improved bounding box localization. Keywords: Object Detection, Bounding Box Regression, Loss Function, Deep Learning, Computer Vision.
