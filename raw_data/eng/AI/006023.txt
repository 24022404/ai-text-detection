The study aims to address the challenge of understanding memes within a multimodal framework by participating in SemEval-2020 Task 8, which focused on the analysis of memetic content combining both textual and visual elements. Memes, which are prevalent in social media, require sophisticated methods for accurate sentiment and emotion detection.

Methods/Approach: This research employs a novel approach incorporating both visual and textual data to enhance the recognition of emotions and sentiment in memes. The methodology leverages deep learning techniques, specifically utilizing convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for image processing and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) alongside transformer models for text analysis. The integration of these modalities aims to improve the predictive power and understanding of the sentiment and emotional impact of memetic content.

Results/Findings: The proposed model demonstrates significant improvements in accuracy compared to unidimensional analyses. Notably, the combined multimodal approach offers enhanced performance in categories like humor, sarcasm, and offensive content detection. The ensemble of text and image analysis platforms achieves superior results in distinguishing sentiment, thus validating the efficacy of the approach in the context of multimodal analysis.

Conclusion/Implications: The findings illustrate the importance of integrating multiple data modalities to improve sentiment analysis in memes. This research contributes to the field by providing a comprehensive framework for memotion analysis, which can be applied to enhance content moderation and user engagement understanding in digital marketing and social media analytics. The study highlights the potential of multimodal analyses to capture complex communication cues inherent in social media content.

Keywords: Multimodal analysis, Memes, Deep learning, Sentiment detection, CNN, RNN, Transformer models, Social media analytics, SemEval-2020.
