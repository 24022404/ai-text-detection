This paper addresses the challenge of stochastic computation offloading in digital twin networks by proposing a novel deep reinforcement learning approach. The objective is to optimize computation offloading decisions in dynamic and uncertain environments, maximizing system performance and minimizing latency. Our method utilizes a deep Q-network to learn optimal offloading policies, adapting to changing network conditions and computation workloads. Simulation results demonstrate significant improvements in system efficiency and reliability compared to traditional offloading strategies. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of our approach in reducing average latency by up to 30% and increasing system throughput by up to 25%. This research contributes to the development of intelligent digital twin networks, enabling efficient and adaptive computation offloading in complex stochastic environments. The proposed framework has potential applications in various fields, including smart manufacturing, healthcare, and transportation systems, where digital twins and edge computing play a crucial role. Key keywords: deep reinforcement learning, stochastic computation offloading, digital twin networks, edge computing, Q-learning, adaptive offloading.
