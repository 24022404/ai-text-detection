This paper presents KeRLym, a novel deep reinforcement learning (DRL) agent designed for radio control and signal detection tasks. The objective of this research is to address the challenges of dynamic spectrum management and efficient signal detection in complex radio environments. KeRLym leverages a custom-built Gym environment to simulate real-world radio scenarios, enabling the agent to learn optimal control policies and signal detection strategies through interaction. The proposed approach combines deep Q-learning with advanced feature extraction techniques to enhance the agent's ability to adapt to varying signal conditions and interference patterns. Experimental results demonstrate that KeRLym outperforms traditional rule-based methods and other DRL approaches in terms of signal detection accuracy, spectrum utilization efficiency, and adaptability to dynamic environments. The findings highlight the potential of KeRLym for applications in cognitive radio networks, IoT communication, and spectrum sharing systems. This work contributes to the growing field of DRL in wireless communication by introducing a scalable and robust framework for radio control and signal detection, paving the way for future advancements in intelligent spectrum management.  

**Keywords**: deep reinforcement learning, radio control, signal detection, cognitive radio, Gym environment, spectrum management, IoT communication.
