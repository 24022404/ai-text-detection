This paper investigates the vulnerability of graph neural networks to adversarial attacks on graph structured data. The objective is to develop a novel attack method that can effectively perturb the graph structure and node attributes to mislead the model's predictions. Our approach utilizes a reinforcement learning framework to learn an optimal attack strategy, which can be applied to various graph-based models. The results show that our proposed method can significantly degrade the performance of state-of-the-art graph neural networks, with a success rate of up to 90% in certain scenarios. Our findings highlight the importance of considering adversarial robustness in graph-based models and demonstrate the potential risks of using these models in security-critical applications. The contributions of this research include the development of a new adversarial attack method for graph structured data and the provision of insights into the vulnerability of graph neural networks. Key keywords: graph neural networks, adversarial attacks, graph structured data, reinforcement learning, robustness.
