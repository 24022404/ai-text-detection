This research addresses the challenge of automated evaluation of root canal therapy by introducing an innovative deep learning framework. The aim is to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of dental diagnostics using cutting-edge technologies while minimizing human error and subjective interpretation.

Methods/Approach: We propose the Anatomy-Guided Parallel Bottleneck Transformer Network, a novel architecture that leverages transformer-based models specifically designed for dental radiographs. This network incorporates anatomical guidance to focus on clinically relevant features and employs parallel bottleneck modules to efficiently process high-dimensional data. The integration of transformer technologies allows for superior representation learning and feature extraction compared to traditional convolutional neural networks.

Results/Findings: The proposed model demonstrates significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency over existing methods, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets. The anatomy-guided approach results in enhanced localization and assessment of root canal fillings. Comparative analyses show our network's superior capability in identifying procedural errors and therapeutic outcomes, with increased precision and recall metrics.

Conclusion/Implications: This research presents a transformative step forward in the field of automated dental diagnostics by integrating advanced neural networks with domain-specific anatomical insights. The Anatomy-Guided Parallel Bottleneck Transformer Network has the potential to revolutionize how root canal therapy evaluation is conducted in clinical settings, offering a scalable solution that could be extended to other dental procedures. Future directions include real-time processing capabilities and expanded applicability to diverse dental imaging modalities.

Keywords: Root Canal Therapy, Transformer Network, Dental Diagnostics, Deep Learning, Anatomy-Guided, Bottleneck Modules, Automated Evaluation.
