This paper addresses the challenge of modeling dynamic graphs where both node attributes and graph structures evolve over time. Our objective is to develop a framework that captures the co-evolutionary dynamics between node attributes and graph topology. We propose a novel approach that leverages graph neural networks and temporal convolutional networks to learn the complex interplay between attribute and structure changes. Our method, termed Attribute-Structure Co-Evolution Network (ASCE-Net), jointly models the temporal dynamics of node attributes and graph structures, enabling the prediction of future graph states. Experimental results on several real-world datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of ASCE-Net in capturing co-evolutionary patterns and outperforming state-of-the-art baselines. Our research contributes to the understanding of dynamic graph evolution and has implications for applications such as social network analysis, recommendation systems, and traffic forecasting. Key contributions include the introduction of a co-evolutionary framework, the development of ASCE-Net, and the demonstration of its efficacy in modeling complex dynamic graphs. Relevant keywords: dynamic graphs, graph neural networks, temporal convolutional networks, co-evolution, attribute-structure interactions.
