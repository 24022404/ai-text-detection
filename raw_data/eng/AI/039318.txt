Multi-view clustering, which leverages diverse data representations to improve clustering performance, is a critical challenge in data analysis. This paper addresses the problem of inconsistent graph embeddings across multiple views, which often leads to suboptimal clustering results. We propose a novel framework, Consistent Multiple Graph Embedding (CMGE), designed to unify graph embeddings from different views while preserving their intrinsic structures. Our approach integrates a shared embedding space with view-specific constraints, ensuring consistency across views without sacrificing their unique characteristics. The framework employs an iterative optimization algorithm to jointly learn the embeddings and clustering assignments, enhancing both accuracy and robustness. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that CMGE outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of clustering accuracy and stability. The proposed method also exhibits superior scalability and adaptability to varying data complexities. This work contributes a significant advancement in multi-view clustering by providing a unified, consistent embedding framework with broad applicability in areas such as social network analysis, bioinformatics, and recommendation systems. Keywords: multi-view clustering, graph embedding, consistency, optimization, data integration.
