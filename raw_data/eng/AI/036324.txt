Cross-person gaze estimation aims to predict a new subject's gaze direction using knowledge learned from other individuals. However, significant variations in eye appearance and gaze behavior across different people pose a considerable challenge. This paper introduces a novel individual-difference-aware model to address this challenge. Our approach incorporates a personalized feature extraction module that learns individual-specific characteristics from limited subject-specific calibration data. Furthermore, we propose a novel adaptive fusion strategy to dynamically integrate information from both generic gaze patterns and personalized features, effectively capturing individual gaze nuances. We evaluated our model on several publicly available datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in gaze estimation accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods. The results highlight the importance of considering individual differences in gaze estimation and the effectiveness of our proposed model in achieving accurate and robust cross-person gaze prediction. Keywords: Gaze Estimation, Cross-person, Individual Differences, Personalized Learning, Computer Vision.
