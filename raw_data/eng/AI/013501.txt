This paper addresses the challenge of learning effective distance metrics for large-scale online data in dynamic environments, where data distributions evolve over time. Traditional deep metric learning methods often struggle to adapt to such scenarios due to their reliance on static datasets and batch-based training. To overcome this limitation, we propose *Online Progressive Deep Metric Learning* (OPDML), a novel framework that incrementally updates the metric model in an online manner while preserving previously learned knowledge. Our approach leverages a progressive learning mechanism to adaptively refine the embedding space, ensuring robustness to concept drift and scalability to streaming data. We introduce a dynamic triplet sampling strategy and a memory-efficient loss function to optimize the model's performance in real-time. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that OPDML outperforms existing online and offline metric learning methods in terms of accuracy, adaptability, and computational efficiency. The proposed framework achieves significant improvements in tasks such as image retrieval and clustering, particularly in scenarios with non-stationary data distributions. This work contributes a scalable and adaptive solution for real-world applications requiring continuous learning, such as recommendation systems and personalized search engines. Keywords: deep metric learning, online learning, progressive learning, concept drift, embedding space, triplet loss.
