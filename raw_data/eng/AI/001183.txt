Continual learning aims to enable machine learning models to acquire new knowledge over time without forgetting previously learned tasks, a challenge known as catastrophic forgetting. This paper introduces a novel rectification-based approach to enhance knowledge retention in continual learning scenarios. The proposed method leverages a rectification mechanism that dynamically adjusts the model's parameters during training, ensuring minimal interference with previously learned representations while effectively incorporating new information. We evaluate our approach on benchmark datasets, comparing it against state-of-the-art continual learning techniques. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in both task retention and adaptability, with our method achieving higher accuracy across sequential tasks. The rectification-based framework also exhibits robustness to varying task complexities and data distributions. This research contributes a scalable and efficient solution to continual learning, with potential applications in real-world systems requiring adaptive and lifelong learning capabilities.  

**Keywords**: continual learning, knowledge retention, catastrophic forgetting, rectification mechanism, adaptive learning, machine learning.
