Scene text detection is a critical task in computer vision, with applications ranging from document analysis to autonomous systems. This paper addresses the challenge of improving detection accuracy and robustness in complex environments by proposing a novel predictive ensemble learning framework. The objective is to enhance the performance of text detection models by leveraging the strengths of multiple base models while mitigating their individual weaknesses. Our approach integrates diverse deep learning architectures, including convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and transformer-based models, into an ensemble framework. A key innovation is the use of a meta-learner that dynamically weights the predictions of individual models based on their confidence and contextual relevance. Experiments were conducted on benchmark datasets such as ICDAR and COCO-Text, demonstrating that the proposed framework achieves state-of-the-art performance with significant improvements in precision, recall, and F1-score compared to existing methods. The results highlight the effectiveness of ensemble learning in handling diverse text appearances, occlusions, and background clutter. This research contributes to the field by providing a scalable and adaptable solution for scene text detection, with potential applications in real-time systems, augmented reality, and multilingual text recognition.  

**Keywords**: Scene text detection, ensemble learning, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, transformers, meta-learner, computer vision.
