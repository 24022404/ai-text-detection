This paper presents a novel approach to continual area sweeping using Deep R-Learning, a reinforcement learning framework designed to optimize sweeping paths in dynamic environments. The objective is to develop an autonomous sweeping system that can adapt to changing floor plans, obstacles, and priority areas. Our method utilizes a deep neural network to learn efficient sweeping policies, leveraging a reward function that balances coverage, time, and energy consumption. The results show that our Deep R-Learning approach outperforms traditional sweeping methods, achieving up to 25% reduction in sweeping time and 30% improvement in coverage rate. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a dynamic priority-based reward function and the development of a transfer learning mechanism that enables the sweeping system to adapt to new environments with minimal retraining. This study has significant implications for autonomous maintenance and cleaning systems, particularly in large-scale facilities such as warehouses, hospitals, and airports. Key keywords: Deep R-Learning, Continual Area Sweeping, Autonomous Systems, Reinforcement Learning, Adaptive Maintenance.
