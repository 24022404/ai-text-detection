Monocular depth estimation, inferring depth information from a single image, is a challenging computer vision problem. This paper introduces a novel deep multi-scale architecture designed to improve the accuracy and robustness of monocular depth estimation. Our approach utilizes a convolutional neural network with multiple encoder-decoder branches operating at different scales to capture both fine-grained details and global contextual information. Specifically, we employ skip connections between corresponding encoder and decoder layers at each scale to facilitate feature propagation and gradient flow. Furthermore, an adaptive fusion module dynamically weights the contributions of each scale based on the input image characteristics. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including KITTI and Make3D, demonstrate that our proposed architecture outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of depth estimation accuracy and visual quality. The multi-scale fusion strategy proves effective in handling complex scenes with varying object sizes and occlusions. This work contributes a novel and effective deep learning approach for monocular depth estimation, with potential applications in autonomous driving, robotics, and augmented reality. Keywords: Monocular Depth Estimation, Deep Learning, Multi-Scale Architecture, Convolutional Neural Networks, Image Understanding.
