This paper addresses the challenge of localization in aerial imagery by proposing a novel approach that leverages Grid Maps and LocGAN, a location-aware generative adversarial network. The objective is to accurately determine the location of an aerial image within a given map, which is crucial for various applications such as geographic information systems, autonomous vehicles, and surveillance. Our method utilizes a grid-based representation of the map, which is then used to train LocGAN to generate location-specific features from aerial images. The results show that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of localization accuracy, with a significant reduction in error rates. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of Grid Maps for aerial image localization and the development of LocGAN, which enables the generation of location-specific features. This work has important implications for the development of more accurate and efficient localization systems, with potential applications in fields such as computer vision, robotics, and geospatial analysis. Key keywords: aerial imagery, localization, Grid Maps, LocGAN, generative adversarial networks, computer vision, geographic information systems.
