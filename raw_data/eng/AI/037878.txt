This paper introduces a novel image-based generator architecture designed for synthetic image refinement, addressing the challenge of generating high-quality, realistic images from synthetic inputs. The proposed architecture leverages a combination of deep learning techniques, including convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and generative adversarial networks (GANs), to enhance the visual fidelity of synthetic images. The model is trained on a diverse dataset of synthetic and real-world images, enabling it to learn intricate patterns and textures for effective refinement. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in image quality, with the refined images exhibiting enhanced realism and reduced artifacts compared to state-of-the-art methods. The architecture also achieves superior performance in downstream tasks such as object recognition and segmentation. This work contributes to the field of computer vision by providing a robust framework for synthetic image refinement, with potential applications in augmented reality, gaming, and autonomous systems. Key innovations include a multi-scale refinement approach and a novel loss function tailored for preserving fine details. Keywords: synthetic image refinement, generative adversarial networks, convolutional neural networks, image quality enhancement, computer vision.
