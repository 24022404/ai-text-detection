Spatiotemporal Graph Neural Networks (STGNNs) have emerged as powerful tools for modeling complex spatiotemporal data, with applications ranging from traffic prediction to climate modeling. However, their robustness against adversarial attacks remains a critical concern. This paper introduces a novel spatially focused attack framework designed to exploit vulnerabilities in STGNNs by targeting specific spatial nodes within the graph structure. The proposed method leverages gradient-based optimization to craft adversarial perturbations that maximize prediction errors while minimizing detectability. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of the attack, achieving significant degradation in model performance with minimal perturbation budgets. Furthermore, the study reveals that spatially localized attacks are more effective than traditional global perturbation strategies, highlighting the importance of spatial awareness in adversarial robustness. The findings underscore the need for developing defense mechanisms tailored to the unique characteristics of STGNNs, offering valuable insights for enhancing the security and reliability of spatiotemporal learning systems.  

**Keywords**: Spatiotemporal Graph Neural Networks, Adversarial Attacks, Spatial Focus, Robustness, Gradient-based Optimization.
