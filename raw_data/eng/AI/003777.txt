Multimodal fusion, the integration of information from multiple data modalities, is a critical challenge in artificial intelligence, particularly for tasks such as image-text understanding and video analysis. This paper introduces *Attention Bottlenecks*, a novel approach designed to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of multimodal fusion by strategically constraining the flow of information between modalities. The proposed method leverages attention mechanisms to selectively focus on the most relevant features from each modality, reducing redundancy and computational overhead. We evaluate our approach on benchmark datasets for multimodal tasks, including image-text retrieval and video captioning, and demonstrate significant improvements in both accuracy and computational efficiency compared to existing fusion techniques. The results highlight the potential of Attention Bottlenecks to streamline multimodal learning while maintaining or even enhancing performance. This work contributes a scalable and interpretable framework for multimodal fusion, with implications for applications in natural language processing, computer vision, and beyond.  

**Keywords**: multimodal fusion, attention mechanisms, deep learning, image-text understanding, video analysis, computational efficiency.
