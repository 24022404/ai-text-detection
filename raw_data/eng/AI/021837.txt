The rapid growth of 3D data in applications such as autonomous driving, robotics, and augmented reality has highlighted the need for efficient and accurate 3D data processing techniques. This paper addresses the challenge of learning meaningful representations of 3D point clouds, which are often unstructured and sparse, by proposing a novel point embedding framework. Our approach leverages deep learning to encode spatial and geometric features of 3D points into a compact, high-dimensional embedding space. We introduce a hierarchical architecture that combines local and global context to capture fine-grained details while maintaining structural integrity. The proposed method is evaluated on benchmark datasets for tasks such as 3D object classification, segmentation, and reconstruction. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in accuracy and computational efficiency compared to existing methods, achieving state-of-the-art performance. The framework’s ability to generalize across diverse 3D datasets underscores its robustness and scalability. This research contributes to advancing 3D data processing by providing a versatile and effective solution for learning point embeddings, with potential applications in real-time 3D scene understanding and interactive systems.  

**Keywords**: 3D point clouds, point embedding, deep learning, 3D object classification, 3D segmentation, 3D reconstruction.
