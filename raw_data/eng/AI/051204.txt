This paper addresses the challenge of globally editing images using textual descriptions, a task that requires precise alignment between visual content and natural language instructions. We propose a novel deep learning framework that integrates advanced vision-language models to enable high-quality, semantically consistent image edits based on user-provided text. Our approach leverages a transformer-based architecture to encode both image and text inputs, followed by a cross-modal attention mechanism to ensure accurate feature alignment. The model is trained on a large-scale dataset of image-text pairs, enabling it to generalize across diverse editing scenarios. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing techniques in terms of edit quality, semantic coherence, and user satisfaction. Notably, the framework achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets, showcasing its ability to handle complex editing tasks such as object replacement, style transfer, and scene modification. The proposed system has significant implications for applications in creative design, content generation, and automated media editing, offering a powerful tool for users to manipulate images with natural language inputs. Keywords: image editing, vision-language models, transformer, cross-modal attention, semantic alignment.
