This paper presents a novel approach to transforming and projecting images into class-conditional generative networks, aiming to enhance the quality and diversity of generated images. Our objective is to develop a framework that can effectively learn and represent complex class-conditional distributions, enabling the generation of high-fidelity images that match specific classes or conditions. We propose a new method that combines the strengths of generative adversarial networks (GANs) and variational autoencoders (VAEs), incorporating a transformation module to project images into a latent space that is conditioned on the desired class or attribute. Our approach is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in image generation quality and diversity compared to existing methods. The results show that our model can generate highly realistic images that are conditioned on specific classes, with applications in image synthesis, data augmentation, and computer vision. Key contributions of this work include the introduction of a novel transformation module, the development of a class-conditional generative network, and the demonstration of state-of-the-art performance on image generation tasks. Relevant keywords: class-conditional generative networks, image transformation, generative adversarial networks, variational autoencoders, image synthesis, data augmentation.
