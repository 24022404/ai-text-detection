The paper addresses the challenge of arbitrary style transfer in image processing, focusing on incorporating depth information to improve visual coherence and realism. The research question revolves around how depth-awareness can enhance style transfer techniques, enabling more accurate and aesthetically pleasing results.

Methods: We propose a novel framework that integrates instance normalization with depth maps to achieve depth-aware style transfer. The approach utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture with a dual-stream design, where one stream processes the content and style, while the other integrates depth features. This integration allows for dynamic adjustment of stylization effects based on perceived spatial layout.

Results: Our method demonstrates significant improvements in capturing geometric consistency and preserving depth cues compared to traditional style transfer models. Quantitative evaluations indicate enhanced performance in visual quality as measured by standard metrics, while qualitative results show increased satisfaction in maintaining the depth of field in stylized outputs. Comparisons with existing techniques reveal that our approach offers superior stylization without compromising the original content's depth information.

Conclusion: The research introduces a depth-aware model that significantly advances the field of style transfer by leveraging instance normalization with depth maps. This contribution offers new possibilities for applications in virtual reality, digital art, and augmented reality, where maintaining depth perception is crucial. The proposed method sets a precedent for future studies to explore the incorporation of spatial characteristics in image manipulation tasks, highlighting the synergy between depth information and style transfer.

Keywords: style transfer, depth-aware, instance normalization, image processing, convolutional neural network, visual coherence, spatial layout.
