Abstract:

The rapid adoption of machine learning models across diverse domains has amplified the need for transparency, accountability, and responsible deployment. This paper introduces the concept of "Model Cards," a standardized reporting framework designed to document essential information about machine learning models. The primary objective is to enhance model interpretability and facilitate informed usage by systematically presenting details such as intended use cases, performance metrics, ethical considerations, and potential limitations. We propose a structured template for model cards, encompassing sections on training data sources, evaluation methodologies, and known biases. The approach was validated through the creation and analysis of model cards for a range of real-world AI models, with feedback collected from practitioners, developers, and stakeholders. Results demonstrate that model cards improve clarity in communicating model characteristics, assist stakeholders in risk assessment, and aid compliance with regulatory requirements. The findings suggest that adoption of model cards can bridge gaps between model developers and users by establishing a common language for evaluating and discussing model behavior. In conclusion, model cards offer a practical, scalable solution to support transparent AI development and deployment, with implications for ethical AI, explainable AI, and fairness in machine learning. 

Keywords: model cards, transparency, model documentation, responsible AI, machine learning, model reporting, explainable AI, ethics.
