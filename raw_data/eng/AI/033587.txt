Predicting genetic inheritance patterns is crucial for understanding and managing inherited diseases. However, the "black box" nature of many machine learning models limits their utility in providing actionable insights for genetic counselors and researchers. This paper introduces a novel approach for predicting inheritance patterns using a genetic algorithm coupled with explainable AI (XAI) techniques. Our method combines feature selection driven by genetic algorithms with a decision-tree based classifier to predict inheritance patterns from genotype data. The use of decision trees enables inherent interpretability, further enhanced by applying SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) values to quantify the contribution of each genetic marker to the prediction. Evaluation on benchmark datasets demonstrates that our approach achieves competitive accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods, while simultaneously providing clear explanations of the factors driving each prediction. This enhanced interpretability offers valuable insights into the underlying genetic mechanisms and facilitates informed decision-making in clinical genetics. Key words: genetic inheritance, prediction, explainable AI, genetic algorithm, decision trees, SHAP values.
