Sequence labeling is a fundamental task in natural language processing (NLP), with applications ranging from named entity recognition to part-of-speech tagging. Traditional approaches often struggle to capture long-range dependencies and complex patterns in sequential data. This paper introduces *Embedded-State Latent Conditional Random Fields (ES-LCRFs)*, a novel framework designed to address these challenges by integrating latent variables within a conditional random field (CRF) architecture. The proposed model leverages embedded states to encode hidden structural information, enabling more robust and context-aware sequence predictions. We evaluate ES-LCRFs on multiple benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in accuracy and F1 scores compared to state-of-the-art methods. The results highlight the model's ability to handle intricate dependencies and its scalability across diverse sequence labeling tasks. This research contributes a scalable and efficient solution for sequence labeling, with potential applications in NLP, bioinformatics, and beyond. Key innovations include the integration of latent states with CRFs and a computationally efficient training algorithm. Keywords: sequence labeling, conditional random fields, latent variables, natural language processing, embedded states.
