This paper addresses the problem of policy discrepancy in deep reinforcement learning, specifically focusing on return-based Deep Q-Networks (DQN). The objective is to develop a qualitative measurement framework to assess and compare the discrepancies between different policies. To achieve this, we employ a novel approach that combines return-based metrics with statistical analysis techniques. Our method involves training multiple DQN agents with varying hyperparameters and evaluating their policies using a set of carefully designed metrics. The results show that our framework can effectively identify and quantify policy discrepancies, providing valuable insights into the decision-making processes of DQN agents. Key findings include the identification of significant discrepancies in policy behavior, even among agents with similar performance metrics. The conclusions drawn from this research contribute to the understanding of policy discrepancy in deep reinforcement learning and have implications for the development of more robust and reliable DQN-based systems. The study highlights the importance of qualitative measurements in evaluating policy discrepancy and demonstrates the potential of return-based metrics in improving the transparency and interpretability of DQN agents. Keywords: Deep Q-Network, policy discrepancy, return-based metrics, reinforcement learning, qualitative measurements.
