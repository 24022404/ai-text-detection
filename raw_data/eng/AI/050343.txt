This paper introduces Hyperbolic Manifold Regression, a novel approach to modeling complex, non-Euclidean data. The objective is to develop a regression framework that effectively captures the intrinsic geometry of hyperbolic spaces, commonly found in social networks, biological systems, and other complex domains. Our method leverages the properties of hyperbolic manifolds to learn embeddings that preserve the hierarchical and geometric structure of the data. We propose a new algorithm that combines the strengths of hyperbolic geometry and deep learning, enabling accurate predictions and robust representations. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our approach, outperforming traditional Euclidean-based methods in various tasks, including link prediction and node classification. The key contributions of this work include the development of a hyperbolic manifold regression framework, the introduction of a new loss function tailored to hyperbolic geometry, and the demonstration of its potential in real-world applications. Our research has significant implications for the analysis and modeling of complex systems, highlighting the importance of geometric deep learning and hyperbolic representations in AI. Key keywords: hyperbolic manifold, regression, geometric deep learning, non-Euclidean data, complex systems.
