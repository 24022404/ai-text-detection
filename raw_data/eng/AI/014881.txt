Autosomal Dominant Polycystic Kidney Disease (ADPKD) is characterized by progressive cyst development, leading to enlarged Total Kidney Volume (TKV). Accurate TKV computation from CT images is crucial for disease monitoring and treatment planning. This paper presents a novel multi-task 3D Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) approach for automated TKV computation. The network performs simultaneous kidney segmentation and cyst segmentation from CT volumes. A shared encoder extracts features relevant to both tasks, while separate decoders generate kidney and cyst masks. The TKV is then calculated from the kidney segmentation mask. Evaluated on a dataset of ADPKD patient CT scans, our method achieves high accuracy in both kidney and cyst segmentation, outperforming single-task CNNs and demonstrating improved robustness to variations in cyst morphology and image quality. The multi-task learning paradigm improves segmentation performance and reduces the need for large training datasets. This automated TKV computation method holds significant potential for clinical applications, enabling efficient and reliable monitoring of ADPKD progression. Keywords: ADPKD, Total Kidney Volume, TKV, 3D CNN, Multi-Task Learning, Kidney Segmentation, Cyst Segmentation, CT Imaging.
