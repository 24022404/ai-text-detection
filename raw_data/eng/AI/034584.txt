This paper introduces a novel actor-critic approach, leveraging masked model-based reinforcement learning to enhance trust in model predictions when confidence is high. Our objective is to improve the efficiency and reliability of decision-making in complex environments by developing an algorithm that adaptively weighs model-based and model-free learning. We employ a masked model that selectively updates its parameters based on confidence estimates, allowing the actor-critic framework to focus on high-confidence predictions. Our approach combines the strengths of model-based and model-free reinforcement learning, enabling more accurate and efficient learning. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art actor-critic algorithms in various environments, showcasing improved sample efficiency and reduced variance. The key contributions of this research include the development of a confidence-aware masked model and its integration with actor-critic methods, highlighting the potential for more robust and reliable decision-making in autonomous systems. Keywords: reinforcement learning, actor-critic, model-based learning, confidence estimation, masked models.
