This paper introduces IAN, a novel approach to imaginative face generation by combining generative adversarial networks (GANs). The objective is to create a system capable of generating highly realistic and diverse facial images. To achieve this, we propose a multi-stage framework that leverages the strengths of both conditional and unconditional GANs. Our method involves training a conditional GAN to learn the structure of faces, followed by an unconditional GAN to introduce randomness and creativity. The results show that IAN outperforms existing face generation models in terms of image quality and diversity. Our approach achieves a state-of-the-art Frechet Inception Distance (FID) score, demonstrating its effectiveness in generating realistic faces. The implications of this research are significant, with potential applications in fields such as computer vision, robotics, and digital entertainment. Key contributions include the development of a novel GAN architecture and the demonstration of its ability to generate highly realistic and imaginative facial images. Relevant keywords: generative adversarial networks, face generation, imaginative faces, conditional GANs, unconditional GANs, computer vision, deep learning.
