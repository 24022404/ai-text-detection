To avoid extended intervals without a pulse, positioning sequences are merged with run length limited (RLL) constraints, forming Run length limited de Bruijn (RdB) sequences, as detailed in chapter 3. These specialized sequences are suitable for the dBTS system and achieve greater rates compared to HdB sequences. The rate for the longest RdB sequences can be expressed as log2.
