This research addresses the challenge of enhancing representation learning within the domain of reinforcement learning (RL), focusing on improving the efficiency and accuracy with which agents learn from their environments. Traditional approaches often struggle with efficiently extracting relevant features, limiting overall performance.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel Masked Contrastive Representation Learning framework tailored for RL applications. This framework leverages contrastive learning techniques by masking portions of the input data to enforce the learning of robust and invariant features. The proposed method integrates seamlessly with existing RL algorithms, enhancing their ability to generalize from limited observations through improved feature sensitivity.

Results/Findings: The implementation of our approach on various benchmark RL environments demonstrates a significant improvement in learning speed and effectiveness compared to conventional methods. Our model consistently outperforms standard baselines in both sample complexity and final policy performance, showcasing its ability to distill pertinent features and improve exploration strategies.

Conclusion/Implications: The Masked Contrastive Representation Learning framework provides a powerful tool for advancing the capabilities of RL systems. By promoting the acquisition of high-quality feature representations, our method not only enhances existing algorithms but also opens up new possibilities for applying RL in more complex and dynamic environments. Future work will explore scaling this approach to more diverse scenarios and integrating it with other representation learning techniques.

Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Contrastive Learning, Representation Learning, Masking, Sample Complexity, Feature Sensitivity, Policy Performance.
