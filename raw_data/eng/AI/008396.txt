Deep learning models for image processing tasks often require extensive and diverse datasets to achieve robust performance. However, acquiring such datasets can be costly and time-consuming. This paper introduces *Adversarial Policy Gradient (APG)*, a novel approach for automated image augmentation that enhances the quality and diversity of training data for deep learning models. The proposed method leverages reinforcement learning to dynamically generate augmentation policies tailored to the specific characteristics of the dataset and task. By employing an adversarial framework, APG optimizes augmentation strategies to maximize model performance while minimizing overfitting. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets, including CIFAR-10 and ImageNet, demonstrate that APG outperforms traditional augmentation techniques and state-of-the-art automated methods in terms of classification accuracy and generalization. The results highlight the effectiveness of APG in improving model robustness and reducing the need for extensive manual data curation. This work contributes a scalable and adaptive solution for image augmentation, with potential applications in computer vision, medical imaging, and other domains reliant on deep learning.  

**Keywords**: Deep learning, image augmentation, adversarial learning, reinforcement learning, policy gradient, computer vision.
