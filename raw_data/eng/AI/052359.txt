**Abstract**

Keyword spotting (KWS) is a critical task in speech recognition, enabling the detection of predefined words or phrases in audio streams with high efficiency and accuracy. Recent advances have leveraged multi-head attention mechanisms to enhance model representation, yet redundancy among attention heads often limits their discriminative power. This paper introduces a novel approach—Orthogonality Constrained Multi-Head Attention (OC-MHA)—designed to improve keyword spotting performance by explicitly promoting diversity among attention heads via orthogonality constraints. Our OC-MHA framework is integrated into a lightweight convolutional neural network, ensuring both computational efficiency and robustness. We evaluate our method on widely-used KWS benchmarks, demonstrating that OC-MHA consistently outperforms standard and state-of-the-art multi-head attention models in terms of accuracy, false acceptance rate, and model compactness. Moreover, ablation studies confirm the effectiveness of the orthogonality constraint in reducing redundancy and enhancing feature diversity. These findings highlight the potential of orthogonality-based regularization in attention mechanisms for robust and efficient KWS systems. Key contributions of this work include the formulation of orthogonality constraints for multi-head attention, an efficient model architecture suitable for low-latency scenarios, and thorough empirical validation. Keywords: keyword spotting, multi-head attention, orthogonality constraint, speech recognition, deep learning.
