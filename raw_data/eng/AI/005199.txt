This paper addresses the challenge of color constancy in digital images, where the goal is to remove unwanted color casts caused by varying lighting conditions. Our objective is to develop a novel approach to achieve accurate color constancy, enabling images to appear more natural and authentic. We propose a cascading convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture that learns to estimate the color illumination of a scene and corrects the image accordingly. Our method leverages the strengths of deep learning and computer vision to improve upon traditional color constancy algorithms. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving superior color accuracy and robustness across diverse datasets. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a cascading CNN framework, which enables efficient and effective color constancy estimation, and the demonstration of its potential applications in image processing, computer vision, and multimedia analysis. Our work has significant implications for fields such as photography, surveillance, and autonomous vehicles, where color constancy is crucial for accurate image interpretation. Key keywords: color constancy, convolutional neural networks, computer vision, image processing, deep learning.
