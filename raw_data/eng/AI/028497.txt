The growing demand for personalized shopping experiences necessitates innovative approaches to image retrieval systems that incorporate natural language feedback. This paper presents a novel conversational fashion image retrieval technique that leverages multiturn natural language interactions to refine and improve search results in real-time.

Methods/Approach: Our approach integrates advanced natural language processing and computer vision technologies to create a seamless dialog-based retrieval system. Using a refined neural network architecture, specifically designed for multimodal understanding, the system processes sequential user feedback to iteratively enhance the relevance of fashion image search results. Key components include a transformer-based language model to interpret complex queries and a convolutional neural network to analyze image features.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate significant improvements in retrieval accuracy and user satisfaction when compared to traditional single-turn query systems. The proposed system shows robust performance in handling intricate and varied natural language inputs, thereby delivering personalized and contextually relevant image suggestions. Notably, the multiturn feedback mechanism enhances the system’s adaptability and precision, showcasing up to a 20% increase in retrieval accuracy.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a groundbreaking method for fashion image retrieval, underscoring the potential of conversational AI in e-commerce platforms. By promoting a dynamic interaction model, this system ensures a tailored shopping experience, paving the way for future developments in personalized digital commerce. Key applications include virtual personal shoppers and interactive marketing tools that can capitalize on real-time user feedback to drive engagement.

Keywords: conversational AI, fashion image retrieval, multiturn feedback, natural language processing, neural networks, personalized shopping.
