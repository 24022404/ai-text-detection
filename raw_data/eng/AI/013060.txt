Understanding the internal decision-making process of deep neural networks (DNNs) remains a paramount challenge within artificial intelligence and machine learning. This study aims to introduce an adversarial approach designed to explain and interpret the predictions made by DNNs, thereby enhancing transparency and trust in AI models.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel adversarial framework that systematically generates perturbations in input data to reveal the decision boundaries of deep neural networks. This adversarial approach utilizes a combination of perturbation analysis and saliency mapping techniques to visualize the critical features influencing model predictions. The methodology is evaluated across multiple neural network architectures and diverse datasets to ensure robustness and generalizability.

Results/Findings: Our experimental results demonstrate that the proposed adversarial approach effectively identifies and highlights features that are pivotal for model predictions. Compared to existing interpretability techniques, our method achieves superior precision in feature attribution and offers insightful explanations with minimal computational overhead. Significant improvements are observed in model transparency, as evidenced by comparative analysis with traditional explanation tools such as LIME and SHAP.

Conclusion/Implications: The research provides an innovative avenue for interpreting the predictions of DNNs by employing adversarial techniques, contributing to the growing field of explainable artificial intelligence (XAI). This approach has potential applications in critical areas such as healthcare and autonomous systems, where understanding model decisions is crucial. The findings propel the discussion on adversarial learning's role in enhancing model interpretability and present opportunities for future research in refining and extending these methods.

Keywords: Deep Neural Networks, Adversarial Approach, Explainable AI, Model Interpretability, Feature Attribution, Saliency Mapping.
