This paper addresses the challenge of effectively learning on hypergraphs, which extend traditional graph representations by enabling edges to connect multiple nodes. Hypergraphs find application in complex data structures such as social networks, biological systems, and collaborative filtering. The research proposes a novel method, termed Line Expansion, to improve the performance and efficiency of hypergraph learning algorithms.

Methods/Approach: We introduce Line Expansion, a technique that transforms hypergraphs into a simpler, more manageable form, facilitating the use of standard graph learning algorithms. This transformation maintains the original hypergraph's connectivity and relational properties while allowing for the application of advanced learning techniques such as graph neural networks and spectral graph theory. The methodology involves representing hyperedges as new auxiliary nodes, thereby converting the hypergraph into a standard graph with enhanced analytical capabilities.

Results/Findings: The empirical evaluation of our Line Expansion method demonstrates significant improvements in learning accuracy and computational efficiency across multiple benchmark datasets. Our approach consistently outperforms existing hypergraph learning models, particularly in terms of scalability and robustness to varying hyperedge cardinalities. Comparative analysis reveals that Line Expansion enhances the capability to capture complex dependencies in hypergraph-structured data, leading to more accurate predictions and insights.

Conclusion/Implications: The proposed Line Expansion method offers a groundbreaking approach to hypergraph learning, presenting a path for more effective application of graph-based algorithms on complex datasets. This advancement not only broadens the scope of hypergraph analysis but also simplifies the integration of hypergraph models with state-of-the-art machine learning frameworks. The implications of this study are vast, suggesting potential applications across various domains that utilize hypergraph structures, including social network analysis, bioinformatics, and recommendation systems.

Keywords: hypergraph learning, Line Expansion, graph transformation, graph neural networks, scalability, complex datasets.
