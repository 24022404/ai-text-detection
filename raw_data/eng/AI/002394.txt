Monocular Visual-Inertial Odometry (VIO) is a critical technology for enabling autonomous navigation in resource-constrained environments. However, achieving accurate and robust performance requires precise initialization and calibration of the sensor system, particularly in online scenarios where real-time operation is essential. This paper addresses the challenge of online initialization and extrinsic spatial-temporal calibration for monocular VIO systems. We propose a novel framework that integrates visual and inertial data to simultaneously estimate the initial state, including scale, gravity, and velocity, while calibrating the extrinsic parameters and time offsets between the camera and inertial measurement unit (IMU). Our approach leverages a sliding-window optimization technique to ensure computational efficiency and real-time applicability. Experimental results on public datasets demonstrate that the proposed method achieves superior accuracy in both initialization and calibration compared to existing state-of-the-art techniques. The framework is robust to varying motion dynamics and sensor noise, making it suitable for practical applications in robotics and augmented reality. This work contributes to advancing the field of VIO by providing a reliable and efficient solution for online initialization and calibration, paving the way for improved performance in real-world deployments.  

**Keywords**: Monocular Visual-Inertial Odometry, Online Initialization, Extrinsic Calibration, Spatial-Temporal Calibration, Sensor Fusion, Real-Time Optimization.
