This paper presents a novel approach to generating bokeh effects in images by leveraging depth-aware blending of smoothed images. The objective is to create a realistic and aesthetically pleasing bokeh effect, simulating the shallow depth of field achieved by high-end cameras. Our method employs a combination of image smoothing techniques and depth mapping to identify regions of interest and apply the bokeh effect accordingly. The approach utilizes a depth-aware algorithm to blend the smoothed images, ensuring a seamless transition between in-focus and out-of-focus areas. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our technique, producing high-quality bokeh effects that rival those captured by professional cameras. The proposed method contributes to the field of image processing and computer vision, offering a unique solution for bokeh effect generation. Key applications include photography, videography, and virtual reality, where realistic depth cues are essential. Relevant keywords: bokeh effect, depth-aware blending, image smoothing, depth mapping, computer vision, image processing.
