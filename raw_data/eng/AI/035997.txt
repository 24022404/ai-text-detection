This paper presents a novel approach to image deblurring and super-resolution, leveraging adaptive sparse domain selection and adaptive regularization techniques. The objective is to effectively restore blurred and low-resolution images by identifying the most suitable sparse domains and adaptively adjusting regularization parameters. Our method employs a hybrid approach, combining the strengths of both model-based and learning-based techniques. We utilize a sparse representation model to select the most relevant domains for image restoration and incorporate an adaptive regularization scheme to optimize the restoration process. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of image quality and restoration accuracy. The proposed technique achieves significant improvements in peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM) compared to existing methods. Our research contributes to the development of advanced image restoration techniques, with potential applications in computer vision, image processing, and multimedia analysis. Key contributions include the introduction of adaptive sparse domain selection and adaptive regularization, enabling more effective and efficient image deblurring and super-resolution. Relevant keywords: image deblurring, super-resolution, sparse domain selection, adaptive regularization, image restoration, computer vision.
