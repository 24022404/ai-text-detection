This paper addresses the challenge of large-scale place recognition using 3D point clouds, a critical task for autonomous navigation and robotics. We introduce PointNetVLAD, a novel deep learning architecture that combines PointNet for efficient point cloud feature extraction with VLAD (Vector of Locally Aggregated Descriptors) for compact and discriminative global descriptor generation. Our approach directly processes raw 3D point clouds, eliminating the need for hand-crafted features or voxelization. PointNetVLAD is trained end-to-end to learn robust and viewpoint-invariant descriptors. We evaluate PointNetVLAD on several large-scale datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in retrieval accuracy and robustness compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. Specifically, our method achieves superior performance in challenging environments with significant viewpoint changes and occlusions. The generated compact global descriptors enable efficient and scalable place recognition for large environments. The results highlight the effectiveness of the proposed architecture for point cloud based place recognition, offering a promising solution for robust localization in autonomous systems.

**Keywords:** Place Recognition, Point Cloud, Deep Learning, PointNet, VLAD, Autonomous Navigation, Robotics.
