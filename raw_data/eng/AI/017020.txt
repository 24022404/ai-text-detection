This paper introduces a novel point embedding method for enhanced 3D data processing. Addressing the limitations of existing techniques in capturing intricate geometric details and relationships, our approach learns a compact and informative representation for each point in a 3D point cloud. We propose a graph-based neural network architecture that aggregates local contextual information and encodes it into a low-dimensional embedding space. This learned embedding preserves geometric proximity and semantic similarity between points. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our method on several 3D data processing tasks, including point cloud classification, segmentation, and registration. Experimental results show significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency compared to state-of-the-art methods. Specifically, our method achieves a 5% increase in segmentation accuracy on the ShapeNet dataset and a 10% reduction in registration error on the ModelNet40 dataset. The proposed point embedding offers a powerful and versatile tool for various 3D applications, enabling more robust and accurate data analysis. Keywords: point cloud, 3D data processing, point embedding, graph neural networks, deep learning, segmentation, classification, registration.
