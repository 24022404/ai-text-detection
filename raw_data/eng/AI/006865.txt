This paper explores the integration of sensing and communication capabilities in cellular networks using NR Sidelink technology. The objective is to enhance the efficiency and reliability of cellular networks by leveraging the sensing capabilities of user equipment to improve communication performance. We propose a novel approach that utilizes NR Sidelink to enable simultaneous sensing and communication, allowing for more accurate channel estimation and improved network throughput. Our method employs advanced signal processing techniques and machine learning algorithms to optimize sensing and communication parameters. Simulation results demonstrate significant improvements in network performance, including increased throughput and reduced latency. The integration of sensing and communication via NR Sidelink has important implications for future cellular networks, enabling new use cases such as intelligent transportation systems and smart cities. Key contributions of this research include the development of a novel NR Sidelink-based sensing and communication framework, and the demonstration of its potential to enhance cellular network performance. Relevant keywords: NR Sidelink, cellular networks, sensing and communication, machine learning, 5G, IoT.
