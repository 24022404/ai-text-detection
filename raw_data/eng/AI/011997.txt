This paper presents a novel approach to unsupervised lifelong learning, leveraging deep Bayesian techniques to enable autonomous systems to learn continuously from streaming data without requiring manual intervention or labeled examples. Our method combines the strengths of Bayesian neural networks and lifelong learning, allowing models to adapt and improve over time while retaining knowledge from previous experiences. The approach utilizes a hierarchical Bayesian framework to model uncertainty and accommodate new tasks and data distributions. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method in various scenarios, including image classification and regression tasks, outperforming existing state-of-the-art lifelong learning algorithms. The key contributions of this research include the development of a scalable and efficient deep Bayesian lifelong learning framework, the introduction of a novel uncertainty-based mechanism for task inference, and the demonstration of improved performance and knowledge retention in unsupervised settings. This work has significant implications for real-world applications, such as autonomous robots, self-driving cars, and intelligent IoT systems, where continuous learning and adaptation are crucial. Key keywords: deep learning, Bayesian neural networks, unsupervised learning, lifelong learning, autonomous systems, uncertainty modeling.
