Environmental Sound Classification (ESC) plays a crucial role in various applications, including urban monitoring and context-aware computing. This paper introduces ESResNet, a novel approach that leverages pre-trained visual domain models, specifically the ResNet architecture, for enhanced ESC performance. By transforming environmental sound data into spectrogram images, we exploit the robust feature extraction capabilities of models pre-trained on large image datasets. Our method involves fine-tuning these models using environmental sound spectrograms, effectively transferring knowledge from the visual domain to the audio domain. Experimental results demonstrate that ESResNet achieves state-of-the-art classification accuracy on benchmark ESC datasets, surpassing existing audio-specific models. Furthermore, we analyze the impact of different pre-training datasets and fine-tuning strategies on classification performance. The findings highlight the effectiveness of transfer learning for ESC and offer insights into optimal model configurations. This research contributes a novel methodology for ESC, demonstrating the viability of visual domain models for audio classification tasks and providing a pathway for further advancements in environmental sound analysis.
Keywords: Environmental Sound Classification, Transfer Learning, Spectrogram, ResNet, Deep Learning, Audio Analysis.
