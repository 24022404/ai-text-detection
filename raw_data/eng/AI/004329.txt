This paper introduces FCA2VEC, a novel approach to embedding formal concept analysis (FCA) into vector spaces, enabling the application of machine learning and data analysis techniques to FCA structures. The objective is to provide a robust method for representing complex relationships and hierarchies inherent in formal contexts. Our approach utilizes a combination of graph embedding and word2vec techniques to map formal concepts to dense vector representations. The results show that FCA2VEC outperforms existing methods in terms of concept similarity measurement and clustering quality. Key findings indicate that the embedded vectors capture essential semantic relationships between concepts, facilitating improved knowledge discovery and visualization. The contributions of this research lie in its ability to bridge the gap between FCA and machine learning, enabling the application of advanced analytics to formal concept analysis. This work has significant implications for fields such as data mining, knowledge representation, and artificial intelligence, particularly in areas involving complex data analysis and decision-making. Relevant keywords include: formal concept analysis, graph embedding, word2vec, vector space representation, knowledge discovery, and machine learning.
