This paper introduces GazeGAN, a novel unpaired adversarial image generation framework designed to improve gaze estimation accuracy. GazeGAN addresses the challenge of limited and biased training data by generating synthetic eye images with diverse gaze directions. Our approach utilizes two generative adversarial networks (GANs) in a cycle-consistent architecture. The first GAN translates real eye images into synthetic ones with controlled gaze, while the second GAN reconstructs the original image from the synthetic one, ensuring content preservation. This unpaired training strategy allows for leveraging readily available real eye images without corresponding gaze labels. Experiments demonstrate that training a gaze estimation model with synthetic data generated by GazeGAN significantly improves its performance, particularly in cross-dataset generalization. Specifically, GazeGAN reduces the error rate by an average of 15% compared to models trained solely on real data. The generated images exhibit realistic appearance and effectively augment existing datasets, leading to more robust and accurate gaze estimation. This work contributes a data augmentation technique for gaze estimation, enabling improved performance with limited labeled data.
Keywords: Gaze Estimation, Generative Adversarial Networks, GAN, Image Synthesis, Data Augmentation, Unpaired Learning.
