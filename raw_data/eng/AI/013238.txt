Semantic segmentation of fine-resolution remotely sensed images is critical for various applications such as urban planning, environmental monitoring, and land use analysis. However, existing methods often struggle to effectively capture both local and contextual information, leading to suboptimal performance in complex scenes. This paper proposes A2-FPN, an enhanced Feature Pyramid Network (FPN) architecture designed specifically for semantic segmentation of fine-resolution remote sensing imagery. The A2-FPN integrates adaptive attention mechanisms and advanced aggregation strategies to dynamically highlight salient features across multiple scales. Extensive experiments were conducted on benchmark remotely sensed datasets, demonstrating that A2-FPN significantly improves segmentation accuracy and boundary delineation compared to standard FPN and other state-of-the-art methods. The proposed approach also exhibits superior robustness in handling heterogeneous land cover and small object segmentation. These results suggest that A2-FPN offers a promising solution for high-precision semantic segmentation tasks in remote sensing, facilitating more reliable geospatial analysis and decision-making. Key keywords: semantic segmentation, remote sensing, fine-resolution images, deep learning, feature pyramid network, attention mechanism.
