Accurate taxi demand forecasting is critical for optimizing urban transportation systems and improving resource allocation. This paper addresses the challenge of predicting taxi demand with high precision by proposing a novel HEDGE-based tessellation strategy. The objective is to enhance forecasting accuracy by dynamically partitioning urban areas into optimal-sized grids, tailored to demand patterns. Our approach integrates the HEDGE algorithm, a multi-armed bandit technique, with spatial tessellation to adaptively adjust grid sizes based on historical demand data and real-time fluctuations. We evaluate the proposed method using a large-scale dataset from a metropolitan city, comparing its performance against traditional fixed-grid and other adaptive tessellation methods. Results demonstrate that our strategy achieves a significant improvement in forecasting accuracy, reducing prediction errors by up to 20% compared to existing approaches. The findings highlight the importance of adaptive spatial partitioning in demand forecasting and provide valuable insights for urban planners and ride-hailing platforms. This research contributes to the field by introducing a scalable and data-driven solution for dynamic urban demand prediction, with potential applications in smart city infrastructure and mobility services.  

**Keywords**: Taxi demand forecasting, HEDGE algorithm, tessellation, urban mobility, adaptive grids, predictive modeling.
