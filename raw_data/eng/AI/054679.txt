This paper addresses the challenge of open-world node classification in graph neural networks (GNNs), where new classes and nodes emerge continuously. Our objective is to develop a lifelong learning framework that enables GNNs to adapt to evolving graph structures and node labels. We propose a novel approach that combines graph attention mechanisms with incremental learning techniques, allowing the model to learn from streaming data and update its parameters without requiring access to the entire history of data. Our method, termed LifelongGNN, achieves state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its ability to balance forgetting and learning of new knowledge. The results show that LifelongGNN outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy, robustness, and efficiency. Our research contributes to the development of open-world learning systems, enabling GNNs to operate effectively in dynamic environments. Key contributions include the introduction of a graph attention-based incremental learning mechanism and the demonstration of its effectiveness in real-world scenarios. Relevant keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Lifelong Learning, Open-World Learning, Node Classification, Incremental Learning, Graph Attention Mechanisms.
