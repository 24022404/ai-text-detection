This paper presents a novel approach to medical image analysis, introducing Deep Geodesic Learning for simultaneous segmentation and anatomical landmarking. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of image processing in medical applications by leveraging the geometric properties of anatomical structures. Our method employs a deep learning framework that incorporates geodesic distances and curvature information to enhance the segmentation of complex anatomical features. The approach utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, combined with a geodesic loss function, to learn robust and informative representations of medical images. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, achieving state-of-the-art performance in segmentation and landmarking tasks on various medical image datasets. The proposed Deep Geodesic Learning framework offers significant contributions to the field, enabling accurate and automated analysis of medical images, with potential applications in computer-assisted diagnosis, surgical planning, and personalized medicine. Key aspects of this research include geodesic learning, deep learning, medical image segmentation, anatomical landmarking, and CNN architectures.
