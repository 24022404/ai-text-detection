This paper introduces RotLSTM, a novel approach to enhancing the memory management of Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) by incorporating a rotating memory mechanism. The objective is to improve the ability of RNNs to capture long-term dependencies and reduce the impact of vanishing gradients. Our method modifies the traditional Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) architecture by introducing a rotation operation that periodically updates the memory cells, allowing for more efficient use of historical information. Experimental results demonstrate that RotLSTM outperforms standard LSTM models on several benchmark tasks, including language modeling and time series forecasting. The key findings indicate that RotLSTM achieves significant improvements in terms of accuracy and stability, particularly in scenarios where long-term memory is crucial. This research contributes to the advancement of RNNs and has potential applications in natural language processing, speech recognition, and other sequence-based tasks. Key keywords: RotLSTM, Recurrent Neural Networks, LSTM, rotating memory, long-term dependencies, vanishing gradients, language modeling, time series forecasting.
