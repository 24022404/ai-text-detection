This paper introduces a novel approach for learning invariant representations by leveraging local transformations within the data. The objective is to develop a representation learning framework that is robust to variations in input data while preserving its discriminative power. We propose a method that enforces invariance by training a neural network to produce similar representations for locally transformed versions of the same input. The proposed architecture incorporates a transformation module that applies a diverse set of local transformations such as small rotations, translations, and scaling operations. We evaluate our approach on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating superior performance in terms of classification accuracy and robustness compared to existing invariance learning techniques. Results show that incorporating local transformations during training significantly improves the generalization capability of the learned representations, particularly in scenarios with limited training data. The learned invariant representations also exhibit improved transferability to related tasks. Our findings contribute to the field of representation learning by providing a practical and effective method for achieving invariance without requiring explicit knowledge of the underlying transformations. Keywords: Invariant Representations, Representation Learning, Local Transformations, Neural Networks, Generalization, Robustness.
