Video summarization aims to generate concise and representative summaries of videos, reducing redundancy and preserving key content. This paper introduces a novel meta-learning framework, Query Twice (QT), to improve the efficiency and adaptability of video summarization models across diverse video datasets. QT employs a dual mixture attention mechanism, enabling the model to attend to both global video context and fine-grained temporal dynamics. By querying the video content twice, the framework captures both the overall structure and the salient events within the video. We train QT using a meta-learning strategy that optimizes for rapid adaptation to new video datasets with limited labeled examples. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that QT achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of summary quality and generalization ability, outperforming existing methods, especially in low-data scenarios. The proposed approach offers a promising direction for developing more robust and adaptable video summarization systems.

Keywords: Video Summarization, Meta Learning, Attention Mechanism, Dual Mixture Attention, Few-Shot Learning, Machine Learning
