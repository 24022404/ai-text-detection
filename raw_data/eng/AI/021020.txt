This paper presents an innovative end-to-end Optical Character Recognition (OCR) approach designed to accurately recognize ultra-low-resolution printed text images. The primary objective is to address the challenge of extracting readable text from poor-quality images, which is a common issue in various applications such as document scanning, image processing, and text analysis. Our approach employs a deep learning-based methodology, leveraging convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) to enhance image quality and recognize text. The proposed system consists of two main stages: image enhancement and text recognition. In the image enhancement stage, a CNN-based model is used to super-resolve the input image, improving its resolution and quality. The enhanced image is then fed into an RNN-based model, which recognizes the text using a sequence-to-sequence approach. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving a significant improvement in recognition accuracy compared to existing OCR methods. The proposed system has potential applications in various fields, including document digitization, text extraction, and image processing. Key contributions of this research include the development of a robust and efficient OCR system for ultra-low-resolution images, and the introduction of a novel image enhancement technique that improves the overall performance of the system. Relevant keywords: OCR, ultra-low-resolution images, deep learning, CNN, RNN, image enhancement, text recognition.
