Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have demonstrated remarkable success in node classification tasks, yet their ability to adapt to evolving, open-world scenarios remains limited. This paper addresses the challenge of lifelong learning in GNNs, where the model must continuously integrate new classes and data without forgetting previously learned knowledge. We propose a novel framework that combines dynamic graph representation learning with memory replay mechanisms to enable efficient and scalable adaptation. Our approach leverages a hybrid architecture that balances stability and plasticity, ensuring robust performance across incremental learning phases. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate significant improvements in classification accuracy and retention of prior knowledge compared to existing methods. The proposed framework also exhibits enhanced scalability, making it suitable for real-world applications such as social network analysis and recommendation systems. This work contributes to advancing lifelong learning in graph-based machine learning, offering a practical solution for open-world node classification tasks.  

**Keywords**: Graph Neural Networks, Lifelong Learning, Open-World Learning, Node Classification, Memory Replay, Dynamic Graph Representation.
