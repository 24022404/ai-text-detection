Objective: Three-dimensional (3D) point clouds are fundamental to applications in robotics, augmented reality, and autonomous driving, yet effective representation of raw 3D data remains challenging. This paper proposes a novel point embedding framework that learns compact, informative feature vectors directly from unordered point sets, enabling improved downstream processing such as classification, segmentation, and retrieval.

Methods: We introduce PointEmbedNet, a lightweight neural architecture that integrates local geometric encoding and global context aggregation. First, local neighborhoods are encoded via shared multilayer perceptrons and spatial attention to capture fine-grained surface details. Next, a self-attention module aggregates these local descriptors into a unified global embedding. During training, a joint reconstruction and metric learning loss encourages embeddings to preserve both shape structure and inter-class distinctions.

Results: Experiments on standard benchmarks (ModelNet40 for object classification and ShapeNetPart for part segmentation) show that PointEmbedNet achieves 92.3% classification accuracy and a mean intersection-over-union (mIoU) of 85.1%, outperforming existing point-based methods by 1.5–3.2%. Ablation studies confirm the effectiveness of the attention modules and the combined loss function. Embeddings also demonstrate robustness under varying point densities and noise levels.

Conclusion: The proposed point embedding approach offers a general-purpose representation that enhances 3D data processing tasks. Its compactness and accuracy make it well suited for real-time applications in robotics and virtual reality. Keywords: point embedding, 3D data processing, point cloud, deep learning, geometric representation, attention.
