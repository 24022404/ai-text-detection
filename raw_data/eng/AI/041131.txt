The detection of small objects in complex scenes poses a significant challenge in computer vision, primarily due to their limited size and intricate background interference. This paper introduces an innovative coarse-to-fine non-local module designed to enhance the detection accuracy of small objects in various contexts.

Methods/Approach: Our proposed method leverages a novel non-local module that operates in a coarse-to-fine manner. Initially, a coarse-level non-local operation captures the contextual information over a broad spatial range, providing a foundational understanding of the scene. Subsequently, a fine-level refinement process targets specific regions, enhancing the module's sensitivity to small objects by integrating more detailed local interactions. This two-step process is seamlessly integrated into existing object detection frameworks, offering an upgrade pathway without altering the core architecture.

Results/Findings: Extensive experiments on publicly available datasets demonstrate that our module significantly improves small object detection rates compared to state-of-the-art methods. The proposed approach achieves a considerable increase in average precision across various benchmark datasets. We also present a comparative analysis showcasing the superiority of our non-local module over traditional approaches, particularly in scenarios with complex backgrounds.

Conclusion/Implications: This research highlights the effectiveness of a coarse-to-fine strategy within non-local modules for small object detection, presenting a substantial advancement in addressing a persistent challenge in the field. Our findings suggest potential applications in areas such as autonomous driving, aerial imaging, and surveillance, where precise detection of small objects is crucial. The proposed method offers a practical and efficient enhancement to current object detection systems, contributing a valuable tool for researchers and practitioners in computer vision.

Keywords: small object detection, coarse-to-fine, non-local module, computer vision, contextual information, object detection frameworks.
