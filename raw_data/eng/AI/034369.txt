This paper introduces a novel approach to incorporating domain knowledge into neural networks through a technique called Neuron-Importance. The objective is to improve the performance and interpretability of deep learning models by selectively emphasizing or de-emphasizing specific neurons based on their relevance to the task at hand. Our method utilizes a importance scoring system to identify and rank neurons according to their contribution to the model's predictions. We then use this information to inform the learning process, allowing the model to focus on the most critical neurons and adapt to new tasks more efficiently. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach leads to significant improvements in model accuracy and robustness, particularly in scenarios where domain knowledge is scarce or noisy. The key contributions of this work include a new framework for neuron-importance scoring, a domain-agnostic method for incorporating domain knowledge, and a set of experiments demonstrating the effectiveness of our approach in various applications, including image classification and natural language processing. By highlighting the importance of individual neurons, our method provides a new perspective on model interpretability and paves the way for more efficient and effective deep learning models. Key keywords: Neuron-Importance, Domain Knowledge, Neural Networks, Deep Learning, Model Interpretability.
