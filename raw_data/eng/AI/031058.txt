This paper proposes FD-GAN, a novel face-demorphing generative adversarial network designed to restore an accomplice's facial image from a morphed image. The objective is to address the challenge of identifying accomplices in criminal investigations where facial images have been intentionally altered. Our approach utilizes a deep learning-based method, combining a generator network and a discriminator network to learn the demorphing process. The FD-GAN model is trained on a large dataset of morphed and original image pairs, enabling it to effectively remove morphing artifacts and recover the original facial features. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of FD-GAN in restoring high-quality facial images, outperforming existing demorphing methods. The proposed method has significant implications for law enforcement and forensic applications, providing a powerful tool for identifying and prosecuting criminals. Key contributions of this research include the introduction of a novel GAN-based architecture for face demorphing and the achievement of state-of-the-art performance in restoring accomplice's facial images. Relevant keywords: face demorphing, generative adversarial networks, deep learning, forensic facial analysis, image restoration.
