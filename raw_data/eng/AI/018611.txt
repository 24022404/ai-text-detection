This paper explores the application of the Sugeno integral in machine learning, specifically in binary classification problems. The objective is to develop a novel approach that leverages the Sugeno integral's ability to handle uncertain and imprecise information, leading to improved classification performance. Our method utilizes a combination of machine learning algorithms and the Sugeno integral to aggregate the outputs of multiple classifiers, resulting in a more robust and accurate classification model. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming traditional machine learning methods in various benchmark datasets. The key findings highlight the potential of the Sugeno integral in enhancing the performance of binary classification models, particularly in situations where data is noisy or uncertain. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and reliable machine learning models, with potential applications in areas such as data mining, pattern recognition, and decision-making systems. Key keywords: Sugeno integral, machine learning, binary classification, uncertain information, robust classification.
