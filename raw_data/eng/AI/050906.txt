This paper presents a comprehensive survey of exploration methods in reinforcement learning, a crucial component for achieving efficient and effective learning in complex environments. The objective of this research is to provide an in-depth analysis of the existing exploration strategies, highlighting their strengths, weaknesses, and applications. We employ a systematic approach to categorize and compare various exploration methods, including epsilon-greedy, entropy regularization, and intrinsic motivation. Our results show that different exploration methods exhibit distinct performance characteristics, and their suitability depends on the specific problem domain and environment. The key findings of this survey emphasize the importance of balancing exploration and exploitation in reinforcement learning, and highlight the potential of recent advances in deep learning and intrinsic motivation to improve exploration efficiency. This research contributes to the understanding of exploration methods in reinforcement learning, providing valuable insights for practitioners and researchers seeking to develop more effective and efficient learning algorithms. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, exploration methods, epsilon-greedy, entropy regularization, intrinsic motivation, deep learning.
