This paper addresses the challenge of improving the efficiency and performance of model-based deep reinforcement learning (RL) by introducing a novel approach to state representation and management. Traditional RL approaches often struggle with scalability and data efficiency, necessitating innovative solutions for real-time complex decision-making environments.

Methods/Approach: We propose a method called Variational State Tabulation (VST) that integrates a variational approach to efficiently compress state information into a tabular form, streamlining the learning process. This technique leverages the strengths of variational inference to dynamically generate a compact yet expressive representation of the state space, enabling the RL agent to focus computational resources more effectively.

Results/Findings: The implementation of VST in model-based deep reinforcement learning demonstrates significant improvements in learning speed and performance compared to baseline models. Experimental results reveal that VST-enhanced models achieve faster convergence and higher reward accumulation in benchmark tasks. Comparisons with traditional state representation schemes illustrate substantial gains in computational efficiency and scalability.

Conclusion/Implications: Our research introduces Variational State Tabulation as a potent enhancement to model-based deep RL, showcasing its potential to transform decision-making processes in complex environments. The findings suggest that VST can serve as a foundational technique in developing more scalable and efficient RL systems with applications ranging from robotics to autonomous systems. This contribution not only provides a path toward more resource-efficient learning but also opens avenues for further exploration in state representation optimization.

Keywords: deep reinforcement learning, model-based RL, variational inference, state representation, Variational State Tabulation, efficiency, scalability.
