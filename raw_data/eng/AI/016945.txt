Reward poisoning attacks pose a significant threat to the security and reliability of reinforcement learning (RL) systems, where adversaries manipulate reward signals to induce undesirable agent behavior. This paper investigates novel defense mechanisms against such attacks. We propose a robust learning framework that integrates a reward validation module based on anomaly detection techniques to identify and mitigate poisoned rewards. Specifically, we employ a combination of statistical analysis and machine learning classifiers to distinguish between genuine and malicious reward signals. Our approach is evaluated on a suite of benchmark RL environments under various attack scenarios, demonstrating its effectiveness in maintaining agent performance and preventing catastrophic failures. The results show a significant improvement in resilience compared to traditional RL algorithms, with minimal impact on learning efficiency in the absence of attacks. This work contributes a practical defense strategy against reward poisoning, enhancing the robustness of RL deployments in real-world applications. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Reward Poisoning, Anomaly Detection, Security, Robustness.
