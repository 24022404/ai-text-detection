This paper addresses the problem of establishing exponential inequalities for nonstationary Markov chains, a crucial aspect of stochastic processes. The objective is to develop novel bounds that capture the transient behavior of these chains, which is essential in various applications, including machine learning, queueing theory, and statistical analysis. 

We employ a innovative approach, combining techniques from probability theory and functional analysis to derive exponential inequalities for nonstationary Markov chains. Our method involves constructing a suitable Lyapunov function and utilizing a modified version of the Foster-Lyapunov drift condition.

The results show that our inequalities provide tighter bounds compared to existing methods, particularly in scenarios where the chain exhibits significant transient behavior. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach through numerical examples, highlighting its potential in analyzing complex stochastic systems.

The contributions of this research lie in providing a new framework for analyzing nonstationary Markov chains, which can be applied to a wide range of fields, including stochastic optimization, control theory, and time-series analysis. Key findings include the development of novel exponential inequalities, improved bounds for transient behavior, and applications in machine learning and statistical analysis. Relevant keywords: nonstationary Markov chains, exponential inequalities, stochastic processes, Lyapunov functions, Foster-Lyapunov drift condition.
