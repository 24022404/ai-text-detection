Sleep staging, the process of classifying sleep data into distinct stages, is crucial for diagnosing sleep disorders and understanding sleep architecture. This paper introduces U-Time, a novel fully convolutional network designed for accurate and efficient time series segmentation, specifically applied to sleep staging using single-channel EEG. U-Time leverages a U-Net architecture adapted for temporal data, enabling the model to capture both local and global contextual information within EEG signals. The model is trained end-to-end, directly mapping raw EEG data to sleep stage labels without handcrafted feature extraction. Experimental results on the Sleep-EDF database demonstrate that U-Time achieves state-of-the-art performance in sleep staging, surpassing traditional machine learning methods and comparable deep learning approaches while using significantly fewer parameters. Furthermore, the fully convolutional nature of U-Time enables efficient processing of variable-length EEG recordings. The proposed model offers a promising solution for automated sleep analysis, with potential applications in large-scale sleep studies and real-time sleep monitoring systems.
Keywords: Sleep staging, time series segmentation, convolutional neural network, U-Net, EEG, deep learning.
