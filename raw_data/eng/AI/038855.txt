Gradient descent is an essential optimization technique widely used in training machine learning models. By iteratively tweaking the model’s parameters, it seeks to minimize the cost function—a measure of prediction error. Through this continuous process of adjusting and recalculating, gradient descent enables models to learn from data and improve their performance over time. As the cost function decreases, the model’s predictions become more accurate, contributing to better outcomes in various applications across artificial intelligence and computer science fields, making gradient descent fundamental for building efficient and intelligent systems.
