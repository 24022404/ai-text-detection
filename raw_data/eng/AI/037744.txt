This paper introduces a novel approach to image correction leveraging deep learning for high dynamic range (HDR) transformation. The objective is to address common image imperfections such as underexposure, overexposure, and color distortion, enhancing visual quality and detail. Our method employs a deep neural network trained to learn a reciprocating HDR mapping function, effectively transforming low dynamic range (LDR) images to HDR and vice versa. This reciprocating capability allows for robust image correction by iteratively refining the image appearance through alternating HDR and LDR transformations. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach through extensive experiments on diverse datasets, comparing it against state-of-the-art image enhancement techniques. Results show that our method achieves superior performance in terms of both objective metrics (PSNR, SSIM) and subjective visual quality, producing images with improved contrast, color fidelity, and detail recovery. The proposed deep reciprocating HDR transformation offers a promising solution for automatic image correction and enhancement, with potential applications in photography, computer vision, and multimedia processing.

Keywords: Image Correction, HDR, Deep Learning, Image Enhancement, Reciprocal Transformation, Neural Networks.
