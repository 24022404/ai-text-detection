This paper presents a novel deep learning framework incorporating frequency and phase attention mechanisms for the accurate detection of partial discharges in insulated overhead conductors. The pervasive issue of partial discharge (PD) is a significant precursor to insulation failure, which necessitates robust detection technologies for timely preventive maintenance. To address this challenge, we propose an attention-based deep learning model that leverages the complementary nature of frequency and phase information inherent in partial discharge signals. Our approach integrates specialized attention mechanisms within a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, enhancing its ability to capture subtle and complex signal patterns associated with PD events. The model is trained and validated on a comprehensive dataset comprising real-world PD instances, demonstrating superior detection performance compared to existing methods. Results reveal marked improvements in accuracy, precision, and recall, highlighting the framework's efficacy in identifying partial discharges amidst noise and other operational conditions. This work not only advances the state-of-the-art in PD detection technologies but also provides scalable solutions applicable to large-scale power grids. The implications of this research are profound, contributing to enhanced reliability and safety in power distribution networks through proactive insulation condition monitoring. Key Keywords: Partial Discharge Detection, Insulated Overhead Conductors, Deep Learning, Frequency Attention, Phase Attention, Convolutional Neural Network (CNN).
