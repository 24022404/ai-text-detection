EgoFace: Egocentric Face Performance Capture and Videorealistic Reenactment

Abstract:

The objective of this research is to address the challenge of capturing and reenacting facial performances from an egocentric perspective, a task critical for enhancing applications in virtual reality and human-computer interaction. We introduce EgoFace, a novel approach designed to seamlessly capture high-fidelity facial expressions and facilitate videorealistic reenactment using egocentric camera arrays. Our method leverages cutting-edge machine learning algorithms for dynamic facial modeling and reconstruction, employing a refined version of neural networks to track and interpret subtle facial movements accurately. The results demonstrate significant improvements in performance capture, showcasing our system's capability to produce lifelike reenactments that closely mimic real human expressions. A comprehensive evaluation reveals that EgoFace outperforms existing approaches in terms of realism and execution speed, while being more adaptable across different lighting conditions and facial geometries. The implications of this study include enhanced user experiences in virtual environments and advancing telepresence technologies where intuitive and natural interaction is paramount. EgoFace marks a significant advancement in the realm of face capture and virtual representation, providing a scalable framework for future developments in immersive technologies. Key keywords: egocentric, facial performance, videorealistic reenactment, machine learning, dynamic facial modeling.
