Instance segmentation on 3D point clouds is critical for numerous applications, including autonomous driving and robotic perception. This paper examines traditional methodologies in this area and proposes a novel framework that rethinks the established tasks and metrics. Our approach introduces a refined model that leverages enhanced geometric representations and adaptive clustering algorithms to improve segmentation accuracy and computational efficiency. Through extensive testing on benchmark datasets, we demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art techniques in both precision and speed. The results indicate a marked improvement, particularly in complex environments where traditional methods struggle. We propose a new set of evaluation metrics designed to address inherent challenges in 3D point cloud segmentation, such as occlusion and varying density. These metrics provide a comprehensive assessment of segmentation quality, fostering a deeper understanding of model performance. Our research contributes to the advancement of 3D instance segmentation by offering a robust framework and practical insights that can be applied to real-world scenarios, enhancing the capabilities of autonomous systems. Keywords: instance segmentation, 3D point clouds, geometric representation, clustering algorithms, evaluation metrics.
