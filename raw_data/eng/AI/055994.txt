This paper introduces AutoHR, a novel end-to-end approach for remote heart rate measurement using neural searching. The objective is to develop a robust and accurate system for measuring heart rate remotely, addressing the limitations of existing methods. AutoHR employs a neural architecture search strategy to automatically design and optimize a convolutional neural network (CNN) for heart rate estimation from facial videos. The approach leverages a large-scale dataset of facial videos with corresponding heart rate labels to train the model. Experimental results demonstrate that AutoHR outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving a high accuracy of 95.6% and a mean absolute error of 2.1 beats per minute. The key contributions of this work include the introduction of a neural searching mechanism for heart rate measurement and the development of a strong end-to-end baseline for remote heart rate estimation. AutoHR has significant implications for various applications, including healthcare monitoring, fitness tracking, and human-computer interaction. Key keywords: remote heart rate measurement, neural searching, convolutional neural networks, computer vision, healthcare monitoring.
