This paper addresses the challenge of bias in the training of deep reinforcement learning (DRL) algorithms, which often impairs their capacity to generalize across diverse environments. The research proposes a novel, unbiased training framework that can enhance the performance and applicability of both existing and future DRL algorithms.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a general training framework that integrates bias reduction techniques directly into the reinforcement learning process. Our approach utilizes a combination of adaptive data augmentation, policy regularization, and reward reshaping to mitigate common sources of bias. The framework is designed to be flexible and modular, allowing for easy integration with various DRL algorithms such as Q-learning, policy gradient methods, and recent advancements in model-based learning.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluation demonstrates that our framework significantly enhances the robustness and efficacy of prominent DRL algorithms across a suite of benchmark environments. The results indicate substantial improvements in generalization capacity, with noticeable reductions in overfitting and increased performance consistency. Comparative analyses exhibit that our framework outperforms standard training protocols by a notable margin in terms of convergence speed and final reward performance.

Conclusion/Implications: The proposed unbiased training framework represents a substantial contribution to the field of deep reinforcement learning, providing a versatile tool for researchers and practitioners aiming to develop more robust and reliable intelligent systems. Its compatibility with a broad spectrum of DRL algorithms underlines its potential for widespread adoption, making it a valuable asset for future advancements in autonomous systems and decision-making AI. Key keywords include: deep reinforcement learning, unbiased training, generalization, policy regularization, and algorithm robustness.
