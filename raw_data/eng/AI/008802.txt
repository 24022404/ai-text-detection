Graph-based learning has gained significant attention due to its ability to capture complex relationships in data. However, the challenge remains in enhancing model performance through effective data augmentation techniques. This paper introduces GABO, a novel framework for Graph Augmentations with Bi-level Optimization. The primary objective of GABO is to dynamically improve graph data representations and model robustness by leveraging bi-level optimization strategies. At its core, the method integrates augmentations into the learning process, optimizing both the model parameters and augmentation strategies concurrently. Through the use of advanced optimization algorithms, GABO iteratively refines augmentation parameters, tailoring transformations that yield optimal performance gains. Our experimental evaluation demonstrates that GABO outperforms traditional augmentation methods, achieving superior accuracy and generalization across multiple benchmark datasets. The results indicate that GABO's innovative approach contributes significantly to the field of graph-based learning by enhancing model accuracy and adaptability without requiring substantial additional computational resources. This research holds potential applications in various domains, including social network analysis, recommendation systems, and bioinformatics, where relational data structures are predominant. Key keywords include graph learning, data augmentation, bi-level optimization, and model robustness.
