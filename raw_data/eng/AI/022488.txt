This paper addresses the challenge of ensuring safety in deep reinforcement learning (DRL) systems, particularly in dynamic and uncertain environments. Traditional DRL approaches often lack explicit mechanisms to guarantee safety during exploration, which limits their applicability in real-world scenarios. To overcome this limitation, we propose a novel safety-guided DRL framework that integrates online Gaussian Process (GP) estimation to model and predict system dynamics in real time. The GP-based approach enables the system to quantify uncertainty and adaptively enforce safety constraints during training and deployment. Our method leverages the probabilistic nature of GPs to identify unsafe regions and guide the agent toward safer actions, ensuring compliance with predefined safety thresholds. Experimental results on benchmark environments demonstrate that the proposed framework significantly improves safety performance without compromising learning efficiency. Compared to existing methods, our approach achieves a higher success rate in safety-critical tasks while maintaining competitive task performance. The findings highlight the potential of combining probabilistic modeling with DRL to enable safe and reliable decision-making in complex systems. This work contributes to advancing the field of safe reinforcement learning by providing a scalable and adaptive solution for real-world applications, such as autonomous systems and robotics.  

**Keywords**: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Safety Constraints, Gaussian Process, Uncertainty Estimation, Autonomous Systems, Probabilistic Modeling.
