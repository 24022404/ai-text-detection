This paper proposes an innovative approach to object recognition using Interpretable Graph Capsule Networks (IGCNs). The objective is to improve the accuracy and interpretability of object recognition models by leveraging the strengths of graph neural networks and capsule networks. Our method utilizes a graph-based representation to model complex relationships between objects and their parts, while incorporating capsule networks to capture hierarchical representations of objects. The IGCN model is trained using a novel routing algorithm that enables the identification of meaningful object parts and their relationships. Experimental results demonstrate that IGCN achieves state-of-the-art performance on several object recognition benchmarks, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of interpretability in deep learning models, enabling a deeper understanding of the decision-making process. This work contributes to the development of more transparent and reliable AI systems, with potential applications in computer vision, robotics, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: Graph Capsule Networks, Object Recognition, Interpretable AI, Deep Learning, Computer Vision.
