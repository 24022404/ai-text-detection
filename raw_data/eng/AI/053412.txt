This paper introduces GIMP-ML, a novel framework that integrates state-of-the-art computer vision models into the GNU Image Manipulation Program (GIMP) through Python-based plugins. The primary objective of this work is to bridge the gap between advanced machine learning techniques and accessible image editing tools, enabling users to leverage powerful AI-driven functionalities within a familiar open-source environment. GIMP-ML employs a modular architecture, allowing seamless integration of pre-trained models for tasks such as image segmentation, object detection, style transfer, and super-resolution. The framework utilizes Python's extensive machine learning libraries, including TensorFlow and PyTorch, to ensure compatibility with a wide range of models. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of GIMP-ML in enhancing image editing workflows, with significant improvements in processing speed and accuracy compared to traditional methods. The framework also supports custom model integration, making it adaptable to diverse user needs. By democratizing access to advanced computer vision capabilities, GIMP-ML contributes to the broader adoption of AI in creative and professional image editing. This work highlights the potential of combining open-source software with machine learning to create versatile, user-friendly tools for the digital art and design community.  

**Keywords**: GIMP, computer vision, Python plugins, image editing, machine learning, open-source software.
