Parameter selection is a critical yet often overlooked aspect of machine learning model development. This paper investigates the significant impact of parameter selection on model performance and generalization. We present a comprehensive analysis of various parameter selection methods, including grid search, random search, and Bayesian optimization, applied to different machine learning algorithms such as Support Vector Machines, Random Forests, and Neural Networks. Our results demonstrate that careful parameter selection can lead to substantial improvements in accuracy, F1-score, and AUC, often exceeding performance gains achieved through more complex model architectures. Furthermore, we show that suboptimal parameter settings can severely hinder model performance, even with high-quality training data. We argue that a greater emphasis on parameter selection is essential for achieving state-of-the-art results and ensuring the robustness and reliability of machine learning models. This study highlights the importance of parameter optimization as a core component of the machine learning pipeline, and offers practical guidelines for effective parameter selection strategies.
Keywords: Parameter Selection, Hyperparameter Optimization, Machine Learning, Model Performance, Grid Search, Bayesian Optimization.
