Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) design is a complex task involving trade-offs between performance, cost, and reliability. This paper introduces DISPATCH, a novel framework for Design Space Exploration (DSE) in CPS. DISPATCH employs a multi-objective optimization approach, integrating simulation-based evaluation with machine learning techniques to efficiently navigate the vast design space. Specifically, it utilizes Gaussian Process Regression to model the performance characteristics of different design configurations, enabling rapid prediction and selection of promising candidates. Experimental results, conducted on a case study of an autonomous vehicle control system, demonstrate that DISPATCH significantly reduces the time required for DSE compared to traditional methods, while identifying Pareto-optimal designs that meet stringent performance and safety requirements. The framework facilitates the exploration of diverse design alternatives and provides valuable insights for CPS engineers in making informed design decisions, enhancing system performance, and reducing development costs. Key words: Cyber-Physical Systems, Design Space Exploration, Optimization, Machine Learning, Gaussian Process Regression, Autonomous Vehicles.
