Fetal echocardiography is crucial for early detection of congenital heart defects. Automatic detection of cardiac chambers in the four-chamber view (4CV) is a critical step towards automated analysis. This paper introduces an attention-based YOLOv4 framework for robust and accurate detection of the left atrium (LA), right atrium (RA), left ventricle (LV), and right ventricle (RV) in fetal echocardiograms. The proposed method incorporates a spatial attention mechanism to enhance feature representation and improve localization accuracy, particularly in challenging cases with low image quality or anatomical variations. We evaluated the performance of our framework on a clinically relevant dataset of fetal echocardiograms. The results demonstrate that the attention-based YOLOv4 achieves significantly higher mean Average Precision (mAP) and F1-score compared to the standard YOLOv4 and other state-of-the-art object detection methods. Specifically, the proposed method shows improved robustness in detecting smaller chambers and handling noisy images. This automated detection framework has the potential to assist clinicians in efficient and accurate cardiac chamber assessment, thereby improving the prenatal diagnosis of congenital heart diseases. Keywords: Fetal Echocardiography, Cardiac Chamber Detection, YOLOv4, Attention Mechanism, Deep Learning, Congenital Heart Defects.
