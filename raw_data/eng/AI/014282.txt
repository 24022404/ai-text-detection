This study aims to develop an automated method for computing total kidney volume from CT images in patients with Autosomal Dominant Polycystic Kidney Disease (ADPKD) using multi-task 3D convolutional neural networks. Our approach utilizes a deep learning model that simultaneously segments kidney boundaries and estimates kidney volume, leveraging a large dataset of annotated CT images. The results show that our method achieves high accuracy in kidney volume computation, outperforming traditional techniques. The proposed network architecture enables robust and efficient processing of 3D medical images, demonstrating potential for clinical applications. This research contributes to the development of non-invasive diagnostic tools for ADPKD, enabling earlier disease detection and monitoring. Key findings highlight the effectiveness of multi-task learning in improving model performance and the importance of accurate kidney volume estimation in ADPKD management. Relevant keywords include: ADPKD, kidney volume computation, 3D convolutional neural networks, multi-task learning, medical image analysis, deep learning, CT images.
