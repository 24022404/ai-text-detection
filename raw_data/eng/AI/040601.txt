**Abstract**

The generation of realistic medical time series data is essential for advancing research in healthcare analytics, disease prediction, and clinical decision support. This paper presents a novel approach for synthesizing real-valued medical time series using Recurrent Conditional Generative Adversarial Networks (RCGANs). Our method leverages the temporal modeling capabilities of recurrent neural networks within the GAN framework, conditioning the generation process on patient attributes and clinical covariates to produce contextually relevant sequences. We evaluate the performance of our model on multiple benchmark medical datasets, including electrocardiogram (ECG) and intensive care unit (ICU) patient monitoring data. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed RCGAN architecture outperforms traditional GANs and baseline generative models in capturing complex temporal dynamics and preserving the statistical characteristics of the original time series. Furthermore, clinical experts verified the plausibility of the generated sequences. This work contributes a practical tool for data augmentation, privacy-preserving data sharing, and simulation in medical AI applications, addressing key challenges in real-world healthcare data scarcity and heterogeneity. Keywords: time series generation, generative adversarial networks, RNN, medical data, data augmentation, synthetic data.
