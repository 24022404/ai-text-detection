Creating 3D photography from a single image presents a significant challenge in computer vision. This paper introduces SLIDE, a novel approach for generating high-quality 3D photographs from a single 2D input. Our method leverages soft layering to decompose the image into multiple depth-ordered layers, enabling realistic parallax effects. We employ a depth-aware inpainting technique to synthesize plausible content in disoccluded regions, guided by an estimated depth map. The framework consists of a convolutional neural network trained to predict both soft segmentation masks for layering and a corresponding depth map. These predictions are then used to render the 3D photograph with view synthesis. Experimental results demonstrate that SLIDE generates visually compelling 3D photographs with fewer artifacts compared to existing single-image 3D conversion techniques. Subjective evaluations confirm the improved realism and visual quality of our approach. This work contributes a robust and efficient pipeline for single-image 3D photography, opening new avenues for immersive visual experiences.

Keywords: 3D Photography, Single Image, Depth Estimation, Image Inpainting, View Synthesis, Neural Networks.
