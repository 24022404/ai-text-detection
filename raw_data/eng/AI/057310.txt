This study aims to develop an interpretable framework for risk and sensitivity analyses in biomanufacturing processes, enabling Quality-by-Design (QbD) and stability control. By leveraging advanced machine learning techniques and process modeling, our approach identifies critical process parameters and their interactions, facilitating the prediction of potential risks and uncertainties. The methodology integrates sensitivity analysis, uncertainty quantification, and fault detection to provide a comprehensive understanding of process variability and its impact on product quality. Key findings demonstrate the effectiveness of our framework in identifying critical parameters, predicting process risks, and optimizing control strategies. The results have significant implications for the biopharmaceutical industry, as they enable the design of more robust and reliable biomanufacturing processes, ensuring consistent product quality and stability. This research contributes to the development of novel risk analysis and control methods, enhancing the interpretability and transparency of biomanufacturing processes. Relevant keywords: biomanufacturing, Quality-by-Design, risk analysis, sensitivity analysis, machine learning, process modeling, uncertainty quantification.
