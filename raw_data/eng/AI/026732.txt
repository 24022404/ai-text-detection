This paper introduces a novel knowledge distillation framework leveraging conditional Generative Adversarial Networks (cGANs) to enhance image classification and regression tasks. Our approach addresses the challenge of transferring knowledge from a large, pre-trained teacher model to a smaller, more efficient student model. Specifically, we utilize a cGAN to generate synthetic data conditioned on class labels and regression targets, effectively augmenting the training dataset for the student network. By training the student on both real and cGAN-generated samples, we enable it to learn complex relationships and generalize better, even with limited real-world data. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our proposed method significantly improves the student model's performance in both classification accuracy and regression precision compared to traditional knowledge distillation techniques and training solely on real data. The framework showcases superior performance in low-data regimes. The study provides a promising avenue for efficient model deployment in resource-constrained environments. Keywords: Knowledge Distillation, cGAN, Image Classification, Regression, Generative Adversarial Networks, Data Augmentation.
