This paper introduces a novel forecasting method for grouped time series data by integrating the k-means clustering algorithm. The objective is to improve forecasting accuracy by leveraging inherent similarities within groups. Our approach first employs k-means to partition time series into distinct clusters based on their historical patterns. Subsequently, a forecasting model, specifically ARIMA, is applied to each cluster, allowing for customized predictions. We evaluated the proposed method on real-world sales datasets, comparing its performance against traditional forecasting techniques applied without clustering. Results demonstrate that the k-means-integrated approach significantly reduces forecast error, achieving an average improvement of 15% in Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE). This indicates that grouping time series based on similarity enhances forecast accuracy. The findings suggest a practical and effective strategy for businesses seeking to improve demand forecasting and resource allocation in scenarios involving grouped time series data. Key words: Time series forecasting, k-means clustering, ARIMA, grouped data, demand forecasting, sales data.
