Stochastic gradient descent (SGD) algorithms are prevalent in large-scale machine learning but often struggle with ill-conditioning and complex geometries inherent in optimization problems on matrix manifolds. This paper introduces a novel family of Riemannian adaptive stochastic gradient algorithms designed to improve convergence and stability on such manifolds. Our approach leverages Riemannian geometry to adapt the stochastic gradient, incorporating manifold-aware preconditioning and step-size adaptation. Specifically, we develop algorithms based on Riemannian analogs of AdaGrad and Adam, tailored to exploit the underlying manifold structure. We evaluate our methods on matrix completion and low-rank matrix approximation tasks, demonstrating superior performance compared to existing Euclidean and Riemannian SGD methods. Empirical results show faster convergence, improved solution quality, and enhanced robustness to noisy gradients. The proposed algorithms offer a practical and effective framework for large-scale optimization problems constrained to matrix manifolds, with broad applicability in machine learning and signal processing.

**Keywords:** Riemannian optimization, stochastic gradient descent, matrix manifolds, adaptive algorithms, machine learning, AdaGrad, Adam.
