This paper addresses the challenge of effectively decomposing images into their spectral and structural components, a critical task in image processing and computer vision. We propose a novel approach, Deeply Learned Spectral Total Variation Decomposition (DL-STVD), which leverages deep learning techniques to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of spectral total variation decomposition. Unlike traditional methods that rely on handcrafted filters and optimization frameworks, DL-STVD employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture trained end-to-end to learn optimal decomposition parameters directly from data. The proposed model integrates spectral and spatial information, enabling robust separation of texture, edges, and smooth regions in complex images. Experimental results demonstrate that DL-STVD outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of decomposition quality, computational efficiency, and adaptability to diverse image types. The framework achieves significant improvements in preserving fine details while minimizing artifacts, as validated through quantitative metrics and visual comparisons. The implications of this work extend to applications such as image denoising, compression, and enhancement, where precise spectral-structural separation is essential. By combining deep learning with spectral total variation principles, DL-STVD offers a scalable and innovative solution for advanced image analysis tasks.  

**Keywords**: spectral decomposition, total variation, deep learning, image processing, convolutional neural networks, texture separation.
