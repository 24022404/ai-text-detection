This paper addresses the challenge of automating the classification of phantom test patterns, a critical task in medical imaging quality assurance. Traditional methods often rely on manual inspection, which is time-consuming and prone to human error. To overcome these limitations, we propose a novel approach leveraging transfer learning with deep neural networks (DNNs). Specifically, we utilize pre-trained convolutional neural networks (CNNs) fine-tuned on a curated dataset of phantom test images. The proposed method demonstrates significant improvements in classification accuracy and efficiency compared to conventional techniques. Experimental results show that our approach achieves an accuracy of over 95% on a diverse set of test patterns, outperforming existing methods by a notable margin. Furthermore, the system exhibits robustness to variations in image quality and noise, making it suitable for real-world deployment. This research contributes to the field of medical imaging by providing a scalable, automated solution for phantom test pattern classification, with potential applications in enhancing diagnostic imaging workflows and ensuring consistent quality control.  

**Keywords**: phantom test patterns, transfer learning, deep neural networks, medical imaging, quality assurance, convolutional neural networks.
