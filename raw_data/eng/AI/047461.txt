Blockchain technology empowers individuals with self-sovereign identities and control over their digital assets and data by decentralizing ownership, a principle demonstrated by cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin and Ethereum. In contrast, traditional web technologies typically prioritize convenience through centralized systems that streamline access and management, often at the expense of user autonomy. By thoughtfully integrating the convenient features that users expect from conventional web platforms into blockchain-based applications, it becomes possible to enhance user experience significantly. This fusion not only encourages broader adoption but also helps bridge the gap between traditional users and those exploring blockchain ecosystems.
