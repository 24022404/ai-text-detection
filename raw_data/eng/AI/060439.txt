This paper presents FlowNet 2.0, a significant advancement in optical flow estimation leveraging deep neural networks. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of optical flow estimation, a crucial task in computer vision. Our approach involves the development of a novel architecture that integrates multiple convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to refine flow estimates iteratively. The proposed method exploits large-scale datasets and advanced training techniques to learn complex motion patterns. Experimental results demonstrate that FlowNet 2.0 outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving superior accuracy and robustness in various scenarios, including fast motion, occlusions, and realistic video sequences. The key contributions of this work include the introduction of a multi-stage refinement process, the use of large-scale datasets for training, and the demonstration of significant improvements in optical flow estimation. This research has important implications for applications such as video analysis, object tracking, and 3D reconstruction, and paves the way for further advancements in computer vision and deep learning, with relevant keywords including optical flow, deep learning, CNNs, and computer vision.
