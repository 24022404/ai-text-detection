Photo adjustment aims to enhance visual aesthetics and correct imperfections in images. Existing methods often struggle to preserve semantic consistency, leading to artifacts and unnatural results. This paper introduces a novel Deep Semantics-Aware Photo Adjustment (DSAPA) framework that integrates deep learning with semantic understanding to achieve more plausible and controllable image enhancements. Our approach leverages a pre-trained semantic segmentation network to extract pixel-level semantic labels, which are then incorporated into a deep convolutional neural network designed for photo adjustment. The network learns to adjust image properties, such as brightness, contrast, and color balance, while respecting semantic boundaries and relationships. We introduce a novel loss function that encourages semantic consistency by penalizing adjustments that significantly alter the appearance of objects within the same semantic class. Experimental results on a diverse set of images demonstrate that DSAPA outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of both visual quality and semantic coherence. Subjective evaluations confirm that our approach generates more pleasing and realistic photo adjustments. This research contributes a new paradigm for photo adjustment, enabling more intelligent and context-aware image enhancement. Keywords: Photo Adjustment, Semantic Segmentation, Deep Learning, Image Enhancement, Semantic Consistency, Convolutional Neural Networks.
