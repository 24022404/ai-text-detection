This paper presents Cascade R-CNN, a novel object detection framework designed to achieve high-quality detection performance. The objective is to address the challenge of detecting objects with varying sizes and aspect ratios, which is a long-standing problem in the field of computer vision. Our approach involves a cascade of detectors, each trained to detect objects at different scales, allowing for more accurate and efficient detection. The method utilizes a sequence of R-CNN models, where each stage refines the detection results from the previous one, leading to improved precision and recall. Experimental results demonstrate that Cascade R-CNN outperforms state-of-the-art object detection systems, achieving significant improvements in detection accuracy on benchmark datasets. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a multi-stage detection framework, which enables the model to capture a wide range of object sizes and shapes, and the development of a novel loss function that facilitates the training of the cascade detectors. The proposed Cascade R-CNN has important implications for applications such as autonomous driving, surveillance, and image understanding, where high-quality object detection is crucial. Key keywords: object detection, R-CNN, cascade detection, computer vision, deep learning.
