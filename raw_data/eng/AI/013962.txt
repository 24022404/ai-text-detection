This paper introduces a novel Contextual Residual Aggregation Network (CRAN) for ultra high-resolution image inpainting. The objective is to improve the coherence and realism of inpainted regions, especially for images exceeding 4K resolution, where existing methods often struggle with detail preservation and contextual understanding. CRAN leverages a multi-scale contextual attention mechanism to effectively capture long-range dependencies and integrate information from both local and global contexts. Furthermore, a residual aggregation module is proposed to progressively refine the inpainted regions by fusing features from different layers of the network, preserving fine-grained details and reducing artifacts. Experimental results on diverse datasets, including high-resolution landscape and architectural images, demonstrate that CRAN outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of both quantitative metrics (PSNR, SSIM) and qualitative visual assessment. The proposed approach generates more visually plausible and contextually consistent inpainting results, even with large missing regions. This work contributes a robust framework for ultra high-resolution image inpainting with enhanced contextual awareness and detail preservation capabilities, offering significant potential for applications in image restoration, editing, and content creation.

Keywords: Image Inpainting, Ultra High-Resolution, Contextual Attention, Residual Aggregation, Deep Learning, Image Restoration.
