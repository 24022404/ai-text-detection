This research addresses the challenge of time series forecasting, with a focus on capturing long-term trends essential for strategic decision-making across various industries. Traditional methods often struggle with accurately projecting extended trends due to their limitations in recognizing complex patterns over time. Our objective is to enhance the forecasting accuracy and reliability for long-term trends utilizing advanced computational techniques.

Methods: We propose a novel approach leveraging Self-Organizing Maps (SOMs), an unsupervised learning algorithm known for its ability to cluster and visualize high-dimensional data. By integrating SOMs into our forecasting model, we aim to better identify and exploit underlying structures within time series data that correspond to long-term patterns. The model was trained and tested on diverse datasets to evaluate its robustness and adaptability across different domains.

Results: The proposed SOM-based forecasting model demonstrated superior performance in capturing long-term trends compared to traditional time series forecasting techniques, such as ARIMA and exponential smoothing. The results indicate a significant improvement in predictive accuracy and trend identification, particularly in datasets with non-linear and non-stationary characteristics. The model's adaptability across various datasets further validates its potential as a versatile forecasting tool.

Conclusion: Our research contributes a novel perspective to long-term trend forecasting by integrating Self-Organizing Maps, showcasing its effectiveness in enhancing prediction accuracy and trend analysis. This approach holds promise for application in areas requiring precise long-term forecasting, such as financial markets, climate modeling, and supply chain management. The improved understanding of trend dynamics facilitated by this method can inform strategic planning and decision-making, thus offering valuable insights to practitioners and researchers in the field of time series analysis.

Keywords: Time Series Forecasting, Long-Term Trends, Self-Organizing Maps, Unsupervised Learning, Predictive Modeling, Trend Analysis.
