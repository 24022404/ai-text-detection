**Abstract**

Ensuring safety in reinforcement learning (RL) is a critical challenge, particularly for applications where unsafe actions can lead to significant risks or system failures. This paper presents a novel framework, Safety-Guided Deep Reinforcement Learning via Online Gaussian Process Estimation, that integrates safety constraints into the learning process of deep RL agents. The proposed approach employs online Gaussian Process (GP) estimation to provide reliable, real-time uncertainty quantification of the environment dynamics. By continuously updating the GP model with new experience, the agent identifies states and actions associated with high risk, enforcing safety through probabilistic constraints during policy optimization. Extensive experiments on benchmark control tasks demonstrate that our method significantly reduces unsafe events compared to baseline deep RL algorithms, while maintaining competitive task performance. Importantly, the framework adapts to novel and dynamic environments by efficiently capturing and incorporating uncertainty, resulting in robust and safe behavior without compromising learning efficiency. This work advances the state-of-the-art in safe reinforcement learning, with practical implications for autonomous systems, robotics, and real-time decision-making domains. Key keywords: deep reinforcement learning, safety, Gaussian Process, uncertainty quantification, online learning, safe policy optimization.
