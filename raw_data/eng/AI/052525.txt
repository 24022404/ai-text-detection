This paper proposes a novel approach to improve the performance of Adder Neural Networks (ANNs) through kernel-based progressive distillation. The objective is to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of ANNs by leveraging the strengths of kernel methods and knowledge distillation. Our method employs a progressive distillation strategy, where a pre-trained teacher network guides the training of a student ANN, using a kernel-based loss function to transfer knowledge. The results show that our approach significantly improves the performance of ANNs on various benchmark datasets, outperforming existing distillation methods. The key findings indicate that the kernel-based distillation loss enables the student network to capture complex patterns and relationships in the data more effectively. This research contributes to the development of more efficient and accurate neural network architectures, with potential applications in areas such as computer vision and natural language processing. The proposed method highlights the benefits of combining kernel methods with deep learning techniques, offering a new perspective on model distillation and knowledge transfer. Key keywords: Adder Neural Networks, Kernel Methods, Progressive Distillation, Knowledge Distillation, Deep Learning.
