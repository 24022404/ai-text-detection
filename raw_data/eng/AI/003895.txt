The CAMELS Multifield Dataset represents a groundbreaking resource for exploring the fundamental parameters of the universe through the lens of artificial intelligence (AI). This study aims to leverage the dataset to train and evaluate AI models capable of inferring cosmological and astrophysical parameters from large-scale simulations of the universe. We employ advanced machine learning techniques, including deep neural networks and convolutional architectures, to analyze multifield data encompassing dark matter, gas, and stellar distributions. Our approach demonstrates significant improvements in parameter estimation accuracy compared to traditional methods, achieving robust performance across diverse cosmological scenarios. The results highlight the potential of AI-driven models to accelerate discoveries in cosmology, offering insights into dark energy, galaxy formation, and the large-scale structure of the universe. This research underscores the transformative role of AI in astrophysics, providing a scalable framework for analyzing complex datasets and advancing our understanding of the cosmos. Key contributions include the development of novel AI methodologies tailored for cosmological inference and the release of a comprehensive, publicly accessible dataset to foster further research.  

**Keywords**: CAMELS dataset, artificial intelligence, cosmology, machine learning, dark matter, galaxy formation, parameter estimation.
