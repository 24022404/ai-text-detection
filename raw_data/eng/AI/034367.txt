This paper introduces the SyntheticFur dataset, a novel collection of synthetic data designed to advance the field of neural rendering, particularly for the realistic rendering of fur and hair in computer-generated imagery. The objective of this research is to provide a comprehensive dataset that addresses the current limitations in rendering complex, dynamic, and high-resolution fur and hair models. Our approach involves generating a large-scale dataset of synthetic fur and hair samples, leveraging advanced physics-based simulations and machine learning techniques to create highly realistic and diverse data. The SyntheticFur dataset consists of over 10,000 images, each accompanied by detailed annotations and ground truth data, enabling the training and evaluation of neural rendering models. Our results demonstrate significant improvements in rendering quality and efficiency when using the SyntheticFur dataset, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods. The contributions of this research include the creation of a unique and extensive dataset, as well as the development of a neural rendering pipeline that can effectively utilize this data to produce highly realistic and detailed renderings of fur and hair. Key applications of this work include computer-generated imagery, video games, and virtual reality, with potential extensions to fields such as animation, special effects, and virtual try-on. Relevant keywords: neural rendering, synthetic data, fur and hair rendering, computer-generated imagery, machine learning.
