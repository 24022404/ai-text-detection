This paper explores the application of hypergraph-based methods in machine learning, focusing on hypergraph convolution and hypergraph attention mechanisms. The objective is to address the limitations of traditional graph-based approaches in modeling complex, higher-order relationships within data. We propose a novel framework that leverages hypergraph convolution to capture multi-node interactions and introduces a hypergraph attention mechanism to dynamically weigh the importance of hyperedges. The methods are evaluated on benchmark datasets for tasks such as node classification and link prediction, demonstrating superior performance compared to conventional graph neural networks (GNNs). Results indicate that our approach achieves significant improvements in accuracy and robustness, particularly in scenarios involving heterogeneous and high-dimensional data. The findings highlight the potential of hypergraph-based models in advancing machine learning applications, offering new insights for tasks requiring the modeling of intricate relational structures. This research contributes to the growing field of graph representation learning by introducing innovative techniques for handling higher-order dependencies, with implications for areas such as social network analysis, recommendation systems, and bioinformatics.  

**Keywords**: Hypergraph, Graph Neural Networks, Attention Mechanism, Higher-Order Relationships, Node Classification, Link Prediction.
