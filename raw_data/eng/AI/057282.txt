3D point cloud registration is a fundamental task in computer vision and robotics, crucial for applications such as autonomous navigation and 3D reconstruction. This paper introduces an end-to-end deep learning framework for point cloud registration that leverages the concept of virtual correspondences to improve accuracy and robustness. Our method circumvents explicit feature engineering by learning geometric relationships directly from raw point cloud data. The network predicts virtual correspondences between point clouds and uses these correspondences within a differentiable Procrustes analysis to estimate the transformation. Key to our approach is a novel loss function that enforces geometric consistency and penalizes incorrect correspondence predictions. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art registration accuracy, particularly in the presence of noise and outliers, surpassing existing methods in terms of registration error and computational efficiency. The results highlight the potential of learned virtual correspondences for robust and accurate 3D point cloud registration. Keywords: Point Cloud Registration, Deep Learning, Virtual Correspondences, 3D Vision, Geometric Learning.
