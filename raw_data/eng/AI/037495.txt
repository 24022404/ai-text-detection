This paper presents a novel learning method for Wasserstein distributionally robust optimization (DRO) that incorporates local perturbations, enhancing the robustness of machine learning models against uncertainty and adversarial attacks. The objective is to develop a principled approach that minimizes the worst-case expected loss over a family of distributions within a Wasserstein ball around the empirical distribution. Our method leverages a combination of robust optimization techniques and local perturbation strategies to improve the model's resilience to data perturbations. The key findings indicate that our approach outperforms existing DRO methods in terms of robustness and accuracy, particularly in scenarios with high levels of noise or adversarial contamination. The proposed method has significant implications for applications in machine learning, including image classification, natural language processing, and reinforcement learning, where robustness against uncertainty is crucial. By incorporating local perturbations into the Wasserstein DRO framework, our work contributes to the development of more robust and reliable AI systems, with potential applications in areas such as computer vision, speech recognition, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: Wasserstein DRO, local perturbations, robust optimization, machine learning, adversarial robustness.
