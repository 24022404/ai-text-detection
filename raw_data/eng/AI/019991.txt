This paper presents a novel approach to 3D face reconstruction using color photometric stereo with uncalibrated near point lights. The objective is to accurately recover the 3D shape and texture of a face from a set of color images captured under varying lighting conditions. Our method employs a color photometric stereo technique, which leverages the color information from the images to estimate the surface normals and albedo of the face. We use an uncalibrated near point light source, which eliminates the need for precise light calibration, making the system more flexible and practical. The results show that our approach can achieve high-quality 3D face reconstructions with detailed texture and geometry. Compared to existing methods, our technique demonstrates improved robustness to lighting variations and reduced sensitivity to noise. The contributions of this research include a novel color photometric stereo algorithm and an uncalibrated lighting setup, which can be applied to various 3D reconstruction applications, including face recognition, animation, and virtual reality. Key keywords: 3D face reconstruction, color photometric stereo, uncalibrated near point lights, surface normals, albedo estimation.
