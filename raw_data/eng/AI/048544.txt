Natural image matting is a fundamental challenge in computer vision, aiming to accurately extract foreground objects from images with complex backgrounds. This paper presents a deep learning-based approach for automatic natural image matting, addressing the limitations of traditional methods that often require user interaction or fail to generalize across diverse scenes. Our proposed framework leverages a novel encoder-decoder architecture with multi-scale feature fusion, enabling precise alpha matte estimation without manual guidance. The model is trained on a large-scale dataset with diverse foreground-background compositions, ensuring robustness across various real-world scenarios. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art techniques in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency, achieving superior performance on benchmark datasets. The proposed approach also exhibits strong generalization capabilities, making it suitable for applications in image editing, video production, and augmented reality. This work contributes to advancing automatic image matting by introducing a scalable and efficient solution that eliminates the need for user intervention while maintaining high-quality results.  

**Keywords**: natural image matting, deep learning, encoder-decoder architecture, alpha matte estimation, computer vision, image editing.
