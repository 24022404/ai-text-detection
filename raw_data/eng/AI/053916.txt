In the realm of healthcare data analysis, the interpretation of electronic medical records (EMRs) presents a significant challenge due to their complex and unstructured nature. This paper presents RetainVis, a novel framework that combines visual analytics with interpretable and interactive recurrent neural networks (RNNs) to enhance the understanding and usability of EMR data. Our approach integrates visual representation techniques with RNNs to facilitate real-time insights while maintaining model interpretability. We conducted extensive experiments on a publicly available EMR dataset, demonstrating that RetainVis significantly improves the accuracy of patient outcome predictions compared to traditional models. Moreover, the interactive dashboard empowers healthcare professionals by allowing them to explore and visualize patterns in the data intuitively, ultimately supporting better clinical decision-making. The results indicate that RetainVis not only provides robust predictive capabilities but also fosters a deeper understanding of the underlying factors influencing patient health. This work contributes to the field of health informatics by bridging the gap between complex model predictions and practical, interpretable visual analytics, with potential applications in patient management and clinical research. 

Keywords: Visual Analytics, Recurrent Neural Networks, Electronic Medical Records, Interpretable Models, Healthcare Data.
