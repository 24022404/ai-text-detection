This research paper introduces SelfAugment, a novel framework designed to automatically generate augmentation policies tailored for self-supervised learning tasks. The primary objective is to enhance the robustness and performance of self-supervised learning models by optimizing the augmentation process, which is often critical yet traditionally manual. The proposed approach employs a reinforcement learning-based method to discover and refine augmentation strategies that align with the unique features of self-supervised models. Experimental evaluations demonstrate that SelfAugment significantly improves the efficiency and accuracy of self-supervised learning compared to existing manual and heuristic approaches. By achieving superior performance on benchmark datasets, SelfAugment underscores the importance of adaptive augmentation policies in harnessing the full potential of self-supervised learning. This research opens new avenues for automated augmentation in machine learning and has potential applications in areas where labeled data is scarce or expensive to obtain. Key innovations include the automatic tailoring of augmentations that maximize data representation quality, leading to more generalized and powerful learning models. Essential keywords: Self-Augment, self-supervised learning, automatic augmentation, reinforcement learning, data representation.
