This paper presents a novel approach to image registration using the Least Squares Normalized Cross Correlation (LSNCC) method. The objective is to develop a robust and efficient algorithm for aligning images with varying lighting conditions and geometric transformations. Our approach utilizes a least squares optimization framework to minimize the difference between the normalized cross-correlation coefficients of the reference and target images. The LSNCC method is compared to existing image registration techniques, demonstrating improved accuracy and robustness in the presence of noise and outliers. Experimental results show that the proposed method outperforms state-of-the-art algorithms in terms of registration accuracy and computational efficiency. The contributions of this research include the development of a novel image registration algorithm, the introduction of a least squares optimization framework for normalized cross-correlation, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in various image registration applications. Key keywords: image registration, least squares optimization, normalized cross-correlation, computer vision, pattern recognition.
