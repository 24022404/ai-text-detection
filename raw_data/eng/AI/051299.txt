This paper investigates the application of reinforcement learning (RL) within the R programming environment, providing a comprehensive exploration of how this powerful framework can be utilized for various problem-solving tasks. The objective of the study is to bridge the gap between theoretical RL concepts and practical implementation using R, making RL techniques more accessible to data scientists and researchers familiar with this programming language. The paper presents a detailed methodology for implementing Q-learning and policy gradient algorithms, two of the foundational RL techniques, within R. The approach involves integrating existing R packages with custom-built functions to create a robust RL toolkit. Our results demonstrate the effectiveness of these implementations through several benchmark tasks commonly used in the RL domain, highlighting the performance of our R-based solutions in comparison to traditional Python-based RL libraries. The findings reveal that R can efficiently handle the computational demands of RL tasks, providing comparable results to more conventional environments. By successfully implementing RL algorithms in R, this research contributes a novel resource for data analysts and researchers, expanding the capability of R in machine learning and AI applications. The implications of this study suggest potential for future expansions into more advanced RL methods and applications in various industrial and academic fields. Key keywords include reinforcement learning, R programming, Q-learning, policy gradient, and machine learning.
