This paper introduces L^NAS, a novel framework for optimizing neural architectures through continuous-action reinforcement learning (RL). The objective of this work is to address the limitations of existing neural architecture search (NAS) methods, which often rely on discrete search spaces or computationally expensive optimization techniques. L^NAS leverages a continuous-action RL approach to efficiently explore the architecture space, enabling the discovery of high-performing neural networks with reduced computational overhead. The proposed method employs a policy gradient-based RL agent that iteratively refines the architecture by optimizing a reward function based on validation performance. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that L^NAS achieves competitive or superior performance compared to state-of-the-art NAS methods, while significantly reducing search time. The results highlight the framework's ability to generalize across diverse tasks, including image classification and natural language processing. This research contributes a scalable and efficient solution to NAS, with potential applications in automating the design of deep learning models for real-world problems.  

**Keywords**: Neural Architecture Search (NAS), Reinforcement Learning, Continuous-Action Optimization, Deep Learning, Policy Gradient, Automated Machine Learning.
