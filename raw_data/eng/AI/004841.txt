This research addresses the challenge of enhancing spatial sound fidelity and semantic object prediction in auditory scenes, utilizing binaural audio sources. The study aims to improve the spatial perception of sound fields and accurately detect objects within audio environments, contributing to advancements in virtual reality and augmented reality applications.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel framework that combines semantic object prediction with spatial sound super-resolution techniques. Our approach leverages machine learning algorithms to process binaural sound data, enabling the prediction of objects and their spatial locations. The framework integrates advanced deep learning models to enhance sound resolution beyond the traditional limits by constructing high-fidelity spatial audio scenes from standard binaural inputs.

Results/Findings: The proposed system demonstrates significant improvements in spatial sound resolution and object prediction accuracy. Comparative analysis with existing methods indicates superior performance, where our model achieves enhanced auditory scene comprehension and more precise localization of sound-emitting objects. Notably, the approach yields high accuracy rates in diverse and complex auditory environments, suggesting its robustness and adaptability.

Conclusion/Implications: Our research presents meaningful contributions to the fields of auditory scene analysis and spatial audio processing. By enhancing spatial sound super-resolution and object prediction capabilities, the framework has potential applications in immersive media technologies, offering richer and more realistic soundscapes for users. Future work will explore the integration of additional sensory modalities, further extending the system's utility across various interactive audio-visual platforms.

Key Keywords: binaural sounds, spatial sound super-resolution, semantic object prediction, deep learning, virtual reality, augmented reality, auditory scenes.
