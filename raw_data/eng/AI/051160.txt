This paper presents a novel approach to image recognition through the development of Structured Binary Neural Networks (SBNNs). The primary objective is to improve the computational efficiency and storage requirements of deep learning models without significantly compromising recognition accuracy. Our methodology introduces a binary quantization scheme combined with structured network architectures that promote sparsity and regular connectivity patterns, optimizing both training and inference processes. Experimental evaluations on standard image classification benchmarks demonstrate that SBNNs achieve comparable accuracy to full-precision networks, while reducing model size and computation by a substantial margin. Furthermore, we analyze the effects of different binary structuring techniques on performance, highlighting the advantages in terms of hardware acceleration and real-world deployment potential. The findings suggest that SBNNs offer a promising solution for resource-constrained environments such as mobile devices and edge computing platforms. This work contributes to the growing field of efficient neural network design by integrating structured binary representations for robust and scalable image recognition. Keywords: binary neural networks, image recognition, model compression, structured sparsity, efficient deep learning.
