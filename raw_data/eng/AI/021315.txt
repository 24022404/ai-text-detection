Permutation synchronization aims to recover a set of unknown permutations from a collection of pairwise relative measurements. This paper introduces a novel probabilistic framework for permutation synchronization by leveraging the Riemannian structure of the Birkhoff polytope. Our approach models the pairwise relationships between permutations as probabilistic constraints, where the probabilities are derived from noisy observations. We formulate a maximum likelihood estimation problem on the Birkhoff polytope, which is then solved using a Riemannian optimization algorithm. This exploits the geometric properties of the Birkhoff polytope, leading to improved accuracy and robustness compared to existing methods. Experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that our algorithm achieves state-of-the-art performance, particularly in the presence of significant noise and outliers. The proposed method offers a robust and geometrically sound approach to permutation synchronization, with applications in various fields such as computer vision, robotics, and structural biology.

Keywords: Permutation Synchronization, Birkhoff Polytope, Riemannian Optimization, Probabilistic Inference, Geometric Optimization.
