The increasing volume and complexity of industrial big data necessitate efficient methods for time series feature extraction to enable real-time decision-making and predictive analytics. This paper addresses the challenge of extracting meaningful features from large-scale time series data in distributed and parallel computing environments. We propose a novel framework that leverages distributed computing architectures, such as Apache Spark, combined with parallelized feature extraction algorithms to handle high-dimensional and high-frequency industrial data. Our approach integrates domain-specific feature engineering techniques with scalable computational methods, ensuring both efficiency and accuracy. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed framework significantly reduces processing time compared to traditional methods, achieving up to a 40% improvement in runtime performance while maintaining high feature extraction quality. Furthermore, the framework is evaluated on real-world industrial datasets, showcasing its applicability in predictive maintenance, anomaly detection, and process optimization. The study highlights the importance of distributed and parallel processing in handling industrial big data and provides a scalable solution for time series analysis. This research contributes to the advancement of industrial data analytics by offering a robust, efficient, and domain-adaptive framework for feature extraction, with potential applications in smart manufacturing, energy management, and IoT-enabled industries.  

**Keywords**: time series analysis, feature extraction, distributed computing, parallel processing, industrial big data, Apache Spark, predictive analytics.
