Bayesian Reinforcement Learning (BRL) offers a principled approach to exploration-exploitation in reinforcement learning by explicitly modeling uncertainty. However, BRL often suffers from scalability issues, particularly in continuous state spaces. This paper introduces Cover Tree Bayesian Reinforcement Learning (CTBRL), a novel algorithm that leverages cover trees to efficiently approximate the Bayesian posterior and accelerate planning. CTBRL uses a cover tree to partition the state space, enabling efficient computation of the posterior distribution over value functions. We demonstrate that CTBRL significantly reduces the computational complexity associated with traditional BRL methods. Experimental results on benchmark continuous control tasks show that CTBRL achieves comparable or superior performance to existing BRL algorithms with significantly reduced computational cost. Specifically, CTBRL demonstrates faster learning rates and improved sample efficiency. These results highlight the potential of cover trees for scaling BRL to more complex, high-dimensional environments. Keywords: Bayesian Reinforcement Learning, Cover Trees, Reinforcement Learning, Exploration-Exploitation, Continuous Control.
