Detecting text that appears in curved or irregular shapes within natural images remains a significant challenge in computer vision and scene text analysis. This paper presents a novel approach, termed Conditional Spatial Expansion (CSE), for robust curve text detection. Our method formulates curve text localization as a region-growing problem, where the model progressively expands text regions based on spatially conditional predictions. A deep neural network backbone extracts multi-scale features, which are then integrated with the CSE module to adaptively capture and delineate arbitrarily shaped text. Extensive experiments on standard benchmarks, such as Total-Text and CTW1500, demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of precision, recall, and F-measure, particularly on images with highly curved and complex text layouts. The proposed approach also shows strong generalization across various challenging scenarios, including background clutter and varying orientations. This work offers a scalable and effective solution for curve text detection, with significant potential in applications such as document analysis, scene understanding, and automated information extraction from images. Keywords: curve text detection, conditional spatial expansion, scene text analysis, deep learning, image understanding.
