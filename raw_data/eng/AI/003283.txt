Cloudinary offers a comprehensive platform for managing images and videos on both websites and mobile apps, streamlining the entire process from uploading and storing media files to manipulating and optimizing them for various devices and use cases. Users benefit from rapid delivery of content through a dependable content delivery network (CDN), while developers enjoy robust APIs and intuitive admin features that make integration with existing systems straightforward. Despite these advantages, it's important to note that the free version of Cloudinary imposes a storage cap of 25 GB, which may necessitate an upgrade for projects requiring more capacity.
