This paper explores the application of deep reinforcement learning (DRL) to synthesize flight controllers for autonomous aerial vehicles. The objective is to develop controllers capable of robust and adaptive flight performance in complex and uncertain environments. We propose a novel DRL framework that directly learns control policies from high-dimensional sensor inputs without relying on explicit system identification or hand-engineered control laws. Our approach utilizes a deep neural network to approximate the optimal control policy, trained through interactions with a simulated flight environment. Results demonstrate that the DRL-synthesized controllers achieve superior performance compared to traditional PID controllers, exhibiting improved tracking accuracy, disturbance rejection, and robustness to parameter variations. Furthermore, the learned controllers show promising generalization capabilities when transferred to unseen environments. This research contributes a data-driven methodology for flight controller design, offering a potential pathway towards more autonomous and resilient aerial systems. Keywords: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Flight Control, Autonomous Vehicles, Neural Networks, Robotics.
