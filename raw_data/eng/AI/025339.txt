This paper proposes GasHis-Transformer, a novel multi-scale visual transformer approach designed for gastric histopathology image classification. The objective is to accurately classify gastric histopathology images into different categories, such as normal, inflammation, and cancer, which is crucial for diagnosis and treatment. Our approach utilizes a transformer-based architecture that incorporates multi-scale features to capture both local and global patterns in histopathology images. The GasHis-Transformer model is trained on a large dataset of gastric histopathology images and achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming traditional convolutional neural network (CNN) based methods. Our results show that the proposed approach can effectively classify gastric histopathology images with high accuracy, demonstrating its potential for clinical applications. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a multi-scale visual transformer approach for histopathology image classification and the achievement of superior performance compared to existing methods. This study highlights the effectiveness of transformer-based architectures in medical image analysis, particularly in the context of gastric histopathology image classification, and has important implications for the development of AI-powered diagnostic tools. Key keywords: gastric histopathology, image classification, visual transformer, multi-scale features, medical image analysis, AI-powered diagnostics.
