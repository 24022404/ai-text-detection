Road detection is a critical task in autonomous driving systems, requiring robust and accurate perception of the environment. This paper presents a novel approach to road detection by fusing LIDAR and camera data using Fully Convolutional Neural Networks (FCNs). The proposed method leverages the complementary strengths of LIDAR's precise depth information and the camera's rich texture and color data to enhance detection accuracy. A multi-modal fusion architecture is designed to integrate these data sources at both feature and decision levels, enabling the network to learn spatial and contextual relationships effectively. The FCN-based model is trained and evaluated on a publicly available autonomous driving dataset, demonstrating superior performance compared to traditional single-sensor approaches and state-of-the-art fusion methods. Experimental results show significant improvements in precision, recall, and intersection-over-union (IoU) metrics, highlighting the effectiveness of the proposed fusion strategy. The findings suggest that LIDAR-camera fusion with FCNs can significantly enhance road detection capabilities, contributing to safer and more reliable autonomous navigation systems. This work provides valuable insights into multi-sensor fusion techniques and their potential applications in advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) and autonomous vehicles.  

**Keywords**: LIDAR, camera fusion, road detection, Fully Convolutional Neural Networks (FCNs), autonomous driving, multi-sensor fusion.
