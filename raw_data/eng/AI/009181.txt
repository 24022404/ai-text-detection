This paper addresses the challenge of multi-agent decision-making in complex environments, where agents must coordinate effectively to achieve shared objectives. Traditional approaches often struggle with scalability and computational efficiency, particularly in large-scale systems. To overcome these limitations, we propose **Deep Coordination Graphs (DCG)**, a novel framework that integrates deep reinforcement learning with graph-based coordination structures. The DCG framework leverages graph neural networks to model agent interactions, enabling efficient learning of decentralized policies while maintaining global coherence. Our approach employs a combination of message-passing mechanisms and value decomposition to optimize joint actions in dynamic environments. Experimental results demonstrate that DCG outperforms existing methods in terms of scalability, convergence speed, and overall performance across a range of benchmark tasks, including cooperative navigation and resource allocation scenarios. The proposed framework not only enhances coordination in multi-agent systems but also provides a scalable solution for real-world applications such as autonomous vehicle coordination and swarm robotics. This work contributes to advancing the field of multi-agent reinforcement learning by introducing a robust and efficient method for modeling complex agent interactions.  

**Keywords**: multi-agent systems, reinforcement learning, coordination graphs, graph neural networks, decentralized decision-making.
