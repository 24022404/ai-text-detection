This paper addresses the challenge of fine-grained age estimation in uncontrolled environments, where existing methods often struggle with variability in lighting, pose, and expression. Our objective is to develop a robust and accurate age estimation system that can operate effectively in real-world settings. To achieve this, we propose an Attention LSTM Network that leverages the strengths of both attention mechanisms and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) architectures. Our approach focuses on learning subtle facial features that are highly correlated with age, while also capturing temporal dependencies in facial expressions. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art age estimation models, achieving a mean absolute error (MAE) of 2.5 years on a large-scale dataset. The key contributions of our research include the introduction of attention-based feature extraction and the application of LSTM networks to model temporal relationships in age estimation. Our work has significant implications for various applications, including forensic analysis, healthcare, and social media analytics, where accurate age estimation is crucial. Key keywords: age estimation, Attention LSTM, deep learning, facial analysis, computer vision.
