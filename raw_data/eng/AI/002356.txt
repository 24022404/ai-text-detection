This paper addresses the challenge of preserving depth perception in arbitrary style transfer, a critical limitation in existing methods that often overlook spatial structure during artistic rendering. We propose a novel depth-aware style transfer framework that integrates instance normalization with depth-aware feature modulation to maintain the spatial coherence of the stylized output. Our approach leverages a pre-trained depth estimation network to extract depth information, which is then used to guide the style transfer process. By incorporating depth-aware constraints into the instance normalization layers, our method ensures that the stylized images retain both the artistic style and the original depth structure. Experimental results demonstrate that our framework outperforms state-of-the-art style transfer methods in preserving depth perception while achieving high-quality artistic rendering. The proposed method is evaluated on diverse datasets, showcasing its robustness and versatility across various styles and content. This work contributes to the field of neural style transfer by introducing a depth-aware mechanism, enabling applications in augmented reality, 3D rendering, and digital art creation.  

**Keywords**: style transfer, instance normalization, depth perception, neural networks, image processing, artistic rendering.
