The Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) algorithm has gained significant attention in reinforcement learning due to its ability to balance exploration and exploitation through entropy regularization. However, the performance of SAC heavily depends on the choice of the entropy temperature, which is typically manually tuned or fixed during training. This paper introduces Meta-SAC, a novel framework that automates the tuning of the entropy temperature in SAC using metagradient optimization. By treating the entropy temperature as a learnable parameter, Meta-SAC dynamically adjusts it to maximize the expected return, eliminating the need for manual hyperparameter tuning. We evaluate Meta-SAC on a suite of continuous control tasks and demonstrate its ability to consistently outperform standard SAC and other baseline methods. Our results show that Meta-SAC achieves higher sample efficiency and final performance, particularly in environments with varying levels of complexity. The proposed approach not only simplifies the training process but also enhances the adaptability of SAC to diverse tasks. This work contributes to the advancement of reinforcement learning by providing a robust and automated solution for entropy temperature optimization, with potential applications in robotics, autonomous systems, and other real-world domains.  

**Keywords**: Reinforcement Learning, Soft Actor-Critic, Entropy Regularization, Metagradient Optimization, Hyperparameter Tuning, Continuous Control.
