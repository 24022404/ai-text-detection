Phase retrieval is a fundamental problem in signal processing with applications in areas such as microscopy, astronomy, and communication systems. This research addresses the challenge of accurately reconstructing signals from intensity-only measurements. Our objective is to introduce an innovative approach utilizing the Expectation Consistent (EC) signal recovery algorithm, enhanced by a hypernetwork structure, to improve the efficiency and accuracy of phase retrieval.

Methods/Approach: We propose an EC signal recovery algorithm that leverages a novel hypernetwork architecture. The hypernetwork is designed to optimize the parameters of the EC algorithm dynamically, adapting to the complexities of varying signal properties. This approach integrates the flexibility of neural networks with the robust theoretical foundation of statistical inference via EC, providing a powerful framework for phase retrieval.

Results/Findings: The proposed method demonstrates significant improvements in signal reconstruction accuracy and computational efficiency compared to traditional phase retrieval algorithms. Extensive experiments on synthetic datasets and phase retrieval benchmark problems reveal that our hypernetwork-based EC algorithm outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods, offering faster convergence rates and enhanced signal fidelity. The results indicate a reduced error rate in amplitude and phase reconstruction, showcasing the potential for scalability across diverse application domains.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a novel methodology for phase retrieval, offering a robust and flexible solution that combines statistical signal processing and advanced machine learning techniques. The successful application of a hypernetwork to tune EC algorithm parameters illustrates the potential of hybrid approaches in complex signal reconstruction tasks. Our findings have significant implications for improving imaging techniques and data analysis in computational optics, biomedical imaging, and beyond. Future work will explore the scalability of this approach to larger and more complex datasets, as well as its integration into real-time phase retrieval systems.

Keywords: Phase Retrieval, Expectation Consistent, Signal Recovery, Hypernetwork, Signal Processing, Computational Imaging, Neural Networks.
