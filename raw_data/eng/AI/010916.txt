This paper introduces AI Playground, a novel data ablation tool built on Unreal Engine, designed to facilitate the development and training of deep learning models. The primary objective is to provide an efficient and intuitive platform for data scientists to generate, manipulate, and analyze synthetic data, thereby enhancing the robustness and generalizability of AI systems. Our approach leverages the capabilities of Unreal Engine to create a versatile environment for data ablation, allowing users to simulate various scenarios, modify data distributions, and evaluate model performance. The results demonstrate the effectiveness of AI Playground in improving model accuracy and reducing overfitting. Key findings include significant enhancements in model robustness and adaptability, particularly in scenarios with limited or noisy data. The implications of this research are substantial, enabling the creation of more reliable and efficient deep learning models for real-world applications. By combining the power of Unreal Engine with advanced data ablation techniques, AI Playground contributes to the advancement of AI research, with potential applications in fields such as computer vision, robotics, and autonomous systems. Relevant keywords: deep learning, data ablation, Unreal Engine, synthetic data, AI models, computer vision, robotics.
