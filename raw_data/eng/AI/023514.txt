This paper addresses the challenge of few-shot segmentation of volumetric images, a crucial task in medical imaging and computer vision. Our objective is to develop an efficient and accurate approach for segmenting volumetric images with limited annotated data. We propose a novel 'Squeeze & Excite' guided framework, which leverages the power of attention mechanisms and few-shot learning techniques. Our approach utilizes a compact convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, combined with a 'Squeeze & Excite' block that selectively weighs the importance of features, allowing the model to focus on relevant regions. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets with significant reductions in annotation requirements. The key findings of this research highlight the potential of 'Squeeze & Excite' guided few-shot learning for volumetric image segmentation, enabling accurate and efficient analysis of medical images with minimal labeled data. This work contributes to the advancement of medical image analysis, computer vision, and few-shot learning, with potential applications in clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, and personalized medicine, and is relevant to keywords such as few-shot learning, volumetric image segmentation, attention mechanisms, and medical imaging.
