In the rapidly evolving field of graph analytics, efficiently computing the similarity between large-scale graphs poses a significant challenge due to the complex nature and sheer size of the data involved. This paper presents CoSimGNN, a novel graph neural network (GNN) framework designed to tackle large-scale graph similarity computation with high accuracy and scalability. The objective of CoSimGNN is to bridge the gap between computational efficiency and similarity precision in the realm of extensive graph datasets.

Our approach leverages advanced GNN architectures to learn hierarchical node embeddings that capture both local and global graph structures. Through a novel similarity learning mechanism, CoSimGNN efficiently computes graph-to-graph similarities by projecting learned embeddings into a lower-dimensional space that preserves their relational information. The framework employs a multi-stage training procedure enhanced by batch normalization and dropout techniques to ensure robustness and convergence.

Empirical evaluations on benchmark graph datasets demonstrate that CoSimGNN significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods in both speed and accuracy, particularly in large-scale scenarios. The results indicate an over twofold improvement in efficiency and a substantial enhancement in similarity precision, highlighting CoSimGNN's potential for practical applications.

In conclusion, CoSimGNN not only introduces a cutting-edge method for graph similarity computation but also sets a new precedent for handling large-scale graph datasets with enhanced computational feasibility. This research contributes to the broader field of graph analytics by providing innovative solutions that can be applied to various domains, including social network analysis, bioinformatics, and recommendation systems. Key keywords: graph neural networks, graph similarity, large-scale computation, node embeddings, CoSimGNN.
