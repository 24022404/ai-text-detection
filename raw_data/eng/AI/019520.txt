This paper introduces a novel approach to controlling dynamics on graphs using Neural Ordinary Differential Equations (NODEs). We address the challenge of learning and controlling complex, time-varying graph dynamics, which are prevalent in diverse applications such as social networks, biological systems, and transportation networks. Our method leverages the continuous-time representation offered by NODEs to model the evolution of node states on the graph. Specifically, we train a neural network to parameterize the derivative of the node states, allowing for flexible and accurate modeling of graph dynamics. Furthermore, we introduce a control mechanism within the NODE framework, enabling the targeted manipulation of node states to achieve desired system-level behaviors. We evaluate our approach on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its superior performance in predicting and controlling graph dynamics compared to existing discrete-time and traditional control methods. The results highlight the potential of NODE-based control for achieving precise and efficient manipulation of complex systems represented as graphs. Keywords: Neural Ordinary Differential Equations, Graph Neural Networks, Control Theory, Dynamical Systems, Graph Dynamics, Continuous-Time Models.
