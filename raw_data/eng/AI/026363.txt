The advancement of wireless communication systems necessitates efficient spectrum usage and dynamic control to enhance network performance. This study introduces a novel approach leveraging deep reinforcement learning (DRL) for radio control and signal detection, addressing the critical need for intelligent, adaptive systems in modern wireless networks.

Methods/Approach: We propose KeRLym, an advanced deep reinforcement learning agent designed to operate within the Gym RL framework. KeRLym utilizes state-of-the-art DRL algorithms to optimize radio frequency control and enhance signal detection accuracy. Leveraging the modular nature of the Gym environment, our agent systematically evaluates and refines its strategies through iterative learning and simulation.

Results/Findings: The application of KeRLym demonstrates significant improvements in both radio control precision and signal detection compared to traditional heuristic methods. Our experimental results indicate enhanced spectral efficiency and reduced error rates, highlighting the effectiveness of utilizing DRL techniques in real-time radio environments. The model's adaptive capabilities were tested across varying network conditions, confirming robust performance and adaptability.

Conclusion/Implications: This research underscores the potential of deep reinforcement learning in revolutionizing wireless communication systems. KeRLym exemplifies a powerful integration of DRL techniques into radio operations, promising substantial gains in efficiency and adaptability. The insights gained from this study pave the way for future advancements in intelligent network management and the development of autonomous communication systems capable of optimizing complex wireless environments.

Key Keywords: deep reinforcement learning, KeRLym, radio control, signal detection, Gym RL agent, wireless communications, network optimization, spectral efficiency.
