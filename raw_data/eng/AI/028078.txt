The objective of this research is to explore and advance spectral learning techniques applied to matrices and tensors, crucial data structures in various domains such as machine learning, signal processing, and data analysis. We propose innovative methods that leverage spectral properties to enable efficient learning directly on matrix and tensor data, extending beyond traditional approaches that typically rely on vectorized representations. By adopting a spectral learning framework, our approach captures intrinsic multi-dimensional patterns and dependencies more effectively. We employ eigenvalue decomposition and singular value decomposition to enhance the interpretability and robustness of learning algorithms. Our empirical study demonstrates that these spectral techniques achieve superior performance in tasks such as dimensionality reduction, clustering, and anomaly detection when compared to conventional methods. Notably, our approach exhibits significant improvements in computational efficiency and scalability, particularly with large-scale matrices and high-order tensors. The findings suggest that spectral learning on matrices and tensors holds promise for applications in areas ranging from computer vision to bioinformatics. This research contributes new insights into harnessing spectral methods for enhanced data analysis capabilities, positioning spectral learning as a powerful tool for tackling complex, multi-dimensional datasets. Key keywords include spectral learning, matrices, tensors, eigenvalue decomposition, and machine learning.
