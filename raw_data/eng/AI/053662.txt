The Jayson library is a highly efficient and lightweight JSON-RPC implementation tailored for Node.js, designed to simplify communication between clients and servers. It enables developers to easily expose server-side methods, allowing them to be executed remotely through the JSON-RPC protocol. By managing the serialization and deserialization of messages, Jayson ensures that data is accurately and efficiently transported between endpoints. Its user-friendly API streamlines the process of handling requests and organizing server-side logic, making it a practical solution for applications requiring robust and scalable remote procedure calls within the Node.js ecosystem.
