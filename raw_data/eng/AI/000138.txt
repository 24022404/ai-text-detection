This paper investigates the optimization of neural network weights and activation functions for time series forecasting. We propose a novel approach that combines adaptive learning rate optimization algorithms with a search strategy for selecting optimal activation functions for each layer of the network. The method aims to enhance prediction accuracy and robustness in time series data. Experiments were conducted on several benchmark datasets, including financial market data and climate measurements. Results demonstrate that the proposed optimization technique outperforms traditional methods, yielding a significant reduction in forecasting error and improved convergence speed. The findings suggest that dynamically adjusting both weights and activation functions can lead to more effective neural network models for time series prediction. This research contributes to the field by providing a practical and efficient solution for building high-performance forecasting systems.

**Keywords:** Neural Networks, Time Series Forecasting, Optimization, Activation Functions, Machine Learning, Adaptive Learning Rates.
