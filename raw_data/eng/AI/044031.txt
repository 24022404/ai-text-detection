This paper addresses the challenge of policy learning and adaptation in dynamic environments, where traditional reinforcement learning methods often struggle with sample inefficiency and poor generalization. We propose a novel approach, Importance Weighted Policy Learning and Adaptation (IWPLA), which leverages importance weighting to enhance the transferability of learned policies across varying tasks and environments. The method focuses on reweighting experience replay data to prioritize transitions that are most informative for the target domain, thereby improving adaptation performance. Our approach integrates a dynamic weighting mechanism into a policy gradient framework, enabling efficient fine-tuning with minimal additional data. Experiments conducted on benchmark environments demonstrate that IWPLA achieves superior adaptation accuracy and faster convergence compared to state-of-the-art baselines. The results highlight the effectiveness of importance weighting in reducing domain shift and improving policy robustness. This work contributes to the field of reinforcement learning by providing a scalable and efficient solution for policy adaptation, with potential applications in robotics, autonomous systems, and real-world decision-making tasks.  

**Keywords**: reinforcement learning, policy adaptation, importance weighting, domain shift, sample efficiency, transfer learning.
