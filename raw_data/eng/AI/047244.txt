The study introduces ASM2TV, an innovative adaptive semi-supervised multi-task multi-view learning framework designed to address challenges in leveraging multiple sources of information and tasks simultaneously, especially when labeled data is scarce. The framework aims to improve learning efficiency and outcomes across diverse applications where data comes from varied perspectives or modalities.

Methods/Approach: ASM2TV leverages the strengths of semi-supervised learning by combining a minimal amount of labeled data with a greater volume of unlabeled data. The framework employs a multi-task learning strategy to boost performance by sharing information across related tasks. Furthermore, it integrates multi-view learning to effectively utilize data from different views, capitalizing on complementary information from each perspective. The adaptive component of the framework ensures that learning dynamically adjusts to the quality and quantity of available data, optimizing the outcome.

Results/Findings: Empirical evaluations of ASM2TV demonstrate significant improvements in performance over traditional single-task or single-view approaches. The framework shows enhanced accuracy and robustness across various datasets and domains, indicating its versatility and effectiveness. Comparative analyses highlight its superiority in terms of generalization capabilities and efficient utilization of unlabeled data, as evidenced by outperforming baseline models and other state-of-the-art methods.

Conclusion/Implications: ASM2TV presents a substantial advancement in the field of machine learning, offering a versatile tool for applications ranging from image classification to natural language processing where multi-view and multi-task data are prevalent. The framework's adaptability to varying data characteristics and its efficient use of limited labeled data suggest promising potential for future research and applications in scenarios where obtaining large labeled datasets is impractical. Key innovations include the integration of adaptive learning with semi-supervised and multi-view methodologies, paving the way for more effective and scalable machine learning solutions.

Key Keywords: semi-supervised learning, multi-task learning, multi-view learning, adaptive framework, machine learning, data integration.
