This paper proposes an Efficient Coarse-to-Fine Non-Local Module designed to enhance the detection of small objects in images. The objective is to address the challenge of accurately identifying tiny targets within complex scenes, which is a common issue in various computer vision applications. Our approach employs a novel two-stage framework, combining a coarse-level attention mechanism with a fine-level non-local feature refinement process. This method enables the model to capture both global context and local details, leading to improved detection performance. Experimental results demonstrate that our module outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant gains in accuracy and efficiency. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a hierarchical non-local module and a sparse attention mechanism, which reduce computational costs while maintaining high detection accuracy. This work has important implications for applications such as object detection, surveillance, and autonomous driving, where the accurate detection of small objects is crucial. Keywords: object detection, non-local module, small object detection, computer vision, attention mechanism.
