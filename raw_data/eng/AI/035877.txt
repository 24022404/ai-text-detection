This paper addresses the challenge of developing artificial intelligence systems capable of continuous, unsupervised learning without catastrophic forgetting. We propose a novel Deep Bayesian Unsupervised Lifelong Learning framework that integrates deep neural networks with Bayesian inference to enable robust knowledge accumulation across sequential tasks. Our approach leverages probabilistic modeling to maintain uncertainty estimates over learned representations, allowing the system to adapt to new data while preserving previously acquired knowledge. We introduce a scalable variational inference technique, which efficiently updates both model parameters and prior distributions in response to evolving data streams. Experiments on benchmark unsupervised learning datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms existing approaches in retaining performance on earlier tasks, while achieving strong representation learning on new, unseen data. The framework also exhibits resilience to distribution shifts and provides reliable uncertainty quantification, supporting its deployment in dynamic, real-world environments. These results highlight the effectiveness of combining deep learning with Bayesian principles for unsupervised lifelong learning, paving the way for more autonomous and adaptable AI systems. 

Keywords: unsupervised learning, lifelong learning, deep learning, Bayesian inference, continual learning, representation learning, uncertainty quantification.
