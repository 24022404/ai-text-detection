3D point clouds are a fundamental data structure in various applications, including autonomous driving, robotics, and computer vision. Spectral analysis using Graph Fourier Transform (GFT) has shown promise for processing point clouds. However, traditional GFT methods often struggle to adapt to local variations in point density and geometry, limiting their effectiveness. This paper introduces a novel Region Adaptive Graph Fourier Transform (RAGFT) designed to address this limitation. RAGFT constructs localized graph Laplacians based on adaptive region growing, enabling the spectral basis to conform to the local structure of the point cloud. This allows for more accurate frequency analysis and improved signal processing. We demonstrate the effectiveness of RAGFT on point cloud denoising, completion, and classification tasks. Experimental results show that RAGFT outperforms existing GFT-based methods and other state-of-the-art techniques, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and robustness. The proposed method provides a powerful tool for analyzing and processing 3D point clouds with varying densities and complex geometries. Keywords: Point cloud processing, Graph Fourier Transform, Spectral analysis, 3D reconstruction, Region adaptive, Denoising, Classification.
