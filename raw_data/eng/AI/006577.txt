This paper addresses the challenge of optimizing learning rate policies in reinforcement learning algorithms, a crucial aspect of achieving efficient and effective learning in complex environments. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that adapts the learning rate dynamically, leading to improved convergence and stability. We propose a new method that utilizes a meta-learning framework to learn the optimal learning rate policy, leveraging techniques from deep learning and probabilistic modeling. Our results show that the proposed approach outperforms existing methods, demonstrating significant improvements in cumulative rewards and reduced training times. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of adaptive learning rate policies in reinforcement learning, with implications for applications in robotics, game playing, and autonomous systems. By introducing a new paradigm for learning rate adaptation, this work contributes to the advancement of reinforcement learning algorithms, enabling more efficient and effective decision-making in complex, dynamic environments. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, learning rate policies, meta-learning, deep learning, adaptive control.
