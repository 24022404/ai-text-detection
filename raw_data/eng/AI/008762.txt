This paper addresses the challenge of learning the base distribution in implicit generative models, a crucial component in generative adversarial networks (GANs) and variational autoencoders (VAEs). Our objective is to develop a novel approach that enables the estimation of the base distribution, which is typically assumed to be a simple distribution, such as a Gaussian or uniform distribution. We propose a method that leverages a combination of adversarial training and maximum likelihood estimation to learn the base distribution. Our approach utilizes a neural network to model the base distribution, allowing for flexible and complex distributions to be learned. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method in learning accurate base distributions, leading to improved performance in image and data generation tasks. Our contributions include a new perspective on learning base distributions, which has significant implications for the development of more efficient and effective generative models. Key keywords: implicit generative models, base distribution, GANs, VAEs, adversarial training, maximum likelihood estimation, neural networks.
