This paper introduces Deep Coordination Graphs, a novel framework designed to facilitate complex decision-making in multi-agent systems. The objective is to develop a more efficient and scalable approach to modeling and optimizing interactions among multiple agents. Our method leverages graph neural networks to learn effective coordination strategies, enabling agents to adapt and respond to dynamic environments. Key findings demonstrate significant improvements in system performance, particularly in scenarios involving partial observability and large numbers of agents. The results show that Deep Coordination Graphs outperform existing methods in terms of convergence speed and solution quality. This research contributes to the development of more sophisticated multi-agent systems, with potential applications in areas such as smart cities, autonomous vehicles, and distributed robotics. By combining concepts from graph theory, game theory, and deep learning, Deep Coordination Graphs offers a unique and innovative approach to addressing complex coordination challenges. Relevant keywords: multi-agent systems, graph neural networks, coordination, game theory, deep learning.
