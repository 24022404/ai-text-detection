This paper proposes a novel approach to image synthesis using Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) integrated with Capsule Networks. The objective is to improve the quality and diversity of generated images by leveraging the strengths of both architectures. Our method employs a Capsule Network as the discriminator in the GAN framework, allowing for more robust feature extraction and better preservation of spatial relationships. The generator network utilizes a variant of the U-Net architecture, enabling efficient image synthesis. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing GAN-based image synthesis methods, producing more realistic and detailed images. The use of Capsule Networks enhances the stability and generalizability of the GAN training process, leading to improved image quality and reduced mode collapse. Our research contributes to the advancement of image synthesis techniques, with potential applications in computer vision, robotics, and artificial intelligence. Key contributions include the introduction of Capsule Networks in GAN architectures and the demonstration of their effectiveness in improving image synthesis quality. Relevant keywords: Generative Adversarial Networks, Capsule Networks, Image Synthesis, Deep Learning, Computer Vision.
