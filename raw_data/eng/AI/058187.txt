This paper introduces a novel approach to evaluating Dst forecast models using Dynamic Time Warping (DTW) in conjunction with machine learning techniques. The objective is to improve the accuracy of Dst forecast evaluations by incorporating a more robust and flexible method for comparing time series data. Our approach employs DTW to measure the similarity between predicted and actual Dst values, allowing for non-linear alignments and providing a more comprehensive understanding of forecast performance. The results show that DTW-based evaluation outperforms traditional methods, particularly in cases where forecast models exhibit non-linear behavior. Key findings indicate that the proposed method can effectively identify subtle patterns and anomalies in Dst forecasts, leading to improved model selection and optimization. The use of DTW in Dst forecast evaluation has significant implications for space weather prediction and mitigation strategies, enabling more accurate and reliable forecasting. This research contributes to the development of more effective machine learning-based Dst forecast models and highlights the potential of DTW as a valuable tool in time series analysis and evaluation. Keywords: Dynamic Time Warping, Dst forecast, machine learning, time series analysis, space weather prediction.
