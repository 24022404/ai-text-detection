Latent variable models (LVMs) are powerful tools for uncovering hidden structure in high-dimensional data. However, learning these models is often computationally challenging, especially for large datasets. This paper introduces a novel spectral method for parameter estimation in LVMs. Our approach leverages tensor decompositions of data moments to provide provably consistent estimates of model parameters, circumventing the need for iterative optimization procedures typically employed. We demonstrate the efficacy of our method on synthetic data, showing significant improvements in computational speed compared to expectation-maximization (EM) algorithms, while maintaining comparable accuracy. Furthermore, we apply our method to a real-world topic modeling task, achieving competitive results with state-of-the-art variational inference techniques. These findings highlight the potential of spectral methods for efficient and scalable learning in latent variable models, offering a promising alternative to traditional iterative approaches. Key words: Latent variable models, spectral methods, tensor decomposition, parameter estimation, topic modeling.
