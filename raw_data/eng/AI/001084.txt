This paper introduces Neural Network iLQR (NN-iLQR), a novel reinforcement learning architecture for efficient trajectory optimization. Addressing the limitations of traditional iterative Linear Quadratic Regulator (iLQR) in complex, high-dimensional environments, NN-iLQR leverages a neural network to learn a control policy that approximates the optimal control sequence. Our method trains a neural network to predict iLQR's control updates, significantly reducing the computational cost associated with online trajectory optimization. We evaluate NN-iLQR on several challenging benchmark tasks, demonstrating its ability to generate smooth, dynamically feasible trajectories with substantial speed improvements compared to standard iLQR and other reinforcement learning algorithms. Results show that NN-iLQR achieves comparable or superior performance while reducing computation time by an order of magnitude. This approach offers a promising avenue for real-time control of complex systems, particularly in robotics and autonomous navigation. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Trajectory Optimization, iLQR, Neural Networks, Optimal Control, Robotics.
