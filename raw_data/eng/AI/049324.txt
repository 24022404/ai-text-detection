This paper presents a novel approach to vehicle make, model, and color recognition using Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), enabling view-independent identification. The objective is to develop a robust system that can accurately recognize vehicles from various angles and lighting conditions. Our method utilizes a deep learning architecture, leveraging a large dataset of images of vehicles from different viewpoints. The CNN model is trained to learn features that are invariant to viewpoint changes, allowing for accurate recognition regardless of the vehicle's orientation. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving high accuracy rates in recognizing vehicle make, model, and color. The proposed system outperforms existing methods, particularly in scenarios with significant viewpoint variations. This research contributes to the development of intelligent transportation systems, surveillance, and automotive applications, highlighting the potential of CNNs in computer vision tasks. Key contributions include the design of a view-independent vehicle recognition system, robust to various environmental conditions, and the demonstration of its superiority over existing approaches. Relevant keywords: Convolutional Neural Networks, Vehicle Recognition, View-Independent, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Intelligent Transportation Systems.
