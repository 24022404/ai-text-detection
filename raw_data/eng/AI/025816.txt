Video semantic segmentation, a crucial task for scene understanding, faces challenges due to distortions arising from compression artifacts and motion blur. This paper introduces a novel distortion-aware feature correction network designed to enhance segmentation accuracy in distorted video sequences. Our approach integrates a distortion estimation module with a feature correction module, enabling the network to dynamically adjust feature representations based on the severity of distortion. Specifically, the distortion estimation module employs a convolutional neural network to predict a distortion map, guiding the feature correction module in refining the feature maps. The feature correction module utilizes attention mechanisms to selectively enhance informative features while suppressing noise induced by distortions. Experiments on benchmark datasets with synthetically and realistically distorted videos demonstrate significant improvements in segmentation accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods. The proposed method effectively mitigates the impact of video distortions, leading to more robust and accurate video semantic segmentation. Keywords: Video Semantic Segmentation, Distortion Correction, Feature Correction, Deep Learning, Attention Mechanisms.
