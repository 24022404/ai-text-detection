The paper addresses the complex problem of airline crew pairing optimization, a critical task in airline operations focused on efficiently matching crews to flight schedules while adhering to regulations and minimizing costs. The objective of this research is to enhance the existing optimization processes by integrating advanced machine learning techniques to learn combinatorial patterns inherent in large-scale datasets. The approach involves the development and implementation of a novel algorithm that leverages deep learning models to identify and learn these patterns effectively, thereby providing valuable insights for decision-making in crew pairing. 

Our findings demonstrate that the proposed model significantly improves the performance of traditional optimization methods by reducing computation time and increasing the overall efficiency of the crew pairing process. Additionally, the system's ability to adapt to diverse flight scenarios and constraints showcases its potential to handle the dynamic nature of airline operations. The results indicate a substantial improvement in solution quality compared to conventional methods, leading to more cost-effective crew schedules and improved operational reliability.

In conclusion, this research contributes to the field of operations research and airline management by introducing a machine learning-driven approach that offers scalable and efficient solutions for crew pairing optimization. The implications of this work extend to enhanced operational planning and reduced operational expenses for airline companies. Keywords: airline crew pairing, optimization, machine learning, combinatorial patterns, large-scale datasets, operations research.
