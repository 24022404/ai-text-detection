This paper introduces a novel deep reinforcement learning (DRL) approach that merges multiple deterministic policy gradient estimations to enhance learning stability and performance. Our method addresses the inherent bias-variance tradeoff in policy gradient estimation by concurrently employing estimators with varying characteristics. Specifically, we combine high-bias, low-variance estimators with low-bias, high-variance estimators to create a more robust and adaptable policy learning framework. This merging is achieved through a dynamic weighting mechanism that adjusts the contribution of each estimator based on real-time performance metrics. We evaluate our approach across a range of continuous control benchmark tasks, demonstrating significant improvements in sample efficiency, final policy performance, and training stability compared to state-of-the-art DRL algorithms. The results highlight the effectiveness of dynamically balancing bias and variance in policy gradient estimation for enhanced DRL performance. Key words: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Policy Gradient, Bias-Variance Tradeoff, Deterministic Policy Gradient, Continuous Control.
