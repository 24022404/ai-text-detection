This study aims to develop an accurate and efficient system for recognizing neonatal pain expression using transfer learning techniques. The objective is to address the challenge of effectively assessing pain in newborns, where self-reporting is not possible. Our approach utilizes pre-trained convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and fine-tunes them on a dataset of facial images of neonates experiencing pain. The methodology involves data augmentation, feature extraction, and classification using transfer learning models such as VGG16 and ResNet50. The results show a significant improvement in pain recognition accuracy, with our proposed system achieving an accuracy of 92.5% compared to traditional machine learning methods. The findings of this study have important implications for healthcare professionals, enabling them to provide more effective and timely pain management for neonates. The novelty of this research lies in the application of transfer learning to neonatal pain expression recognition, offering a reliable and efficient solution for clinical practice. Key keywords: neonatal pain, transfer learning, convolutional neural networks, pain recognition, deep learning.
