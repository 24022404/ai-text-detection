This paper addresses the challenge of options discovery in complex environments, where an agent must learn to make decisions with limited resources. The objective is to develop a framework that enables efficient exploration and exploitation of options, given a budget constraint. We propose a novel budgeted reinforcement learning approach, which integrates a budget-aware mechanism into the option discovery process. Our method utilizes a hierarchical reinforcement learning architecture, combining a high-level planner with a low-level executor, to optimize option selection and execution under budget constraints. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of cumulative reward and option discovery efficiency. The key findings highlight the importance of budget awareness in options discovery, leading to more effective and efficient decision-making. This research contributes to the field of reinforcement learning, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, autonomous systems, and resource-constrained decision-making. Key keywords: budgeted reinforcement learning, options discovery, hierarchical reinforcement learning, decision-making under uncertainty.
