This paper addresses the challenge of balancing exploration and exploitation in reinforcement learning, particularly in safety-critical environments where early failures can be costly. We introduce a novel exploration strategy, Diverse Exploration for Policy Improvement (DEPI), designed to promote efficient and safe policy learning. DEPI leverages a set of diverse, behavior-agnostic exploration policies to sample a wide range of state-action pairs. These samples are then used to construct a conservative policy update that maximizes expected return while minimizing the risk of violating safety constraints. The diversity of the exploration policies is maintained through a repulsion-based mechanism, encouraging exploration of uncharted territories in the state space. We evaluate DEPI on a suite of benchmark tasks, demonstrating significant improvements in sample efficiency and safety compared to state-of-the-art reinforcement learning algorithms. Our results show that DEPI achieves faster convergence to optimal policies while consistently maintaining lower constraint violation rates. The proposed approach provides a practical solution for deploying reinforcement learning in real-world applications where both performance and safety are paramount.

**Keywords:** Reinforcement Learning, Safe Exploration, Policy Optimization, Constrained Optimization, Diversity, Robotics.
