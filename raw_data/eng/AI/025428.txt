This paper presents a novel shot boundary detection method, addressing the challenge of accurately identifying scene transitions in videos. The objective is to develop a robust approach that can handle diverse video content. To achieve this, a new extensive dataset is created, comprising a wide range of videos with varying characteristics. A mixed feature set is proposed, combining traditional and deep learning-based features to capture both local and global cues. The method is evaluated on the newly created dataset, as well as existing benchmarks, demonstrating improved performance and robustness compared to state-of-the-art techniques. The results show significant enhancements in precision and recall, particularly in cases with gradual transitions or complex scenes. The proposed approach contributes to the field of video analysis by providing a more accurate and reliable shot boundary detection method, with potential applications in video editing, summarization, and retrieval. Key keywords: shot boundary detection, video analysis, mixed features, deep learning, video processing.
