This paper presents a novel Bayesian and Machine Learning (ML) approach to estimating parameters for Influence Models (IM) in the context of Influence Model-based Reinforcement Optimization (IM-RO). The primary objective is to address the challenge of accurately inferring IM parameters, which are critical for modeling complex interactions and decision-making processes in dynamic systems. Traditional methods often struggle with scalability and precision, especially in high-dimensional and noisy environments. To overcome these limitations, we propose a hybrid framework that integrates Bayesian inference for probabilistic parameter estimation with ML techniques to enhance adaptability and computational efficiency. Our approach leverages a combination of Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling and neural network-based optimization to refine parameter estimates iteratively. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in parameter accuracy and system performance compared to conventional methods, particularly in scenarios involving large-scale networks and uncertain data. The proposed framework not only advances the state-of-the-art in IM-RO but also offers broader applications in areas such as social network analysis, multi-agent systems, and adaptive control. This research contributes a scalable, robust, and data-driven solution for influence modeling, paving the way for more effective optimization strategies in complex, real-world environments.  

**Keywords**: Influence Models, Bayesian Inference, Machine Learning, Reinforcement Optimization, Parameter Estimation, MCMC, Neural Networks.
