**Abstract:**  
Understanding precise part boundaries within 3D objects is critical for applications in robotics, computer graphics, and automated manufacturing. This paper addresses the challenge of learning accurate part boundaries directly from raw 3D point cloud data. We propose a novel deep learning framework that integrates hierarchical feature extraction with boundary-aware loss mechanisms to effectively segment point clouds at the part level. Our method employs a multi-scale neural architecture that captures both global context and local geometric cues, enabling robust recognition of complex object structures. Comprehensive experiments were conducted on standard benchmark datasets, demonstrating that our approach outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in both segmentation accuracy and boundary localization. The results highlight significant improvements in detecting fine-grained boundaries, particularly in scenarios with noisy or incomplete data. The proposed framework advances the field by enabling more reliable and efficient processing of raw 3D data, with promising implications for downstream tasks such as automated assembly, object manipulation, and 3D scene understanding.  
**Keywords:** 3D point cloud, part boundary detection, deep learning, segmentation, geometric feature extraction, object recognition.
