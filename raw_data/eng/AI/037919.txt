Breast cancer subtype classification is crucial for personalized treatment strategies. This paper introduces a novel hybrid approach combining Relation Networks (RN) and Localized Graph Convolutional Filtering (LGCF) to improve the accuracy of this classification task. Our method leverages RN to capture complex relationships between gene expressions, while LGCF focuses on extracting localized features within gene interaction networks. By integrating these two complementary techniques, the model effectively captures both global and local dependencies in gene expression data. Experiments on benchmark breast cancer datasets demonstrate that our hybrid model outperforms existing methods, achieving superior classification accuracy and robustness. The results highlight the potential of combining relational reasoning with graph-based feature extraction for enhanced cancer subtype prediction. Keywords: Breast Cancer, Subtype Classification, Relation Network, Graph Convolutional Network, Gene Expression, Machine Learning.
