This paper presents a novel approach to monocular depth estimation, leveraging deep multi-scale architectures to accurately predict depth maps from single RGB images. Our objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of depth estimation, a crucial task in various computer vision applications. We propose a hierarchical framework that integrates features from multiple scales, allowing our model to capture both local and global contextual information. Our approach utilizes a combination of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and upsampling modules to effectively fuse multi-scale features. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art techniques, achieving significant improvements in depth estimation accuracy and reducing errors by up to 20%. The key contributions of our research include the design of a novel multi-scale architecture and the introduction of a new loss function that optimizes depth estimation at multiple scales. Our work has important implications for applications such as autonomous driving, robotics, and 3D reconstruction, where accurate depth estimation is critical. Key keywords: monocular depth estimation, multi-scale architectures, deep learning, computer vision, CNNs.
