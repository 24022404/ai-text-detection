This paper presents a novel dynamic programming algorithm designed to optimize path-planning by incorporating informative measurements. The objective is to develop an efficient method for determining near-optimal paths in complex environments, balancing exploration and exploitation. Our approach utilizes a probabilistic framework to model uncertainty and integrates informative measurements to guide the path-planning process. The algorithm is evaluated through simulations, demonstrating its ability to achieve near-optimal solutions while reducing computational complexity. Key findings indicate that our method outperforms existing approaches in terms of path efficiency and information gain. The proposed algorithm has significant implications for autonomous systems, robotics, and other fields requiring efficient path-planning. By leveraging dynamic programming and informative measurements, this research contributes to the development of more effective and adaptive path-planning strategies, with potential applications in areas such as robotic exploration, surveillance, and navigation. Keywords: dynamic programming, path-planning, informative measurements, near-optimal solutions, autonomous systems, robotics.
