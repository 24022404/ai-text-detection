This paper proposes a novel approach to event prediction by introducing scalable joint models that incorporate uncertainty awareness. The primary objective is to develop a reliable and efficient framework for predicting events in complex systems, where uncertainty is inherent. Our approach combines advances in machine learning and probabilistic modeling to create a robust and scalable joint model. The model is designed to capture complex relationships between variables and quantify uncertainty in predictions. We evaluate our model on several benchmark datasets and demonstrate its superiority over existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and reliability. Key findings include significant improvements in prediction performance and uncertainty estimation, particularly in scenarios with high levels of noise and ambiguity. The proposed framework has important implications for applications in risk management, decision-making, and event forecasting, where reliable uncertainty-aware predictions are crucial. Our contributions include the development of a scalable and efficient joint modeling approach, which can be applied to a wide range of event prediction tasks, including those involving complex and dynamic systems. Relevant keywords: uncertainty-aware prediction, joint models, scalable machine learning, event forecasting, probabilistic modeling.
