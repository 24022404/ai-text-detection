This paper explores the intersection of deep graph matching and mixed-integer linear programming, investigating the efficacy of relaxation techniques in solving graph matching problems. Our objective is to develop a novel framework that leverages the strengths of both deep learning and optimization methods to improve the accuracy and efficiency of graph matching algorithms. We propose a new approach that combines deep graph neural networks with mixed-integer linear programming solvers, allowing for the incorporation of domain-specific constraints and relaxations. Our results show that careful relaxation of integer constraints can significantly improve the performance of graph matching models, but also introduce the risk of decreased accuracy if not properly controlled. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach on several benchmark datasets, achieving state-of-the-art results in terms of matching accuracy and computational efficiency. The key contributions of this work include the development of a flexible and scalable framework for deep graph matching, the introduction of a relaxation strategy that balances accuracy and efficiency, and the provision of insights into the trade-offs between relaxation and accuracy in graph matching problems. Our research has important implications for applications in computer vision, network analysis, and data science, where graph matching plays a critical role. Key keywords: deep graph matching, mixed-integer linear programming, relaxation techniques, graph neural networks, optimization methods.
