This paper introduces OpenSpiel, a novel framework designed to facilitate reinforcement learning research in games. The primary objective is to provide a unified platform for exploring various reinforcement learning algorithms and techniques in a wide range of game environments. OpenSpiel achieves this through a modular and extensible architecture, allowing for easy integration of new games and algorithms. The framework utilizes a combination of tree-based search methods and neural network architectures to enable efficient exploration and learning in complex game spaces. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of OpenSpiel in training agents to play multiple games at a high level, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods in several cases. The key contributions of this work include the development of a scalable and flexible framework for reinforcement learning in games, as well as the introduction of new algorithms and techniques for improving agent performance. OpenSpiel has significant implications for the field of artificial intelligence, enabling researchers to advance the state-of-the-art in game playing and explore new applications in areas such as multi-agent systems and human-computer interaction. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, game playing, artificial intelligence, OpenSpiel, multi-agent systems.
