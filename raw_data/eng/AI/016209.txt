This paper introduces a novel approach to model-based deep reinforcement learning, addressing the challenge of efficient exploration and accurate state representation in complex environments. We propose Variational State Tabulation (VST), a method that leverages variational inference to learn a probabilistic representation of the environment's state space. VST constructs a compact, tabular approximation of the state space, facilitating efficient planning and policy optimization. This learned state tabulation enables the deployment of classical reinforcement learning algorithms within the latent space learned by a variational autoencoder (VAE), significantly reducing the computational cost associated with deep reinforcement learning. Empirical evaluations across a range of benchmark tasks demonstrate that VST achieves superior sample efficiency and performance compared to existing model-free and model-based deep reinforcement learning algorithms. Key findings indicate that VST effectively balances exploration and exploitation, leading to faster convergence and improved policy quality. The method's ability to generalize across different environments highlights its potential for application in real-world robotic control and decision-making problems. Keywords: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Model-Based Learning, Variational Autoencoders, State Representation, Sample Efficiency, Planning.
