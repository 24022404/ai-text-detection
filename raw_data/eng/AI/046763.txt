Data-free compression has emerged as a critical challenge in deep learning, particularly for deploying models on resource-constrained devices. This paper introduces **MixMix**, a novel framework that leverages feature and data mixing to achieve efficient data-free compression without requiring access to the original training data. The proposed approach combines feature-level mixing, which enhances the generalization of compressed models, with data-level mixing, which synthesizes pseudo-data to approximate the original data distribution. By integrating these two strategies, MixMix effectively addresses the limitations of existing data-free compression methods, such as overfitting and performance degradation. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that MixMix achieves state-of-the-art compression rates while maintaining competitive accuracy, outperforming traditional data-free compression techniques by a significant margin. The framework is lightweight, scalable, and applicable to a wide range of neural network architectures. This work contributes a practical and innovative solution for deploying compressed models in real-world scenarios, particularly in edge computing and IoT applications.  

**Keywords**: data-free compression, feature mixing, data mixing, model compression, deep learning, edge computing.
