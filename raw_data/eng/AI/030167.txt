This paper addresses the challenge of short-term load forecasting by introducing a novel reinforcement learning-based approach for dynamic model selection. The objective is to improve forecasting accuracy by adaptively selecting the most suitable model from a pool of candidates based on real-time data. Our method employs a reinforcement learning framework that learns to optimize model selection through trial and error, leveraging historical data to inform decision-making. The results show that our approach outperforms traditional static model selection methods, achieving significant improvements in forecasting accuracy. Key findings indicate that the dynamic selection of models based on reinforcement learning leads to better handling of non-stationary load patterns and improved adaptability to changing environmental conditions. The proposed approach contributes to the development of more accurate and reliable short-term load forecasting systems, with potential applications in smart grid management and energy efficiency optimization. Relevant keywords include reinforcement learning, dynamic model selection, short-term load forecasting, and energy forecasting.
