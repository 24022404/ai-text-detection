This paper introduces MaskGAN, a novel approach for diverse and interactive facial image manipulation. The objective is to enable users to intuitively edit facial attributes in images while maintaining high realism and generating diverse outputs. Our method utilizes a generative adversarial network (GAN) conditioned on binary masks, allowing for localized and controlled modifications. The generator network learns to synthesize realistic facial regions based on the provided mask and surrounding context, while the discriminator encourages both realism and adherence to the specified modifications. We introduce a novel loss function that promotes diversity in the generated outputs. Experimental results demonstrate that MaskGAN can effectively manipulate facial attributes such as hair color, age, and expression, producing diverse and realistic results exceeding those of existing methods. The interactive editing capabilities and the diversity of generated faces represent a significant advancement in facial image manipulation. Keywords: GAN, facial image manipulation, image editing, conditional generation, diversity, interactive system.
