Visual attribute prediction is a challenging task due to subtle differences in attribute appearance and complex inter-attribute dependencies. This paper introduces Progressive Attention Networks (PAN) to address these challenges by progressively refining attention maps and leveraging attribute relationships. PAN employs a novel attention mechanism that iteratively focuses on relevant image regions, enhancing feature discrimination for individual attributes. Furthermore, a dependency modeling module captures and exploits correlations between attributes, improving prediction accuracy. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that PAN achieves state-of-the-art performance in visual attribute prediction. The results showcase the effectiveness of progressive attention refinement and attribute dependency modeling for improving attribute recognition. This research provides a valuable approach for attribute-based image understanding with potential applications in image retrieval, fine-grained recognition, and visual search.

Keywords: Visual Attribute Prediction, Attention Mechanism, Deep Learning, Convolutional Neural Networks, Attribute Dependency Modeling.
