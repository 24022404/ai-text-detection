This paper presents a novel approach to inverse reinforcement learning, introducing Deep Adaptive Multi-Intention Inverse Reinforcement Learning (DAMIRL). The objective is to develop an algorithm that can effectively learn multiple intentions behind an expert's behavior in complex environments. To achieve this, we propose a deep learning-based framework that adaptively identifies and learns from demonstrations of multiple intentions. Our approach utilizes a combination of reinforcement learning and intention-aware modeling to improve the efficiency and accuracy of learning. The results show that DAMIRL outperforms existing methods in learning complex behaviors and generalizing to new situations. Key findings include improved performance in multi-intention scenarios and enhanced robustness to variations in expert demonstrations. The proposed method has significant implications for areas such as robotics, autonomous driving, and human-computer interaction, where understanding and replicating complex human behaviors is crucial. By introducing a novel deep adaptive framework, this research contributes to the advancement of inverse reinforcement learning and intention-aware modeling, with potential applications in AI, machine learning, and robotics. Keywords: inverse reinforcement learning, multi-intention learning, deep learning, adaptive modeling, intention-aware systems.
