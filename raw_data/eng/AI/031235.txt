This paper addresses the challenge of achieving fast, universal style transfer for both artistic and photorealistic rendering. Current methods either require significant computational resources or are limited in the range of styles they can effectively replicate. We aim to provide a solution that unifies these capacities in a single framework.

Methods: Our approach leverages advanced neural network architectures, integrating convolutional layers designed for texture manipulation and color adaptation. By employing a transformer-based mechanism, our method enhances the efficiency and adaptability of the style transfer process. Additionally, we incorporate a dynamic content encoder to maintain the integrity of the original image during the transformation.

Results: Experiments demonstrate that our model significantly outperforms existing methods in terms of speed, achieving real-time rendering capabilities with minimal resources. The system is adept at handling a diverse spectrum of styles, providing high-quality results that convincingly preserve the artistic or photorealistic intent. Quantitative evaluations show improved results on standard benchmarks, including style diversity and content preservation metrics.

Conclusion: This research presents a pivotal advancement in the field of style transfer, offering a robust, efficient solution for both artistic and photorealistic applications. By marrying speed with versatility, the proposed method expands the potential for real-time artistic rendering in various domains, such as digital art creation, virtual reality applications, and image editing software. Keywords: Style Transfer, Neural Networks, Transformer, Artistic Rendering, Photorealistic Rendering, Real-Time Processing.
