This paper addresses the challenge of Vehicle Re-Identification (Re-ID) in surround-view camera systems, aiming to accurately match vehicles across different cameras and viewpoints. Our approach leverages a deep learning-based framework, combining convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and metric learning techniques to learn robust vehicle representations. The proposed method is evaluated on a large-scale dataset, demonstrating significant improvements in Re-ID accuracy and efficiency compared to existing methods. Key findings include the effectiveness of multi-task learning and the importance of viewpoint-aware feature extraction. The research contributes to the development of intelligent transportation systems, enabling enhanced vehicle tracking, surveillance, and safety applications. The novelty of this work lies in its ability to handle the complexities of surround-view camera systems, providing a reliable and efficient solution for vehicle Re-ID. Relevant keywords: Vehicle Re-Identification, Surround-view Camera System, Deep Learning, CNNs, Metric Learning, Intelligent Transportation Systems.
