This paper addresses the challenge of accurate facial expression recognition, a critical component in human-computer interaction, by enhancing the performance of Vision Transformers. Traditional facial expression recognition systems often struggle with capturing subtle nuances in expressions due to inadequate feature extraction.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel integration of Squeeze and Excitation (SE) networks within the Vision Transformer framework to improve attention to significant features and enhance expression classification. Our approach employs SE modules to adaptively recalibrate feature responses, thus refining the attention mechanism inherent in transformers and yielding more relevant facial feature representations.

Results/Findings: The proposed model demonstrates superior performance compared to conventional convolutional neural networks and standard Vision Transformer models on multiple benchmark datasets. Through extensive experiments, we show that incorporating SE modules significantly increases accuracy in recognizing a diverse set of facial expressions. Our method achieves a notable improvement in precision and recall metrics, evidencing its robustness and efficacy in complex, real-world scenarios.

Conclusion/Implications: By enhancing the Vision Transformer with Squeeze and Excitation blocks, we establish a new benchmark for facial expression recognition systems, offering a scalable and adaptable solution that can be potentially applied to other domains requiring nuanced image classification. This research contributes to the development of more responsive and intuitive AI systems for improved human-computer interaction, with implications for a wide range of applications from virtual reality to emotional analytics.

Keywords: Vision Transformer, Facial Expression Recognition, Squeeze and Excitation, Attention Mechanism, Human-Computer Interaction, Image Classification.
