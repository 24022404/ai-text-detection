Object detection remains a fundamental challenge in computer vision, particularly in achieving high accuracy across objects of varying scales and complex backgrounds. This paper introduces RON: Reverse Connection with Objectness Prior Networks, a novel architecture designed to enhance object detection performance by integrating reverse connections and objectness prior modules within deep convolutional neural networks. The proposed framework addresses limitations of conventional forward-only feature propagation by allowing higher-level semantic information to flow backward to earlier layers, thus enabling more robust multi-scale feature representation. Additionally, the incorporation of objectness prior modules guides the network to focus on potential object locations, effectively reducing computational redundancy and improving detection precision. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that RON achieves superior accuracy and efficiency compared to state-of-the-art object detection models, particularly in detecting small and occluded objects. The proposed method is end-to-end trainable and compatible with various backbone networks. These findings underscore the advantages of integrating reverse connections and objectness priors, offering valuable contributions for future advancements in intelligent image analysis and real-time object detection systems. Keywords: object detection, reverse connection, objectness prior, convolutional neural networks, multi-scale features, deep learning.
