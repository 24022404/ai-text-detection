De Bruijn sequences and their generalizations, such as universal cycles, along with the associated graphs, have garnered considerable interest due to their extensive range of important applications. Initially, their utility was recognized in the context of shift-register sequences and feedback registers, where they played a crucial role in designing efficient coding and cryptographic systems. Over time, the relevance of de Bruijn sequences has only expanded, as researchers have discovered new uses across a variety of fields, demonstrating the sequences’ versatility and impact. The subsequent section delves deeper into these diverse applications.
