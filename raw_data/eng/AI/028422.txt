This paper addresses the challenge of optimizing correlation clustering on large-scale datasets, a crucial task in data mining and machine learning. The objective is to develop an efficient algorithm that can handle massive amounts of data while maintaining high clustering quality. To achieve this, we propose a novel approach that leverages a combination of distributed computing and advanced optimization techniques. Our method utilizes a parallelized framework to speed up the clustering process, while incorporating a robust objective function to ensure accurate correlation clustering. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of scalability, efficiency, and clustering accuracy. Key findings include a significant reduction in computational time and an improvement in cluster quality, particularly on large-scale datasets. The implications of this research are substantial, enabling the application of correlation clustering to real-world problems in fields such as social network analysis, recommendation systems, and bioinformatics. Our contributions include a scalable and efficient correlation clustering algorithm, with potential applications in big data analytics, machine learning, and data science, and relevant keywords include correlation clustering, distributed computing, optimization techniques, and large-scale data analysis.
