Facial Expression Recognition (FER) plays a crucial role in human-computer interaction. While Vision Transformers (ViTs) have shown promise in various vision tasks, their application to FER has been limited, often overlooking fine-grained local features critical for recognizing subtle expressions. This paper proposes a novel Vision Transformer architecture incorporating Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) blocks to enhance the feature representation learning for FER. Our method, named SE-ViT, integrates SE blocks into the ViT architecture to adaptively recalibrate channel-wise feature responses, emphasizing informative facial regions and suppressing less relevant ones. We evaluate SE-ViT on several benchmark FER datasets, including AffectNet and FER-2013. Experimental results demonstrate that SE-ViT achieves state-of-the-art performance, surpassing existing CNN-based and ViT-based methods. Specifically, SE-ViT shows improved accuracy in recognizing expressions with subtle muscle movements. The results highlight the effectiveness of integrating channel attention mechanisms within ViTs for FER. The proposed SE-ViT architecture offers a promising approach to improve the accuracy and robustness of FER systems.
Keywords: Facial Expression Recognition, Vision Transformer, Squeeze-and-Excitation, Channel Attention, Deep Learning.
