This paper introduces Matrix Nets, a novel deep architecture designed to enhance object detection capabilities. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of detecting objects within images by leveraging a unique matrix-based representation. Our approach employs a combination of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and matrix factorization techniques to learn robust features and reduce computational complexity. The Matrix Nets architecture is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in detection accuracy and speed compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. Key findings include the ability to detect small objects with high precision and the robustness to varying lighting conditions. The proposed architecture contributes to the field of computer vision by providing a more efficient and effective solution for object detection, with potential applications in areas such as autonomous vehicles, surveillance, and robotics. The novelty of Matrix Nets lies in its ability to capture complex object relationships and contextual information, making it a valuable addition to the field of deep learning-based object detection, including keywords: object detection, deep learning, CNNs, matrix factorization, computer vision.
