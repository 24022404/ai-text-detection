This paper introduces a novel approach, "Choose Your Neuron" (CYN), to incorporate domain knowledge into neural networks by strategically influencing neuron importance during training. Our method leverages a domain knowledge representation to guide the network's learning process, emphasizing neurons that align with established principles and down-weighting those that contradict them. CYN utilizes a regularized loss function that penalizes deviations from the desired neuron importance profile, allowing for a flexible integration of expert knowledge without requiring extensive retraining or architectural changes. We evaluate CYN on image classification and time series forecasting tasks, demonstrating significant improvements in accuracy, robustness, and interpretability compared to standard training methods and existing knowledge injection techniques. Results show that CYN allows for faster convergence and improved generalization, especially in data-scarce scenarios. The proposed method offers a practical and effective means of aligning neural network behavior with domain expertise, enhancing the reliability and trustworthiness of AI systems. Keywords: domain knowledge, neuron importance, neural networks, knowledge injection, machine learning, interpretability.
