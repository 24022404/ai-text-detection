This paper proposes a novel approach to embedding multiplex bipartite networks using dual hypergraph convolutional networks. The objective is to effectively capture the complex relationships and structural information in these networks, which consist of multiple types of interactions between two sets of nodes. Our method employs two hypergraph convolutional networks, one for each set of nodes, to learn node representations that preserve both local and global network structures. The dual-network architecture enables the capture of heterogeneous relationships between nodes, while the hypergraph convolutional layers allow for the aggregation of information from high-order neighbors. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our approach over existing network embedding methods, with significant improvements in node classification, link prediction, and network reconstruction tasks. The proposed framework has important implications for various applications, including recommendation systems, social network analysis, and knowledge graph embedding. Key contributions include the introduction of dual hypergraph convolutional networks for multiplex bipartite network embedding and the demonstration of their effectiveness in capturing complex network structures. Relevant keywords: network embedding, hypergraph convolutional networks, multiplex bipartite networks, node representation learning, graph neural networks.
