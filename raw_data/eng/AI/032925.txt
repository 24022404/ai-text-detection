Facial Expression Recognition (FER) is a critical task in computer vision with applications in human-computer interaction, emotion analysis, and security. This paper presents a novel approach that enhances Vision Transformer (ViT) architecture with Squeeze and Excitation (SE) modules to improve FER performance. The proposed model, termed SE-ViT, integrates SE blocks into the transformer layers, enabling dynamic recalibration of channel-wise feature responses and promoting more discriminative representation learning. Extensive experiments were conducted on benchmark FER datasets, including FER2013 and AffectNet, to evaluate the effectiveness of the approach. Results demonstrate that SE-ViT consistently outperforms baseline ViT and several state-of-the-art FER methods in terms of classification accuracy and robustness to facial variations. The study further analyzes the impact of SE integration on the attention mechanism, highlighting its role in adaptive feature enhancement. The findings indicate that combining transformer-based architectures with SE modules provides a powerful framework for FER, leading to more accurate and reliable expression classification in real-world scenarios. This research contributes a new perspective to architectural design for FER systems and opens avenues for future exploration in efficient and effective deep learning models for facial analysis.

Keywords: facial expression recognition, vision transformer, squeeze and excitation, deep learning, emotion analysis, computer vision.
