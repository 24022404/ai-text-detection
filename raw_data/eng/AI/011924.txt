Image-to-image translation is a critical task in computer vision, enabling the transformation of images from one domain to another while preserving essential features. This paper introduces U-GAT-IT, a novel unsupervised generative attentional network that leverages adaptive layer-instance normalization (AdaLIN) to enhance the quality and flexibility of image translation. The proposed framework incorporates attention mechanisms to focus on domain-specific regions, improving the translation of fine-grained details. AdaLIN dynamically adjusts normalization parameters, enabling the model to adaptively control the balance between style and content during the translation process. Experiments on multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate that U-GAT-IT outperforms existing methods in terms of visual quality and structural consistency. The results highlight the effectiveness of attention mechanisms and adaptive normalization in unsupervised image-to-image translation. This work contributes to advancing generative models by providing a robust and versatile framework for domain adaptation tasks, with potential applications in image editing, style transfer, and data augmentation.  

**Keywords**: Image-to-image translation, unsupervised learning, generative networks, attention mechanisms, adaptive normalization, AdaLIN.
