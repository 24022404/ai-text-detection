This paper introduces a Multivariate Bayesian Structural Time Series (MBSTS) model designed to address the challenges of forecasting and analyzing complex, high-dimensional time series data. The objective of this research is to provide a robust framework capable of capturing dependencies across multiple time series while incorporating uncertainty quantification. The proposed MBSTS model leverages Bayesian inference to estimate latent components such as trends, seasonality, and external regressors, enabling flexible and interpretable modeling. A key innovation lies in its ability to handle missing data and adapt to non-stationary patterns, making it suitable for real-world applications in finance, healthcare, and environmental monitoring. Experimental results demonstrate the model's superior performance compared to traditional univariate and multivariate time series approaches, particularly in scenarios with sparse or noisy data. The MBSTS model also offers computational efficiency through scalable inference algorithms, ensuring practicality for large-scale datasets. The findings highlight the model's potential for improving decision-making processes in dynamic environments, offering a versatile tool for researchers and practitioners. Keywords: Bayesian inference, time series forecasting, multivariate analysis, uncertainty quantification, latent components.
