This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing low-resolution images into high-resolution ones with an interactive, user-centered approach. Traditional super-resolution methods often lack transparency and user control, limiting their applicability in scenarios requiring detailed visual exploration and modification.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel system, termed Explorable Super Resolution, integrating advanced neural networks with an intuitive user interface. The system leverages a combination of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and attention mechanisms to achieve high-quality image upscaling. It uniquely facilitates user interaction, allowing adjustments in real-time to focus on specific image areas, thus providing enhanced oversight and customization in the super-resolution process.

Results/Findings: Through extensive experimentation, our approach demonstrates superior performance over conventional methods, achieving state-of-the-art results in both qualitative and quantitative metrics. The interactive component significantly improves user satisfaction and engagement by allowing tailored enhancements, particularly in professional fields like digital forensics, medical imaging, and content creation, where precise resolution adjustments are crucial.

Conclusion/Implications: The Explorable Super Resolution framework not only advances the technical boundaries of image enhancement but also introduces a paradigm shift by placing users at the center of the super-resolution process. This opens new avenues for research and development in interactive image processing applications, offering significant implications for industries reliant on high-resolution imagery.

Keywords: Super Resolution, Interactive Image Processing, Convolutional Neural Networks, User-Centered Design, Image Enhancement.
