The creation of large, labeled datasets is a significant bottleneck in training high-performing machine learning models, particularly in areas like image generation and manipulation. This paper introduces DatasetGAN, a novel framework designed to efficiently generate labeled data with minimal human effort. Our approach leverages the generative power of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) coupled with a user-friendly interface for specifying data characteristics. Instead of manually labeling individual samples, users interact with the GAN's latent space to define semantic attributes, effectively creating a labeled dataset by mapping latent vectors to corresponding labels. We demonstrate the efficacy of DatasetGAN in generating high-quality labeled data for various image domains. Experiments show that models trained on data synthesized using DatasetGAN achieve comparable or superior performance to those trained on manually labeled datasets, while significantly reducing human annotation time. This approach offers a promising avenue for democratizing access to labeled data and accelerating the development of data-hungry machine learning applications. Keywords: GAN, data augmentation, labeled data, synthetic data, machine learning, image generation, data efficiency.
