**Abstract:**  
This paper introduces Graph-to-3D, an end-to-end framework for the automatic generation and interactive manipulation of 3D scenes from high-level scene graphs. Scene graphs, which provide structured semantic representations of objects and their relationships, are increasingly popular for visual understanding tasks, yet their direct translation to comprehensive 3D environments remains a significant challenge. Our approach leverages deep neural architectures that encode the input scene graph, decode it into parameterized 3D object configurations, and assemble coherent scenes while preserving spatial and relational constraints. We incorporate a novel scene graph encoder, a differentiable layout generator, and a 3D asset synthesizer that work jointly to achieve semantics-aware scene construction. Experimental results on standard datasets demonstrate that Graph-to-3D outperforms existing methods in generating spatially accurate and visually realistic 3D scenes, with further support for real-time user-driven modifications through interactive graph editing. This work opens pathways for applications in virtual reality, simulation, and content creation, offering enhanced controllability and interpretability for 3D scene generation. Key contributions include the end-to-end mapping of scene graphs to 3D spaces, support for interactive manipulation, and comprehensive performance benchmarking.  
**Keywords:** 3D scene generation, scene graphs, deep learning, end-to-end framework, semantic modeling, computer graphics, interactive manipulation, virtual reality.
