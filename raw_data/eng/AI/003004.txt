This paper presents a novel approach to time series forecasting using Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks, incorporating symbolic representation to enhance prediction accuracy. The objective is to improve forecasting performance by leveraging the strengths of both numerical and symbolic data processing. Our method employs a hybrid framework, combining LSTM's capability to learn complex patterns with symbolic techniques to extract meaningful features from time series data. The results show significant improvements in forecasting accuracy compared to traditional LSTM-based approaches, particularly in handling non-stationary and noisy data. Key findings indicate that the proposed symbolic LSTM approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods in various benchmark datasets. The conclusion highlights the potential of this approach to revolutionize time series forecasting, enabling more accurate predictions and informed decision-making in fields such as finance, economics, and environmental monitoring. Keywords: Time Series Forecasting, LSTM Networks, Symbolic Approach, Deep Learning, Predictive Modeling.
