This paper addresses the critical issue of privacy preservation in Visual Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) systems. The objective is to develop a framework that enables accurate and efficient mapping while protecting sensitive visual information from being exposed. Our approach involves a novel combination of differential privacy and homomorphic encryption techniques to ensure that visual data remains confidential during the SLAM process. We propose a modified SLAM algorithm that incorporates privacy-preserving mechanisms, allowing for the creation of maps without revealing individual image features. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method in maintaining privacy while achieving comparable mapping accuracy to traditional SLAM systems. The key findings of this research highlight the potential for privacy-preserving Visual SLAM to be applied in various fields, including robotics, autonomous vehicles, and augmented reality, where privacy concerns are paramount. Our contributions include the development of a privacy-preserving Visual SLAM framework, which fills a significant gap in the existing literature, and demonstrates the feasibility of balancing privacy and mapping accuracy in SLAM systems. Key keywords: Visual SLAM, privacy preservation, differential privacy, homomorphic encryption, robotics, autonomous systems.
