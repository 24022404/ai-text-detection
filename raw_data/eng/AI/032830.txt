This paper revisits the calibration of modern neural networks, addressing the critical issue of mismatch between predicted probabilities and true probabilities. Our objective is to develop a more accurate and reliable calibration method, overcoming the limitations of existing approaches. We propose a novel approach that combines Bayesian neural networks with a specially designed calibration layer, leveraging techniques from uncertainty estimation and deep learning. Our method is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in calibration performance compared to state-of-the-art methods. The results show that our approach achieves better calibration, particularly in cases where the data distribution is complex or shifted. Our research contributes to the development of more trustworthy and reliable neural networks, with potential applications in areas such as computer vision, natural language processing, and decision-making systems. Key aspects of our work include neural network calibration, uncertainty estimation, Bayesian deep learning, and reliable AI systems.
