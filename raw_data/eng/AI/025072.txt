Object detection and pose estimation are fundamental tasks in computer vision, crucial for applications ranging from robotics to augmented reality. Monocular vision-based approaches are particularly appealing due to their simplicity and cost-effectiveness, but present challenges in handling scale ambiguity and occlusions. This survey provides a comprehensive overview of recent advances in joint object detection and pose estimation from a single RGB image. We categorize existing methods based on their underlying techniques, including direct regression, keypoint-based approaches, and template matching, highlighting their strengths and weaknesses. Furthermore, we analyze the datasets and evaluation metrics commonly used in this field, identifying open challenges and future research directions. This survey contributes to a better understanding of the current state-of-the-art and offers insights for developing more robust and accurate monocular vision-based systems. Keywords: object detection, pose estimation, monocular vision, computer vision, survey.
