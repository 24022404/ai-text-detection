This paper introduces a novel multi-task based lifelong learning approach, Regularize, Expand and Compress (REC), which leverages NonExpansive AutoML to efficiently adapt to continuously arriving tasks. The objective is to develop a flexible and scalable framework that can learn from a stream of tasks without requiring prior knowledge of the task sequence or suffering from catastrophic forgetting. Our approach combines regularization techniques to prevent overfitting, expansion methods to incorporate new tasks, and compression algorithms to retain essential knowledge. The REC framework is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its ability to outperform existing lifelong learning methods in terms of accuracy, efficiency, and adaptability. Key findings include improved performance on subsequent tasks, reduced forgetting of previously learned tasks, and enhanced robustness to task variability. The proposed REC approach contributes to the field of lifelong learning by providing a versatile and automated framework for multi-task learning, with potential applications in areas such as autonomous systems, recommender systems, and edge AI. Relevant keywords: lifelong learning, multi-task learning, AutoML, non-expansive learning, regularization, compression, edge AI.
