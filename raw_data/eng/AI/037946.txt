Graph generation is a critical task in various domains, including social network analysis, molecular design, and recommendation systems. This paper introduces *GraphGen-Redux*, a fast and lightweight recurrent model designed for efficient labeled graph generation. The primary objective of this work is to address the computational inefficiencies and scalability limitations of existing graph generation methods while maintaining high-quality outputs. *GraphGen-Redux* leverages a novel recurrent architecture that integrates graph structure and node/edge label information into a unified framework, enabling the generation of diverse and realistic graphs. The model employs a lightweight encoding strategy to reduce memory overhead and computational complexity, making it suitable for large-scale applications. Experimental results demonstrate that *GraphGen-Redux* outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of generation speed, achieving up to 2.5× faster performance, while maintaining competitive accuracy in graph quality metrics such as edge reconstruction and label consistency. Furthermore, the model exhibits robust scalability, handling graphs with thousands of nodes and edges efficiently. The proposed approach opens new avenues for real-time graph generation tasks and provides a practical solution for applications requiring rapid and scalable graph synthesis. Key contributions include the development of a streamlined recurrent architecture, significant performance improvements, and a comprehensive evaluation across multiple benchmark datasets. Keywords: graph generation, recurrent models, labeled graphs, scalability, lightweight architectures.
