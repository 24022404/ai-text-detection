This paper presents a novel approach to multi-object tracking, introducing an unsupervised learning framework termed Tracking by Animation. The objective is to develop attentive trackers that can effectively follow multiple objects within a scene without requiring manual annotations. Our method leverages animation principles to generate synthetic data, which is then used to train a deep neural network. The network learns to focus attention on specific objects, enabling the tracking of multiple targets across frames. Results demonstrate the efficacy of our approach, outperforming existing unsupervised tracking methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. Key findings include improved handling of occlusions and enhanced ability to track objects with complex motions. The proposed framework contributes to the field of computer vision by providing a unique, annotation-free solution for multi-object tracking, with potential applications in areas such as surveillance, autonomous vehicles, and human-computer interaction. Our approach highlights the importance of attentive mechanisms in tracking tasks, paving the way for future research in unsupervised learning and multi-object tracking. Relevant keywords: multi-object tracking, unsupervised learning, attentive trackers, deep learning, computer vision.
