Video enhancement is crucial for improving visual quality in various applications, yet existing methods often struggle with computational efficiency and accuracy, especially when dealing with complex scenes. This paper introduces a novel approach to video enhancement leveraging sparse depth information to achieve both fast processing and high-quality results. Our method utilizes a lightweight depth estimation network to generate sparse depth maps, which are then fused with video frames to guide the enhancement process. This depth-guided approach enables targeted adjustments to contrast, brightness, and sharpness, effectively reducing noise and artifacts while preserving important details. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our method through extensive experiments on diverse video datasets. The results show that our approach achieves comparable or superior performance to state-of-the-art video enhancement techniques, while significantly reducing processing time. This makes it suitable for real-time applications and resource-constrained environments. The proposed method represents a significant advancement in video enhancement by balancing accuracy and computational efficiency.

Keywords: Video enhancement, sparse depth, depth estimation, image processing, real-time processing, noise reduction.
