Point cloud processing is crucial for various applications, including 3D reconstruction and autonomous navigation. While convolutional neural networks (CNNs) have achieved success in processing regular data, their application to irregular point clouds remains challenging. This paper introduces a novel convolutional decoder architecture designed specifically for generating high-quality point clouds. Our approach utilizes adaptive instance normalization (AdaIN) within the decoder to effectively control the style and distribution of the generated point cloud, enabling fine-grained control over the output. The proposed network architecture leverages hierarchical feature aggregation and spatially adaptive upsampling to progressively refine the point cloud structure. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of reconstruction accuracy and visual quality, surpassing existing point cloud generation techniques. The AdaIN-based convolutional decoder offers a robust and efficient solution for point cloud generation, enabling improved detail and fidelity in synthesized 3D structures. Keywords: Point cloud, 3D reconstruction, convolutional neural networks, adaptive instance normalization, generative models, deep learning.
