This paper investigates the application of deep learning architectures for automated video surveillance of highway traffic events. The primary objective is to develop a robust and efficient system capable of detecting and classifying various traffic incidents, including accidents, congestion, and unusual vehicle behaviors, in real-time. We propose a novel approach that integrates convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for feature extraction with recurrent neural networks (RNNs), specifically Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks, to model temporal dependencies within video sequences. The system is trained and evaluated on a large-scale dataset of highway traffic videos, achieving a significant improvement in detection accuracy compared to traditional methods. Experimental results demonstrate the system's ability to accurately identify and classify traffic events with a precision rate of 92% and a recall rate of 88%. This research contributes a practical and scalable solution for enhancing highway traffic management and safety through intelligent video surveillance. Keywords: Video Surveillance, Deep Learning, Traffic Events, CNN, RNN, LSTM, Intelligent Transportation Systems.
