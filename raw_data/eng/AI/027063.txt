Social media platforms are increasingly susceptible to the rapid spread of rumors, necessitating effective detection methods. This paper introduces a novel approach for rumor detection leveraging ensemble deep learning on time-series representations of tweet content. Our methodology involves transforming tweet text into time-series data using word embeddings and subsequently applying a combination of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) to capture both local and temporal features. An ensemble model is then constructed to integrate the outputs of these diverse deep learning architectures, enhancing the robustness and accuracy of rumor classification. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our ensemble approach outperforms state-of-the-art rumor detection techniques, achieving significant improvements in precision, recall, and F1-score. The proposed method provides a more nuanced understanding of rumor propagation patterns, contributing to the development of more reliable and efficient tools for combating misinformation in online social networks. Keywords: Rumor Detection, Social Media, Deep Learning, Ensemble Methods, Time-Series Analysis, Natural Language Processing.
