This paper introduces ProcK, a novel machine learning framework designed to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of knowledge-intensive processes. The primary objective of ProcK is to leverage advanced machine learning techniques to automate and optimize complex decision-making workflows, particularly in domains requiring extensive knowledge integration. 

To achieve this, ProcK employs a hybrid approach combining graph neural networks and natural language processing to model intricate process dependencies and knowledge relationships. The framework is trained on a comprehensive dataset of process instances and knowledge graphs, enabling it to learn patterns and make predictions that improve process outcomes.

Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of ProcK in reducing process execution time and enhancing decision quality compared to traditional rule-based systems. The framework's ability to adapt to changing process conditions and learn from feedback also shows significant potential for long-term process improvement.

The contributions of this research lie in its innovative application of machine learning to knowledge-intensive processes, offering a scalable and flexible solution for real-world applications. ProcK's impact is expected to be substantial in areas such as business process management, healthcare, and financial services, where complex decision-making is critical. Key aspects of this work include machine learning, process automation, knowledge graph embedding, and natural language processing, making it a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners in the field of AI and process management.
