This paper introduces Hue-Net, a novel image-to-image translation framework that leverages intensity-based information and differentiable histogram loss functions to achieve high-quality translations. The objective is to develop an approach that can effectively transfer the color palette and intensity distribution from a reference image to a target image. To accomplish this, we propose a deep learning model that utilizes a histogram-based loss function, enabling the network to learn the intricate relationships between pixel intensities and color distributions. Our method is evaluated on various image translation tasks, demonstrating superior performance compared to existing approaches. The results show that Hue-Net can successfully capture the nuances of color and intensity, resulting in more realistic and visually appealing translations. The contributions of this research lie in the introduction of a differentiable histogram loss function, which allows for efficient optimization and improved translation quality. This work has significant implications for applications such as image editing, colorization, and style transfer, and can be extended to other domains like video processing and 3D reconstruction. Key keywords: image-to-image translation, histogram loss functions, deep learning, color transfer, intensity-based translation.
