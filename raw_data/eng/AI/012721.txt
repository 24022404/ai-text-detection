This study aims to investigate the molecular structure-property relationships using a novel graph convolutional network (GCN) architecture, augmented with attention and gate mechanisms. The objective is to develop a deep learning model that can effectively learn the complex relationships between molecular structures and their corresponding properties. Our approach employs a GCN framework that incorporates attention mechanisms to focus on relevant molecular substructures and gate mechanisms to control the flow of information. The model is trained on a large dataset of molecular structures and their properties, and its performance is evaluated on several benchmark datasets. The results show that our model outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in predicting molecular properties, demonstrating the effectiveness of the attention- and gate-augmented GCN architecture. The key findings of this study highlight the importance of incorporating attention and gate mechanisms in GCNs for learning molecular structure-property relationships. This research contributes to the development of novel machine learning models for molecular property prediction, with potential applications in drug discovery, materials science, and chemical engineering. The proposed model provides a new framework for understanding the complex relationships between molecular structures and properties, and its innovations in attention- and gate-augmented GCNs pave the way for future research in this field. Key keywords: graph convolutional network, attention mechanism, gate mechanism, molecular structure-property relationships, deep learning, machine learning, molecular property prediction.
