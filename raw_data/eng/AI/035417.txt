This paper presents a novel approach to estimating Influence Model parameters for Intent-Based Radio Optimization (IM-RO) using Bayesian inference and Machine Learning techniques. The objective is to develop a robust and efficient method for modeling complex interactions between radio parameters and network performance. Our approach combines Bayesian modeling with machine learning algorithms to learn the relationships between input parameters and Influence Model outputs. The results show significant improvements in estimation accuracy and computational efficiency compared to traditional methods. Key findings include the ability to handle high-dimensional parameter spaces and non-linear relationships, as well as the potential for real-time optimization. The proposed method has important implications for the development of autonomous radio networks and can be applied to various wireless communication systems. Our research contributes to the advancement of Intent-Based Networking and Radio Optimization, with potential applications in 5G and beyond. Keywords: Influence Model, Bayesian inference, Machine Learning, Intent-Based Radio Optimization, IM-RO, wireless communication systems.
