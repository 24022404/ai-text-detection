This paper introduces a novel Multivariate Bayesian Structural Time Series (MBSTS) model designed for enhanced forecasting and causal inference in complex, high-dimensional time series data. Addressing the limitations of traditional univariate approaches, our MBSTS model leverages a Bayesian framework to capture intricate dependencies and dynamic relationships among multiple time series. The methodology incorporates a hierarchical structure with time-varying coefficients and latent factors, enabling the model to adapt to evolving patterns and uncertainties. We develop an efficient Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) algorithm for posterior inference, providing robust estimates of model parameters and forecasts. Empirical evaluations on synthetic and real-world datasets, including financial and economic indicators, demonstrate superior performance compared to existing state-space models and vector autoregression (VAR) techniques. The MBSTS model exhibits improved forecast accuracy, more reliable causal effect estimates, and enhanced interpretability through component decomposition. These results highlight the potential of the MBSTS model for a wide range of applications, including macroeconomic forecasting, financial risk management, and anomaly detection. Keywords: Bayesian time series, structural time series, multivariate models, MCMC, forecasting, causal inference.
