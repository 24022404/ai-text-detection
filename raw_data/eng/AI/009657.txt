This paper investigates the connection between free energy principles and optimality in sequential decision-making problems. We introduce a novel framework that generalizes the standard optimality equations, such as the Bellman equation, by incorporating a free energy term. This term, inspired by statistical physics and Bayesian inference, promotes exploration and robustness to model uncertainty. Our approach utilizes a path integral formulation to derive these generalized optimality equations. We demonstrate that solutions to these equations correspond to stochastic policies that balance expected reward and free energy. We analyze the theoretical properties of these policies, including their convergence and optimality guarantees. Furthermore, we present experimental results on benchmark reinforcement learning tasks, demonstrating improved performance and exploration compared to traditional methods, particularly in environments with sparse rewards or model uncertainty. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Free Energy, Optimality Equations, Sequential Decision Making, Stochastic Control, Path Integrals.
