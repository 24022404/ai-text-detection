The system's advantage lies in the self-located nature of its positioning sequence, which enhances its overall performance. However, in dBTS, encoding requires 2 pulse slots for each bit to maintain a balance between encoding efficiency and timing jitter. This approach leads to an information rate for the HdB sequence of 0.5, as formally defined in section 2.1.2. Maximizing this information rate is a key objective in optimizing system design.
