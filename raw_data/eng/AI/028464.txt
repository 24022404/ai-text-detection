The research addresses the challenge of achieving accurate volumetric segmentation in medical imaging, which is crucial for diagnosing and assessing various health conditions. Traditional methods often struggle with achieving high precision and detail due to computational constraints and noise present in medical scans.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel 3D coarse-to-fine framework designed to enhance volumetric medical image segmentation. This approach involves a dual-phase process: an initial coarse segmentation to quickly identify general regions of interest, followed by a fine segmentation phase that refines these areas for detailed analysis. The framework leverages deep learning techniques, specifically convolutional neural networks (CNNs), optimized for 3D image processing.

Results/Findings: The developed framework has been evaluated on established medical imaging datasets, demonstrating superior performance in terms of segmentation accuracy and computational efficiency compared to existing methods. Notably, it achieved higher precision and sensitivity metrics while reducing processing time, making it practical for clinical applications.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a robust and efficient method for volumetric medical image segmentation, providing enhanced accuracy and operational feasibility in medical diagnostics. Our framework advances current techniques, offering potential applications in various medical fields where precise image analysis is critical. Future work may explore further integration with real-time diagnostic tools.

Keywords: 3D segmentation, volumetric medical imaging, deep learning, CNNs, coarse-to-fine framework, medical diagnostics.
