The objective of this research is to address the limitations in existing bounding box regression methods, which often struggle with alignment precision in object detection tasks. We propose a novel loss function, SCALoss (Side and Corner Aligned Loss), explicitly designed to improve the accuracy of bounding box regression by aligning both the sides and corners of the predicted bounding boxes with the ground truth. Our approach integrates seamlessly with deep learning models commonly used in object detection pipelines. Extensive experiments, conducted on benchmark datasets, demonstrate that SCALoss consistently improves regression accuracy, outperforming conventional loss functions such as L1 and IoU-based losses. Our findings show an average increase in mean Average Precision (mAP) by 5%, highlighting the effectiveness of SCALoss in enhancing object detection performance. Notably, SCALoss provides robust enhancements without introducing significant computational overhead, making it highly applicable for real-time detection systems. This research contributes to the field by introducing a more precise loss function for bounding box regression, which can be particularly beneficial in applications requiring high localization accuracy, such as autonomous driving and surveillance systems. Key innovations of SCALoss lie in its unique alignment strategy, offering a promising advancement for future developments in object detection frameworks. Keywords: bounding box regression, SCALoss, object detection, mean Average Precision, deep learning models.
