**Abstract**

Robust preprocessing of time series data is a critical step in ensuring the reliability and accuracy of subsequent analyses and modeling tasks. This paper introduces **tsrobprep**, a comprehensive R package designed to address the challenges associated with noisy, incomplete, or outlier-contaminated time series data. The proposed package provides a suite of robust algorithms for data cleaning, outlier detection and correction, imputation of missing values, and normalization, all tailored specifically for time-dependent datasets. Through an intuitive and flexible interface, tsrobprep enables users to efficiently preprocess time series data with minimal manual intervention, ensuring resilience against common data quality issues. Extensive benchmarking against existing tools demonstrates that tsrobprep consistently achieves superior performance in terms of both accuracy and computational efficiency across a variety of real-world and simulated datasets. The package’s modular design supports integration into existing R workflows, making it suitable for data preprocessing in finance, environmental monitoring, industrial processes, and other domains reliant on time series analysis. By streamlining robust data preparation, tsrobprep facilitates more reliable forecasting, anomaly detection, and machine learning applications. **Keywords:** time series, robust preprocessing, R package, outlier detection, missing data imputation, data cleaning, anomaly detection.
