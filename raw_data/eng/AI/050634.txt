This paper presents a novel approach to modeling object dynamics in reinforcement learning, with a focus on relevance-guided modeling. The objective is to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of reinforcement learning algorithms by selectively focusing on the most relevant aspects of object dynamics. Our method employs a hybrid approach, combining model-based and model-free reinforcement learning techniques to learn a compact and informative representation of object dynamics. We utilize a relevance-guided mechanism to identify and prioritize the most critical factors influencing object behavior, thereby reducing the complexity of the modeling task. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of learning speed and accuracy, particularly in environments with complex object interactions. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of relevance-guided modeling in reinforcement learning, enabling more efficient and adaptive decision-making in dynamic environments. Our contributions have significant implications for applications in robotics, game playing, and autonomous systems, where accurate modeling of object dynamics is crucial for successful reinforcement learning. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, object dynamics, relevance-guided modeling, model-based reinforcement learning, autonomous systems.
