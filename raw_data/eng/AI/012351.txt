This paper addresses the challenge of identifying and geotagging objects from street view imagery, a task that is crucial for a wide range of applications such as urban planning, autonomous navigation, and augmented reality services. By automating this process, our research aims to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of identifying objects in diverse urban environments.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel approach that utilizes deep learning frameworks, specifically convolutional neural networks (CNNs), integrated with geographic information systems (GIS) to automatically discover and geotag objects from street view images. Our system processes images collected from public sources, applying image classification models to detect objects, followed by a coordination system that accurately assigns geographical data points to each detected object.

Results/Findings: The proposed method was evaluated using a large dataset of street view images, demonstrating a significant improvement in object detection accuracy and geotagging precision compared to existing methods. Specifically, our system achieved an average accuracy rate of over 90% for commonly found objects such as vehicles, street signs, and store fronts. Comparison with traditional manual tagging and other automated approaches highlights the method's efficiency and reliability.

Conclusion/Implications: The study contributes a robust solution for automatic object discovery and geotagging from street view imagery, offering a scalable and cost-effective tool for urban analysis. This research is significant for industries involved in geographical data processing and highlights the potential for integrating advanced AI technologies into mapping and data-collection processes. Future work will explore the extension of this system to support real-time applications and integration with broader geographic data analysis platforms.

Keywords: street view imagery, object detection, geotagging, convolutional neural networks, geographic information systems, urban planning, AI technologies.
