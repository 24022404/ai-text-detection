This paper explores the multiview differential geometry of curves, addressing the challenge of understanding and reconstructing 3D curves from multiple 2D projections. The objective is to develop a mathematical framework that leverages differential geometry principles to analyze and model curves in multiview settings, enabling accurate 3D reconstruction from limited 2D data. We propose a novel approach that combines differential geometric properties, such as curvature and torsion, with multiview geometry constraints to derive a unified model for curve reconstruction. The method employs optimization techniques to align 2D projections with their corresponding 3D representations, ensuring consistency across views. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed approach, achieving high accuracy in 3D curve reconstruction compared to existing methods. The framework also exhibits robustness to noise and occlusions, making it suitable for applications in computer vision, robotics, and medical imaging. This work contributes a new perspective to multiview geometry by integrating differential geometry, offering a foundation for future research in 3D curve analysis and reconstruction.  

**Keywords**: multiview geometry, differential geometry, 3D curve reconstruction, curvature, torsion, computer vision.
