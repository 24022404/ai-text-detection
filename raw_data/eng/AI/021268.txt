Reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms are highly sensitive to the choice of learning rate, significantly impacting convergence speed and final performance. This paper addresses the challenge of manually tuning the learning rate by introducing a novel approach for automating the learning rate policy. We propose a meta-learning framework that learns optimal learning rate schedules for a given RL algorithm and environment. Our method leverages a recurrent neural network to dynamically adjust the learning rate based on the observed training progress. Experiments conducted on a range of benchmark RL environments, including those from the OpenAI Gym suite, demonstrate that our approach consistently outperforms fixed learning rate strategies and several adaptive learning rate methods. Specifically, our meta-learned learning rate policies lead to faster convergence, improved sample efficiency, and higher overall rewards. These findings highlight the potential of meta-learning to enhance the robustness and applicability of RL algorithms in complex, real-world scenarios. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Meta-Learning, Learning Rate Policy, Optimization, Adaptive Learning.
