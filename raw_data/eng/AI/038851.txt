This paper addresses the challenge of self-supervised scene flow estimation, where reliance on neighboring inputs may inadvertently degrade performance. Our research aims to enhance scene flow estimation by employing an adversarial metric learning framework that reduces dependency on potentially misleading neighboring data, improving the robustness of scene flow predictions.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel adversarial metric learning approach that incorporates adversarial training techniques to facilitate the self-supervised learning of scene flow. This approach leverages a discriminator network to identify and mitigate the influence of neighboring inconsistencies while a generator learns a more reliable scene flow representation. We utilize a contrastive loss to model the similarity between beneficial samples and a distinguishing adversarial loss to automate the detection of harmful neighbors.

Results/Findings: The proposed method demonstrates significant improvements in scene flow estimation accuracy compared to existing self-supervised methods. Through extensive experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets, our approach consistently achieves reduced error rates and outperforms state-of-the-art techniques in scenarios with misleading or noisy neighboring data. The integration of adversarial metric learning effectively enhances the robustness and precision of scene flow predictions.

Conclusion/Implications: Our research contributes a novel adversarial metric learning framework that advances the capabilities of self-supervised scene flow estimation. By reducing reliance on neighboring data, this methodology offers a more reliable and adaptable solution for real-world applications, such as autonomous driving and augmented reality, where accurate scene understanding is crucial. Future work could extend this approach to other domains requiring robust self-supervised learning strategies.

Keywords: self-supervised learning, scene flow estimation, adversarial metric learning, autonomous driving, robustness, discriminator network.
