Reliable event prediction is critical in various applications, yet quantifying uncertainty remains a significant challenge, particularly in complex and dynamic systems. This paper introduces a scalable joint modeling framework designed to predict future events while providing well-calibrated uncertainty estimates. Our approach integrates a novel deep learning architecture that combines event sequence modeling with variational inference to learn a joint distribution over event occurrences and model parameters. This enables the capture of both aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty. We employ efficient training techniques to handle large-scale event datasets. Experimental results on real-world datasets demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of prediction accuracy and calibration, particularly in situations with sparse data or high levels of noise. The proposed framework offers a robust and scalable solution for uncertainty-aware event prediction, with implications for risk management, proactive decision-making, and improved system reliability. Keywords: Event prediction, uncertainty quantification, joint modeling, deep learning, variational inference, scalability.
