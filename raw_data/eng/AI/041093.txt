The increasing computational demand of neural networks necessitates innovative strategies for optimizing runtime efficiency without compromising performance. This paper introduces AntiDote, an attention-based dynamic optimization framework designed to enhance the operational efficiency of neural networks.

Methods/Approach: AntiDote employs a novel attention-based mechanism to analyze and adaptively reconfigure network parameters in real-time according to computational demands and resource availability. By integrating dynamic adjustment algorithms, our approach ensures optimal use of available resources, reducing unnecessary computational overhead while maintaining model accuracy.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that AntiDote significantly improves the runtime efficiency across various neural network architectures when compared to traditional optimization techniques. On average, our framework reduces computational costs by 30% without any notable decline in predictive performance, as evidenced by benchmark testing on diverse datasets.

Conclusion/Implications: AntiDote advances the field of neural network optimization by offering a dynamic solution that intelligently balances resource consumption and computational efficiency. This framework has the potential for broad applicability in scenarios requiring real-time processing and resource optimization, such as mobile and edge computing environments. The attention-based approach sets a precedent for future research in adaptive neural network optimization, promising more sustainable and efficient deployment of AI technologies.

Keywords: neural network optimization, attention-based mechanism, dynamic optimization, runtime efficiency, computational demands, resource allocation.
