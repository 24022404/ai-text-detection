This paper introduces GraphiT, a novel approach to encoding graph structure in transformer-based architectures. The objective is to effectively integrate graph-structured data into transformer models, enhancing their ability to process complex relational information. Our method employs a graph-aware positional encoding scheme, allowing the transformer to capture long-range dependencies and structural relationships within the graph. We evaluate GraphiT on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its ability to outperform existing state-of-the-art models in graph classification and node classification tasks. The results show significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency, particularly in cases where the graph structure is complex or sparse. Our approach contributes to the growing field of graph neural networks and transformer-based models, with potential applications in areas such as social network analysis, recommendation systems, and molecular biology. Key contributions include the development of a scalable and flexible graph encoding scheme, enabling the application of transformer models to a wide range of graph-structured data. Relevant keywords: graph neural networks, transformers, graph structure, positional encoding, node classification, graph classification.
