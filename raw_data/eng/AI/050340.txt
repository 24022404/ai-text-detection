This paper addresses the challenge of 3D object detection in point clouds, a crucial task in various applications such as autonomous driving, robotics, and scene understanding. Our objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of voting-based 3D object detection methods. We propose a novel approach, Back-tracing Representative Points (BRP), which enhances the detection process by effectively selecting and back-tracing representative points in the point cloud. Our method utilizes a combination of geometric and semantic features to identify key points that contribute to the detection of objects. The BRP approach is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in detection accuracy and efficiency compared to state-of-the-art methods. Our results show that BRP achieves a notable increase in precision and recall, particularly in scenarios with complex scenes and occlusions. The proposed method has important implications for various 3D vision tasks, including object recognition, tracking, and scene reconstruction. Key contributions of this research include the introduction of a robust and efficient back-tracing mechanism, the integration of geometric and semantic features, and the demonstration of improved performance in challenging 3D object detection scenarios. Relevant keywords: 3D object detection, point clouds, voting-based methods, back-tracing, representative points, geometric features, semantic features.
