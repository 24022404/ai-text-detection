This study investigates the performance of keypoint detectors and binary descriptors under various photometric and geometric transformations, aiming to identify robust feature extraction methods for computer vision applications. A comprehensive evaluation framework is developed, incorporating state-of-the-art keypoint detectors and binary descriptors, to assess their sensitivity to changes in illumination, rotation, scale, and affine transformations. The results show that certain keypoint detectors and binary descriptors outperform others in handling specific types of transformations, with significant implications for tasks such as object recognition, tracking, and 3D reconstruction. Notably, the study reveals that the combination of a robust keypoint detector with an efficient binary descriptor can achieve superior performance under real-world conditions. The findings of this research contribute to the development of more reliable and efficient computer vision systems, with potential applications in fields like robotics, autonomous vehicles, and surveillance. Key keywords: keypoint detection, binary descriptors, photometric transformations, geometric transformations, computer vision, feature extraction, object recognition.
