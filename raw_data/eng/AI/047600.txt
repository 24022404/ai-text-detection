Federated learning (FL) enables collaborative model training without direct data sharing, preserving data privacy. However, performance can be limited by data heterogeneity across participating clients. This paper introduces a novel federated transfer learning framework with dynamic gradient aggregation to address this challenge. Our approach leverages transfer learning to share knowledge between clients with different data distributions, enhancing model generalization. The core innovation lies in a dynamic gradient aggregation mechanism, which adaptively weights client updates based on their relevance and contribution to the global model. We evaluate our framework on image classification and natural language processing tasks, demonstrating significant improvements in model accuracy and convergence speed compared to traditional FL algorithms and existing transfer learning techniques. Experimental results show that dynamic gradient aggregation effectively mitigates the impact of data heterogeneity, leading to a more robust and accurate global model. This research contributes to advancing personalized and privacy-preserving AI, with potential applications in healthcare and finance.

Keywords: Federated Learning, Transfer Learning, Gradient Aggregation, Data Heterogeneity, Distributed Learning, Privacy-Preserving AI.
