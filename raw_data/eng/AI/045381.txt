This paper addresses the computational inefficiency of existing neural network training approaches, particularly when applied to large datasets. By leveraging the theoretical framework of neural tangent kernels (NTKs), we explore a method for accelerating the training process, achieving significant improvements in time complexity.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel algorithm that computes the NTK in near input sparsity time, utilizing advanced techniques in kernel methods and sparse matrix computations. Our approach involves a strategic combination of optimization techniques and parallel processing to maintain accuracy while drastically reducing computational resources required. 

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that our method achieves a substantial reduction in computation time compared to traditional neural network training, without compromising on prediction accuracy. The model outperforms existing approaches especially in scenarios with high-dimensional data and sparse input structures, showcasing superior scalability and efficiency.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of an NTK-based training algorithm that operates in near input sparsity time presents a significant advancement in the field of machine learning. This work not only contributes to the theoretical understanding of NTKs but also has practical implications for developing faster AI models applicable in real-time data environments. Keywords: Neural Tangent Kernels, NTK, input sparsity, machine learning, neural network training, computation efficiency.
