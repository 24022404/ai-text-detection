Automated evaluation of root canal therapy (RCT) is crucial for efficient dental diagnosis and treatment planning. This paper introduces an Anatomy-Guided Parallel Bottleneck Transformer Network (AG-PBTNet) for accurate and automated RCT evaluation. Our method leverages anatomical information to guide a parallel bottleneck transformer architecture, enhancing feature extraction and contextual understanding of dental radiographs. Specifically, AG-PBTNet incorporates anatomical segmentation as an auxiliary task to provide spatial guidance to the transformer network. The parallel bottleneck structure reduces computational complexity while preserving essential features for RCT assessment. Experimental results on a large clinical dataset demonstrate that AG-PBTNet significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods in RCT evaluation metrics, including accuracy, precision, and recall. The proposed method offers a promising solution for improving the efficiency and accuracy of dental diagnostics, with potential applications in clinical practice and teledentistry. Keywords: Root Canal Therapy, Automated Evaluation, Transformer Network, Anatomical Guidance, Dental Radiography, Artificial Intelligence.
