Explainable AI (XAI) for tabular data is crucial for building trust in machine learning models used in critical applications. While feature importance methods are common, they often lack the granularity to explain individual predictions. This paper introduces a novel approach to explain tabular data predictions, drawing inspiration from the attention mechanism in deep learning. Our method, Tabular Attention Explanation (TAE), learns an attention-like score for each feature in each instance, highlighting its contribution to the specific prediction. TAE is model-agnostic and can be applied to various machine learning models. We evaluate TAE on several benchmark tabular datasets, demonstrating its effectiveness in providing instance-level feature explanations that are both accurate and interpretable. Experiments show that TAE outperforms existing feature importance methods in terms of explanation fidelity and provides more nuanced insights into model behavior. TAE offers a valuable tool for understanding and debugging machine learning models applied to tabular data, enhancing transparency and accountability. Keywords: Explainable AI, Tabular Data, Feature Explanation, Attention Mechanism, Model-Agnostic, Interpretability.
