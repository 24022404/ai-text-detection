Declarative process models offer flexibility in representing business processes but pose challenges in automated discovery due to their expressive power. This paper introduces DisCoveR, a novel approach for accurately and efficiently discovering declarative process models from event logs. DisCoveR employs a two-phase strategy: first, it identifies potential constraints using a pattern-based technique that analyzes event relationships. Second, it leverages an Integer Linear Programming (ILP) formulation to select the most representative and concise subset of constraints, ensuring a high level of fitness and precision. Experiments on both synthetic and real-world event logs demonstrate that DisCoveR achieves superior accuracy compared to existing declarative process discovery techniques while maintaining competitive efficiency. Specifically, DisCoveR significantly reduces the number of redundant or irrelevant constraints in the discovered models, leading to improved model understandability and generalization. The discovered models can be used for process monitoring, conformance checking, and process improvement.

**Keywords:** Process Discovery, Declarative Process Models, Constraint-based Mining, Integer Linear Programming, Business Process Management.
