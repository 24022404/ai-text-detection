This paper addresses the challenge of estimating the structure of sparse graphical models from truncated samples, a common problem in various domains where data is often incomplete or censored. We propose a novel approach that leverages truncated data to efficiently estimate model parameters and graph structure. Our method combines a modified score matching estimator with a regularization technique specifically designed to handle truncation bias. This approach allows us to recover the underlying graph structure with significantly fewer samples compared to traditional methods that either discard truncated data or rely on complex imputation techniques. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our method on both synthetic and real-world datasets, showing improved accuracy in graph recovery and parameter estimation. Our findings contribute to the development of more robust and efficient algorithms for learning graphical models from incomplete data. Key words: graphical models, sparse estimation, truncated data, score matching, regularization, structure learning.
