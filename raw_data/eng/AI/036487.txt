This paper introduces a novel dynamic programming algorithm designed to optimize informative measurements and near-optimal path planning in scenarios involving uncertainty. The objective is to efficiently determine a sequence of measurements that maximizes information gain while simultaneously minimizing the path cost to acquire these measurements. Our approach leverages dynamic programming to iteratively build a cost-to-go function that represents the optimal trade-off between information acquisition and travel distance. Specifically, the algorithm considers the predicted information gain from potential measurement locations and the cost of traversing to those locations, integrating these factors within the dynamic programming framework. The performance of the proposed algorithm is evaluated through simulations in environments with varying levels of complexity and uncertainty. Results demonstrate that the algorithm achieves near-optimal path plans, significantly improving the efficiency of information gathering compared to traditional greedy methods. The algorithm’s ability to balance information gain and path cost enables efficient exploration and decision-making in uncertain environments. This work contributes a practical and effective approach to path planning under uncertainty, with potential applications in robotics, environmental monitoring, and search-and-rescue operations.

Keywords: Dynamic Programming, Path Planning, Information Gain, Uncertainty, Robotics, Optimization.
