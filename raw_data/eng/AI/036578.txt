This paper addresses the challenge of rumor detection in social media by proposing an ensemble deep learning approach that leverages time-series representation of tweets. The objective is to accurately identify and classify rumors on social media platforms, which can have significant societal implications. Our method combines multiple deep learning models, including convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and long short-term memory (LSTM) networks, to analyze the temporal patterns and semantic features of tweets. The results show that our ensemble approach outperforms individual models, achieving a significant improvement in rumor detection accuracy. Key findings indicate that the time-series representation of tweets captures crucial information about the spread of rumors, allowing for more effective detection. The conclusion highlights the importance of this research in combating misinformation on social media, with potential applications in public health, finance, and politics. Our approach contributes to the development of more robust and accurate rumor detection systems, leveraging the strengths of ensemble deep learning and time-series analysis. Keywords: rumor detection, social media, ensemble deep learning, time-series analysis, natural language processing, tweet representation.
