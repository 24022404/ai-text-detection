This paper presents a novel attack strategy, termed Spatially Focused Attack, designed to compromise the integrity of Spatiotemporal Graph Neural Networks (STGNNs). The objective is to investigate the vulnerability of STGNNs to targeted attacks, which can have significant implications for applications such as traffic forecasting, smart city management, and social network analysis. Our approach leverages the spatial and temporal dependencies inherent in spatiotemporal graphs to craft attacks that are both efficient and effective. By focusing on specific spatial locations and temporal intervals, our method can significantly degrade the performance of STGNNs, even when the attack budget is limited. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our attack strategy, outperforming existing methods in terms of attack success rate and perturbation magnitude. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of considering spatial and temporal factors when designing robust STGNNs. Our contributions include the development of a novel attack strategy, which can be used to improve the robustness of STGNNs, and the identification of potential vulnerabilities in existing architectures. The implications of this research are significant, as it can inform the development of more secure and reliable STGNNs for a wide range of applications, including intelligent transportation systems, urban planning, and public health monitoring. Key keywords: Spatiotemporal Graph Neural Networks, Spatially Focused Attack, Adversarial Attacks, Graph Neural Networks, Deep Learning.
