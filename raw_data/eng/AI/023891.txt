Semantic segmentation is a critical task in computer vision, enabling precise pixel-level understanding of images. This paper introduces a novel approach to semantic segmentation using a Highly Fused Convolutional Network (HFCN) combined with multiple soft cost functions. The proposed HFCN architecture enhances feature extraction and fusion across multiple layers, addressing the limitations of traditional convolutional networks in capturing fine-grained details and contextual information. To optimize the model, we employ a set of soft cost functions that dynamically balance the learning process, improving robustness and accuracy across diverse datasets. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets, outperforming existing approaches in terms of both segmentation accuracy and computational efficiency. The integration of multiple soft cost functions further enhances the model's adaptability to complex scenes and varying object scales. This research contributes to advancing semantic segmentation by providing a scalable and efficient framework with potential applications in autonomous driving, medical imaging, and remote sensing.  

**Keywords**: Semantic segmentation, convolutional neural networks, feature fusion, soft cost functions, computer vision.
