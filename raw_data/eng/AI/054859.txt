Multi-view clustering aims to group data points described by multiple feature sets, or views, into clusters. A key challenge lies in effectively integrating information from different views while ensuring consistency across the resulting cluster assignments. This paper introduces a novel Consistent Multiple Graph Embedding (CMGE) approach for multi-view clustering. CMGE constructs a graph for each view, then learns embeddings for each view’s graph, explicitly enforcing consistency between the learned embeddings by minimizing the disagreement between them. We formulate an objective function that simultaneously optimizes graph embedding and consistency regularization, and develop an efficient iterative algorithm to solve it. Empirical evaluations on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that CMGE achieves significant improvements in clustering accuracy compared to state-of-the-art multi-view clustering algorithms. Furthermore, the learned embeddings can be used for other downstream tasks. This research offers a new perspective on multi-view clustering by directly enforcing embedding consistency and provides a promising approach for integrating information from diverse data sources.
Keywords: Multi-view clustering, graph embedding, consistency regularization, spectral clustering, data integration.
