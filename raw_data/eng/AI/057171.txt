This paper introduces CR-Fill, a novel generative image inpainting framework that enhances reconstruction quality by incorporating auxiliary contextual reconstruction. The primary objective is to address the limitations of existing inpainting techniques, which often struggle with generating coherent and semantically consistent content in regions with complex or large missing areas. CR-Fill leverages a dual-stage pipeline: an initial generative model predicts the missing content, followed by an auxiliary contextual reconstruction module that further refines the results by integrating global and local image information. Experimental evaluations conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that CR-Fill surpasses state-of-the-art inpainting methods in terms of both visual fidelity and quantitative metrics such as PSNR and SSIM. Ablation studies confirm the effectiveness of the auxiliary reconstruction mechanism in improving texture consistency and edge continuity. The proposed approach shows strong generalization across diverse image categories and mask patterns, marking a significant advancement in generative image inpainting. Potential applications include photo restoration, object removal, and digital content editing. Keywords: image inpainting, generative models, contextual reconstruction, deep learning, image editing, CR-Fill.
