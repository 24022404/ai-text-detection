Accurate image segmentation is crucial for quantitative analysis in medical imaging, yet manual quality control (QC) is time-consuming and subjective. This paper presents an automated quality control (QC) framework for image segmentation, specifically applied to cardiac magnetic resonance (CMR) imaging data from the UK Biobank. Our approach leverages a convolutional neural network (CNN) trained on a large dataset of manually assessed segmentations to predict QC scores. We evaluate the framework’s performance in identifying segmentation errors by comparing its predictions with expert annotations. Results demonstrate that the automated QC system achieves high accuracy in detecting both global and regional segmentation failures, significantly reducing the workload associated with manual review. This automated QC method offers a robust and efficient solution for large-scale medical imaging studies, improving data quality and accelerating downstream analysis. Keywrods: Image Segmentation, Quality Control, Cardiac MRI, Deep Learning, UK Biobank, Automation.
