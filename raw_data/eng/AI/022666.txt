This paper addresses the challenge of early stopping in deep learning models without relying on a separate validation set, a common requirement that can limit data efficiency. We propose a novel approach leveraging *Channel-Wise Early Stopping* based on *NNK (Non-Negative Kernel) Polytope Interpolation*. The method dynamically monitors the learning process at the channel level, identifying optimal stopping points by analyzing the geometric properties of the feature space. By interpolating the NNK polytope, our approach estimates model performance directly from training data, eliminating the need for a validation set. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed method achieves competitive performance compared to traditional early stopping techniques while significantly reducing computational overhead. The results highlight the potential of NNK-based interpolation for improving training efficiency in resource-constrained environments. This work contributes a practical and theoretically grounded solution for early stopping, with applications in scenarios where data availability is limited or validation sets are impractical.  

**Keywords**: Early stopping, NNK polytope, channel-wise training, deep learning, validation-free training, interpolation.
