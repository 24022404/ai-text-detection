This paper presents an innovative approach to Optical Braille Recognition (OBR) utilizing the Circular Hough Transform (CHT) technique. The objective is to develop an efficient and accurate method for recognizing Braille characters from images, enhancing accessibility for visually impaired individuals. Our approach employs CHT to detect the circular patterns of Braille dots, which are then processed using image processing algorithms to extract relevant features. The proposed system is evaluated on a dataset of images containing various Braille characters, yielding a high recognition accuracy. The results demonstrate the effectiveness of the CHT-based method in comparison to existing OBR techniques. This research contributes to the development of assistive technologies, providing a novel and efficient solution for Braille recognition. Key aspects of this work include image processing, feature extraction, and pattern recognition, with potential applications in accessibility tools and assistive devices. Relevant keywords: Optical Braille Recognition, Circular Hough Transform, Image Processing, Assistive Technology, Braille Character Recognition.
