This paper addresses the challenge of learning effective feature embeddings in high-dimensional data spaces, a critical task for applications such as image retrieval, clustering, and classification. Traditional methods often fail to capture local structural relationships, leading to suboptimal performance in downstream tasks. To overcome this limitation, we propose a novel approach called Local Similarity-Aware Deep Feature Embedding (LS-DFE). Our method leverages deep neural networks to learn embeddings that preserve local similarity structures within the data, ensuring that nearby points in the original space remain close in the embedding space. The LS-DFE framework incorporates a custom loss function that emphasizes local neighborhood preservation while maintaining global discriminability. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that LS-DFE outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of retrieval accuracy and clustering performance. The results highlight the importance of local similarity awareness in feature embedding and suggest that our approach can significantly enhance the effectiveness of representation learning in various IT applications. This work contributes a scalable and robust solution for embedding high-dimensional data, with potential applications in computer vision, recommendation systems, and beyond.  

**Keywords**: feature embedding, local similarity, deep learning, representation learning, image retrieval, clustering.
