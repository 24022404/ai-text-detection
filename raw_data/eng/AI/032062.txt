Accurate crowd flow prediction is crucial for urban planning, public safety, and resource allocation. This paper addresses the challenge of modeling complex and heterogeneous relationships across multiple data modalities for improved crowd flow prediction. We propose a novel multi-modal graph neural network (MM-GNN) that integrates diverse data sources, including historical crowd flow, points of interest (POIs), and transportation networks. Our approach explicitly models the heterogeneous relationships between these modalities using a relational graph structure, allowing for effective information propagation and fusion. The MM-GNN leverages node-specific attention mechanisms to adaptively weigh the importance of different neighbors and modalities. Experiments on real-world datasets demonstrate that our model outperforms state-of-the-art methods in crowd flow prediction accuracy, particularly in capturing the influence of POIs and transportation networks on crowd movement. The results highlight the importance of modeling heterogeneous relations across multiple modalities for accurate and robust crowd flow prediction, offering significant implications for urban management and intelligent transportation systems.

Keywords: Crowd Flow Prediction, Multi-Modal Learning, Graph Neural Networks, Heterogeneous Relations, Urban Computing, Transportation Networks.
