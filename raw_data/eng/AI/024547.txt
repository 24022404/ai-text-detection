This paper presents Explorable Super Resolution, a novel approach to image and video enhancement that leverages advanced deep learning techniques to achieve high-quality super-resolution results. The objective of this research is to develop a flexible and efficient method for improving the resolution of low-quality visual content, enabling users to explore and interact with enhanced images and videos in real-time. Our approach utilizes a combination of convolutional neural networks and generative models to learn a mapping between low-resolution inputs and high-resolution outputs. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, achieving significant improvements in image quality and outperforming existing state-of-the-art super-resolution techniques. The proposed framework has numerous applications in fields such as computer vision, virtual reality, and multimedia processing, and contributes to the development of more sophisticated and interactive visual systems. Key contributions of this work include the introduction of a novel exploratory framework for super-resolution, enabling users to navigate and refine enhanced images and videos, and the demonstration of improved performance and efficiency compared to existing methods. Relevant keywords: super-resolution, deep learning, image enhancement, computer vision, generative models.
