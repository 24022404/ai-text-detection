Camouflaged object detection (COD) is a challenging task in computer vision, where objects blend seamlessly into their surroundings, making them difficult to distinguish. This paper introduces a novel deep learning framework that leverages texture-aware features to improve the accuracy of camouflaged object detection. The proposed approach integrates a multi-scale texture analysis module within a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, enabling the model to capture fine-grained texture patterns that are critical for identifying camouflaged objects. By combining texture information with spatial and semantic features, the framework achieves superior performance in detecting objects that are intentionally concealed in complex backgrounds. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms state-of-the-art techniques in terms of detection accuracy and robustness. The findings highlight the importance of texture-aware features in addressing the challenges of COD and provide a foundation for future research in object detection and scene understanding. This work has potential applications in fields such as surveillance, wildlife monitoring, and medical imaging, where detecting hidden objects is crucial.  

**Keywords**: Camouflaged object detection, texture-aware features, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, multi-scale analysis, computer vision.
