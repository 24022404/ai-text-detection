Blur detection is a critical task in image processing, with applications ranging from image enhancement to computer vision systems. This paper introduces MSDU-net, a novel Multi-Scale Dilated U-net architecture designed to improve the accuracy and robustness of blur detection in complex visual scenes. The proposed model leverages a multi-scale feature extraction framework combined with dilated convolutions to capture both local and global contextual information effectively. By integrating these components into a U-net structure, MSDU-net enhances the ability to distinguish between blurred and sharp regions, even in images with varying blur types and intensities. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that MSDU-net outperforms existing methods in terms of precision, recall, and F1-score, achieving state-of-the-art performance. The model’s efficiency and scalability make it suitable for real-time applications, such as autonomous driving and medical imaging. This work contributes to the field by providing a robust and versatile solution for blur detection, with potential implications for improving image quality assessment and scene understanding in diverse domains.  

**Keywords**: Blur detection, multi-scale feature extraction, dilated convolutions, U-net, image processing, computer vision.
