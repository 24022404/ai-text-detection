This paper presents a novel neural-based approach to answering questions about images, leveraging the capabilities of deep learning models to bridge the gap between visual and textual information. The objective is to develop an intelligent system that can accurately respond to user queries related to image content, such as object identification, scene understanding, and activity recognition. Our approach utilizes a multimodal fusion framework, combining convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for image feature extraction and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) for question encoding, to generate informative and relevant answers. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our model, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a neural-based question answering architecture and the exploration of attention mechanisms to focus on relevant image regions. This work has significant implications for applications such as visual question answering, image retrieval, and human-computer interaction, and paves the way for future research in multimodal learning and neural-based image understanding, with relevant keywords including neural networks, deep learning, computer vision, and multimodal fusion.
