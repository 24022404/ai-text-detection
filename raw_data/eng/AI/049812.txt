The objective of this study is to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of electrocardiogram (ECG) arrhythmia classification by employing transfer learning techniques with 2-dimensional deep convolutional neural network (CNN) features. Recognizing that ECG arrhythmia detection is crucial for timely diagnosis and treatment of cardiac disorders, our approach leverages the power of pre-trained deep CNNs to extract significant features from ECG data transformed into two-dimensional representations. The methodology involves utilizing a transfer learning framework that harnesses the rich feature hierarchies learned by CNNs from large image datasets, adapting them to classify ECG signals effectively. Our experiments demonstrate that such a technique markedly improves classification performance, achieving higher accuracy and reduced computational costs compared to traditional methods without transfer learning. In particular, the proposed model outperforms existing models in terms of precision, recall, and overall classification accuracy, highlighting its potential for practical applications in clinical settings. The implications of this research suggest that transfer learning, combined with deep CNN feature extraction, offers a robust solution to the challenges of ECG arrhythmia classification, providing a significant step forward in the integration of AI in healthcare diagnostics. Keywords associated with this study include ECG, arrhythmia classification, transfer learning, deep convolutional neural networks, and 2D feature extraction.
