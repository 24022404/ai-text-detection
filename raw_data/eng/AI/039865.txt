The increasing demand for deploying neural networks on edge devices necessitates exploring efficient models with reduced computational and memory requirements. This research addresses the challenge of training low-bit neural networks without significant performance degradation, introducing a novel approach to achieving better quantization results.

Methods/Approach: We propose a method called Semi-Relaxed Quantization with DropBits, which utilizes a unique bit-wise regularization technique. This approach strategically relaxes certain bits during the training process, allowing more flexible bit activity. By incorporating DropBits, a stochastic method that dynamically drops less critical bits, the model effectively maintains accuracy while minimizing bit-width.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method significantly enhances the performance of low-bit neural networks compared to existing quantization techniques. Our approach achieves competitive accuracy on benchmark datasets with fewer bits, reducing computational complexity and memory usage. In comparison tests, our method achieved better resilience to quantization noise and surpassed traditional fixed-point quantization models.

Conclusion/Implications: The Semi-Relaxed Quantization with DropBits provides a robust solution for training efficient low-bit neural networks, presenting a substantial advancement in neural network quantization. This approach contributes to the deployment of resource-efficient models on edge devices, enabling broader applications of neural network models in constrained environments. Future work may extend this method to various network architectures and further optimize the balance between accuracy and efficiency.

Keywords: low-bit neural networks, quantization, bit-wise regularization, DropBits, Semi-Relaxed Quantization, efficient AI models.
