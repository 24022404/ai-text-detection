Human instance segmentation is a critical task in computer vision, enabling applications such as video surveillance, human-computer interaction, and augmented reality. Traditional methods often rely on object detection as a preliminary step, which can introduce inefficiencies and inaccuracies. This paper introduces **Pose2Seg**, a novel framework for human instance segmentation that eliminates the need for detection by directly leveraging human pose estimation. The proposed approach utilizes keypoint information to guide the segmentation process, enabling precise delineation of human instances without relying on bounding boxes. We employ a deep learning-based architecture that integrates pose estimation and segmentation into a unified pipeline, enhancing both accuracy and computational efficiency. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that Pose2Seg outperforms state-of-the-art detection-based methods in terms of segmentation quality and inference speed. The framework also exhibits robustness in challenging scenarios, such as occlusions and crowded scenes. By removing the dependency on detection, Pose2Seg offers a streamlined and effective solution for human instance segmentation, with potential applications in real-time systems and resource-constrained environments. This work contributes to advancing the field by introducing a detection-free paradigm that prioritizes efficiency and precision.  

**Keywords**: Human instance segmentation, pose estimation, detection-free, deep learning, computer vision.
