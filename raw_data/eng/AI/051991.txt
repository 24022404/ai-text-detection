This paper proposes Convolutional Kernel Networks (CKNs), a novel deep learning framework designed to effectively process graph-structured data. The objective is to develop a model that can learn meaningful representations from complex graph data, addressing the limitations of existing methods. Our approach combines the strengths of convolutional neural networks and kernel methods, enabling the extraction of valuable information from graph-structured data. We evaluate the performance of CKNs on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating their ability to outperform state-of-the-art models in graph classification and node classification tasks. The results show that CKNs achieve significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency, making them a promising tool for applications involving graph data, such as social network analysis, molecular chemistry, and recommendation systems. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a flexible and scalable framework for graph-structured data processing, and the demonstration of its potential in real-world applications. Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Convolutional Neural Networks, Kernel Methods, Deep Learning, Graph Classification.
