This paper introduces *NeuralSampler*, a novel framework for encoding and sampling Euclidean point clouds using an auto-encoder architecture. The objective of this research is to address the challenges of efficiently representing and reconstructing high-dimensional point cloud data, which is critical for applications in 3D modeling, robotics, and augmented reality. *NeuralSampler* leverages a deep neural network-based auto-encoder to learn compact latent representations of point clouds, enabling both accurate reconstruction and adaptive sampling. The proposed approach incorporates a hierarchical sampling mechanism that dynamically adjusts the density of sampled points based on local geometric features, ensuring high fidelity in reconstructed outputs. Experimental results demonstrate that *NeuralSampler* outperforms existing methods in terms of reconstruction accuracy and computational efficiency, achieving a 15% improvement in mean squared error (MSE) on benchmark datasets. Furthermore, the framework exhibits robust generalization capabilities across diverse point cloud geometries. The implications of this work extend to real-time 3D rendering, autonomous navigation, and digital twin applications, where efficient point cloud processing is essential. *NeuralSampler* represents a significant advancement in point cloud representation, offering a scalable and adaptive solution for modern 3D data challenges.  

**Keywords**: point cloud, auto-encoder, 3D reconstruction, neural networks, adaptive sampling, Euclidean geometry.
