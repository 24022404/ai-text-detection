This paper introduces WiSoSuper, a novel benchmark dataset designed to evaluate super-resolution (SR) techniques specifically for wind and solar irradiance data. The objective is to address the limitations of existing SR benchmarks, which often lack the specific characteristics of meteorological datasets crucial for renewable energy forecasting. WiSoSuper comprises high-resolution wind speed and solar irradiance data obtained from diverse geographical locations and time periods. We evaluate a range of state-of-the-art SR methods, including both traditional interpolation techniques and deep learning-based approaches, on the WiSoSuper dataset. Our findings reveal significant performance variations across different methods, highlighting the importance of considering data-specific characteristics when selecting SR algorithms for renewable energy applications. Moreover, we identify the strengths and weaknesses of each SR method, providing insights for future research and development in this domain. The WiSoSuper dataset and evaluation results contribute to advancing the accuracy and reliability of wind and solar energy forecasting.

**Keywords:** Super-Resolution, Wind Energy, Solar Energy, Renewable Energy, Benchmark Dataset, Deep Learning, Irradiance, Forecasting.
