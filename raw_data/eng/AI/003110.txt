This paper presents a novel approach for reduced-order Markov modeling of time series data using symbolic analysis. The objective is to address the challenge of efficiently modeling complex temporal dependencies in large-scale datasets while maintaining computational tractability. We propose a symbolic transformation method that discretizes continuous time series into symbolic sequences, enabling the construction of a reduced-order Markov model. The approach leverages graph-based techniques to identify and retain only the most significant state transitions, thereby reducing model complexity without sacrificing predictive accuracy. Experimental evaluations on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that the proposed method achieves a significant reduction in model size compared to traditional Markov models, while maintaining competitive performance in terms of prediction accuracy and computational efficiency. The results highlight the potential of this approach for applications in areas such as anomaly detection, forecasting, and system behavior analysis. This work contributes a scalable and interpretable framework for time series modeling, offering a balance between model simplicity and predictive power.  

**Keywords**: Symbolic analysis, Markov modeling, time series, reduced-order models, graph-based techniques, predictive modeling.
