This paper introduces a novel method for fuzzy time series forecasting that leverages interval index numbers and membership values in conjunction with Support Vector Machine (SVM). The proposed approach addresses the limitations of traditional fuzzy time series models, which often struggle with handling uncertainty and achieving high prediction accuracy. The method involves constructing interval index numbers to represent fuzzy relationships and incorporating membership values to enhance the precision of the forecasting model. SVM is employed to optimize the prediction process, leveraging its ability to handle non-linear relationships and improve generalization. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms existing fuzzy time series forecasting techniques in terms of accuracy and robustness across multiple datasets. The findings highlight the effectiveness of integrating interval index numbers, membership values, and SVM for time series forecasting, offering a significant contribution to the field of predictive analytics. This research has practical implications for applications requiring accurate time series predictions, such as financial forecasting, weather prediction, and demand planning. Keywords: fuzzy time series, interval index number, membership value, Support Vector Machine, forecasting, predictive analytics.
