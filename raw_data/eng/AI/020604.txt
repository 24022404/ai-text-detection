The objective of this research is to address the challenging problem of night-time haze and glow removal from images, which adversely affects visual quality and impairs computer vision applications. We propose a novel approach utilizing a Deep Dilated Convolutional Network designed specifically to handle the complexities of night-time atmospheric conditions. Our method leverages the dilation operation to effectively capture contextual information over larger regions, enhancing the network's ability to distinguish and separate the haze and glow effects from the underlying scene content. The model is trained on a specially curated dataset of night-time images afflicted with varying levels of haze and glow to ensure robustness and versatility. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing techniques, delivering superior haze-free and glow-reduced images while preserving critical image details and color fidelity. The implications of this study suggest wide-ranging applications in night-time security surveillance, automotive vision systems, and photography, enhancing the performance and reliability of image-based systems under low visibility conditions. Keywords for this study include night-time image enhancement, haze removal, glow reduction, deep learning, and dilated convolution.
