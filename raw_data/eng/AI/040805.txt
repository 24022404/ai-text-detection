This paper presents an innovative approach to video super-resolution, focusing on the development of an end-to-end learning framework that incorporates motion compensation. The objective is to enhance the resolution of low-quality videos while effectively handling complex motion patterns. Our method utilizes a deep neural network architecture that integrates optical flow estimation and motion compensation, allowing for more accurate and efficient video super-resolution. The proposed model is trained end-to-end, enabling the joint optimization of motion estimation and super-resolution. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in video quality, with our approach outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM). The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a novel motion compensation module and the development of a comprehensive training dataset. This work has important implications for various applications, including video surveillance, entertainment, and virtual reality, where high-quality video is essential. Key keywords: video super-resolution, motion compensation, deep learning, end-to-end learning, optical flow estimation.
