This paper introduces Patch Shortcuts, a novel approach to identifying vulnerabilities in black-box systems through the use of interpretable proxy models. The objective is to efficiently detect potential weaknesses in complex systems without requiring extensive knowledge of their internal workings. Our method employs a combination of machine learning and model interpretation techniques to create proxy models that mimic the behavior of the black-box system, allowing for the identification of potential vulnerabilities. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of Patch Shortcuts in finding vulnerabilities, outperforming existing methods in terms of efficiency and accuracy. The key findings highlight the importance of interpretability in proxy models, enabling the identification of critical patches that can be used to exploit vulnerabilities. This research contributes to the field of cybersecurity by providing a novel, efficient, and interpretable approach to vulnerability detection, with potential applications in AI security, software testing, and system hardening. Key keywords: black-box systems, interpretable models, proxy models, vulnerability detection, AI security, model interpretation.
