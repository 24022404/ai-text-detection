Graph Variational Autoencoders (GVAEs) have shown promise in learning latent representations of graph-structured data. However, existing GVAEs often struggle to capture both local and global graph properties effectively. This paper introduces a novel Multiresolution Graph Variational Autoencoder (MGVAE) designed to address this limitation. The MGVAE leverages a hierarchical graph representation, constructing multiple graph resolutions through iterative node clustering. This multiresolution approach allows the model to capture graph structures at different scales, from fine-grained node interactions to coarse-grained community structures. We employ a variational inference framework to learn latent embeddings at each resolution, which are then aggregated to form a comprehensive graph representation. Experimental results on benchmark graph datasets demonstrate that MGVAE outperforms existing GVAE methods in terms of link prediction, node clustering, and graph classification. The learned multiresolution embeddings provide valuable insights into the hierarchical organization of complex graphs, with potential applications in social network analysis, bioinformatics, and recommendation systems. Keywords: Graph Variational Autoencoder, Multiresolution Analysis, Graph Representation Learning, Hierarchical Clustering, Variational Inference.
