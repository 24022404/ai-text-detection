This paper introduces a novel graph-driven communication framework to enhance multi-agent coordination in complex environments. The objective is to enable agents to learn effective communication strategies that facilitate efficient task completion. Our approach involves constructing dynamic communication graphs, where nodes represent agents and edges indicate communication channels, optimized using a reinforcement learning algorithm. This graph structure allows agents to selectively share information with relevant peers, reducing communication overhead and improving decision-making. We evaluate our framework on several benchmark cooperative tasks, demonstrating significant improvements in team performance compared to state-of-the-art methods with fixed or no communication structures. Specifically, agents learn to adapt the communication graph based on the task requirements and agent roles, leading to more robust and scalable coordination. Our findings highlight the importance of adaptive communication topologies for effective multi-agent learning. Keywords: multi-agent systems, reinforcement learning, graph neural networks, communication, coordination, distributed AI.
