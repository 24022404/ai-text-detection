The identification of linear systems is a fundamental problem in control theory, often complicated by the need for safe exploration to prevent system instability. This research addresses the challenge of identifying linear systems while ensuring safety by leveraging robust optimization techniques.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel framework that integrates robust optimization into the safe exploration process for identifying linear systems. Our approach emphasizes maintaining system stability throughout the exploration phase. By incorporating a robust optimization model, we are able to adaptively determine exploration strategies that minimize risk while maximizing information gain about the system's dynamics.

Results/Findings: The proposed method demonstrated superior performance in safely identifying various linear systems, as evidenced by comprehensive simulations. Our approach achieved significant improvements in system identification accuracy compared to traditional exploration techniques, while consistently maintaining system safety. Additionally, the framework effectively adjusted to different system uncertainties, showcasing its robustness and adaptability.

Conclusion/Implications: This research introduces a safe and efficient method for identifying linear systems through robust optimization. The integration of safety constraints within the exploration process enhances the reliability of system identification, presenting a valuable tool for control engineering applications. The implications of this study are far-reaching, potentially benefiting industries requiring precise system modeling under safety constraints, such as aerospace and robotics. Keywords: linear systems, safe exploration, robust optimization, system identification, control theory.
