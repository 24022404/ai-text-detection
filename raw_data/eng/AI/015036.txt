Face age progression aims to synthesize realistic facial images at different ages, a challenging task due to the complex and subtle changes in facial appearance over time. This paper introduces a novel framework for face age progression that incorporates attribute manipulation to enhance the realism and control of the generated results. Our approach leverages a conditional generative adversarial network (GAN) to disentangle age-related features from identity information. Specifically, we manipulate facial attributes such as wrinkles, skin texture, and facial shape, guided by age labels. We introduce a novel attention mechanism within the GAN architecture to focus on age-sensitive regions of the face, enabling more precise and realistic age transformations. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of visual quality and age estimation accuracy, surpassing existing methods. The proposed framework also allows for controlled attribute manipulation, enabling users to customize age progression by adjusting specific facial features. This work contributes a more controllable and realistic approach to face age progression, with potential applications in entertainment, forensics, and age-invariant face recognition.

**Keywords:** Face Age Progression, Generative Adversarial Networks, Attribute Manipulation, Image Synthesis, Facial Attributes, Deep Learning.
