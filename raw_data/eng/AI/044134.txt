The rapid advancement of video processing technologies has created a growing demand for efficient and accurate 3D visualization techniques. This paper addresses the challenge of reconstructing 3D models from 2D video frames, a task critical for applications in augmented reality, virtual environments, and multimedia analysis. We propose a novel framework that leverages deep learning-based depth estimation and structure-from-motion (SfM) algorithms to transform sequential video frames into high-fidelity 3D representations. Our approach integrates temporal consistency and spatial coherence to enhance the accuracy and robustness of the reconstructed models. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in reconstruction quality compared to existing methods, with reduced computational overhead. The proposed framework achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets, showcasing its potential for real-world applications. This research contributes to the field by providing a scalable and efficient solution for 3D visualization from video, paving the way for advancements in immersive technologies and interactive media.  

**Keywords**: 3D reconstruction, video processing, depth estimation, structure-from-motion, deep learning, immersive technologies.
