This paper addresses the problem of defending against gradient-based adversarial attacks, which pose a significant threat to the security of deep learning models. Our objective is to develop a robust defense strategy that can effectively counter such attacks. We propose a minimax defense approach, which combines a novel regularization technique with a robust optimization algorithm to improve the model's resilience against adversarial perturbations. Our method utilizes a game-theoretic framework to formulate the defense problem as a minimax optimization problem, where the goal is to minimize the maximum loss over all possible attacks. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing defense methods, achieving state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of robust optimization and regularization in defending against gradient-based attacks. Our contributions have significant implications for the development of secure and reliable deep learning models, particularly in applications where security is a top priority, such as computer vision and natural language processing. Key keywords: adversarial attacks, deep learning, minimax defense, robust optimization, regularization, computer vision, natural language processing.
