This paper presents Deep Burst Denoising, a novel approach to image denoising that leverages the power of deep learning and burst photography. The objective is to effectively remove noise from images captured in low-light conditions, a common challenge in various applications such as smartphone photography and surveillance. Our method utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture to process bursts of noisy images, exploiting the redundant information across frames to produce a single, high-quality denoised image. The proposed approach is evaluated on a range of datasets, demonstrating significant improvements over state-of-the-art denoising techniques in terms of peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and visual quality. The key contributions of this research include the development of a robust and efficient denoising algorithm, as well as insights into the benefits of burst photography for image denoising. With its potential applications in fields such as computer vision, photography, and image processing, Deep Burst Denoising offers a promising solution for enhancing image quality in low-light environments, and is relevant to keywords such as image denoising, deep learning, burst photography, and low-light imaging.
