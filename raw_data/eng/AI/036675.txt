The objective of this paper is to address the challenge of regression with unlabeled data, exploiting semi-supervised learning techniques to improve the accuracy and robustness of predictive models. Specifically, the research focuses on integrating deep kernel learning within a semi-supervised framework to enhance predictive performance through variability reduction.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel algorithm that combines semi-supervised learning with deep kernel methods, leveraging both labeled and unlabeled data. The approach prioritizes minimizing predictive variance as a metric for model optimization, allowing the system to capture uncertainties associated with unlabeled data. Our model employs Gaussian processes within the deep learning architecture to dynamically learn the kernel functions in a data-driven manner, enhancing the adaptation to the underlying data manifold.

Results/Findings: Extensive experiments demonstrate that our semi-supervised deep kernel learning approach significantly improves regression accuracy over traditional supervised methods. The incorporation of unlabeled data through variance minimization leads to reduced prediction errors and better generalization on unseen datasets. Comparative analyses indicate that our method outperforms existing semi-supervised and supervised regression models in terms of both prediction accuracy and computational efficiency.

Conclusion/Implications: This research introduces a promising direction for regression in scenarios where labeled data is scarce but unlabeled data is abundant. By minimizing predictive variance, the proposed semi-supervised deep kernel learning framework not only enhances model performance but also provides a robust mechanism for uncertainty quantification. The methodology presents significant potential applications in fields where data labeling is expensive or infeasible, offering contributions to the advancement of machine learning techniques in real-world environments.

Keywords: Semi-supervised learning, Deep kernel learning, Regression, Unlabeled data, Predictive variance, Gaussian processes, Model optimization.
