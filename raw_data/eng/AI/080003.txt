This paper introduces LSALSA, a novel approach to source separation that leverages learned sparse coding to accelerate the separation process. The objective is to improve the efficiency and accuracy of source separation in complex audio signals. LSALSA employs a deep learning-based method that learns sparse representations of sources, enabling fast and accurate separation. The approach utilizes a combination of sparse coding and adaptive filtering to separate mixed signals into their individual sources. Experimental results demonstrate that LSALSA outperforms state-of-the-art source separation methods, achieving significant improvements in separation quality and computational efficiency. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of learned sparse coding in source separation, with potential applications in audio processing, music information retrieval, and speech recognition. The contributions of this research include the development of a novel sparse coding framework and its application to source separation, making LSALSA a valuable tool for audio signal processing. Key keywords: source separation, sparse coding, deep learning, audio signal processing, adaptive filtering.
