This paper addresses the challenge of generating realistic and attribute-aware facial images using Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs). While existing GAN-based methods excel in producing high-quality images, they often lack fine-grained control over specific facial attributes such as age, gender, and expression. To overcome this limitation, we propose a novel GAN architecture that integrates attribute-aware conditioning mechanisms, enabling precise manipulation of facial features during the generation process. Our approach leverages a multi-branch discriminator and a conditional generator, trained on a large-scale dataset annotated with diverse facial attributes. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed model outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of image quality, attribute accuracy, and diversity. Additionally, we introduce a quantitative evaluation framework to assess the controllability and realism of generated images. The findings highlight the potential of our method for applications in facial recognition, virtual avatars, and personalized content generation. This work contributes to advancing GAN-based techniques by providing a robust solution for attribute-aware face synthesis, paving the way for more nuanced and customizable image generation systems.  

**Keywords**: Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), face generation, attribute-aware synthesis, conditional GANs, facial attributes, image manipulation.
