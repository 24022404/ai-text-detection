This paper presents a novel approach to sparse training, leveraging the concept of boosting pruning plasticity with neuroregeneration. The objective is to develop an efficient and adaptive method for training sparse neural networks, addressing the challenges of existing pruning techniques. Our approach combines the principles of boosting and neuroregulation to enhance the plasticity of neural connections, allowing for more effective pruning and regrowth of connections. The method utilizes a dynamic pruning strategy, which identifies and removes redundant connections while promoting the growth of new connections through a neuroregenerative process. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art sparse training methods, achieving significant improvements in model accuracy and efficiency. The key findings highlight the importance of boosting pruning plasticity with neuroregeneration, enabling the development of more robust and adaptable sparse neural networks. This research contributes to the advancement of sparse training techniques, with potential applications in edge AI, autonomous systems, and other resource-constrained environments, and is relevant to keywords such as sparse neural networks, pruning, neuroregeneration, and efficient AI models.
