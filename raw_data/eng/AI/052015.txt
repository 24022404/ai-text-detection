The selection of optimal data sources is a critical challenge in Bayesian learning, as it directly impacts the accuracy and efficiency of inference processes. This paper addresses the problem of near-optimal data source selection to enhance Bayesian learning outcomes. We propose a novel algorithmic framework that leverages probabilistic modeling and optimization techniques to identify the most informative data sources while minimizing computational overhead. Our approach integrates a hybrid sampling strategy with adaptive weighting mechanisms to dynamically prioritize data sources based on their contribution to the learning objective. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves significant improvements in prediction accuracy and computational efficiency compared to traditional selection methods. Furthermore, the framework exhibits robust performance across diverse datasets and learning scenarios, highlighting its versatility. The findings underscore the importance of strategic data source selection in Bayesian learning and provide a scalable solution for real-world applications, such as decision-making systems and predictive analytics. This research contributes to advancing the field by introducing a computationally efficient and theoretically grounded approach to data source optimization.  

**Keywords**: Bayesian learning, data source selection, probabilistic modeling, optimization, adaptive weighting, computational efficiency.
