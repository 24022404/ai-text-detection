This paper explores the application of spectral learning techniques to matrices and tensors, addressing the challenge of extracting valuable insights from complex, high-dimensional data. Our objective is to develop a novel framework that leverages spectral decomposition to learn meaningful representations of matrices and tensors, enabling improved analysis and processing of multi-dimensional data. We propose a new approach that combines spectral methods with machine learning algorithms to learn compact and informative representations of matrices and tensors. Our results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method in various applications, including data compression, feature extraction, and anomaly detection. The key findings of this research include improved performance compared to existing methods, as well as the ability to handle large-scale datasets efficiently. The contributions of this work include the introduction of a new spectral learning framework for matrices and tensors, which has potential applications in fields such as computer vision, signal processing, and data mining. Key keywords: spectral learning, matrix factorization, tensor decomposition, machine learning, data analysis.
