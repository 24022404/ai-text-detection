Deep neural networks (DNNs) have achieved remarkable success in various applications, yet their predictions often lack reliable uncertainty estimates, which are critical for decision-making in high-stakes environments. This paper addresses the challenge of constructing *Probably Approximately Correct (PAC) confidence sets* for DNNs, ensuring that the true label lies within the predicted set with a user-specified probability. We propose a novel framework that leverages *calibrated prediction* to generate confidence sets with rigorous PAC guarantees. Our approach combines conformal prediction techniques with temperature scaling to calibrate model outputs, ensuring both statistical validity and practical utility. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves tighter confidence sets compared to existing approaches while maintaining calibration accuracy. The proposed framework is model-agnostic, scalable, and applicable to a wide range of tasks, including classification and regression. This work contributes to the growing field of trustworthy AI by providing a principled and interpretable method for uncertainty quantification in deep learning, with potential applications in healthcare, autonomous systems, and beyond.  

**Keywords**: PAC confidence sets, deep neural networks, calibrated prediction, conformal prediction, uncertainty quantification, trustworthy AI.
