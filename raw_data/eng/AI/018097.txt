This research addresses the challenge of extracting characters from obfuscated text images, a critical issue in information security and document analysis. Traditional OCR systems are often ineffective against obfuscation techniques that distort text to prevent automated reading. "MailLeak: Obfuscation-Robust Character Extraction Using Transfer Learning" seeks to bridge this gap by employing advanced machine learning techniques to enhance character recognition accuracy in obfuscated environments.

Methods/Approach: Our approach leverages transfer learning, utilizing pre-trained convolutional neural networks (CNNs) as a foundation. By fine-tuning these networks on a dataset comprising various obfuscation styles, we enhance the model's ability to generalize and accurately extract characters. Unlike standard OCR technology, our methodology emphasizes robustness against obfuscation, employing innovative augmentation techniques to simulate real-world text distortions consistently.

Results/Findings: The proposed method demonstrates superior performance in comparison to traditional OCR systems, achieving significant improvements in character recognition accuracy across diverse obfuscation patterns. Experimental results show an increase of up to 20% in character extraction accuracy when tested on benchmark datasets designed for obfuscation robustness. This performance underscores the model's capacity to generalize well to unseen obfuscation styles, thereby validating the efficacy of transfer learning in this domain.

Conclusion/Implications: The findings of this study highlight the potential of using transfer learning to enhance OCR systems for tackling obfuscated text. By achieving high levels of accuracy and robustness, "MailLeak" presents a viable solution for applications in information security, fraud detection, and digital forensics. The approach contributes to the broader field of computer vision by demonstrating the effectiveness of fine-tuning pre-trained models in specialized tasks, paving the way for future advancements in character recognition technologies.

Keywords: Character extraction, Obfuscation, Transfer learning, Convolutional neural networks, OCR, Information security.
