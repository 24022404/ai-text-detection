Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have demonstrated remarkable performance across various graph-related tasks. However, designing optimal GNN architectures often requires extensive manual effort and expert knowledge. This paper introduces a novel approach to simplify the architecture search process for GNNs, aiming to automate the discovery of high-performing models. Our method leverages a differentiable search space combined with a reinforcement learning-based search strategy to efficiently explore various architectural configurations. We introduce a cell-based search space that significantly reduces computational complexity while maintaining architectural diversity. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves competitive or superior performance compared to state-of-the-art manually designed and automatically searched GNN architectures, while requiring significantly less computational resources. The discovered architectures exhibit strong generalization capabilities across different datasets and tasks. This work contributes a practical and efficient framework for automated GNN architecture design, reducing the reliance on manual tuning and enabling broader adoption of GNNs. Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Architecture Search, Neural Architecture Search, Automation, Deep Learning.
