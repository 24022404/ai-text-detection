Mobile computational photography has emerged as a transformative field, leveraging advancements in hardware and software to enhance image quality and enable novel imaging capabilities on smartphones. This paper provides a comprehensive overview of the key techniques, challenges, and innovations in mobile computational photography. We explore the integration of machine learning, computer vision, and sensor technologies to address limitations such as low-light performance, motion blur, and depth perception. The study highlights state-of-the-art methods, including multi-frame fusion, neural image processing, and real-time 3D scene reconstruction, which have significantly improved the quality and versatility of mobile imaging. Furthermore, we discuss the implications of these advancements for applications in augmented reality, medical imaging, and social media. By synthesizing recent developments, this paper aims to provide a clear understanding of the current landscape and future directions in mobile computational photography, emphasizing its potential to revolutionize how we capture and interact with visual content.  

**Keywords**: mobile imaging, computational photography, machine learning, image enhancement, 3D reconstruction, augmented reality.
