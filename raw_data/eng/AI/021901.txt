Shot boundary detection (SBD) is a fundamental task in video analysis, crucial for applications like video summarization, indexing, and editing. This paper introduces a novel approach to SBD, addressing the limitations of existing methods through the creation of a new extensive dataset and the utilization of mixed features. Our dataset encompasses diverse video content, including varying camera motions, illumination changes, and object movements, providing a more robust evaluation benchmark. The proposed method combines visual features, such as color histograms and edge information, with motion features derived from optical flow analysis. A machine learning classifier, specifically a Support Vector Machine (SVM), is trained on these mixed features to accurately identify shot boundaries. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms state-of-the-art SBD methods on the new dataset and achieves comparable or superior performance on existing benchmark datasets. The improved accuracy and robustness of our SBD method contribute to more effective video processing and analysis workflows.

Keywords: Shot Boundary Detection, Video Analysis, Mixed Features, Dataset, SVM, Machine Learning.
