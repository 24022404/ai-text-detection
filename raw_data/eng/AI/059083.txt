Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in various graph-based tasks. However, effectively propagating information across diverse node features remains a challenge. This paper introduces a novel heterogeneous aggregation method to enhance information propagation in GNNs. Our approach leverages distinct aggregation functions tailored to different types of node features, enabling the model to capture richer and more nuanced relationships within the graph. We propose a learnable aggregation scheme that adaptively weights the contributions of different feature types during message passing. The proposed method is evaluated on several benchmark datasets for node classification and graph classification tasks. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing GNN models, achieving state-of-the-art performance. The heterogeneous aggregation strategy improves the model's ability to discern complex patterns and propagate relevant information, leading to enhanced representation learning and improved downstream task performance. This work provides a novel and effective solution for enhancing information propagation in GNNs, offering valuable insights for future research in graph representation learning.

Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Heterogeneous Aggregation, Information Propagation, Graph Representation Learning, Node Classification, Graph Classification.
