This paper investigates the integration of physical modeling techniques within active contour models to enhance image segmentation performance. Traditional active contours often struggle with complex image features, leading to inaccurate or incomplete segmentation. To address this, we propose a novel approach that incorporates physical principles, specifically mass, damping, and elasticity, into the active contour's energy functional. This allows the contour to behave more realistically under image forces, resulting in smoother and more robust segmentation. We introduce a finite element method to discretize and solve the equations of motion derived from the physical model. The effectiveness of our method is demonstrated through experiments on a variety of images, including medical images and natural scenes. Results show significant improvements in segmentation accuracy and robustness compared to traditional active contour models and other state-of-the-art techniques. The proposed method offers a promising avenue for improving image segmentation in applications where accuracy and robustness are paramount.

**Keywords:** Active Contours, Image Segmentation, Physical Modeling, Finite Element Method, Medical Imaging, Computer Vision.
