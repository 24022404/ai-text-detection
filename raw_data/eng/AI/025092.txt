Abstract:

Precise object detection in densely packed scenes presents significant challenges due to severe object occlusions, overlapping boundaries, and high intra-class similarity. This paper addresses these challenges by proposing a novel detection framework that integrates an adaptive region proposal mechanism with context-aware feature aggregation. The method employs an enhanced convolutional backbone, leveraging multi-scale feature extraction and relational modeling to effectively distinguish closely situated objects. A custom loss function is introduced to improve both localization accuracy and robustness against overlapping instances. Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets, including crowded scenes and high-density object scenarios, demonstrate that the proposed approach achieves superior precision and recall compared to state-of-the-art detectors. The results highlight notable improvements in detecting small and partially occluded objects, validating the method’s effectiveness in real-world applications such as urban surveillance, crowd analysis, and automated inventory management. This research advances the field by offering a scalable, accurate solution for object detection in complex, densely populated environments. Key keywords: densely packed scenes, object detection, occlusion handling, multi-scale features, computer vision.
