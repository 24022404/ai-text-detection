This paper addresses the challenge of robust object detection in Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) imagery, where performance is significantly degraded by nuisances such as varying illumination, weather conditions, and viewpoint changes. We propose a novel deep nuisance disentanglement approach that learns to separate nuisance-related features from object-specific representations. Our method employs an adversarial training framework coupled with a dedicated nuisance encoder, enabling the model to extract and suppress nuisance factors while preserving crucial object characteristics. Extensive experiments on challenging UAV datasets demonstrate that our approach significantly improves object detection accuracy, particularly in adverse environmental conditions. Compared to state-of-the-art object detectors, our method achieves superior performance in terms of mean Average Precision (mAP) and robustness to nuisance variations. The findings suggest that disentangling nuisance factors is a promising direction for enhancing the reliability of UAV-based object detection systems. Keywords: Object Detection, UAV, Deep Learning, Nuisance Disentanglement, Adversarial Training, Robustness.
