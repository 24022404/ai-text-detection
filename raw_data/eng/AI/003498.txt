This paper explores the relationship between entropy and policy optimization in reinforcement learning, with the objective of improving the efficiency and stability of policy optimization algorithms. We employ a novel approach that incorporates entropy regularization into the policy optimization process, utilizing a modified trust region method to balance exploration and exploitation. Our results show that entropy regularization can significantly enhance the performance of policy optimization, leading to more robust and adaptive policies. Key findings include improved convergence rates and increased resilience to environmental changes. The conclusions drawn from this research highlight the importance of entropy in policy optimization and demonstrate the potential for entropy-based methods to improve the overall performance of reinforcement learning systems. This study contributes to the field of reinforcement learning by providing new insights into the role of entropy in policy optimization, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, game playing, and autonomous systems, and is relevant to keywords including reinforcement learning, policy optimization, entropy regularization, and trust region methods.
