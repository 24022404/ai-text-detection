This paper explores the concept of dropout as an optimization technique in deep learning models. The objective is to investigate the role of dropout in preventing overfitting and improving model generalization. We employ a combination of theoretical analysis and empirical experiments to understand the effects of dropout on model optimization. Our approach involves analyzing the dropout algorithm and its interaction with stochastic gradient descent, as well as conducting experiments on various deep learning architectures. The results show that dropout can be viewed as a regularization technique that adaptively adjusts the learning rate and batch size, leading to improved model performance and robustness. Our findings suggest that dropout is a simple yet effective optimization trick that can be used to improve the training of deep neural networks. The implications of this research are significant, as it provides new insights into the workings of dropout and its potential applications in deep learning. Key keywords: dropout, optimization, deep learning, regularization, stochastic gradient descent.
