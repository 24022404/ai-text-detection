This paper introduces SketchGen, a novel system for generating constrained CAD sketches. The objective is to automate the creation of valid and manufacturable CAD designs, addressing the long-standing challenge of efficiently producing high-quality sketches that meet specific geometric and topological constraints. Our approach leverages a combination of geometric reasoning and machine learning techniques to generate sketches that satisfy a given set of constraints. The SketchGen system utilizes a graph-based representation of the sketch and a constraint satisfaction algorithm to ensure that the generated sketches are valid and consistent. Our results show that SketchGen can efficiently generate a wide range of constrained CAD sketches, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and speed. The implications of this research are significant, enabling the rapid creation of custom CAD designs for various applications, including architecture, engineering, and product design. Key contributions include the development of a novel graph-based representation of CAD sketches and a scalable constraint satisfaction algorithm. Relevant keywords: CAD sketching, geometric constraint satisfaction, machine learning, graph-based modeling, computer-aided design.
