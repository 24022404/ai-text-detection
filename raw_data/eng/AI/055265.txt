This paper introduces SecDD, a novel method for remotely training neural networks that prioritizes both efficiency and security. The objective is to enable multiple parties to collaboratively train neural networks on private data while protecting the confidentiality and integrity of the data. SecDD achieves this by employing a combination of advanced cryptographic techniques and optimized communication protocols. The approach utilizes secure multi-party computation and homomorphic encryption to ensure that data remains private throughout the training process. Experimental results demonstrate that SecDD significantly reduces communication overhead and improves training time compared to existing methods, making it a viable solution for real-world applications. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of SecDD in preserving data privacy while maintaining model accuracy. This research contributes to the development of secure and efficient distributed machine learning, with potential applications in areas such as healthcare, finance, and edge computing. Key keywords: secure multi-party computation, homomorphic encryption, distributed machine learning, neural networks, privacy-preserving.
