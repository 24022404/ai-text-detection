De Bruijn sequences, which have been recently adapted as HdB sequences by Zhang, Oi, Lowndes, and collaborators for synchronizing quantum communication channels, offer distinct advantages over other synchronization techniques. However, these HdB sequences are not without limitations, highlighting the need for further improvement. This thesis seeks to address these shortcomings by designing a new constrained de Bruijn sequence and aims to demonstrate how it can achieve better efficiency for quantum channel synchronization compared to existing HdB sequences.
