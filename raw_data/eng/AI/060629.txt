The increasing incidence of breast cancer necessitates accurate and efficient diagnostic tools. This paper introduces ICADx, an interpretable computer-aided diagnosis (CAD) system designed to assist radiologists in the detection and characterization of breast masses in mammograms. ICADx employs a deep learning model incorporating attention mechanisms to identify salient image features relevant to diagnosis. The system not only predicts the likelihood of malignancy but also provides visual explanations highlighting the specific regions of interest used in the decision-making process. We evaluated ICADx on a large dataset of mammograms, demonstrating its ability to achieve state-of-the-art diagnostic accuracy while maintaining high sensitivity and specificity. Furthermore, the generated attention maps offer insights into the model's reasoning, enhancing transparency and trust in the AI-driven diagnosis. ICADx holds promise for improving the efficiency and accuracy of breast cancer screening, ultimately leading to better patient outcomes. Keywords: Breast Cancer, Computer-Aided Diagnosis, Deep Learning, Interpretability, Mammography, Attention Mechanisms.
