This paper addresses the challenge of learning invariant representations, crucial for robust machine learning models, without relying on computationally expensive and often unstable adversarial training. We propose a novel approach that leverages a regularization technique based on minimizing the Hilbert-Schmidt Independence Criterion (HSIC) between feature representations and sensitive attributes. This method encourages the model to learn features that are statistically independent of these attributes, effectively removing unwanted correlations. Unlike adversarial methods, our approach is stable, efficient to train, and does not require careful tuning of adversarial hyperparameters. We evaluate our method on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating superior performance in terms of both invariance and predictive accuracy compared to existing adversarial and non-adversarial techniques. Our results show that minimizing HSIC provides a powerful and practical alternative for learning invariant representations, leading to more robust and fair machine learning models. Keywords: Invariant Representations, Fairness, Hilbert-Schmidt Independence Criterion, HSIC, Regularization, Machine Learning, Adversarial Training, Robustness.
