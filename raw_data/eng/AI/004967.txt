Accurate assessment of peanut maturity is crucial for optimizing harvest time and ensuring high-quality yields. This paper presents a novel approach for classifying peanut maturity stages using hyperspectral imagery. Our method leverages spectral reflectance data in the visible and near-infrared regions (400-1000 nm) to distinguish between immature, mature, and over-mature peanuts. We employed a Support Vector Machine (SVM) classifier, trained on a carefully curated dataset of hyperspectral images acquired from peanut pods. The classification model achieved an overall accuracy of 92% in differentiating the maturity stages, surpassing traditional visual inspection methods. This research demonstrates the potential of hyperspectral imaging as a rapid and non-destructive tool for peanut maturity assessment, enabling precision agriculture practices and improving peanut production efficiency. Keywords: Hyperspectral Imaging, Peanut Maturity, Classification, Support Vector Machine, Precision Agriculture.
