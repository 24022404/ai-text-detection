This paper addresses the challenge of accurately forecasting taxi demand in urban areas, a crucial aspect of intelligent transportation systems. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that enhances the precision of taxi demand prediction, leveraging the potential of spatial analysis and machine learning. We propose a HEDGE (Hierarchical Spatial-Temporal Graph Embedding) based tessellation strategy, which partitions the urban space into homogeneous regions based on spatial and temporal patterns of taxi demand. Our method utilizes a graph convolutional network to learn the complex relationships between these regions and forecast future demand. The results show that our approach outperforms existing methods, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and reducing errors by up to 15%. The key contributions of this research lie in its ability to capture nuanced spatial-temporal dependencies and its scalability for large-scale urban environments. This study has important implications for taxi fleet management, traffic optimization, and smart city planning, highlighting the potential of HEDGE-based tessellation for improved taxi demand forecasting. Key keywords: taxi demand forecasting, HEDGE, graph convolutional network, spatial-temporal analysis, intelligent transportation systems.
