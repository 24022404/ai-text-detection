This paper introduces MOFit, a novel framework leveraging machine learning and IoT technologies to address the growing concern of obesity. MOFit aims to provide personalized and proactive interventions by continuously monitoring users' physical activity and dietary habits through wearable sensors and smart devices. The system employs machine learning algorithms to analyze collected data, identify patterns indicative of unhealthy behaviors, and predict potential weight gain. Specifically, MOFit incorporates a hybrid model combining a convolutional neural network for activity recognition from sensor data and a recurrent neural network for dietary pattern analysis based on user-reported food logs. Preliminary results demonstrate that MOFit can accurately classify activity levels with an average F1-score of 92% and predict weight gain trends with an accuracy of 85%. The framework also delivers personalized recommendations and motivational feedback to encourage healthier lifestyle choices. MOFit offers a promising approach to obesity management by integrating real-time data analysis with tailored intervention strategies, contributing to improved health outcomes and promoting sustainable weight management. Keywords: Obesity, Machine Learning, IoT, Wearable Sensors, Personalized Health, Activity Recognition, Dietary Analysis.
