Predicting future object movements is critical for autonomous driving systems to ensure safety and enable proactive planning. This paper introduces FIERY, a novel approach for future instance prediction in bird's-eye view (BEV) using surround monocular cameras. FIERY leverages a transformer-based architecture to aggregate multi-view features from individual cameras into a unified BEV representation. This representation is then used to predict future object locations, orientations, and velocities. A key innovation of FIERY is its ability to explicitly model inter-agent interactions through a dedicated interaction module, leading to more accurate and consistent future trajectory predictions. We evaluate FIERY on the nuScenes dataset, demonstrating significant improvements over existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of prediction accuracy and trajectory consistency, particularly in complex scenarios with multiple interacting agents. The results highlight the effectiveness of FIERY's BEV representation and interaction modeling for future prediction. This research contributes a robust and accurate method for predicting future agent behaviors, enhancing the safety and reliability of autonomous vehicles.

Keywords: Future Prediction, Bird's-Eye View, Autonomous Driving, Monocular Cameras, Transformer, Trajectory Prediction, Interaction Modeling.
