This paper introduces ProLab, a novel perceptually uniform colour coordinate system designed for improved colour manipulation and analysis in projective spaces. Addressing the limitations of existing colour spaces in representing and processing colours under projective transformations, ProLab leverages principles of perceptual uniformity to ensure that equal numerical changes in colour coordinates correspond to approximately equal changes in perceived colour. The ProLab system is constructed through a multi-step process involving optimized non-linear transformations applied to device-independent colour data. Experimental results demonstrate that ProLab significantly reduces colour distortions and enhances colour consistency compared to conventional colour spaces such as sRGB and CIELAB when subjected to projective transformations. Furthermore, ProLab facilitates more accurate colour-based object recognition and image segmentation in scenarios involving perspective distortions. This research contributes a valuable tool for applications in computer graphics, image processing, and computer vision, particularly where projective geometry plays a central role. Keywords: colour coordinate system, perceptual uniformity, projective space, colour manipulation, image processing, computer vision.
