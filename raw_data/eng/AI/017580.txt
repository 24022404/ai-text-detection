This paper proposes a novel approach to inductive graph embeddings by leveraging locality encodings, aiming to improve the representation learning of graph-structured data. Our objective is to develop an efficient and scalable method for embedding nodes in unseen graphs, enabling effective node classification, link prediction, and graph clustering tasks. We introduce a locality encoding scheme that captures the structural properties of graphs, which is then integrated into a graph neural network framework to learn inductive embeddings. Our approach outperforms existing methods on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its ability to generalize to new, unseen graphs. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of incorporating locality information into graph embeddings, leading to improved performance and robustness. This work contributes to the advancement of graph representation learning, with potential applications in social network analysis, recommendation systems, and bioinformatics. Key keywords: inductive graph embeddings, locality encodings, graph neural networks, node classification, link prediction.
