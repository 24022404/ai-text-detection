This paper presents a novel approach to 3D detection, leveraging an open-source simulator to eliminate the need for manual labeling. The objective is to develop a highly accurate and efficient 3D detection system, utilizing simulated data to train deep learning models. Our approach employs a combination of computer vision and machine learning techniques, integrating a 3D simulation environment with state-of-the-art object detection algorithms. The results show significant improvements in detection accuracy and speed, outperforming traditional manual-label-based methods. Key findings include the ability to detect objects in complex 3D scenes with high precision, and the potential to reduce annotation time and costs. The proposed method has important implications for various applications, including autonomous vehicles, robotics, and surveillance systems. By utilizing simulated data and open-source tools, this research contributes to the development of more efficient and accessible 3D detection systems, with potential applications in fields such as computer vision, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. Keywords: 3D detection, open-source simulator, manual-label free, deep learning, computer vision, object detection.
