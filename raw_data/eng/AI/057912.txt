**Abstract**

Accurate and real-time segmentation of robotic surgical instruments is vital for enhancing the safety and autonomy of robot-assisted surgeries. However, achieving high segmentation accuracy while maintaining low computational complexity remains a significant challenge, especially for deployment on resource-constrained medical robots. This paper presents an Attention-Guided Lightweight Network (AGLN) specifically designed for real-time segmentation of robotic surgical instruments from endoscopic video streams. The proposed architecture integrates attention mechanisms into a compact convolutional neural network, enabling efficient extraction of discriminative features for precise instrument localization and boundary delineation. Our method leverages spatial and channel attention modules to adaptively focus on relevant regions and suppress background noise, significantly improving segmentation performance without incurring excessive computational cost. Extensive experiments conducted on publicly available robotic surgery datasets demonstrate that AGLN achieves competitive accuracy and superior inference speed compared to state-of-the-art models, with a marked reduction in model size and latency. The results validate the effectiveness and practicality of our approach for real-time clinical applications. This work contributes a robust, interpretable, and easily deployable solution for instrument segmentation, with potential implications for enhancing surgical navigation, workflow automation, and human-robot collaboration in minimally invasive procedures. **Keywords:** surgical instrument segmentation, real-time processing, attention mechanism, lightweight network, robotic surgery, deep learning.
