This paper presents a novel approach to vertebral body segmentation using the GrowCut algorithm, focusing on its initial experience, workflow, and practical applications in medical imaging. The objective of this research is to address the challenges of accurate and efficient segmentation of vertebral bodies in complex medical datasets, which is critical for diagnostic and therapeutic planning. The proposed method leverages the GrowCut algorithm, a graph-based segmentation technique, to automate the delineation of vertebral structures from 3D medical images. The workflow integrates preprocessing steps, such as image normalization and noise reduction, followed by the application of GrowCut to achieve precise segmentation. Experimental results demonstrate the algorithm's effectiveness in handling variations in image quality and anatomical complexity, achieving high accuracy and robustness compared to traditional segmentation methods. The findings highlight the potential of GrowCut for clinical applications, including spinal pathology assessment and surgical planning. This study contributes to the field by providing a practical, automated solution for vertebral body segmentation, reducing manual intervention, and improving reproducibility. The implications of this research extend to broader applications in medical image analysis, particularly in scenarios requiring high precision and computational efficiency.  

**Keywords**: Vertebral body segmentation, GrowCut algorithm, medical imaging, 3D reconstruction, automated segmentation, clinical workflow.
