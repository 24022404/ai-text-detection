This research explores the potential of utilizing free-text clinical notes for unstructured mortality prediction in healthcare settings. Traditional models often rely on structured data, limiting the depth and contextual richness available in electronic health records. This study aims to develop a novel approach that harnesses the narrative content in clinical notes to enhance mortality prediction accuracy.

Methods/Approach: We propose a machine learning framework that leverages natural language processing (NLP) techniques to extract meaningful insights from free-text clinical notes. Our model integrates transformer-based architectures, adapted for healthcare data, to process and analyze text, capturing both explicit information and implicit patterns that could indicate patient risk. We compare our model's performance against traditional structured data models to evaluate improvements in prediction capability.

Results/Findings: The implementation of our unstructured data approach demonstrated a significant increase in predictive accuracy and reliability over conventional methods. Our model successfully identified nuanced language and context-specific signals that correspond to higher mortality risks. The findings suggest that integrating free-text analysis can enhance early detection and intervention strategies in clinical practice.

Conclusion/Implications: This study underscores the value of incorporating unstructured clinical notes into mortality prediction models, offering a more holistic view of patient health. The innovative use of NLP and transformer architectures in this domain presents opportunities for improved patient outcomes and resource allocation in healthcare systems. Future work will focus on refining model adaptability and exploring broader applications across diverse clinical settings.

Keywords: Mortality Prediction, Free-text Clinical Notes, Natural Language Processing, Transformer Models, Healthcare AI, Unstructured Data Analysis.
