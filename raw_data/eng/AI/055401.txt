This paper introduces a novel unsupervised learning framework for recovering scene geometry and understanding holistic 3D motion from monocular video. Our approach leverages the principle that every pixel's motion contributes to the overall scene understanding, moving beyond sparse feature-based methods. We propose a differentiable framework that jointly estimates depth, camera pose, and object motion by minimizing a photometric reconstruction loss. A key innovation is the incorporation of a holistic motion prior, encouraging coherent and physically plausible movements of objects within the scene. This prior is enforced through a novel regularization term based on motion smoothness and object rigidity. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in unsupervised depth estimation and 3D scene flow, surpassing existing methods particularly in challenging scenarios with complex object interactions. The learned geometric representations and motion understanding have potential applications in robotics, autonomous driving, and augmented reality.

Keywords: Unsupervised Learning, 3D Motion, Scene Geometry, Depth Estimation, Monocular Video, Holistic Motion, Computer Vision.
