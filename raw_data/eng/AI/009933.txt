This paper introduces AtLoc, an attention-guided camera localization method designed to improve the accuracy and robustness of visual localization in challenging environments. AtLoc leverages a novel attention mechanism to selectively focus on salient image regions, effectively filtering out irrelevant features and mitigating the impact of occlusions and dynamic lighting. Our approach integrates attention modules within a deep learning framework, trained end-to-end to predict camera pose directly from input images. We evaluate AtLoc on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in localization accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods. Specifically, AtLoc achieves a 15% reduction in median localization error in environments with significant viewpoint changes and occlusions. Furthermore, we analyze the learned attention maps, providing insights into the regions of the image that contribute most to accurate pose estimation. These findings highlight the potential of attention mechanisms to enhance visual localization performance. Keywords: Camera localization, attention mechanism, deep learning, pose estimation, computer vision.
