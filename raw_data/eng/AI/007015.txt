This paper introduces a novel approach to learning plannable representations using Causal InfoGAN, a variant of the InfoGAN model that incorporates causal relationships between variables. The objective is to learn disentangled and plannable representations that can be used for downstream tasks such as planning and decision-making. Our approach utilizes a causal graph to model the relationships between variables and incorporates this graph into the InfoGAN framework. We evaluate our model on several benchmark datasets and demonstrate its ability to learn representations that are both disentangled and plannable. The results show that our model outperforms existing methods in terms of representation quality and planning performance. Our research contributes to the development of more effective and efficient methods for learning plannable representations, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, computer vision, and natural language processing. Key keywords: Causal InfoGAN, plannable representations, disentangled representations, causal graph, planning, decision-making, deep learning, generative models.
