This paper addresses the challenge of learning part boundaries from 3D point clouds, a crucial task in various computer vision and robotics applications. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that can accurately identify and separate individual parts of an object from a given 3D point cloud. We propose a deep learning-based method that utilizes a combination of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and graph convolutional networks (GCNs) to learn effective representations of 3D point clouds. Our approach involves a two-stage process, where the first stage learns to identify potential part boundaries, and the second stage refines these boundaries through a graph-based optimization process. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. Our method outperforms existing techniques in terms of accuracy and efficiency, with potential applications in fields such as 3D reconstruction, object recognition, and robotic manipulation. Key contributions of this research include the introduction of a novel architecture for learning part boundaries and the demonstration of its efficacy in handling complex 3D point cloud data. Relevant keywords: 3D point clouds, part boundary detection, deep learning, CNNs, GCNs, 3D reconstruction, object recognition.
