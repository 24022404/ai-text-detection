This paper presents a novel approach to RGB-D salient object detection, leveraging collaborative learning to achieve accurate and efficient detection. The objective is to develop a model that effectively utilizes both RGB and depth information to identify salient objects in a scene. Our method employs a collaborative learning framework, where two parallel streams, one for RGB and one for depth, are fused to learn complementary features. A novel loss function is designed to encourage the model to focus on the most salient regions. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in detection accuracy and speed. The key findings indicate that the collaborative learning strategy enables the model to capture more comprehensive and accurate saliency information. This research contributes to the field of computer vision, particularly in salient object detection, and has potential applications in areas such as robotics, autonomous driving, and image editing. Key keywords: RGB-D salient object detection, collaborative learning, deep learning, computer vision, saliency detection.
