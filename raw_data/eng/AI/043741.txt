**Abstract:**

The COVID-19 pandemic has highlighted the critical need for effective monitoring tools to enforce social distancing and assess infection risks in public spaces. This paper introduces DeepSOCIAL, an AI-driven framework designed for automated social distancing monitoring and dynamic infection risk assessment. Leveraging deep learning-based object detection and multi-person tracking algorithms, DeepSOCIAL processes real-time video feeds to accurately identify and track individuals in crowded environments. The system calculates inter-personal distances, flags potential social distancing violations, and generates heatmaps to visualize high-risk zones based on crowd density and exposure duration. Extensive experiments on benchmark surveillance datasets demonstrate the robustness and accuracy of DeepSOCIAL, outperforming baseline methods in real-time detection and risk assessment tasks. Our results show that DeepSOCIAL provides valuable insights for public health officials by enabling proactive intervention and efficient space management. The proposed framework offers a scalable, privacy-preserving solution suitable for deployment in transportation hubs, retail environments, and other high-traffic locations. Key contributions include the integration of advanced computer vision, real-time analytics, and adaptive risk modeling to address pandemic-related challenges. Keywords: social distancing, COVID-19, infection risk assessment, deep learning, object detection, surveillance, computer vision.
