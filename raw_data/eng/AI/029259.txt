This paper proposes a novel approach to multi-label classification by integrating cross-modality attention with semantic graph embedding. The objective is to improve the accuracy of classification models by effectively capturing complex relationships between labels and leveraging features from multiple modalities. Our method employs a graph embedding technique to represent semantic relationships between labels, which are then incorporated into a cross-modality attention mechanism to selectively focus on relevant features from different modalities. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in classification accuracy and F1-score. The key contributions of this research lie in its ability to model intricate label relationships and adaptively weigh features from different modalities, making it particularly suitable for applications involving multi-label classification, such as image and text analysis. By combining cross-modality attention with semantic graph embedding, this study provides a new perspective on multi-label classification, highlighting the potential of graph-based methods and attention mechanisms in enhancing model performance. Key keywords: multi-label classification, cross-modality attention, semantic graph embedding, graph-based methods, attention mechanisms.
