Abstract: This paper addresses the growing importance of visual tracking systems in mobile robotics and provides a comprehensive review of state-of-the-art visual trackers. The objective is to explore how visual tracking technologies can enhance the navigational and operational capabilities of mobile robots. The review systematically categorizes existing visual trackers, including correlation filters, deep learning-based trackers, and feature-based approaches, highlighting their strengths and limitations. We conduct an in-depth analysis of each category's performance metrics, such as accuracy, speed, and computational efficiency, in the context of real-time applications. Furthermore, the paper presents an empirical study where a selection of visual trackers is integrated into a mobile robot's framework to evaluate their efficacy in dynamic environments. The findings demonstrate that advanced deep learning-based trackers offer superior performance in complex scenarios but may require optimization for real-time execution on resource-constrained mobile platforms. The conclusion emphasizes the significance of choosing the appropriate visual tracking system tailored to specific robotic tasks, contributing to improvements in autonomous navigation and task execution. This research paves the way for future developments in integrating visual tracking technologies with mobile robots, facilitating advancements in areas such as autonomous delivery, search and rescue operations, and human-robot interaction. Keywords: visual tracking, mobile robotics, deep learning, real-time applications, autonomous navigation.
