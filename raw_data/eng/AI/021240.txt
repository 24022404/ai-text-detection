This paper presents a novel approach to synthesizing facial action units (FAUs) using region-based adversarial learning. The objective is to generate realistic and detailed FAUs that can be used in various applications, including facial expression analysis and synthesis. Our method employs a deep neural network architecture that utilizes a region-based approach to focus on specific areas of the face, allowing for more precise control over FAU synthesis. The adversarial learning framework enables the model to learn from real facial images and generate synthetic FAUs that are highly realistic. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, showing significant improvements in FAU synthesis quality compared to existing methods. The proposed method has important implications for facial expression analysis, human-computer interaction, and related fields, and contributes to the development of more sophisticated facial synthesis technologies. Key contributions include the introduction of a region-based adversarial learning framework for FAU synthesis and the demonstration of its superiority over existing approaches. Relevant keywords: facial action units, adversarial learning, deep neural networks, facial expression analysis, computer vision.
