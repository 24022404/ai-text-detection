Understanding and executing complex manipulation tasks in dynamic environments is a key challenge for robotic systems. This paper addresses the problem by proposing an integrated framework that bridges visual perception with contextual semantics to enhance robot task comprehension and execution. Our approach combines state-of-the-art deep learning models for object detection and scene understanding with a semantic reasoning module that interprets contextual information, such as task objectives and relational cues between objects. We develop a multi-modal architecture that enables robots to jointly analyze visual inputs and task descriptions, facilitating more accurate recognition of manipulation intents and required actions. Experimental results on benchmark datasets and real-world robotic platforms demonstrate that our method outperforms conventional approaches by achieving higher accuracy in task identification and successful completion rates, especially in ambiguous or cluttered scenes. The proposed framework showcases improved generalization to novel tasks and adaptability in unstructured environments. This work advances the field of robot learning by tightly coupling visual perception with semantic reasoning, enabling more intelligent, context-aware manipulation. Potential applications include household robotics, industrial automation, and human-robot collaboration scenarios. Keywords: robot manipulation, visual perception, contextual semantics, deep learning, semantic reasoning, task understanding, human-robot interaction.
