This paper introduces MOPS-Net, a novel matrix optimization-driven network designed for task-oriented 3D point cloud downsampling. The objective of this research is to address the challenge of efficiently reducing the size of 3D point clouds while preserving critical geometric and semantic features essential for downstream tasks such as object recognition, segmentation, and reconstruction. Traditional downsampling methods often fail to maintain task-relevant information, leading to degraded performance in subsequent applications. To overcome this limitation, MOPS-Net leverages a matrix optimization framework integrated with deep learning to dynamically adapt the downsampling process based on the specific requirements of the target task. The proposed network employs a multi-objective loss function that balances point cloud sparsity, feature preservation, and task performance. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that MOPS-Net significantly outperforms existing methods in terms of both downsampling efficiency and task accuracy. The network achieves state-of-the-art performance across various tasks, showcasing its robustness and versatility. This work contributes a new paradigm for task-aware point cloud processing, with potential applications in autonomous driving, robotics, and augmented reality. Key innovations include the integration of matrix optimization with deep learning and the development of a flexible, task-oriented downsampling framework.  

**Keywords**: 3D point cloud, downsampling, matrix optimization, deep learning, task-oriented processing, feature preservation.
