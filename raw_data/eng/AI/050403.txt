Neonatal pain assessment is critical yet challenging due to the limited communication abilities of infants. This paper presents a novel approach for recognizing neonatal pain expression using transfer learning. The primary objective is to develop a robust and automated system for pain detection based on facial expressions. We employed a pre-trained convolutional neural network (CNN) model, fine-tuned with a dataset of neonatal facial images exhibiting varying pain intensities. The transfer learning approach leverages knowledge gained from large-scale image datasets to improve performance with limited neonatal-specific data. Experimental results demonstrate that our system achieves high accuracy in classifying pain levels, outperforming traditional machine learning methods and other fine-tuning strategies. The proposed method offers a non-invasive and efficient tool for continuous pain monitoring in neonates. This research contributes to improved pain management practices in neonatal care and provides a foundation for further development of automated pain assessment technologies. Keywords: Neonatal Pain Recognition, Transfer Learning, Convolutional Neural Network, Facial Expression Analysis, Pain Assessment.
