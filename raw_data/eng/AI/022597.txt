Adversarial attacks pose a significant threat to the reliability of deep neural networks. This paper introduces a novel defense mechanism, Channel-wise Activation Suppressing (CAS), designed to enhance adversarial robustness. The core idea behind CAS is to selectively suppress activations of feature channels that are highly susceptible to adversarial perturbations. We achieve this by introducing a trainable suppression module that dynamically adjusts the activation scale for each channel based on input characteristics. This approach effectively mitigates the impact of adversarial noise while preserving the network's ability to generalize to clean data. Experiments on benchmark datasets, including CIFAR-10 and ImageNet, demonstrate that CAS significantly improves adversarial robustness against various attack strategies, such as FGSM and PGD, compared to state-of-the-art defense methods, with minimal impact on clean accuracy. The results highlight the effectiveness of channel-wise suppression as a promising direction for building more resilient deep learning systems. Keywords: adversarial robustness, adversarial attack, deep neural networks, channel suppression, defense mechanism, machine learning.
