Knowledge-augmented Visual Question Answering (VQA) aims to improve VQA systems by leveraging external knowledge sources. However, existing benchmarks often lack the complexity required to thoroughly evaluate the integration and utilization of such knowledge. This paper introduces a novel benchmark, Select, Substitute, Search (SSS), designed to assess a VQA model's ability to selectively retrieve, appropriately substitute, and effectively search for relevant knowledge. The SSS benchmark includes questions requiring multi-hop reasoning and the integration of diverse knowledge types. We evaluate several state-of-the-art VQA models on the SSS benchmark, demonstrating their limitations in effectively utilizing external knowledge. The results reveal a significant performance gap compared to human performance, highlighting the challenges in knowledge selection and reasoning. The SSS benchmark provides a more rigorous evaluation framework, paving the way for future research in developing more robust and knowledge-aware VQA systems. Keywords: Visual Question Answering, Knowledge Augmentation, Benchmark, Reasoning, Multi-hop Reasoning.
