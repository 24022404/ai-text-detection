Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) have shown promise in explaining complex classifier decisions, but often lack control over the explanation's visual attributes. This paper introduces a novel approach: training a GAN to generate explanations in the StyleSpace of a pre-trained StyleGAN2. Our method aims to produce interpretable and visually controllable explanations for arbitrary classifiers. By mapping classifier decisions to the StyleSpace latent code, we achieve fine-grained control over explanation style, enabling users to manipulate features such as color, texture, and shape independently. We employ a novel loss function that encourages both fidelity to the classifier's decision boundary and disentanglement in the StyleSpace representation. Experiments demonstrate that our method generates high-quality, diverse explanations while offering unprecedented control over visual style. Quantitative evaluations, including perceptual metrics and user studies, validate the effectiveness of our approach compared to existing GAN-based explanation techniques. Our work contributes a new paradigm for generating interpretable explanations, facilitating deeper understanding and trust in classifier decisions. Keywords: GAN, Explainable AI, StyleGAN2, StyleSpace, Classifier Explanation, Interpretable Machine Learning.
