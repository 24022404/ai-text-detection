Autosomal Dominant Polycystic Kidney Disease (ADPKD) is a genetic disorder characterized by the growth of numerous cysts in the kidneys, leading to progressive kidney enlargement and functional decline. Accurate computation of Total Kidney Volume (TKV) from CT images is critical for disease monitoring and treatment planning. This study proposes a novel Multi-Task 3D Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) approach to automate TKV estimation from CT scans. The proposed model simultaneously performs kidney segmentation and volume calculation, leveraging shared feature representations to improve accuracy and efficiency. The network is trained on a dataset of annotated CT images, incorporating data augmentation techniques to enhance robustness. Experimental results demonstrate that the model achieves high segmentation accuracy (Dice coefficient of 0.95) and precise TKV estimation, outperforming traditional single-task methods and reducing manual intervention. The approach also shows strong generalizability across diverse patient datasets. This work contributes a scalable and efficient solution for ADPKD assessment, with potential applications in clinical decision-making and large-scale patient monitoring. Key innovations include the integration of multi-task learning for improved performance and the use of 3D CNNs to capture spatial relationships in volumetric data. Keywords: Autosomal Dominant Polycystic Kidney Disease (ADPKD), Total Kidney Volume (TKV), 3D Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Multi-Task Learning, Medical Image Segmentation.
