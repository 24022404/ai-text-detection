This paper presents a novel methodology for state registration and model comparison in Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) based on an Aggregated Wasserstein Metric. Addressing the inherent challenge of non-identifiability and state permutation in HMMs, our approach introduces both an efficient algorithm for aligning the latent states between different HMM instances and a rigorous, theoretically grounded metric that quantifies differences in their emission distributions. By leveraging the principles of optimal transport, our aggregated Wasserstein metric provides a robust and interpretable distance measure that captures both structural and parametric discrepancies between HMMs, supporting applications such as model selection, clustering, and anomaly detection. Experimental results on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that our method achieves superior performance in state correspondence and model discrimination compared to conventional metrics, while remaining computationally feasible for models of practical size. The proposed framework enhances the interpretability and robustness of HMM-based analyses, enabling more reliable downstream applications in speech recognition, bioinformatics, and time series modeling. Keywords: Hidden Markov Models, Wasserstein Metric, State Registration, Optimal Transport, Model Comparison, Time Series Analysis.
