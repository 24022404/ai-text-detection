This paper addresses the challenges of accurate vehicle tracking and vehicle re-identification (Re-ID) in multi-camera networks, a critical component for intelligent transportation systems and urban surveillance. We propose a novel framework utilizing teamed classifiers, which synergistically combine multiple machine learning models to improve robustness, extensibility, and computational efficiency. Our approach leverages deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for fine-grained feature extraction, supplemented by ensemble learning strategies that adapt dynamically to diverse camera perspectives and varying environmental conditions. Extensive experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our teamed classifiers outperform state-of-the-art methods in both tracking accuracy and Re-ID performance. In particular, the system maintains reliable vehicle identity association across disjoint camera views and exhibits resilience to occlusions, lighting changes, and heavy traffic scenarios. The architecture is designed for scalability, enabling seamless integration of new cameras and classifier components with minimal retraining. Our results establish the proposed framework as a robust, extensible, and fast solution for real-world multi-camera vehicle tracking and re-identification applications, with significant implications for smart city development, automated traffic analysis, and security monitoring. Key keywords: vehicle tracking, vehicle re-identification, multi-camera networks, teamed classifiers, deep learning, ensemble methods, computer vision.
