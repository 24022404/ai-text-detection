This paper introduces a novel semi-supervised regression approach based on deep kernel learning (DKL) to leverage unlabeled data effectively. Our objective is to improve regression performance by minimizing the predictive variance on unlabeled samples. The proposed method combines a deep neural network for feature extraction with a Gaussian process (GP) to model the regression function. Specifically, we train the neural network to minimize the GP's predictive variance on unlabeled data while simultaneously optimizing the supervised regression loss on labeled data. This encourages the model to learn features that lead to more confident and accurate predictions. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach on several benchmark datasets, showing that it outperforms existing semi-supervised regression methods and standard DKL models, especially when labeled data is scarce. The results highlight the potential of predictive variance minimization as a regularization technique for semi-supervised learning with deep kernel models. Keywords: Semi-supervised learning, Deep Kernel Learning, Regression, Gaussian Processes, Predictive Variance, Unlabeled Data.
