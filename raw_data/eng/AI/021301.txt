This paper presents *gundapusunil*, a novel approach for SemEval-2020 Task 8: Multimodal Memotion Analysis, which focuses on understanding and classifying internet memes based on their emotional and semantic content. The objective of this research is to address the challenge of analyzing memes, which combine visual and textual elements, to predict sentiment, emotion, and humor. To achieve this, we propose a multimodal deep learning framework that integrates state-of-the-art image and text processing techniques. Specifically, we employ pre-trained convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for visual feature extraction and transformer-based models for textual analysis, followed by a fusion mechanism to combine these modalities effectively. Our experiments demonstrate that the proposed approach achieves competitive performance on the task, outperforming baseline models in sentiment and emotion classification. The results highlight the importance of leveraging both visual and textual cues for accurate meme analysis. This work contributes to the growing field of multimodal sentiment analysis and provides insights into the challenges of interpreting internet memes, with potential applications in social media monitoring, content moderation, and user engagement analysis. Keywords: multimodal sentiment analysis, memes, deep learning, transformer models, emotion classification.
