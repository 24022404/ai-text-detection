The objective of this research is to address the problem of image de-quantization, a process aimed at reconstructing high-quality images from low-bit-depth quantized images. This challenge is critical in applications where images are subjected to lossy compression or storage constraints, leading to significant quality degradation.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel approach leveraging generative models as priors for image de-quantization. Specifically, we utilize state-of-the-art generative adversarial networks (GANs) to model the distribution of high-quality image data. These models are integrated into a de-quantization framework to provide probabilistic priors that guide the reconstruction of full-fidelity images from their quantized counterparts. The approach effectively captures fine details and textures by learning from extensive training datasets.

Results/Findings: Our experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in image quality when using generative models as priors compared to traditional de-quantization techniques. Evaluations across various datasets show that our method achieves superior quantitative metrics such as peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM), along with qualitative assessments indicating enhanced visual fidelity. The approach also shows robustness across different levels of quantization.

Conclusion/Implications: The integration of generative models as priors marks a significant advancement in the field of image de-quantization. This methodology not only improves upon existing techniques in terms of quality and detail preservation but also opens avenues for further research into applying deep learning models for various image restoration tasks. Our findings have potential implications for enhancing image quality in end-user applications, digital archives, and any domain where image compression is prevalent.

Keywords: image de-quantization, generative models, GANs, image reconstruction, lossy compression, deep learning, image quality restoration.
