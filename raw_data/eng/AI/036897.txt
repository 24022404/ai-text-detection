The construction process of de Bruijn graphs can be modified to create a transition graph for Sn, in which each vertex represents a permutation and edges are formed according to the order-isomorphism of their length n−1 suffixes. For instance, in S3, the vertex 231 is connected to 312, 213, and 321, as they all satisfy this specific relationship.
