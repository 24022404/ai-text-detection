The paper addresses the significant challenge of tracking road users accurately in real-time, which is crucial for traffic management and autonomous vehicle systems. The goal is to develop a robust methodology that enhances the precision and reliability of tracking multiple road users simultaneously.

Methods/Approach: The research leverages Constraint Programming (CP), a powerful paradigm in computational problem-solving, to create a model that captures the dynamic and unpredictable nature of road environments. The approach integrates spatial and temporal constraints to effectively monitor and track the movements of vehicles, bicycles, and pedestrians, ensuring comprehensive coverage and analysis.

Results/Findings: The proposed method demonstrates superior performance in accurately tracking road users compared to traditional techniques. Empirical evaluations on simulated and real-world datasets show an improvement in tracking accuracy and efficiency, even in complex scenarios with high user density and occlusions. The system exhibits a high degree of flexibility and adaptability to various road conditions.

Conclusion/Implications: This study contributes a novel application of Constraint Programming to road user tracking, offering significant advancements over existing approaches. The findings have important implications for the development of intelligent transportation systems, enhancing road safety, and supporting the deployment of autonomous traffic solutions. Future applications may expand to include integration with smart city infrastructures for broader impacts.

Keywords: Constraint Programming, road user tracking, traffic management, autonomous vehicles, intelligent transportation systems, real-time monitoring, spatial-temporal analysis.
