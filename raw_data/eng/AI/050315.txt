Human Attribute Recognition (HAR) aims to automatically identify visual attributes of individuals in images, a task challenged by background clutter and irrelevant object distractions. This paper introduces a novel Distraction-Aware Feature Learning (DAFL) framework for HAR, leveraging a coarse-to-fine attention mechanism to mitigate the impact of distracting regions. Our approach first employs a coarse attention module to globally suppress irrelevant background features. Subsequently, a fine-grained attention module refines the feature representation by focusing on subtle but discriminative attribute-related details. Furthermore, we introduce a distraction suppression loss that explicitly penalizes the model for attending to distracting regions, promoting robust feature learning. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our DAFL framework achieves state-of-the-art performance in HAR, outperforming existing methods, particularly in challenging scenarios with significant distractions. The proposed approach offers improved accuracy and robustness for HAR systems, making it suitable for real-world applications such as intelligent surveillance and person re-identification. Keywords: Human Attribute Recognition, Attention Mechanism, Distraction Suppression, Feature Learning, Computer Vision.
