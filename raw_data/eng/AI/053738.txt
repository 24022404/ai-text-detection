This paper investigates the problem of stochastic computation offloading in digital twin networks (DTNs) using deep reinforcement learning (DRL). We aim to minimize the long-term cost associated with latency and energy consumption in a dynamic DTN environment, where task arrivals and channel conditions are uncertain. We propose a novel DRL-based offloading strategy that leverages a deep Q-network (DQN) to learn optimal offloading decisions without requiring prior knowledge of the system's stochastic behavior. The DQN agent intelligently determines whether to process tasks locally or offload them to edge servers, considering the current network state represented by task queue lengths, channel quality, and server utilization. Simulation results demonstrate that our DRL-based approach significantly outperforms traditional offloading policies and other reinforcement learning baselines in terms of reducing latency and energy consumption. The proposed solution offers a promising avenue for efficient resource management in DTNs, enabling real-time applications and enhanced decision-making capabilities.

**Keywords:** Digital Twin Networks, Computation Offloading, Deep Reinforcement Learning, Stochastic Optimization, Edge Computing, DQN.
