**Abstract:**

Accurate 6D pose estimation of objects is a critical challenge in computer vision with significant applications in robotics, augmented reality, and autonomous systems. However, many existing methods suffer from limited robustness to occlusions and background clutter, often yielding inaccurate initial pose proposals. To address this limitation, we introduce Pose Proposal Critic, a novel pose refinement framework that leverages deep learning to robustly evaluate and improve pose hypotheses by learning to predict reprojection errors directly from images. Our approach consists of two main components: a pose proposal generator that produces multiple candidate poses for a given object, and a critic network that assesses the quality of each proposal by estimating its reprojection error. The critic network is trained end-to-end on large-scale datasets with synthetic and real-world images, enabling it to capture complex appearance and geometric cues. During inference, the critic efficiently selects and refines the best pose estimate, correcting systematic errors that are often overlooked by conventional geometric metrics. Experimental results demonstrate that Pose Proposal Critic consistently outperforms state-of-the-art pose refinement methods on standard benchmarks, achieving higher accuracy under challenging conditions of noise, occlusion, and varying lighting. Our framework is model-agnostic and can be easily integrated with a wide range of initial pose generators. These results highlight the effectiveness of learning-based reprojection error prediction for robust pose refinement and open new directions for high-precision object localization in dynamic environments. **Keywords:** pose estimation, pose refinement, reprojection error, deep learning, computer vision, robust localization.
