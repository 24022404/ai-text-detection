This paper presents D3DLO, a novel deep learning-based approach for 3D LiDAR odometry, addressing the challenges of accurate and robust localization in dynamic environments. Traditional LiDAR odometry methods often struggle with computational complexity and sensitivity to environmental changes. To overcome these limitations, D3DLO leverages a deep neural network architecture designed to extract and correlate spatial features from sequential LiDAR point clouds. The proposed method integrates a self-supervised learning framework, enabling the model to adapt to varying environments without requiring extensive labeled datasets. Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets demonstrate that D3DLO achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of localization accuracy and computational efficiency, outperforming existing geometric and learning-based approaches. The results highlight the system's robustness to noise and dynamic obstacles, making it suitable for real-world applications such as autonomous navigation and robotics. This work contributes to the advancement of LiDAR-based localization by introducing a scalable and adaptive solution that bridges the gap between traditional methods and modern deep learning techniques.  

**Keywords**: 3D LiDAR odometry, deep learning, self-supervised learning, localization, autonomous navigation, robotics.
