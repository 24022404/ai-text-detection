This paper presents D3DLO, a novel deep learning-based framework for 3D LiDAR odometry, designed to accurately estimate the motion of autonomous systems in complex environments. The objective of this study is to address the challenges associated with conventional odometry methods, such as sensitivity to noise, dynamic objects, and computational inefficiency, by leveraging deep neural networks to learn robust feature representations from raw 3D LiDAR data. Our approach utilizes a multi-stage architecture that combines point cloud processing and temporal sequence modeling to extract both spatial and temporal cues essential for precise pose estimation. Extensive experiments were conducted on publicly available LiDAR odometry datasets, where D3DLO demonstrates superior accuracy and robustness compared to existing state-of-the-art algorithms, particularly in scenarios involving sparse or partially occluded scans. Furthermore, D3DLO achieves real-time performance, making it suitable for deployment in practical autonomous navigation and robotics applications. The results indicate that deep learning can significantly enhance 3D odometry by providing richer scene understanding and improved generalizability across different operating conditions. The contributions of this work include an end-to-end deep neural network for 3D LiDAR odometry, a detailed analysis of feature learning from raw LiDAR scans, and comprehensive evaluations establishing new benchmarks in the field. The findings highlight the potential of deep learning to advance the accuracy, reliability, and efficiency of odometry systems for autonomous vehicles, robotics, and related domains. Key keywords: 3D LiDAR, deep learning, odometry, pose estimation, autonomous navigation, point cloud processing.
