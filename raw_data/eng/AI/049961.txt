This paper addresses the critical issue of ensuring the robustness of deep neural networks (DNNs) when processing video data. The objective is to develop a framework that provides rigorous guarantees on the robustness of DNNs against adversarial perturbations in video inputs. To achieve this, we propose a novel approach that combines techniques from adversarial training and robust optimization to enhance the resilience of DNNs on videos. Our method involves training DNNs with a custom-designed loss function that accounts for temporal dependencies in video sequences, thereby improving the model's ability to withstand adversarial attacks. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly improves the robustness of DNNs on various video classification tasks, outperforming state-of-the-art methods. The key findings of this research contribute to the development of more reliable and secure DNNs for video analysis applications, with potential implications for areas such as surveillance, autonomous vehicles, and healthcare monitoring. Our work highlights the importance of robustness guarantees in deep learning-based video processing and paves the way for future research in this field, with relevant keywords including deep neural networks, robustness guarantees, adversarial attacks, video analysis, and computer vision.
