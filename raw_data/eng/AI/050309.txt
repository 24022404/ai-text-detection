Melanoma, a deadly form of skin cancer, necessitates early and accurate detection for improved patient outcomes. This paper explores the application of knowledge transfer techniques using deep learning to enhance melanoma screening performance, particularly in scenarios with limited labeled data. We leverage pre-trained convolutional neural networks (CNNs) on large-scale image datasets and fine-tune them using a smaller, specialized melanoma image dataset. Our approach incorporates various fine-tuning strategies and data augmentation techniques to mitigate overfitting and improve generalization. The results demonstrate that knowledge transfer significantly boosts the accuracy, sensitivity, and specificity of melanoma classification compared to training CNNs from scratch. Specifically, our fine-tuned models achieve an average improvement of 15% in AUC score. This research highlights the effectiveness of knowledge transfer for medical image analysis, enabling more accurate and efficient melanoma screening, especially in resource-constrained environments. Keywords: Melanoma, Deep Learning, Knowledge Transfer, Convolutional Neural Networks, Skin Cancer, Image Classification, Medical Imaging.
