The rapid proliferation of Internet of Things (IoT) devices and edge computing has created a demand for efficient machine learning (ML) solutions on resource-constrained embedded systems. This paper explores TensorFlow Lite Micro (TFLM), a lightweight ML framework designed for TinyML applications, enabling on-device inference for microcontrollers and low-power devices. The objective of this research is to evaluate TFLM's capabilities in optimizing ML models for deployment on hardware with limited memory, processing power, and energy resources. We present a systematic approach to model optimization, including quantization, pruning, and efficient kernel implementations, tailored for embedded systems. Experimental results demonstrate TFLM's ability to achieve real-time inference with minimal latency and memory footprint, outperforming traditional ML frameworks in constrained environments. Furthermore, we compare TFLM's performance across various microcontroller architectures, highlighting its versatility and scalability. The findings underscore TFLM's potential to revolutionize embedded ML applications, enabling intelligent decision-making at the edge. This research contributes to the growing field of TinyML by providing insights into model optimization techniques and practical deployment strategies, paving the way for innovative applications in healthcare, agriculture, and industrial automation.  

**Keywords**: TensorFlow Lite Micro, TinyML, embedded systems, machine learning, model optimization, edge computing, IoT.
