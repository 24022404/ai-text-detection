Point cloud registration is a crucial task in various applications, including robotics, autonomous driving, and 3D reconstruction. This paper introduces PointNetLK, a novel and efficient approach for point cloud registration that leverages the representational power of PointNet within the Lucas-Kanade (LK) optimization framework. PointNetLK directly aligns point clouds by estimating a rigid transformation, enabling robust and accurate registration even with noisy or incomplete data. The method utilizes PointNet to extract global features from point clouds, subsequently employed within the LK iterative optimization to estimate the transformation parameters. We demonstrate that PointNetLK achieves superior registration accuracy and robustness compared to existing iterative closest point (ICP) based methods and other learning-based approaches, particularly in the presence of significant initial misalignment and noise. Furthermore, PointNetLK exhibits significantly improved computational efficiency due to the efficient feature extraction of PointNet. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate the effectiveness and efficiency of PointNetLK, highlighting its potential for real-time applications. Keywords: Point cloud registration, PointNet, Lucas-Kanade, 3D vision, ICP, Deep Learning.
