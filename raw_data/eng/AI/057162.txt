This paper explores the connectivity of neural networks through a topological lens, aiming to uncover the underlying structural properties that influence their performance. By leveraging tools from algebraic topology, we develop a novel framework to analyze and learn the connectivity patterns of neural networks. Our approach utilizes persistent homology to capture the topological features of network architectures, enabling the identification of critical connections and the detection of bottlenecks. Experimental results demonstrate that our method can effectively predict network performance and facilitate the design of more efficient architectures. The key findings of this study highlight the importance of topological connectivity in neural networks and provide new insights into the optimization of deep learning models. Our research contributes to the development of more interpretable and robust neural networks, with potential applications in areas such as computer vision, natural language processing, and AI model optimization. Key keywords: neural networks, topological data analysis, persistent homology, deep learning, network architecture.
