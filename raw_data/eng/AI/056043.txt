**Abstract**

Bayesian structure learning in graphical models is a central task in modern data analysis, enabling the discovery of complex dependency structures among variables. This paper introduces BDgraph, an R package designed to facilitate Bayesian structure learning in both Gaussian and discrete graphical models. BDgraph provides a comprehensive suite of algorithms that leverage Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) and birth-death processes for efficient exploration of graph space. The package accommodates various types of data, including continuous, discrete, and mixed measurements, and offers support for high-dimensional datasets. Extensive experiments demonstrate the package’s accuracy and scalability, outperforming existing tools in terms of model selection and computational performance. BDgraph features intuitive interfaces for model specification, result visualization, and posterior analysis, making it accessible to users with diverse backgrounds. The package’s modular design allows for easy integration with other R tools and custom workflows. The contributions of BDgraph advance the current practice of Bayesian graphical modeling by providing a robust, flexible, and user-friendly platform for researchers and practitioners in statistics, machine learning, and computational biology. Keywords: Bayesian structure learning, graphical models, Markov Chain Monte Carlo, R package, model selection, high-dimensional data.
