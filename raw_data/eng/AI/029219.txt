This paper presents a novel approach to 3D shape reconstruction from sketches using multi-view convolutional networks. The objective is to recover accurate 3D models from 2D sketches, addressing the challenge of incomplete and ambiguous input data. Our method employs a deep learning framework that integrates information from multiple views of the sketch, leveraging the strengths of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) in image processing. The proposed network architecture takes as input a set of 2D sketches from different viewpoints and outputs a 3D shape representation. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving high-quality 3D reconstructions that surpass existing state-of-the-art methods. The key contributions of this research include the development of a multi-view CNN architecture and a novel view-pooling strategy, enabling the reconstruction of complex 3D shapes from sparse and incomplete sketch data. This work has significant implications for applications in computer-aided design, robotics, and computer vision, and highlights the potential of deep learning techniques in 3D shape reconstruction. Key keywords: 3D reconstruction, multi-view convolutional networks, sketch-based modeling, deep learning, computer vision.
