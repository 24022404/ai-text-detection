The paper addresses the challenge of accurately and robustly explaining machine learning model predictions through Shapley Values, focusing on identifying and highlighting locally important variables. The research aims to enhance the interpretability of complex models by offering a clearer understanding of input feature contributions.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel framework that refines the computation of Shapley Values, optimizing their accuracy and stability while maintaining computational efficiency. The approach involves leveraging advanced sampling techniques and optimized algorithms to ensure precise attribution of feature importance across various model architectures.

Results/Findings: Our framework significantly improves the robustness of Shapley Value estimations compared to traditional methods. Experimental results demonstrate enhanced accuracy in identifying crucial local variables for diverse datasets and models, including deep learning architectures. We also illustrate the framework's superior performance in terms of computational efficiency and stability through comprehensive benchmarks.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes to the field of explainable AI by providing more reliable tools for model interpretation, crucial for applications in sensitive domains like healthcare, finance, and autonomous systems. By focusing on local variable importance, our method not only aids in transparent decision-making but also supports model debugging and feature selection processes. This advancement in Shapley Value computation makes it a valuable asset for practitioners and researchers seeking to enhance the interpretability of complex machine learning models. Key keywords: Shapley Values, explainable AI, feature importance, local variables, model interpretability.
