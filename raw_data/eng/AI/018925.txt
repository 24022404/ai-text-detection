Electrocardiogram (ECG) arrhythmia classification is crucial for timely diagnosis and treatment of cardiovascular diseases. This paper investigates a transfer learning approach for improved arrhythmia classification accuracy using two-dimensional deep convolutional neural network (CNN) features. Specifically, we leverage features extracted from a pre-trained CNN model on a large image dataset and fine-tune a classifier on these features using ECG data. This method addresses the limited availability of labeled ECG data, which often hinders the training of robust deep learning models from scratch. Our approach significantly reduces training time and improves classification performance compared to traditional machine learning methods and CNNs trained directly on ECG signals. Experimental results on a benchmark ECG dataset demonstrate that our transfer learning model achieves a higher accuracy and F1-score in identifying various arrhythmia types. The findings suggest that transfer learning from 2D deep CNN features offers a promising solution for ECG arrhythmia classification, particularly in scenarios with limited labeled data. Keywords: ECG, Arrhythmia Classification, Transfer Learning, Convolutional Neural Networks, Deep Learning, Healthcare.
