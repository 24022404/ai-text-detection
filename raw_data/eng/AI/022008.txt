Deep metric learning (DML) aims to learn effective embeddings for data points, enabling similarity-based tasks. Traditional DML methods often operate in an offline setting, requiring the entire dataset to be available during training. This paper addresses the challenge of adapting DML to online scenarios where data arrives sequentially. We introduce Online Progressive Deep Metric Learning (OPDML), a novel approach that progressively updates the embedding space with each incoming batch of data. OPDML employs a dynamic triplet selection strategy to maintain a balance between exploration and exploitation, ensuring efficient learning from streaming data. Furthermore, we incorporate a momentum-based update mechanism to stabilize the training process and prevent catastrophic forgetting. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that OPDML achieves comparable or superior performance to existing offline DML methods, while significantly outperforming online DML baselines. The proposed approach provides a practical solution for real-time applications such as online recommendation systems and continuous data stream analysis. Keywords: Deep Metric Learning, Online Learning, Progressive Learning, Embedding, Similarity Learning, Streaming Data.
