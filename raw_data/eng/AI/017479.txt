This paper presents a novel approach to semantic object prediction and spatial sound super-resolution using binaural sounds. The objective is to enhance the immersive experience in virtual and augmented reality environments by accurately predicting object locations and reconstructing high-quality 3D audio. Our method employs a deep learning-based framework that leverages binaural audio cues to predict semantic objects and their spatial locations. The approach utilizes a combination of convolutional neural networks and recurrent neural networks to learn spatial and temporal patterns in binaural sounds. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in object prediction accuracy and sound super-resolution quality compared to existing methods. The proposed system achieves a mean average precision of 85% in object prediction and a peak signal-to-noise ratio of 25 dB in sound super-resolution. Our research contributes to the development of more realistic and engaging virtual environments, with potential applications in gaming, film, and therapy. Key contributions include the introduction of a novel binaural sound-based approach for semantic object prediction and spatial sound super-resolution, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in enhancing immersive experiences. Relevant keywords: binaural sounds, semantic object prediction, spatial sound super-resolution, deep learning, virtual reality, augmented reality, 3D audio reconstruction.
