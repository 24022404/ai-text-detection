Semi-supervised hashing aims to learn efficient binary codes by leveraging both labeled and unlabeled data. This paper introduces a novel Pairwise Teacher-Student Network (PTSN) for improved semi-supervised hashing. The proposed PTSN framework consists of a teacher network trained on labeled data to generate pseudo-labels for unlabeled data, and a student network trained on both labeled data and pseudo-labeled data. To ensure the quality of pseudo-labels and mitigate confirmation bias, we introduce a pairwise constraint that enforces consistency between the teacher's and student's predictions on unlabeled data pairs. Specifically, the student network is trained to predict hash codes similar to the teacher network's output for similar pairs, while predicting dissimilar hash codes for dissimilar pairs. The teacher network is updated using an exponential moving average of the student's weights, promoting stable and reliable pseudo-label generation. Experiments on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that PTSN achieves significant improvements in retrieval accuracy compared to state-of-the-art semi-supervised hashing methods. The results highlight the effectiveness of the proposed pairwise teacher-student learning strategy in exploiting unlabeled data and learning high-quality hash codes. Keywords: Semi-Supervised Hashing, Teacher-Student Learning, Hashing, Information Retrieval, Pairwise Constraints, Binary Codes.
