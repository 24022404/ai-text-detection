This paper explores the concept of cooperative learning in multi-agent environments through policy search methods. The objective is to develop a framework that enables agents to learn cooperative behaviors and achieve common goals. We propose a novel approach that utilizes policy search algorithms to optimize agent policies, promoting cooperation and mutual benefit. Our method involves a decentralized learning process, where each agent learns to adapt its policy based on the actions and outcomes of other agents. The results show that our approach outperforms traditional methods in various cooperative tasks, demonstrating improved cooperation and increased overall performance. The key findings highlight the importance of adaptive policy search in facilitating cooperation among agents. This research contributes to the development of more effective cooperative learning methods, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, game theory, and social network analysis. Key keywords: multi-agent systems, cooperative learning, policy search, decentralized learning, adaptive algorithms.
