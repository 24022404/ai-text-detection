Geodesic Active Contours (GACs) are powerful tools for image segmentation, but their performance is highly dependent on the initial contour and can be sensitive to noise. This paper introduces a novel algorithm for GACs based on the characteristic function, designed to improve robustness and accuracy. The proposed method replaces the traditional level set function with a characteristic function, offering a more stable representation of the evolving contour and enabling a more accurate approximation of the geodesic distance. The algorithm utilizes a narrow band implementation to enhance computational efficiency. Experimental results on synthetic and real images demonstrate that the proposed method achieves improved segmentation accuracy and noise resilience compared to conventional GAC implementations, particularly in images with weak object boundaries and high levels of noise. Key findings indicate a significant reduction in the sensitivity to initial contour placement and improved convergence. This work contributes a robust and efficient GAC framework suitable for various image segmentation applications. Keywords: Geodesic Active Contours, Image Segmentation, Characteristic Function, Level Set, Active Contours, Computer Vision.
