Multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) faces challenges in coordinating multiple agents in complex environments with partial observability. This paper introduces MAGNet, a novel Multi-Agent Graph Network architecture designed to enhance inter-agent communication and decision-making in MARL systems. MAGNet employs a graph neural network to explicitly model agent relationships and propagate information between agents, enabling the learning of more sophisticated coordination strategies. The proposed architecture integrates a message-passing mechanism that allows agents to selectively attend to relevant information from their neighbors, improving robustness to noisy or irrelevant communications. We evaluate MAGNet on several benchmark MARL environments, demonstrating significant performance improvements in terms of both learning speed and final performance compared to state-of-the-art MARL algorithms. Ablation studies validate the importance of the graph network structure and the selective message-passing mechanism. The results highlight MAGNet's ability to learn effective communication protocols and achieve superior cooperative behavior, advancing the state-of-the-art in deep MARL. Keywords: Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning, Graph Neural Networks, Deep Learning, Multi-Agent Systems, Communication, Coordination.
