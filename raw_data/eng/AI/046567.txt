Integrating blockchain technology, exemplified by innovative platforms like Bitcoin and Ethereum, with traditional web technologies presents a promising pathway toward a hybrid digital ecosystem. By weaving together blockchain’s core values of transparency, security, and decentralization with the familiar usability and accessibility of the conventional web, such an approach expands the practical reach and user-friendliness of blockchain applications. This fusion not only enhances trust and reliability but also paves the way for a more interconnected and inclusive digital future. The central focus of the thesis is to create a robust social login solution for decentralized applications (Dapps) by utilizing Shamir’s Secret Sharing (SSS) and verifying identities through Distributed Key Generation (DKG), thereby harnessing the combined strengths of both technological domains.
