Time series classification is a critical task in various domains, including finance, healthcare, and IoT, where understanding temporal patterns is essential. This paper introduces a novel approach leveraging the Sinkhorn divergence of topological signature estimates to enhance classification accuracy. The proposed method combines topological data analysis (TDA) with optimal transport theory to capture both the geometric and temporal structures of time series data. Specifically, we compute persistent homology-based topological signatures to represent the underlying shape of the data and employ the Sinkhorn divergence to measure the similarity between these signatures efficiently. Our approach addresses the limitations of traditional distance metrics by incorporating a robust, scalable, and computationally efficient framework. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate significant improvements in classification performance compared to state-of-the-art methods, particularly in handling noisy and high-dimensional time series. The findings highlight the potential of integrating TDA and optimal transport for time series analysis, offering a new perspective for feature extraction and similarity measurement. This work contributes to advancing time series classification by providing a mathematically grounded and interpretable solution with broad applicability across diverse domains.  

**Keywords**: Time series classification, Sinkhorn divergence, topological data analysis, persistent homology, optimal transport, feature extraction.
