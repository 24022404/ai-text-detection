This paper investigates the interplay between deep learning-based object detectors and Siamese network-based trackers within the context of video surveillance. The objective is to analyze how the integration of robust deep detectors and efficient Siamese trackers can enhance the accuracy and reliability of multi-object tracking systems in dynamic surveillance environments. We propose a hybrid framework that systematically combines state-of-the-art convolutional neural network (CNN) detectors with advanced Siamese tracking architectures. Our approach evaluates the impact of different interaction strategies—such as periodic detector updates, confidence-based fusion, and adaptive tracker re-initialization—on long-term tracking performance and real-time processing requirements. Experimental results on several challenging video surveillance datasets demonstrate that our integrated system substantially improves object localization accuracy, reduces tracking drift, and maintains high frame rates compared to standalone detection or tracking methods. The findings reveal key insights into designing synergistic detection-tracking pipelines for critical surveillance applications. By optimizing the interaction between deep detectors and Siamese trackers, our work contributes to the development of more intelligent, resilient, and scalable video surveillance solutions. Keywords: video surveillance, deep learning, object detection, Siamese tracker, multi-object tracking, real-time monitoring, system integration.
