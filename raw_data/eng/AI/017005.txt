This paper proposes a novel approach to encode inertial sensor data into images for human action recognition, addressing the challenge of effectively representing complex temporal and spatial information. Our method utilizes a combination of signal processing and computer vision techniques to transform inertial sensor data into a visual representation, enabling the application of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for action recognition. The proposed approach is evaluated on a large-scale dataset, demonstrating significant improvements in recognition accuracy compared to traditional methods. Key findings include the ability to capture subtle patterns in sensor data and robustness to noise and variability. The contributions of this research lie in the development of a unique encoding scheme, which enables the leveraging of powerful CNN architectures for human action recognition. This work has important implications for applications such as healthcare, surveillance, and human-computer interaction, and highlights the potential of inertial sensor data for action recognition. Relevant keywords include: inertial sensors, human action recognition, convolutional neural networks, sensor data encoding, computer vision.
