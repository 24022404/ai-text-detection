This paper investigates the robustness of regularized policies in reinforcement learning, with a focus on their ability to maximize rewards in the presence of uncertainty. Our objective is to understand how regularization techniques, such as entropy regularization, can improve the reward robustness of policies. We employ a novel approach that combines policy regularization with robust optimization methods to develop policies that are resilient to reward perturbations. Our results show that regularized policies outperform their unregularized counterparts in terms of reward robustness, demonstrating improved performance in a variety of environments. We find that the proposed approach leads to policies that are more adaptable and able to recover from unexpected changes in the reward structure. The key contribution of this work is the development of a framework for training reward-robust policies using regularization techniques, which has important implications for the deployment of reinforcement learning agents in real-world applications. Our research highlights the importance of regularization in reinforcement learning and provides a foundation for future work on reward robustness and policy regularization, with relevant keywords including reinforcement learning, policy regularization, reward robustness, and robust optimization.
