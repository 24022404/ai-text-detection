The increasing deployment of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) in critical applications necessitates robust mechanisms to guarantee their reliability and safety. This paper introduces CNN-Cert, a comprehensive framework devised to certify the robustness of CNN models against adversarial attacks.

Methods/Approach: CNN-Cert utilizes a novel combination of analytical techniques and computational methods to evaluate the robustness bounds of CNNs. The framework employs a uniquely structured algorithm that integrates interval arithmetic with verification strategies tailored to handle the high-dimensional input spaces typical of CNN architectures. This robust certification process is designed to be efficient, reducing the computational burden generally associated with such evaluations.

Results/Findings: Through extensive experimentation, CNN-Cert demonstrates superior performance in certifying robustness, achieving significant improvements in verification times compared to existing methods. Our results highlight the framework's ability to evaluate larger networks with higher precision, providing comprehensive robustness certifications without compromising on speed or computational efficiency.

Conclusion/Implications: CNN-Cert presents a significant advancement in the field of neural network verification by offering a scalable and efficient solution for robustness certification. The framework's innovative approach not only enhances the reliability of CNN models but also supports their deployment in safety-critical environments. By establishing rigorous robustness guarantees, CNN-Cert sets a new standard for the evaluation and improvement of neural network resilience against adversarial threats.

Keywords: CNN-Cert, Convolutional Neural Networks, robustness certification, adversarial attacks, interval arithmetic, neural network verification.
