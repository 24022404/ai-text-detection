This paper presents a novel approach to reconstructing 3D face shapes from diverse sources, including images, videos, and 2D sketches. The objective is to develop a robust and flexible method that can learn face shape from various types of input data. Our approach utilizes a deep learning-based framework that leverages a large dataset of faces with ground truth 3D models to learn a mapping between 2D and 3D face representations. The method employs a transformer-based architecture to effectively capture the complex relationships between different facial features and shapes. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our approach in reconstructing accurate 3D face models from a wide range of input sources, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods. The key contributions of this research include the development of a versatile 3D face reconstruction framework, the introduction of a novel transformer-based architecture for face shape learning, and the creation of a large-scale dataset for training and evaluation. This work has significant implications for various applications, including face recognition, animation, and virtual reality, and highlights the potential of deep learning techniques for 3D face reconstruction from diverse sources. Key keywords: 3D face reconstruction, deep learning, transformer architecture, face shape learning, computer vision.
