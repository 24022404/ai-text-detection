This paper introduces a novel approach to geodesic regression, focusing on computational efficiency and predictive accuracy. Traditional geodesic regression methods, while effective, often suffer from high computational costs, particularly when dealing with large datasets or complex manifolds. To address this, we propose Fast Predictive Simple Geodesic Regression (FPSGR), a method that leverages a simplified geodesic model coupled with a fast prediction strategy. FPSGR approximates geodesics using a streamlined iterative process, significantly reducing the computational burden. Furthermore, it incorporates a predictive framework that allows for efficient estimation of geodesic paths for new data points. Our experiments, conducted on both synthetic and real-world datasets, demonstrate that FPSGR achieves comparable or superior predictive performance to existing geodesic regression techniques, while offering a substantial reduction in computation time. Specifically, FPSGR shows a speedup of up to an order of magnitude in certain scenarios. These results highlight the potential of FPSGR for applications requiring fast and accurate geodesic regression, such as shape analysis, medical image analysis, and machine learning on manifolds. Key words: Geodesic Regression, Manifolds, Shape Analysis, Predictive Modeling, Computational Efficiency.
