Graph similarity computation is a fundamental problem in various domains, including social network analysis, bioinformatics, and recommendation systems. However, existing methods struggle to scale efficiently for large graphs while maintaining accuracy. This paper introduces **CoSimGNN**, a novel graph neural network (GNN)-based framework designed for large-scale graph similarity computation. The proposed approach leverages a combination of graph coarsening and hierarchical embedding techniques to reduce computational complexity without sacrificing performance. CoSimGNN employs a multi-level graph representation, where coarse-grained embeddings are progressively refined to capture both local and global structural features. We evaluate the framework on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its ability to outperform state-of-the-art methods in terms of both accuracy and scalability. Experimental results show that CoSimGNN achieves up to a 30% improvement in similarity computation accuracy while reducing runtime by 40% compared to existing approaches. The framework's efficiency makes it particularly suitable for real-world applications involving massive graphs. This research contributes a scalable and effective solution to graph similarity computation, paving the way for broader adoption in large-scale graph analytics.  

**Keywords**: Graph similarity computation, graph neural networks (GNNs), large-scale graphs, graph coarsening, hierarchical embedding, scalability.
