This paper introduces *Neural Network iLQR*, a novel reinforcement learning architecture designed for trajectory optimization in complex dynamic systems. The objective of this research is to address the limitations of traditional iterative Linear Quadratic Regulator (iLQR) methods, which often struggle with scalability and adaptability in high-dimensional environments. To overcome these challenges, we propose a hybrid approach that integrates deep neural networks with the iLQR framework, enabling efficient learning and optimization of trajectories in real-time. Our method leverages the expressive power of neural networks to approximate system dynamics and cost functions, while retaining the analytical rigor of iLQR for precise control.  

Experiments conducted on simulated robotic systems and autonomous vehicle navigation tasks demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed architecture. Results show significant improvements in convergence speed, trajectory accuracy, and robustness compared to conventional iLQR and other state-of-the-art reinforcement learning methods. The Neural Network iLQR achieves up to 30% faster convergence and 20% higher precision in trajectory tracking, particularly in scenarios with nonlinear dynamics and uncertain environments.  

The key contribution of this work lies in its ability to bridge the gap between model-based and model-free reinforcement learning, offering a scalable and adaptive solution for trajectory optimization. This architecture has broad applications in robotics, autonomous systems, and control engineering, where efficient and accurate trajectory planning is critical. Keywords: trajectory optimization, iLQR, reinforcement learning, neural networks, robotics, autonomous systems.
