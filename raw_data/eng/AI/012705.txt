**Abstract**

Accurate identification of the primary tumor origin of lung nodules is critical for effective patient management and tailored therapy in oncology. This study proposes a novel approach for classifying the primary origin of lung nodules using spectral computed tomography (CT) imaging and transfer learning techniques. The developed framework utilizes a pre-trained deep convolutional neural network, fine-tuned on a curated spectral CT dataset, to extract discriminative features from lung nodule images. The system’s performance was evaluated on a multi-institutional dataset encompassing diverse primary tumor types, with extensive validation against expert-annotated labels. Comparative analysis demonstrated that the transfer learning-based model outperformed conventional machine learning pipelines and standard CT image analysis, achieving an overall classification accuracy of 90.2% and a significant improvement in sensitivity for rarer tumor types. These findings highlight the potential of integrating spectral CT with advanced deep learning algorithms to enhance diagnostic precision in pulmonary oncology. The proposed method offers a promising, non-invasive solution to assist radiologists in clinical decision-making, with implications for improved diagnostic workflows and patient outcomes. Keywords: spectral CT, transfer learning, lung nodule classification, deep learning, primary tumor origin, medical imaging.
