This paper addresses the challenge of anomaly detection in multivariate time-series data, which is critical for applications ranging from industry monitoring to cybersecurity. The existing methods struggle with capturing complex dependencies and timely detection in dynamic environments.

Methods/Approach: We introduce Event2Graph, an innovative anomaly detection framework that leverages an event-driven bipartite graph model. This approach captures both temporal and inter-variable relationships to effectively identify anomalies. The framework constructs bipartite graphs from time-series events, enabling the integration of heterogeneous data sources and the dynamic relationships therein. Advanced graph-based algorithms are employed to analyze the pattern deviations, thus enhancing detection speed and accuracy.

Results/Findings: Experimentation on diverse, real-world datasets demonstrates that Event2Graph significantly outperforms traditional and state-of-the-art anomaly detection techniques. Our model achieves superior accuracy and lower false positive rates, benefiting from its robust handling of intricate variable interactions and scalability. Additionally, detailed case studies illustrate Event2Graph's proficiency in real-time anomaly identification with high precision.

Conclusion/Implications: Event2Graph provides a novel contribution to multivariate time-series anomaly detection by enabling enhanced precision and adaptability through its event-driven bipartite graph approach. This framework holds substantial potential for various applications requiring timely anomaly detection, such as predictive maintenance, financial fraud detection, and security monitoring. The integration of heterogeneous data and advanced graph processing paves the way for future developments in real-time data monitoring systems.

Keywords: anomaly detection, multivariate time-series, bipartite graph, event-driven model, real-time monitoring, time-series analysis.
