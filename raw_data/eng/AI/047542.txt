Stereo depth estimation is a fundamental task in computer vision, crucial for applications like autonomous navigation and 3D scene understanding. This paper introduces MTStereo 2.0, an enhanced algorithm building upon the Max-tree representation to achieve improved accuracy in stereo matching. Our approach refines the initial disparity map generated from Census transform by incorporating adaptive support weights derived from Max-tree filtering. Specifically, we introduce novel cost aggregation strategies within the Max-tree framework, allowing for more robust handling of occlusions and textureless regions. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including Middlebury and KITTI, demonstrate that MTStereo 2.0 achieves state-of-the-art performance, significantly reducing disparity errors compared to existing Max-tree based and other leading stereo algorithms. The proposed enhancements demonstrate the effectiveness of Max-trees for accurate and efficient stereo depth estimation, offering a promising direction for real-time 3D reconstruction and scene analysis. Keywords: Stereo vision, Depth estimation, Max-tree, Cost aggregation, Computer vision, 3D reconstruction.
