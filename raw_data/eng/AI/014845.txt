The objective of this study is to introduce a comprehensive benchmark for Image Scene Graph Generation (SGG), a critical task in visual understanding that involves detecting objects and predicting their relationships within images. Our approach involves the development of a standardized dataset and evaluation protocol to effectively compare and assess various SGG methodologies. We utilize state-of-the-art deep learning models, including convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and graph-based networks, to generate refined scene graphs that capture complex semantic relationships. Our findings indicate that our benchmark facilitates the identification of strengths and limitations within current SGG techniques, presenting a clear landscape of performance across different models. We demonstrate that our benchmark not only robustly compares existing methods but also highlights key areas for future research and development. The implications of this work include providing the research community with an essential tool for driving innovation in scene graph generation, thereby enhancing machine vision applications such as image retrieval, automatic image annotation, and human-computer interaction. Key contributions of this research are the establishment of a unified evaluation framework and the advancement of computational techniques in SGG. Keywords include scene graph generation, visual understanding, deep learning, CNNs, graph networks, and image semantic relationships.
