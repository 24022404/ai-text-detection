Inferring the structure of ordinary differential equations (ODEs) from observed data is a challenging yet crucial problem in various scientific domains. This paper introduces a novel approach that combines sparse regression techniques with a library of candidate functions to identify the governing equations of dynamical systems. Our method leverages an adaptive thresholding algorithm within a least-squares optimization framework, effectively selecting the most relevant terms and determining their corresponding coefficients. We demonstrate the efficacy of our approach on several benchmark systems, including the Lorenz attractor and the Van der Pol oscillator, showcasing its ability to accurately recover the underlying ODE structure even in the presence of noise. The results indicate a significant improvement in equation discovery compared to existing methods, particularly in identifying nonlinear interactions and handling limited data. This work contributes a robust and efficient technique for model discovery, enabling a deeper understanding of complex systems across various disciplines. Keywords: Ordinary Differential Equations, System Identification, Sparse Regression, Dynamical Systems, Equation Discovery.
