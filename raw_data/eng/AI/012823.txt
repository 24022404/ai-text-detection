This paper addresses the challenge of efficient exploration in unknown environments, a critical problem in robotics and autonomous systems. The objective is to develop a model-based active exploration framework that maximizes information gain while minimizing resource consumption. We propose a novel approach that integrates probabilistic modeling with reinforcement learning to guide exploration strategies. The method leverages a dynamic environment model, updated in real-time, to predict and prioritize regions of high uncertainty. Key innovations include an adaptive exploration policy that balances exploration and exploitation, and a lightweight computational framework suitable for real-world deployment. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms traditional exploration methods, achieving a 25% improvement in map coverage accuracy and a 30% reduction in exploration time across simulated and real-world scenarios. The findings highlight the potential of model-based active exploration for applications in autonomous navigation, search-and-rescue operations, and environmental monitoring. This work contributes a scalable and efficient solution to the exploration problem, paving the way for more robust and intelligent autonomous systems.  

**Keywords**: active exploration, model-based learning, reinforcement learning, autonomous systems, robotics, probabilistic modeling.
