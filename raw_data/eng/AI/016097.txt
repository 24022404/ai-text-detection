This paper introduces DirectShape, a novel approach for visual vehicle pose and shape estimation. The objective is to accurately align shape priors with input images, leveraging photometric information to refine both pose and shape estimates. Our method employs a direct photometric alignment strategy, combining shape priors with a robust optimization framework to achieve precise vehicle pose and shape reconstruction. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of DirectShape, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency. Key findings include improved pose estimation and shape reconstruction on various benchmark datasets, with significant reductions in computational complexity. The proposed approach contributes to the field of computer vision and 3D reconstruction, with potential applications in autonomous driving, robotics, and surveillance. By highlighting the importance of direct photometric alignment and shape priors, this research provides a new perspective on visual vehicle pose and shape estimation, with relevant keywords including 3D reconstruction, shape priors, photometric alignment, and vehicle pose estimation.
