This paper introduces a novel Structure Coherence Component (SCC) designed to enhance the speed and accuracy of face alignment. Addressing the challenges of accurately locating facial landmarks, particularly under varying poses and expressions, our method leverages the inherent structural relationships between these landmarks. The proposed SCC incorporates a graph-based representation of facial structure, allowing for efficient propagation of information and robust handling of occlusions. We present a fast optimization algorithm that integrates SCC into a cascaded regression framework, significantly reducing computational complexity. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art accuracy while maintaining significantly faster processing speeds compared to existing methods. Specifically, our approach shows a 20% improvement in accuracy and a 30% reduction in processing time. The SCC effectively enforces structural constraints, leading to more consistent and reliable face alignment. This work contributes a practical and efficient solution for real-time face alignment applications.
Keywords: Face Alignment, Facial Landmarks, Structure Coherence, Cascaded Regression, Real-time Performance.
