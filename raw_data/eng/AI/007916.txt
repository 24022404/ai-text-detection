This paper addresses the challenge of credit assignment in multi-agent reinforcement learning, where the goal is to identify the contribution of each agent to the team's overall performance. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that enables efficient and effective learning in cooperative multi-agent environments. We propose a meta-policy gradient method that adapts to the credit assignment problem by learning a meta-policy that guides the optimization of individual agent policies. Our approach leverages the idea of meta-learning to learn a policy that can adapt to different credit assignment scenarios. The results show that our method outperforms existing credit assignment methods in several cooperative multi-agent tasks, demonstrating improved learning efficiency and scalability. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of adaptive credit assignment in multi-agent reinforcement learning and demonstrate the potential of meta-policy gradient methods in this domain. This work contributes to the development of more effective and efficient multi-agent reinforcement learning algorithms, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, game playing, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: multi-agent reinforcement learning, credit assignment, meta-policy gradient, meta-learning, cooperative learning.
