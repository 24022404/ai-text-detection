This paper addresses the challenge of orphan screening by introducing a novel approach based on corresponding projections. The objective is to develop an efficient method for identifying potential matches between orphan data points and existing datasets. Our approach utilizes a combination of dimensionality reduction and projection techniques to create a robust and scalable framework. The methodology involves applying a variant of the Johnson-Lindenstrauss lemma to project high-dimensional data onto a lower-dimensional space, while preserving the underlying structure and relationships. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in identifying corresponding projections, with a significant improvement in accuracy and efficiency compared to existing methods. The findings of this research have important implications for various applications, including data integration, entity resolution, and anomaly detection. By providing a robust and efficient solution for orphan screening, this work contributes to the development of more accurate and reliable data analysis pipelines, with potential applications in fields such as bioinformatics, finance, and cybersecurity, leveraging key technologies like data mining, machine learning, and data visualization.
