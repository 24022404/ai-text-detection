This paper addresses the challenge of accurately forecasting grouped time series data, which is prevalent in various domains such as economics, finance, and weather prediction. The objective is to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of forecasting by leveraging advanced clustering techniques to handle large and complex datasets.

Methods/Approach: We present a novel forecasting method that integrates the k-Means clustering algorithm with traditional time series forecasting models. The approach begins by grouping similar time series using k-Means, which minimizes intra-cluster variance and helps identify underlying patterns. Post-clustering, a forecasting model tailored to each group is applied, allowing for more specialized and accurate predictions.

Results/Findings: Through extensive experiments conducted on multiple real-world datasets, our method demonstrates a substantial improvement in forecasting accuracy when compared to conventional single-model approaches. The k-Means-based clustering enhances model performance by effectively capturing group-specific characteristics. Quantitative results show a significant reduction in forecasting errors, evidenced by metrics such as Mean Absolute Error (MAE) and Root Mean Square Error (RMSE).

Conclusion/Implications: The integration of k-Means clustering with time series forecasting models presents a promising direction for improving prediction accuracy in grouped datasets. This method not only optimizes forecast efficiency but also adapts flexibly to various applications, including economic trend analysis, stock market prediction, and resource allocation. Our findings suggest that employing clustering techniques can substantially refine forecasting strategies, paving the way for more informed decision-making in fields that rely on accurate time series predictions.

Keywords: time series forecasting, k-Means clustering, grouped datasets, prediction accuracy, economic trend analysis, resource allocation.
