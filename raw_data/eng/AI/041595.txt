This paper proposes an innovative approach to breast cancer image classification by integrating an attention model into a deep neural network. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of breast cancer diagnosis using medical imaging. Our method employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) as the backbone, enhanced with an attention mechanism that focuses on the most relevant regions of the image. The attention model allows the network to weigh the importance of different features, leading to more accurate classifications. Experimental results demonstrate that our attention model enhanced network outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving a high accuracy rate and robust performance on various breast cancer image datasets. The key findings of this study highlight the potential of attention mechanisms in medical image analysis, particularly in breast cancer diagnosis. Our approach contributes to the development of more effective and reliable computer-aided diagnosis systems, with potential applications in clinical practice. Key keywords: breast cancer, image classification, attention model, deep learning, convolutional neural network, medical imaging.
