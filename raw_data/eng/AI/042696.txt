The increasing reliance on Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) networks for disaster relief operations necessitates efficient spectrum management to ensure reliable communication in dynamic and resource-constrained environments. This paper proposes an autonomous spectrum management scheme tailored for UAV networks, addressing the challenges of interference, limited bandwidth, and fluctuating network conditions during disaster scenarios. The proposed approach leverages a distributed reinforcement learning framework, enabling UAVs to autonomously allocate and optimize spectrum resources in real-time. By integrating adaptive channel selection and power control mechanisms, the scheme minimizes interference and maximizes network throughput. Simulation results demonstrate significant improvements in spectral efficiency and communication reliability compared to traditional centralized and static allocation methods. The proposed system achieves up to a 30% reduction in packet loss and a 25% increase in data transmission rates under high-density UAV deployments. This research contributes a novel, scalable solution for enhancing UAV network performance in disaster relief operations, with potential applications in emergency response, search-and-rescue missions, and critical infrastructure monitoring.  

**Keywords**: UAV networks, spectrum management, disaster relief, reinforcement learning, autonomous systems, interference mitigation.
