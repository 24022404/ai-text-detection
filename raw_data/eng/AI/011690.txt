This paper addresses the critical challenge of accurately and efficiently detecting fire and flames in real-time for enhanced safety and rapid response in various environments. We propose a robust solution that leverages advanced computer vision techniques, combining deep learning-based convolutional neural networks (CNNs) with traditional image processing methods to improve detection accuracy and minimize false alarms. Our approach utilizes a curated dataset of diverse fire and non-fire scenarios to train the model, ensuring high generalizability across different lighting and background conditions. Experimental results demonstrate that our system achieves superior detection performance, with an accuracy exceeding 96% and low latency suitable for real-time deployment on edge devices. Furthermore, a comparative analysis with existing methods confirms notable improvements in both speed and reliability. The versatility of the proposed model allows integration into surveillance systems, smart cameras, and IoT-enabled safety networks. In conclusion, our solution represents a significant step towards practical and dependable real-time fire and flame detection, offering substantial benefits for public safety, industrial monitoring, and emergency response applications. 

Keywords: fire detection, flame detection, real-time, deep learning, computer vision, CNN, safety systems, edge computing.
