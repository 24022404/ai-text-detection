This paper introduces MOPS-Net, a novel network architecture designed for task-oriented 3D point cloud downsampling. The objective is to develop an efficient and effective method for reducing the complexity of large-scale 3D point clouds while preserving critical information for downstream tasks. MOPS-Net leverages matrix optimization techniques to guide the downsampling process, ensuring that the resulting point cloud retains the most relevant features for specific applications. The network employs a unique combination of convolutional neural networks and matrix optimization algorithms to achieve high-quality downsampling. Experimental results demonstrate that MOPS-Net outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of point cloud reconstruction quality, feature preservation, and computational efficiency. The proposed approach has significant implications for various 3D vision tasks, including object recognition, scene understanding, and 3D reconstruction. Key contributions of this research include the integration of matrix optimization with deep learning techniques and the development of a task-oriented downsampling framework. Relevant keywords: 3D point cloud downsampling, matrix optimization, deep learning, task-oriented processing, point cloud reconstruction.
