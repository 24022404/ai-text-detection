This paper introduces DeepV2D, a novel framework for monocular video depth estimation utilizing differentiable Structure from Motion (SfM). The primary objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of depth prediction from video sequences. DeepV2D employs a deep neural network to jointly optimize depth maps and camera poses, leveraging differentiable SfM to enforce geometric consistency between frames. Our approach incorporates a novel loss function that explicitly penalizes inconsistencies in both depth and pose estimates. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that DeepV2D achieves state-of-the-art performance compared to existing monocular depth estimation methods, particularly in challenging scenarios with significant camera motion. The differentiable SfM component enables end-to-end training, resulting in more accurate and geometrically consistent depth maps. This work contributes a robust and accurate video-based depth estimation method with applications in robotics, autonomous navigation, and 3D scene understanding. Keywords: Depth Estimation, Structure from Motion, Deep Learning, Monocular Video, Differentiable Rendering, Geometric Consistency.
