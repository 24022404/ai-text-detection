Recent advancements in mobile technology have escalated the need for efficient video object detection capabilities directly on mobile devices. This paper addresses the challenge of achieving high-performance video object detection within the constraints of mobile hardware, aiming to optimize both speed and accuracy while minimizing resource consumption.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel framework that leverages lightweight deep learning models tailored for mobile environments. Our approach involves the modification of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and integration of real-time optimization techniques that reduce computational load without compromising detection speed or precision. Techniques such as model pruning, quantization, and the use of efficient neural architecture search (NAS) are employed to fine-tune models specifically for mobile use.

Results/Findings: Extensive experiments demonstrate that our framework significantly enhances the video object detection performance on popular mobile platforms. Our system achieves up to a 30% improvement in computational efficiency compared to existing state-of-the-art methods, maintaining high accuracy even in complex video scenarios. Benchmark tests reveal real-time performance with minimal latency and resource utilization, suitable for various mobile applications.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes to the field of mobile video object detection by presenting a scalable solution that balances the trade-offs between detection accuracy and computational efficiency. The proposed methodologies offer substantial implications for the development of intelligent mobile applications, such as augmented reality, mobile gaming, and real-time video analytics, expanding the potential use cases for advanced video processing on mobile devices.

Keywords: video object detection, mobile devices, CNNs, edge computing, model optimization, real-time processing.
