This paper presents the design and implementation of a large-scale multimodal knowledge base system capable of answering complex visual queries. The objective of this research is to bridge the gap between visual content understanding and knowledge retrieval, enabling users to obtain accurate, context-aware answers from diverse data sources such as images, text, and structured databases. Our approach integrates advanced computer vision techniques with natural language processing to extract, represent, and interlink heterogeneous information. The system employs deep learning models for visual feature extraction, entity recognition, and relationship mapping, combined with a scalable graph-based knowledge representation. Extensive experiments were conducted using benchmark datasets and real-world scenarios, demonstrating the system's effectiveness in retrieving relevant answers to image-based and cross-modal queries. Results show significant improvements in both accuracy and response time compared to unimodal or text-only knowledge base systems. The proposed platform enables new applications in fields such as intelligent search engines, visual question answering, and multimedia data analytics. This work contributes a robust, extensible framework for multimodal information retrieval and highlights the potential of integrating visual and textual data for next-generation knowledge-based systems. Key keywords: multimodal knowledge base, visual query answering, deep learning, information retrieval, knowledge graph, artificial intelligence.
