The objective of this research is to address the challenge of evaluating conditional image generation models, which are pivotal in tasks that require the synthesis of images based on given conditions or inputs. Current practices in image generation evaluation often lack consistency and comprehensiveness, leading to unreliable assessments of model efficacy. We propose a novel set of evaluation metrics specifically tailored to assess conditional image generation models, emphasizing both visual quality and fidelity to input conditions. Our approach integrates quantitative methods, such as Inception Score and Frechet Inception Distance, with new metrics designed to capture the semantic alignment between input conditions and generated outputs. Extensive experiments were conducted on benchmark datasets utilizing state-of-the-art models to validate our proposed metrics. The findings indicate that our approach delivers a more nuanced and robust assessment than existing methodologies, particularly in diverse and complex generation scenarios. This research contributes significantly to the field by offering a standardized and comprehensive framework for the evaluation of conditional image generation, promoting consistency and facilitating advancements in model development and comparison. These contributions have the potential to enhance applications in areas like image editing, content creation, and augmented reality systems. Key keywords include conditional image generation, evaluation metrics, semantic alignment, and image fidelity.
