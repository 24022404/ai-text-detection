The accurate detection of cardiac chambers in fetal echocardiography is critical for diagnosing congenital heart diseases. However, manual analysis is time-consuming and prone to variability. This study proposes an automatic detection framework based on an attention-enhanced YOLOv4 model to identify cardiac chambers from the four-chamber view of fetal echocardiography. The proposed approach integrates a channel attention mechanism into the YOLOv4 architecture to improve feature extraction and localization accuracy, particularly for small and overlapping structures in ultrasound images. A dataset of annotated fetal echocardiography images was used to train and evaluate the model. Experimental results demonstrate that the attention-based YOLOv4 framework achieves superior performance compared to traditional YOLOv4 and other state-of-the-art object detection models, with a mean average precision (mAP) of 92.3% and a detection speed of 45 frames per second. The framework also exhibits robustness to variations in image quality and fetal positioning. This research contributes a novel, efficient, and reliable tool for automating cardiac chamber detection, which can assist clinicians in early diagnosis and reduce diagnostic workload. The proposed method has potential applications in prenatal screening and real-time ultrasound analysis.  

**Keywords**: fetal echocardiography, cardiac chamber detection, YOLOv4, attention mechanism, object detection, prenatal diagnosis.
