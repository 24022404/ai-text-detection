As of June 2021, Vietnam reached nearly 70 million Internet users, accounting for over 70% of the population, marking a 0.8% increase from 2020 to 2021. Additionally, the country recorded about 76 million social network users, representing 73.7% of the population and showing a remarkable rise of almost 10 million users within a year. On average, Vietnamese users spent up to 7 hours each day engaged in various online activities, reflecting the nation’s high digital engagement.
