Temporal Knowledge Graphs (TKGs) represent dynamic relationships between entities over time, posing unique challenges for prediction and inference tasks. Traditional methods often require extensive labeled data, limiting their applicability in scenarios where data is scarce. This paper addresses the problem of one-shot learning for TKGs, aiming to enable accurate predictions with minimal training examples. We propose a novel framework that leverages meta-learning techniques to adapt quickly to new temporal patterns and relationships. Our approach combines graph neural networks with temporal attention mechanisms to capture both structural and temporal dependencies effectively. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms existing baselines in terms of prediction accuracy and robustness, particularly in low-data regimes. The framework also shows significant improvements in handling unseen entities and relationships, highlighting its generalization capabilities. This research contributes to advancing the field of TKGs by providing a scalable and efficient solution for one-shot learning, with potential applications in areas such as recommendation systems, event forecasting, and dynamic network analysis.  

**Keywords**: Temporal Knowledge Graphs, One-shot Learning, Meta-learning, Graph Neural Networks, Temporal Attention, Dynamic Networks.
