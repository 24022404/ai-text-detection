Face forgery detection has become increasingly critical due to the proliferation of manipulated facial content. Current detection methods often struggle with generalization across diverse datasets and attack types. This paper introduces Face X-ray, a novel approach that leverages frequency analysis to expose subtle inconsistencies introduced by forgery manipulations. We propose a frequency-aware decomposition module that isolates high-frequency components, revealing forgery artifacts often imperceptible in the spatial domain. A convolutional neural network is then trained to classify real and fake faces based on these frequency signatures. Experimental results on multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate that Face X-ray achieves state-of-the-art performance in cross-dataset and cross-attack scenarios, surpassing existing methods in generalization ability. Our approach provides a robust and interpretable solution for detecting face forgeries, contributing to improved security and trustworthiness of digital media. Keywords: Face Forgery Detection, Deepfake, Frequency Analysis, Generalization, Convolutional Neural Network.
