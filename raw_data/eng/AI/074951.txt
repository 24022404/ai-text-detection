Accurate pedestrian trajectory prediction is critical for the safe and efficient operation of autonomous vehicles in dynamic urban environments. This paper introduces Attentional-Graph Convolutional Neural Network (Attentional-GCNN), a novel framework designed to address the challenges of adaptive and generic trajectory prediction. The proposed model leverages graph-based representations to capture spatial-temporal interactions among pedestrians and their surroundings, while an attention mechanism dynamically prioritizes relevant contextual information. Unlike existing approaches, Attentional-GCNN adapts to diverse and complex scenarios, making it suitable for a wide range of autonomous vehicle use cases. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the model outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of prediction accuracy and robustness, particularly in crowded and unstructured environments. The results highlight the model's ability to generalize across varying conditions, offering significant improvements in handling real-world uncertainties. This research contributes to advancing autonomous vehicle technologies by providing a scalable and adaptive solution for pedestrian trajectory prediction, with potential applications in urban mobility and intelligent transportation systems.  

**Keywords**: Pedestrian trajectory prediction, graph convolutional networks, attention mechanism, autonomous vehicles, spatial-temporal modeling.
