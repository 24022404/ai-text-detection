This paper explores the potential of evolution strategies as a scalable alternative to traditional reinforcement learning methods. The objective is to address the challenges of applying reinforcement learning to complex, high-dimensional problems, where it often suffers from poor sample efficiency and limited scalability. We propose a novel approach that leverages evolution strategies to optimize policies in a parallel and distributed manner, allowing for significant improvements in computational efficiency. Our method utilizes a population-based search algorithm to evolve optimal policies, eliminating the need for gradient-based updates and enabling more efficient exploration of the solution space. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in achieving comparable or superior performance to state-of-the-art reinforcement learning methods on a range of benchmark tasks, while requiring significantly less computational resources. The key findings of this research highlight the potential of evolution strategies as a viable alternative to reinforcement learning, particularly in scenarios where scalability and efficiency are critical. This work contributes to the development of more efficient and scalable optimization methods, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, game playing, and autonomous systems, and is relevant to keywords such as evolution strategies, reinforcement learning, scalable optimization, and parallel computing.
