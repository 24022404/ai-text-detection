This paper introduces a general framework for hypercomplex-valued Extreme Learning Machines (ELMs), extending the capabilities of traditional ELMs to handle multidimensional data with inherent structural relationships. The proposed framework leverages hypercomplex algebra, specifically quaternions and octonions, to process data as a single entity, preserving correlations and dependencies often lost in real-valued or complex-valued ELMs. We present a unified mathematical formulation adaptable to various hypercomplex number systems. The methodology encompasses a novel initialization strategy for input weights and biases within the hypercomplex domain, designed to enhance network stability and generalization. The performance of the hypercomplex ELM is evaluated on benchmark datasets related to image recognition and signal processing, demonstrating significant improvements in accuracy and training speed compared to conventional ELMs and other hypercomplex neural networks. These results underscore the potential of hypercomplex-valued ELMs for efficiently processing high-dimensional, structured data, opening avenues for applications in fields such as computer vision, robotics, and quantum computing. Keywords: Extreme Learning Machine, Hypercomplex Numbers, Quaternions, Octonions, Neural Networks, Machine Learning, Pattern Recognition.
