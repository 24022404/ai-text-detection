This paper introduces PIE, a novel Portrait Image Embedding framework designed for semantic control of facial images. The objective is to enable intuitive and fine-grained control over portrait images, allowing for precise editing and manipulation. To achieve this, we propose a deep learning-based approach that combines a generative model with a semantic embedding space. Our method learns a compact and meaningful representation of facial features, enabling efficient and effective control over image semantics. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of PIE, showcasing its ability to produce high-quality, photorealistic images with precise control over attributes such as expression, pose, and identity. The key contributions of this work include a novel embedding framework, a robust and efficient control mechanism, and a comprehensive evaluation protocol. PIE has significant implications for applications in image editing, computer vision, and artificial intelligence, particularly in areas such as facial analysis, synthesis, and manipulation. Key keywords: portrait image embedding, semantic control, facial image editing, deep learning, generative models.
