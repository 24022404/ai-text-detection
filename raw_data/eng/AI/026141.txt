This paper introduces Pose2Seg, a novel approach to human instance segmentation that eliminates the need for a human detection stage. Current state-of-the-art methods typically rely on bounding box detectors, which can introduce errors and limit performance. Pose2Seg directly predicts instance masks from human pose estimations. Our method leverages a graph convolutional network (GCN) to encode human pose keypoints and learn relationships between different body parts. This encoded representation is then used to generate high-quality instance segmentation masks. Experimental results on the COCO dataset demonstrate that Pose2Seg achieves competitive segmentation accuracy compared to detection-based methods while significantly reducing computational complexity. Furthermore, Pose2Seg exhibits robustness in crowded scenes and can effectively segment instances even with partially occluded bodies. The proposed approach offers a promising alternative for human instance segmentation, paving the way for more efficient and accurate vision systems. Key words: instance segmentation, pose estimation, graph convolutional network, human pose, deep learning.
