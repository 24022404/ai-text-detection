Arbitrary style transfer aims to render a content image in the style of any given style image. Existing methods often struggle with preserving the depth information of the content image, leading to distorted or unrealistic results, particularly in scenes with significant depth variation. This paper introduces a novel depth-aware arbitrary style transfer approach that leverages instance normalization to effectively incorporate depth information into the style transfer process. Our method utilizes a depth map, extracted from the content image, to modulate the instance normalization statistics within a deep neural network. This modulation allows the network to adaptively control the stylization intensity based on the depth of different regions, thus preserving the content's spatial structure. We further propose a refined architecture that incorporates a multi-scale style representation to capture both local and global style features. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly improves the quality of style transfer, especially in preserving depth and generating visually pleasing results, compared to state-of-the-art methods. The proposed method offers a robust solution for depth-aware style transfer, enhancing the realism and artistic quality of stylized images. Keywords: Style Transfer, Depth Awareness, Instance Normalization, Neural Networks, Image Stylization.
