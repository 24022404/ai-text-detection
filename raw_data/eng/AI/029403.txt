This paper presents a novel approach to optimizing snake locomotion gaits for energy efficiency using deep reinforcement learning. The objective is to develop a gait policy that minimizes energy consumption while maintaining effective locomotion. A deep reinforcement learning framework is employed, utilizing a combination of convolutional neural networks and policy gradient methods to learn an optimal gait policy. The results show that the learned policy achieves significant energy savings compared to traditional gait patterns, with a reduction of up to 30% in energy consumption. The findings have important implications for the development of energy-efficient snake-like robots and other biomimetic systems. The use of deep reinforcement learning enables the discovery of complex, non-intuitive gait patterns that would be difficult to design manually. Key contributions include the application of deep reinforcement learning to snake locomotion and the demonstration of substantial energy savings. Relevant keywords: deep reinforcement learning, snake locomotion, energy efficiency, gait policy, biomimetic robotics.
