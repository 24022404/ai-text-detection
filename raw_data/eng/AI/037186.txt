Synthesizing dynamic talking faces with high fidelity and naturalness remains a challenging task. This paper introduces FACIAL, a novel framework leveraging implicit attribute learning for enhanced talking face generation. FACIAL decouples identity and expression attributes using an implicit neural representation, enabling fine-grained control over facial movements and appearance. Our method employs a multi-stage training strategy, first learning a static face representation and subsequently incorporating dynamic expressions through a learned latent space. This approach allows for synthesizing realistic and temporally coherent video sequences from audio inputs. Experiments demonstrate that FACIAL outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of visual quality, lip synchronization accuracy, and identity preservation, as evaluated by both quantitative metrics and subjective user studies. The proposed framework offers significant improvements in generating realistic and expressive talking faces, with potential applications in virtual avatars, teleconferencing, and personalized content creation. Key words: Talking face generation, implicit neural representation, dynamic synthesis, facial animation, deep learning.
