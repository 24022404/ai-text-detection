The increasing demand for efficient neural network performance in edge computing has driven interest in Processing-In-Memory (PIM) architectures. These systems face challenges with robustness due to inherent noise from memory units. This paper addresses the need for enhanced neural network performance on PIM systems by introducing noise-aware normalization techniques.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel noise-aware normalization method designed specifically for PIM architectures to mitigate noise-induced errors. The technique involves modifying standard normalization layers to factor in noise characteristics inherent to the memory units. Our approach is integrated with existing neural network models to improve tolerance against computational inaccuracies.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed noise-aware normalization significantly enhances the robustness and performance of neural networks on PIM systems. Comparative analysis shows that our method reduces error rates by up to 30% compared to traditional normalization techniques, with a negligible impact on computation overhead.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of noise-aware normalization offers a promising pathway for improving neural network robustness on PIM architectures, ensuring reliable machine learning applications in memory-constrained environments. This contribution holds potential for broader applications in edge devices and IoT systems, providing a foundation for future work in developing noise-resilient neural network models.

Key Keywords: Processing-In-Memory, neural networks, noise-aware normalization, robustness, edge computing, IoT systems.
