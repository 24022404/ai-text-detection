Pedestrian action prediction plays a critical role in the development of safe and intelligent autonomous systems, particularly in urban environments with dense and dynamic human movement. This paper introduces Graph-SIM, a novel graph-based spatiotemporal interaction modelling framework designed to enhance the accuracy and reliability of pedestrian action prediction. The proposed approach represents pedestrians and their interactions as nodes and edges in a dynamic graph structure that captures both spatial proximity and temporal behavior patterns. By leveraging a combination of graph neural networks (GNNs) and temporal sequence models, Graph-SIM effectively models complex interactions among multiple pedestrians over time. Extensive experiments were conducted on benchmark pedestrian datasets, where Graph-SIM demonstrated superior predictive performance compared to existing baseline models, achieving significant improvements in both short-term and long-term action prediction metrics. The results highlight the framework’s capacity to capture subtle inter-agent influences and adapt to varying urban scenarios. In conclusion, Graph-SIM advances the state-of-the-art in pedestrian action prediction by providing a scalable and interpretable model suitable for real-time applications in autonomous vehicles, video surveillance, and intelligent transportation systems.

Keywords: pedestrian action prediction, spatiotemporal modeling, graph neural networks, interaction modeling, autonomous systems, trajectory prediction
