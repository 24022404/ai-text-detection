This paper presents a novel dynamic programming algorithm designed to address the dual challenges of informative measurements and near-optimal path-planning in complex environments. The objective is to optimize the acquisition of critical data while minimizing resource expenditure, such as time or energy, during navigation. The proposed approach integrates a dynamic programming framework with probabilistic models to evaluate and prioritize measurement points based on their informational value. By leveraging recursive optimization techniques, the algorithm efficiently computes paths that balance exploration and exploitation, ensuring both high-quality data collection and near-optimal traversal. Experimental results demonstrate the algorithm’s superior performance compared to traditional methods, achieving significant improvements in measurement accuracy and path efficiency across various simulated and real-world scenarios. The findings highlight the algorithm’s potential for applications in robotics, autonomous systems, and environmental monitoring, where informed decision-making and resource-efficient navigation are critical. This work contributes a scalable and adaptable solution to the growing demand for intelligent systems capable of operating in dynamic and uncertain environments.  

**Keywords**: dynamic programming, informative measurements, path-planning, optimization, autonomous systems, probabilistic models.
