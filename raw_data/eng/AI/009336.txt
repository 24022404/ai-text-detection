Video-based person re-identification (Re-ID) aims to match pedestrian trajectories across disjoint camera views, a challenging task due to variations in pose, illumination, and viewpoint. This paper introduces a novel Concentrated Multi-Grained Multi-Attention Network (CMGMAN) to address these challenges. Our approach leverages a multi-grained representation, dividing each video frame into multiple regions and extracting features at different scales. Furthermore, CMGMAN incorporates a multi-attention mechanism that concentrates on the most discriminative features within each region and across different granularities. This allows the network to focus on salient parts of the body and learn robust representations. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including MARS and DukeMTMC-VideoReID, demonstrate that CMGMAN significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods. The proposed network achieves superior accuracy and robustness by effectively capturing fine-grained details and suppressing irrelevant information. The concentrated attention mechanism improves the discriminative power of the learned features, leading to more accurate person re-identification. Keywords: person re-identification, video analysis, attention mechanism, multi-grained representation, deep learning.
