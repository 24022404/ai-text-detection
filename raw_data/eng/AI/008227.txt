This paper addresses the critical issue of detecting occluded prohibited items in X-ray security inspection images, a challenging task due to the overlap of objects. The objective is to develop an effective method for identifying hidden threats, enhancing airport security and passenger safety. We propose a novel De-occlusion Attention Module, which leverages advanced deep learning techniques to focus on occluded regions and improve detection accuracy. Our approach is evaluated on a newly introduced benchmark dataset, specifically designed for occluded prohibited items detection. The results demonstrate significant improvements over existing state-of-the-art methods, with a notable increase in detection precision and recall. The proposed module can be integrated into existing X-ray security inspection systems, providing a valuable tool for security personnel. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and reliable security screening systems, with potential applications in aviation security, border control, and other high-stakes inspection scenarios. Key keywords: X-ray security inspection, occluded object detection, De-occlusion Attention Module, deep learning, airport security.
