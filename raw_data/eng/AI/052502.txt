This paper addresses the challenge of neural network pruning, a crucial technique for reducing computational costs and improving model efficiency. Our objective is to identify the key factor that enables effective pruning while preserving network performance. We introduce the concept of dynamical isometry, a novel approach that ensures the pruned network maintains a balanced and efficient structure. Our method utilizes a combination of spectral normalization and iterative pruning to achieve dynamical isometry, resulting in significant improvements in pruning ratios and accuracy retention. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing pruning techniques, achieving state-of-the-art performance on various benchmark datasets. The findings of this research have important implications for the development of efficient and scalable neural networks, highlighting the critical role of dynamical isometry in neural network pruning. Key contributions include the introduction of dynamical isometry as a guiding principle for pruning, the development of a spectral normalization-based pruning algorithm, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in preserving network performance. Relevant keywords: neural network pruning, dynamical isometry, spectral normalization, efficient neural networks, deep learning.
