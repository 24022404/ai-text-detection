The objective of this research is to address the challenge of person re-identification in video data by developing a Concentrated Multi-Grained Multi-Attention Network (CMG-MAN). Traditional methods often struggle with occlusions, variations in illumination, and pose changes, which can degrade performance. Our approach leverages a multi-grained attention mechanism that emphasizes salient features in different granularity levels of the video frames, enhancing the model’s ability to differentiate between individuals more accurately. We employ a unique architecture that integrates hierarchical feature extraction with a sophisticated attention module, concentrating on distinctive attributes and temporal dynamics.

Our experiments, conducted on benchmark datasets, demonstrate that CMG-MAN significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art models in terms of both accuracy and efficiency. The network shows remarkable robustness against common video re-identification challenges, achieving superior re-identification rates and faster processing times. These results suggest that our method provides a more reliable and swift solution for practical applications in surveillance and security systems.

In conclusion, the introduction of the CMG-MAN presents a substantial advancement in video-based person re-identification. Our contributions not only enhance the precision of individual identification in complex scenarios but also pave the way for future research in video analysis utilizing multi-attention mechanisms. Key implications of this work include its application in smart city surveillance, automated security checks, and advanced monitoring systems. Keywords: person re-identification, video analysis, multi-attention network, feature extraction, security systems.
