**Abstract**

Accurate and efficient point cloud registration is fundamental in various 3D computer vision and robotics applications, such as object recognition, pose estimation, and autonomous navigation. This paper presents PointNetLK, a novel framework that leverages the deep neural network architecture of PointNet in conjunction with a differentiable, iterative registration algorithm inspired by the classic Lucas-Kanade (LK) method. Unlike traditional geometric or iterative closest point (ICP) approaches, PointNetLK utilizes the global feature representation capability of PointNet to robustly encode point clouds, regardless of their density, permutation, or presence of outliers. The registration process incorporates a differentiable alignment module that iteratively estimates the optimal rigid transformation between source and target point clouds. Experimental results demonstrate that PointNetLK achieves superior accuracy and convergence speed compared to conventional registration techniques, especially in the presence of noise and partial occlusion. Furthermore, the proposed method is highly scalable and suitable for real-time applications due to its efficient end-to-end learning paradigm. PointNetLK advances the state-of-the-art in point cloud registration by combining deep learning robustness with the interpretability and geometric guarantees of classic alignment algorithms. Keywords: point cloud registration, PointNet, deep learning, 3D alignment, Lucas-Kanade algorithm, robust registration, computer vision.
