This paper introduces *MailLeak*, a novel approach for obfuscation-robust character extraction in document images, leveraging transfer learning to address the challenges posed by obscured or distorted text. The objective of this research is to enhance the accuracy and reliability of text extraction systems, particularly in scenarios where traditional optical character recognition (OCR) methods fail due to intentional obfuscation techniques, such as noise, overlays, or stylized fonts. To achieve this, we propose a deep learning-based framework that utilizes pre-trained convolutional neural networks (CNNs) fine-tuned on a diverse dataset of obfuscated text samples. Our method incorporates data augmentation and domain adaptation techniques to improve generalization across varying obfuscation patterns. Experimental results demonstrate that *MailLeak* outperforms existing OCR systems, achieving a 15% improvement in character recognition accuracy on heavily obfuscated datasets. Furthermore, the system exhibits robust performance across multiple languages and font styles, showcasing its versatility. The implications of this research extend to applications in digital forensics, document analysis, and secure communication systems, where accurate text extraction is critical. This work contributes to the field by providing a scalable, transfer learning-based solution that addresses the limitations of conventional OCR methods in handling obfuscated text.  

**Keywords**: Character extraction, transfer learning, obfuscation robustness, optical character recognition (OCR), deep learning, document analysis.
