In recent years, the rise of machine learning and deep neural networks has heightened the focus on adversarial attacks, which pose significant threats to the security and reliability of AI systems. This paper aims to provide a comprehensive survey on universal adversarial attacks, examining their methodologies, effectiveness, and implications across various applications. We categorize existing attack strategies, highlighting techniques that generate perturbations applicable to multiple inputs, thus ensuring broad efficacy. Our analysis encompasses a wide range of experimental results showcasing the performance of these attacks in comparison to traditional localized methods. The findings reveal that universal adversarial attacks maintain a high success rate while requiring minimal computational resources, demonstrating their potential to exploit real-world vulnerabilities in AI models. We conclude by discussing the broader implications for cybersecurity and machine learning robustness, urging the need for enhanced defense mechanisms to mitigate these threats. This survey not only clarifies the current landscape of universal adversarial attacks but also sets the stage for future research in developing resilient AI systems. 

Keywords: adversarial attacks, universal attacks, machine learning security, deep learning, cybersecurity.
