Autonomous driving requires robust and efficient semantic understanding of the environment. LiDAR point clouds provide rich geometric information, but their processing remains computationally intensive. This paper introduces SalsaNext, a novel deep learning architecture for fast and uncertainty-aware semantic segmentation of LiDAR point clouds. SalsaNext builds upon the SalsaNet architecture, incorporating improvements to reduce computational cost while maintaining high accuracy. Specifically, we introduce a novel residual dilated block and optimized upsampling techniques. Furthermore, we propose a method for estimating pixel-wise uncertainty, providing valuable information for downstream decision-making processes. Experimental results on the SemanticKITTI dataset demonstrate that SalsaNext achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of both speed and accuracy, outperforming existing methods by up to 20% in inference time while maintaining comparable or improved segmentation accuracy. The estimated uncertainty is shown to be well-calibrated and indicative of segmentation errors. Our findings suggest that SalsaNext is a promising solution for real-time semantic perception in autonomous driving systems, enabling more reliable and safe navigation. Keywords: LiDAR, Semantic Segmentation, Autonomous Driving, Deep Learning, Uncertainty Estimation.
