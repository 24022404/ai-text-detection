Retrosynthesis, the process of planning a sequence of reactions to synthesize a target molecule from readily available starting materials, is a challenging problem in organic chemistry. This paper introduces RetCL, a novel retrosynthesis approach leveraging contrastive learning to improve reaction selection accuracy. RetCL trains a model to differentiate between correct and incorrect reaction pathways by contrasting known successful routes with randomly sampled alternatives. This contrastive learning framework enables the model to learn more robust and discriminative representations of chemical reactions and molecular contexts. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that RetCL significantly outperforms existing template-based and neural network-based retrosynthesis methods in terms of top-k accuracy and route efficiency. Specifically, RetCL achieves state-of-the-art performance on reaction prediction and enhances the quality of generated retrosynthetic routes. The proposed approach offers a promising direction for automated retrosynthesis planning and has the potential to accelerate the discovery of novel synthetic routes. Keywords: Retrosynthesis, Contrastive Learning, Reaction Prediction, Route Planning, Chemical Synthesis, Artificial Intelligence.
