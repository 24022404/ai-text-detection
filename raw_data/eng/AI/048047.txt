This paper investigates the Greedy-GQ algorithm, a reinforcement learning method for off-policy policy evaluation, focusing on improving its finite-time performance. While Greedy-GQ is known for its asymptotic convergence, its practical applicability is often hindered by high variance. We introduce a variance reduction technique within the Greedy-GQ framework, resulting in a novel algorithm termed Variance-Reduced Greedy-GQ (VR-Greedy-GQ). We provide a rigorous finite-time analysis of VR-Greedy-GQ, demonstrating significant improvements in sample complexity compared to the standard Greedy-GQ. Specifically, our theoretical results show a refined dependence on key problem parameters. Empirical evaluations on benchmark reinforcement learning tasks validate our theoretical findings, illustrating that VR-Greedy-GQ achieves faster convergence and improved stability. This work contributes a provably efficient and practically effective approach for off-policy policy evaluation, addressing a critical challenge in reinforcement learning. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Off-Policy Evaluation, Greedy-GQ, Variance Reduction, Finite-Time Analysis, Sample Complexity.
