This paper addresses the challenge of constructing reliable confidence sets for deep neural networks, a crucial aspect of ensuring the trustworthiness and robustness of AI models. Our objective is to develop a framework that provides Probably Approximately Correct (PAC) confidence sets, which guarantee a certain level of accuracy with high probability. To achieve this, we propose a novel approach based on calibrated prediction, where we leverage techniques from conformal prediction to generate sets that contain the true output with a specified probability. Our method utilizes a calibration procedure to adjust the predictions of a deep neural network, resulting in a well-calibrated model that produces accurate confidence sets. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, showing that it outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The key contributions of this research include the development of a PAC confidence set framework for deep neural networks, the introduction of a calibration procedure for conformal prediction, and the demonstration of the approach's efficacy in real-world applications. Our work has significant implications for the development of reliable and trustworthy AI systems, particularly in high-stakes domains such as healthcare and finance, where confidence sets can be used to quantify uncertainty and improve decision-making. Key keywords: deep neural networks, PAC confidence sets, calibrated prediction, conformal prediction, uncertainty quantification, AI reliability.
