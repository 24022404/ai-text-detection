**Abstract:**

Visible watermarks are widely used to protect digital images, yet their presence can hinder downstream processing and content reuse. This paper introduces WDNet, a novel Watermark-Decomposition Network designed specifically for the removal of visible watermarks from digital images. The primary objective is to achieve effective watermark removal while preserving image quality and underlying content integrity. WDNet employs a dual-branch deep neural network architecture, which explicitly separates the learning tasks of watermark extraction and background reconstruction. Leveraging a combination of convolutional layers and attention mechanisms, the model distinguishes and isolates watermark patterns from original image features. Our approach is evaluated on a large-scale dataset containing diverse watermarked images, demonstrating superior performance compared to existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM). Qualitative results further showcase WDNet’s ability to handle complex watermark patterns and varying transparency levels, with minimal visual artifacts. The results indicate that WDNet effectively generalizes across different watermark types and host images. The proposed network can benefit applications in digital image restoration, content repurposing, and copyright management. Keywords: visible watermark removal, deep learning, image restoration, WDNet, convolutional neural network, watermark decomposition.
