Nanopore sequencing has emerged as a transformative technology for DNA sequencing, offering real-time, long-read capabilities. However, achieving high base-calling accuracy remains a critical challenge, particularly for complex genomic regions. This paper introduces a novel approach, **Dynamic Pooling**, designed to enhance the accuracy of nanopore base calling by adaptively optimizing signal processing during sequencing. Unlike traditional static pooling methods, Dynamic Pooling leverages real-time feedback mechanisms to adjust pooling parameters dynamically, ensuring robust performance across diverse sequencing conditions. We evaluate the proposed method using a comprehensive dataset, comparing it against state-of-the-art base-calling algorithms. Experimental results demonstrate that Dynamic Pooling achieves a significant improvement in base-calling accuracy, particularly in regions with high error rates, while maintaining computational efficiency. The method also exhibits enhanced adaptability to varying sequencing environments, making it a promising solution for improving the reliability of nanopore sequencing. This research contributes to advancing genomic analysis by addressing a key limitation in nanopore technology, with potential applications in precision medicine, metagenomics, and beyond.  

**Keywords**: Nanopore sequencing, base calling, Dynamic Pooling, signal processing, genomic analysis, real-time sequencing.
