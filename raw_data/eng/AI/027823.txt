The objective of this research is to address the longstanding challenge of credit assignment in multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL), where determining the contribution of each agent to a team's success is crucial for effective learning. We propose a novel approach using Meta-Policy Gradient (MPG) methods that enhances the allocation of credit among agents in a cooperative setting. Our approach leverages meta-learning techniques to adaptively tune policy gradients, thus refining the reward distributions and optimizing individual agent strategies. Through comprehensive simulations and experiments in standard multi-agent environments, our method demonstrates significant improvements in convergence speed and overall team performance compared to existing MARL frameworks. The findings reveal that the integration of MPG leads to a more efficient cooperation among agents, which can be particularly beneficial in complex environments with sparse feedback. This research contributes to the advancement of MARL by providing a robust framework for credit assignment, potentially impacting various applications such as autonomous vehicles, distributed robotics, and collaborative AI systems. Key keywords include Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning, Credit Assignment, Meta-Policy Gradient, Cooperative Systems, and Adaptive Learning.
