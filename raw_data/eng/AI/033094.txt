This paper introduces a dynamic programming algorithm designed for efficient selection of informative measurements and near-optimal path-planning in environments where decision-making under uncertainty and resource constraints is critical. The main objective is to maximize the utility of collected data while minimizing traversal costs, a challenge prevalent in applications such as robotics, environmental monitoring, and mobile sensor networks. The proposed approach models the measurement and path-planning process as a sequential decision problem, employing dynamic programming to systematically balance the trade-off between information gain and operational efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate that our algorithm consistently outperforms baseline greedy and heuristic methods both in terms of the informativeness of measurements and overall path efficiency. Furthermore, the approach adapts effectively to dynamic changes in the environment and allows incorporation of various utility functions, making it highly versatile. The findings indicate that the method offers scalable performance as problem complexity increases, enabling practical deployment in real-world scenarios. This research advances the state-of-the-art by integrating informative measurement selection with near-optimal path-planning, providing a robust solution for autonomous systems operating in uncertain and dynamically changing environments. 

Keywords: dynamic programming, path-planning, informative measurements, decision-making, robotics, sensor networks, optimization, data collection.
