This paper presents an unbiased deep reinforcement learning framework designed to provide a general training approach for existing and future algorithms. The primary objective is to address the bias inherent in traditional reinforcement learning methods, which can lead to suboptimal policies. Our approach utilizes a novel combination of techniques, including off-policy corrections and adaptive exploration strategies, to ensure unbiased estimation of the expected return. We evaluate our framework using a range of state-of-the-art algorithms, demonstrating significant improvements in performance and stability. The results show that our unbiased framework can effectively mitigate bias and variance, leading to more reliable and efficient learning. This research contributes to the development of more robust and generalizable reinforcement learning methods, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, game playing, and autonomous systems. Key aspects of this work include deep reinforcement learning, off-policy learning, exploration-exploitation trade-offs, and unbiased estimation, making it a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners in the field of artificial intelligence and machine learning.
