Incremental learning, a critical capability for evolving artificial intelligence systems, faces challenges in balancing the retention of previously learned knowledge with the integration of new information. This paper introduces *DeeSIL* (Deep-Shallow Incremental Learning), a novel framework designed to address these challenges by combining deep and shallow learning paradigms. The objective of DeeSIL is to enhance the adaptability of machine learning models in dynamic environments while minimizing catastrophic forgetting. The proposed approach leverages deep neural networks for feature extraction and shallow classifiers for efficient incremental updates, enabling robust performance across diverse tasks. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that DeeSIL achieves superior accuracy and stability compared to existing incremental learning methods, particularly in scenarios with limited computational resources. The framework also exhibits scalability, making it suitable for real-world applications such as autonomous systems and personalized recommendation engines. By integrating deep and shallow learning, DeeSIL offers a significant contribution to the field of incremental learning, providing a practical solution for deploying adaptive AI systems in resource-constrained environments.  

**Keywords**: incremental learning, deep learning, shallow learning, catastrophic forgetting, adaptive systems, DeeSIL.
