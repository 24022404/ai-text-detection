The SemanticKITTI dataset addresses the challenge of semantic scene understanding using LiDAR point clouds in dynamic outdoor environments. This paper introduces a novel large-scale dataset comprising over 43,000 scans from the KITTI Vision Benchmark, annotated with semantic labels for each point. We present a methodology for projecting sparse 3D LiDAR data onto 360° spherical coordinates, enabling efficient processing and annotation. The dataset includes 22 semantic classes representing common urban objects, facilitating training and evaluation of semantic segmentation algorithms. Experiments demonstrate the performance of several baseline methods on the dataset, highlighting the challenges of long-range dependencies and class imbalance. SemanticKITTI provides a valuable resource for advancing research in autonomous driving, robotics, and 3D scene understanding, enabling the development of robust and accurate semantic perception systems. Key words: LiDAR, semantic segmentation, autonomous driving, point clouds, dataset, 3D scene understanding, SemanticKITTI.
