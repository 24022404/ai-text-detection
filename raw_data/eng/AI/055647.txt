This paper addresses the challenge of efficient and robust dictionary learning for sparse signal representation, a critical task in signal processing and machine learning. We propose a novel algorithm, Equiprobable Matching Pursuit (EMP), which enhances the traditional dictionary learning process by incorporating a probabilistic approach to atom selection. Unlike conventional methods that rely on greedy strategies, EMP ensures equiprobable selection of dictionary atoms during the sparse coding phase, promoting a more balanced utilization of the dictionary and improving reconstruction accuracy. The proposed method is evaluated on both synthetic and real-world datasets, demonstrating superior performance in terms of convergence speed, sparsity, and reconstruction error compared to state-of-the-art techniques such as K-SVD and Orthogonal Matching Pursuit (OMP). Experimental results reveal that EMP achieves up to a 15% reduction in reconstruction error while maintaining comparable computational efficiency. The implications of this work extend to applications in image compression, denoising, and feature extraction, where robust and efficient sparse representation is essential. By introducing a probabilistic framework into dictionary learning, this research contributes a significant advancement in the field, offering a scalable and effective solution for sparse signal processing tasks.  

**Keywords**: Dictionary learning, sparse representation, Equiprobable Matching Pursuit, signal processing, machine learning, sparse coding.
