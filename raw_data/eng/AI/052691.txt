This paper addresses the challenge of generating high-quality object proposals in computer vision, a crucial step for object detection and recognition tasks. Our objective is to develop an efficient and effective method for sequential optimization of object proposal generation, aiming to reduce computational costs while maintaining accuracy. We propose a novel approach that leverages a sequential optimization framework, incorporating a combination of deep learning models and traditional computer vision techniques. Our method utilizes a cascade of refinement stages, each stage improving the quality of object proposals through iterative pruning and ranking. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of proposal quality and computational efficiency. Key findings include significant reductions in computational time and improvements in average recall and precision. The contributions of this research lie in its ability to efficiently generate high-quality object proposals, making it suitable for real-time object detection and recognition applications. Our work has important implications for computer vision tasks, including object detection, segmentation, and tracking, and can be applied to various domains such as autonomous driving, surveillance, and robotics. Relevant keywords: object proposal generation, sequential optimization, deep learning, computer vision, object detection.
