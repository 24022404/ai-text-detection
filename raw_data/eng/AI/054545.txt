This paper presents an innovative approach to incremental sequence learning, addressing the challenge of continuously updating models with new data sequences without requiring complete retraining. Our objective is to develop a flexible and efficient method that adapts to evolving sequence patterns, enabling real-time learning and improved predictive performance. We propose a novel algorithm that leverages a combination of online learning and meta-learning techniques to incrementally update the model, allowing it to capture new sequence relationships and forget outdated information. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming traditional batch learning methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. Our findings have significant implications for applications involving sequential data, such as natural language processing, time-series forecasting, and recommender systems. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a new incremental learning framework, the development of a meta-learning based approach for sequence learning, and the demonstration of its potential in real-world scenarios, highlighting the importance of incremental sequence learning in the context of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science.
