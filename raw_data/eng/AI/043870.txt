This paper addresses the challenge of high-dimensional multivariate forecasting by introducing a novel approach based on low-rank Gaussian Copula Processes. The objective is to develop an efficient and accurate method for predicting multiple related time series, which is crucial in various applications such as finance, climate modeling, and traffic flow prediction. Our approach combines the flexibility of Gaussian Copula Processes with the computational efficiency of low-rank approximations, enabling the modeling of complex dependencies between variables. We propose a probabilistic framework that captures non-Gaussian relationships and tail dependencies, while reducing the computational complexity associated with high-dimensional data. The results show that our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art approaches in terms of forecasting accuracy and computational efficiency. The key contributions of this research include the development of a scalable and interpretable model for high-dimensional multivariate forecasting, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in real-world applications. Our approach has significant implications for decision-making in fields where accurate predictions of multiple related variables are critical. Key keywords: Gaussian Copula Processes, low-rank approximation, high-dimensional multivariate forecasting, probabilistic modeling, time series prediction.
