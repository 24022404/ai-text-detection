This paper introduces Adaptive Symmetric Reward Noising (ASRN), a novel method designed to enhance the efficiency and robustness of reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms. The objective is to improve the exploration-exploitation trade-off by injecting adaptive noise into the reward signals, thereby encouraging agents to explore their environment more effectively. Our approach utilizes a symmetric noising strategy, which adaptively adjusts the noise level based on the agent's performance, ensuring that the exploration process is both efficient and targeted. Experimental results demonstrate that ASRN significantly outperforms existing methods in various RL benchmarks, achieving improved convergence rates and enhanced overall performance. The key findings highlight the importance of adaptive reward noising in RL, particularly in complex and dynamic environments. This research contributes to the development of more robust and efficient RL algorithms, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, game playing, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Adaptive Noise, Exploration-Exploitation Trade-off, Symmetric Noising, RL Algorithms.
