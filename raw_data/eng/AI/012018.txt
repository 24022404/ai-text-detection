Recent advancements in data science and quantum communication are exemplified by two notable research papers: Tran Ba Trung et al. proposed verification-free techniques to efficiently identify locally densest subgraphs, presented at the 39th IEEE International Conference on Data Engineering, while P. Zhang et al. introduced new timing and synchronization methods for high-loss free-space quantum communication using hybrid de Bruijn codes, documented in IET Quantum Communication, vol. 2, no. 3, pp. 80–89, 2021.
