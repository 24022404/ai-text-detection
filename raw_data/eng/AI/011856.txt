Earth Mover's Distance (EMD) is a powerful metric for comparing distributions, widely used in computer vision, machine learning, and data analysis. However, its high computational cost limits its applicability to large datasets. This paper introduces a novel, low-complexity approximation algorithm for EMD calculation designed for data-parallel architectures. Our approach leverages a hierarchical clustering strategy combined with efficient parallel processing techniques to significantly reduce computational complexity without sacrificing accuracy. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves a substantial speedup compared to existing EMD approximation algorithms, while maintaining competitive accuracy. Furthermore, the data-parallel nature of our algorithm makes it highly suitable for implementation on modern GPUs and multi-core CPUs, enabling efficient processing of large-scale datasets. This work provides a practical and scalable solution for EMD computation, facilitating its broader adoption in various applications. Keywords: Earth Mover's Distance, approximation algorithms, data-parallelism, hierarchical clustering, computational complexity, large-scale data.
