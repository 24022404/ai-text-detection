Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) have emerged as a powerful technique for synthesizing photorealistic 3D scenes from 2D images. However, their computational complexity and slow rendering speeds limit their applicability in real-time scenarios. This paper introduces PlenOctrees, a novel approach that accelerates NeRF rendering by leveraging an octree-based data structure to store and query radiance fields efficiently. Our method precomputes and encodes the volumetric scene representation into a hierarchical octree, enabling real-time rendering with minimal quality degradation. We propose an optimization framework to train the PlenOctree directly from multi-view images, ensuring high fidelity while maintaining computational efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach achieves rendering speeds up to 1000× faster than traditional NeRF methods, with comparable visual quality. Additionally, PlenOctrees support dynamic scene editing and interactive applications, making them suitable for real-time graphics, virtual reality, and augmented reality. This work bridges the gap between high-quality neural rendering and practical real-time performance, offering a significant advancement in the field of 3D scene reconstruction and visualization.  

**Keywords**: Neural Radiance Fields, NeRF, PlenOctrees, real-time rendering, 3D reconstruction, octree, volumetric rendering.
