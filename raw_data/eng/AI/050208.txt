This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing the predictive performance of online decision trees, which are widely utilized in real-time data stream processing. Traditional online decision tree algorithms often struggle with adapting quickly to varying data distributions, potentially leading to suboptimal performance.

Methods: We introduce a novel framework called Online Local Boosting, designed to improve the accuracy and adaptability of online decision trees. Our approach involves integrating a boosting strategy directly within the online framework. Specifically, we develop a model that iteratively reweights misclassified instances and updates the decision tree structure incrementally, allowing for dynamic adjustments as new data arrives.

Results: Experiments demonstrate that our Online Local Boosting method significantly outperforms standard online decision trees and existing boosting techniques when applied to various real-time data streams. The proposed model exhibits enhanced accuracy and robustness, maintaining high performance even in the presence of concept drift and noisy data. Comparative analysis shows notable improvements in classification precision, particularly in scenarios with fluctuating data distributions.

Conclusion: The Online Local Boosting framework offers substantial contributions to the field of online machine learning by seamlessly incorporating boosting into an adaptable decision tree structure. This innovative technique not only improves decision-making processes in dynamic environments but also presents a scalable solution applicable to a wide range of real-time applications, such as financial forecasting and adaptive traffic management systems. Key keywords include online decision trees, boosting strategy, data streams, concept drift, and adaptive learning.
