This paper presents Morph, a flexible acceleration framework designed to enhance the efficiency of three-dimensional convolutional neural network (3D CNN)-based video understanding. The increasing complexity and computational demands of 3D CNNs, widely used for tasks such as action recognition and video classification, have become a critical challenge, especially for real-time and resource-constrained deployment. Morph addresses this challenge by introducing a reconfigurable architecture that can dynamically adapt to diverse 3D CNN models and varying workloads without sacrificing accuracy. The proposed framework integrates scalable dataflow optimizations and adaptive memory management strategies to maximize throughput and energy efficiency. Experimental evaluations on popular video understanding benchmarks demonstrate that Morph achieves up to 2.7× improvement in processing speed and up to 45% reduction in energy consumption, compared to conventional fixed-function accelerators. Moreover, Morph provides seamless support for a range of 3D CNN architectures, enabling broader applicability in both cloud-based and edge computing environments. The results highlight Morph’s potential to accelerate next-generation video analytics and intelligent surveillance systems while maintaining model flexibility and application versatility.  
Keywords: 3D CNN, video understanding, hardware acceleration, reconfigurable architecture, energy efficiency, real-time processing.
