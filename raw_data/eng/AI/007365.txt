Abstract:

The rapid evolution of data streams in real-world applications necessitates adaptive and efficient approaches for similarity learning. This paper introduces Online Progressive Deep Metric Learning, a novel framework designed to learn discriminative distance metrics in an online fashion, continuously updating models as new data arrives. Our approach leverages deep neural networks within a progressive learning paradigm, enabling dynamic adaptation to evolving data distributions without catastrophic forgetting. Unlike traditional batch metric learning methods, our system processes data incrementally, making it suitable for large-scale and streaming scenarios. We propose a dual-loss function that balances hard-sample mining and temporal consistency, thereby enhancing representation robustness over time. Experimental results on multiple benchmark datasets, including image retrieval and face verification tasks, demonstrate significant improvements in accuracy, convergence rate, and computational efficiency compared to existing online and offline metric learning solutions. The findings underscore the value of integrating progressive learning dynamics with deep metric learning for applications such as real-time recommendation systems, anomaly detection, and online authentication. Our work provides a scalable and adaptive solution, paving the way for further advancements in continuous learning and deep metric-based analysis. Keywords: deep metric learning, online learning, progressive learning, adaptive models, representation learning, streaming data.
