This paper proposes DUP-Net, a novel Denoiser and Upsampler Network designed to defend against 3D adversarial point clouds. The objective is to develop an effective defense mechanism that can remove noise and recover the original point cloud data from adversarial attacks. Our approach combines a denoising module and an upsampling module to restore the clean and dense point cloud. The denoising module utilizes a deep neural network to remove noise, while the upsampling module employs a point-wise upsampling strategy to recover the missing points. Experimental results demonstrate that DUP-Net outperforms existing defense methods, achieving a significant improvement in point cloud reconstruction quality and robustness against various adversarial attacks. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a dual-module architecture for point cloud denoising and upsampling, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in defending against 3D adversarial point clouds. This work has important implications for the development of robust 3D point cloud processing systems, with potential applications in fields such as computer vision, robotics, and autonomous driving. Key keywords: 3D point clouds, adversarial defense, denoising, upsampling, deep learning, computer vision.
