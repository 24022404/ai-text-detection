This paper presents Luna: Linear Unified Nested Attention, a novel attention mechanism aimed at enhancing efficiencies and performance in data processing models. Traditional attention mechanisms, while powerful, often suffer from quadratic time complexity as input size scales, posing challenges for applications needing large sequence processing.

Methods/Approach: Luna employs a linear complexity model through the integration of unified nested attention operations. By redefining the interaction pathways within the attention module, it achieves significant computational reductions. The approach leverages linear projections and nested structures to maintain information integrity while optimizing the processing overhead.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate that Luna significantly reduces computational resources compared to conventional attention mechanisms, achieving comparable or superior accuracy on benchmark datasets across various domains such as natural language processing and computer vision. Notably, Luna maintains robustness in long-sequence tasks, indicating its scalability and utility in practical applications.

Conclusion/Implications: Luna introduces a paradigm shift in attention-based models by offering a scalable solution without compromising on performance. Its linear complexity opens new avenues for deploying attention mechanisms in resource-constrained environments, making it a strong candidate for real-time applications. This advance supports broader adoption of attention models in a wide range of IT applications, enhancing their accessibility and efficiency. Key Keywords: Linear Attention, Nested Structures, Computational Efficiency, Scalable Attention Models, Data Processing Models.
