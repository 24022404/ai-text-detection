This paper presents a novel approach to deep reinforcement learning by merging deterministic policy gradient estimations with varied bias-variance tradeoff. The objective is to improve the stability and efficiency of policy optimization in complex environments. Our method combines multiple estimators with different bias-variance characteristics, allowing for a more robust and adaptive learning process. We employ a weighted averaging scheme to integrate the estimations, where the weights are dynamically adjusted based on the estimated variance of each estimator. The results show that our approach outperforms existing methods in several benchmark tasks, demonstrating improved convergence rates and enhanced overall performance. The key findings highlight the importance of balancing bias and variance in policy gradient estimation, and our approach provides a flexible framework for achieving this balance. This research contributes to the development of more effective deep reinforcement learning algorithms, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, game playing, and autonomous systems, and is relevant to keywords such as deep reinforcement learning, policy gradient methods, bias-variance tradeoff, and adaptive learning.
