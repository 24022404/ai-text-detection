Accurate time series forecasting is crucial for informed decision-making across various domains. This paper presents a comprehensive theoretical framework and novel algorithmic approaches for enhancing the accuracy and efficiency of time series prediction. We introduce a novel spectral-temporal decomposition method that separates time series into distinct frequency components, enabling targeted modeling. Furthermore, we propose an adaptive learning algorithm based on recurrent neural networks (RNNs) with attention mechanisms that dynamically adjusts model parameters based on the characteristics of the input time series. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our proposed algorithms outperform state-of-the-art methods in terms of prediction accuracy, particularly for complex and non-stationary time series. The theoretical analysis provides insights into the convergence properties and limitations of the algorithms. This research contributes to the advancement of time series forecasting techniques, with potential applications in finance, climate modeling, and supply chain management.

**Keywords:** Time series forecasting, spectral analysis, recurrent neural networks, attention mechanisms, adaptive learning, prediction accuracy.
