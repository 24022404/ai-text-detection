Tensor factorization techniques, particularly higher-order principal component analysis (HOPCA), are increasingly used for dimensionality reduction and feature extraction in diverse domains. However, ill-posed problems and overfitting can hinder their performance, especially with high-dimensional data. This paper investigates the use of regularization techniques to improve the stability and generalization capability of tensor factorizations and HOPCA. We propose and analyze several regularization strategies, including L1, L2, and total variation penalties, tailored for tensor decompositions. These regularizers are incorporated into standard HOPCA algorithms and evaluated on both synthetic and real-world datasets. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed regularized tensor factorization methods significantly enhance performance in terms of reconstruction accuracy, noise robustness, and feature interpretability compared to unregularized approaches. Specifically, L1 regularization promotes sparsity in the factor matrices, leading to more interpretable components, while L2 regularization improves stability and prevents overfitting. The findings suggest that regularized tensor factorizations offer a powerful tool for robust dimensionality reduction and feature extraction in high-dimensional data analysis. Keywords: Tensor Factorization, Higher-Order PCA, Regularization, Dimensionality Reduction, Machine Learning.
