This paper introduces an end-to-end trainable deep learning framework for video super-resolution with explicit motion compensation. Addressing the limitations of existing methods which often rely on hand-engineered motion estimation, our approach integrates motion estimation, compensation, and high-resolution frame reconstruction into a single network. The network leverages a novel recurrent architecture to effectively capture temporal dependencies and adaptively align adjacent frames using learned motion vectors. This enables robust handling of complex motion and occlusions. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of both quantitative metrics (PSNR and SSIM) and perceptual quality. Furthermore, ablation studies validate the effectiveness of the learned motion compensation module. The proposed framework offers a more efficient and accurate solution for video super-resolution, paving the way for improved performance in video processing applications.

Keywords: Video Super-Resolution, Motion Compensation, Deep Learning, Recurrent Neural Networks, End-to-End Learning.
