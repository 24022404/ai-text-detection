This paper introduces FACIAL, a novel framework for synthesizing dynamic talking faces with implicit attribute learning. The objective of this research is to address the challenge of generating realistic and expressive facial animations that accurately synchronize with speech while capturing subtle attributes such as emotions, head movements, and personalized facial features. To achieve this, we propose a deep learning-based approach that integrates implicit attribute learning into a generative model, enabling the synthesis of high-quality, context-aware talking faces. Our method leverages a combination of audio-visual data and latent space representations to model both explicit speech-driven facial dynamics and implicit attributes, ensuring a more natural and personalized output. Experimental results demonstrate that FACIAL outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of visual quality, synchronization accuracy, and attribute fidelity. The framework also exhibits robustness across diverse datasets and real-world scenarios. The key contribution of this work lies in its ability to implicitly learn and control nuanced facial attributes without requiring explicit annotations, making it highly adaptable for applications in virtual avatars, video conferencing, and entertainment. Keywords: talking face synthesis, implicit attribute learning, facial animation, deep learning, audio-visual synchronization.
