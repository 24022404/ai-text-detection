Accurate network-wide traffic speed prediction is critical for intelligent transportation systems, yet it remains challenging due to the complex spatiotemporal dependencies in traffic data. This paper proposes a novel deep learning framework that integrates bidirectional and unidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) recurrent neural networks to address this problem. The proposed model leverages bidirectional LSTM to capture past and future traffic patterns, while unidirectional LSTM focuses on sequential dependencies. By combining these architectures, the model effectively captures both temporal and spatial correlations across the network. The framework is evaluated on a large-scale real-world traffic dataset, demonstrating superior performance compared to state-of-the-art methods in terms of prediction accuracy and computational efficiency. Experimental results show a significant reduction in mean absolute error (MAE) and root mean square error (RMSE), highlighting the model's ability to handle dynamic traffic conditions. The proposed approach offers a robust solution for traffic management systems, enabling more accurate and reliable predictions for urban planning and congestion mitigation. This research contributes to advancing deep learning applications in transportation by introducing a hybrid LSTM architecture tailored for network-wide traffic speed prediction.  

**Keywords**: Traffic speed prediction, LSTM, bidirectional LSTM, spatiotemporal dependencies, deep learning, intelligent transportation systems.
