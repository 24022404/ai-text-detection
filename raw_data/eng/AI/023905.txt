Heterogeneous multi-task learning (MTL) leverages the relationships between different tasks to improve overall performance, but manually designing network architectures for each task and their interactions is challenging. This paper introduces a novel approach for the automatic construction of multi-network models tailored for heterogeneous MTL. Our method employs a search strategy guided by task relatedness, automatically generating network topologies that efficiently share and specialize parameters across tasks. We introduce a multi-objective optimization framework that balances model complexity and predictive accuracy. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our automatically constructed models achieve comparable or superior performance compared to manually designed state-of-the-art MTL architectures, while significantly reducing the human effort required for model design. The proposed framework offers a practical solution for deploying MTL in scenarios with diverse and interrelated tasks.

**Keywords:** Multi-task learning, neural architecture search, heterogeneous learning, automated machine learning, deep learning.
