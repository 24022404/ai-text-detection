Object recognition is a fundamental challenge in computer vision, and effective representation of objects' contours is crucial for accurate identification. This paper addresses the problem by introducing a novel contour sparse representation technique utilizing Shape Distribution Descriptor (SDD) features, aimed at enhancing object recognition performance.

Methods: Our approach leverages the power of sparse representation, focusing on the contours of objects. We integrate Shape Distribution Descriptors to capture essential geometric properties, ensuring that object features are efficiently encoded. By applying advanced optimization algorithms, our method constructs a sparse representation that enhances the discriminatory power of the feature space.

Results: Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed contour sparse representation significantly improves object recognition accuracy compared with existing contour-based methods. The approach shows robustness across diverse datasets, with a notable increase in recognition efficiency and speed. Our findings indicate a reduction in computational complexity while maintaining high performance, attributable to the concise representation of object contours via SDD features.

Conclusion: The research offers a substantial contribution to the field of object recognition by providing a framework that not only boosts recognition accuracy but also optimizes computational resources. The use of SDD features in sparse representations provides a unique advantage over traditional methods, particularly in applications requiring real-time processing or dealing with large-scale data. Our study opens avenues for further exploration into contour-based recognition systems and their potential applications in autonomous systems and multimedia indexing.

Keywords: Object Recognition, Contour Sparse Representation, Shape Distribution Descriptor, Computer Vision, Sparse Coding, Feature Representation.
