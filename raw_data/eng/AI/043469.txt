The rapid proliferation of mobile devices has created a growing demand for efficient and accurate video object detection systems that can operate under resource constraints. This paper addresses the challenge of achieving high-performance video object detection on mobile platforms, where computational power and energy efficiency are critical. We propose a novel lightweight framework that leverages advanced neural network architectures and optimization techniques tailored for mobile environments. Our approach integrates temporal information from video frames to enhance detection accuracy while minimizing computational overhead. Through extensive experiments on benchmark datasets, we demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of both accuracy and inference speed, outperforming existing solutions by a significant margin. The proposed framework is highly scalable and can be deployed across a wide range of mobile devices without compromising performance. This research contributes to the advancement of real-time video analytics on mobile platforms, with potential applications in augmented reality, surveillance, and autonomous navigation. Key innovations include a streamlined model design, efficient memory utilization, and adaptive frame sampling strategies. The findings highlight the feasibility of deploying robust video object detection systems on resource-constrained devices, paving the way for future developments in mobile-centric computer vision technologies.  

**Keywords**: video object detection, mobile computing, neural networks, real-time processing, resource optimization.
