In Figure 4.1, the architecture demonstrates that the “Customer”, “Agent”, and “Admin” packages all rely on the “Authentication” package for managing user authentication and authorization. Both the “Ticket” and “Project” packages also depend on these foundational packages for similar security and access control functions. The “Helpdesk” package specifically utilizes “Project” for acquiring project-related information and acts as an intermediary connecting “Agent” and “Customer”, thus facilitating the creation of tickets from “Customer” to both “Agent” and “Admin”. The “Authentication” package is depicted as vital, providing critical security across all packages.
