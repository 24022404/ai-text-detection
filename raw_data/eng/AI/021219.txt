This paper introduces DH3D, a novel approach to large-scale 6DoF relocalization using deep hierarchical 3D descriptors. The objective is to enable robust and accurate relocalization in complex environments, addressing the challenges of existing methods that struggle with scale, rotation, and translation variations. Our approach leverages a hierarchical descriptor extraction framework, combining the strengths of both local and global feature representations. The method utilizes a deep neural network to learn compact and informative 3D descriptors, which are then used for efficient and effective relocalization. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of DH3D over state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in relocalization accuracy and robustness. The key contributions of this research include the design of a hierarchical 3D descriptor framework, the development of a deep learning-based approach for 6DoF relocalization, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in large-scale environments. This work has important implications for applications such as augmented reality, robotics, and 3D reconstruction, where robust and accurate relocalization is crucial. Key keywords: 6DoF relocalization, 3D descriptors, deep learning, hierarchical feature extraction, large-scale environments.
