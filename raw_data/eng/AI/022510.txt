dBTS employs a Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) algorithm to generate positioning sequences efficiently, although the method necessitates selecting an appropriate primitive polynomial to ensure optimal performance. To identify subsequence locations, dBTS relies on a computationally expensive look-up table. More in-depth explanations of the algorithm, the structure and function of the look-up table, as well as a comprehensive system analysis, will be discussed in the following chapter.
