This paper addresses the challenge of improving audio classification accuracy by leveraging shift-invariant sparse coding techniques. Traditional sparse coding methods often struggle with temporal variations in audio signals, leading to suboptimal feature representations. To overcome this limitation, we propose a novel shift-invariant sparse coding framework that enhances the robustness of feature extraction by accounting for temporal shifts in audio data. Our approach integrates a shift-invariant dictionary learning algorithm, which adapts to variations in signal timing, and a sparse coding optimization process to generate discriminative features. We evaluate the proposed method on benchmark audio datasets, comparing its performance against conventional sparse coding and state-of-the-art classification models. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in classification accuracy, particularly for tasks involving time-sensitive audio patterns. The proposed framework also exhibits superior computational efficiency, making it suitable for real-time applications. This research contributes to the field of audio signal processing by introducing a shift-invariant approach that enhances feature representation and classification performance, with potential applications in speech recognition, music analysis, and environmental sound detection.  

**Keywords**: Shift-invariant sparse coding, audio classification, feature extraction, dictionary learning, signal processing.
