This paper addresses the challenge of optimizing battery energy storage system (BESS) dispatch in dynamic energy markets, where traditional rule-based and model-based approaches often struggle to adapt to real-time uncertainties. We propose a novel hybrid framework that combines reinforcement learning (RL) with a model-based optimizer to enhance decision-making for BESS operations. The RL agent is trained to learn optimal dispatch policies by interacting with a simulated environment, while the model-based optimizer refines these decisions by incorporating domain-specific constraints and physical system dynamics. Our approach leverages the adaptability of RL to handle complex, non-linear market conditions, while the optimizer ensures operational feasibility and efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed framework outperforms standalone RL and traditional optimization methods, achieving a 15% improvement in revenue generation and a 20% reduction in operational inefficiencies. The study highlights the potential of integrating data-driven and model-based techniques for real-world energy storage applications, offering a scalable solution for grid operators and energy market participants. Key contributions include a robust hybrid architecture, improved dispatch accuracy, and insights into the synergy between RL and optimization in energy systems. Keywords: reinforcement learning, battery energy storage, model-based optimization, energy dispatch, hybrid systems.
