This paper introduces SIGAN, a novel image generation method designed to enhance solar cell defect segmentation and augmentation. The objective is to address the challenge of limited datasets in solar cell defect detection by generating high-quality, synthetic images that can augment existing datasets and improve the accuracy of defect segmentation models. SIGAN employs a generative adversarial network (GAN) architecture, incorporating a custom-designed generator and discriminator to produce realistic images of solar cells with various defects. The results show that SIGAN-generated images can significantly enhance the performance of state-of-the-art segmentation models, achieving a mean intersection over union (IoU) of 85.6% on a benchmark dataset. The proposed method contributes to the field by providing a robust and efficient way to generate synthetic images for solar cell defect segmentation, enabling the development of more accurate and reliable defect detection systems. Key contributions include the design of a novel GAN architecture for solar cell image generation and the demonstration of its effectiveness in augmenting datasets for improved defect segmentation. Relevant keywords: solar cell defect segmentation, image generation, GAN, data augmentation, deep learning.
