This paper proposes RSINet, a novel deep learning-based approach for online visual tracking that achieves rotation-scale invariance. The objective is to develop a robust tracking model that can accurately follow objects across varying orientations and scales in real-time. To address this challenge, we design a Rotation-Scale Invariant Network (RSINet) that leverages a combination of convolutional neural networks and spatial attention mechanisms. Our approach enables the model to learn rotation-scale invariant features, which are essential for accurate tracking. Experimental results demonstrate that RSINet outperforms state-of-the-art tracking methods, achieving superior accuracy and robustness in diverse scenarios. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a novel rotation-scale invariant module and a lightweight yet effective tracking architecture. Our work has significant implications for various applications, including surveillance, robotics, and autonomous driving, where online visual tracking is crucial. Key keywords: online visual tracking, rotation-scale invariance, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, spatial attention mechanisms.
