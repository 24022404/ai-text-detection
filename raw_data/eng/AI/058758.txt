Major deep learning libraries—including Keras, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Caffe2, CNTK, and MxNet—are actively supported by prominent technology companies such as Google, Facebook, Microsoft, and Amazon, all of which also provide cloud computing platforms to attract users and facilitate machine learning adoption. Among these, Keras stands out as a high-level neural network API written in Python, which has been integrated as part of the TensorFlow project. Developed by François Chollet, Keras is specifically designed to simplify the construction and training of deep learning models, such as convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs), thereby making powerful AI tools more accessible to researchers and practitioners.
