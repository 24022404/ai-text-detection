This paper presents a novel dynamic programming algorithm designed to enhance informative measurements and optimize path-planning in the context of autonomous systems. The primary objective of our research is to address the challenges associated with efficiently acquiring and utilizing information from a given environment to inform path-planning decisions. Our approach leverages a robust dynamic programming framework that systematically analyzes and prioritizes measurement points to yield the most informative data. By integrating this method into path-planning, we achieve results that closely approximate optimal trajectories while maintaining computational feasibility. Our experimental evaluations demonstrate that the proposed algorithm significantly outperforms existing methods in terms of both accuracy and computational efficiency, particularly in complex and dynamic environments. The ability to dynamically adjust path-planning based on real-time measurements opens new avenues for applications in robotics, surveillance, environmental monitoring, and autonomous navigation. Ultimately, our research contributes to the field by providing a scalable, effective solution for optimizing path-planning through informative measurement strategies. Key concepts explored in this work include dynamic programming, path-planning optimization, and real-time information gathering.
