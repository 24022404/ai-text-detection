This paper addresses the challenge of Weakly Supervised Object Localization (WSOL), a task that involves locating objects in images with only image-level labels provided for training. Accurate localization with minimal supervision is crucial for reducing annotation costs and increasing the efficiency of computer vision systems.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel Dual-attention Guided Dropblock Module (DGDM) designed to enhance object localization accuracy by focusing on relevant features within images. Our approach leverages dual attention mechanisms to dynamically highlight critical regions, while the Dropblock technique stochastically removes inconsequential regions, thereby reinforcing the model's ability to discern primary objects from background noise.

Results/Findings: The DGDM significantly improves localization performance, achieving superior precision compared to existing WSOL methods on benchmark datasets. Comprehensive experiments demonstrate that our model consistently identifies objects with higher accuracy, effectively handling challenging scenarios with overlapping or occluded objects. Furthermore, DGDM shows robustness across varied datasets, showcasing its generalizability and effectiveness in real-world applications.

Conclusion/Implications: This research introduces a significant advancement in WSOL by integrating dual-attention and Dropblock strategies. Our module not only enhances the model's attention to pertinent features but also promotes a deeper understanding of spatial regions, yielding practical implications for fields requiring minimal annotation effort, such as autonomous vehicles and robotics vision systems. By providing a more precise and efficient method for object localization, this study contributes to the development of robust computer vision applications.

Keywords: Weakly Supervised Object Localization, Dual-attention, Dropblock, Computer Vision, Feature Extraction, Image Annotation.
