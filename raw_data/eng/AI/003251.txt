This paper addresses the challenge of accurately tracking road users in complex traffic environments, a critical task for intelligent transportation systems and autonomous driving technologies. Traditional tracking methods often struggle with occlusions, dynamic interactions, and scalability in real-world scenarios. To overcome these limitations, we propose a novel approach based on constraint programming, which leverages logical constraints to model the behavior and interactions of road users. Our method formulates the tracking problem as a constraint satisfaction problem, integrating spatial, temporal, and contextual information to ensure robust and efficient tracking. Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency, particularly in scenarios with high occlusion rates and dense traffic. The results highlight the potential of constraint programming for real-time applications in traffic monitoring and autonomous systems. This research contributes a scalable and interpretable framework for road user tracking, offering significant advancements in the field of intelligent transportation and paving the way for safer and more efficient urban mobility solutions.  

**Keywords**: road user tracking, constraint programming, intelligent transportation systems, autonomous driving, traffic monitoring.
