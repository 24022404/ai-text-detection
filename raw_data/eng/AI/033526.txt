This paper investigates the implicit bias of algorithms, a critical issue in machine learning and artificial intelligence. The objective is to develop a framework for bounding bias via margin, providing a deeper understanding of how algorithms can inadvertently perpetuate and amplify existing biases. Our approach utilizes a novel margin-based analysis to quantify and mitigate bias in various algorithms. The key findings indicate that by optimizing the margin, it is possible to effectively bound the bias of any algorithm, leading to more fair and reliable outcomes. The results demonstrate significant improvements in reducing bias compared to existing methods. The conclusion highlights the importance of considering implicit bias in algorithm design and the potential applications of this research in developing more equitable AI systems. Key contributions include a new theoretical framework for analyzing bias, an efficient algorithm for bias reduction, and empirical evaluations demonstrating the effectiveness of the proposed approach. Relevant keywords: implicit bias, algorithmic fairness, margin analysis, bias reduction, AI equity.
