This paper addresses the challenge of integrating multiple datasets with complex relationships using multi-layered Gaussian Graphical Models (GGMs). The objective is to develop a joint estimation and inference framework that can effectively combine information from diverse sources, accounting for the inherent dependencies and structures within each dataset. Our approach employs a novel Bayesian methodology to integrate multiple GGMs, enabling the estimation of model parameters and the inference of relationships between variables across different layers and datasets. The results demonstrate the efficacy of our method in recovering underlying structures and improving predictive performance, outperforming existing approaches in simulations and real-world applications. The key contributions of this research lie in its ability to handle complex, multi-layered dependencies and its potential to unlock new insights in data integration problems. Our work has significant implications for various fields, including bioinformatics, social network analysis, and finance, where the integration of multiple datasets is crucial for informed decision-making. Key keywords: Gaussian Graphical Models, data integration, multi-layered models, Bayesian inference, joint estimation.
