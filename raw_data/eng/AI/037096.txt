This paper explores the integration of policy gradient and Q-learning methods to enhance the performance of reinforcement learning agents. The objective is to develop a hybrid approach that leverages the strengths of both techniques, allowing for more efficient and effective learning in complex environments. Our method combines the policy gradient's ability to directly optimize policies with Q-learning's capability to learn accurate value functions. We employ a novel architecture that seamlessly integrates these two paradigms, enabling the agent to adaptively switch between policy gradient and Q-learning updates. Experimental results demonstrate that our hybrid approach outperforms traditional policy gradient and Q-learning methods in various benchmark tasks, achieving improved convergence rates and enhanced overall performance. The key contributions of this research include the development of a unified framework for policy gradient and Q-learning, and the demonstration of its potential to accelerate reinforcement learning in real-world applications. Our work has significant implications for the development of more efficient and scalable reinforcement learning algorithms, with potential applications in robotics, game playing, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, policy gradient, Q-learning, hybrid approach, deep learning.
