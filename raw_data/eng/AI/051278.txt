Object detection remains a critical challenge in computer vision, particularly in complex scenarios with varying object scales and occlusions. This paper introduces WW-Nets, a novel object detection framework employing dual neural networks designed to enhance both localization accuracy and classification performance. WW-Nets leverage a Wide-perception Network (W-Net) to capture global contextual information and a Weighted-attention Network (W-Net) to focus on salient object features, thereby improving robustness against background clutter and scale variations. The W-Net utilizes dilated convolutions to effectively enlarge the receptive field without increasing computational complexity, while the W-Net incorporates a spatial attention mechanism to adaptively weight feature maps based on their relevance to the target objects. Experimental results on the PASCAL VOC and COCO datasets demonstrate that WW-Nets achieve state-of-the-art detection accuracy compared to existing single-network detectors and competitive performance against more complex, multi-stage approaches. Notably, WW-Nets exhibit significant improvements in detecting small and occluded objects. The proposed dual-network architecture offers a promising direction for future object detection research, balancing performance and computational efficiency.
Keywords: Object Detection, Convolutional Neural Networks, Attention Mechanism, Deep Learning, Computer Vision.
