This paper introduces MUST-GAN, a novel approach to self-driven person image generation, leveraging multi-level statistics transfer to enhance the realism and diversity of generated images. The objective is to address the challenge of generating high-quality person images with varying poses, clothing, and backgrounds. Our method employs a generative adversarial network (GAN) framework, incorporating a statistics transfer module that captures and transfers multi-level features from the source domain to the target domain. The results demonstrate significant improvements in image quality, diversity, and realism, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods. Our approach enables the generation of person images with unprecedented fidelity, making it suitable for various applications, including data augmentation, virtual try-on, and computer vision tasks. The key contributions of this work include the introduction of a multi-level statistics transfer mechanism, which effectively captures and transfers complex features, and the demonstration of its efficacy in self-driven person image generation. This research has significant implications for the development of more sophisticated and realistic image generation models, with potential applications in fields such as artificial intelligence, computer vision, and graphics. Key keywords: GAN, person image generation, multi-level statistics transfer, self-driven generation, computer vision, AI models.
