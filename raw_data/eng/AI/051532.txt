This paper addresses the challenge of effectively embedding deep features by incorporating local similarity information, a critical aspect in tasks such as image recognition, clustering, and retrieval. The goal is to enhance feature interpretation by making embeddings more sensitive to local similarities within the data.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel Local Similarity-Aware Deep Feature Embedding model that integrates local similarity information into the process of creating deep embeddings. The model leverages a specially designed neural network architecture featuring a custom loss function aimed at preserving local similarity relationships while optimizing feature space representation.

Results/Findings: Our experiments demonstrate that the proposed approach significantly outperforms existing embedding methods in terms of accuracy and similarity preservation across multiple benchmark datasets. Specifically, our method improved classification accuracy and retrieval precision, highlighting the effectiveness of local similarity awareness in enhancing feature embedding. Additional comparisons showcase the superiority of our approach in maintaining local cluster integrity compared to traditional deep embedding techniques.

Conclusion/Implications: The proposed Local Similarity-Aware Deep Feature Embedding framework presents a notable advancement in the field of feature embedding by improving local similarity preservation. This contribution is pivotal for applications requiring precise similarity evaluations, such as image search and pattern recognition. The findings suggest potential expansions into various domains like natural language processing and 3D object representations, where preserving local feature similarities can enhance model performance.

Keywords: Local similarity, deep feature embedding, image recognition, neural networks, feature space representation, clustering, retrieval precision.
