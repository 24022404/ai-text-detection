This paper introduces a novel approach to robust subspace discovery, addressing the challenges posed by noise and outliers in high-dimensional data. We propose a Block-diagonal Adaptive Locality-constrained Representation (BALR) method. BALR learns a structured representation by enforcing block-diagonal structure and adaptively adjusting locality constraints, thereby enhancing robustness to corrupted data. The block-diagonal constraint encourages data points from the same subspace to be grouped together, while adaptive locality constraints further improve representation accuracy. An efficient optimization algorithm is developed to solve the resulting non-convex problem. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that BALR outperforms state-of-the-art subspace clustering and representation methods in terms of clustering accuracy and robustness. The improved performance highlights the efficacy of block-diagonal and adaptive locality constraints in handling noisy and corrupted data for subspace discovery. Keywords: Subspace Clustering, Robust Representation, Block-diagonal Structure, Locality-constrained Representation, Adaptive Learning, Data Mining.
