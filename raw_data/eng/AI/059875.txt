Deep neural networks (DNNs) have become fundamental in a wide range of applications; however, their large model sizes and computational demands hinder real-time execution on resource-constrained mobile devices. Weight pruning is a common approach to reduce model size and computational overhead, but existing methods predominantly generate unstructured or block sparsity patterns, which often fail to fully exploit hardware acceleration capabilities on mobile platforms. In this paper, we introduce PCONV, a novel pruning methodology that induces a highly efficient and hardware-friendly structured sparsity in DNN weights. PCONV identifies and preserves computationally significant connections while promoting sparsity that aligns with mobile processor architectures, enabling faster and more energy-efficient inference. We rigorously evaluate PCONV on standard mobile deep learning benchmarks, demonstrating substantial reductions in model size and inference latency compared to conventional pruning techniques, while maintaining competitive accuracy. Our experiments show real-time speedups of up to 3× on representative mobile devices. The proposed approach bridges the gap between algorithmic sparsity and practical hardware execution, delivering both strong compression and tangible acceleration. PCONV paves the way for the deployment of high-performance DNNs in resource-limited environments, and presents new directions for structured model compression. Keywords: weight pruning, structured sparsity, deep neural networks, mobile devices, model compression, real-time inference.
