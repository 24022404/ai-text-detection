This paper addresses the challenge of forecasting time series systems with unknown clusters of forecast tasks by introducing a novel approach to learn predictive leading indicators. The objective is to develop a method that can automatically identify and leverage relevant indicators to improve forecast accuracy in complex systems. Our approach employs a combination of machine learning and clustering techniques to discover hidden patterns and relationships between time series data. The methodology involves training a model to learn predictive leading indicators that can be used to forecast future values of the time series. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in improving forecast accuracy and identifying unknown clusters of forecast tasks. The key findings indicate that our method outperforms traditional forecasting techniques, particularly in systems with multiple underlying patterns. The contributions of this research include the development of a new framework for learning predictive leading indicators and the application of clustering techniques to identify unknown forecast tasks. This work has significant implications for real-world applications, such as demand forecasting, financial forecasting, and resource allocation, where accurate predictions are crucial. Key keywords: time series forecasting, leading indicators, clustering, machine learning, predictive modeling.
