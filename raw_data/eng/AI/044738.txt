This paper addresses the challenge of automatically generating captions for images of heavy rain scenes, a task that is crucial for various applications such as weather reporting, autonomous vehicles, and smart city infrastructure. Our objective is to develop a novel encoder learning approach that leverages semantic visual feature matching to improve the accuracy of image captioning in adverse weather conditions. We propose a deep learning model that integrates a semantic visual feature extractor with a caption generator, enabling the system to effectively capture and describe the essential elements of heavy rain images. Our approach utilizes a unique feature matching mechanism that aligns visual features with semantic concepts, resulting in more accurate and informative captions. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our method over existing state-of-the-art models, with significant improvements in captioning quality and robustness. The proposed encoder learning framework has important implications for various computer vision applications, including image captioning, visual question answering, and image retrieval, particularly in scenarios where weather conditions are a critical factor. Key contributions of this research include the introduction of a novel semantic visual feature matching technique and the development of a robust encoder learning approach for heavy rain image captioning, with relevant keywords including image captioning, heavy rain, semantic visual features, deep learning, and computer vision.
