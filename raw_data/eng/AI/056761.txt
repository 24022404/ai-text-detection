Express.js plays a crucial role as the server framework in the system architecture, efficiently handling, processing, and managing metadata-related requests. Its robust routing capabilities allow for clear definition and management of API endpoints, while its extensible middleware system supports tasks such as authentication, logging, and error-handling with ease. Express.js also provides high performance, minimizing the latency in request-response cycles and enabling faster data processing. These strengths make Express.js particularly suitable for constructing scalable, reliable backends that can adapt to growing user demand and complex data operations without sacrificing efficiency or maintainability.
