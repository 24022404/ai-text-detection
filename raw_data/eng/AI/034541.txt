Robustness of machine learning models against adversarial perturbations has become a critical concern in ensuring reliable deployment in real-world applications. This paper presents a novel framework for automatic perturbation analysis, designed to provide scalable and certified robustness guarantees for deep learning systems. The proposed approach employs an efficient algorithm that systematically explores the input space, quantifies model sensitivity to varying perturbations, and generates verifiable robustness certificates for each prediction. Unlike existing methods that are often limited by scalability or conservative estimations, our framework integrates adaptive sampling strategies and efficient bounding techniques to achieve both high accuracy and computational efficiency, even on large-scale datasets and complex models. Experimental results demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms traditional robustness certification techniques in terms of scalability and tightness of the robustness guarantees, while maintaining or improving upon prior standards in time and resource consumption. Furthermore, the framework’s generality enables its extension to broader security and reliability tasks beyond standard adversarial robustness, including data poisoning detection and model interpretability analysis. Our findings contribute important advancements toward practical, certifiable robustness and broaden the applicability of automated analysis in trustworthy machine learning. 

Keywords: certified robustness, automatic perturbation analysis, adversarial examples, scalability, trustworthy AI, deep learning, security, reliability.
