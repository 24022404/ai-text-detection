This paper introduces TSI, a novel framework for video action recognition that leverages temporal saliency integration to enhance the accuracy of action detection. The objective is to address the challenge of capturing complex and dynamic actions in videos by developing a more effective and efficient approach. Our method utilizes a multi-scale temporal saliency map to highlight important regions and frames, which are then integrated into a deep neural network to learn robust action representations. Experimental results demonstrate that TSI outperforms state-of-the-art methods on several benchmark datasets, achieving significant improvements in action recognition accuracy. The key findings indicate that the proposed temporal saliency integration strategy can effectively capture long-range dependencies and subtle action cues, leading to better performance in recognizing complex actions. This research contributes to the field of computer vision and has potential applications in areas such as surveillance, human-computer interaction, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: video action recognition, temporal saliency, deep learning, computer vision, action detection.
