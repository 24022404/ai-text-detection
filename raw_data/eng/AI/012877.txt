LiDAR point cloud semantic segmentation is crucial for autonomous driving and robotics. Existing methods often suffer from information loss during feature extraction and up-sampling, leading to decreased accuracy. This paper introduces FIDNet, a novel network architecture employing Fully Interpolation Decoding to address these limitations. FIDNet utilizes a hierarchical feature extraction module to capture multi-scale contextual information. The core innovation lies in the Fully Interpolation Decoding module, which adaptively interpolates features from different levels based on learned weights, thereby preserving fine-grained details and enhancing feature representation. We evaluate FIDNet on benchmark datasets such as SemanticKITTI and nuScenes. Experimental results demonstrate that FIDNet achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods by a significant margin in terms of mean Intersection over Union (mIoU). The proposed method effectively mitigates information loss and improves segmentation accuracy, offering a promising solution for LiDAR point cloud semantic segmentation. Keywords: LiDAR, Point Cloud, Semantic Segmentation, Deep Learning, Interpolation, Autonomous Driving.
