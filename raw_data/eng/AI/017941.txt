The objective of this research is to address the limitations of conventional kernel methods in capturing complex structures within high-dimensional data. We introduce Convolutional Spectral Kernel Learning (CSKL), a novel approach that combines the principles of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) with spectral kernel methods to enhance learning capabilities and flexibility in machine learning models. Our method leverages the feature extraction power of CNNs to transform input data into a spectral domain, where a new class of convolutional kernels is applied to learn representations efficiently. The approach is designed to integrate spectral filtering techniques with the convolutive nature of CNNs, which enriches the learning process by emphasizing multi-scale feature analysis. The key findings demonstrate that CSKL significantly outperforms traditional kernel methods and existing convolutional models on a variety of benchmark datasets, yielding higher accuracy and robustness in classification tasks. Furthermore, our model shows improved computational efficiency and scalability, making it applicable for real-time processing in large-scale data scenarios. The study highlights the potential of this hybrid method to contribute to the fields of computational learning and data-driven engineering by providing a more dynamic and adaptive tool for complex data analysis. Keywords include convolutional kernels, spectral methods, machine learning, CNNs, and data representation.
