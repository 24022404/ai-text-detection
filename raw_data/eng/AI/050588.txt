This paper addresses the critical need for explainable online validation of machine learning models in practical applications, aiming to enhance model reliability and trustworthiness. Our approach combines model interpretability techniques with real-time data processing to facilitate the continuous monitoring and validation of machine learning models in dynamic environments. We propose a novel framework that integrates feature attribution methods, model performance metrics, and data quality assessment to provide insights into model behavior and decision-making processes. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in identifying model drift, data quality issues, and performance degradation, allowing for timely interventions and model updates. The key contributions of this research include the development of a scalable and flexible validation framework, the introduction of a new feature attribution method tailored to online learning settings, and the demonstration of improved model explainability and reliability in real-world applications. Our work has significant implications for the deployment of machine learning models in high-stakes domains, such as healthcare, finance, and autonomous systems, where model transparency and accountability are essential. Key keywords: explainable AI, machine learning, online validation, model interpretability, real-time data processing.
