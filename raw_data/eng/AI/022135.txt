This paper proposes a novel probabilistic framework, Variational Mixture of Normalizing Flows, which addresses the challenge of modeling complex distributions in deep learning. The objective is to develop a flexible and efficient approach for density estimation and generative modeling. Our method combines the strengths of variational inference and normalizing flows, leveraging a mixture of flow-based models to capture multi-modal distributions. We employ a hierarchical Bayesian approach to learn the mixture components and the flow parameters jointly. The results demonstrate that our model outperforms existing methods in terms of likelihood and mode coverage on several benchmark datasets. Our approach also enables efficient sampling and inference, making it suitable for applications such as image and data generation. The key contributions of this work include a scalable and parallelizable architecture, improved mode capture, and a robust inference procedure. This research has significant implications for advances in generative modeling, unsupervised learning, and probabilistic deep learning, with potential applications in computer vision, natural language processing, and robotics. Key keywords: normalizing flows, variational inference, generative modeling, deep learning, probabilistic models.
