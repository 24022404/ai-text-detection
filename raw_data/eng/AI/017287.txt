Nanopore sequencing has emerged as a powerful technology for real-time DNA and RNA analysis, yet achieving high base calling accuracy remains a significant challenge due to the inherent noise and signal complexity of nanopore readouts. This paper introduces a dynamic pooling approach to enhance the performance of neural network-based base callers. Unlike conventional fixed pooling methods, dynamic pooling adaptively aggregates temporal signal features based on local context, enabling the model to better capture variable-length patterns in nanopore signal data. We implement dynamic pooling within a recurrent neural network architecture specifically tailored for nanopore base calling tasks. Experimental evaluations on publicly available nanopore sequencing datasets demonstrate that our method consistently outperforms existing base callers, achieving significant improvements in both read accuracy and computational efficiency. Notably, our approach yields up to a 2% increase in base calling accuracy and reduces error rates across various genomic regions. These results highlight the potential of dynamic pooling as a fundamental enhancement for signal processing in nanopore sequencing workflows. The proposed method offers a promising direction for future developments in real-time, high-accuracy base calling and can be readily integrated into current nanopore sequencing pipelines. Keywords: nanopore sequencing, base calling, dynamic pooling, neural networks, signal processing, genomic analysis.
