This paper presents a novel approach for time series prediction and dynamic network estimation through the introduction of the Monotone Single Index Multi-variate Autoregressive (MSI-MAR) model. The primary objective is to address the challenges of accurately forecasting multi-variate temporal data while simultaneously inferring evolving network structures, especially in high-dimensional settings with complex dependencies. The MSI-MAR model leverages a monotonic function to capture nonlinear interactions among variables, combined with a single index framework to reduce dimensionality without compromising interpretability. To estimate the model parameters, we develop an efficient algorithm based on iterative optimization and regularization techniques, enabling scalable computation and improved robustness against overfitting. Experimental results on synthetic datasets and real-world applications, including financial and biological systems, demonstrate that our method outperforms traditional linear and standard nonlinear autoregressive models in both prediction accuracy and network recovery. The MSI-MAR’s ability to discover latent dynamic relationships and scale to larger networks marks a significant advancement in time series analysis. The proposed framework holds promise for applications in fields such as econometrics, neuroscience, and environmental monitoring, where accurate forecasting and network estimation are crucial. Keywords: time series prediction, network estimation, multi-variate autoregressive model, monotonic function, dimensionality reduction, nonlinear modeling, dynamic networks.
