This paper proposes Bucket Renormalization, a novel approach for approximate inference in probabilistic models. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of inference algorithms, particularly in complex models with large state spaces. Our method leverages a bucketing strategy to partition the state space into manageable regions, followed by a renormalization step to ensure proper probability distributions. We evaluate the performance of Bucket Renormalization on various benchmark models, demonstrating significant improvements in accuracy and computational cost compared to existing approximate inference methods. The key findings indicate that our approach achieves better balance between exploration and exploitation, leading to more reliable estimates of marginal probabilities. The contributions of this research include a new perspective on approximate inference, with potential applications in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and probabilistic modeling. Key keywords: approximate inference, probabilistic models, bucketing, renormalization, AI, machine learning.
