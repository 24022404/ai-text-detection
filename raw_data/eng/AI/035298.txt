This paper proposes a novel approach to signed graph metric learning, leveraging Gershgorin disc theory to achieve perfect alignment. The objective is to develop an efficient method for learning metrics on signed graphs, which is crucial for various applications in network analysis and machine learning. Our approach utilizes a disc perfect alignment strategy, ensuring that the learned metric aligns with the underlying graph structure. We employ a Gershgorin disc-based method to optimize the metric learning process, resulting in improved performance and robustness. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The key findings of this research include the development of a scalable and flexible metric learning framework, which can be applied to various signed graph-based applications. Our contributions highlight the importance of geometric and algebraic techniques in graph metric learning, paving the way for future research in this area. Key keywords: signed graph, metric learning, Gershgorin disc, perfect alignment, graph analysis, machine learning.
