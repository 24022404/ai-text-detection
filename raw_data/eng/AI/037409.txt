This paper introduces a novel approach to image colorization, termed Pixelated Semantic Colorization, which leverages semantic segmentation to guide the colorization process at a granular pixel level. The objective is to enhance colorization accuracy and visual appeal by incorporating semantic understanding. Our method employs a deep convolutional neural network that integrates semantic labels directly into the colorization pipeline, allowing for spatially consistent and semantically meaningful color assignments. We discretize the color space into a set of representative color palettes and learn to predict a color distribution for each pixel conditioned on its semantic category. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing colorization techniques, producing more vibrant and realistic images with fewer artifacts. Specifically, we show improved performance in preserving object boundaries and assigning consistent colors to semantically similar regions. The key contribution of this work is a pixel-level semantic guidance mechanism that enables more accurate and controllable image colorization. Keywords: Image Colorization, Semantic Segmentation, Deep Learning, Pixel-Level Prediction, Computer Vision.
