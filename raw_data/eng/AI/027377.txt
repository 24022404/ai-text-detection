This paper presents an empirical study on the effectiveness of spatial attention mechanisms in deep neural networks. The objective is to investigate how these mechanisms enhance the performance of deep models in various computer vision tasks. We employ a range of spatial attention techniques, including channel attention, spatial attention, and combined attention models, and evaluate their impact on model accuracy, robustness, and computational efficiency. Our results show that spatial attention mechanisms can significantly improve model performance, particularly in tasks that require focusing on specific regions of interest. We also find that the choice of attention mechanism and its placement within the network architecture can substantially affect the outcomes. The key contributions of this research are the identification of the most effective spatial attention mechanisms and the development of guidelines for their integration into deep networks. Our study has important implications for the design of more accurate and efficient deep learning models for computer vision applications, including image classification, object detection, and segmentation. Key keywords: spatial attention, deep neural networks, computer vision, channel attention, model performance.
