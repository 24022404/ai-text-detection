This paper presents a novel approach to bypassing Google reCAPTCHA v3 using reinforcement learning techniques. The objective is to investigate the vulnerability of reCAPTCHA v3 to automated attacks, leveraging the capabilities of artificial intelligence to mimic human behavior. Our method employs a custom-designed reinforcement learning framework, utilizing a combination of machine learning algorithms and browser automation tools to interact with the reCAPTCHA system. The results show that our approach can successfully bypass reCAPTCHA v3 with a significant success rate, outperforming existing methods. Our findings have important implications for the security of online services relying on reCAPTCHA v3, highlighting the need for more robust verification mechanisms. The contributions of this research include the development of a novel reinforcement learning-based attack strategy and the demonstration of its effectiveness in evading reCAPTCHA v3. Key keywords: reCAPTCHA v3, reinforcement learning, AI-powered attacks, browser automation, machine learning, cybersecurity.
