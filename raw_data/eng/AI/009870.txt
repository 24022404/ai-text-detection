This paper proposes a novel High-order Differentiable Autoencoder (HDA) for nonlinear model reduction, aiming to address the challenges of capturing complex dynamics in high-dimensional systems. Our approach combines the strengths of autoencoders and high-order differentiation to learn compact, interpretable representations of nonlinear systems. The HDA model utilizes a hierarchical architecture to extract salient features and capture higher-order interactions between variables. We demonstrate the effectiveness of HDA through numerical experiments on various nonlinear systems, showcasing its ability to accurately reconstruct system dynamics and outperform existing model reduction techniques. The key findings highlight the importance of high-order differentiation in capturing nonlinear relationships and the potential of HDA for applications in fields such as fluid dynamics, structural mechanics, and control systems. Our research contributes to the development of more efficient and accurate model reduction methods, enabling the simulation and analysis of complex systems with reduced computational cost. Key keywords: nonlinear model reduction, high-order differentiation, autoencoders, compact representations, system dynamics.
