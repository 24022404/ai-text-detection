This paper addresses the challenge of balancing safety and efficiency in reinforcement learning (RL) by introducing a novel Constrained Policy Improvement (CPI) framework. The objective is to develop an RL approach that ensures safe exploration while optimizing policy performance. Our method employs a constrained optimization technique to modify the policy update process, guaranteeing that the improved policy satisfies predefined safety constraints. We utilize a trust region-based approach to limit the policy updates, preventing large deviations from the current policy and ensuring safe exploration. The results demonstrate that CPI achieves significant improvements in safety and efficiency compared to existing RL methods, with a substantial reduction in the number of unsafe episodes and faster convergence to optimal policies. Our approach has important implications for real-world applications of RL, such as robotics and autonomous systems, where safety is a primary concern. The key contributions of this work include the development of a safe and efficient RL framework, the introduction of a constrained policy improvement algorithm, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in various environments. Relevant keywords: reinforcement learning, safe exploration, constrained optimization, trust region methods, policy improvement.
