This paper introduces Pose2Seg, a novel approach to human instance segmentation that eliminates the need for detection. The objective is to accurately segment individual humans in images without relying on traditional detection-based methods. Our approach utilizes a pose estimation model to identify key points on the human body, which are then used to generate segmentation masks. The method leverages a transformer-based architecture to effectively capture long-range dependencies and contextual information. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of Pose2Seg, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets with significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency. The key findings highlight the importance of pose information in facilitating detection-free segmentation. This research contributes to the field of computer vision by providing a unique and innovative solution for human instance segmentation, with potential applications in areas such as autonomous driving, surveillance, and human-computer interaction. Key keywords: human instance segmentation, pose estimation, transformer architecture, detection-free segmentation, computer vision.
