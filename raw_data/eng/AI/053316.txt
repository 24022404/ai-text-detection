The nuclear domain presents unique challenges for natural language processing (NLP) due to its specialized terminology, limited publicly available datasets, and high-stakes applications. To address these challenges, we introduce NukeBERT, a pre-trained language model specifically tailored for low-resource nuclear domain tasks. NukeBERT leverages domain-specific corpora and advanced transfer learning techniques to achieve robust performance in tasks such as nuclear text classification, entity recognition, and question answering. Our approach involves fine-tuning a state-of-the-art transformer-based architecture on curated nuclear-related datasets, ensuring the model captures domain-specific semantics and context. Experimental results demonstrate that NukeBERT outperforms general-purpose language models in nuclear domain tasks, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency. Furthermore, NukeBERT's ability to generalize across low-resource scenarios highlights its potential for real-world applications, such as nuclear safety documentation analysis and regulatory compliance. This research contributes to the growing field of domain-specific NLP by providing a scalable and effective solution for specialized domains with limited data.  

**Keywords**: NukeBERT, pre-trained language model, low-resource NLP, nuclear domain, transformer, domain adaptation.
