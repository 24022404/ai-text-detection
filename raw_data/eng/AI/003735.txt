This paper addresses the challenge of model misspecification in continuous control tasks using reinforcement learning (RL). The objective is to develop a robust RL framework that can effectively handle uncertainties and inaccuracies in the system dynamics model. We propose a novel approach that combines model-based RL with robust control techniques to mitigate the effects of model misspecification. Our method utilizes a probabilistic ensemble of models to capture the uncertainty in the system dynamics and incorporates this uncertainty into the RL algorithm. The results show that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of robustness and stability, achieving improved control performance in the presence of model errors. The key findings highlight the importance of accounting for model uncertainty in continuous control tasks and demonstrate the potential of our approach for real-world applications. This research contributes to the development of more reliable and efficient RL algorithms for complex control tasks, with implications for fields such as robotics, autonomous systems, and process control. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, continuous control, model misspecification, robust control, uncertainty quantification.
