Neural Style Transfer (NST) has emerged as a powerful technique for transforming images by combining content and artistic style. However, existing methods often struggle with preserving structural consistency and generating high-quality outputs, especially when applied to specialized datasets such as lunar imagery from the Chang'E-4 mission. This paper proposes a novel approach leveraging Cycle-consistent Generative Adversarial Networks (CycleGANs) to address these challenges. By incorporating cycle-consistency constraints, the proposed model ensures robust style transfer while maintaining the integrity of the original content. The methodology involves training the CycleGAN on a curated dataset of Chang'E-4 images, enabling the network to learn domain-specific features and produce visually coherent results. Experimental evaluations demonstrate significant improvements in both qualitative and quantitative metrics compared to traditional NST methods. The proposed framework achieves superior style transfer fidelity, particularly in preserving fine details and textures unique to lunar imagery. This research contributes to advancing NST techniques for specialized datasets and highlights the potential of CycleGANs in enhancing image processing tasks for space exploration and beyond. Key applications include improving visual representations of lunar data for scientific analysis and public outreach.  

**Keywords**: Neural Style Transfer, CycleGAN, Chang'E-4, lunar imagery, image processing, generative models.
