Abstract:

Anomaly detection in video sequences is a critical task in information security, surveillance, and autonomous systems, aimed at automatically identifying unusual or unexpected events within dynamic environments. This paper presents a comprehensive benchmark for video anomaly detection and introduces a novel computational model designed to enhance detection accuracy and efficiency. The proposed benchmark aggregates a diverse collection of real-world video datasets, standardizing evaluation protocols and metrics to facilitate consistent comparison across various detection methods. Building upon recent advances in deep learning, our computational model leverages convolutional neural networks (CNNs) combined with temporal sequence modeling to analyze both spatial and temporal patterns inherent in video data. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods, achieving higher precision and recall rates on multiple benchmark datasets. Furthermore, extensive ablation studies highlight the effectiveness of integrating motion cues and context-aware features in detecting subtle anomalies. The findings of this research offer significant contributions to the development of robust, scalable video anomaly detection systems, with potential applications in real-time surveillance, public safety, and automated monitoring. Keywords: anomaly detection, video sequences, benchmark, computational model, deep learning, convolutional neural network, surveillance.
