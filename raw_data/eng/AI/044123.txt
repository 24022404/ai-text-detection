Scene text recognition (STR) is a fundamental task in computer vision, enabling machines to extract textual information from images in complex environments. This paper introduces MASTER, a novel Multi-Aspect Non-local Network designed to advance the accuracy and efficiency of STR. The proposed framework leverages a multi-aspect attention mechanism that captures both local and global contextual dependencies within text images, addressing challenges posed by distortions, diverse fonts, and complex backgrounds. MASTER integrates a non-local module to enhance contextual feature learning, enabling more robust character representation without sacrificing computational efficiency. Extensive experiments were conducted on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating that MASTER achieves superior performance compared to state-of-the-art STR methods in terms of recognition accuracy and speed. The results highlight the model's ability to generalize across diverse scene text conditions. This research contributes a new architecture that balances high accuracy with computational practicality, paving the way for more reliable text reading in real-world applications such as automated document analysis, navigation systems, and assistive technologies. Key keywords: scene text recognition, deep learning, non-local network, attention mechanism, image understanding, MASTER.
