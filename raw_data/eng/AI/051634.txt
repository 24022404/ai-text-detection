This paper addresses the widespread challenge of enhancing the quality of images captured in low-light conditions, which often suffer from noise, low contrast, and loss of detail. The primary aim is to develop a practical solution suitable for real-time applications, improving the visibility and usability of such images across various domains such as surveillance, autonomous vehicles, and photography.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel low-light image enhancement algorithm that combines advanced deep learning techniques with efficient processing mechanisms. The approach leverages a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture optimized for low-light scenarios, incorporating image denoising, contrast adjustment, and detail enhancement functions. The model is trained on a specially curated dataset of low-light images to achieve optimal performance.

Results/Findings: The proposed method demonstrates superior enhancement capabilities compared to existing state-of-the-art techniques. Quantitative assessments indicate significant improvements in key metrics such as peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM). Additionally, qualitative analyses show clear enhancements in image detail and color fidelity. The system outperforms traditional methods, especially in maintaining natural aesthetics while reducing noise.

Conclusion/Implications: Our approach offers a robust and efficient solution to the problem of low-light image enhancement, with potential applications in areas demanding real-time processing. The contributions of this research not only advance current methodologies but also provide practical benefits for technologies dependent on quality image capture under suboptimal lighting. Future work may involve adapting the model for specific use cases, such as night-time surveillance systems or enhancement in consumer-grade cameras.

Keywords: low-light image enhancement, convolutional neural network, real-time processing, image denoising, contrast adjustment.
