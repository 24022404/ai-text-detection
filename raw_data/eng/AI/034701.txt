The increasing demand for machine learning models trained on decentralized datasets necessitates privacy-preserving training techniques. This paper introduces SecDD, a novel and efficient method for securely training neural networks on remote, distributed data. SecDD leverages homomorphic encryption and differential privacy to protect sensitive data during the training process. Our approach minimizes communication overhead by employing a secure aggregation protocol with reduced computational complexity. We evaluate SecDD on benchmark datasets, demonstrating its ability to achieve comparable accuracy to traditional training methods while providing strong privacy guarantees. Experimental results show that SecDD reduces training time by up to 30% compared to existing secure training frameworks, making it a practical solution for real-world applications. The key contributions of this work are a new secure aggregation scheme, a comprehensive analysis of the privacy-accuracy trade-off, and an efficient implementation of remote neural network training. Keywords: Secure Training, Federated Learning, Homomorphic Encryption, Differential Privacy, Neural Networks, Distributed Computing.
