**Abstract**

This paper presents a novel approach for time series analysis using symbolic analysis-based reduced order Markov modeling. The primary objective is to address the challenges of computational complexity and data interpretation in large-scale, high-dimensional time series data. The proposed methodology first transforms raw time series into symbolic sequences, effectively capturing the essential dynamics while minimizing noise and redundancy. Subsequently, a reduced order Markov model is constructed to succinctly represent the temporal dependencies within the symbolic data. Experimental evaluations were conducted on both synthetic and real-world datasets to assess the accuracy, scalability, and computational efficiency of the proposed model. Results demonstrate that our approach achieves comparable or superior predictive performance compared to traditional full-order Markov models, while significantly reducing the model size and computational requirements. Furthermore, the symbolic representation enhances interpretability, making the model particularly suitable for applications in anomaly detection, forecasting, and complex system monitoring. The research contributes an efficient and interpretable framework for time series modeling, offering scalable solutions for data mining, pattern recognition, and advanced time series analytics. Key keywords: time series analysis, symbolic analysis, Markov modeling, reduced order models, temporal patterns, anomaly detection, data mining.
