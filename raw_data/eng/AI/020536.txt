When q equals 2 and k equals 4, there exist exactly 16 unique de Bruijn sequences, all of which are presented in figure 2.3. The process of encoding these sequences requires generating one that satisfies a specific set of constraints. Importantly, the problem of finding a suitable de Bruijn sequence can be transformed into the problem of identifying an Eulerian cycle within a corresponding de Bruijn graph. This equivalence not only provides a systematic approach for the construction of de Bruijn sequences but also highlights the deep connection between graph theory and sequence generation, making it a powerful concept in combinatorics and information theory.
