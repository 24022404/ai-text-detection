Facial Action Unit (AU) intensity estimation is a critical task in affective computing, enabling more nuanced understanding of human emotions and behaviors. This paper presents a transfer learning approach to heatmap regression for robust and accurate estimation of AU intensities from facial images. The proposed method leverages deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) pre-trained on large-scale face datasets to extract meaningful feature representations, which are then fine-tuned for the AU intensity estimation task. Heatmap regression is employed to localize and quantify the intensities of multiple AUs simultaneously, improving precision over traditional regression methods. Experimental results demonstrate that our transfer learning framework substantially outperforms baseline models in terms of both accuracy and generalization, particularly when labeled data is scarce. Key findings indicate that the adoption of heatmap-based outputs enables more detailed spatial interpretation of AU intensities, while transfer learning significantly reduces the need for extensive training samples. This work contributes an effective solution for AU intensity estimation, with potential applications in automated facial expression analysis, human-computer interaction, and behavioral research. Keywords: Action Unit intensity, heatmap regression, transfer learning, facial expression analysis, convolutional neural networks.
