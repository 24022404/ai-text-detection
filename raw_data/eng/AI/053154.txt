Abstract:

Accurate crowd counting in high-density scenes remains a challenging task due to severe occlusions, scale variations, and complex backgrounds. This paper introduces a novel approach that leverages Segmentation Guided Attention Networks (SGAN) in conjunction with a curriculum loss strategy to address these challenges. The proposed model integrates a segmentation module to generate spatial attention maps, effectively highlighting regions of interest and suppressing background noise. This segmentation-guided attention mechanism is seamlessly coupled with a convolutional backbone for density map estimation. Furthermore, we introduce curriculum loss, a progressive training scheme that adaptively adjusts the learning emphasis according to the complexity of crowd regions, facilitating robust model convergence. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art approaches in both accuracy and localization of counts, particularly in highly congested scenarios. The presented framework offers significant improvements in both robustness and interpretability for crowd analysis tasks. Our contributions lay the foundation for more reliable crowd management systems and have broad applications in public safety, urban planning, and event monitoring. Keywords: crowd counting, attention networks, segmentation, curriculum learning, density estimation, deep learning.
