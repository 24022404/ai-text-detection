This paper presents a novel strategy to enhance learning on small datasets by moving beyond traditional fine-tuning methods. In the era where data scarcity remains a challenge in various domains, our research introduces a modular approach that addresses the limitations of conventional fine-tuning techniques in machine learning models.

Methods/Approach: We propose a modular learning framework that decomposes the learning process into distinct, interchangeable units. This approach allows for reusable modules that can be applied across different tasks, leveraging pre-trained models in a more dynamic and efficient manner. The framework is designed to optimize module selection and integration, thereby maximizing adaptation to new, smaller datasets.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that our modular approach significantly outperforms standard fine-tuning methods in scenarios with limited data. The adaptability of the framework was tested across multiple domains, revealing improvements in performance metrics, reduced training times, and enhanced capacity for knowledge transfer. Comparisons with traditional approaches highlight the efficacy of our model in maintaining high levels of accuracy, even with minimal data input.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a robust alternative to fine-tuning by introducing a modular learning approach that not only adapts effectively to small datasets but also offers scalability and versatility for various applications. The implications of this work are far-reaching, impacting fields such as healthcare, where data is often scarce, and proving beneficial for rapid development cycles in AI-driven products. By enhancing the learning efficiency on small datasets, our modular strategy opens new pathways for innovation in machine learning and artificial intelligence.

Keywords: small data, modular learning, fine-tuning, machine learning models, knowledge transfer, adaptability, data scarcity.
