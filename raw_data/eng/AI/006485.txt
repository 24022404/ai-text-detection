The increasing prevalence of video data necessitates efficient methods for video understanding. This paper addresses the challenge of adapting the Vision Transformer (ViT) architecture, which has shown remarkable success in image recognition, to the more complex domain of video. We introduce a novel spatio-temporal transformer model that efficiently encodes video data by treating a video as a sequence of frame-level patch embeddings. Our approach, named Video Transformer (VidTr), reduces the quadratic computational complexity inherent in standard transformer models by incorporating a factorized attention mechanism, separating spatial and temporal attention. VidTr significantly outperforms existing 3D convolutional neural networks and inflated 2D networks on benchmark video action recognition datasets, including Kinetics-400 and Something-Something V2, while requiring fewer computational resources. Specifically, VidTr achieves state-of-the-art accuracy with a reduced parameter count and faster inference time. These results demonstrate the potential of transformer-based architectures for video understanding and provide a foundation for future research in efficient video representation learning. Keywords: Video Transformer, Action Recognition, Vision Transformer, Spatio-Temporal Modeling, Attention Mechanism, Deep Learning.
