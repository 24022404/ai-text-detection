Machine Learning algorithms are generally divided into four main categories: Supervised learning, Unsupervised learning, Semi-supervised learning, and Reinforcement learning. However, certain classifications might exclude Semi-supervised or Reinforcement learning. Among these, Supervised learning is distinguished by its ability to predict outcomes for new data by utilizing known pairs of inputs and associated outcomes, as illustrated in Figure 2.3. This approach plays a significant role in many practical applications where labeled data is available.
