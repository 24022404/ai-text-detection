This paper investigates exponential inequalities for nonstationary Markov chains, providing refined bounds on the tail probabilities of sums of functions of the chain. The primary objective is to establish concentration inequalities that hold under weaker conditions than those typically required for stationary Markov chains. Our approach utilizes a novel combination of martingale techniques and coupling arguments, tailored to address the challenges posed by the nonstationary nature of the chain. We derive new exponential inequalities that explicitly account for the time-varying properties of the Markov chain. The results demonstrate significant improvements over existing bounds, particularly when applied to chains with rapidly changing dynamics. These inequalities are applicable to a wide range of problems, including performance analysis of time-varying communication networks and adaptive control systems. Key words: Nonstationary Markov chains, exponential inequalities, concentration inequalities, martingale methods, coupling.
