Camera localization is a critical task in computer vision, enabling applications such as augmented reality, robotics, and autonomous navigation. This paper introduces *AtLoc*, a novel attention-guided framework for accurate and robust camera localization. The proposed approach leverages attention mechanisms to enhance feature extraction and improve the model's ability to focus on salient regions in the input images, thereby addressing challenges such as occlusions and dynamic environments. AtLoc integrates a deep neural network architecture with a self-attention module, enabling it to adaptively weigh spatial features and improve pose estimation accuracy. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that AtLoc outperforms existing methods in terms of both precision and robustness, particularly in complex scenes with varying lighting conditions and occlusions. The results highlight the effectiveness of attention mechanisms in improving camera localization performance. This work contributes to the field by providing a scalable and efficient solution for real-world applications, with potential implications for augmented reality systems, autonomous vehicles, and robotics.  

**Keywords**: camera localization, attention mechanisms, pose estimation, deep learning, computer vision, augmented reality.
