This paper addresses the challenge of interpreting complex models on high-dimensional data, a crucial aspect of trustworthy artificial intelligence. Our objective is to develop a human-interpretable model explainability framework that provides insights into the decision-making process of machine learning models. We propose a novel approach that combines feature attribution methods with dimensionality reduction techniques to identify relevant features and their contributions to model predictions. Our method is evaluated on several high-dimensional datasets, demonstrating its ability to provide accurate and interpretable explanations. The results show that our framework outperforms existing explainability methods in terms of fidelity and understandability. This research contributes to the development of more transparent and reliable machine learning models, with potential applications in areas such as healthcare, finance, and computer vision. Key aspects of our work include model interpretability, feature attribution, dimensionality reduction, and explainable AI, making it a significant step towards increasing trust in AI systems.
