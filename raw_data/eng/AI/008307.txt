Deep Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have shown promise in various graph-related tasks, but their training remains challenging due to issues like over-smoothing and vanishing gradients. This paper introduces a novel search strategy to automatically discover effective deep GNN architectures. Our approach employs a differentiable architecture search method, optimizing both the network topology and node embedding propagation mechanisms. We utilize a carefully designed search space that incorporates skip connections and different aggregation functions to mitigate over-smoothing. The discovered architectures are evaluated on benchmark datasets for node classification and graph classification, demonstrating significant performance improvements compared to manually designed deep GNNs and automatically searched shallow GNNs. The results indicate that our search strategy effectively identifies deep GNNs that are robust to over-smoothing and capable of capturing complex graph dependencies. Our work offers a practical approach to designing high-performing deep GNNs, contributing to the advancement of graph representation learning.

Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Deep Learning, Neural Architecture Search, Graph Representation Learning, Over-smoothing.
