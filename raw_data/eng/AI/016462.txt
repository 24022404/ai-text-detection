Image quantization, a crucial compression technique, often introduces visually disturbing artifacts. This paper introduces a novel image de-quantization method leveraging generative models as learned priors to mitigate these artifacts and enhance image quality. Our approach employs a conditional generative adversarial network (GAN), trained to map quantized images to their high-quality counterparts. Specifically, the generator learns a mapping from the quantized image to a refined image, guided by the discriminator which distinguishes between de-quantized and real images. We investigate the use of both vanilla GANs and Wasserstein GANs with gradient penalty (WGAN-GP) to stabilize training and improve the quality of generated images. Experimental results demonstrate that our generative model-based approach significantly reduces quantization artifacts, achieving superior Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR) and Structural Similarity Index Measure (SSIM) compared to traditional de-quantization techniques and other deep learning-based methods. Furthermore, subjective evaluations confirm the improved visual quality of de-quantized images. This work highlights the potential of generative models as effective priors for image restoration tasks, offering a promising direction for enhancing compressed image quality.

Keywords: Image de-quantization, Generative Adversarial Networks, GAN, Image restoration, Image compression, Artifact removal.
