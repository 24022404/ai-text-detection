This paper introduces a novel hybrid modeling approach for simulating the human cardiovascular system, combining the strengths of both data-driven and physics-based methodologies. The proposed method leverages Neural Functional Mock-up Units (NeuralFMUs) to represent complex cardiovascular components, allowing for seamless integration of neural networks within a traditional lumped-parameter model. This hybrid architecture captures intricate physiological dynamics while maintaining computational efficiency. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach through simulations of systemic arterial pressure and flow, comparing the results against clinical data and established cardiovascular models. The NeuralFMU-based hybrid model exhibits improved accuracy and robustness in capturing individual-specific cardiovascular responses compared to purely physics-based models. This framework provides a powerful tool for personalized cardiovascular simulations, with potential applications in virtual clinical trials, medical device design, and patient-specific risk assessment. Keywords: Cardiovascular System, Hybrid Modeling, NeuralFMU, System Identification, Physiological Simulation, Neural Networks.
