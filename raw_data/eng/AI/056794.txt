This paper proposes a novel approach to spectral kernel learning, leveraging convolutional neural networks to improve the efficiency and accuracy of kernel estimation. The objective is to develop a flexible and adaptive framework for learning spectral kernels from data, enabling enhanced performance in various applications such as image and signal processing. Our method, Convolutional Spectral Kernel Learning, utilizes a convolutional architecture to learn spatially-aware kernel functions, which are then used to compute the spectral kernel. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming traditional kernel learning methods in terms of computational efficiency and accuracy. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of incorporating spatial information into spectral kernel learning, leading to improved performance in real-world applications. This work contributes to the advancement of kernel learning and has potential implications for various fields, including computer vision, signal processing, and machine learning, with relevant keywords including convolutional neural networks, spectral kernel learning, and adaptive kernel estimation.
