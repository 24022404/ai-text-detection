This paper addresses the challenge of integrating end-to-end image processing and perception systems, focusing on the impact of "dirty pixels"—noisy, corrupted, or low-quality image data—on downstream tasks such as object detection and scene understanding. Traditional pipelines often treat image preprocessing and perception as separate stages, leading to inefficiencies and suboptimal performance. To overcome this, we propose a unified framework that jointly optimizes image enhancement and perception tasks using a deep learning-based approach. Our method leverages a novel multi-task neural network architecture that simultaneously denoises, enhances, and interprets images, ensuring robust performance even under challenging conditions. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate significant improvements in both image quality and perception accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods. The proposed framework achieves a 15% increase in object detection precision and a 20% reduction in computational latency, making it suitable for real-time applications. This work contributes to the field by bridging the gap between image processing and perception, offering a scalable solution for applications in autonomous systems, medical imaging, and surveillance. Key innovations include the integration of adaptive noise suppression and task-aware feature extraction, which enhance the system's ability to handle diverse and imperfect visual data. Keywords: image processing, perception, dirty pixels, deep learning, multi-task learning, real-time systems.
