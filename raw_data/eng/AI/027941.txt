Neural Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs) have emerged as a powerful framework for modeling continuous-depth neural networks. However, training these models remains computationally challenging, often relying on first-order optimization methods. This paper introduces a novel second-order optimizer tailored for Neural ODEs, leveraging adjoint sensitivity analysis for efficient gradient computation. Our approach incorporates second-order information to accelerate convergence and improve the stability of training, particularly in scenarios with stiff or ill-conditioned ODE systems. We develop a memory-efficient implementation of the second-order updates, addressing the high memory requirements typically associated with these methods. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our second-order Neural ODE optimizer achieves significant speedups and improved generalization performance compared to state-of-the-art first-order methods, while maintaining comparable memory footprints. This work contributes a practical and efficient optimization technique for Neural ODEs, opening avenues for exploring deeper and more complex continuous-depth models.

**Keywords:** Neural ODE, Optimization, Second-Order Methods, Deep Learning, Adjoint Sensitivity Analysis.
