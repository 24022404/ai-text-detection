This paper addresses the challenge of accurate and robust depth prediction from single images, a crucial task for applications like autonomous navigation and 3D scene understanding. We propose a novel deep learning architecture, leveraging Fully Convolutional Residual Networks (FCRNs), to enhance depth estimation accuracy. Our FCRN incorporates deeper residual blocks and skip connections to effectively capture both local and global contextual information, mitigating the vanishing gradient problem associated with increased network depth. We also introduce a refined loss function that combines pixel-wise depth differences with structural similarity metrics to preserve fine details and sharp boundaries in the predicted depth maps. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including KITTI and Make3D, demonstrate that our proposed FCRN architecture achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of depth accuracy and visual quality. The improved depth prediction contributes to better scene understanding and facilitates more reliable performance in downstream applications. Keywords: Depth Prediction, Fully Convolutional Networks, Residual Networks, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Scene Understanding.
