This paper addresses the challenge of accurate and robust object detection in multiview settings, where variations in viewpoint and complex scene conditions often degrade performance. We propose the Shadow Transformer, a novel transformer-based architecture tailored for integrating multiview features while effectively modeling spatial and contextual dependencies across all views. To enhance the consistency and diversity of training data, we introduce View-Coherent Data Augmentation (VCDA), a new augmentation technique that maintains geometric and photometric coherence across views, thereby improving generalization and reducing overfitting. Extensive experiments on standard multiview detection benchmarks demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of detection accuracy and robustness, especially in scenarios with challenging lighting and occlusions. Ablation studies further validate the individual contributions of the Shadow Transformer and VCDA. This research provides a significant advance in multiview detection, with potential applications in autonomous systems, surveillance, and 3D scene understanding. Key keywords: multiview detection, transformer, data augmentation, spatial coherence, object detection, 3D reconstruction.
