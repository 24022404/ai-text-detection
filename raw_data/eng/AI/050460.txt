This paper explores the concept of fine-tuning in meta-learning, with the objective of enhancing the adaptability and performance of artificial intelligence (AI) models. We propose a novel approach that leverages fine-tuning to facilitate effective meta-learning, enabling models to learn from a few examples and adapt to new tasks efficiently. Our method utilizes a transformer-based architecture and incorporates a unique fine-tuning strategy that allows for rapid adaptation to new tasks. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing meta-learning methods, achieving state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of fine-tuning in meta-learning, enabling AI models to learn and adapt more effectively. This study contributes to the development of more efficient and adaptable AI systems, with potential applications in areas such as natural language processing, computer vision, and robotics. Key keywords: meta-learning, fine-tuning, transformer, AI models, adaptability, few-shot learning.
