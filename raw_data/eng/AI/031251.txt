This paper introduces CNN-Cert, an efficient framework for formally certifying the robustness of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) against adversarial attacks. The objective is to provide provable guarantees on the network's behavior within a specified input perturbation range. CNN-Cert leverages a novel combination of linear relaxation and interval bound propagation techniques, significantly reducing the computational overhead associated with robustness verification. We present a new algorithm for tightening the linear relaxations within convolutional layers, leading to tighter robustness certificates. Experimental results on standard benchmark datasets demonstrate that CNN-Cert achieves significantly improved certification accuracy and speed compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. Specifically, CNN-Cert provides up to a 50% improvement in certified accuracy for comparable computational cost. The framework facilitates the deployment of CNNs in safety-critical applications where provable robustness is essential. Keywords: Convolutional Neural Networks, Robustness Certification, Adversarial Attacks, Formal Verification, Linear Relaxation, Interval Bound Propagation.
