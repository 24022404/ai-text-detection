Segmenting glass-like objects in digital images presents unique challenges due to their transparency, ambiguous boundaries, and complex interactions with light. This paper proposes an enhanced boundary learning framework tailored for accurate segmentation of glass-like objects. The proposed method combines a multi-scale convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture with a novel boundary-aware loss function designed to emphasize and preserve fine boundary details. Additionally, we introduce a specialized data augmentation strategy that simulates lighting variations and reflections commonly observed in glass objects, improving the model’s robustness. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing state-of-the-art segmentation methods in terms of both boundary accuracy and overall segmentation quality. Qualitative and quantitative analyses highlight significant improvements in distinguishing subtle edges and translucent regions compared to traditional edge-detection and segmentation techniques. The proposed framework has potential applications in robotics, augmented reality, and industrial automation, where precise detection of transparent and reflective materials is critical. Keywords: glass object segmentation, boundary learning, convolutional neural networks, transparency, image processing, computer vision.
