This paper addresses the problem of safely exploring unknown linear systems to identify their dynamics. The objective is to design an exploration strategy that balances the need for accurate identification with the requirement for safety, ensuring that the system remains within specified constraints during the exploration process. We propose a robust optimization approach that formulates the exploration problem as a sequence of convex optimization problems, incorporating uncertainty models and safety constraints. Our method, which we term "Safe Exploration for Linear Systems" (SELS), uses a combination of robust linear programming and model predictive control to generate safe and informative input sequences. The results show that SELS achieves accurate system identification while satisfying safety constraints, outperforming existing methods in terms of identification accuracy and safety violation rates. The key contributions of this work include the development of a robust optimization framework for safe exploration and the demonstration of its effectiveness in identifying linear systems. This research has important implications for control and robotics applications, where safe exploration is crucial for learning and adaptation. Key keywords: safe exploration, linear systems, robust optimization, model predictive control, system identification.
