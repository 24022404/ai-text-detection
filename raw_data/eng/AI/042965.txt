Understanding the contributions of individual features to model predictions is critical for interpretability and decision-making in machine learning. This paper addresses the challenge of accurately and robustly computing Shapley Values, a widely used method for explaining predictions by attributing importance to input variables. We propose a novel approach that enhances the accuracy of Shapley Value estimation while ensuring robustness against noise and data perturbations. Our method leverages advanced sampling techniques and optimization strategies to reduce computational complexity without sacrificing precision. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach outperforms existing methods in terms of both accuracy and stability across diverse datasets and models. Furthermore, we introduce a mechanism to focus on locally important variables, enabling more granular insights into model behavior. The implications of this work extend to applications in healthcare, finance, and other domains where interpretable and reliable explanations are essential. This research contributes to the growing field of explainable AI by providing a scalable and robust framework for Shapley Value computation, paving the way for more transparent and trustworthy machine learning systems.  

**Keywords**: Shapley Values, explainable AI, feature importance, interpretability, machine learning, local explanations.
