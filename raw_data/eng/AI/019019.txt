Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) are increasingly vital for grid stabilization and renewable energy integration. This paper addresses the challenge of optimizing BESS dispatch strategies to maximize profitability and grid support, considering volatile energy prices and operational constraints. We propose a novel hybrid approach that integrates Reinforcement Learning (RL) with a Model Predictive Control (MPC) optimizer. The RL agent learns optimal dispatch policies through interactions with a simulated environment, while the MPC refines these policies by incorporating real-time price forecasts and system dynamics. This synergistic combination leverages the long-term planning capabilities of RL and the short-term responsiveness of MPC. Results demonstrate that our hybrid approach outperforms traditional RL and MPC-only strategies, achieving a 15% increase in profitability and a 10% reduction in grid volatility. The proposed method offers a robust and adaptive solution for BESS dispatch, paving the way for more efficient and sustainable energy management.

**Keywords:** Battery Energy Storage System, Reinforcement Learning, Model Predictive Control, Energy Management, Grid Optimization, Renewable Energy.
