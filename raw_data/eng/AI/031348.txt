This paper presents a novel dataset for sheep identity recognition, age, and weight estimation, addressing a significant gap in livestock monitoring technologies. The objective is to develop an accurate and efficient system for identifying individual sheep and estimating their age and weight, which can improve farm management and animal welfare. A deep learning approach is employed, utilizing convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to analyze images of sheep and predict their identity, age, and weight. The dataset consists of a large collection of images of sheep with corresponding labels, providing a valuable resource for training and testing machine learning models. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed approach, achieving high accuracy in sheep identity recognition and reasonable estimates of age and weight. The contributions of this research include the creation of a unique dataset and the development of a robust method for sheep identification and estimation, with potential applications in precision livestock farming and animal monitoring systems. Key keywords: sheep identification, age estimation, weight estimation, deep learning, CNNs, livestock monitoring, precision agriculture.
