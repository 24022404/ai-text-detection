This paper presents an innovative approach to 3D point cloud registration using end-to-end learning with virtual correspondences. The objective is to accurately align two point clouds by establishing correspondences between them, a crucial task in various computer vision and robotics applications. Our method leverages a deep neural network to learn a robust representation of point clouds, enabling the establishment of virtual correspondences that facilitate the registration process. The proposed approach employs a novel architecture that combines feature extraction, matching, and transformation estimation into a single, unified framework. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our method, outperforming state-of-the-art registration techniques in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The key findings highlight the importance of virtual correspondences in improving registration performance, particularly in scenarios with limited overlap or significant noise. This research contributes to the advancement of 3D point cloud processing, with potential applications in fields such as autonomous driving, scene understanding, and 3D reconstruction. Key keywords: 3D point cloud registration, end-to-end learning, virtual correspondences, deep neural networks, computer vision, robotics.
