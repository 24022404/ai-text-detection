Image restoration, a critical task in computer vision, often demands substantial computational resources. This paper introduces xUnit, a novel spatial activation function designed to enhance the efficiency of image restoration models. Our approach learns a spatially varying activation pattern, allowing the network to selectively focus on relevant image regions while suppressing noise and irrelevant details. This is achieved through a lightweight convolutional module that dynamically modulates the activation thresholds across the spatial domain. We integrate xUnit into several state-of-the-art image restoration architectures and evaluate its performance on benchmark datasets for denoising, deblurring, and super-resolution. Experimental results demonstrate that xUnit significantly reduces computational cost and memory footprint, achieving comparable or superior restoration quality to existing activation functions. The proposed method offers a practical solution for deploying high-performance image restoration models on resource-constrained platforms. Keywords: Image Restoration, Activation Function, Convolutional Neural Networks, Efficiency, Spatial Attention, Deep Learning.
