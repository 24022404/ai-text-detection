This paper presents a novel approach to accelerate the training of large language models by introducing layered gradient accumulation and modular pipeline parallelism. The objective is to overcome the computational bottlenecks and memory constraints associated with training massive models. Our method employs a hierarchical gradient accumulation strategy, allowing for more efficient use of GPU resources, and a modular pipeline parallelism framework, which enables flexible and scalable model partitioning. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach achieves significant speedups and reduced memory usage compared to existing parallelization techniques. The proposed method enables the efficient training of large language models, such as transformer-based architectures, and achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets. The key contributions of this work include improved training efficiency, reduced computational costs, and enhanced scalability, making it an attractive solution for large-scale natural language processing applications. Keywords: large language models, gradient accumulation, pipeline parallelism, distributed training, transformer architectures, AI models.
