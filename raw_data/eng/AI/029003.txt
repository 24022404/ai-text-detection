Fine-grained fashion similarity prediction is a critical task in e-commerce and online retail, where accurately identifying similar items based on subtle attribute variations enhances product discovery and user experience. This paper proposes a novel attribute-specific embedding learning framework to improve the precision of fashion similarity assessment at a detailed level. Our approach leverages deep neural networks to extract multi-level semantic features and integrates attribute-aware modules that disentangle visual cues along dimensions such as color, pattern, texture, and shape. By optimizing embeddings in an attribute-specific manner, the model learns to differentiate even closely related fashion items with fine visual distinctions. We evaluate the proposed method on benchmark fashion datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in similarity prediction accuracy compared to baseline models employing generic or category-based embeddings. The experiments show that our method offers superior retrieval performance, particularly in challenging cases involving minor attribute variations. The results indicate that embedding learning guided by attribute information contributes to more interpretable and relevant similarity matches. This research advances the state-of-the-art in fashion image retrieval and item recommendation, with practical implications for intelligent product search, personalized suggestions, and enhanced user engagement in fashion platforms. Keywords: fine-grained similarity, attribute-specific embedding, deep learning, fashion image retrieval, product recommendation.
