Image captioning aims to automatically generate descriptive text for images. Existing approaches often lack fine-grained control over the generated content, leading to captions that omit important details or include irrelevant information. This paper introduces a novel approach to image captioning that leverages Abstract Scene Graphs (ASGs) to provide fine-grained control over the generated narrative. Our method uses a graph-to-sequence model that conditions caption generation on an ASG, allowing users to specify desired objects, attributes, and relationships. This enables control over the level of detail and focus of the caption. We demonstrate that our model significantly improves caption accuracy and relevance compared to state-of-the-art methods, particularly in scenarios requiring specific content control. Experiments on benchmark datasets show that our approach generates more informative and controllable captions, offering a promising direction for applications requiring targeted image descriptions. Keywords: Image Captioning, Scene Graphs, Controlled Generation, Graph-to-Sequence, Artificial Intelligence.
