Object-aware feature aggregation has emerged as a promising strategy for enhancing the accuracy and robustness of video object detection. This paper introduces a novel object-aware feature aggregation framework designed to leverage temporal and spatial information more effectively across video frames. The proposed approach identifies and aggregates features from semantically consistent object regions throughout video sequences, thereby reducing the impact of occlusion, motion blur, and appearance variation. Our method employs an attention-based feature alignment mechanism that dynamically assigns aggregation weights based on object-level correspondence, ensuring that the most relevant temporal information contributes to each detection. Extensive experiments on benchmark video object detection datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of both mean Average Precision (mAP) and detection stability. The results highlight improved handling of challenging video scenarios and demonstrate significant reductions in false positives and missed detections. This work provides new insights into object-centric temporal modeling, offering a scalable and effective tool for real-time video analysis applications such as surveillance, autonomous driving, and human-computer interaction. Key contributions include the introduction of a dynamic object-aware aggregation scheme and comprehensive evaluations validating its effectiveness. Keywords: video object detection, feature aggregation, attention mechanism, temporal modeling, object tracking.
