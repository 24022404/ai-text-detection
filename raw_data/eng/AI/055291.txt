The advent of blockchain technology introduced a transparent and tamper-resistant ledger that guarantees data integrity and immutability. Originating from Nakamoto’s pioneering work, blockchain rapidly moved past its initial use in cryptocurrencies and found valuable applications in diverse sectors such as finance, supply chain management, and healthcare. Its decentralized design promotes greater transparency, boosts operational efficiency, and fosters trust among participants, empowering organizations to adopt innovative solutions and develop new business models across various industries.
