This paper introduces AAG (Auxiliary Augmentation with GNT-Xent Loss), a novel self-supervised representation learning framework designed to enhance feature extraction and generalization in deep learning models. The primary objective of this work is to address the limitations of existing self-supervised methods by leveraging auxiliary data augmentations and a generalized normalized temperature-scaled cross-entropy (GNT-Xent) loss function. The proposed approach employs a multi-view augmentation strategy, where auxiliary augmentations are applied to input data to generate diverse representations, enabling the model to capture more robust and discriminative features. The GNT-Xent loss function is introduced to improve the stability and convergence of the training process by normalizing the temperature scaling and optimizing the contrastive learning objective. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that AAG achieves state-of-the-art performance in downstream tasks such as image classification and object detection, outperforming existing self-supervised methods by a significant margin. The framework also exhibits strong generalization capabilities across various domains, making it suitable for real-world applications with limited labeled data. This research contributes to the advancement of self-supervised learning by providing a scalable and efficient solution for representation learning, with potential applications in computer vision, natural language processing, and beyond.  

**Keywords**: self-supervised learning, representation learning, data augmentation, contrastive learning, GNT-Xent loss, deep learning.
