Semi-supervised learning leverages both labeled and unlabeled data to improve classification accuracy, addressing the challenge of limited labeled data availability. This paper introduces CycleCluster, a novel clustering regularization technique designed to enhance deep semi-supervised classification models. CycleCluster enforces consistency between cluster assignments across different perturbations of the input data, promoting robust and meaningful cluster structures. The approach involves training a deep neural network classifier alongside a clustering module, penalizing inconsistencies in cluster assignments generated from differently augmented versions of the same unlabeled data. We evaluate CycleCluster on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in classification accuracy compared to existing semi-supervised learning methods. Specifically, CycleCluster exhibits enhanced performance on datasets with complex data distributions. These results highlight the effectiveness of CycleCluster in learning more discriminative features and improving the generalization capabilities of deep semi-supervised classification models. CycleCluster offers a promising direction for semi-supervised learning by effectively leveraging unlabeled data through robust clustering regularization. Keywords: Semi-Supervised Learning, Clustering, Regularization, Deep Learning, Classification, Data Augmentation.
