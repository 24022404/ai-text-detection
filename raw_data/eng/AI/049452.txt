This paper presents a novel approach to image segmentation of cross-country scenes captured in the infrared (IR) spectrum. The objective is to accurately identify and delineate distinct regions within IR images, such as vegetation, terrain, and water bodies, which is crucial for applications like environmental monitoring and autonomous navigation. Our method combines a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture with a conditional random field (CRF) post-processing step to enhance segmentation accuracy, particularly at object boundaries. The CNN is trained on a custom-annotated dataset of IR cross-country images, incorporating data augmentation techniques to improve robustness. Results demonstrate that our approach achieves superior segmentation performance compared to traditional methods and other deep learning models, with an average Intersection over Union (IoU) of 87% on the test dataset. The proposed method effectively addresses challenges unique to IR imagery, such as low contrast and varying illumination. Key contributions include a novel CNN-CRF architecture tailored for IR image segmentation and a comprehensive evaluation on challenging cross-country scenes. Keywords: Image Segmentation, Infrared Imagery, Convolutional Neural Networks, Conditional Random Fields, Remote Sensing, Computer Vision.
