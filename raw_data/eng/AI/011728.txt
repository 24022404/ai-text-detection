This paper proposes a novel approach to facial expression recognition by integrating Squeeze and Excitation (SE) blocks into Vision Transformers (ViT). The objective is to enhance the model's ability to capture subtle facial features and improve recognition accuracy. Our method, dubbed SE-ViT, leverages the attention mechanism of ViT and the feature recalibration capability of SE blocks to selectively emphasize important facial regions. We evaluate SE-ViT on several benchmark datasets, achieving state-of-the-art performance and demonstrating significant improvements over existing ViT-based models. The results show that SE-ViT can effectively recognize facial expressions with high accuracy, outperforming other methods in terms of precision, recall, and F1-score. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and efficient facial expression recognition systems, with potential applications in human-computer interaction, affective computing, and social robotics. Key keywords: Vision Transformer, Squeeze and Excitation, Facial Expression Recognition, Deep Learning, Computer Vision.
