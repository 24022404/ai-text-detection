Neural Architecture Search (NAS) automates the design of neural networks, but its computational cost remains a significant challenge. This paper introduces a novel Graph-based NAS approach that leverages operation embeddings to efficiently explore the architecture search space. Our method constructs a directed acyclic graph where nodes represent operations and edges define connections, enabling a flexible and expressive representation of neural architectures. We learn continuous embeddings for each operation type and use these embeddings to predict the performance of unseen architectures, guiding the search towards promising candidates. This approach significantly reduces the search cost by evaluating only a fraction of the total search space. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves comparable or superior performance to state-of-the-art NAS algorithms, while substantially decreasing computational requirements. The use of operation embeddings provides a powerful mechanism for knowledge transfer between architectures, accelerating the discovery of optimal network configurations. Our findings highlight the potential of graph-based NAS with operation embeddings as a practical and efficient solution for automated neural network design.

Keywords: Neural Architecture Search, Graph Neural Networks, Operation Embeddings, Automated Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Performance Prediction.
