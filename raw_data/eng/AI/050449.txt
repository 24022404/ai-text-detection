Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) have emerged as a powerful tool for modeling and generating time series data across diverse domains. This paper presents a comprehensive survey and novel taxonomy of GAN-based approaches applied to time series analysis. We categorize existing research based on several key aspects, including the architecture of the generator and discriminator, the specific loss functions employed, and the target applications in areas such as finance, healthcare, and anomaly detection. The survey identifies prevalent trends, highlights the strengths and weaknesses of different GAN architectures for time series, and discusses open challenges. A novel taxonomy is proposed to better organize the existing literature and identify gaps for future research. This paper serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners interested in utilizing GANs for time series data generation, forecasting, and related tasks. Keywords: Generative Adversarial Networks, GANs, Time Series, Deep Learning, Survey, Taxonomy, Data Generation, Anomaly Detection, Forecasting.
