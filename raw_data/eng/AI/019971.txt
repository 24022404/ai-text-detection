This paper addresses the challenge of transforming one deep neural network (DNN) architecture into another while retaining performance, facilitating model migration, and improving interoperability among diverse systems. With the rapid development of DNN architectures, seamless transitions between models are crucial for optimizing resources and leveraging advancements in deep learning.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel framework for model transformations, incorporating neural network interpolation techniques along with architectural conversion algorithms. Our approach employs layer-wise mapping strategies and domain adaptation methodologies to guide the transformation process efficiently. By leveraging a combination of parameter sharing and realignment mechanisms, our framework ensures effective transfer of knowledge across different architectures without significant loss in accuracy.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations on diverse benchmarks demonstrate the efficacy of our framework, showcasing successful transformations between various popular DNNs, such as from Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) to Transformer-based models and vice versa. Our results indicate minimal degradation in model performance, maintaining up to 98% of the original accuracy in most scenarios. Furthermore, the transformed models exhibit improved robustness and reduced computational cost, highlighting the practical benefits of our approach.

Conclusion/Implications: The proposed transformation framework advances the capabilities of model interoperability within the realm of deep learning, providing a pathway for researchers and practitioners to adapt existing models to new architectures with ease. The insights from this research open avenues for more flexible deep learning systems, where model evolution can occur dynamically in response to emerging needs and technologies. This work contributes a significant step toward universal compatibility among diverse neural network models, enhancing the utility and lifespan of deep learning solutions.

Keywords: deep neural networks, model transformation, neural network interpolation, architectural conversion, parameter sharing, domain adaptation, CNN, Transformer.
