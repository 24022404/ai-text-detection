This paper aims to enhance the performance of k-means clustering by leveraging disentangled internal representations. The objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of clustering results by learning more informative and compact representations of the data. To achieve this, we propose a novel approach that integrates disentanglement techniques into the k-means clustering framework. Our method utilizes a deep neural network to learn disentangled representations of the input data, which are then used as input to the k-means clustering algorithm. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms traditional k-means clustering and other state-of-the-art methods, particularly in the presence of noisy or high-dimensional data. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of disentangled representations in improving clustering performance, and our approach has significant implications for applications such as data mining, image segmentation, and anomaly detection. The novelty of our work lies in the effective integration of disentanglement techniques with k-means clustering, enabling more accurate and robust clustering results. Key keywords: k-means clustering, disentangled representations, deep learning, unsupervised learning, data mining.
