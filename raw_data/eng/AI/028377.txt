This paper addresses the challenge of reconstructing 3D shapes from 2D sketches, a task with significant applications in computer graphics, animation, and design. We propose a novel approach leveraging multi-view convolutional networks to bridge the gap between 2D sketches and 3D representations. Our method employs a deep learning framework that processes multiple views of a sketch, enabling the network to infer spatial relationships and generate accurate 3D models. The architecture integrates convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to extract features from sketch inputs and a 3D reconstruction module to predict volumetric outputs. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of reconstruction accuracy and computational efficiency. The system achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets, with improved handling of complex shapes and occlusions. This research contributes to the field by introducing a scalable and robust solution for sketch-based 3D modeling, with potential applications in virtual reality, gaming, and industrial design. Keywords: 3D reconstruction, sketch-based modeling, multi-view convolutional networks, deep learning, computer vision.
