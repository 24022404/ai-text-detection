Instance segmentation aims to accurately differentiate and delineate individual objects within an image, yet achieving precise boundary localization remains a key challenge. This paper proposes a novel contour-based loss function, termed Contour Loss, built upon k-step Distance Transformation Images (DTIs), to enhance the performance of instance segmentation models. The approach encodes the pixel-wise distance of each object’s contour, enabling the learning process to focus more effectively on boundaries and improve segmentation accuracy. We integrate Contour Loss into popular segmentation architectures and evaluate its impact on standard benchmark datasets. Experimental results demonstrate that our method significantly improves the delineation of object boundaries compared to conventional loss functions, yielding higher Intersection over Union (IoU) and boundary F-1 scores. The proposed technique efficiently addresses issues of boundary ambiguity and instance overlap, contributing to more precise and reliable segmentation outcomes. Our findings suggest that k-step DTI-based Contour Loss is a promising direction for advancing the accuracy of instance segmentation tasks in computer vision applications such as autonomous driving, medical imaging, and robotics.

Keywords: instance segmentation, contour loss, distance transformation image, boundary localization, k-step, computer vision, deep learning.
