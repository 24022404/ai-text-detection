The objective of this research is to address the challenge of multi-view clustering by developing a novel consistent multiple graph embedding framework. Multi-view clustering is critical for effectively analyzing data from different perspectives, yet traditional approaches often struggle with consistency and efficiency. Our proposed method leverages the power of consistent graph embeddings to integrate multiple views into a unified representation, enabling enhanced clustering performance. The approach relies on constructing a set of graph embeddings for each view and then aligning these into a consistent multi-view representation using advanced embedding techniques. Key findings indicate that this method not only improves clustering accuracy but also offers superior results in terms of computational efficiency compared to state-of-the-art algorithms. Our results show its applicability to a range of domains that rely on multi-view data, such as image recognition, social network analysis, and bioinformatics. Conclusively, this research contributes to the field by providing a robust framework for consistent multi-view clustering, opening avenues for more dynamic data integration and analysis. Key keywords include multi-view clustering, graph embeddings, data integration, and clustering performance.
