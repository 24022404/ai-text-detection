Document Layout Analysis (DLA) aims to automatically identify and categorize the different structural elements within a document image. This paper addresses the limitations of existing DLA systems, which often struggle with complex or degraded layouts. We propose a novel human-in-the-loop approach that integrates human expertise with automated analysis to improve DLA accuracy and robustness. Our system incorporates a user interface for correcting and refining initial layout predictions made by a convolutional neural network. We introduce an adaptive learning mechanism that leverages human feedback to continuously update and improve the underlying model. Experimental results on a diverse set of document images demonstrate a significant improvement in layout segmentation accuracy compared to fully automated methods. The integration of human input enhances the system's ability to handle challenging layouts, leading to more reliable and accurate document analysis. This work contributes a practical framework for human-AI collaboration in document understanding, with potential applications in digital archiving, information retrieval, and automated document processing. Keywords: Document Layout Analysis, Human-in-the-Loop, Convolutional Neural Networks, Active Learning, Document Understanding.
