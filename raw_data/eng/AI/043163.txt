This paper introduces Seglearn, a novel Python package designed for learning sequences and time series data. The primary objective of Seglearn is to provide a comprehensive and user-friendly framework for segmenting, feature learning, and modeling complex sequential data. To achieve this, Seglearn employs a combination of machine learning algorithms and statistical techniques, including change point detection, regression, and clustering. The package is built on top of popular Python libraries, ensuring seamless integration and scalability. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of Seglearn in various applications, including activity recognition, anomaly detection, and forecasting. Compared to existing libraries, Seglearn offers improved performance, flexibility, and interpretability, making it an attractive solution for researchers and practitioners working with sequential data. Key features of Seglearn include automated feature engineering, robust handling of missing values, and support for multiple data formats. By providing a unified and efficient framework for sequence learning, Seglearn contributes to the advancement of time series analysis and enables new applications in fields such as IoT, finance, and healthcare, with relevant keywords including time series analysis, sequence learning, Python package, machine learning, and data science.
