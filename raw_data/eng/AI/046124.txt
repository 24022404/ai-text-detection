This paper addresses the challenge of identifying long-term trends in time series forecasting, a crucial aspect of decision-making in various fields. Our objective is to develop an innovative approach using Self-Organizing Maps (SOM) to improve the accuracy of long-term predictions. We propose a methodology that leverages the capabilities of SOM to identify complex patterns and relationships in time series data, enabling the extraction of underlying trends. Our approach involves training SOM on historical data to create a topology-preserving map that captures the essential characteristics of the time series. The results show that our SOM-based method outperforms traditional forecasting techniques in identifying long-term trends, achieving higher accuracy and robustness. The key findings of this research contribute to the advancement of time series forecasting, offering a novel and effective solution for applications such as financial forecasting, climate modeling, and resource planning. By highlighting the potential of SOM in time series analysis, this study paves the way for further research in this area, with implications for improved decision-making and strategic planning. Key keywords: Time Series Forecasting, Self-Organizing Maps, Long-Term Trends, Pattern Recognition, Predictive Modeling.
