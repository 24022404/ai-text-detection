Deep reinforcement learning (DRL) has achieved significant advances in solving complex decision-making tasks, particularly in the domain of continuous control. This paper presents a comprehensive benchmarking study of state-of-the-art DRL algorithms for continuous control environments. Our objective is to systematically evaluate and compare the performance, stability, and sample efficiency of prominent DRL methods, including Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG), Twin Delayed DDPG (TD3), and Soft Actor-Critic (SAC), across standard simulated benchmarks such as MuJoCo and Roboschool. Using a unified experimental framework, we analyze learning curves, policy robustness, and sensitivity to hyperparameters. The results indicate notable differences in convergence speed, reliability, and overall control performance among the evaluated algorithms. Furthermore, we discuss insights into the strengths and limitations of each approach, providing guidance for both researchers and practitioners in selecting suitable DRL algorithms for specific continuous control applications. Our findings contribute to a deeper understanding of DRL for continuous action spaces and establish baseline results to foster future advancements in the field. Keywords: deep reinforcement learning, continuous control, benchmarking, policy gradient, sample efficiency, MuJoCo, DDPG, SAC, TD3.
