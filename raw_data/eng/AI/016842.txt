This paper introduces a novel approach to channel-wise early stopping for neural networks, eliminating the need for a separate validation set. By leveraging NNK polytope interpolation, our method enables efficient and effective stopping criteria without incurring additional computational costs. The proposed approach utilizes a geometric interpretation of neural network training, allowing for the identification of optimal stopping points on a per-channel basis. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our method, showcasing improved generalization performance and reduced overfitting compared to traditional early stopping techniques. The key contributions of this research include a unique interpolation-based framework for channel-wise early stopping, as well as insights into the geometric properties of neural network training dynamics. Our work has significant implications for deep learning applications, particularly in scenarios where validation data is scarce or expensive to obtain. Relevant keywords: neural networks, early stopping, polytope interpolation, channel-wise regularization, deep learning optimization.
