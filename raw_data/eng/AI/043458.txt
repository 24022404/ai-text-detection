This study introduces a novel approach, DCT-Mask, which leverages the Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) for efficient instance segmentation. The research addresses the limitation of current mask representation methods in scaling and computational efficiency, aiming to enhance segmentation performance across diverse datasets.

Methods/Approach: DCT-Mask employs the Discrete Cosine Transform to represent object masks in a compressed and computationally efficient manner. By transforming the segmented masks into a series of DCT coefficients, the method reduces the complexity of mask representation, facilitating faster processing and reduced memory usage. The DCT-Mask is integrated into existing deep learning frameworks for instance segmentation, allowing seamless application with neural networks.

Results/Findings: Experiments conducted on standard benchmark datasets demonstrate that DCT-Mask offers a significant improvement in segmentation accuracy and processing speed compared to traditional mask representation methods. The use of DCT allows for better handling of intricate object boundaries with reduced computational overhead. Quantitative results show enhanced performance metrics, including precision and recall, across various challenging scenarios.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of DCT-Mask offers a breakthrough in instance segmentation by providing a more efficient and effective means of mask representation. This advancement opens new possibilities for real-time applications and large-scale image analysis, contributing to the fields of computer vision and artificial intelligence. The proposed method's ability to integrate with existing segmentation models ensures its potential for widespread adoption and impact on future segmentation tasks.

Keywords: DCT-Mask, Discrete Cosine Transform, instance segmentation, mask representation, computer vision, efficiency.
