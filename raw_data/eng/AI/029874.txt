This paper addresses the challenge of accurately recognizing human actions in video sequences, a crucial task in fields such as video surveillance, human-computer interaction, and sports analytics. The goal is to improve action recognition performance by focusing on effective feature extraction and temporal dynamics.

Methods: We introduce Hierarchical Multi-scale Attention Networks, a novel neural network architecture designed to capture both spatial and temporal relationships in video data. Our approach leverages a hierarchical structure to model different temporal scales, utilizing multi-scale attention mechanisms to dynamically focus on salient features across frames. This architecture efficiently integrates spatial and temporal cues, enhancing feature representation through attention-based feature refinement.

Results: Experiments conducted on standard action recognition datasets, including UCF101 and HMDB51, demonstrate superior performance of our proposed model compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. Our approach achieves significant improvements in classification accuracy, showcasing its ability to effectively discriminate between complex actions. The incorporation of multi-scale attention aids in capturing finer details and contextual information, thereby enhancing the model's robustness.

Conclusion: The Hierarchical Multi-scale Attention Networks present a significant advancement in action recognition, offering a scalable solution for effectively modeling intricate dynamics in video data. This work contributes to the field by providing a robust architecture that integrates spatial-temporal attention, paving the way for more accurate and computationally efficient action recognition systems. Potential applications span various domains, including automated monitoring systems, enhanced user interfaces, and advanced content analysis in multimedia environments.

Keywords: action recognition, multi-scale attention, hierarchical networks, video analysis, temporal dynamics.
