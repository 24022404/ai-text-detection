This paper proposes a novel ranking-based, balanced loss function that unifies classification and localization in object detection tasks. The objective is to address the inherent imbalance between classification and localization errors, which can lead to suboptimal performance in modern object detection systems. Our approach employs a ranking-based mechanism to weigh the importance of each sample, allowing for a more balanced optimization of both classification and localization losses. We utilize a combination of a ranking-based loss function and a balanced weighting scheme to adaptively adjust the importance of each task. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art object detection algorithms, achieving significant improvements in both classification accuracy and localization precision. The key contributions of this research include a unified loss function that seamlessly integrates classification and localization, and a ranking-based weighting scheme that adaptively balances the two tasks. Our approach has important implications for a wide range of applications, including autonomous driving, surveillance, and medical image analysis, where accurate object detection is crucial. Key keywords: object detection, ranking-based loss function, balanced optimization, classification, localization, computer vision, deep learning.
