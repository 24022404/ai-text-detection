Data augmentation is a crucial technique for improving the generalization ability of deep learning models, especially when training data is limited. While existing methods have shown promise, many lack the ability to selectively focus on the most relevant parts of an image during augmentation, potentially introducing noise or irrelevant features. This paper introduces SaliencyMix, a novel data augmentation strategy that leverages saliency maps to guide the mixing of image regions. SaliencyMix identifies the salient regions of input images and mixes them proportionally, encouraging the model to focus on the most informative features. We evaluate SaliencyMix on several benchmark datasets for image classification, including CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet. The experimental results demonstrate that SaliencyMix consistently outperforms state-of-the-art data augmentation techniques, leading to improved accuracy and robustness. Specifically, SaliencyMix achieves significant gains in top-1 accuracy on ImageNet, while requiring minimal computational overhead. These findings highlight the effectiveness of saliency guidance in data augmentation, offering a more targeted and efficient approach to regularization.
Keywords: Data Augmentation, Saliency Map, Deep Learning, Regularization, Image Classification.
