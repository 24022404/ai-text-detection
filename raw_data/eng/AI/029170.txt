This paper presents GIMP-ML, a novel set of Python plugins designed to integrate computer vision models into GIMP, a popular open-source raster graphics editor. The objective is to leverage machine learning and computer vision techniques to enhance image editing capabilities, making it more efficient and accessible for users. Our approach involves developing plugins that utilize pre-trained models for tasks such as image segmentation, object detection, and image filtering. The plugins are built using Python and utilize popular computer vision libraries, ensuring ease of use and flexibility. Our results show that GIMP-ML enables users to perform complex image editing tasks with improved accuracy and speed. The plugins have been tested on various image datasets, demonstrating their effectiveness in real-world scenarios. This research contributes to the field of computer vision and image editing, providing a unique solution for integrating machine learning models into GIMP. The GIMP-ML plugins have potential applications in graphic design, photography, and digital art, making image editing more efficient and creative. Key keywords: GIMP, computer vision, machine learning, Python plugins, image editing.
