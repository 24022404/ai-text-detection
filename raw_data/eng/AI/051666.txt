Spatiotemporal Graph Neural Networks (ST-GNNs) have demonstrated remarkable success in modeling dynamic systems across diverse applications, from traffic forecasting to human motion prediction. However, their vulnerability to adversarial attacks, particularly those targeting specific spatial regions, remains largely unexplored. This paper introduces a novel spatially focused attack strategy against ST-GNNs. Our method identifies and perturbs the most influential nodes within a network's spatial graph structure to maximize performance degradation in a targeted spatiotemporal region. We develop a perturbation strategy that exploits the graph's adjacency matrix and node features to identify critical nodes and craft adversarial perturbations. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our spatially focused attack significantly outperforms existing global attack strategies, achieving a substantial reduction in prediction accuracy within the targeted areas while minimizing perturbations to other regions. This highlights the susceptibility of ST-GNNs to spatially localized attacks and underscores the need for robust defense mechanisms. Keywords: Spatiotemporal Graph Neural Networks, Adversarial Attacks, Graph Security, Spatial Perturbation, Targeted Attack.
