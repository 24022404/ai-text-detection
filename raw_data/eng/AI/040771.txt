Neural network compression is crucial for deploying deep learning models on resource-constrained devices. This paper introduces the Teacher-Class Network (TCN), a novel compression mechanism that leverages a teacher-student learning paradigm with a class-aware approach. Unlike traditional knowledge distillation methods, TCN organizes neurons into "classes" based on their activation patterns and trains a smaller student network to mimic the behavior of these classes in the teacher network. This approach promotes more efficient knowledge transfer and allows for greater compression rates. We evaluated TCN on several benchmark datasets, including CIFAR-10 and ImageNet, demonstrating significant reductions in model size and computational cost compared to state-of-the-art compression techniques, while maintaining competitive accuracy. Specifically, our experiments show that TCN can achieve up to a 10x reduction in parameters with minimal performance degradation. The proposed TCN offers a practical solution for deploying complex neural networks on edge devices, making deep learning more accessible in resource-limited environments. Keywords: Neural Network Compression, Knowledge Distillation, Teacher-Student Learning, Model Optimization, Deep Learning.
