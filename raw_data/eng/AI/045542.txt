This paper proposes a novel approach to learning latent graphical models through sequential local learning. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of existing methods by leveraging local dependencies and sequential information. Our approach employs a sequential learning framework, where each node in the graph is learned locally using a subset of relevant variables, and then iteratively updated to capture global dependencies. The method utilizes a combination of probabilistic graphical models and neural networks to learn effective representations of the data. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy and scalability, particularly in cases where the graph structure is complex or the data is high-dimensional. The key contributions of this research include the development of a sequential local learning algorithm, the integration of neural networks with probabilistic graphical models, and the demonstration of improved performance on latent graphical models. This work has significant implications for applications in computer vision, natural language processing, and recommender systems, where latent graphical models are widely used. Key keywords: latent graphical models, sequential learning, local learning, neural networks, probabilistic graphical models.
