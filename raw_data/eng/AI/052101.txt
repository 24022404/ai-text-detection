Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) have shown remarkable progress in image generation, yet their architecture often relies on convolutional layers. This paper introduces TransGAN, a novel GAN architecture that leverages the power of pure transformers in both the generator and discriminator networks. By replacing convolutional layers with self-attention mechanisms, TransGAN offers improved global context modeling and scalability. Our approach demonstrates that a carefully designed transformer-based GAN can achieve state-of-the-art performance on standard image generation benchmarks. Experimental results show that TransGAN not only matches but also surpasses the performance of traditional CNN-based GANs, particularly when scaling up the model size and dataset complexity. We analyze the impact of different transformer configurations on GAN training stability and image quality. This work provides a new direction for GAN research, highlighting the potential of transformers for generative modeling. Keywords: Generative Adversarial Networks, Transformers, Image Generation, Deep Learning, Self-Attention.
