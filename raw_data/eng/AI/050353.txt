The detection of anomalies in large datasets is a critical task across various domains, including manufacturing, finance, and security. Conventional anomaly detection methods often fall short in accurately identifying subtle deviations. This research introduces an innovative Attention Map-guided Two-stage Anomaly Detection framework utilizing hard augmentation to enhance detection accuracy.

Methods/Approach: This study proposes a two-stage approach where attention maps guide the anomaly detection process. In the first stage, attention maps are generated to identify potential areas of interest or concern within a dataset. These maps help to focus the anomaly detection efforts more precisely. In the second stage, we employ a hard augmentation strategy, a data enhancement technique that intentionally introduces challenging variations to train our model. This approach ensures robust feature learning and improves the model's ability to distinguish normal from anomalous patterns.

Results/Findings: Through extensive experimentation, our method demonstrated superior performance over traditional single-stage anomaly detection techniques. The introduction of attention maps significantly enhanced the system's ability to localize anomalies accurately, while hard augmentation improved the system's robustness. This resulted in higher precision and recall rates across various datasets, showcasing the efficacy of this method in practical applications.

Conclusion/Implications: The proposed Attention Map-guided Two-stage Anomaly Detection framework represents a significant advancement in the field of anomaly detection. It not only improves accuracy and robustness but also provides a scalable solution applicable to diverse datasets and industries. This research offers insights into leveraging attention mechanisms and augmentation strategies to address complex anomaly detection challenges effectively.

Keywords: Anomaly detection, attention map, two-stage framework, hard augmentation, robust models, precision, recall.
