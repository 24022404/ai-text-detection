This paper proposes a novel periodicity-based parallel time series prediction algorithm designed for cloud computing environments. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of time series forecasting by leveraging the periodic patterns inherent in many real-world datasets. Our approach utilizes a hybrid model that combines machine learning techniques with parallel processing to analyze and predict large-scale time series data. The algorithm is optimized for cloud computing infrastructure, enabling scalable and rapid processing of big data. Experimental results demonstrate that our algorithm outperforms existing methods in terms of prediction accuracy and computational efficiency. The key findings highlight the importance of incorporating periodicity analysis into time series prediction models, particularly in applications such as financial forecasting, traffic flow prediction, and resource allocation in cloud computing. The proposed algorithm contributes to the development of more accurate and efficient time series prediction systems, with potential applications in various fields, including finance, transportation, and energy management. Key keywords: time series prediction, cloud computing, parallel processing, periodicity analysis, machine learning, big data analytics.
