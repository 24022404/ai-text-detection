This paper introduces a novel hybrid deep learning approach for enhanced classification performance. The proposed method, Hybrid Embedded Deep Stacked Sparse Autoencoder with weighted Linear Probability Product of Discriminants (LPPD) Support Vector Machine (SVM) Ensemble, integrates feature extraction and classification within a unified framework. Initially, a deep stacked sparse autoencoder is employed for unsupervised feature learning, capturing high-level representations from the input data. Subsequently, the learned features are fed into a weighted LPPD SVM ensemble, where multiple SVM classifiers are combined using a product-of-discriminants approach, enhanced with a weighting scheme based on classifier performance. This ensemble strategy improves decision boundary robustness and overall classification accuracy. Experimental results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed hybrid model outperforms existing methods, achieving superior accuracy and generalization capability. Key contributions include the novel integration of deep feature learning with a weighted LPPD SVM ensemble and the demonstrated performance improvements in classification tasks. Keywords: Deep Learning, Stacked Sparse Autoencoder, SVM Ensemble, Classification, Feature Extraction, LPPD, Hybrid Model.
