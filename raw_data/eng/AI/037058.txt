This paper presents YuruGAN, a novel generative model for creating Yuru-Chara mascots using Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) with clustering techniques, designed to effectively utilize small datasets. The objective is to generate diverse and high-quality Yuru-Chara characters, which are popular in Japanese culture, by leveraging the strengths of GANs in image generation and clustering methods to enhance data utilization. Our approach employs a custom GAN architecture that incorporates clustering to better capture the underlying patterns in the limited dataset, allowing for more efficient training and improved generation capabilities. Experimental results demonstrate that YuruGAN outperforms existing methods in generating Yuru-Chara characters, both in terms of visual quality and diversity. The key findings highlight the potential of combining GANs with clustering techniques for small dataset scenarios, contributing to the advancement of generative modeling in character design and related applications. This research has significant implications for the field of computer-generated imagery and character design, offering a novel solution for generating unique and engaging characters with minimal data requirements. Key keywords: Generative Adversarial Networks, GANs, Clustering, Small Dataset, Yuru-Chara, Character Generation, Computer-Generated Imagery.
