This paper presents a novel approach to manga colorization, leveraging generative adversarial networks (GANs) to create vibrant and style-aware colored manga pages. The objective is to develop a system that can automatically colorize manga line arts while preserving the original painting style of the artist. Our method employs a GAN-based architecture that learns to predict colors based on the input line art and a reference painting style image. The network is trained on a large dataset of paired line art and colored manga pages, allowing it to capture the nuances of various painting styles. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming existing colorization methods in terms of visual quality and style consistency. Our system has significant implications for the manga and anime industry, enabling efficient and high-quality colorization of large volumes of line art. Key contributions include the introduction of a style-aware loss function and a novel dataset of paired manga line art and colored pages. Overall, our research contributes to the advancement of generative models for image processing and opens up new possibilities for automated manga colorization, with potential applications in digital art, entertainment, and media production. Keywords: manga colorization, generative adversarial networks, painting style transfer, digital art, image processing.
