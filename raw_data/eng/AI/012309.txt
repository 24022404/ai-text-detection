Optical flow estimation is a fundamental challenge in computer vision, with applications ranging from motion analysis to video processing. This paper addresses the problem of accurately estimating optical flow by focusing on motion feature recovery, which is often hindered by occlusions, complex textures, and fast-moving objects. We propose a novel approach that leverages a hybrid deep learning architecture to recover lost or ambiguous motion features, enhancing the robustness and precision of optical flow estimation. Our method integrates a multi-scale feature extraction module with a motion-aware refinement network, enabling the system to capture both fine-grained and large-scale motion patterns. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency, particularly in challenging scenarios with dynamic scenes and occlusions. The proposed framework not only advances the field of optical flow estimation but also provides a foundation for improving related tasks such as video interpolation, object tracking, and 3D reconstruction. This research highlights the potential of motion feature recovery as a key component in next-generation optical flow algorithms.  

**Keywords**: optical flow, motion feature recovery, deep learning, computer vision, motion analysis, video processing.
