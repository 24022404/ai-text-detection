This paper presents a novel deep generative model designed for fragment-based molecule generation, addressing the challenge of efficiently generating chemically valid and diverse molecular structures. Our approach utilizes a combination of graph neural networks and generative adversarial networks to learn the patterns and relationships within molecular fragments, enabling the generation of novel molecules with desired properties. The model is trained on a large dataset of molecular structures and demonstrates superior performance in generating valid and diverse molecules compared to existing methods. Key findings include the ability to generate molecules with specific functional groups and the capacity to control the generated molecules' physical and chemical properties. The proposed model has significant implications for drug discovery, materials science, and other fields where molecule generation is crucial. By leveraging the power of deep learning, our model provides a valuable tool for researchers and scientists to explore the vast chemical space efficiently. Relevant keywords: deep generative models, fragment-based molecule generation, graph neural networks, generative adversarial networks, molecular structure generation, drug discovery.
