This paper addresses the challenge of efficient policy learning and adaptation in complex environments. The objective is to develop a novel approach that enables agents to learn from experiences and adapt to new situations effectively. We propose an importance weighted policy learning framework that leverages experience replay and weighted sampling to focus on the most informative experiences. Our approach utilizes a combination of model-free and model-based reinforcement learning techniques to improve the efficiency and robustness of policy learning. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing approaches in terms of sample efficiency and adaptability, achieving significant improvements in cumulative rewards and convergence rates. The key contributions of this research include the development of a weighted sampling strategy and the integration of importance weighting into policy learning, enabling more effective adaptation to changing environments. This work has important implications for applications in robotics, autonomous systems, and other areas where efficient policy learning and adaptation are crucial. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, policy learning, importance weighting, experience replay, adaptive control.
