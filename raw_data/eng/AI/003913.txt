This paper proposes a novel approach to signal processing by integrating variational models with Graph Neural Networks (GNNs). The objective is to develop a robust and flexible framework for processing signals on graph-structured data, which is common in various applications such as sensor networks, traffic forecasting, and recommendation systems. Our approach leverages the strengths of both variational inference and GNNs to learn effective representations of signals and capture complex relationships between nodes in the graph. We employ a variational autoencoder (VAE) framework to model the signal distribution, and a GNN to learn the graph topology and node relationships. The results show that our model outperforms existing methods in terms of signal reconstruction and prediction accuracy. The key contributions of this work include the development of a novel variational-GNN framework, which enables efficient and accurate signal processing on graph-structured data. Our approach has significant implications for various applications, including signal processing, graph analysis, and machine learning. Key keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Variational Autoencoders, Signal Processing, Graph-Structured Data.
