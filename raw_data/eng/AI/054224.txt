Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models have shown promising results in scaling model capacity and improving performance across various AI tasks. However, training MoE models remains computationally expensive and time-consuming due to the increased model size and complex routing mechanisms. This paper introduces FastMoE, a novel system designed to accelerate the training of MoE models. FastMoE employs a combination of techniques, including optimized data parallelism, efficient expert dispatching, and communication-avoiding algorithms, to minimize training overhead. We evaluate FastMoE on large-scale language modeling tasks, demonstrating significant speedups compared to existing MoE training frameworks. Results show that FastMoE achieves up to a 3x reduction in training time while maintaining comparable model accuracy. The proposed system enables faster experimentation and deployment of large MoE models, facilitating advancements in various AI domains. Key words: Mixture-of-Experts, distributed training, deep learning, large language models, system optimization.
