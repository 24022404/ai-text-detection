This paper proposes a novel approach to video-based person re-identification, leveraging a Concentrated Multi-Grained Multi-Attention Network (CMGMAN) to enhance the accuracy of identifying individuals across different camera views. The objective is to address the challenges of variations in pose, illumination, and occlusion in video sequences. Our method employs a multi-grained attention mechanism to focus on both local and global features, while a concentrated module helps to refine feature representations. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of CMGMAN, outperforming state-of-the-art methods on benchmark datasets. The key findings highlight the importance of multi-grained attention and feature refinement in video-based person re-identification. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and robust person re-identification systems, with potential applications in surveillance, security, and smart cities. The proposed CMGMAN model showcases innovations in attention mechanisms and feature learning, making it a valuable addition to the field of computer vision and person re-identification. Key keywords: person re-identification, multi-attention network, video analysis, computer vision, deep learning.
