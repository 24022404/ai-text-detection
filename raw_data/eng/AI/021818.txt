This paper addresses the challenge of online initialization and extrinsic spatial-temporal calibration in monocular visual-inertial odometry (VIO) systems. Existing methods often require specific motions or controlled environments for initialization and assume perfect synchronization between the camera and IMU. We propose a novel framework that estimates the initial state, including initial pose, velocity, gravity vector, and IMU biases, and refines the extrinsic spatial and temporal calibration parameters online, without requiring specific motion profiles. Our approach utilizes a tightly-coupled, sliding-window optimization that jointly estimates the VIO state and calibration parameters. We introduce a robust cost function that mitigates the impact of outliers and noisy measurements during the calibration process. Experimental results on both synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that our method achieves accurate and robust online initialization and calibration, leading to improved VIO performance compared to existing approaches. Specifically, our method reduces drift and improves pose accuracy in challenging scenarios with aggressive motions and poor initial calibration. Keywords: Visual-Inertial Odometry, Online Calibration, Initialization, Sensor Fusion, Robotics.
