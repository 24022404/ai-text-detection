Local Climate Zone (LCZ) classification is crucial for urban climate studies and sustainable city planning. This paper addresses the challenge of creating high-quality training datasets for deep learning-based LCZ classification in the context of Korea, characterized by diverse landscapes and complex urban environments. We propose a novel methodology that integrates multi-source geospatial data, including satellite imagery, GIS data, and street view images, coupled with expert knowledge and rigorous quality control measures to generate a reliable training sample set. The approach involves a stratified sampling strategy to ensure balanced representation of different LCZ types and incorporates a comprehensive validation process using independent field surveys and inter-rater reliability analysis. Experimental results demonstrate that deep learning models trained on this dataset achieve significantly higher accuracy compared to those trained on existing, less curated datasets. Specifically, we observe a 15% improvement in overall classification accuracy and enhanced performance in identifying under-represented LCZ classes. This research contributes a valuable, high-quality training resource for LCZ mapping in Korea and offers a transferable framework for developing similar datasets in other regions. Keywords: Local Climate Zone, Deep Learning, Training Data, Urban Climate, Korea, Land Cover Classification.
