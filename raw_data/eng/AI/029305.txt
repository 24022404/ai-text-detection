This paper explores the application of transfer learning and online learning techniques for traffic forecasting, addressing the challenges posed by varying data availability conditions. The objective is to develop robust and adaptable forecasting models that can effectively utilize available data, whether abundant or scarce. We employ a range of transfer learning approaches, including fine-tuning pre-trained models and using domain adaptation techniques, in conjunction with online learning methods that enable real-time updates and adaptation to changing traffic patterns. Our results show that these techniques can significantly improve forecasting accuracy, particularly in situations where data is limited or noisy. Key findings highlight the importance of carefully selecting and tuning transfer learning and online learning parameters to avoid pitfalls such as overfitting and concept drift. The proposed approach has important implications for intelligent transportation systems, contributing to the development of more efficient and responsive traffic management strategies. Our research demonstrates the potential of transfer learning and online learning for traffic forecasting, with applications in areas such as smart cities and autonomous vehicles, and highlights the need for further investigation into the optimal combination of these techniques for real-world traffic forecasting scenarios, including key keywords: traffic forecasting, transfer learning, online learning, intelligent transportation systems, smart cities.
