Reinforcement Learning (RL) has shown remarkable success in various domains, yet modeling object dynamics in complex environments remains a significant challenge. This paper introduces a novel relevance-guided approach to enhance the modeling of object dynamics, enabling more efficient and accurate decision-making in RL tasks. The proposed method leverages a relevance-based filtering mechanism to identify and prioritize critical objects and their interactions within the environment, reducing computational overhead and improving learning efficiency. We employ a hybrid architecture combining attention mechanisms with traditional RL frameworks to dynamically adjust the focus on relevant objects during training. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of convergence speed and task performance across multiple benchmark environments. The findings highlight the importance of relevance-guided modeling in improving RL scalability and adaptability, particularly in environments with high-dimensional state spaces. This work contributes to advancing RL by providing a robust framework for object dynamics modeling, with potential applications in robotics, autonomous systems, and real-time decision-making scenarios.  

**Keywords**: Reinforcement Learning, Object Dynamics, Relevance-Guided Modeling, Attention Mechanisms, Decision-Making, Scalability.
