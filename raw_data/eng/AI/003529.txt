This paper explores the integration of reproducing kernel Banach spaces (RKBS) with neural networks to provide a deeper understanding and potential improvements in their structure and function. By addressing inadequacies in traditional methods, this research aims to enhance the analytical power of neural network models for complex data representations.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel framework that employs RKBS to analyze and interpret neural network architectures. This approach leverages the mathematical properties of RKBS to offer a robust, flexible, and efficient analysis of neural networks. We utilize this framework to develop new algorithms that improve upon the accuracy and interpretability of neural network models.

Results/Findings: Our theoretical analysis demonstrates that integrating RKBS with neural networks leads to enhanced model performance and generalization capabilities. Experimental results across various benchmarks indicate superior accuracy and reduced computational complexity compared to traditional approaches. Furthermore, visualizations derived from our framework provide clearer interpretations of neural network behavior, offering insights into model decision-making processes.

Conclusion/Implications: This research presents a pioneering approach to understanding and improving neural networks using reproducing kernel Banach spaces, delivering significant advancements in model analysis and application. The integration of RKBS provides a promising direction for developing more interpretable and efficient neural network models, with potential applications across diverse areas such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science.

Keywords: neural networks, reproducing kernel Banach spaces, interpretability, model analysis, computational complexity, data science.
