Time series classification is a crucial task in various domains, ranging from finance to healthcare. This paper investigates the application of deep learning models for improved time series classification accuracy. We explore the efficacy of several deep learning architectures, including recurrent neural networks (RNNs), convolutional neural networks (CNNs), and transformer networks, adapting them to effectively capture temporal dependencies and extract relevant features from time series data. A comparative analysis is conducted on benchmark datasets, assessing the performance of each model in terms of accuracy, precision, and F1-score. Experimental results demonstrate that transformer-based models outperform traditional RNN and CNN approaches, achieving state-of-the-art classification accuracy on several datasets. The findings suggest that the attention mechanism inherent in transformer networks allows for more effective modeling of long-range dependencies within time series. This research provides valuable insights into the optimal deep learning architectures for time series classification and highlights the potential of transformer networks for advancing the field. Keywords: Time series classification, deep learning, recurrent neural networks, convolutional neural networks, transformer networks, attention mechanism.
