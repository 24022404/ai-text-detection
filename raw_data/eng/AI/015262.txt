This paper presents a novel approach to assessing aesthetic attributes of images, aiming to bridge the gap between human perception and computational evaluation. Our objective is to develop a robust model that can accurately predict the aesthetic quality of images based on various attributes such as color harmony, composition, and texture. We employ a deep learning-based method, utilizing a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture to extract features from images and a regression model to predict aesthetic scores. Our results show that the proposed model outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods, achieving a significant improvement in prediction accuracy. The key findings of this study highlight the importance of considering multiple aesthetic attributes and demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in evaluating image aesthetics. This research contributes to the field of image analysis and has potential applications in areas such as image retrieval, recommendation systems, and digital art. Key keywords: aesthetic attributes, image assessment, deep learning, CNN, image analysis.
