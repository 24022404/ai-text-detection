This paper introduces tsrobprep, a novel R package designed for robust preprocessing of time series data. The primary objective is to provide a comprehensive and reliable tool for handling missing values, outliers, and non-stationarity in time series, which are common challenges in data analysis. The package employs a combination of statistical methods and machine learning algorithms to identify and correct anomalies, ensuring the quality and integrity of the data. Key features of tsrobprep include robust imputation techniques, outlier detection using density-based methods, and stationarity checks using unit root tests. The package is evaluated on various real-world datasets, demonstrating its effectiveness in improving the accuracy of subsequent time series analysis and modeling tasks. The results show that tsrobprep outperforms existing methods in terms of robustness and flexibility, making it a valuable contribution to the field of time series analysis. Overall, tsrobprep provides a user-friendly and efficient solution for preprocessing time series data, facilitating reliable insights and decision-making in fields such as finance, economics, and environmental science, with key keywords including time series analysis, robust preprocessing, R package, and data quality.
