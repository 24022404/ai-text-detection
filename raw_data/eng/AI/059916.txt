In the rapidly evolving field of adversarial machine learning, robust defenses against adversarial attacks are paramount. This study tackles the challenge of defending neural networks from gradient-based adversarial attacks, which threaten the integrity and reliability of AI systems in critical applications. 

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel minimax defense framework designed to fortify machine learning models against such attacks. Our approach leverages a game-theoretic perspective, where we model the interaction between the attacker and defender as a zero-sum game. By optimizing for the worst-case scenario, our framework utilizes dynamic gradient-based strategies to anticipate and nullify adversarial maneuvers effectively. The minimax defense algorithm is implemented on convolutional neural networks, employing a defensive training process that iterates between adjusting the model parameters and evaluating potential adversarial scenarios.

Results/Findings: Through extensive experimentation on standard benchmarks, including the CIFAR-10 and MNIST datasets, our minimax defense demonstrates significant improvements in model robustness. Our method not only outperforms existing state-of-the-art defenses in terms of accuracy under attack but also reduces vulnerability to a wide range of gradient-based adversarial strategies. Results show an increase in accuracy of up to 10% against strong adversarial examples, showcasing the framework's efficiency.

Conclusion/Implications: The proposed minimax defense framework advances the field of adversarial machine learning by providing a robust, strategic approach to safeguarding AI models. This innovative framework promises increased resilience in security-critical applications, paving the way for further research into adaptive defense mechanisms. Keywords: adversarial attacks, minimax defense, gradient-based methods, neural networks, robust machine learning, AI security.
