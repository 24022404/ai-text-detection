The analysis of correlated multivariate time series is a critical challenge in various domains, including finance, healthcare, and environmental monitoring. This paper proposes a novel deep structural model designed to capture complex dependencies and interactions within such datasets. The model integrates advanced neural network architectures with a structured approach to handle temporal correlations and cross-variable relationships effectively. Specifically, it employs a combination of recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and attention mechanisms to enhance feature extraction and interpretability. Experimental evaluations on real-world datasets demonstrate that the proposed model outperforms existing methods in terms of prediction accuracy and robustness. Key findings reveal significant improvements in capturing long-term dependencies and reducing error rates by up to 15% compared to state-of-the-art baselines. The model's ability to provide interpretable insights into variable interactions further enhances its practical utility. This research contributes to the field by offering a scalable and efficient solution for analyzing correlated multivariate time series, with potential applications in predictive maintenance, anomaly detection, and decision support systems. Keywords: multivariate time series, deep learning, recurrent neural networks, attention mechanisms, temporal dependencies, interpretability.
