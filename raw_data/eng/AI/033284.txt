This paper introduces a novel local active contour model designed for accurate image segmentation, particularly in the presence of intensity inhomogeneity. The proposed model leverages a localized region-based energy functional that adapts dynamically to local intensity variations within the image. Unlike traditional global active contour models, our approach effectively handles images with non-uniform illumination and noise. The method incorporates a kernel-based estimation of local image statistics to drive contour evolution. We employ a level set formulation for efficient and robust implementation. Experimental results on synthetic and real-world images demonstrate that the proposed model outperforms existing active contour methods and other state-of-the-art segmentation techniques in terms of accuracy and robustness to intensity inhomogeneity. The model shows significant improvement in segmenting medical images and other challenging datasets where intensity variations are prevalent. This research contributes a robust and efficient solution for image segmentation, offering a valuable tool for applications in medical imaging, computer vision, and related fields.

Keywords: Image Segmentation, Active Contour, Level Set, Intensity Inhomogeneity, Local Region, Kernel Estimation.
