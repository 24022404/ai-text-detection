This study presents YuruGAN, an innovative approach to generating Yuru-Chara mascots using Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs). Yuru-Chara mascots, known for their cultural and regional significance in Japan, often require creative and distinct design elements. The challenge addressed in this research is generating diverse mascot designs from a limited dataset, a common constraint in specialized creative domains.

Methods/Approach: The proposed methodology incorporates a novel combination of GANs with a clustering technique tailored for small datasets. By leveraging clustering, our approach effectively enhances data utilization, allowing the GAN to learn intricate design features from a limited number of examples. The model architecture is designed to generate high-quality mascot images while preserving unique stylistic elements characteristic of Yuru-Chara designs. 

Results/Findings: The YuruGAN system successfully generates a wide variety of mascot designs, demonstrating high visual fidelity and stylistic accuracy. Comparative analysis with existing GAN models indicates superior performance in terms of diversity and originality of generated mascots. Our approach shows notable improvement in handling small datasets, reducing overfitting and enhancing creativity in image synthesis.

Conclusion/Implications: YuruGAN offers a significant contribution to the field of AI-driven creative design, particularly in the context of limited data availability. This research not only advances the capabilities of GANs in generating culturally relevant mascots but also provides a framework applicable to other creative domains facing similar constraints. Potential applications extend beyond mascot generation, with implications for personalized content creation and cultural artifact replication using AI technologies.

Keywords: YuruGAN, Yuru-Chara, Generative Adversarial Networks, Clustering, Small Dataset, Creative Design, AI-generated Mascots.
