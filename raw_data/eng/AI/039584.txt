This paper introduces a novel automatic face recognition system leveraging Local Fourier-Bessel Features (LFB) for enhanced performance and robustness. The objective is to improve face recognition accuracy, particularly under challenging conditions such as varying illumination and facial expression. Our method utilizes a novel approach for extracting discriminative features from facial images by applying a Fourier-Bessel expansion within localized regions. This captures both frequency and spatial information, leading to a more comprehensive representation of facial characteristics. Experimental results on benchmark face databases, including LFW and FERET, demonstrate that the proposed LFB-based system achieves superior recognition rates compared to traditional methods like PCA and LBP, and comparable performance to state-of-the-art deep learning approaches, while requiring significantly less computational resources. The system exhibits strong robustness against variations in lighting and expression. The findings highlight the potential of Fourier-Bessel features for efficient and accurate face recognition, offering a computationally effective alternative to deep learning models in resource-constrained environments. Key words: Face Recognition, Fourier-Bessel Transform, Feature Extraction, Pattern Recognition, Biometrics.
