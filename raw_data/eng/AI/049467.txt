The objective of this research is to advance the understanding and development of Multiple-Input Multiple-Output (MIMO) channel modes that are transferable in the context of 6G Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) communications. As vehicular networks evolve, providing efficient and reliable communication channels becomes crucial for the deployment of 6G technologies. Our approach utilizes deep learning techniques to identify and learn transferable MIMO channel modes, facilitating enhanced data transmission between vehicles and infrastructure. We employ a convolutional neural network model to analyze the channel state information, enabling the identification of optimal channel configurations capable of adapting to dynamic vehicular environments. The results indicate a significant improvement in channel capacity and reliability compared to traditional static channel models, demonstrating superior performance in highly mobile and diverse scenarios characteristic of V2X communications. Our findings suggest that leveraging deep learning for MIMO channel adaptation can provide more robust and scalable solutions for 6G networks. In conclusion, this research contributes to the field by addressing the challenges of dynamic channel conditions in V2X environments and by proposing a novel method for enhancing V2X communication efficacy using deep learning. Potential applications include improved autonomous driving communication systems and enhanced data exchange protocols within smart city infrastructures. Key keywords include deep learning, MIMO, 6G, V2X communications, channel modes, and transfer learning.
