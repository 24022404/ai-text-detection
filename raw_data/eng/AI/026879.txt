The paper presents Part2Word, a novel framework designed to bridge the gap between 3D point clouds and natural language descriptions by learning a joint embedding space that aligns individual parts of 3D objects with descriptive words. This research addresses the challenge of enabling precise and meaningful interactions between visual and textual modalities, which is critical for advancing tasks such as 3D object retrieval, scene understanding, and robotic manipulation guided by language.

Methods/Approach: The proposed approach employs a multi-modal learning strategy that integrates 3D point cloud data with natural language text. Our method utilizes a part-to-word matching mechanism, where the geometric features of segmented 3D parts are learned to correspond with semantically relevant words. The joint embedding space is trained using a combination of supervised learning with carefully curated datasets and a novel cross-modal attention mechanism to optimize the association between parts and words.

Results/Findings: Experiments demonstrate that Part2Word significantly enhances the correlation between point cloud parts and descriptive language over existing models. Our framework achieves superior performance in benchmarks for tasks such as cross-modal retrieval and language-driven shape manipulation. Comparative results highlight the robustness and precision of our method in aligning visual components with language, yielding a more intuitive human-computer interaction experience.

Conclusion/Implications: Part2Word contributes a pivotal advancement in the integration of linguistic and visual data, facilitating improved cross-modal applications. The joint embedding approach not only paves the way for enhanced multi-modal retrieval systems but also shows potential for applications in fields ranging from virtual reality to assistive technologies. Future work will explore scaling the model for diverse datasets and integrating real-time interaction capabilities.

Keywords: 3D point clouds, natural language processing, joint embedding, cross-modal learning, part-to-word matching, multi-modal retrieval.
