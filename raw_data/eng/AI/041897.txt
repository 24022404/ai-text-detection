Link prediction in N-ary relational data is crucial for uncovering hidden relationships and patterns in complex datasets. This paper introduces a novel approach for link prediction based on a comprehensive relatedness evaluation between entities. Our method leverages a multi-faceted evaluation framework, incorporating structural, semantic, and contextual information to quantify the relatedness of entities involved in N-ary relations. We propose a new relatedness metric that captures the strength and nature of connections, considering both direct and indirect associations. The proposed method is evaluated on several real-world N-ary relational datasets, demonstrating superior performance compared to existing state-of-the-art link prediction techniques. Results show significant improvements in prediction accuracy and recall, particularly in sparse and heterogeneous relational data. The findings highlight the effectiveness of our relatedness evaluation framework in capturing complex dependencies and enhancing link prediction performance. This research contributes a valuable tool for analyzing and predicting relationships in a wide range of domains, including social networks, knowledge graphs, and biological networks. Keywords: Link Prediction, N-ary Relations, Relational Data, Relatedness Evaluation, Knowledge Graphs, Data Mining.
