This paper introduces SlimConv, a novel approach to reducing channel redundancy in convolutional neural networks (CNNs) by leveraging weights flipping. The objective is to improve the efficiency of CNNs by eliminating redundant channels, thereby decreasing computational costs and memory usage. Our method employs a flipping technique to reorganize weights, allowing for the identification and removal of redundant channels. Experimental results demonstrate that SlimConv achieves significant reductions in channel redundancy, resulting in faster inference times and lower memory requirements, while maintaining comparable accuracy to state-of-the-art models. The key findings indicate that SlimConv can reduce the number of channels by up to 30% without compromising performance. This research contributes to the development of more efficient CNN architectures, with potential applications in edge AI, real-time object detection, and image classification. By highlighting the benefits of weights flipping in reducing channel redundancy, SlimConv offers a unique solution for optimizing CNNs, making it an attractive approach for researchers and practitioners in the field of deep learning, computer vision, and artificial intelligence. Key keywords: CNNs, channel redundancy, weights flipping, efficient architectures, deep learning, computer vision.
