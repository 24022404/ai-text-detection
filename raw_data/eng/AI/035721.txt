This paper introduces a novel approach for mining heterogeneous multivariate time-series data to discover meaningful patterns, with a specific application to home health telecare. The objective is to identify correlations and predictive features within diverse streams of data collected from patients in remote monitoring environments. We present a framework that integrates data preprocessing, feature extraction, and pattern recognition techniques tailored to handle the complexities of home health data, including varying sampling rates and data types. Our methodology employs a combination of dynamic time warping for time-series alignment, feature selection using mutual information, and a deep learning model for pattern classification. Experimental results on a real-world home health telecare dataset demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in identifying clinically relevant patterns, such as early indicators of patient deterioration or adherence to medication schedules. The findings offer potential for proactive interventions and personalized care plans, ultimately improving patient outcomes and reducing healthcare costs. Keywords: time-series mining, pattern recognition, heterogeneous data, home health telecare, deep learning, remote monitoring.
