This paper proposes a novel approach to temporal network embedding, leveraging tensor factorization to capture the dynamic evolution of networks over time. The objective is to develop a method that can effectively represent temporal networks in a low-dimensional space, preserving both structural and temporal information. Our approach utilizes a tensor factorization technique to decompose the temporal network into a set of latent factors, which are then used to learn node embeddings. The results show that our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art techniques in terms of link prediction and node classification tasks, demonstrating its ability to accurately capture the temporal dynamics of the network. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a tensor-based framework for temporal network embedding and the development of a scalable and efficient algorithm for factorizing large-scale temporal networks. This work has significant implications for various applications, including social network analysis, traffic forecasting, and recommendation systems, and highlights the importance of incorporating temporal information into network embedding models. Key keywords: temporal network embedding, tensor factorization, dynamic networks, node embeddings, link prediction.
