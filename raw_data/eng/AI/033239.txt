This paper explores the integration of Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) with Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) regions to enhance control system performance in dynamic environments. The primary objective is to address the limitations of traditional LQR methods, which often struggle with nonlinearities and uncertainties in real-world systems. We propose a novel approach that combines the robustness of DRL with the stability guarantees of LQR, leveraging the strengths of both techniques. The method involves partitioning the state space into LQR regions, where DRL agents are trained to optimize control policies within these regions while ensuring stability through LQR-based constraints.  

Our experiments demonstrate that the proposed approach achieves superior performance compared to standalone DRL and LQR methods, particularly in scenarios with high-dimensional state spaces and complex dynamics. The results highlight significant improvements in convergence speed, control accuracy, and system stability. Furthermore, the framework is shown to be adaptable to various applications, including robotics and autonomous systems.  

The key contribution of this work lies in the seamless integration of DRL and LQR, offering a scalable and stable solution for control problems in uncertain environments. This research opens new avenues for applying reinforcement learning in safety-critical systems, where stability and robustness are paramount.  

**Keywords**: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Linear Quadratic Regulator, Control Systems, Stability, Nonlinear Dynamics, Autonomous Systems.
