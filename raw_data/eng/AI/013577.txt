Sequential budgeted learning addresses the challenge of training machine learning models under a limited budget across multiple rounds. This paper introduces a novel approach, Confidence-Budget Matching (CBM), that optimizes resource allocation in each round by aligning the model's confidence in its predictions with the assigned budget. CBM leverages uncertainty estimation to identify instances where the model is most uncertain, focusing the budget on these high-value samples to maximize learning efficiency. We propose a theoretical framework for analyzing the convergence properties of CBM and derive regret bounds that demonstrate its performance guarantees. Empirical evaluations on several benchmark datasets show that CBM significantly outperforms existing budgeted learning strategies, achieving higher accuracy and faster convergence with the same budget. Furthermore, CBM demonstrates robustness to noisy data and varying budget constraints. This research contributes a practical and theoretically sound approach to sequential budgeted learning, enhancing the efficiency and effectiveness of machine learning in resource-constrained environments.

Keywords: Budgeted Learning, Active Learning, Uncertainty Estimation, Resource Allocation, Machine Learning, Sequential Learning.
