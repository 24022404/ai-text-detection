This paper addresses the challenge of permutation synchronization, a fundamental problem in computer vision and data alignment, where the goal is to consistently align multiple permutations across datasets. We propose a novel probabilistic approach leveraging the Riemannian geometry of the Birkhoff polytope, the space of doubly stochastic matrices. By modeling permutations as points on this manifold, our method incorporates geometric constraints to ensure consistency and robustness in synchronization. We introduce an efficient optimization framework that exploits the Riemannian structure, enabling scalable and accurate solutions even for large-scale problems. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of both accuracy and computational efficiency, particularly in scenarios with noisy or incomplete data. The proposed method not only advances the theoretical understanding of permutation synchronization but also offers practical applications in multi-object matching, 3D reconstruction, and graph alignment. This work highlights the potential of Riemannian optimization in solving combinatorial problems, providing a new direction for future research in synchronization and manifold-based learning.  

**Keywords**: Permutation synchronization, Birkhoff polytope, Riemannian optimization, doubly stochastic matrices, combinatorial optimization, multi-object matching.
