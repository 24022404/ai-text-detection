This paper presents a novel hybrid approach for enhancing classification performance by integrating a Deep Stacked Sparse Autoencoder (DSSAE) with a weighted Local Preserving Projection Discriminant (wLPPD) Support Vector Machine (SVM) ensemble. The primary objective is to address the challenges of high-dimensional data classification, where traditional methods often struggle with feature extraction and discriminative representation. The proposed method leverages the unsupervised learning capabilities of DSSAE to extract hierarchical and sparse features from raw data, followed by the wLPPD SVM ensemble, which enhances discriminative power by preserving local structures and optimizing class separability. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the hybrid model achieves superior classification accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods, with significant improvements in handling complex and noisy data. The key contributions of this work include the integration of deep feature learning with a weighted ensemble framework, offering a robust solution for high-dimensional classification tasks. This research has potential applications in areas such as image recognition, bioinformatics, and anomaly detection, where accurate and efficient classification is critical. Keywords: Deep Stacked Sparse Autoencoder, wLPPD SVM, ensemble learning, feature extraction, high-dimensional classification.
