This research addresses the challenge of efficiently identifying relevant regions within video content by leveraging human eye fixation data. The primary aim is to develop a system that can mimic human attention patterns to enhance video analysis and understanding.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel model that integrates a machine learning framework with eye-tracking data to learn attention patterns in video sequences. By utilizing a combination of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and attention mechanisms, the model is trained to predict fixation points, allowing it to focus on significant segments of a video that typically draw human attention.

Results/Findings: The proposed system demonstrates superior performance in accurately identifying relevant regions across diverse video datasets. Our model significantly reduces processing time and computational load by prioritizing areas of interest, while maintaining high analytical accuracy. Benchmark comparisons reveal that our approach outperforms existing methods in both accuracy and efficiency, indicating robust generalization across various video contents.

Conclusion/Implications: This study presents a significant advancement in video content analysis by utilizing eye fixation data to drive attention models. The integration of human attention patterns into video processing offers potential applications in fields such as video surveillance, content curation, and user experience enhancement. Our findings underscore the importance of biologically inspired models in improving machine perception and interaction with multimedia data.

Keywords: Video analysis, Eye fixations, Attention mechanism, Convolutional neural networks, Human attention pattern, Machine learning, Video content understanding.
