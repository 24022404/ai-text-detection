This paper addresses the challenge of multi-view clustering by proposing a novel framework for consistent multiple graph embedding. The objective is to integrate multiple views of a dataset into a unified representation, enabling more accurate and robust clustering results. Our approach employs a deep learning-based method to learn a consistent embedding across different views, leveraging the complementary information from each view to enhance the clustering performance. The key to our method is a specially designed loss function that encourages consistency between the embeddings of different views, while also preserving the intrinsic structure of each view. Experimental results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate the superiority of our approach over state-of-the-art multi-view clustering methods, with significant improvements in clustering accuracy and robustness. The proposed framework has important implications for various applications, including data mining, computer vision, and network analysis, where multi-view data is prevalent. Our contributions include a novel consistent multiple graph embedding framework, a new loss function for promoting consistency, and a comprehensive evaluation of the approach on benchmark datasets, highlighting its potential for advancing the field of multi-view clustering. Key keywords: multi-view clustering, graph embedding, deep learning, consistent embedding, clustering accuracy.
