Ensuring the robustness of machine learning models against adversarial perturbations is a critical challenge in deploying AI systems in real-world applications. This paper introduces a novel framework for *automatic perturbation analysis* that enables scalable and certified robustness verification for deep neural networks. The proposed approach leverages a combination of symbolic interval propagation and optimization techniques to efficiently compute tight bounds on model outputs under input perturbations. Unlike existing methods, our framework is designed to handle a wide range of perturbation types, including norm-bounded and geometric transformations, while maintaining computational efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate that the method achieves state-of-the-art performance in certified robustness across multiple benchmark datasets, with significant improvements in both accuracy and scalability compared to prior approaches. Furthermore, the framework extends beyond robustness verification, offering insights into model interpretability and enabling the design of more robust training algorithms. This work contributes a practical and versatile tool for enhancing the reliability of AI systems in adversarial environments, with potential applications in security-critical domains such as autonomous driving and cybersecurity.  

**Keywords**: certified robustness, adversarial robustness, perturbation analysis, deep learning, symbolic interval propagation, optimization, AI security.
