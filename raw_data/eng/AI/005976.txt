Deep reinforcement learning (DRL) agents often operate as black boxes, hindering understanding and trust. This paper addresses the challenge of agent transparency by introducing Action-Conditional β-VAE (ACβ-VAE), a novel approach for learning interpretable representations of agent behavior in DRL. The objective is to improve the efficacy of governing agents by providing insights into their decision-making processes. Our method integrates a variational autoencoder conditioned on the agent's actions to disentangle latent factors influencing state transitions. This allows for the reconstruction of state representations based on specific actions, facilitating the identification of key factors driving agent behavior. Experiments demonstrate that ACβ-VAE effectively learns disentangled representations, enabling accurate prediction of future states and identification of action-relevant features. Furthermore, we show how these representations can be utilized for debugging and improving agent policies. The results indicate that ACβ-VAE offers a significant step towards transparent DRL, contributing to more effective agent governance and fostering trust in AI systems. Keywords: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Transparency, Interpretability, Variational Autoencoder, Agent Governance, Disentangled Representation.
