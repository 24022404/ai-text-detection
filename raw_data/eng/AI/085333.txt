This paper introduces *GAMesh*, a novel framework for guided and augmented meshing tailored for deep point networks. The objective of this research is to address the challenges of generating high-quality 3D meshes from sparse or unstructured point clouds, a critical task in applications such as 3D reconstruction, computer vision, and augmented reality. Traditional methods often struggle with preserving geometric details and maintaining computational efficiency. To overcome these limitations, *GAMesh* integrates a deep learning-based approach with a guided meshing pipeline, leveraging both geometric priors and learned features to enhance mesh generation. The proposed method employs a hybrid architecture that combines point cloud feature extraction with a differentiable mesh refinement module, enabling end-to-end training and optimization. Experimental results demonstrate that *GAMesh* outperforms existing techniques in terms of mesh accuracy, detail preservation, and computational efficiency, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets. The framework’s ability to handle noisy and incomplete point clouds further highlights its robustness. The implications of this work extend to various domains, including autonomous systems, medical imaging, and virtual reality, where accurate and efficient 3D mesh generation is essential. *GAMesh* represents a significant advancement in deep point network-based meshing, offering a scalable and adaptable solution for real-world applications.  

**Keywords**: 3D meshing, point clouds, deep learning, 3D reconstruction, geometric priors, differentiable refinement.
