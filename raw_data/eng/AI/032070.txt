This paper introduces ASM2TV, a novel adaptive semi-supervised multi-task multi-view learning framework designed to enhance the performance of machine learning models in complex, dynamic environments. The objective of this research is to develop a flexible and efficient approach that can leverage multiple views and tasks to improve model generalizability and adaptability. ASM2TV employs a unique combination of semi-supervised learning and multi-task learning, incorporating an adaptive weighting mechanism to balance the importance of different views and tasks. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of ASM2TV, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in various multi-view and multi-task learning benchmarks. The key findings highlight the framework's ability to adapt to new tasks and views, reducing the need for extensive labeled data and improving overall model robustness. The proposed framework has significant implications for real-world applications, including computer vision, natural language processing, and recommender systems, where multi-view and multi-task learning are essential. By introducing an adaptive and semi-supervised approach, ASM2TV contributes to the advancement of multi-task multi-view learning, enabling more efficient and effective model development in diverse domains, with relevant keywords including multi-task learning, multi-view learning, semi-supervised learning, and adaptive weighting.
