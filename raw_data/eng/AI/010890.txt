Reconstructing accurate 3D face shapes from diverse data sources, denoted as 'X,' remains a challenging problem in computer vision. This paper introduces a novel deep learning framework, 3D Face From X (3DFX), capable of learning face shape representations from heterogeneous inputs such as single images, sparse landmarks, or even textual descriptions. 3DFX leverages a multi-modal encoder to extract relevant features from each input source, followed by a shared decoder that reconstructs a consistent 3D face mesh. A key innovation is the introduction of an adaptive fusion module that dynamically weights the contribution of each modality based on its reliability and relevance to the final shape prediction. Experimental results on publicly available datasets demonstrate that 3DFX outperforms state-of-the-art methods, especially in scenarios with limited or noisy input data. The framework exhibits strong generalization capabilities and provides a robust solution for applications such as facial animation, recognition, and personalized avatars. Key words: 3D face reconstruction, multi-modal learning, deep learning, computer vision, face shape estimation.
