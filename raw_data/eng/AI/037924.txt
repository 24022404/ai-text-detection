Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have shown promise in skeleton-based action recognition by modeling the human skeleton as a graph. However, existing methods primarily focus on pairwise relationships between joints, neglecting higher-order dependencies crucial for understanding complex actions. This paper introduces a novel approach for fusing higher-order features within GNNs to enhance action recognition accuracy. Our method constructs hypergraphs to capture multi-joint relationships and propagates information using a hypergraph convolutional layer, effectively integrating these higher-order features into the GNN framework. We evaluate our approach on two benchmark datasets, NTU RGB+D and Kinetics Skeleton, demonstrating significant improvements over state-of-the-art methods. Specifically, our model achieves superior performance by effectively capturing complex spatio-temporal dependencies in human actions. This research highlights the importance of higher-order feature integration in GNNs for skeleton-based action recognition and provides a promising direction for future research. Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Action Recognition, Skeleton Data, Hypergraphs, Higher-Order Features, Deep Learning.
