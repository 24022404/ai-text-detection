Video action classification benefits from diverse modalities, such as RGB frames and optical flow. However, effectively integrating these modalities remains a challenge. This paper introduces a novel mutual modality learning framework designed to enhance video action classification by fostering synergistic learning between modalities. Our approach leverages a co-attention mechanism to dynamically weigh and combine features from different modalities, enabling the model to focus on the most relevant information for action recognition. Furthermore, we introduce a modality interaction module that explicitly models the relationships between modalities, promoting information exchange and reducing redundancy. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including HMDB51 and UCF101, demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing approaches. The proposed framework enhances the robustness and accuracy of video action classification by effectively exploiting complementary information across modalities. Key words: Action Classification, Video Understanding, Multi-modal Learning, Co-attention, Deep Learning.
