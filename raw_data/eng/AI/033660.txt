This paper presents the development of a Globally-scalable Automated Target Recognition (GATR) system, designed to accurately identify and classify targets in complex environments. The objective of GATR is to address the limitations of existing target recognition systems, which often struggle with scalability and accuracy in real-world scenarios. Our approach leverages a combination of deep learning algorithms and high-performance computing to enable rapid and reliable target detection. The GATR system utilizes a novel architecture that integrates convolutional neural networks (CNNs) with advanced feature extraction techniques, allowing for efficient processing of large datasets. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of GATR, achieving high accuracy and robustness in various environments. The key contributions of this research include the development of a scalable and adaptable target recognition framework, which can be applied to a wide range of applications, including surveillance, autonomous systems, and defense. The GATR system has significant implications for enhancing situational awareness and decision-making in complex operational environments, and its globally-scalable design enables seamless integration with existing infrastructure. Key keywords: automated target recognition, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, scalable computing, computer vision.
