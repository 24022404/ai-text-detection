This paper introduces a novel reversible image merging technique designed to enhance the performance of low-level machine vision tasks. The objective is to create a single, information-rich image representation from multiple input images while ensuring perfect reconstruction of the originals. Our approach utilizes a carefully designed wavelet-based decomposition followed by a merging process that preserves essential image features and minimizes information loss. The reversibility is achieved through a corresponding demerging process based on the inverse wavelet transform and carefully tracked merging parameters. Experimental results demonstrate that the merged image representation significantly improves the accuracy and robustness of tasks such as edge detection and feature extraction compared to using individual input images or irreversible merging methods. Furthermore, the perfect reconstruction capability allows for subsequent analysis of the original images without any degradation. This reversible image merging technique offers a valuable tool for various applications, including multi-sensor data fusion, medical imaging, and remote sensing.

Keywords: Image Merging, Reversible Transformation, Wavelet Decomposition, Machine Vision, Feature Extraction, Edge Detection.
