Accurate estimation of articulated 3D hand-object pose is crucial for enabling natural human-computer interaction. Existing methods often struggle with limited real-world data and the complexity of hand-object occlusions and interdependencies. This paper introduces ArtiBoost, a novel framework that significantly improves the accuracy of 3D hand-object pose estimation by leveraging online exploration and synthesis. ArtiBoost employs an active exploration strategy to identify informative viewpoints and poses where the current pose estimator performs poorly. Using these viewpoints, we synthesize new training data with automatically generated, physically plausible hand-object interactions. This synthesized data is then used to continuously boost the performance of the pose estimator in an online fashion. Our experiments on challenging benchmark datasets demonstrate that ArtiBoost achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods by a significant margin, especially in handling occlusions and complex interactions. The results highlight the effectiveness of online exploration and synthesis for improving the robustness and accuracy of articulated 3D hand-object pose estimation. Keywords: 3D Hand-Object Pose Estimation, Articulated Pose, Online Learning, Data Synthesis, Active Exploration, Computer Vision.
