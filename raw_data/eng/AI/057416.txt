This paper proposes a novel approach to action detection, introducing a three-branch framework that leverages richer features to improve accuracy and efficiency. The objective is to address the challenges of existing action detection methods, which often struggle with complex scenes and variable action durations. Our approach combines a spatial branch, a temporal branch, and a semantic branch to extract comprehensive features from video data. The spatial branch focuses on object detection and localization, while the temporal branch models action dynamics and duration. The semantic branch incorporates high-level semantic information to disambiguate actions with similar visual features. Experimental results demonstrate that our three-branch framework outperforms state-of-the-art methods on benchmark datasets, achieving significant improvements in action detection accuracy and recall. The key contributions of this research include the design of a multi-branch architecture, the integration of richer features, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in real-world action detection scenarios. This work has important implications for applications such as video surveillance, human-computer interaction, and autonomous systems, and highlights the potential of multi-branch models for action detection and recognition tasks, with relevant keywords including action detection, computer vision, deep learning, and video analysis.
