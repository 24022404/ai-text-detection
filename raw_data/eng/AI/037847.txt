Gesture recognition is crucial for human-computer interaction, yet effectively integrating multi-rate and multi-modal temporal information remains a significant challenge. This paper introduces a novel approach, searching Multi-Rate and Multi-Modal Temporal Enhanced Networks (MMTEN), to automatically discover optimal network architectures for gesture recognition. MMTEN employs a differentiable neural architecture search strategy to explore diverse temporal processing modules tailored to different modalities and sampling rates. These modules learn enhanced temporal representations that are fused to improve recognition accuracy. We evaluate MMTEN on benchmark datasets, demonstrating state-of-the-art performance compared to existing methods. The results show that MMTEN effectively captures intricate temporal dynamics across modalities, leading to more robust and accurate gesture recognition. This automated network design offers a promising direction for developing adaptive and high-performing gesture recognition systems. Keywords: Gesture Recognition, Multi-Modal, Multi-Rate, Temporal Networks, Neural Architecture Search, Human-Computer Interaction.
