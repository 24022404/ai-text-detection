Optical flow estimation, a fundamental problem in computer vision, often suffers from inaccuracies in regions with occlusions, motion discontinuities, and textureless areas. This paper introduces an adaptive mixed norm regularization approach to enhance the robustness and accuracy of optical flow estimation. The proposed method leverages a combination of L1 and L2 norms to effectively handle both small and large displacement scenarios. Specifically, an adaptive weighting strategy is employed to dynamically adjust the influence of each norm based on local image characteristics. This adaptive scheme promotes sparsity in the flow field where appropriate, while also allowing for smooth flow transitions in continuous regions. Experimental results on standard benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance, exhibiting improved accuracy, particularly in challenging scenarios with significant motion and occlusions, compared to existing optical flow algorithms. The adaptive mixed norm approach provides a more flexible and robust framework for optical flow computation, offering potential benefits in various applications, including video analysis, autonomous navigation, and robotic vision.

Keywords: Optical Flow, Adaptive Regularization, Mixed Norm, L1 Norm, L2 Norm, Motion Estimation, Computer Vision.
