This paper proposes a novel approach to early time series classification using deep convolutional networks, with a focus on earliness-aware learning. The objective is to develop a model that can accurately classify time series data as early as possible, while minimizing unnecessary computations and improving real-time decision-making. Our approach utilizes a customized convolutional neural network architecture that incorporates earliness-aware loss functions and early exit mechanisms, allowing the model to adaptively determine the optimal classification time. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art early classification techniques, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of earliness-aware deep learning frameworks and the development of novel evaluation metrics for early time series classification. This study has important implications for real-time applications, such as anomaly detection, predictive maintenance, and healthcare monitoring, where early and accurate classification is crucial. Key keywords: early time series classification, deep convolutional networks, earliness-aware learning, real-time decision-making, anomaly detection.
