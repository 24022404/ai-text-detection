The accuracy and reliability of breast ultrasound diagnosis are critical for effective patient management. Traditional AI models often face limitations in sensitivity and specificity when used independently. This research aims to enhance diagnostic precision by integrating multiple AI techniques, thereby providing a more reliable AI solution for breast ultrasound diagnosis.

Methods/Approach: We developed a novel multi-AI combination framework that synergizes various AI algorithms, including deep learning networks, ensemble methods, and heuristic approaches. By leveraging the unique strengths of each model, our methodology enhances feature extraction, classification accuracy, and robustness. The framework was evaluated using a comprehensive dataset of breast ultrasound images, ensuring diverse and representative sample coverage for model training and validation.

Results/Findings: The proposed multi-AI combination demonstrated significant improvements in diagnostic accuracy, achieving a sensitivity of 95% and a specificity of 92%, outperforming individual AI models and conventional diagnostic techniques. Comparative analyses indicate that the ensemble approach not only enhances performance metrics but also reduces false negatives and false positives, contributing to more reliable clinical decision-making.

Conclusion/Implications: This study showcases the potential of integrating multiple AI methodologies to advance the reliability of breast ultrasound diagnosis. The improved accuracy and robustness of our solution underscore its potential applicability in clinical settings, offering clinicians a powerful tool for early and accurate breast cancer detection. This work highlights the importance of collaborative AI systems in medical diagnostics and provides a roadmap for future research in multi-AI solutions.

Keywords: Breast Ultrasound, Multi-AI Combination, Diagnostic Accuracy, Ensemble Methods, Deep Learning, Medical Diagnostics.
