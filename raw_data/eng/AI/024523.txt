This paper introduces a novel approach for automatic classification of phantom test patterns, crucial for quality assurance in medical imaging. Our objective is to improve the efficiency and accuracy of identifying these patterns, replacing manual inspection methods. We leverage transfer learning with deep convolutional neural networks, adapting pre-trained models to classify various phantom test patterns. Specifically, we fine-tune models such as ResNet and EfficientNet on a dataset of phantom images, exploring different fine-tuning strategies. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves significantly higher accuracy compared to traditional image processing techniques, with an average classification accuracy exceeding 95%. Furthermore, the proposed method exhibits robustness to noise and variations in image acquisition parameters. The automated classification framework offers a practical solution for enhancing quality control workflows in medical imaging, reducing the reliance on manual analysis and improving diagnostic reliability. Keywords: Phantom test patterns, medical imaging, transfer learning, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, automatic classification, quality assurance.
