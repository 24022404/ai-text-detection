Clustering ensemble methods have shown promise in improving the robustness and accuracy of clustering solutions by combining multiple base clusterings. However, most existing ensemble approaches rely on fully labeled data or assume that all data points are equally important. This paper introduces a novel semi-supervised clustering ensemble framework based on a voting mechanism to address these limitations. Our approach leverages a small set of labeled data points to guide the ensemble process, improving the consensus clustering performance while handling noisy or irrelevant data points. Specifically, we propose a weighted voting scheme where base clusterings are weighted based on their agreement with the labeled data. Furthermore, we incorporate a data-driven weighting strategy to emphasize more reliable data points in the ensemble process. Experimental results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that our semi-supervised clustering ensemble approach outperforms state-of-the-art unsupervised and semi-supervised clustering methods. The proposed method offers a flexible and effective solution for clustering in scenarios with limited labeled data, enhancing clustering accuracy and robustness.

Keywords: Clustering ensemble, semi-supervised learning, voting, data weighting, consensus clustering.
