This paper addresses the vulnerability of object detection models to adversarial attacks, with a focus on developing transferable attacks that can be applied to both image and video object detection systems. Our approach utilizes a novel algorithm that generates adversarial examples by perturbing the input data in a way that is imperceptible to humans, yet effectively deceives the object detection models. We evaluate the effectiveness of our method on various state-of-the-art object detection architectures, including YOLO and Faster R-CNN, and demonstrate its ability to transfer across different models and datasets. Our results show that the proposed attack can successfully evade object detection in both images and videos, with a significant reduction in detection accuracy. The implications of this research highlight the need for more robust object detection systems, and our contributions pave the way for the development of more secure and reliable computer vision models. Key keywords: adversarial attacks, object detection, image and video analysis, computer vision, deep learning, transferable attacks, YOLO, Faster R-CNN.
