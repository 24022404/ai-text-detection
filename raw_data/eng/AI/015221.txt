In the realm of sports video analysis, identifying key events in figure skating videos remains a significant challenge due to the sport's dynamic and artistic nature. This paper presents a novel approach to highlight detection by estimating blink probability, which correlates with viewer attention and emotional engagement, providing a unique perspective on capturing crucial moments in figure skating performances.

Methods/Approach: We propose a comprehensive model integrating computer vision algorithms and machine learning techniques to estimate blink probability from video footage. The model extracts facial landmarks and utilizes a neural network to predict blink occurrences, correlating them with potential highlights. The dataset comprises annotated figure skating videos, allowing for training and testing of the predicted blink probability model.

Results/Findings: Our approach demonstrates a significant improvement in highlight detection accuracy compared to traditional methods, which rely solely on performance metrics or manual annotation. The blink probability model achieves a high correlation with viewer-ranked highlights, offering a more nuanced understanding of engaging moments. Experimental results show that incorporating blink probability into video analysis enhances the precision of highlight identification by an average margin of 15%.

Conclusion/Implications: This research introduces an innovative methodology for sports video analysis by leveraging human physiological responses as a proxy for viewer engagement. The incorporation of blink probability into figure skating highlight detection offers a fresh avenue for automated sports analytics, with potential applications extending to other sports and media industries requiring sophisticated event detection. By bridging physiological insights and computational models, this study contributes to advancing the paradigm of automated highlight detection in video analysis.

Keywords: Blink Probability, Highlight Detection, Figure Skating Videos, Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Sports Video Analysis.
