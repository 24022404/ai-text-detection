Facial expression manipulation is a critical task in computer vision and graphics, with applications ranging from virtual reality to human-computer interaction. However, achieving fine-grained control over facial expressions while preserving identity and naturalness remains a significant challenge. This paper presents a novel framework for fine-grained facial expression manipulation, leveraging advancements in generative adversarial networks (GANs) and facial action coding systems (FACS). Our approach introduces a multi-level feature disentanglement mechanism that separates identity, expression, and pose attributes, enabling precise control over individual facial components. We propose a hybrid training strategy combining supervised and unsupervised learning to enhance the model's ability to generalize across diverse datasets. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art techniques in terms of expression accuracy, identity preservation, and visual quality. Additionally, we provide a comprehensive evaluation using both quantitative metrics and user studies, highlighting the robustness and practicality of our framework. The proposed method opens new avenues for applications in emotion-aware systems, digital avatars, and personalized content creation.  

**Keywords**: Facial expression manipulation, GANs, FACS, feature disentanglement, emotion-aware systems, computer vision.
