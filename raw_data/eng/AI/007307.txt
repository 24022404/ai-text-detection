The TLS/SSL Security Tester allows users to evaluate the security of TLS/SSL configurations in their uploaded Android applications. By analyzing the protocols used, reviewing how certificates are validated, and scrutinizing encryption practices, it helps identify vulnerabilities such as weak cipher suites, obsolete protocol versions, and improper certificate validation. Detecting these flaws is crucial, as their presence could potentially expose apps to man-in-the-middle (MITM) attacks or sensitive data breaches.
