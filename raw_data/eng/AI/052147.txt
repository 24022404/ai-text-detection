This paper introduces CaDIS, a comprehensive dataset designed for image segmentation tasks in the context of cataract diagnosis. The objective is to provide a robust dataset that enables the development of accurate and reliable image segmentation models for cataract detection. To achieve this, a large collection of high-quality images of cataracts was compiled and annotated by medical experts. A deep learning approach was employed, utilizing a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture to segment cataract-affected areas from healthy tissues. The results show that the proposed CaDIS dataset significantly improves the performance of image segmentation models, achieving high accuracy and precision in cataract detection. The CaDIS dataset contributes to the field by providing a standardized and accessible resource for researchers and clinicians, facilitating the development of AI-powered diagnostic tools for cataract detection. Key features of the dataset include high-resolution images, precise annotations, and a large sample size, making it an invaluable resource for medical image analysis and computer vision applications, particularly in ophthalmology and healthcare. Relevant keywords: cataract detection, image segmentation, deep learning, CNN, medical imaging, ophthalmology.
