Autonomous driving systems rely heavily on LiDAR sensors for accurate 3D environmental perception. However, the limited range of conventional LiDARs, particularly in adverse weather conditions, poses a significant challenge. This paper introduces LEAD (LiDAR Extender for Autonomous Driving), a novel method to enhance the effective range of LiDAR systems by fusing LiDAR data with high-resolution radar data using a deep learning framework. LEAD employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) to learn a mapping from radar point clouds to pseudo-LiDAR point clouds, effectively extending the LiDAR’s perception range. The network is trained on a large-scale, synchronized LiDAR-radar dataset. Experimental results demonstrate that LEAD significantly improves the detection range and density of point clouds, especially at longer distances and in inclement weather, outperforming existing LiDAR-radar fusion techniques by up to 20% in object detection accuracy at 150 meters. This extended perception range enhances the safety and reliability of autonomous driving systems. Keywords: Autonomous Driving, LiDAR, Radar, Sensor Fusion, Deep Learning, Object Detection.
