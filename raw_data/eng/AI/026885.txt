The increasing complexity of biomanufacturing processes necessitates robust methods for ensuring product quality and stability. This study addresses the critical challenge of identifying and mitigating risks in biomanufacturing through interpretable risk and sensitivity analyses, aligned with the Quality-by-Design (QbD) framework. We propose a novel computational approach that integrates advanced statistical modeling and machine learning techniques to systematically evaluate process parameters and their impact on product quality attributes. Our methodology employs interpretable models to quantify risk levels and sensitivity indices, enabling the identification of critical control points and optimization of process stability. Experimental validation using real-world biomanufacturing data demonstrates the effectiveness of our approach in reducing variability and enhancing process robustness. Key findings reveal significant improvements in predictive accuracy and decision-making compared to traditional methods. The proposed framework not only supports compliance with regulatory requirements but also provides actionable insights for process engineers and quality assurance teams. This research contributes to the advancement of biomanufacturing by offering a scalable, data-driven solution for risk management and stability control, with potential applications across diverse biopharmaceutical production systems.  

**Keywords**: Biomanufacturing, Quality-by-Design, Risk Analysis, Sensitivity Analysis, Process Stability, Machine Learning, Interpretable Models.
