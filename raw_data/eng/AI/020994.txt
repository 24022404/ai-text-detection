This paper addresses the challenge of link prediction on n-ary relational data, where traditional pairwise methods fall short. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that effectively evaluates relatedness among multiple entities to predict missing links. We propose a relatedness evaluation framework that leverages the structural and semantic properties of n-ary relations to assess the likelihood of link formation. Our method employs a graph-based representation learning model to capture complex interactions among entities and a relatedness scoring function to quantify the strength of relationships. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our approach over state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in prediction accuracy and robustness. The proposed framework has important implications for applications such as recommender systems, social network analysis, and knowledge graph completion. Our research contributes to the advancement of link prediction on complex relational data, highlighting the potential of relatedness evaluation in uncovering hidden patterns and relationships. Key keywords: link prediction, n-ary relational data, relatedness evaluation, graph representation learning, knowledge graph completion.
