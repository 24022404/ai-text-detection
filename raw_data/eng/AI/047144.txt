This paper addresses the challenge of designing robust control systems in the presence of uncertainties and nonlinearities. Traditional methods often struggle to balance computational efficiency with robustness guarantees. To overcome this, we propose a novel framework that integrates alternating optimization with advanced quadrature techniques. The alternating optimization approach iteratively refines control policies while ensuring stability, while the quadrature component efficiently handles the integration of uncertain parameters and system dynamics. Our method is validated through extensive simulations on benchmark control problems, demonstrating superior performance in terms of robustness and computational efficiency compared to existing approaches. The results highlight the framework's ability to achieve high-precision control under significant uncertainties, making it suitable for applications in autonomous systems, robotics, and industrial automation. This work contributes a scalable and theoretically grounded solution to robust control, bridging the gap between optimization and uncertainty quantification.  

**Keywords**: robust control, alternating optimization, quadrature, uncertainty quantification, nonlinear systems, autonomous systems.
