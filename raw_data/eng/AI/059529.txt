**Abstract**

This paper addresses the challenge of extracting meaningful patterns from heterogeneous multivariate time-series data with a focus on advancing home health telecare applications. The objective of the research is to develop an effective data mining framework capable of handling disparate sensor sources, variable sampling rates, and complex temporal relationships inherent in telecare environments. The proposed methodology integrates advanced preprocessing techniques for data synchronization and normalization, followed by the application of a hybrid deep learning architecture that combines recurrent neural networks (RNNs) with attention mechanisms to model long-term dependencies and highlight critical health-related events. Experimental evaluation was performed using real-world telecare datasets collected from multiple health sensors deployed in smart home settings. The results demonstrate that the proposed approach significantly improves the accuracy in detecting anomalies and recognizing patterns associated with patient well-being compared to traditional single-modality or univariate analysis techniques. The study's findings highlight the potential of multivariate time-series mining to enhance remote health monitoring, support early intervention, and personalize care for elderly and chronically ill individuals. Overall, this research contributes a robust and scalable solution for learning meaningful patterns from complex health data streams, paving the way for more intelligent and responsive home health telecare systems.

**Keywords:** multivariate time-series, pattern mining, home health telecare, anomaly detection, deep learning, sensor data fusion, remote monitoring
