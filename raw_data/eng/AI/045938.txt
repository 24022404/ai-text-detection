This research addresses the challenge of generating accurate image captions for scenes captured during heavy rainfall, which often suffer from visual obfuscation and noise. Traditional image captioning models struggle with this task due to the diminished visibility of semantic features. 

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel encoder-based learning framework that enhances captioning performance by implementing semantic visual feature matching. Our approach utilizes a specialized encoder that aligns visual inputs with semantic information, improving clarity and contextual understanding of rain-obscured images. The system leverages advancements in deep learning and natural language processing to refine feature extraction and synthesis.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate that our model outperforms existing image captioning techniques in heavy rain scenarios, as evidenced by improved BLEU and METEOR scores. The proposed method successfully enhances the recognition of key visual elements, resulting in more accurate and contextually relevant captions. Comparative analysis shows significant improvements in robustness and accuracy over baseline models.

Conclusion/Implications: The research offers a significant advancement in the field of image captioning under challenging weather conditions. The innovative semantic visual feature matching framework not only enhances image understanding in poor visibility but also has potential applications in autonomous driving, outdoor surveillance, and weather-affected imagery processing. Our study paves the way for further exploration of encoder-based techniques in adverse environmental conditions.

Keywords: image captioning, encoder learning, heavy rain, semantic visual feature matching, deep learning, natural language processing, image processing.
