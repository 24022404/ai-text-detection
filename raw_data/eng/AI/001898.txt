This paper introduces Copyspace, a novel approach to automatically identifying suitable regions for text overlay on images. The objective is to determine optimal areas where text can be placed without obscuring important visual content, enhancing image readability and aesthetics. Our method employs a deep learning-based algorithm that analyzes image features, such as texture, color, and object detection, to predict suitable text regions. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of Copyspace in identifying copyspace regions, outperforming existing approaches in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The proposed system has significant implications for various applications, including image editing, graphic design, and augmented reality. By providing a reliable and efficient means of identifying suitable text regions, Copyspace contributes to the development of more effective and visually appealing image-text overlays, with potential applications in fields such as advertising, education, and social media. Key keywords: image processing, text overlay, copyspace, deep learning, computer vision.
