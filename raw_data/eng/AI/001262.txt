This paper proposes a novel approach to geodesic regression, termed Fast Predictive Simple Geodesic Regression (FPSGR), which aims to efficiently predict regression outcomes on Riemannian manifolds. The objective is to address the limitations of existing geodesic regression methods, which often suffer from high computational complexity and poor scalability. Our approach leverages a simple yet effective framework that combines the strengths of geodesic interpolation and predictive modeling. The FPSGR method utilizes a fast and robust algorithm to estimate the geodesic regression model, enabling accurate predictions on complex manifold-valued data. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of FPSGR over state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in computational efficiency and predictive accuracy. The proposed approach has important implications for various applications, including shape analysis, medical imaging, and computer vision. Key contributions of this work include the development of a fast and scalable geodesic regression framework, the introduction of a novel predictive modeling approach, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in real-world scenarios. Relevant keywords: geodesic regression, Riemannian manifolds, predictive modeling, computational efficiency, shape analysis.
