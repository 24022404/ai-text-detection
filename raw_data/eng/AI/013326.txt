**Abstract**

Accurate depth estimation is a fundamental task in computer vision, critical for applications such as autonomous driving, robotics, and augmented reality. Existing monocular depth estimation methods, while cost-effective, often suffer from scale ambiguity and limited accuracy in challenging scenes. This paper proposes LiDARTouch, a novel approach that leverages sparse depth measurements from a few-beam LiDAR sensors to enhance monocular metric depth estimation. Our method fuses high-resolution image data from a single camera with sparse but precise range information from a low-density LiDAR, using a deep neural network designed to efficiently integrate both modalities. Experimental results on urban driving datasets demonstrate that LiDARTouch significantly outperforms state-of-the-art monocular and sparse depth completion techniques, achieving superior accuracy in both global and local metric depth estimation while operating in real time. By requiring only minimal additional hardware compared to pure monocular setups, LiDARTouch offers a practical and scalable solution for robust depth perception. The combination of cost efficiency, ease of deployment, and improved performance highlights the potential of LiDARTouch for broad adoption in real-world IT applications. **Keywords:** depth estimation, monocular vision, LiDAR, sensor fusion, deep learning, autonomous vehicles.
