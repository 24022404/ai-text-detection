Accurate salient object detection from RGB-D data is crucial for various applications, including robotics and image editing. However, effectively fusing and exploiting the complementary information from RGB and depth modalities remains a challenge. This paper introduces a novel collaborative learning framework for RGB-D salient object detection. Our approach leverages a two-stream network architecture with dedicated RGB and depth encoders, enhanced with cross-modal attention mechanisms to facilitate information exchange and feature refinement. A collaborative training strategy is employed, where each modality benefits from the guidance of the other, promoting robustness and accuracy. We further propose a novel depth refinement module that leverages RGB context to enhance the quality of depth features, mitigating the impact of noisy or incomplete depth data. Experimental results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing approaches in terms of both accuracy and robustness. The proposed collaborative learning framework and depth refinement module significantly contribute to the field of RGB-D salient object detection by effectively fusing multi-modal information and improving depth feature quality.
Keywords: Salient Object Detection, RGB-D, Collaborative Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision.
