CNNs have evolved significantly over the years, beginning with their introduction by a Japanese scientist in 1980. The development continued when Yann Le Cun successfully trained them with backpropagation for handwriting recognition in 1998. However, these networks achieved widespread prominence in 2012 when Alex Krizhevsky utilized GPUs to train the pioneering AlexNet architecture, leading to a landmark victory in a major computer vision competition and sparking renewed interest in deep learning.
