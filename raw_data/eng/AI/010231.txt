This paper presents a novel Deep Spiking Convolutional Neural Network (DSCNN) for single object localization, leveraging the principles of deep continuous local learning. The objective of this research is to address the challenges of accurate object localization in dynamic environments, where traditional convolutional neural networks (CNNs) often struggle with computational efficiency and real-time adaptability. To achieve this, we propose a biologically inspired spiking neural network architecture that integrates continuous local learning mechanisms, enabling the model to adaptively refine its predictions over time. The approach combines spiking neurons with convolutional layers to process spatial-temporal data efficiently, while the continuous learning framework ensures robust performance under varying conditions. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed DSCNN achieves superior localization accuracy compared to conventional CNNs and other state-of-the-art methods, particularly in scenarios with limited computational resources. Additionally, the model exhibits significant improvements in energy efficiency, making it suitable for deployment in resource-constrained environments such as edge devices. The findings highlight the potential of spiking neural networks and continuous local learning for advancing object localization tasks, offering a promising direction for future research in real-time, energy-efficient AI systems. Keywords: spiking neural networks, object localization, continuous local learning, convolutional neural networks, energy efficiency, real-time adaptation.
