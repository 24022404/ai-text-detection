This paper introduces a novel deep learning approach for burst image denoising, addressing the limitations of traditional methods in handling complex noise distributions and real-world image degradation. Our method leverages a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture trained end-to-end to effectively remove noise from a sequence of images captured in burst mode. The network incorporates a multi-frame attention mechanism to selectively aggregate information from multiple noisy frames, allowing for robust denoising even in challenging scenarios with significant motion and varying noise levels. We propose a new loss function that combines spatial and frequency domain constraints, encouraging the network to preserve fine details and suppress artifacts. Experimental results on both synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art burst denoising algorithms in terms of PSNR, SSIM, and visual quality. The proposed method exhibits superior performance in handling spatially varying noise and complex correlations between burst frames. Key words: Burst Denoising, Deep Learning, Image Processing, Convolutional Neural Network, Image Restoration.
