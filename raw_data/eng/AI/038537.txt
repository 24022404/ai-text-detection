Understanding and interpreting ancient coin images is a significant challenge in the intersection of computer vision, cultural heritage, and numismatics. This paper presents a comprehensive study aimed at automating the analysis of ancient coin images using advanced image recognition and machine learning techniques. The primary objective is to develop a robust system capable of distinguishing coin types, identifying inscriptions, and classifying iconographic motifs despite the high variability in wear, lighting conditions, and image quality.

We propose a novel approach combining convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for feature extraction with a multi-task learning framework that enables simultaneous classification and segmentation of coin elements. The dataset comprises thousands of annotated coin images collected from museum databases and online repositories, ensuring diverse geographical and historical representation. Through rigorous evaluation, our method demonstrates significant improvements in accuracy and robustness over traditional manual and feature-based techniques.

Key findings highlight the system's ability to generalize across different ancient empires and periods, achieving high precision in both coin identification and legend transcription tasks. The implications of this research extend to automated cataloging, digital archiving, and enhanced accessibility for historians and collectors. Our contributions pave the way for more effective digitization and preservation of ancient numismatic artifacts, showcasing the potential of machine learning in cultural heritage analysis.

Keywords: ancient coins, image recognition, computer vision, machine learning, convolutional neural networks, cultural heritage, numismatics, image classification, artifact digitization.
