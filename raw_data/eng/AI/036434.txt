This paper introduces Visual Transformers, a novel approach to image representation and processing in computer vision. The objective is to leverage the strengths of transformer models, originally designed for natural language processing, to improve image understanding and analysis. Our method utilizes a token-based representation, where images are divided into patches and treated as sequences of tokens, enabling the application of transformer architectures. We propose a unique approach to tokenization, allowing for efficient and effective image processing. The results demonstrate the efficacy of Visual Transformers in various computer vision tasks, including image classification, object detection, and segmentation, outperforming traditional convolutional neural networks in some cases. The key findings highlight the potential of Visual Transformers to revolutionize computer vision, enabling more accurate and efficient image analysis. This research contributes to the advancement of computer vision and has significant implications for applications such as image recognition, autonomous vehicles, and medical imaging, with relevant keywords including transformer models, token-based representation, computer vision, image processing, and deep learning.
