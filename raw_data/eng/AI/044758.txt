Reinforcement learning (RL) agents often struggle in sparse reward environments, hindering their ability to learn complex behaviors. Intrinsic motivation (IM) addresses this challenge by providing agents with internally generated rewards to encourage exploration and skill acquisition. This survey provides a comprehensive overview of intrinsic motivation in reinforcement learning, categorizing and analyzing various IM approaches, including novelty-based, prediction-based, and empowerment-based methods. We examine the theoretical foundations of these methods, highlighting their strengths and limitations in different application domains. Furthermore, we analyze the impact of IM on exploration, sample efficiency, and generalization in RL agents. The survey also addresses open challenges and future research directions, such as the integration of multiple intrinsic rewards, the development of more robust and adaptive IM mechanisms, and the application of IM to real-world problems. This work serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners seeking to understand and utilize intrinsic motivation to enhance reinforcement learning algorithms.
Keywords: Reinforcement learning, intrinsic motivation, exploration, sparse rewards, artificial intelligence.
