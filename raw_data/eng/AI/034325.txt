This paper introduces a novel approach to temporal difference learning, termed Parameter-free Gradient Temporal Difference Learning. The objective is to develop a reinforcement learning algorithm that eliminates the need for manual parameter tuning, a longstanding challenge in the field. Our method leverages gradient-based optimization to adaptively adjust the learning rate and other key parameters, ensuring efficient and stable convergence. The approach utilizes a gradient descent framework to minimize the mean squared error of the value function, resulting in improved performance and robustness. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our algorithm in various environments, outperforming traditional temporal difference learning methods. The key findings highlight the benefits of parameter-free learning, including reduced sensitivity to hyperparameters and improved adaptability to changing environments. This research contributes to the development of more autonomous and efficient reinforcement learning systems, with potential applications in robotics, game playing, and other domains. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, temporal difference learning, gradient descent, parameter-free learning, adaptive optimization.
