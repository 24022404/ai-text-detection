**Abstract:**  
Efficient signal detection in large-scale Multiple-Input Multiple-Output (MIMO) systems remains a significant challenge due to the exponentially increasing search space and computational complexity. This paper addresses the problem by introducing a novel deep learning-based framework designed to achieve optimally efficient search in large-scale MIMO environments. The proposed approach leverages a custom deep neural network architecture trained to approximate the maximum likelihood detector, significantly reducing computational costs while maintaining high detection accuracy. Extensive simulations are conducted on synthetic and real-world MIMO datasets, evaluating key performance metrics such as bit error rate (BER), latency, and computational resource utilization. The results demonstrate that our deep learning model achieves near-optimal detection performance, outperforming traditional algorithms such as sphere decoding and approximate message passing in terms of speed and scalability. Furthermore, the network exhibits strong generalization across various channel conditions and system sizes. The findings highlight the potential of deep learning to enable real-time, efficient detection in future massive MIMO deployments, paving the way for the practical realization of next-generation wireless communication systems. Key keywords include: deep learning, MIMO systems, signal detection, neural networks, large-scale optimization, wireless communications.
