Reinforcement Learning (RL) with rich observations, such as images or high-dimensional sensory data, presents significant challenges due to the complexity of extracting meaningful state representations. This paper addresses the problem of provably efficient RL in environments with rich observations by introducing a novel framework based on latent state decoding. Our approach leverages unsupervised learning techniques to decode latent states from high-dimensional observations, enabling efficient policy optimization. We propose a model that combines representation learning with RL algorithms, ensuring theoretical guarantees on sample efficiency and convergence. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing approaches in terms of both learning speed and final performance across a range of benchmark environments. The key contribution of this work lies in its ability to bridge the gap between theoretical RL guarantees and practical applications in complex, high-dimensional observation spaces. This research has significant implications for real-world applications, such as robotics and autonomous systems, where efficient learning from rich sensory data is critical.  

**Keywords**: Reinforcement Learning, Latent State Decoding, Rich Observations, Sample Efficiency, Representation Learning.
