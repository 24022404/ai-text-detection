This paper proposes a novel Multivariate Bayesian Structural Time Series (MBST) model, designed to analyze complex temporal relationships between multiple variables. The objective is to develop a robust and flexible framework for forecasting and understanding the dynamics of multivariate time series data. Our approach combines the strengths of Bayesian inference and structural time series modeling, allowing for the estimation of latent components and the incorporation of prior knowledge. The MBST model is evaluated on several real-world datasets, demonstrating its ability to outperform existing methods in terms of accuracy and interpretability. Key findings include the model's capacity to capture complex patterns and relationships, as well as its robustness to noise and missing data. The contributions of this research lie in its ability to provide a probabilistic and multivariate approach to time series analysis, with potential applications in fields such as economics, finance, and healthcare. The MBST model offers a unique combination of flexibility, interpretability, and predictive power, making it a valuable tool for researchers and practitioners working with complex time series data. Keywords: Bayesian inference, structural time series, multivariate analysis, time series forecasting, probabilistic modeling.
