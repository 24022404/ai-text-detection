This paper presents a novel approach to sequence prediction using Spectral Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs). The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of sequence prediction models by leveraging the spectral domain. Our method utilizes a spectral decomposition technique to transform the input sequences into the frequency domain, where a specially designed RNN architecture is applied to capture long-range dependencies and patterns. The proposed Spectral RNN model is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in prediction accuracy and computational efficiency compared to traditional RNNs and other state-of-the-art models. Key findings include the ability of Spectral RNNs to effectively model complex sequences with multiple frequencies and the robustness of the model to noise and missing data. The contributions of this research include the introduction of a new spectral-based approach to sequence prediction, which has potential applications in areas such as time series forecasting, natural language processing, and audio processing. Overall, this work highlights the benefits of incorporating spectral analysis into RNN architectures for sequence prediction tasks, with key keywords including Spectral RNNs, sequence prediction, time series forecasting, and deep learning.
