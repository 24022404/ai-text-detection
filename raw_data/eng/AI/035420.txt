This paper addresses the challenge of generating high-quality object proposals efficiently, a critical task in computer vision applications such as object detection and image segmentation. Traditional methods often struggle to balance proposal quality with computational efficiency, leading to either suboptimal results or excessive resource consumption. To overcome this limitation, we propose a novel sequential optimization framework that iteratively refines object proposals while maintaining computational efficiency. Our approach leverages a combination of lightweight feature extraction and adaptive refinement techniques, enabling the generation of precise object proposals with minimal computational overhead. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of both proposal quality and processing speed, outperforming existing methods by a significant margin. The proposed framework is highly scalable and can be integrated into various vision pipelines, offering practical benefits for real-time applications. This work contributes to advancing object proposal generation by introducing a computationally efficient solution without compromising on accuracy, paving the way for improved performance in downstream vision tasks.  

**Keywords**: object proposal generation, sequential optimization, computer vision, computational efficiency, adaptive refinement, real-time applications.
