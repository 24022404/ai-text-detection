This paper presents gundapusunil's approach to SemEval-2020 Task 8: Multimodal Memotion Analysis. The task involved classifying memes based on humor, sentiment, and offensiveness, requiring the integration of both textual and visual modalities. Our approach leverages a multi-layered architecture, combining pre-trained language models (BERT) for text processing with convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for image feature extraction. We employed early and late fusion techniques to effectively integrate these multimodal features. Furthermore, we experimented with various attention mechanisms to focus on the most relevant features in both modalities. Our best performing model achieved competitive results on the task, particularly in humor and sentiment classification. Analysis of the results reveals the importance of effective multimodal fusion and the potential of attention mechanisms to improve meme understanding. The findings suggest that leveraging both visual and textual cues through sophisticated fusion techniques is crucial for accurate memotion analysis. Keywords: Multimodal, Memotion Analysis, BERT, CNN, Attention Mechanisms, Humor Detection, Sentiment Analysis.
