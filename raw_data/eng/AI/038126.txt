This paper introduces a novel Bayesian reinforcement learning (RL) algorithm that leverages deep neural networks for efficient, sparse sampling of the posterior distribution over value functions.  The objective is to improve exploration and reduce sample complexity in complex RL environments.  Our approach utilizes a deep neural network to represent the value function and employs a sparse variational inference technique to approximate the Bayesian posterior.  Specifically, we introduce a novel sampling strategy based on minimizing the information gain, encouraging exploration of under-explored regions of the state space.  Empirical results on a suite of benchmark RL tasks demonstrate that our method achieves significantly improved performance compared to state-of-the-art deep RL algorithms, particularly in scenarios with sparse rewards and high-dimensional state spaces. The proposed algorithm exhibits enhanced sample efficiency and robustness, allowing for faster learning and better generalization.  Our findings suggest that deep, sparse Bayesian sampling provides a promising direction for addressing the exploration-exploitation trade-off in reinforcement learning, offering a practical approach for deploying RL agents in real-world applications.

Keywords: Bayesian Reinforcement Learning, Deep Learning, Sparse Sampling, Exploration, Variational Inference, Neural Networks.
