Visual Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) is a critical technology for autonomous systems, enabling real-time environment mapping and localization. However, the widespread adoption of Visual SLAM raises significant privacy concerns, as it inherently captures and processes sensitive visual data. This paper addresses the challenge of preserving privacy in Visual SLAM systems without compromising their accuracy and efficiency. We propose a novel framework that integrates advanced encryption techniques and privacy-aware data processing algorithms to ensure that sensitive information, such as personal identifiers or private spaces, is protected during SLAM operations. Our approach leverages lightweight cryptographic methods and selective data obfuscation to minimize computational overhead while maintaining robust privacy guarantees. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves comparable localization and mapping accuracy to traditional Visual SLAM systems while significantly enhancing privacy protection. The implications of this research extend to applications in robotics, augmented reality, and smart environments, where privacy-preserving technologies are increasingly essential. This work contributes a scalable and practical solution to the growing demand for secure and privacy-conscious SLAM systems.  

**Keywords**: Visual SLAM, privacy preservation, encryption, data obfuscation, autonomous systems, robotics.
