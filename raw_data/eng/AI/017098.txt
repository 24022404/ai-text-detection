Point cloud completion, a critical task in 3D vision, aims to reconstruct complete and accurate 3D shapes from partial or incomplete point cloud data. This paper introduces a novel Skip-attention Network with Hierarchical Folding (SAN-HF) to address the challenges of missing data and geometric detail preservation. The proposed method leverages a skip-attention mechanism to effectively aggregate multi-level features from the encoder to the decoder, enabling the network to capture both global structures and fine-grained details. Additionally, a hierarchical folding strategy is employed to progressively refine the point cloud, ensuring high-quality reconstruction. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that SAN-HF outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of completion accuracy and visual quality. The model achieves significant improvements in Chamfer Distance and F1 score, showcasing its ability to handle complex shapes and large missing regions. This research contributes to advancing 3D reconstruction technologies, with potential applications in autonomous driving, robotics, and augmented reality. Key innovations include the integration of skip-attention and hierarchical folding, offering a robust and efficient solution for point cloud completion tasks.  

**Keywords**: Point cloud completion, 3D reconstruction, skip-attention mechanism, hierarchical folding, deep learning, 3D vision.
