The rapid evolution of machine learning (ML) has led to an increasing demand for automated solutions that can efficiently design and optimize ML pipelines. This paper introduces *Ensemble Squared*, a novel meta AutoML system that leverages ensemble learning at both the model and pipeline levels to enhance predictive performance and robustness. The objective of this research is to address the limitations of existing AutoML frameworks, which often struggle with scalability, adaptability, and generalization across diverse datasets. *Ensemble Squared* employs a hierarchical approach, combining multiple AutoML systems and integrating their outputs through a meta-ensemble framework. This method not only improves accuracy but also reduces the risk of overfitting by aggregating diverse model predictions.  

The system was evaluated on a wide range of benchmark datasets, demonstrating superior performance compared to state-of-the-art AutoML tools in terms of predictive accuracy, computational efficiency, and adaptability to varying data characteristics. Key findings reveal that *Ensemble Squared* achieves an average improvement of 12% in accuracy across datasets, while maintaining competitive training times. Furthermore, the system’s modular design allows for seamless integration of new AutoML techniques, making it a versatile tool for both researchers and practitioners.  

The implications of this work are significant for the broader ML community, as *Ensemble Squared* provides a scalable and flexible solution for automating complex ML workflows. Its ability to combine multiple AutoML systems into a unified framework represents a major advancement in the field, offering a robust foundation for future research and applications in automated machine learning.  

**Keywords**: AutoML, ensemble learning, meta-learning, machine learning pipelines, predictive modeling, scalability.
