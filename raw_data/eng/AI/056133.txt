Data augmentation is a crucial technique for improving the generalization ability of deep learning models in image classification. This paper introduces Attentive CutMix, an enhanced data augmentation method that builds upon CutMix by incorporating an attention mechanism. The objective is to create more semantically meaningful composite images, guiding the model to focus on informative regions during training. Our approach utilizes a spatial attention module to identify salient areas within images before applying the CutMix operation. This ensures that important features are retained in the mixed samples, preventing the introduction of irrelevant or noisy patches. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet, demonstrate that Attentive CutMix consistently outperforms standard CutMix and other state-of-the-art data augmentation techniques. Ablation studies validate the effectiveness of the attention module in generating more effective training samples. The findings suggest that Attentive CutMix improves model robustness and accuracy by promoting attention to relevant image regions. Keywords: Data augmentation, CutMix, attention mechanism, image classification, deep learning, convolutional neural networks.
