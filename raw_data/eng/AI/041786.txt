This paper addresses the challenge of solving inverse problems, which are ubiquitous in various fields of science and engineering. Our objective is to develop a novel framework for analyzing inverse problems using invertible neural networks. We propose a new approach that leverages the properties of invertible neural networks to solve inverse problems efficiently and accurately. Our method involves training an invertible neural network to learn the inverse mapping between the observation space and the parameter space. The network is designed to be invertible, allowing for the direct computation of the posterior distribution over the parameters. We evaluate our approach on several benchmark inverse problems, including image deblurring and tomography reconstruction, and demonstrate its superiority over existing methods. Our results show that invertible neural networks can effectively solve inverse problems with high accuracy and efficiency. The key contributions of this research are the development of a novel invertible neural network architecture and the demonstration of its effectiveness in solving inverse problems. This work has significant implications for various applications, including image processing, medical imaging, and scientific computing, and highlights the potential of invertible neural networks in solving complex inverse problems. Key keywords: invertible neural networks, inverse problems, deep learning, image processing, scientific computing.
