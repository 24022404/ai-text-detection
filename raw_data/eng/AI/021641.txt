LiDAR simulation is essential for developing and validating perception algorithms in autonomous vehicles and robotics. Generating accurate and efficient synthetic LiDAR point clouds from virtual scenes typically requires complex ray-tracing pipelines, resulting in high computational costs. This paper presents a novel method for fast LiDAR rendering by leveraging spherical UV unwrapping of equirectangular Z-buffer images. By projecting 3D scene data onto a 2D equirectangular depth map, our approach efficiently maps LiDAR scan patterns onto the unwrapped spherical surface, enabling rapid retrieval of simulated range measurements without exhaustive ray tracing. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves a significant speedup in synthetic LiDAR generation while maintaining high fidelity in point cloud distribution when compared to conventional GPU-based ray-tracing techniques. The proposed technique is readily compatible with real-time applications, scalable to dense and complex environments, and adaptable to various LiDAR sensor models. Our contributions substantially lower the barrier for large-scale synthetic LiDAR data generation and accelerate virtual testing workflows in autonomous systems research. Keywords: LiDAR simulation, synthetic data, equirectangular projection, spherical mapping, Z-buffer, autonomous vehicles, 3D rendering.
