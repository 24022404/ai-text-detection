This paper presents a comprehensive survey on universal adversarial attacks, a type of attack that can fool machine learning models by adding a fixed perturbation to any input. The objective of this research is to provide an in-depth analysis of the current state of universal adversarial attacks, including their methods, applications, and defenses. We employ a systematic approach to review existing literature, categorizing attacks into different types and evaluating their effectiveness on various machine learning models. Our results show that universal adversarial attacks can be highly effective, with some attacks achieving success rates of over 90% on certain models. We also identify key challenges and open issues in the field, including the development of robust defenses and the need for more comprehensive evaluation metrics. The conclusion of this survey highlights the importance of considering universal adversarial attacks in the design and deployment of machine learning systems, and we discuss potential applications and future research directions. Key contributions of this work include a thorough review of existing attacks, a framework for categorizing and evaluating universal adversarial attacks, and insights into the development of more robust machine learning models. Relevant keywords: universal adversarial attacks, machine learning security, deep learning, adversarial robustness, computer vision.
