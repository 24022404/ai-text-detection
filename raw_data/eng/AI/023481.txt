This paper addresses the challenge of transductive inference in higher-order Markov Random Fields (MRFs), a critical problem in computer vision and machine learning. Traditional methods often struggle with the computational complexity and scalability of higher-order MRFs, particularly when dealing with discrete and continuous variables simultaneously. To overcome these limitations, we propose a novel Discrete-Continuous Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (DC-ADMM) framework. This approach integrates discrete and continuous optimization techniques, enabling efficient inference in higher-order MRFs by leveraging the strengths of both domains. Our method employs a decomposition strategy to handle complex energy functions, ensuring scalability and robustness. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that DC-ADMM achieves superior performance in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency compared to state-of-the-art methods. The proposed framework not only advances the theoretical understanding of transductive inference but also provides practical tools for applications such as image segmentation, 3D reconstruction, and structured prediction. This work highlights the potential of hybrid optimization techniques in addressing challenging problems in machine learning and computer vision.  

**Keywords**: Transductive inference, higher-order MRFs, ADMM, discrete-continuous optimization, structured prediction, computer vision.
