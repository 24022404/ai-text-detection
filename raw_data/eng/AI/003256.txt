The Overview page serves as a personal dashboard tailored to the logged-in user, presenting key information such as their Open Ticket, Major Ticket, Critical Ticket, Open Hour of Ticket, and the Amount of Failed Ticket. In contrast, the DashBoard page offers a general summary of all ticket statuses, including “Open,” “Closed,” “All,” and “Unassigned,” and also displays the number of Rating Tickets.
