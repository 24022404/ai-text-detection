Blockchain technology has emerged as a transformative tool for decentralized systems, yet analyzing its underlying graph structures remains a challenging task. This paper introduces **ChainNet**, a novel framework for learning on blockchain graphs by leveraging their unique topological features. The objective of this research is to enable efficient and scalable analysis of blockchain networks, focusing on transaction patterns, node behaviors, and network dynamics. To achieve this, ChainNet employs a graph neural network (GNN) architecture specifically designed to capture the hierarchical and temporal properties of blockchain graphs. The proposed approach integrates topological metrics such as centrality, clustering coefficients, and community structures to enhance feature representation. Experiments conducted on real-world blockchain datasets demonstrate that ChainNet outperforms traditional graph analysis methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. Key findings reveal that the framework effectively identifies anomalous transactions, predicts network growth, and classifies node roles with high precision. The implications of this work extend to applications in fraud detection, network optimization, and decentralized system design. ChainNet represents a significant advancement in blockchain analytics, offering a scalable and interpretable solution for understanding complex blockchain ecosystems.  

**Keywords**: Blockchain, Graph Neural Networks, Topological Features, Decentralized Systems, Network Analysis, Anomaly Detection.
