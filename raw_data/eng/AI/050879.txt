In a Cosmos-SDK-based smart contract system, the entities Member, Config, Membershare, and RoundInfo are defined with specific structures and required attributes, serving as foundational elements for organizing system participants, configurations, and operational logic. The Member entity (Table 4.8) encapsulates participant identification and attributes, forming the basis for individual authority and interaction within the contract ecosystem. Config (Table 4.9) holds critical system parameters and customizable settings, enabling contract deployers and administrators to adapt system behavior according to evolving needs or policy changes. The Membershare entity (Table 4.10) records information related to the distribution or holding of shares by different members, often tying in with governance, access rights, and reward mechanisms that underpin community-driven or decentralized applications. RoundInfo (Table 4.11), meanwhile, tracks the progress and state of iterative processes within the contract—such as voting rounds, distribution cycles, or resource allocation—by recording crucial timing, participation, and state-change events. Together, these detailed entity definitions enable a high degree of modularity, clarity, and auditability in the management of smart contract participants and operations. Underpinning these structures is CosmWasm, an extension built atop the Cosmos SDK, which implements smart contracts using the actor model paradigm. In this model, each contract instance functions as a distinct, isolated actor that cannot directly access the internal state of others but instead interacts solely through asynchronous message exchange. This approach not only enhances security, by strictly encapsulating contract state and minimizing attack surfaces, but also increases efficiency and scalability, as contracts safely operate in parallel without risk of unintended interference. The reliance on message-based communication fosters transparent and predictable workflows between contracts and their users, making it particularly well-suited to distributed DApp development where composability and robust cross-contract interactions are essential. Overall, this architecture supports the creation of secure, scalable, and maintainable decentralized applications that leverage Cosmos-SDK infrastructure’s flexibility and security guarantees.
