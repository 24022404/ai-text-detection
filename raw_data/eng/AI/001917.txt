A simple path in a de Bruijn graph forms an Eulerian cycle, and by concatenating the edge symbols along the longest simple path, one obtains a cyclic de Bruijn sequence of order k, which contains every possible string of length k exactly once. To form the acyclic version of this sequence, the sequence corresponding to the first vertex is appended to the end of the cyclic sequence, ensuring that all unique substrings are represented non-cyclically.
