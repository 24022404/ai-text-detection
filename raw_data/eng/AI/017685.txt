This paper introduces the Second-Order Neural ODE Optimizer, a novel approach to optimizing neural networks using ordinary differential equations (ODEs). The objective is to improve the efficiency and accuracy of neural network training by leveraging second-order information. Our method employs a neural ODE-based optimizer that incorporates curvature information to adaptively adjust the learning rate and update direction. The approach utilizes a continuous-time formulation to model the optimization process, allowing for more flexible and accurate updates. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, showing significant improvements in training speed and generalization performance compared to existing optimizers. The Second-Order Neural ODE Optimizer contributes to the field of deep learning by providing a new perspective on optimization, with potential applications in areas such as computer vision, natural language processing, and reinforcement learning. Key aspects of this work include neural ODEs, second-order optimization, adaptive learning rates, and deep learning.
