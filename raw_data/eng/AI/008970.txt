Heavy rain significantly degrades image quality, posing substantial challenges for automatic image captioning systems that rely on visual feature clarity for accurate semantic interpretation. This paper introduces a novel encoder learning framework specifically designed for captioning images captured under heavy rain conditions. The proposed method leverages semantic visual feature matching to enhance the extraction and alignment of meaningful image features, even in highly obscured scenarios. Our approach integrates a robust feature extraction module with an innovative semantic matching layer, enabling the encoder to distinguish and prioritize critical semantic elements in rain-affected images. Experimental evaluations conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed framework outperforms existing captioning models in terms of both accuracy and descriptiveness, particularly under adverse weather conditions. The findings indicate a marked improvement in the generation of contextually rich and coherent captions, bridging the performance gap caused by environmental impairments. This research provides key advancements for vision-language tasks and real-world applications in autonomous vehicles, surveillance, and remote sensing, promoting more reliable image understanding in challenging environments.

Keywords: image captioning, heavy rain, encoder learning, semantic visual feature matching, deep learning, computer vision.
