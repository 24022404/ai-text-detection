Deep neural networks have achieved remarkable success across various applications, but their substantial computational and memory demands hinder deployment on resource-constrained devices. This paper introduces a novel compression technique that leverages the inherent correlations between filters in deep models to achieve significant model size reduction without substantial performance degradation. Our method identifies and groups highly correlated filters, replacing each group with a single representative filter and a lightweight reconstruction module. This module reconstructs the functionality of the original filters using a linear combination of the representative filter's output. We propose an efficient algorithm for identifying filter correlations based on cosine similarity and introduce a fine-tuning procedure to optimize the reconstruction modules and maintain accuracy. Experimental results on benchmark datasets and network architectures demonstrate that our approach achieves comparable or superior compression rates compared to state-of-the-art methods, while preserving accuracy. Specifically, on ResNet-50, our method achieves a 5x compression with only a 1% drop in top-1 accuracy on ImageNet. This technique offers a practical solution for deploying deep models on platforms with limited resources, enabling wider adoption of AI in edge computing and mobile applications. Keywords: Deep Learning, Model Compression, Filter Pruning, Neural Networks, Correlation Analysis, Edge Computing.
