**Abstract:**

This paper presents TensorFlow Lite Micro, an optimized framework designed to enable embedded machine learning on resource-constrained devices, commonly referred to as TinyML systems. The objective of this research is to address the growing demand for deploying neural network models on microcontrollers and edge devices with limited memory and processing capabilities. We detail the architecture of TensorFlow Lite Micro, highlighting its lightweight kernel design, efficient memory footprint, and portability across a wide range of hardware platforms. Through systematic experiments, we evaluate the deployment of various machine learning models, such as keyword spotting and anomaly detection, demonstrating low-latency inference and minimal resource consumption on devices with as little as 16KB RAM. Comparative analysis with existing inference engines shows superior adaptability and ease of integration into embedded workflows. The results underscore how TensorFlow Lite Micro empowers real-time, on-device AI, eliminating the need for constant cloud connectivity and enhancing privacy and responsiveness in IoT applications. Our study concludes by discussing the framework's potential to accelerate the adoption of TinyML in industrial, healthcare, and smart home scenarios, establishing a foundation for further advancements in embedded AI. Key keywords: TinyML, embedded systems, microcontrollers, machine learning, TensorFlow Lite, on-device inference, edge AI.
