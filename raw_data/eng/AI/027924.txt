This paper proposes a novel approach to video classification, leveraging saliency-guided techniques to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of existing methods. The objective is to develop a framework that adaptively weights the importance of different video segments, focusing on the most salient regions to improve classification performance. Our approach employs a deep learning model that integrates saliency detection and adaptively weighted learning, allowing the system to dynamically emphasize relevant features and suppress irrelevant information. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, achieving state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. The key findings indicate that our saliency-guided approach outperforms traditional methods, particularly in scenarios with complex backgrounds or multiple objects of interest. The proposed framework has significant implications for various applications, including video surveillance, human-computer interaction, and multimedia analysis. By highlighting the importance of saliency guidance in video classification, this research contributes to the development of more accurate and efficient video analysis systems, with potential applications in AI, computer vision, and machine learning. Key keywords: saliency detection, video classification, deep learning, adaptively weighted learning, computer vision.
