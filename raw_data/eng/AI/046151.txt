This paper proposes a novel approach to software defect prediction by utilizing multiple ensemble learning models based on different datasets. The objective is to improve the accuracy and reliability of defect prediction in software development. Our approach employs a combination of machine learning algorithms, including random forest, support vector machine, and neural networks, to create ensemble models that leverage the strengths of each individual model. We evaluate the performance of our approach using multiple datasets, including publicly available and proprietary datasets, to demonstrate its effectiveness in various software development contexts. The results show that our multiple ensemble learning models outperform single-model approaches and achieve state-of-the-art performance in software defect prediction. Our research contributes to the development of more accurate and reliable software defect prediction systems, which can help reduce software development costs and improve software quality. Key contributions include the introduction of a novel ensemble learning framework, the evaluation of multiple datasets, and the demonstration of improved performance compared to existing approaches. Relevant keywords: software defect prediction, ensemble learning, machine learning, multiple datasets, artificial intelligence.
