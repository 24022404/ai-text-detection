This paper proposes a novel approach to video retrieval by leveraging graph-based temporal aggregation techniques. The objective is to improve the efficiency and accuracy of video search systems by effectively capturing temporal relationships between video frames. Our method utilizes a graph-based framework to model the temporal structure of videos, allowing for the aggregation of frame-level features into compact and informative video representations. We employ a combination of graph convolutional networks and temporal attention mechanisms to learn robust and context-aware video embeddings. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art video retrieval methods in terms of retrieval accuracy and efficiency. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a graph-based temporal aggregation framework for video retrieval, and the demonstration of its potential to enhance video search systems. Our approach has significant implications for applications such as video recommendation, event detection, and multimedia analysis, and highlights the importance of temporal modeling in video understanding. Key keywords: graph-based models, temporal aggregation, video retrieval, graph convolutional networks, temporal attention.
