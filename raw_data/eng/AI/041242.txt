Temporal Action Localization (TAL) aims to identify and classify actions within untrimmed videos. Existing methods often struggle with complex scenarios due to limitations in feature representation. This paper introduces a novel approach to enhance feature extraction for TAL by integrating multi-scale temporal context and attention mechanisms. Our method, termed Context-Aware Attentive Feature Enhancement (CAAFE), leverages a stacked temporal convolutional network to capture both short-term and long-term dependencies. An attention module dynamically weights feature channels based on their relevance to action instances. Experiments on the ActivityNet-1.3 and THUMOS14 datasets demonstrate that CAAFE significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving improvements of X% in average precision. The results highlight the effectiveness of our approach in learning stronger, more discriminative features for accurate temporal action localization. This advancement facilitates improved understanding and analysis of complex video content.
Keywords: Temporal Action Localization, Feature Extraction, Attention Mechanism, Video Understanding, Deep Learning.
