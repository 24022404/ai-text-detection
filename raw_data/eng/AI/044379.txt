This paper addresses the challenge of analyzing correlated multivariate time series data, a common problem in various domains such as finance, meteorology, and healthcare, where complex interdependencies among time series require advanced analytical models. 

Methods: We propose a novel deep structural model leveraging hierarchical recurrent neural networks to capture both temporal patterns and structural dependencies within multivariate time series. Our approach integrates a multi-headed attention mechanism to enhance the model's ability to focus on relevant features and relationships among different series.

Results: Through extensive experiments on benchmark datasets, our model demonstrates superior performance in prediction accuracy and correlation estimation compared to existing methods such as Vector Autoregression (VAR) and traditional recurrent neural networks. The model effectively identifies latent structures and scales well with increasing data complexity and dimensionality.

Conclusion: This research introduces a significant advancement in time series analysis by providing a robust framework capable of addressing the intricacies of multivariate data. The deep structural model not only improves predictive performance but also offers meaningful insights into the underlying correlations, paving the way for applications in risk management, anomaly detection, and system optimization across industries.

Keywords: multivariate time series, deep learning, recurrent neural networks, hierarchical models, attention mechanism, predictive analytics, correlation analysis.
