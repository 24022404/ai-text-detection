ChainerRL: A Deep Reinforcement Learning Library

Abstract:  
This paper presents ChainerRL, an open-source deep reinforcement learning (DRL) library designed to facilitate both research and practical implementation of advanced RL algorithms. The primary objective of ChainerRL is to provide a flexible, extensible, and efficient platform for developing and evaluating DRL agents across a range of environments. ChainerRL is built on top of the Chainer deep learning framework, leveraging its dynamic computational graph capabilities to support complex model architectures and rapid prototyping. The library offers implementations of widely used RL algorithms, including Deep Q-Networks (DQN), Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG), Asynchronous Advantage Actor-Critic (A3C), and more. Key features include modular agent interfaces, standardized training loops, support for distributed learning, and comprehensive logging and visualization tools. Benchmark experiments demonstrate that ChainerRL achieves competitive performance against existing libraries, while its highly modular design enables easy customization and extension to novel algorithms. In summary, ChainerRL streamlines the workflow for RL researchers and practitioners by combining state-of-the-art algorithm implementations with a user-friendly and versatile development environment. This work contributes to the reinforcement learning community by lowering the barrier to entry for implementing, experimenting with, and deploying DRL methods.  
Keywords: deep reinforcement learning, Chainer, library, DQN, A3C, DDPG, open-source, agent, RL algorithms
