This paper presents a novel approach to automatic perturbation analysis, enabling scalable certified robustness for deep neural networks. The objective is to develop an efficient method for analyzing the robustness of neural networks against adversarial attacks, which is crucial for ensuring the reliability of AI systems in various applications. Our approach leverages a combination of theoretical insights and algorithmic innovations to provide certified robustness guarantees for large-scale neural networks. The key methodology involves a perturbation analysis framework that can be integrated with existing training pipelines, allowing for the certification of robustness without requiring significant modifications to the underlying network architecture. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in achieving state-of-the-art certified robustness on various benchmark datasets, while also showcasing its scalability and efficiency. The findings of this research have important implications for the development of trustworthy AI systems, highlighting the potential for automatic perturbation analysis to enhance the robustness and reliability of neural networks in real-world applications, including computer vision, natural language processing, and autonomous systems. Key contributions include scalable certified robustness, automatic perturbation analysis, and robustness certification for deep neural networks.
