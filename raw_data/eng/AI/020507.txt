This paper addresses the challenge of learning and planning in complex action spaces, a crucial problem in the realm of artificial intelligence and robotics. Our focus is on developing strategies that enable efficient decision-making in environments characterized by vast and intricate sets of potential actions.

Methods/Approach: We propose an innovative framework combining reinforcement learning with advanced search algorithms to navigate these expansive action spaces effectively. By integrating a hierarchical learning structure with model-based planning, our approach allows for dynamic adaptation and optimization. Key components of our framework include a novel policy network design specifically tailored to manage high-dimensional action spaces and an adaptive learning mechanism that enhances exploration and exploitation balance.

Results/Findings: Our experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in convergence speed and solution quality compared to existing methods. The framework was tested across a range of complex simulated environments, showcasing its ability to scale efficiently and maintain robust performance. Compared with traditional approaches, our model achieved notably higher reward metrics and reduced computation times, confirming its effectiveness in managing complex action dynamics.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes to the field by offering a scalable solution that enhances both the learning and planning phases in high-dimensional action landscapes. The proposed framework holds potential for applications in advanced AI systems, robotics, and autonomous agents requiring real-time decision-making capabilities. Our findings suggest a promising direction for future research in expanding the capabilities of AI systems to handle even more complex tasks with heightened efficiency and effectiveness.

Keywords: complex action spaces, reinforcement learning, hierarchical learning, model-based planning, decision-making, AI, robotics.
