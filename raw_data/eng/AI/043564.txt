This paper addresses the critical problem of selecting the most informative data sources for Bayesian learning, a crucial task in machine learning and artificial intelligence. The objective is to develop a near-optimal data source selection method that maximizes the learning efficiency and accuracy of Bayesian models. We propose a novel approach that leverages a combination of information-theoretic measures and probabilistic modeling to identify the most relevant data sources. Our method utilizes a Bayesian optimization framework to efficiently search for the optimal subset of data sources, balancing exploration and exploitation. The results show that our approach significantly outperforms existing methods in terms of learning accuracy and computational efficiency. The key findings indicate that the proposed method can effectively handle high-dimensional data and complex Bayesian models, making it a valuable tool for real-world applications. This research contributes to the development of more efficient and accurate Bayesian learning systems, with potential applications in areas such as predictive modeling, decision-making, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: Bayesian learning, data source selection, Bayesian optimization, machine learning, artificial intelligence.
