This paper presents a novel approach to approximating the Earth Mover's Distance (EMD) with low computational complexity, leveraging data-parallel architectures to achieve efficient and scalable computations. The objective is to address the high computational cost associated with exact EMD calculations, which hinders its application in large-scale data analysis and machine learning tasks. Our method employs a combination of random sampling and parallel processing to reduce the computational burden, while maintaining a high degree of accuracy. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing approximation techniques in terms of speedup and accuracy, with a maximum error rate of 5% compared to the exact EMD calculation. The proposed technique has significant implications for various applications, including content-based image retrieval, object recognition, and clustering analysis. By enabling fast and accurate EMD approximations, our work contributes to the development of more efficient and effective data analysis pipelines, particularly in the context of big data and artificial intelligence. Key keywords: Earth Mover's Distance, data-parallel computing, approximation algorithms, machine learning, big data analytics.
