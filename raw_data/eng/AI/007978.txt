Face anonymization is crucial for protecting individual privacy in images and videos, yet existing methods often struggle with maintaining visual realism and utility for downstream tasks. This paper introduces DeepPrivacy, a novel face anonymization framework based on a generative adversarial network (GAN). Our approach learns to replace facial regions with synthetic faces that preserve the overall appearance and pose while effectively concealing identity. The generator network is trained to create anonymized faces, while the discriminator network distinguishes between real and anonymized faces, encouraging the generator to produce realistic outputs. We employ a combination of adversarial loss, perceptual loss, and identity-preserving loss to ensure both privacy and visual quality. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that DeepPrivacy achieves state-of-the-art performance in anonymization effectiveness and visual realism, surpassing existing methods. Furthermore, we show that images anonymized with DeepPrivacy maintain sufficient information for face attribute recognition tasks. Our work offers a practical and effective solution for face anonymization with applications in surveillance, social media, and video conferencing.

Keywords: Face Anonymization, Generative Adversarial Networks, GAN, Privacy, Deep Learning, Image Processing.
