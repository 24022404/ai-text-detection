This paper proposes HAR-Net, a novel single-stage object detection framework that integrates hybrid attention mechanisms to enhance detection accuracy. The objective is to improve the robustness and efficiency of object detection models by jointly learning channel attention, spatial attention, and scale attention. Our approach employs a multi-branch architecture, where each branch focuses on a specific attention type, and features are fused to capture comprehensive contextual information. Experimental results demonstrate that HAR-Net outperforms state-of-the-art single-stage detectors on popular benchmarks, such as PASCAL VOC and MS COCO, with significant improvements in precision and recall. The key contributions of this work lie in its ability to effectively model complex attention interactions and adapt to various object scales and contexts. By leveraging the strengths of hybrid attention, HAR-Net achieves a balance between accuracy and computational efficiency, making it a promising solution for real-time object detection applications, including autonomous driving, surveillance, and robotics, with relevant keywords: object detection, attention mechanisms, deep learning, computer vision.
