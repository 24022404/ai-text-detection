This paper introduces an innovative approach to multivariate time series analysis by enhancing the Monotone Single Index Multivariate Autoregressive (MSIMAR) model for improved prediction and network estimation. The objective is to develop a model that captures complex dependencies in high-dimensional time series data while maintaining computational efficiency. We propose a novel algorithm that integrates a monotone single index structure with a regularized estimation framework to address challenges associated with parameter estimation in high-dimensional settings. The MSIMAR model effectively reduces dimensionality while preserving crucial dynamic relationships between variables. Empirical results on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that the proposed method achieves superior prediction accuracy and more reliable network structure estimation compared to existing methods, especially when dealing with limited sample sizes. This offers significant implications for diverse applications, including financial econometrics, climate science, and systems biology, where accurate modeling of multivariate time series is critical.

**Keywords:** Time series analysis, Multivariate Autoregression, Network estimation, High-dimensional data, Monotone Single Index Model, Regularization.
