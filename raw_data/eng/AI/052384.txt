Implicit neural representations have shown promise in arbitrary-scale super-resolution (SR) due to their continuous nature. However, current methods often struggle with fine details and sharp edges, especially at high upscaling factors. This paper identifies the limited ability of existing spatial encoding techniques to capture high-frequency information as a key bottleneck. We propose UltraSR, a novel spatial encoding strategy that significantly improves the representation capacity of implicit image functions. UltraSR utilizes a multi-scale frequency encoding scheme combined with a learnable spatial modulation module to effectively capture both global structure and local details. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that UltraSR achieves state-of-the-art performance in arbitrary-scale SR, surpassing existing methods by a significant margin in terms of PSNR and perceptual quality. Furthermore, ablation studies validate the effectiveness of each component of our proposed encoding strategy. UltraSR offers a robust and efficient approach to generate high-quality SR images, opening new possibilities for various applications such as medical imaging and satellite imagery. Keywords: Super-resolution, Implicit Neural Representation, Spatial Encoding, Image Reconstruction, Neural Networks.
