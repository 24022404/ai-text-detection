This paper presents a comprehensive search for deep graph neural networks, aiming to improve the state-of-the-art in graph representation learning. The objective is to develop a systematic approach to designing and evaluating deep graph neural networks, addressing the challenges of scalability, expressiveness, and generalizability. Our approach employs a hierarchical search strategy, combining reinforcement learning and evolutionary algorithms to explore the vast architecture space of graph neural networks. The results show that our method can discover novel, high-performance architectures that outperform existing models on several benchmark datasets. Key findings include the importance of multi-resolution graph representations, attention mechanisms, and adaptive aggregation functions. Our research contributes to the advancement of graph neural networks, enabling applications in node classification, graph classification, and link prediction. The proposed search methodology can be applied to various domains, including social networks, molecular graphs, and traffic networks, highlighting the potential for graph neural networks to drive innovation in AI and machine learning. Keywords: graph neural networks, deep learning, architecture search, reinforcement learning, evolutionary algorithms.
