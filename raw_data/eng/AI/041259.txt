This paper introduces an innovative approach to point feature embedding using an Irregularly Tabulated Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) architecture, designed to enhance computational efficiency and accuracy in processing irregularly structured data. Traditional MLPs often struggle with irregular data formats, leading to inefficiencies in feature extraction and embedding. To address this, we propose a novel tabulated MLP framework that dynamically adapts to irregular data structures, enabling faster and more effective point feature embedding. Our method leverages a combination of sparse tensor representations and adaptive weight allocation to optimize memory usage and computational speed. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach achieves significant improvements in processing time, reducing latency by up to 40% compared to conventional MLP models, while maintaining competitive accuracy on benchmark datasets. Furthermore, the model exhibits robust performance across diverse applications, including 3D point cloud processing and graph-based data analysis. The findings highlight the potential of irregularly tabulated MLPs as a scalable solution for real-time feature embedding in complex datasets. This research contributes to advancing machine learning techniques for irregular data, offering a practical and efficient alternative for applications in computer vision, robotics, and beyond.  

**Keywords**: Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP), point feature embedding, irregular data, sparse tensor, computational efficiency, 3D point cloud, graph-based data.
