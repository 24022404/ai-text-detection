This paper addresses the challenge of accurately segmenting workpiece regions in bin picking scenarios, a crucial task in industrial robotics and computer vision. Our objective is to develop a robust and efficient deep learning approach to identify and separate individual workpieces from cluttered bins, enabling reliable picking and placement operations. We propose a novel convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture that leverages advanced techniques such as attention mechanisms and transfer learning to improve segmentation accuracy. Our method is evaluated on a comprehensive dataset of bin picking scenes, demonstrating significant improvements over existing state-of-the-art approaches. The results show that our model achieves high precision and recall rates, even in cases with heavy occlusion and varying lighting conditions. This research contributes to the development of more efficient and autonomous bin picking systems, with potential applications in manufacturing, logistics, and warehouse automation. Key contributions include the introduction of a new CNN architecture, a large-scale dataset for bin picking segmentation, and a thorough evaluation of the proposed method. Relevant keywords: bin picking, workpiece segmentation, deep learning, CNN, industrial robotics, computer vision.
