This paper proposes a novel deep learning approach that leverages an attention mechanism to predict driver intention at intersections. The objective is to develop a system that can accurately forecast driver behavior, such as turning or going straight, to enhance safety and efficiency in autonomous vehicles. Our method utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) and long short-term memory (LSTM) architecture, combined with an attention mechanism that focuses on relevant features, including driver gaze, vehicle speed, and traffic signals. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods, achieving a prediction accuracy of 92.5%. The key findings highlight the importance of incorporating attention mechanisms in deep learning models for driver intention prediction. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and reliable autonomous driving systems, with potential applications in intelligent transportation systems and vehicle safety. Key keywords: deep learning, attention mechanism, driver intention prediction, autonomous vehicles, intelligent transportation systems.
