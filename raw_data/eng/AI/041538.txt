This paper explores the geometric information content of multiclass classification problems, examining both coarse- and fine-scale geometric structures. The objective is to uncover the underlying data-driven intelligence that enables accurate classification across multiple classes. A novel approach is proposed, leveraging advances in machine learning and geometric deep learning to analyze the information content of complex datasets. The method involves a multi-resolution analysis of the data manifold, capturing both local and global geometric features. Results show that the proposed approach can effectively extract relevant geometric information, leading to improved classification performance and insights into the underlying data structure. The findings have significant implications for the development of more efficient and effective classification algorithms, particularly in applications where geometric information is crucial, such as computer vision and robotics. Key contributions include the introduction of a new framework for analyzing geometric information content and the demonstration of its potential to enhance data-driven intelligence in multiclass classification tasks. Relevant keywords: geometric deep learning, multiclass classification, data-driven intelligence, machine learning, information geometry.
