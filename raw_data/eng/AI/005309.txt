This research focuses on developing a novel approach to neural style transfer by leveraging Cycle-consistent Generative Adversarial Networks (CycleGANs) utilizing data obtained from Chang'E-4. The objective is to enhance the quality and consistency of style transfer in images with lunar surface textures.

Methods/Approach: We employed CycleGANs, a state-of-the-art model that includes two adversarial networks and cycle consistency loss to enable style translation without paired examples. The model is trained on high-resolution images acquired from the Chang'E-4 lunar mission, ensuring that the resulting style transfers maintain fidelity to the lunar characteristics. By incorporating domain-specific constraints and optimization techniques, our approach ensures effective style rendition and invariance across various image conditions.

Results/Findings: The proposed method demonstrated superior performance in capturing fine-grained lunar textures and accurately transferring them to target images across multiple tests. Compared to existing neural style transfer methods, our approach yielded visually coherent and perceptually pleasing results, preserving structural integrity while offering enhanced stylistic expressiveness. Quantitative metrics confirmed improved consistency, reduced artifacts, and higher quality in output images.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a significant advancement in the domain of neural style transfer by integrating CycleGANs with unique datasets from Chang'E-4. The findings illustrate potential applications in scientific visualization, digital art, and space exploration imagery. Our model’s success in handling unique lunar styles opens new avenues for artistic interpretations and practical applications in diverse fields relying on sophisticated image transfer technologies.

Keywords: CycleGAN, neural style transfer, Chang'E-4, lunar imagery, generative adversarial networks, image processing, style transfer consistency.
