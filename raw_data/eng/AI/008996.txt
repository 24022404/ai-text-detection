This paper addresses the challenge of analyzing and extracting actionable insights from machine data generated within a Computer-Assisted Telephone Interviewing (CATI) network. The objective is to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of data processing in such networks using machine learning (ML) techniques. We propose a novel ML-based framework that leverages supervised and unsupervised learning algorithms to classify, cluster, and predict patterns in machine-generated data. The approach integrates feature engineering and model optimization to handle the high dimensionality and noise inherent in CATI network data. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves a 15% improvement in classification accuracy and a 20% reduction in processing time compared to traditional statistical methods. Additionally, the unsupervised clustering component identifies previously undetected patterns in call behavior, enabling more targeted survey strategies. The findings highlight the potential of ML to transform data-driven decision-making in CATI networks, offering scalability and adaptability for large-scale applications. This research contributes to the growing body of work on ML applications in telecommunications and provides a foundation for future advancements in intelligent data analysis for survey systems.  

**Keywords**: Machine Learning, CATI Network, Data Classification, Clustering, Predictive Modeling, Feature Engineering.
