**Abstract:**  
Accurate segmentation of dermoscopic images is critical for the timely and reliable diagnosis of skin lesions, including melanoma. This paper presents an enhanced Convolutional-Deconvolutional Network (CDN) architecture tailored for dermoscopic image segmentation. The proposed approach integrates advanced convolutional layers with improved skip connections and attention mechanisms, enabling the network to capture both local and global context more effectively. The method is evaluated on publicly available dermoscopic image datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in segmentation accuracy, boundary delineation, and robustness against artifacts compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. Quantitative results show an increase in mean Intersection over Union (IoU) and Dice coefficient, validating the effectiveness of the enhancements. The findings highlight the potential of the proposed architecture to support automated skin lesion analysis, aiding dermatologists in clinical decision-making and contributing to early detection of skin cancer. Key innovations of this work include refined feature extraction, enhanced multi-scale fusion, and robust artifact handling. The improved model sets a benchmark for future research in medical image segmentation and offers practical implications for real-world dermoscopic applications.  
**Keywords:** dermoscopic image segmentation, convolutional-deconvolutional networks, deep learning, skin lesion analysis, medical image processing, semantic segmentation.
