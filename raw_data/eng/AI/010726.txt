Skeleton-based action recognition has gained significant attention due to its robustness in representing human motion. However, effectively capturing both spatial and temporal dependencies in skeleton sequences remains a challenge. This paper proposes an Attention Enhanced Graph Convolutional LSTM Network (AE-GCLSTM) to address this issue. The proposed model integrates graph convolutional networks (GCNs) to model spatial relationships between joints and LSTM networks to capture temporal dynamics. Additionally, a novel attention mechanism is introduced to selectively focus on the most informative joints and frames, enhancing the model's ability to recognize complex actions. Experiments are conducted on widely used datasets, including NTU RGB+D and Kinetics-Skeleton, demonstrating that AE-GCLSTM achieves state-of-the-art performance. The results show significant improvements in accuracy compared to existing methods, highlighting the effectiveness of the attention mechanism in refining feature extraction. The proposed approach not only advances skeleton-based action recognition but also provides a framework for integrating attention mechanisms into spatiotemporal modeling tasks. This research has potential applications in human-computer interaction, surveillance, and healthcare monitoring.  

**Keywords**: Skeleton-based action recognition, Graph Convolutional Networks, LSTM, Attention Mechanism, Spatiotemporal Modeling.
