This paper introduces Part2Word, a novel approach for learning a joint embedding space of 3D point clouds and natural language descriptions. Our method addresses the challenge of aligning visual and textual data by focusing on the semantic relationships between object parts and individual words. Specifically, we propose a deep learning architecture that learns to match localized regions within a point cloud to corresponding words in a descriptive sentence. This is achieved by a carefully designed loss function that encourages the alignment of semantically related parts and words while discouraging incorrect associations. We evaluate Part2Word on benchmark datasets for 3D object retrieval and text-based 3D shape generation, demonstrating significant improvements over existing methods. The experimental results show that our part-aware matching strategy yields more accurate and interpretable joint embeddings, leading to better performance in cross-modal tasks. Furthermore, we provide qualitative analysis to visualize the learned part-word correspondences. This work contributes a new perspective on 3D-language understanding by leveraging the fine-grained semantic relationships between object parts and their linguistic descriptions.

Keywords: 3D Point Clouds, Natural Language Processing, Joint Embedding, Cross-Modal Learning, Part-Aware Matching, Deep Learning.
