The objective of this research is to explore and enhance material classification processes through the application of transfer learning using convolutional neural networks (CNNs). Material classification is a critical task in various fields such as manufacturing, quality control, and robotics, where precise identification of material types can lead to improved operational efficiency and product quality. Traditional methods often require extensive datasets and computational resources. This paper introduces a transfer learning framework that leverages pre-trained CNNs, thereby minimizing the need for large labeled datasets while maintaining high classification accuracy. The proposed approach involves repurposing existing CNN architectures, specifically optimized for object recognition tasks, to achieve efficient material classification. Key results demonstrate that our methodology achieves superior classification accuracy compared to conventional methods and requires significantly less computational time and data preparation. These results were consistently validated across various datasets representing diverse material categories. The implications of this study suggest that transfer learning can revolutionize material classification by reducing the dependency on extensive datasets and expensive computational infrastructure. This research contributes to the field by providing a scalable and effective solution for material classification, with potential applications spanning automated quality control, inventory management, and beyond. Keywords: transfer learning, material classification, convolutional neural networks, CNN, pre-trained models, automated quality control.
