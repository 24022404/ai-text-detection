This paper addresses the challenge of efficiently computing the 2D Least Median of Squares (LMedS) in the context of robust line detection. Traditional methods for LMedS are computationally expensive, making them impractical for real-time applications. 

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel GPU-based approach to significantly accelerate the computation of 2D LMedS. Our methodology leverages parallel processing capabilities inherent in modern Graphics Processing Units (GPUs) to perform large-scale data analysis using high-performance computing techniques. We incorporate optimized algorithms within this framework to enhance data throughput and minimize latency, enabling rapid processing of large datasets. 

Results/Findings: The proposed GPU-based system demonstrates a marked improvement in computational speed over standard CPU implementations, achieving up to a tenfold increase in processing efficiency for LMedS calculations. The efficacy of the approach is validated through extensive empirical testing on various datasets, showing robust performance in line detection tasks. Our method not only retains high accuracy levels but also outperforms existing robust estimators regarding speed without compromising robustness.

Conclusion/Implications: The findings suggest that our GPU-accelerated approach for LMedS is highly effective for applications requiring fast and robust line detection, such as in computer vision and image processing tasks. The use of GPU technology in this context provides a critical advancement, offering a scalable solution that can be adapted to other computational-heavy robust statistical estimators. Our work lays the groundwork for future developments in the efficient processing of robust statistical techniques, contributing significantly to the fields of computer vision and machine learning. 

Keywords: GPU computing, 2D Least Median of Squares, LMedS, robust line detection, high-performance computing, parallel processing, real-time applications.
