**Abstract**  
Video action recognition remains a challenging problem due to the complexity of extracting discriminative spatial and temporal features from dynamic visual data. This paper introduces TSI (Temporal Saliency Integration), a novel framework designed to enhance video action recognition by explicitly integrating temporal saliency information into the recognition pipeline. The proposed approach leverages a two-stage architecture: initially, a temporal saliency module identifies and weights keyframes and motion patterns that are most indicative of target actions; subsequently, these saliency-weighted features are fed into a deep convolutional-neural-network-based backbone for precise action classification. Comprehensive experiments are conducted on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating that TSI consistently outperforms state-of-the-art methods in both accuracy and computational efficiency. Notably, TSI exhibits improved robustness in scenarios with complex backgrounds and partial occlusions, highlighting its adaptability in real-world settings. Our findings indicate that temporal saliency integration not only improves action recognition but also provides critical insights into feature importance and interpretability. The proposed framework has broad implications for surveillance, content analysis, and human-computer interaction applications.  
**Keywords:** video action recognition, temporal saliency, deep learning, feature integration, computer vision.
