Irregularly-sampled clinical time series data pose significant challenges for accurate patient monitoring and prediction. This paper introduces a novel multi-view integration learning framework to effectively handle these irregularities. Our approach leverages multiple representations of the time series data, including time-aware embeddings and frequency domain features, to capture complementary information. We employ a dynamic weighting mechanism to adaptively integrate these views, prioritizing informative signals while mitigating the impact of noisy or missing data. Experimental results on real-world clinical datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art imputation and single-view learning techniques in tasks such as disease prediction and patient risk stratification. The proposed framework offers improved robustness and accuracy for analyzing irregularly-sampled clinical time series, leading to potentially better clinical decision-making.

Keywords: Time series, multi-view learning, irregular sampling, clinical data, disease prediction.
