Skeleton-based action recognition is a challenging task due to the complex spatial and temporal dependencies within human movements. This paper introduces an Attention Enhanced Graph Convolutional LSTM (AGCL) network to effectively model these dependencies. Our approach leverages a graph convolutional network (GCN) to capture spatial relationships between joints in each frame, followed by a long short-term memory (LSTM) network to model temporal dynamics across frames. To further enhance the network's ability to focus on informative joints and frames, we incorporate attention mechanisms within both the GCN and LSTM components. Specifically, a spatial attention module learns to weight the importance of different joints during graph convolution, while a temporal attention module identifies and emphasizes salient frames within the action sequence. Experiments on the NTU RGB+D and Kinetics Skeleton datasets demonstrate that the proposed AGCL network achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods. The results validate the effectiveness of integrating attention mechanisms into graph convolutional LSTM networks for improved skeleton-based action recognition. Keywords: Action Recognition, Skeleton Data, Graph Convolutional Network, LSTM, Attention Mechanism, Deep Learning.
