Stereo depth estimation is a fundamental task in computer vision, crucial for applications such as autonomous driving, 3D scene reconstruction, and robotics. This paper introduces MTStereo 2.0, an enhanced framework for stereo depth estimation that harnesses the power of Max-trees, a hierarchical image representation method, to achieve improved accuracy and robustness. Our approach leverages Max-trees to effectively encode local and global spatial structures, facilitating more precise matching and disparity computation between stereo image pairs. We integrate Max-tree features within a modern deep learning architecture, enabling end-to-end training and optimization. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that MTStereo 2.0 outperforms existing state-of-the-art stereo matching methods in both accuracy and computational efficiency. Notably, our method shows significant improvements in challenging scenarios involving textureless regions and occlusions. The results affirm the benefits of incorporating Max-tree representations for depth estimation tasks. MTStereo 2.0 offers a scalable and adaptable solution suitable for real-time applications requiring reliable depth perception. 

Keywords: stereo depth estimation, Max-trees, disparity computation, computer vision, 3D reconstruction, deep learning, image matching.
