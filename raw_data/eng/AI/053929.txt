Unsupervised Video Object Segmentation (UVOS) aims to identify and segment primary objects in videos without manual annotations, presenting significant challenges in dynamic and complex scenes. This paper introduces a novel approach leveraging instance embedding transfer to enhance UVOS performance. The proposed method transfers knowledge from pre-trained instance segmentation models to the UVOS task, enabling robust object representation and segmentation across frames. Specifically, we employ a two-stage framework: first, instance embeddings are extracted from a pre-trained model to capture object-level features; second, these embeddings are adapted and propagated across video frames using a lightweight temporal module to ensure consistency and accuracy. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of segmentation accuracy and temporal coherence. The key contribution lies in the effective transfer of instance-level knowledge to unsupervised settings, reducing reliance on labeled data while maintaining high segmentation quality. This work opens new avenues for leveraging pre-trained models in unsupervised video analysis, with potential applications in video editing, surveillance, and autonomous systems.  

**Keywords**: Unsupervised Video Object Segmentation, Instance Embedding, Transfer Learning, Temporal Consistency, Object Representation.
