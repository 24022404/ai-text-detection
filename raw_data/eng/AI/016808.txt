Unsupervised many-to-many image translation has garnered significant interest due to its ability to translate between multiple domains without paired data. However, evaluating the correctness of these translations remains challenging. This research aims to assess the accuracy and reliability of translation outputs in unsupervised settings, providing a framework for determining translation effectiveness across multiple image domains.

Methods/Approach: We propose a new evaluation metric for unsupervised many-to-many image translation that incorporates structural similarity, content preservation, and domain-specific features. This metric is designed to quantitatively assess the correctness of image translations. The approach is benchmarked using state-of-the-art translations models like CycleGAN and MUNIT, focusing on datasets with diverse image domains to ensure comprehensive evaluation.

Results/Findings: Our results indicate that the proposed evaluation metric correlates well with human judgment and outperforms existing metrics in measuring translation quality. The framework effectively distinguishes between successful and erroneous translations, providing insights into the strengths and weaknesses of current unsupervised translation models. Notably, the method enhances the assessment of structural and domain-specific attributes maintained during the translation process.

Conclusion/Implications: This study introduces a robust metric for evaluating the correctness of unsupervised many-to-many image translations, offering a more reliable tool for model assessment in various application domains. Our contributions enable researchers and practitioners to better gauge model performance, guiding improvements in unsupervised translation techniques. Potential applications extend to areas such as autonomous driving, medical imaging, and digital media, where accurate domain translation is critical.

Keywords: Unsupervised image translation, many-to-many translation, evaluation metric, model performance, CycleGAN, MUNIT, image domains.
