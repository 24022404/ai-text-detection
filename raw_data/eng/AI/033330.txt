The rapid evolution of 6G Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) communications demands advanced solutions to address the challenges of high mobility, dynamic environments, and stringent latency requirements. This paper proposes a novel deep learning-based framework for learning transferable Multiple-Input Multiple-Output (MIMO) channel modes tailored for 6G V2X scenarios. The objective is to enhance channel estimation and prediction accuracy, enabling robust and efficient communication in highly dynamic vehicular networks. Our approach leverages a transformer-based neural network architecture to model complex MIMO channel characteristics, capturing spatial and temporal dependencies across diverse environments. The model is trained on a comprehensive dataset of simulated and real-world vehicular channel measurements, ensuring generalizability across varying scenarios. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in channel prediction accuracy, achieving up to a 30% reduction in mean squared error compared to state-of-the-art methods. Furthermore, the proposed framework exhibits strong transferability, maintaining high performance when applied to unseen environments and scenarios. The findings highlight the potential of deep learning in enabling reliable and low-latency communication for 6G V2X, paving the way for safer and more efficient autonomous driving systems. This research contributes to the advancement of AI-driven wireless communication technologies, offering a scalable solution for next-generation vehicular networks.  

**Keywords**: 6G, V2X, MIMO, deep learning, transformer models, channel estimation, autonomous driving.
