Event prediction is a critical task in various domains, from healthcare to finance, where accurate and reliable forecasts are essential for decision-making. However, existing models often struggle to provide scalable and uncertainty-aware predictions, limiting their practical applicability. This paper introduces a novel framework for scalable joint models that address these challenges by integrating uncertainty quantification directly into the prediction process. The proposed approach leverages probabilistic graphical models and scalable optimization techniques to jointly model event dependencies and uncertainty, enabling robust predictions even in large-scale datasets. Experiments on real-world datasets demonstrate that the framework outperforms state-of-the-art methods in both prediction accuracy and uncertainty estimation, achieving significant improvements in computational efficiency. The results highlight the model's ability to scale to high-dimensional data while maintaining interpretability and reliability. This research contributes a practical and innovative solution for uncertainty-aware event prediction, with potential applications in risk assessment, resource allocation, and decision support systems. Key keywords include event prediction, uncertainty quantification, probabilistic models, scalability, and decision-making.
