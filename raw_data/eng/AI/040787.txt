This paper proposes a novel framework for discriminative learning using score function features, leveraging matrix and tensor representations to enhance model performance. The objective is to develop a robust and efficient approach for extracting informative features from complex data, addressing the challenges of existing methods. Our approach employs a score function-based technique to learn discriminative features, which are then represented as matrices and tensors to capture higher-order relationships. The results demonstrate significant improvements in classification accuracy and robustness compared to state-of-the-art methods. Key findings include the ability to handle high-dimensional data and noisy environments, with applications in computer vision, natural language processing, and recommender systems. The proposed framework contributes to the advancement of discriminative learning, offering a unique and innovative solution for real-world problems. By highlighting the importance of score function features and matrix/tensor representations, this research provides new insights and opportunities for future studies in machine learning, deep learning, and data analysis, with relevant keywords including score functions, discriminative learning, matrix factorization, tensor decomposition, and feature extraction.
