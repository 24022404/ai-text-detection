This paper introduces the Auto-Parsing Network (APNet), a novel approach aimed at enhancing performance in image captioning and visual question answering (VQA) tasks. The challenge addressed is the effective extraction and interpretation of visual and contextual information from images to generate accurate and contextually relevant captions and answers.

Methods/Approach: APNet employs a hybrid deep learning architecture that integrates convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for image feature extraction and transformers for context processing. The network incorporates an auto-parsing mechanism that dynamically segments elements of an image, assigning semantic roles to facilitate comprehensive understanding. This mechanism enables the model to effectively parse images and engage with multi-modal data, making it adept at generating detailed descriptions and answering complex questions related to images.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate that APNet surpasses state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets such as MSCOCO for image captioning and VQA v2.0 for visual question answering. APNet shows significant improvements in evaluation metrics such as BLEU, METEOR, and CIDEr scores for caption generation and accuracy in VQA. The results suggest that the auto-parsing capability enhances the system’s ability to comprehend and synthesize visual information efficiently.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of the Auto-Parsing Network presents advancements in the fields of image captioning and visual question answering by improving the granularity and accuracy of image interpretation. The research contributes to the development of more intelligent visual AI systems, with potential applications spanning from accessibility technologies to advanced human-computer interaction. APNet lays the groundwork for future exploration into dynamic parsing methods in multi-modal AI frameworks. Key keywords include image captioning, visual question answering, auto-parsing, deep learning, CNN, transformer.
