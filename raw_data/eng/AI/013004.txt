Network adaptation, a critical task in transfer learning, often requires substantial computational resources and can lead to catastrophic forgetting. This paper introduces Side-Tuning, a novel and efficient adaptation technique that utilizes additive side networks to modify pre-trained models. Side-Tuning freezes the original network weights and learns only the parameters of the small side networks, thereby significantly reducing the training overhead and mitigating forgetting. We evaluate Side-Tuning across various benchmark datasets and architectures, demonstrating its competitive performance compared to full fine-tuning while using significantly fewer trainable parameters. Experimental results show that Side-Tuning achieves comparable or superior accuracy in several transfer learning scenarios, particularly in low-data regimes. The simplicity and efficiency of Side-Tuning make it a practical baseline for network adaptation, offering a resource-friendly alternative to traditional fine-tuning approaches. Keywords: Transfer Learning, Network Adaptation, Side Networks, Fine-tuning, Parameter-Efficient Learning.
