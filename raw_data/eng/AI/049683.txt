This paper introduces CNN-Cert, a novel framework designed to efficiently certify the robustness of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) against adversarial attacks. The objective is to provide a reliable and scalable method for evaluating the robustness of CNNs, which is crucial for their deployment in security-critical applications. Our approach leverages a combination of formal verification techniques and neural network analysis to certify the robustness of CNNs. The key innovation of CNN-Cert lies in its ability to efficiently compute tight bounds on the robustness of CNNs, allowing for the certification of large-scale networks. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of CNN-Cert in certifying the robustness of various CNN architectures, outperforming existing methods in terms of efficiency and scalability. The contributions of this research include the development of a novel certification framework, the introduction of new bounds for robustness analysis, and the demonstration of the framework's efficacy in real-world scenarios. This work has significant implications for the development of reliable and secure CNN-based systems, with potential applications in areas such as computer vision, autonomous vehicles, and healthcare. Key keywords: CNNs, robustness certification, adversarial attacks, formal verification, neural network analysis.
