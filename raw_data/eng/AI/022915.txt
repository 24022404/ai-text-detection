This paper addresses the challenge of efficiently integrating sensing and communication functionalities within cellular networks, focusing on the New Radio (NR) sidelink as a means to enhance network capability and resilience. The work aims to advance the convergent use of wireless technologies for both communication and environment sensing, a necessity fueled by emerging applications such as autonomous vehicles and smart cities.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel framework leveraging NR sidelink technology to enable seamless integration of sensing and communication. Our approach utilizes the inherent capabilities of sidelinks for direct device-to-device communication, optimizing the use of spectral resources. The framework incorporates advanced signal processing techniques to accomplish accurate environment sensing while maintaining effective communication pathways, thus capitalizing on the sidelink's proximity awareness and direct communication features.

Results/Findings: Our experiments demonstrate that the proposed framework significantly improves the efficiency of resource utilization in cellular networks. We observe enhanced sensing accuracy and communication reliability compared to traditional centralized approaches. The integrated system outperforms conventional methods, showcasing up to a 30% improvement in network robustness and a reduction in latency for critical applications requiring real-time data exchange and environmental sensing.

Conclusion/Implications: This research presents a pioneering step towards the holistic integration of sensing and communication in cellular networks through NR sidelinks. The proposed framework not only strengthens network performance and flexibility but also paves the way for future technological advancements in 5G and beyond. The implications of this work are broad, offering valuable insights for developing integrated systems that can adapt to the dynamic requirements of next-generation wireless environments.

Keywords: NR Sidelink, cellular networks, sensing integration, device-to-device communication, signal processing, 5G, network robustness.
