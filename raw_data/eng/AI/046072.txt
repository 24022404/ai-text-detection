Video summarization is a challenging task due to the high volume of data and complex temporal dependencies within video sequences. This paper introduces a novel video summarization approach leveraging the power of self-attention mechanisms within a Universal Transformer architecture. Unlike traditional methods that rely on recurrent neural networks or convolutional networks, our approach captures long-range dependencies and global context effectively by applying self-attention across the entire video sequence. The Universal Transformer allows for adaptive computation time, focusing on salient segments and improving efficiency. We evaluate our model on standard video summarization datasets, demonstrating superior performance in terms of F-score and coverage compared to state-of-the-art techniques. The results highlight the effectiveness of self-attention for capturing crucial video content, leading to more accurate and concise video summaries. Our approach offers a scalable and efficient solution for automated video summarization, with potential applications in video surveillance, content recommendation, and automated video editing. Keywords: Video Summarization, Self-Attention, Universal Transformers, Deep Learning, Sequence Modeling.
