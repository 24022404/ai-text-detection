This paper addresses the challenge of learning semantic attributes from weakly supervised data, where only image-level labels are available. Our objective is to accurately recover fine-grained semantic attributes without relying on expensive and time-consuming manual annotation. We propose a novel framework that leverages a combination of attention mechanisms and a multi-instance learning (MIL) approach. Specifically, we employ an attention module to identify relevant image regions corresponding to specific attributes, and integrate it into a MIL framework to handle the ambiguity inherent in weak supervision. Our method effectively learns to associate image regions with semantic attributes using only image-level labels during training. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing weakly supervised methods for semantic attribute prediction, achieving state-of-the-art performance in recovering semantic attributes. The recovered attributes can be used for various downstream tasks, such as zero-shot learning and fine-grained image retrieval. The code and models will be publicly available. Keywords: Weakly Supervised Learning, Semantic Attributes, Multi-Instance Learning, Attention Mechanisms, Image Understanding.
