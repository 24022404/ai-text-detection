This paper proposes VSSA-NET, a novel Vertical Spatial Sequence Attention Network designed to enhance traffic sign detection accuracy. The objective is to address the challenges of detecting traffic signs in complex scenes with varying sizes, orientations, and occlusions. Our approach integrates a vertical spatial sequence attention mechanism into a deep neural network, allowing the model to focus on relevant regions and sequences of traffic signs. The VSSA-NET model is trained on a large dataset of traffic images and achieves state-of-the-art performance in detecting traffic signs. Experimental results show that our method outperforms existing traffic sign detection algorithms, with a significant improvement in precision and recall. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a vertical spatial sequence attention mechanism and the demonstration of its effectiveness in traffic sign detection. This work has important implications for the development of intelligent transportation systems and autonomous vehicles, highlighting the potential of VSSA-NET to improve road safety and efficiency. Key keywords: traffic sign detection, vertical spatial sequence attention, deep learning, computer vision, autonomous vehicles.
