This paper introduces InverseForm, a novel loss function designed to improve the accuracy of structured boundary-aware segmentation tasks. The objective is to address the challenge of segmenting objects with complex boundaries, where existing methods often struggle to balance region and boundary information. Our approach leverages a unique formulation that inversely weighs the importance of boundary pixels, allowing the model to focus on both regional and boundary cues. We evaluate InverseForm on various benchmark datasets, demonstrating its effectiveness in improving segmentation performance, particularly in scenarios with ambiguous or noisy boundaries. The results show that InverseForm outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving higher intersection-over-union scores and better boundary precision. The key contributions of this work include the introduction of a boundary-aware loss function, which enables more accurate segmentation of complex objects, and the demonstration of its applicability to a range of computer vision tasks, including medical image segmentation and autonomous driving. Keywords: boundary-aware segmentation, loss function, computer vision, deep learning, image segmentation.
