Given a k-MdB sequence x as established in Lemma 6 and a specific Lyndon word uk,s characterized by a predetermined sequence of 0's and 1's, there exists a unique index i within x such that the substring at this position exactly matches uk,s. By strategically appending s consecutive zeros to the suffix of x starting immediately after index i, a new word ck,s is generated. Theorem 4 asserts that this resulting word ck,s indeed forms a (k, s)-RdB sequence, satisfying the required properties.
