Bin picking, a critical task in industrial automation, requires precise segmentation of workpieces to enable robotic systems to accurately identify and grasp objects. This paper addresses the challenge of deep workpiece region segmentation in cluttered and unstructured environments, where traditional methods often struggle with occlusion and varying object orientations. We propose a novel deep learning-based approach that leverages a multi-scale convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture enhanced with attention mechanisms to improve segmentation accuracy. The model is trained on a synthetic dataset generated using domain randomization techniques to ensure robustness across diverse real-world scenarios. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves a significant improvement in segmentation performance, with a mean Intersection-over-Union (IoU) score of 92.5%, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods by 8.2%. Furthermore, the proposed approach exhibits superior generalization capabilities when tested on unseen datasets with varying lighting conditions and object configurations. The findings highlight the potential of this method to enhance the efficiency and reliability of bin-picking systems in industrial applications, paving the way for more advanced automation solutions.  

**Keywords**: Bin picking, workpiece segmentation, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, attention mechanisms, industrial automation.
