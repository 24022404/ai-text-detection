This paper proposes a novel target-specific object proposal generation method designed to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of visual tracking systems. The objective is to address the challenge of generating high-quality object proposals that accurately capture the target object's location and boundaries in complex scenes. Our approach leverages a deep learning-based architecture that integrates a target-specific attention mechanism with a region proposal network, allowing for more precise and relevant object proposals. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art object proposal generation techniques, achieving significant improvements in terms of precision, recall, and tracking accuracy. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of target-specific object proposal generation in visual tracking, and our method's ability to effectively handle occlusions, deformations, and background clutter. The proposed technique has significant implications for various computer vision applications, including surveillance, robotics, and autonomous vehicles, and contributes to the advancement of visual tracking research by providing a more effective and efficient object proposal generation solution. Key keywords: visual tracking, object proposal generation, deep learning, target-specific attention, region proposal network.
