This paper addresses the challenge of efficient knowledge transfer in federated learning environments, where data is decentralized and privacy is a concern. Our objective is to develop a novel federated transfer learning framework that leverages dynamic gradient aggregation to improve model performance and adaptability. We propose a method that combines the benefits of transfer learning with the privacy-preserving aspects of federated learning, using a dynamic gradient aggregation approach to selectively update model parameters based on client contributions. Our results show that this approach outperforms traditional federated learning methods, achieving significant improvements in model accuracy and convergence speed. The key findings of this research highlight the potential of dynamic gradient aggregation in federated transfer learning, enabling more efficient and effective collaboration among clients. This work contributes to the development of more robust and adaptable federated learning systems, with potential applications in areas such as edge AI, IoT, and distributed machine learning. Key keywords: federated learning, transfer learning, dynamic gradient aggregation, edge AI, distributed machine learning.
