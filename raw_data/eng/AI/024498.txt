Accurate boundary delineation in image segmentation is a critical challenge in computer vision, particularly for tasks requiring precise object localization and structured outputs. This paper introduces *InverseForm*, a novel loss function designed to enhance boundary-aware segmentation by explicitly modeling the structural relationships between pixels. Unlike traditional loss functions that focus solely on pixel-wise accuracy, *InverseForm* incorporates geometric and topological constraints to improve segmentation quality, especially at object boundaries. The proposed approach leverages an inverse transformation framework to align predicted segmentation maps with ground truth boundaries, ensuring better preservation of fine details and structural integrity. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that *InverseForm* outperforms existing loss functions, achieving significant improvements in boundary precision and overall segmentation accuracy. The results highlight its effectiveness across diverse domains, including medical imaging and autonomous driving. This work contributes a robust, generalizable solution for structured segmentation tasks, with potential applications in areas requiring high-precision boundary detection.  

**Keywords**: boundary-aware segmentation, loss function, inverse transformation, structured prediction, computer vision, image segmentation.
