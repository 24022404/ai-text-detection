This paper presents a comprehensive statistical analysis of classification accuracies for adversarial training, aiming to enhance the robustness of deep learning models against adversarial attacks. Our approach employs a novel methodology that combines statistical hypothesis testing with robust optimization techniques to evaluate the efficacy of adversarial training methods. We investigate the performance of various state-of-the-art adversarial training algorithms, including PGD and FGSM, and compare their classification accuracies on multiple benchmark datasets. Our results show that the proposed statistical framework provides a more accurate and reliable assessment of model robustness, revealing significant improvements in classification accuracy under adversarial attacks. The key findings of this study highlight the importance of statistical analysis in evaluating the effectiveness of adversarial training methods, contributing to the development of more robust and reliable deep learning models. This research has significant implications for the field of machine learning, particularly in applications where model robustness is crucial, such as computer vision and natural language processing. Key keywords: adversarial training, statistical analysis, classification accuracy, deep learning, robust optimization, PGD, FGSM.
