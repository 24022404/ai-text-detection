Constrained optimization in reinforcement learning (RL) is critical for ensuring that policies adhere to safety and operational constraints while maximizing performance. This paper addresses the challenge of optimizing policies under constraints by proposing a novel approach called Constrained Policy Optimization (CPO). The objective is to develop a robust and efficient algorithm that balances reward maximization with constraint satisfaction, particularly in environments where safety and feasibility are paramount.  

The proposed method builds on the foundation of policy gradient algorithms, integrating constraint-handling mechanisms directly into the optimization process. By leveraging Lagrangian duality and adaptive penalty methods, CPO ensures that constraints are respected throughout the learning process. The approach is evaluated on a range of benchmark tasks, including robotic control and autonomous navigation, where constraint violations can have significant real-world consequences.  

Experimental results demonstrate that CPO outperforms existing constrained RL methods in terms of both constraint satisfaction and cumulative reward. The algorithm achieves superior sample efficiency and stability, making it suitable for real-world applications with limited data availability. Furthermore, the method is shown to generalize well across diverse environments, highlighting its robustness.  

This research contributes a practical and scalable solution to constrained policy optimization, offering significant implications for safe and reliable deployment of RL systems in critical domains such as healthcare, robotics, and autonomous systems. The proposed framework opens new avenues for developing AI systems that operate safely and effectively in complex, constrained environments.  

**Keywords**: Reinforcement Learning, Constrained Optimization, Policy Gradient, Safety Constraints, Lagrangian Duality, Autonomous Systems.
