Neural Architecture Search (NAS) automates the design of neural networks, but remains computationally expensive. This paper introduces Smooth Variational Graph Embeddings (SVGE), a novel approach to efficiently explore the architecture search space. SVGE leverages a variational autoencoder to generate smooth, continuous embeddings of graph-based neural architectures. By enforcing smoothness in the latent space, we enable efficient Bayesian optimization for architecture selection. Specifically, the smoothness constraint encourages exploration of architectures with gradual changes, leading to more stable and predictable performance. We evaluate SVGE on several NAS benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in search efficiency compared to state-of-the-art methods. Results show that SVGE achieves comparable or superior performance with significantly fewer architecture evaluations. The proposed method offers a promising avenue for accelerating NAS and discovering high-performing neural architectures more efficiently. Keywords: Neural Architecture Search, Graph Embeddings, Variational Autoencoder, Bayesian Optimization, Smoothness, Deep Learning.
