This paper presents a novel approach to video surveillance of highway traffic events using deep learning architectures. The objective is to develop an automated system that can accurately detect and classify various traffic events, such as accidents, congestion, and road closures, in real-time. A convolutional neural network (CNN) based approach is employed, utilizing a combination of spatial and temporal features extracted from video feeds. The methodology involves training the model on a large dataset of annotated traffic videos, allowing it to learn patterns and anomalies in traffic flow. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed system, achieving high accuracy and efficiency in detecting traffic events. The system's performance is evaluated using metrics such as precision, recall, and mean average precision, showcasing its potential for real-world applications. The contributions of this research include the development of a robust and scalable deep learning framework for traffic event detection, with implications for improved traffic management and safety. Key keywords: deep learning, computer vision, traffic surveillance, convolutional neural networks, intelligent transportation systems.
