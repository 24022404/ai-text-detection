This paper presents a novel algorithm for geodesic active contours, leveraging characteristic functions to enhance segmentation accuracy and computational efficiency. The objective of this research is to address the limitations of traditional active contour models, particularly in handling complex boundaries and noisy images. The proposed approach integrates characteristic functions into the geodesic active contour framework, enabling robust representation of object boundaries and improved convergence properties. The method employs a variational formulation to minimize an energy functional, combining edge-based and region-based information for precise segmentation. Experimental results on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate the algorithm's superior performance compared to existing methods, achieving higher accuracy and reduced computational time. The findings highlight the algorithm's effectiveness in applications such as medical imaging and object detection. This work contributes a significant advancement in active contour techniques, offering a scalable and efficient solution for image segmentation tasks. Keywords: geodesic active contours, characteristic functions, image segmentation, variational methods, edge detection.
