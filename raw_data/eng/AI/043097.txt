Safe exploration in reinforcement learning (RL) remains a critical challenge, particularly in environments where premature termination can lead to catastrophic outcomes. This paper addresses the problem of safe exploration by formulating it as an Early Terminated Markov Decision Process (ET-MDP), a novel framework designed to model and mitigate risks associated with early termination. The proposed approach integrates risk-aware policies with adaptive exploration strategies, enabling agents to learn optimal behaviors while minimizing the likelihood of unsafe states. We introduce a dynamic penalty mechanism that penalizes unsafe actions based on their proximity to termination conditions, ensuring a balance between exploration and safety. Experimental results on benchmark environments demonstrate that our method significantly reduces the occurrence of early terminations compared to traditional RL algorithms, while maintaining competitive performance in achieving task objectives. The framework is further validated in simulated robotic navigation tasks, showcasing its applicability to real-world scenarios. This research contributes a robust and scalable solution for safe exploration, with potential applications in autonomous systems, robotics, and safety-critical domains.  

**Keywords**: Safe exploration, reinforcement learning, early termination, Markov Decision Process, risk-aware policies, adaptive exploration.
