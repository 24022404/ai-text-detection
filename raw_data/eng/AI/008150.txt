This paper addresses the challenge of inefficient sampling in Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) by introducing a novel reparameterized sampling approach. The objective is to improve the quality and diversity of generated samples while reducing the computational cost. Our method leverages a reparameterization technique to transform the latent space, enabling more efficient exploration and sampling. We employ a combination of adversarial training and probabilistic modeling to learn the reparameterized distribution. The results show significant improvements in sample quality, diversity, and mode coverage, outperforming existing sampling methods. Our approach also demonstrates faster convergence and reduced training time. The contributions of this research include a new sampling paradigm for GANs, which can be applied to various applications such as image and data generation, and has potential implications for AI models, deep learning, and computer vision. Key keywords: GANs, reparameterized sampling, generative models, adversarial training, probabilistic modeling, deep learning, AI models.
