This paper presents a novel approach to large-scale interactive motion forecasting for autonomous driving, leveraging the newly introduced Waymo Open Motion Dataset. The objective is to predict the future motions of multiple agents in complex scenarios, enabling safer and more efficient autonomous vehicle navigation. Our method utilizes a combination of machine learning algorithms and graph-based models to forecast the trajectories of vehicles, pedestrians, and other agents. The results demonstrate significant improvements in forecasting accuracy and robustness, particularly in interactive scenarios where agents influence each other's motions. Key findings include the ability to predict complex interactions, such as lane changes and merges, with high accuracy. The contributions of this research lie in its scalability, generalizability, and ability to handle large-scale, real-world datasets. The proposed approach has significant implications for the development of autonomous driving systems, enabling more accurate and reliable motion forecasting in dynamic environments. Relevant keywords: autonomous driving, motion forecasting, interactive systems, graph-based models, machine learning, Waymo Open Motion Dataset.
