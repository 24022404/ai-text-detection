This paper introduces SaliencyMix, a novel data augmentation strategy that leverages saliency maps to guide the mixing of images, enhancing model regularization. The objective is to improve model generalization by focusing on the most relevant features of the input data. Our approach utilizes a saliency detection algorithm to identify critical regions in images and then applies a mixing strategy to combine these regions with other images, creating new, informative training samples. Experimental results demonstrate that SaliencyMix outperforms existing data augmentation techniques, leading to improved model performance and reduced overfitting. The key findings indicate that SaliencyMix enhances model robustness and accuracy, particularly in scenarios with limited training data. The proposed method has significant implications for deep learning applications, offering a simple yet effective way to regularize models and improve their ability to generalize to new, unseen data. By highlighting the importance of saliency-guided data augmentation, this research contributes to the development of more robust and accurate AI models, with potential applications in computer vision, image classification, and object detection. Key keywords: data augmentation, saliency maps, model regularization, deep learning, computer vision.
