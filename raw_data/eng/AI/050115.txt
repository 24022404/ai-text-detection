Deep Convolutional Neural Networks (DCNNs) have achieved remarkable success in various computer vision tasks. However, understanding their decision-making process remains a challenge. Gradient-based visualization techniques like Grad-CAM are crucial for interpreting DCNNs. This paper introduces Smooth Grad-CAM++, an enhanced inference-level visualization technique that addresses the limitations of existing methods, particularly noise sensitivity and gradient saturation. Smooth Grad-CAM++ integrates a smoothing process with Grad-CAM++ to generate more refined and visually interpretable heatmaps. We evaluate our approach on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its ability to provide sharper, less noisy, and more localized visualizations that better highlight the salient image regions contributing to the network's prediction. Comparative analysis shows that Smooth Grad-CAM++ outperforms existing methods in terms of visual quality and localization accuracy, providing more faithful explanations of DCNN decisions. This improved interpretability can aid in model debugging, refinement, and building trust in DCNN-based applications. Keywords: Deep Learning, Explainable AI, Visualization, Grad-CAM, Convolutional Neural Networks, Interpretability.
