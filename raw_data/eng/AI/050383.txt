**Abstract:**

Image denoising remains a critical challenge in computer vision, especially under low-light conditions or high sensor noise, where traditional single-frame approaches often struggle. This paper presents a novel deep learning framework for burst image denoising, termed Deep Burst Denoising. The proposed method leverages sequences of rapidly captured noisy images ("bursts") and exploits inter-frame information to enhance denoising performance. Our approach introduces a deep neural network architecture designed to align and aggregate information across multiple frames, effectively reducing noise while preserving fine image details and textures. Extensive experiments on public burst photography datasets demonstrate that Deep Burst Denoising consistently outperforms state-of-the-art single-frame and multi-frame denoising algorithms, yielding significant improvements in PSNR and SSIM metrics. Furthermore, our model shows robust generalization to various noise patterns and real-world scenarios. The proposed framework not only advances burst denoising techniques but also enables high-quality imaging for smartphone cameras and other consumer devices operating in challenging environments. Keywords: burst denoising, image processing, deep learning, neural networks, computer vision, multi-frame enhancement.
