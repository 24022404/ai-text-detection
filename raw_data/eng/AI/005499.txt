This paper introduces Supervised Topological Maps, a novel approach to combining the strengths of topological data analysis and supervised learning. The objective is to enhance the understanding and interpretation of complex, high-dimensional datasets by leveraging the power of topological features in a supervised learning framework. Our method utilizes a unique algorithm that integrates persistent homology with traditional machine learning techniques, allowing for the identification of meaningful topological patterns that correlate with specific labels or outcomes. The results demonstrate significant improvements in classification accuracy and robustness compared to conventional supervised learning methods, particularly in scenarios with noisy or incomplete data. Key findings highlight the ability of Supervised Topological Maps to uncover hidden relationships and structures within the data, leading to more accurate predictions and deeper insights. This research contributes to the advancement of data analysis and machine learning, with potential applications in fields such as computer vision, biomedical research, and network analysis, and is particularly relevant to areas involving topological data analysis, supervised learning, and feature extraction.
