The research addresses the challenge of efficiently improving reinforcement learning (RL) policies with a focus on balancing exploration with safety. The objective is to propose a method that accelerates policy improvement by diversifying exploration while ensuring the safe execution of actions within an environment.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel framework for RL that integrates a diverse exploration strategy with a safety-aware policy optimization process. This approach employs an ensemble of policies that facilitate broad exploration and a safety module that evaluates the potential risk of actions. By leveraging a combination of stochastic sampling techniques and safety constraints, our method extends the versatility of standard RL algorithms, aiming to enhance their exploration capabilities without compromising safety.

Results/Findings: Experimentation with various simulated control tasks demonstrates that our approach achieves faster policy convergence and higher reward acquisition compared to conventional exploration strategies. Key findings indicate that the diversification of the exploration process leads to a more comprehensive understanding of the environment, while the safety constraints effectively mitigate the risk of harmful actions. Comparative analysis with existing exploration and safety protocols highlights the efficiency and robustness of our model, showing marked improvements in both policy performance and operational safety.

Conclusion/Implications: The proposed approach offers significant advancements in the realm of safe and efficient RL policy improvement. By pioneering a balanced exploration strategy, this research contributes to the development of RL systems that are better equipped to handle complex environments with inherent risks. Potential applications span various domains requiring reliable autonomous systems, such as robotics, finance, and resource management, where both performance and safety are paramount. Key Keywords: reinforcement learning, policy improvement, exploration, safety, ensemble methods, stochastic sampling.
