Deep learning, a prominent branch of machine learning, leverages artificial neural networks that simulate the intricate structure of the human brain, utilizing interconnected layers of artificial neurons to process information. These multi-layered networks allow automatic extraction and learning of important features from raw data, significantly reducing the need for manual feature engineering and enabling more accurate and flexible solutions. Thanks to these powerful capabilities, machine learning and deep learning techniques have found extensive applications in diverse fields such as finance, biology, agriculture, robotics, and the internet, transforming industries and driving innovation in countless real-world scenarios.
