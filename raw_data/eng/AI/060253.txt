The increasing involvement of cyclists in traffic accidents highlights the need for effective safety measures. This paper introduces a novel context-aware and video-based risk descriptor designed to enhance cyclist safety by providing an assessment of risk levels in real-time.

Methods/Approach: The proposed system utilizes machine learning algorithms to process video data captured from cameras mounted on bicycles or within surrounding environments. By incorporating sensor data and contextual information, such as weather conditions and traffic density, the model assesses the risk of potential collisions or hazardous situations. The system employs cutting-edge computer vision techniques to analyze cyclist behavior and evaluate environmental factors influencing safety.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate the effectiveness of our risk descriptor, showing an improvement in the detection and prediction of high-risk scenarios for cyclists in various urban settings. Our approach outperforms existing methods by achieving a higher accuracy rate in real-world conditions. The implementation also allows for seamless integration with existing smart city infrastructure, offering real-time alerts to cyclists and potentially reducing accident rates.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes significantly to the field of intelligent transportation systems by introducing an innovative solution to enhance cyclist safety. The development of a context-aware risk assessment tool has profound implications for urban planning and smart city initiatives. Future applications could extend to other vulnerable road users, fostering a safer and more efficient traffic ecosystem. Our study lays the groundwork for further advancements in video-based and sensor-enabled safety technologies.

Keywords: cyclist safety, risk assessment, context-aware, machine learning, computer vision, urban transportation, intelligent transportation systems.
