The project’s system is not yet fully complete, primarily because of time and capacity constraints, resulting in several unresolved issues and only a handful of implemented functions. Additionally, the system has not been deployed widely enough to gather comprehensive user feedback, and some planned features remain unimplemented. Despite these shortcomings, the team acknowledges these limitations and intends to address them in future updates. Nevertheless, the current version of the system fulfills the primary objectives established at the outset of the project, providing a solid foundation for further enhancements.
