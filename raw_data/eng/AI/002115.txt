This thesis is meticulously organized to provide a comprehensive exploration of a cutting-edge solution in the field of blockchain-based authentication. The structure begins in Chapter 1, which introduces the motivation behind the research, outlines the primary contributions, and presents an overview of the organization of the thesis. This initial framework is crucial for orienting the reader and clarifying the aims and scope of the work. Chapter 2 delves into foundational concepts, including blockchain technology, smart contracts, distributed key generation, and the principles of social login within the context of Web3. These core technologies form the backbone of the proposed approach, equipping readers with the essential knowledge required to fully comprehend subsequent chapters. In addition, the background section supplements this foundational knowledge, providing deeper insight into the key technologies that underpin the thesis’s innovations. Chapter 3 is dedicated to a detailed account of the novel solution put forth to address the challenges previously identified within the scope of secure and user-friendly authentication for decentralized applications. It discusses the architecture, mechanisms, and rationale for the design choices, thereby illustrating how the solution advances the field. Following this, Chapter 4 provides a critical analysis of the technical and design challenges encountered during the implementation phase, offering valuable lessons and practical considerations drawn from real-world development experiences. Chapter 5 presents a rigorous evaluation of the proposed solution, focusing on its performance metrics and usability characteristics, to demonstrate both its effectiveness and potential impact. The work concludes in Chapter 6, where the key findings are synthesized and future research directions are proposed, encouraging further exploration and refinement of the technologies discussed. Finally, the reference section documents all sources cited throughout the thesis, ensuring the highest standards of academic integrity and enabling readers to trace the lineage of ideas and findings that have informed this research.
