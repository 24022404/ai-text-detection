Graph matching is a fundamental problem in computer science with applications in pattern recognition, image analysis, and network analysis. Traditional algorithms for computing optimal assignments between graphs often have high computational complexity, making them impractical for large datasets or real-time applications. This paper presents a novel linear-time algorithm for computing optimal assignments in the context of approximate graph matching. The proposed method leverages efficient data structures and approximation strategies to significantly reduce the computational burden while maintaining high assignment quality. Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves comparable or superior matching accuracy to existing methods, with a substantial reduction in runtime. The results indicate that the algorithm scales well with increasing graph size and structural complexity. This work provides an important contribution to the field by enabling fast and accurate approximate graph matching, with potential applications in large-scale data analytics, computer vision, and bioinformatics. Key keywords: approximate graph matching, optimal assignment, linear-time algorithm, pattern recognition, large-scale data analysis.
