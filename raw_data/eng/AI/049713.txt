Energy harvesting sensors are pivotal in enabling sustainable and autonomous IoT systems, yet their configuration and scalability remain challenging due to dynamic environmental conditions and resource constraints. This paper addresses the problem of optimizing the configuration of energy harvesting sensors to maximize efficiency and longevity while maintaining reliable performance. We propose a novel reinforcement learning (RL)-based approach that dynamically adapts sensor parameters, such as sampling rates and transmission intervals, based on real-time energy availability and environmental data. Our method leverages a deep Q-network (DQN) to learn optimal policies for energy allocation and task scheduling, ensuring robust operation under varying energy harvesting conditions. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms traditional static and heuristic-based methods, achieving up to 30% improvement in energy utilization and extending sensor lifetime by 25%. Furthermore, the proposed framework is scalable and adaptable to diverse sensor networks, making it a promising solution for large-scale IoT deployments. This research contributes to advancing energy-efficient sensor networks and highlights the potential of RL in addressing complex configuration challenges in resource-constrained environments.  

**Keywords**: energy harvesting sensors, reinforcement learning, IoT, deep Q-network, energy efficiency, scalability.
