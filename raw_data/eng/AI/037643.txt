This paper proposes an innovative approach to image augmentation using adversarial policy gradient methods in deep learning. The objective is to improve the robustness and generalizability of image classification models by generating diverse and realistic augmented images. Our approach employs a policy gradient framework, where an agent learns to perturb input images in a way that maximizes the uncertainty of the classifier, thereby enhancing its ability to recognize varied patterns. We utilize a deep reinforcement learning algorithm to train the agent, which adaptively selects the most effective augmentation policies. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms traditional augmentation techniques, achieving state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of adversarial policy gradient in generating informative and diverse augmentations, leading to improved model robustness and accuracy. This research contributes to the development of more reliable and generalizable image classification systems, with potential applications in computer vision, robotics, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: adversarial policy gradient, deep learning, image augmentation, reinforcement learning, computer vision.
