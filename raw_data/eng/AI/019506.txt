Person re-identification (Re-ID) is a critical task in computer vision, aiming to match individuals across non-overlapping camera views. Despite significant progress, existing methods often struggle with intra-class variations and inter-class similarities, limiting their effectiveness. This paper introduces **Cluster Loss**, a novel loss function designed to enhance feature discrimination and clustering in person Re-ID. Unlike traditional triplet or contrastive losses, Cluster Loss leverages the inherent structure of feature clusters to optimize both intra-class compactness and inter-class separation. We propose a deep learning framework that integrates Cluster Loss with a convolutional neural network (CNN) backbone, enabling end-to-end training. Experiments on benchmark datasets, including Market-1501 and DukeMTMC-reID, demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of rank-1 accuracy and mean average precision (mAP). The results highlight the effectiveness of Cluster Loss in improving feature representation for person Re-ID. This work contributes a robust and scalable solution to the Re-ID problem, with potential applications in surveillance, security, and video analytics.  

**Keywords**: Person re-identification, Cluster Loss, feature discrimination, deep learning, computer vision, CNN.
