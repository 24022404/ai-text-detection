Spectral clustering is a powerful technique for community detection in graphs, but its performance degrades significantly in sparse graphs due to unreliable eigenvalue estimates. This paper introduces a novel unified framework that addresses this challenge by integrating adaptive graph filtering with a robust spectral embedding. Our method utilizes a data-driven approach to design a graph filter that enhances the signal-to-noise ratio of the spectral embedding, leading to improved clustering accuracy in sparse scenarios. The framework incorporates a regularization term that promotes sparsity and stability in the learned filter. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach on a variety of synthetic and real-world sparse graph datasets, showing significant improvements over existing spectral clustering algorithms, including normalized cuts and ratio cuts. The proposed framework offers a practical and scalable solution for clustering sparse graphs, with applications in social network analysis, bioinformatics, and image segmentation. Key words: Spectral Clustering, Sparse Graphs, Graph Filtering, Community Detection, Spectral Embedding.
