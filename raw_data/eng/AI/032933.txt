Time series classification is a critical task in various domains, including finance, healthcare, and IoT, where accurate and efficient classification methods are essential. This paper introduces the Canonical Interval Forest (CIF) classifier, a novel ensemble-based approach designed to address the challenges of time series classification. The CIF classifier leverages a combination of canonical interval features and ensemble learning to improve classification accuracy and robustness. The proposed method extracts discriminative features from time series intervals, applies a canonical transformation to enhance feature representation, and employs a forest of decision trees to aggregate predictions. Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the CIF classifier outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. Key findings reveal that the CIF classifier achieves superior performance, particularly in handling complex and high-dimensional time series data. The results highlight the potential of the CIF classifier as a powerful tool for time series classification tasks, offering significant improvements over traditional approaches. This research contributes to the field by introducing a scalable and effective method that can be applied across diverse real-world applications.  

**Keywords**: Time series classification, Canonical Interval Forest, ensemble learning, feature extraction, decision trees, machine learning.
