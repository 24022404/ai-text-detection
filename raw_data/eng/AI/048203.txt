**Abstract**

Image quantization, a fundamental process in digital image compression and transmission, often results in visible artifacts and loss of detail, hindering visual quality and downstream image analysis. This paper addresses the image de-quantization problem by leveraging generative models as powerful priors to restore high-quality images from quantized inputs. We propose a novel framework that integrates a state-of-the-art deep generative model—specifically, a pre-trained Generative Adversarial Network (GAN)—to guide the inverse mapping from quantized images to their original, high-resolution counterparts. Our method formulates de-quantization as an optimization problem where the solution is constrained to the natural image manifold learned by the generative model. Experimental results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms traditional restoration techniques and recent data-driven methods in terms of both quantitative metrics, such as PSNR and SSIM, and perceptual quality. Importantly, our framework generalizes to various quantization schemes and does not require retraining for different settings. The results suggest that deep generative priors provide a robust and flexible solution for recovering lost visual fidelity. This work advances the state-of-the-art in image restoration and highlights the potential of generative models in addressing fundamental challenges in image processing. Keywords: image de-quantization, generative models, GAN, image restoration, deep learning, inverse problems.
