Time series forecasting is a critical task in numerous domains, including finance, healthcare, and energy management. However, training deep learning models for accurate time series predictions remains challenging due to the complex temporal dependencies and the need to preserve both shape and temporal characteristics of the data. This paper introduces a novel loss function, the *Shape and Time Distortion Loss (STDL)*, designed to address these challenges by simultaneously optimizing for shape accuracy and temporal alignment in predicted sequences. The proposed loss function integrates dynamic time warping (DTW) to account for temporal distortions and a shape-based component to ensure fidelity in the predicted patterns. We evaluate STDL on multiple benchmark datasets, comparing it against traditional loss functions such as Mean Squared Error (MSE) and DTW-based losses. Experimental results demonstrate that models trained with STDL achieve superior performance in both shape preservation and temporal alignment, outperforming existing methods in terms of prediction accuracy and robustness. The findings highlight the importance of jointly optimizing for shape and time distortions in time series forecasting, offering a significant advancement in the training of deep learning models for this domain. This work has broad implications for applications requiring precise and reliable time series predictions, such as anomaly detection, resource planning, and predictive maintenance.  

**Keywords**: Time series forecasting, deep learning, dynamic time warping, shape distortion, temporal alignment, loss function.
