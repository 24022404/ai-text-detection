Vision Transformers (ViTs) have shown promising performance in various computer vision tasks, but their computational cost and memory requirements often limit their applicability to high-resolution images. This paper introduces PSViT, a novel Vision Transformer architecture designed to improve efficiency and performance through token pooling and attention sharing mechanisms. PSViT employs a learnable pooling strategy to reduce the number of tokens processed in deeper layers, thereby decreasing computational overhead. Furthermore, it introduces an attention sharing module that reuses attention weights across multiple transformer blocks, reducing the model's parameter count without significantly impacting accuracy. Experimental results on ImageNet classification demonstrate that PSViT achieves comparable or superior accuracy to existing ViT models while requiring fewer computational resources and parameters. Specifically, PSViT achieves a 1.2% improvement in top-1 accuracy compared to DeiT-S with a 20% reduction in parameters. The proposed architecture offers a more efficient and scalable solution for vision transformer-based applications, enabling deployment in resource-constrained environments. Keywords: Vision Transformer, Token Pooling, Attention Sharing, Image Classification, Deep Learning, Computer Vision.
