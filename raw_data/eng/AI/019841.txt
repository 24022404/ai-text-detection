Real-time physically-based rendering (PBR) on embedded devices presents a significant challenge due to limited computational resources. This paper introduces LumiPath, a novel rendering pipeline optimized for achieving high-fidelity visuals on resource-constrained platforms. LumiPath employs a hybrid approach combining path tracing with precomputed radiance transfer (PRT) to balance accuracy and performance. We leverage a spatially-aware sampling strategy and a customized material representation tailored for embedded systems to minimize computational overhead. The system is implemented using a custom rendering engine, allowing for fine-grained control over the rendering process. Performance evaluation demonstrates that LumiPath achieves interactive frame rates while maintaining visual quality comparable to offline PBR renderers. The results highlight the feasibility of implementing advanced rendering techniques on embedded devices, opening up new possibilities for mobile gaming, augmented reality, and other applications. Keywords: Physically-based rendering, path tracing, embedded systems, real-time rendering, mobile graphics, precomputed radiance transfer.
