This paper introduces a novel Knowledge-Guided Multi-Label Few-Shot Learning (KG-MLFSL) approach for general image recognition, addressing the challenge of limited labeled data. Our method leverages external knowledge graphs to augment the feature space and guide the learning process, enabling effective multi-label classification with only a few examples per class. Specifically, we incorporate semantic relationships extracted from knowledge graphs to construct a knowledge-aware feature representation, which enhances the discriminative power of learned features. A multi-label classification module is then designed to predict the presence of multiple objects in an image based on the augmented features. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that KG-MLFSL significantly outperforms existing few-shot learning methods, especially in scenarios with highly overlapping labels. The proposed approach achieves superior performance in both classification accuracy and generalization ability, highlighting the benefits of integrating external knowledge into the few-shot learning framework. This work contributes to advancing the state-of-the-art in few-shot learning and provides a promising direction for general image recognition with limited supervision.

Keywords: Few-Shot Learning, Multi-Label Classification, Knowledge Graph, Image Recognition, Meta-Learning.
