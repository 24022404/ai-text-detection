This paper presents a novel approach to forecasting time series data, addressing the long-standing challenge of accurately predicting future values in complex and dynamic systems. Our objective is to develop a robust and efficient framework for time series forecasting, leveraging recent advances in machine learning and statistical modeling. We propose a new algorithm that combines the strengths of traditional autoregressive integrated moving average (ARIMA) models with the flexibility of deep learning techniques, such as recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and long short-term memory (LSTM) networks. Our results show significant improvements in forecasting accuracy and computational efficiency compared to existing methods, particularly in cases with non-stationary and nonlinear data. The key contributions of this research include the development of a hybrid model that adapts to changing patterns in time series data and the introduction of a new evaluation metric that assesses both accuracy and uncertainty in forecasting. Our approach has important implications for applications in finance, economics, and environmental monitoring, where accurate time series forecasting is critical for informed decision-making. Key keywords: time series forecasting, machine learning, deep learning, ARIMA, RNN, LSTM, hybrid models.
