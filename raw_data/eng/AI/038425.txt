The thesis derives explicit formulas for the maximal length and asymptotic rate of RdB sequences, contributing valuable theoretical foundations to the field. It also introduces an optimal encoding algorithm that achieves constant amortized time per symbol, ensuring practical efficiency in sequence generation. Furthermore, a novel decoding algorithm, inspired by the granddaddy sequence decoder, is proposed with sub-linear computational complexity. Both the encoding and decoding algorithms are state-of-the-art, supporting advanced features such as efficient substring position locating within the encoded sequence.
