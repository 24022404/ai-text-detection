The increasing frequency and intensity of natural disasters necessitate efficient and accurate assessment of building damage to support disaster management and recovery efforts. This study introduces BDANet, a novel multiscale convolutional neural network (CNN) enhanced with cross-directional attention mechanisms, designed to improve the assessment of building damage using satellite imagery.

Methods/Approach: BDANet leverages advanced deep learning techniques to process multiscale features within satellite images, incorporating a unique cross-directional attention mechanism that captures context from both local and global perspectives. The architecture enables the network to focus on critical image areas, enhancing damage detection accuracy. The proposed model was tested on satellite image datasets that contain varied environmental and infrastructural contexts to ensure robustness and generalizability.

Results/Findings: The evaluation of BDANet showed superior performance in identifying and quantifying building damage when compared to established state-of-the-art methodologies. Key findings include improved precision and recall rates, demonstrating the model’s ability to reduce false positives and enhance detection sensitivity. Quantitative comparisons illustrate significant advancements over traditional CNNs, showcasing the effectiveness of the integrated attention mechanisms.

Conclusion/Implications: This research presents BDANet as a significant contribution to the field of remote sensing and disaster management, offering an innovative tool for rapid and precise building damage assessment. The cross-directional attention framework within a multiscale CNN architecture sets a new benchmark for image analysis in post-disaster scenarios. The model’s adaptability to different satellite image resolutions and environmental conditions suggests wide applicability in various geographical and disaster contexts. Potential applications include real-time disaster response, urban planning, and resilience building strategies. 

Keywords: BDANet, building damage assessment, satellite images, multiscale CNN, cross-directional attention, disaster management, remote sensing.
