This paper introduces a novel video summarization approach leveraging multi-concept attention to generate concise and informative summaries. Existing methods often struggle to capture the semantic richness of videos, leading to suboptimal summaries. Our method addresses this by transforming attention weights derived from multiple concepts into a coherent representation that highlights salient segments within the video. Specifically, we employ a multi-head attention mechanism, where each head focuses on a distinct visual concept extracted from pre-trained models. The resulting attention maps are then fused and refined using a temporal smoothing filter to ensure temporal consistency. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of both F-score and user preference, outperforming existing methods by a significant margin. The proposed method offers a robust and effective solution for video summarization, with potential applications in video surveillance, content recommendation, and automated video editing. Keywords: Video Summarization, Multi-Concept Attention, Attention Mechanism, Deep Learning, Temporal Smoothing, Video Analysis.
