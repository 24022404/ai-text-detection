Network compression techniques are crucial for deploying deep learning models on resource-constrained devices. This paper introduces a novel adversarial network compression framework that leverages a generative adversarial network (GAN) to guide the compression process. The generator network learns to compress the original model, while the discriminator network distinguishes between the outputs of the compressed and original models. This adversarial training process encourages the compressed model to maintain the performance of the original model while significantly reducing its size and computational complexity. Our method is evaluated on several benchmark datasets and model architectures, demonstrating superior compression rates compared to existing pruning and quantization techniques, with minimal impact on accuracy. The results show a substantial reduction in model parameters and computational cost, making it suitable for edge deployment scenarios. Key words: Network Compression, Adversarial Learning, Generative Adversarial Networks, Model Optimization, Deep Learning, Pruning, Quantization.
