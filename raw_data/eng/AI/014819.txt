Time series forecasting is a critical task in numerous domains, including finance, energy, and healthcare, where accurate predictions are essential for informed decision-making. Traditional forecasting models often struggle to capture the complex temporal dependencies and the underlying geometric structures present in time series data. In this paper, we introduce a novel Topological Attention mechanism, which integrates topological data analysis (TDA) with attention-based neural networks, to enhance time series forecasting performance. The proposed approach leverages persistent homology to extract topological features that characterize the data's intrinsic shape and multiscale patterns. These features are then incorporated into an attention framework, enabling the model to dynamically prioritize relevant temporal and topological relationships. We evaluate our method on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in forecasting accuracy compared to conventional recurrent, convolutional, and transformer-based models. Furthermore, our ablation studies confirm the effectiveness of integrating topological information within the attention paradigm. The results suggest that Topological Attention offers a promising direction for the fusion of geometric and neural approaches in temporal modeling. This work advances the state-of-the-art in time series forecasting and opens new avenues for combining topology and deep learning in sequence analysis.

Keywords: time series forecasting, topological data analysis, persistent homology, attention mechanism, deep learning, neural networks
