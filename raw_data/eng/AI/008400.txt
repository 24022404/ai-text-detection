This paper introduces Vispi, an automated system for visual perception and interpretation of chest X-ray images, addressing the challenge of efficient and accurate radiological diagnosis. Vispi employs a novel deep learning architecture, integrating convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for feature extraction with recurrent neural networks (RNNs) for contextual understanding of anatomical structures and disease patterns. The system is trained on a large dataset of labeled chest X-rays, utilizing a multi-task learning approach to simultaneously detect multiple thoracic diseases and abnormalities. Experimental results demonstrate that Vispi achieves state-of-the-art performance in detecting various conditions, including pneumonia, pneumothorax, and cardiomegaly, surpassing existing automated methods in terms of both accuracy and speed. Furthermore, Vispi provides interpretable visual explanations for its diagnoses, enhancing clinician trust and facilitating clinical decision-making. This research contributes a robust and efficient tool for automated chest X-ray analysis, with potential applications in radiology departments, emergency rooms, and remote healthcare settings.

**Keywords:** Chest X-ray, Deep Learning, Medical Imaging, Computer-Aided Diagnosis, Pneumonia Detection, CNN, RNN, Multi-task Learning.
