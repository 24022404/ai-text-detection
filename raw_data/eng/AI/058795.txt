Face attribute manipulation is a critical task in computer vision with applications in image editing, privacy preservation, and entertainment. This paper introduces a novel approach to face attribute manipulation by learning residual images, which capture the subtle differences required to modify specific facial attributes while preserving the overall identity and natural appearance. Our method leverages a deep learning framework that combines convolutional neural networks (CNNs) with a residual learning mechanism, enabling precise and efficient attribute adjustments. The proposed model is trained on a large-scale dataset of facial images annotated with multiple attributes, ensuring robustness and generalization across diverse scenarios. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of attribute manipulation accuracy, image quality, and identity preservation. Furthermore, the model exhibits superior performance in handling complex attributes such as age, expression, and hairstyle. The key contribution of this work lies in its ability to generate high-quality, realistic images with minimal artifacts, making it suitable for real-world applications. This research advances the state-of-the-art in face attribute manipulation and provides a foundation for future developments in image synthesis and editing technologies.  

**Keywords**: Face attribute manipulation, residual learning, convolutional neural networks, image editing, deep learning, computer vision.
