Abstract:

This paper presents a novel approach to arbitrary style transfer by incorporating depth-awareness into the style transfer pipeline using instance normalization. Recent advances in neural style transfer have achieved impressive results in transferring artistic styles onto content images, but often at the expense of spatial coherence, leading to distorted depth and object boundaries. Our method introduces a depth estimation module to extract depth information from the input image, which is then integrated with instance normalization layers within the style transfer network. By leveraging depth cues, the model is able to preserve important structural features and depth consistency while performing style transfer. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our depth-aware instance normalization method significantly improves the perceptual quality of stylized images, particularly in preserving object contours and background separation, compared to traditional style transfer techniques. Qualitative and quantitative evaluations reveal enhancements in both subjective visual appeal and objective metrics such as structural similarity. The proposed system operates efficiently and is compatible with real-time processing requirements. The contributions of this study highlight the effectiveness of combining geometric priors, specifically depth information, with normalization strategies in neural style transfer, setting a foundation for further advancements in 3D-aware image synthesis, content creation, and augmented reality applications. Keywords: style transfer, depth estimation, instance normalization, neural networks, image synthesis, computer vision.
