This paper addresses the challenge of optimal data collection for context-conditional dynamics models, a crucial aspect of improving prediction accuracy in complex systems. Our objective is to develop a framework that efficiently gathers relevant data to enhance model performance in dynamic environments. We propose a novel approach that leverages reinforcement learning and Bayesian optimization to identify the most informative data points, reducing the need for extensive and costly data collection. Our method, termed Context-Opt, outperforms existing techniques by adaptively selecting data that maximizes the model's predictive power. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in model accuracy and reduced data requirements. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of context-aware data collection and its potential applications in areas such as robotics, autonomous systems, and time-series forecasting. By exploring the context in which data is collected, our work contributes to the development of more efficient and effective dynamics models, with implications for improved decision-making and control in complex systems. Key keywords: context-conditional dynamics models, optimal data collection, reinforcement learning, Bayesian optimization, predictive modeling.
