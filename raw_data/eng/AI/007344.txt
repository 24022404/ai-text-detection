This paper addresses the challenging problem of tracking pedestrian heads in dense crowds, a crucial task for various applications such as surveillance, crowd management, and autonomous vehicles. Our objective is to develop a robust and efficient tracking system that can accurately detect and track individual heads in highly congested environments. We propose a novel approach that combines deep learning-based detection with a multi-target tracking algorithm, leveraging the strengths of both to achieve state-of-the-art performance. Our method utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) to detect pedestrian heads and a Kalman filter-based tracker to predict and update head trajectories. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving a significant improvement in tracking accuracy and robustness compared to existing methods. The key contributions of this research include the development of a highly accurate head detection model and a robust tracking framework that can handle dense crowds and occlusions. Our work has important implications for various real-world applications, including crowd monitoring, safety management, and intelligent transportation systems. Key keywords: pedestrian tracking, crowd analysis, deep learning, computer vision, surveillance.
