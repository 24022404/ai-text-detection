This paper introduces a novel approach to the differential geometry of curves by leveraging multiview geometric principles. The objective is to reconstruct and analyze the geometric properties of curves from multiple perspective views, addressing the challenges posed by occlusions and noisy data in single-view analysis. Our method employs a combination of epipolar geometry and spline-based curve representations to establish correspondences between views and estimate tangent and curvature properties. We present a multiview curvature estimator that significantly improves accuracy compared to single-view estimations, particularly in regions of high curvature. Experimental results on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate the robustness and effectiveness of our approach, achieving a reduction in curvature estimation error of up to 30% compared to existing methods. The findings contribute to enhanced 3D curve reconstruction, shape analysis, and object recognition in computer vision applications. Keywords: Multiview Geometry, Differential Geometry, Curves, 3D Reconstruction, Curvature Estimation, Computer Vision.
