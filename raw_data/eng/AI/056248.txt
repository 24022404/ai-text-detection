This paper addresses the challenge of accurately identifying vehicle make, model, and color from images captured at various angles, a task that has significant applications in areas such as traffic monitoring and security. Traditional methods often depend on fixed viewpoints, which limits their effectiveness in diverse real-world scenarios.

Methods/Approach: We propose a robust approach utilizing convolutional neural networks (CNN) to achieve view-independent vehicle recognition. Our model is designed to process vehicle images and learn distinguishing features that are invariant to changes in viewing angle. This is achieved by employing a multi-branch architecture that independently predicts vehicle make, model, and color, allowing for comprehensive vehicle characterization.

Results/Findings: The proposed model was evaluated on a large-scale dataset comprising images of vehicles captured from multiple perspectives. Our findings demonstrate that the CNN-based model significantly outperforms existing methods, achieving high accuracy rates in recognizing vehicle make, model, and color irrespective of the viewpoint. Comparative analysis shows marked improvements in system performance over traditional viewpoint-dependent techniques.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a novel approach to vehicle recognition that enhances accuracy across various viewing angles, expanding the practicality of automated vehicle identification systems. The implications of this work are profound, facilitating advancements in intelligent transportation systems, law enforcement, and urban planning. Future development could explore extending this model to integrate additional vehicle attributes and adapt to real-time processing environments.

Keywords: Vehicle recognition, CNN, viewpoint invariance, traffic monitoring, automated identification.
