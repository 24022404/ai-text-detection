Photorealistic style transfer aims to synthesize images with the content of a source photograph and the style of a reference image, while preserving photorealism. This paper introduces a novel approach leveraging wavelet transforms to achieve high-quality photorealistic style transfer. Our method decomposes images into different frequency bands using a wavelet transform, allowing for independent manipulation of style at various scales. Specifically, we transfer the style by adapting the statistical characteristics of wavelet coefficients between the style and content images, followed by an inverse wavelet transform to reconstruct the stylized image. To further enhance photorealism, we introduce an adaptive regularization term in the wavelet domain, minimizing artifacts and preserving local structure. Experiments demonstrate that our method generates visually compelling results, outperforming existing style transfer techniques in terms of both style fidelity and photorealism, particularly in preserving fine details and avoiding distortions. Key words: style transfer, wavelet transform, photorealism, image processing, computer vision.
