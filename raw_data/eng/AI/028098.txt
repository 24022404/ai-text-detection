**Abstract:**

Video summarization aims to condense lengthy video content into concise and informative summaries, facilitating efficient browsing and information retrieval. In this paper, we introduce a novel approach, Query Twice: Dual Mixture Attention Meta Learning (QMAML), designed to enhance video summarization performance by leveraging dual attention mechanisms and meta learning strategies. Our method employs a dual mixture attention framework that captures both temporal dependencies and diverse semantic features within video sequences. By querying video features twice with mixed attention heads, QMAML dynamically adjusts its focus to highlight key information while suppressing redundancy. Furthermore, we incorporate meta learning techniques to enable rapid adaptation to new video genres with limited supervision, significantly improving generalization across diverse video datasets. Experiments conducted on benchmark video summarization datasets demonstrate that QMAML outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of summary quality, coverage, and diversity. The results indicate substantial gains in both F-score and user satisfaction metrics, highlighting the model’s effectiveness and robustness. Our approach offers a scalable and adaptive solution for automatic video summarization, with potential applications in media management, video analytics, and content recommendation systems.

**Keywords:** video summarization, dual mixture attention, meta learning, temporal dependencies, adaptive video analysis, deep learning, content summarization
