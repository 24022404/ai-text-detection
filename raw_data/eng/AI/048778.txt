This paper presents a comprehensive survey of benchmarking frameworks for reinforcement learning, aiming to provide a unified understanding of the current landscape and identify key challenges. We employ a systematic approach to categorize and compare existing frameworks, highlighting their strengths, weaknesses, and applicability to various reinforcement learning scenarios. Our analysis reveals significant variations in evaluation metrics, environment simulations, and agent performance assessments across different frameworks. The results show that while some frameworks excel in specific domains, others demonstrate broader versatility and adaptability. We conclude that a standardized benchmarking framework is essential for fair comparison and reproducibility in reinforcement learning research. Our survey contributes to the development of more robust and reliable evaluation methodologies, ultimately advancing the field of reinforcement learning and its applications in areas like robotics, game playing, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, benchmarking frameworks, evaluation metrics, agent performance, reproducibility.
