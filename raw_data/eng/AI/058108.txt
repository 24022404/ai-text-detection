Reinforcement Learning (RL) has emerged as a powerful paradigm for decision-making in complex environments. However, ensuring rational behavior in RL agents remains a significant challenge, particularly in scenarios requiring long-term planning and adaptability. This paper introduces a set of formal axioms designed to guide the development of rational reinforcement learning agents. These axioms address key aspects such as reward consistency, optimality under uncertainty, and adaptability to dynamic environments. We propose a novel framework that integrates these axioms into existing RL algorithms, ensuring that agents exhibit rational decision-making while maintaining computational efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms traditional RL methods in both simulated and real-world environments, particularly in tasks requiring strategic foresight and robustness to environmental changes. The proposed axioms and framework provide a foundational basis for advancing the field of rational RL, with potential applications in autonomous systems, robotics, and adaptive AI. Keywords: reinforcement learning, rational decision-making, axioms, adaptability, optimality, autonomous systems.
