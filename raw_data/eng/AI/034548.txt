This paper introduces the Monash University, UEA, and UCR Time Series Extrinsic Regression Archive, a comprehensive and publicly accessible repository designed to advance research in time series extrinsic regression. The archive addresses the growing need for standardized datasets to evaluate and compare regression models applied to time series data. It consolidates a diverse collection of real-world and synthetic datasets, spanning multiple domains such as healthcare, finance, and environmental monitoring. Each dataset is carefully curated, annotated, and preprocessed to ensure consistency and usability. To demonstrate the utility of the archive, we evaluate several state-of-the-art regression algorithms, including deep learning and traditional machine learning approaches, across the datasets. Our results highlight the varying performance of these methods, emphasizing the importance of domain-specific considerations in model selection. The archive not only provides a benchmark for future research but also fosters reproducibility and collaboration in the time series regression community. This work contributes to the field by offering a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners, enabling the development of more robust and generalizable regression models.  

**Keywords**: time series, extrinsic regression, dataset archive, machine learning, deep learning, benchmarking.
