The increasing demand for efficient and scalable target recognition systems in diverse environments has driven the development of Globally-scalable Automated Target Recognition (GATR). This paper addresses the challenge of achieving high accuracy and adaptability in target recognition across global scales, where variations in environmental conditions, data quality, and target diversity pose significant obstacles. We propose a novel framework that integrates advanced machine learning techniques, including deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and transfer learning, to enhance the system's ability to generalize across different geographical and operational contexts. The approach leverages large-scale, multi-domain datasets to train robust models capable of recognizing targets with minimal retraining. Experimental results demonstrate that GATR achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy, scalability, and computational efficiency. The system's ability to adapt to new environments with limited data further highlights its practical applicability. This research contributes to the field by providing a globally scalable solution for automated target recognition, with potential applications in defense, surveillance, and autonomous systems. Key innovations include the integration of domain adaptation techniques and a modular architecture that supports seamless deployment across diverse platforms. Keywords: Automated Target Recognition, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Scalability, Domain Adaptation, Convolutional Neural Networks.
