The rapid proliferation of Internet of Things (IoT) devices has introduced significant challenges in detecting anomalies in real-time, particularly in resource-constrained environments. This paper addresses the problem of adaptive anomaly detection in IoT systems by leveraging hierarchical edge computing architectures. We propose a novel contextual-bandit approach that dynamically adapts to varying network conditions and device behaviors, ensuring efficient and accurate anomaly detection. Our method integrates lightweight machine learning models deployed at the edge with a centralized decision-making framework, enabling real-time adaptation to evolving data patterns. Experimental evaluations on real-world IoT datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves superior detection accuracy and reduced latency compared to traditional static methods. The proposed system also exhibits significant improvements in resource utilization, making it highly suitable for scalable IoT deployments. This research contributes to the advancement of adaptive anomaly detection techniques, offering a robust solution for enhancing the reliability and security of IoT ecosystems in dynamic environments.  

**Keywords**: Internet of Things (IoT), anomaly detection, hierarchical edge computing, contextual bandits, adaptive systems, machine learning.
