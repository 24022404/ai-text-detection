This paper introduces Deep Scattering, a novel approach to rendering atmospheric clouds using radiance-predicting neural networks. The objective is to accurately capture the complex light transport phenomena in cloudy atmospheres, enabling realistic and efficient rendering of dynamic cloud scenes. Our method leverages a deep learning framework to predict the radiance distribution within clouds, incorporating physical models of light scattering and absorption. The neural network is trained on a comprehensive dataset of cloud formations and lighting conditions, allowing it to learn the intricate relationships between cloud properties, illumination, and radiance. Results demonstrate significant improvements in rendering accuracy and speed compared to traditional methods, with applications in fields such as computer-generated imagery, virtual reality, and climate modeling. The Deep Scattering approach contributes to the advancement of realistic cloud rendering, enabling more immersive and interactive visual experiences. Key aspects of this research include neural rendering, cloud simulation, and physics-based deep learning, with potential applications in graphics, visualization, and environmental modeling.
