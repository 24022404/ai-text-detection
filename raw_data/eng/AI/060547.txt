This paper proposes an innovative Auto-Parsing Network (APN) designed to enhance performance in image captioning and visual question answering tasks. The objective is to develop a model that can effectively parse images into meaningful regions and generate accurate captions or answer questions based on visual content. Our approach integrates a novel parsing mechanism with a deep learning framework, enabling the network to automatically identify and represent salient image regions. The APN model is trained on large-scale datasets and evaluated on benchmark tasks, demonstrating significant improvements in captioning and question answering accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods. Key findings include the ability of APN to capture complex visual relationships and generate more informative captions. The contributions of this research lie in its ability to automate the parsing process, reducing the need for manual annotation and improving the overall efficiency of image understanding systems. With applications in areas such as artificial intelligence, computer vision, and human-computer interaction, the APN model has the potential to advance the field of visual understanding and enable more effective image-based communication. Relevant keywords include: image captioning, visual question answering, auto-parsing, deep learning, computer vision, artificial intelligence.
