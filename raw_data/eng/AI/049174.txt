Visual Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) has revolutionized robotics and augmented reality, but its reliance on visual data raises significant privacy concerns. This paper introduces a novel privacy-preserving Visual SLAM framework that minimizes the risk of exposing sensitive visual information. Our approach integrates differential privacy mechanisms within the feature extraction and optimization stages of a standard Visual SLAM pipeline. Specifically, we employ a differentially private feature extraction algorithm based on randomized response techniques and introduce a noise-aware bundle adjustment optimization process. We evaluate our system on benchmark datasets, demonstrating that it achieves a substantial reduction in privacy leakage, quantified using established privacy metrics, while maintaining acceptable localization accuracy. Results show that our approach reduces the risk of re-identification of individuals and sensitive objects in the mapped environment, with a trade-off in trajectory accuracy ranging from 5% to 10%, depending on the privacy budget. This work contributes a practical and effective method for deploying Visual SLAM in privacy-sensitive applications, such as elderly care and indoor navigation. Keywords: Visual SLAM, Privacy Preservation, Differential Privacy, Robotics, Computer Vision, Feature Extraction.
