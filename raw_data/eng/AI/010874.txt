This paper proposes a novel Structure Coherence Component (SCC) for face alignment, aiming to improve the accuracy and efficiency of facial landmark detection. The objective is to address the challenges of existing methods, which often struggle with large pose variations, occlusions, and low-resolution images. Our approach integrates a hierarchical representation of facial structures with a coherence constraint, ensuring that the detected landmarks adhere to the underlying anatomical layout of the face. The SCC is designed to be lightweight and flexible, allowing for seamless integration with various face alignment architectures. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets, outperforming existing approaches in terms of accuracy, robustness, and computational efficiency. The key contributions of this work include the introduction of the SCC, which enhances the coherence and consistency of facial landmark detection, and the development of a fast and accurate face alignment framework. This research has significant implications for various applications, including facial recognition, expression analysis, and human-computer interaction, and highlights the importance of structure coherence in computer vision tasks, particularly in the context of face alignment, facial landmark detection, and deep learning-based methods.
