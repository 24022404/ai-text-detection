This paper introduces a novel GPU-based algorithm for computing the 2D Least Median of Squares (LMedS), designed for efficient and robust line detection in image processing applications. The primary objective is to accelerate LMedS computation, a robust regression technique known for its resilience to outliers, which is traditionally computationally expensive. Our approach leverages the parallel processing capabilities of modern GPUs to significantly reduce the execution time compared to conventional CPU-based implementations. We employ a strategy that decomposes the LMedS problem into smaller, independent tasks suitable for parallel execution on the GPU architecture. Experimental results demonstrate a substantial speedup, achieving a 10x to 50x improvement in performance compared to optimized CPU implementations, depending on the image size and outlier ratio. Furthermore, we showcase the application of this fast LMedS computation to real-time line detection in noisy images, demonstrating its effectiveness in scenarios where traditional methods fail. This work contributes a practical and efficient solution for robust line detection, enabling real-time applications in areas such as autonomous driving and computer vision. Keywords: Least Median of Squares, GPU computing, robust regression, line detection, image processing, parallel algorithms.
