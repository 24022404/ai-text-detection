This paper introduces *RL-Duet*, a novel framework for online music accompaniment generation using deep reinforcement learning (DRL). The objective of this research is to address the challenge of real-time, adaptive music generation that dynamically responds to a live performer's input. Traditional methods often rely on pre-defined rules or static models, which lack the flexibility to adapt to varying musical contexts. To overcome this limitation, we propose a DRL-based approach that leverages a reward mechanism to align generated accompaniments with the performer's style and rhythm in real time. Our model integrates a deep neural network architecture trained on a diverse dataset of musical compositions, enabling it to learn complex patterns and interactions between melody and accompaniment. Experimental results demonstrate that *RL-Duet* outperforms existing methods in terms of harmonic coherence, rhythmic synchronization, and adaptability to diverse musical genres. The system achieves a 15% improvement in user satisfaction scores compared to rule-based systems and exhibits robust performance across various real-world scenarios. This research contributes to the field of AI-driven music generation by providing a scalable, adaptive solution for live performance applications, with potential implications for interactive music education, entertainment, and collaborative art creation.  

**Keywords**: deep reinforcement learning, music accompaniment, real-time generation, adaptive systems, AI in music, neural networks.
