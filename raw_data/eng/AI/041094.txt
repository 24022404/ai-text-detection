Time series forecasting is a critical task across numerous fields such as finance, healthcare, and climatology, yet accurately capturing complex temporal patterns remains a challenge. This paper introduces a novel method, Topological Attention, designed to enhance the predictive performance of time series forecasting models by leveraging topological data analysis (TDA).

Methods/Approach: We present an innovative framework that integrates topological features into attention mechanisms, allowing for a deeper perceptive layer in time series models. Our approach utilizes persistent homology, a TDA technique, to extract robust topological signatures. These features are then incorporated into a transformer-based architecture to form Topological Attention layers that enhance the model's ability to focus on structurally significant temporal dynamics.

Results/Findings: Empirical evaluation on diverse benchmark datasets demonstrates significant improvement in forecasting accuracy compared to traditional methods and baseline attention models. Our results show that Topological Attention achieves lower error rates and faster convergence, effectively capturing intricate dynamics and patterns that conventional attention mechanisms might overlook.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of Topological Attention represents a substantial advancement in the field of time series forecasting, offering an innovative approach to incorporating topological insights into machine learning models. This research not only provides a new direction for improving time series prediction accuracy but also suggests broader applications where topological semantics can enhance pattern recognition tasks. Keywords: time series forecasting, topological data analysis, attention mechanisms, persistent homology, transformer architecture.
