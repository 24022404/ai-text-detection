This paper addresses the challenge of forecasting grouped time series data, a common problem in domains such as finance, supply chain management, and energy consumption. Traditional forecasting methods often struggle to capture the hierarchical and interdependent nature of grouped time series, leading to suboptimal predictions. To overcome this limitation, we propose a novel forecasting method that leverages the k-means clustering algorithm to group similar time series, enabling more accurate and efficient predictions. The approach involves clustering time series based on their patterns and trends, followed by applying a forecasting model tailored to each cluster. We evaluate the proposed method using real-world datasets and compare its performance against conventional forecasting techniques. Results demonstrate significant improvements in prediction accuracy and computational efficiency, particularly for large-scale datasets with complex hierarchical structures. The key contribution of this research lies in the integration of clustering and forecasting, offering a scalable and adaptable solution for grouped time series analysis. This method has practical implications for industries requiring precise and timely forecasts, such as inventory management and demand planning.  

**Keywords**: time series forecasting, k-means clustering, grouped time series, hierarchical forecasting, predictive modeling.
