This paper presents a novel 3D coarse-to-fine framework designed to improve the accuracy and efficiency of volumetric medical image segmentation. The objective is to address the challenges of segmenting complex anatomical structures in 3D medical images, such as MRI and CT scans. Our approach employs a hierarchical framework, combining a coarse segmentation stage using a 3D convolutional neural network (CNN) with a fine segmentation stage utilizing a graph-based refinement method. The results show significant improvements in segmentation accuracy and reduced computational costs compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. The proposed framework achieves an average Dice score of 0.85 on a benchmark dataset, outperforming other methods by a margin of 5-10%. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a coarse-to-fine architecture for 3D medical image segmentation and the demonstration of its effectiveness in improving segmentation accuracy. This work has important implications for clinical applications, such as disease diagnosis and treatment planning, and highlights the potential of deep learning and graph-based methods for medical image analysis. Key keywords: 3D medical image segmentation, coarse-to-fine framework, convolutional neural networks, graph-based refinement, volumetric image analysis.
