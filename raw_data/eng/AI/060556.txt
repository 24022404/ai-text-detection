Online visual tracking is a critical task in computer vision, requiring robust and efficient algorithms to handle dynamic environments. This paper introduces RSINet, a novel Rotation-Scale Invariant Network designed to address the challenges of object tracking under varying orientations and scales. The proposed approach leverages a deep learning-based architecture that integrates rotation and scale invariance into the feature extraction process, enabling the model to adapt to complex transformations in real-time. RSINet employs a multi-branch convolutional neural network (CNN) to extract invariant features, coupled with a lightweight online update mechanism to ensure computational efficiency. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that RSINet achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and robustness, particularly in scenarios involving significant rotation and scale changes. The results highlight the network's ability to maintain tracking precision while minimizing computational overhead. This work contributes to the advancement of online visual tracking by providing a scalable and adaptable solution with potential applications in autonomous systems, surveillance, and augmented reality.  

**Keywords**: Online visual tracking, rotation-scale invariance, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, real-time tracking, object tracking.
