The Legendre Memory Unit (LMU) has emerged as a promising recurrent neural network architecture capable of efficiently learning long-range dependencies. However, training LMU networks remains computationally intensive, particularly for large-scale datasets and complex tasks. This paper investigates parallelization strategies to accelerate LMU training. We propose a novel parallel training approach that leverages data parallelism and model parallelism to distribute the computational workload across multiple processing units. Specifically, we explore techniques for partitioning the input data and the LMU's internal state, while minimizing communication overhead. We evaluate our parallel training approach on several benchmark sequence modeling tasks, demonstrating significant speedups compared to traditional sequential training methods. Our results show near-linear scalability with increasing numbers of processing units, enabling faster training of LMU networks for complex, real-world applications. This research contributes a practical and efficient solution for accelerating LMU training, facilitating its wider adoption in various sequence learning domains.

**Keywords:** Legendre Memory Unit, LMU, parallel training, recurrent neural networks, sequence modeling, deep learning, high-performance computing.
