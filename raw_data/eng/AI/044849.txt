Continual Area Sweeping (CAS) poses a significant challenge in robotics, requiring agents to efficiently explore and map unknown environments over extended periods. This paper introduces Deep R-Learning for Continual Area Sweeping (DRL-CAS), a novel reinforcement learning approach designed to address the exploration-exploitation trade-off in CAS. DRL-CAS employs a deep neural network to approximate the optimal Q-function, enabling the agent to learn effective sweeping strategies from raw sensor data. The proposed method incorporates a reward function that encourages both exploration of undiscovered areas and efficient coverage of previously visited regions. We evaluate DRL-CAS in simulated environments, demonstrating its superior performance compared to traditional sweeping algorithms and other reinforcement learning techniques. Results show that DRL-CAS achieves significantly higher coverage rates with lower redundancy, indicating improved efficiency and adaptability. This research contributes a practical, learning-based solution for autonomous area sweeping, offering potential applications in environmental monitoring, search and rescue, and autonomous navigation. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Area Sweeping, Robotics, Exploration, Deep Learning, Autonomous Navigation.
