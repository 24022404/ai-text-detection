This paper addresses the challenge of applying Transformer models to time series forecasting, where the traditional architecture often suffers from locality issues and memory bottlenecks. Our objective is to enhance the locality of the Transformer model and break the memory bottleneck, enabling efficient and accurate forecasting of time series data. We propose a novel approach that incorporates a locality-aware attention mechanism and a memory-efficient encoding scheme. Our method leverages a combination of temporal convolutional layers and self-attention mechanisms to capture both short-term and long-term dependencies in time series data. Experimental results demonstrate that our proposed model outperforms state-of-the-art Transformer-based models on several benchmark datasets, achieving significant improvements in forecasting accuracy and reducing memory usage by up to 50%. Our research contributes to the development of more efficient and effective time series forecasting models, with potential applications in finance, energy, and other fields. Key keywords: Transformer, time series forecasting, locality-aware attention, memory-efficient encoding, temporal convolutional layers.
