This paper explores the feasibility of leveraging image classification models for video understanding tasks, challenging the conventional wisdom that video-specific models are necessary. Our approach utilizes a state-of-the-art image classifier and adapts it for video analysis by applying a temporal sampling strategy. We evaluate the performance of our method on several benchmark video datasets, achieving competitive results compared to dedicated video understanding models. Our findings suggest that image classifiers can be effectively repurposed for video tasks, reducing the need for video-specific model training and deployment. This research contributes to the development of more efficient and versatile video analysis systems, with potential applications in areas such as surveillance, healthcare, and autonomous vehicles. Key aspects of our work include transfer learning, temporal modeling, and multimodal fusion, highlighting the potential for image classification models to suffice for video understanding tasks.
