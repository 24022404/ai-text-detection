Forecasting time series data across diverse systems presents a significant challenge, particularly when forecast tasks exhibit inherent but unknown cluster structures. This paper introduces a novel approach to enhance forecasting accuracy by learning predictive leading indicators tailored to these unknown clusters. Our method combines unsupervised learning to identify clusters of forecast tasks based on their historical data characteristics, with a meta-learning framework to train predictive models that leverage leading indicators relevant to each cluster. Specifically, we employ a dynamic time warping-based clustering algorithm to group similar forecast tasks, followed by a neural network architecture that learns to predict future time series values using cluster-specific leading indicators. Experiments on several real-world datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms traditional forecasting methods and static leading indicator selection techniques. The results highlight the importance of adaptive leading indicator selection based on the underlying cluster structure of forecast tasks, offering significant improvements in forecasting accuracy and robustness, especially in complex and dynamic environments. Key words: time series forecasting, leading indicators, clustering, meta-learning, predictive modeling.
