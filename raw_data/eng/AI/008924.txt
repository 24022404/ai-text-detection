This study aims to enhance the accuracy of glioma classification using Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) data by integrating explanation-guided training into a neural network model. The approach involves analyzing the model's explanations to identify key features and patterns in MRI scans that contribute to glioma classification. A novel training framework is proposed, which leverages these explanations to guide the model's learning process, resulting in improved performance and robustness. Experimental results demonstrate that the explanation-guided training approach outperforms traditional training methods, achieving higher classification accuracy and better generalization capabilities. The findings of this research contribute to the development of more reliable and accurate glioma classification systems, with potential applications in clinical diagnosis and treatment planning. Key contributions include the introduction of a novel explanation-guided training framework, improved model interpretability, and enhanced classification performance, highlighting the value of explainable AI in medical imaging analysis. Relevant keywords: neural networks, explanation-guided training, glioma classification, MRI data, medical imaging analysis, explainable AI.
