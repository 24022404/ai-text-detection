Offline reinforcement learning (RL) has gained significant attention for its ability to learn policies from pre-collected datasets without online interaction. However, existing methods often struggle under partial coverage, where the dataset does not fully represent the state-action space, leading to suboptimal or unsafe policies. This paper introduces a novel pessimistic model-based offline RL framework designed to address these challenges. Our approach leverages a conservative estimation of the value function, ensuring robust performance even under partial coverage conditions. We provide theoretical guarantees in the form of Probably Approximately Correct (PAC) bounds, demonstrating that our method achieves near-optimal performance with high probability. Additionally, we propose a posterior sampling strategy that efficiently balances exploration and exploitation within the offline setting. Experimental results on benchmark datasets show that our framework outperforms state-of-the-art offline RL methods in terms of policy robustness and sample efficiency. The proposed method not only advances the theoretical understanding of offline RL under partial coverage but also offers practical implications for real-world applications, such as robotics and healthcare, where data collection is costly or risky.  

**Keywords**: offline reinforcement learning, partial coverage, PAC bounds, pessimistic models, posterior sampling, robust policies.
