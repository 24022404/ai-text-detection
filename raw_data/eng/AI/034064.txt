Knowledge-intensive processes (KIPs) are critical in domains requiring complex decision-making and expertise, yet they often lack efficient automation due to their unstructured nature. This paper introduces **ProcK**, a novel machine learning framework designed to enhance the automation and optimization of KIPs. The objective of this research is to address the challenges of modeling and executing KIPs by leveraging advanced machine learning techniques to extract, analyze, and utilize process-related knowledge effectively. The proposed approach integrates natural language processing (NLP) and deep learning models to capture unstructured data, such as textual documents and expert knowledge, and transform it into actionable insights. ProcK employs a hybrid architecture combining transformer-based models for semantic understanding and reinforcement learning for adaptive decision-making.  

Experimental results demonstrate that ProcK outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy, scalability, and adaptability across diverse KIP scenarios. Key findings include a 25% improvement in process execution efficiency and a 30% reduction in manual intervention compared to traditional rule-based systems. The framework also exhibits robustness in handling dynamic and evolving process requirements.  

The implications of this research are significant for industries reliant on KIPs, such as healthcare, legal, and finance, where ProcK can streamline operations, reduce costs, and improve decision-making. By bridging the gap between unstructured knowledge and automated process management, ProcK represents a substantial advancement in the field of process mining and intelligent automation.  

**Keywords**: Knowledge-intensive processes, machine learning, natural language processing, process automation, reinforcement learning, transformer models.
