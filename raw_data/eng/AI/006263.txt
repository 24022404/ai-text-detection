This paper proposes a novel approach to image decomposition using Deeply Learned Spectral Total Variation (DLSTV) decomposition. The objective is to separate an image into its constituent components, such as texture and cartoon-like structures, while preserving spectral information. Our method leverages the strengths of deep learning and total variation regularization to achieve this goal. We employ a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture to learn the spectral total variation decomposition, allowing for adaptive and data-driven decomposition. The results demonstrate the effectiveness of DLSTV in preserving image details and structures, outperforming traditional total variation-based methods. The key findings highlight the importance of spectral information in image decomposition and the potential applications of DLSTV in image processing, computer vision, and remote sensing. This research contributes to the development of advanced image decomposition techniques, enabling improved image analysis and understanding. Key keywords: Deep Learning, Total Variation, Spectral Decomposition, Image Processing, Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs).
