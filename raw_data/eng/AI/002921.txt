This paper addresses the critical challenge of model interpretability in deep learning by proposing a novel approach that leverages Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to explain image classifier decisions within the StyleSpace latent domain. The primary objective is to bridge the gap between black-box classifier outputs and human-understandable visual explanations, enabling more transparent and trustworthy AI systems. Our method trains a GAN, specifically tuned to manipulate the StyleSpace—a disentangled latent representation derived from state-of-the-art style-based generators—so as to generate minimal and semantically meaningful image modifications that highlight the features influencing the classifier’s prediction. We implement an end-to-end framework where the GAN is guided by the targeted classifier’s gradients, ensuring that the generated explanations faithfully reflect the decision boundaries. Experimental results on benchmark image datasets demonstrate that our approach produces intuitive and high-fidelity visual explanations that outperform existing pixel-space and activation-space methods in terms of both interpretability and localization accuracy. Furthermore, the use of StyleSpace enables fine-grained control over image attributes, facilitating more nuanced explorations of classifier behavior. This research advances the field of explainable AI (XAI) by integrating GAN-based generative models with interpretable latent spaces, contributing new methodology for transparent decision analysis in image classification systems. Key keywords: explainable AI (XAI), GAN, StyleSpace, classifier interpretability, visual explanation, latent space, image classification.
