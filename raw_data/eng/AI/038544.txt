This paper introduces a novel exploration strategy for reinforcement learning agents based on maximizing information gain about the environment's latent dynamics. The approach leverages a learned latent dynamics model to predict future states and quantifies uncertainty in these predictions to guide exploration. Specifically, the agent selects actions that lead to maximal reduction in its uncertainty about the latent state transitions, encouraging exploration of uncharted territories. We demonstrate that this information-maximizing exploration strategy significantly outperforms existing exploration methods, such as random exploration and curiosity-driven exploration, in a range of challenging benchmark environments. The results highlight the effectiveness of using latent dynamics models for efficient exploration and improved sample complexity. This method has implications for robotics, autonomous navigation, and other domains requiring efficient exploration in complex environments.
**Keywords:** Reinforcement Learning, Exploration, Latent Dynamics Model, Information Maximization, Uncertainty, Autonomous Navigation.
