Transfer learning leverages knowledge from pre-trained models to improve performance on downstream tasks, but often requires substantial computational resources for fine-tuning. This paper introduces SMILE, a novel self-distilled mixup approach designed to enhance the efficiency of transfer learning. SMILE incorporates a self-distillation framework where the pre-trained model acts as a teacher, guiding the learning of a student model through mixup augmentation. By creating mixed examples of input data and their corresponding labels, SMILE encourages the student model to learn smoother decision boundaries and improve generalization. Experiments on image classification datasets demonstrate that SMILE achieves comparable or superior performance to traditional fine-tuning methods, while significantly reducing training time and computational costs. The proposed approach enhances robustness against adversarial attacks and noisy labels. SMILE offers a practical and effective strategy for efficient transfer learning, particularly beneficial in resource-constrained environments. Keywords: Transfer Learning, Self-Distillation, Mixup, Efficient Learning, Image Classification.
