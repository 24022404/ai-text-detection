Persistence homology is a powerful tool in topological data analysis, enabling the extraction of meaningful features from complex datasets. This paper addresses the critical challenge of identifying and leveraging key points that significantly influence the computation of persistence homology. The objective is to develop a systematic approach to determine these critical points, which are essential for accurate and efficient topological feature extraction. We propose a novel algorithm that integrates geometric and topological properties to identify critical points in datasets, reducing computational complexity while preserving topological integrity. Our method employs a combination of filtration techniques and optimization strategies to prioritize points that contribute most to the persistence diagram. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach achieves a significant reduction in computational time compared to traditional methods, without compromising the accuracy of topological features. Furthermore, the algorithm outperforms existing techniques in terms of scalability and robustness across various datasets, including high-dimensional and noisy data. The findings highlight the importance of critical points in persistence homology and provide a practical framework for their identification. This research contributes to the advancement of topological data analysis by offering a more efficient and reliable method for computing persistence homology, with potential applications in fields such as machine learning, bioinformatics, and computer vision.  

**Keywords**: Persistence homology, topological data analysis, critical points, filtration, computational efficiency, topological features.
