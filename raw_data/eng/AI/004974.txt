Object detection in autonomous driving faces challenges due to domain shift between training and deployment environments. This paper proposes a novel domain adaptation approach that aligns both pixel-level and feature-level representations to mitigate this issue. Our method employs adversarial learning at the pixel level to reduce discrepancies in image styles, enhancing the realism of synthetic training data. Simultaneously, a feature-level alignment module minimizes the distribution gap between source and target domains by learning domain-invariant features using a gradient reversal layer. The architecture integrates seamlessly with existing object detection frameworks, allowing for end-to-end training. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach significantly improves object detection accuracy in unseen target domains, outperforming state-of-the-art domain adaptation techniques. The results highlight the effectiveness of joint pixel and feature alignment for robust object detection in autonomous driving scenarios. Keywords: Domain Adaptation, Object Detection, Autonomous Driving, Adversarial Learning, Feature Alignment.
