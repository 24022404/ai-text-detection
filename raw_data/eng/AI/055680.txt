This paper explores the application of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) for accurate and efficient leaf recognition. The objective is to develop a robust system capable of identifying plant species based on leaf images. Our approach utilizes a CNN architecture trained on a comprehensive dataset of leaf images, focusing on extracting distinctive features directly from the raw pixel data. We evaluated the performance of the CNN model, achieving a recognition accuracy of 92% on a held-out test set. The results demonstrate that CNN-based feature extraction provides a powerful and automated solution for leaf identification, surpassing traditional methods that rely on handcrafted features. This research contributes to the field of plant identification and has potential applications in botany, agriculture, and environmental monitoring. Keywords: Leaf Recognition, Convolutional Neural Networks, CNN, Plant Identification, Image Classification, Deep Learning.
