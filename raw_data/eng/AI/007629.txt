Breast cancer diagnosis using ultrasound imaging remains a critical, yet challenging, task due to variability in image quality and interpretation. This paper introduces a novel, more reliable AI-driven solution that leverages a multi-AI combination framework to enhance diagnostic accuracy and consistency for breast ultrasound analysis. Our approach integrates multiple state-of-the-art deep learning models, including convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and transformer-based architectures, in a collaborative ensemble. The multi-model system is designed to analyze and cross-validate imaging data, reducing false positives and negatives by dynamically combining model predictions. Through rigorous experiments on publicly available breast ultrasound datasets, our framework demonstrated significant improvements in sensitivity, specificity, and overall classification performance compared to standalone AI models and traditional approaches. The results indicate a substantial reduction in diagnostic errors and increased robustness against variations in image acquisition. In conclusion, our multi-AI combination strategy offers a more dependable and accurate solution for breast ultrasound diagnosis, highlighting its potential for practical deployment in clinical workflows and supporting radiologists in early breast cancer detection. This research emphasizes the value of ensemble learning, medical imaging, AI reliability, breast cancer detection, and clinical decision support as key advancements in the application of artificial intelligence to healthcare.
