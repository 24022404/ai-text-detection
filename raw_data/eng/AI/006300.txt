This paper addresses the growing interest in applying deep reinforcement learning (DRL) techniques for continuous control tasks, which are essential in robotics, autonomous vehicles, and other adaptive systems. We aim to provide a comprehensive benchmark to evaluate different DRL algorithms' effectiveness in such environments.

Methods/Approach: We have conducted an extensive investigation using several state-of-the-art DRL models, including Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG), Trust Region Policy Optimization (TRPO), and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). These models were evaluated on a set of continuous control tasks within simulated environments. Our framework ensures consistent measurement of performance across algorithms by standardizing initial conditions and evaluation metrics.

Results/Findings: The benchmark reveals significant variations in performance across different DRL algorithms, with PPO demonstrating superior stability and efficiency in most tested scenarios. The results highlight distinct advantages and limitations inherent in each model, showcasing how task-specific requirements significantly influence the choice of DRL algorithm. Our findings suggest that while some methodologies excel in certain environments, they may underperform or exhibit instability in others.

Conclusion/Implications: The insights from this benchmark provide invaluable guidance for practitioners and researchers choosing DRL strategies for continuous control applications. By delineating the strengths and weaknesses of popular DRL approaches, this study contributes essential data and analysis that can guide future advancements in algorithmic development and optimization. Potential applications extend to enhancing robotic automation, improving adaptive systems, and refining AI strategies in real-world continuous action spaces.

Keywords: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Continuous Control, DDPG, TRPO, PPO, Benchmarking, Robotics, Simulation Environments.
