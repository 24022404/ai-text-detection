Graph-based methods have gained significant attention in machine learning for their ability to model complex relationships in data. However, learning effective metrics for signed graphs, which capture both positive and negative relationships, remains a challenging problem. This paper introduces a novel approach for signed graph metric learning by leveraging Gershgorin Disc Perfect Alignment (GDPA). The proposed method optimizes the graph Laplacian matrix to ensure that its eigenvalues align perfectly with the Gershgorin discs, enabling robust and interpretable metric learning. We formulate the problem as a constrained optimization framework and propose an efficient algorithm to solve it. Experimental results on real-world datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of clustering accuracy and robustness to noise. The key contribution of this work lies in its ability to handle signed graphs effectively while providing theoretical guarantees on the learned metrics. This research has potential applications in social network analysis, recommendation systems, and anomaly detection, where signed relationships play a critical role.  

**Keywords**: Signed graphs, metric learning, Gershgorin discs, graph Laplacian, clustering, optimization.
