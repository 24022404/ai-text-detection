This paper introduces SCOUTER, a novel image recognition framework that leverages slot attention mechanisms to enhance explainability in deep learning-based classification models. The primary objective of SCOUTER is to address the transparency and interpretability challenges inherent in traditional image recognition systems. To achieve this, we employ a slot attention-based approach, which allows the model to selectively focus on relevant regions of the input image, thereby providing insights into the decision-making process. Our methodology involves training a classifier that learns to allocate attention to specific slots, each representing a distinct aspect of the image, and then aggregates these slots to produce the final classification output. Experimental results demonstrate that SCOUTER achieves competitive performance with state-of-the-art image recognition models while providing meaningful explanations for its predictions. The key findings of this research highlight the effectiveness of slot attention in improving model interpretability, with significant implications for applications requiring transparent and trustworthy AI systems, such as medical imaging and autonomous vehicles. By contributing to the development of more explainable and reliable image recognition models, SCOUTER has the potential to enhance the adoption of AI technologies in high-stakes domains, with relevant keywords including explainable AI, image recognition, slot attention, and deep learning.
