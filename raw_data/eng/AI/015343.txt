This paper addresses the critical challenge of toxicity prediction in chemical compounds, which is essential for ensuring safety in various industries. The objective of this research is to develop an efficient predictive model that leverages simple features from chemical structures to forecast toxicity outcomes. We propose a novel approach that combines shallow neural networks with decision tree algorithms to achieve robust classification performance. Our methodology involves the extraction of minimal but significant features from chemical data, subsequently inputted into the neural network and decision tree frameworks. Through extensive experiments, we demonstrate that our model not only surpasses conventional prediction methods in accuracy but also significantly reduces computational time and resource requirements. Key findings indicate that the integration of shallow neural networks with decision trees enhances interpretability while maintaining high predictive power. The implications of this research are significant, as it offers a streamlined and effective solution for toxicity assessment, which can be applied in drug development, environmental monitoring, and regulatory compliance. Our approach highlights the potential of utilizing simpler models with effective feature selection for complex prediction tasks in the field of toxicology. 

Keywords: toxicity prediction, shallow neural networks, decision trees, chemical compounds, feature extraction, machine learning.
