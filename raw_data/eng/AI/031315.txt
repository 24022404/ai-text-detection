This paper addresses the challenge of unsupervised image matching and object discovery, formulating it as an optimization problem. The objective is to develop a framework that can automatically identify and match objects across different images without prior labeling or supervision. Our approach leverages advances in deep learning and optimization techniques to learn a representation that captures the underlying structure of objects within images. We propose a novel optimization-based method that jointly learns to match images and discover objects, leveraging a combination of convolutional neural networks and graph-based optimization algorithms. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of matching accuracy and object discovery. Our contributions include a new formulation of the image matching problem as an optimization task, and the development of a scalable and efficient algorithm for solving it. The implications of this research are significant, enabling applications in computer vision, robotics, and autonomous systems, where unsupervised image matching and object discovery are crucial. Key keywords: unsupervised learning, image matching, object discovery, optimization, deep learning, computer vision.
