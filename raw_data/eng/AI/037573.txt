Weakly supervised object localization (WSOL) aims to identify object bounding boxes using only image-level labels, posing a significant challenge. This paper introduces an attention-based dropout layer designed to enhance feature discrimination and localization accuracy in WSOL. Our approach leverages the attention maps generated by a convolutional neural network to selectively drop less relevant feature activations, encouraging the network to focus on discriminative regions corresponding to the target objects. By adaptively pruning feature maps based on attention scores during training, the proposed layer promotes the learning of more robust and localized feature representations. Experimental results on the benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance compared to existing WSOL techniques, with significant improvements in both localization accuracy and classification performance. The attention-based dropout layer effectively mitigates the common issue of focusing on background clutter and improves the network's ability to identify the complete extent of objects. This research offers a novel and effective strategy for improving weakly supervised object localization. Keywords: Weakly Supervised Object Localization, Attention Mechanism, Dropout, Convolutional Neural Networks, Feature Discrimination.
