Link prediction in dynamic graphs is crucial for various applications, but faces challenges with new nodes and limited labeled data. This paper introduces a novel subgraph-aware meta-learning framework for few-shot inductive link prediction. Our approach, termed SAME, learns transferable knowledge from meta-training tasks constructed from historical graph data. SAME utilizes a subgraph extraction module to identify relevant neighborhood structures around query links, enabling the model to focus on informative patterns. A meta-learner then adapts this subgraph-specific knowledge to predict links for unseen nodes in new graphs with only a few labeled examples. Experiments on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that SAME significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods, especially in scenarios with extremely limited labeled data. The results highlight the effectiveness of subgraph-aware meta-learning for addressing the cold-start problem in inductive link prediction. Keywords: Link Prediction, Inductive Learning, Meta-Learning, Graph Neural Networks, Few-Shot Learning, Subgraphs.
