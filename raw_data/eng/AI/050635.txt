This paper addresses the challenge of algorithmic fairness in machine learning by introducing a novel approach based on learning orthogonal disentangled representations. The objective is to mitigate bias propagation in predictive models by separating sensitive attributes (e.g., race, gender) from task-relevant features. We propose a learning framework that encourages the decomposition of input data into two orthogonal subspaces: one capturing task-specific information and the other encoding sensitive attributes. This is achieved through a combination of adversarial training and orthogonality constraints enforced during representation learning. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art fairness metrics while maintaining competitive predictive accuracy. Specifically, our approach reduces disparate impact and equal opportunity difference compared to existing fairness-aware algorithms. The learned disentangled representations provide insights into the model's decision-making process and facilitate the development of more transparent and equitable AI systems. Our findings suggest that orthogonal disentanglement is a promising direction for building fair and reliable machine learning models.

Keywords: Fairness, Disentanglement, Representation Learning, Orthogonality, Machine Learning, Bias Mitigation.
