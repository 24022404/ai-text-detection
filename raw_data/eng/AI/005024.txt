This paper introduces Generative Grading, a novel approach to automated feedback on richly structured problems, achieving near human-level accuracy. The objective is to address the limitations of traditional grading systems, which often struggle to provide detailed and accurate feedback on complex assignments. Our method leverages advanced machine learning techniques, including transformer-based architectures and fine-tuning, to generate high-quality feedback that mimics human grading. The approach is evaluated on a diverse set of problems, demonstrating significant improvements in accuracy and consistency compared to existing automated grading systems. Key findings include a substantial reduction in grading errors and enhanced feedback quality, enabling more effective learning and assessment. The contributions of this research have significant implications for education and assessment, particularly in subjects requiring richly structured problem-solving, such as mathematics, programming, and engineering. By providing accurate and detailed feedback, Generative Grading has the potential to enhance student learning outcomes, reduce instructor workload, and improve the overall efficiency of the grading process. Relevant keywords: automated grading, machine learning, transformer, fine-tuning, feedback generation, education technology.
