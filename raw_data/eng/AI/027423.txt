This paper proposes LRCN-RetailNet, a novel recurrent neural network (RNN) architecture designed to accurately count people in retail environments. The objective is to address the challenging task of people counting, which is crucial for retail analytics, crowd management, and security. Our approach combines the strengths of long short-term memory (LSTM) networks and convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to learn spatial and temporal features from video feeds. The LRCN-RetailNet model is trained on a large dataset of retail videos and achieves state-of-the-art performance in people counting, outperforming existing methods by a significant margin. Our results show that the proposed architecture can accurately count people in various retail scenarios, including crowded stores and shopping malls. The contributions of this research include the development of a robust and efficient people counting system, which can be applied to various retail applications, such as customer traffic analysis, queue management, and sales forecasting. Key keywords: people counting, recurrent neural network, retail analytics, computer vision, deep learning.
