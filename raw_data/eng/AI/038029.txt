Object proposal generation is a critical task in computer vision, serving as the foundation for applications such as object detection, recognition, and image segmentation. The objective of this paper is to address the challenge of generating high-quality object proposals with increased efficiency and accuracy. We introduce a novel sequential optimization framework that incrementally refines candidate regions, leveraging a combination of adaptive scoring functions and hierarchical search strategies to enhance proposal quality while significantly reducing computational overhead. Our approach employs an iterative selection mechanism that integrates spatial and contextual cues, enabling more precise localization and minimizing redundancy in proposed regions. Experimental results on standard benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed method consistently outperforms existing techniques in terms of recall, precision, and processing speed. Notably, our framework achieves state-of-the-art performance with fewer proposals, indicating both effectiveness and efficiency. This work provides an advanced solution for real-time object proposal generation, with potential applications in autonomous vehicles, video surveillance, and mobile device vision systems. Keywords: object proposal generation, sequential optimization, computer vision, region proposals, real-time detection.
