VGG’s 2D convolutional layers utilize the LeakyReLU activation function, which is a modification of the traditional ReLU. Unlike ReLU, which completely suppresses negative inputs by outputting zero, LeakyReLU maintains a small, fixed slope for negative values. This design choice helps accelerate training by allowing a small gradient to flow even when the unit is not active, addressing the “dying ReLU” problem. The function is particularly advantageous in cases with sparse gradients, such as training generative adversarial networks, improving convergence and overall model performance.
