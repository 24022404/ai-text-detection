This paper addresses the challenge of domain and task transferability in machine learning by introducing a novel clustering-based approach. The objective is to develop a flexible and adaptable framework that enables the transfer of knowledge across different domains and tasks. Our method leverages clustering algorithms to identify shared patterns and structures in the data, facilitating the learning of domain-agnostic representations. We employ a combination of deep learning and clustering techniques to learn transferable features, which are then fine-tuned for specific tasks. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, showing significant improvements in performance on various domain and task transfer scenarios. The key findings highlight the importance of clustering in capturing underlying data structures, allowing for more efficient and accurate transfer learning. Our research contributes to the development of more robust and adaptable machine learning models, with potential applications in areas such as computer vision, natural language processing, and reinforcement learning. Key keywords: transfer learning, domain adaptation, clustering, deep learning, task transferability.
