Functional data, where observations are curves or functions, presents unique challenges for classification and regression tasks. This paper introduces Measure Inducing Classification and Regression Trees (MI-CART), a novel tree-based method specifically designed for functional data analysis. MI-CART utilizes a measure-based approach to induce splits in the feature space, capturing the underlying characteristics of functional data more effectively than traditional methods. The proposed algorithm constructs decision trees by recursively partitioning the data based on optimal splits determined by minimizing impurity measures adapted for functional data. These measures quantify the variability within each node with respect to the functional nature of the data. The performance of MI-CART is evaluated through extensive simulations and real-world datasets, demonstrating its superior predictive accuracy and interpretability compared to existing functional data classification and regression techniques. Key results indicate that MI-CART effectively handles complex functional relationships and high-dimensional functional spaces. The MI-CART method offers a powerful and flexible tool for analyzing functional data in various applications, including biometrics, environmental science, and finance. Keywords: Functional Data Analysis, Classification Trees, Regression Trees, Machine Learning, Measure Theory, Data Mining.
