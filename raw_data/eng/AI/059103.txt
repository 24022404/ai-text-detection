Optical Motion Capture (MoCap) systems are essential tools in various fields, but manual marker labeling remains a time-consuming bottleneck. This paper introduces a novel permutation learning approach for the automatic labeling of markers in MoCap data, significantly reducing the need for manual intervention. Our method leverages a deep learning architecture to predict the optimal permutation matrix that aligns the detected marker positions with a predefined anatomical template. We introduce a permutation loss function that encourages accurate and consistent labeling across frames. Evaluated on a diverse dataset of human motions, our approach achieves state-of-the-art accuracy in marker labeling, demonstrating a substantial improvement over existing methods, particularly in scenarios with occlusions and marker swaps. The proposed system offers robust and efficient auto-labeling, enabling faster and more reliable MoCap data processing. This advancement facilitates broader applications of motion capture technology in biomechanics, animation, and virtual reality.
Keywords: Motion Capture, Marker Labeling, Permutation Learning, Deep Learning, Auto-labeling.
