Convolutional Neural Networks (ConvNets) have achieved remarkable success in various computer vision tasks. However, traditional ConvNets often rely on fixed-size filters with limited receptive fields, which can restrict their ability to capture diverse spatial features effectively. This paper introduces a novel approach, Dynamic Filtering with Large Sampling Field (DF-LSF), designed to enhance the adaptability and performance of ConvNets. The proposed method dynamically adjusts filter weights based on the input data, enabling the network to adaptively focus on relevant spatial regions. Additionally, DF-LSF incorporates a large sampling field mechanism, allowing the model to capture broader contextual information without significantly increasing computational complexity. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that DF-LSF outperforms conventional ConvNets in tasks such as image classification and object detection, achieving higher accuracy while maintaining computational efficiency. The results highlight the potential of DF-LSF to advance the capabilities of ConvNets in handling complex visual data. This research contributes to the field by introducing a flexible and scalable filtering mechanism that can be integrated into existing architectures, paving the way for more robust and adaptive deep learning models.  

**Keywords**: Convolutional Neural Networks, Dynamic Filtering, Large Sampling Field, Adaptive Learning, Computer Vision.
