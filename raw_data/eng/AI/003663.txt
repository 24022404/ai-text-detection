This paper addresses the challenge of k-shot learning, where a model must learn from a limited number of examples. Our objective is to develop an efficient k-shot learning approach using regularized deep networks. We propose a novel method that combines a deep neural network with regularization techniques to prevent overfitting. Our approach utilizes a few-shot learning framework, where a base model is pre-trained on a large dataset and fine-tuned on a small number of examples from the target task. We employ dropout and weight decay as regularization techniques to improve the model's generalization ability. Experimental results show that our method outperforms existing k-shot learning approaches, achieving state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. Our findings demonstrate the effectiveness of regularized deep networks in k-shot learning, highlighting the importance of regularization in preventing overfitting. The proposed method has significant implications for applications where data is scarce or expensive to collect, such as image classification, object detection, and natural language processing. Key keywords: k-shot learning, few-shot learning, deep neural networks, regularization, dropout, weight decay, transfer learning.
