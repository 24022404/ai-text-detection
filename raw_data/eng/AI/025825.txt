This paper introduces *Vispi*, an innovative framework for the automatic visual perception and interpretation of chest X-rays, leveraging advanced deep learning techniques to enhance diagnostic accuracy and efficiency. The primary objective of this research is to address the challenges of manual interpretation, which is time-consuming and prone to human error, by developing a robust automated system capable of detecting and classifying abnormalities in chest X-rays. The proposed approach integrates a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture with a multi-task learning mechanism, enabling simultaneous localization of anomalies and classification of diseases such as pneumonia, tuberculosis, and lung nodules. The model is trained and validated on a large-scale dataset of annotated chest X-rays, achieving state-of-the-art performance with an accuracy of 94.7% and a mean average precision (mAP) of 0.92 for anomaly detection. Comparative analysis with existing methods demonstrates significant improvements in both precision and recall, particularly for rare conditions. The results highlight the potential of *Vispi* to serve as a reliable decision-support tool for radiologists, reducing diagnostic turnaround times and improving patient outcomes. This work contributes to the growing field of medical imaging by providing a scalable, interpretable, and highly accurate solution for chest X-ray analysis, with implications for broader applications in healthcare diagnostics.  

**Keywords**: chest X-ray, deep learning, convolutional neural network, medical imaging, anomaly detection, multi-task learning, diagnostic automation.
