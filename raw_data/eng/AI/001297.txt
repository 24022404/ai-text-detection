Achieving invariant representations is crucial for building robust machine learning models capable of generalizing across variations in input data. Traditional approaches often leverage adversarial training to encourage invariance, which can be computationally intensive and challenging to optimize. This paper introduces a novel framework for learning invariant representations without relying on adversarial methods. Our approach employs a combination of feature normalization and carefully designed regularization techniques to explicitly disentangle relevant information from nuisance factors within latent spaces. We demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method on benchmark datasets in both computer vision and natural language processing tasks, showing that our approach achieves comparable or superior performance in terms of accuracy and invariance compared to adversarial-based baselines. Additionally, our method reduces training instability and computational overhead, making it practical for large-scale applications. The findings suggest that invariant representations can be efficiently learned through non-adversarial means, opening new directions for interpretable and robust machine learning systems. Keywords: invariant representations, feature disentanglement, regularization, robustness, deep learning.
