This paper presents an in-depth examination of k-Nearest Neighbour (k-NN) classifiers, a fundamental algorithm in machine learning and data classification. The objective of this research is to provide a comprehensive overview of the k-NN approach, including its theoretical foundations, implementation, and applications. The methods employed in this study involve a detailed analysis of the k-NN algorithm, including distance metrics, choice of k, and handling of ties. Python examples are used to illustrate the implementation and application of k-NN classifiers. The results show that k-NN classifiers can achieve high accuracy in various classification tasks, and the choice of k and distance metric significantly impacts performance. This research contributes to the existing body of knowledge by providing a clear and concise explanation of k-NN classifiers, making it accessible to both beginners and experienced practitioners. The implications of this study are significant, as k-NN classifiers can be applied to a wide range of fields, including image classification, natural language processing, and recommender systems. Key keywords: k-Nearest Neighbour, machine learning, data classification, Python, distance metrics.
