Person image generation, a challenging task in computer vision, aims to synthesize realistic images of individuals in varying poses while preserving identity and appearance details. This paper introduces a novel framework, Progressive and Aligned Pose Attention Transfer (PA-PAT), designed to address the limitations of existing methods in generating high-quality, pose-aligned person images. The proposed approach leverages a progressive attention mechanism to iteratively refine pose transfer by aligning spatial and semantic features across source and target poses. A key innovation lies in the integration of a pose-aligned attention module, which ensures precise localization of body parts and enhances the preservation of fine-grained details such as textures and clothing patterns. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that PA-PAT achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of visual quality, pose accuracy, and identity preservation. The framework’s ability to generalize across diverse poses and complex scenarios highlights its potential for applications in virtual try-on, animation, and augmented reality. This work contributes a robust and scalable solution to the person image generation problem, offering significant advancements in pose transfer fidelity and realism.  

**Keywords**: Person image generation, pose transfer, attention mechanism, computer vision, generative models.
