This paper addresses the challenge of generating accurate and contextually relevant image captions for marketing analysis by leveraging multimodal learning techniques. The objective is to enhance the understanding of visual content in marketing campaigns, enabling businesses to derive actionable insights from images. We propose a novel multimodal framework that integrates state-of-the-art vision and language models, combining convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for image feature extraction with transformer-based architectures for natural language generation. The approach is trained on a diverse dataset of marketing-related images paired with descriptive captions, ensuring robustness across various domains. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in caption quality, with our model outperforming existing methods in terms of both accuracy and relevance, as measured by BLEU and CIDEr metrics. The findings highlight the potential of multimodal image captioning to automate and refine marketing analysis, offering businesses a scalable solution for interpreting visual data. This research contributes to the growing field of AI-driven marketing tools, providing a foundation for future advancements in multimodal learning applications.  

**Keywords**: Multimodal learning, image captioning, marketing analysis, convolutional neural networks, transformers, natural language generation.
