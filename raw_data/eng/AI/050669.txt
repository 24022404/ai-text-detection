This paper introduces Explorable Super Resolution (ESR), a novel approach to image super-resolution that allows users to interactively control the characteristics of the generated high-resolution output. Unlike existing methods that produce a single, deterministic super-resolved image, ESR leverages a conditional generative adversarial network (cGAN) architecture augmented with a user-defined style vector. This style vector enables exploration of a diverse range of plausible high-resolution reconstructions, trading off sharpness, texture, and noise levels according to user preferences. Our method incorporates a perceptual loss function and an adversarial loss to ensure both visual fidelity and realism in the generated images. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that ESR achieves comparable or superior quantitative performance to state-of-the-art super-resolution techniques, while providing the crucial added benefit of user control. Furthermore, a user study confirms the effectiveness of ESR in generating visually appealing and customized super-resolved images. The proposed ESR framework offers a significant advancement in super-resolution, enabling applications in image editing, artistic creation, and personalized image enhancement.

Keywords: Super-Resolution, Generative Adversarial Networks, Image Enhancement, Interactive Control, Style Transfer, cGAN.
