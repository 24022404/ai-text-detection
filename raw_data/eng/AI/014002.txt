This paper addresses the challenge of conversational fashion image retrieval, where users refine search results through multiturn natural language feedback. Traditional image retrieval systems often fail to adapt dynamically to user preferences expressed in natural language, limiting their effectiveness in real-world applications. To overcome this, we propose a novel framework that integrates multimodal learning and conversational AI to enable iterative refinement of search results based on user feedback. Our approach leverages a combination of pre-trained vision-language models and a reinforcement learning-based feedback mechanism to interpret user inputs and update retrieval results in real time. Experiments conducted on a large-scale fashion dataset demonstrate that our system achieves significant improvements in retrieval accuracy and user satisfaction compared to existing methods. Key findings reveal that the multiturn feedback loop enhances the system’s ability to capture nuanced user preferences, leading to more precise and personalized results. This research contributes to the advancement of conversational AI in fashion e-commerce and highlights the potential of integrating natural language feedback into image retrieval systems for improved user interaction and experience.  

**Keywords**: conversational AI, fashion image retrieval, natural language feedback, multimodal learning, reinforcement learning, personalized search.
