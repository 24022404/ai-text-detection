This paper addresses the correspondence problem on point sets, a fundamental challenge in computer vision and machine learning, by introducing a novel quantum computational approach. The objective is to efficiently establish correspondences between two point sets, enabling applications such as 3D reconstruction, object recognition, and tracking. Our method leverages the power of quantum computing to develop a quantum algorithm that solves the correspondence problem by representing point sets as quantum states and utilizing quantum parallelism to explore the solution space. The results demonstrate a significant improvement in computational efficiency and accuracy compared to classical methods, particularly for large-scale point sets. Key findings include the development of a quantum circuit architecture for point set representation and a quantum-inspired optimization technique for correspondence estimation. The implications of this research are substantial, enabling faster and more accurate solutions for correspondence problems, with potential applications in fields such as robotics, computer vision, and artificial intelligence. Keywords: quantum computing, correspondence problem, point sets, computer vision, machine learning, 3D reconstruction.
