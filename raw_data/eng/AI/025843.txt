This paper introduces a novel adversarial approach to automated image augmentation for deep learning. The primary objective is to improve the generalization performance of image classifiers, particularly when training data is limited. We propose an Adversarial Policy Gradient (APG) framework, where a policy network learns an optimal augmentation policy by interacting with a target classifier in an adversarial manner. The policy network generates augmentation parameters, while the classifier acts as the environment, providing feedback signals based on its validation performance. We employ a policy gradient algorithm to train the policy network, optimizing for augmentation policies that maximize classifier accuracy and robustness. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including CIFAR-10 and ImageNet, demonstrate that APG significantly outperforms state-of-the-art augmentation techniques, achieving higher classification accuracy and improved resilience to adversarial attacks. This research offers a new paradigm for automating the design of effective image augmentation strategies, reducing the reliance on manual tuning and enabling more robust deep learning models. Keywords: Image Augmentation, Adversarial Learning, Policy Gradient, Deep Learning, Generalization, Reinforcement Learning.
