This paper investigates the integration of options discovery within the framework of budgeted reinforcement learning (BRL) to address the challenge of efficient policy learning under explicit resource constraints. The objective is to enable agents to autonomously learn temporal abstractions—options—that adhere to a predefined cost or risk budget, thereby improving both policy performance and resource management. We propose a novel algorithm that jointly discovers useful options while optimizing for cumulative rewards within budgetary limits. Our approach leverages a hierarchical structure, where the agent constructs and selects options based on both expected reward and budget consumption, utilizing a modified policy gradient method tailored for BRL settings. Extensive experiments on benchmark environments demonstrate that the proposed method naturally discovers interpretable, reusable options, achieving superior trade-offs between performance and budget usage when compared to flat and non-budgeted baselines. Results show enhanced learning efficiency, robustness to dynamic budget constraints, and greater policy flexibility. These findings present new opportunities for scalable, safe, and cost-aware autonomous decision-making in domains such as robotics, finance, and resource-constrained AI systems. Keywords: reinforcement learning, options discovery, budgeted learning, temporal abstraction, autonomous agents, hierarchical policies.
