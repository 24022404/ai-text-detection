Video object segmentation (VOS) is a challenging task requiring accurate spatiotemporal reasoning. This paper introduces SSTVOS, a novel approach leveraging Sparse Spatiotemporal Transformers for efficient and robust VOS. SSTVOS employs a sparse attention mechanism to selectively attend to relevant spatiotemporal features, reducing computational complexity compared to dense transformers. The method aggregates information across frames using a cascade of transformer layers, capturing both short-term and long-term dependencies. Experimental results on standard VOS benchmarks demonstrate that SSTVOS achieves state-of-the-art performance while maintaining high computational efficiency. Specifically, SSTVOS shows improvements in accuracy metrics, such as region similarity and contour accuracy, compared to existing transformer-based and recurrent approaches, with a significant reduction in processing time. The proposed method offers a promising solution for real-time VOS applications. Keywords: Video Object Segmentation, Transformers, Sparse Attention, Spatiotemporal Modeling, Deep Learning.
