This paper addresses the challenge of rectifying images affected by radial distortion and scale changes, a critical problem in computer vision and 3D reconstruction. We propose a novel set of minimal solvers designed to accurately estimate the parameters required for rectification under these conditions. Our approach leverages geometric constraints and algebraic formulations to derive efficient solutions that require only a minimal number of correspondences. The methods are validated through extensive experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets, demonstrating superior accuracy and robustness compared to existing techniques. The solvers achieve real-time performance, making them suitable for applications in augmented reality, robotics, and image-based modeling. Key contributions include the introduction of new mathematical formulations for handling radial distortion and scale changes simultaneously, as well as the development of efficient algorithms that significantly reduce computational complexity. This work provides a foundation for future research in image rectification and offers practical tools for improving the accuracy of vision-based systems.  

**Keywords**: radial distortion, scale change, minimal solvers, image rectification, computer vision, 3D reconstruction.
