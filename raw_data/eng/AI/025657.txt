Gesture recognition is a critical challenge in human-computer interaction, with applications ranging from virtual reality to assistive technologies. This paper addresses the limitations of existing methods in handling multi-rate and multi-modal temporal data, which are essential for accurate and robust gesture recognition. We propose a novel framework, the Multi-Rate and Multi-Modal Temporal Enhanced Network (MRMTEN), designed to effectively process and fuse temporal information from diverse data sources, such as video, motion sensors, and depth maps. Our approach leverages a hierarchical architecture that integrates multi-rate temporal modeling with attention mechanisms to capture both short-term and long-term dependencies across modalities. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that MRMTEN outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving superior recognition accuracy and robustness to temporal variations. The proposed framework also exhibits significant improvements in computational efficiency, making it suitable for real-time applications. This research contributes to advancing gesture recognition by introducing a scalable and adaptable solution for multi-modal temporal data, with potential applications in healthcare, gaming, and robotics.  

**Keywords**: gesture recognition, multi-modal fusion, temporal modeling, attention mechanisms, human-computer interaction, real-time systems.
