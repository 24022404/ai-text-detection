This paper addresses the challenge of synthesizing dynamic textures, a crucial aspect of generating realistic visual content in various applications. The objective is to develop a framework that learns to predict the synthesizability of dynamic texture samples, enabling the efficient creation of high-quality, dynamic textures. Our approach employs a deep learning model that analyzes the spatial and temporal characteristics of input textures to determine their synthesizability. The model is trained on a large dataset of dynamic textures, allowing it to learn the underlying patterns and structures that contribute to successful synthesis. Our results show that the proposed framework achieves high accuracy in predicting synthesizability, outperforming existing methods. The implications of this research are significant, as it enables the automated generation of realistic dynamic textures for applications such as video production, gaming, and virtual reality. Key contributions include the introduction of a novel synthesizability metric and the development of a robust prediction model, paving the way for future research in dynamic texture synthesis and analysis. Relevant keywords: dynamic texture synthesis, synthesizability prediction, deep learning, computer vision, texture analysis.
