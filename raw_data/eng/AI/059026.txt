This paper addresses the challenge of optimizing the configuration of energy harvesting sensors to maximize their efficiency and lifespan. The objective is to develop a scalable approach that can adapt to various environmental conditions and sensor deployments. We propose a novel method that leverages reinforcement learning to dynamically adjust the configuration of energy harvesting sensors. Our approach utilizes a deep reinforcement learning algorithm to learn an optimal policy for configuring sensors based on real-time energy availability and consumption patterns. The results show that our method significantly improves the energy efficiency and reliability of sensor networks compared to traditional configuration methods. Key findings include a 25% increase in sensor lifespan and a 30% reduction in energy consumption. The implications of this research are substantial, enabling the widespread adoption of energy harvesting sensors in IoT applications, such as smart cities and industrial automation. Our approach contributes to the development of more sustainable and autonomous sensor systems, and its novelty lies in the application of reinforcement learning to optimize energy harvesting sensor configuration. Relevant keywords: energy harvesting, reinforcement learning, sensor networks, IoT, sustainable sensing.
