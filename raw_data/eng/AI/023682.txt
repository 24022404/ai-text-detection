This paper introduces MOFit, a novel framework designed to combat obesity by leveraging machine learning and Internet of Things (IoT) technologies. The primary objective of MOFit is to provide a personalized and interactive approach to weight management, addressing the growing concern of obesity worldwide. Our approach utilizes wearable devices and mobile applications to collect user data, which is then analyzed using machine learning algorithms to predict calorie intake, physical activity, and other health metrics. The results show significant improvements in weight loss and overall health outcomes, outperforming traditional methods. MOFit's innovative use of IoT sensors and machine learning models enables real-time monitoring and tailored recommendations, making it an effective tool for obesity reduction. The framework's potential applications extend to various healthcare domains, highlighting its contribution to the development of AI-driven health interventions. Key features of MOFit include automated data analysis, personalized feedback, and integration with popular health tracking devices. By combining machine learning, IoT, and behavioral science, MOFit offers a unique solution to the obesity epidemic, with implications for public health policy and future research in digital health.
