This paper introduces SIGAN, a novel image generation method designed to address the challenges of solar cell defect segmentation and data augmentation. The accurate segmentation of defects in solar cell images is crucial for quality control and performance optimization, but the limited availability of labeled defect data hinders the training of robust segmentation models. SIGAN leverages a generative adversarial network (GAN) architecture, incorporating a specialized segmentation loss within the generator's objective function, to synthesize realistic solar cell images with accurately segmented defects. The method allows for controlled defect placement, size, and type within the generated images. Experimental results demonstrate that training segmentation models using a dataset augmented with SIGAN-generated images significantly improves segmentation accuracy compared to training with solely real data, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets. The synthesized data effectively enhances the model's ability to generalize to unseen defect variations. SIGAN offers a powerful tool for improving solar cell defect detection and analysis, with potential applications in automated inspection and quality assurance. Keywords: Image Generation, GAN, Solar Cell, Defect Segmentation, Data Augmentation, Deep Learning.
