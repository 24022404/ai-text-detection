This paper addresses the challenging credit assignment problem in multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL), where it is difficult to determine each agent's contribution to the overall team reward. We propose a novel approach that utilizes a meta-policy gradient to learn effective credit assignment strategies. Our method introduces a meta-learner that dynamically adjusts the reward shaping function for each agent based on the observed team performance and individual agent actions. This allows for a more nuanced and adaptive distribution of credit compared to traditional MARL algorithms. We evaluate our approach on several benchmark cooperative MARL environments, demonstrating significant improvements in learning speed, convergence, and overall team performance compared to state-of-the-art methods. The results indicate that learning credit assignment strategies via a meta-policy gradient enables agents to more effectively coordinate and achieve superior collective outcomes. This work offers a promising direction for tackling complex cooperative tasks in MARL by optimizing the learning process itself.

**Keywords:** Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning, Credit Assignment, Meta-Learning, Policy Gradient, Reward Shaping, Cooperative Learning.
