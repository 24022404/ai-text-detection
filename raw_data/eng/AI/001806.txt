Neural sequence models have become a cornerstone of modern natural language processing, yet their computational demands often limit their practical deployment. This paper investigates the trade-off between computational efficiency and output quality in neural sequence models, aiming to provide a framework for balancing these critical factors. We propose a novel adaptive computation mechanism that dynamically adjusts the computational resources allocated to different parts of the input sequence based on their complexity. Our approach leverages lightweight heuristics and reinforcement learning to optimize resource allocation without compromising model performance. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method reduces computational overhead by up to 40% while maintaining competitive accuracy compared to state-of-the-art baselines. The results highlight the potential of adaptive computation as a scalable solution for deploying neural sequence models in resource-constrained environments. This work contributes to the growing body of research on efficient AI systems and offers practical insights for real-world applications in machine translation, text generation, and beyond.  

**Keywords**: neural sequence models, computational efficiency, adaptive computation, reinforcement learning, natural language processing.
