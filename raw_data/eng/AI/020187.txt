This paper introduces Event2Graph, a novel approach for multivariate time-series anomaly detection using an event-driven bipartite graph. The objective is to effectively identify anomalies in complex systems by modeling the relationships between different variables and events. Our approach utilizes a bipartite graph structure to represent the interactions between variables and events, enabling the detection of subtle anomalies that may not be apparent through traditional univariate or multivariate analysis methods. The Event2Graph model is trained using a graph neural network, which learns to identify patterns and anomalies in the data. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of Event2Graph in detecting anomalies in real-world datasets, outperforming state-of-the-art methods. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a bipartite graph structure for anomaly detection and the development of a graph neural network-based approach for modeling complex relationships between variables and events. This work has significant implications for applications such as monitoring and diagnostics in industrial systems, financial fraud detection, and healthcare monitoring, where timely anomaly detection is critical. Key keywords: multivariate time-series analysis, anomaly detection, bipartite graph, graph neural network, event-driven modeling.
