This paper introduces TempoRL, a novel reinforcement learning framework that focuses on learning the optimal timing for actions in complex environments. The objective is to enable agents to adapt their action frequency based on the current state, leading to more efficient and effective decision-making. Our approach combines traditional reinforcement learning methods with a tempo-based module that predicts the optimal action timing. The results show that TempoRL outperforms existing methods in various environments, demonstrating improved performance and sample efficiency. The key findings highlight the importance of adaptive action timing in reinforcement learning and the potential applications of TempoRL in real-world scenarios, such as robotics and autonomous systems. By learning when to act, TempoRL contributes to the development of more intelligent and adaptive agents, paving the way for future research in reinforcement learning and artificial intelligence. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, action timing, adaptive decision-making, robotics, autonomous systems.
