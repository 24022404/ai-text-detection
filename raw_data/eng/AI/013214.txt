Breast cancer classification from histopathological images is crucial for early diagnosis and treatment planning. However, accurately identifying cancerous regions remains challenging due to the complex and subtle variations in cellular morphology. This paper introduces an Attention Model Enhanced Network (AMEN) to improve the accuracy and robustness of breast cancer image classification. AMEN integrates a novel attention mechanism that dynamically emphasizes salient features within the images, enabling the network to focus on the most relevant regions for classification. We utilize a convolutional neural network (CNN) backbone augmented with a spatial attention module and a channel attention module, learned jointly to refine feature representations. Experimental results on a publicly available dataset demonstrate that AMEN achieves significantly higher classification accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods, with improvements observed in sensitivity and specificity. The proposed approach enhances the interpretability of the model by highlighting the areas of importance for diagnosis. AMEN offers a promising solution for improving automated breast cancer diagnosis, potentially reducing the burden on pathologists and improving patient outcomes. Keywords: Breast Cancer Classification, Histopathological Images, Attention Mechanism, Convolutional Neural Network, Deep Learning, Image Analysis.
