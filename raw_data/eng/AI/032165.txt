This paper introduces Online Local Boosting, a novel approach designed to enhance the performance of online decision trees. The objective is to address the challenge of maintaining accuracy in real-time decision-making systems, where data streams continuously and models must adapt swiftly. Our method combines the strengths of online learning with local boosting techniques, allowing for the selective enhancement of decision tree nodes based on their performance on recent data. The approach utilizes a dynamic weighting scheme to focus boosting efforts on the most critical regions of the tree, thereby improving overall accuracy and adaptability. Experimental results demonstrate that Online Local Boosting outperforms traditional online decision tree algorithms, particularly in scenarios with concept drift or noisy data. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of our method in improving prediction accuracy, reducing error rates, and enhancing the robustness of online decision trees. This research contributes to the advancement of online machine learning and has significant implications for applications in real-time data processing, streaming analytics, and edge computing, with relevant keywords including online learning, decision trees, local boosting, adaptive algorithms, and real-time decision-making.
