This paper addresses the challenge of Bayesian inference in high-dimensional time-series data by introducing the Orthogonal Stochastic Linear Mixing Model. The objective is to develop a robust and efficient approach for modeling complex temporal relationships in large datasets. Our method leverages a novel combination of orthogonal transformations and stochastic processes to capture the underlying patterns and structures in the data. The Orthogonal Stochastic Linear Mixing Model is applied to high-dimensional time-series data, and its performance is evaluated using various metrics. The results show that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The key findings of this research include the development of a scalable and flexible framework for Bayesian inference in high-dimensional time-series data, which has significant implications for applications in finance, climate modeling, and signal processing. The contributions of this work include the introduction of a new model that improves upon existing methods and provides a foundation for future research in this area. Key keywords: Bayesian inference, high-dimensional time-series, orthogonal stochastic linear mixing model, scalable algorithms, signal processing.
