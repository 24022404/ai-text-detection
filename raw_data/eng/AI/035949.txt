This paper introduces Semi-Relaxed Quantization with DropBits (SRQ-DropBits), a novel approach for training low-bit neural networks. The primary objective is to mitigate the performance degradation commonly associated with aggressive quantization. SRQ-DropBits employs a bit-wise regularization strategy that dynamically drops less significant bits during training, effectively promoting a structured sparsity in the quantized weights. Specifically, we utilize a semi-relaxed quantization function that allows for smoother gradients during backpropagation, combined with a learnable masking mechanism to identify and deactivate redundant bit representations. Experiments on benchmark image classification datasets (CIFAR-10, ImageNet) demonstrate that SRQ-DropBits achieves state-of-the-art accuracy compared to existing quantization methods, particularly in extremely low-bit settings (e.g., 2-bit and 3-bit). Notably, our method shows significant improvements in maintaining accuracy while substantially reducing memory footprint and computational complexity. The proposed bit-wise regularization offers a new direction for efficient neural network compression and deployment on resource-constrained devices. Keywords: Neural Network Quantization, Low-Bit Training, Bit-wise Regularization, Model Compression, Deep Learning.
