This paper addresses the challenge of optimizing the dispatch strategy for battery energy storage systems (BESS) in dynamic environments. Effective dispatch is crucial for maximizing energy efficiency, reducing operational costs, and ensuring grid reliability. We propose a novel framework combining reinforcement learning (RL) with a model-based optimizer to enhance decision-making processes.

Methods/Approach: Our approach integrates a reinforcement learning algorithm with a model-based optimizer to create a hybrid system capable of adaptive and real-time energy storage dispatch. The RL component learns optimal policies based on historical energy usage and current environmental conditions, while the model-based optimizer fine-tunes these policies by incorporating domain-specific constraints and predictive models. This dual-layer method builds on the strengths of both reinforcement learning and deterministic optimization, ensuring robust performance in varying scenarios.

Results/Findings: Experiments conducted on simulated and real-world datasets demonstrate significant improvements in BESS dispatch compared to conventional methods. The hybrid system achieves a substantial increase in energy efficiency and reduction in cost over traditional rule-based and standalone RL strategies. Additionally, our approach showed enhanced adaptability to changes in demand and supply patterns, maintaining stability and optimizing battery lifecycle management.

Conclusion/Implications: The integration of reinforcement learning with a model-based optimizer provides a powerful tool for the efficient dispatch of battery energy storage systems. This research contributes to the development of more intelligent and responsive energy management solutions, with potential applications in smart grids and renewable energy integrations. Our findings suggest that the proposed method can play a pivotal role in advancing sustainable energy technologies and practices. 

Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Battery Energy Storage, Dispatch Optimization, Model-based Optimization, Smart Grids, Sustainable Energy Management.
