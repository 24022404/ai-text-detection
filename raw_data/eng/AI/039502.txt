High-dimensional multivariate forecasting is a critical challenge in many real-world applications, including finance, climate modeling, and sensor networks, due to complex dependencies and computational constraints. This paper addresses the problem by introducing a novel approach based on low-rank Gaussian copula processes, designed to efficiently model and predict large collections of interdependent variables. Our method leverages the flexibility of copula processes to capture non-linear and non-Gaussian dependencies, while the integration of low-rank approximations reduces computational complexity and mitigates scalability issues inherent in high-dimensional settings. We develop an efficient inference algorithm that balances predictive accuracy and resource requirements, enabling practical deployment for large-scale datasets. Experiments conducted on synthetic and real-world benchmarks demonstrate that our approach achieves superior forecasting performance compared to traditional multivariate time series models, particularly in scenarios with strong inter-variable dependencies and limited observations. The results highlight notable improvements in both prediction accuracy and computational efficiency. This work advances the state-of-the-art in multivariate forecasting by providing a scalable, robust, and interpretable framework suitable for a wide range of high-dimensional data analysis tasks. Key keywords: high-dimensional forecasting, multivariate time series, Gaussian copula process, low-rank approximation, scalable inference, dependency modeling.
