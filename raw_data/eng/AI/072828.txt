The accurate prediction of future Humphrey Visual Fields (HVF) is critical for early detection and management of progressive eye diseases such as glaucoma. This study proposes a novel deep learning-based approach to forecast future HVF measurements, leveraging historical patient data to predict visual field progression. A convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture is designed to capture spatial and temporal patterns in HVF data, integrating longitudinal measurements to enhance prediction accuracy. The model is trained and validated on a large dataset of HVF records, achieving superior performance compared to traditional statistical methods and existing machine learning models. Results demonstrate that the proposed approach significantly reduces prediction error and provides reliable estimates of future visual field loss. This work contributes to the field by introducing a robust, data-driven tool for early intervention in glaucoma management, with potential applications in clinical decision-making and personalized treatment planning. Keywords: Humphrey Visual Fields, deep learning, glaucoma, visual field prediction, convolutional neural networks.
