Vehicle re-identification (ReID) is a critical task in intelligent transportation systems, aiming to accurately identify and track vehicles across multiple non-overlapping cameras. This paper addresses the challenges of vehicle ReID, including variations in viewpoint, lighting, and occlusion, by proposing a novel multi-modal approach that integrates visual and contextual features. Our method leverages deep learning architectures, specifically convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and transformer-based models, to extract discriminative features from vehicle images. Additionally, we introduce a spatio-temporal reasoning module to enhance the model's ability to associate vehicles across different camera views. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The proposed framework not only improves vehicle ReID but also provides insights into the integration of multi-modal data for complex computer vision tasks. This research contributes to advancing intelligent surveillance systems, with potential applications in traffic management, security, and autonomous driving.  

**Keywords**: Vehicle re-identification, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, transformer models, spatio-temporal reasoning, intelligent transportation systems.
