This paper investigates the interaction between deep learning-based object detectors and Siamese network-based trackers in the context of video surveillance systems. The objective is to analyze how the performance of Siamese trackers is influenced by the accuracy and robustness of deep detectors, particularly in dynamic and cluttered environments. We propose a hybrid framework that integrates state-of-the-art deep detectors, such as YOLOv7 and Faster R-CNN, with advanced Siamese trackers, including SiamRPN++ and SiamMask. The approach leverages the strengths of both components to enhance object detection and tracking accuracy in real-time scenarios. Experimental evaluations are conducted on benchmark datasets, such as MOTChallenge and UAV123, to assess the system's performance in terms of precision, recall, and tracking robustness. Results demonstrate that the integration of high-accuracy detectors significantly improves the initialization and re-detection capabilities of Siamese trackers, leading to a 15% increase in tracking accuracy compared to standalone trackers. Furthermore, the proposed framework exhibits superior performance in handling occlusions and scale variations, which are common challenges in video surveillance. The findings highlight the importance of detector-tracker synergy and provide valuable insights for designing more efficient and reliable video surveillance systems. This research contributes to advancing the field by offering a novel perspective on optimizing the interaction between detection and tracking modules, with potential applications in security, traffic monitoring, and autonomous systems.  

**Keywords**: Deep learning, object detection, Siamese trackers, video surveillance, YOLOv7, SiamRPN++, MOTChallenge.
