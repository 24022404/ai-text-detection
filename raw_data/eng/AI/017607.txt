The proliferation of facial mask usage, especially in public settings, has posed significant challenges in face recognition systems, necessitating advancements in masked face super-resolution. This paper introduces JDSR-GAN, an innovative network framework designed for enhancing the resolution of masked facial images. The objective of this research is to address the deteriorating performance of conventional face super-resolution methods in scenarios involving partial obstructions like facial masks. The proposed JDSR-GAN employs a joint and collaborative learning approach, integrating generative adversarial networks (GANs) tailored to enhance image detail while preserving facial features critical for recognition tasks.

Our method utilizes a dual-stage process: initially, a specialized encoder extracts relevant features from the masked regions, followed by a collaborative learning network that refines the image resolution and simultaneously reconstructs occluded facial regions. Comparative evaluations demonstrate that JDSR-GAN outperforms existing methodologies by achieving superior visual fidelity and sharpness, particularly in maintaining crucial identity features around masked areas. Additionally, experiments indicate notable improvements in face recognition accuracy post-resolution enhancement.

Concluding, the JDSR-GAN framework offers substantial contributions to the field of computer vision by providing a robust solution for high-quality face reconstruction in the presence of masks. The implications of this work are profound, potentially enhancing the effectiveness of biometric systems and surveillance technologies under current global conditions where mask usage is prevalent. Keywords such as GAN, super-resolution, masked face, and collaborative learning are integral to this study, underscoring its relevance and innovative stride in facial image processing.
