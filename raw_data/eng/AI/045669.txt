This paper introduces a novel multi-modal graph neural network (MM-GNN) for joint reasoning on visual and scene text information within images. The objective is to improve performance in tasks requiring comprehensive scene understanding by effectively integrating these two crucial modalities. Our approach constructs a heterogeneous graph where nodes represent visual objects and text tokens, and edges capture relationships within and between modalities. The MM-GNN then leverages specialized message-passing mechanisms to propagate information across the graph, enabling joint reasoning and context aggregation. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our MM-GNN achieves state-of-the-art performance compared to existing methods that process vision and text independently or with simpler fusion techniques. The proposed model effectively captures complex inter-modal relationships, leading to improved accuracy in scene understanding tasks. This work contributes a powerful framework for joint visual-textual reasoning with potential applications in image captioning, visual question answering, and object detection within complex scenes. Keywords: Multi-modal learning, Graph Neural Networks, Scene Text Recognition, Computer Vision, Joint Reasoning.
