This paper introduces *EgoFace*, a novel framework for egocentric face performance capture and videorealistic reenactment. The objective of this research is to address the challenges of capturing and reenacting facial expressions from egocentric viewpoints, which are critical for applications in virtual reality, telepresence, and human-computer interaction. Unlike traditional methods that rely on external cameras, *EgoFace* leverages a single egocentric camera to reconstruct and reenact facial performances with high fidelity. The proposed approach combines advanced deep learning techniques with 3D facial reconstruction to accurately model facial dynamics and textures from limited egocentric data. A key innovation is the integration of a neural rendering pipeline that synthesizes photorealistic facial animations while preserving the user’s identity and expression details. Experimental results demonstrate that *EgoFace* outperforms existing methods in terms of visual quality, expression accuracy, and computational efficiency. The framework achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets and shows robustness across diverse lighting conditions and head movements. This research contributes a scalable and practical solution for egocentric face capture, enabling new possibilities for immersive communication and interactive systems. Keywords: egocentric vision, facial performance capture, neural rendering, 3D reconstruction, videorealistic reenactment.
