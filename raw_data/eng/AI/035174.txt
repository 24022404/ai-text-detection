Predicting driver intention at intersections is a critical challenge in advancing intelligent transportation systems and enhancing road safety. This paper proposes a novel deep learning model enhanced with an attention mechanism to accurately predict driver intentions, such as turning left, turning right, or proceeding straight, at intersections. The proposed approach leverages a combination of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks to capture spatial and temporal features from vehicle trajectory data. The attention mechanism is integrated to prioritize the most relevant features, improving the model's interpretability and prediction accuracy. Experiments were conducted on a publicly available dataset of real-world driving scenarios, and the results demonstrate that the proposed model outperforms traditional machine learning and baseline deep learning methods in terms of precision, recall, and F1-score. The model achieves an accuracy of 94.7%, showcasing its effectiveness in real-time applications. This research contributes to the development of safer and more efficient autonomous driving systems by providing a robust framework for understanding and anticipating driver behavior in complex traffic environments.  

**Keywords**: driver intention prediction, deep learning, attention mechanism, intelligent transportation systems, autonomous driving, LSTM, CNN.
