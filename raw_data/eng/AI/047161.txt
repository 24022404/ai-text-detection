Social login has revolutionized the way users access digital services by allowing them to quickly authenticate on websites or applications using their existing social media accounts such as Facebook, Google, or Twitter. This approach eliminates the traditional hassle of creating new accounts and remembering additional usernames and passwords for each platform, thereby reducing friction in the user registration and login process. Powered by the OAuth2 protocol, social login works by securely redirecting the user to their chosen social network for authentication. After the user provides consent, the network returns an access token to the requesting website or app. This token not only confirms the user’s identity but also enables the retrieval of basic profile data—sometimes including email address, profile picture, name, and even a user's interests, depending on the granted permissions. By delegating credential storage and management to established social media platforms, social login enhances security, as these platforms generally employ robust security measures and regularly update their systems to counteract emerging threats. Furthermore, the seamless login flow provides a streamlined and convenient user experience, encouraging more people to sign up and engage with digital services. Additionally, websites can leverage access to users’ social data to personalize content, tailor recommendations, and deliver targeted offers, fostering greater user satisfaction and engagement.
