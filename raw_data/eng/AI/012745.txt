This paper presents SalsaNext, a novel framework designed for efficient and uncertainty-aware semantic segmentation of LiDAR point clouds in autonomous driving systems. The primary objective is to address the increasing demands for real-time performance, high segmentation accuracy, and reliable uncertainty estimation in perception pipelines for self-driving vehicles. SalsaNext builds upon an encoder-decoder architecture, introducing improved residual modules and attentive mechanisms to better capture spatial dependencies within sparse LiDAR data. The approach integrates a Bayesian inference technique, enabling robust quantification of both aleatoric and epistemic uncertainties, which is crucial for safety-critical decision-making in autonomous navigation. Extensive evaluations on benchmark datasets demonstrate that SalsaNext achieves state-of-the-art segmentation accuracy while significantly reducing computation time, making it suitable for deployment on resource-constrained platforms. Comparative analysis highlights marked improvements in performance over previous methods, particularly in challenging environments with dynamic objects and varying sensor conditions. The results also show that the generated uncertainty maps enhance the interpretability and reliability of segmentation outputs. In summary, SalsaNext provides an effective, fast, and uncertainty-aware solution for semantic segmentation of LiDAR point clouds, advancing the capability of autonomous driving perception systems. Key contributions include architectural innovations for efficiency, integrated uncertainty estimation, and comprehensive performance assessment. Keywords: semantic segmentation, LiDAR point clouds, autonomous driving, deep learning, uncertainty estimation, real-time processing.
