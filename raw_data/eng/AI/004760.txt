Weakly supervised object localization (WSOL) aims to identify object locations using only image-level labels. This paper introduces a novel Dual-Attention Guided DropBlock (DAG-DropBlock) module to enhance feature discrimination and localization accuracy in WSOL. The proposed module leverages both channel and spatial attention mechanisms to guide the DropBlock operation, adaptively dropping less relevant feature regions while preserving crucial object-related information. This approach encourages the network to attend to more discriminative parts for accurate localization. Experimental results on the benchmark datasets, including CUB-200-2011 and ImageNet, demonstrate that the DAG-DropBlock module significantly improves the localization performance compared to existing state-of-the-art WSOL methods. Furthermore, visualizations of attention maps confirm that our module facilitates a more accurate focus on object regions. Our approach offers a simple yet effective way to improve WSOL without requiring additional supervision, providing a promising direction for future research in weakly supervised learning. Keywords: Weakly Supervised Object Localization, Attention Mechanism, DropBlock, Deep Learning, Computer Vision.
