LiDAR point cloud segmentation is crucial for autonomous driving and robotics, yet effectively processing unstructured 3D data remains a challenge. This paper introduces Cylindrical and Asymmetrical 3D Convolution Networks (CA-3DCNN), a novel architecture designed to improve LiDAR segmentation accuracy and efficiency. Our method employs a cylindrical coordinate system to better leverage the inherent structure of LiDAR data, enabling efficient 3D convolutions. We further introduce an asymmetrical convolutional block that selectively aggregates features across different resolutions, enhancing fine-grained detail capture. The proposed CA-3DCNN was evaluated on the SemanticKITTI dataset, demonstrating significant improvements in segmentation performance compared to state-of-the-art methods, achieving a 4% increase in mean Intersection-over-Union (mIoU). Furthermore, the asymmetrical convolutional block reduces computational costs by 15% relative to conventional 3D CNNs. The results highlight the potential of cylindrical partitioning and asymmetrical convolutions for efficient and accurate LiDAR point cloud segmentation.
Keywords: LiDAR Segmentation, 3D Convolutional Neural Networks, Cylindrical Coordinates, Point Cloud Processing, Autonomous Driving.
