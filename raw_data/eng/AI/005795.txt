This paper addresses the challenge of learning effective feature aggregation for deep 3D morphable models (3DMMs), which are critical for tasks such as 3D face reconstruction, animation, and analysis. Traditional 3DMMs often struggle with capturing fine-grained details and generalizing across diverse datasets due to limitations in feature representation. To overcome this, we propose a novel deep learning framework that integrates hierarchical feature aggregation mechanisms, enabling the model to learn both global shape structures and localized details. Our approach leverages multi-scale feature fusion and attention mechanisms to enhance the expressiveness of the 3DMM. We evaluate the proposed method on benchmark datasets for 3D face reconstruction, demonstrating significant improvements in reconstruction accuracy and detail preservation compared to state-of-the-art methods. The results highlight the effectiveness of our feature aggregation strategy in capturing intricate facial geometries and textures. This work contributes to advancing 3DMMs by providing a robust framework for learning discriminative features, with potential applications in facial animation, virtual reality, and biometric systems.  

**Keywords**: 3D morphable models, feature aggregation, deep learning, 3D face reconstruction, attention mechanisms, multi-scale fusion.
