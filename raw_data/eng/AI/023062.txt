Self-supervised scene flow estimation leverages geometric constraints between consecutive frames to learn motion patterns without ground truth. However, relying solely on local neighborhood consistency can be vulnerable to adversarial perturbations, leading to inaccurate flow predictions. This paper introduces an adversarial metric learning approach to enhance the robustness of self-supervised scene flow models. We propose a novel training strategy that incorporates adversarial examples generated by perturbing feature embeddings in a metric space, specifically designed to mislead the neighborhood-based matching process. Our method learns a more robust distance metric that is less susceptible to these adversarial attacks, improving the accuracy and stability of the estimated scene flow. Experimental results on standard benchmarks demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing self-supervised methods, especially in challenging scenarios with occlusions and complex motions. Key innovations include the adversarial metric learning framework and its effective integration with self-supervised scene flow training. Keywords: Scene Flow, Self-Supervised Learning, Adversarial Training, Metric Learning, Robustness.
