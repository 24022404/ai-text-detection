This paper addresses the challenge of improving the adaptability and performance of reinforcement learning (RL) agents in offline environments by integrating offline meta-reinforcement learning (meta-RL) with an innovative online self-supervision mechanism. The aim is to enhance the agent's ability to generalize from past experiences and self-improve its strategies without constant external feedback.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel framework combining offline meta-reinforcement learning with online self-supervision, wherein the agent learns a meta-policy from a diverse dataset of tasks offline. During online deployment, the agent applies self-supervised learning to refine its strategies based on new task experiences. Our approach leverages a reinforcement learning architecture that incorporates an offline policy optimization algorithm to learn transferable skills, which are further refined through an online feedback loop guided by self-supervised signals.

Results/Findings: The proposed framework demonstrates significant performance improvements over traditional offline RL methods across several benchmark environments. Our results highlight the agent's enhanced ability to generalize, with accelerated adaptation to new tasks and improved cumulative rewards. In comparative analysis, our method outperformed existing state-of-the-art meta-RL approaches, emphasizing its effectiveness and increased robustness under diverse scenarios.

Conclusion/Implications: This research presents a significant advancement in the field of reinforcement learning by effectively combining offline learning and online self-supervision. The implications of this work suggest a pathway towards more autonomous and resilient learning systems capable of superior performance without extensive manual guidance or additional data. Our approach opens new avenues for applications in robotics, autonomous systems, and personalized AI, where adaptability and self-improvement are crucial.

Keywords: meta-reinforcement learning, offline learning, self-supervision, adaptability, generalization, policy optimization, autonomous systems.
