Instance segmentation, a critical task in computer vision, requires precise and efficient separation of individual instances within an image. Traditional methods often struggle with boundary delineation, which impacts segmentation quality. This paper introduces a novel approach termed "Contour Loss," leveraging a k-step distance transformation image, to enhance the accuracy of instance segmentation.

Methods: We propose a contour-driven loss function integrated with a k-step distance transformation, which systematically quantifies the proximity of each pixel to the nearest contour line. This innovative loss function is incorporated into a standard convolutional neural network (CNN) instance segmentation framework, resulting in improved boundary awareness. The approach emphasizes contour refinement, blending traditional boundary prediction methods with advanced transformation techniques to bolster segmentation performance.

Results: Our experimentation demonstrates that employing Contour Loss significantly improves segmentation accuracy compared to standard loss functions. Evaluation on benchmark datasets reveals enhanced boundary precision and a reduction in overlap errors between segmented instances. Comparative analyses show that our method outperforms existing techniques in scenarios with complex boundaries and varying object scales.

Conclusion: The introduction of Contour Loss with k-step distance transformation represents a meaningful advancement in instance segmentation. By refining boundary prediction and improving segmentation detail, this approach has the potential for applications in areas requiring high precision, such as medical imaging and autonomous driving. This technique not only broadens the understanding of contour-based segmentation but also sets a foundation for future work in developing more sophisticated loss functions designed for complex visual tasks.

Keywords: Instance segmentation, Contour Loss, k-step distance transformation, boundary delineation, convolutional neural network, computer vision.
