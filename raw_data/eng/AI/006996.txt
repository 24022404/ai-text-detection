Generating synthetic LiDAR data is crucial for training and evaluating autonomous systems, but traditional ray-tracing methods are computationally expensive. This paper introduces a novel and efficient approach for fast synthetic LiDAR rendering leveraging spherical UV unwrapping of equirectangular z-buffer images. Our method initially renders a depth map from a virtual scene in equirectangular projection. We then unwrap this equirectangular image onto a sphere, creating a UV map representing the range and bearing of each point. This spherical UV representation allows for rapid generation of LiDAR point clouds by directly sampling the UV map based on simulated LiDAR sensor characteristics. We demonstrate that our method achieves significant speed improvements compared to conventional ray tracing, while maintaining high fidelity in the generated point clouds. Experiments show a 10x speedup in LiDAR rendering time with comparable accuracy in object detection tasks. This technique enables faster prototyping and training of perception algorithms for autonomous vehicles and robotics. Keywords: LiDAR simulation, synthetic data, equirectangular projection, UV unwrapping, autonomous systems, point cloud rendering, z-buffer.
