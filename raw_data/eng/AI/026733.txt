This study aims to develop an innovative approach for automated evaluation of root canal therapy using a deep learning-based framework. The proposed Anatomy-Guided Parallel Bottleneck Transformer Network leverages the strengths of transformer architectures and anatomical guidance to accurately assess the quality of root canal treatments. Our method utilizes a parallel bottleneck structure to efficiently process dental radiographs and identify key anatomical features relevant to root canal therapy. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our approach, achieving high accuracy and robustness in evaluating treatment outcomes. The proposed network outperforms existing methods, particularly in cases with complex anatomical structures. This research contributes to the development of AI-powered diagnostic tools in dentistry, enabling more efficient and accurate evaluation of root canal therapy. Key findings highlight the potential of transformer-based architectures in medical image analysis, with implications for improved patient care and clinical decision-making. Relevant keywords include: root canal therapy, transformer network, anatomy-guided, deep learning, medical image analysis, dental radiographs.
