This paper introduces SI-Score, a novel image dataset designed for fine-grained analysis of robustness to object location, rotation, and size variations in computer vision models. The objective of this research is to address the limitations of existing datasets in evaluating the sensitivity of models to spatial transformations, which are critical for real-world applications such as autonomous driving, robotics, and medical imaging. The dataset comprises a diverse collection of images with systematically controlled variations in object location, rotation, and scale, enabling precise assessment of model performance under challenging conditions.  

To construct SI-Score, we developed a pipeline for generating synthetic images with annotated ground truth, ensuring high-quality data for training and evaluation. We employed state-of-the-art deep learning models, including convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and vision transformers (ViTs), to benchmark performance on the dataset. Our experiments reveal significant performance degradation in existing models when subjected to spatial transformations, highlighting the need for improved robustness.  

Key findings demonstrate that models trained on SI-Score exhibit enhanced generalization capabilities, with up to a 15% improvement in accuracy under spatial variations compared to baseline datasets. The dataset also facilitates the development of novel algorithms for spatial invariance, contributing to advancements in computer vision. SI-Score is publicly available to support further research in robustness evaluation and model optimization.  

This work provides valuable insights into the challenges of spatial invariance in computer vision and offers a standardized benchmark for future studies. The dataset and associated methodologies have broad implications for improving the reliability and applicability of vision systems in dynamic environments.  

**Keywords**: computer vision, robustness evaluation, spatial invariance, image dataset, deep learning, object detection, rotation invariance, scale invariance.
