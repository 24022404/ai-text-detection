Deep neural networks (DNNs) have achieved remarkable success in various applications, but their deployment in privacy-sensitive scenarios remains a challenge due to the risk of data leakage. This paper introduces a novel algorithmic-cryptographic co-design approach for scalable and privacy-preserving DNN inference. Our method integrates a customized homomorphic encryption scheme with a carefully designed DNN architecture to minimize computational overhead while maximizing security. We leverage algorithmic transformations, including polynomial approximations and quantization, to reduce the complexity of cryptographic operations. The resulting system enables secure inference without revealing either the model or the input data to the server. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach achieves significant performance improvements in terms of latency and throughput compared to existing solutions, while maintaining high accuracy. This work provides a practical framework for deploying DNNs in privacy-critical applications such as healthcare and finance. Keywords: Privacy-preserving deep learning, Homomorphic encryption, Algorithmic-cryptographic co-design, Secure inference, Scalable DNNs.
