This paper provides an in-depth review of learning curves, a fundamental concept in machine learning and artificial intelligence. The objective is to explore the characteristics, shapes, and implications of learning curves in various contexts, including supervised and unsupervised learning. We employ a comprehensive approach, analyzing existing models and algorithms to identify key factors influencing the shape of learning curves. Our findings reveal that the shape of learning curves can significantly impact model performance, generalization, and optimization. We compare different learning curve shapes, including linear, exponential, and sigmoidal curves, and discuss their relationships with factors such as dataset size, model complexity, and regularization techniques. The conclusions drawn from this review highlight the importance of understanding learning curves in optimizing machine learning models and improving their performance. This research contributes to the field by providing a unified framework for analyzing learning curves, with potential applications in areas such as neural networks, deep learning, and AI model optimization. Key keywords: learning curves, machine learning, artificial intelligence, model optimization, neural networks.
