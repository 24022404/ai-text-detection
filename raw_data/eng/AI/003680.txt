This paper introduces PISE, a novel framework for Person Image Synthesis and Editing based on a decoupled Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) architecture. The objective is to achieve high-fidelity image generation and flexible attribute editing in human portraits. PISE decouples the latent space into identity and attribute components, enabling independent control over these factors during image synthesis. The proposed method employs a two-stage GAN, where the first stage generates a coarse image based on identity information, and the second stage refines the image with attribute details. We introduce a novel attribute manipulation module that allows for intuitive and precise control over attributes such as clothing, hair style, and pose. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that PISE achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of image quality, editing flexibility, and identity preservation, surpassing existing methods. The decoupled latent space and attribute manipulation module offer significant improvements in controlling the generated images, opening new possibilities for virtual try-on, avatar creation, and personalized image generation. Keywords: Image Synthesis, Image Editing, GAN, Person Re-identification, Deep Learning, Attribute Manipulation.
