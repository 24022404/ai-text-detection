Transfer learning has emerged as a powerful technique in machine learning, enabling the adaptation of pre-trained models to new tasks with limited data. However, its reliability and generalization capabilities remain inconsistent across diverse domains. This paper addresses the challenge of improving the reliability of transfer learning by proposing a novel framework that enhances model adaptability and robustness. Our approach integrates domain-specific feature alignment and uncertainty-aware fine-tuning to mitigate domain shifts and reduce overfitting. We evaluate the framework on multiple benchmark datasets, including image classification and natural language processing tasks, demonstrating significant improvements in accuracy and stability compared to existing methods. The results highlight the framework's ability to generalize across varying data distributions while maintaining computational efficiency. This research contributes to advancing transfer learning by providing a more reliable and scalable solution, with potential applications in healthcare, autonomous systems, and other data-sensitive domains. Key innovations include the integration of uncertainty quantification and adaptive feature alignment, offering a pathway for more dependable model deployment in real-world scenarios.  

**Keywords**: Transfer learning, domain adaptation, uncertainty quantification, fine-tuning, machine learning, generalization.
