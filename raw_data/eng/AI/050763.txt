Abstract:

Neural network models have achieved state-of-the-art performance in various machine learning tasks, yet their increasingly large size and computational demands pose significant challenges for deployment in resource-constrained environments. This paper introduces the Teacher-Class Network (TCN), a novel neural network compression mechanism designed to optimize model efficiency while preserving predictive accuracy. The TCN framework operates by leveraging a hierarchical knowledge distillation approach, where a high-capacity teacher network guides multiple class-specific student networks. Each student network specializes in a subset of classes, significantly reducing the overall parameter count without compromising the representational power needed for accurate classification. Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the TCN achieves substantial reductions in model size and inference latency—up to 50% more compact than conventional models—while maintaining comparable or improved accuracy. Additionally, comparative analysis with standard pruning and quantization techniques highlights the superior scalability and adaptability of the proposed mechanism. The Teacher-Class Network offers a promising direction for deploying deep learning models on edge devices, Internet of Things (IoT) systems, and mobile computing platforms. Key contributions include a novel class-specialization strategy, effective teacher-student knowledge transfer, and demonstrable improvements in compression rates and inference efficiency. Keywords: model compression, neural networks, knowledge distillation, class specialization, efficient deep learning.
