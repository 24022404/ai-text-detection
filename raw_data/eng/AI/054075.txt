The paper introduces ICON, a novel framework aimed at enhancing the learning of regular maps by leveraging the principle of inverse consistency. Our research addresses the challenge of ensuring the accuracy and smoothness of map transformations, particularly in scenarios requiring robust spatial regularization.

Methods/Approach: ICON is designed using a learning-based approach, integrating inverse consistency constraints into the training process of neural networks. This method creates a feedback loop that iteratively refines the learned transformations to minimize discrepancies between direct and inverse mappings. We utilize advanced optimization techniques and neural architectures to achieve precise and consistent map representations.

Results/Findings: Our experiments demonstrate that ICON significantly improves map prediction accuracy and consistency compared to existing methods. The framework excels in maintaining map regularity, reducing mapping errors, and ensuring stable transformation quality. Extensive evaluations on benchmark datasets showcase its superiority in yielding efficient and smooth mappings, outperforming traditional and recent state-of-the-art methodologies.

Conclusion/Implications: ICON presents a significant advancement in the field of map learning by introducing a robust inverse consistency mechanism. The contributions of this research lie in its ability to improve map reliability and fidelity, making it an invaluable tool in applications such as computer vision, geographic information systems, and robotic navigation. The framework's potential for enhancing automated spatial analysis and data interpretation offers promising prospects for future innovations.

Keywords: ICON, inverse consistency, map learning, neural networks, spatial regularization, transformation accuracy, map prediction.
