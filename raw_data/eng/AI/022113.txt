This paper introduces ASM2TV, an Adaptive Semi-Supervised Multi-Task Multi-View learning framework designed to leverage both labeled and unlabeled data across multiple views and tasks. The objective is to improve model generalization and performance in scenarios where data is heterogeneous and labels are scarce. ASM2TV employs an adaptive weighting mechanism to dynamically adjust the contribution of each view and task based on their respective relevance and uncertainty. A novel semi-supervised learning approach is integrated to effectively utilize unlabeled data, enhancing the model's ability to capture underlying data distributions. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that ASM2TV outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and robustness, particularly when dealing with incomplete or noisy data. The proposed framework offers a promising solution for complex learning problems involving multi-view and multi-task data, contributing to advancements in areas such as data fusion and transfer learning. Keywords: Multi-view learning, Multi-task learning, Semi-supervised learning, Adaptive weighting, Data fusion.
