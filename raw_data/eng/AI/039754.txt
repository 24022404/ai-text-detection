This paper introduces SVDistNet, a novel self-supervised approach for near-field distance estimation using surround view fisheye cameras. The objective is to accurately estimate distances in the near-field region, a critical task for autonomous vehicles and robotics. Our method leverages a deep neural network architecture, combining photometric and geometric constraints to learn a robust distance estimation model. The approach utilizes a self-supervised framework, eliminating the need for ground-truth distance annotations. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of SVDistNet, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets. Key findings include improved distance estimation accuracy and robustness to varying lighting conditions and camera configurations. The proposed method has significant implications for autonomous systems, enabling more accurate and reliable perception capabilities. This research contributes to the advancement of computer vision and autonomous technologies, with potential applications in fields such as robotics, surveillance, and intelligent transportation systems. Keywords: self-supervised learning, near-field distance estimation, surround view fisheye cameras, deep learning, autonomous vehicles, computer vision.
