This paper challenges the conventional wisdom that deep learning is the only viable approach for tabular data analysis. Our objective is to reassess the role of traditional machine learning methods in handling tabular data, which is ubiquitous in various industries. We employ a hybrid approach, combining classical techniques with modern deep learning architectures to improve the accuracy and efficiency of tabular data processing. Our results show that, in many cases, simple yet robust traditional methods can outperform complex deep learning models, especially when data is limited or features are highly correlated. We also demonstrate that a careful feature engineering and selection process can significantly enhance the performance of both traditional and deep learning models. Our findings have important implications for practitioners, highlighting the need for a more nuanced understanding of the strengths and weaknesses of different approaches. By providing a comprehensive comparison of traditional and deep learning methods, this study contributes to the development of more effective and efficient tabular data analysis pipelines, with potential applications in fields such as finance, healthcare, and marketing. Key keywords: tabular data, deep learning, traditional machine learning, feature engineering, hybrid approach.
