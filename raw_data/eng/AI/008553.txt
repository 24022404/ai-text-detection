Color constancy is a critical challenge in computer vision, aiming to ensure consistent color representation across varying lighting conditions. This paper introduces *Cascading Convolutional Color Constancy (C4)*, a novel deep learning-based approach designed to improve the accuracy and robustness of color constancy algorithms. Unlike traditional methods that rely on handcrafted features or single-stage processing, C4 employs a cascaded convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture to iteratively refine color estimates. The model processes input images through multiple stages, each focusing on progressively finer adjustments to illumination parameters, enabling more precise color correction. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets, such as the Gehler-Shi and NUS-8 datasets, demonstrate that C4 outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of angular error reduction and computational efficiency. The proposed approach achieves a 15% improvement in accuracy compared to existing techniques, while maintaining real-time performance. The cascaded design also enhances adaptability to diverse lighting scenarios, making it suitable for applications in autonomous vehicles, robotics, and digital photography. This work contributes a scalable and effective solution to the color constancy problem, with potential implications for improving visual perception systems in dynamic environments.  

**Keywords**: Color constancy, convolutional neural networks, cascaded architecture, illumination estimation, computer vision, deep learning.
