The objective of this research is to develop an advanced reinforcement learning (RL) framework for the soccer dribbling task, a complex and dynamic problem requiring real-time decision-making and adaptability in uncertain environments. The study aims to enhance the performance of soccer-playing agents by optimizing their dribbling strategies to outperform traditional methods.

Methods/Approach: We employ a deep reinforcement learning approach, integrating neural networks with policy gradient methods to enable the agent to learn optimal dribbling policies through interaction with a simulated soccer environment. The model leverages both state-of-the-art reward mechanisms and adversarial scenarios to fine-tune dribbling skills in the presence of opposing defenders. Key components include action-space design tailored for soccer dynamics, and enhanced exploration strategies to improve learning efficiency.

Results/Findings: The developed RL model demonstrates superior dribbling capabilities compared to conventional rule-based systems and baseline RL methods. Over extensive simulations, our approach consistently achieved higher success rates in maintaining ball possession and successfully navigating through defender setups. Additionally, the model exhibits adaptable strategy refinements based on real-time game dynamics, highlighting its robustness in variable conditions.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes to the field of sports robotics and AI by providing a robust framework for enhancing automated soccer dribbling performance through reinforcement learning. The novel integration of adversarial training and advanced exploration techniques highlights potential applications in both robotic soccer competitions and real-world training simulations. Our findings pave the way for future work in developing comprehensive soccer-playing agents capable of executing a wide range of skills using AI-driven approaches.

Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Soccer Dribbling, Deep Learning, Policy Gradient, Autonomous Agents, Robotics, Game Dynamics.
