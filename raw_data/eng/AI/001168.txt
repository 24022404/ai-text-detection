This paper addresses the challenge of efficient learning in temporal difference (TD) methods, a crucial aspect of reinforcement learning. The objective is to improve the convergence and stability of TD learning by introducing source traces, a novel approach that modifies the traditional TD update rule. Our method utilizes a trace-based framework to incorporate additional information from the environment, enhancing the learning process. The key findings indicate that source traces lead to faster convergence and more accurate value function estimates compared to conventional TD methods. The results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in various environments, showcasing its potential for applications in complex decision-making tasks. This research contributes to the advancement of reinforcement learning by providing a new perspective on TD learning, highlighting the importance of incorporating environmental information to improve learning efficiency. Key aspects of this work include temporal difference learning, reinforcement learning, and trace-based methods, making it relevant to researchers and practitioners in the field of artificial intelligence and machine learning.
