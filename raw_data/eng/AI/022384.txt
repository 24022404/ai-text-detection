This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing the noise robustness of skeleton-based action recognition systems, which often suffer from performance degradation due to noisy input data. We propose a novel Predictively Encoded Graph Convolutional Network (PE-GCN) designed specifically to handle noise-impacted skeleton data. Our approach integrates predictive encoding with graph convolutional networks to effectively capture spatial-temporal dependencies in skeleton sequences, while simultaneously mitigating the adverse effects of noise. The PE-GCN dynamically adjusts to data irregularities through a predictive module that encodes reliable action context, paired with a noise-robust convolution mechanism. Experimental evaluations demonstrate that our model significantly outperforms existing methods on several benchmark datasets, showcasing improved accuracy and resilience under various noise conditions. Our results highlight the potential of the PE-GCN in real-world action recognition applications, where noise is an unavoidable factor. This research contributes to the advancement of graph-based neural networks and brings new insights into developing robust action recognition solutions. Keywords: Graph Convolutional Network, Skeleton-based Action Recognition, Noise Robustness, Predictive Encoding, Spatial-Temporal Analysis.
