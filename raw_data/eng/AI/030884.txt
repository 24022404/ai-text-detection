This paper proposes a novel image recognition approach using a Multi-Subspace Neural Network (MSNN) that leverages the strengths of subspace learning and deep neural networks. The objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of image recognition systems by learning multiple subspaces that capture diverse features of images. The MSNN model employs a hierarchical architecture that combines multiple subspace layers with convolutional neural networks, allowing for the extraction of rich and complementary features. Experimental results demonstrate that the MSNN outperforms state-of-the-art image recognition models on several benchmark datasets, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of the MSNN in capturing complex patterns and relationships in images, leading to enhanced recognition performance. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and robust image recognition systems, with potential applications in computer vision, robotics, and autonomous systems, and is relevant to keywords such as deep learning, neural networks, image recognition, and subspace learning.
