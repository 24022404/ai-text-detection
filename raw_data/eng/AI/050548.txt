Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) offer impressive capabilities for novel view synthesis, but their high computational cost hinders real-time rendering. This paper introduces PlenOctrees, a novel hybrid explicit-implicit representation that significantly accelerates NeRF rendering. Our approach converts a trained NeRF into an octree structure where each node stores both density and feature information. Empty regions are efficiently pruned, while dense regions are represented with high fidelity, facilitating hierarchical ray marching. A small, learnable neural network is used to decode features stored within the octree, reconstructing the radiance field. We demonstrate that PlenOctrees achieve real-time rendering frame rates while maintaining comparable visual quality to the original NeRF. Experiments on benchmark datasets show a significant speedup, up to two orders of magnitude, compared to standard NeRF rendering techniques. This enables interactive exploration and manipulation of complex 3D scenes represented as neural radiance fields. Keywords: Neural Radiance Fields, NeRF, Real-time Rendering, Octree, Implicit Representation, Volume Rendering.
