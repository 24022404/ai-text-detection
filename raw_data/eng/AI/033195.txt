Although ANSV offers a broad range of electronic telecommunications equipment and advanced IT solutions to its customers, the company’s IT helpdesk primarily deals with support requests via email and phone calls. This reliance on traditional communication channels has resulted in inefficiencies when it comes to managing, tracking, and prioritizing support requests, often causing delays and making it difficult to retrieve crucial information when needed. As ANSV continues to grow and expand its business, it is imperative for its IT support department to implement a more effective system that can streamline request management, ensure data is systematically stored and easily retrieved, and ultimately provide better support to both clients and internal teams.
