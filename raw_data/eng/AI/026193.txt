This paper proposes a novel approach for human activity recognition and fall detection using Fourier domain features. The objective is to develop a robust and efficient system that can accurately identify various human activities and detect falls in real-time. Our method utilizes a Fourier transform to extract relevant features from accelerometer and gyroscope sensors, which are then fed into a machine learning model for classification. The results show that our approach achieves high accuracy and outperforms existing methods in terms of precision and recall. The key findings indicate that the Fourier domain features are highly effective in capturing the underlying patterns of human activities and falls. This research contributes to the field of human activity recognition and fall detection by introducing a new feature extraction technique that can be used in various applications, including healthcare and surveillance. The proposed system has the potential to improve the quality of life for elderly individuals and people with disabilities by providing timely assistance in case of falls. Key keywords: human activity recognition, fall detection, Fourier domain features, machine learning, accelerometer, gyroscope.
