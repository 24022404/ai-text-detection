This paper addresses the challenge of off-policy deep reinforcement learning, where an agent learns from logged data without the need for exploration. The objective is to develop a method that can effectively learn policies from existing data, eliminating the requirement for additional environment interactions. We propose a novel approach that leverages a combination of offline data and advanced function approximation techniques to learn high-performing policies. Our method utilizes a modified deep Q-network architecture and introduces a new regularization technique to mitigate the distributional shift between the logged data and the learned policy. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing off-policy methods, achieving significant improvements in policy performance and sample efficiency. The key findings of this research highlight the potential of off-policy deep reinforcement learning for real-world applications, where exploration is often impractical or costly. Our contributions include a novel off-policy learning algorithm, improved sample efficiency, and enhanced policy performance, making it a valuable addition to the field of reinforcement learning, with relevant keywords including off-policy learning, deep reinforcement learning, Q-learning, and sample efficiency.
