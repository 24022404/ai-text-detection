This paper aims to improve the accuracy of temporal action localization by exploring stronger feature representations. Our approach involves designing a novel architecture that integrates both spatial and temporal features, leveraging the strengths of 3D convolutional neural networks and graph-based models. We propose a multi-stage framework that first extracts features from video clips using a 3D convolutional neural network, followed by a graph-based module that models the temporal relationships between features. Our experiments demonstrate that the proposed architecture outperforms state-of-the-art methods on popular benchmarks, achieving significant improvements in terms of precision and recall. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a novel feature fusion mechanism and the development of a more effective temporal modeling approach. Our work has important implications for applications such as video analysis, surveillance, and human-computer interaction, and highlights the potential of stronger feature representations for temporal action localization. Key keywords: temporal action localization, 3D convolutional neural networks, graph-based models, feature fusion, video analysis.
