Healthcare cost prediction is crucial for effective resource allocation and financial planning in healthcare systems. This paper introduces a novel approach for predicting patient-specific healthcare costs by learning hidden patterns from multivariate time series data using Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs). We propose a CNN-based model that automatically extracts complex temporal dependencies and inter-variable relationships from patient data, including diagnoses, procedures, and medication history, represented as multivariate time series. The model is trained and evaluated on a large-scale real-world healthcare dataset. Our results demonstrate that the proposed CNN architecture significantly outperforms traditional statistical models and other machine learning methods in predicting healthcare costs, achieving a substantial improvement in prediction accuracy (e.g., reduced Mean Absolute Error by X%). The learned hidden patterns offer valuable insights into the underlying drivers of healthcare expenditures. This study highlights the potential of CNNs for uncovering complex relationships in patient data and improving the accuracy of healthcare cost prediction, enabling more informed decision-making and resource management in healthcare settings. Keywords: Convolutional Neural Networks, Healthcare Cost Prediction, Multivariate Time Series, Deep Learning, Healthcare Analytics.
