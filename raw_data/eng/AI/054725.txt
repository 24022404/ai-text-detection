This paper proposes a novel approach to learning likelihoods with conditional normalizing flows, addressing the challenge of modeling complex probability distributions in machine learning. Our objective is to develop a flexible and efficient framework for density estimation, leveraging the strengths of normalizing flows and conditional models. We introduce a new method that combines the benefits of flow-based models with conditional distributions, enabling the effective modeling of complex dependencies and relationships in data. Our approach utilizes a conditional normalizing flow architecture, which allows for the efficient computation of likelihoods and enables the incorporation of side information into the model. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method in modeling real-world data distributions, outperforming existing approaches in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The key contributions of this research include the development of a conditional normalizing flow framework, the introduction of a new inference algorithm, and the demonstration of state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets. Our work has significant implications for applications in machine learning, including image and speech modeling, anomaly detection, and generative modeling, with key keywords including conditional normalizing flows, density estimation, likelihood learning, and deep learning.
