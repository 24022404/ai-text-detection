Video super-resolution (VSR) aims to generate high-resolution video frames from low-resolution counterparts, a challenging task due to complex motion and texture details. This paper introduces Temporally Deformable Alignment Network (TDAN), a novel VSR framework designed to explicitly handle temporal misalignment between frames. TDAN employs a deformable convolution-based alignment module that learns spatio-temporal offsets to accurately register neighboring frames. Furthermore, a temporal attention mechanism dynamically aggregates information from aligned frames, focusing on relevant features for reconstruction. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that TDAN achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of both quantitative metrics (PSNR, SSIM) and perceptual quality. Specifically, TDAN exhibits superior performance in handling complex motion and occlusions compared to existing alignment-based methods. The proposed approach offers significant improvements in VSR, particularly for videos with substantial inter-frame motion, contributing to enhanced video quality and visual experience. Keywords: Video Super-Resolution, Deformable Convolution, Temporal Alignment, Attention Mechanism, Deep Learning.
