Abstract:

Semantic inpainting, the process of filling in missing or corrupted regions of an image in a visually plausible way, plays a critical role in fields such as digital restoration, object removal, and augmented reality. However, traditional inpainting methods often struggle to reconstruct complex structures and maintain global image consistency, particularly when large or contextually significant regions are missing. This paper proposes a novel context-aware semantic inpainting framework that leverages deep learning techniques to infer missing content based on a comprehensive understanding of both local and global image contexts. The core approach integrates a multi-scale convolutional neural network (CNN) with an attention mechanism, allowing the model to dynamically focus on relevant features and enhance semantic coherence within the inpainted regions. Extensive experiments on benchmark image datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art inpainting solutions in terms of both quantitative metrics and perceptual quality. The proposed framework not only restores high-fidelity textures but also preserves the overall semantic integrity of complex scenes. Key contributions include the introduction of a context-sensitive architecture and advanced feature aggregation strategies, which significantly improve reconstruction accuracy. The findings highlight the potential of context-aware semantic inpainting in applications such as intelligent photo editing, virtual content creation, and digital heritage preservation. Keywords: semantic inpainting, context-aware, deep learning, CNN, attention mechanism, image restoration, computer vision.
