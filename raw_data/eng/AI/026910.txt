This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing the geometric consistency of 3D mesh models, which are often utilized in various applications such as virtual reality, gaming, and architectural visualization. Inconsistencies in mesh geometry can lead to visual artifacts and inaccuracies in rendering, necessitating effective correction methods.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel approach involving a machine learning framework that leverages geometric deep learning techniques to automatically correct mesh inconsistencies. Our method employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) designed specifically for mesh processing, enabling it to learn and predict the necessary corrections while maintaining an overall geometrically consistent structure.

Results/Findings: The proposed model demonstrates superior performance in correcting mesh anomalies compared to traditional methods, achieving enhanced visual accuracy and consistency. Through extensive experimentation on various benchmark datasets, our approach shows an improvement in metrics such as geometric fidelity and surface smoothness. The model's capability to generalize across different types of mesh data highlights its robustness and effectiveness.

Conclusion/Implications: The development of this framework contributes significantly to the field of 3D modeling by providing a reliable solution for mesh correction tasks. Our findings suggest potential applications in industries requiring high-fidelity 3D representations, such as entertainment, construction, and medical imaging. By automating the correction process, this research not only improves efficiency but also paves the way for future innovations in geometrically consistent mesh processing.

Keywords: Geometric consistency, 3D mesh correction, geometric deep learning, convolutional neural networks, mesh processing, visual accuracy.
