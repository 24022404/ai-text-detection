PyKEEN 1.0 is a comprehensive Python library designed to facilitate the training and evaluation of knowledge graph embeddings, addressing the need for robust tools in this rapidly evolving research area. The primary objective of our work is to provide a flexible and user-friendly framework that supports a wide array of embedding models and evaluation protocols, enabling researchers and developers to advance the field of knowledge representation. The library implements a modular architecture, allowing for seamless integration of various embedding techniques such as TransE, DistMult, and ComplEx, alongside custom user-defined models. Our approach includes rigorous benchmarking tools to compare the performance of different models on standard datasets. Key findings from our experiments demonstrate that PyKEEN 1.0 achieves competitive results and facilitates reproducibility in embedding research. The contributions of this work lie in its extensive support for hyperparameter optimization, ease of extensibility, and comprehensive documentation, making it an invaluable resource for both academic and industry applications. By providing an accessible and powerful toolset, PyKEEN 1.0 aims to catalyze innovation in the domain of knowledge graph embeddings, ultimately enhancing the capabilities of AI systems in handling complex relational data. Keywords associated with our research include knowledge graph, embeddings, Python, machine learning, and benchmarking.
