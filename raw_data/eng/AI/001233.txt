Human pose estimation is a critical task in computer vision with applications ranging from healthcare to augmented reality. Despite significant advancements, existing methods often struggle with complex poses and occlusions, limiting their accuracy and robustness. This paper introduces a novel Knowledge-Guided Deep Fractal Neural Network (KG-DFNN) framework designed to address these challenges. The proposed approach integrates domain-specific knowledge with a fractal-based deep learning architecture to enhance feature extraction and spatial reasoning. The fractal structure enables multi-scale hierarchical learning, while the knowledge-guided mechanism ensures the model leverages anatomical constraints and prior pose information. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets, including COCO and MPII, demonstrate that KG-DFNN achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and robustness, particularly in challenging scenarios. The framework’s ability to generalize across diverse datasets and its computational efficiency further highlight its practical applicability. This research contributes to the field by combining domain knowledge with advanced neural architectures, offering a scalable and effective solution for human pose estimation. Key innovations include the fractal-based design and the integration of anatomical priors, which collectively improve model interpretability and performance. Keywords: Human Pose Estimation, Deep Learning, Fractal Neural Networks, Knowledge-Guided Models, Computer Vision.
