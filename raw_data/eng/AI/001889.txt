This paper addresses the challenge of clustering multi-view data, where samples are represented from multiple perspectives or modalities, by proposing a novel Deep Embedded Multi-view Clustering framework with Collaborative Training (DEMVC-CT). The objective is to effectively harness complementary information from different views to enhance clustering accuracy, overcoming the limitations of traditional single-view and non-deep approaches that often struggle with complex feature relationships and missing view data. The proposed approach integrates deep autoencoders to learn robust, low-dimensional representations for each view, followed by a shared embedding space that captures consistent and discriminative features across all modalities. A collaborative training strategy is introduced, enabling mutual guidance and knowledge transfer between views during both representation learning and clustering optimization. Experimental evaluations on several benchmark multi-view datasets demonstrate that DEMVC-CT significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art multi-view clustering methods in terms of clustering accuracy, normalized mutual information, and robustness to incomplete views. The findings suggest that collaborative training within deep embedded architectures offers a powerful mechanism for multi-view learning, with potential applications in computer vision, bioinformatics, and social network analysis requiring integrated multi-modal data understanding. Keywords: multi-view clustering, deep learning, collaborative training, representation learning, unsupervised learning.
