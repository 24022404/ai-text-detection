This paper proposes a novel communication-efficient approach to distributed Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) by leveraging local power iterations. The objective is to reduce the communication overhead in distributed SVD computations, which is a critical bottleneck in large-scale data processing. Our method employs a decentralized framework, where each node performs local power iterations to approximate the dominant singular vectors, and then combines these approximations through a consensus protocol. The key findings indicate that our approach achieves significant communication savings while maintaining comparable accuracy to traditional distributed SVD methods. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our method in various scenarios, including distributed data processing and parallel machine learning applications. The proposed technique contributes to the development of efficient distributed algorithms for large-scale data analysis, with potential applications in data mining, recommendation systems, and artificial intelligence. Key contributions include the design of a communication-efficient distributed SVD algorithm, the analysis of its convergence properties, and the demonstration of its scalability in real-world applications. Relevant keywords: distributed SVD, power iterations, communication efficiency, decentralized algorithms, parallel computing, big data processing.
