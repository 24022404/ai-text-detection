The rapid proliferation of mobile devices has necessitated the development of efficient neural network architectures tailored for on-device inference. This paper introduces MnasNet, a novel platform-aware neural architecture search (NAS) framework designed to optimize deep learning models for mobile platforms. Unlike traditional NAS approaches that focus solely on accuracy, MnasNet incorporates latency as a key optimization objective, ensuring that the resulting models achieve a balance between performance and efficiency. The proposed method leverages reinforcement learning to explore a diverse search space of mobile-friendly architectures, guided by a multi-objective reward function that accounts for both accuracy and real-world latency on target hardware. Experimental results demonstrate that MnasNet generates models that outperform state-of-the-art handcrafted and NAS-based architectures, achieving up to 1.8x faster inference speeds on mobile CPUs while maintaining competitive accuracy on ImageNet classification. The framework's ability to adapt to specific hardware constraints highlights its potential for enabling efficient deployment of deep learning applications across a wide range of mobile devices. This work contributes a scalable and practical solution for designing lightweight neural networks, paving the way for future advancements in mobile AI.  

**Keywords**: Neural Architecture Search, Mobile Optimization, Deep Learning, Reinforcement Learning, Latency-Aware Models, MnasNet.
