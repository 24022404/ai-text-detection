This paper addresses the challenge of co-salient object detection, where the goal is to identify common salient objects within multiple images. Our objective is to develop an effective group collaborative learning approach to improve the accuracy and robustness of co-salient object detection models. We propose a novel framework that leverages the strengths of both deep learning and collaborative filtering techniques. Our method utilizes a multi-task learning strategy, where a shared encoder is trained to learn common features across different images, while separate decoders are used to detect salient objects in each image. The results show that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in precision, recall, and F1-score. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a collaborative learning paradigm for co-salient object detection and the development of a robust framework that can handle diverse image datasets. Our work has important implications for applications such as image retrieval, object recognition, and computer vision. Key keywords: co-salient object detection, group collaborative learning, deep learning, computer vision, object recognition.
