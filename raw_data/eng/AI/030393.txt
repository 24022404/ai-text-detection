3D point clouds are increasingly used in applications such as autonomous driving, robotics, and augmented reality, but they are vulnerable to adversarial attacks that compromise their integrity. This paper introduces DUP-Net, a novel Denoiser and Upsampler Network designed to defend 3D point clouds against adversarial perturbations. The proposed framework leverages a two-stage approach: first, a denoising module removes adversarial noise while preserving the geometric structure of the point cloud, and second, an upsampling module reconstructs the point cloud to enhance its resolution and robustness. DUP-Net integrates advanced deep learning techniques, including graph-based feature extraction and adversarial training, to achieve state-of-the-art defense performance. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that DUP-Net significantly improves the robustness of 3D point clouds against various adversarial attacks, outperforming existing methods in terms of both accuracy and computational efficiency. The proposed network also exhibits strong generalization capabilities across different attack scenarios and point cloud densities. This work contributes to the growing field of 3D adversarial defense by providing a scalable and effective solution for securing point cloud data in real-world applications.  

**Keywords**: 3D point clouds, adversarial defense, denoising, upsampling, deep learning, robustness, graph-based feature extraction.
