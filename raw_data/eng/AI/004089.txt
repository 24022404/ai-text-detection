Incremental sequence learning addresses the challenge of continuously updating sequence models with new data without retraining from scratch. This paper introduces a novel approach, the Dynamically Expanding Memory Network (DEMN), designed to efficiently incorporate new sequence information while preserving previously learned patterns. DEMN utilizes a modular memory architecture that allocates new memory units for incoming data, mitigating catastrophic forgetting. The method incorporates a self-attention mechanism to weigh the relevance of past and present information. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that DEMN achieves comparable accuracy to traditional batch learning methods with significantly reduced training time and computational resources. Furthermore, DEMN exhibits superior performance in dynamic environments where the underlying data distribution evolves over time. This research offers a practical solution for sequence learning tasks requiring continuous adaptation, such as real-time speech recognition and online anomaly detection. Keywords: incremental learning, sequence learning, memory networks, online learning, continual learning, self-attention.
