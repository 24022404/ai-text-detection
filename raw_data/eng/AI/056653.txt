The increasing deployment of LiDAR sensors in autonomous vehicles and robotics necessitates robust semantic scene understanding. This paper introduces SemanticKITTI, a comprehensive dataset aimed at advancing semantic interpretation of LiDAR sequences in dynamic environments.

Methods/Approach: SemanticKITTI provides a large-scale, diverse, and richly annotated dataset derived from the KITTI odometry benchmark. It encompasses fully labeled 3D point cloud sequences captured by LiDAR sensors, offering per-point semantic class annotations across 22 distinct categories. Our detailed dataset facilitates the training and evaluation of machine learning models for semantic segmentation and orientation tasks.

Results/Findings: Through extensive experimentation, we highlight the dataset's capacity to improve model performance on semantic scene understanding tasks. Benchmarks using various state-of-the-art deep learning architectures demonstrate enhanced accuracy in distinguishing between diverse semantic classes. Comparative analysis with existing datasets underscores SemanticKITTI's superior scope and applicability in real-world autonomous driving scenarios.

Conclusion/Implications: SemanticKITTI represents a significant advancement in resources available for LiDAR-based semantic comprehension, enabling researchers to develop more sophisticated and accurate models. Its comprehensive nature positions SemanticKITTI as a cornerstone for future research and development in autonomous systems, facilitating enhanced perception capabilities. Key applications of this dataset include autonomous vehicle navigation, environmental mapping, and robotic interaction in complex environments.

Keywords: SemanticKITTI, LiDAR, semantic scene understanding, autonomous vehicles, 3D point cloud, dataset, semantic segmentation.
