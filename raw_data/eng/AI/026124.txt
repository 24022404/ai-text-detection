This paper addresses the challenge of accurate depth prediction from single images, a critical task in computer vision with applications in robotics, augmented reality, and autonomous systems. We propose a novel approach leveraging Fully Convolutional Residual Networks (FCRNs) to enhance depth estimation accuracy. Unlike traditional methods that rely on handcrafted features or shallow networks, our model integrates deep residual learning with fully convolutional architectures, enabling end-to-end training and improved feature extraction. The network is trained on large-scale RGB-D datasets, optimizing for pixel-wise depth prediction. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets, outperforming existing methods in terms of both accuracy and computational efficiency. Key contributions include the introduction of a lightweight yet powerful network architecture and a robust training strategy that mitigates overfitting. The proposed method shows significant potential for real-time applications, offering a balance between precision and speed. This work advances the field of depth prediction by providing a scalable and efficient solution, paving the way for further research in 3D scene understanding and reconstruction.  

**Keywords**: Depth prediction, Fully Convolutional Networks, Residual Networks, Computer Vision, 3D Reconstruction.
