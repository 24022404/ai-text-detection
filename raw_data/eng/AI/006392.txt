This paper proposes a novel deep structural model for analyzing correlated multivariate time series, aiming to capture complex temporal dependencies and relationships between variables. Our approach leverages a combination of recurrent neural networks and graph convolutional networks to learn representations of time series data, incorporating both autoregressive and cross-variable correlations. The model is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating improved performance in forecasting and anomaly detection tasks compared to existing methods. Key findings include the ability to identify latent patterns and relationships between variables, as well as robustness to noise and missing data. The proposed model contributes to the field of time series analysis by providing a flexible and scalable framework for modeling complex correlations, with potential applications in finance, climate science, and healthcare. Our research highlights the importance of integrating deep learning techniques with structural modeling to uncover insights in multivariate time series data, with relevant keywords including deep learning, time series analysis, graph neural networks, and multivariate modeling.
