This paper explores the application of cinematic rendering techniques to fine-tune deep neural networks for medical image analysis. The objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of deep learning models by leveraging photorealistic medical images. Our approach involves integrating cinematic rendering with deep learning architectures, allowing for the generation of highly realistic and detailed medical images. These images are then used to fine-tune deep neural networks, enhancing their ability to detect and classify medical conditions. Our results show significant improvements in model performance, with increased accuracy and reduced false positives. The use of cinematic rendering enables the creation of diverse and realistic training data, reducing the need for large amounts of real-world data. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and reliable deep learning models for medical image analysis, with potential applications in computer-aided diagnosis and personalized medicine. Key keywords: deep learning, cinematic rendering, medical imaging, photorealistic images, fine-tuning, neural networks, computer-aided diagnosis.
