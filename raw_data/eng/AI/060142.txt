This paper addresses the problem of approximate graph matching, a fundamental challenge in computer science with applications in data analysis, computer vision, and network science. Our objective is to develop an efficient algorithm for computing optimal assignments in graph matching, overcoming the limitations of existing methods that often suffer from high computational complexity. We propose a novel approach based on a linear-time algorithm that leverages advanced techniques from combinatorial optimization and graph theory. Our method achieves significant improvements in computational efficiency while maintaining high accuracy in matching results. Experimental evaluations demonstrate the superiority of our algorithm, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of speed and scalability. The key contributions of this research include a new linear-time approximation algorithm for graph matching, which enables fast and accurate analysis of large-scale networks. This breakthrough has important implications for various fields, including pattern recognition, image processing, and social network analysis, where graph matching plays a crucial role. Our approach is particularly relevant to applications involving big data, where computational efficiency is essential. Key keywords: graph matching, linear-time algorithm, approximate matching, combinatorial optimization, network science.
