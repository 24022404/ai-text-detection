This paper addresses the challenge of question answering in the context of video understanding, specifically focusing on learning from narrated videos at scale. We introduce a novel approach, "Just Ask," that leverages millions of narrated videos to train a model capable of answering diverse questions about video content. Our method combines visual and auditory information extracted from videos with corresponding natural language narrations using a transformer-based architecture. This architecture enables the model to effectively attend to relevant video segments and narrations when answering questions. We demonstrate that training on a massive dataset of narrated videos significantly improves performance on various video question answering benchmarks. Specifically, our model achieves state-of-the-art results on the benchmark datasets, surpassing existing methods by a significant margin. The results indicate that our approach successfully learns rich representations from weakly supervised data, leading to improved video understanding and question answering abilities. This research provides a scalable and effective solution for video question answering, with potential applications in areas such as video search, education, and accessibility. Keywords: Video Question Answering, Narrated Videos, Video Understanding, Deep Learning, Transformer Networks, Multi-modal Learning.
