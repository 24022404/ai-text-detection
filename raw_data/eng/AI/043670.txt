The growing demand for efficient and scalable neural network architectures has driven the exploration of Processing-In-Memory (PIM) systems, which aim to reduce data movement and energy consumption. However, PIM systems are inherently susceptible to noise and variability, which can degrade the performance of neural networks. This paper introduces a novel noise-aware normalization technique designed to enhance the robustness of neural networks deployed in PIM environments. Our approach dynamically adjusts normalization parameters to account for noise-induced variations during computation, ensuring stable and reliable performance. We evaluate the proposed method on benchmark datasets using a simulated PIM framework, demonstrating significant improvements in accuracy and resilience compared to traditional normalization techniques. The results highlight the effectiveness of noise-aware normalization in mitigating the impact of hardware-level noise, making it a promising solution for deploying robust neural networks in PIM systems. This work contributes to advancing the reliability of AI applications in next-generation computing architectures, with potential implications for edge computing, IoT, and energy-efficient AI systems.  

**Keywords**: Processing-In-Memory, neural networks, noise-aware normalization, robustness, AI hardware, edge computing.
