This paper presents a novel Deep Spiking Convolutional Neural Network (DSCNN) architecture for single object localization, leveraging the strengths of deep continuous local learning. The objective is to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of object localization tasks by incorporating spiking neural networks (SNNs) and continuous learning mechanisms. Our approach utilizes a hybrid model that combines the benefits of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) with the event-driven processing of SNNs, allowing for more robust and adaptive feature extraction. The DSCNN is trained using a deep continuous local learning framework, which enables the network to learn and adapt from continuous streams of data. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our approach, achieving state-of-the-art performance in single object localization tasks. The proposed DSCNN architecture offers significant improvements in terms of accuracy, computational efficiency, and real-time processing capabilities, making it suitable for applications such as robotics, autonomous vehicles, and surveillance systems. Key contributions of this work include the introduction of a novel SNN-based architecture for object localization, the integration of deep continuous local learning, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in real-world scenarios, highlighting the potential of spiking neural networks and continuous learning for computer vision tasks.
