This paper addresses the challenge of accurately recognizing and classifying human actions in videos, which is a crucial component in various applications such as surveillance, human-computer interaction, and autonomous systems. Recognizing temporal correlations within action sequences remains a significant task that this research aims to tackle by developing a novel learning framework.

Methods/Approach: We propose a Temporal Distinct Representation Learning (TDRL) model designed specifically for capturing intrinsic temporal features across video frames. Our approach employs a specialized neural network architecture that integrates recurrent layers with attention mechanisms to effectively enhance the discrimination between similar yet distinct actions over time. This facilitates the extraction of robust temporal features that support accurate action recognition.

Results/Findings: The TDRL model demonstrates superior performance on benchmark datasets, outperforming existing models in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. Through comprehensive experiments, our model achieves an increase in recognition accuracy of up to 5%, compared to state-of-the-art methods. Additionally, our approach significantly reduces training times, confirming its suitability for real-time applications.

Conclusion/Implications: The innovative temporal feature extraction capability of the TDRL model provides significant contributions to the field of action recognition, offering enhanced accuracy and efficiency. This research not only lays the groundwork for further advancements in temporal representation learning but also extends the practical applications of action recognition systems in industries including security and entertainment.

Keywords: Temporal Representation, Action Recognition, Neural Networks, Attention Mechanisms, Video Analytics, Real-Time Applications.
