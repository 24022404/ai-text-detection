Safe exploration is a critical challenge in reinforcement learning (RL), particularly in environments where unsafe actions can lead to system failures or costly outcomes. This paper addresses this issue by introducing a novel framework for safe exploration based on Early Terminated Markov Decision Processes (ETMDPs). In our approach, the standard MDP is augmented with early termination states that model catastrophic or undesirable outcomes, allowing the agent to learn safety constraints inherently during policy optimization. We propose an algorithm that explicitly accounts for the probability of early termination, enabling the agent to avoid unsafe trajectories while maximizing cumulative rewards. Experimental evaluations are conducted on a variety of benchmark tasks, demonstrating that our method significantly reduces the rate of unsafe events compared to conventional RL algorithms, without sacrificing overall performance. The results indicate that solving ETMDPs enables more reliable exploration in complex environments where safety is paramount. Our contributions pave the way for safer deployment of RL-based systems in real-world applications such as robotics, autonomous driving, and healthcare. Keywords: safe exploration, Markov Decision Process, early termination, reinforcement learning, safety constraints, policy optimization.
