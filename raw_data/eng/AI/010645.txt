This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing the resolution of wavelet-encoded images, a critical task in applications such as medical imaging, satellite imagery, and digital communications. The objective is to develop a robust super-resolution framework capable of reconstructing high-resolution images from low-resolution wavelet-compressed data. To achieve this, we propose a novel deep learning-based approach that integrates wavelet transform properties with advanced convolutional neural networks (CNNs). Our method leverages the multi-resolution analysis of wavelets to preserve structural details while employing a generative adversarial network (GAN) architecture to synthesize high-frequency components lost during compression. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in image quality, with our approach outperforming traditional interpolation methods and existing super-resolution techniques in terms of peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM). The proposed framework also exhibits superior generalization across diverse datasets, including natural and medical images. This research contributes to the field by introducing a computationally efficient and scalable solution for wavelet-based super-resolution, with potential applications in real-time image processing and remote sensing.  

**Keywords**: super-resolution, wavelet transform, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, generative adversarial networks, image reconstruction.
