This paper introduces PanRep, a novel graph neural network (GNN) framework designed to generate universal node embeddings across diverse heterogeneous graphs. The objective is to learn node representations that capture both structural and semantic information inherent in graphs with varying node and edge types. PanRep employs a meta-learning approach, training on a distribution of heterogeneous graphs to enable effective generalization to unseen graph structures. The model leverages type-specific aggregation and propagation mechanisms, allowing it to adapt to the unique characteristics of each node and edge type. Experimental results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that PanRep achieves state-of-the-art performance in node classification and link prediction tasks compared to existing GNN methods. Furthermore, PanRep exhibits strong transferability, successfully generating high-quality embeddings for graphs with entirely new node and edge types. These findings indicate that PanRep offers a powerful and versatile solution for learning universal node embeddings in heterogeneous graph environments, with broad applicability in domains such as social networks, knowledge graphs, and biological networks. Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Heterogeneous Graphs, Node Embeddings, Meta-Learning, Graph Representation Learning.
