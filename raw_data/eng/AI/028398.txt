The increasing availability of heterogeneous multivariate time-series (HMTS) data in domains such as healthcare presents both opportunities and challenges for extracting meaningful patterns. This paper addresses the problem of effectively mining HMTS data to uncover actionable insights, with a specific focus on home health telecare applications. We propose a novel framework that integrates advanced machine learning techniques, including deep learning and unsupervised clustering, to handle the complexity and variability inherent in HMTS data. Our approach leverages temporal dependencies and cross-variable relationships to identify patterns that are both interpretable and clinically relevant. Experimental results on real-world telecare datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of our method in detecting anomalies, predicting patient outcomes, and supporting personalized care recommendations. Compared to existing methods, our framework achieves superior performance in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The findings highlight the potential of our approach to enhance decision-making in home health telecare, offering a scalable solution for improving patient monitoring and intervention strategies. This research contributes to the growing body of work on HMTS analysis by introducing a robust and adaptable methodology that can be extended to other domains with similar data characteristics.  

**Keywords**: heterogeneous multivariate time-series, home health telecare, machine learning, deep learning, anomaly detection, personalized care.
