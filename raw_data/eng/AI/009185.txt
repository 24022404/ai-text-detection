This paper introduces O-CNN, a novel octree-based convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture designed for efficient and effective 3D shape analysis. The objective is to develop a deep learning model that can accurately process and understand complex 3D geometries. Our approach utilizes an octree data structure to hierarchically represent 3D shapes, enabling the proposed O-CNN to learn multi-scale features and capture detailed geometric information. The network is trained on a large dataset of 3D models and demonstrates superior performance in 3D shape classification, segmentation, and reconstruction tasks. Experimental results show that O-CNN outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a novel octree-based CNN architecture and the demonstration of its effectiveness in 3D shape analysis. This work has significant implications for various applications, including computer-aided design, robotics, and computer vision. Key keywords: 3D shape analysis, octree-based CNN, deep learning, 3D reconstruction, geometric processing.
