This paper explores the innovative use of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) for image and video synthesis, focusing on the development and application of novel algorithms in this domain. The primary objective is to address the challenges in generating realistic and high-quality visual content from diverse datasets. Leveraging the dual neural network structure of GANs, the research presents advancements in algorithm design that enhance the stability and efficacy of training processes, resulting in improved image and video outputs. Key findings demonstrate that the proposed approaches significantly outperform existing methods in terms of fidelity and diversity of synthesized images and videos. Comparative analysis with state-of-the-art techniques highlights the superior performance of our models in various benchmarks and real-world applications. The practical implications of this work are far-reaching, with potential applications ranging from entertainment and film production to medical imaging and augmented reality. This study contributes to the field by refining GAN architectures and offering insights into the broader applicability of AI-driven synthesis techniques. Keywords: Generative Adversarial Networks, GAN, image synthesis, video synthesis, neural networks, artificial intelligence.
