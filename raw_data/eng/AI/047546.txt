This paper addresses the challenge of band selection from hyperspectral images, a crucial step for improving the efficiency and accuracy of image processing and analysis. Our objective is to develop an effective method for selecting the most informative bands from hyperspectral images. To achieve this, we propose an attention-based convolutional neural network (CNN) approach that leverages the strengths of both attention mechanisms and deep learning techniques. Our method learns to focus on the most relevant bands and suppress irrelevant ones, resulting in a significant reduction in dimensionality while preserving critical information. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our approach over traditional band selection methods, with improved classification accuracy and reduced computational complexity. The key findings of this study highlight the potential of attention-based CNNs for hyperspectral image analysis, enabling more efficient and accurate processing of large-scale hyperspectral data. This research contributes to the advancement of hyperspectral imaging and has important implications for various applications, including remote sensing, environmental monitoring, and agricultural management. Key keywords: hyperspectral images, band selection, attention-based CNNs, deep learning, dimensionality reduction.
