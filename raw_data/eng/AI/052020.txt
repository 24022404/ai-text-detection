This paper aims to address the challenge of providing efficient explanations for complex machine learning models by introducing a novel framework for empirical explainers. Our objective is to develop a method that can generate accurate and interpretable explanations while minimizing computational overhead. We propose a hybrid approach that combines model-agnostic explanations with empirical analysis, leveraging techniques from feature attribution and model interpretability. Our results show that the proposed framework outperforms existing explanation methods in terms of efficiency and accuracy, particularly for large-scale datasets. Key findings include significant reductions in explanation time and improvements in explanation fidelity. The implications of this research are substantial, enabling the widespread adoption of explainable AI in high-stakes applications such as healthcare and finance. Our contributions include a novel explanation algorithm, empirical evaluations on benchmark datasets, and insights into the trade-offs between explanation accuracy and computational efficiency. Relevant keywords: explainable AI, empirical explainers, model interpretability, feature attribution, efficient explanations.
