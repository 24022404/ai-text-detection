This paper addresses the challenge of sequential budgeted learning, where decisions must be made under limited computational or resource constraints. The objective is to develop a framework that optimally allocates a fixed budget across sequential learning tasks to maximize performance while minimizing uncertainty. We propose a novel approach called Confidence-Budget Matching (CBM), which dynamically adjusts resource allocation based on the confidence levels of predictions and the remaining budget. The method integrates probabilistic modeling with adaptive decision-making to ensure efficient utilization of resources. Experimental results demonstrate that CBM outperforms traditional budget allocation strategies across multiple benchmarks, achieving higher accuracy and lower variance in outcomes. The findings highlight the importance of adaptive confidence-based budgeting in sequential learning scenarios. This research contributes a scalable and practical solution for applications such as online recommendation systems, active learning, and resource-constrained AI deployments. Key innovations include the integration of confidence estimation with budget optimization and the development of a lightweight, real-time decision-making algorithm. The proposed framework has broad implications for improving the efficiency of machine learning systems operating under strict computational or financial constraints.  

**Keywords**: sequential learning, budget allocation, confidence estimation, adaptive decision-making, resource optimization, machine learning efficiency.
