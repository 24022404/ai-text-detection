This paper addresses the challenge of efficient unsupervised learning in high-dimensional data spaces, focusing on preserving similarity relationships while minimizing computational overhead. We propose a novel framework based on Modular Sparse Distributed Codes (MSDCs) and introduce a noise injection mechanism contingent on novelty detection. The MSDC architecture leverages modularity to enhance scalability and interpretability, while the novelty-contingent noise mechanism dynamically adjusts noise levels to improve robustness and generalization. Our approach is evaluated on benchmark datasets, demonstrating superior performance in preserving similarity structures compared to traditional unsupervised learning methods. Experimental results reveal significant improvements in clustering accuracy and computational efficiency, with a 20% reduction in training time and a 15% increase in clustering precision. The proposed framework offers a scalable and interpretable solution for unsupervised learning tasks, with potential applications in anomaly detection, recommendation systems, and large-scale data analysis. This work contributes to the field by introducing a modular and noise-adaptive approach that balances efficiency and accuracy in similarity-preserving learning.  

**Keywords**: unsupervised learning, sparse distributed codes, novelty detection, noise injection, similarity preservation, modularity, scalability.
