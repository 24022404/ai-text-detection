This study aims to develop a deep learning model, EasiCSDeep, for the identification of Cervical Spondylosis using surface electromyography (sEMG) signals. The proposed approach utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture to extract relevant features from sEMG signals, which are then classified to detect the presence of Cervical Spondylosis. The model is trained and validated on a dataset of sEMG signals collected from patients with and without Cervical Spondylosis. The results show that EasiCSDeep achieves a high accuracy of 92.5% in identifying Cervical Spondylosis, outperforming traditional machine learning methods. The key findings of this study highlight the potential of deep learning models in analyzing sEMG signals for the diagnosis of musculoskeletal disorders. The EasiCSDeep model contributes to the existing literature by providing a non-invasive and accurate method for Cervical Spondylosis identification, which can be used in clinical settings to improve diagnosis and treatment outcomes. The study's novelty lies in its use of deep learning techniques for sEMG signal analysis, demonstrating the effectiveness of this approach in identifying complex patterns in biomedical signals. Keywords: Cervical Spondylosis, surface electromyography, deep learning, convolutional neural network, musculoskeletal disorders.
