This paper presents a novel approach to photo adjustment, leveraging deep learning techniques to create a semantics-aware model. The objective is to develop an intelligent photo editing system that can automatically adjust images based on their semantic content, rather than relying solely on low-level visual features. Our approach utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, incorporating semantic segmentation and object detection to identify key elements within an image. The model is then fine-tuned to adjust parameters such as brightness, contrast, and color balance, tailored to the specific semantic context. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in image quality and aesthetic appeal, outperforming traditional adjustment methods. The proposed system has potential applications in various fields, including photography, graphic design, and social media. Key contributions include the integration of semantic understanding into photo adjustment and the development of a robust, data-driven framework for image enhancement. Relevant keywords: deep learning, photo adjustment, semantic segmentation, object detection, image enhancement, CNN.
