Accurate and fine-grained urban traffic flow inference is crucial for intelligent transportation systems. However, existing methods often struggle with data sparsity and the complex spatial dependencies inherent in urban traffic. This paper introduces a novel road network guided approach for fine-grained traffic flow inference. Our method leverages the underlying road network structure to regularize and enhance the inference process. We propose a graph-based deep learning model that explicitly incorporates road network connectivity and hierarchy as inductive biases. This model aggregates traffic information from neighboring road segments, effectively mitigating the impact of data sparsity. Furthermore, we design a specialized loss function that promotes consistency between inferred traffic flows and the road network topology. Experimental results on real-world traffic datasets demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms state-of-the-art approaches in terms of accuracy and robustness, particularly in scenarios with limited data availability. The proposed approach provides more accurate and reliable traffic flow estimates, enabling better traffic management and urban planning. Keywords: Traffic Flow Inference, Road Network, Graph Neural Networks, Deep Learning, Intelligent Transportation Systems.
