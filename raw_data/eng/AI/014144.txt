Sleep staging is a critical task in sleep analysis, enabling the diagnosis and treatment of sleep disorders. However, existing methods often lack interpretability, making it challenging for clinicians to trust and understand the decisions made by automated systems. This paper introduces **SLEEPER**, a novel framework for interpretable sleep staging that leverages expert rules to generate prototypes, ensuring both accuracy and transparency. SLEEPER combines rule-based reasoning with machine learning to create a hybrid model that aligns with clinical expertise while maintaining high performance. The proposed approach extracts key features from polysomnography (PSG) data and maps them to interpretable prototypes derived from established sleep staging guidelines. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that SLEEPER achieves competitive accuracy compared to state-of-the-art black-box models while providing clear, human-understandable explanations for its predictions. The framework’s interpretability is validated through case studies and expert evaluations, highlighting its potential for clinical adoption. SLEEPER represents a significant step toward bridging the gap between automated sleep staging and clinical practice, offering a trustworthy and explainable solution for sleep analysis.  

**Keywords**: sleep staging, interpretability, expert rules, prototypes, polysomnography, machine learning, clinical decision support.
