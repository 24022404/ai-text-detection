Vision Transformers (ViTs) have shown remarkable performance in image classification, but their robustness against adversarial examples remains a concern. This paper investigates the vulnerability of ViTs to various adversarial attacks, including gradient-based and optimization-based methods. We evaluate ViT models of varying sizes on benchmark datasets, analyzing their susceptibility to both targeted and untargeted attacks. Our study reveals that ViTs, while generally robust to common image corruptions, are surprisingly vulnerable to carefully crafted adversarial perturbations, even at low perturbation budgets. Furthermore, we examine the transferability of adversarial examples between different ViT architectures. The findings highlight potential weaknesses in the attention mechanisms and patch embedding layers of ViTs, suggesting avenues for developing more robust architectures. Key insights include the identification of specific layers most susceptible to attacks and the limited transferability of adversarial examples across significantly different ViT variants. This research underscores the need for specialized defense strategies tailored to the unique characteristics of ViT models. Keywords: Vision Transformer, Adversarial Examples, Robustness, Deep Learning, Image Classification, Adversarial Attack, Transferability.
