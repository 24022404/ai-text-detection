This paper addresses the challenge of learning robust and expressive latent representations in adversarial autoencoders. Traditional latent space approaches in this domain often neglect the geometric properties essential for effective data representation. We introduce adversarial autoencoders integrated with constant-curvature latent manifolds to enhance the quality and versatility of learned representations.

Methods/Approach: We extend the framework of adversarial autoencoders to incorporate a curvature-aware latent space, leveraging Riemannian geometry to manage constant curvature manifolds within the latent variable model. By utilizing a principled approach to enforce curvature constraints, the autoencoder better aligns with the natural data distribution. The manifold structure is dynamically optimized during the adversarial training to maintain a consistent curvature throughout the latent space, thus enabling more meaningful data embeddings.

Results/Findings: Experimental validations demonstrate that our model significantly outperforms standard adversarial autoencoders in capturing the intrinsic geometry of data distributions, resulting in improved generation quality and more accurate reconstructions. Our approach shows competitive performance benchmarks across various datasets, illustrating enhanced capacity for dimensionality reduction and representation learning.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of constant-curvature latent manifolds in adversarial autoencoders represents a novel contribution to the domain of representational learning, providing a more geometrically coherent framework. This innovation paves the way for broader applications in fields requiring intricate data representation and synthesis, such as image processing, natural language processing, and anomaly detection. Future work will explore extending this approach to accommodate varying curvature scenarios, further enhancing adaptability and performance.

Keywords: adversarial autoencoders, latent manifolds, constant curvature, Riemannian geometry, representation learning, data embeddings.
