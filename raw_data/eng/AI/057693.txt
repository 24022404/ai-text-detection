In reinforcement learning (RL), the robustness of policies to reward function perturbations is critical for ensuring reliable performance in real-world applications. This paper investigates the relationship between policy regularization and reward robustness, aiming to enhance the stability and generalization of learned policies under varying reward conditions. We propose a novel framework that integrates regularization techniques into policy optimization to mitigate the sensitivity of policies to reward function uncertainties. Our approach leverages entropy regularization and gradient-based constraints to encourage smoother policy updates, thereby improving robustness. Through extensive experiments on benchmark RL environments, we demonstrate that regularized policies exhibit significantly higher reward robustness compared to traditional methods, particularly in scenarios with noisy or misspecified reward functions. The results highlight the importance of regularization in achieving stable and reliable RL systems, offering practical insights for deploying RL in dynamic and uncertain environments. This work contributes to the broader understanding of policy robustness in RL and provides a foundation for developing more resilient learning algorithms.  

**Keywords**: reinforcement learning, policy regularization, reward robustness, entropy regularization, gradient constraints, stable policies.
