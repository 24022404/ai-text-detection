**Abstract:**  
Blockchain networks generate complex transaction graphs, where the understanding of structural patterns plays a pivotal role in tasks such as fraud detection, anomaly identification, and user behavior analysis. This paper introduces ChainNet, a novel graph neural network framework designed to learn from blockchain graphs by incorporating topological features unique to distributed ledger structures. ChainNet leverages both local and global graph properties—including node centrality, clustering coefficients, and transaction flow motifs—to enrich standard graph embeddings. The proposed method is evaluated on multiple real-world blockchain datasets, where it demonstrates significant improvement in classifying entities and detecting suspicious transaction patterns, outperforming existing baseline models in both accuracy and scalability. Experiments also highlight ChainNet’s efficiency in handling large-scale, dynamic transaction graphs, a common challenge in blockchain analytics. The findings indicate that integrating topological features with advanced graph learning not only enhances predictive performance but also provides deeper interpretability of blockchain activity. This research paves the way for more robust analytics on cryptocurrency networks, facilitating advancements in security, compliance, and forensic investigations. Keywords: Blockchain, graph neural networks, topological features, graph learning, fraud detection, cryptocurrency analytics.
