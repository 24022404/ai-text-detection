Vehicle re-identification (Re-ID) is a crucial task in intelligent transportation systems, enabling applications such as traffic monitoring and vehicle tracking. This paper explores novel methods for vehicle Re-ID, addressing challenges posed by viewpoint variations, illumination changes, and occlusions. Our approach integrates deep learning techniques with feature fusion strategies to extract robust vehicle representations. Specifically, we propose a multi-branch convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture that learns complementary features from different vehicle viewpoints. A novel attention mechanism is incorporated to focus on salient regions, mitigating the impact of occlusions and background clutter. Furthermore, a feature fusion module combines global and local features to enhance discriminative power. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art approaches in terms of identification accuracy and robustness. The proposed techniques significantly improve the performance of vehicle Re-ID systems, contributing to more effective and reliable intelligent transportation solutions.

Keywords: Vehicle Re-identification, Deep Learning, Feature Fusion, Attention Mechanism, Intelligent Transportation Systems.
