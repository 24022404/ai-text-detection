This study aims to develop a predictive model for county-level corn yields using deep Long Short Term Memory (LSTM) models, addressing the need for accurate yield forecasts in agricultural planning and decision-making. Our approach utilizes a combination of historical climate data, soil characteristics, and crop management practices as inputs to train the LSTM network. The results show that our model achieves high accuracy in predicting corn yields, outperforming traditional statistical methods and demonstrating the potential for LSTM models in agricultural yield prediction. Key findings include the identification of critical climate and soil factors influencing corn yields, as well as the model's ability to capture complex temporal relationships between these factors. The study contributes to the development of advanced predictive analytics in agriculture, with implications for improved crop management, resource allocation, and policy decision-making. By leveraging deep learning techniques, such as LSTM, this research provides a novel approach to predicting county-level corn yields, with potential applications in precision agriculture, sustainability, and food security. Keywords: deep learning, LSTM, corn yields, agricultural forecasting, precision agriculture.
