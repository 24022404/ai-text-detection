This paper introduces WarpedGANSpace, a novel method for traversing GAN latent spaces along non-linear paths to achieve richer and more controlled image manipulation. Current techniques often rely on linear interpolations, which limit the expressiveness of edits. We propose a method to discover non-linear paths modeled by Radial Basis Functions (RBFs) within the GAN latent space. Our approach optimizes the RBF control points to achieve desired semantic changes in the generated images, guided by a user-defined target image or attribute. We demonstrate that WarpedGANSpace enables more nuanced and complex image manipulations compared to linear interpolation methods, particularly for tasks such as facial expression editing and object shape transformation. Furthermore, we introduce a novel loss function that encourages smooth and semantically consistent transitions along the RBF path. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate the efficacy of WarpedGANSpace in generating high-quality and diverse image edits. Key words: GAN, latent space interpolation, image manipulation, Radial Basis Functions, non-linear paths.
