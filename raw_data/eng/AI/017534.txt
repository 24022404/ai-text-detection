Abstract

The increasing deployment of complex machine learning models in critical decision-making raises significant concerns about interpretability, particularly for "black-box" models whose internal decision processes are opaque. This paper introduces a novel counterfactual explanation framework designed to provide smooth, intuitive insights into model predictions. Our method generates minimally altered input instances—counterfactuals—that lead to different outcomes, thereby enabling users to understand the decision boundaries of black-box models without compromising model integrity or accuracy. Unlike prior approaches, our framework incorporates a smooth optimization objective that ensures counterfactual instances are realistic, continuous, and closely aligned with valid input distributions. We evaluate our approach using a range of benchmark datasets and black-box classifiers, including deep neural networks and ensemble methods. Results demonstrate that our method produces more coherent and actionable explanations compared to baseline counterfactual and perturbation-based techniques, while maintaining computational efficiency and scalability. The proposed framework supports transparency in automated systems, enhances user trust, and paves the way for regulatory compliance in sensitive applications such as finance and healthcare. Key contributions include a smooth counterfactual generation process, robust evaluation across diverse models, and actionable explanations that bridge the gap between model accuracy and interpretability. Keywords: explainable AI, black-box models, counterfactual explanations, model interpretability, machine learning transparency.
