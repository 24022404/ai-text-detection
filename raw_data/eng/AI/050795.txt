This paper addresses the challenge of expert selection in complex systems by leveraging graphical models to enhance decision-making processes. The objective is to develop a robust framework for selecting Gaussian experts, which are critical for tasks such as probabilistic modeling, prediction, and uncertainty quantification. We propose a novel approach that integrates graphical models with Gaussian processes to identify and rank experts based on their performance and reliability. The method employs a probabilistic graphical structure to model dependencies among experts, enabling efficient selection under varying conditions. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, showing significant improvements in accuracy and computational efficiency compared to traditional selection methods. The proposed framework not only outperforms existing techniques but also provides interpretable insights into expert contributions, making it suitable for applications in machine learning, decision support systems, and real-time analytics. This research contributes to the field by introducing a scalable and adaptable solution for expert selection, with potential applications in areas such as autonomous systems, healthcare, and financial modeling.  

**Keywords**: Gaussian processes, graphical models, expert selection, probabilistic modeling, decision support systems.
