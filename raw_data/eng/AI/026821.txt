After data collection and processing, the model undergoes training and is then evaluated using accuracy metrics, with a threshold of over 80% typically regarded as a strong result. If the accuracy falls short of expectations, the process includes retraining and re-evaluation, repeating these steps in a cycle until the desired level of performance is achieved. Notably, these final phases—training, evaluating, and potential retraining—account for around 30% of the overall execution time, highlighting the iterative and resource-intensive nature of optimizing model accuracy.
