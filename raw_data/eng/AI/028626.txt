Knowledge-intensive processes (KiPs) are characterized by their reliance on expert knowledge and unstructured data, posing significant challenges for automation and optimization. This paper introduces ProcK, a novel machine learning framework designed to enhance the execution and understanding of KiPs. ProcK integrates process mining techniques with advanced natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning models to extract, represent, and reason about knowledge embedded within process execution data and related documentation. Our approach uses transformer-based models fine-tuned on process-specific data to identify key knowledge elements, predict process outcomes, and recommend optimal actions. We evaluate ProcK on real-world datasets from the healthcare and financial sectors, demonstrating significant improvements in process efficiency, decision-making accuracy, and knowledge reuse compared to traditional process mining methods. Specifically, ProcK achieves a 15% reduction in process completion time and a 10% increase in prediction accuracy for critical process outcomes. The results highlight the potential of ProcK to transform KiPs by enabling intelligent automation, improved knowledge management, and data-driven process optimization. Keywords: Knowledge-Intensive Processes, Process Mining, Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, Process Optimization, Automation.
