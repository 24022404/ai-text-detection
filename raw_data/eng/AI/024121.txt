This paper explores the equivalence between Recurrent Neural Network (RNN) time series models and Variational Bayesian (VB) models, shedding light on the connections between these two seemingly disparate approaches. Our objective is to investigate the relationship between RNNs, commonly used for time series forecasting, and VB models, which provide a probabilistic framework for modeling complex data. We employ a combination of theoretical analysis and empirical experiments to demonstrate the equivalence between these two models. Our results show that RNN time series models can be reformulated as VB models, and vice versa, under certain conditions. This equivalence has significant implications for the development of more accurate and efficient time series forecasting models. Our findings contribute to a deeper understanding of the underlying mechanisms of RNNs and VB models, and highlight the potential for hybrid models that leverage the strengths of both approaches. Key contributions include the derivation of a theoretical framework for establishing the equivalence between RNNs and VB models, and the demonstration of improved forecasting performance using hybrid models. Relevant keywords: Recurrent Neural Networks, Variational Bayesian Models, Time Series Forecasting, Deep Learning, Probabilistic Modeling.
