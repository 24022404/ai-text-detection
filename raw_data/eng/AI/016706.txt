Neural style transfer aims to render a content image in the style of another image. However, existing style transfer methods often struggle with preserving fine details and handling complex textures, particularly when applied to scientific imagery. This paper introduces a novel approach leveraging Cycle-Consistent Generative Adversarial Networks (CycleGANs) for neural style transfer, specifically trained on data acquired from the Chang'E-4 lunar mission. Our method learns a mapping between lunar surface textures and artistic styles without requiring paired training data, thus overcoming the limitations of traditional supervised learning. The CycleGAN architecture ensures style consistency and content preservation through a cycle-consistency loss. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach by transferring styles from various artistic paintings to Chang'E-4 lunar images, producing visually compelling and scientifically plausible results. Quantitative evaluations using metrics such as style loss and content loss demonstrate superior performance compared to existing style transfer techniques. This research offers a new tool for visualizing and interpreting lunar data and opens avenues for style transfer in other scientific domains. Keywords: CycleGAN, Neural Style Transfer, Generative Adversarial Networks, Chang'E-4, Lunar Imagery, Image Processing.
