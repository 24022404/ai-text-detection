This paper introduces a novel approach for localizing hue modifications in digital images, a crucial task in image forensics and manipulation detection. Our method, Hue Modification Localization by Pair Matching (HML-PM), leverages the inherent relationships between neighboring pixels to identify inconsistencies indicative of hue tampering. Specifically, HML-PM establishes pixel pairs and analyzes the chromatic differences within these pairs, comparing them to the overall hue distribution of the image. We employ statistical analysis to identify regions where pair-wise hue variations deviate significantly from the expected distribution, thereby highlighting potential manipulation areas. Experimental results on a diverse dataset of tampered images demonstrate that HML-PM achieves superior localization accuracy compared to existing state-of-the-art techniques, particularly in scenarios involving subtle or localized hue alterations. The proposed method offers a robust and efficient solution for detecting hue-based image manipulations, contributing significantly to the field of digital image forensics. Keywords: Image Forensics, Hue Modification, Tampering Detection, Image Manipulation, Pair Matching, Localization.
