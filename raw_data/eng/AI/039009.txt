The configuration of neurons in each layer of a neural network can differ, but it is common practice for the hidden layers to maintain a consistent number of neurons across the network, with all layers typically being fully connected to promote efficient information flow. The overall size and complexity of an artificial neural network are determined by how many layers it contains and the number of neurons within each layer. These sophisticated computational systems have found widespread applications across diverse domains, including finance, trading, business analysis, weather forecasting, marketing research, risk assessment, and fraud detection, revolutionizing the way data is processed and insights are generated.
