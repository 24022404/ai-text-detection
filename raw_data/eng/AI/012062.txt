This paper introduces a novel approach to conversational fashion image retrieval, addressing the limitations of single-turn queries by incorporating multi-turn natural language feedback. Our objective is to improve retrieval accuracy and user satisfaction through iterative refinement of search results. We propose a framework that combines a pre-trained vision-language model with a reinforcement learning agent to navigate the conversational search space. The agent learns to select informative questions based on user feedback, guiding the retrieval process towards the desired fashion item. Experimental results on a benchmark fashion dataset demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing single-turn and limited multi-turn retrieval methods in terms of precision, recall, and user interaction efficiency. The proposed framework effectively leverages user feedback to resolve ambiguity and personalize search results, leading to a more intuitive and accurate fashion image retrieval experience. This work contributes a novel reinforcement learning-based conversational framework for fashion retrieval and provides a valuable benchmark for future research in this domain.

Keywords: Fashion Image Retrieval, Conversational AI, Reinforcement Learning, Vision-Language Models, Natural Language Processing.
