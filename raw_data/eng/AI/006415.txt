The paper aims to explore the advancements in deep learning technologies with a focus on enhancing automatic threat detection capabilities within X-ray security imaging systems. As global security demands intensify, the need for efficient, accurate, and scalable threat detection solutions becomes paramount.

Methods/Approach: We conducted a comprehensive survey of recent progress in the field of deep learning, specifically convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and related architectures, that are employed in the processing and analysis of X-ray imagery. Our focus was on assessing the effectiveness of these methods in identifying various threat objects and improving system performance.

Results/Findings: The study highlights significant improvements in detection accuracy and processing speed, attributed to the implementation of deep learning algorithms. Innovations such as transfer learning and ensemble methods have shown substantial potential in enhancing the robustness of threat detection systems. Comparative analysis with traditional methods indicates notable advancements in both sensitivity and specificity in identifying concealed threats.

Conclusion/Implications: Deep learning presents a transformative approach to automatic threat detection in X-ray security imaging, offering improved performance over conventional techniques. The insights gathered underscore the potential for deploying such algorithms in real-world security settings, with implications for increased safety and operational efficiency. Future research directions include further enhancement of algorithmic accuracy and the adaptation of these techniques to evolving threat profiles.

Keywords: Deep Learning, Threat Detection, X-ray Security Imaging, Convolutional Neural Networks, Automatic Detection, Transfer Learning.
