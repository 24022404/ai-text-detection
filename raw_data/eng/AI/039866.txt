Training large language models (LLMs) is computationally expensive, hindering rapid experimentation. This paper introduces Layered Gradient Accumulation (LGA) and Modular Pipeline Parallelism (MPP), two novel techniques designed to accelerate and enhance the efficiency of LLM training. LGA strategically accumulates gradients across layers, reducing memory footprint and communication overhead without compromising convergence. MPP decomposes the model into independent modules that are executed in parallel across multiple devices, maximizing hardware utilization and minimizing idle time. We demonstrate that LGA, when combined with MPP, significantly reduces the time-to-train for LLMs compared to traditional data parallelism and pipeline parallelism approaches. Experimental results on benchmark datasets show that our proposed method achieves up to a 2.5x speedup while maintaining comparable model accuracy. This work enables faster iteration cycles for LLM development and facilitates training of larger, more complex models. Keywords: large language models, distributed training, gradient accumulation, pipeline parallelism, deep learning, model training efficiency.
