This paper addresses the challenge of learning temporal relationships in knowledge graphs with limited training data, through the development of a one-shot learning approach. Our objective is to enable the efficient learning of temporal knowledge graph embeddings, which capture the dynamic nature of relationships between entities over time. We propose a novel method that leverages meta-learning and graph neural networks to learn effective representations from a single example, eliminating the need for extensive training datasets. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its ability to accurately predict temporal relationships and outperform existing methods. The key findings of this research highlight the potential of one-shot learning for temporal knowledge graphs, enabling applications such as event forecasting, temporal question answering, and dynamic recommendation systems. By advancing the field of temporal knowledge graph learning, this work contributes to the development of more intelligent and adaptive systems, with significant implications for areas like artificial intelligence, natural language processing, and data science, and is particularly relevant to keywords such as one-shot learning, temporal knowledge graphs, graph neural networks, and meta-learning.
