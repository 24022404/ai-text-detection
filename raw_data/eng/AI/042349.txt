This paper addresses the challenge of efficient decision-making in complex environments by proposing a novel Bayesian Reinforcement Learning (BRL) framework that integrates deep learning with sparse sampling techniques. The objective is to enhance the scalability and computational efficiency of BRL in high-dimensional state-action spaces, where traditional methods often struggle due to computational intractability. Our approach leverages deep neural networks to approximate posterior distributions over value functions and policies, while sparse sampling reduces the computational burden by selectively exploring the most promising trajectories. We evaluate the proposed method on benchmark environments, including continuous control tasks and large-scale grid-world problems. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in sample efficiency and policy performance compared to state-of-the-art BRL algorithms. The framework also exhibits robustness to uncertainty and adaptability to dynamic environments. This work contributes to the advancement of BRL by providing a scalable and computationally efficient solution, with potential applications in robotics, autonomous systems, and real-time decision-making under uncertainty. Key innovations include the integration of deep learning for probabilistic inference and the use of sparse sampling to optimize exploration. Keywords: Bayesian Reinforcement Learning, Deep Learning, Sparse Sampling, Decision-Making, Uncertainty, Scalability.
