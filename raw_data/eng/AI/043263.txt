Graph coarsening, an essential technique in the realm of scientific computing, plays a pivotal role in efficiently reducing the complexity of large-scale graph-based data. This paper explores the multifaceted applications and advancements in graph coarsening methods, extending its utility from scientific computing to cutting-edge machine learning. The objective of our research is to establish an innovative framework that enhances graph coarsening by leveraging contemporary machine learning algorithms. Our approach involves the integration of recursive algorithms with data-driven models to achieve significant reductions in graph size while preserving crucial structural properties. The findings reveal that our proposed method not only outperforms traditional coarsening techniques in terms of computational efficiency but also demonstrates superior accuracy in representing original graph structures. This translates into improved performance of machine learning tasks, such as clustering and classification, when applied to coarsened graphs. In conclusion, this research contributes a novel perspective by aligning graph coarsening with machine learning methodologies, paving the way for its application in domains that rely heavily on large-scale graph processing. Key implications include the potential for enhanced graph neural networks and more scalable solutions in various data-intensive industries. Keywords: graph coarsening, scientific computing, machine learning, graph processing, data-driven models.
