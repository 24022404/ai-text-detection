This paper presents a novel approach to pedestrian trajectory prediction, leveraging context-augmented transformer networks to improve forecasting accuracy. The objective is to develop a robust model that can effectively capture complex interactions between pedestrians and their environment. Our method utilizes a transformer-based architecture, incorporating contextual information such as scene layout, pedestrian intentions, and social norms to enhance prediction performance. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art models, achieving significant improvements in prediction accuracy and robustness. The key findings highlight the importance of contextual information in pedestrian trajectory prediction, enabling more accurate and informed decision-making in applications such as autonomous vehicles and smart cities. This research contributes to the development of more sophisticated and human-centered AI systems, with potential applications in fields like computer vision, robotics, and urban planning. Key keywords: pedestrian trajectory prediction, transformer networks, context-aware modeling, autonomous systems, computer vision.
