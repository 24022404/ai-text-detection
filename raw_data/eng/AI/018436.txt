This paper introduces SOM-VAE, a novel approach for learning interpretable discrete representations from time series data. Our method integrates the Self-Organizing Map (SOM) with the Variational Autoencoder (VAE) framework. The SOM component enables the formation of a structured, discretized latent space, while the VAE facilitates efficient encoding and decoding of time series. Specifically, the SOM imposes topological constraints on the latent space of the VAE, encouraging the formation of clusters that correspond to distinct temporal patterns. We evaluate SOM-VAE on several time series datasets, demonstrating its ability to learn meaningful and interpretable discrete representations. Experimental results show that SOM-VAE achieves competitive performance in time series reconstruction and classification tasks compared to state-of-the-art methods. Furthermore, the discrete latent space enables intuitive visualization and analysis of the underlying temporal dynamics. SOM-VAE provides a powerful tool for extracting actionable insights from complex time series data, opening new avenues for time series analysis and forecasting.
Keywords: Time Series, Representation Learning, Variational Autoencoder, Self-Organizing Map, Discrete Latent Space, Interpretability.
