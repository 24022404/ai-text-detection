**Abstract:**

Zero-shot learning (ZSL) has emerged as a promising paradigm for recognizing unseen categories by leveraging semantic knowledge, yet its application to 3D point cloud data remains an open challenge due to the high-dimensional, unordered, and sparse nature of such data. This paper proposes a novel framework for zero-shot learning on 3D point cloud objects, aiming to bridge the semantic gap between geometric representations and class attributes. Our approach integrates a robust point cloud feature extractor with a semantic embedding module, enabling the model to align geometric point features with semantic vectors representing both seen and unseen classes. We introduce a novel loss function to enhance the discriminative power of latent features and facilitate more effective transfer to unseen categories. Comprehensive experiments on widely used 3D object datasets demonstrate our method’s superior performance over existing ZSL and baseline approaches in accuracy and generalization to novel classes. Furthermore, we extend our framework to related modalities and scenarios, highlighting its adaptability and effectiveness beyond conventional 3D object classification. The results validate the potential of zero-shot learning for advancing 3D shape understanding and open new directions for applications in robotics, autonomous systems, and virtual reality. Keywords: zero-shot learning, 3D point cloud, semantic embedding, feature extraction, object recognition, transfer learning.
