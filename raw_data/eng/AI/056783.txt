Explainable image recognition is crucial for building trust and understanding in AI systems. This paper introduces SCOUTER, a novel image classification framework leveraging Slot Attention to provide interpretable and accurate predictions. SCOUTER learns to decompose an image into a set of salient object slots, each represented by a feature vector and an attention mask, enabling the classifier to focus on relevant image regions. By attending to these discovered slots, SCOUTER achieves state-of-the-art classification performance while simultaneously offering visual explanations for its decisions. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that SCOUTER outperforms existing attention-based models in both classification accuracy and explanation quality. Furthermore, we show that the learned slots correspond to meaningful object parts, enhancing the interpretability of the model. SCOUTER offers a promising approach for explainable AI, with potential applications in diverse fields such as medical image analysis and autonomous driving. Keywords: Explainable AI, Image Recognition, Slot Attention, Attention Mechanisms, Interpretability, Computer Vision.
