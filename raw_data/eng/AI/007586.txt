This paper addresses the challenge of real-world degradation encountered in smartphone photo scanning, where scanned documents often suffer from distortions, shadows, and varying lighting conditions. We propose Deep Photo Scan, a semi-supervised learning framework leveraging both labeled and unlabeled scanned document images to improve the robustness and accuracy of document image enhancement. Our approach combines a convolutional neural network (CNN) for degradation estimation with a generative adversarial network (GAN) for image restoration. The CNN learns to predict degradation parameters from labeled data, while the GAN utilizes unlabeled data to refine the restoration process, enabling the model to generalize better to unseen real-world degradations. Experimental results on a newly collected dataset of real-world scanned documents demonstrate that Deep Photo Scan significantly outperforms existing methods in terms of image quality metrics and visual appearance. The proposed framework offers a practical solution for enhancing the quality of scanned documents captured by smartphones, reducing the need for manual correction and improving the accuracy of downstream document processing tasks. Keywords: Photo Scan, Document Enhancement, Semi-Supervised Learning, GAN, CNN, Image Restoration, Degradation Estimation.
