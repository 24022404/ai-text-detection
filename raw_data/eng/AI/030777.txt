This paper addresses the challenge of forecasting hands and objects in future frames, a critical task for applications in human-computer interaction, robotics, and augmented reality. The objective is to predict the spatial and temporal dynamics of hands and interacting objects in video sequences, enabling accurate anticipation of future interactions. To achieve this, we propose a novel deep learning framework that integrates temporal modeling with spatial attention mechanisms. Our approach leverages a combination of recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and transformer-based architectures to capture long-term dependencies and fine-grained spatial relationships. The model is trained on a large-scale dataset of annotated hand-object interaction videos, ensuring robust generalization across diverse scenarios. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art techniques in terms of prediction accuracy and temporal consistency. Key findings include improved handling of occlusions and complex motion patterns, as well as superior performance in multi-object scenarios. The implications of this research extend to real-time applications such as gesture recognition, autonomous systems, and immersive virtual environments. This work contributes a scalable and efficient solution for future frame prediction, setting a new benchmark for hand-object interaction forecasting.  

**Keywords**: future frame prediction, hand-object interaction, deep learning, temporal modeling, spatial attention, human-computer interaction.
