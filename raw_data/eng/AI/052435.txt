This paper proposes a novel seismic inverse modeling method leveraging Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to accurately estimate subsurface properties from seismic data. The objective is to address the long-standing challenge of non-uniqueness in seismic inversion by utilizing the generative capabilities of GANs. Our approach employs a deep learning framework, where a generator network produces synthetic seismic data, and a discriminator network distinguishes between real and synthetic data. The method is tested on synthetic and real seismic datasets, demonstrating improved accuracy and efficiency compared to traditional inversion methods. Key findings include the ability to recover high-resolution subsurface models and reduce uncertainty in seismic interpretation. The proposed method has significant implications for the oil and gas industry, as well as other fields relying on seismic exploration. By introducing GANs to seismic inverse modeling, this research contributes to the development of more accurate and efficient seismic imaging techniques, with potential applications in subsurface characterization, reservoir modeling, and seismic hazard assessment. Keywords: Seismic Inverse Modeling, Generative Adversarial Networks, Deep Learning, Subsurface Characterization, Reservoir Modeling.
