This paper explores the effectiveness of bonus-based exploration methods in the Arcade Learning Environment (ALE), a popular platform for evaluating reinforcement learning algorithms. The objective is to investigate how bonus-based incentives can improve exploration efficiency in complex, high-dimensional state and action spaces. We propose a novel approach that combines intrinsic motivation with extrinsic rewards, using a bonus function to encourage agents to visit novel states and actions. Our method is evaluated on a range of ALE games, demonstrating significant improvements in exploration efficiency and overall performance compared to traditional epsilon-greedy and entropy-based exploration methods. The results show that our bonus-based approach can lead to faster discovery of optimal policies and improved robustness to hyperparameter tuning. This research contributes to the development of more efficient and effective exploration strategies for reinforcement learning, with potential applications in areas such as game playing, robotics, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, exploration methods, Arcade Learning Environment, intrinsic motivation, bonus-based incentives.
