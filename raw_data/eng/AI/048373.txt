Objective/Purpose:  
Managing diabetes relies heavily on accurate blood glucose prediction to prevent critical events such as hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia. While deep learning models have demonstrated high predictive accuracy in this domain, their lack of interpretability poses challenges for clinical adoption. This paper aims to enhance transparency by applying an interpretable deep learning architecture, RETAIN (Reverse Time Attention Model), to predict glucose levels in diabetic individuals and elucidate influential factors driving model decisions.

Methods/Approach:  
We developed a predictive framework leveraging RETAIN, an attention-based recurrent neural network specifically designed for interpretable sequence modeling. The model was trained and evaluated on longitudinal continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) datasets, incorporating input features such as insulin dosing, carbohydrate intake, and physical activity. The attention mechanism within RETAIN was utilized to highlight and analyze the temporal significance of input variables contributing to blood glucose forecasts.

Results/Findings:  
Our experimental results demonstrate that the RETAIN-based model achieves competitive prediction accuracy comparable to state-of-the-art deep learning methods. Crucially, the interpretability afforded by the attention weights allows for intuitive visualization and understanding of which historical inputs most influence individual predictions. This enables healthcare professionals and patients to gain actionable insights and supports the validation of model reasoning against clinical knowledge.

Conclusion/Implications:  
By combining high predictive performance with clear interpretability, the proposed RETAIN framework addresses key barriers to deploying AI-driven glucose monitoring in real-world healthcare settings. The study underscores the importance of explainable machine learning in clinical applications and provides a practical tool for fostering trust and informed decision-making among diabetic patients and care providers.

Keywords: glucose prediction, diabetes, interpretability, deep learning, RETAIN, attention mechanism, explainable AI, continuous glucose monitoring, healthcare AI.
