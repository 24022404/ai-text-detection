This paper addresses the challenge of designing robust control strategies for dynamic systems under uncertainty, where exact model knowledge is usually unattainable and traditional approaches may struggle with robustness and computational efficiency. We propose a novel framework that integrates alternating optimisation techniques with advanced quadrature methods to improve control performance in the presence of model and environmental uncertainties. Our method alternately refines control parameters and evaluates the expected control cost using efficient quadrature schemes, enabling accurate estimation of performance metrics while maintaining computational tractability. Through a series of benchmark experiments on nonlinear control systems, we demonstrate that our approach achieves superior robustness compared to conventional optimization and sampling-based methods. The results show enhanced system stability and reduced sensitivity to model perturbations. This research highlights the potential of combining alternating optimisation and quadrature for practical robust control design, offering a scalable and adaptable solution for complex real-world applications in robotics, automation, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: robust control, alternating optimisation, numerical quadrature, uncertainty, dynamic systems.
