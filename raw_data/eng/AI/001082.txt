This paper proposes Embedded-State Latent Conditional Random Fields (ES-LCRFs), a novel approach for sequence labeling tasks. The objective is to improve the accuracy of sequence labeling by incorporating latent variables into the model, allowing for more nuanced representations of complex sequences. Our method employs a conditional random field framework, combining it with embedded state representations to capture long-range dependencies and latent structures in the data. The ES-LCRF model is trained using a discriminative algorithm, enabling efficient inference and learning of model parameters. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of ES-LCRFs in achieving state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark sequence labeling datasets, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and F1-score. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of embedded state representations into LCRFs, enabling the model to capture complex sequence patterns, and the development of an efficient training algorithm. This work has significant implications for natural language processing, information extraction, and other sequence labeling applications, highlighting the potential of ES-LCRFs for improving the accuracy and robustness of sequence labeling models. Key keywords: sequence labeling, conditional random fields, latent variables, embedded state representations, natural language processing.
