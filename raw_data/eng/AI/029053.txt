The increasing use of facial recognition technologies raises significant privacy concerns, necessitating robust methods for face anonymization. This paper introduces *DeepPrivacy*, a novel Generative Adversarial Network (GAN)-based framework designed to anonymize facial images while preserving natural appearance and usability. The proposed approach leverages a conditional GAN architecture to generate realistic, privacy-preserving face replacements that maintain contextual integrity, such as pose, lighting, and background. Unlike traditional methods that rely on blurring or pixelation, *DeepPrivacy* ensures high-quality anonymization without compromising visual coherence. The framework is trained on a diverse dataset of facial images, enabling it to generalize across various demographics and scenarios. Experimental results demonstrate that *DeepPrivacy* outperforms existing anonymization techniques in terms of visual quality and privacy protection, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets. Furthermore, the system is computationally efficient, making it suitable for real-time applications. This research contributes to the growing field of privacy-preserving technologies by offering a scalable and effective solution for face anonymization, with potential applications in surveillance, social media, and data sharing.  

**Keywords**: Face anonymization, Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), privacy preservation, deep learning, image synthesis.
