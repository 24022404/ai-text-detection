This paper introduces a novel real-time policy distillation framework for deep reinforcement learning (DRL) that addresses the challenge of deploying complex, computationally intensive policies in resource-constrained environments. Our approach, Real-time Distillation Network (RTDN), enables the transfer of knowledge from a sophisticated teacher policy to a compact student policy within a continuous learning loop. RTDN employs an asynchronous training scheme, allowing the student policy to learn online from the teacher's actions and experiences in real-time without interrupting the environment interaction. We evaluate RTDN on a set of challenging continuous control tasks, demonstrating significant improvements in student policy performance compared to offline distillation methods and traditional DRL algorithms. Specifically, RTDN achieves comparable performance to the teacher policy while reducing computational overhead by an average of 60%. The findings suggest that RTDN offers a promising solution for deploying high-performing DRL agents in real-world applications with limited computational resources. Keywords: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Policy Distillation, Real-time Learning, Knowledge Transfer, Continuous Control.
