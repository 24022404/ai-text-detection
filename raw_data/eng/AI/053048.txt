Cardiac arrhythmias are critical indicators of heart health, and their accurate classification is essential for timely diagnosis and treatment. This paper presents a novel approach for ECG arrhythmia classification using transfer learning from 2-dimensional deep convolutional neural network (CNN) features. The proposed method leverages pre-trained CNN models to extract discriminative features from 2D representations of ECG signals, which are then fine-tuned for arrhythmia classification. The study utilizes publicly available ECG datasets, transforming 1D signals into 2D spectrograms to enhance feature extraction. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves superior classification accuracy compared to traditional machine learning and deep learning approaches. The model achieves an accuracy of 98.2% on the MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database, outperforming state-of-the-art methods. Key contributions include the innovative use of 2D CNN features for ECG signal analysis and the effective application of transfer learning to improve classification performance. This approach has significant potential for real-time arrhythmia detection in clinical settings, offering a robust and scalable solution for automated ECG analysis.  

**Keywords**: ECG arrhythmia classification, transfer learning, 2D CNN, deep learning, spectrogram, MIT-BIH database.
