Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) have emerged as powerful platforms for real-time object detection in diverse applications such as surveillance, environmental monitoring, and disaster response. However, aerial imagery is often challenged by varying altitudes, dynamic backgrounds, scale changes, and environmental nuisances that negatively impact detection performance. This paper introduces a novel deep nuisance disentanglement approach to enhance robust object detection from UAV-acquired images. Our method integrates a dual-branch deep neural network designed to separately encode critical object semantics and disentangle complex nuisance factors such as illumination variations, background motion, and atmospheric distortions. To train and validate the proposed architecture, we compile a comprehensive UAV dataset that captures a wide range of nuisance scenarios. Comparative experiments demonstrate significant improvements in detection accuracy and robustness over state-of-the-art models, especially under challenging conditions. Our results highlight reduced false positives, better localization, and improved adaptation across diverse flight environments. The proposed nuisance disentanglement framework sets a new benchmark for UAV object detection, offering valuable insights for deploying reliable and versatile vision systems in real-world aerial applications. Key keywords: UAV, object detection, deep learning, nuisance disentanglement, aerial imagery, robust vision systems.
