This paper presents a novel approach to modelling Bahdanau attention mechanisms using election methods aided by Q-Learning. The objective is to improve the efficiency and accuracy of attention-based neural networks by incorporating a reinforcement learning framework. Our method utilizes Q-Learning to optimize the election process, allowing the model to selectively focus on the most relevant input elements. The proposed approach is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in performance compared to traditional attention mechanisms. Key findings include enhanced accuracy and reduced computational complexity. The conclusions highlight the potential of this innovative approach to advance the state-of-the-art in natural language processing and related applications, with implications for improved language understanding and generation capabilities. The use of Q-Learning and election methods in attention modelling introduces a new paradigm, offering a unique combination of reinforcement learning and deep learning techniques. Relevant keywords: Bahdanau attention, Q-Learning, election methods, reinforcement learning, deep learning, natural language processing.
