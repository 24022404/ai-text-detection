Anomaly detection in high-dimensional data remains a challenging problem, particularly when dealing with subtle anomalies and limited labeled data. This paper introduces a novel two-stage anomaly detection framework guided by attention maps and hard augmentation techniques to address these challenges. The first stage employs an attention mechanism to identify salient features relevant to anomaly discrimination, generating attention maps that highlight anomalous regions. These attention maps then guide a hard augmentation strategy, creating challenging synthetic anomalies to enhance the robustness of the second-stage anomaly detector. Specifically, we introduce a perturbation method guided by the attention map, creating more realistic anomalous samples to enhance model training. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves superior performance compared to state-of-the-art methods, particularly in scenarios with limited anomaly samples and complex data distributions. The proposed framework offers a promising solution for improving anomaly detection accuracy and robustness in various real-world applications.

**Keywords:** Anomaly Detection, Attention Mechanism, Hard Augmentation, Two-Stage Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision.
