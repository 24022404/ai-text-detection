**Abstract:**  
The increasing complexity and diversity of modern machine learning (ML) workflows present significant challenges in terms of portability, automation, and reproducibility across different environments. This paper introduces CodeReef, an open-source MLOps platform designed to streamline the development, deployment, and benchmarking of ML solutions. CodeReef provides a unified interface for managing reusable automation actions, such as model training, data preprocessing, and system profiling, through portable workflows that can easily adapt to various hardware, software stacks, and cloud infrastructures. The platform supports containerization and declarative configuration, enabling seamless integration with popular ML frameworks and continuous integration (CI) pipelines. Extensive experiments demonstrate that CodeReef substantially reduces time-to-deployment and mitigates environment-related inconsistencies in performance benchmarking. Key evaluation scenarios highlight CodeReef’s ability to enable reproducible and transparent benchmarking, as well as collaborative sharing of automation workflows in both academic and industrial settings. By facilitating modular and portable MLOps practices, CodeReef empowers researchers and practitioners to accelerate innovation, ensure reproducibility, and foster open collaboration in the rapidly evolving AI ecosystem.

**Keywords:** MLOps, automation, reproducibility, benchmarking, open platform, machine learning workflows, containerization, portability.
