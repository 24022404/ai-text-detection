The executor serves as an integral backend component within a blockchain-based decentralized application (DApp), bridging the gap between the user-facing frontend and the blockchain itself. Its core responsibilities include submitting user-initiated transactions to the blockchain network, orchestrating seamless interactions with deployed smart contracts, and efficiently managing the underlying transaction workflow. A pivotal aspect of the executor's function involves the secure handling of users' private keys or mnemonic phrases, which are fundamental to authenticating transactions and verifying user identities. By safeguarding these sensitive credentials, the executor upholds both the security and the integrity of blockchain operations, ensuring that users maintain control and privacy over their digital assets and activities within the DApp ecosystem.
