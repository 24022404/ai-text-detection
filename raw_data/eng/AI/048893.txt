**Abstract:**  
Video summarization aims to generate concise and informative synopses of lengthy video content, enabling efficient browsing and retrieval. Existing methods often overlook user-specific needs, resulting in generic summaries that lack relevance to user queries. In this paper, we propose a novel Query-Conditioned Three-Player Adversarial Network (Q3AN) for personalized video summarization. The core innovation of our approach lies in its adversarial network architecture, comprising three players: a video summary generator conditioned on user queries, a real summary discriminator, and a random summary discriminator. The generator leverages both visual and textual features to produce query-relevant summaries, while the two discriminators collaboratively encourage the generator to create outputs that are not only realistic but also specifically tailored to the given queries. We evaluate Q3AN on publicly available benchmark datasets using standard performance metrics, demonstrating significant improvements in summary relevance and diversity compared to state-of-the-art methods. Our findings underscore the effectiveness of adversarial learning in capturing user intent and enhancing summary quality for personalized video navigation. The proposed framework offers new opportunities for query-aware video analysis and intelligent content delivery in video retrieval systems.

**Keywords:** video summarization, query-conditioned, adversarial network, personalized summarization, deep learning, user intent, three-player GAN
