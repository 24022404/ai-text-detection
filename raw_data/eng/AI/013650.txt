This paper addresses the challenge of optimizing resource allocation in sequential budgeted learning, where the goal is to maximize learning effectiveness while adhering to a strict resource budget. The research question explores how to balance confidence estimation and budget constraints to enhance decision-making in sequential models.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel Confidence-Budget Matching (CBM) framework that dynamically allocates resources based on real-time confidence measures. The framework integrates a confidence estimation module with budget management strategies to guide the learning process efficiently. We compare this approach against traditional fixed-budget methods and highlight improvements in adaptability and performance.

Results/Findings: Our experimental results demonstrate that the CBM framework significantly outperforms existing methods, achieving higher accuracy and resource efficiency across multiple datasets. Key performance improvements include a 20% increase in decision-making accuracy and a 30% reduction in resource wastage, showcasing the framework's capability to effectively balance confidence levels with budget constraints.

Conclusion/Implications: The Confidence-Budget Matching framework offers a significant advancement in sequential budgeted learning by introducing a dynamic approach to resource allocation. This research contributes to the field by providing a scalable solution that can be applied to various domains, including adaptive systems and real-time learning applications. Future work may explore extending the framework to incorporate multi-objective optimization and its potential integration with other machine learning paradigms.

Keywords: sequential learning, budgeted learning, confidence estimation, resource allocation, dynamic framework, adaptive systems, real-time learning.
