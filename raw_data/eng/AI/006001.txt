Blur detection is a critical task in image processing, affecting downstream applications like image restoration and enhancement. Existing methods often struggle with accurately identifying blur across varying scales and complex image structures. This paper introduces MSDU-net, a novel Multi-Scale Dilated U-net architecture designed for robust blur detection. MSDU-net leverages dilated convolutions at multiple scales within a U-net structure to effectively capture contextual information and detect subtle blur patterns, even in challenging scenarios. The model incorporates a multi-scale feature extraction module to aggregate information from different receptive fields. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that MSDU-net achieves state-of-the-art performance in blur detection, surpassing existing methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The proposed architecture significantly improves blur detection accuracy, providing a valuable tool for various image processing applications. Keywords: Blur detection, U-net, dilated convolution, multi-scale feature extraction, image processing.
