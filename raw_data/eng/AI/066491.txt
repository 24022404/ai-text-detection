This study aims to investigate the performance of retinal image quality assessment networks in various color-spaces, addressing the need for accurate image quality evaluation in medical imaging applications. We propose a comprehensive approach, utilizing convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to assess image quality in different color-spaces, including RGB, YCbCr, and HSV. Our methodology involves training and testing the networks on a large dataset of retinal images, evaluating their performance using metrics such as peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM). The results show that the networks achieve high accuracy in assessing image quality, with the YCbCr color-space yielding the best performance. Our findings suggest that the choice of color-space significantly impacts the performance of retinal image quality assessment networks, highlighting the importance of color-space selection in medical image analysis. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and reliable image quality assessment methods, with potential applications in retinal disease diagnosis and treatment. Key keywords: retinal image quality assessment, color-spaces, convolutional neural networks, medical imaging, image analysis.
