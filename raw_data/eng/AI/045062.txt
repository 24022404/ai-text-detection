**Abstract**

Spatio-temporal forecasting, critical for applications such as traffic prediction, climate modeling, and demand forecasting, poses significant challenges due to complex spatial dependencies and temporal dynamics. This paper introduces HyperST-Net, a novel framework leveraging hypernetworks for enhanced spatio-temporal forecasting. The proposed approach employs a hypernetwork architecture to dynamically generate weights for a primary forecasting network, allowing adaptive modeling of heterogeneous spatial regions and evolving temporal patterns. HyperST-Net integrates spatial graphs and temporal sequences to jointly capture multi-dimensional dependencies, addressing limitations in static model architectures. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that HyperST-Net consistently outperforms state-of-the-art methods in both accuracy and robustness, particularly in scenarios with high spatial variability and non-stationary temporal trends. Sensitivity analyses further illustrate the model’s adaptability across diverse spatio-temporal contexts. Our results suggest that hypernetwork-based spatio-temporal modeling provides a promising direction for scalable, generalizable forecasting solutions. The study’s insights contribute to advancing automated, context-aware forecasting models and have broad implications for intelligent transportation systems, urban planning, and environmental monitoring.  
**Keywords:** spatio-temporal forecasting, hypernetworks, dynamic modeling, neural networks, adaptive learning, graph learning, time series analysis
