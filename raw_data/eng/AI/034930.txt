This paper proposes a novel deep learning framework, Encoded Prior Sliced Wasserstein AutoEncoder (EPSWAE), designed to learn latent manifold representations. The objective is to develop an efficient and robust method for dimensionality reduction and generative modeling. Our approach combines the strengths of sliced Wasserstein distance and autoencoders, with an encoded prior to regularize the latent space. The EPSWAE model utilizes a unique blend of adversarial training and reconstruction loss to learn a compact and informative latent representation. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of EPSWAE in learning latent manifolds, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of reconstruction quality and diversity. The proposed framework has significant implications for applications such as image and data generation, anomaly detection, and representation learning. Key contributions include the introduction of an encoded prior to improve latent space regularization and the integration of sliced Wasserstein distance to enhance mode coverage. Relevant keywords: autoencoders, sliced Wasserstein distance, latent manifold representations, generative models, deep learning.
