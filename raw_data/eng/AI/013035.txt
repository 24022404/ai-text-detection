The selection of relevant bands from hyperspectral images is a crucial step in enhancing the efficiency and accuracy of further image analysis. However, the high dimensionality and correlation among bands pose significant challenges. This study presents a novel approach utilizing Attention-based Convolutional Neural Networks (ACNNs) to effectively select informative bands from hyperspectral images.

Methods/Approach: We propose an attention mechanism integrated within a convolutional neural network framework designed specifically for high-dimensional data. The model incorporates attention modules that prioritize crucial spectral bands, thereby reducing redundancy and preserving essential information for classification tasks. The ACNN model is trained and evaluated on benchmark hyperspectral datasets, comparing its performance against traditional band selection methods.

Results/Findings: The experimental results demonstrate that our ACNN model not only improves the accuracy of hyperspectral image classification but also significantly reduces computational complexity by selecting fewer bands. The attention mechanism effectively identifies the most informative bands, leading to higher model performance metrics compared to existing techniques. The proposed method outperforms state-of-the-art methods, providing a more efficient solution for hyperspectral image analysis.

Conclusion/Implications: This research highlights the potential of attention-based convolutional neural networks in optimizing the band selection process for hyperspectral images, offering substantial improvements in both performance and computational efficiency. The approach can be applied to various hyperspectral imaging applications such as environmental monitoring, agriculture, and medical imaging, where efficient data processing is essential. Our findings suggest that integrating attention mechanisms into CNNs is a promising direction for future studies aimed at tackling high-dimensional data challenges.

Keywords: Hyperspectral images, band selection, attention mechanism, convolutional neural networks, high-dimensional data, image classification, ACNN.
