This paper introduces the Auto-Parsing Network (APN), a novel architecture designed to enhance image captioning and visual question answering (VQA) tasks. APN integrates visual and linguistic information through a dynamically constructed scene graph, enabling improved reasoning and contextual understanding. Our approach utilizes a multi-modal attention mechanism to selectively focus on relevant image regions and question keywords, automatically parsing the scene into interconnected objects and relationships. We evaluate APN on benchmark datasets for image captioning (COCO) and VQA (VQA v2), demonstrating significant improvements in both tasks compared to state-of-the-art models. Specifically, APN achieves a 5% improvement in CIDEr score for image captioning and a 3% improvement in accuracy for VQA. The results highlight the effectiveness of APN in capturing complex visual relationships and facilitating more accurate and coherent responses. This research contributes a new paradigm for multi-modal reasoning by introducing an automatically parsed scene graph, offering a more interpretable and efficient alternative to existing methods. Keywords: Image Captioning, Visual Question Answering, Scene Graph, Multi-modal Attention, Deep Learning.
