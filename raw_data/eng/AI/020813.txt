This paper introduces a novel reinforcement learning approach for autonomous driving on highways, focusing on improving the efficiency and safety of decision-making. We address the challenges of high-dimensional state spaces and complex traffic dynamics by proposing an Amortized Q-learning framework integrated with model-based action proposals. Our method utilizes a learned dynamics model to generate promising action candidates, which are then evaluated using a Q-network trained with an amortized scheme to accelerate learning and improve generalization. The proposed approach is evaluated in simulated highway environments, demonstrating superior performance compared to traditional Q-learning and imitation learning methods in terms of driving safety, efficiency, and adherence to traffic rules. The results highlight the potential of combining model-based planning with amortized reinforcement learning for safe and efficient autonomous driving. Keywords: Autonomous Driving, Reinforcement Learning, Q-learning, Model-Based Planning, Amortized Learning, Highway Driving.
