This paper introduces SecDD, an innovative framework designed to address the challenges of securely and efficiently training neural networks in distributed environments. With the increasing demand for collaborative training of AI models across remote computational resources, ensuring data privacy and computational efficiency has become a critical concern.

Methods/Approach: SecDD utilizes a novel combination of secure multi-party computation (SMPC) and differential privacy techniques to safeguard sensitive data during the training process. The method is structured to allow neural networks to be trained across multiple devices or locations without exposing raw data, thus maintaining confidentiality. Additionally, SecDD employs a decentralized distribution strategy to enhance computational speed and reduce communication overhead, leveraging a blockchain-based ledger to ensure data integrity and consistent model updates.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that SecDD achieves state-of-the-art performance regarding both security and efficiency metrics. The proposed framework effectively reduces latency and computational burdens compared to traditional centralized training methods while maintaining a high level of model accuracy. Furthermore, comparative analysis with existing distributed training approaches reveals significant improvements in scalability and robustness against data breaches.

Conclusion/Implications: The integration of secure computation and privacy-preserving techniques in SecDD offers a viable solution for secure remote neural network training, paving the way for broader adoption of collaborative AI development. The contributions of this research lay the groundwork for future innovations in secure AI methodologies, with potential applications across various sectors including healthcare, finance, and large-scale scientific research. Key keywords: Secure Multi-Party Computation, Differential Privacy, Distributed Training, Neural Networks, Blockchain Integration, Data Privacy, Decentralized AI.
