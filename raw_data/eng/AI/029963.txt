This paper addresses the challenge of knowledge distillation and transfer in deep learning models for image classification and regression tasks. Our objective is to develop an efficient method for transferring knowledge from a complex model to a simpler one, leveraging the capabilities of conditional Generative Adversarial Networks (cGANs). We propose a novel approach that utilizes cGAN-generated samples to facilitate knowledge transfer, enabling the simpler model to learn from the complex model's representations. Our method involves training a cGAN to generate synthetic samples that capture the essential features of the original data, which are then used to fine-tune the simpler model. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, showing significant improvements in performance on various image classification and regression benchmarks. The key findings of our study highlight the potential of cGAN-generated samples for knowledge distillation and transfer, particularly in scenarios where data is limited or expensive to obtain. Our research contributes to the development of more efficient and adaptable deep learning models, with potential applications in computer vision, robotics, and autonomous systems, and is relevant to keywords such as knowledge distillation, transfer learning, cGAN, image classification, and regression.
