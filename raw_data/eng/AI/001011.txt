The growing complexity and volume of time series data necessitate effective representation learning to improve performance in various downstream tasks. This research addresses the challenge of extracting meaningful representations from time series data without relying on labeled samples. We propose a novel self-supervised learning framework that harnesses both inter and intra-series relational reasoning to enhance time series representation quality.

Methods/Approach: Our method introduces a dual-domain relational reasoning mechanism that captures both inter-series relationships among different time series and intra-series structures within a single time series. We leverage a combination of contrastive learning techniques and relational reasoning modules to develop a robust self-supervised architecture that is capable of unsupervised feature extraction. The model is trained using a contrastive loss function designed to discriminate between true temporal patterns and artificially generated temporal shifts.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that our proposed method significantly outperforms existing self-supervised and unsupervised approaches on benchmark datasets. The representation quality is validated through improved accuracy and robustness in downstream tasks such as time series classification and anomaly detection. Our framework exhibits superior adaptability and generalization across diverse datasets, highlighting its versatility and effectiveness.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduced self-supervised learning framework offers a promising advancement in time series representation learning. By effectively reasoning about inter and intra-series relationships, the proposed model enhances the extraction of meaningful temporal features without the need for labeled data. This work has implications for a wide range of applications, from finance to healthcare, where robust and scalable time series analysis is critical. Key advancements include the integration of relational reasoning into self-supervised learning, contributing to the field of time series analysis and machine learning.

Keywords: self-supervised learning, time series representation, inter-series relational reasoning, intra-series relational reasoning, contrastive learning, unsupervised feature extraction.
