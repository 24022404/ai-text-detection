In the era of data-driven decision-making, sharing data across entities to track global trends often raises privacy and security concerns. This paper addresses the challenge of leveraging collective knowledge without compromising individual data privacy. We propose a novel decentralized online learning framework that enables entities to collaboratively learn global trends while keeping their data private. The approach employs a distributed optimization algorithm that allows participants to update their local models using only aggregated insights from others, eliminating the need for direct data sharing. Our method integrates federated learning principles with adaptive online learning techniques to ensure scalability and real-time adaptability in dynamic environments. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves competitive performance compared to centralized approaches while maintaining data privacy. The system exhibits robustness in handling non-stationary data distributions and scales efficiently with increasing numbers of participants. This research contributes a privacy-preserving solution for decentralized trend tracking, with potential applications in finance, healthcare, and IoT systems. The framework’s ability to balance privacy, accuracy, and scalability makes it a promising tool for collaborative learning in distributed environments.  

**Keywords**: decentralized learning, online learning, federated learning, data privacy, global trend tracking, distributed optimization.
