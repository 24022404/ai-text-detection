Video object detection on mobile devices presents significant challenges due to limited computational resources and power constraints. This paper introduces a novel approach to achieve high-performance video object detection on mobile platforms by optimizing both the detection model and the processing pipeline. Our method incorporates a lightweight yet effective object detection network based on MobileNetV3, coupled with a frame selection strategy that prioritizes frames containing motion and reduces redundant processing. We further employ quantization and model pruning techniques to minimize the model size and computational complexity. Experimental results on benchmark video datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves a significant improvement in frames per second (FPS) and energy efficiency compared to existing methods, while maintaining comparable detection accuracy. Specifically, our system achieves a 25% increase in FPS with a 15% reduction in energy consumption. These results indicate the feasibility of deploying high-performance video object detection systems on mobile devices.

Keywords: Video Object Detection, Mobile Devices, Deep Learning, Model Optimization, Frame Selection, Energy Efficiency.
