This paper addresses the challenge of vehicle make, model, and color recognition from arbitrary viewpoints, a critical task for applications in intelligent transportation systems, surveillance, and automated vehicle monitoring. Traditional methods often struggle with viewpoint variations, leading to reduced accuracy and robustness. To overcome this limitation, we propose a novel approach leveraging Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) designed to achieve view-independent recognition. Our method employs a multi-branch CNN architecture that extracts and fuses features from diverse viewpoints, enabling robust identification regardless of the vehicle's orientation. The model is trained on a large-scale dataset containing images of vehicles captured from multiple angles, ensuring generalization across varying perspectives. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in recognition accuracy compared to existing methods, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets. The proposed system also exhibits strong robustness to lighting conditions and occlusions, further enhancing its practical applicability. This research contributes to advancing vehicle recognition technologies by providing a scalable and efficient solution for real-world scenarios. Key innovations include the integration of viewpoint-invariant feature learning and the development of a lightweight yet powerful CNN architecture. The findings highlight the potential of deep learning in addressing complex recognition tasks, with implications for autonomous systems, traffic management, and security applications.  

**Keywords**: Vehicle recognition, Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), view independence, intelligent transportation systems, deep learning, feature fusion.
