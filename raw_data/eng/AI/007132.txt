Accurate pancreas segmentation in medical images remains a challenging task due to the organ’s variable shape, size, and low contrast with surrounding tissues. This paper presents Attention U-Net, an enhanced encoder-decoder neural network designed to improve localization and segmentation of the pancreas in computed tomography (CT) images. The proposed method integrates attention gates into the standard U-Net architecture, enabling the model to autonomously focus on the most relevant features in both spatial and channel dimensions while suppressing irrelevant regions. Experimental evaluation on benchmark medical image datasets demonstrates that Attention U-Net outperforms traditional U-Net and other state-of-the-art segmentation models in terms of Dice Similarity Coefficient and Hausdorff Distance, leading to more precise and robust pancreas boundaries. Our results indicate that incorporating attention mechanisms significantly improves the model's sensitivity to faint or small organ regions while maintaining computational efficiency. This approach advances automated pancreas segmentation and holds promise for enhanced decision support in clinical applications, including disease diagnosis and treatment planning. Key contributions include the integration of attention mechanisms within a U-Net framework, systematic validation on challenging datasets, and the demonstration of improved performance for medical image analysis tasks. Keywords: attention mechanism, U-Net, pancreas segmentation, medical image analysis, deep learning, CT images.
