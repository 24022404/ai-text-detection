This paper introduces HyperColor, a novel approach to synthesizing auto-colored 3D models for game scene population using hypernetworks. The objective is to automate the process of generating visually appealing and realistic 3D content, reducing the need for manual modeling and texturing. Our method employs a hypernetwork architecture to learn a probabilistic distribution of colors and textures, allowing for the generation of diverse and coherent 3D models. The HyperColor system utilizes a combination of 3D reconstruction and generative modeling techniques to produce high-quality, auto-colored 3D models. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of HyperColor in generating realistic and detailed 3D content, outperforming existing methods in terms of visual fidelity and diversity. The contributions of this research include a novel hypernetwork-based approach to 3D model synthesis, a robust method for generating auto-colored 3D models, and a significant reduction in the time and effort required for game scene population. Key applications of HyperColor include game development, virtual reality, and architectural visualization, with potential benefits in terms of increased efficiency, reduced costs, and enhanced visual realism. Relevant keywords: hypernetworks, 3D reconstruction, generative modeling, auto-colored 3D models, game scene population, computer-aided design.
