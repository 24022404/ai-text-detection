This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing the robustness of Conditional Generative Adversarial Networks (cGANs) in the presence of noisy or adversarial conditional information. Despite their widespread use in image synthesis, data augmentation, and domain adaptation, cGANs often suffer from instability and reduced performance when inputs are corrupted or manipulated. To overcome these limitations, we propose a novel robust cGAN framework that introduces adversarial conditioning regularization and adaptive loss balancing mechanisms. Our approach systematically perturbs conditional labels during training, encouraging the generator and discriminator to become resilient against erroneous or adversarially modified conditions. Extensive experiments are conducted on benchmark datasets, including CIFAR-10 and CelebA, under various perturbation regimes. Results demonstrate that the proposed method significantly outperforms baseline cGANs in terms of image fidelity, conditional accuracy, and resilience to label noise. Furthermore, our framework requires minimal modifications to standard cGAN architectures and incurs negligible computational overhead. This research provides a practical and effective solution for deploying conditional generative models in real-world scenarios where data integrity cannot be guaranteed. Keywords: conditional GAN, robustness, adversarial training, image synthesis, label noise, deep learning.
