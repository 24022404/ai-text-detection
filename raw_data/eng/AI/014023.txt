This paper presents a novel, certifiably polynomial algorithm designed to solve the Best Group Subset Selection problem efficiently. The objective is to identify the optimal subset of groups that maximizes a given objective function, which is crucial in various applications such as feature selection, data mining, and machine learning. Our approach leverages a combination of mathematical programming and combinatorial optimization techniques to develop a scalable and reliable method. The algorithm's performance is evaluated through extensive numerical experiments, demonstrating its ability to solve large-scale instances accurately and efficiently. Key findings indicate that our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art algorithms in terms of computational speed and solution quality. The significance of this research lies in its ability to provide a guaranteed polynomial-time solution, addressing a long-standing open problem in the field. This breakthrough contributes to the development of more efficient and effective subset selection methods, with potential applications in data science, artificial intelligence, and operations research. Relevant keywords include: subset selection, polynomial algorithm, combinatorial optimization, mathematical programming, and data mining.
