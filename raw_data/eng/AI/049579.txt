Gradient-based adversarial attacks pose a significant threat to the robustness of deep learning models. This paper introduces a novel minimax defense strategy designed to mitigate the impact of such attacks. Our approach formulates the defense as a minimax optimization problem, where the inner maximization seeks to identify the most effective adversarial perturbation and the outer minimization aims to train a robust model resilient to these perturbations. Specifically, we incorporate an adaptive perturbation bound within the minimax framework, allowing the model to dynamically adjust its sensitivity to adversarial noise based on the local curvature of the loss landscape. We evaluate the proposed defense on benchmark image classification datasets, including MNIST, CIFAR-10, and ImageNet, against a range of gradient-based attacks such as FGSM, PGD, and C\&W. Experimental results demonstrate that our minimax defense achieves superior robustness compared to existing defense mechanisms, while maintaining competitive clean accuracy. The adaptive perturbation bound proves crucial in preventing overfitting to specific attack strategies and enhancing generalization to unseen adversarial examples. This work offers a promising direction for developing more resilient and trustworthy deep learning systems.

**Keywords:** Adversarial Attacks, Adversarial Defense, Minimax Optimization, Gradient-based Attacks, Robustness, Deep Learning.
