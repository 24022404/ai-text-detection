Background replacement has become a common feature in video conferencing and content creation, yet current methods often fail when presented with challenging scenarios such as motion blur, complex backgrounds, or low-light conditions, resulting in inaccurate segmentation and visual artifacts. This paper introduces a novel tracking-enhanced background replacement technique, named "One More Check," designed to improve the robustness and accuracy of background replacement, particularly in dynamic scenes. Our approach integrates a lightweight optical flow module into existing segmentation networks to provide temporal consistency and refine segmentation masks. The optical flow provides motion cues that guide the segmentation, enabling it to better handle moving objects and blurred edges. We introduce a novel loss function that penalizes temporal inconsistency in the generated masks. The experimental results demonstrate that "One More Check" outperforms state-of-the-art background replacement methods across a range of challenging scenarios, yielding more accurate segmentation, reduced artifacts, and improved visual quality. The improved performance is quantified using standard metrics like Intersection over Union (IoU) and compared against existing methods using both synthetic and real-world datasets. The implications of this research include enhanced user experiences in virtual meetings, improved video editing capabilities, and potential applications in augmented reality.

Keywords: Background replacement, video segmentation, optical flow, motion tracking, deep learning, computer vision.
