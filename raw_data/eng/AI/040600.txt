This paper introduces the Dual-Glance Model, a novel approach for deciphering social relationships through the analysis of human behavior and interaction patterns. The objective is to develop a robust and accurate method for identifying and characterizing social relationships, such as friendships, family ties, and professional connections. Our approach combines computer vision and machine learning techniques to analyze visual cues, including gaze patterns, body language, and facial expressions. The Dual-Glance Model utilizes a two-stage framework, where the first stage detects and tracks individuals in a scene, and the second stage analyzes their interactions to infer social relationships. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our model, achieving a significant improvement in accuracy compared to existing methods. The proposed model has important implications for various applications, including social network analysis, human-computer interaction, and affective computing. Key contributions of this research include the development of a novel dual-stage framework, the incorporation of gaze patterns and body language analysis, and the demonstration of improved performance in social relationship inference. Relevant keywords: social relationship analysis, computer vision, machine learning, gaze patterns, human behavior analysis.
