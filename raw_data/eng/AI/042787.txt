Constrained Policy Optimization (CPO) is a pivotal reinforcement learning technique for safely navigating environments with constraints. This paper addresses the challenge of achieving stable and efficient learning in CPO, which often suffers from conservative policy updates or constraint violations. We introduce a novel approach that dynamically adjusts the trust region size and constraint penalty based on real-time feedback from the environment. Our method, Adaptive Constrained Policy Optimization (ACPO), employs a meta-learning framework to learn optimal adaptation strategies for these parameters. Experimental results across a suite of benchmark control tasks demonstrate that ACPO significantly improves both sample efficiency and constraint satisfaction compared to existing CPO algorithms. Furthermore, ACPO exhibits superior robustness to varying environment conditions and constraint configurations. The findings highlight the potential of adaptive parameter tuning in CPO to enhance its practical applicability in real-world robotic and autonomous systems.
Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Constrained Optimization, Policy Optimization, Trust Region, Meta-Learning, Robotics.
