Heterogeneous Domain Adaptation (HDA) addresses the challenge of transferring knowledge between domains with different feature spaces, a critical task in real-world applications such as cross-platform data analysis and multimodal learning. This paper introduces a novel approach, Cross-Domain Structure Preserving Projection (CDSPP), designed to effectively align heterogeneous domains while preserving the intrinsic structure of data. The proposed method leverages a joint optimization framework that simultaneously minimizes domain discrepancy and maintains the geometric relationships within both source and target domains. By employing a projection matrix to map heterogeneous features into a shared latent space, CDSPP ensures robust adaptation without requiring explicit feature correspondence. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that CDSPP outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of classification accuracy and domain alignment. The approach is particularly effective in scenarios with significant domain shifts and limited labeled data. The findings highlight the potential of CDSPP for applications in cross-domain recommendation systems, medical diagnosis, and other fields requiring knowledge transfer across diverse data modalities.  

**Keywords**: Heterogeneous Domain Adaptation, Cross-Domain Learning, Structure Preservation, Feature Projection, Knowledge Transfer.
