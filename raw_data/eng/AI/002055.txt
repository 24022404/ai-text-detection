3D object detection in point clouds is a critical task for applications such as autonomous driving and robotics. This paper addresses the challenge of accurately detecting objects in sparse and unstructured point cloud data by proposing a novel back-tracing representative points (BRP) method for voting-based 3D object detection. The objective is to improve the precision of object localization and classification by refining the voting mechanism used in existing approaches. Our method introduces a back-tracing module that identifies and leverages representative points within the point cloud, enabling more robust feature aggregation and reducing the impact of noise and outliers. We employ a deep learning framework that integrates the BRP module with a voting-based detection pipeline, optimizing the process of generating high-quality object proposals. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance, with significant improvements in both detection accuracy and computational efficiency compared to existing methods. The proposed method also exhibits strong generalization capabilities across diverse environments. This work contributes to advancing 3D object detection by introducing a novel mechanism for enhancing voting-based systems, with potential applications in real-world scenarios requiring reliable and efficient object detection.  

**Keywords**: 3D object detection, point clouds, voting-based detection, representative points, deep learning, autonomous systems.
