Distributed learning systems, such as federated learning frameworks, have attracted significant attention due to their ability to leverage decentralized data while preserving privacy. However, these systems remain vulnerable to a variety of attacks, including data poisoning and model evasion, which compromise their integrity and reliability. This paper introduces Aspis, a robust detection system designed to safeguard distributed learning against sophisticated adversaries. Aspis employs a combination of anomaly detection algorithms and behavioral analysis, integrating statistical profiling and machine learning-based techniques to identify malicious clients and suspicious updates in real-time. Extensive experiments were conducted on benchmark datasets across various distributed learning scenarios, demonstrating that Aspis achieves superior detection rates and maintains system performance, even under high levels of adversarial activity and communication noise. Compared to existing solutions, Aspis offers improved scalability, adaptability to different learning architectures, and resilience against coordinated attacks. The proposed system provides an effective defense layer, promoting the secure adoption of distributed machine learning in real-world applications such as Internet of Things (IoT), healthcare, and edge computing environments. Keywords: distributed learning, security, anomaly detection, federated learning, robust detection, adversarial attacks, scalable systems.
