This paper introduces Aspis, a novel detection system designed to enhance the security and integrity of distributed learning environments. The objective of Aspis is to identify and mitigate potential threats and anomalies in distributed learning systems, ensuring the reliability and trustworthiness of the learning process. Our approach leverages a combination of machine learning algorithms and statistical analysis to detect suspicious patterns and behaviors in real-time. The Aspis system is built on a robust framework that utilizes a federated learning architecture, enabling the detection of anomalies across multiple nodes and devices. Our results show that Aspis outperforms existing detection systems in terms of accuracy and efficiency, with a significant reduction in false positives and false negatives. The implications of this research are substantial, as Aspis has the potential to protect distributed learning systems from malicious attacks and data breaches, thereby ensuring the integrity of the learning process. Key contributions of this work include the development of a robust and scalable detection system, the application of federated learning to anomaly detection, and the evaluation of Aspis in real-world distributed learning scenarios. Relevant keywords: distributed learning, anomaly detection, federated learning, machine learning, security, robust detection system.
