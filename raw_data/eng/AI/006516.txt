This paper presents a novel approach to human activity recognition in RGB videos, focusing on skeleton-based features to enhance accuracy and efficiency. The objective is to develop a robust system that can accurately identify and classify various human activities from video data. Our method utilizes a deep learning-based framework that extracts skeleton information from RGB videos and leverages this data to recognize activities. The approach employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) to learn spatial features from skeleton sequences, combined with a recurrent neural network (RNN) to model temporal dependencies. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our skeleton-focused approach, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets with significant improvements in accuracy and computational efficiency. The proposed system has potential applications in various fields, including surveillance, healthcare, and human-computer interaction. Key contributions of this research include the development of a lightweight and efficient skeleton extraction algorithm and the introduction of a novel fusion strategy for combining spatial and temporal features. Relevant keywords for this study include human activity recognition, skeleton-based features, deep learning, CNN, RNN, and RGB video analysis.
