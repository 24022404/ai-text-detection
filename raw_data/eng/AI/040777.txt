In the Big Data era, where the volume of available information is immense, small sample learning presents a significant challenge due to the scarcity of annotated data in certain domains. This research paper aims to address the problem of efficient learning from limited sample sizes while leveraging the vast unannotated datasets that characterize modern data environments. We propose a novel approach that integrates advanced machine learning techniques, including transfer learning and data augmentation, to enhance the learning capability from small samples. Our methodology involves the use of pre-trained models on large-scale datasets as a baseline, refined with domain-specific data through fine-tuning, and the implementation of sophisticated augmentation strategies to artificially expand the training dataset. The experimental results demonstrate substantial improvements in prediction accuracy and model robustness compared to traditional methods, showcasing superior performance on various small-sample datasets. This study underscores the potential of small sample learning models in extracting valuable insights from large but sparsely annotated data warehouses. The findings have significant implications for fields such as healthcare and personalized recommendations, where data annotation is often costly and time-consuming. This research contributes to the foundational understanding of small sample learning in big data contexts, paving the way for further advancements in efficient data utilization. Keywords: small sample learning, big data, transfer learning, data augmentation, machine learning.
