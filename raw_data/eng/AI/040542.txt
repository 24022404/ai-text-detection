Graph matching is a fundamental problem in computer vision and pattern recognition, with applications ranging from image correspondence to network alignment. Deep graph matching approaches, leveraging learned node embeddings and edge similarities, have shown promising results. However, these methods often rely on heuristic matching strategies that may not guarantee optimality. This paper investigates the integration of deep graph matching with mixed-integer linear programming (MILP) to obtain provably optimal solutions. We explore different relaxation techniques within the MILP framework to enhance computational efficiency. Our findings demonstrate that while MILP provides a powerful tool for achieving optimal matchings, aggressive relaxation strategies can significantly degrade solution quality in the context of deep graph matching. We analyze the trade-offs between computational cost and solution accuracy, providing insights into the practical limitations of MILP-based optimization for deep graph matching. Keywords: Graph Matching, Deep Learning, Mixed-Integer Linear Programming, Optimization, Relaxation.
