Accurate and robust 6DoF (six degrees of freedom) camera relocalization remains a crucial challenge in computer vision, particularly for large-scale environments with significant variations in appearance and geometry. This paper introduces DH3D (Deep Hierarchical 3D Descriptors), a novel deep learning framework designed to enhance the reliability and precision of 6DoF relocalization. The proposed method leverages a hierarchical neural architecture to extract multi-level 3D descriptors from point cloud data, effectively capturing both local geometric details and global structural context. By integrating these hierarchical representations, DH3D constructs robust descriptors that significantly improve feature matching and pose estimation accuracy, even in environments with repeated structures or occlusions. Comprehensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that DH3D outperforms existing hand-crafted and deep learning-based approaches in terms of localization precision, computational efficiency, and resilience to noise and environmental changes. The enhanced capability of DH3D for large-scale relocalization paves the way for practical applications in autonomous navigation, augmented reality, and 3D scene reconstruction. Key contributions include the hierarchical descriptor design, end-to-end trainable architecture, and extensive benchmarking that underscore DH3D’s state-of-the-art performance in challenging real-world scenarios. Keywords: 6DoF relocalization, deep learning, hierarchical descriptors, 3D point clouds, feature matching, large-scale environments.
