This paper proposes an enhanced approach to prediction and network estimation using the Monotone Single Index Multi-variate Autoregressive (MSIMAR) model. The objective is to address the limitations of existing multivariate autoregressive models by incorporating a monotone single index structure, enabling more accurate and efficient modeling of complex relationships between variables. The MSIMAR model utilizes a novel combination of dimension reduction and autoregressive techniques to capture non-linear dependencies and improve predictive performance. Results demonstrate that the MSIMAR model outperforms traditional methods in terms of prediction accuracy and network estimation, particularly in the presence of high-dimensional data. The key contributions of this research include the development of a flexible and interpretable modeling framework, as well as the introduction of a new estimation procedure that leverages the monotone single index structure to reduce computational complexity. The proposed approach has significant implications for applications in finance, neuroscience, and social network analysis, where accurate prediction and network estimation are crucial. Key keywords: MSIMAR model, multivariate autoregressive models, dimension reduction, network estimation, predictive modeling, high-dimensional data.
