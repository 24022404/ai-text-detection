Software defect prediction (SDP) is crucial for improving software quality and reducing development costs. This paper introduces a novel multiple ensemble learning approach to enhance SDP performance by leveraging diverse datasets. Our method constructs several ensemble models trained on different subsets of software metrics, each focusing on unique aspects of the software development process. These individual ensemble models are then combined using a weighted voting strategy, optimized using a genetic algorithm to maximize prediction accuracy. Experimental results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms traditional single ensemble methods and state-of-the-art SDP techniques in terms of precision, recall, and F1-score. The proposed method offers a robust and adaptive solution for SDP, effectively capturing complex relationships within software metrics and leading to more accurate defect identification. This research contributes to the field of software engineering by providing a practical and effective approach for improving software reliability and maintainability. Keywords: Software Defect Prediction, Ensemble Learning, Genetic Algorithm, Software Metrics, Machine Learning, Weighted Voting.
