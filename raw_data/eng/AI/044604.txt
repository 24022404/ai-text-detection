This paper presents a novel approach to object detection and tracking using a fully convolutional neural network (FCN) that leverages heatmap representations. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of object detection and tracking in various applications, such as surveillance and autonomous vehicles. Our approach employs a FCN to generate heatmaps that highlight the locations of objects of interest, which are then used to detect and track objects across frames. The methodology involves training the FCN on a large dataset of annotated images, allowing it to learn robust features and patterns that enable accurate object detection and tracking. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets. The key findings include improved detection accuracy, reduced false positives, and enhanced tracking capabilities. The proposed method has significant implications for various applications, including real-time object tracking, surveillance, and autonomous systems. Our contributions include the development of a robust and efficient FCN-based approach for object detection and tracking, which outperforms existing methods. Key keywords: object detection, tracking, fully convolutional neural network, heatmap, deep learning, computer vision.
