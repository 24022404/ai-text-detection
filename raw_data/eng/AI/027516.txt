Face age progression techniques have gained significant importance in applications such as digital entertainment, forensic investigations, and personalized user interfaces. This paper presents a novel approach for face age progression that integrates controllable attribute manipulation, enabling fine-grained modification of facial features such as hairstyle, expression, and accessories alongside age transformation. Our method utilizes a generative adversarial network (GAN)-based framework, incorporating an auxiliary attribute encoder to disentangle age-related and attribute-specific features effectively. To validate the approach, we conducted extensive experiments on publicly available face datasets, demonstrating superior performance in generating realistic age-progressed images while preserving identity and user-specified facial attributes. Comparative analysis with existing age progression models shows improvements in visual fidelity, attribute controllability, and quantitative metrics such as identity preservation scores. The proposed system offers a flexible tool for age progression and attribute editing, with potential implications in areas like cross-age recognition, virtual try-on services, and augmented reality applications. Keywords: face age progression, attribute manipulation, generative adversarial networks, facial synthesis, identity preservation, computer vision.
