Image harmonization aims to seamlessly blend a foreground object into a background image, creating a visually realistic composite. Existing methods often struggle with high-resolution images due to computational demands and memory limitations. We introduce a novel Spatial-Separated Curve Rendering Network (SSCR-Net) that efficiently and effectively addresses these challenges. Our approach leverages spatial separation to process different image regions independently, enabling the handling of high-resolution images. Specifically, SSCR-Net learns spatially adaptive curves that characterize the color distribution and adjusts the foreground object's colors accordingly. This rendering network employs a dedicated module to refine blending at object boundaries. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that SSCR-Net achieves state-of-the-art harmonization performance, exhibiting superior visual quality and significantly reduced processing time compared to existing methods, particularly at higher resolutions. The proposed method offers a practical solution for realistic image composition in various applications.

**Keywords:** Image Harmonization, Image Compositing, Curve Rendering, Neural Networks, High-Resolution Images, Spatial Separation
