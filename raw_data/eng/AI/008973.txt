**Abstract**

Underwater object detection presents unique challenges due to poor visibility, varying illumination, and image distortions caused by water. This paper introduces RoIMix, a novel proposal-fusion technique that enhances underwater object detection by combining region proposals from multiple images. Our approach leverages the spatial and spectral diversity available from different viewpoints and temporal captures, generating mixed region-of-interest (RoI) samples that improve training diversity and robustness. We utilize a multi-image fusion pipeline, wherein candidate RoIs are extracted using a standard detection backbone, and novel fusion strategies are applied to create composite training samples. Experimental evaluations are conducted on benchmark underwater datasets, demonstrating that RoIMix significantly improves detection accuracy compared to traditional single-image and mixup augmentation baselines. Ablation studies further reveal the contribution of proposal-fusion at various network stages. The results indicate that RoIMix not only boosts detection performance in challenging underwater scenarios but also enhances generalization to unseen environments. Our method offers an effective solution for robust underwater object detection, with promising applications in marine robotics, environmental monitoring, and maritime search and rescue.  
**Keywords:** underwater object detection, proposal fusion, region of interest, data augmentation, multi-image fusion, deep learning, RoIMix
