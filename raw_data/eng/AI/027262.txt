This paper presents a novel approach to improving the efficiency of Expectation Propagation (EP) for approximate Bayesian inference. The objective is to develop fast convergent algorithms that can accurately estimate posterior distributions in complex models. We propose a new class of algorithms that combine advanced optimization techniques with EP, enabling significant reductions in computational cost while maintaining accuracy. Our methods leverage stochastic gradient descent and quasi-Newton updates to accelerate convergence, making EP more viable for large-scale applications. Experimental results demonstrate that our algorithms achieve state-of-the-art performance in terms of convergence speed and accuracy, outperforming existing EP methods on several benchmark tasks. The key contributions of this research include the development of scalable and efficient EP algorithms, which have important implications for applications in machine learning, artificial intelligence, and data science. Our work enables the use of EP in scenarios where computational resources are limited, making approximate Bayesian inference more accessible and widely applicable. Key keywords: Expectation Propagation, Approximate Bayesian Inference, Stochastic Gradient Descent, Quasi-Newton Updates, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence.
