This paper presents a novel approach to speech-driven facial reenactment using conditional generative adversarial networks (CGANs). The objective is to synthesize realistic facial expressions and lip movements that are synchronized with audio speech inputs. Our method employs a CGAN architecture that conditions on speech features extracted from audio signals, allowing for accurate and expressive facial reenactment. The approach utilizes a combination of convolutional and recurrent neural networks to model the complex relationships between speech and facial movements. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method in generating high-quality, speech-synchronized facial reenactments that outperform existing state-of-the-art approaches. The proposed technique has significant implications for applications such as video conferencing, film production, and virtual reality. Key contributions include the introduction of a novel CGAN-based framework for speech-driven facial reenactment and the demonstration of its potential for generating realistic and expressive facial animations. Relevant keywords: speech-driven facial reenactment, conditional generative adversarial networks, facial animation, audio-visual synthesis, deep learning.
