Accurate prediction of multiphase flow behavior is critical in various industrial applications, yet remains challenging due to its complex, nonlinear dynamics. This paper presents a novel deep neural network (DNN) architecture for predicting multiphase flow regimes and parameters. The proposed model integrates convolutional and recurrent layers to effectively capture both spatial and temporal dependencies within flow data. A large dataset of simulated multiphase flow scenarios, generated using computational fluid dynamics (CFD), was used to train and validate the DNN. Results demonstrate that the DNN model achieves significantly higher prediction accuracy compared to traditional machine learning methods and existing empirical correlations, with an average error reduction of 15% in flow regime identification. Furthermore, the model exhibits robust generalization capability across different flow conditions and geometries. The developed DNN framework offers a promising approach for real-time multiphase flow monitoring, optimization, and control in industrial processes.

**Keywords:** Multiphase Flow, Deep Neural Networks, Machine Learning, Flow Regime Prediction, Computational Fluid Dynamics, Artificial Intelligence.
