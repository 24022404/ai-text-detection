This paper introduces Face X-ray, a novel approach for detecting face forgeries in digital images and videos. The objective is to develop a more general and effective method for identifying manipulated faces, which is a critical challenge in the field of computer vision and cybersecurity. Our approach utilizes a deep learning-based model that analyzes the inconsistencies in the facial features and patterns to distinguish between authentic and forged faces. The Face X-ray method is designed to be robust against various types of face forgeries, including those created using advanced techniques such as deepfakes. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of Face X-ray in detecting face forgeries, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods. The key contributions of this research include the development of a more general and accurate face forgery detection method, which has significant implications for applications such as identity verification, surveillance, and social media authentication. The Face X-ray approach highlights the importance of leveraging deep learning techniques for enhancing the security and integrity of digital media, and its potential applications extend to areas such as facial recognition, image forensics, and AI-powered content analysis. Key keywords: face forgery detection, deepfakes, face x-ray, deep learning, computer vision, cybersecurity.
