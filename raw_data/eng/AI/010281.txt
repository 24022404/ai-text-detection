This paper presents a novel approach to fast universal style transfer, enabling efficient artistic and photorealistic rendering of images. The objective is to develop a method that can adapt a given style to any input image, achieving high-quality results while minimizing computational overhead. Our approach leverages a deep learning-based framework, incorporating a specially designed transformer architecture that facilitates flexible and efficient style transfer. The key findings indicate that our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art style transfer algorithms in terms of speed and visual quality, with applications in various fields such as computer vision, graphics, and digital art. The proposed technique achieves a significant reduction in processing time, making it suitable for real-time applications and large-scale image processing tasks. Our research contributes to the advancement of style transfer technology, offering a fast, universal, and high-quality solution for artistic and photorealistic rendering, with potential applications in areas like image editing, virtual reality, and multimedia production, utilizing relevant keywords such as style transfer, deep learning, transformer, computer vision, and digital art.
