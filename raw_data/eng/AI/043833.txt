This paper presents a novel approach to generating universal perturbations that can effectively attack image retrieval systems. The objective is to design a perturbation that, when added to any query image, can significantly degrade the performance of the retrieval system. Our method utilizes a deep learning-based approach to craft perturbations that can fool the system into returning irrelevant images. We employ a generative model to produce perturbations that are imperceptible to humans yet highly effective in compromising the retrieval system's accuracy. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our approach, showing a significant decrease in retrieval performance across various datasets and systems. Our findings have important implications for the security and reliability of image retrieval systems, highlighting the need for robust defenses against such attacks. The contributions of this research include the development of a universal perturbation attack that can be applied to various image retrieval systems, making it a significant threat to their integrity. Key keywords: universal perturbation attack, image retrieval, deep learning, adversarial attacks, computer vision.
