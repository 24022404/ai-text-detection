This paper proposes a novel evaluation metric for breast cancer screening using Bayesian Neural Networks (BNNs), focusing on the development of an efficient confidence measure-based approach. The objective is to improve the accuracy and reliability of breast cancer diagnosis by leveraging the uncertainty estimation capabilities of BNNs. Our method employs a Bayesian framework to model the uncertainty associated with neural network predictions, allowing for the calculation of confidence measures that reflect the reliability of the diagnosis. The results show that our proposed metric outperforms traditional evaluation metrics, achieving higher accuracy and area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC-ROC) scores. The key findings indicate that the integration of confidence measures with BNNs enhances the robustness and trustworthiness of breast cancer screening systems. This research contributes to the advancement of AI-assisted diagnosis in healthcare, highlighting the potential of Bayesian neural networks and confidence measure-based evaluation metrics to improve patient outcomes. The proposed approach has significant implications for the development of more accurate and reliable breast cancer screening systems, and its novelty lies in the combination of Bayesian inference and confidence measure-based evaluation, making it a valuable contribution to the field of medical imaging and AI-assisted diagnosis. Key keywords: Bayesian Neural Networks, Breast Cancer Screening, Confidence Measure, Evaluation Metric, Uncertainty Estimation, AI-assisted Diagnosis.
