This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing the performance of Adder Neural Networks (ANNs), which are known for reducing power consumption as compared to traditional neural networks while potentially sacrificing accuracy. The research focuses on developing a novel distillation approach to improve the accuracy of ANNs without significantly impacting their efficiency.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a Kernel Based Progressive Distillation (KPD) method that leverages kernel functions to iteratively refine the knowledge transfer process from a pre-trained teacher model to an ANN student model. The proposed method progressively incorporates diverse kernel functions through multiple stages to enhance the learning capacity of the student network. This systematic distillation enables the student ANN to approximate the performance of its teacher while maintaining the computational advantages of adder networks.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that our KPD approach significantly improves the accuracy of ANNs across various benchmark datasets, achieving results that are on par with, or surpass those of state-of-the-art models utilizing traditional neural networks. The KPD method proves effective in balancing the trade-off between accuracy and computational efficiency, showing noteworthy enhancements in system performance with minimal additional resource consumption.

Conclusion/Implications: The Kernel Based Progressive Distillation technique presents a substantial advancement in the field of efficient neural network models. By integrating kernel-based methods into the distillation process, this research offers a pathway to develop highly accurate yet energy-efficient ANNs. The implications of this study are vast, offering potential applications in edge computing and other resource-constrained environments where efficient yet powerful AI models are necessary.

Keywords: Adder Neural Networks, Kernel Functions, Progressive Distillation, Energy Efficiency, Knowledge Transfer, Edge Computing.
