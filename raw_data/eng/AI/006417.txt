Image super-resolution (SR) is a critical task in computer vision, aiming to reconstruct high-resolution images from low-resolution inputs. While Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) have shown promise in this domain, they often struggle with balancing perceptual quality and fidelity. This paper introduces **RankSRGAN**, a novel framework that integrates a ranker into the GAN architecture to address this challenge. The proposed method leverages a ranking-based approach to guide the generator in producing images that not only exhibit high perceptual quality but also maintain structural accuracy. The ranker evaluates intermediate outputs during training, providing feedback to optimize the generator's performance. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that RankSRGAN outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of both visual quality and quantitative metrics, such as PSNR and SSIM. The framework's ability to prioritize perceptual realism while preserving fine details makes it particularly suitable for applications in medical imaging, satellite imagery, and digital restoration. This work contributes a significant advancement in SR by combining ranking mechanisms with adversarial training, offering a robust solution for real-world image enhancement tasks.  

**Keywords**: Image super-resolution, Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), Ranker, Perceptual quality, PSNR, SSIM.
