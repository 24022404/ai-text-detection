The rapid growth of Internet of Things (IoT) devices and the increasing complexity of network environments have created significant challenges in computation offloading, particularly in dynamic and stochastic scenarios. This paper addresses the problem of optimizing computation offloading in Digital Twin Networks (DTNs) by leveraging Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL). The objective is to minimize latency and energy consumption while ensuring efficient resource allocation in stochastic network conditions. We propose a novel DRL-based framework that integrates a twin-environment model to simulate real-time network dynamics and predict optimal offloading decisions. The framework employs a deep Q-network (DQN) to learn and adapt to varying network states, enabling robust decision-making under uncertainty. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms traditional heuristic and rule-based methods, achieving a 25% reduction in latency and a 18% improvement in energy efficiency. The proposed solution also exhibits superior scalability and adaptability to fluctuating network loads. This research contributes to the advancement of DTNs by providing a data-driven, adaptive offloading strategy that enhances network performance and resource utilization. The findings have significant implications for future IoT applications, particularly in smart cities and industrial automation, where real-time decision-making is critical.  

**Keywords**: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Computation Offloading, Digital Twin Networks, IoT, Resource Allocation, Stochastic Optimization.
