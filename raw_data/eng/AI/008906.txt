This research explores the intersection of differential privacy and federated learning, addressing the unique challenges posed by communication constraints in distributed environments. The study aims to enhance data privacy while maintaining efficient and effective model training on decentralized datasets.

Methods/Approach: We developed a novel approach that integrates differential privacy mechanisms within a federated learning framework. Our method strategically reduces the amount of information exchanged during model updates by employing a quantization algorithm, thereby adhering to communication constraints. The solution ensures that privacy is preserved without significantly compromising the accuracy and convergence rate of the federated model.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that our approach successfully balances privacy and performance. It achieves competitive model accuracy compared to existing methods while significantly reducing communication costs through efficient parameter updates. Our findings highlight the effectiveness of integrating privacy-preserving techniques in federated learning systems, particularly in scenarios with limited bandwidth.

Conclusion/Implications: The proposed method offers a robust solution for achieving differential privacy in federated learning under communication constraints, making it suitable for applications in mobile and IoT environments. This research contributes to the growing field of privacy-preserving machine learning by providing a scalable and efficient framework that can be extended to various real-world applications. Future work may explore further optimization strategies and potential trade-offs between privacy levels and system performance.

Keywords: differential privacy, federated learning, communication constraints, distributed systems, privacy-preserving machine learning, quantization algorithm.
