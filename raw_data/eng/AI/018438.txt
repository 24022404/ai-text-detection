The rapid growth of multivariate datasets in various domains has created a pressing need for effective methods to interpret and explain complex data patterns. This paper addresses the challenge of identifying and visualizing meaningful patterns in multivariate data, focusing on the concept of Jumping Emerging Patterns (JEPs). JEPs are significant changes in data distributions that can reveal critical insights across different classes or conditions. The objective of this research is to develop a novel visualization framework that enhances the interpretability of JEPs in high-dimensional datasets.  

We propose a hybrid approach combining dimensionality reduction techniques with interactive visualization tools to extract and display JEPs effectively. Our method leverages advanced clustering algorithms to identify emerging patterns and employs a user-friendly interface to enable domain experts to explore and interpret these patterns intuitively. The framework is evaluated on benchmark datasets from healthcare and finance, demonstrating its ability to uncover hidden trends and anomalies with high accuracy.  

The results show that our approach outperforms traditional methods in terms of both computational efficiency and interpretability, providing actionable insights for decision-making. The proposed framework contributes to the field of data visualization by offering a scalable and intuitive solution for multivariate data explanation. Its potential applications include fraud detection, disease diagnosis, and market trend analysis, making it a valuable tool for researchers and practitioners in data-driven domains.  

**Keywords**: Jumping Emerging Patterns, multivariate data, data visualization, dimensionality reduction, clustering, interpretability.
