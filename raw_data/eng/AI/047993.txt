Video summarization aims to condense lengthy videos into concise, informative summaries, a task that remains challenging due to the complexity of capturing both temporal and semantic dependencies. This paper introduces *Query Twice: Dual Mixture Attention Meta Learning (QT-DMAML)*, a novel framework designed to enhance video summarization by leveraging dual attention mechanisms and meta-learning principles. The proposed approach employs a dual mixture attention module to simultaneously focus on local and global video features, enabling more comprehensive content understanding. Additionally, QT-DMAML integrates meta-learning to adaptively optimize summarization models across diverse video domains, addressing the limitations of traditional methods that struggle with generalization. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that QT-DMAML outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving superior performance in terms of summarization accuracy and efficiency. The framework’s ability to generalize across varying video types highlights its potential for real-world applications, such as video indexing, surveillance, and content recommendation. This research contributes a robust and adaptive solution to video summarization, paving the way for future advancements in multimedia analysis.  

**Keywords**: Video summarization, dual attention, meta-learning, adaptive optimization, multimedia analysis.
