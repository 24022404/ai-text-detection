This paper addresses the challenge of safe exploration in identifying linear systems, a critical task in control theory and reinforcement learning. The objective is to develop a method that ensures system stability and safety while efficiently learning the system dynamics. To achieve this, we propose a robust optimization-based approach that integrates safety constraints directly into the exploration process. Our method leverages convex optimization techniques to formulate a constrained exploration problem, ensuring that the system remains within predefined safe operating limits during identification. We evaluate the proposed approach through extensive simulations and compare it with existing methods, demonstrating its ability to maintain safety while achieving accurate system identification. The results show significant improvements in both safety guarantees and exploration efficiency, particularly in high-dimensional and uncertain environments. This work contributes a novel framework for safe exploration in linear systems, with potential applications in autonomous systems, robotics, and adaptive control. Key innovations include the integration of robust optimization with exploration strategies and the development of safety-aware learning algorithms. Keywords: safe exploration, linear systems, robust optimization, system identification, control theory, reinforcement learning.
