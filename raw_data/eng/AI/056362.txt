Co-salient object detection (CoSOD) aims to identify common salient objects across multiple images, a task that is crucial for applications such as image retrieval, video analysis, and scene understanding. Despite recent advancements, existing methods often struggle with complex scenarios involving diverse backgrounds and varying object appearances. This paper introduces a novel Group Collaborative Learning (GCL) framework to address these challenges. Our approach leverages group-level feature interactions and collaborative learning mechanisms to enhance the detection of co-salient objects. Specifically, we propose a multi-stage architecture that integrates intra-group feature refinement and inter-group collaboration, enabling the model to effectively capture shared saliency patterns across image groups. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed GCL framework outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The results highlight the effectiveness of group-based learning in improving co-salient object detection, particularly in scenarios with high intra-group variability. This research contributes a scalable and efficient solution for CoSOD, with potential applications in multimedia analysis and computer vision systems.  

**Keywords**: Co-salient object detection, group collaborative learning, feature interaction, image analysis, computer vision.
