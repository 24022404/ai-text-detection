This paper investigates the robustness of Vision Transformers (ViTs) against adversarial examples, which are inputs designed to deceive image classification models. Traditional convolutional neural networks (CNNs) have exhibited vulnerabilities to such attacks, and our research aims to determine whether ViTs, a recent development in computer vision, exhibit similar susceptibilities or offer enhanced resilience.

Methods/Approach: We conducted extensive experimentation using a range of adversarial attack techniques on Vision Transformer models. These experiments included both black-box and white-box settings to comprehensively evaluate the defensive capabilities of ViTs. Comparative analysis was performed against prevalent CNN architectures to assess relative robustness.

Results/Findings: Our findings reveal that Vision Transformers exhibit improved resistance to adversarial examples compared to CNNs, particularly in scenarios involving complex attacks. ViTs demonstrated a higher success rate in maintaining classification accuracy under attack, indicating a potential advantage in security-sensitive applications. Despite this, certain attack vectors were still effective, pointing out areas for further improvement.

Conclusion/Implications: The study significantly contributes to understanding the defensive strengths of Vision Transformers and paves the way for developing even more secure AI models in the field of computer vision. Our insights suggest that ViTs could play a crucial role in applications requiring robust image classification, though ongoing research is necessary to bolster defenses against sophisticated adversarial threats.

Keywords: Vision Transformers, adversarial examples, robustness, image classification, computer vision, deep learning, security.
