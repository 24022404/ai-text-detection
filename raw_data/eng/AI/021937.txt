This paper introduces SketchGen, a novel framework designed for the generation of constrained Computer-Aided Design (CAD) sketches. Addressing the increasing demand for automated drafting in engineering and design workflows, SketchGen leverages deep learning techniques to produce geometry-constrained sketches that uphold design intent and manufacturability standards. The core of the system integrates a conditional generative model with a constraint satisfaction module, enabling the creation of sketches that not only replicate observed patterns from trained datasets but also adhere to user-specified geometric and topological constraints. Extensive experiments were conducted using publicly available and proprietary CAD datasets, demonstrating SketchGen’s ability to produce high-quality sketches that fulfill both aesthetic and functional requirements. Comparative evaluations indicate a significant improvement in constraint satisfaction and diversity of generated outputs relative to existing generative approaches. The implications of this research suggest substantial time savings in the conceptual design phase and the potential for seamless integration into modern CAD platforms. Key contributions include the introduction of a dual-stage architecture for geometry-conditioned generation, methods for explicit constraint integration, and a publicly available benchmark for constrained CAD sketch generation. Keywords: CAD, sketch generation, constraint satisfaction, deep learning, generative models, geometry processing.
