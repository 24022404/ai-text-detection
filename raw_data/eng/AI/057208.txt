This paper introduces SLIDE, a novel framework for single-image 3D photography that leverages soft layering and depth-aware inpainting to generate high-quality 3D reconstructions from a single 2D image. The objective of this research is to address the challenges of depth estimation and occlusion handling in monocular 3D reconstruction, which are critical for applications in augmented reality, virtual environments, and digital content creation. SLIDE employs a two-stage approach: first, it utilizes a soft layering mechanism to decompose the input image into depth-ordered layers, enabling precise depth estimation and scene segmentation. Second, a depth-aware inpainting module is applied to reconstruct occluded regions, ensuring seamless and realistic 3D outputs. Experimental results demonstrate that SLIDE outperforms existing methods in terms of reconstruction accuracy, visual fidelity, and computational efficiency. The framework achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets, with significant improvements in handling complex scenes and occlusions. The proposed method not only advances the field of single-image 3D photography but also provides a robust solution for real-world applications requiring efficient and accurate 3D scene generation.  

**Keywords**: 3D photography, depth estimation, occlusion handling, soft layering, depth-aware inpainting, monocular reconstruction.
