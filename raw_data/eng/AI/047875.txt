This paper explores the application of reinforcement learning to representation learning on graphs, aiming to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of graph embedding methods. Our approach utilizes a reinforcement learning framework to learn optimal graph traversal policies, enabling the discovery of informative node and edge representations. We employ a novel graph convolutional network architecture to process graph-structured data and integrate it with a reinforcement learning model to learn adaptive representation learning strategies. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our method over existing graph embedding techniques, achieving significant improvements in node classification, link prediction, and graph clustering tasks. The proposed framework contributes to the advancement of graph representation learning, offering a new perspective on leveraging reinforcement learning to enhance the performance of graph-based models. Key contributions include the introduction of a reinforcement learning-based graph traversal policy and the development of a scalable graph convolutional network architecture, paving the way for future research in graph representation learning and reinforcement learning applications.
