This paper introduces a novel approach to automatically generating building floor plan images from textual descriptions in English. The objective is to bridge the gap between natural language specifications and visual representations in architectural design. Our method employs a deep learning model that combines natural language processing techniques with generative adversarial networks (GANs). Specifically, we leverage a transformer-based encoder to extract semantic features from the textual input and utilize these features to guide the generation of floor plan images through a conditional GAN architecture. The model is trained on a dataset of paired textual descriptions and corresponding floor plan images. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach can generate realistic and coherent floor plan images that accurately reflect the content of the textual descriptions. Furthermore, we compare our method with existing image synthesis techniques and show significant improvements in terms of visual fidelity and semantic consistency. The proposed method offers a potential tool for architects and designers to quickly visualize design concepts from textual specifications, accelerating the initial design phase. Keywords: Floor plan generation, text-to-image synthesis, generative adversarial networks, natural language processing, deep learning, architectural design.
