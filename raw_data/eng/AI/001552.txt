Salient object detection (SOD) plays a critical role in computer vision tasks, enabling the identification of the most visually distinctive objects in an image. Despite significant progress, existing methods often struggle with complex scenes involving multi-scale objects and varying contextual information. This paper introduces a novel Multi-scale Interactive Network (MINet) designed to address these challenges by effectively capturing and integrating multi-scale features. The proposed network employs a hierarchical architecture with interactive modules that enhance feature representation across different scales, enabling precise localization and boundary refinement of salient objects. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that MINet achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The results highlight the network's ability to handle diverse scenarios, including cluttered backgrounds and objects with varying sizes. This research contributes a scalable and efficient solution for SOD, with potential applications in image segmentation, video analysis, and autonomous systems.  

**Keywords**: Salient object detection, multi-scale feature integration, interactive modules, computer vision, hierarchical architecture.
