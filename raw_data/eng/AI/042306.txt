This paper introduces *SketchGen*, a novel framework for generating constrained Computer-Aided Design (CAD) sketches. The objective of this research is to address the challenge of automating the creation of precise and constraint-compliant 2D sketches, which are foundational to 3D modeling in CAD systems. Traditional methods often require extensive manual input, limiting efficiency and scalability. To overcome this, *SketchGen* leverages a hybrid approach combining deep learning techniques with geometric constraint solvers. The proposed model employs a transformer-based architecture to predict sketch primitives and their relationships, followed by a constraint satisfaction module to ensure geometric and dimensional accuracy. Experiments conducted on a diverse dataset of CAD sketches demonstrate that *SketchGen* achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of sketch validity and constraint adherence, outperforming existing methods by a significant margin. The results highlight the framework's ability to generate complex, constraint-compliant sketches with minimal user intervention. This research contributes to the advancement of automated CAD tools, offering potential applications in industrial design, manufacturing, and rapid prototyping. Key innovations include the integration of learning-based prediction with constraint-based refinement, enabling robust and scalable sketch generation. Keywords: CAD, sketch generation, geometric constraints, deep learning, transformer models, automation.
