Autonomous driving systems require robust and efficient methods for detecting moving objects in dynamic environments. This paper presents BEV-MODNet, a novel framework for Bird's Eye View (BEV) moving object detection using a single monocular camera. The proposed approach addresses the challenge of accurately localizing and tracking moving objects in 3D space from 2D image inputs, a critical task for safe navigation in autonomous vehicles. BEV-MODNet leverages a multi-stage deep learning architecture that integrates feature extraction, spatial transformation, and temporal consistency modeling to generate precise BEV representations of moving objects. The system employs a combination of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and transformer-based modules to enhance feature learning and spatial reasoning. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that BEV-MODNet achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of detection accuracy and computational efficiency, outperforming existing monocular-based methods. The framework also shows robustness in diverse driving scenarios, including urban and highway environments. The proposed method contributes to the advancement of autonomous driving technologies by providing a lightweight yet effective solution for real-time moving object detection, with potential applications in path planning, collision avoidance, and traffic analysis.  

**Keywords**: Autonomous driving, Bird's Eye View, moving object detection, monocular camera, deep learning, spatial transformation, real-time systems.
