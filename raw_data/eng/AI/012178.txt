The processing of time series data is fundamental in many domains, yet it often encounters challenges such as outliers, missing values, and variability that can adversely affect analysis accuracy. The objective of this research is to introduce "tsrobprep," a novel R package developed for the robust preprocessing of time series data, addressing common issues with efficiency and flexibility. Our approach involves implementing a suite of algorithms that emphasize data cleansing, imputation, and normalization, while maintaining robustness against anomalies and noise. The tsrobprep package offers tools for automated anomaly detection and correction, employing methods that minimize distortion in historical data trends. In our evaluations, we benchmark the package's performance against existing preprocessing tools, demonstrating superior resilience to common time series data disruptions and a significant reduction in preprocessing time. The findings indicate that tsrobprep enhances the preparation of datasets, leading to more accurate downstream analyses and modeling. This contribution is crucial for practitioners in fields like finance, meteorology, and healthcare, where reliable time series preprocessing decisively impacts subsequent data-driven decision-making. The tsrobprep package is poised to fill a gap in the current R ecosystem, providing researchers and analysts with an accessible, scalable, and robust preprocessing solution. Key keywords include time series, preprocessing, data imputation, anomaly detection, and robust analysis.
