Tkey is an open-source library available on GitHub designed to streamline cryptographic key management for developers by providing an array of essential features, including key generation, secure storage, and easy key usage across multiple cryptographic algorithms. With a strong emphasis on security, Tkey employs industry best practices to protect sensitive keys, implementing measures such as secure storage mechanisms and robust safeguards against unauthorized access. The library further facilitates secure key sharing procedures, enabling collaboration while maintaining protection and confidentiality. By adhering to recognized industry standards, Tkey ensures that cryptographic keys remain private and maintain their integrity, while also being readily accessible to authorized entities. This makes Tkey a reliable solution for developers seeking to manage cryptographic keys efficiently and safely within their applications.
