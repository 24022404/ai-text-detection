This paper presents the participation of the gundapusunil team in SemEval-2020 Task 8, which focuses on Multimodal Memotion Analysis—a challenging task involving the detection and classification of emotions and sentiment within internet memes using both textual and visual content. The objective of this research is to develop an effective approach that leverages multimodal data to enhance the accuracy of emotion and sentiment classification in memes, a popular form of social media communication. The proposed method integrates advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques, including transformer-based models for textual analysis, with state-of-the-art deep learning architectures for image feature extraction. Features from both modalities are fused using a late fusion strategy and fed into ensemble classifiers to predict emotion categories and corresponding intensity scores. Experimental results on the official SemEval-2020 Task 8 dataset demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed approach, achieving competitive performance in sub-tasks related to sentiment classification and emotion quantification, and outperforming several baseline models. The analysis highlights the importance of multimodal fusion and the complementary strengths of textual and visual cues in understanding memes. This study contributes a robust, scalable framework for multimodal sentiment analysis and provides insights into the unique challenges of analyzing humor and emotion in online multimedia content. Key keywords: multimodal analysis, memotion, sentiment classification, emotion detection, deep learning, transformer models, social media.
