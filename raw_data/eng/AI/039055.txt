Persistence homology is a powerful tool in topological data analysis for extracting robust topological features from data. A critical aspect of computing persistence homology lies in identifying critical points in a filtration. This paper introduces an efficient and accurate method for determining these critical points, crucial for downstream persistence calculations. Our approach leverages discrete Morse theory principles, combined with an optimized search strategy, to significantly reduce computational overhead compared to traditional methods. We demonstrate the efficacy of our algorithm on several datasets, showing substantial improvements in runtime while maintaining accuracy in identifying critical points. These improvements translate directly into faster and more scalable computation of persistence homology, enabling the analysis of larger and more complex datasets. The results showcase a significant step towards practical applications of topological data analysis in various domains. Keywords: persistence homology, critical points, topological data analysis, discrete Morse theory, computational topology.
