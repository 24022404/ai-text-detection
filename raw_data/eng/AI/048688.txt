The Form view provides a comprehensive interface that displays customer details alongside various ticket-related fields (Figure 4.22), enabling users to both view and interact with their support tickets. Customers can directly use the chatter functionality integrated into the form to communicate with support agents, provide updates, or send necessary file attachments that may assist in resolving their issues more efficiently. Once a ticket submission is complete, the administrative workflow begins; here, the admin reviews the new ticket, ensures its data is accurate, and then approves it before assigning it to the most appropriate agent according to internal processes (Figure 4.23). In scenarios where a Service Level Agreement (SLA) is associated with the ticket, the admin plays a key role in filling out required fields that capture additional criteria dictated by the SLA. These SLA criteria, in conjunction with the selected target stage, are crucial in determining when the specific SLA policy will be automatically enforced and what the mandatory resolution timeframe will be for that ticket, as illustrated in Figure 4.24. This system ensures both accountability and timely resolution of customer issues.
