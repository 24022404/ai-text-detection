This paper addresses the challenge of improving the interpretability of neural networks in the context of forecasting aggregated renewable energy generation, which is crucial for efficient energy management and grid stability. 

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel approach integrating interpretable artificial intelligence techniques with advanced neural network models to enhance the transparency of forecasting processes. Our method involves leveraging explainable AI frameworks to visualize and analyze feature importance and decision-making pathways within the neural network. The study employs a combination of time-series analysis and neural architecture techniques, specifically designed to handle the complexities of renewable energy data.

Results/Findings: The proposed model demonstrates superior performance in both accuracy and interpretability when compared to traditional forecasting methods. Results indicate that our method not only improves the forecasting accuracy of aggregated renewable energy generation but also provides actionable insights into underlying patterns and anomalies in the data. Key performance metrics, such as mean absolute error and explainability scores, highlight the effectiveness of our approach.

Conclusion/Implications: This research significantly contributes to the understanding of neural network interpretability in the renewable energy sector, offering a viable solution for stakeholders to trust and utilize AI-driven forecasts in operational decisions. The findings have broad implications for enhancing grid management and fostering the integration of renewable resources. Future applications of this model can extend to various domains requiring interpretable AI in complex forecasting scenarios.

Keywords: Neural network interpretability, Forecasting, Renewable energy generation, Explainable AI, Time-series analysis.
