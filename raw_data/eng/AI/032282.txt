This paper introduces *Language2Pose*, a novel framework for natural language grounded pose forecasting, which bridges the gap between linguistic descriptions and human motion prediction. The objective of this research is to enable systems to generate accurate and contextually relevant human poses based on textual input, addressing the challenge of aligning semantic language cues with dynamic motion sequences. To achieve this, we propose a multimodal deep learning architecture that integrates natural language processing (NLP) techniques with pose forecasting models. The framework leverages transformer-based encoders to process textual descriptions and combines them with temporal convolutional networks (TCNs) to model the sequential nature of human motion. Our approach is evaluated on benchmark datasets, demonstrating superior performance in terms of pose accuracy and semantic alignment compared to existing methods. The results highlight the effectiveness of the proposed model in capturing nuanced relationships between language and motion. This work contributes to advancing human-computer interaction, virtual reality, and robotics by enabling more intuitive and context-aware motion generation systems. Key innovations include the integration of multimodal data and the development of a robust framework for language-driven pose forecasting. Keywords: pose forecasting, natural language processing, multimodal learning, human motion prediction, transformer models.
