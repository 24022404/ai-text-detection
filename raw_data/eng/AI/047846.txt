This paper introduces EgoFace, a novel system for high-fidelity egocentric face performance capture and photorealistic reenactment. Our approach addresses the challenges of capturing detailed facial expressions and subtle head movements from a first-person perspective, crucial for applications in VR/AR and telepresence. EgoFace utilizes a lightweight, head-mounted camera setup combined with a deep learning framework to reconstruct dynamic 3D facial geometry and appearance. A personalized blendshape model, driven by a neural network trained on captured egocentric video data, facilitates accurate and robust tracking of facial expressions. We propose a novel rendering pipeline that synthesizes photorealistic video of the reconstructed face, enabling seamless reenactment onto a target actor or avatar. Experimental results demonstrate that EgoFace achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of reconstruction accuracy and visual fidelity compared to existing methods. The system’s ability to capture and reenact nuanced facial performances opens new possibilities for immersive communication and personalized digital experiences. Keywords: Egocentric Vision, Face Capture, Performance Reenactment, 3D Reconstruction, Deep Learning, Photorealistic Rendering.
