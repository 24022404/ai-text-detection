The research focuses on developing a novel framework that utilizes student feedback to enhance teaching strategies and optimize educational outcomes. The study addresses the challenge of effectively incorporating feedback mechanisms into the learning process to promote adaptive teaching methods.

Methods/Approach: We propose an innovative approach combining machine learning techniques and a feedback analytics model to interpret and respond to student feedback in real-time. By employing natural language processing algorithms and sentiment analysis, the system evaluates student input and provides actionable insights to educators for iterative improvement in teaching methods.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that the proposed framework significantly improves teaching effectiveness, as evidenced by increased student engagement and satisfaction scores. The system's ability to dynamically adapt teaching strategies based on feedback has shown superior performance compared to traditional, static teaching methods. The results highlight the potential for real-time, data-driven educational approaches to transform instructional practices.

Conclusion/Implications: This research introduces a pioneering approach to integrating student feedback in educational systems, emphasizing the importance of adaptive teaching facilitated by advanced technologies. The findings suggest that leveraging machine learning for feedback analysis not only enhances the teaching-learning process but also empowers educators to tailor their methods in response to the evolving needs of students. This study paves the way for future applications in personalized education and reinforces the critical role of technology in modern pedagogical development.

Keywords: adaptive teaching, student feedback, machine learning, natural language processing, educational technology, feedback analytics, personalized education.
