Facial expression manipulation holds significant promise for applications in virtual reality, entertainment, and human-computer interaction. However, existing methods often struggle with fine-grained control and realistic expression synthesis. This paper introduces a novel framework for fine-grained facial expression manipulation based on disentangled representation learning. Our approach employs a deep neural network architecture that decomposes facial expressions into identity, expression, and detail components. Specifically, we leverage a conditional variational autoencoder (VAE) to learn a latent space where expression attributes are independently controllable. This allows for precise manipulation of specific facial muscles and expression intensities. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of both realism and controllability, surpassing existing techniques in generating subtle and nuanced expressions. We also present a user study validating the improved realism and expressiveness of our manipulated faces. The proposed framework offers a powerful tool for creating highly realistic and customizable facial animations with applications in diverse fields.

Keywords: Facial Expression Manipulation, Disentangled Representation, Deep Learning, VAE, Expression Synthesis.
