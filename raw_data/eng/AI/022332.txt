This paper introduces a novel approach for reasoning over knowledge graphs by leveraging entity-aware step mixture models. Our objective is to improve multi-hop reasoning accuracy and interpretability in complex knowledge graphs. We propose a model that combines graph neural networks (GNNs) with a hierarchical mixture mechanism. The GNN component learns entity embeddings and performs message passing to capture relationships between entities. The step mixture component dynamically selects relevant reasoning paths at each step of the reasoning process, allowing the model to adaptively focus on different aspects of the graph. We evaluate our approach on several benchmark datasets for knowledge graph completion and multi-hop reasoning, demonstrating significant improvements in accuracy compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. Furthermore, we show that the learned mixture weights provide insights into the reasoning process, enhancing the interpretability of the model. Our findings highlight the effectiveness of combining graph-based representations with adaptive reasoning strategies for knowledge graph reasoning. Keywords: Knowledge Graph, Reasoning, Graph Neural Networks, Step Mixture, Entity Embedding, Multi-hop Reasoning, Interpretability.
