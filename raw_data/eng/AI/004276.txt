This paper addresses the challenge of small sample learning in the big data era, where traditional machine learning methods often struggle with limited training data. Our objective is to develop an effective approach to learn from small samples while leveraging the abundance of big data. We propose a novel framework that combines transfer learning and meta-learning to adapt pre-trained models to small sample tasks. Our method utilizes a few-shot learning paradigm, enabling the model to learn from a minimal number of examples. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our approach over state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency. The key findings of this research highlight the potential of small sample learning in big data applications, such as image classification, natural language processing, and recommender systems. Our contributions include a new perspective on small sample learning, a scalable and efficient algorithm, and empirical evidence of its effectiveness. This study has important implications for real-world applications where data is scarce or expensive to collect, and paves the way for further research in few-shot learning, meta-learning, and big data analytics, with relevant keywords including small sample learning, few-shot learning, transfer learning, meta-learning, and big data.
