This paper addresses the challenge of efficiently transferring knowledge between agents in deep reinforcement learning (DRL) environments by introducing a framework for real-time policy distillation. The objective is to enable the continuous and adaptive distillation of a high-performing teacher policy into a smaller, more efficient student network during ongoing training and deployment, without significant interruptions or retraining phases. Our approach leverages online distillation techniques that dynamically align the student policy with the evolving teacher, facilitating rapid adaptation to changing environments and tasks. Through extensive experiments in benchmark DRL environments, we demonstrate that our real-time distillation method achieves comparable or superior performance to standard offline distillation, with notable gains in scalability and sample efficiency. Results indicate reduced training overhead and faster convergence, making the approach suitable for real-world scenarios where immediate deployment and adaptability are critical. The proposed framework also supports multi-teacher settings and varying student capacities, further enhancing its flexibility. These findings highlight the potential of real-time policy distillation to accelerate knowledge transfer, optimize resource usage, and broaden the applicability of deep reinforcement learning in dynamic contexts. Key keywords: deep reinforcement learning, policy distillation, real-time adaptation, knowledge transfer, sample efficiency.
