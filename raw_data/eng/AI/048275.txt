Accurate and complete urban traffic data are crucial for intelligent transportation systems. However, missing data, caused by sensor failures or communication interruptions, poses a significant challenge. This paper introduces a novel spatiotemporal tensor completion method for improved traffic data imputation. Our approach leverages the inherent spatiotemporal correlations within traffic data by representing it as a high-dimensional tensor. We propose a new tensor completion algorithm based on a low-rank tensor factorization model with adaptive weighting, which effectively captures the dynamic spatiotemporal dependencies and reduces the impact of noise. Experimental results on real-world traffic datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms existing imputation techniques, achieving higher accuracy and robustness, particularly under high missing data rates. The proposed method offers a practical and efficient solution for handling missing data in urban traffic management, contributing to more reliable and data-driven transportation systems.

Keywords: Tensor Completion, Traffic Imputation, Spatiotemporal Data, Low-Rank Tensor Factorization, Intelligent Transportation Systems.
