Neural Architecture Search (NAS) has shown promise in automating the design of high-performance deep learning models. However, the high computational cost associated with evaluating numerous architectures remains a significant bottleneck. This paper introduces BRP-NAS, a novel Prediction-based NAS framework utilizing Graph Convolutional Networks (GCNs) to efficiently predict the performance of neural architectures. BRP-NAS leverages a bi-level routing proxy strategy to optimize both search efficiency and prediction accuracy. Specifically, the GCN is trained to predict architecture performance based on a limited number of training epochs, guided by a routing proxy that adaptively allocates resources to promising architectures. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that BRP-NAS achieves state-of-the-art performance with significantly reduced search costs compared to existing NAS methods. The proposed framework accelerates the NAS process while maintaining competitive accuracy, offering a practical solution for automated model design. Key keywords: Neural Architecture Search, NAS, Graph Convolutional Networks, GCN, Performance Prediction, Automated Machine Learning.
