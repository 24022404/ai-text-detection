In recent years, style transfer has become a significant area of research in the field of computer vision, aiming to adapt the style of an image while retaining its original content. This paper presents a novel approach to photorealistic style transfer by leveraging wavelet transforms. The primary objective is to address the limitations of existing methods that often result in artifacts and unnatural outputs. Our method employs wavelet decompositions to separately process image details and textures, allowing for precise style modifications without compromising structural integrity. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly reduces artifacts and maintains high fidelity in output images compared to conventional style transfer techniques. The photorealistic transformations achieved are assessed through quantitative and qualitative evaluations, indicating superior performance in preserving fine details and achieving seamless style integration. This research offers valuable contributions to the field of image processing by proposing an efficient and robust technique for photorealistic style transfer, with potential applications in digital art, photography, and media production. Key innovations include the application of wavelet transforms for improved detail preservation and the ability to enhance image realism. Keywords: style transfer, photorealism, wavelet transforms, image processing, computer vision.
