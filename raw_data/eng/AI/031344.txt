Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) is a widely used probabilistic model for topic discovery in text corpora. This paper introduces a novel spectral algorithm for estimating the parameters of LDA models. Unlike traditional iterative methods, such as Gibbs sampling or Expectation-Maximization, our approach leverages spectral decomposition techniques to directly estimate topic-word distributions and document-topic distributions. The proposed algorithm offers provable guarantees for consistent parameter estimation under mild conditions. We demonstrate, through experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets, that our spectral algorithm achieves comparable or superior accuracy to existing methods, while exhibiting significantly faster convergence rates. The results highlight the potential of spectral methods for large-scale topic modeling tasks, offering an efficient alternative to traditional inference algorithms. Keywords: Latent Dirichlet Allocation, Topic Modeling, Spectral Algorithms, Parameter Estimation, Text Analysis.
