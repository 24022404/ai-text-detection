In modern manufacturing environments, 25mm lenses integrated into industrial cameras play a crucial role in automation and quality control processes, especially when paired with sophisticated RGB lighting systems and related inspection tools as indicated in Figures 3.3 and 3.4. These technological components ensure precise image capture, enabling accurate detection and evaluation of products on the production line. After the initial collection of extensive image data, the workflow included cropping more than 300 large images with the aid of a proprietary tool developed in-house. This process ultimately yielded over 600 individual training samples, each resized to a standard 480x500 pixel resolution, thereby significantly enhancing the effectiveness of machine learning models used for defect detection and quality assessment.
