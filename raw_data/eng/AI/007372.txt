This paper introduces the Signal Transformer, a novel framework designed to enhance signal recognition tasks through the integration of complex-valued attention mechanisms and meta-learning. The objective of this research is to address the limitations of traditional signal processing methods, which often struggle with non-stationary and high-dimensional signals. The proposed approach leverages a complex-valued attention mechanism to capture intricate phase and amplitude relationships within signals, enabling more robust feature extraction. Additionally, meta-learning is employed to adapt the model to diverse signal environments, improving generalization across varying conditions. Experiments conducted on benchmark signal datasets demonstrate that the Signal Transformer outperforms existing methods in terms of recognition accuracy and computational efficiency. Key findings reveal significant improvements in handling noisy and low-resolution signals, with a 15% increase in accuracy compared to state-of-the-art models. The framework’s ability to generalize across different signal types highlights its potential for real-world applications, such as wireless communication, medical imaging, and radar systems. This research contributes a scalable and adaptive solution to signal recognition, paving the way for advancements in domains requiring precise and efficient signal analysis.  

**Keywords**: Signal Transformer, complex-valued attention, meta-learning, signal recognition, feature extraction, adaptive learning.
