**Abstract:**  
Accurate segmentation of overlapping chromosomes in microscopic images remains a critical challenge in biomedical image analysis due to complex spatial interactions and indistinct boundaries. This paper proposes a novel adversarial multiscale feature learning framework to address the segmentation of overlapping chromosomes. The approach leverages a convolutional neural network (CNN) with embedded multiscale feature extraction modules to capture detailed structural information at various levels of granularity. An adversarial training strategy is employed, wherein a discriminator network guides the segmentation network to produce outputs that closely resemble expert annotations, thereby enhancing boundary delineation and reducing segmentation ambiguities. Extensive experiments on publicly available chromosome datasets demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms state-of-the-art segmentation algorithms in terms of accuracy and robustness, particularly in regions of complex overlaps. The research provides a scalable and generalizable solution for biomedical image segmentation tasks that involve intricate object interactions. Key contributions include the integration of adversarial learning with multiscale feature representation and a comprehensive evaluation of performance improvements in challenging scenarios. Potential applications extend to automated karyotyping, genetic disorder analysis, and high-throughput medical diagnostics, where precise chromosome segmentation is crucial.  
**Keywords:** chromosome segmentation, adversarial learning, multiscale feature extraction, deep learning, biomedical image analysis, overlapping detection.
