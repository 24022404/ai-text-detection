Deep learning (DL) techniques are increasingly employed in fundamental physics research, yet progress is often hindered by data scarcity and limited access to specialized algorithms. This paper introduces a collaborative framework for sharing both physics-simulated datasets and optimized DL algorithms to address these challenges. We present a platform that facilitates the secure and standardized sharing of large-scale datasets generated from particle physics simulations and cosmological models. Furthermore, we provide a repository of pre-trained DL models and custom algorithms tailored for specific physics applications, such as particle identification and event reconstruction. Results demonstrate that utilizing shared datasets and algorithms improves the performance of DL models, reducing the need for extensive individual training and accelerating research cycles. Specifically, we observe a 15% improvement in particle classification accuracy using models fine-tuned on shared datasets compared to models trained on isolated, smaller datasets. This shared resource fosters collaboration and accelerates the adoption of DL across diverse areas of fundamental physics. Keywords: Deep Learning, Fundamental Physics, Data Sharing, Algorithm Sharing, Particle Physics, Cosmology, Open Science.
