The rapid advancement of deep learning techniques has led to increasingly sophisticated face forgery methods, posing significant challenges for detection systems. This paper introduces *Face X-ray*, a novel approach designed to enhance the generalizability of face forgery detection across diverse manipulation techniques. Unlike existing methods that rely on specific artifacts or patterns unique to certain forgery algorithms, Face X-ray focuses on identifying inconsistencies in the blending boundaries between forged and authentic regions within an image. We propose a unified framework that leverages a self-supervised learning mechanism to generate Face X-ray maps, which highlight potential manipulation boundaries without requiring prior knowledge of the forgery method. Our experiments demonstrate that Face X-ray achieves state-of-the-art performance on multiple benchmark datasets, including those containing unseen manipulation techniques, showcasing its robustness and generalizability. The proposed method not only outperforms existing approaches in cross-dataset evaluations but also provides interpretable visual evidence of forgery, making it a valuable tool for real-world applications. This research contributes to the field of digital forensics by offering a more universal and reliable solution for detecting face forgeries, with potential applications in cybersecurity, media integrity verification, and content authentication.  

**Keywords**: Face forgery detection, Face X-ray, deep learning, digital forensics, generalizability, self-supervised learning.
