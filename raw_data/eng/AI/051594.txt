The proliferation of image colorization techniques necessitates robust methods for detecting colorized images, which are often indistinguishable from genuine photographs. This paper introduces a novel convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture specifically designed to identify colorized images with high accuracy and generalization capability. Our approach leverages a deep learning model trained on a large, diverse dataset of both authentic and artificially colorized images, incorporating techniques such as data augmentation and adversarial training to enhance robustness against various colorization artifacts. The proposed model achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets, demonstrating a significant improvement in detection accuracy compared to existing methods. Furthermore, we analyze the model's feature representations to gain insights into the distinguishing characteristics of colorized images. The experimental results indicate the model's ability to generalize well to unseen colorization algorithms and image content, making it a valuable tool for applications such as forensic analysis, content authentication, and misinformation detection. Keywords: Image Colorization Detection, Convolutional Neural Networks, Deep Learning, Image Forensics, Generalization.
