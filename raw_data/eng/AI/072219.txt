Non-convex composition optimization, where the objective function is a composition of multiple functions, presents significant challenges for traditional optimization algorithms. This paper introduces novel variance reduced stochastic methods designed to efficiently tackle this class of problems. We propose two algorithms, Variance Reduced Compositional Gradient (VRCG) and its accelerated variant, Accelerated Variance Reduced Compositional Gradient (AVRCG), which leverage variance reduction techniques to decrease the stochastic gradient noise inherent in compositional optimization. Theoretical analysis demonstrates that VRCG achieves a sample complexity of O(ε^{-6}) to find an ε-stationary point, improving upon existing results. Furthermore, AVRCG achieves an even better sample complexity of O(ε^{-5}). We validate the effectiveness of our proposed methods through extensive experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets, including policy optimization tasks. The results show that VRCG and AVRCG outperform state-of-the-art algorithms in terms of convergence speed and solution quality, particularly in high-dimensional settings. Our work provides new insights into efficient optimization for non-convex compositional problems, offering practical algorithms with provable convergence guarantees.

**Keywords:** Non-convex Optimization, Composition Optimization, Variance Reduction, Stochastic Gradient Methods, Policy Optimization, Sample Complexity.
