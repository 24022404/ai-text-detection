Conditional Normalizing Flows (CNFs) offer a powerful framework for learning complex probability distributions, yet likelihood estimation remains a significant challenge. This paper introduces a novel approach to enhance likelihood estimation within CNFs by leveraging a tailored training objective. We propose a modified loss function that directly optimizes for likelihood accuracy, mitigating issues arising from indirect optimization strategies commonly employed in CNF training. Our method incorporates a regularized term that promotes smoother and more accurate density estimation. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach on a range of benchmark datasets, including image and tabular data, showing significant improvements in likelihood scores compared to existing CNF models and other generative methods. The results highlight the potential of our technique to improve the accuracy and reliability of CNFs for density estimation tasks, offering a valuable tool for uncertainty quantification and downstream applications such as anomaly detection and Bayesian inference. Keywords: Normalizing Flows, Conditional Generative Models, Likelihood Estimation, Density Modeling, Machine Learning.
