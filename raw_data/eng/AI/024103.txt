**Abstract:**

Accurate pedestrian trajectory prediction is critical for the safe operation of autonomous vehicles, especially in complex and dynamic urban environments. This paper introduces Attentional-GCNN, a novel graph-based convolutional neural network architecture augmented with attention mechanisms, designed to provide adaptive and robust pedestrian trajectory prediction across diverse autonomous vehicle scenarios. Our approach models the spatial-temporal interactions among pedestrians and between pedestrians and the environment, allowing the network to dynamically focus on the most relevant agents and contextual cues at each time step. We evaluate Attentional-GCNN on multiple real-world datasets, demonstrating substantial improvements over baseline models in terms of both prediction accuracy and adaptability to varying crowd densities and environmental conditions. The results show that our model not only captures intricate social interactions but also generalizes well to unseen use cases, supporting the versatility required for broad deployment in generic autonomous vehicle systems. This work highlights the importance of attention-based graph neural architectures in enhancing situational awareness for autonomous navigation, with implications for improved safety, efficiency, and real-world applicability in diverse urban settings. 

**Keywords:** pedestrian trajectory prediction, autonomous vehicles, graph neural network, attention mechanism, adaptive modeling, urban environments, situational awareness
