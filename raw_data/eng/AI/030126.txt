This paper presents a novel approach to enhancing the robustness of Processing-In-Memory (PIM) neural networks by introducing a noise-aware normalization technique. The objective is to mitigate the impact of noise and variability inherent in PIM architectures, which can significantly degrade neural network performance. Our method leverages a customized normalization scheme that takes into account the noise characteristics of PIM systems, allowing for more accurate and reliable computations. Experimental results demonstrate that our noise-aware normalization technique outperforms traditional normalization methods, achieving improved accuracy and robustness in various neural network models. The key findings of this study highlight the importance of considering noise and variability in PIM systems and demonstrate the potential of our approach to enable more efficient and reliable neural network processing. This research contributes to the development of robust PIM neural networks, with implications for applications in edge AI, IoT, and autonomous systems, and paves the way for further innovations in the field of noise-resilient neural network design, including keywords such as PIM, neural networks, noise-aware normalization, and robust AI models.
