Accurate RGB-D salient object detection (SOD) is a critical task in computer vision, with applications in image segmentation, robotics, and augmented reality. This paper addresses the challenge of effectively leveraging both RGB and depth information to improve detection accuracy. We propose a novel collaborative learning framework that integrates multi-modal features through a dual-stream network architecture. The framework employs a cross-modal fusion module to dynamically align and combine RGB and depth features, enhancing the model's ability to distinguish salient objects from complex backgrounds. Additionally, a self-supervised learning mechanism is introduced to refine depth data quality, addressing common issues such as noise and missing depth values. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of precision, recall, and F-measure. The proposed framework also exhibits robustness across diverse scenarios, including low-contrast and cluttered environments. This research contributes to the field by providing a scalable and efficient solution for RGB-D SOD, with potential applications in autonomous systems and interactive technologies.  

**Keywords**: RGB-D salient object detection, collaborative learning, cross-modal fusion, self-supervised learning, dual-stream network, computer vision.
