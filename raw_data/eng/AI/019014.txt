The growing demand for explainable machine learning (ML) models in real-world applications has highlighted the need for efficient computational frameworks. This paper explores the hardware acceleration of explainable ML models using Tensor Processing Units (TPUs), addressing the challenge of balancing interpretability with computational efficiency. We propose a novel approach that leverages TPUs to optimize the execution of explainable ML algorithms, such as SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) and LIME (Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations), while maintaining high performance and scalability. Our methodology involves adapting these algorithms to exploit the parallel processing capabilities of TPUs, enabling faster inference and reduced latency. Experimental results demonstrate significant speedups compared to traditional CPU and GPU implementations, with up to a 5x improvement in processing time for large-scale datasets. Furthermore, the proposed framework maintains the interpretability of the models, ensuring that the explanations remain accurate and meaningful. This research contributes to the advancement of explainable AI by providing a hardware-accelerated solution that bridges the gap between interpretability and computational efficiency, paving the way for real-time deployment in critical applications such as healthcare, finance, and autonomous systems.  

**Keywords**: Explainable AI, Tensor Processing Units, Hardware Acceleration, Machine Learning, SHAP, LIME, Interpretability.
