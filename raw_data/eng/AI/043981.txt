Monocular 3D object detection plays a pivotal role in applications such as autonomous driving and robotics, yet it remains challenging due to the absence of explicit depth information. This paper introduces a novel Deep Fitting Degree Scoring Network (DFDSN), designed to enhance the reliability and accuracy of 3D object detection using only single-view images. The proposed approach leverages a deep convolutional neural network to extract representative features, followed by a fitting degree scoring mechanism that quantitatively evaluates how well candidate 3D bounding boxes align with object projections in the image plane. The DFDSN integrates this fitting score into the end-to-end training process, enabling the model to prioritize geometrically plausible predictions and suppress false positives. Comprehensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms existing monocular 3D detection frameworks, achieving higher precision and recall, particularly in cluttered or occluded scenes. The results indicate improved localization accuracy and robustness without additional computational overhead. The insights from this research provide valuable contributions to the advancement of perception systems for intelligent vehicles and other vision-based 3D applications. Key contributions include the introduction of the fitting degree concept, improved end-to-end network architecture, and thorough evaluation against state-of-the-art methods. Keywords: Monocular 3D Object Detection, Deep Learning, Fitting Degree, Bounding Box Alignment, Computer Vision, Autonomous Driving.
