Deep Metric Learning (DML) has emerged as a powerful approach for learning semantic similarity in high-dimensional data, particularly in applications such as image retrieval, clustering, and classification. However, the lack of interpretability in DML models poses a significant challenge, limiting their adoption in critical domains where transparency is essential. This paper addresses the problem of providing visual explanations for DML models to enhance their interpretability and trustworthiness. We propose a novel framework that integrates gradient-based visualization techniques with DML architectures to generate intuitive visual explanations of similarity metrics learned by the model. Our approach leverages class activation maps and feature attribution methods to highlight regions of input data that contribute most to the learned embeddings. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed framework not only maintains competitive performance in terms of retrieval accuracy but also provides meaningful insights into the decision-making process of DML models. The results show significant improvements in interpretability without compromising model efficiency. This work contributes to bridging the gap between model performance and explainability in DML, paving the way for its broader application in domains requiring both accuracy and transparency.  

**Keywords**: Deep Metric Learning, Visual Explanation, Interpretability, Feature Attribution, Image Retrieval, Explainable AI.
