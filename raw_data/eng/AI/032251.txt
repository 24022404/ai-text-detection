This paper introduces NeXtVLAD, a novel and efficient neural network architecture for aggregating frame-level visual features in large-scale video classification tasks. Addressing the computational challenges associated with processing high-dimensional video data, NeXtVLAD builds upon the VLAD (Vector of Locally Aggregated Descriptors) framework, incorporating improvements for enhanced representational power and reduced computational complexity. Specifically, NeXtVLAD introduces a next generation VLAD module that uses a learned residual vector quantization approach. Through experimentation on large video datasets, including Kinetics-400 and YouTube-8M, we demonstrate that NeXtVLAD achieves state-of-the-art classification accuracy with significantly fewer parameters and faster inference times compared to existing aggregation methods. The results highlight NeXtVLAD’s ability to effectively capture complex temporal dynamics and semantic information from video frames, making it well-suited for real-world video analysis applications. Key words: Video Classification, Feature Aggregation, VLAD, Neural Networks, Deep Learning.
