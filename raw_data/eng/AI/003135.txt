This paper proposes an innovative approach to skeleton-based action recognition by introducing an Attention Enhanced Graph Convolutional LSTM Network. The objective is to improve the accuracy of action recognition in videos by effectively modeling spatial and temporal relationships between human joints. Our method employs graph convolutional networks to capture spatial dependencies and LSTM to model temporal dynamics, with an attention mechanism that focuses on the most relevant joints and frames. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in recognition accuracy. The key findings highlight the importance of attention in selectively weighting joint importance and the effectiveness of graph convolutional networks in capturing complex spatial relationships. This research contributes to the field of human action recognition, with potential applications in areas such as surveillance, healthcare, and human-computer interaction. The proposed network architecture and attention mechanism provide a novel and effective solution for skeleton-based action recognition, with key keywords including graph convolutional networks, LSTM, attention mechanisms, and action recognition.
