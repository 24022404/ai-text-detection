This paper introduces a novel super-resolution (SR) technique specifically designed for wavelet-encoded images. The objective is to enhance the visual quality and resolution of images compressed using wavelet transforms, which are prevalent in image storage and transmission. Our method employs a deep convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture optimized for learning the inverse wavelet transform and upsampling simultaneously. Unlike traditional SR approaches, this network leverages the inherent multi-resolution structure of wavelet coefficients to guide the upscaling process. We propose a custom loss function that incorporates both pixel-level and wavelet-domain errors to preserve fine details and reduce artifacts. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art SR methods in terms of PSNR, SSIM, and visual perception, especially for highly compressed images. The proposed technique offers significant improvements in image quality for applications such as medical imaging, remote sensing, and digital archiving. Keywords: Super-resolution, Wavelet transform, Image compression, Deep learning, Convolutional neural network, Image enhancement.
