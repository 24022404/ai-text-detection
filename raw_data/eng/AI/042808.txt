This paper introduces a novel real-time dense 3D reconstruction system that combines simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) with multi-spectral photometric stereo. The objective is to achieve high-quality 3D models with improved accuracy and robustness compared to traditional RGB-D SLAM methods, particularly in challenging lighting conditions. Our approach integrates a multi-spectral photometric stereo technique within a visual SLAM framework, utilizing multiple light sources and spectral bands to estimate surface normals and albedo. This information is then fused with depth measurements from SLAM to refine the 3D geometry. Experimental results on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that our system significantly enhances the accuracy and completeness of 3D reconstructions, especially for objects with complex surface properties. The system achieves real-time performance, making it suitable for interactive applications. Key contributions include the development of a multi-spectral photometric stereo SLAM pipeline and the evaluation of its performance under varying illumination conditions. This work has implications for robotics, augmented reality, and industrial inspection. Keywords: SLAM, photometric stereo, 3D reconstruction, multi-spectral imaging, real-time systems.
