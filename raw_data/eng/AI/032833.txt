This paper presents a novel approach to generating fast and low-distortion adversarial examples, pushing the boundaries of current adversarial attack methods. Our objective is to develop an efficient algorithm that can craft adversarial examples with minimal distortion, while maintaining a high success rate in misleading deep neural networks. We propose a new methodology that leverages the edge cases of the input data distribution, hence the name "Walking on the Edge." Our approach utilizes a combination of gradient-based optimization and geometric constraints to produce high-quality adversarial examples. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art attacks in terms of distortion and efficiency, with significant improvements in generating adversarial examples for image classification tasks. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of edge cases in adversarial example generation and demonstrate the potential of our approach in enhancing the robustness of deep learning models. Our contributions have important implications for the development of more secure and reliable AI systems, particularly in applications where adversarial attacks can have significant consequences. Key keywords: adversarial examples, deep neural networks, edge cases, gradient-based optimization, geometric constraints, AI security.
