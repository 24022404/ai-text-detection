This paper introduces BDgraph, an R package designed for Bayesian structure learning in graphical models. The objective is to provide a comprehensive tool for researchers to learn the structure of complex networks from data, addressing the challenges of model uncertainty and complexity. BDgraph employs a Bayesian approach, utilizing Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) simulations to sample from the posterior distribution of graph structures. The package implements a range of algorithms, including graph sampling and model selection, to identify the most plausible graph structures given the data. Results demonstrate the effectiveness of BDgraph in recovering the true graph structure in various simulated and real-world datasets, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The package contributes to the field by providing a flexible and user-friendly framework for Bayesian structure learning, with potential applications in genetics, social networks, and finance. Key features of BDgraph include its ability to handle large datasets, incorporate prior knowledge, and perform model comparison using Bayesian metrics. Overall, BDgraph offers a significant advancement in graphical model structure learning, enabling researchers to uncover complex relationships in data and make more informed decisions. Keywords: Bayesian structure learning, graphical models, R package, MCMC, model selection, network analysis.
