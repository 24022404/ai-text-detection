Abstract:

This paper presents a novel context-aware, video-based risk descriptor designed to enhance the safety of cyclists in urban environments. The primary objective is to improve real-time risk assessment by leveraging video data to capture and analyze complex traffic scenarios that cyclists encounter. Our approach integrates advanced computer vision algorithms with contextual information such as traffic density, environmental conditions, and cyclist maneuvering patterns. The system utilizes deep learning models for object detection and semantic segmentation, enabling robust identification of potential hazards in the cyclist’s vicinity. Extensive experiments were conducted using real-world cycling footage gathered from multiple urban scenarios, and the results demonstrate that our risk descriptor outperforms traditional sensor-based approaches in both accuracy and responsiveness. Key findings show significant improvements in early hazard detection, with the system providing actionable alerts to users in high-risk situations. The proposed framework offers a scalable solution for integration into smart helmet systems, cycling apps, and urban mobility monitoring platforms. Our research contributes to the advancement of intelligent transportation systems by providing an effective tool for proactive cyclist safety and supporting future developments in context-aware risk management. Keywords: context-aware systems, video analysis, risk assessment, cyclist safety, computer vision, deep learning, intelligent transportation systems.
