This paper introduces a novel target-specific object proposal generation method designed to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of visual tracking algorithms. Existing object proposal methods often generate numerous generic proposals, many of which are irrelevant to the specific target being tracked, leading to computational overhead and potential tracking drift. To address this, our approach integrates target-specific information into the proposal generation process. Specifically, we leverage a lightweight feature extraction network, fine-tuned on the initial target appearance, to guide the proposal generation towards regions more likely to contain the target. Furthermore, we introduce a novel proposal refinement module based on contextual information to further improve the quality of generated proposals. Experimental results on several challenging benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method generates a significantly smaller number of high-quality, target-relevant proposals compared to state-of-the-art generic proposal methods, resulting in improved tracking accuracy and reduced computational cost. The proposed method demonstrates superior performance, achieving a 15% increase in tracking accuracy while reducing the number of proposals by 60%, compared to existing methods. This research offers a promising direction for robust and efficient visual tracking, particularly in scenarios with significant occlusions and background clutter.
Keywords: Visual Tracking, Object Proposal Generation, Target-Specific Tracking, Feature Extraction, Proposal Refinement, Computer Vision.
