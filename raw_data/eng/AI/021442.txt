This paper addresses the challenge of navigating the complex latent spaces of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), focusing on the discovery of non-linear paths that allow for more expressive and efficient manipulations of generated content. We propose WarpedGANSpace, a novel approach to identify Radial Basis Function (RBF) paths within GAN latent spaces, enabling enhanced model interpretability and control over generative capabilities.

Methods/Approach: WarpedGANSpace leverages the properties of radial basis functions to deform the latent space in a non-linear fashion, facilitating the exploration of complex transformations that are often challenging to achieve with linear methodologies. This approach provides a framework for analyzing and manipulating the latent space of well-established GAN architectures, such as StyleGAN, through non-linear trajectories that maintain semantic coherence.

Results/Findings: Our experimental results demonstrate that WarpedGANSpace can effectively uncover a diverse set of non-linear paths in latent space, leading to more nuanced manipulations of generated images. Compared to traditional linear interpolation methods, our approach significantly improves the quality and flexibility of transformations, as evidenced by both qualitative assessments and quantitative metrics evaluating image realism and diversity.

Conclusion/Implications: WarpedGANSpace introduces a powerful tool for the GAN community to enhance control over generative models, offering new opportunities for creative industries, personalized content generation, and other applications requiring nuanced content manipulation. This work bridges a gap between linear explorations of latent spaces and the need for intricate, non-linear transformations, setting the stage for future research in advanced GAN interpretability and flexibility.

Keywords: WarpedGANSpace, GAN latent space, non-linear paths, RBF, image manipulation, generative modeling, StyleGAN.
