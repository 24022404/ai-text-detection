Neural network pruning has emerged as a critical technique for reducing the computational and memory overhead of deep learning models. However, existing pruning methods often struggle to maintain model performance, particularly in highly compressed regimes. This paper identifies dynamical isometry—a property ensuring stable gradient propagation during training—as a key missing ingredient for effective neural network pruning. We propose a novel pruning framework that integrates dynamical isometry principles to preserve gradient flow and improve generalization in pruned networks. Our approach leverages spectral analysis to identify and retain critical connections that maintain isometric properties, enabling more aggressive pruning without sacrificing accuracy. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art pruning techniques, achieving higher compression rates while maintaining competitive performance. The proposed framework not only advances the theoretical understanding of pruning but also provides practical insights for deploying efficient deep learning models in resource-constrained environments. Keywords: neural network pruning, dynamical isometry, gradient propagation, model compression, deep learning efficiency.
