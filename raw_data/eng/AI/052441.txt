Interactive Video Object Segmentation (IVOS) is a critical task in computer vision, enabling precise object segmentation in videos through user interaction. However, selecting the optimal frame for user annotation remains a significant challenge, especially in real-world scenarios with diverse video content. This paper introduces a novel framework for learning to recommend the most informative frame for user interaction, enhancing the efficiency and accuracy of IVOS systems. Our approach leverages a deep learning-based model that analyzes video sequences to predict frames with high segmentation potential, considering factors such as object visibility, motion patterns, and scene complexity. We evaluate the proposed method on benchmark datasets and real-world videos, demonstrating its ability to reduce annotation effort while maintaining high segmentation quality. Experimental results show that our frame recommendation strategy outperforms existing methods in terms of both user interaction efficiency and segmentation accuracy. The proposed framework has broad applications in video editing, autonomous driving, and surveillance systems, where precise object segmentation is essential. This work contributes to advancing IVOS by introducing a data-driven approach to frame selection, paving the way for more user-friendly and effective video analysis tools.  

**Keywords**: Interactive Video Object Segmentation, Frame Recommendation, Deep Learning, Video Analysis, User Interaction, Computer Vision.
