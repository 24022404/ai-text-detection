This paper addresses the challenge of robust pedestrian detection in thermal imagery, a crucial task for various applications including autonomous vehicles and surveillance systems. Our objective is to improve the accuracy and reliability of pedestrian detection in thermal images by leveraging synthesized images. We propose a novel approach that utilizes a combination of real and synthesized thermal images to train a deep learning model, enhancing its ability to detect pedestrians in diverse environmental conditions. Our method involves generating synthesized thermal images that mimic real-world scenarios, which are then used to augment the training dataset. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly improves the detection accuracy and robustness compared to existing methods, particularly in low-visibility conditions. The key findings of this research highlight the effectiveness of using synthesized images to enhance pedestrian detection in thermal imagery, with potential applications in autonomous systems, security, and surveillance. Our contributions include a novel image synthesis technique and a robust pedestrian detection framework, which can be extended to other computer vision tasks. Key keywords: pedestrian detection, thermal imagery, image synthesis, deep learning, autonomous vehicles, surveillance systems.
