This paper addresses the challenge of estimation bias in Q-learning, a popular reinforcement learning algorithm. The objective is to develop a novel approach that controls the bias in Q-value estimation, leading to more accurate and reliable decision-making. We propose Maxmin Q-learning, a new methodology that combines the benefits of maximization and minimization techniques to reduce the estimation bias. Our approach employs a robust optimization framework that adaptively adjusts the learning rate and exploration-exploitation trade-off to minimize the bias. Experimental results demonstrate that Maxmin Q-learning outperforms traditional Q-learning methods in various environments, achieving significant improvements in cumulative rewards and convergence rates. The key findings highlight the importance of bias control in Q-learning and demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in real-world applications. This research contributes to the development of more reliable and efficient reinforcement learning algorithms, with potential applications in robotics, game playing, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: Q-learning, reinforcement learning, estimation bias, maximization, minimization, robust optimization.
