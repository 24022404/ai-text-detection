This paper introduces *HyperColor*, a novel hypernetwork-based framework designed to automate the synthesis of auto-colored 3D models for populating game scenes. The objective is to address the challenge of efficiently generating visually consistent and contextually appropriate 3D assets, which is a time-consuming and labor-intensive process in game development. Leveraging the capabilities of hypernetworks, *HyperColor* dynamically generates color textures and material properties for 3D models based on high-level scene descriptors, such as environment type, lighting conditions, and artistic style. The proposed approach integrates a conditional generative model that learns to map scene context to color palettes and texture patterns, ensuring coherence across diverse game environments. Experimental results demonstrate that *HyperColor* outperforms traditional manual and procedural methods in terms of both visual quality and generation speed, achieving a 30% reduction in production time while maintaining high artistic fidelity. The framework also supports real-time adjustments, enabling developers to fine-tune outputs interactively. This research contributes a scalable and adaptive solution for automating 3D asset creation, with potential applications extending beyond gaming to virtual reality, simulation, and digital content creation. Key innovations include the integration of hypernetworks for conditional texture synthesis and the ability to generalize across multiple artistic styles. Keywords: hypernetworks, 3D modeling, auto-coloring, game development, texture synthesis, generative models.
