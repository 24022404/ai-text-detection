In disaster relief operations, rapid deployment and reliable communication among Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) are critical for effective response and coordination. However, the dynamic and congested spectrum environment in such scenarios poses significant challenges to maintaining robust wireless communications. This paper proposes an autonomous spectrum management scheme tailored for UAV networks operating in disaster-affected areas. The proposed approach leverages machine learning-based spectrum sensing and adaptive frequency allocation to enable UAVs to independently assess spectrum availability, avoid interference, and optimize data throughput without reliance on fixed infrastructure. Through extensive simulations and practical scenarios, the scheme demonstrates superior performance in terms of spectrum utilization efficiency, communication reliability, and adaptability compared to conventional static and rule-based allocation methods. The results indicate that our approach not only reduces communication outages but also supports scalable, real-time coordination among UAVs in highly dynamic environments. This research contributes a novel, self-organizing framework for autonomous UAV networks, facilitating more resilient and efficient disaster relief operations. Key keywords: unmanned aerial vehicles, spectrum management, disaster relief, machine learning, adaptive communication, autonomous systems, wireless networks.
