This paper introduces a novel approach to learning relational representations by integrating auto-encoding techniques with logic programming. The core idea is to leverage the expressive power of logic programs to define relational structures, which are then encoded and decoded using neural networks. This framework allows for learning latent representations of entities and their relationships directly from data. We propose a differentiable architecture that combines neural networks for encoding and decoding with a logic programming layer for reasoning and constraint satisfaction. Experimental results on knowledge graph completion and relational reasoning tasks demonstrate that our method outperforms existing approaches in terms of accuracy and generalization ability. Key benefits include the ability to handle complex relational structures, learn interpretable representations, and incorporate background knowledge through logical constraints. This work contributes to the field of neuro-symbolic AI by providing a new way to bridge the gap between neural networks and symbolic reasoning. Keywords: relational representation learning, auto-encoding, logic programming, neuro-symbolic AI, knowledge graph completion.
