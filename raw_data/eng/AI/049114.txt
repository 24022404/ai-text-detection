Accurately forecasting short-term transit passenger flow is critical for optimizing public transportation systems and improving urban mobility. This paper proposes a novel Multi-Graph Convolutional-Recurrent Neural Network (MGC-RNN) model designed to address the complex spatiotemporal dependencies inherent in transit networks. The proposed method integrates multi-graph convolutional layers to capture diverse topological relationships among transit stations and lines, while recurrent neural network components model temporal patterns in passenger flow data. Extensive experiments were conducted using real-world transit datasets, where the MGC-RNN consistently outperformed traditional statistical models and state-of-the-art deep learning approaches in terms of prediction accuracy and robustness. The results demonstrate that the multi-graph structure enables more comprehensive spatial feature extraction, and the recurrent mechanism effectively learns dynamic temporal trends. This research highlights the effectiveness of combining graph-based and sequential modeling techniques for passenger flow prediction and offers practical implications for real-time transit management, route planning, and resource allocation. Keywords: passenger flow forecasting, multi-graph convolution, recurrent neural networks, urban transit, spatiotemporal prediction, deep learning.
