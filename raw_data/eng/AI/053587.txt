Deep video object tracking (VOT) has achieved remarkable progress; however, existing approaches often struggle with distractors and occlusions due to their reliance on generic feature representations. This paper introduces TDIOT: Target-Driven Inference for Deep Video Object Tracking, a novel framework that enhances tracking robustness by dynamically adapting feature extraction based on target-specific information. TDIOT employs a target-driven attention mechanism that refines feature maps, emphasizing target-relevant cues and suppressing irrelevant background clutter. Furthermore, we introduce a target-aware inference module that explicitly models target appearance variations over time. Experimental results on challenging VOT benchmarks, including VOT2020 and TrackingNet, demonstrate that TDIOT significantly outperforms state-of-the-art trackers, particularly in scenarios with significant occlusions and distractors. Our approach improves tracking accuracy by an average of 5% while maintaining real-time performance. The proposed TDIOT framework provides a promising direction for robust and accurate deep video object tracking by effectively integrating target-specific information into the tracking process. Keywords: video object tracking, deep learning, attention mechanism, target-driven inference, robust tracking.
