Ocean front detection is crucial for various applications, including coastal management, maritime surveillance, and environmental monitoring. Existing methods often struggle with complex coastal environments due to variations in location, seasonality, and weather conditions. This paper introduces LSENet, a novel deep learning framework designed for robust multi-class ocean front detection by explicitly incorporating location and seasonality information. LSENet employs a dual-branch architecture: a spatial feature extraction branch using a convolutional neural network and a location-seasonality embedding branch. The location-seasonality branch leverages geographical coordinates and temporal information to generate embeddings, which are then fused with spatial features to enhance the model's ability to differentiate between diverse ocean front types. Evaluated on a large, geographically diverse dataset, LSENet achieves state-of-the-art performance, demonstrating significant improvements in accuracy and robustness compared to existing methods. The results highlight the importance of incorporating location and seasonality information for accurate ocean front detection. The proposed LSENet offers a practical solution for real-world applications, enabling more effective coastal monitoring and management strategies.

Keywords: Ocean Front Detection, Deep Learning, Location Encoding, Seasonality, Remote Sensing, Coastal Monitoring.
