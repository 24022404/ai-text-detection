In this paper, we address the challenge of accurate time series prediction in cloud computing environments, which is critical for various applications including resource management and forecasting. The primary objective is to develop a periodicity-based parallel time series prediction algorithm that effectively leverages the computational power of cloud infrastructures. Our proposed approach utilizes a hybrid model that combines periodicity detection and a parallel computing framework, enhancing the predictive accuracy and processing speed. We implemented the algorithm and conducted a series of experiments using benchmark datasets, demonstrating that our method outperforms traditional prediction algorithms in terms of accuracy and efficiency. Key findings indicate a significant improvement in prediction performance, particularly in handling large datasets with complex seasonal patterns. This research contributes to the field of time series analysis by providing a novel algorithm that can harness cloud computing capabilities, offering promising implications for real-time data processing and decision-making across various industries. Our findings suggest potential applications in domains such as finance, healthcare, and IoT systems. 

Keywords: time series prediction, periodicity detection, parallel computing, cloud computing, machine learning.
