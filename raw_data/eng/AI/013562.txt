Accurate 3D object localization from monocular images is a critical challenge in computer vision applications such as autonomous driving and robotics. This paper presents MonoGRNet, a novel Geometric Reasoning Network designed for effective monocular 3D object localization without requiring depth sensors or multi-view data. The proposed approach integrates geometric reasoning with deep learning to infer 3D object locations, sizes, and orientations from a single 2D image. MonoGRNet introduces a unified end-to-end architecture that predicts 2D object bounding boxes, keypoint projections, and corresponding geometric parameters, enabling direct estimation of 3D bounding boxes. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that MonoGRNet significantly outperforms existing monocular methods in terms of localization accuracy and robustness, while maintaining real-time inference speed. The network’s geometric reasoning module effectively captures spatial relationships and enhances estimation precision, distinguishing it from prior approaches that rely solely on regression. Our findings highlight MonoGRNet’s potential in enabling affordable and efficient 3D perception for intelligent systems. Keywords: 3D object localization, monocular images, geometric reasoning, deep learning, autonomous driving, real-time perception.
