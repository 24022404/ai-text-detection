This paper investigates a novel deep learning approach for characterizing and transferring channel state information (CSI) in 6G vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communication systems. Addressing the challenge of rapidly changing and heterogeneous vehicular environments, we propose a deep neural network architecture to learn transferable channel modes from multi-input multi-output (MIMO) channel data. Our method leverages a convolutional autoencoder to extract dominant channel features and facilitate knowledge transfer across different V2X scenarios. Results demonstrate that the proposed deep learning model effectively captures essential channel characteristics, enabling accurate CSI prediction and significant performance gains in beamforming and interference management. Specifically, the learned channel modes exhibit strong generalization capabilities, improving spectral efficiency by up to 25% compared to traditional channel estimation techniques in unseen vehicular environments. The findings suggest a promising pathway for robust and efficient V2X communications in future 6G networks. Keywords: Deep Learning, MIMO, Channel Estimation, V2X, 6G, Transfer Learning, CSI prediction.
