Real-time segmentation of surgical instruments is crucial for enhancing robotic surgery systems by providing contextual awareness and enabling advanced functionalities. This paper introduces an attention-guided lightweight network designed for efficient and accurate instrument segmentation. Our approach leverages a novel attention mechanism to focus on relevant image features, improving segmentation precision while minimizing computational overhead. The proposed network architecture incorporates a streamlined convolutional neural network backbone and a refined attention module tailored for surgical instrument characteristics. Experimental results on a publicly available surgical instrument dataset demonstrate that our network achieves state-of-the-art segmentation accuracy with significantly reduced computational complexity compared to existing methods. Specifically, our network achieves a Dice score of X% while maintaining a processing speed of Y frames per second on a standard GPU. This makes it suitable for real-time applications in robotic surgery, enabling more reliable and efficient surgical procedures. The proposed method offers a promising solution for instrument segmentation, with potential for integration into next-generation robotic surgery platforms. Keywords: surgical instrument segmentation, real-time, attention mechanism, lightweight network, robotic surgery.
