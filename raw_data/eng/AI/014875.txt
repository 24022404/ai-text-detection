Variational Bayes (VB) offers a powerful framework for approximate Bayesian inference in deep learning models, but its practical application is often hampered by sensitivity to initialization. Poor initializations can lead to suboptimal solutions and hinder effective learning. This paper investigates novel initialization strategies for variational parameters in deep VB models to improve convergence and performance. We propose a layer-wise pretraining approach that leverages unsupervised learning to obtain informed initial estimates for the variational parameters. Specifically, we use autoencoders to pretrain each layer of the deep model and then map the learned parameters to initialize the corresponding variational distributions. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our initialization scheme on several benchmark datasets, including image classification and natural language processing tasks. Empirical results show that our method consistently outperforms random initialization and other common initialization techniques, leading to faster convergence, improved model accuracy, and better uncertainty estimates. Our findings highlight the critical role of good initializations in deep VB and provide a practical solution for enhancing their applicability. Keywords: Variational Bayes, Deep Learning, Initialization, Bayesian Inference, Autoencoders.
