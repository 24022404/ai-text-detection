This paper addresses the challenge of accurately recognizing human actions based on skeletal data, a crucial aspect of computer vision tasks. We propose a novel Attention Enhanced Graph Convolutional LSTM Network (AE-GCLSTM) tailored for skeleton-based action recognition. The approach integrates the merits of graph convolutional networks (GCNs), which effectively capture spatial dependencies within skeletal data, with long short-term memory (LSTM) networks that are adept at modeling temporal sequences. Novel attention mechanisms are incorporated to dynamically focus on key parts of the skeleton structure, enhancing the model's interpretability and performance. Our AE-GCLSTM model was rigorously tested against several benchmark datasets for skeleton-based action recognition. Our findings show a marked improvement in accuracy and efficiency, outperforming existing state-of-the-art techniques by a significant margin. The research underscores the model's robustness across varying action datasets and demonstrates its potential for practical applications in areas such as surveillance, human-computer interaction, and sports analytics. This work not only advances the field of action recognition by offering a highly effective, scalable solution but also sets a new baseline for future researches leveraging attention-enhanced graph convolutional frameworks. Keywords: skeleton-based action recognition, graph convolutional networks, long short-term memory, attention mechanism, computer vision.
