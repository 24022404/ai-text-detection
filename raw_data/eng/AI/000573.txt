This study aims to develop an accurate electrocardiogram (ECG) arrhythmia classification system using transfer learning from 2-dimensional deep Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) features. The approach involves leveraging pre-trained CNN models to extract relevant features from ECG signals, which are then fine-tuned for arrhythmia classification. The methodology utilizes a large dataset of ECG recordings to train and validate the model, achieving high accuracy in distinguishing between different types of arrhythmias. Results show that the proposed system outperforms traditional machine learning methods, with a significant improvement in classification accuracy. The key findings of this research highlight the effectiveness of transfer learning in ECG arrhythmia classification, demonstrating the potential for improved diagnosis and treatment of cardiac disorders. This study contributes to the field of cardiovascular disease diagnosis, showcasing the benefits of deep learning techniques in analyzing complex biomedical signals. Relevant keywords include: ECG arrhythmia classification, transfer learning, deep CNN, cardiovascular disease diagnosis, machine learning.
