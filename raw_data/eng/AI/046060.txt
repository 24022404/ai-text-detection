This paper addresses the growing concern of attributing Deepfakes, which are synthetic media generated using advanced deep learning techniques. The objective is to develop a reliable method for identifying the source of Deepfakes, thereby mitigating their potential misuse. We propose a novel approach based on analyzing the subtle patterns and artifacts introduced by deep learning models during the generation process. Our method utilizes a combination of computer vision and machine learning techniques to extract unique signatures from Deepfakes, allowing for attribution to specific models or generators. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in attributing Deepfakes with high accuracy, outperforming existing methods. The key findings of this research have significant implications for digital forensics, cybersecurity, and the detection of misinformation. By providing a reliable means of attributing Deepfakes, this work contributes to the development of more secure and trustworthy digital ecosystems, highlighting the importance of attribution techniques in the context of AI-generated content, deep learning, and media forensics.
