The aim of this study is to explore an innovative approach for vertebral body segmentation using the GrowCut algorithm, a method known for its applicability in image segmentation tasks. This research addresses the challenges associated with accurate, efficient segmentation of vertebral bodies, which is critical for various medical analyses and interventions.

Methods/Approach: We implemented the GrowCut algorithm to perform vertebral body segmentation. GrowCut is a cellular automaton-based method that utilizes user-defined seeds to propagate labels across images, effectively capturing complex structures. The workflow developed includes preprocessing steps for enhancing image quality and interactive user input for seed placement, ensuring optimal segmentation results.

Results/Findings: Our findings indicate that the GrowCut-based segmentation method delivers high accuracy and consistency in delineating vertebral bodies when tested on medical imaging datasets. Comparative analysis with conventional methods demonstrated that this approach significantly reduces manual intervention time while maintaining precision. The system's performance was evaluated in terms of computational efficiency and segmentation accuracy, showing promising results for clinical application.

Conclusion/Implications: This study demonstrates the practical utility of the GrowCut algorithm in vertebral body segmentation, presenting an effective workflow that enhances both speed and accuracy. The contributions include a robust segmentation technique that can be integrated into clinical environments, aiding in diagnostic and therapeutic procedures related to spinal conditions. Further research could expand its application to other anatomical regions and explore integration with automated medical imaging systems.

Key Keywords: Vertebral body segmentation, GrowCut algorithm, medical imaging, image segmentation, cellular automaton, clinical application.
