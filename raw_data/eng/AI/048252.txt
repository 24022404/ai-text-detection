This paper addresses the challenge of training low-bit neural networks, which are critical for deploying efficient deep learning models on resource-constrained devices. We propose *Semi-Relaxed Quantization with DropBits*, a novel framework that combines bit-wise regularization with a semi-relaxed quantization approach to optimize the trade-off between model accuracy and computational efficiency. Our method introduces a stochastic bit-dropping mechanism, DropBits, which randomly deactivates bits during training to enhance robustness and generalization in low-bitwidth settings. The semi-relaxed quantization strategy allows for flexible bit-width adaptation, enabling the model to dynamically adjust its precision during training. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance in low-bit neural network training, outperforming existing quantization methods in terms of both accuracy and efficiency. The proposed framework also exhibits improved robustness to bit-width reduction, making it suitable for applications in edge computing and IoT devices. This work contributes a scalable and effective solution for training low-bit neural networks, with potential implications for advancing energy-efficient AI systems.  

**Keywords**: Low-bit neural networks, quantization, bit-wise regularization, DropBits, semi-relaxed quantization, edge computing, efficient AI.
