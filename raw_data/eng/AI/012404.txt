Lesion segmentation in medical imaging is a critical task for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning. However, existing methods often struggle with complex lesion boundaries and varying intensities, leading to suboptimal performance. This paper proposes a novel Deep Attention-Guided Fusion Network (DAGFN) to address these challenges. The proposed model integrates a multi-scale feature extraction mechanism with an attention-guided fusion module, enabling precise localization and segmentation of lesions across diverse medical imaging modalities. The attention mechanism dynamically weights feature maps to focus on relevant regions, while the fusion module combines multi-scale information to enhance boundary delineation. Experiments were conducted on publicly available datasets, including brain MRI and chest CT scans, demonstrating that DAGFN outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of Dice coefficient and Hausdorff distance. The results highlight the model's robustness in handling heterogeneous lesion characteristics and its potential for clinical applications. This research contributes to the field by introducing an innovative architecture that improves segmentation accuracy and generalizability, paving the way for more reliable diagnostic tools in medical imaging.  

**Keywords**: lesion segmentation, attention mechanism, deep learning, medical imaging, multi-scale fusion, convolutional neural networks.
