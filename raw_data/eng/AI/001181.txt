This paper presents a novel approach to explaining the decisions of a classifier by training a Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) to generate explanations in StyleSpace. The objective is to provide insights into the classifier's decision-making process by visualizing the most relevant features and attributes that contribute to its predictions. Our method utilizes a GAN to learn a mapping between the classifier's input space and a StyleSpace, where the generated explanations are semantically meaningful and visually interpretable. The results show that our approach can effectively generate explanations that are consistent with the classifier's decisions, and can be used to identify biases and errors in the classification process. Our contributions include a new framework for explainable AI, which can be applied to various classification tasks and domains. The use of StyleSpace and GANs enables the generation of high-quality explanations that are easy to understand, making our approach a valuable tool for improving the transparency and trustworthiness of AI models. Key keywords: explainable AI, GANs, StyleSpace, classifier interpretation, transparency, trustworthiness.
