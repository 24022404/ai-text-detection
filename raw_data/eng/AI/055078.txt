This paper introduces Ego-CNN, a novel distributed approach for representing graph structures using egocentric perspectives, designed to enhance the detection of critical structural elements within complex networks. Unlike traditional graph neural networks that process the entire graph at once, Ego-CNN constructs localized, node-centric representations by aggregating information from a node's immediate neighborhood. Each node learns its own representation independently, facilitating parallel computation and scalability. Our method leverages convolutional neural networks (CNNs) applied to these egocentric views to identify structural motifs and critical nodes indicative of specific graph properties. We demonstrate the effectiveness of Ego-CNN in detecting critical structures such as bridges, articulation points, and high-influence nodes across various synthetic and real-world networks. Experimental results show that Ego-CNN achieves competitive performance with state-of-the-art graph analysis techniques, while offering significant advantages in computational efficiency and distributed processing. The egocentric approach provides valuable insights into local structural patterns, enabling more targeted and efficient network analysis. Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Distributed Computing, Egocentric Networks, Convolutional Neural Networks, Critical Structure Detection, Network Analysis.
