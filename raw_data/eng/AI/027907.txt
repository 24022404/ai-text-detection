The study focuses on improving the efficiency and performance of deep reinforcement learning (DRL) models by introducing a novel real-time policy distillation framework. This addresses the computational challenges and decision-making latency often encountered in traditional DRL applications.

Methods/Approach: The proposed approach combines policy distillation techniques with a real-time adaptation mechanism, allowing for the seamless transfer of knowledge from larger, pre-trained models to smaller, more efficient models used in deployment. This framework is integrated into the DRL pipeline and dynamically updates the target policy based on environmental interactions and performance feedback. Key technological components include a teacher-student model architecture and an adaptive learning algorithm, ensuring continuous optimization and reduced computational load.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that the real-time policy distillation method significantly enhances the system's overall performance, achieving faster convergence and superior policy accuracy compared to conventional DRL models. The approach also exhibits notable improvements in resource efficiency, making it suitable for deployment in resource-constrained environments such as mobile and embedded systems.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a novel and dynamic element to DRL methodologies, highlighting the potential for effective real-time learning and policy adaptation in complex environments. The findings suggest practical applications in various domains requiring rapid decision-making and efficiency, such as autonomous vehicles, robotic control, and real-time strategy games. This work sets the stage for further exploration of real-time adaptive learning strategies in reinforcement learning. 

Keywords: Deep reinforcement learning, policy distillation, real-time adaptation, teacher-student model, efficiency, decision-making, autonomous systems.
