Moving object detection in dynamic scenes remains a challenging problem due to illumination changes, camera motion, and complex backgrounds. This paper introduces a novel approach for moving object detection based on detecting contiguous outliers within a low-rank representation of the video sequence. Our method leverages the observation that background scenes can be effectively represented by a low-rank matrix, while moving objects manifest as outliers. Instead of treating all outliers equally, we focus on identifying spatially and temporally contiguous outliers, which are more likely to correspond to actual moving objects. We propose an algorithm that decomposes the video sequence into a low-rank component and a sparse outlier component, incorporating a contiguity regularization term to encourage the detection of connected regions. Experimental results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of detection accuracy and robustness to noise. The proposed method effectively suppresses background clutter and accurately identifies moving objects, offering a promising solution for real-time video surveillance and analysis applications. Keywords: Moving Object Detection, Low-Rank Representation, Outlier Detection, Contiguous Outliers, Video Surveillance.
