The objective of this research is to address the challenge of efficient 3D point cloud downsampling, which is crucial for various applications such as real-time rendering, data compression, and geometric analysis in resource-constrained environments. We introduce MOPS-Net, a novel network architecture driven by matrix optimization techniques specifically designed for task-oriented point cloud downsampling. The proposed model leverages a matrix optimization framework to effectively preserve critical geometric features while reducing data density, ensuring high fidelity and performance for downstream analysis and processing tasks. Our approach is systematically evaluated against existing methods, demonstrating superior effectiveness in maintaining accuracy and geometric integrity, as evidenced by quantitative metrics and qualitative assessments. The findings suggest that MOPS-Net not only offers a robust solution for 3D point cloud downsampling but also sets a new standard in terms of adaptability and efficiency for task-specific applications. The implications of this research indicate a significant advancement in the fields of computer graphics, computer vision, and robotics, providing an optimized pathway for handling large-volume 3D data. Overall, this work contributes to the ongoing discourse on efficient data processing in complex computational environments and expands the toolkit available for handling point cloud data. Key keywords include 3D point cloud, downsampling, matrix optimization, MOPS-Net, and task-oriented processing.
