This paper provides a comprehensive review of the application of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) in medical image analysis, aiming to explore the current state-of-the-art methods and techniques used in this field. The approach involves a thorough examination of existing CNN architectures, such as U-Net and ResNet, and their adaptations for various medical imaging tasks, including image classification, segmentation, and detection. The results highlight the significant improvements achieved by CNNs in medical image analysis, including enhanced accuracy, efficiency, and robustness compared to traditional methods. The conclusion emphasizes the substantial contributions of CNNs in medical imaging, enabling accurate diagnoses, personalized treatment, and improved patient outcomes. This review also discusses the potential applications and future directions of CNNs in medical image analysis, including multimodal imaging, transfer learning, and explainability. Key keywords: Convolutional Neural Networks, Medical Image Analysis, Deep Learning, Image Classification, Segmentation, Detection.
