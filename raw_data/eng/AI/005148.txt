This paper introduces a novel approach for image deblurring and super-resolution, leveraging adaptive sparse domain selection and adaptive regularization techniques. The objective is to enhance image quality by simultaneously reducing blur and increasing resolution. Our method adaptively selects relevant sparse domains based on local image characteristics, enabling efficient representation of image structures. Furthermore, we employ an adaptive regularization strategy that adjusts regularization parameters according to the local image content, effectively balancing noise suppression and detail preservation. Experimental results demonstrate that our proposed approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of both quantitative metrics (PSNR and SSIM) and visual quality, particularly in handling complex blur kernels and significant upscaling factors. The adaptive nature of our method makes it robust to varying image conditions and blur types, offering significant improvements in image restoration performance. Keywords: Image Deblurring, Super-resolution, Sparse Representation, Adaptive Regularization, Image Restoration.
