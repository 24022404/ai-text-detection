Despite the abundance of social networks available in Vietnam, none is dedicated specifically to tourism. Mainstream platforms such as Facebook offer an unfocused environment, bombarding users with excessive information that makes finding relevant travel advice or destination recommendations challenging. At the same time, international sites like TripAdvisor tend to prioritize hotel bookings over the authentic, informative exchange of user travel experiences that many travelers seek. Recognizing this gap, Vifrin was established as a specialized social network with the goal of enhancing the travel planning process. It provides a focused platform where users can share their personal journeys, offer genuine recommendations of remarkable places, and support each other in making informed decisions to achieve more meaningful, satisfying trips.
