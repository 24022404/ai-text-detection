Reinforcement learning (RL) offers a powerful paradigm for autonomous agents to learn optimal policies through interaction with an environment. However, ensuring the safety of these policies during learning remains a critical challenge, especially in safety-critical applications. This paper introduces a novel Lyapunov-based approach to safe RL that guarantees constraint satisfaction throughout the learning process. We formulate a control Lyapunov function (CLF) that encodes the desired safety constraints and integrates it within the RL framework. Our method leverages the CLF to modify the agent's actions, ensuring that the system state remains within a safe region. We present a theoretical analysis demonstrating that the proposed algorithm guarantees safety while converging to an optimal policy. Experimental results on benchmark control tasks demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in achieving both safety and performance objectives, outperforming existing safe RL algorithms. The proposed Lyapunov-based safe RL framework offers a promising avenue for deploying RL in real-world scenarios where safety is paramount.

Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Safe Reinforcement Learning, Lyapunov Stability, Control Lyapunov Function, Constrained Optimization, Autonomous Systems.
