Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) is a widely used imaging technique in medical diagnostics and treatment planning. However, sparse-view CBCT reconstructions often suffer from severe artifacts due to limited projection data, compromising image quality and diagnostic accuracy. This paper addresses the challenge of artifact reduction in sparse-view CBCT by proposing a novel adversarial learning framework. Our approach integrates a generative adversarial network (GAN) with a sparse-view reconstruction pipeline to enhance image quality while preserving critical anatomical details. The generator network is designed to learn artifact patterns and suppress them, while the discriminator ensures the realism of the reconstructed images. We evaluate the proposed method on both synthetic and real-world CBCT datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in artifact reduction compared to traditional reconstruction techniques and state-of-the-art deep learning methods. Quantitative metrics, including peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM), confirm the superiority of our approach. The results highlight the potential of adversarial learning for improving sparse-view CBCT imaging, with implications for enhancing diagnostic precision and reducing radiation exposure in clinical settings.  

**Keywords**: CBCT, sparse-view reconstruction, artifact reduction, generative adversarial networks (GANs), medical imaging, deep learning.
