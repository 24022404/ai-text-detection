Hierarchical agglomerative clustering (HAC) is a widely used method for revealing hierarchical structures in data. However, standard HAC algorithms do not inherently respect pre-existing order constraints among data points, which is a crucial requirement in various domains such as time series analysis and genomic data processing. This paper introduces an order-preserving HAC algorithm designed to maintain the original order of data points throughout the clustering process. The proposed method modifies the standard linkage criteria to only consider merging adjacent clusters that respect the initial data order. We evaluate the performance of our algorithm on both synthetic and real-world datasets, demonstrating its ability to preserve order while maintaining competitive clustering accuracy compared to traditional HAC. Results show that the order-preserving constraint introduces a trade-off between cluster quality and adherence to the original ordering. This algorithm provides a valuable tool for applications where maintaining data order is paramount for interpretability and downstream analysis. Keywords: Hierarchical Clustering, Agglomerative Clustering, Order Preservation, Constrained Clustering, Data Analysis.
