This paper proposes DC-GNet, a novel graph convolution network designed to capture complex mesh relations for 3D human shape reconstruction. The objective is to improve the accuracy and detail of reconstructed human shapes by effectively modeling the intricate relationships between mesh vertices. DC-GNet employs a deep learning approach, utilizing graph convolutional layers to learn spatial and structural information from 3D mesh data. The network architecture incorporates a unique mesh relation capturing mechanism, enabling the model to better understand and represent the complex geometry of human bodies. Experimental results demonstrate that DC-GNet outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods, achieving higher reconstruction accuracy and fidelity. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a novel graph convolution network architecture and the application of mesh relation capturing techniques to 3D human shape reconstruction. This work has significant implications for various applications, including computer vision, robotics, and virtual reality, where accurate 3D human modeling is essential. Key keywords: 3D human shape reconstruction, graph convolution network, mesh relation capturing, deep learning, computer vision.
