Hyperspectral image (HSI) analysis offers rich spectral information for various remote sensing applications. However, the high dimensionality of HSI data presents challenges due to computational complexity and data redundancy. This paper introduces a novel attention-based convolutional neural network (CNN) for automatic band selection from HSIs. The proposed method leverages a CNN architecture to learn spectral-spatial features, combined with an attention mechanism that identifies and selects the most informative spectral bands. The attention module dynamically weights the importance of each band, allowing the network to focus on the most discriminative features for improved classification accuracy. Experiments conducted on benchmark HSI datasets demonstrate that the proposed approach achieves superior performance compared to traditional band selection methods and other deep learning-based techniques, while significantly reducing the number of bands required for accurate classification. The results highlight the effectiveness of the attention mechanism in capturing crucial spectral information and its potential for efficient HSI processing. Keywords: Hyperspectral Imaging, Band Selection, Convolutional Neural Networks, Attention Mechanism, Remote Sensing.
