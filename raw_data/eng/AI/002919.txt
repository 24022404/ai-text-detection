Multi-label classification is a critical task in information technology, particularly in domains requiring the simultaneous prediction of multiple labels from complex data. This paper addresses the challenge of effectively leveraging cross-modality data and semantic relationships for improved classification accuracy. We propose a novel framework that integrates **Cross-Modality Attention** with **Semantic Graph Embedding** to capture both inter-modal dependencies and label correlations. The approach employs a dual-stream architecture to process heterogeneous data modalities, such as text and images, while a graph-based embedding mechanism encodes semantic relationships among labels. The cross-modality attention mechanism dynamically aligns and fuses features from different modalities, enhancing the model's ability to learn discriminative representations. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms state-of-the-art approaches in terms of precision, recall, and F1-score. The results highlight the effectiveness of combining cross-modality attention with semantic graph embedding for multi-label classification tasks. This research contributes a scalable and interpretable framework with potential applications in areas such as medical diagnosis, multimedia analysis, and recommendation systems.  

**Keywords**: Multi-label classification, cross-modality attention, semantic graph embedding, heterogeneous data fusion, label correlation.
