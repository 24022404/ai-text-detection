Accurate prediction of chaotic time series is a long-standing challenge in computational science, especially when observational data are contaminated by noise and the underlying system model is unknown. This paper presents a model-free approach utilizing deep learning techniques to forecast noisy chaotic time series without prior knowledge of the governing dynamics. We develop and evaluate a recurrent neural network architecture, specifically designed to capture complex nonlinear dependencies inherent in chaotic systems. The proposed method is tested on synthetic and real-world datasets, including the Lorenz and Mackey-Glass systems with varying degrees of noise. Experimental results demonstrate that our deep learning model achieves superior short-term prediction accuracy compared to traditional model-based forecasting and standard machine learning regressors, exhibiting robust performance even under high-noise conditions. Our findings suggest that model-free deep learning offers a powerful and flexible tool for time series prediction in chaotic and uncertain environments where explicit models are unavailable or unreliable. The approach has broad applicability in fields such as climate science, financial forecasting, and engineering diagnostics. Keywords: deep learning, chaotic time series, model-free prediction, noise robustness, recurrent neural networks.
