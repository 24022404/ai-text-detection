This paper addresses the challenge of video super-resolution (SR) in dynamically changing environments, where unpredictable innovations such as motion artifacts, occlusions, or sudden scene changes often degrade reconstruction quality. We propose a new adaptive video super-resolution algorithm designed to enhance both the robustness and accuracy of high-resolution video frame generation. Our approach integrates a multi-scale recurrent neural network (RNN) framework with an online innovation detection module, which automatically identifies and compensates for novel patterns and disruptions in video sequences. Experiments were conducted on multiple benchmark datasets with diverse motion and scene conditions. Results demonstrate that our algorithm consistently outperforms state-of-the-art video SR methods, achieving higher peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM) scores, particularly in the presence of substantial innovations. The proposed system is also efficient, enabling real-time processing without significant computational overhead. Our findings indicate that adaptive innovation handling is critical for robust video super-resolution, and our method can be effectively deployed in applications such as surveillance, broadcasting, and medical imaging. Keywords: video super-resolution, robustness, adaptive algorithms, recurrent neural networks, innovation detection, real-time processing.
