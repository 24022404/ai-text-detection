This paper reexamines the fundamental task and evaluation metrics of instance segmentation on 3D point clouds, a crucial component in various computer vision applications. Our objective is to address the limitations of current approaches, which often struggle with complex scenes and varying object densities. We propose a novel framework that leverages a combination of geometric and semantic features to improve instance segmentation accuracy. Our method utilizes a state-of-the-art deep learning architecture, incorporating a multi-scale point cloud encoding scheme and a graph-based clustering algorithm. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements over existing methods, with a notable increase in precision and recall on benchmark datasets. Our research contributes to the development of more robust and accurate instance segmentation models for 3D point clouds, with potential applications in fields such as autonomous driving, robotics, and urban planning. Key innovations include the introduction of a new evaluation metric that accounts for object fragmentation and a comprehensive analysis of the impact of point cloud density on instance segmentation performance. Relevant keywords: instance segmentation, 3D point clouds, deep learning, computer vision, geometric features, semantic features.
