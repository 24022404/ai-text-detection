This paper addresses the problem of outlier detection in multi-armed bandit (MAB) settings, where a subset of arms may exhibit anomalous reward distributions. Traditional MAB algorithms assume stationary reward distributions for all arms, which can lead to suboptimal performance in the presence of outliers. We propose a novel generic outlier detection framework applicable to a wide range of MAB algorithms. Our approach involves monitoring the reward distributions of each arm and employing statistical tests to identify arms deviating significantly from expected behavior. Upon detecting an outlier, the algorithm dynamically adjusts arm selection strategies to mitigate its impact. We evaluate the performance of our framework by integrating it with several benchmark MAB algorithms, including UCB1 and Thompson Sampling, across diverse simulated environments with varying outlier characteristics. Experimental results demonstrate that our outlier detection mechanism significantly improves the cumulative regret compared to standard MAB algorithms, especially in scenarios with non-stationary or adversarial outliers. This generic framework provides a practical and effective solution for robust decision-making in dynamic environments. Keywords: Multi-Armed Bandit, Outlier Detection, Anomaly Detection, Reinforcement Learning, Robust Optimization.
