This paper addresses the challenge of inferring the structure of ordinary differential equations (ODEs) from observed data, a critical task in modeling dynamic systems across various scientific and engineering domains. We propose a novel data-driven approach that combines sparse regression techniques with symbolic computation to identify the underlying equations governing system behavior. Our method leverages a library of candidate functions and employs optimization strategies to select the most parsimonious model that accurately describes the observed dynamics. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed approach on both synthetic and real-world datasets, showcasing its ability to recover ODE structures with high accuracy and robustness to noise. Compared to existing methods, our approach achieves superior performance in terms of computational efficiency and model interpretability. The findings highlight the potential of this framework for applications in system identification, predictive modeling, and control theory, offering a scalable and automated solution for uncovering the governing principles of complex systems. Keywords: ordinary differential equations, sparse regression, symbolic computation, system identification, dynamic modeling.
