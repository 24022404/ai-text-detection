Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) algorithms have demonstrated impressive performance across various domains, yet their susceptibility to bias, stemming from factors like biased datasets or reward structures, remains a significant challenge. This paper introduces a novel, general training framework designed to mitigate bias in both existing and future DRL algorithms. Our approach incorporates a bias detection module that identifies and quantifies bias during training, coupled with a debiasing mechanism that adaptively adjusts the learning process to counteract identified biases. We demonstrate the efficacy of our framework by integrating it with several state-of-the-art DRL algorithms, including DQN and PPO, and evaluating their performance on benchmark environments exhibiting different forms of bias. Results show that our framework consistently improves the robustness and generalization capability of these algorithms, leading to higher rewards and more stable learning curves compared to their original counterparts. This framework provides a valuable tool for developing more reliable and trustworthy DRL agents.

**Keywords:** Deep Reinforcement Learning, Bias Mitigation, Debiasing, Reinforcement Learning, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence.
