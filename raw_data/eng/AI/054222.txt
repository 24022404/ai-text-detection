Point cloud completion, inferring complete 3D shapes from partial scans, is a crucial task for various applications, including autonomous driving and robotic manipulation. This paper introduces a novel skip-attention network with hierarchical folding (SAN-HF) for high-quality point cloud completion. Our approach leverages a skip-attention mechanism to selectively propagate features from coarse to fine levels, enhancing the preservation of geometric details. Furthermore, a hierarchical folding module is designed to reconstruct intricate structures by progressively deforming a latent space representation. The SAN-HF network effectively captures both global shape context and local geometric features. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that SAN-HF outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of completion accuracy and visual fidelity. The proposed network exhibits robustness to varying input densities and noise levels. These results suggest that SAN-HF represents a significant advancement in point cloud completion, offering improved performance and greater applicability for real-world scenarios.

Keywords: Point cloud completion, skip-attention, hierarchical folding, 3D reconstruction, deep learning.
