**Abstract**

This paper introduces CoCoNets, a novel framework for continuous contrastive 3D scene representations that enhances both the efficiency and quality of 3D scene understanding and reconstruction. Traditional methods for 3D scene representation often face challenges with discretized data and struggle to capture scene details across varying scales. To address these limitations, CoCoNets leverages a contrastive learning approach in a continuous domain, enabling the network to encode fine-grained structural and semantic information directly from raw observations. Our method employs a contrastive loss to learn discriminative features, facilitating accurate scene embedding without the need for dense supervision or pre-defined voxel grids. Comprehensive experiments on standard 3D benchmarks demonstrate that CoCoNets achieves superior performance in tasks such as novel view synthesis, object localization, and scene segmentation, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods in both accuracy and computational efficiency. The findings highlight the effectiveness of continuous contrastive learning in capturing robust 3D representations, paving the way for improved applications in computer vision, robotics, and virtual reality. Keywords: 3D scene representation, contrastive learning, continuous representation, scene reconstruction, deep learning, computer vision.
