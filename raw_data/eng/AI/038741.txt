This paper introduces Ask & Explore, a novel framework for grounded question answering that facilitates curiosity-driven exploration in complex environments. The objective is to enable autonomous agents to ask informative questions and explore their surroundings to gather relevant information. Our approach combines natural language processing and reinforcement learning to develop a question answering system that adapts to the agent's current knowledge and goals. We evaluate our framework in a simulated environment, demonstrating significant improvements in exploration efficiency and question answering accuracy compared to existing methods. The results show that Ask & Explore outperforms baseline models in terms of knowledge acquisition and decision-making. Our research contributes to the development of more intelligent and curious agents, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, education, and human-computer interaction. Key aspects of our work include grounded question answering, curiosity-driven exploration, and reinforcement learning, making it a valuable contribution to the fields of artificial intelligence, natural language processing, and human-computer interaction.
