The problem of selecting the best subset of groups from a dataset is a critical challenge in machine learning and data analysis, with applications ranging from feature selection to interpretable model building. This paper introduces a certifiably polynomial algorithm designed to address the best group subset selection problem efficiently. The proposed approach leverages advanced optimization techniques and combinatorial methods to ensure both computational efficiency and optimality guarantees. Unlike existing methods that often rely on heuristics or approximations, our algorithm provides a rigorous mathematical framework, ensuring that the selected subset is provably optimal under defined constraints. Experimental results on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that the algorithm outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency, particularly for high-dimensional data. The proposed method also scales gracefully with increasing dataset size and group complexity, making it suitable for practical applications. This work contributes a novel, theoretically grounded solution to group subset selection, offering significant implications for interpretable machine learning, feature engineering, and data-driven decision-making.  

**Keywords**: group subset selection, polynomial algorithm, optimization, interpretable models, machine learning, feature selection.
