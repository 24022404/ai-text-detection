This paper addresses the challenge of limited defective samples in training robust surface defect segmentation models. We propose a novel approach leveraging Neural Style Transfer (NST) to simulate realistic defective samples, thereby augmenting training datasets and improving segmentation performance. Our method utilizes a pre-trained NST network to transfer the texture and appearance of real defective regions onto non-defective surface images, generating synthetic yet plausible defective samples. We quantitatively and qualitatively evaluate our approach on a benchmark dataset for surface defect segmentation. The results demonstrate that training with the augmented dataset, including NST-generated samples, significantly improves the accuracy and robustness of the segmentation model, achieving superior performance compared to training with original data alone and other data augmentation techniques. This research offers a practical solution to the data scarcity problem in surface defect segmentation, enabling the development of more reliable automated quality control systems. Keywords: Surface Defect Segmentation, Neural Style Transfer, Data Augmentation, Defect Simulation, Deep Learning, Image Processing.
