This paper explores the integration of constant-curvature latent manifolds into adversarial autoencoders (AAEs) to enhance the representation learning capabilities of generative models. Traditional AAEs often rely on Euclidean latent spaces, which may not adequately capture the geometric structure of complex data distributions. To address this limitation, we propose a novel framework that embeds hyperbolic and spherical latent manifolds into the AAE architecture, enabling the model to learn more expressive and geometrically meaningful representations. Our approach leverages Riemannian optimization techniques to efficiently train the model on non-Euclidean manifolds. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms conventional AAEs in terms of reconstruction accuracy and generative quality, particularly for data with hierarchical or cyclical structures. The findings highlight the importance of incorporating geometric priors into generative models and suggest potential applications in domains such as natural language processing, computer vision, and bioinformatics. This work contributes to advancing the understanding of latent space geometry in deep generative models and provides a foundation for future research on manifold-based representation learning.  

**Keywords**: adversarial autoencoders, constant-curvature manifolds, hyperbolic geometry, spherical geometry, generative models, representation learning.
