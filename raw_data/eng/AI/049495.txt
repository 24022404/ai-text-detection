This paper introduces DH3D, a novel approach for robust and scalable 6DoF relocalization in large-scale 3D environments. Our method leverages deep learning to generate hierarchical 3D descriptors that encode both local geometric details and global structural information. By learning hierarchical features, DH3D achieves superior robustness to viewpoint changes, occlusions, and environmental variations compared to existing 3D descriptor-based methods. We employ a multi-scale convolutional neural network architecture trained on a large synthetic dataset to extract discriminative features from 3D point clouds. The learned descriptors are then organized hierarchically, enabling efficient matching at different levels of granularity. Extensive experiments on several challenging benchmark datasets demonstrate that DH3D significantly outperforms state-of-the-art techniques in terms of relocalization accuracy and computational efficiency. Specifically, DH3D achieves higher recall rates and lower localization errors, while maintaining real-time performance. The hierarchical representation allows for coarse-to-fine matching, accelerating the search process and reducing the impact of outliers. These results highlight the potential of DH3D for enabling robust and efficient 6DoF relocalization in large-scale applications such as autonomous navigation, augmented reality, and robotics.

**Keywords:** 6DoF Relocalization, 3D Descriptors, Deep Learning, Hierarchical Features, Point Clouds, Computer Vision.
