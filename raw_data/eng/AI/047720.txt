Video captioning, the task of automatically generating textual descriptions for videos, remains a challenging problem due to the complexity of capturing temporal dynamics and semantic information. This paper introduces a novel Dynamic Loss Network (DLN) designed to enhance video captioning performance. The DLN dynamically adjusts the weighting of different loss components during training, focusing on more informative aspects of the video and caption. Specifically, we employ a recurrent neural network to model the temporal evolution of video features and integrate an attention mechanism to highlight salient visual cues. Simultaneously, a loss prediction module learns to dynamically adjust the weights of captioning loss and auxiliary losses based on the current state of the learning process. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including MSVD and MSR-VTT, demonstrate that our proposed DLN significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of caption quality metrics such as BLEU, METEOR, and CIDEr. The DLN improves caption accuracy and relevance by adaptively emphasizing crucial video features and caption elements, offering a promising direction for future research in video understanding and multimodal learning. Keywords: Video Captioning, Dynamic Loss, Attention Mechanism, Deep Learning, Multimodal Learning.
