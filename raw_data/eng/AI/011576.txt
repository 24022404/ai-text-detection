**Abstract**

Reinforcement learning (RL) in continuous action spaces often suffers from challenges related to interpretability and stability, particularly when deployed in critical applications where understanding agent behavior is essential. This paper introduces a novel framework for continuous action reinforcement learning based on a mixture of interpretable experts. Each expert is designed using transparent, human-understandable models—such as decision trees, linear policies, or rule-based systems—which collectively contribute to a composite policy through an adaptive weighting mechanism. The proposed approach enables the agent to leverage the strengths of multiple interpretable strategies, dynamically adjusting their influence according to environmental feedback. Extensive experiments on benchmark continuous control tasks demonstrate that our method achieves competitive performance compared to state-of-the-art black-box RL algorithms, while offering significant improvements in policy transparency and explainability. Furthermore, qualitative analyses reveal that the mixture model facilitates better understanding and debugging of agent decisions. This work highlights the feasibility and benefits of combining interpretability with high-performance RL in continuous domains, making it well-suited for applications requiring both reliability and accountability. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, continuous action spaces, interpretable models, mixture of experts, explainability, policy learning.
