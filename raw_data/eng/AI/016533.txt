Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have shown promise in various applications, but their performance heavily relies on the quality of graph structures. This paper introduces GABO, a novel framework for Graph Augmentations with Bi-level Optimization, designed to automatically learn optimal graph augmentations that enhance GNN performance. GABO formulates the augmentation process as a bi-level optimization problem, where the outer level aims to optimize the GNN's performance on a downstream task, and the inner level learns the optimal graph augmentation strategy by manipulating edge probabilities. We employ a differentiable relaxation of the augmentation process, allowing for gradient-based optimization of both the GNN parameters and the augmentation policy. Experimental results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that GABO significantly outperforms existing graph augmentation techniques and standard GNN models. The learned augmentations often reveal important structural information within the graph, leading to improved generalization and robustness. GABO offers an effective and automated approach for improving GNN performance by adaptively refining graph structures, providing a valuable tool for graph-based machine learning.

Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Graph Augmentation, Bi-level Optimization, Machine Learning, Differentiable Optimization, Graph Structure Learning.
