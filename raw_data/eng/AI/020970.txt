Recent research into the complexity of de Bruijn sequences has yielded notable advancements, including the development by Chan et al. of a fast algorithm to efficiently determine the complexity and its distribution within binary sequences of period 2ⁿ. Expanding the scope beyond binary generators, Edwin provided an analysis that extends these concepts to non-binary cases, broadening the applicability of de Bruijn sequence theory. Additionally, Tuvi et al. investigated the error complexity spectrum, while Tuvi and Lampel constructed sequences that demonstrate the lower bound of complexity, specifically (2ⁿ−1+n), can be consistently achieved for all values of n.
