This paper presents Ensemble Squared, a novel meta-automated machine learning (AutoML) system designed to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of predictive modeling tasks. The objective is to address the challenges associated with model selection, hyperparameter optimization, and ensemble generation in diverse datasets. Our approach integrates multiple machine learning algorithms and meta-learning strategies to automate the entire model-building process, significantly reducing the need for expert intervention. We implemented a series of experiments based on publicly available benchmark datasets to evaluate the system’s performance. Results indicate that Ensemble Squared achieves a notable improvement in prediction accuracy and computational efficiency when compared to existing AutoML frameworks. The outcomes suggest that our system effectively leverages ensemble techniques to adapt to varying data characteristics, thereby optimizing model performance across different scenarios. The implications of this research extend to various domains relying on predictive analytics, offering a practical tool for both researchers and practitioners. Ensemble Squared not only showcases advancements in the AutoML landscape but also provides a scalable solution capable of accommodating complex machine learning tasks, thus contributing to the broader field of artificial intelligence.

Keywords: AutoML, ensemble learning, predictive modeling, machine learning, meta-learning.
