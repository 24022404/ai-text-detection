Formal Concept Analysis (FCA) is a lattice-based data analysis technique used for knowledge representation and information retrieval. However, traditional FCA struggles with scalability and the integration of semantic information. This paper introduces FCA2VEC, a novel approach that leverages embedding techniques to represent formal concepts as vectors in a continuous space. We propose several strategies for generating concept embeddings, including structure-based methods that consider the lattice structure and content-based methods that incorporate attribute and object information. The resulting embeddings capture semantic relationships between concepts, enabling efficient similarity computations and improved knowledge discovery. We evaluate FCA2VEC on benchmark datasets, demonstrating its effectiveness in concept classification, relation prediction, and visualization tasks. Our results show that FCA2VEC outperforms traditional FCA methods and other embedding techniques in terms of accuracy and scalability. The proposed approach provides a powerful tool for analyzing and leveraging the rich information encoded in formal concept lattices, with potential applications in semantic search, recommender systems, and ontology engineering.
Keywords: Formal Concept Analysis, Embeddings, Knowledge Representation, Semantic Similarity, Lattice Structure, Machine Learning.
