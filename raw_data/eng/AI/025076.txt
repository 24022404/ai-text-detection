The objective of this research is to address the challenges associated with image distortion and color inaccuracies that often arise in standard imaging processes by proposing a novel method called Deep Reciprocating HDR Transformation. This method leverages advanced deep learning techniques to enhance image quality and ensure consistent representation across diverse lighting conditions. Our approach integrates a specially designed neural network architecture capable of detecting and correcting high dynamic range (HDR) discrepancies. By employing a reciprocating transformation mechanism, the model iteratively refines image quality, preserving crucial details and achieving superior visual fidelity. The results demonstrate that our method consistently outperforms existing HDR correction techniques, yielding significant improvements in both qualitative and quantitative assessments, including sharper details and more accurate color representation. These findings underline the potential of our approach to elevate image processing in applications such as digital photography and video post-production. In conclusion, the study presents a robust framework for image correction, contributing valuable insights into HDR transformations and offering a promising tool for enhancing digital media quality. Key keywords include image correction, HDR transformation, deep learning, neural network, and color fidelity.
