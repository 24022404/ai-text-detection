This paper investigates the isometry robustness of deep 3D point cloud models when subjected to adversarial attacks. The objective is to analyze the vulnerability of these models to geometric transformations that preserve distances and angles, yet significantly alter the point cloud's structure. To address this problem, we employ a novel approach that combines adversarial attack generation with isometry-invariant feature extraction. Our method utilizes a transformer-based architecture to learn robust representations of 3D point clouds, which are then fine-tuned to withstand adversarial perturbations. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of robustness to isometric transformations, achieving a significant improvement in accuracy under various attack scenarios. The findings of this research have important implications for the development of reliable 3D point cloud models in applications such as computer vision, robotics, and autonomous systems, where robustness to adversarial attacks is crucial. Key contributions include the introduction of a novel isometry-invariant feature extraction method and the demonstration of its effectiveness in enhancing the robustness of deep 3D point cloud models. Relevant keywords: 3D point clouds, isometry robustness, adversarial attacks, transformer-based architectures, geometric deep learning.
