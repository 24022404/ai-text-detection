This paper addresses the challenge of pedestrian attribute recognition and localization, a critical task in video surveillance and intelligent transportation systems. Traditional fully-supervised learning methods require extensive labeled datasets, which are costly and time-consuming to obtain. To overcome these limitations, we propose a weakly-supervised learning framework that effectively learns mid-level features to enhance pedestrian attribute analysis. 

Methods/Approach: Our approach leverages weakly-supervised learning techniques to discover and learn discriminative mid-level features from partially labeled data. We develop a novel framework that integrates convolutional neural networks (CNNs) with attention mechanisms to localize relevant pedestrian attributes without detailed pixel-wise annotations. The model is trained on pedestrian images annotated only at the image level, enabling significant reductions in labeling effort. 

Results/Findings: The proposed method demonstrates superior performance in both recognition accuracy and localization precision compared to state-of-the-art methods that rely on full supervision. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets illustrate the efficacy of our framework, showing improved attribute classification rates and robust attribute localization, even in challenging scenarios with occlusions and varying viewpoints.

Conclusion/Implications: This study introduces a significant advance in pedestrian attribute analysis by reducing reliance on full supervision while maintaining high performance. The findings suggest potential applications in real-time video analysis systems, where quick adaptation to new environments with minimal labeling is essential. Our work paves the way for more efficient, scalable solutions in pedestrian attribute recognition, enhancing automated surveillance and smart city applications.

Keywords: Weakly-supervised learning, pedestrian attribute recognition, mid-level features, CNNs, attention mechanisms, attribute localization, video surveillance.
