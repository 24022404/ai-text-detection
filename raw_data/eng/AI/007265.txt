This paper addresses the challenge of efficiently solving the problem of graph matching, a fundamental task in computer science with applications ranging from biological network analysis to image recognition. The goal is to devise a linear time algorithm for computing optimal assignments in approximate graph matching, enabling faster and more accurate results.

Methods/Approach: We introduce an innovative approach to approximate graph matching by developing a novel algorithm that operates in linear time. The method leverages advanced techniques in graph theory and optimization, focusing on reducing computational complexity while maintaining high levels of accuracy. This is achieved through a unique combination of heuristic strategies and probabilistic methods to optimize assignment solutions.

Results/Findings: The proposed algorithm demonstrates superior performance in terms of speed and reliability compared to existing methods. Experimental evaluations show that the algorithm not only outperforms traditional approaches in computational efficiency but also maintains competitive accuracy in the obtained approximate matches. The results are consistent across diverse datasets, illustrating the robustness and adaptability of the method.

Conclusion/Implications: Our findings contribute a significant advancement in the field of graph matching by providing a scalable solution that reduces time complexity dramatically. The linear time complexity introduces the potential for real-time applications in various domains such as computer vision, network analysis, and pattern recognition. This research paves the way for broader adoption and application of graph matching techniques in both academic and industrial settings, offering a robust tool for large-scale data analysis. Key Keywords: graph matching, linear time algorithm, optimization, approximate matching, computational efficiency.
