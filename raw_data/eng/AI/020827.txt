This paper introduces Gym-ANM, an open-source software framework designed to facilitate the application of reinforcement learning (RL) to power system management. The objective is to provide a comprehensive platform for researchers and educators to explore the potential of RL in optimizing power system operations, such as automatic generation control and load management. Gym-ANM utilizes a model-free RL approach, integrating with popular RL libraries to enable the development and testing of customized RL algorithms. The software includes a range of features, including simulation environments, data analysis tools, and visualization interfaces. Results demonstrate the effectiveness of Gym-ANM in training RL agents to optimize power system performance, with significant improvements in stability and efficiency. The contributions of this research lie in the development of a flexible and accessible platform for power system management, enabling the exploration of innovative RL applications. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, power system management, open-source software, automatic generation control, load management.
