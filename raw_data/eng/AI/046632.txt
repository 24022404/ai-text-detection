Document layout analysis is a critical task in document understanding, enabling the extraction of structured information from unstructured documents. While automated methods have shown promise, they often struggle with complex layouts and diverse document types. This paper introduces a Human-In-The-Loop (HITL) approach to enhance document layout analysis by integrating human expertise with machine learning models. The proposed method leverages iterative feedback loops, where human annotators refine model predictions, and the model adapts to improve accuracy over time. We employ a deep learning-based framework combined with an intuitive annotation interface to facilitate seamless collaboration between humans and machines. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate significant improvements in layout segmentation and element classification accuracy compared to fully automated methods. The HITL approach not only addresses the limitations of existing techniques but also reduces annotation effort and enhances model generalizability across diverse document formats. This research highlights the potential of combining human intelligence with machine learning to tackle complex document analysis challenges, offering practical applications in digitization, information retrieval, and automated document processing.  

**Keywords**: Document layout analysis, Human-In-The-Loop, machine learning, deep learning, annotation interface, document understanding.
