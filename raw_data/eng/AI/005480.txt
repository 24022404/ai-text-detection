This paper proposes SAC-Net, a novel deep learning architecture designed to enhance salient object detection by incorporating spatial attenuation context. The objective is to improve the accuracy of detecting salient objects in images by considering the spatial relationships and contextual information. Our approach utilizes a multi-scale feature extraction module and a spatial attenuation mechanism to selectively emphasize or suppress features based on their spatial locations and contextual importance. Experimental results demonstrate that SAC-Net outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in precision, recall, and F1-score on benchmark datasets. The key contributions of this research lie in its ability to effectively model spatial attenuation context, leading to more accurate and robust salient object detection. This work has potential applications in computer vision tasks such as image segmentation, object recognition, and visual attention modeling, with relevant keywords including salient object detection, deep learning, spatial context, and computer vision.
