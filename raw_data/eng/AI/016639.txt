Explainable AI (XAI) methods, particularly Shapley Values, are increasingly used to interpret machine learning model predictions. However, accurate computation of Shapley Values remains challenging, especially for complex models or large datasets. This paper introduces a novel approach for approximating Shapley Values that enhances both accuracy and robustness. Our method focuses on identifying and utilizing locally important variables, thereby reducing computational complexity and improving the fidelity of the explanations. We propose a new sampling strategy combined with a variance reduction technique tailored for local feature importance estimation. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach provides more accurate Shapley Value approximations compared to existing methods, particularly in high-dimensional feature spaces. Furthermore, the robustness of the explanations is improved, leading to more reliable insights into model behavior. This work contributes to the development of more practical and trustworthy XAI techniques for real-world applications. Keywords: Explainable AI, Shapley Values, Feature Importance, Model Interpretability, Variance Reduction.
