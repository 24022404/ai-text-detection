Scene text detection, a crucial component in various computer vision applications, remains challenging due to the variability in text appearance and complex background interference. This paper introduces a novel predictive ensemble learning framework designed to enhance the accuracy and robustness of scene text detection. The proposed approach integrates multiple text detectors, each with complementary strengths, and employs a meta-learner to predictively weight their outputs based on input image characteristics. Specifically, convolutional neural networks are used to extract image features, which are then fed into a prediction network to estimate the optimal weighting coefficients for each detector. This dynamic weighting strategy allows the ensemble to adapt to different scene complexities and text styles. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including ICDAR 2013 and ICDAR 2015, demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance, surpassing existing ensemble techniques and individual detectors. The proposed framework exhibits improved precision and recall, particularly in challenging scenarios with low resolution or distorted text. This research contributes a flexible and effective ensemble learning paradigm for scene text detection, with potential applications in image retrieval, autonomous navigation, and document analysis. Keywords: Scene Text Detection, Ensemble Learning, Predictive Modeling, Computer Vision, Deep Learning.
