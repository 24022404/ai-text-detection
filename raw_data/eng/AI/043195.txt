Deep learning models applied to 3D point cloud data have demonstrated remarkable performance in various applications. However, their robustness to adversarial attacks, particularly those that exploit geometric vulnerabilities, remains a significant concern. This paper investigates the isometry robustness of deep 3D point cloud models against adversarial perturbations. We propose a novel adversarial attack strategy, Isometry-Aware Perturbation (IAP), that crafts perturbations while preserving the underlying geometric structure of the point cloud. IAP leverages isometric transformations to generate imperceptible yet effective attacks that significantly degrade model performance. Through extensive experiments on ModelNet40 and ScanObjectNN datasets, we demonstrate that IAP consistently outperforms existing attack methods in terms of attack success rate and transferability. Furthermore, we evaluate the effectiveness of several defense mechanisms against IAP and identify vulnerabilities in current defense strategies. Our findings reveal the inherent susceptibility of deep 3D models to isometry-based adversarial attacks, highlighting the need for robust training methods that account for geometric invariance. This work contributes a new perspective on adversarial robustness in 3D point cloud analysis and provides insights for developing more resilient deep learning models.

**Keywords:** 3D Point Clouds, Adversarial Attacks, Isometry Robustness, Deep Learning, Geometric Perturbations, Security.
