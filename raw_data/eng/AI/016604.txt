This paper presents a novel predictive ensemble learning approach for scene text detection, addressing the challenges of accurately identifying and recognizing text in uncontrolled environments. Our objective is to improve the robustness and accuracy of scene text detection systems by combining the strengths of multiple machine learning models. We propose a hybrid approach that integrates convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) to extract features and predict text locations. The ensemble learning method is designed to leverage the complementary strengths of individual models, resulting in improved detection performance. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving a significant increase in precision and recall. The key contributions of this research include the development of a robust ensemble learning framework and the application of this framework to scene text detection, with potential applications in areas such as autonomous vehicles, surveillance, and image analysis. Our approach highlights the effectiveness of predictive ensemble learning in computer vision tasks, particularly in scenarios where individual models may struggle with complex or variable data. Key keywords: scene text detection, ensemble learning, convolutional neural networks, recurrent neural networks, computer vision.
