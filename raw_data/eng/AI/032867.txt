This paper addresses the challenge of leveraging large-scale narrated videos to enhance question-answering (QA) systems. While traditional QA models rely on curated datasets, we propose a novel approach that utilizes millions of narrated videos as a rich, multimodal source of information. Our method involves extracting visual and textual data from these videos to train a multimodal QA model capable of answering questions based on both visual and contextual cues. We introduce a scalable framework that integrates state-of-the-art vision and language models, enabling the system to process and learn from diverse video content efficiently. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing methods, achieving higher accuracy and robustness across multiple QA benchmarks. The model’s ability to generalize across domains and its improved performance on complex, real-world questions highlight its potential for applications in education, entertainment, and automated assistance. This research contributes to the advancement of multimodal learning by showcasing the untapped potential of narrated videos as a resource for training next-generation QA systems.  

**Keywords**: question-answering, narrated videos, multimodal learning, vision-language models, scalable frameworks, AI systems.
