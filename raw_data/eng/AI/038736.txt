This paper addresses the challenge of leveraging multitask learning techniques for clinical time series data, which are characterized by high dimensionality, temporal dependencies, and complex relationships among clinical variables. We propose a comprehensive framework that integrates multiple predictive tasks—such as patient risk stratification, outcome prediction, and treatment recommendation—within a unified deep learning architecture. Our approach exploits shared representations learned from diverse clinical datasets, enhancing model generalizability and robustness compared to single-task methods. To rigorously evaluate the effectiveness of our multitask framework, we introduce a novel benchmarking suite using publicly available electronic health records (EHRs). Performance is assessed across multiple clinically relevant tasks using standard metrics, demonstrating superior predictive accuracy, improved calibration, and increased data efficiency. Notably, our experiments highlight significant gains in rare event prediction and transferability across heterogeneous patient cohorts. The findings suggest that multitask learning offers scalable and flexible solutions for real-world clinical applications, paving the way for more personalized and data-driven healthcare. Keywords: multitask learning, clinical time series, deep learning, benchmarking, electronic health records, predictive modeling, healthcare AI.
