Electronic Medical Records (EMRs) offer valuable longitudinal patient data, yet effectively leveraging this information for clinical decision support remains challenging. Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) have shown promise in modeling temporal dependencies within EMR data, but their inherent complexity hinders interpretability. This paper introduces RetainVis, a novel visual analytics system designed to enhance the interpretability and interactivity of RNNs applied to EMR data. RetainVis integrates a hierarchical attention mechanism, inspired by the RETAIN model, with interactive visualization techniques to allow clinicians to explore the RNN's decision-making process. The system enables users to examine the contribution of individual visits and medical codes to the final prediction, fostering trust and understanding in the model's outputs. We demonstrate the utility of RetainVis through case studies involving heart failure and diabetes prediction, showcasing its ability to reveal clinically relevant patterns and risk factors. Our findings indicate that RetainVis facilitates a deeper understanding of RNN-based predictive models, promoting their adoption in clinical practice and enabling data-driven insights for improved patient care. Keywords: Electronic Medical Records, Recurrent Neural Networks, Interpretability, Visual Analytics, Healthcare, Artificial Intelligence.
