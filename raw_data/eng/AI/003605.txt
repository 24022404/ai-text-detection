This paper aims to develop an innovative approach for mining heterogeneous multivariate time-series data to discover meaningful patterns, with a specific application to home health telecare. Our objective is to design a framework that can effectively handle the complexity and variability of multivariate time-series data, which is commonly encountered in telecare settings. We propose a hybrid approach that combines advanced signal processing techniques with machine learning algorithms to identify patterns and relationships in the data. The methodology involves using dimensionality reduction and feature extraction techniques to preprocess the data, followed by the application of clustering and classification algorithms to identify meaningful patterns. Our results show that the proposed approach can effectively identify patterns and anomalies in the data, leading to improved predictive accuracy and decision-making in home health telecare. The key contributions of this research include the development of a novel framework for mining heterogeneous multivariate time-series data and the demonstration of its effectiveness in a real-world telecare application. The proposed approach has significant implications for the development of personalized and preventive healthcare services, and can be extended to other applications involving multivariate time-series data, such as smart homes and IoT-based healthcare systems. Key keywords: multivariate time-series analysis, telecare, machine learning, pattern recognition, healthcare analytics.
