Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) networks are increasingly vital for disaster relief, but efficient spectrum management remains a significant challenge. This paper introduces a novel autonomous spectrum management scheme designed to optimize communication performance in UAV networks operating in disaster-stricken areas. Our approach utilizes a distributed Q-learning algorithm, enabling UAVs to dynamically adapt their spectrum usage based on real-time interference measurements and network conditions, without requiring centralized control. The proposed scheme considers the unique characteristics of disaster scenarios, including dynamic network topologies and limited infrastructure. Simulation results demonstrate that our autonomous spectrum management scheme significantly improves network throughput, reduces packet loss, and enhances overall communication reliability compared to static spectrum allocation strategies and traditional centralized approaches. Specifically, the proposed method achieves a 30% increase in network throughput under high interference conditions. The findings suggest that autonomous spectrum management offers a robust and scalable solution for ensuring reliable communication in UAV-based disaster relief operations. Keywords: UAV networks, spectrum management, disaster relief, autonomous systems, Q-learning, interference mitigation.
