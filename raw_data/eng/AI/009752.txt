This paper addresses the challenge of forecasting in multivariate time series data where observations are irregularly sampled and contain missing values, a common problem in various domains such as finance, healthcare, and environmental monitoring. We propose a novel deep learning model based on a temporal attention mechanism combined with a recurrent neural network, specifically designed to handle irregular sampling patterns and impute missing values simultaneously. The model learns temporal dependencies from observed data while adaptively weighting the importance of different time points and variables. We evaluate the proposed approach on several real-world datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in forecasting accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods, including traditional imputation techniques and other deep learning models. Furthermore, we analyze the learned attention weights to gain insights into the temporal dynamics of the multivariate time series. The results highlight the effectiveness of the proposed model in capturing complex relationships and handling data irregularities, offering a robust and accurate solution for forecasting in challenging real-world scenarios. Keywords: time series forecasting, irregular sampling, missing values, deep learning, temporal attention, recurrent neural networks.
