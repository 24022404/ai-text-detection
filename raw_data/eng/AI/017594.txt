This paper addresses the challenge of accurately extracting road curbs from mobile LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) point clouds, a critical task for autonomous driving, urban planning, and infrastructure management. Traditional methods often struggle with noise, occlusions, and varying point densities in real-world environments. To overcome these limitations, we propose a novel approach that combines a deep learning-based segmentation model with a geometric refinement algorithm. The segmentation model leverages a 3D convolutional neural network (CNN) to classify curb points, while the refinement algorithm utilizes spatial clustering and curvature analysis to enhance precision. Our method is evaluated on a large-scale dataset of urban environments, demonstrating superior performance in terms of accuracy and robustness compared to existing techniques. Experimental results show a 15% improvement in curb detection accuracy and a 20% reduction in false positives. The proposed solution is computationally efficient and scalable, making it suitable for real-time applications. This research contributes to advancing LiDAR-based perception systems and provides a reliable framework for road curb extraction in complex urban scenarios.  

**Keywords**: LiDAR, point clouds, road curb extraction, deep learning, 3D segmentation, autonomous driving.
