Video action recognition is a fundamental task in computer vision with numerous applications. However, accurately identifying actions in videos remains challenging due to factors such as complex backgrounds, camera motion, and variations in action execution. This paper introduces Temporal Saliency Integration (TSI), a novel approach that leverages temporal saliency to enhance video action recognition performance. TSI integrates frame-level saliency maps across time, emphasizing important regions and suppressing irrelevant background clutter. We propose a recurrent neural network-based architecture to effectively model temporal dynamics and integrate saliency information. The model learns to focus on salient regions over time, improving feature representation for action classification. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including UCF101 and HMDB51, demonstrate that TSI achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods. The results also confirm that the proposed method is robust to noisy environments. The TSI method provides a robust and efficient approach to video action recognition and can be easily integrated into existing deep learning frameworks. Keywords: video action recognition, temporal saliency, recurrent neural network, deep learning, computer vision.
