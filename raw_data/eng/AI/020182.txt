This paper introduces DatasetGAN, a novel approach to generating high-quality labeled datasets with minimal human effort. The objective is to address the longstanding challenge of obtaining large amounts of labeled data, a crucial component in training accurate machine learning models. DatasetGAN leverages the power of generative adversarial networks (GANs) to produce synthetic data that mimics real-world distributions, thereby reducing the need for manual labeling. Our approach utilizes a unique combination of data augmentation techniques and adversarial training to create diverse and realistic datasets. Experimental results demonstrate that DatasetGAN can generate datasets of comparable quality to those created through extensive human labeling, while significantly reducing the required human effort. The key findings show that DatasetGAN achieves state-of-the-art performance in various machine learning tasks, including image classification and object detection. The implications of this research are substantial, as DatasetGAN has the potential to democratize access to high-quality training data, enabling the development of more accurate and reliable AI models. Key contributions include the introduction of a novel GAN-based architecture, the development of an efficient data generation pipeline, and the demonstration of DatasetGAN's effectiveness in real-world applications. Relevant keywords: GANs, synthetic data generation, data augmentation, machine learning, labeled datasets, minimal human effort.
