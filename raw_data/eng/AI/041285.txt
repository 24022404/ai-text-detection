Video super-resolution (VSR) aims to enhance the resolution of low-quality videos, a critical task in applications such as video streaming, surveillance, and medical imaging. However, existing VSR methods often overlook the impact of compression artifacts, which degrade video quality and hinder reconstruction performance. This paper introduces **COMISR**, a novel **Compression-Informed Video Super-Resolution** framework designed to address this challenge. COMISR integrates a compression-aware module into the VSR pipeline, enabling the model to explicitly account for and mitigate compression artifacts during the reconstruction process. Leveraging a hybrid deep learning architecture, the framework combines convolutional neural networks (CNNs) with attention mechanisms to enhance spatial and temporal feature extraction. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that COMISR outperforms state-of-the-art VSR methods in terms of both quantitative metrics (e.g., PSNR and SSIM) and visual quality. The proposed approach achieves significant improvements in artifact reduction and detail preservation, particularly for highly compressed videos. This work highlights the importance of compression-aware modeling in VSR and provides a robust solution for real-world video enhancement tasks. Keywords: video super-resolution, compression artifacts, deep learning, attention mechanisms, video enhancement.
