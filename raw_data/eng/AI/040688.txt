Place recognition in large-scale environments is a critical challenge for applications such as autonomous navigation and robotics. Traditional methods often rely on 2D image-based approaches, which struggle with viewpoint variations and environmental changes. This paper introduces **PointNetVLAD**, a novel deep learning framework for large-scale place recognition using 3D point clouds. The proposed method combines the strengths of PointNet, a neural network for processing point cloud data, with NetVLAD, a feature aggregation technique, to create a robust and efficient retrieval system. PointNetVLAD extracts global descriptors directly from raw point clouds, enabling accurate and scalable place recognition across diverse environments. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that PointNetVLAD outperforms existing methods in terms of retrieval accuracy and robustness to viewpoint changes. The framework also shows significant improvements in computational efficiency, making it suitable for real-time applications. This research contributes to the advancement of 3D-based place recognition by providing a scalable and effective solution for large-scale environments, with potential applications in autonomous systems, augmented reality, and geospatial mapping.  

**Keywords**: PointNet, NetVLAD, 3D point clouds, place recognition, deep learning, large-scale retrieval.
