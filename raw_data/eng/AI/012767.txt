This paper introduces a novel model-based active exploration (MBAE) framework designed to enhance the efficiency and robustness of reinforcement learning in complex environments. The objective is to address the limitations of traditional exploration strategies by leveraging a learned environment model to predict and select informative actions. Our approach integrates a deep neural network to represent the environment dynamics, enabling the agent to simulate potential outcomes of different actions. We introduce an information gain-based acquisition function to quantify the expected reduction in model uncertainty resulting from each action. This allows the agent to actively explore regions of the state space where the model is most uncertain. Experimental results across several benchmark environments demonstrate that MBAE significantly outperforms existing exploration methods, including random exploration and intrinsic motivation-based approaches, in terms of sample efficiency and final performance. Furthermore, MBAE exhibits improved robustness to model inaccuracies compared to purely model-predictive control. These findings suggest that MBAE offers a promising direction for developing more effective and reliable reinforcement learning agents. Keywords: Active Exploration, Reinforcement Learning, Model-Based Learning, Exploration-Exploitation, Deep Learning, Information Gain.
