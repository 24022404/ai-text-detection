A helpdesk system for customer support was developed, utilizing multi-criteria analysis and real-time visual reporting tools, such as column and pie charts, to aggregate and display requests based on key criteria like completion status and processing time. By incorporating these features, the system gives managers a clear overview of request volumes and processing statuses, enabling them to allocate staff resources more efficiently and identify bottlenecks in the workflow. Furthermore, the system supplies businesses with valuable statistical data, empowering them to make informed decisions aimed at improving overall service quality. The thesis documents the successful design and implementation of this flexible, customizable system using the open-source OdooErp platform. As a result, ANSV was able to significantly enhance the management and tracking of customer requests, achieving greater transparency and operational control while leveraging open-source technology.
