This paper introduces a novel dataset-level geometric framework designed to enhance the performance and robustness of ensemble classifiers. The objective is to improve the accuracy and reliability of classification tasks by leveraging geometric relationships between datasets. Our approach employs a unique combination of geometric transformations and manifold learning techniques to create a robust ensemble framework. The methodology involves representing each dataset as a geometric object, allowing for the identification of intrinsic patterns and relationships that can inform the ensemble classification process. Experimental results demonstrate that our framework outperforms traditional ensemble methods, particularly in scenarios with diverse or noisy datasets. Key findings include significant improvements in classification accuracy and reduced sensitivity to hyperparameter tuning. The proposed framework contributes to the field of machine learning by providing a innovative, geometry-driven approach to ensemble classification, with potential applications in areas such as data integration, anomaly detection, and multi-modal learning. Relevant keywords include ensemble learning, geometric deep learning, manifold learning, and classification algorithms.
