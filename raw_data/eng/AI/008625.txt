This paper explores the application of multimodal image captioning for enhanced marketing analysis. The objective is to automatically generate descriptive captions from product images, incorporating both visual and textual data to gain deeper insights into consumer perception and market trends. We propose a novel deep learning architecture that integrates convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for image feature extraction with recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and attention mechanisms for caption generation. The model is trained on a large dataset of product images and associated marketing text, enabling it to produce captions that accurately reflect the visual content and relevant marketing context. Experimental results demonstrate that our model outperforms existing image captioning techniques in terms of caption quality, relevance, and informativeness, as measured by BLEU, ROUGE, and CIDEr scores. Furthermore, we show how the generated captions can be used to extract key marketing attributes, perform sentiment analysis, and identify emerging trends. This research contributes a valuable tool for marketers to efficiently analyze visual content, understand consumer preferences, and optimize marketing strategies. Keywords: Multimodal Image Captioning, Marketing Analysis, Deep Learning, Convolutional Neural Networks, Recurrent Neural Networks, Attention Mechanisms, Sentiment Analysis.
