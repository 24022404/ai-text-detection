This paper addresses the challenge of robust decision-making in Markov Decision Processes (MDPs) by introducing tight Bayesian ambiguity sets. The objective is to develop a framework that can effectively handle model uncertainty, leading to more reliable and robust policies. Our approach utilizes Bayesian inference to construct ambiguity sets, which are then used to derive robust policies. The key innovation lies in the formulation of tight ambiguity sets, ensuring that the resulting policies are both robust and efficient. Our method outperforms existing approaches in terms of computational efficiency and solution quality. The results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in various MDP scenarios, showcasing improved robustness and adaptability. This research contributes to the development of more reliable decision-making systems, with potential applications in areas such as autonomous systems, robotics, and healthcare. Key keywords: Robust MDPs, Bayesian Ambiguity Sets, Decision-making under Uncertainty, Markov Decision Processes, Robust Optimization.
