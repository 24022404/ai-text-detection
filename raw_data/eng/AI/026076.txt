This paper explores a novel approach to breast ultrasound diagnosis using a Multi-AI Combination (MAC) framework, aiming to enhance diagnostic reliability and accuracy. The study addresses the limitations of individual AI models in interpreting complex ultrasound images by integrating the complementary strengths of multiple AI algorithms. Our method involves training and deploying several distinct AI models, including convolutional neural networks and transfer learning models, each optimized for specific image features relevant to breast lesion detection and characterization. The MAC framework then aggregates the predictions from these individual models using a weighted voting mechanism refined through a genetic algorithm. Experimental results on a large dataset of breast ultrasound images demonstrate that the MAC framework significantly improves diagnostic performance, achieving higher sensitivity and specificity compared to any single AI model. Specifically, the MAC approach reduced false negatives by 15% and increased overall accuracy by 8%. This research contributes a more reliable and robust AI solution for breast ultrasound diagnosis, potentially leading to earlier and more accurate detection of breast cancer. Keywords: Breast Ultrasound, Artificial Intelligence, Medical Imaging, Multi-AI Combination, Deep Learning, Diagnosis.
