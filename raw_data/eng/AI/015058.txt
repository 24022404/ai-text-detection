Model-based reinforcement learning (MBRL) has gained significant attention for its ability to leverage learned dynamics models to improve sample efficiency. However, a critical challenge in MBRL is the propagation of uncertainty from the learned model to the policy optimization process, which can lead to suboptimal or unstable performance. This paper introduces an uncertainty-aware model-based policy optimization framework that explicitly addresses this challenge. Our approach integrates probabilistic dynamics models with a novel uncertainty quantification mechanism, enabling the policy to account for model inaccuracies during training. We propose a robust optimization algorithm that balances exploration and exploitation by dynamically adjusting the policy's reliance on the learned model based on its confidence estimates. Experimental results on benchmark control tasks demonstrate that our method achieves superior performance compared to existing MBRL approaches, particularly in environments with high stochasticity or partial observability. The proposed framework not only enhances sample efficiency but also improves the robustness and reliability of learned policies, making it a promising solution for real-world applications where uncertainty is inherent. Key contributions include the integration of uncertainty quantification into policy optimization and a scalable algorithm that generalizes across diverse environments. Keywords: model-based reinforcement learning, uncertainty quantification, policy optimization, probabilistic models, sample efficiency.
