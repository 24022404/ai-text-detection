Active semantic segmentation aims to minimize annotation costs by strategically selecting the most informative regions for labeling. This paper introduces Attend and Segment (A&S), a novel attention-guided approach that enhances active learning in semantic segmentation. A&S leverages a spatial attention mechanism to identify uncertain regions within an image, focusing annotation efforts on areas that contribute most significantly to overall segmentation accuracy. Our method integrates an attention module into a deep learning framework, enabling the model to dynamically weight the importance of different image regions during both training and active sample selection. We demonstrate that A&S outperforms existing active learning strategies by achieving superior segmentation accuracy with fewer labeled samples. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including Cityscapes and Pascal VOC, show significant improvements in performance, particularly in scenarios with limited annotation budgets. The proposed approach offers a practical solution for reducing annotation costs while maintaining high segmentation quality, facilitating the application of semantic segmentation in resource-constrained environments. Keywords: active learning, semantic segmentation, attention mechanism, deep learning, annotation efficiency.
