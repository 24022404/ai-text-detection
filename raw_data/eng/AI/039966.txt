This paper explores the application of transfer learning with deep Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) for gender recognition and age estimation from facial images. The objective is to develop an efficient and accurate system that can leverage pre-trained models to reduce the need for large datasets and computational resources. A transfer learning approach is employed, utilizing pre-trained CNN models such as VGGFace and ResNet50, fine-tuned on a smaller dataset of facial images. The results show significant improvements in gender recognition and age estimation accuracy, outperforming traditional machine learning methods. The proposed system achieves a gender recognition accuracy of 97% and a mean absolute error of 3.5 years for age estimation. The contributions of this research include the demonstration of the effectiveness of transfer learning for facial analysis tasks and the development of a robust and efficient system for gender recognition and age estimation. Key keywords: transfer learning, deep CNNs, gender recognition, age estimation, facial analysis, VGGFace, ResNet50, fine-tuning.
