This paper addresses the challenge of efficiently computing and backpropagating gradients under angle-parametrized orthogonal matrices, a critical component in various machine learning and optimization tasks. While orthogonal matrices are widely used for their stability and interpretability, their computational complexity and gradient propagation often hinder scalability, especially in large-scale applications. To overcome this, we propose a novel parallelized framework that leverages angle-based parametrization to decompose orthogonal matrices into a sequence of elementary rotations. Our approach enables efficient computation of matrix operations and gradients by exploiting parallelism across multiple processing units. We introduce a specialized backpropagation algorithm tailored for angle-parametrized matrices, which significantly reduces memory overhead and computational time compared to traditional methods. Experimental results demonstrate that our framework achieves up to 40% faster training times on benchmark datasets while maintaining numerical stability and accuracy. Furthermore, we showcase its applicability in deep learning architectures, such as recurrent neural networks and generative models, where orthogonal constraints are essential. This work contributes a scalable and practical solution for integrating orthogonal matrices into modern machine learning pipelines, paving the way for broader adoption in resource-intensive applications.  

**Keywords**: orthogonal matrices, angle parametrization, parallel computation, backpropagation, machine learning, optimization.
