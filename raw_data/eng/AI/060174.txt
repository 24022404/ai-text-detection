Tracking pedestrian heads in dense crowds is a challenging task due to occlusion, variations in appearance, and complex movements. This paper proposes a novel approach to address these challenges by combining deep learning-based head detection with a robust tracking framework. Our method utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) optimized for head detection in crowded scenes, achieving high accuracy and recall. We then employ a Kalman filter-based tracker, enhanced with a data association technique based on appearance and motion cues, to maintain identity consistency across frames. To further mitigate the impact of occlusions, we introduce a novel occlusion handling mechanism that predicts head locations based on historical trajectories and scene context. Experimental results on publicly available datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of tracking accuracy, identity switches, and robustness to occlusions. The proposed method offers significant improvements for applications such as crowd behavior analysis, surveillance, and autonomous navigation.

Keywords: Pedestrian Tracking, Head Detection, Crowd Analysis, Deep Learning, Kalman Filter, Occlusion Handling.
