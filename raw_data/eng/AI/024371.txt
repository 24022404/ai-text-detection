This paper addresses the challenge of designing robust control systems by introducing a novel approach that combines alternating optimisation and quadrature techniques. The objective is to develop a framework that can efficiently handle uncertainty and non-linearity in complex systems, leading to improved stability and performance. Our method employs an alternating optimisation algorithm to solve a sequence of optimisation problems, while incorporating quadrature rules to approximate the system's behaviour under uncertainty. The results show that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of robustness and computational efficiency, with key findings including significant reductions in control effort and improved disturbance rejection. The conclusion highlights the importance of this research in enabling the design of robust control systems for a wide range of applications, including process control, robotics, and autonomous systems. The novelty of our approach lies in its ability to balance exploration and exploitation in the optimisation process, making it a valuable contribution to the field of control systems. Key keywords: robust control, alternating optimisation, quadrature, uncertainty, non-linearity, control systems.
