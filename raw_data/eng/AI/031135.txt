This paper addresses the challenge of urban traffic imputation by proposing a novel spatiotemporal tensor completion approach. The objective is to accurately estimate missing traffic data in urban networks, which is crucial for intelligent transportation systems and traffic management. Our method leverages a tensor-based framework to model the complex spatiotemporal relationships inherent in traffic data. By integrating spatial and temporal information, our approach captures the dynamic patterns and correlations in traffic flow, enabling more accurate imputations. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, outperforming existing imputation techniques in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The key findings highlight the importance of considering both spatial and temporal dependencies in traffic data, leading to improved imputation performance. This research contributes to the development of more reliable and efficient urban traffic management systems, with potential applications in smart cities and transportation planning. Key keywords: spatiotemporal tensor completion, urban traffic imputation, intelligent transportation systems, traffic management, smart cities.
