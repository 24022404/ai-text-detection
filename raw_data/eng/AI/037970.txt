This paper introduces RL-Duet, a novel online music accompaniment generation system that leverages deep reinforcement learning to create harmonious and adaptive musical pieces. The objective is to develop an intelligent music generation model that can accompany a melody in real-time, enhancing the overall musical experience. Our approach employs a reinforcement learning framework, where an agent learns to generate accompaniments based on rewards received from a music theory-based evaluation module. The system utilizes a combination of convolutional neural networks and recurrent neural networks to process musical inputs and generate outputs. Experimental results demonstrate that RL-Duet outperforms existing music generation models in terms of musicality, coherence, and adaptability. The system's ability to generate high-quality accompaniments in real-time has significant implications for music creation, education, and entertainment. Key contributions include the application of deep reinforcement learning to music generation, the development of a music theory-based evaluation module, and the creation of a scalable and adaptive online music accompaniment system. Relevant keywords: music generation, deep reinforcement learning, online accompaniment, convolutional neural networks, recurrent neural networks, music theory.
