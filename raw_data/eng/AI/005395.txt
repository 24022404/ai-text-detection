This paper explores the instance-based interpretability of Variational Auto-Encoders (VAEs), a deep learning model widely used for dimensionality reduction and generative tasks. The objective is to provide insights into the decision-making process of VAEs, enabling a better understanding of their strengths and limitations. We propose a novel approach based on feature importance and latent space analysis to interpret VAEs at the instance level. Our method utilizes techniques from explainable AI to identify the most relevant input features contributing to the encoding and decoding processes. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in providing instance-based interpretations, allowing for a more nuanced understanding of VAEs' behavior. The key findings highlight the importance of considering individual data points when evaluating VAE performance, rather than relying solely on aggregate metrics. This research contributes to the development of more transparent and trustworthy VAE models, with potential applications in areas such as computer vision, natural language processing, and recommender systems. Key keywords: Variational Auto-Encoders, instance-based interpretability, explainable AI, deep learning, feature importance, latent space analysis.
