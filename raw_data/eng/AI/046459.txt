This paper presents a novel point-based neural rendering framework that incorporates per-view optimization to enhance the quality and realism of 3D scene reconstructions. Traditional neural rendering approaches often struggle with artifacts and inconsistencies under diverse viewing conditions due to limited adaptation to view-dependent effects. We address this challenge by proposing a method that represents the target scene as a set of 3D points, each enriched with neural features, and applies per-view neural parameter optimization during rendering. Our approach leverages a differentiable renderer and view-specific feature updates to allow the network to adapt dynamically to each input image, greatly improving the handling of complex lighting and occlusions. Experimental results on multiple benchmarks demonstrate that our method achieves superior image fidelity, sharper details, and more consistent color reproduction compared to existing point cloud and neural rendering techniques. The proposed per-view optimization strategy significantly reduces common rendering artifacts and enhances the potential of point-based representations for applications in immersive virtual reality, digital content creation, and real-time 3D visualization. Keywords: neural rendering, point cloud, per-view optimization, 3D reconstruction, differentiable rendering, view-dependent effects.
