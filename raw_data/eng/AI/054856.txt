Tracking pedestrian heads in dense crowds is a critical challenge in computer vision, with applications in surveillance, crowd management, and autonomous systems. This paper addresses the limitations of existing methods in accurately detecting and tracking heads in highly congested environments, where occlusion and overlapping are prevalent. We propose a novel deep learning-based framework that integrates a multi-scale feature extraction network with a robust tracking algorithm to enhance head detection and trajectory estimation. Our approach leverages spatial-temporal information and employs a lightweight attention mechanism to prioritize head regions, improving accuracy in cluttered scenes. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves a significant improvement in tracking precision and recall compared to state-of-the-art techniques, particularly in scenarios with extreme crowd density. The proposed system also exhibits real-time performance, making it suitable for practical deployment. This research contributes to advancing pedestrian tracking technologies by offering a scalable and efficient solution for dense crowd analysis, with potential applications in public safety and urban planning.  

**Keywords**: Pedestrian tracking, dense crowds, head detection, deep learning, computer vision, attention mechanism.
