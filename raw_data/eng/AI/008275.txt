This paper proposes RON, a novel object detection framework that leverages Reverse Connection with Objectness Prior Networks. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of object detection by incorporating objectness priors into the detection process. Our approach utilizes a reverse connection mechanism to refine feature representations and an objectness prior network to guide the detection of objects. Experimental results demonstrate that RON outperforms state-of-the-art object detection models, achieving significant improvements in detection accuracy and speed. The key findings include the effectiveness of objectness priors in reducing false positives and improving detection recall. The proposed RON framework contributes to the field of computer vision by providing a robust and efficient object detection solution, with potential applications in autonomous driving, surveillance, and robotics. By highlighting the importance of objectness priors and reverse connections, this research paves the way for future innovations in object detection, with relevant keywords including object detection, deep learning, computer vision, and objectness prior networks.
