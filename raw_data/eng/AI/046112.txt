Human activity recognition (HAR) in RGB videos is a challenging task due to variations in viewpoint, illumination, and background clutter. This paper presents a novel skeleton-focused approach for HAR, leveraging 3D human pose estimation to improve robustness and accuracy. Our method first extracts 3D skeleton data from RGB video frames using a state-of-the-art pose estimation network. Then, we propose a temporal graph convolutional network (TGCN) that operates directly on the skeleton sequences. This TGCN is specifically designed to capture the spatio-temporal dependencies between joints, effectively modeling human actions. We introduce a skeleton-aware attention mechanism to emphasize informative joints and suppress noise. Experimental results on the NTU RGB+D and Kinetics datasets demonstrate that our proposed method achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing RGB-based and skeleton-based HAR techniques. The skeleton-focused approach is shown to be more resilient to visual variations, leading to improved generalization and accuracy in complex scenarios. Our work highlights the effectiveness of combining 3D pose estimation with deep learning for robust and accurate human activity recognition.

Keywords: Human Activity Recognition, RGB Video, 3D Pose Estimation, Temporal Graph Convolutional Network, Skeleton Data, Deep Learning.
