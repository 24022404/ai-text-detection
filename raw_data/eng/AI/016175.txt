This paper presents an innovative approach to unsupervised learning, leveraging modular sparse distributed codes and novelty-contingent noise to achieve efficient similarity-preserving representations. The objective is to develop a robust and scalable method for discovering meaningful patterns in complex datasets without relying on labeled data. Our approach employs a modular architecture that combines sparse coding with distributed representations, allowing for flexible and adaptive learning. The introduction of novelty-contingent noise enables the model to capture subtle variations in the data and adapt to changing distributions. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing unsupervised learning techniques in terms of representation quality and computational efficiency. The proposed approach has significant implications for applications such as image and speech recognition, natural language processing, and recommender systems. Key contributions include the development of a novel modular sparse coding framework, the introduction of novelty-contingent noise for improved representation learning, and the demonstration of state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets. Relevant keywords: unsupervised learning, sparse coding, distributed representations, novelty-contingent noise, similarity-preserving representations, modular architectures, deep learning, artificial intelligence.
