This paper introduces a novel approach to predictive modeling of multidimensional data using Tensor-Train (TT) networks. Addressing the challenges of high dimensionality and computational complexity often encountered in traditional methods, we propose a TT-network architecture specifically designed for efficient representation and learning from high-order tensor data. The method leverages the compact parameterization offered by the TT format to mitigate the curse of dimensionality, enabling accurate predictive modeling with significantly reduced computational cost. Experimental results on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that the proposed TT-network outperforms existing tensor decomposition and machine learning techniques in terms of prediction accuracy and computational efficiency. Furthermore, we investigate the impact of TT-rank selection on model performance, providing insights into optimal network configuration. The findings suggest that TT-networks offer a promising framework for predictive modeling in various domains characterized by multidimensional data, such as image processing, video analysis, and spatiotemporal forecasting. Key words: Tensor-Train, Predictive Modeling, Multidimensional Data, Tensor Decomposition, Machine Learning, High-Dimensional Data.
