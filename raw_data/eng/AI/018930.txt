This study aims to develop an automatic detection system for cardiac chambers in fetal echocardiography using an attention-based YOLOv4 framework. The objective is to accurately identify and localize the four cardiac chambers from the four-chamber view of fetal echocardiography images. Our approach employs a modified YOLOv4 model integrated with an attention mechanism to enhance the detection accuracy of small and variable-sized cardiac chambers. The results show that our proposed framework achieves a high detection accuracy and outperforms traditional object detection methods. The system's performance is evaluated using metrics such as precision, recall, and mean average precision. Our findings indicate that the attention-based YOLOv4 framework can effectively detect cardiac chambers in fetal echocardiography images, which can assist clinicians in diagnosing congenital heart defects. The contributions of this research include the development of a novel attention-based YOLOv4 framework for cardiac chamber detection and its potential application in fetal echocardiography. Key keywords: fetal echocardiography, cardiac chamber detection, attention-based YOLOv4, object detection, congenital heart defects, medical imaging.
