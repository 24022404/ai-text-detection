This paper introduces BEV-MODNet, a novel approach for moving object detection (MOD) in Bird's Eye View (BEV) using only monocular camera input, addressing a critical challenge in autonomous driving. Our method leverages a unique network architecture that combines feature extraction, BEV transformation, and temporal modeling to accurately detect and track moving objects. Specifically, we employ a spatial transformer network to project image features into the BEV space, followed by a recurrent neural network to capture temporal dependencies and improve detection consistency. BEV-MODNet achieves state-of-the-art performance on the nuScenes dataset, demonstrating significant improvements in MOD accuracy compared to existing monocular vision-based approaches. The proposed method offers a cost-effective and robust solution for autonomous vehicles by providing reliable perception of moving agents in complex traffic scenarios. Keywords: Bird's Eye View, Moving Object Detection, Monocular Camera, Autonomous Driving, Deep Learning, Temporal Modeling.
