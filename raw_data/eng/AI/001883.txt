This paper proposes a unified framework for spectral clustering in sparse graphs, addressing the challenge of clustering nodes in graphs with limited connectivity. Our objective is to develop a robust and efficient method that can handle sparse graphs with varying densities and structures. We introduce a novel approach that combines spectral decomposition with a sparse graph representation, leveraging the strengths of both techniques to improve clustering accuracy. Our method utilizes a sparse eigenvalue decomposition to reduce the dimensionality of the graph, followed by a clustering algorithm that incorporates local and global graph structures. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our framework, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in clustering quality and computational efficiency. The proposed framework has significant implications for applications in network analysis, data mining, and machine learning, particularly in scenarios where graph sparsity is a major concern. Key contributions include a unified framework for spectral clustering, improved clustering accuracy, and enhanced computational efficiency, making it a valuable tool for researchers and practitioners in the field of graph-based data analysis, spectral clustering, and sparse graph modeling.
