Spectral methods are powerful tools for analyzing and learning from high-dimensional data represented as matrices and tensors. This paper investigates novel spectral learning algorithms for tensor decomposition and matrix completion, addressing challenges associated with noisy data and computational complexity. We introduce a regularized spectral tensor decomposition method based on tensor power iterations, providing robustness to noise and improved convergence guarantees compared to existing approaches. Furthermore, we propose a scalable spectral matrix completion algorithm leveraging randomized sketching techniques to reduce computational costs. Our theoretical analysis establishes error bounds for both algorithms under various noise models. Empirical evaluations on synthetic and real-world datasets, including image and recommendation systems, demonstrate the superior performance of our algorithms in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The results highlight the potential of regularized and randomized spectral methods for large-scale matrix and tensor learning tasks. Keywords: Spectral Learning, Tensor Decomposition, Matrix Completion, Regularization, Randomized Algorithms, Machine Learning.
