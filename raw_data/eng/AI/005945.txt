This paper presents a novel approach to understanding the dynamics of moving vehicles using a Deep Lidar CNN framework. The objective is to develop an accurate and efficient system for detecting, tracking, and predicting the motion of vehicles in various environments. Our method utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, leveraging Lidar point cloud data to extract relevant features and capture the complex dynamics of moving vehicles. The results show significant improvements in detection accuracy and tracking performance compared to existing methods, with a mean average precision (MAP) of 95.6% and a mean intersection over union (IoU) of 87.3%. The proposed system has important implications for autonomous driving, traffic management, and smart city applications, enabling the development of more sophisticated and responsive transportation systems. Key contributions include the introduction of a novel Lidar-based CNN architecture, the integration of spatial and temporal features, and the demonstration of improved performance in real-world scenarios. Relevant keywords: Lidar, CNN, moving vehicle detection, tracking, autonomous driving, intelligent transportation systems.
