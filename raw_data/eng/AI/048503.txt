This paper proposes a novel self-learning approach with a rectification strategy for human parsing, aiming to improve the accuracy and efficiency of parsing models. The objective is to develop a framework that can learn from its own mistakes and adapt to complex human parsing scenarios. Our method employs a self-supervised learning mechanism, leveraging a rectification strategy to refine the parsing results. The approach utilizes a deep neural network architecture, incorporating a feedback loop to identify and correct errors. Experimental results demonstrate that our self-learning framework with rectification strategy achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets, outperforming existing human parsing models. The key findings indicate that the proposed method can effectively reduce parsing errors and improve the overall accuracy. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and robust human parsing systems, with potential applications in computer vision, robotics, and human-computer interaction. Key keywords: human parsing, self-learning, rectification strategy, deep learning, computer vision.
