This paper presents a novel approach to dense 3D object reconstruction from a single depth view, addressing the long-standing challenge of accurately capturing complex object geometries with limited input data. Our method leverages a deep learning-based framework, combining the strengths of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and graph convolutional networks (GCNs) to predict detailed 3D models from sparse depth information. The proposed approach achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of reconstruction accuracy and efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our technique in reconstructing a wide range of objects, from simple shapes to complex scenes. The key contributions of this research include a new depth-to-mesh architecture, a robust normal estimation module, and a mesh refinement strategy. Our work has significant implications for various applications, including robotics, computer vision, and 3D printing, where accurate 3D reconstruction is crucial. Key keywords: 3D reconstruction, depth sensing, deep learning, CNNs, GCNs, mesh generation.
