The robustness of deep learning models for 3D point cloud processing has become a critical concern, particularly in applications requiring high precision, such as autonomous driving and robotics. This paper investigates the isometry robustness of deep 3D point cloud models under adversarial attacks, focusing on their ability to maintain performance when subjected to geometric transformations and perturbations. We propose a novel evaluation framework to systematically assess the vulnerability of state-of-the-art point cloud models, including PointNet, PointNet++, and DGCNN, under various adversarial scenarios. Our approach incorporates targeted and untargeted attacks, leveraging gradient-based optimization techniques to generate adversarial examples that exploit model weaknesses. Experimental results demonstrate that existing models exhibit significant sensitivity to isometric transformations and adversarial perturbations, leading to substantial performance degradation. Furthermore, we introduce a defense mechanism based on adversarial training and data augmentation, which significantly enhances model robustness without compromising accuracy. The findings highlight the importance of developing isometry-aware architectures and training strategies to improve the reliability of 3D point cloud models in real-world applications. This work contributes to advancing the understanding of adversarial robustness in 3D deep learning and provides practical insights for building more secure and resilient systems.  

**Keywords**: 3D point clouds, adversarial attacks, isometry robustness, deep learning, adversarial defense, PointNet, DGCNN.
