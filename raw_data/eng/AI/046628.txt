Gaussian Processes (GPs) are powerful tools for probabilistic modeling, but their application to real-world, nonstationary data remains challenging due to computational complexity and the difficulty of learning flexible covariance functions. This paper introduces a novel approach for efficiently learning nonstationary GPs by leveraging localized spectral mixture kernels and a scalable variational inference framework. Our method decomposes the nonstationary kernel into a mixture of stationary kernels, each adapted to a specific region of the input space, enabling efficient computation and adaptation to changing data characteristics. We further enhance scalability through a variational approach that approximates the posterior distribution over GP hyperparameters. Experimental results on several real-world datasets, including time-series forecasting and spatial modeling tasks, demonstrate that our method achieves comparable or superior predictive performance compared to state-of-the-art nonstationary GP models, while significantly reducing computational costs. This efficient and accurate nonstationary GP learning framework facilitates the deployment of GPs in a wider range of real-world applications with dynamic and complex data. Keywords: Gaussian Processes, Nonstationary Kernels, Variational Inference, Spectral Mixture, Machine Learning, Scalable Inference.
