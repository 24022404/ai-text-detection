This paper addresses the challenge of reconstructing accurate 3D face shapes from diverse and heterogeneous data sources, such as 2D images, videos, and depth maps. The objective is to develop a robust framework capable of learning facial geometry from varied inputs, overcoming limitations in existing methods that rely on single-source data. We propose a novel deep learning-based approach that integrates multi-modal data through a unified architecture, leveraging advancements in convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and generative adversarial networks (GANs). Our method employs a self-supervised training strategy to enhance generalization across datasets with varying quality and resolution. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves state-of-the-art performance in 3D face reconstruction, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The system also shows improved adaptability to real-world scenarios, such as low-resolution images or incomplete depth data. This research contributes to the field of computer vision by providing a versatile solution for 3D face modeling, with potential applications in facial recognition, animation, and virtual reality. Key innovations include the integration of diverse data sources and the development of a self-supervised learning pipeline, which reduces dependency on large annotated datasets.  

**Keywords**: 3D face reconstruction, deep learning, multi-modal data, self-supervised learning, computer vision, facial geometry.
