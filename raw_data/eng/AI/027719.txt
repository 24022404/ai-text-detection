Forecasting in multivariate irregularly sampled time series with missing values presents significant challenges due to the complexity of handling incomplete and non-uniform data. This paper addresses the problem by proposing a novel deep learning-based framework designed to effectively model and predict such time series. The proposed approach leverages a combination of recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and attention mechanisms to capture temporal dependencies and handle irregular sampling intervals. Additionally, a specialized imputation module is integrated to address missing values, ensuring robust feature representation. Experiments conducted on real-world datasets demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms existing state-of-the-art techniques in terms of prediction accuracy and computational efficiency. Key findings reveal that the framework is particularly effective in scenarios with high missing data rates and irregular sampling patterns. The results highlight the potential of this approach for applications in healthcare, finance, and environmental monitoring, where such data irregularities are common. This research contributes to advancing time series forecasting by providing a scalable and adaptive solution for complex, real-world datasets.  

**Keywords**: time series forecasting, irregular sampling, missing data, deep learning, attention mechanisms, recurrent neural networks.
