This paper proposes a novel Region Adaptive Graph Fourier Transform (RAGFT) for 3D point clouds, addressing the challenge of efficiently processing and analyzing complex geometric data. The objective is to develop a framework that adapts to local variations in point cloud density and geometry, enabling more accurate and robust signal processing. Our approach combines graph signal processing techniques with a region-adaptive strategy, allowing for the decomposition of point cloud data into frequency components that capture both local and global structural information. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of RAGFT in various applications, including point cloud filtering, feature extraction, and classification, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The proposed method has significant implications for numerous fields, including computer vision, robotics, and 3D reconstruction, and contributes to the development of more sophisticated and adaptive signal processing techniques for 3D point cloud data. Key contributions include the introduction of a region-adaptive graph construction method and the application of graph Fourier transform to 3D point clouds, enabling more effective and efficient processing of complex geometric data. Relevant keywords: 3D point clouds, graph signal processing, region-adaptive, graph Fourier transform, computer vision, 3D reconstruction.
