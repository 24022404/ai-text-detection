The increasing demand for efficient agricultural practices necessitates advanced technologies for precise crop and weed detection. This study presents an innovative approach to automatically generate model-based datasets to enhance the speed and accuracy of identifying crops and weeds in agricultural fields.

Methods/Approach: Our approach utilizes a cutting-edge model designed to generate realistic synthetic datasets that mimic various agricultural scenarios. By leveraging advanced computer vision techniques and machine learning algorithms, the model can simulate diverse environmental conditions and plant growth stages. The method involves automatic annotation and labeling, facilitating the training of detection models without the need for extensive manual data collection.

Results/Findings: The dataset generation model significantly outperformed traditional data collection methods in terms of time efficiency and resource utilization. Experimental results on benchmark datasets indicated an improvement in detection accuracy by 20%, alongside a reduction in processing time by 35%. These results demonstrate the reliability and effectiveness of the proposed method in real-world applications.

Conclusion/Implications: This research introduces a novel automated solution for dataset generation, providing a valuable tool for the agricultural sector to achieve faster and more accurate crop and weeds detection. The implications of this work are far-reaching, with potential applications in precision agriculture, automated farming, and sustainable land management. By improving detection accuracy and reducing the time required for dataset preparation, this study contributes to the development of smarter agricultural systems.

Keywords: automatic dataset generation, crop detection, weed detection, machine learning, computer vision, precision agriculture.
