This paper presents a novel framework for real-time topology optimization in three-dimensional (3D) design spaces utilizing deep transfer learning techniques. The primary objective is to significantly accelerate the topology optimization process, which is traditionally computationally intensive and time-consuming, by leveraging the knowledge learned from prior optimization tasks. Our approach employs a deep neural network model pre-trained on a diverse set of 3D geometry and structural datasets, enabling it to rapidly predict optimal material distributions for unseen problem instances with minimal computational overhead. We further integrate transfer learning to adapt the model to new domains or design constraints with limited additional training, ensuring flexibility and generalization across various engineering applications. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves comparable or improved performance in structural efficiency and material usage relative to traditional iterative solvers, while reducing computation time by orders of magnitude. The implications of our research extend to interactive design, real-time simulation, and on-the-fly engineering decision-making in areas such as additive manufacturing and architectural design. Key contributions include the introduction of a scalable deep transfer learning architecture for 3D topology optimization and comprehensive analysis of its effectiveness in diverse real-world scenarios. Keywords: topology optimization, real-time 3D design, deep learning, transfer learning, structural analysis, computational design.
