Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) have demonstrated remarkable success in video analysis tasks, such as action recognition and object tracking. However, their robustness to adversarial perturbations and real-world noise in video data remains a critical challenge. This paper addresses the problem of ensuring robustness guarantees for DNNs when applied to video datasets. We propose a novel framework that combines adversarial training with spatiotemporal feature regularization to enhance the resilience of DNNs against both adversarial attacks and natural distortions. Our approach leverages temporal consistency across video frames and introduces a lightweight yet effective defense mechanism. Experimental results on benchmark video datasets demonstrate that our method significantly improves robustness metrics, outperforming existing state-of-the-art techniques by up to 15% in adversarial accuracy. Furthermore, we provide theoretical guarantees on the robustness bounds of our framework, ensuring reliable performance under varying levels of input perturbations. The findings highlight the potential of our approach for real-world applications, such as surveillance and autonomous systems, where robustness is paramount. This work contributes to advancing the reliability of DNNs in video analysis, offering a scalable and practical solution for deploying robust models in dynamic environments.  

**Keywords**: Deep Neural Networks, Video Analysis, Robustness Guarantees, Adversarial Training, Spatiotemporal Features, Adversarial Attacks.
