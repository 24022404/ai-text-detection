Human Activity Recognition (HAR) and fall detection are critical for various applications, including elderly care and automated surveillance. This paper introduces a novel approach to HAR and fall detection using Fourier domain features to capture temporal dynamics in sensor data. Our method transforms raw sensor signals into the frequency domain, extracting salient features that represent different activity patterns. We employ a machine learning classifier trained on these Fourier domain features to distinguish between various human activities and accurately detect falls. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our proposed approach achieves superior performance compared to state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy, sensitivity, and specificity. The key innovation lies in the effective utilization of frequency domain information for robust and efficient activity recognition. This approach offers a promising solution for real-time HAR and fall detection systems.
Keywords: Human Activity Recognition, Fall Detection, Fourier Transform, Frequency Domain, Machine Learning, Sensor Data.
