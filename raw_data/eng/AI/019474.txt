The paper presents Gym-ANM, an open-source software designed to facilitate the application of reinforcement learning (RL) in power system management research and education. Addressing the critical need for effective tools in handling the challenges of dynamic power systems, this software aims to provide a robust platform for both academic and practical exploration.

Methods/Approach: Gym-ANM integrates reinforcement learning algorithms within a configurable environment replicating real-world power system operations. The software is built upon the widely used OpenAI Gym interface, ensuring easy adoption and adaptability for various use cases. By providing predefined environments and the flexibility to create custom scenarios, Gym-ANM serves as both an educational tool and a research framework.

Results/Findings: Initial experiments demonstrate Gym-ANM's capability to train RL agents to optimize power system performance in terms of stability and efficiency. The platform's comparative flexibility allowed for rapid testing and benchmarking against traditional control methods, highlighting its potential to enhance power grid management solutions.

Conclusion/Implications: Gym-ANM represents a significant contribution to the intersection of reinforcement learning and power systems, promoting advanced study and experimentation. Its open-source nature encourages widespread utilization and community-driven improvements, potentially leading to innovative applications in smart grid management and sustainable energy solutions. Keywords relevant to this work include reinforcement learning, power systems, OpenAI Gym, smart grid, and open-source software.
