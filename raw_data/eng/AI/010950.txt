Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) have emerged as a powerful framework for synthesizing high-quality images and videos. This paper provides a comprehensive overview of GAN algorithms and their diverse applications in image and video generation. We explore various GAN architectures, including conditional GANs, progressive GANs, and 3D GANs, detailing their underlying principles and training methodologies. The paper further investigates the application of GANs in several domains, such as image super-resolution, image-to-image translation, video prediction, and content creation. We present a comparative analysis of different GAN-based approaches, evaluating their performance in terms of visual quality, computational efficiency, and training stability. Furthermore, we discuss the challenges associated with GAN training, including mode collapse and discriminator overfitting, and explore existing solutions. Finally, we outline potential future research directions, including improving GAN robustness, generating high-resolution and long-term coherent videos, and exploring novel applications of GANs in areas such as virtual reality and augmented reality. Keywords: Generative Adversarial Networks, GAN, Image Synthesis, Video Synthesis, Deep Learning, Generative Models.
