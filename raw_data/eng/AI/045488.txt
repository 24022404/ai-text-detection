This paper addresses the problem of image de-quantization, a process aimed at restoring continuous-valued images from their quantized counterparts. Our objective is to develop an effective method for recovering high-quality images by leveraging generative models as priors. We propose a novel approach that utilizes deep generative models, specifically Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), to learn the distribution of natural images and effectively de-quantize them. Our method involves training these models on a dataset of high-quality images and then using them as priors to guide the de-quantization process. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods, achieving significant improvements in image quality and fidelity. The use of generative models as priors enables our method to capture complex image statistics and restore subtle details, making it particularly effective for de-quantizing images with nuanced textures and patterns. Our research contributes to the development of advanced image processing techniques, with potential applications in image restoration, compression, and enhancement, and highlights the benefits of integrating generative models into image de-quantization pipelines. Key keywords: image de-quantization, generative models, GANs, VAEs, image restoration, deep learning.
