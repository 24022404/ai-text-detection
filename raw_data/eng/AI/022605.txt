This paper addresses the challenge of entity resolution, a crucial task in data integration aimed at identifying and merging records that represent the same real-world entity across heterogeneous data sources. Our objective is to develop a cost-effective methodology for active entity resolution that adapts dynamically to the data characteristics and minimizes manual curation effort.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel variational active learning framework for entity resolution that combines probabilistic modeling with active learning techniques. This framework leverages variational inference to model the uncertainty in entity matching decisions. Active learning is utilized to strategically select the most informative record pairs for manual validation, thereby minimizing human intervention. The methodology introduces a cost-sensitive object function that prioritizes both accuracy and resource efficiency.

Results/Findings: Our experimental evaluation demonstrates that the proposed variational active entity resolution method achieves superior performance compared to traditional entity resolution techniques. The approach significantly reduces the manual annotation workload while maintaining high accuracy in resolving entity records. We showcase the system's robustness across multiple benchmark datasets, effectively handling varying data quality and complexity levels.

Conclusion/Implications: The research provides a tailored solution to the active entity resolution problem by integrating variational inference with active learning strategies, emphasizing cost-effectiveness and adaptability. This contribution not only advances the field of data integration but also offers practical implications for organizations seeking scalable and efficient data management solutions. Future work may explore expanding the approach to other domains such as social network analysis and knowledge graph construction.

Keywords: entity resolution, active learning, variational inference, data integration, cost-effective, probabilistic modeling.
