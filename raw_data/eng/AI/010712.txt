This paper introduces a novel approach for latent space manipulation in generative models, leveraging a surrogate gradient field to enable precise and interpretable control over generated outputs. The objective of this work is to address the challenge of disentangling and manipulating latent representations in high-dimensional spaces, which is critical for applications such as image synthesis, style transfer, and data augmentation. We propose a method that constructs a surrogate gradient field within the latent space, allowing for efficient traversal and modification of latent vectors while preserving semantic coherence. Our approach combines principles from differentiable optimization and geometric deep learning to ensure robustness and scalability across diverse datasets. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms existing techniques in terms of manipulation accuracy, computational efficiency, and visual quality of generated outputs. Furthermore, the surrogate gradient field enables fine-grained control over specific attributes, such as object orientation, texture, and color, without compromising the overall structure of the generated content. This research contributes a powerful tool for latent space exploration, with potential applications in creative design, data generation, and interpretable AI systems. Keywords: latent space manipulation, generative models, surrogate gradient field, interpretable AI, image synthesis.
