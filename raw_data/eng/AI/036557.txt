This research paper introduces Hue-Net, a novel deep learning framework designed for intensity-based image-to-image translation, addressing challenges in color consistency and style adaptation in computer vision tasks. The primary objective is to enhance the quality and accuracy of image translation by improving color fidelity and visual consistency across varying image domains.

Methods: Hue-Net leverages a unique combination of neural network architectures with differentiable histogram loss functions. This innovative approach allows for direct manipulation of color distribution, enabling more exact control over image hue and intensity. The network is built on a convolutional neural network (CNN) backbone, integrated with layers designed to specifically capture and adjust the histogram characteristics of input images, thus facilitating precise color transformations.

Results: Through comprehensive experiments, Hue-Net demonstrates superior performance in intensity-based image translation when benchmarked against existing state-of-the-art models, achieving notable improvements in color accuracy and visual realism. The application of differentiable histogram loss functions significantly enhances the network's ability to adaptively retarget chromatic distributions, delivering high fidelity in translated images across diverse datasets.

Conclusion: Hue-Net offers a significant advancement in the field of image-to-image translation, providing an effective tool for industries requiring high-quality color adaptation, such as digital imaging and film post-processing. By incorporating differentiable histogram losses, this method sets a new standard for fidelity in image translation tasks, bridging the gap between technical precision and perceptual quality. Potential applications extend to autonomous driving, augmented reality, and other domains where accurate image translation is paramount.

Keywords: Hue-Net, image-to-image translation, differentiable histogram loss, color consistency, neural networks, computer vision, intensity-based image transformation.
