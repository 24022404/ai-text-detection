Graph similarity computation is a fundamental operation in various domains, including social network analysis, bioinformatics, and cheminformatics. However, existing graph similarity algorithms often suffer from scalability issues when dealing with large graphs. This paper introduces CoSimGNN, a novel Graph Neural Network (GNN) based framework designed to efficiently compute graph similarity at scale. CoSimGNN leverages a carefully designed graph embedding module based on a contrastive learning objective to capture structural similarity information. Furthermore, we employ a hierarchical clustering strategy to group similar graphs, enabling efficient similarity computation within and between clusters. We evaluate CoSimGNN on several large-scale graph datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in both speed and accuracy compared to state-of-the-art graph similarity algorithms. The proposed framework achieves up to an order of magnitude speedup while maintaining comparable or superior accuracy. CoSimGNN's ability to efficiently compute graph similarity on large datasets opens up new possibilities for analyzing and understanding complex network structures.

Keywords: Graph Similarity, Graph Neural Networks, Contrastive Learning, Scalable Algorithms, Graph Embedding, Hierarchical Clustering.
