The growing deployment of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) for surveillance and monitoring has necessitated the development of robust object detection techniques under varying environmental conditions. This paper addresses the challenges in object detection from UAVs caused by nuisances like occlusion, illumination changes, and motion blur.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel deep learning approach that leverages nuisance disentanglement to improve object detection performance. Our method integrates a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture specifically designed to separate nuisance factors from relevant object features. The model incorporates multiple disentanglement layers that isolate the nuisances, thus enhancing the detection capabilities of the core detection network.

Results/Findings: The proposed approach demonstrates significant improvements in object detection accuracy when tested on several benchmark UAV datasets, including those with challenging nuisance factors. Our model achieves state-of-the-art performance, with a notable increase in precision and recall rates compared to existing methods. Additionally, the system shows robustness against diverse environmental variations, ensuring reliable detection across different scenarios.

Conclusion/Implications: The study highlights the effectiveness of nuisance disentanglement in enhancing object detection systems for UAV applications. The results suggest that deep learning models that isolate nuisance effects can greatly advance UAV-based surveillance and reconnaissance operations. This research opens avenues for further development of intelligent UAV systems and their potential deployment in various domains such as disaster management, traffic monitoring, and environmental assessment.

Keywords: UAV, object detection, nuisance disentanglement, deep learning, convolutional neural network, environmental variations, surveillance.
