This paper introduces Signal Transformer, a novel deep learning architecture for signal recognition that leverages complex-valued attention mechanisms and meta-learning. Addressing the limitations of real-valued representations in capturing intricate signal characteristics, we propose a complex-valued self-attention module capable of processing signals in their native complex domain, enhancing feature extraction and representation learning. Furthermore, we incorporate a meta-learning framework that enables rapid adaptation to new signal types with limited data. Our approach is evaluated on diverse signal recognition tasks, including modulation classification and biomedical signal analysis. Experimental results demonstrate that Signal Transformer outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and generalization ability. The proposed architecture showcases the benefits of complex-valued processing and meta-learning for robust and efficient signal recognition, offering a promising direction for future research in various signal processing applications.

**Keywords:** Signal Recognition, Transformer, Complex-valued Attention, Meta-Learning, Deep Learning, Signal Processing.
