With the rapid expansion of data available for mining, efficient and accurate classification of datasets has become critical in facilitating insightful data analysis. This paper introduces a new method aimed at enhancing the classification of datasets within the context of data mining, focusing on improved accuracy and efficiency. 

Methods: The proposed method leverages a novel hybrid algorithm that integrates decision tree classifiers with deep learning techniques. The approach combines the interpretability and speed of decision trees with the pattern recognition capabilities of deep learning models, optimizing the classification process. The algorithm is designed to preprocess data effectively, managing both structured and unstructured data types, and apply an adaptive learning rate to adjust for varying data distributions. 

Results: Empirical evaluations on publicly available benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves superior classification accuracy compared to existing state-of-the-art approaches. The hybrid algorithm shows significant improvements in processing time and is able to handle large-scale datasets efficiently. Comparative analysis indicates an average increase in classification precision of 8% over traditional techniques.

Conclusion: The newly developed classification method offers a powerful tool for data mining applications, providing valuable contributions through its innovative integration of decision tree and deep learning strategies. The study's findings have substantial implications for industries reliant on data-driven decision-making, such as finance, healthcare, and marketing. The method's robustness and scalability position it as a promising solution for future challenges in dataset classification.

Keywords: Dataset classification, data mining, decision tree, deep learning, hybrid algorithm, adaptive learning.
