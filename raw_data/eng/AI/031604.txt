This paper introduces a novel approach to molecule representation learning, incorporating chemical reaction awareness to enhance the accuracy and applicability of molecular embeddings. The objective is to develop a model that can effectively capture the complex relationships between molecules and their reaction pathways. Our method utilizes a graph neural network architecture, combined with a reaction-aware attention mechanism, to learn molecular representations that are informed by chemical reaction data. The results demonstrate significant improvements in molecular property prediction and reaction outcome forecasting, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods. Key findings include the ability to identify crucial reaction intermediates and predict reaction yields with high accuracy. The proposed approach has important implications for drug discovery, materials science, and other fields where chemical reactions play a critical role. By leveraging chemical reaction awareness, our model contributes to the development of more accurate and informative molecule representations, enabling better decision-making in molecular design and optimization. Relevant keywords: molecule representation learning, chemical reactions, graph neural networks, reaction-aware attention, molecular property prediction.
