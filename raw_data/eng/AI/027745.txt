The integration of domain knowledge into neural network architectures has emerged as a critical challenge in enhancing model interpretability and performance. This paper introduces *Choose Your Neuron* (CYN), a novel framework that incorporates domain-specific insights by leveraging neuron-importance mechanisms within deep learning models. The objective of this research is to enable more targeted and efficient learning by prioritizing neurons that align with domain-relevant features. To achieve this, we propose a dynamic neuron-importance scoring system that evaluates and adjusts the contribution of individual neurons during training, guided by domain-specific constraints and objectives. Our approach is evaluated on multiple benchmark datasets across diverse domains, including healthcare and natural language processing. Results demonstrate that CYN not only improves model accuracy by up to 12% compared to baseline architectures but also enhances interpretability, as evidenced by clearer alignment between neuron activations and domain-specific features. Furthermore, the framework exhibits robust generalization capabilities, making it applicable to a wide range of tasks. The implications of this work extend to domains where interpretability and domain knowledge are paramount, such as medical diagnosis and legal text analysis. By bridging the gap between domain expertise and neural network design, CYN offers a significant step forward in creating more transparent and effective AI systems.  

**Keywords**: neuron-importance, domain knowledge, interpretability, deep learning, neural networks, AI systems.
