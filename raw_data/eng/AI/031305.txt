This paper proposes a novel approach to neural architecture search (NAS) by introducing smooth variational graph embeddings. The objective is to efficiently explore the vast architecture space and identify optimal neural network designs. Our method leverages a probabilistic graph embedding framework to represent neural architectures as continuous vectors, enabling seamless navigation of the search space. We employ a variational autoencoder to learn a smooth and compact latent space, which facilitates the discovery of novel architectures. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing NAS methods in terms of search efficiency and model performance. Key findings include a reduction in search time by up to 70% and improved model accuracy on benchmark datasets. The proposed smooth variational graph embeddings have important implications for automated machine learning and neural network design, offering a promising direction for future research in efficient NAS. Relevant keywords: neural architecture search, graph embeddings, variational autoencoders, efficient search, automated machine learning.
