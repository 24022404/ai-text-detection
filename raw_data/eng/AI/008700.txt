Multi-person 3D pose estimation in real-world scenarios faces significant challenges due to severe occlusions. This paper introduces a novel approach to address this issue by leveraging gated convolutions within a deep learning framework. Our method incorporates a gating mechanism into convolutional layers to selectively filter features, emphasizing informative body parts while suppressing irrelevant or occluded regions. This allows the network to effectively reason about occluded joints and improve the accuracy of 3D pose estimation. We evaluate our method on challenging benchmark datasets containing significant occlusions, demonstrating state-of-the-art performance compared to existing approaches. Our findings show that the proposed gated convolution significantly enhances the robustness of 3D pose estimation in the presence of severe occlusions, leading to more accurate and reliable results. This work contributes a practical solution for 3D human pose estimation in complex, real-world environments, with potential applications in areas such as human-computer interaction, robotics, and surveillance.

Keywords: 3D Pose Estimation, Occlusion Handling, Gated Convolutions, Deep Learning, Multi-Person Pose Estimation.
