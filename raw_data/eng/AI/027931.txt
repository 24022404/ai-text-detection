This paper presents a novel approach for smartphone-based colorimetric detection leveraging machine learning techniques. The objective of this research is to address the growing need for portable, cost-effective, and accurate colorimetric analysis systems, which are widely used in fields such as healthcare, environmental monitoring, and food safety. We propose a framework that integrates smartphone cameras with a machine learning model to analyze colorimetric data in real time. The system employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) trained on a diverse dataset of colorimetric reactions to improve detection accuracy and robustness under varying lighting conditions. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves a high detection accuracy of 95.7%, outperforming traditional colorimetric analysis techniques. Furthermore, the system is validated across multiple use cases, including pH level detection and glucose concentration measurement, showcasing its versatility. The findings highlight the potential of combining smartphone technology with machine learning for accessible and scalable colorimetric detection solutions. This research contributes to the advancement of portable diagnostic tools and offers significant implications for point-of-care testing and resource-limited settings.  

**Keywords**: smartphone-based detection, colorimetric analysis, machine learning, convolutional neural network (CNN), portable diagnostics, real-time analysis.
