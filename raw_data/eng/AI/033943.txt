Single image dehazing is a challenging task due to the inherent ambiguity in estimating scene depth and atmospheric light from a single hazy image. This paper introduces FFA-Net, a novel Feature Fusion Attention Network designed to effectively remove haze and recover clear images. FFA-Net employs a feature fusion module that adaptively integrates multi-level features extracted from different layers of a convolutional neural network. Furthermore, an attention mechanism is incorporated to selectively focus on haze-relevant features while suppressing irrelevant information. This attention mechanism enhances the network's ability to distinguish between haze and scene details, leading to improved dehazing performance. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that FFA-Net outperforms state-of-the-art dehazing methods in terms of both quantitative metrics and qualitative visual quality. The proposed network effectively removes haze, restores color fidelity, and preserves fine details, offering a significant advancement in single image dehazing. Keywords: Image Dehazing, Feature Fusion, Attention Mechanism, Convolutional Neural Network, Image Restoration.
