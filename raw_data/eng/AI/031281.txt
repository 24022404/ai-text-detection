Scene text recognition (STR) presents a significant challenge due to the diverse textual content and varying environmental conditions in which text appears. This paper introduces MASTER, a Multi-Aspect Non-local Network designed to enhance accuracy and robustness in STR.

Methods: MASTER leverages a novel architecture that integrates multi-aspect features and non-local operations to capture both global and local contextual information within scene images. The network employs an attention mechanism to refine text feature extraction and a sequence-to-sequence model to facilitate recognition, capitalizing on advancements in deep learning for improved performance in dynamic text recognition scenarios.

Results: Experimental results demonstrate that MASTER achieves superior recognition accuracy across multiple benchmark datasets, outperforming existing state-of-the-art STR methods. The non-local network architecture allows for efficient feature learning, significantly reducing the error rate while maintaining a low computational overhead.

Conclusion: The introduction of the MASTER network represents a significant step forward in STR technology, offering robust and scalable solutions for automatic text recognition in complex scenes. This advancement has wide-ranging applications, including autonomous navigation systems, augmented reality, and document digitization. The integration of multi-aspect non-local networks could serve as a foundational model for future research in scalable and efficient text recognition systems.

Keywords: Scene Text Recognition, Non-local Network, Multi-Aspect Features, Attention Mechanism, Deep Learning, Autonomous Systems, Text Feature Extraction.
