This paper presents a comprehensive review of visual trackers and their application to mobile robots, with the objective of evaluating their performance and suitability for real-time object tracking. A thorough analysis of existing visual tracking algorithms, including deep learning-based methods and traditional computer vision techniques, is conducted to identify their strengths and limitations. The review focuses on the application of these trackers to mobile robots, highlighting the challenges and opportunities in this domain. The results show that deep learning-based trackers outperform traditional methods in terms of accuracy and robustness, but often require significant computational resources. The analysis also reveals that the choice of tracker depends on the specific application and environment, such as indoor or outdoor navigation. The paper concludes by emphasizing the importance of visual tracking in mobile robotics and highlighting the potential of leveraging advanced computer vision techniques to improve the autonomy and navigation capabilities of mobile robots. Key contributions include a detailed comparison of visual tracking algorithms and an in-depth analysis of their application to mobile robots, with implications for robotics, computer vision, and artificial intelligence. Relevant keywords: visual tracking, mobile robots, deep learning, computer vision, object tracking, robotics.
