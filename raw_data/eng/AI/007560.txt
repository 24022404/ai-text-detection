This paper addresses the challenge of improving the accuracy and efficiency of image matching and template matching tasks, which are critical in computer vision and pattern recognition applications. We propose a novel approach based on Least Squares Normalized Cross Correlation (LSNCC) to enhance the robustness of similarity measures under varying lighting conditions, noise, and geometric distortions. The LSNCC method integrates least squares optimization with normalized cross-correlation to minimize error and improve alignment precision. Our approach is evaluated on a diverse dataset of real-world images, comparing its performance against traditional normalized cross-correlation and other state-of-the-art methods. Experimental results demonstrate that LSNCC achieves superior matching accuracy, particularly in scenarios with high noise levels and illumination variations. Furthermore, the method exhibits computational efficiency, making it suitable for real-time applications. The proposed technique contributes to advancing image processing by providing a reliable and scalable solution for template matching, with potential applications in medical imaging, robotics, and autonomous systems. Keywords: Least Squares Normalized Cross Correlation, image matching, template matching, computer vision, pattern recognition.
