This paper introduces a novel approach to text generation using implicit deep latent variable models (IDLVMs). Addressing limitations in traditional latent variable models, which often suffer from posterior collapse and lack of explicit control over generated text, our method leverages implicit variational inference to learn a more informative and disentangled latent space. The IDLVM incorporates a deep neural network to map the latent variables to the text space, enabling the generation of diverse and coherent text. We employ a novel training objective that combines adversarial training with a reconstruction loss, encouraging the model to learn a latent space that captures meaningful semantic information. Experiments on several benchmark text generation datasets demonstrate that our IDLVM significantly outperforms existing latent variable models in terms of text quality, diversity, and controllability. Specifically, the generated text exhibits improved fluency and relevance to the input latent code. The results highlight the potential of implicit deep latent variable models for high-quality text generation and controllable text synthesis. Keywords: text generation, latent variable models, variational inference, implicit models, deep learning.
