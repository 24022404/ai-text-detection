This paper introduces DisCoveR, a novel approach for the discovery of declarative process models, aiming to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of existing methodologies. The increasing complexity of business processes necessitates robust tools that can effectively capture the dynamic and flexible nature of process execution.

Methods/Approach: DisCoveR leverages advanced algorithms and optimization techniques to automatically extract declarative models from event logs. The approach uses a combination of constraint-based mining and machine learning algorithms to identify and refine process constraints, significantly improving model quality and reducing computational overhead.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations on standard process mining datasets demonstrate that DisCoveR achieves superior performance in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency compared to traditional methods. The system consistently produces more accurate models, reducing error rates by up to 30% and improving processing speed by 50%.

Conclusion/Implications: DisCoveR represents a significant advancement in process mining, offering a practical solution for organizations seeking to understand and optimize complex, flexible processes. Its ability to deliver precise and efficient modeling opens new avenues for applications in business process management and intelligent automation, promoting increased adaptability and operational efficiency. Key keywords include process mining, declarative models, machine learning, optimization, and event logs.
