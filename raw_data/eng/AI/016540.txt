This paper presents a novel approach for training deep neural network control policies for autonomous aerial vehicles (AAVs). The objective is to develop robust and efficient controllers capable of navigating complex environments. Our method leverages Model Predictive Control (MPC) to guide the policy search process. Specifically, we utilize MPC as an expert to generate training data, which is then used to supervise the learning of a deep neural network policy. This approach allows the learned policy to mimic the optimal control actions derived from MPC, while simultaneously achieving real-time performance. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed MPC-guided policy search significantly outperforms traditional reinforcement learning methods and exhibits superior generalization capabilities to unseen environments. The learned policies achieve accurate trajectory tracking and disturbance rejection, showcasing their effectiveness in challenging scenarios. The contributions of this work include a practical framework for training high-performance AAV controllers using deep learning and MPC, with demonstrated improvements in robustness and computational efficiency.

**Keywords:** Autonomous Aerial Vehicles, Deep Reinforcement Learning, Model Predictive Control, Policy Search, Neural Networks, Robotics.
