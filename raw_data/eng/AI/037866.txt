Knowledge graphs (KGs) are critical for representing structured information in various domains, but their utility depends on effective embedding techniques to capture relationships between entities. This paper introduces PyKEEN 1.0, a comprehensive Python library designed to streamline the training and evaluation of knowledge graph embeddings. The library supports a wide range of state-of-the-art embedding models, including TransE, DistMult, and ComplEx, and provides tools for dataset preprocessing, model training, and performance evaluation. PyKEEN 1.0 integrates novel features such as hyperparameter optimization and reproducible experimental pipelines, enabling researchers to efficiently compare and improve embedding methods. Experimental results demonstrate that PyKEEN 1.0 achieves competitive performance on benchmark datasets, outperforming existing frameworks in terms of flexibility and ease of use. The library’s modular architecture and extensive documentation make it accessible to both novice and experienced users, fostering advancements in KG research. By offering a unified platform for embedding model development, PyKEEN 1.0 contributes to the broader adoption of knowledge graph technologies in applications such as recommendation systems, natural language processing, and semantic search.  

**Keywords**: knowledge graphs, embedding models, Python library, PyKEEN, hyperparameter optimization, reproducibility.
