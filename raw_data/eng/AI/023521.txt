This paper introduces SCALoss, a novel loss function designed to improve the accuracy of bounding box regression in object detection tasks. The objective is to enhance the alignment of predicted boxes with ground truth boxes, particularly focusing on the often-ignored side and corner regions. To achieve this, SCALoss employs a unique approach that combines the alignment of sides and corners of bounding boxes, providing a more comprehensive regression loss. The method is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in box regression accuracy and overall object detection performance. Key findings show that SCALoss outperforms state-of-the-art loss functions, such as Smooth L1 Loss and IoU Loss, in terms of precision and recall. The proposed loss function has important implications for applications requiring precise object localization, such as autonomous driving, surveillance, and robotics. By highlighting the importance of side and corner alignment, SCALoss contributes to the advancement of object detection research, offering a valuable tool for improving the accuracy of bounding box regression models. Relevant keywords: object detection, bounding box regression, loss functions, computer vision, deep learning.
