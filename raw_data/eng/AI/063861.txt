This paper presents BoA-PTA, a novel Bayesian optimization accelerated error-free SPICE solver designed to efficiently analyze complex electronic circuits. The objective of this research is to address the limitations of traditional SPICE solvers, which often struggle with convergence issues and numerical errors. To achieve this, we employ a Bayesian optimization framework that leverages probabilistic modeling to accelerate the convergence of the solver. Our approach integrates a robust optimization algorithm with a physics-informed model to ensure accurate and reliable circuit simulations. The results demonstrate that BoA-PTA outperforms existing solvers in terms of simulation speed and accuracy, while maintaining error-free convergence. The key findings of this study highlight the potential of Bayesian optimization in revolutionizing circuit simulation methodologies. The implications of this research are significant, enabling faster and more accurate design verification and validation of complex electronic systems. This work contributes to the advancement of electronic design automation, with potential applications in fields such as analog and mixed-signal design, and high-performance computing. Key keywords: Bayesian optimization, SPICE solver, error-free simulation, electronic circuit analysis, design automation.
