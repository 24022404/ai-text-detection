This paper addresses the challenge of nonlinear disentanglement in natural data, a crucial task for understanding complex patterns and relationships. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that leverages temporal sparse coding to disentangle nonlinear factors of variation in data. We propose a new model that integrates temporal sparse coding with nonlinear dimensionality reduction techniques, enabling the discovery of meaningful and interpretable representations. Our approach is evaluated on various natural data sets, including videos and audio recordings, and demonstrates superior performance in disentangling nonlinear factors compared to existing methods. The results show that our model can effectively capture complex patterns and relationships in data, leading to improved representational learning and feature extraction. Our research contributes to the development of more effective and efficient methods for nonlinear disentanglement, with potential applications in areas such as computer vision, audio processing, and time-series analysis. Key aspects of our work include nonlinear dimensionality reduction, temporal sparse coding, and representation learning, making it a valuable contribution to the field of machine learning and data analysis.
