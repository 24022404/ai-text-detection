This study explores the local geometry of hyperedges within hypergraphs, aiming to enhance the understanding and analysis of complex structures found in social networks. By introducing novel geometric interpretations of hyperedges, we address the intricate interplay of relationships in hypergraphs that traditional graph theory may overlook.

Methods/Approach: We developed a framework that redefines hyperedges through a local geometric lens, employing mathematical models to capture hypergraph connectivity and interaction dynamics. These models are integrated with algorithms to map and analyze the structural attributes of hypergraphs, specifically tailored to the context of social networks.

Results/Findings: Our findings reveal that the local geometric properties of hyperedges unveil significant insights into the connectivity patterns and community structures within social networks. This approach effectively distinguishes unique relational intricacies that traditional edge-based graph analysis fails to capture. Comparative analysis demonstrates the superiority of our method in identifying clusters and influence patterns, with increased accuracy and computational efficiency.

Conclusion/Implications: The research introduces a paradigm shift in the analysis of hypergraphs, providing theoretical and practical advancements for applications in social network analysis. The proposed approach not only deepens the analytical capabilities in understanding complex social interactions but also offers a foundation for future enhancements in network modeling and data-driven decision-making. This work contributes a novel methodology that can be applied to various domains requiring sophisticated network analysis.

Keywords: hyperedges, hypergraphs, local geometry, social networks, network connectivity, community structures, relational analysis.
