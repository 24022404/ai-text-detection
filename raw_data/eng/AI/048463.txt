This paper introduces Mini-DDSM, a novel approach for automatic age estimation using mammograms. Age estimation from medical images has significant potential in risk assessment and personalized medicine. Our method leverages a compact convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture trained on a large-scale mammography dataset, Digital Database for Screening Mammography (DDSM), to predict patient age. We explored various CNN architectures and training strategies, ultimately developing a lightweight model that achieves competitive accuracy with significantly reduced computational cost. Experimental results demonstrate that Mini-DDSM achieves a mean absolute error (MAE) of 6.2 years in age prediction, outperforming existing methods while requiring fewer parameters and less training time. The proposed method offers a practical solution for integrating age information into computer-aided breast cancer diagnosis and risk stratification systems. Key words: Age Estimation, Mammography, Convolutional Neural Network, DDSM, Deep Learning, Computer-Aided Diagnosis.
