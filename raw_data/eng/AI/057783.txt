Lifelong learning (LLL) aims to continually learn from sequential tasks while mitigating catastrophic forgetting. However, existing LLL approaches often suffer from parameter explosion and computational inefficiency as the number of tasks increases. This paper introduces a novel multi-task based LLL framework called Regularize, Expand and Compress (REC) to address these challenges. REC leverages AutoML to dynamically expand the network architecture based on task complexity, while employing regularization techniques to prevent overfitting and forgetting. Furthermore, a compression module is integrated to periodically prune redundant parameters, ensuring a compact and efficient model throughout the lifelong learning process. Experiments on various benchmark datasets demonstrate that REC achieves superior performance compared to state-of-the-art LLL methods, with significantly reduced parameter growth and computational cost. The proposed approach offers a promising solution for deploying LLL systems in resource-constrained environments. Keywords: Lifelong Learning, AutoML, Multi-task Learning, Regularization, Model Compression, Catastrophic Forgetting.
