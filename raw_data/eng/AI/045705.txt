This paper addresses the challenge of object detection in diverse domains by introducing a novel approach called Vector-Decomposed Disentanglement. The objective is to enhance domain-invariant object detection capabilities, enabling models to perform robustly across varying environments and conditions. Our method leverages a vector-decomposed disentanglement technique to separate domain-specific and domain-invariant features, allowing for more accurate and reliable object detection. We employ a deep learning framework that incorporates this disentanglement strategy, facilitating the learning of domain-agnostic representations. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of detection accuracy and robustness across multiple domains. The key findings highlight the importance of disentangling domain-specific factors in achieving domain-invariant object detection. This research contributes to the development of more versatile and adaptive object detection systems, with potential applications in autonomous vehicles, surveillance, and robotics. Key keywords: domain-invariant object detection, disentanglement, vector decomposition, deep learning, computer vision.
