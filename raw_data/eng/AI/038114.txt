This paper introduces Talk-to-Edit, a novel dialog-driven approach for fine-grained facial image editing. The objective is to enable intuitive and precise manipulation of facial attributes through natural language instructions. Our method leverages a pre-trained large language model (LLM) to parse dialog histories and map them to specific editing operations. We propose a multi-stage architecture that integrates the LLM with a generative adversarial network (GAN) to disentangle and modify facial features based on the dialog context. Experimental results demonstrate that Talk-to-Edit achieves superior performance compared to existing image editing techniques, allowing for complex and nuanced facial modifications guided by conversational input. Specifically, our approach enables users to iteratively refine edits based on feedback, resulting in more accurate and personalized outcomes. The model is evaluated using both quantitative metrics and qualitative user studies, showing significant improvements in edit fidelity and user satisfaction. This work contributes a new paradigm for interactive image editing, bridging the gap between natural language and visual manipulation. Keywords: facial editing, dialog systems, generative adversarial networks, large language models, interactive image manipulation.
