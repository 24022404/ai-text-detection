Vision Transformers (ViTs) have emerged as a powerful alternative to convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for image recognition tasks. However, their computational complexity and memory requirements remain significant challenges, particularly for high-resolution images. This paper introduces **PSViT**, a novel Vision Transformer architecture that addresses these limitations through two key innovations: **Token Pooling** and **Attention Sharing**. Token Pooling reduces redundancy in the token sequence by dynamically aggregating less informative tokens, significantly lowering computational overhead without sacrificing accuracy. Attention Sharing optimizes the self-attention mechanism by enabling multiple layers to reuse attention maps, further enhancing efficiency. Experiments on benchmark datasets, including ImageNet and CIFAR-10, demonstrate that PSViT achieves state-of-the-art performance with up to 30% fewer parameters and 40% lower computational cost compared to existing ViT models. These improvements make PSViT a scalable and efficient solution for real-world applications, particularly in resource-constrained environments. The proposed architecture not only advances the field of vision transformers but also provides a foundation for future research in efficient deep learning models.  

**Keywords**: Vision Transformer, Token Pooling, Attention Sharing, Efficient Deep Learning, Image Recognition, Computational Efficiency.
