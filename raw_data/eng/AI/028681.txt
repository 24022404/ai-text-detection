**Abstract:**

Detecting and recognizing traffic signs accurately is essential for developing advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS) and autonomous vehicles, contributing significantly to road safety and intelligent transportation. This paper presents a deep learning-based system for efficient traffic sign detection and recognition, addressing challenges posed by varying lighting, orientations, occlusions, and environmental conditions. The proposed system employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, combining state-of-the-art object detection models, such as YOLO (You Only Look Once), with a robust classification network. Extensive experiments were conducted on publicly available traffic sign datasets, demonstrating the system’s ability to achieve high detection precision and recognition accuracy, outperforming traditional machine learning methods and previously reported deep learning models. The end-to-end solution allows for real-time processing, making it suitable for deployment in embedded automotive platforms. The study not only highlights the improvements in feature extraction and classification but also discusses the scalability and generalization of the model across diverse sign types and road scenarios. The findings indicate the proposed deep learning framework’s potential for integration into practical transportation systems, paving the way for enhanced road safety and reliable autonomous vehicle navigation.  
**Keywords:** Traffic sign detection, traffic sign recognition, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, object detection, autonomous vehicles, ADAS.
