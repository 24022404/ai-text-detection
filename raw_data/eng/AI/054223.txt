This paper explores the integration of machine learning and control theory to develop innovative solutions for complex systems. The objective is to design and implement intelligent control systems that can learn from data and adapt to changing conditions. A novel approach is proposed, combining model-based control methods with machine learning algorithms to achieve optimal performance and robustness. The methodology involves training neural networks to predict system dynamics and using reinforcement learning to optimize control policies. Results show significant improvements in system stability and tracking accuracy compared to traditional control methods. The findings have important implications for applications in robotics, autonomous vehicles, and process control, highlighting the potential of machine learning to enhance control theory. Key contributions include the development of a unified framework for machine learning and control, and the demonstration of improved system performance using real-world examples. Relevant keywords: machine learning, control theory, reinforcement learning, neural networks, optimal control, robotics, autonomous systems.
