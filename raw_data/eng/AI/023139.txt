This paper introduces MG-GAN, a novel multi-generator model designed to prevent out-of-distribution samples in pedestrian trajectory prediction. The objective is to improve the accuracy and reliability of predicting pedestrian trajectories in complex environments. Our approach combines multiple generators to learn diverse patterns and a discriminator to evaluate the validity of predicted trajectories. The MG-GAN model is trained on a large dataset of pedestrian trajectories and evaluated on its ability to prevent out-of-distribution samples. Results show that MG-GAN outperforms existing state-of-the-art models in terms of prediction accuracy and robustness. The key findings indicate that the multi-generator architecture effectively captures complex pedestrian behaviors and reduces the likelihood of out-of-distribution samples. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and reliable pedestrian trajectory prediction systems, with potential applications in autonomous vehicles, surveillance, and smart cities. The novelty of MG-GAN lies in its ability to prevent out-of-distribution samples, making it a significant improvement over existing models. Key keywords: pedestrian trajectory prediction, multi-generator model, out-of-distribution samples, GAN, autonomous vehicles, surveillance, smart cities.
