This paper addresses the challenge of learning latent tree models at scale, a crucial problem in machine learning and probabilistic graphical models. Our objective is to develop a guaranteed scalable approach for learning latent tree models from large datasets. We propose a novel method that leverages a combination of advanced optimization techniques and distributed computing to efficiently learn latent tree structures. Our approach utilizes a parallelizable algorithm that can handle massive datasets, ensuring scalability and accuracy. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, outperforming existing state-of-the-art approaches in terms of speed and model quality. The key findings of this research include the development of a scalable learning framework, significant improvements in computational efficiency, and enhanced model interpretability. Our contributions have important implications for applications in data mining, bioinformatics, and computer vision, where latent tree models are widely used. This work highlights the potential of scalable learning methods for latent tree models, enabling their application to large-scale real-world problems. Key keywords: latent tree models, scalable learning, distributed computing, probabilistic graphical models, machine learning.
