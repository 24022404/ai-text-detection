**Abstract**

Understanding and forecasting human movement is critical for a variety of real-world applications, including autonomous navigation, surveillance, and human-computer interaction. This paper introduces TRiPOD, a novel framework for forecasting both human trajectories and pose dynamics in unconstrained, real-world environments. The objective of this research is to predict not only the spatial path (trajectory) of individuals but also their detailed bodily configurations (pose) over future time frames, addressing the challenges posed by natural, dynamic scenes. TRiPOD employs a unified deep learning approach that integrates spatio-temporal feature extraction and multi-modal data fusion to simultaneously model human movement and pose evolution. We evaluate TRiPOD on several challenging, in-the-wild datasets, demonstrating its superior ability to predict realistic and physically plausible human motion compared to state-of-the-art baselines. Extensive experiments show significant improvements in both trajectory accuracy and pose fidelity, highlighting the model’s capacity to handle social interactions, environmental variability, and occlusions. The findings indicate that joint modeling of trajectory and pose yields richer and more predictive representations of human motion. This work advances the field of human motion forecasting by providing an integrated solution for dynamic, real-world applications. Key contributions include the TRiPOD framework, the exploration of joint trajectory-pose prediction, and empirical benchmarks demonstrating robustness in diverse scenarios. Potential applications of this technology span robotics, smart surveillance, animation, and crowd analysis. **Keywords:** human trajectory forecasting, pose dynamics, deep learning, spatio-temporal model, human motion prediction, in-the-wild datasets, TRiPOD.
