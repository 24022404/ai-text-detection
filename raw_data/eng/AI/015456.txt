This paper introduces the Signal Transformer, a novel approach to signal recognition that leverages complex-valued attention and meta-learning. The objective is to improve the accuracy and adaptability of signal recognition systems in diverse applications. Our method employs a transformer-based architecture that incorporates complex-valued attention mechanisms, enabling the model to effectively capture intricate patterns in signals. We also integrate meta-learning techniques to enhance the model's ability to generalize across different signal types and environments. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of the Signal Transformer, achieving state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. The key findings highlight the importance of complex-valued attention in signal processing and the benefits of meta-learning for adapting to new signal distributions. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and robust signal recognition systems, with potential applications in areas such as audio processing, biomedical signal analysis, and wireless communication. Key keywords: signal recognition, complex-valued attention, meta-learning, transformer architecture, signal processing.
