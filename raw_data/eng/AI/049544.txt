This paper addresses the challenge of domain adaptation in 3D point cloud representation, a significant obstacle for robust performance in cross-domain applications such as autonomous driving, robotics, and augmented reality. We propose PointDAN, a novel multi-scale 3D domain adaptation network designed to bridge the domain gap between different point cloud datasets. Our approach integrates hierarchical feature extraction and adaptive alignment modules, enabling effective learning of domain-invariant representations across varying scales. PointDAN leverages both local geometric structures and global contextual information to enhance feature robustness and transferability. Extensive experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that PointDAN outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in domain adaptation tasks, achieving superior accuracy and generalization in cross-domain 3D shape classification and segmentation. These results validate the effectiveness of our multi-scale alignment strategy and its capability to handle the complex variations inherent in real-world 3D data. The proposed framework offers significant implications for the development of resilient 3D vision systems and paves the way for further research in domain-adaptive learning for point cloud analysis. Keywords: point cloud, domain adaptation, multi-scale representation, 3D deep learning, feature alignment, cross-domain learning.
