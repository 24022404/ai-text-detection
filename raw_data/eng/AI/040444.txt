The proliferation of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) has significantly advanced the field of image generation, but their resource-intensive nature poses challenges for deployment on devices with limited computational power. This research addresses the problem of efficiently compressing GANs without compromising their performance.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel online multi-granularity distillation framework for GAN compression. Our approach involves a hierarchical distillation process that operates at multiple levels of abstraction within the GAN architecture, including layers, blocks, and complete models. By dynamically adjusting the granularity of the distillation process, our methodology effectively captures and transfers essential generative capacities from the original model to a compressed variant. This is achieved through a carefully designed loss function that ensures the retention of high-fidelity output characteristics while significantly reducing the model size.

Results/Findings: Empirical evaluations demonstrate the effectiveness of our distillation framework in compressing state-of-the-art GAN models. Our compressed models achieve up to a 4x reduction in size and computational requirements while maintaining image quality benchmarks comparable to the original GANs. Comparisons with existing compression techniques underscore the superiority of our method in preserving image fidelity, diversity, and realism in generated outputs.

Conclusion/Implications: The online multi-granularity distillation framework presents a significant advancement in the field of GAN compression, offering a scalable solution for deploying high-performing generative models in resource-constrained environments. The implications of this research extend to various applications, including mobile image processing, real-time video services, and edge AI, where the balance between performance and efficiency is crucial. Our work lays the foundation for further exploration into adaptive compression techniques that can be generalized across other types of deep learning models.

Keywords: GAN compression, multi-granularity distillation, model efficiency, generative models, resource-constrained environments.
