This paper addresses the challenge of exploration in hard games by introducing a novel approach that leverages YouTube videos to improve gameplay. The objective is to develop an artificial intelligence (AI) system capable of learning from human players and adapting to complex game environments. Our method utilizes a combination of computer vision and reinforcement learning to analyze YouTube gameplay videos and extract valuable insights. The AI agent learns to recognize patterns, identify key game elements, and develop effective exploration strategies. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms traditional exploration methods, achieving state-of-the-art performance in several hard exploration games. The key findings highlight the importance of multimodal learning and the potential of leveraging large-scale video data to improve AI decision-making. This research contributes to the development of more efficient and effective exploration algorithms, with potential applications in areas such as game development, robotics, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: exploration games, YouTube, computer vision, reinforcement learning, AI, game development, multimodal learning.
