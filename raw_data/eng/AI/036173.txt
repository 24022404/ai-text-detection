This paper introduces SLEEPER, a novel approach to sleep staging that leverages interpretable prototypes derived from expert rules. The objective is to improve the accuracy and transparency of sleep stage classification, a crucial aspect of sleep research and clinical diagnosis. SLEEPER utilizes a unique combination of machine learning and expert knowledge to generate prototypes that capture the complex patterns and relationships inherent in sleep data. The approach is evaluated on a large dataset of polysomnography recordings, demonstrating superior performance compared to state-of-the-art methods. Key findings include improved accuracy in identifying sleep stages, particularly in cases with ambiguous or noisy data. The SLEEPER framework provides important insights into the decision-making process, enabling clinicians and researchers to better understand the underlying factors influencing sleep stage classification. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and interpretable sleep staging systems, with potential applications in sleep medicine, neuroscience, and related fields. Relevant keywords include sleep staging, interpretable machine learning, prototype-based classification, and expert systems.
