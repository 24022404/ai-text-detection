This paper proposes a novel approach to LiDAR segmentation using cylindrical and asymmetrical 3D convolution networks. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of point cloud segmentation in various applications, such as autonomous driving and robotics. Our method utilizes a unique combination of cylindrical and asymmetrical convolutional layers to capture the complex geometric structures of 3D point clouds. The network architecture is designed to adapt to the sparse and irregular nature of LiDAR data, allowing for more accurate and robust segmentation results. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of cylindrical convolutions for capturing long-range dependencies and the use of asymmetrical convolutions for handling complex geometric relationships. Our work has significant implications for the development of more accurate and efficient LiDAR segmentation systems, with potential applications in fields such as computer vision, robotics, and geospatial analysis. Key keywords: LiDAR segmentation, 3D convolution networks, cylindrical convolutions, asymmetrical convolutions, point cloud processing, autonomous driving, robotics.
