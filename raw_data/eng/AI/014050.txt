This paper presents a novel approach to person image generation, focusing on the Progressive Pose Attention Transfer (PPAT) method. The objective is to generate high-quality, pose-transferred person images that maintain the identity and appearance of the original image. To achieve this, we propose a deep learning-based framework that leverages attention mechanisms to selectively transfer pose information from a reference image to a target image. Our approach employs a progressive transfer strategy, allowing for more accurate and efficient pose transfer. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of PPAT, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of image quality and pose transfer accuracy. The proposed method has significant implications for applications such as person re-identification, virtual try-on, and image editing. Key contributions include the introduction of a progressive attention transfer mechanism and a novel pose transfer loss function, which enable more accurate and robust person image generation. Relevant keywords: person image generation, pose transfer, attention mechanisms, deep learning, image editing.
