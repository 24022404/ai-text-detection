Generating realistic person images with arbitrary poses remains a challenging task. Current pose-guided person image generation methods often struggle with preserving identity details and synthesizing realistic textures, especially under significant pose variations. To address these limitations, we propose a novel Progressive Pose Attention Transfer Network (PPATN) for high-quality person image generation. Our approach leverages a progressive transfer learning strategy that gradually transfers pose information from the target pose to the source image. Specifically, we introduce a Pose Attention Module (PAM) that learns pose-aware feature representations, enabling efficient and accurate transfer of pose information while preserving appearance details. Furthermore, a multi-scale refinement network is employed to progressively enhance the generated image quality. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our PPATN significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of visual quality, identity preservation, and pose accuracy, as evaluated by both quantitative metrics and qualitative assessments. The proposed progressive pose attention transfer mechanism offers a robust and effective solution for pose-guided person image generation, enabling realistic synthesis of person images with diverse poses.

Keywords: Person Image Generation, Pose Transfer, Attention Mechanism, Image Synthesis, Deep Learning.
