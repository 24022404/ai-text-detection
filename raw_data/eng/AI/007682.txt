Neonatal pain assessment is a critical yet challenging task in healthcare, as infants cannot verbally communicate their discomfort. This paper addresses the problem of accurately recognizing neonatal pain expressions through advanced machine learning techniques. We propose a novel approach leveraging transfer learning, where pre-trained deep learning models are fine-tuned on a curated dataset of neonatal facial expressions. Specifically, we utilize convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to extract discriminative features from facial images, enabling robust pain expression classification. Our methodology involves preprocessing steps such as image normalization and augmentation to enhance model generalization. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach achieves superior performance compared to traditional methods, with an accuracy of 92.5% on a benchmark dataset. The model also exhibits strong robustness to variations in lighting and facial orientation. This research contributes to the field by providing a scalable and efficient solution for neonatal pain recognition, which can be integrated into clinical settings to improve patient care. The findings highlight the potential of transfer learning in medical imaging and pave the way for future applications in automated healthcare diagnostics.  

**Keywords**: neonatal pain recognition, transfer learning, convolutional neural networks, facial expression analysis, healthcare AI.
