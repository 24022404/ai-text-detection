The growing demand for interpretability in machine learning models has led to the development of empirical explainers, which provide insights into model behavior. This paper addresses the challenge of generating efficient and scalable explanations without compromising accuracy. We propose a novel framework that leverages lightweight, model-agnostic techniques to extract meaningful explanations from complex models. Our approach combines feature importance analysis with dynamic sampling strategies to reduce computational overhead while maintaining high fidelity in explanations. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves a 30% reduction in explanation generation time compared to state-of-the-art techniques, with minimal loss in explanation quality. Furthermore, the framework is evaluated across diverse domains, including healthcare and finance, showcasing its adaptability and robustness. The results highlight the potential of our approach to enable real-time explainability in resource-constrained environments. This work contributes to the field by offering a scalable solution for deploying explainable AI systems, paving the way for broader adoption in practical applications.  

**Keywords**: Explainable AI, model interpretability, empirical explainers, feature importance, scalable explanations.
