This paper introduces a novel approach for point cloud processing using a recurrent set encoding framework. Point clouds, being unordered and unstructured, pose challenges for traditional deep learning methods. To address this, we propose a recurrent architecture that iteratively encodes point sets into a latent representation. Our method leverages a permutation-invariant pooling operation within a recurrent unit to capture global structural information while preserving the unordered nature of point clouds. Specifically, the network learns to aggregate point features and update its internal state recursively, effectively summarizing the geometry of the point cloud. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach on shape classification and part segmentation tasks, achieving competitive results on established benchmark datasets. The experiments highlight the ability of our recurrent set encoding to learn robust and informative representations from point clouds. Our method outperforms existing set encoding techniques, particularly in scenarios with varying point densities and noise. The proposed framework offers a flexible and efficient solution for point cloud analysis, opening avenues for applications in 3D scene understanding, robotics, and computer vision. Keywords: Point cloud, Recurrent Neural Networks, Set Encoding, 3D Deep Learning, Shape Classification, Part Segmentation.
