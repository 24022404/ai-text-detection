**Abstract:**

Instruction-following is a critical capability for intelligent agents, yet current models often struggle with suboptimal performance due to inefficient learning from diverse user commands. This paper introduces HIGhER (Hindsight Generation for Experience Replay), a novel framework designed to enhance instruction-following by integrating hindsight generation techniques into experience replay workflows. Our method leverages failed or suboptimal agent trajectories and automatically synthesizes alternative instructions that would have made these experiences successful. By replaying these hindsight-based experiences during training, HIGhER enables models to generalize better from limited data and sparse rewards. We implement HIGhER within both language-based agents and reinforcement learning environments, conducting extensive experiments across benchmark instruction-following tasks. Our approach yields substantial improvements in success rates and sample efficiency compared to traditional experience replay and recent instruction-following baselines. The results demonstrate that HIGhER not only accelerates convergence but also improves robustness to unseen and ambiguous instructions. This work highlights the value of hindsight generation for enhancing data efficiency and adaptability in interactive AI systems. **Keywords:** instruction following, experience replay, hindsight generation, reinforcement learning, natural language understanding, sample efficiency, AI agents.
