This study addresses the challenge of improving multiview detection accuracy by introducing a novel approach that leverages a Shadow Transformer model integrated with a view-coherent data augmentation technique. Traditional multiview detection methods often struggle with inconsistent data representation and occlusion across different viewpoints.

Methods: The proposed Shadow Transformer is designed to enhance the modeling of spatial and contextual relationships across multiple views. It employs a transformer architecture adept at capturing dependencies in complex visual data. Complementarily, the research introduces view-coherent data augmentation, a strategy that preserves geometric and contextual consistency across augmented views, enhancing the robustness of the detection model.

Results: Our evaluation demonstrates that the Shadow Transformer significantly outperforms existing multiview detection methods, achieving higher accuracy and lower error rates on benchmark datasets. The view-coherent data augmentation further contributes to consistent performance improvements, reducing biases caused by view discrepancies and enhancing model generalization.

Conclusion: The integration of the Shadow Transformer with view-coherent data augmentation presents a powerful framework for multiview detection, offering significant improvements over conventional techniques. This research advances the field of visual detection by providing a scalable and effective solution for applications requiring accurate multiview analysis, such as autonomous vehicles and surveillance systems. Keywords: multiview detection, Shadow Transformer, view-coherent data augmentation, spatial relationships, visual analysis.
