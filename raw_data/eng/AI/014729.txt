Time series data plays a critical role in various applications, including finance, healthcare, and IoT systems. However, the susceptibility of time series models to adversarial attacks poses significant security risks. This paper investigates the vulnerability of time series models to adversarial perturbations and proposes a novel framework to generate and defend against such attacks. We introduce an adversarial attack generation method tailored for time series data, leveraging gradient-based optimization to craft perturbations that maximize model prediction errors. To evaluate the robustness of existing models, we conduct extensive experiments on benchmark datasets, comparing the performance of traditional and state-of-the-art time series models under adversarial conditions. Our results demonstrate that even small, imperceptible perturbations can significantly degrade model accuracy, highlighting the need for robust defense mechanisms. To address this, we propose a defense strategy based on adversarial training and noise injection, which improves model resilience against adversarial attacks. The findings of this study provide valuable insights into the vulnerabilities of time series models and offer practical solutions to enhance their security in real-world applications. This research contributes to the growing body of work on adversarial machine learning, emphasizing the importance of robustness in time series analysis.  

**Keywords**: adversarial attacks, time series, robustness, machine learning, security, adversarial training.
