Multimodal fusion, the integration of diverse data types such as text, images, and audio, is a critical challenge in advancing machine learning systems for more comprehensive understanding and reasoning. This paper introduces a novel approach, "Attention Bottlenecks," designed to enhance multimodal fusion by selectively filtering and condensing information exchanged between modality-specific encoders. Unlike traditional fusion mechanisms that concatenate or merge high-dimensional feature spaces, our method employs learnable bottleneck units within a transformer-based architecture to focus attention on the most salient cross-modal features. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets for tasks such as visual question answering and multimedia retrieval demonstrate that Attention Bottlenecks significantly improve performance, achieving higher accuracy and efficiency compared to conventional fusion techniques. The proposed approach not only reduces computational complexity but also mitigates overfitting risks by restricting redundant information flow between modalities. Our findings highlight the effectiveness of bottlenecked attention in promoting robust multimodal representation learning. This work offers a scalable solution for multimodal AI systems and opens avenues for further research in attention mechanisms, deep learning, and cross-modal integration. Key keywords: attention, bottlenecks, multimodal fusion, transformer, cross-modal learning, deep learning, representation learning.
