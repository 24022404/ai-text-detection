This paper introduces CycleCluster, a novel approach to clustering regularisation for deep semi-supervised classification. The objective is to improve the performance of deep neural networks on partially labelled datasets by leveraging clustering information to regularise the learning process. Our method employs a cyclic clustering mechanism that iteratively updates cluster assignments and network parameters, promoting more accurate and robust classification. The CycleCluster approach utilises a combination of contrastive learning and cluster-based regularization to enhance the quality of the learned representations. Experimental results demonstrate that CycleCluster outperforms state-of-the-art semi-supervised methods on several benchmark datasets, achieving significant improvements in classification accuracy and robustness. The key contributions of this work include the introduction of a cyclic clustering mechanism, the integration of contrastive learning with cluster-based regularization, and the demonstration of the effectiveness of CycleCluster in deep semi-supervised classification. This research has important implications for applications where labelled data is scarce or expensive to obtain, such as image classification, natural language processing, and biomedical analysis, and highlights the potential of clustering regularisation to improve the performance of deep learning models. Key keywords: deep semi-supervised learning, clustering regularisation, contrastive learning, cyclic clustering, classification.
