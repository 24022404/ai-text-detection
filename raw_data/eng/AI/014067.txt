This paper addresses the challenge of generating realistic face age progression images while enabling precise manipulation of facial attributes, such as expression, hairstyle, and accessories. Traditional methods often struggle to maintain identity consistency and attribute control across age transformations. To overcome these limitations, we propose a novel deep learning framework that integrates a conditional generative adversarial network (cGAN) with an attribute-aware attention mechanism. Our approach leverages multi-scale feature extraction and adaptive normalization to ensure high-quality, identity-preserving age progression while allowing fine-grained attribute editing. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms existing techniques in terms of visual realism, identity preservation, and attribute manipulation accuracy. Quantitative evaluations, including Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) and identity similarity metrics, confirm the superiority of our framework. The proposed model has potential applications in entertainment, forensics, and personalized avatar generation, offering a robust solution for controllable face aging synthesis.  

**Keywords**: face age progression, attribute manipulation, generative adversarial networks, deep learning, identity preservation, facial synthesis.
