This paper introduces a novel deep reinforcement learning (DRL) algorithm designed to improve sample efficiency and stability by integrating Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) regions. The objective is to address the challenge of training DRL agents in complex environments where exploration is difficult and rewards are sparse. Our method leverages LQR control within local regions to guide the agent's exploration and provide stable policy updates. Specifically, we approximate the environment dynamics with a linear model and design an LQR controller for local stabilization. This LQR policy is then incorporated into the DRL agent's action space, allowing for guided exploration and improved convergence. We evaluate the proposed algorithm on a set of benchmark control tasks, demonstrating significant improvements in sample efficiency and overall performance compared to existing state-of-the-art DRL methods. The results indicate that integrating LQR regions effectively stabilizes the learning process and accelerates the discovery of optimal policies. This approach offers a promising direction for developing more robust and efficient DRL agents for real-world applications.
Keywords: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Linear Quadratic Regulator, Sample Efficiency, Policy Optimization, Control.
