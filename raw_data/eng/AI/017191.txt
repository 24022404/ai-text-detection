This paper introduces the Temporal Modulation Network (TMN), a novel framework for controllable space-time video super-resolution (STVSR). The objective of TMN is to reconstruct high-quality video frames with enhanced spatial resolution and customizable temporal frame rates from low-resolution, low-frame-rate input sequences. In contrast to existing approaches that are limited to fixed space-time scaling factors, TMN employs a progressive and dynamic modulation strategy that enables continuous control over both spatial and temporal upscaling. The proposed network integrates temporal interpolation modules and spatial super-resolution blocks, guided by learnable modulation codes that explicitly adjust output video properties according to user-specified parameters. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that TMN achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity (SSIM), while also offering flexible frame rate and resolution adjustments without retraining. Qualitative and quantitative comparisons validate the effectiveness of the modulation mechanism in preserving temporal coherence and fine-grained spatial details. This work advances the field of video enhancement by providing a unified, user-controllable solution for high-fidelity STVSR, with significant implications for entertainment, surveillance, and real-time visual applications. Key keywords include video super-resolution, temporal modulation, space-time processing, controllable upscaling, deep learning, and frame interpolation.
