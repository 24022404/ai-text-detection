This paper explores the concept of utilizing programs as black-box explanations, aiming to enhance the interpretability and transparency of complex systems. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that leverages programmatic representations to provide insightful explanations for opaque models, such as neural networks. We propose a method that employs a combination of symbolic execution and machine learning techniques to generate explanatory programs, which can be used to elucidate the decision-making processes of black-box models. Our results demonstrate the effectiveness of this approach in providing concise and accurate explanations, outperforming existing explanation methods in terms of fidelity and understandability. The key findings of this research contribute to the development of more transparent and trustworthy AI systems, with potential applications in areas such as model validation, debugging, and knowledge discovery. By introducing programs as a means of explanation, we pave the way for a new paradigm in explainable AI, enabling the creation of more interpretable and reliable models. Key keywords: explainable AI, programmatic explanations, black-box models, symbolic execution, machine learning.
