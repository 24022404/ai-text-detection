Class imbalance presents a significant challenge in deep learning, often leading to poor classification performance on minority classes. This paper introduces a novel Class Expert Generative Adversarial Network (CE-GAN) designed to address this issue. CE-GAN employs multiple generators, each specialized in generating synthetic samples for a specific minority class, guided by expert discriminators trained to distinguish real samples from their corresponding generated counterparts. This approach enhances the diversity and quality of synthetic data, mitigating mode collapse and improving feature representation. Experiments on various imbalanced datasets, including both image and tabular data, demonstrate that CE-GAN outperforms existing oversampling techniques and traditional GAN-based methods in terms of F1-score, G-mean, and AUC. The results highlight the effectiveness of class-specific generation and expert discrimination in improving classification accuracy for minority classes, offering a promising solution for imbalance learning problems in deep learning. Keywords: Imbalanced Data, Deep Learning, Generative Adversarial Networks, Data Augmentation, Classification.
