The research addresses the challenge of discovering optimal options within reinforcement learning environments while adhering to budget constraints. This study aims to enhance the decision-making capabilities of reinforcement learning agents by efficiently identifying valuable sub-policies or "options" that improve long-term performance without exceeding specified resource limits.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel framework that integrates budgeted reinforcement learning with options discovery algorithms. Our approach leverages a cost-sensitive learning model that allows the agent to balance between exploration and exploitation while maintaining adherence to predefined resource budgets. The model incorporates a hierarchical structure that facilitates the automatic identification and evaluation of beneficial options within the reinforcement learning process.

Results/Findings: Through extensive simulations and experimental evaluations, our method demonstrates significant improvements in resource efficiency and policy performance compared to traditional reinforcement learning approaches. The results show that our budgeted reinforcement learning framework discovers more effective options, leading to faster convergence and reduced computational cost.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a robust mechanism for option discovery under budget constraints, which can be applied to a broad range of reinforcement learning tasks where resource limitations are a critical consideration. The proposed framework not only enhances the efficiency of learning agents but also opens avenues for applying reinforcement learning in resource-sensitive real-world applications such as robotics, autonomous systems, and financial decision-making. Key Keywords: reinforcement learning, options discovery, budget constraints, hierarchical reinforcement learning, resource efficiency.
