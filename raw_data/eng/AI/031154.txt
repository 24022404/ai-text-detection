**Abstract:**

Effective temporal credit assignment remains a critical challenge in episodic reinforcement learning, where rewards are often delayed and must be accurately distributed to preceding actions across complex sequences. This paper proposes a novel sequence modeling framework that explicitly addresses the temporal credit assignment problem by leveraging advanced sequential architectures, such as recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and transformers, to capture long-range dependencies in episodic tasks. Our approach integrates temporal attention mechanisms to selectively weigh and propagate reward signals over significant time intervals, enabling more precise correlation between actions and future outcomes. Empirical evaluations on a diverse set of benchmark environments demonstrate that the proposed model significantly outperforms traditional temporal difference learning and existing credit assignment methods, particularly in scenarios with sparse or highly delayed rewards. The results highlight improved sample efficiency, faster convergence, and greater stability during training. This work contributes a robust and scalable method for enhancing episodic reinforcement learning by bridging sequence modeling with advanced temporal credit assignment strategies. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Temporal Credit Assignment, Sequence Modeling, Episodic Learning, Attention Mechanisms, RNN, Transformers.
