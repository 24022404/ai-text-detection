This paper proposes an innovative image-based generator architecture designed to refine synthetic images, enhancing their realism and quality. The primary objective is to address the challenge of generating highly realistic synthetic images that can seamlessly integrate with real-world scenes. Our approach leverages a novel combination of generative adversarial networks (GANs) and image refinement techniques to achieve this goal. The methodology involves training the generator network on a dataset of synthetic and real images, allowing it to learn the subtle differences between them and refine the synthetic images accordingly. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in the realism and diversity of the generated images, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods. The key findings indicate that our architecture can effectively reduce artifacts and enhance texture details, making the synthetic images virtually indistinguishable from real ones. This research contributes to the field of computer vision and graphics, with potential applications in areas such as data augmentation, image editing, and virtual reality. The novelty of our approach lies in its ability to learn and refine synthetic images based on real-world examples, making it a valuable tool for various industries. Key keywords: synthetic image refinement, generative adversarial networks, image-based generator architecture, computer vision, graphics.
