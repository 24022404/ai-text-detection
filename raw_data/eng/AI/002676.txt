This paper presents a novel dynamic filtering method with a large sampling field designed to enhance the performance of Convolutional Neural Networks (ConvNets) in a variety of computer vision tasks. Traditional ConvNet architectures often employ fixed spatial filters, which can limit their capacity to adaptively capture complex, context-dependent features. In response, we introduce Dynamic Large Sampling Field (DLSF), a framework that generates position-specific filters covering an expanded spatial area around each input location. Our method leverages an attention-based mechanism to dynamically determine both the location and weighting of sampled features, thereby enabling the network to flexibly aggregate information from a broader context. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that ConvNets equipped with DLSF achieve superior accuracy compared to standard static-filter and small-field dynamic approaches, particularly in the domains of image classification, object detection, and semantic segmentation. Furthermore, our approach maintains computational efficiency through streamlined dynamic filter generation and optimized sampling strategies. The findings suggest that dynamic filtering with large sampling fields can significantly enhance the representational power of ConvNets, offering a promising direction for future developments in deep learning and computer vision. Keywords: Dynamic filtering, large sampling field, ConvNets, computer vision, adaptive feature extraction, deep learning.
