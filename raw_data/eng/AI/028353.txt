This paper introduces a novel approach to fine-grained age estimation from facial images captured in unconstrained "in the wild" conditions. The primary objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of age estimation by leveraging the power of Attention-based Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks. Our method utilizes a deep convolutional neural network to extract facial features, followed by an Attention LSTM network that selectively focuses on age-related features across different facial regions. The attention mechanism enhances the model's ability to discern subtle age cues, improving performance particularly for fine-grained age differences. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including Adience and MORPH, demonstrate that our proposed method achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing age estimation techniques. The results highlight the effectiveness of the attention mechanism in capturing nuanced age-related information, leading to more accurate and reliable age predictions. This research contributes to the advancement of age estimation technology, with potential applications in various fields such as social media analysis, demographic studies, and personalized advertising.

Keywords: Age estimation, Attention mechanism, LSTM, Deep learning, Facial analysis, Computer vision.
