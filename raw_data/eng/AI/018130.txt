This paper addresses the challenge of facial pose estimation, a critical task in computer vision with applications in areas such as face recognition, augmented reality, and human-computer interaction. Traditional methods often struggle with variations in lighting, occlusions, and head orientations. Our research investigates the application of deep learning to improve accuracy and robustness in facial pose estimation by leveraging label distributions.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel deep learning framework that utilizes label distribution learning to enhance pose estimation. The model is based on a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture designed to parse input images and generate accurate estimations of facial orientations. The learning process is guided by a cost function tailored to accommodate label distributions, effectively capturing the nuanced variations in pose data.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly enhances performance when compared to current state-of-the-art methods. Through extensive testing on benchmark datasets, the proposed model achieved improved accuracy and generalization across diverse conditions and subjects, showcasing robustness to challenging variations commonly encountered in real-world scenarios.

Conclusion/Implications: The findings suggest that incorporating label distributions into deep learning models can offer substantial improvements in facial pose estimation tasks. Our work provides a foundation for further exploration into label distribution-based learning in other domains. The implications extend to various applications, where accurate head pose estimation is pivotal, including in security systems, virtual reality environments, and AI-driven user interfaces.

Keywords: facial pose estimation, deep learning, label distribution, convolutional neural network (CNN), computer vision, head orientation, robustness.
