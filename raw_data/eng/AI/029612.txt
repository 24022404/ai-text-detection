This paper introduces Hue-Net, a novel approach to image-to-image translation focused on precise color manipulation through intensity-based transformations. Our method utilizes a deep learning framework to learn mappings between the intensity distributions of source and target images, enabling accurate color transfer and artistic style transformations. A key contribution is the development of differentiable histogram loss functions, which directly compare and optimize the intensity histograms of generated and target images. These loss functions, incorporating both global and local histogram matching, allow for fine-grained control over color distribution. We demonstrate the effectiveness of Hue-Net on a variety of image translation tasks, including colorization, style transfer, and image enhancement. Experimental results show that Hue-Net achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of color fidelity and visual quality, outperforming existing methods that rely on pixel-wise or adversarial losses. The proposed differentiable histogram loss functions offer a powerful and interpretable tool for image manipulation, with potential applications in photography editing, medical imaging, and computer graphics. Keywords: Image-to-Image Translation, Color Transfer, Histogram Matching, Differentiable Loss, Deep Learning, Intensity Distribution.
