This paper proposes a novel approach to training deep generative models using curriculum learning with clustering. The objective is to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of training these models by leveraging the structure of the data. Our method employs a clustering algorithm to group similar data points together, and then uses a curriculum learning strategy to gradually increase the complexity of the training data. We utilize a deep generative model, such as a Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) or Variational Autoencoder (VAE), and modify the training process to incorporate the clustered data. The results show that our approach leads to faster convergence, improved model performance, and increased robustness to mode collapse. Our findings have significant implications for the development of more efficient and effective deep generative models, with potential applications in image and video generation, data augmentation, and anomaly detection. Key contributions of this research include the introduction of a clustering-based curriculum learning strategy, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in improving the training of deep generative models. Relevant keywords: deep generative models, curriculum learning, clustering, GAN, VAE, mode collapse, data augmentation.
