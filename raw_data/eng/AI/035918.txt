This paper addresses the challenge of analyzing irregularly-sampled clinical time series data, a common problem in healthcare settings where patient measurements are often taken at uneven intervals. Our objective is to develop a novel framework that integrates multiple views of the data to improve the accuracy and robustness of clinical predictions. We propose a multi-view integration learning approach that leverages a combination of machine learning and deep learning techniques to handle the irregular sampling issue. Our method uses a multi-resolution architecture to extract features from the time series data at different scales, and then integrates these features using a graph-based attention mechanism. The results show that our approach outperforms existing methods in predicting clinical outcomes, such as patient mortality and disease progression. The key findings of this study demonstrate the effectiveness of multi-view integration learning in handling irregularly-sampled time series data, with important implications for clinical decision support systems and personalized medicine. Our contributions include the development of a novel multi-view framework, the application of graph-based attention mechanisms to clinical time series analysis, and the demonstration of improved predictive performance in real-world clinical datasets. Key keywords: multi-view learning, irregularly-sampled time series, clinical prediction, graph-based attention, deep learning.
