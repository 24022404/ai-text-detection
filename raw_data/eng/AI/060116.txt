Accurate uncertainty estimation is crucial for the reliable deployment of machine learning models in healthcare, especially when dealing with sensitive medical tabular data. This paper addresses the challenge of quantifying uncertainty in classification and risk prediction tasks using such data, where inherent noise and limited sample sizes can significantly impact model confidence. We propose a novel ensemble method that combines conformal prediction with deep learning models to provide well-calibrated uncertainty estimates. Our approach leverages the strengths of both techniques: deep learning for accurate point predictions and conformal prediction for robust uncertainty quantification without distributional assumptions. We evaluate our method on several publicly available medical datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in calibration metrics and risk stratification compared to existing uncertainty estimation techniques. The results highlight the importance of accurate uncertainty estimation for informed decision-making in healthcare and demonstrate the potential of our method to enhance the reliability and trustworthiness of machine learning models in clinical settings. Keywords: Uncertainty Estimation, Medical Tabular Data, Risk Prediction, Conformal Prediction, Deep Learning, Calibration.
