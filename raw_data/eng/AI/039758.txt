Time series classification (TSC) is a critical task in various domains, including finance, healthcare, and environmental monitoring, where accurate predictions depend on identifying patterns in sequential data. This paper explores the application of deep learning techniques to improve the performance and scalability of TSC. We propose a novel deep neural network architecture that integrates convolutional layers for feature extraction and recurrent layers for capturing temporal dependencies. The model is trained and evaluated on multiple benchmark datasets, demonstrating superior classification accuracy compared to traditional machine learning approaches and state-of-the-art methods. Our results highlight the effectiveness of combining spatial and temporal feature learning in handling complex time series data. The proposed approach achieves an average accuracy improvement of 5-10% across datasets, with significant reductions in computational complexity. This research contributes to advancing TSC by providing a robust, scalable solution that can be applied to real-world scenarios. Key implications include enhanced predictive capabilities for applications such as anomaly detection, forecasting, and decision support systems. Keywords: time series classification, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, recurrent neural networks, temporal data analysis.
