Fairness in regression models is increasingly important due to their widespread use in decision-making processes. This paper introduces a general framework for fair regression that addresses disparate impact across various sensitive attributes. Our approach, unlike existing methods focused on specific fairness constraints, offers a modular design, allowing practitioners to incorporate diverse fairness metrics and regularizers tailored to their specific application. We formulate fairness as a constraint optimization problem, solved using a novel augmented Lagrangian method that efficiently handles complex fairness constraints. Experimental results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that our framework achieves state-of-the-art fairness-accuracy trade-offs compared to existing fair regression techniques. Furthermore, we show that the modularity of our framework enables seamless integration of new fairness metrics and improved model performance in various real-world scenarios. The proposed framework provides a practical and versatile tool for developing fair regression models with enhanced flexibility and performance.

Keywords: Fair regression, Algorithmic fairness, Disparate impact, Optimization, Machine learning, Bias mitigation.
