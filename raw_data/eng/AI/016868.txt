This paper presents PFDet, the 2nd place solution to the Open Images Challenge 2018 Object Detection Track. The objective of this work is to address the challenges of large-scale object detection in complex, real-world images, leveraging the Open Images dataset, which contains millions of annotated objects across diverse categories. To achieve this, we propose a novel multi-model ensemble approach that combines state-of-the-art object detection frameworks, including Faster R-CNN, RetinaNet, and Cascade R-CNN, enhanced with advanced techniques such as deformable convolutions, feature pyramid networks, and context-aware modules. Our method incorporates extensive data augmentation, model fine-tuning, and post-processing strategies to improve detection accuracy and robustness. Experimental results demonstrate that PFDet achieves a mean Average Precision (mAP) of 62.29 on the Open Images validation set, outperforming many existing approaches. The key contributions of this work include the integration of diverse detection architectures, the optimization of ensemble strategies, and the effective handling of class imbalance and scale variation in large-scale datasets. This research highlights the potential of ensemble-based methods for advancing object detection in real-world applications, offering valuable insights for future work in the field.  

**Keywords**: Object Detection, Open Images Challenge, Ensemble Learning, Faster R-CNN, RetinaNet, Cascade R-CNN, Deformable Convolutions, Feature Pyramid Networks.
