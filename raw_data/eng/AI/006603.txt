For each vertex \( v \) in \( L \) that is not the end-vertex of the last edge in path \( P \), an edge-disjoint path \( P_v \) of length \( s-j \) exists, ending at \( v \) and containing only edges not present in \( P \). Furthermore, in \( \ell(G_{k,s}) \), there is at most one path ending at the last edge’s end-vertex, and the combined number of edges from all the paths \( P_v \) is specified.
