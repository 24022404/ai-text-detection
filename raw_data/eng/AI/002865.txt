Convolutional filters play a crucial role in image processing by detecting both simple and complex features, independent of their position or orientation within an image. A fundamental aspect of these filters is their size, which not only determines the number of parameters that the network will learn but also influences the receptive field, or the area of the image that contributes to each output value. Among the various sizes, the 3x3 convolutional filter is the most widely used, striking a balance between computational efficiency and the ability to capture essential patterns in visual data.
