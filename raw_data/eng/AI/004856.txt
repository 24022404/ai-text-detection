This paper addresses the persistent challenge of robust image classification in the presence of various types of noise, which often degrade the performance of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) in real-world applications. We propose a novel architecture that integrates wavelet transform techniques with CNNs to enhance noise resilience while maintaining high classification accuracy. By incorporating multi-level discrete wavelet decomposition as a preprocessing stage, our method captures both spatial and frequency-domain features that are less susceptible to noise. The extracted wavelet coefficients are seamlessly integrated into the CNN pipeline, enabling the model to learn more robust feature representations. Experimental evaluations on widely used image classification benchmarks, including artificially corrupted versions of CIFAR-10 and ImageNet datasets, demonstrate that the proposed Wavelet Integrated CNNs consistently outperform traditional CNN models and several state-of-the-art denoising approaches under a variety of noise conditions, such as Gaussian, salt-and-pepper, and speckle noise. Our results highlight significant improvements in classification accuracy and model stability, especially at higher noise levels. This work provides an effective and computationally efficient solution for deploying deep learning-based image classification systems in noisy environments, with potential applications in surveillance, medical imaging, and autonomous vision systems. Keywords: noise-robust classification, convolutional neural networks, wavelet transform, image classification, deep learning.
