Mobile computational photography has revolutionized image capture and processing on ubiquitous devices. This paper provides a comprehensive tour of the field, exploring its evolution, core techniques, and future trends. We survey the key methodologies employed, including burst photography, high dynamic range (HDR) imaging, panoramic stitching, and depth estimation, detailing the algorithmic approaches and hardware accelerations that enable real-time performance on mobile platforms. We analyze the trade-offs between image quality, computational cost, and energy consumption inherent in mobile implementations. Furthermore, we discuss the impact of machine learning, particularly deep learning, on tasks such as semantic segmentation, image enhancement, and style transfer within the mobile context. Finally, we address emerging challenges and opportunities, including computational imaging for augmented reality (AR), virtual reality (VR), and the integration of novel sensors. This paper serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners seeking to understand and contribute to the rapidly advancing field of mobile computational photography.

Keywords: Computational Photography, Mobile Devices, Image Processing, Computer Vision, Machine Learning, HDR, Depth Estimation.
