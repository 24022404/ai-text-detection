This paper explores the generation of realistic human action sequences using Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs). The objective is to develop a novel framework capable of synthesizing diverse and plausible human movements. We propose a new GAN architecture, the ActionGAN, incorporating a temporal attention mechanism within the generator to capture long-range dependencies in action sequences and a multi-scale discriminator to enforce both local and global realism. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that ActionGAN outperforms existing methods in terms of visual fidelity, action diversity, and adherence to physical plausibility, as measured by Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) and action classification accuracy. Furthermore, we show the potential of ActionGAN for applications such as animation, virtual reality, and robotics. The key contribution of this work is a GAN-based framework capable of high-quality human action generation with improved temporal coherence and realism, paving the way for more natural and interactive virtual environments.
Keywords: Generative Adversarial Networks, Human Action Generation, Motion Synthesis, Deep Learning, Temporal Attention, Computer Animation.
