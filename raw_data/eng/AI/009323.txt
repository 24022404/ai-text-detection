This paper investigates the robustness of ensemble stumps and trees under $\ell_p$-norm perturbations, aiming to understand their resilience against adversarial attacks. We propose a novel approach to analyze the robustness of these models, leveraging techniques from robust optimization and sensitivity analysis. Our method provides a tight bound on the robustness of ensemble stumps and trees, shedding light on their vulnerability to $\ell_p$-norm bounded perturbations. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of robustness and accuracy, particularly in scenarios where data is noisy or corrupted. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of considering $\ell_p$-norm robustness in the design of ensemble models, contributing to the development of more robust and reliable machine learning systems. Our work has significant implications for applications in adversarial learning, robust classification, and decision-making under uncertainty, with relevant keywords including ensemble methods, robust optimization, $\ell_p$-norm perturbations, and adversarial robustness.
