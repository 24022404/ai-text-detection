Understanding market dynamics necessitates identifying and characterizing the different participants that influence market behavior. This study introduces a novel method to discern market participants by leveraging time series data and employing adversarial learning techniques.

Methods/Approach: This research employs a multi-agent simulator designed to replicate market dynamics, wherein different agent types represent varied market participants. The simulator is calibrated using adversarial techniques, allowing it to learn from real-world time series data and effectively mimic actual market behaviors. The calibration process involves iterative refinement to align simulated outputs with observed market data, thus enabling the discovery of distinct participant profiles.

Results/Findings: The proposed adversarial calibration approach successfully distinguishes between various market participant types with high accuracy. The study demonstrates that this method yields robust and interpretable agent behavior models that reflect real market dynamics. Comparative analyses indicate that our method outperforms traditional techniques in terms of both precision and reliability in identifying market participants.

Conclusion/Implications: This innovative approach facilitates deeper insights into market dynamics by accurately profiling market participants from historical data. The findings have significant implications for financial analysts and policymakers, providing a powerful tool for market surveillance and strategy development. The methodology's adaptability suggests potential applications in diverse markets beyond the financial sector, heralding advancements in understanding and modeling complex adaptive systems.

Keywords: market dynamics, multi-agent simulators, adversarial learning, time series, participant discovery, market behavior, agent-based modeling.
