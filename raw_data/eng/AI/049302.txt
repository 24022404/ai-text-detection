This paper presents a unified framework for spectral clustering specifically designed to handle sparse graphs, addressing the challenges inherent in clustering within these contexts. Traditional spectral clustering techniques often struggle with sparse graphs due to the low-density nature and irregular connectivity. Our framework advances the domain by integrating innovative preprocessing steps and novel algorithmic adjustments that enhance the robustness and accuracy of spectral clustering. The framework leverages advanced matrix factorization techniques to improve the spectral embedding process, ensuring more stable eigenvalue solutions. To validate our approach, we conducted extensive experiments on various synthetic and real-world sparse graph datasets. Our framework consistently outperforms existing methods in terms of clustering accuracy and computational efficiency. The results demonstrate significant improvements in identifying community structures within sparse graphs compared to state-of-the-art techniques. This research not only provides a powerful tool for applications in network analysis, bioinformatics, and social network clustering but also sets the stage for future enhancements in spectral methods adapted to sparse environments. Keywords include spectral clustering, sparse graphs, eigenvalue solutions, and community detection.
