The objective of this research is to advance the modeling of the human cardiovascular system by integrating neural network-based systems with Functional Mock-up Units (FMUs) to create a cohesive Hybrid Neural FMU model. The approach employs feedforward neural networks that are interfaced with FMUs to exploit the strengths of both data-driven and mechanistic modeling techniques. Through this hybrid modeling methodology, we aim to achieve a more accurate representation of cardiovascular dynamics, accommodating both physiological mechanisms and data-driven insights. Key findings indicate that the Hybrid Neural FMUs significantly enhance model fidelity compared to traditional FMU-based simulations alone, particularly in predicting complex cardiovascular behaviors under varying conditions. The results demonstrate improvements in accuracy benchmarks by 15-20% over standalone models. This study contributes a novel framework for integrating machine learning with system modeling, offering potential implications for improved diagnostic tools and personalized treatment strategies in cardiac healthcare. Our findings underscore the value of fusing neural networks with conventional modeling practices, and we suggest further exploration into similar hybrid approaches for other biological systems. Keywords: Hybrid modeling, NeuralFMUs, cardiovascular system, neural networks, Functional Mock-up Units, machine learning, physiological modeling.
