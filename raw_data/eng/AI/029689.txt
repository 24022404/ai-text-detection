Arbitrary style transfer aims to render a content image in the style of any given style image. Existing methods often struggle with generating high-quality stylized images, especially when faced with large style variations or complex content structures. This paper introduces a novel Multi-Adaptation Network (MAN) designed to address these limitations. Our approach employs a two-stage adaptation strategy, first aligning global style features through an adaptive instance normalization module and then refining local textures using a style-aware attention mechanism. The MAN architecture facilitates effective feature adaptation across multiple scales, enabling robust transfer of diverse artistic styles while preserving content details. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that MAN achieves state-of-the-art performance, producing visually compelling stylized images with improved fidelity and stylistic accuracy compared to existing methods. The proposed network offers a significant advancement in arbitrary style transfer, paving the way for more creative and controllable image stylization applications. Keywords: Style Transfer, Neural Networks, Image Processing, Deep Learning, Generative Models.
