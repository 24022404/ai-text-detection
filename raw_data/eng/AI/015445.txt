Time series classification is a critical task in various domains, including finance, healthcare, and environmental monitoring. Early classification of time series, which aims to predict class labels using partial observations, has gained significant attention due to its potential to enable timely decision-making. This paper provides a comprehensive review of approaches and applications in early classification of time series. We first outline the challenges and motivations behind early classification, emphasizing the need for efficient and accurate methods that operate on incomplete data. Next, we categorize and analyze existing techniques, including distance-based, feature-based, and model-based approaches, highlighting their strengths and limitations. Additionally, we explore applications of early classification in real-world scenarios, such as anomaly detection, predictive maintenance, and medical diagnosis. Our review identifies key trends, such as the increasing use of deep learning models and hybrid methods, which combine multiple strategies to improve performance. Finally, we discuss open challenges and future research directions, including the need for interpretability, scalability, and robustness in early classification systems. This review aims to serve as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners seeking to advance the field of early time series classification.  

**Keywords**: time series, early classification, machine learning, deep learning, predictive analytics, anomaly detection.
