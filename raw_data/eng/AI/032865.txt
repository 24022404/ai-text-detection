Semantic segmentation aims to classify each pixel in an image, a task crucial for various applications including autonomous driving and medical imaging. However, achieving accurate segmentation, especially along object boundaries, remains a challenge. This paper introduces Boundary Neural Fields (BNF), a novel approach that integrates neural implicit representations into the semantic segmentation pipeline to explicitly model and refine object boundaries. BNF learns a continuous, signed distance function representing object boundaries, allowing for precise localization. We train BNF jointly with a standard semantic segmentation network, enabling the segmentation network to leverage boundary information. Experiments on Cityscapes and Pascal VOC datasets demonstrate that BNF improves segmentation accuracy, particularly at object boundaries, outperforming state-of-the-art methods. The proposed method effectively combines the strengths of neural implicit representations and semantic segmentation, leading to enhanced boundary delineation and overall segmentation quality. This research contributes a new perspective on semantic segmentation by incorporating continuous boundary information, opening avenues for further exploration in high-precision segmentation tasks.

Keywords: Semantic Segmentation, Neural Implicit Representation, Boundary Detection, Deep Learning, Computer Vision.
