This paper presents a novel approach to scene search by abstracting objects within images, enabling efficient and effective retrieval of scenes based on their semantic content. Our objective is to develop a system that can accurately identify and retrieve scenes containing specific objects or combinations of objects, despite variations in viewpoint, lighting, and context. We achieve this through a deep learning-based method that abstracts objects into compact, semantic representations, which are then used to index and search scenes. Our approach leverages a convolutional neural network (CNN) to extract features from images, combined with a graph-based model to capture relationships between objects. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our method, outperforming existing scene search algorithms in terms of precision and recall. The key contributions of this research include the development of a scalable and robust scene search system, with potential applications in image and video retrieval, surveillance, and autonomous systems. Our approach highlights the importance of abstracting objects in scenes to facilitate efficient and accurate search, and paves the way for future research in this area, with relevant keywords including scene search, object abstraction, deep learning, CNN, and graph-based models.
