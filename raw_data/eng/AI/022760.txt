This paper addresses the challenge of accurate human face identification by leveraging deep learning techniques on three-dimensional (3D) facial data. Traditional face recognition methods often encounter limitations due to variations in pose, illumination, and facial expressions when relying solely on two-dimensional (2D) images. To overcome these constraints, we propose a novel framework that utilizes deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for extracting robust and discriminative features from 3D face scans. Our approach incorporates an end-to-end architecture that processes raw 3D point clouds and meshes, enabling the model to effectively capture geometric intricacies of facial structures. The system is evaluated on publicly available 3D face datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in identification accuracy compared to state-of-the-art 2D and hybrid models. Experimental results reveal enhanced resilience to common challenges such as occlusions and expression changes. This work contributes a scalable and effective solution to 3D face identification, with potential applications in security, authentication, and human-computer interaction. Key innovations include advanced feature extraction from 3D data, robust performance under diverse conditions, and integration of deep learning strategies into the 3D face recognition domain. Keywords: 3D face identification, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, biometrics, facial recognition, point clouds, computer vision.
