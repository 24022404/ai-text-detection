Single image dehazing is a critical challenge in computer vision, with applications ranging from autonomous driving to surveillance systems. This paper introduces FFA-Net, a novel Feature Fusion Attention Network designed to effectively remove haze from single images while preserving fine details and enhancing visual quality. The proposed approach leverages a multi-scale feature fusion mechanism combined with an attention module to adaptively emphasize important features and suppress irrelevant ones. FFA-Net integrates both spatial and channel attention mechanisms, enabling the network to focus on haze-relevant regions and improve feature representation. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, such as RESIDE and D-Hazy, demonstrate that FFA-Net outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of both quantitative metrics (e.g., PSNR and SSIM) and visual quality. The network achieves superior performance by effectively handling varying haze densities and complex scenes. The proposed method offers a robust and efficient solution for real-world dehazing applications, with potential implications for improving vision systems in adverse weather conditions.  

**Keywords**: Single image dehazing, feature fusion, attention mechanism, computer vision, deep learning.
