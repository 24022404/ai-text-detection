This paper presents a novel approach to accelerating explainable machine learning (ML) using Tensor Processing Units (TPUs). The objective is to improve the efficiency and scalability of ML model interpretability, a critical aspect of trustworthy AI. Our method leverages the capabilities of TPUs to accelerate the computation of feature importance and model explanations, reducing the computational overhead associated with existing explainable ML techniques. We propose a customized TPU-based architecture that integrates with popular ML frameworks, enabling seamless acceleration of model training and explanation generation. Experimental results demonstrate significant performance gains, with up to 10x speedup in explanation generation and 5x reduction in training time, compared to traditional CPU-based approaches. Our approach has important implications for the deployment of explainable ML in real-world applications, including healthcare, finance, and autonomous systems, where model transparency and accountability are essential. Key contributions include the design of a TPU-accelerated explainable ML framework, optimization techniques for efficient explanation generation, and demonstration of the approach using popular ML models and datasets. Relevant keywords: Explainable ML, Tensor Processing Units, Hardware Acceleration, Model Interpretability, AI Transparency.
