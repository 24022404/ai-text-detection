This paper proposes RankSRGAN, a novel image super-resolution approach that leverages generative adversarial networks (GANs) and a ranker to enhance the quality of low-resolution images. The objective is to develop a method that can effectively restore high-frequency details and textures in images, outperforming existing super-resolution techniques. Our approach employs a GAN-based architecture, where a generator network produces super-resolved images, and a discriminator network assesses the generated images' realism. Additionally, a ranker is introduced to guide the generator towards producing images with better perceptual quality. Experimental results demonstrate that RankSRGAN achieves state-of-the-art performance in image super-resolution, surpassing other GAN-based methods in terms of visual quality and quantitative metrics. The proposed method has significant implications for applications such as image and video enhancement, surveillance, and virtual reality. Key contributions include the integration of a ranker into the GAN framework, allowing for more efficient and effective training, and the demonstration of improved image super-resolution capabilities. Relevant keywords: image super-resolution, generative adversarial networks, ranker, GANs, deep learning, computer vision.
