Video-based person re-identification (Re-ID) aims to match pedestrian identities across non-overlapping camera views. This paper addresses the challenge of effectively aggregating spatio-temporal features from video sequences for robust person representation. We propose a novel Multi-Granularity Reference-Aided Attentive Feature Aggregation (MRAA) method. Our approach leverages reference frames within a video sequence to enhance feature learning through a multi-granularity attention mechanism. Specifically, MRAA captures both local and global dependencies between each frame and the reference frames, enabling the model to focus on discriminative features at different scales. The multi-granularity aspect allows for a more comprehensive understanding of the person's appearance and movement patterns. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets, including MARS and DukeMTMC-VideoReID, demonstrate that our MRAA method significantly outperforms state-of-the-art video-based person Re-ID approaches. The results highlight the effectiveness of the proposed reference-aided attentive feature aggregation in improving the accuracy and robustness of person re-identification systems. Keywords: Person Re-identification, Video Analysis, Feature Aggregation, Attention Mechanism, Multi-Granularity.
