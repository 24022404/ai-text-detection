This paper introduces a novel approach for transforming and projecting real images into the latent space of class-conditional generative networks, enabling effective image manipulation and editing. We present a transformation network that learns to map input images to latent codes conditioned on a target class. This network is trained adversarially, encouraging generated images to be both realistic and consistent with the specified class. Unlike existing methods that rely on iterative optimization or complex inversion techniques, our approach offers a fast and efficient feedforward mechanism for image projection. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves superior performance in terms of image reconstruction quality and class-conditional image manipulation compared to state-of-the-art techniques. Furthermore, we showcase the ability to perform semantic image editing by smoothly interpolating between latent codes corresponding to different classes. This work provides a practical and effective tool for various applications, including image editing, data augmentation, and content creation. Keywords: Generative Adversarial Networks, Image Transformation, Class-Conditional Generation, Image Editing, Latent Space Manipulation.
