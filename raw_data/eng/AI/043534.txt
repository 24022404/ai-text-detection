This research addresses the challenge of automatic age estimation from mammographic images, a task that holds significant implications for personalized healthcare and early disease detection. The study presents a novel approach called Mini-DDSM, focusing on leveraging mammography for age assessment, which has been relatively unexplored compared to traditional age estimation methods.

Methods/Approach: The proposed Mini-DDSM framework utilizes advanced machine learning techniques to analyze mammographic data. By incorporating deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs), the model extracts relevant features from the mammograms, enabling accurate age prediction. The research employs a comprehensive dataset compiled specifically for this purpose, facilitating model training and validation using robust algorithmic assessments.

Results/Findings: Our experiments demonstrate that Mini-DDSM achieves state-of-the-art performance in automatic age estimation with mammography, outperforming existing methods. The model showed significant accuracy improvements, with a reduction in mean absolute error compared to conventional approaches. Additionally, the findings indicate the model’s capability in maintaining consistent performance across diverse demographic cohorts.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of Mini-DDSM represents a substantial advancement in the field of medical imaging and age estimation. This research not only enhances the precision of age prediction in clinical settings but also opens new avenues for age-related studies utilizing mammographic data. The implications of this work suggest potential applications in risk stratification, tailored screening programs, and improved patient outcomes. Key Keywords: mammography, age estimation, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, medical imaging, healthcare personalization.
