This paper presents a novel context-aware and video-based risk descriptor designed to enhance the safety of cyclists in urban environments. The objective is to develop an intelligent system that can accurately identify and assess potential hazards, providing cyclists with critical information to mitigate risks. Our approach leverages computer vision and machine learning techniques to analyze video feeds from cameras mounted on bicycles, detecting and classifying various types of hazards such as vehicles, pedestrians, and road obstacles. The system integrates contextual information, including weather, lighting, and road conditions, to provide a comprehensive risk assessment. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our system in accurately detecting hazards and predicting potential collisions. The proposed risk descriptor has significant implications for the development of smart cycling infrastructure and can be integrated into various applications, including cycling navigation systems and bike-sharing platforms. Key contributions of this research include the introduction of a video-based risk assessment framework and the incorporation of contextual awareness, enhancing the overall safety and experience of cyclists. Relevant keywords: computer vision, machine learning, cyclist safety, context-aware systems, video analysis, intelligent transportation systems.
