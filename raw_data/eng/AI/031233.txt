This paper proposes a simple yet effective baseline algorithm for graph classification, a fundamental problem in graph-structured data analysis. The objective is to develop a straightforward approach that can serve as a benchmark for more complex models. Our method leverages basic graph properties and applies a novel combination of node and edge features to classify graphs. The algorithm is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating competitive performance with state-of-the-art models. Key findings show that our approach achieves high accuracy and efficiency, making it a viable alternative to more sophisticated techniques. The simplicity and interpretability of our algorithm make it an attractive choice for real-world applications, such as social network analysis and bioinformatics. Overall, this research contributes to the development of graph classification methods by providing a simple, efficient, and effective baseline algorithm, with potential applications in various fields, including machine learning, data mining, and network science, using relevant techniques such as graph neural networks and node embedding.
