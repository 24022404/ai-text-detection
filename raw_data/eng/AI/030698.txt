This paper addresses the challenging problem of real-world degradation in smartphone photo scanning, aiming to improve the quality of scanned images that suffer from common issues such as blur, noise, and color distortion. Our research focuses on developing an advanced method to enhance smartphone-scanned images through cutting-edge machine learning techniques.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel semi-supervised learning framework, Deep Photo Scan, which leverages both labeled and unlabeled data to effectively model and mitigate degradation artifacts. The architecture integrates a deep convolutional neural network with a transformer module to capture complex visual patterns and dependencies. By utilizing a large dataset of real-world smartphone scans, our approach is trained to identify and correct various types of degradation, thereby improving the overall quality of digital reproductions.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate that Deep Photo Scan significantly outperforms existing methods in terms of visual quality and restoration accuracy. Key metrics indicate notable improvements in sharpness, noise reduction, and color fidelity, surpassing state-of-the-art approaches. Comparative analysis shows that our technique provides higher fidelity reconstructions, preserving essential details and producing images that closely resemble original photographs.

Conclusion/Implications: The findings highlight the efficacy of semi-supervised learning in addressing real-world challenges in smartphone photo scanning. Our research contributes to the field by introducing a robust framework that can be integrated into mobile applications, offering enhanced photo scanning capabilities for consumers. The proposed approach holds the potential for widespread adoption, improving digital archiving, photo restoration, and preserving photographic memories across a variety of contexts.

Keywords: smartphone photo scanning, semi-supervised learning, deep learning, image enhancement, real-world degradation, convolutional neural network, transformer.
