This paper presents a novel approach to network-wide traffic speed prediction using deep bidirectional and unidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) recurrent neural networks. The objective is to accurately forecast traffic speeds across an entire network, addressing the complexities of spatial and temporal dependencies. Our method combines the strengths of both bidirectional and unidirectional LSTMs to capture comprehensive traffic patterns. The proposed model is trained on real-world traffic data, leveraging historical speed measurements to predict future speeds. Results show that our approach outperforms existing methods, achieving significant improvements in prediction accuracy and reducing errors by up to 15%. The deep learning architecture demonstrates its ability to learn complex traffic dynamics, making it a valuable tool for intelligent transportation systems. This research contributes to the development of more efficient and reliable traffic management systems, with potential applications in route optimization, traffic signal control, and autonomous vehicles. Key keywords: LSTM, recurrent neural networks, traffic speed prediction, deep learning, intelligent transportation systems.
