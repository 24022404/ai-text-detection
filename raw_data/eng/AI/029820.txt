3D reconstruction from point clouds is a fundamental problem in computer vision and robotics. This paper introduces a novel approach using Deep Implicit Moving Least-Squares (DIMLS) functions for high-quality surface reconstruction. Our method combines the flexibility of Moving Least-Squares (MLS) with the representational power of deep neural networks to learn implicit surface functions directly from unorganized point clouds. Unlike traditional MLS, DIMLS leverages a deep network to learn adaptive weighting functions and local coordinate frames, enabling robust reconstruction in the presence of noise and varying point densities. We demonstrate that DIMLS outperforms state-of-the-art methods on benchmark datasets, producing reconstructions with higher accuracy and smoother surfaces. Furthermore, we show that our learned implicit representation facilitates downstream tasks such as surface normal estimation and mesh generation. Our key contributions include the DIMLS formulation, a novel training strategy for learning adaptive MLS parameters, and a comprehensive evaluation demonstrating superior reconstruction performance. Keywords: 3D Reconstruction, Implicit Surfaces, Moving Least-Squares, Deep Learning, Point Clouds.
