This paper presents a novel approach to automated feedback on richly structured problems through a method we term "Generative Grading." The objective of this research is to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of educational assessment by leveraging advanced machine learning techniques. We propose a generative model that utilizes natural language processing and deep learning to evaluate student submissions with near human-level accuracy. The model is trained on a comprehensive dataset that includes a diverse range of problem types and corresponding expert feedback. Our results demonstrate that Generative Grading outperforms traditional automated grading systems in terms of accuracy and consistency, while also providing detailed, context-aware feedback. This approach not only addresses common challenges associated with automated assessment, such as bias and lack of depth in feedback, but also offers insights into student learning pathways. The implications of this research extend to educational technology, where improving the quality of feedback can significantly enhance student learning outcomes. Key contributions of this work include the development of a robust evaluation framework and a scalable solution that can be adopted across different subjects and educational contexts. Keywords: Generative Grading, automated feedback, machine learning, educational assessment, natural language processing.
