The process of user registration and authentication involves several interconnected steps to ensure security and consensus. It begins with the AssignKeyCommitmentRequest, in which executor signatures are aggregated prior to proceeding with the AssignKeyRequest. During this phase, users register themselves by submitting their idToken alongside the aggregated signatures, which are essential for reaching consensus within the system. Once registered, users utilize the CommitmentRequest to obtain aggregated node signatures. These are specifically required for the subsequent ShareRequest. The ShareRequest step then enables users to retrieve encrypted key shares, which they must collect in order to reconstruct their encKey. This reconstructed key is crucial for secure authentication within the platform, safeguarding user information and enabling robust cryptographic protection.
