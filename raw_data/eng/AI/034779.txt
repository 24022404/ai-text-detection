This paper introduces a novel approach to probabilistic modeling by proposing a Variational Mixture of Normalizing Flows (VMoNF), a framework that combines the flexibility of normalizing flows with the variational inference paradigm. The objective of this research is to address the limitations of existing density estimation methods, particularly in capturing complex, multi-modal distributions. The proposed method leverages a mixture model architecture, where each component is parameterized by a normalizing flow, enabling the model to learn highly expressive and diverse probability distributions. The training process employs variational inference to optimize the evidence lower bound (ELBO), ensuring efficient and scalable learning. Experimental results demonstrate that VMoNF outperforms traditional normalizing flows and other state-of-the-art density estimation techniques on benchmark datasets, achieving superior performance in terms of log-likelihood and sample quality. The framework also exhibits robustness in handling high-dimensional data and offers interpretability through its mixture components. The key contribution of this work lies in its ability to seamlessly integrate normalizing flows with variational methods, providing a powerful tool for probabilistic modeling in applications such as generative modeling, anomaly detection, and uncertainty quantification. Keywords: normalizing flows, variational inference, mixture models, density estimation, probabilistic modeling.
