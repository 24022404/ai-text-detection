This paper addresses the growing concern of video face manipulation, where malicious actors can create realistic yet fake videos, by proposing an innovative detection approach. Our objective is to develop a robust and accurate method to identify manipulated faces in videos. To achieve this, we employ an ensemble of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) that leverages the strengths of multiple models to improve detection performance. Our approach utilizes a combination of spatial and temporal features extracted from videos, which are then fed into the ensemble framework. The results show that our method outperforms state-of-the-art techniques, achieving a significant improvement in detection accuracy. The key findings of this research highlight the effectiveness of ensemble learning in detecting video face manipulation, demonstrating its potential to counter emerging threats in deepfake technology. This study contributes to the field of computer vision and cybersecurity, with implications for applications such as video authentication, surveillance, and social media monitoring. Key keywords: face manipulation detection, ensemble learning, CNNs, deepfakes, video authentication.
