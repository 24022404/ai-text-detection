Clinical time series data, often irregularly sampled and multi-view in nature, present significant challenges for accurate analysis and prediction. This paper introduces a novel Multi-view Integration Learning (MvIL) framework designed to address these challenges by effectively integrating heterogeneous clinical data streams. The proposed approach leverages advanced deep learning techniques to model temporal dependencies and cross-view relationships, enabling robust handling of irregular sampling intervals and missing data. Specifically, MvIL employs a combination of attention mechanisms and recurrent neural networks to dynamically weigh the importance of different views and time steps, ensuring optimal feature representation. Experimental results on real-world clinical datasets demonstrate that MvIL outperforms existing methods in predictive accuracy, achieving state-of-the-art performance in tasks such as patient outcome prediction and disease progression modeling. The framework’s ability to handle irregularly-sampled data and integrate multi-view information offers significant potential for improving clinical decision-making and personalized healthcare. This research contributes to the growing field of AI-driven healthcare analytics by providing a scalable and interpretable solution for complex clinical time series analysis.  

**Keywords**: clinical time series, multi-view learning, irregular sampling, deep learning, healthcare analytics, attention mechanisms.
