Exascale computing presents unprecedented opportunities and challenges in the efficient execution of applications that require significant tuning to optimal performance. This research explores the implementation of multitask and transfer learning techniques to enhance autotuning processes for exascale applications, addressing the complex challenge of parameter optimization in such extreme-scale environments.

Methods/Approach: We developed a novel framework combining multitask learning and transfer learning strategies to optimize autotuning parameters. The framework leverages shared knowledge across multiple tasks and transfers learned optimization strategies from smaller, related problems to larger, more complex exascale applications. The approach was implemented using a combination of neural networks and reinforcement learning models to adaptively identify and apply the best tuning parameters across diverse computing scenarios.

Results/Findings: The proposed framework significantly outperforms traditional autotuning techniques, demonstrating reduced tuning times and improved application performance. Experimental evaluations on benchmark exascale applications indicate a notable increase in efficiency, with an average performance gain of up to 30% over baseline methods. This advancement not only accelerates the tuning process but also enhances the scalability and adaptability of applications across different computing environments.

Conclusion/Implications: This research provides a powerful method for autotuning exascale applications, highlighting the practicality and effectiveness of integrating multitask and transfer learning in this context. The contributions of this study have the potential to transform how exascale applications are optimized, fostering advancements in computational science and enabling superior performance in critical applications across various scientific fields. Future work will explore scaling this approach to broader problem domains and integrating advanced machine learning models for further improvements.

Keywords: Exascale computing, autotuning, multitask learning, transfer learning, parameter optimization, neural networks, reinforcement learning.
