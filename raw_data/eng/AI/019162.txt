This paper introduces ECGadv, a novel approach to generating adversarial electrocardiograms (ECGs) designed to misguide arrhythmia classification systems. The objective is to investigate the vulnerability of deep learning-based arrhythmia classification models to adversarial attacks. Our method utilizes a combination of generative adversarial networks (GANs) and gradient-based optimization techniques to craft subtle perturbations in ECG signals, resulting in misclassifications. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of ECGadv in deceiving state-of-the-art arrhythmia classification systems, with a significant reduction in classification accuracy. Our findings highlight the potential risks of relying solely on deep learning models for arrhythmia diagnosis and emphasize the need for developing more robust and secure classification systems. The contributions of this research include the development of a novel adversarial attack framework for ECGs and insights into the vulnerabilities of deep learning-based arrhythmia classification models, with implications for the development of more secure and reliable medical diagnosis systems. Key keywords: ECG, adversarial attacks, arrhythmia classification, deep learning, GANs, medical diagnosis security.
