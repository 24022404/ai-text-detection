Time series forecasting is crucial for decision-making across various domains, yet accurately capturing long-term trends remains a significant challenge. This paper introduces a novel approach leveraging Self-Organizing Maps (SOMs) to effectively extract and model these trends. Our method utilizes SOMs to cluster time series data based on similar temporal patterns, enabling the identification of underlying long-term trends within each cluster. Subsequently, forecasting models are trained on these clustered series, allowing for specialized predictions tailored to specific trend behaviors. The results demonstrate that our SOM-based approach outperforms traditional forecasting methods, particularly in scenarios with noisy or complex data, by providing more accurate long-term predictions. This research offers a valuable tool for enhancing time series forecasting accuracy and provides insights into the applicability of SOMs in temporal data analysis. Keywords: Time Series Forecasting, Self-Organizing Maps, Long-Term Trends, Clustering, Machine Learning, Temporal Data Analysis.
