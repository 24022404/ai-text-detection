Video-based person re-identification (Re-ID) is a critical task in surveillance and security systems, aiming to match individuals across non-overlapping camera views. Despite significant progress, existing methods often struggle with challenges such as occlusions, pose variations, and background clutter. This paper introduces the Concentrated Multi-Grained Multi-Attention Network (CMGMA-Net), a novel framework designed to enhance feature representation and discriminability in video-based Re-ID. The proposed approach leverages a multi-grained attention mechanism to capture both global and fine-grained spatial-temporal features, while a concentrated attention module focuses on the most discriminative regions across video frames. Additionally, a multi-attention fusion strategy is employed to integrate diverse feature representations effectively. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets, including MARS, DukeMTMC-VideoReID, and iLIDS-VID, demonstrate that CMGMA-Net achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of rank-1 accuracy and mean average precision. The results highlight the effectiveness of the proposed approach in addressing complex real-world scenarios, making it a promising solution for advanced video-based person Re-ID systems.  

**Keywords**: Video-based person re-identification, multi-grained attention, concentrated attention, feature fusion, surveillance systems.
