Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) has shown promise in solving complex continuous control tasks. However, a comprehensive and standardized benchmark for evaluating the performance of DRL algorithms in this domain remains a challenge. This paper addresses this gap by presenting a novel benchmark suite encompassing a diverse range of continuous control environments, including locomotion, manipulation, and simulated robotics tasks with varying levels of complexity and physical realism. We evaluate a set of state-of-the-art DRL algorithms, including variants of TD3, SAC, and PPO, across this benchmark, providing a detailed comparative analysis of their performance, sample efficiency, and robustness. Our findings reveal significant performance disparities between algorithms across different environments and highlight key challenges in applying DRL to continuous control. Furthermore, we identify crucial factors influencing the success of these algorithms, such as hyperparameter sensitivity and exploration strategies. This benchmark and the associated analysis offer valuable insights for researchers and practitioners, facilitating the development and evaluation of more effective DRL methods for continuous control. Keywords: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Continuous Control, Benchmarking, TD3, SAC, PPO, Robotics, Performance Evaluation.
