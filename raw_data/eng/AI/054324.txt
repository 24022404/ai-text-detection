This paper addresses the challenge of error detection in noisy knowledge graphs by proposing a novel approach that leverages path-ranking methods to guide graph embeddings. The objective is to improve the accuracy of error detection in knowledge graphs contaminated with noisy or erroneous information. Our approach utilizes a path-ranking algorithm to identify and weight important paths in the graph, which are then used to guide the graph embedding process. The resulting embeddings capture both local and global structural information, enabling more effective error detection. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art approaches in identifying errors in noisy knowledge graphs. The key contributions of this research include the development of a path-ranking guided graph embedding framework and its application to error detection in knowledge graphs. This work has significant implications for improving the reliability and accuracy of knowledge graphs, with potential applications in areas such as question answering, entity disambiguation, and data integration. Key keywords: graph embeddings, path-ranking, error detection, knowledge graphs, noisy data.
