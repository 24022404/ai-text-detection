Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) have achieved remarkable success across various applications, but their computational and memory demands often hinder real-time execution on resource-constrained mobile devices. Weight pruning, a technique to reduce model size and computational overhead, has emerged as a promising solution. However, existing pruning methods often fail to achieve the desired sparsity patterns that align with hardware acceleration capabilities, limiting their practical deployment. This paper introduces **PCONV**, a novel pruning framework designed to address this gap by identifying and enforcing the "missing but desirable" sparsity in DNN weight pruning. PCONV leverages a structured pruning approach that optimizes for both sparsity and hardware efficiency, ensuring compatibility with mobile device architectures. Through extensive experiments on benchmark datasets and models, we demonstrate that PCONV achieves significant reductions in model size and inference latency while maintaining competitive accuracy. Compared to state-of-the-art pruning methods, PCONV improves real-time execution performance by up to 30% on mobile devices, making it a practical solution for deploying DNNs in resource-constrained environments. This work contributes a scalable and hardware-aware pruning framework, paving the way for efficient DNN deployment in mobile and edge computing scenarios.  

**Keywords**: Deep Neural Networks, Weight Pruning, Sparsity, Mobile Devices, Real-time Execution, Hardware Efficiency, PCONV.
