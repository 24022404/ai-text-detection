A micro-services architecture offers a modern approach to building applications by dividing the system into smaller, independent services, each handling a specific piece of functionality with its own logic and database. Unlike monolithic architectures, where all components are tightly connected and updates often impact the whole application, micro-services communicate through APIs and allow for independent updates, deployment, and scaling. This design enhances flexibility, as developers can update or replace individual services without disrupting the entire system, ultimately improving maintainability and scalability for complex applications.
