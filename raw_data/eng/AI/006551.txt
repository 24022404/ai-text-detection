This paper proposes FIDNet, a novel deep learning framework designed for LiDAR point cloud semantic segmentation. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of point cloud segmentation by addressing the challenges of sparse and irregular data. FIDNet employs a fully interpolation decoding approach, which enables the effective propagation of semantic information from sparse to dense regions. The method utilizes a combination of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and graph-based models to extract features from the point cloud data. Experimental results demonstrate that FIDNet achieves state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a fully interpolation decoding mechanism, which enhances the model's ability to capture complex geometric and semantic relationships in point cloud data. The proposed framework has significant implications for various applications, including autonomous driving, robotics, and urban planning, where accurate point cloud segmentation is crucial. Keywords: LiDAR point cloud, semantic segmentation, deep learning, fully interpolation decoding, CNNs, graph-based models.
