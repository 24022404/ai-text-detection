Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) have revolutionized the field of generative modeling, enabling the creation of highly realistic data samples. However, controlling the generation process—referred to as "steerability"—remains a significant challenge. This paper investigates the steerability of GANs, focusing on how effectively and efficiently the latent space can be manipulated to produce desired outputs. We propose a novel framework that leverages disentangled latent representations and introduces a hierarchical control mechanism to enhance steerability. Our approach combines unsupervised learning techniques with adaptive optimization to improve the interpretability and precision of latent space navigation. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves superior control over generated outputs compared to existing approaches, as measured by both qualitative and quantitative metrics. The findings highlight the potential of our framework for applications in image synthesis, data augmentation, and creative design, where precise control over generative models is critical. This work contributes to advancing the understanding of GANs' latent space dynamics and provides a practical solution for improving their steerability in real-world scenarios.  

**Keywords**: Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), steerability, latent space manipulation, disentangled representations, generative modeling, adaptive optimization.
