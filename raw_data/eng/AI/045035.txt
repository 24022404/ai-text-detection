This paper addresses the challenge of efficient reinforcement learning (RL) in complex environments with rich observations. Our objective is to develop a provably efficient RL algorithm that can effectively decode latent states from high-dimensional observations, thereby improving decision-making and exploration. We propose a novel approach that combines latent state decoding with model-based RL, leveraging techniques from deep learning and probabilistic modeling. Our method, termed Latent State Decoding for RL (LSD-RL), learns a compact and informative latent representation of the environment, which enables efficient exploration and exploitation. Experimental results demonstrate that LSD-RL achieves significant improvements in sample efficiency and cumulative rewards compared to existing RL algorithms. Our key findings highlight the importance of latent state decoding in RL, particularly in environments with rich observations. The contributions of this research include a novel algorithmic framework, theoretical guarantees on efficiency, and empirical evaluations on benchmark environments. This work has important implications for applications in robotics, computer vision, and autonomous systems, where efficient RL with rich observations is crucial. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, latent state decoding, model-based RL, deep learning, probabilistic modeling.
