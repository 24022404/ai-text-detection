This paper presents a novel approach to face generation using Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) that incorporates attribute awareness, enabling the controlled generation of faces with specific characteristics. The objective is to develop a system that can produce highly realistic faces while allowing for explicit control over attributes such as age, gender, and ethnicity. Our method employs a conditional GAN architecture, where the generator and discriminator are trained on a dataset of faces with annotated attributes. The results show significant improvements in face generation quality and attribute control, compared to existing methods. Our system achieves state-of-the-art performance in generating faces that are not only realistic but also exhibit the desired attributes. The contributions of this research include the development of a flexible and efficient attribute-aware face generation framework, which has potential applications in fields such as computer vision, graphics, and security. Key keywords: Generative Adversarial Networks, face generation, attribute awareness, conditional GANs, computer vision.
