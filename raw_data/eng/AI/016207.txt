This paper addresses the challenge of few-shot learning in conformal prediction, where the goal is to provide reliable and efficient prediction intervals with limited training data. Our objective is to develop a framework that leverages auxiliary tasks to improve the accuracy and robustness of conformal predictors in few-shot settings. We propose a novel approach that combines few-shot learning with conformal prediction, utilizing auxiliary tasks to augment the training data and enhance the model's ability to generalize. Our method employs a multi-task learning framework, where the primary task is conformal prediction and the auxiliary tasks provide additional supervision signals. The results show that our approach significantly improves the efficiency and accuracy of conformal predictors in few-shot settings, outperforming state-of-the-art methods. The key findings highlight the importance of auxiliary tasks in few-shot conformal prediction, demonstrating improved calibration and robustness. Our research contributes to the development of reliable and efficient conformal prediction methods, with potential applications in areas such as uncertainty quantification, robustness, and trustworthiness in AI models, including those using transformer architectures and deep learning techniques. Key keywords: few-shot learning, conformal prediction, auxiliary tasks, multi-task learning, uncertainty quantification, robustness.
