This paper presents a novel approach to object detection and tracking using a fully convolutional neural network (FCNN) with heatmap-based techniques. The primary objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of detecting and tracking objects in complex environments, particularly in scenarios with occlusions and dynamic backgrounds. The proposed method leverages a heatmap representation to localize objects, enabling precise detection and robust tracking across consecutive frames. The FCNN architecture is designed to process input images directly, eliminating the need for region proposal networks and reducing computational overhead.  

The approach was evaluated on benchmark datasets, including COCO and MOTChallenge, and compared against state-of-the-art methods. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in detection accuracy and tracking robustness, with a notable reduction in false positives and computational latency. The heatmap-based framework also exhibits superior performance in handling occlusions and maintaining object identity over time.  

This research contributes to the field of computer vision by introducing a lightweight yet powerful solution for real-time object detection and tracking. The proposed method has potential applications in autonomous vehicles, surveillance systems, and robotics, where accurate and efficient object localization is critical. Key innovations include the integration of heatmap-based localization with FCNN, enabling end-to-end training and inference without compromising performance.  

**Keywords**: Object detection, object tracking, fully convolutional neural network, heatmap, computer vision, real-time systems.
