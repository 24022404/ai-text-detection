This paper proposes a novel Query-Conditioned Three-Player Adversarial Network (QCTPAN) for video summarization, addressing the challenge of effectively condensing lengthy videos into concise, query-relevant summaries. Our approach involves a three-player framework, comprising a generator, a discriminator, and a query encoder, which interact to produce summaries that balance relevance, diversity, and coherence. The query encoder incorporates user queries to condition the summarization process, while the generator and discriminator engage in an adversarial game to optimize summary quality. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of QCTPAN over state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in summary relevance, fluency, and overall quality. The key contributions of this research lie in its ability to adapt to user-specific queries and its innovative application of adversarial training to video summarization. This work has important implications for applications such as video search, recommendation systems, and content analysis, highlighting the potential of query-conditioned summarization to enhance user experience and efficiency. Key keywords: video summarization, query-conditioned, adversarial networks, deep learning, computer vision.
