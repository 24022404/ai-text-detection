Object proposal generation is a crucial step in many computer vision tasks, including object detection and instance segmentation. While numerous approaches exist, achieving both high recall and computational efficiency remains a significant challenge. This paper introduces a novel sequential optimization framework designed to generate high-quality object proposals with improved efficiency. Our approach iteratively refines an initial set of proposals through a cascade of lightweight stages, each focusing on specific criteria such as objectness, size, and shape. We employ a learned scoring function at each stage to filter out low-quality proposals early in the process, significantly reducing computational cost. We evaluate our method on standard object detection benchmarks, including PASCAL VOC and COCO, demonstrating that it achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of both proposal quality (recall) and speed. Specifically, our method achieves comparable or superior recall to existing methods while requiring significantly less computation time. The proposed sequential optimization framework offers a promising approach for efficient and high-quality object proposal generation, facilitating faster and more accurate object recognition systems.

Keywords: Object proposal generation, object detection, sequential optimization, computer vision, high recall, efficiency.
