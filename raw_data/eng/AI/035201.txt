This paper introduces SI-Score, a novel image dataset designed to facilitate fine-grained analysis of robustness in computer vision models. The objective is to evaluate and improve model performance under varying object locations, rotations, and sizes. To achieve this, we employ a systematic approach to generate a diverse set of images with controlled transformations, enabling comprehensive assessment of model robustness. Our dataset comprises a wide range of objects and scenes, allowing for detailed examination of model behavior. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of SI-Score in identifying vulnerabilities in state-of-the-art models, highlighting the need for improved robustness to object transformations. The key findings and contributions of this research include the development of a standardized evaluation framework and the identification of critical factors influencing model robustness. This work has significant implications for applications requiring reliable computer vision, such as autonomous vehicles, robotics, and surveillance systems. By utilizing SI-Score, researchers can develop more robust models, ultimately enhancing the performance and reliability of computer vision systems. Key keywords: computer vision, robustness, object recognition, image dataset, fine-grained analysis.
