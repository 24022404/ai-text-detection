Content-based image retrieval (CBIR) on 3D brain MRI is challenging due to variations in image appearance caused by diverse MRI scanners and acquisition protocols. This paper introduces a novel disease-oriented image embedding method incorporating pseudo-scanner standardization to improve CBIR performance. Our approach first employs a deep learning model to generate image embeddings capturing disease-specific features. To mitigate scanner-related variability, we implement a pseudo-scanner standardization technique that aligns the embedding spaces of different scanners. Specifically, we train a domain adaptation module to map embeddings to a scanner-invariant space. We evaluate our method on a large multi-scanner dataset of 3D brain MRI scans with various neurological diseases. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing CBIR methods, achieving higher retrieval accuracy and improved robustness across different scanners. The proposed method enhances the clinical utility of CBIR systems by enabling more accurate and reliable retrieval of similar brain MRI scans for diagnostic support. Keywords: Content-based image retrieval, 3D brain MRI, image embedding, pseudo-scanner standardization, domain adaptation, deep learning.
