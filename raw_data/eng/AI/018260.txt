The increasing adoption of machine learning (ML) in cloud environments has highlighted the need for efficient resource optimization to reduce costs and improve performance. This paper introduces *TrimTuner*, a novel framework designed to optimize ML jobs in the cloud through intelligent sub-sampling. The primary objective of TrimTuner is to minimize computational overhead while maintaining model accuracy by dynamically selecting representative subsets of data during training and hyperparameter tuning. Our approach leverages lightweight profiling and statistical sampling techniques to identify optimal configurations without requiring exhaustive full-dataset evaluations. Experiments conducted on diverse ML workloads demonstrate that TrimTuner achieves up to 40% reduction in training time and 35% cost savings compared to traditional methods, with negligible impact on model performance. The framework is particularly effective for large-scale datasets and complex models, making it a practical solution for cloud-based ML deployments. By addressing the challenges of resource inefficiency, TrimTuner contributes to the broader goal of sustainable and scalable ML in cloud environments. Key innovations include its adaptive sub-sampling strategy and seamless integration with existing cloud infrastructure. This research has significant implications for organizations seeking to optimize ML workflows, reduce operational costs, and enhance resource utilization in cloud computing.  

**Keywords**: machine learning, cloud computing, sub-sampling, resource optimization, hyperparameter tuning, cost efficiency.
