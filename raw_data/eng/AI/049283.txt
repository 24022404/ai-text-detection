This paper introduces *ArtiBoost*, a novel framework designed to enhance articulated 3D hand-object pose estimation through online exploration and synthesis. The accurate estimation of hand-object interactions in 3D space is critical for applications in robotics, virtual reality, and human-computer interaction. However, existing methods often struggle with occlusions, complex articulations, and limited training data. To address these challenges, *ArtiBoost* leverages a two-stage approach: (1) an online exploration module that dynamically generates diverse hand-object interaction scenarios in real-time, and (2) a synthesis module that augments training data by creating realistic, high-quality synthetic samples. The framework integrates a lightweight neural network architecture optimized for real-time performance, enabling efficient learning from both real and synthetic data. Experimental results demonstrate that *ArtiBoost* achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The proposed approach significantly reduces errors in pose estimation under challenging conditions, such as heavy occlusions and extreme articulations. Furthermore, the framework’s ability to generate diverse training data on-the-fly reduces reliance on large-scale annotated datasets, making it highly practical for real-world applications. This work contributes to advancing 3D hand-object pose estimation by introducing a scalable, data-efficient solution with broad applicability in interactive systems and beyond.  

**Keywords**: 3D pose estimation, hand-object interaction, online exploration, data synthesis, articulated objects, real-time systems.
