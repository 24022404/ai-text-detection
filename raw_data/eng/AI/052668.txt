This paper introduces Face Transformer, a novel architecture for face recognition leveraging the transformer network to capture global dependencies within facial features. Unlike existing convolutional neural network (CNN)-based approaches that primarily focus on local feature extraction, Face Transformer models long-range relationships between different facial regions, enhancing discriminative power. Our method divides the face image into a sequence of patches and employs a transformer encoder to learn the relationships between these patches. This allows the model to effectively capture subtle variations in facial expression and appearance, leading to improved recognition accuracy. We evaluated Face Transformer on several benchmark datasets, including LFW, YTF, and MegaFace. Results demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance, surpassing existing CNN-based and attention-based models. Furthermore, we present an ablation study to analyze the contribution of different components of the Face Transformer architecture. The findings highlight the importance of global context modeling for robust face recognition. The proposed Face Transformer offers a promising alternative to traditional CNNs, paving the way for more accurate and reliable face recognition systems. Keywords: Face Recognition, Transformer, Deep Learning, Attention Mechanism, Computer Vision.
