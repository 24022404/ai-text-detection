This paper addresses the challenge of accurately predicting semantic object presence and achieving spatial sound super-resolution using binaural audio signals. The objective is to enhance the understanding of complex audio environments by jointly analyzing semantic content and fine-grained spatial cues from binaural sounds. We propose a novel deep learning framework that integrates semantic object prediction with spatial sound enhancement. Our approach leverages convolutional neural networks and attention mechanisms to extract meaningful spatial features while simultaneously identifying objects associated with auditory cues. The model is trained and evaluated on publicly available binaural sound datasets, demonstrating superior performance compared to baseline methods in both object recognition accuracy and spatial sound localization fidelity. Experimental results reveal significant improvements in spatial resolution, enabling more precise separation and identification of audio sources within a scene. The findings have important implications for applications in augmented reality, robotics, auditory scene analysis, and assistive technologies, where both semantic and spatial auditory information are crucial. Our research advances the state-of-the-art in audio scene understanding by linking high-level object semantics with low-level spatial sound details, and paves the way for more immersive and intelligent auditory systems. Key keywords: binaural sound, semantic object prediction, spatial sound super-resolution, deep learning, auditory scene analysis.
