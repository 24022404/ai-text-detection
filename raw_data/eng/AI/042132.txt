This paper presents an innovative approach to 3D human pose estimation from videos, leveraging an attention-based neural network integrated with dilated convolutions. The objective is to accurately capture the complex spatial and temporal relationships in human movements, addressing the limitations of existing methods. Our approach employs a novel neural network architecture that combines attention mechanisms with dilated convolutions, enabling the model to focus on relevant features and expand its receptive field. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in pose estimation accuracy, outperforming state-of-the-art methods. The proposed model achieves enhanced performance in handling occlusions, varying lighting conditions, and complex backgrounds. This research contributes to the advancement of 3D human pose estimation, with potential applications in fields such as computer vision, robotics, and healthcare. Key contributions include the introduction of attention-based dilated convolutions, improved handling of temporal dependencies, and enhanced robustness to environmental factors. Relevant keywords: 3D human pose estimation, attention-based neural networks, dilated convolutions, computer vision, deep learning.
