Multi-view clustering has emerged as a critical challenge in data analysis, aiming to integrate information from multiple data sources to improve clustering performance. Traditional methods often struggle to effectively capture the complex relationships between views or fail to leverage the complementary information embedded within them. This paper introduces **Deep Embedded Multi-view Clustering with Collaborative Training (DEMVCT)**, a novel framework designed to address these limitations. DEMVCT employs deep neural networks to learn low-dimensional embeddings for each view while simultaneously optimizing a collaborative training mechanism that enhances view consistency and discriminative power. The proposed approach integrates a joint loss function that balances view-specific and shared representations, ensuring robust clustering performance. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that DEMVCT outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of clustering accuracy and stability. The framework also exhibits superior scalability and adaptability to diverse data types. The results highlight the potential of DEMVCT for applications in areas such as image analysis, bioinformatics, and social network analysis. This work contributes to the field by providing a scalable, end-to-end solution for multi-view clustering that effectively leverages deep learning and collaborative training paradigms.  

**Keywords**: Multi-view clustering, deep learning, collaborative training, data integration, neural networks, clustering accuracy.
