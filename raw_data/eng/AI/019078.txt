Universal style transfer aims to render content images in the style of any arbitrary style image, but often suffers from slow processing speeds, hindering real-time applications. This paper introduces a novel approach to fast universal style transfer capable of generating both artistic and photorealistic renderings. Our method leverages a lightweight feed-forward network architecture trained using a multi-style adversarial loss and a novel style normalization technique. This allows for efficient style feature extraction and transfer without requiring per-style fine-tuning. We demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of both speed and visual quality, surpassing existing methods by a significant margin in processing time while maintaining competitive or superior artistic and photorealistic quality. Specifically, our method achieves a 10x speedup compared to previous iterative optimization techniques. The results indicate a significant advancement in practical style transfer, enabling real-time artistic effects on resource-constrained devices. Keywords: Style Transfer, Neural Networks, Real-Time Rendering, Image Processing, Deep Learning, Photorealism, Artistic Style.
