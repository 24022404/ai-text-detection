Facial pose estimation is a critical task in computer vision with applications in human-computer interaction, augmented reality, and biometric systems. This paper addresses the challenge of accurately estimating facial poses under varying conditions, such as occlusions and lighting changes. We propose a novel deep learning framework that leverages label distributions to enhance pose estimation accuracy. Unlike traditional methods that rely on direct regression or classification, our approach models the uncertainty in pose labels by learning a distribution over possible poses, enabling more robust predictions. The proposed method utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture trained on a large-scale dataset with annotated pose distributions. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and robustness, particularly in challenging scenarios. The framework achieves a significant reduction in mean angular error compared to existing techniques. Furthermore, we highlight the potential of our method for real-time applications due to its computational efficiency. This research contributes to advancing facial pose estimation by introducing a distribution-based learning paradigm, offering improved performance and broader applicability in real-world settings.  

**Keywords**: Facial pose estimation, deep learning, label distributions, convolutional neural networks, computer vision, human-computer interaction.
