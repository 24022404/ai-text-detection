This paper introduces Part2Word, a novel approach to learning joint embeddings of point clouds and text by establishing correspondences between parts of 3D objects and their descriptive words. The objective is to enable effective cross-modal retrieval and understanding between 3D point clouds and natural language descriptions. Our method utilizes a multi-task learning framework, combining a point cloud segmentation module with a text encoding module, to learn a shared embedding space where parts of point clouds are aligned with their corresponding words. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of Part2Word in achieving state-of-the-art performance on cross-modal retrieval tasks, outperforming existing methods by a significant margin. The key findings highlight the importance of joint embedding learning and part-to-word matching in facilitating semantic understanding and retrieval between 3D point clouds and text. This research contributes to the development of multimodal learning and has potential applications in fields such as 3D modeling, robotics, and virtual reality, with relevant keywords including point cloud analysis, natural language processing, cross-modal retrieval, and joint embedding learning.
