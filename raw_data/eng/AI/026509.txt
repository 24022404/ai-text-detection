This paper addresses the persistent challenge of accurately forecasting operating room data to optimize resource allocation and scheduling within healthcare facilities. The primary goal is to enhance prediction accuracy through the integration of advanced machine learning techniques.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel hybrid model that combines Gradient Boosting Trees (GBT) and Neural Networks (NN) to leverage the strengths of both algorithms. The Gradient Boosting Trees component is utilized for its ability to handle complex, non-linear relationships and feature interactions, while the Neural Networks component contributes its capability to model intricate data patterns. The hybrid model is designed to process historical operating room data and provide precise forecasts, utilizing cross-validation techniques to optimize model parameters and improvements.

Results/Findings: The proposed hybrid model demonstrated superior performance compared to traditional forecasting methods and standalone models of GBT and NN. The integration of these methodologies resulted in a significant reduction in prediction error, with an observed improvement of up to 15% in accuracy metrics. Comparative analysis with benchmark datasets showcased the model's robustness and efficiency in real-world applications, highlighting its potential in managing resource demands.

Conclusion/Implications: The findings indicate that the hybrid approach significantly enhances forecasting precision in healthcare settings, enabling more efficient utilization of operating room facilities. This innovation presents a valuable tool for hospital administrators and decision-makers aiming to optimize operational efficiency. The research provides a foundation for further exploration into hybrid models involving advanced machine learning techniques for various predictive analytics applications in healthcare and beyond.

Keywords: Gradient Boosting Trees, Neural Networks, Forecasting, Operating Room Data, Hybrid Model, Machine Learning, Healthcare Optimization.
