The increasing complexity of neural networks has led to significant challenges in achieving runtime efficiency, particularly in resource-constrained environments. This paper introduces *AntiDote*, a novel attention-based dynamic optimization framework designed to enhance the runtime efficiency of neural networks without compromising accuracy. The proposed approach leverages attention mechanisms to dynamically identify and prioritize critical computational paths during inference, enabling adaptive resource allocation and reducing redundant computations. We evaluate *AntiDote* on a diverse set of benchmark datasets and neural architectures, demonstrating significant improvements in inference speed and computational efficiency compared to state-of-the-art optimization techniques. Experimental results show an average reduction of 30% in runtime latency while maintaining model accuracy within 1% of baseline performance. The framework is particularly effective for real-time applications, such as edge computing and mobile devices, where computational resources are limited. By integrating attention-based dynamic optimization, *AntiDote* offers a scalable and practical solution for deploying efficient neural networks in resource-constrained environments. This work contributes to the growing field of neural network optimization by introducing a lightweight, adaptive approach that bridges the gap between efficiency and performance.  

**Keywords**: neural network optimization, attention mechanisms, runtime efficiency, dynamic computation, edge computing, resource-constrained environments.
