This paper proposes an innovative approach to keyword spotting by introducing Orthogonality Constrained Multi-Head Attention (OCMHA). The objective is to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of keyword spotting systems, which are crucial for various applications such as voice assistants and speech recognition. Our method leverages multi-head attention mechanisms, constraining the attention weights to be orthogonal to reduce redundancy and improve feature representation. The OCMHA model is trained on a large dataset of speech signals, utilizing a combination of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) to extract robust features. Experimental results demonstrate that OCMHA outperforms state-of-the-art keyword spotting systems, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and reducing false alarm rates. The proposed approach contributes to the development of more efficient and accurate speech recognition systems, with potential applications in voice-controlled devices, smart home systems, and automotive interfaces. Key contributions include the introduction of orthogonality constraints to multi-head attention, enabling more effective feature learning and improved keyword spotting performance. Relevant keywords: keyword spotting, multi-head attention, orthogonality constraints, speech recognition, voice assistants.
