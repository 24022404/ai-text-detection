Accurate forecasting of operating room (OR) data, such as surgery duration and resource utilization, is crucial for efficient hospital management and patient care. This paper introduces a novel hybrid model combining Gradient Boosting Trees (GBT) and Neural Networks (NN) to improve the accuracy of OR data forecasting. Our approach leverages GBT to capture non-linear relationships and feature importance, while NN refines predictions through pattern recognition in residual errors. The model was trained and validated on a comprehensive dataset of surgical procedures, encompassing multiple features like patient demographics, surgical team, and procedure type. Results demonstrate that the hybrid GBT-NN model outperforms standalone GBT and NN models, achieving a 15% reduction in Mean Absolute Error (MAE) for surgery duration prediction. Furthermore, the hybrid approach provides more reliable estimates of resource consumption, leading to better scheduling and resource allocation. This hybrid model offers a significant advancement in OR data forecasting, contributing to optimized hospital operations and enhanced patient outcomes. Keywords: Operating Room, Forecasting, Gradient Boosting Trees, Neural Networks, Hybrid Model, Healthcare, Resource Allocation.
