This paper addresses the challenging problem of image degradation caused by haze and glow in nighttime scenes, which significantly affects the performance of computer vision systems. We propose a novel deep dilated convolutional network (DDCN) specifically designed for simultaneous haze and glow removal in nighttime images. Our DDCN architecture incorporates dilated convolutions to effectively capture both local and global contextual information, enabling the network to better estimate the atmospheric light and transmission map, key components in atmospheric scattering models. The network is trained end-to-end using a synthetic dataset generated with realistic nighttime haze and glow effects. Experimental results on both synthetic and real-world nighttime hazy images demonstrate that our DDCN significantly outperforms state-of-the-art dehazing and low-light enhancement methods in terms of quantitative metrics (PSNR, SSIM) and visual quality. The proposed approach effectively removes haze and glow artifacts, enhances image contrast, and restores details, leading to improved visibility in nighttime scenes. This research contributes a robust and efficient solution for nighttime image restoration, with potential applications in surveillance systems, autonomous driving, and other computer vision tasks.

Keywords: Nighttime Dehazing, Glow Removal, Deep Learning, Dilated Convolution, Image Restoration, Low-Light Enhancement.
