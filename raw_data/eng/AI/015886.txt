This paper proposes a novel Hybrid Embedded Deep Stacked Sparse Autoencoder with weighted Local Preserving Projection Distance (LPPD) Support Vector Machine (SVM) Ensemble, designed to enhance the accuracy and robustness of classification tasks. The objective is to address the challenges of complex data analysis by integrating the strengths of deep learning and traditional machine learning techniques. The approach combines a deep stacked sparse autoencoder for feature learning with a weighted LPPD SVM ensemble for classification, leveraging the benefits of both methodologies. The results demonstrate significant improvements in classification performance compared to existing methods, with the proposed model achieving higher accuracy and F1-score on benchmark datasets. The conclusion highlights the importance of this research, which contributes to the development of more accurate and reliable classification systems, with potential applications in image recognition, natural language processing, and biomedical data analysis. Key contributions include the introduction of a weighted LPPD SVM ensemble and the integration of deep stacked sparse autoencoders, making this study a valuable addition to the field of machine learning and pattern recognition, with relevant keywords including deep learning, sparse autoencoder, SVM ensemble, and LPPD.
