The generation of convincing bokeh effects in digital imagery has become increasingly important for both photography enthusiasts and modern imaging applications, particularly within computational photography and smartphone cameras. This paper presents a novel depth-aware image blending technique for generating high-quality bokeh effects by leveraging depth maps and advanced image smoothing methods. Our proposed approach first estimates the scene depth to accurately segment foreground and background regions. Subsequently, adaptive smoothing is applied to the background areas based on their depth values while retaining the sharpness of objects in the foreground. The final bokeh effect is produced by seamlessly blending the smoothed background with the untouched foreground using a depth-weighted fusion algorithm that minimizes visual artifacts and preserves natural edge transitions. Experimental results on a variety of synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms traditional, depth-agnostic image blurring techniques in terms of both perceptual quality and edge preservation. The proposed framework is computationally efficient and easily adaptable for integration into real-time camera systems. These findings highlight the potential for depth-aware, smoothed image blending to enhance visual storytelling and improve the quality of digital images across diverse imaging platforms. Key keywords: bokeh effect, depth estimation, image blending, image smoothing, edge preservation, computational photography.
