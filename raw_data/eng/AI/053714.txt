The automated identification of sheep, along with accurate age and weight estimation, is crucial for modern livestock management, enabling precise feeding strategies, health monitoring, and optimized breeding programs. This paper introduces three novel datasets designed to facilitate research in these areas. The sheep identity dataset comprises a large collection of images of individual sheep, each uniquely labeled. The age estimation dataset contains images of sheep with corresponding age labels, spanning various breeds and developmental stages. The weight estimation dataset includes images of sheep paired with their corresponding weights, captured under diverse conditions. These datasets were constructed to address the limitations of existing resources and to encourage the development of robust and accurate algorithms for sheep biometrics. We describe the data acquisition process, annotation methodology, and dataset characteristics. Baseline experiments using convolutional neural networks demonstrate the datasets' utility, providing benchmarks for future research. These datasets are publicly available to promote advancements in computer vision applications for precision livestock farming. Keywords: Sheep identification, age estimation, weight estimation, computer vision, livestock management, datasets.
