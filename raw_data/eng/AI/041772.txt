This paper presents a novel approach to reduced order Markov modeling of time series data using symbolic analysis. The objective is to develop an efficient and accurate method for modeling complex systems, capturing key patterns and dynamics in large datasets. Our approach employs symbolic analysis to extract meaningful representations of the time series data, which are then used to construct a reduced order Markov model. This model is shown to effectively preserve the essential characteristics of the original system, while significantly reducing the computational complexity. The results demonstrate the efficacy of our method in accurately predicting future states and identifying key system parameters. The proposed technique has important implications for a wide range of applications, including signal processing, control systems, and data-driven modeling. Key contributions of this research include the development of a symbolic analysis-based framework for reduced order modeling and the demonstration of its potential for improving the accuracy and efficiency of time series analysis. Relevant keywords: time series analysis, Markov modeling, symbolic analysis, reduced order modeling, data-driven modeling.
