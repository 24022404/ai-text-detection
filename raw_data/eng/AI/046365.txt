The objective of this research is to address the challenge of point cloud completion, a crucial task in 3D computer vision, which involves reconstructing missing parts of a point cloud to generate a complete 3D model. This paper introduces a novel Skip-attention Network with Hierarchical Folding (SHF-Net) designed to enhance the completion process of point clouds. The proposed SHF-Net leverages an innovative skip-attention mechanism that selectively attends to important features across varying scales, while the hierarchical folding approach incrementally reconstructs point clouds by utilizing intermediate deep representations. This combination aids in capturing both local and global structures, leading to more accurate and efficient reconstruction. Experimental results demonstrate that SHF-Net outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency on benchmark datasets. The findings reveal significant improvements in system performance, particularly in dealing with dense and complex 3D structures. The novel architecture of SHF-Net not only advances theoretical understanding in 3D reconstruction but also offers potential applications in areas such as autonomous driving, augmented reality, and robotics, where quick and precise 3D environment mapping is essential. Key innovations include the integration of skip-attention and hierarchical processes to leverage symmetrical and contextual information, setting a new benchmark in the field of point cloud processing. Keywords: point cloud completion, 3D reconstruction, skip-attention, hierarchical folding, computer vision.
