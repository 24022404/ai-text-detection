Accurate lesion segmentation in medical imaging is vital for diagnosis and treatment planning. Traditional methods struggle with variability in lesion appearance due to size, shape, and contrast. Our research introduces a novel deep attention-guided fusion network to enhance lesion segmentation performance by addressing these challenges effectively.

Methods: This paper presents a deep learning approach leveraging attention mechanisms within a fusion network architecture. The proposed network integrates attention modules to focus on critical lesion characteristics, enhancing the feature extraction process. Multi-level features are fused to provide a comprehensive understanding of lesion boundaries and textures. The model was trained and evaluated using diverse medical imaging datasets to validate its robustness and adaptability.

Results: Experimental results demonstrate that our deep attention-guided fusion network significantly outperforms existing lesion segmentation methods. The model achieves superior accuracy and precision, particularly in complex cases with varying lesion sizes and irregular shapes. Comparative analyses show marked improvements over baseline models in terms of Dice coefficient and Intersection over Union metrics.

Conclusion: The introduction of attention mechanisms within a fusion network framework presents a significant advancement in medical image analysis, specifically for lesion segmentation tasks. This research contributes a powerful tool for medical professionals by improving segmentation accuracy and reliability, which is critical for patient diagnosis and management. Future applications of this approach can extend to various modalities and other segmentation challenges in medical imaging.

Keywords: Lesion Segmentation, Deep Learning, Attention Mechanisms, Fusion Network, Medical Imaging, Feature Extraction, Dice Coefficient.
