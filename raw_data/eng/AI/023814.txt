This paper introduces a novel deep learning approach for understanding and predicting the dynamics of moving vehicles using LiDAR data. Our method, Deep Lidar CNN (DL-CNN), leverages convolutional neural networks to directly process sequential LiDAR point clouds, extracting spatio-temporal features indicative of vehicle motion. Unlike previous methods that rely on object detection or tracking, DL-CNN operates on raw point clouds, preserving fine-grained geometric information and enabling the capture of subtle dynamic cues. We designed a custom 3D convolutional architecture optimized for LiDAR data, incorporating temporal attention mechanisms to weigh informative frames. Experiments on a large-scale driving dataset demonstrate that DL-CNN significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in predicting vehicle speed and trajectory, achieving a 15% improvement in trajectory prediction accuracy. These findings suggest that directly processing LiDAR data with deep CNNs offers a promising avenue for robust and accurate perception of vehicle dynamics. This approach has significant implications for autonomous driving and advanced driver-assistance systems. Keywords: LiDAR, Convolutional Neural Networks, Deep Learning, Vehicle Dynamics, Autonomous Driving, Trajectory Prediction.
