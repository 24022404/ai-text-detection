3D Multi-Object Tracking (MOT) is a crucial task for autonomous driving and robotics. This paper introduces GNN3DMOT, a novel approach leveraging Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) for robust 3D MOT. Our method addresses the challenges of data association in complex scenarios by constructing a dynamic graph representing object relationships across frames. Each node represents a detected object, and edges encode spatial, appearance, and motion cues extracted through multi-feature learning. A GNN is then employed to reason about these relationships and predict association probabilities. We propose a novel multi-feature learning module to effectively integrate different modalities. The GNN is trained end-to-end to optimize tracking performance. Experiments on the nuScenes dataset demonstrate that GNN3DMOT achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of MOTA and IDF1 scores. The results highlight the effectiveness of GNNs and multi-feature learning for robust 3D MOT. This approach offers significant improvements in tracking accuracy and robustness, advancing the capabilities of autonomous systems.

**Keywords:** 3D Multi-Object Tracking, Graph Neural Networks, Data Association, Autonomous Driving, Multi-Feature Learning, Object Detection.
