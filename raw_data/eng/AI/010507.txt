Outlier detection in multi-armed bandit (MAB) problems is a critical challenge in decision-making systems, particularly in scenarios where anomalous data can significantly impact performance. This paper addresses the problem of identifying and mitigating outliers in MAB frameworks, which are widely used in recommendation systems, online advertising, and resource allocation. We propose a novel generic outlier detection approach that integrates robust statistical methods with adaptive learning algorithms to enhance the accuracy and reliability of MAB models. Our method dynamically identifies outliers during the exploration-exploitation process, ensuring minimal disruption to the learning mechanism. Through extensive simulations and real-world datasets, we demonstrate that our approach outperforms traditional outlier detection techniques in terms of both detection accuracy and system performance. The results highlight the effectiveness of our method in maintaining optimal decision-making under noisy and uncertain environments. This research contributes to the advancement of robust MAB algorithms, offering practical implications for applications requiring reliable and adaptive decision-making in the presence of outliers.  

**Keywords**: Multi-Armed Bandit, Outlier Detection, Robust Algorithms, Adaptive Learning, Decision-Making Systems.
