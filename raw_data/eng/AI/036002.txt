This paper introduces Deep Value Networks (DVNs), a novel approach for learning to evaluate and iteratively refine structured outputs. DVNs leverage deep neural networks to approximate a value function that estimates the quality of partially constructed structures. This value function guides a search process, enabling iterative refinement of outputs through targeted modifications. Our method is particularly effective for tasks where direct prediction of complex structures is challenging. We demonstrate the efficacy of DVNs on tasks involving graph generation and combinatorial optimization. Experimental results show that DVNs outperform existing methods by effectively exploring the solution space and converging to higher-quality solutions. The learned value functions provide valuable insights into the underlying structure of the problem, enabling more informed decision-making during the refinement process. These findings suggest that DVNs offer a promising avenue for addressing complex structured prediction problems in various domains. Key words: Deep Learning, Value Function, Structured Prediction, Graph Generation, Combinatorial Optimization, Iterative Refinement.
