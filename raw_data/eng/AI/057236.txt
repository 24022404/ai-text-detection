This paper presents a novel transfer learning approach for heatmap regression to improve the estimation of Action Unit (AU) intensity in facial expression analysis. The objective is to address the challenges of limited labeled data and the complexity of modeling subtle facial muscle movements. We propose a deep learning framework that leverages pre-trained convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to generate heatmaps representing AU intensity distributions. By fine-tuning the model on domain-specific datasets, our approach effectively transfers knowledge from general image recognition tasks to the specialized task of AU intensity estimation. The proposed method is evaluated on benchmark datasets, demonstrating superior performance in terms of accuracy and robustness compared to traditional regression-based techniques. Key findings reveal that our approach achieves state-of-the-art results, particularly in capturing fine-grained intensity variations. The implications of this research extend to applications in emotion recognition, human-computer interaction, and psychological studies, where precise AU intensity estimation is critical. This work contributes to the field by introducing a scalable and efficient solution that reduces the dependency on large annotated datasets while maintaining high performance. Keywords: Action Unit intensity, heatmap regression, transfer learning, facial expression analysis, deep learning.
