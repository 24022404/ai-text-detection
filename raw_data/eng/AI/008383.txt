The increasing complexity of modern power systems necessitates advanced tools for effective management and optimization. This paper introduces Gym-ANM, an open-source software framework designed to facilitate the application of reinforcement learning (RL) in power system management. Gym-ANM provides a flexible and customizable environment for simulating power system operations, enabling researchers and educators to explore RL-based solutions for challenges such as load balancing, renewable energy integration, and grid stability. The framework integrates key power system components, including generators, loads, and transmission lines, and supports the development of RL agents capable of optimizing system performance under dynamic conditions.  

We demonstrate the capabilities of Gym-ANM through a series of case studies, showcasing its ability to simulate realistic power system scenarios and evaluate the performance of RL algorithms. Results indicate that Gym-ANM effectively bridges the gap between theoretical RL research and practical power system applications, offering a valuable tool for both academic and industrial use. The software’s modular design and open-source nature encourage collaboration and innovation, making it a versatile platform for advancing research and education in power system management.  

**Keywords**: reinforcement learning, power system management, open-source software, renewable energy integration, grid optimization, Gym-ANM.
