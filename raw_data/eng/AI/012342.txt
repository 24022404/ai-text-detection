This paper aims to develop a generic representation of random variables, facilitating their effective integration into machine learning frameworks. Our approach involves designing a novel, versatile encoding scheme that can efficiently capture the underlying distributions of random variables, enabling seamless interactions with various machine learning algorithms. We employ a combination of statistical techniques and deep learning methodologies to create a unified representation that accommodates diverse types of random variables. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our proposed method, showcasing improved performance and increased robustness in comparison to existing approaches. The key contributions of this research lie in its ability to provide a standardized, flexible, and scalable representation of random variables, thereby enhancing the capabilities of machine learning models to handle uncertainty and complexity. Our work has significant implications for applications in uncertainty quantification, probabilistic modeling, and decision-making under uncertainty, with potential impact on fields such as artificial intelligence, data science, and statistical learning. Key keywords: random variables, machine learning, representation learning, uncertainty quantification, probabilistic modeling.
