This research introduces an innovative approach to enhance night vision obstacle detection and avoidance using bio-inspired vision sensors. The study addresses the critical challenge of improving night-time navigation in autonomous systems, where conventional sensors fall short due to low-light conditions.

Methods/Approach: The proposed system leverages bio-inspired vision sensors, which mimic the capabilities of the human visual system, particularly in low-light environments. These sensors are integrated into an advanced algorithm designed for real-time obstacle detection and avoidance. The algorithm utilizes dynamic image processing techniques to accurately identify and respond to environmental obstacles under reduced visibility conditions.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that the bio-inspired sensor-based system significantly outperforms traditional night vision technologies in terms of accuracy and response time. The system excels in scenarios with varying light intensities, achieving a higher detection rate and fewer false positives. Comparative analysis shows improved efficiency and reliability in obstacle avoidance for night-time navigation tasks.

Conclusion/Implications: The findings suggest that integrating bio-inspired vision sensors in autonomous systems represents a substantial advancement in night vision capabilities. This research contributes valuable insights into the development of efficient night-time navigation solutions, with potential applications in sectors such as autonomous vehicles, robotics, and surveillance. The novel approach promises to enhance safety and operational efficiency in environments traditionally challenging for night vision technologies.

Keywords: Bio-Inspired Vision Sensors, Night Vision, Obstacle Detection, Obstacle Avoidance, Autonomous Systems, Image Processing.
