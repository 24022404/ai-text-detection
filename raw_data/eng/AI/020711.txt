This paper addresses the challenge of artifact reduction in sparse-view Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) images. The objective is to develop an effective method for mitigating artifacts that arise from insufficient projection data, which can significantly degrade image quality. To achieve this, we propose an adversarial approach that leverages deep learning techniques to reduce artifacts and improve image reconstruction. Our method utilizes a generative model to learn the underlying distribution of artifact-free images and an adversarial loss function to promote realistic image reconstruction. The results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of artifact reduction and image quality, with potential applications in medical imaging and radiation therapy. The key contributions of this research include the development of a novel adversarial framework for sparse-view CBCT artifact reduction and the demonstration of its efficacy in improving image reconstruction. Keywords: CBCT, artifact reduction, sparse-view reconstruction, deep learning, adversarial networks, medical imaging.
