This paper presents a novel approach to learning relational representations by integrating auto-encoding techniques with logic programs. The objective is to develop a framework that can effectively capture complex relationships between entities in a given domain. Our method employs a neural-symbolic approach, leveraging the strengths of both connectionist and symbolic AI paradigms. We utilize a variational auto-encoder to learn compact and informative representations of relational data, while incorporating logic programs to provide a structured and interpretable framework for knowledge representation. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in learning relational representations that outperform existing methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The key findings of this research highlight the potential of combining deep learning and logic programming to tackle complex relational learning tasks. Our approach has significant implications for applications in knowledge graph embedding, relational reasoning, and decision support systems, and contributes to the growing field of neural-symbolic integration. Key keywords: relational learning, auto-encoding, logic programs, neural-symbolic integration, knowledge graph embedding.
