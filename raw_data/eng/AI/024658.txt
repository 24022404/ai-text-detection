This paper investigates the application of Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) to the challenging problem of 3D multi-object tracking in dynamic environments. Traditional tracking approaches often struggle to capture the complex spatial-temporal relationships between multiple objects, especially in three-dimensional space. To address these limitations, we propose a novel framework that models objects and their interactions as a spatio-temporal graph, where nodes represent object detections and edges encode their pairwise relations across consecutive frames. Our approach leverages a GNN architecture to effectively aggregate information from neighboring objects and infer consistent object trajectories over time. Experiments are conducted on standard 3D tracking benchmarks, demonstrating that our method achieves superior accuracy and robustness compared to state-of-the-art baselines, particularly in crowded or occluded scenes. The results highlight the framework’s ability to handle data association and trajectory prediction simultaneously, reducing errors caused by identity switches and fragmentation. In summary, our work introduces an advanced GNN-based solution for 3D multi-object tracking, offering significant improvements in tracking performance and generalizability. The proposed method has broad implications for autonomous driving, robotics, and surveillance, where accurate tracking of multiple objects in complex scenarios is essential.  
Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, 3D Object Tracking, Multi-Object Tracking, Spatio-Temporal Graphs, Deep Learning, Data Association
