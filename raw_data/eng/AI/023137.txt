Data augmentation is a critical technique for improving the generalization of machine learning models, particularly in computer vision tasks. However, traditional augmentation methods often fail to preserve the semantic integrity of objects in images, leading to suboptimal regularization. This paper introduces **SaliencyMix**, a novel data augmentation strategy that leverages saliency maps to guide the mixing of image regions, ensuring that semantically meaningful parts of objects are preserved during augmentation. The proposed approach identifies salient regions in images using a lightweight saliency detection module and strategically blends these regions to create augmented samples. Experiments on benchmark datasets, including CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet, demonstrate that SaliencyMix significantly improves model performance compared to existing augmentation techniques such as CutMix and MixUp. Specifically, SaliencyMix achieves higher accuracy and better regularization, reducing overfitting while maintaining computational efficiency. The results highlight the importance of preserving semantic information in data augmentation and provide a robust framework for future research in regularization strategies. This work contributes to the field by introducing a saliency-guided approach that enhances the effectiveness of data augmentation, particularly for deep learning models in computer vision.  

**Keywords**: Data augmentation, saliency maps, regularization, deep learning, computer vision, SaliencyMix.
