This paper presents a novel approach to saliency detection in Atari agents, leveraging attention mechanisms to identify relevant features in game environments. The objective is to develop a free-lunch saliency method that can be easily integrated into existing deep reinforcement learning architectures without requiring additional supervision or modifications. Our approach utilizes a attention-based model to focus on critical game elements, such as objects, obstacles, or rewards, and generates saliency maps that highlight their importance. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing saliency detection techniques in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The key findings show that attention-driven saliency can improve agent performance in complex Atari games by providing more informative and relevant visual cues. This research contributes to the development of more interpretable and efficient reinforcement learning agents, with potential applications in areas like game playing, robotics, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: saliency detection, attention mechanisms, deep reinforcement learning, Atari games, explainable AI.
