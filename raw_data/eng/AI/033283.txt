This study addresses the challenge of link prediction in N-ary relational data, which is prevalent in complex data environments involving multiple entities and relationships. The research aims to enhance the accuracy of predicting potential links by evaluating the relatedness among entities in such datasets.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel framework for link prediction that leverages a relatedness evaluation mechanism. The approach utilizes an advanced algorithm to assess entity relationships, integrating machine learning techniques to optimize prediction accuracy. The framework incorporates diverse feature extraction methods to capture the intricate patterns present in N-ary data, allowing for a comprehensive analysis of entity interactions.

Results/Findings: The proposed method demonstrates superior performance in predicting links compared to existing models. Through extensive experimentation using benchmark datasets, our model achieves higher prediction accuracy and better robustness in handling complex relational structures. The findings highlight the model's ability to discern subtle entity connections that traditional binary link prediction methods often overlook.

Conclusion/Implications: Our research offers significant contributions to the field of link prediction in relational data by introducing an effective relatedness evaluation approach. This innovation not only improves predictive capabilities but also opens new avenues for applying the framework in various domains such as social network analysis, knowledge graphs, and bioinformatics. Future work will explore the scalability of the framework and its adaptation to dynamic data environments.

Keywords: link prediction, N-ary relational data, relatedness evaluation, machine learning, entity interactions, knowledge graphs.
