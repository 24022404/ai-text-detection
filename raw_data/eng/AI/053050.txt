Facial attribution classification is a critical task in computer vision, with applications ranging from security to personalized services. However, understanding and directing the attention of Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) in this domain remains a challenge. This paper proposes a novel approach leveraging Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) to guide and interpret the attention mechanisms of DNNs for facial attribute classification. Our method enhances the model's focus on relevant facial regions, improving both interpretability and classification accuracy. We evaluate the proposed approach on benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant performance improvements over baseline models. The results highlight the effectiveness of Grad-CAM in directing DNN attention, leading to more robust and explainable facial attribute classification systems. This work contributes to advancing interpretable AI in computer vision, with potential applications in biometrics, healthcare, and human-computer interaction.  

**Keywords**: Facial attribution classification, Deep Neural Networks (DNNs), Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM), interpretable AI, computer vision.
