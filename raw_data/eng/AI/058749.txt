The demand for robust sentiment analysis models is increasing, yet developing these models for low-resource languages presents significant challenges due to limited datasets. This research addresses the problem of enhancing sentiment analysis performance in low-resource settings by employing advanced data augmentation techniques.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel data augmentation framework utilizing Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to generate synthetic data that enriches the available datasets. Our approach involves training a GAN model specifically tailored for text data augmentation, optimizing it to produce high-quality, diverse, and contextually relevant synthetic examples. The augmented datasets are then used to train sentiment analysis models, significantly enhancing their performance.

Results/Findings: Experiments were conducted across multiple low-resource languages, demonstrating substantial improvements in sentiment classification accuracy. The use of GAN-generated data yielded an average performance increase of 12% compared to non-augmented models. Additionally, the method outperformed traditional augmentation techniques by generating more contextually accurate data, effectively bridging gaps in data scarcity.

Conclusion/Implications: The innovative integration of GAN-based augmentation for sentiment analysis in low-resource languages has proven effective, showcasing its potential to improve model accuracy where data availability is limited. This research paves the way for broader applications of GAN-based augmentation in other natural language processing tasks, highlighting the importance of leveraging generative models to bolster low-resource computational linguistics. Keywords include data augmentation, sentiment analysis, low-resource languages, generative adversarial networks, and text generation.
