The objective of this research is to address the pervasive issue of bid rigging, a fraudulent collusion among businesses that undermines fair competition in various sectors. Our study focuses on developing a novel approach utilizing deep learning techniques to detect and flag cartel participants efficiently. Specifically, we propose a model based on convolutional neural networks (CNNs) that can analyze and identify patterns indicative of collusive behaviors within bidding data. The methodology involves preprocessing historical bid data to extract relevant features, which are then input into our CNN model to learn and generalize bid rigging patterns. Our approach outperforms traditional detection methods by achieving higher accuracy in identifying suspicious bidding activities while maintaining robustness across diverse datasets. Key findings demonstrate the model's ability to discern subtle anomalies and discrepancies that are often overlooked by conventional statistical methods. The implications of this research are significant, offering regulatory bodies and organizations a powerful tool to enhance their surveillance and enforcement efforts against anti-competitive practices. By providing a scalable and automated solution, this study contributes to the broader field of economic security, emphasizing the potential of machine learning in regulatory technology. Keywords: bid rigging detection, collusion, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, cartel surveillance, regulatory technology.
