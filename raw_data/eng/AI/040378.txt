This paper addresses the challenge of client selection in federated learning, where the goal is to select a representative subset of clients to participate in model training while minimizing variance in the selected group. Our objective is to develop a novel client selection method that improves the representativity of the selected clients and reduces the variance of the model updates. We propose Clustered Sampling, a method that leverages clustering techniques to group similar clients together and then selects a representative subset of clients from each cluster. Our approach utilizes a combination of clustering algorithms and stratified sampling to ensure that the selected clients are diverse and representative of the overall client population. Experimental results demonstrate that Clustered Sampling outperforms existing client selection methods in terms of model accuracy and variance reduction. The key findings of our research show that Clustered Sampling achieves improved representativity and lower variance compared to random sampling and other state-of-the-art methods. Our research contributes to the field of federated learning by providing a novel and effective client selection method, with potential applications in distributed machine learning, edge computing, and privacy-preserving AI. Key keywords: federated learning, client selection, clustered sampling, representativity, variance reduction, distributed machine learning.
