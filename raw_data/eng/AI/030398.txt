This paper investigates a novel parallelized computational framework for neural network backpropagation using angle-parametrized orthogonal matrices. The research addresses the challenge of efficiently training deep learning models while maintaining numerical stability and reducing redundant computations commonly associated with orthogonal matrix constraints. Our proposed approach employs angle-based parametrization, which facilitates the representation and manipulation of orthogonal matrices while enabling straightforward parallelization across modern computing hardware. Experimental evaluations were performed on standard machine learning benchmarks, demonstrating that our method achieves significant improvements in training speed and resource utilization compared to traditional orthogonalization methods, such as QR decomposition and reorthogonalization techniques. Furthermore, the imposed orthogonality during backpropagation enhances model generalization and stability, resulting in superior performance metrics on classification tasks. These findings underscore the potential of angle-parametrized techniques for scalable and robust neural network training. The contributions of this work include the development of an efficient parallel algorithm, empirical demonstration of training acceleration, and insights into the benefits of orthogonal constraints in deep learning. Key keywords: parallel computation, backpropagation, orthogonal matrices, angle parametrization, neural networks, deep learning, model stability.
