Statistical measures such as percentiles, averages, and standard deviation play a crucial role in analyzing server performance metrics, including requests per second and throughput (MB/s). Each measure offers unique insights: percentiles help identify the distribution of performance, revealing how often a server meets or exceeds specific criteria, while averages provide a general sense of overall processing speed and data transmission efficiency. Standard deviation, meanwhile, indicates the consistency or variability in performance, signaling whether performance is steady or prone to fluctuations. Collectively, these metrics enable a comprehensive evaluation of server efficiency, reliability, and stability.
