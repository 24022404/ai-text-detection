This paper presents comprehensive datasets designed for the tasks of sheep identity recognition, age estimation, and weight prediction, addressing critical needs in modern livestock management and animal husbandry. The objective of this research is to facilitate the development and benchmarking of intelligent computer vision systems that enable accurate identification and biometric analysis of sheep using non-invasive image and annotation data. The datasets consist of high-resolution visual records from diverse sheep populations, labeled with unique identity markers, verified age information, and precise weight measurements. Advanced data acquisition protocols were employed to ensure consistency, quality, and variability under real-world farm conditions. The utility and effectiveness of the datasets are evaluated through baseline experiments employing state-of-the-art deep learning models for facial recognition, regression-based age estimation, and weight prediction. Results demonstrate notable improvements in recognition accuracy and estimation precision compared to existing resources, supporting robust model training and transfer learning applications. This work provides a valuable open resource for academic researchers and industry practitioners, enabling data-driven approaches to livestock monitoring, breeding selection, and welfare assessment. Keywords: sheep recognition, biometric analysis, age estimation, weight prediction, livestock datasets, computer vision, deep learning.
