This paper presents a novel approach to analyzing ancient coin images, leveraging computer vision and machine learning techniques to automatically identify and classify numismatic objects. The primary objective is to develop a robust system capable of recognizing and extracting relevant features from coin images, facilitating a deeper understanding of ancient economies and cultures. Our method employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, fine-tuned for image classification and object detection tasks. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving high accuracy in identifying coin types, denominations, and mint marks. The system's performance is evaluated using a comprehensive dataset of ancient coin images, showcasing its potential for applications in numismatics, archaeology, and cultural heritage preservation. The key contributions of this research include the development of a specialized CNN model for ancient coin image analysis and the creation of a large, annotated dataset for future studies. This work has significant implications for the field of numismatics, enabling researchers to efficiently analyze and interpret large collections of ancient coins, and ultimately advancing our understanding of ancient civilizations. Relevant keywords: ancient coins, computer vision, machine learning, image classification, numismatics, cultural heritage preservation.
