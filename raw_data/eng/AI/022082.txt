This paper presents a novel approach to globally editing images based on textual descriptions, enabling users to modify images using natural language inputs. Our objective is to develop an intelligent system that can understand and execute complex editing instructions, such as changing object colors, adding or removing elements, and altering scene layouts. We propose a deep learning model that combines computer vision and natural language processing techniques to achieve this goal. Our approach involves training a neural network to learn the relationships between textual descriptions and corresponding image edits, using a large dataset of image-text pairs. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, which outperforms existing image editing techniques in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The key findings of our research include the ability to edit images with high fidelity and the potential to apply our approach to various applications, such as image synthesis, data augmentation, and content creation. Our work contributes to the development of more intuitive and user-friendly image editing tools, and has significant implications for the fields of computer vision, human-computer interaction, and artificial intelligence. Key keywords: image editing, textual description, deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing.
