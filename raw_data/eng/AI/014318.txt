This paper introduces a novel curriculum learning strategy for training deep generative models, enhancing both sample quality and training stability. The proposed method leverages a clustering algorithm to organize the training data into distinct clusters, each representing a different level of complexity. The generative model is then trained iteratively, starting with simpler clusters and gradually progressing to more complex ones. This structured approach mitigates the challenges associated with training on highly complex and diverse datasets, preventing mode collapse and improving convergence. We evaluate our method on several benchmark datasets, including image and sequence generation tasks. Experimental results demonstrate that our curriculum learning strategy consistently outperforms standard training procedures and other curriculum learning techniques, achieving significant improvements in Inception Score, Fréchet Inception Distance, and generation diversity. Key improvements are seen in handling multi-modal data distributions. Keywords: Curriculum Learning, Deep Generative Models, Clustering, Generative Adversarial Networks, Training Stability, Mode Collapse.
