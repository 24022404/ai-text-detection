This paper presents a comprehensive study on anomaly detection in video sequences, an essential task in the field of computer vision and surveillance systems. The objective is to establish a benchmark for evaluating different anomaly detection methods and to propose a novel computational model that enhances detection accuracy in diverse video scenarios. Our approach employs a hybrid model combining deep learning techniques with temporal feature extraction to effectively identify abnormal events. Specifically, we utilize a convolutional neural network (CNN) for spatial feature extraction and a long short-term memory (LSTM) network to capture temporal dependencies within video frames. The proposed model is evaluated against standard benchmarks, demonstrating significant improvements in both precision and recall over existing methods. Our findings indicate that incorporating temporal dynamics into the model substantively aids in recognizing subtle and complex anomalies that are often missed by traditional approaches. The research underscores the implications for real-world applications in security and monitoring systems, providing a robust framework for automated anomaly detection in video streams. Notably, the benchmark dataset and source code are made available to facilitate further research and development in this domain. Key innovations include the integration of spatio-temporal modeling and the establishment of a new standard for anomaly detection performance evaluation. Keywords: Anomaly Detection, Video Sequences, Deep Learning, CNN, LSTM, Computer Vision, Surveillance.
