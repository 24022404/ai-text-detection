This paper introduces Talk-to-Edit, a novel approach to fine-grained facial editing that leverages dialog-based interaction. The objective is to enable users to edit facial images using natural language descriptions, allowing for intuitive and precise control over the editing process. Our approach employs a deep learning model that combines computer vision and natural language processing techniques to parse user input and generate corresponding edits. The model is trained on a large dataset of facial images and dialog pairs, enabling it to learn the nuances of language and visual relationships. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of Talk-to-Edit in achieving high-quality, fine-grained edits that meet user intentions. Compared to existing editing methods, our approach offers improved accuracy, flexibility, and user experience. The contributions of this research include the development of a dialog-based interface for facial editing and the introduction of a new paradigm for human-computer interaction in image editing. Key applications of Talk-to-Edit include facial image editing, virtual try-on, and social media content creation, with potential impacts on the fields of computer vision, human-computer interaction, and artificial intelligence. Relevant keywords: facial editing, dialog-based interaction, natural language processing, computer vision, deep learning, image editing.
