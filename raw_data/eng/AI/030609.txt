This paper challenges the prevailing practice of relying on black box machine learning models for high-stakes decision-making, advocating instead for the adoption of interpretable models that provide transparent and explainable results. The objective is to address the limitations and risks associated with opaque models, particularly in critical domains such as healthcare, finance, and law. We propose a novel approach that leverages techniques from explainable AI and model interpretability to develop models that are not only accurate but also provide insights into their decision-making processes. Our results demonstrate that interpretable models can achieve comparable performance to black box models while offering significant advantages in terms of transparency, accountability, and trustworthiness. The implications of this research are far-reaching, suggesting a paradigm shift towards the development and deployment of interpretable models in high-stakes applications, thereby enhancing the reliability, fairness, and safety of machine learning-based decision-making systems. Key contributions include the development of novel interpretable models, the evaluation of their performance in real-world scenarios, and the provision of guidelines for practitioners to adopt interpretable models in their workflows. Relevant keywords: interpretable machine learning, explainable AI, transparent models, high-stakes decision-making, model interpretability.
