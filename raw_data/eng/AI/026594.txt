This paper presents a novel approach to active exploration, leveraging model-based techniques to efficiently discover and learn about unknown environments. The objective is to develop an autonomous system capable of adapting to new situations and making informed decisions about where to explore next. Our approach utilizes a combination of probabilistic modeling and reinforcement learning to guide the exploration process, allowing the system to balance the trade-off between exploiting known areas and exploring unknown regions. The results demonstrate significant improvements in exploration efficiency and knowledge acquisition compared to traditional methods, with applications in fields such as robotics, autonomous vehicles, and environmental monitoring. Key contributions include the development of a flexible and scalable framework for model-based active exploration, as well as insights into the importance of incorporating uncertainty and adaptability into the exploration process. Relevant keywords: active exploration, model-based reinforcement learning, autonomous systems, probabilistic modeling, uncertainty-aware planning.
