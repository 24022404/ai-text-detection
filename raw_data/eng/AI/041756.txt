This paper addresses the challenge of fine-grained visual recognition using web images, which often contain significant noise. We propose a novel framework to automatically eliminate noisy samples and effectively utilize hard examples from web-crawled data. Our method employs a two-stage approach: first, a noise filtering module identifies and removes irrelevant or mislabeled images using a confidence-based selection strategy. Second, we introduce a hard sample mining technique that focuses on informative and challenging examples to improve model discrimination. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing methods in fine-grained classification accuracy. Specifically, our system achieves state-of-the-art performance by effectively mitigating the impact of noisy web data and leveraging the discriminative power of hard samples. The findings suggest that our method provides a robust solution for training high-performance fine-grained recognition models from weakly supervised web images. Keywords: Fine-grained recognition, Web images, Noisy data, Hard sample mining, Machine learning, Image classification.
