**Abstract**

The proliferation of high-dimensional biological data from diverse experimental platforms presents a major challenge for integrative analysis, as measurements from different sources often exist on distinct manifolds. This paper introduces MAGAN (Manifold Aligning Generative Adversarial Network), a novel deep learning framework designed to align biological datasets from heterogeneous domains onto a common latent manifold. MAGAN leverages adversarial training and cycle-consistency principles to learn mappings between domains without requiring paired samples or explicit correspondence, making it suitable for integrating data such as single-cell transcriptomics and proteomics. Experimental results on multiple real-world datasets demonstrate that MAGAN consistently outperforms existing methods in aligning biological manifolds, offering improved accuracy in downstream tasks such as cell type classification and data visualization. The model is robust to noise and scalable to large datasets, making it applicable for a wide range of integrative bioinformatics problems. By enabling more effective and interpretable comparisons across biological conditions and measurement techniques, MAGAN provides a powerful tool for advancing understanding in systems biology and precision medicine. **Keywords:** manifold alignment, generative adversarial network, data integration, single-cell analysis, biological datasets, machine learning.
