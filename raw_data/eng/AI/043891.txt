Image inpainting, the task of filling in missing regions of an image, remains a challenging problem, particularly in preserving fine details and maintaining contextual coherence. This paper introduces a novel Pyramid-Context Encoder Network (PCENet) designed to generate high-quality inpainted images by effectively capturing and leveraging multi-scale contextual information. Our method employs a pyramid-structured encoder to extract features at various resolutions, enabling the network to understand both local textures and global semantic context. A novel contextual attention mechanism is integrated to selectively attend to relevant features across different pyramid levels, ensuring coherent information propagation. Furthermore, an adversarial training scheme is utilized to enhance the realism of the generated content. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that PCENet significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art inpainting methods, both quantitatively and qualitatively, producing visually plausible and contextually consistent results with sharper details and fewer artifacts. This research contributes a robust framework for image inpainting, offering a significant advancement in handling complex image structures and textures. Keywords: Image Inpainting, Contextual Attention, Deep Learning, Generative Adversarial Networks, Pyramid Network.
