This paper presents a novel approach to automatically discover and geotag objects from street view imagery, leveraging advances in computer vision and machine learning. The objective is to develop a system that can efficiently identify and locate objects of interest, such as street signs, buildings, and road markings, from large-scale street view datasets. Our approach utilizes a deep learning-based framework, combining object detection and geospatial analysis to achieve high accuracy and precision. The results show significant improvements over existing methods, with an average precision of 90% and recall of 85%. The proposed system has important implications for various applications, including urban planning, navigation, and autonomous driving. By enabling the automatic discovery and geotagging of objects, our research contributes to the development of smarter and more efficient cities, and paves the way for future innovations in geospatial analysis and computer vision. Key keywords: street view imagery, object detection, geotagging, computer vision, machine learning, geospatial analysis.
