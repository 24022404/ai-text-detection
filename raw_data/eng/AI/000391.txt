This paper presents a novel approach to arbitrary style transfer, leveraging depth awareness and instance normalization to achieve more realistic and context-aware image stylization. The objective is to overcome the limitations of existing style transfer methods, which often fail to preserve the depth and semantic information of the input image. Our approach utilizes a depth-aware neural network architecture, incorporating instance normalization to effectively transfer styles while maintaining the depth cues and content structure of the original image. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our method, outperforming state-of-the-art style transfer techniques in terms of visual quality and depth preservation. The proposed method has significant implications for various applications, including image editing, computer vision, and robotics, where depth-aware image stylization can enhance the visual realism and accuracy of scene understanding. Key contributions include the introduction of depth awareness in style transfer and the effective utilization of instance normalization for improved stylization results, making our approach a valuable addition to the field of computer vision and image processing, with relevant keywords including depth-aware style transfer, instance normalization, neural networks, and image stylization.
