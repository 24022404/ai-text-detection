Video super-resolution (VSR) aims to enhance the spatial resolution of low-resolution video sequences, a critical task in applications such as video streaming, surveillance, and medical imaging. Traditional VSR methods often rely on handcrafted motion estimation and compensation techniques, which can be computationally expensive and prone to errors. This paper presents an end-to-end deep learning framework for VSR that integrates motion compensation directly into the learning process. Our approach leverages a novel neural network architecture that jointly optimizes motion estimation, alignment, and super-resolution in a unified pipeline. By training on large-scale video datasets, the model learns to effectively handle complex motion patterns and occlusions, eliminating the need for explicit motion estimation modules. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets, outperforming existing approaches in terms of both visual quality and computational efficiency. The framework’s ability to generalize across diverse video content highlights its potential for real-world applications. This work contributes to advancing VSR by introducing a streamlined, end-to-end solution that addresses key challenges in motion handling and resolution enhancement.  

**Keywords**: Video super-resolution, motion compensation, deep learning, end-to-end learning, neural networks, video enhancement.
