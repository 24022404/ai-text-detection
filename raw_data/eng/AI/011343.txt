This paper explores the intersection of differential privacy and federated learning, with a focus on overcoming communication constraints. The objective is to develop a framework that enables the training of accurate machine learning models while preserving data privacy and minimizing communication overhead. We propose a novel approach that combines differential privacy mechanisms with federated learning algorithms, allowing participants to jointly train models without sharing sensitive data. Our method utilizes a carefully designed compression technique to reduce the amount of data transmitted between participants, thereby alleviating communication constraints. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach achieves significant improvements in model accuracy and privacy preservation compared to existing methods. The key findings of this study highlight the potential of differential privacy and federated learning to enable secure and efficient collaborative machine learning. Our research contributes to the development of privacy-preserving machine learning and has important implications for applications in areas such as healthcare, finance, and edge computing, where data privacy and communication efficiency are critical. Key keywords: differential privacy, federated learning, communication constraints, privacy-preserving machine learning, edge computing.
