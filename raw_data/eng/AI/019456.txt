Knowledge graph embeddings (KGEs) have become a central technique for reasoning and prediction in knowledge graphs. This paper introduces PyKEEN 1.0, a comprehensively redesigned Python library for training, evaluating, and analyzing KGE models. PyKEEN 1.0 provides a modular and extensible framework built on PyTorch, facilitating both rapid experimentation and the development of novel KGE methods. The library incorporates a comprehensive suite of evaluation protocols, including filtered ranking and link prediction metrics, enabling robust benchmarking of KGE performance. Key improvements in this version include enhanced data handling capabilities, streamlined model configuration, and improved computational efficiency through optimized GPU utilization. We demonstrate the library's capabilities through benchmark experiments across multiple datasets and models. PyKEEN 1.0 simplifies the development and application of KGEs, promoting reproducibility and accelerating research in knowledge graph completion and related tasks. The library is open-source and readily available.

Keywords: Knowledge Graph Embeddings, Knowledge Graphs, Link Prediction, Machine Learning, Python, PyTorch, Reproducibility.
