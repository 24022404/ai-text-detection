Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) is a popular off-policy reinforcement learning algorithm that maximizes both expected return and entropy. The entropy temperature, controlling the exploration-exploitation trade-off, is a crucial hyperparameter for SAC's performance. However, manually tuning this parameter is often time-consuming and sub-optimal. This paper introduces Meta-SAC, a novel approach to automatically tune the entropy temperature of SAC using a metagradient. Meta-SAC employs a bi-level optimization framework where the inner loop optimizes the SAC policy and value function, while the outer loop updates the entropy temperature based on the performance of the inner loop optimization. We derive a computationally efficient approximation of the metagradient to make the algorithm practical. Experimental results on a range of continuous control tasks demonstrate that Meta-SAC significantly improves the sample efficiency and robustness of SAC compared to existing entropy temperature tuning methods and fixed temperature baselines. The ability to adapt the entropy temperature online allows Meta-SAC to achieve superior performance, particularly in complex and non-stationary environments. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Soft Actor-Critic, Entropy Temperature, Meta-Learning, Metagradient, Automatic Tuning.
