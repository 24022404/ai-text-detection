This paper addresses the challenge of scene graph generation by introducing a novel target-tailored source-transformation approach. The objective is to improve the accuracy of scene graph generation by adapting the source data to better match the target scene, thereby enhancing the overall performance of computer vision systems. Our method employs a unique transformation algorithm that tailors the source data to the specific characteristics of the target scene, allowing for more effective object detection and relationship identification. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in scene graph generation accuracy. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of adapting source data to target scenes, leading to potential applications in areas such as image understanding, robotic vision, and autonomous systems. By leveraging this innovative target-tailored source-transformation technique, our work contributes to the advancement of scene graph generation, with implications for the development of more sophisticated computer vision technologies, including object detection, relationship modeling, and visual reasoning. Key keywords: scene graph generation, source-transformation, target-tailored approach, computer vision, object detection, relationship identification.
