This paper addresses the challenging problem of registering noisy point clouds, a crucial task in various computer vision and robotics applications. The objective is to develop a robust and efficient method for aligning multiple point clouds in the presence of noise and outliers. We propose a novel approach based on a globally optimal registration framework, which leverages a combination of advanced techniques from optimization and machine learning to achieve accurate and reliable results. Our method utilizes a hierarchical representation of the point clouds and a robust cost function to handle noise and outliers, ensuring a high degree of accuracy and efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our approach over state-of-the-art methods, with significant improvements in registration accuracy and robustness. The proposed method has important implications for various applications, including 3D reconstruction, object recognition, and autonomous systems. Key contributions include a novel globally optimal registration framework, a robust cost function, and a hierarchical point cloud representation, making this work a significant advancement in the field of point cloud registration, with relevant keywords including point cloud registration, globally optimal registration, noise robustness, and 3D reconstruction.
