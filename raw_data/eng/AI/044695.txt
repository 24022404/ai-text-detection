The paper addresses the challenge of generating accurate and contextually relevant image captions in the realm of marketing analysis. The primary objective is to develop a multimodal approach that integrates both visual and textual data to enhance the interpretability and utility of image captioning for marketing professionals.

Methods/Approach: To achieve this, we propose a novel multimodal image captioning framework that leverages advanced neural networks, integrating a convolutional neural network (CNN) for image feature extraction and a transformer-based architecture for generating captions. The model is trained on a diverse dataset featuring marketing images and their corresponding descriptive texts, ensuring adaptability and precision in various marketing scenarios.

Results/Findings: The experimental results demonstrate that our multimodal model outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of caption accuracy and contextuality, as evaluated by standard metrics such as BLEU, METEOR, and CIDEr scores. Furthermore, the qualitative analysis indicates a significant improvement in the model's ability to generate marketing-relevant captions that align with human interpretations.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a robust and innovative tool for the marketing sector, facilitating enhanced decision-making and strategy development through automated, high-fidelity image captioning. The implications of this work extend to improving digital marketing campaigns, enriching consumer engagement, and driving more effective content management strategies. Keywords include multimodal image captioning, marketing analysis, CNN, transformer, and automated content generation.
