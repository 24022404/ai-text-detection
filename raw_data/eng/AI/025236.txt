This paper addresses the challenge of predicting future instances in a bird's-eye view using data from surrounding monocular cameras. The goal is to enhance autonomous systems' understanding of dynamic environments by accurately anticipating future positions and movements of objects, which is crucial for safe navigation.

Methods: We propose FIERY, a novel framework that leverages deep learning techniques to transform monocular camera inputs into a coherent bird's-eye view representation. The model is designed to handle the complexities of perspective transformation while predicting future trajectories of objects. Our approach integrates a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture with temporal fusion techniques, enabling the system to learn spatial and temporal dynamics efficiently.

Results: Experiments demonstrate that FIERY significantly outperforms existing methods in future instance prediction accuracy. Our model provides robust predictions with high spatial precision, reducing error margins in dynamic scenarios such as road intersections and busy pedestrian areas. Compared to baseline methods, FIERY shows improved predictive capabilities with reduced computational overhead, making it suitable for real-time applications.

Conclusion: FIERY represents a substantial advancement in bird's-eye view instance prediction from monocular cameras. The system's ability to anticipate future instances accurately and in real-time has potential applications in autonomous driving, surveillance, and smart city infrastructure. Our research contributes to the field by offering an effective solution to a complex prediction problem, paving the way for further innovations in visual perception systems.

Keywords: bird's-eye view prediction, monocular cameras, future instance prediction, convolutional neural networks, autonomous systems, real-time applications.
