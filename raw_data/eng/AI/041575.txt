Time series forecasting is critical in numerous domains, yet traditional methods often struggle to capture complex temporal dependencies. This paper introduces Topological Attention, a novel attention mechanism that integrates topological data analysis (TDA) to enhance time series forecasting accuracy. Our approach leverages persistent homology to extract topological features from time series data, capturing multi-scale dependencies and underlying data structure. These topological features are then incorporated into an attention mechanism, guiding the model to focus on relevant temporal patterns. We evaluate our method on a diverse set of benchmark datasets, including financial, energy, and weather time series. Results demonstrate that Topological Attention consistently outperforms state-of-the-art forecasting models, exhibiting superior accuracy and robustness. Furthermore, visualization of attention weights reveals that our model effectively captures long-range dependencies and identifies critical time intervals. This research offers a new perspective on time series analysis by integrating topological insights, leading to more accurate and interpretable forecasting models. Keywords: Time Series Forecasting, Attention Mechanism, Topological Data Analysis, Persistent Homology, Deep Learning.
