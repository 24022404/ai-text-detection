This paper introduces WW-Nets, an innovative dual neural network architecture designed to enhance object detection capabilities. Object detection remains a crucial challenge in computer vision, demanding high accuracy and efficiency across diverse environments. Our research targets performance improvements in detecting varying objects in complex scenes.

Methods/Approach: WW-Nets employs a dual neural network structure that synergistically combines a coarse detection network with a fine detection network. The coarse detection network initially processes input images to identify potential regions of interest with minimal computational overhead. Subsequently, the fine detection network performs detailed analysis on these regions, utilizing advanced convolutional operations to increase detection accuracy and handle complex object features. Both networks are trained in a coordinated manner using a custom loss function designed to optimize end-to-end detection performance.

Results/Findings: Experiments conducted on standard object detection benchmarks demonstrate that WW-Nets significantly outperform conventional single-network architectures in terms of precision and recall. Our dual network system achieves a remarkable balance between accuracy and computational efficiency, reducing false positives and enhancing detection rates of small and obscured objects, as shown through extensive comparative analysis with leading methods.

Conclusion/Implications: The WW-Nets architecture represents a substantial contribution to object detection methodologies by introducing a dual neural network strategy that effectively integrates coarse and fine features. This innovation could be transformative for real-time applications in autonomous vehicles, security surveillance, and robotics, where robust and rapid object detection is essential. Future research may explore the integration of WW-Nets with other advanced AI methods to further elevate the capabilities of visual recognition systems.

Keywords: WW-Nets, dual neural networks, object detection, convolutional operations, computer vision, deep learning, detection accuracy, real-time applications.
