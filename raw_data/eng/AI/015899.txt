Anomaly detection in video sequences is a challenging task with applications in surveillance, traffic monitoring, and event recognition. This paper presents a new benchmark dataset, composed of diverse real-world scenarios, for evaluating video anomaly detection algorithms. We introduce a novel computational model leveraging a hybrid approach that combines convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for feature extraction with a recurrent neural network (RNN) architecture for temporal context analysis. The CNN component learns spatial representations from video frames, while the RNN captures the temporal dynamics and dependencies between these features. We evaluate our model, alongside several state-of-the-art anomaly detection techniques, on the proposed benchmark dataset. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach achieves superior performance in terms of both detection accuracy and robustness to variations in scene complexity and anomaly types. Furthermore, we provide a comprehensive analysis of the strengths and weaknesses of different methods, offering insights into the challenges and future directions for video anomaly detection research. The benchmark dataset and our model provide valuable resources for advancing the field. Keywords: Anomaly Detection, Video Surveillance, Deep Learning, Convolutional Neural Networks, Recurrent Neural Networks, Benchmark Dataset.
