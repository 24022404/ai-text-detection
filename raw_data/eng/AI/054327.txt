This paper introduces Label-PEnet, a novel approach for weakly supervised instance segmentation, addressing the challenge of accurately segmenting instances from images with limited annotation information. Our objective is to develop an effective method that can learn from image-level labels and propagate labels to individual instances. We propose a sequential label propagation and enhancement network that combines the strengths of both label propagation and network enhancement techniques. Our approach utilizes a graph-based label propagation module to generate initial instance masks, followed by an enhancement network that refines these masks through iterative refinement. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of Label-PEnet, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets. The key contributions of this research include a novel sequential label propagation and enhancement framework, and the ability to handle weak supervision with significant improvements in instance segmentation accuracy. This work has important implications for applications such as object detection, image segmentation, and computer vision, particularly in scenarios where annotated data is scarce. Key keywords: weakly supervised learning, instance segmentation, label propagation, network enhancement, computer vision, deep learning.
