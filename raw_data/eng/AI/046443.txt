Graph matching is a fundamental problem in computer science with applications in areas such as computer vision, bioinformatics, and network analysis. This paper explores the integration of deep learning techniques with mixed-integer linear programming (MILP) for solving graph matching problems. While deep graph matching methods have shown promise in learning complex relational patterns, their reliance on continuous relaxations often leads to suboptimal solutions. In contrast, MILP provides exact solutions but suffers from scalability issues. We propose a novel hybrid approach that leverages the strengths of both paradigms, combining deep learning for feature extraction and MILP for precise matching. Our method introduces a relaxation-aware optimization framework that dynamically adjusts the trade-off between computational efficiency and solution accuracy. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of matching accuracy and computational scalability. The findings highlight the risks of over-reliance on relaxations in deep graph matching and provide insights into the design of robust hybrid models. This work contributes to advancing graph matching techniques by bridging the gap between learning-based and optimization-based methods, with potential applications in large-scale data analysis and pattern recognition.  

**Keywords**: Graph matching, deep learning, mixed-integer linear programming, optimization, relaxation-aware models, hybrid algorithms.
