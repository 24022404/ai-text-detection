This paper presents an innovative approach to optical flow estimation, introducing an adaptive mixed norm method that enhances accuracy and robustness in various imaging scenarios. The objective is to develop a reliable technique for estimating optical flow, which is crucial in computer vision applications such as object tracking, motion analysis, and 3D reconstruction. Our approach combines the benefits of different norm regularization techniques, adaptively selecting the most suitable norm based on the local image structure. The method utilizes a novel optimization framework that efficiently handles large displacements and preserves flow details. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our adaptive mixed norm approach over state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The proposed technique has important implications for various computer vision tasks, including video analysis, surveillance, and autonomous systems, and contributes to the advancement of optical flow estimation research. Key contributions include the development of a flexible and efficient optimization algorithm, the introduction of an adaptive norm selection strategy, and the demonstration of improved performance on benchmark datasets. Relevant keywords: optical flow estimation, mixed norm regularization, adaptive methods, computer vision, image processing.
