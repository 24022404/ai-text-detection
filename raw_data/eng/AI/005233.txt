Ranking structured objects is a fundamental task in various domains, from social networks to knowledge graphs. Traditional ranking methods often struggle to capture the complex relationships and dependencies inherent in structured data. This paper introduces a novel approach to ranking structured objects by leveraging Graph Neural Networks (GNNs). Our method, which we call GraphRank, learns node embeddings that encode both the object's features and its structural context within the graph. We employ a message-passing architecture to aggregate information from neighboring nodes, enabling the model to capture high-order relationships crucial for effective ranking. GraphRank incorporates a novel ranking loss function that directly optimizes for pairwise ranking accuracy. We evaluate GraphRank on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in ranking performance compared to state-of-the-art methods, including traditional machine learning algorithms and existing GNN-based approaches. Our results highlight the effectiveness of GNNs for capturing structural information in ranking tasks. GraphRank offers a powerful and generalizable framework for ranking structured objects with applications in recommendation systems, information retrieval, and link prediction.

Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Ranking, Structured Objects, Graph Embedding, Machine Learning, Information Retrieval.
