This paper proposes an innovative approach to point feature embedding using an irregularly tabulated Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) architecture. The objective is to efficiently capture complex geometric and spatial relationships in 3D point cloud data. Our method employs a novel tabulation strategy to irregularly sample and aggregate point features, which are then fed into an MLP for embedding. This approach enables fast and accurate feature learning, outperforming traditional methods in terms of computational efficiency and feature representation quality. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our irregularly tabulated MLP in various point cloud processing tasks, including classification, segmentation, and registration. The key contributions of this research lie in its ability to handle irregularly structured data, reduce computational complexity, and improve feature embedding quality. Our approach has significant implications for applications in computer vision, robotics, and 3D reconstruction, where fast and accurate point feature embedding is crucial. Key keywords: point feature embedding, irregularly tabulated MLP, 3D point cloud processing, geometric deep learning, efficient feature learning.
