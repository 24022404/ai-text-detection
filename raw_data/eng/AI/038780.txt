Reinforcement Learning (RL) has shown remarkable success in various domains, but ensuring safety during training and deployment remains a critical challenge. This paper proposes a novel Lyapunov-based approach to Safe Reinforcement Learning (SafeRL) that guarantees stability and safety while optimizing performance. The objective is to develop a framework that prevents unsafe actions and states, even in uncertain environments, by leveraging Lyapunov stability theory. Our method integrates Lyapunov functions into the RL policy optimization process, ensuring that the system remains within predefined safe regions. We employ a model-free algorithm that dynamically adjusts the policy to satisfy safety constraints without compromising learning efficiency. Experimental results on benchmark environments demonstrate that our approach significantly reduces the occurrence of unsafe actions compared to traditional RL methods, while maintaining competitive performance in terms of task completion. The proposed framework is scalable and applicable to a wide range of real-world applications, including robotics and autonomous systems. This work contributes to the SafeRL field by providing a theoretically grounded and practical solution for balancing safety and performance, paving the way for safer deployment of RL in critical systems.  

**Keywords**: Safe Reinforcement Learning, Lyapunov Stability, Safety Constraints, Model-Free Algorithms, Autonomous Systems.
