This paper introduces a novel deep learning approach for detecting bid rigging in procurement processes, a critical challenge for maintaining fair competition. We propose a convolutional neural network (CNN) model to analyze bidding patterns and flag potential cartel participants. The model is trained on a large dataset of historical bidding data, extracting features indicative of collusive behavior, such as price coordination and bidder rotation. Experimental results demonstrate that our CNN-based approach significantly outperforms traditional statistical methods in identifying bid rigging schemes, achieving a precision of X% and a recall of Y%. Furthermore, the model exhibits strong generalization capabilities when applied to unseen datasets from different industries. This research offers a valuable tool for regulatory agencies and procurement organizations to proactively detect and prevent bid rigging, thereby fostering greater transparency and efficiency in public and private sectors. Keywords: Bid Rigging, Cartel Detection, Deep Learning, Convolutional Neural Networks, Procurement, Collusion.
