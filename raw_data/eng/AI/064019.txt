This paper proposes a novel decentralized online learning framework that enables individuals to leverage others' data without compromising their own privacy. The objective is to track global trends and make informed decisions without requiring direct data sharing. Our approach utilizes a distributed architecture, where participants collaboratively update a shared model using their local data, while maintaining the confidentiality of their own information. We employ a federated learning methodology, incorporating advanced encryption techniques to ensure secure and private knowledge transfer. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our framework in achieving accurate global trend tracking, outperforming traditional centralized approaches. The key findings highlight the potential of decentralized learning in preserving data privacy while promoting collective intelligence. This research contributes to the development of privacy-preserving machine learning and has significant implications for applications in social networks, recommender systems, and edge computing, with relevant keywords including decentralized learning, federated learning, privacy-preserving, online learning, and collaborative intelligence.
