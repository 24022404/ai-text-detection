This paper introduces ResOT, a novel machine learning framework designed to deliver resource-efficient and high-accuracy classification of neural signals. The primary objective is to address the computational and memory challenges inherent to real-time neural data interpretation, crucial for applications such as brain-computer interfaces and neural prosthetics. ResOT leverages oblique decision trees optimized for resource-constrained environments by integrating linear decision boundaries and dynamic pruning strategies. The proposed approach employs a combination of lightweight feature extraction and adaptive splitting criteria to enhance classification precision while minimizing computational overhead. Experiments conducted on benchmark neural signal datasets demonstrate that ResOT achieves competitive or superior accuracy compared to traditional axis-parallel trees and standard ensemble methods, with significant reductions in model size and inference time. Notably, the framework provides robust generalization across varying signal types and noise conditions, making it well-suited for deployment on embedded hardware and wearable devices. In summary, ResOT advances the field of neural signal processing by offering a scalable, interpretable, and efficient solution, laying the groundwork for practical, real-world neural interface systems. Keywords: resource efficiency, oblique trees, neural signal classification, decision trees, embedded systems, brain-computer interface.
