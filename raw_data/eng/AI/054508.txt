This paper addresses the challenge of segmenting glass-like objects in images, a task complicated by their transparent and reflective properties. Our objective is to develop an enhanced boundary learning approach to improve the accuracy of segmentation models in such scenarios. We propose a novel method that combines a refined edge detection algorithm with a deep learning-based segmentation framework, leveraging techniques such as boundary refinement and conditional random fields to better capture the subtle boundaries of glass-like objects. Our approach is evaluated on a comprehensive dataset of images containing various glass-like objects, demonstrating significant improvements in segmentation accuracy and boundary precision compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. The results show that our enhanced boundary learning technique achieves superior performance, particularly in cases where objects have complex or ambiguous boundaries. This research contributes to the field of computer vision by providing a more effective and robust solution for glass-like object segmentation, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, autonomous vehicles, and surveillance systems. Key keywords: glass-like object segmentation, boundary learning, edge detection, deep learning, computer vision.
