This paper introduces a novel approach for detecting errors in noisy knowledge graphs by guiding graph embeddings with path-ranking methods. The objective is to improve the accuracy and reliability of knowledge graph data by identifying likely false statements. We propose a model that combines knowledge graph embedding techniques with path-ranking algorithms to leverage both the global structure and local path information within the graph. Specifically, path-ranking methods are used to identify informative paths between entities, which then guide the learning process of the graph embeddings, emphasizing relationships supported by reliable path evidence. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms existing knowledge graph embedding and error detection techniques in terms of precision, recall, and F1-score. The results highlight the effectiveness of integrating path-based reasoning with embedding models for enhancing knowledge graph quality. This approach provides a valuable tool for improving the accuracy and trustworthiness of knowledge graphs in various applications, including information retrieval and data integration.
Keywords: Knowledge Graph, Error Detection, Graph Embeddings, Path-Ranking, Data Quality, Relation Extraction.
