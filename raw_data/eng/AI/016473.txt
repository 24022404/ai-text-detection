Cervical spondylosis (CS) is a common degenerative condition affecting the cervical spine. Early and accurate diagnosis is crucial for effective management. This paper introduces EasiCSDeep, a novel deep learning model for identifying CS using surface electromyography (sEMG) signals. The model utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture optimized for feature extraction from raw sEMG data collected from cervical muscles. The EasiCSDeep model was trained and validated on a dataset of sEMG signals acquired from both CS patients and healthy controls. The results demonstrate that EasiCSDeep achieves a high classification accuracy of 95%, outperforming traditional machine learning methods and existing sEMG-based diagnostic approaches. The model's ability to automatically extract relevant features from sEMG signals offers a non-invasive and efficient method for CS screening and diagnosis. This research contributes a valuable tool for clinicians, facilitating earlier and more accurate detection of CS, potentially improving patient outcomes. Keywords: Cervical Spondylosis, sEMG, Deep Learning, CNN, Electromyography, Diagnosis, Classification.
