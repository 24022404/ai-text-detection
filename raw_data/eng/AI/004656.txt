The analysis of sequential and time series data is a critical task in various domains, including finance, healthcare, and IoT. However, existing tools often lack flexibility and scalability for handling diverse data types and learning tasks. This paper introduces *Seglearn*, a Python package designed to address these challenges by providing a unified framework for learning from sequences and time series. The package integrates preprocessing, feature extraction, and machine learning into a modular pipeline, enabling seamless adaptation to different applications. *Seglearn* supports a wide range of algorithms, including traditional machine learning models and deep learning architectures, while emphasizing interpretability and efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate its superior performance in tasks such as classification, regression, and anomaly detection, outperforming existing tools in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The package’s open-source nature and extensible design make it a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners. By bridging the gap between theory and practice, *Seglearn* contributes to advancing the state-of-the-art in sequence and time series analysis, with potential applications in predictive maintenance, medical diagnostics, and beyond.  

**Keywords**: time series analysis, sequence learning, Python, machine learning, feature extraction, anomaly detection.
