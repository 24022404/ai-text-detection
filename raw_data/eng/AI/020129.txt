The Monash University, UEA, UCR Time Series Extrinsic Regression Archive (MUTR Archive) addresses the growing need for standardized and accessible time series regression datasets. This archive provides a comprehensive collection of datasets designed for evaluating and comparing time series regression algorithms. Datasets were collected and curated from diverse domains, ensuring broad applicability and relevance to real-world problems. The archive includes detailed metadata for each dataset, facilitating appropriate algorithm selection and experimental design. We evaluated a diverse set of established time series regression methods on the MUTR Archive, providing benchmark results and identifying performance characteristics across different data modalities. The MUTR Archive serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners, promoting reproducible research and accelerating advancements in time series regression. Keywords: Time Series Regression, Benchmark Datasets, Machine Learning, Extrinsic Regression, Archive.
