This paper addresses the critical challenge of enhancing the robustness of autoregressive models by exploring potential vulnerabilities when these models are subjected to environmental manipulations. Understanding such vulnerabilities is essential for developing more secure AI systems, as autoregressive models play a significant role in numerous applications, ranging from language processing to time series prediction.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel approach for identifying and exploiting weaknesses in autoregressive models through strategic environmental manipulations. Our method involves simulating a variety of environmental changes and assessing the model's performance and adaptability under these conditions. By leveraging an innovative attack framework, we systematically alter environmental parameters to locate and maximize impacts on model output.

Results/Findings: The study reveals significant insights into the susceptibility of autoregressive models to environmental shifts. Our experiments demonstrate that the proposed attack method can effectively degrade model performance, revealing critical points where the model fails. Comparative analysis with existing attack techniques highlights the superiority of our approach in terms of efficiency and impact, showing up to a 30% decrease in model accuracy under optimal attack scenarios.

Conclusion/Implications: The findings underscore the importance of incorporating environmental variability in the evaluation and training of autoregressive models. Our research contributes to the field by offering a potent tool for stress-testing these models, paving the way for more resilient AI systems. By understanding and mitigating the identified vulnerabilities, developers can enhance the robustness and security of autoregressive applications against adversarial environmental manipulations.

Keywords: Autoregressive models, environmental manipulation, adversarial attacks, model robustness, AI security.
