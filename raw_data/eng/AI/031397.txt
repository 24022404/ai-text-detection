This paper introduces a novel unsupervised learning algorithm based on competing hidden units within a neural network. The objective is to discover meaningful representations of input data without relying on labeled examples. Our method employs a competitive learning approach where hidden units actively compete to respond to different features in the input. This competition is enforced through lateral inhibition, enabling the network to automatically specialize and extract distinct, salient patterns. We present experimental results on benchmark datasets, demonstrating that our algorithm effectively learns robust and interpretable features. The performance is evaluated by measuring clustering accuracy and feature visualization. Compared to traditional unsupervised techniques, our model exhibits improved generalization and reduced sensitivity to noise. The learned representations facilitate tasks such as data visualization and anomaly detection. This research contributes a scalable and efficient unsupervised learning framework with applications in various domains, including image processing and data mining.

**Keywords:** Unsupervised Learning, Competitive Learning, Neural Networks, Feature Extraction, Lateral Inhibition, Clustering.
