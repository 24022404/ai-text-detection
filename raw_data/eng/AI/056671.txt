This paper addresses the problem of safe exploration for identifying linear systems, focusing on ensuring system safety while learning system dynamics. We introduce a novel robust optimization framework that integrates safety constraints directly into the exploration policy. Our approach leverages a data-driven uncertainty set to model the unknown system parameters, enabling the formulation of a risk-averse exploration strategy. The proposed method minimizes the worst-case deviation from a desired state trajectory, subject to probabilistic safety constraints. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our framework through numerical simulations on various linear systems, showing that our robust optimization-based exploration significantly reduces the probability of constraint violation compared to traditional exploration techniques. The results highlight the trade-off between exploration aggressiveness and safety guarantees. This research provides a practical approach to safely identify and control linear systems in real-world applications, such as robotics and autonomous driving. Keywords: Safe Exploration, System Identification, Robust Optimization, Linear Systems, Safety Constraints, Uncertainty Quantification.
