This paper revives and enhances the SphereFace approach for hyperspherical face recognition, addressing the long-standing challenge of optimizing face representation on a hyperspherical manifold. Our objective is to unify various face recognition methods under a single, coherent framework, leveraging the strengths of deep neural networks and geometric insights. We propose a novel, unified model that integrates a hyperspherical loss function with a state-of-the-art convolutional neural network architecture, enabling more accurate and robust face recognition. Our approach yields significant improvements over existing methods, achieving superior performance on several benchmark datasets. The key findings of this study highlight the importance of hyperspherical geometry in face recognition and demonstrate the effectiveness of our unified framework in capturing discriminative and compact face representations. This research contributes to the advancement of face recognition technology, with potential applications in security, surveillance, and biometrics, and paves the way for further exploration of hyperspherical representations in computer vision and machine learning, particularly in deep learning, face verification, and image classification.
