This paper addresses the challenge of unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) path planning for efficient wireless data harvesting. The objective is to develop an optimal flight path that maximizes data collection while minimizing energy consumption. A deep reinforcement learning approach is employed, utilizing a novel algorithm that integrates UAV mobility and wireless communication models. The method involves training a neural network to learn the optimal policy for UAV trajectory planning, taking into account factors such as data density, transmission power, and UAV speed. Simulation results demonstrate significant improvements in data harvesting efficiency and reduced energy consumption compared to traditional path planning methods. The proposed approach offers a novel solution for UAV-based wireless data harvesting, with potential applications in various fields, including environmental monitoring, smart cities, and IoT networks. Key contributions include the development of a deep reinforcement learning framework for UAV path planning and the demonstration of its effectiveness in optimizing wireless data harvesting. Relevant keywords: UAV, path planning, deep reinforcement learning, wireless data harvesting, IoT, smart cities.
