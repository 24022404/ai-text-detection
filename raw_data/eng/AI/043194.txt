This paper presents a novel approach to single image dehazing, leveraging classification-driven techniques to effectively remove haze from images. The objective is to enhance image visibility and restore original colors, addressing the challenging problem of image degradation due to atmospheric conditions. Our method employs a deep learning-based approach, utilizing a convolutional neural network (CNN) to classify haze densities and subsequently apply appropriate dehazing operations. The proposed algorithm integrates a unique fusion of feature extraction and image refinement, yielding superior dehazing performance. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of image quality metrics such as PSNR and SSIM. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a classification-driven framework for single image dehazing and the achievement of significant improvements in image restoration quality. This work has important implications for various applications, including autonomous driving, surveillance, and image processing. Key keywords: single image dehazing, classification-driven approach, deep learning, CNN, image restoration, computer vision.
