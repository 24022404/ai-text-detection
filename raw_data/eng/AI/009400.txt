This paper introduces a novel, generic representation for random variables (RVs) designed to enhance their usability within machine learning (ML) algorithms. Current ML methods often struggle with RVs due to their inherent complexity and the need for specialized handling. Our approach leverages kernel embeddings to map RVs into a reproducing kernel Hilbert space (RKHS), enabling a unified treatment of various RV types, including continuous, discrete, and mixed distributions. We present a kernel specifically designed for RVs, capturing their statistical properties and facilitating operations such as distance computation and conditional expectation estimation. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our representation in several ML tasks, including density estimation, classification, and regression, showing improved performance compared to traditional methods that rely on restrictive assumptions or approximations. Our experiments, conducted on both synthetic and real-world datasets, highlight the robustness and versatility of the proposed RV representation. The generic nature of this approach offers a significant step toward integrating probabilistic reasoning into a wider range of ML models, fostering more robust and interpretable AI systems.

Keywords: Random Variables, Kernel Embedding, Machine Learning, Reproducing Kernel Hilbert Space, Probabilistic Modeling, Representation Learning.
