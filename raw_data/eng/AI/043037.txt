This paper presents a novel approach to point-based neural rendering, leveraging per-view optimization to enhance the visual fidelity and efficiency of 3D scene rendering. The objective is to address the challenges of rendering complex scenes with high-quality textures and lighting, while minimizing computational costs. Our method employs a neural network architecture that optimizes rendering on a per-view basis, allowing for adaptive adjustment of rendering parameters to achieve optimal results. The approach utilizes a point-based representation of 3D scenes, enabling efficient rendering and reducing the need for expensive mesh processing. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in rendering quality and speed compared to existing methods, with applications in fields such as computer-aided design, video games, and virtual reality. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of per-view optimization for point-based neural rendering, and the development of a flexible and efficient rendering framework that can be applied to a wide range of 3D rendering tasks. Keywords: neural rendering, point-based rendering, per-view optimization, 3D rendering, computer graphics.
