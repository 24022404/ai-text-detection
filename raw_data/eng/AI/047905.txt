This paper introduces MixMix, a novel approach to data-free compression that leverages the synergies of feature and data mixing. The objective is to develop an efficient compression method that does not require access to the original dataset, addressing a significant challenge in modern data-intensive applications. Our approach employs a unique combination of feature mixing and data mixing techniques to generate synthetic data that captures the essential characteristics of the original dataset. We utilize a generative model to produce mixed features and data, which are then used to train a compression model. The results demonstrate that MixMix outperforms existing data-free compression methods, achieving significant improvements in compression ratios and reconstruction quality. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of MixMix in preserving the semantic information of the original data, while reducing storage requirements. This research contributes to the development of efficient data-free compression techniques, with potential applications in edge computing, cloud storage, and data-intensive industries. The proposed MixMix method offers a promising solution for data-free compression, enabling the widespread adoption of compression technologies in various fields, including computer vision, natural language processing, and multimedia processing, with relevant keywords including data-free compression, feature mixing, data mixing, generative models, and compression algorithms.
