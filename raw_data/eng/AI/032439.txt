Partial discharge (PD) detection in insulated overhead conductors is critical for maintaining grid reliability and preventing failures. This paper introduces a novel deep learning framework utilizing frequency and phase attention mechanisms to enhance the accuracy of PD detection from noisy field data. Our approach employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) to extract features from time-frequency representations of PD signals, followed by frequency and phase attention modules that selectively emphasize informative components. This allows the model to effectively filter out noise and focus on subtle PD signatures. We evaluate our framework on a real-world dataset of PD signals collected from overhead conductors, demonstrating superior performance compared to traditional CNN-based methods and other state-of-the-art techniques. Specifically, our method achieves a significant improvement in F1-score, indicating a better balance between precision and recall. The proposed framework offers a robust and accurate solution for automated PD detection, enabling proactive maintenance and reducing the risk of conductor failures. Keywords: Partial Discharge Detection, Deep Learning, Attention Mechanism, Convolutional Neural Network, Overhead Conductors, Time-Frequency Analysis.
