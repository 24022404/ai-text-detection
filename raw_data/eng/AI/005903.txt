This paper presents a novel approach to computing the 2D Least Median of Squares (LMedS) using Graphics Processing Units (GPUs), with a focus on fast and robust line detection in images. The objective is to improve the efficiency and accuracy of LMedS, a robust estimation technique commonly used in computer vision. Our method leverages the parallel processing capabilities of GPUs to accelerate the computation of LMedS, resulting in significant performance gains. The approach involves a parallelized algorithm that takes advantage of the massive parallelism offered by modern GPUs, allowing for real-time processing of large datasets. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our GPU-based LMedS computation, achieving speeds up to 10 times faster than traditional CPU-based implementations. The proposed method has important implications for various computer vision applications, including line detection, feature extraction, and robust estimation. Key contributions include the development of a highly efficient and scalable LMedS computation framework, enabling fast and robust line detection in images. Relevant keywords: GPU computing, Least Median of Squares, line detection, computer vision, robust estimation, parallel processing.
