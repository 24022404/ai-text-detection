The objective of this research is to address the limitations of current depth estimation techniques from single-view images by introducing CAM-Convs: Camera-Aware Multi-Scale Convolutions. This novel approach recognizes the significant role of camera parameters in obtaining accurate depth information. We propose a multi-scale convolutional model that integrates these parameters directly into the convolution operations, enhancing the network’s ability to adjust for variations across different camera settings. Our method leverages convolutional neural networks (CNNs) at varying scales to capture both local and global depth cues, producing more robust and precise depth predictions. The findings from our experiments indicate that CAM-Convs outperforms traditional single-view depth estimation models, achieving superior results in standard benchmarks with a noticeable increase in depth accuracy and consistency. Through extensive comparative analysis, we demonstrate that incorporating camera-aware elements into the network notably improves its adaptability and performance across diverse datasets and camera specifications. The contributions of this study not only advance the state-of-the-art in single-view depth estimation but also open new pathways for developing camera-aware systems in 3D computer vision. Potential applications include augmented reality, robotics, and autonomous driving, where accurate and reliable depth perception is critical. Keywords: depth estimation, single-view, multi-scale convolutions, camera-aware, CNN, 3D computer vision.
