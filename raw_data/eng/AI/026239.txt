This paper introduces a novel approach to synthesizing realistic river images using deep learning techniques. The primary objective is to generate high-quality, photorealistic images of rivers, addressing the limitations of existing methods in capturing the dynamic and complex textures of natural water bodies. We employ a Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) architecture, enhanced with spectral normalization and attention mechanisms, to improve the stability of training and the fidelity of generated images. The discriminator incorporates a multi-scale gradient loss to enforce realistic edge details and overall image consistency. Our results demonstrate that the proposed model outperforms existing GAN-based image synthesis methods, producing river images with enhanced visual realism, including realistic water reflections, surface textures, and environmental integration. Quantitative evaluations using Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) and qualitative assessments confirm the superior performance of our approach. This research contributes to the field of computer graphics and environmental modeling by providing a robust tool for generating realistic river images, which can be applied in various applications such as virtual environment creation, data augmentation for river monitoring, and flood simulation. Keywords: GAN, image synthesis, river, deep learning, computer graphics, generative models, photorealistic rendering.
