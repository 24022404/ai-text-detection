Temporal networks, which capture dynamic interactions over time, are increasingly important in modeling real-world systems such as social networks, communication systems, and biological networks. This paper addresses the challenge of effectively embedding temporal networks into low-dimensional representations while preserving their dynamic structural properties. We propose a novel approach based on tensor factorization, which leverages the multi-dimensional nature of temporal data to capture both temporal and structural dependencies. Our method decomposes the temporal network into a series of tensors, enabling the extraction of latent features that represent the evolving relationships between nodes. Experimental evaluations on real-world datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of link prediction accuracy and temporal consistency. The results highlight the effectiveness of tensor factorization in capturing complex temporal patterns, offering a robust framework for analyzing dynamic networks. This research contributes to the advancement of temporal network analysis by providing a scalable and interpretable embedding technique with potential applications in anomaly detection, recommendation systems, and network forecasting.  

**Keywords**: Temporal networks, tensor factorization, network embedding, dynamic systems, link prediction.
