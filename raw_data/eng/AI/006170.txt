This study addresses the critical challenge of quality control in image segmentation, focusing specifically on cardiac MRI data from the UK Biobank project. The objective is to develop and validate an automated system that ensures the precision and reliability of image segmentation outputs, which are essential for large-scale epidemiological studies and comprehensive data analysis.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel automated quality control approach leveraging machine learning techniques, including convolutional neural networks (CNNs), to evaluate the accuracy of cardiac MRI segmentations. Our model is trained on a diverse dataset representative of the UK Biobank repository, incorporating robust image preprocessing and data augmentation strategies to enhance model performance. The system is designed to automatically detect and flag segmentation anomalies, promoting reliable data utilization.

Results/Findings: The automated quality control system demonstrated a high level of accuracy in identifying segmentation errors compared to manual assessment, achieving a significant reduction in false positives. The model's performance was benchmarked against existing quality control methods, showcasing superior detection capabilities and efficiency. Notably, when applied to a large subset of the UK Biobank cardiac MRI data, our system promptly highlighted segmentation inconsistencies, underscoring its practical utility in real-world applications.

Conclusion/Implications: The findings highlight a significant advancement in automating quality assurance in image segmentation for large-scale medical datasets. The proposed system not only enhances data integrity but also supports efficient and scalable processing of cardiac MRI data, thereby facilitating more reliable epidemiological analysis and scientific insights. This approach sets the stage for broader application across various imaging modalities and studies, marking a pivotal shift towards automated quality control in medical imaging research.

Key Keywords: Automated Quality Control, Image Segmentation, Cardiac MRI, UK Biobank, Machine Learning, Convolutional Neural Networks, Medical Imaging, Epidemiological Studies.
