This paper introduces MailLeak, a novel approach to character extraction from scanned documents, leveraging transfer learning to overcome obfuscation techniques. The objective is to develop a robust method for extracting text from images of documents, even when intentional obfuscation methods are applied to protect sensitive information. MailLeak employs a deep learning-based approach, utilizing pre-trained models and fine-tuning them on a dataset of obfuscated documents. The results demonstrate significant improvements in character extraction accuracy, outperforming existing methods on a range of obfuscation techniques. Key findings include the ability to extract text with high accuracy, even when documents are intentionally distorted or degraded. The implications of this research are substantial, with potential applications in document analysis, information retrieval, and security. MailLeak contributes to the field by providing a robust and effective solution for character extraction, highlighting the importance of transfer learning in overcoming obfuscation techniques. Relevant keywords include: character extraction, transfer learning, obfuscation, document analysis, and information security.
