Visual Question Answering (VQA) systems have made significant strides in recent years, yet they often struggle with tasks requiring external knowledge beyond the visual content. This paper introduces *Select, Substitute, Search (S³)*, a novel benchmark designed to evaluate the ability of VQA models to integrate external knowledge effectively. The S³ benchmark challenges models to perform three critical tasks: selecting relevant knowledge from a given context, substituting ambiguous or incomplete information with accurate external data, and searching for missing knowledge to answer complex questions. We propose a hybrid approach combining transformer-based architectures with dynamic knowledge retrieval mechanisms to address these challenges. Our experiments demonstrate that the proposed method achieves a 12% improvement in accuracy over state-of-the-art baselines on the S³ benchmark. The results highlight the importance of robust knowledge integration in VQA systems and provide insights into the limitations of current approaches. This work contributes a new evaluation framework for knowledge-augmented VQA and advances the development of models capable of handling real-world, knowledge-intensive visual reasoning tasks.  

**Keywords**: Visual Question Answering, Knowledge Integration, Transformer Models, Benchmark Evaluation, Knowledge Retrieval.
