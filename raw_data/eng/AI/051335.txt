The rapid advancement of automotive cyber-physical systems (CPS) has heightened the demand for efficient and accurate scene understanding to ensure safety and performance. This paper addresses the challenge of deploying deep neural networks (DNNs) in resource-constrained automotive environments, where computational efficiency is critical. We propose a novel framework that accelerates DNN inference while maintaining high accuracy for tasks such as object detection, semantic segmentation, and trajectory prediction. Our approach integrates model compression techniques, including quantization and pruning, with hardware-aware optimizations tailored for embedded systems. Additionally, we introduce a lightweight attention mechanism to enhance feature extraction without significantly increasing computational overhead. Experimental results demonstrate that our framework achieves a 40% reduction in inference time and a 30% decrease in memory usage compared to state-of-the-art models, while maintaining competitive accuracy on benchmark datasets. The proposed solution is validated in real-world automotive CPS scenarios, showcasing its practicality for deployment in autonomous vehicles and advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS). This work contributes to the field by bridging the gap between high-performance DNNs and the stringent resource constraints of automotive CPS, paving the way for safer and more efficient intelligent transportation systems.  

**Keywords**: deep neural networks, automotive cyber-physical systems, model compression, scene understanding, embedded systems, autonomous vehicles.
