This paper presents a novel approach to combining graphical models with neural networks, enabling the creation of structured representations that facilitate fast and accurate inference. The objective is to leverage the strengths of both paradigms, where graphical models provide a robust framework for modeling complex relationships, and neural networks offer a powerful tool for learning rich representations. Our method employs a modular architecture that composes graphical models with neural networks, allowing for the integration of domain knowledge and the learning of probabilistic dependencies. The results demonstrate significant improvements in inference speed and representation quality, outperforming existing approaches on several benchmark datasets. The key contributions of this work include the development of a flexible and scalable framework for composing graphical models with neural networks, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in a range of applications, including image and text analysis. Our approach has important implications for the development of more efficient and interpretable AI models, and highlights the potential of structured representations for fast and accurate inference. Key keywords: graphical models, neural networks, structured representations, fast inference, probabilistic dependencies, modular architecture.
