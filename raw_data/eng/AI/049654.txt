Image super-resolution (SR) aims to reconstruct high-resolution (HR) images from their low-resolution (LR) counterparts. Deep learning-based SR methods have achieved remarkable performance but often suffer from high computational costs and large model sizes, limiting their deployment on resource-constrained devices. This paper introduces a lightweight image super-resolution network called Adaptive Weighted Learning Network (AWLN) designed for efficient and effective SR. AWLN employs a novel adaptive weighted learning strategy that dynamically adjusts the importance of different feature channels, enabling the network to focus on more informative features and suppress noise. The network incorporates a streamlined architecture with depthwise separable convolutions and efficient channel attention mechanisms to reduce computational complexity. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that AWLN achieves comparable or superior SR performance to state-of-the-art lightweight SR methods while significantly reducing the number of parameters and computational cost. The proposed AWLN offers a practical solution for deploying high-quality image SR on mobile devices and other resource-limited platforms. Keywords: Image Super-Resolution, Lightweight Network, Deep Learning, Adaptive Weighted Learning, Mobile Devices.
