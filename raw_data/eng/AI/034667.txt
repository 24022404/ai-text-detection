Semi-supervised transfer learning (SSTL) leverages knowledge from a labeled source domain to improve performance on a related, but unlabeled, target domain. However, domain shift and limited target labels pose significant challenges. This paper introduces an adaptive consistency regularization (ACR) approach to address these challenges. ACR dynamically adjusts the consistency regularization strength based on the uncertainty of target domain predictions. Specifically, we employ an entropy-based metric to quantify prediction uncertainty and modulate the regularization, encouraging consistent predictions for confident samples while allowing flexibility for uncertain ones. This adaptive approach mitigates the risk of over-regularization in the presence of domain shift. We evaluate ACR on several benchmark SSTL datasets, demonstrating significant performance gains compared to state-of-the-art methods. The results indicate that ACR effectively leverages unlabeled target data by promoting robust and consistent learning, even with substantial domain divergence. Keywords: Semi-Supervised Learning, Transfer Learning, Consistency Regularization, Domain Adaptation, Machine Learning.
