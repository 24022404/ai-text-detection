This paper presents an innovative approach to belief propagation, a fundamental algorithm in probabilistic graphical models, by introducing anytime belief propagation using sparse domains. The objective is to improve the efficiency and scalability of belief propagation in large-scale networks. Our method leverages sparse domain representations to reduce computational complexity, enabling anytime inference that can provide approximate solutions at any point during the computation. We employ a novel sparse domain construction technique, which adaptively prunes irrelevant variables and factors, resulting in significant speedups without sacrificing accuracy. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming traditional belief propagation methods in terms of computational efficiency and accuracy. The key contributions of this research include the development of a sparse domain-based anytime belief propagation algorithm, which has significant implications for real-time applications, such as computer vision, robotics, and artificial intelligence. Our approach combines probabilistic graphical models, sparse representations, and anytime algorithms, making it a valuable contribution to the field of machine learning and AI. Key keywords: belief propagation, sparse domains, anytime algorithms, probabilistic graphical models, machine learning, artificial intelligence.
