This paper introduces BDgraph, an R package designed to facilitate Bayesian structure learning in graphical models. The package aims to address the challenges associated with determining probabilistic dependencies among a set of variables, which is a critical task in various domains, including bioinformatics, social network analysis, and economics.

Methods/Approach: BDgraph employs advanced Bayesian methodologies to perform estimation and model selection for graphical models. The package utilizes Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) algorithms and employs efficient sampling techniques to explore the space of possible graph structures. BDgraph provides users with the flexibility to incorporate domain knowledge in the form of informative priors and supports both Gaussian and non-Gaussian data through customizable model specifications.

Results/Findings: Through extensive simulations and real-world applications, BDgraph demonstrates superior performance in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency compared to existing tools. The package achieves robust structure learning even with high-dimensional datasets, providing insights into underlying data relationships that are often missed by traditional approaches. Benchmark comparisons show that BDgraph improves upon existing methods, particularly in terms of scalability and adaptability.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of BDgraph significantly contributes to the field of graphical model structure learning by offering a comprehensive, user-friendly tool equipped with state-of-the-art Bayesian inference techniques. The package's modular design and extensive documentation ensure ease of use for researchers and practitioners alike. BDgraph opens new opportunities for advanced data analysis and inference, paving the way for more informed decision-making and deeper insights across varied applications. Key keywords include Bayesian structure learning, graphical models, MCMC, R package, and data dependencies.
