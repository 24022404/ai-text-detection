Salient object detection (SOD) is a critical task in computer vision, aiming to identify and segment the most visually distinctive objects in an image. Despite significant progress, existing methods often struggle with complex scenes where objects exhibit varying scales, occlusions, or low contrast. To address these challenges, we propose SAC-Net, a novel deep learning framework that leverages Spatial Attenuation Context to enhance salient object detection. SAC-Net integrates a multi-scale feature extraction module with a context-aware attention mechanism, enabling the model to dynamically focus on salient regions while suppressing irrelevant background information. The proposed spatial attenuation mechanism adaptively adjusts feature weights across different scales, improving the model's ability to handle objects of varying sizes and complex spatial relationships. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that SAC-Net outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and robustness, achieving superior performance in challenging scenarios. The results highlight the effectiveness of the spatial attenuation context in improving feature representation and object localization. SAC-Net's contributions include a novel architecture design and a scalable approach to salient object detection, with potential applications in image segmentation, video analysis, and autonomous systems.  

**Keywords**: Salient object detection, spatial attenuation, context-aware attention, deep learning, computer vision, multi-scale feature extraction.
