This paper proposes a novel local active contour model for image segmentation, specifically designed to address intensity inhomogeneity challenges. The objective is to develop a robust and efficient method for segmenting images with varying intensity levels, which is a common issue in medical and natural image processing. Our approach utilizes a local intensity clustering technique to guide the active contour evolution, allowing for more accurate segmentation of regions with intensity inhomogeneity. The method is based on a variational level set formulation, which enables the capture of complex object boundaries. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our model in segmenting images with intensity inhomogeneity, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a local intensity-based energy term and a novel contour evolution scheme, which improve the robustness and accuracy of image segmentation. This work has significant implications for various applications, including medical image analysis, object detection, and image editing, with potential applications in computer vision, machine learning, and image processing. Key keywords: image segmentation, active contour model, intensity inhomogeneity, level set method, local clustering, computer vision.
