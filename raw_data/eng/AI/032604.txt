Understanding the dynamics of moving vehicles is crucial for the advancement of intelligent transportation systems and autonomous driving. This paper presents a novel approach leveraging deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) trained on Lidar sensor data to analyze and predict vehicle motion with high accuracy. Our methodology integrates spatial-temporal Lidar point cloud sequences as input to a tailored CNN architecture, enabling robust feature extraction and dynamic object tracking in real-time. We propose a specialized network design optimized for three-dimensional data, incorporating layers that efficiently encode geometric and motion cues crucial for vehicular context. Experiments were conducted using a benchmark dataset comprising diverse urban and highway scenarios, where the proposed deep Lidar CNN outperformed existing state-of-the-art methods in both trajectory prediction and classification tasks. Quantitative results demonstrate significant improvements in detection accuracy, speed, and resilience to sensor noise. The study's findings highlight the effectiveness of deep learning techniques in processing Lidar data for dynamic scene understanding. Our contributions pave the way for more reliable motion analysis in self-driving vehicles, traffic monitoring, and advanced driver-assistance systems. Keywords: deep learning, convolutional neural network, Lidar, vehicle dynamics, 3D object detection, autonomous driving, motion prediction.
