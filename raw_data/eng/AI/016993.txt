Forecasting future interactions between hands and objects is a challenging problem crucial for various applications, including robotics and augmented reality. This paper presents a novel approach to predict the future locations and poses of both hands and manipulated objects in video sequences. We propose a multi-modal, end-to-end trainable framework that leverages a spatio-temporal graph convolutional network (ST-GCN) to model the complex relationships and dynamics between hands and objects. Our model incorporates attention mechanisms to focus on salient features and predict multiple plausible future scenarios. We evaluate our approach on a challenging benchmark dataset, demonstrating significant improvements in prediction accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods. The results highlight the effectiveness of our graph-based representation and attention mechanism in capturing the intricate interplay between hands and objects for accurate future forecasting. This research contributes a robust and efficient method for anticipating human-object interactions, paving the way for more intuitive and responsive AI systems.
Keywords: Hand-Object Interaction, Future Prediction, Spatio-Temporal Graph Convolutional Network, Attention Mechanism, Video Forecasting, Robotics.
