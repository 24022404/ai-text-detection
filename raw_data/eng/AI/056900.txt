Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) is a fundamental matrix factorization technique with widespread applications in data analysis and machine learning.  This paper addresses the challenge of computing the SVD of a large matrix distributed across multiple computing nodes, focusing on minimizing communication overhead.  We propose a novel distributed SVD algorithm that leverages local power iterations on each node to estimate the dominant singular vectors.  By performing power iterations locally and only exchanging intermediate vector estimates periodically, we significantly reduce inter-node communication compared to traditional distributed SVD approaches.  Theoretical analysis demonstrates that our algorithm achieves comparable accuracy to centralized SVD with significantly reduced communication costs, particularly for matrices with rapidly decaying singular values.  Experimental results on synthetic and real-world datasets validate the communication efficiency and scalability of our approach.  The proposed algorithm offers a practical solution for large-scale SVD computations in communication-constrained distributed environments.

Keywords: Distributed SVD, Power Iteration, Communication Efficiency, Large-Scale Matrix Factorization, Distributed Algorithms.
