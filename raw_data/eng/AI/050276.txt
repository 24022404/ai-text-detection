Earlier activation functions such as sigmoid and tanh often faced the issue of saturation, meaning their outputs would approach values close to 1 or 0 (or -1 for tanh) when given large input values. As a result, these functions were only sensitive to inputs near the center of their range, where the gradient was substantial; outside this zone, gradients became extremely small, which hindered the model’s ability to learn effectively—a challenge known as the vanishing gradient problem.
