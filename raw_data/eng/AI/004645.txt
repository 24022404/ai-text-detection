Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) has shown promise in solving complex control tasks, but ensuring safety during exploration remains a critical challenge. This paper introduces a novel approach, Safety-Guided Deep Reinforcement Learning via Online Gaussian Process Estimation (SG-DRL-GP), to address this issue. Our method integrates a Gaussian Process (GP) model, learned online, to estimate safety constraints and guide exploration. Specifically, the GP predicts the probability of constraint violation, allowing the DRL agent to prioritize actions that minimize risk while maximizing reward. We employ a Lyapunov-based safety filter that leverages the GP's uncertainty quantification to dynamically adjust the agent's policy, guaranteeing safety with a high probability. Experiments in benchmark environments demonstrate that SG-DRL-GP significantly improves safety performance compared to state-of-the-art DRL algorithms, while maintaining competitive reward acquisition. The results highlight the effectiveness of online GP estimation for enabling safe and efficient exploration in DRL. Keywords: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Safety, Gaussian Process, Lyapunov Stability, Online Learning, Safe Exploration.
