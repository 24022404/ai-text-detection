This paper introduces Globally-scalable Automated Target Recognition (GATR), a novel framework for robust and efficient object detection across diverse geographic locations and environmental conditions. The objective of GATR is to overcome the limitations of existing automated target recognition systems that often struggle with variations in image quality, lighting, and object appearance across different global regions. Our approach leverages a combination of self-supervised learning for feature extraction and a geographically-aware meta-learning algorithm for model adaptation. GATR achieves state-of-the-art performance on a newly curated, large-scale global target recognition dataset, demonstrating a 20% improvement in average precision compared to existing methods, particularly in challenging low-resource environments. Key findings reveal the importance of incorporating geographic context into the model training process for enhanced generalization. GATR's scalability and adaptability make it suitable for a wide range of applications, including remote sensing, disaster response, and autonomous navigation. Keywords: Automated Target Recognition, Object Detection, Meta-Learning, Self-Supervised Learning, Remote Sensing, Global Scalability.
