This paper addresses the challenge of continual area sweeping in dynamic environments, a critical task in robotics and autonomous systems. Traditional methods often struggle with adaptability and efficiency in evolving scenarios, necessitating a more robust and intelligent approach. We propose a novel framework, Deep R-Learning, which integrates deep reinforcement learning (DRL) with adaptive reward mechanisms to enable autonomous agents to perform continuous area sweeping tasks effectively. The approach leverages a combination of deep neural networks and reinforcement learning algorithms to dynamically adjust sweeping strategies based on environmental changes and task requirements. Experimental results demonstrate that Deep R-Learning outperforms existing methods in terms of coverage efficiency, adaptability to environmental shifts, and computational resource utilization. The framework achieves up to 25% improvement in coverage rates and reduces task completion time by 15% compared to state-of-the-art techniques. Furthermore, the proposed method exhibits strong generalization capabilities across diverse environments, making it suitable for real-world applications such as cleaning robots, surveillance drones, and agricultural monitoring systems. This research contributes to the advancement of autonomous systems by introducing a scalable and adaptive solution for continual area sweeping, with potential implications for industries requiring persistent environmental monitoring and maintenance.  

**Keywords**: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Area Sweeping, Autonomous Systems, Adaptive Algorithms, Robotics, Environmental Monitoring.
