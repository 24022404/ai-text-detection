This paper proposes a novel approach to inducing hierarchical compositional models by sparsifying generator networks. The objective is to develop a more efficient and interpretable method for learning complex data distributions. Our approach utilizes a sparsity-inducing technique to regularize the generator network, resulting in a hierarchical representation of the data. The method involves training a generator network with a sparsity penalty term, which encourages the network to produce sparse and hierarchical representations of the input data. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of representation quality and computational efficiency. The induced hierarchical compositional model exhibits state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets, including image and text generation tasks. Our contributions include a novel sparsity-inducing technique, a hierarchical compositional model, and a more efficient training algorithm. The proposed method has significant implications for applications in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science, particularly in areas such as generative modeling, representation learning, and unsupervised feature learning. Key keywords: hierarchical compositional models, sparsity-inducing techniques, generator networks, representation learning, artificial intelligence, machine learning.
