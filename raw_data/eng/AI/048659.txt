This paper proposes an extension of the Isolation Forest algorithm, a widely used anomaly detection technique in machine learning. The objective is to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of anomaly detection in complex datasets by incorporating additional features and improving the decision-making process. Our approach introduces a novel method for selecting the optimal number of decision trees and utilizes a new scoring function that takes into account the density of the data points. The results show that the Extended Isolation Forest outperforms the traditional Isolation Forest in terms of precision, recall, and F1-score, particularly in datasets with high-dimensional features and noisy data. The conclusion highlights the significance of this research in advancing anomaly detection capabilities, with potential applications in fraud detection, network security, and healthcare monitoring. Key contributions include improved handling of high-dimensional data, enhanced robustness to noise and outliers, and increased interpretability of the results. Relevant keywords: anomaly detection, Isolation Forest, machine learning, outlier detection, data mining.
