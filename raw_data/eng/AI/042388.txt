Image-to-video synthesis aims to generate realistic videos from a single input image, a challenging task due to the inherent uncertainty and multi-modality of future frames. This paper introduces a novel stochastic image-to-video synthesis approach leveraging conditional Invertible Neural Networks (cINNs). Our method utilizes cINNs to model the conditional distribution of video frames given the input image, enabling the generation of diverse and plausible video sequences. The cINN architecture facilitates efficient sampling and allows for explicit control over the stochasticity of the generated video. We trained and evaluated our model on benchmark datasets, demonstrating its ability to produce high-quality videos with significant diversity compared to existing deterministic and GAN-based approaches. Quantitative results show improved Fréchet Video Distance (FVD) scores, indicating enhanced realism and temporal consistency. The proposed method offers a powerful framework for stochastic video generation with applications in animation, virtual reality, and content creation. Keywords: Image-to-video synthesis, Invertible Neural Networks, cINNs, Stochastic Generation, Video Generation, Conditional Generation, Deep Learning.
