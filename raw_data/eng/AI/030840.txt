This paper presents a novel approach for extracting road curbs from mobile LiDAR point clouds, addressing the challenges of accurate and efficient curb detection in urban environments. Our method utilizes a combination of point cloud filtering, feature extraction, and machine learning techniques to identify and delineate road curbs. The approach is tested on a large-scale dataset of mobile LiDAR point clouds, demonstrating high accuracy and robustness in various scenarios. The results show significant improvements over existing methods, with a mean accuracy of 95% and a reduction in processing time of up to 30%. The proposed technique has important implications for autonomous vehicles, urban planning, and infrastructure management, enabling the creation of highly accurate and up-to-date maps of road networks. Key contributions include the development of a robust curb extraction algorithm, the integration of mobile LiDAR point clouds with machine learning techniques, and the evaluation of the approach on a large-scale dataset. Relevant keywords: LiDAR point clouds, road curb extraction, mobile mapping, machine learning, autonomous vehicles, urban planning.
