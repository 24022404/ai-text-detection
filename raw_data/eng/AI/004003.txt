Explainable AI (XAI) methods aim to enhance the transparency and trustworthiness of machine learning models. However, existing techniques often struggle to provide comprehensive and interpretable explanations for complex models. This paper introduces SIDU, a novel method based on Similarity, Difference, and Uniqueness, designed to offer more nuanced and informative explanations. SIDU identifies key features influencing model predictions by analyzing similar and dissimilar instances, highlighting the unique contributions of each feature. We evaluate SIDU on image classification and natural language processing tasks, comparing its performance against state-of-the-art XAI methods. Results demonstrate that SIDU provides more accurate and human-interpretable explanations, leading to improved model understanding and trust. Furthermore, SIDU’s ability to identify unique feature contributions allows for targeted model debugging and refinement. The SIDU method offers a valuable tool for developing and deploying trustworthy AI systems. Keywords: Explainable AI, XAI, interpretability, feature importance, model understanding, similarity, difference, uniqueness.
