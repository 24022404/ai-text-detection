Anomaly detection in video sequences is a critical task in surveillance, security, and automated monitoring systems. This paper addresses the challenge of identifying anomalous events in video data, which often involves complex patterns and high-dimensional inputs. We propose a novel computational model that leverages deep learning techniques, specifically a hybrid architecture combining convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs), to effectively capture spatial and temporal features in video sequences. To evaluate the model, we introduce a comprehensive benchmark dataset comprising diverse scenarios, including crowded public spaces, traffic monitoring, and industrial environments. Our experiments demonstrate that the proposed model achieves state-of-the-art performance, with a significant improvement in detection accuracy and reduced false-positive rates compared to existing methods. The results highlight the model's robustness in handling varying lighting conditions, occlusions, and dynamic backgrounds. This research contributes a scalable and efficient solution for real-time anomaly detection, with potential applications in smart cities, industrial automation, and public safety. The benchmark dataset and model implementation are made publicly available to foster further research in this domain.  

**Keywords**: anomaly detection, video sequences, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, recurrent neural networks, benchmark dataset, real-time monitoring.
