Temporal Knowledge Graphs (TKGs) represent facts that evolve over time, capturing crucial information for dynamic reasoning and prediction. However, acquiring complete TKG data is challenging, leading to the problem of learning new entities and relations from limited examples, known as one-shot learning. This paper introduces a novel approach for one-shot learning in TKGs, leveraging meta-learning techniques to enable rapid adaptation to unseen entities and relations. Our method, Temporal Meta-Relational Learner (TMRL), combines graph neural networks with a temporal attention mechanism to capture both structural and temporal dependencies within the TKG. TMRL is trained on a meta-dataset of TKG snapshots, allowing it to quickly generalize to new entities and relations given only a single supporting example. Experiments on benchmark TKG datasets demonstrate that TMRL significantly outperforms existing one-shot learning methods in terms of link prediction accuracy and generalization ability. Furthermore, we analyze the impact of different temporal attention mechanisms on performance. Our work provides a promising direction for addressing data scarcity challenges in TKGs and facilitating more robust temporal reasoning. Keywords: Temporal Knowledge Graph, One-shot Learning, Meta-Learning, Graph Neural Networks, Temporal Attention.
