This paper introduces a novel training methodology for neural networks that leverages $\ell 1$-regularization in conjunction with bi-fidelity data to enhance model performance and reduce overfitting. The objective is to improve the generalization capability of neural networks when trained with limited high-fidelity data. Our approach uses a combination of computationally inexpensive low-fidelity data and a smaller set of accurate high-fidelity data. We employ $\ell 1$-regularization to promote sparsity in the network's weights, effectively pruning less important connections and improving robustness. We demonstrate that training with bi-fidelity data, guided by $\ell 1$-regularization, yields significant improvements in prediction accuracy compared to training solely on high-fidelity data or using traditional regularization techniques. Experimental results on benchmark datasets show a substantial reduction in test error and improved model interpretability. This method offers a practical solution for training accurate neural networks in scenarios where acquiring large amounts of high-fidelity data is challenging or costly. Key words: Neural networks, $\ell 1$-regularization, bi-fidelity data, machine learning, training algorithms, sparse models, generalization.
