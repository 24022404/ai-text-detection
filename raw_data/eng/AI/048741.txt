Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in generating high-quality images by learning latent representations of data. However, navigating the latent space to achieve meaningful and interpretable transformations remains a challenge. This paper introduces *WarpedGANSpace*, a novel framework that leverages non-linear Radial Basis Function (RBF) paths to explore and manipulate the latent space of GANs. Unlike traditional linear interpolation methods, *WarpedGANSpace* enables the discovery of complex, non-linear trajectories that better align with semantic attributes and disentangled features in the generated images. Our approach utilizes a combination of RBF kernels and optimization techniques to identify paths that preserve image quality while enabling precise control over specific attributes, such as facial expressions, object orientation, or style transitions. Experimental results demonstrate that *WarpedGANSpace* outperforms existing methods in terms of both visual fidelity and attribute manipulation accuracy across multiple datasets. The framework’s ability to uncover non-linear relationships in the latent space provides new insights into the structure of GAN representations and opens avenues for applications in image editing, data augmentation, and creative design. This work contributes to advancing the interpretability and controllability of GANs, offering a powerful tool for researchers and practitioners in computer vision and generative modeling.  

**Keywords**: GANs, latent space manipulation, non-linear interpolation, RBF paths, image generation, attribute control.
