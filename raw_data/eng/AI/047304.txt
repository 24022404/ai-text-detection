This paper addresses the challenge of robust image processing and perception in real-world scenarios where images are often corrupted by noise, blur, or other artifacts, termed "dirty pixels." We propose an end-to-end trainable framework that integrates image restoration and high-level perception tasks, such as object detection and classification. Our approach utilizes a deep convolutional neural network architecture that simultaneously learns to remove image degradations and extract relevant features for downstream perception. By jointly optimizing restoration and perception objectives, the network learns representations that are more resilient to image imperfections. Experimental results on benchmark datasets with synthetically and realistically corrupted images demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms traditional pipelines that treat image restoration and perception as separate stages. Specifically, our end-to-end approach achieves improved object detection accuracy and classification performance under various noise levels and blur types. This research offers a novel approach to handling dirty pixels, leading to more robust and reliable image understanding systems. Keywords: Image restoration, perception, end-to-end learning, object detection, deep learning, dirty pixels.
