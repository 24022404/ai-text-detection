This paper addresses the challenge of extracting meaningful patterns from complex, multi-dimensional data by introducing a novel approach to regularized tensor factorizations and higher-order principal components analysis. Our objective is to develop a robust and efficient method for decomposing high-order tensors into interpretable components, enabling insights into underlying structures and relationships. We propose a new algorithm that integrates regularization techniques with tensor factorization, allowing for improved generalization and reduced overfitting. Our approach is evaluated on various real-world datasets, demonstrating its effectiveness in capturing higher-order correlations and outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The results show that our regularized tensor factorization method can identify meaningful patterns and principal components in high-dimensional data, with significant implications for applications in data mining, machine learning, and signal processing. Key contributions of this research include the development of a scalable and flexible tensor factorization framework, the introduction of novel regularization techniques, and the demonstration of improved performance in higher-order principal components analysis. Relevant keywords: tensor factorization, higher-order principal components analysis, regularization techniques, multi-dimensional data analysis, machine learning, data mining.
