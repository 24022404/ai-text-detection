Time series representation learning is crucial for various applications, yet acquiring labeled data for training remains a significant bottleneck. This paper introduces a novel self-supervised learning framework for time series representation, focusing on Inter-Intra Relational Reasoning. Our approach leverages both intra-segment relationships within individual time series and inter-segment relationships across different time series to learn robust and discriminative representations. Specifically, we employ a contrastive learning objective that encourages representations of temporally adjacent segments within the same series to be similar, while pushing apart representations from dissimilar series. Furthermore, we introduce a relation reasoning module that explicitly models the dependencies between different time series segments. Experimental results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance compared to existing self-supervised and supervised approaches in time series classification and forecasting tasks. The learned representations exhibit strong generalization capability and can be effectively transferred to downstream tasks with limited labeled data. Our work provides a promising direction for unsupervised time series analysis, reducing reliance on labeled data and improving the performance of various time series applications.

**Keywords:** Time Series, Self-Supervised Learning, Representation Learning, Contrastive Learning, Relational Reasoning.
