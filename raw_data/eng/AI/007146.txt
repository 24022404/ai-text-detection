This paper proposes a novel approach to graph neural networks by introducing hypergraph convolution and hypergraph attention mechanisms. The objective is to enhance the representation learning of complex relationships in hypergraph-structured data, which is commonly found in social networks, recommendation systems, and knowledge graphs. Our method leverages hypergraph convolution to aggregate information from neighboring nodes and hypergraph attention to weigh the importance of different relationships. The results show that our approach outperforms existing graph neural network models in node classification, link prediction, and graph classification tasks. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of hypergraph convolution and attention in capturing higher-order relationships and improving model performance. This research contributes to the development of more advanced graph neural network architectures, with potential applications in network analysis, recommendation systems, and natural language processing. Key keywords: hypergraph neural networks, graph attention, hypergraph convolution, representation learning, node classification.
