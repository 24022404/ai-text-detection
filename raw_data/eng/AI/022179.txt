Person image generation aims to synthesize realistic images of individuals in arbitrary poses, a challenging task due to significant pose variations and complex occlusions. This paper introduces a novel Progressive and Aligned Pose Attention Transfer Network (P<sup>2</sup>A-Net) to address these challenges. Our approach employs a progressive generation strategy, starting with coarse structural synthesis and gradually refining details. We propose an aligned pose attention module that learns to transfer relevant features from the source person to the target pose, explicitly considering spatial correspondence. This module incorporates a novel attention mechanism that aligns features based on pose keypoints, enabling precise feature transfer and minimizing artifacts. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that P<sup>2</sup>A-Net significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of visual quality, pose accuracy, and identity preservation. The results highlight the effectiveness of our progressive generation and aligned pose attention mechanisms for high-quality person image generation. Keywords: Person Image Generation, Pose Transfer, Attention Mechanism, Image Synthesis, Deep Learning.
