This paper introduces TrimTuner, a novel approach to optimize machine learning jobs in cloud environments through strategic sub-sampling. The objective is to reduce computational costs and enhance efficiency without compromising model accuracy. TrimTuner achieves this by employing a hybrid method that combines intelligent sub-sampling techniques with automated hyperparameter tuning. By selectively reducing the dataset size while maintaining representative data points, TrimTuner significantly decreases training times and resource utilization. Experimental results demonstrate that TrimTuner outperforms traditional optimization methods, yielding up to 70% reduction in computational costs while preserving model performance. The key contributions of this research include the development of a scalable and adaptive sub-sampling algorithm, integration with popular machine learning frameworks, and a user-friendly interface for seamless deployment in cloud infrastructures. TrimTuner has significant implications for businesses and researchers relying on cloud-based machine learning services, enabling faster model development, reduced expenses, and improved productivity. Relevant keywords: machine learning optimization, cloud computing, sub-sampling, hyperparameter tuning, efficient computing.
