Partial discharge (PD) detection in insulated overhead conductors is critical for ensuring the reliability and safety of power transmission systems. Traditional methods for PD detection often struggle with accuracy and robustness, particularly in noisy environments. This paper proposes a novel deep learning framework that leverages frequency and phase attention mechanisms to enhance PD detection performance. The proposed approach integrates a convolutional neural network (CNN) with attention modules specifically designed to focus on frequency and phase characteristics of PD signals, enabling the model to distinguish between genuine PD events and noise more effectively. Experimental results on a real-world dataset demonstrate that the framework achieves superior detection accuracy compared to existing methods, with a significant reduction in false positives. The model also exhibits strong generalization capabilities across varying environmental conditions. This research contributes to the field by introducing a robust, attention-based solution for PD detection, offering potential applications in predictive maintenance and fault diagnosis for power infrastructure. Key innovations include the integration of frequency and phase attention mechanisms, which provide a new perspective for analyzing PD signals. Keywords: partial discharge detection, deep learning, attention mechanisms, insulated overhead conductors, frequency analysis, phase analysis.
