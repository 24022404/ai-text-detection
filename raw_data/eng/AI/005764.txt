Image captioning models often struggle with generating accurate descriptions for images containing novel objects not seen during training. This paper introduces a novel approach to address this limitation by leveraging an online vocabulary expansion technique. Our method dynamically updates the model's vocabulary during inference by incorporating relevant object names extracted from external knowledge sources, such as pre-trained word embeddings and object detectors. Specifically, we employ a pipeline that first detects potential novel objects within an image. Then, we retrieve candidate textual descriptions for these objects and integrate them into the captioning model's vocabulary. This allows the model to generate more accurate and descriptive captions, even for images containing previously unseen objects. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach significantly improves the accuracy of image captions, particularly for images with novel objects, compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. This work contributes a practical and effective solution for enhancing the robustness of image captioning systems in real-world scenarios.
Keywords: Image Captioning, Novel Object Recognition, Online Learning, Vocabulary Expansion, Computer Vision, Deep Learning.
