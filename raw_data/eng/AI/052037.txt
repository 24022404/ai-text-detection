Unsupervised Video Object Segmentation (UVOS) aims to identify and segment salient objects in a video without any manual annotation. This paper introduces a novel approach leveraging instance embedding transfer to improve the performance of UVOS. Our method utilizes instance embeddings learned from static images to guide the segmentation process in videos. Specifically, we transfer the learned semantic understanding of objects from still images to the video domain, enabling the model to better distinguish between foreground objects and background clutter. This is achieved through a specifically designed loss function that encourages temporal consistency and semantic alignment of instance embeddings across video frames. Experimental results on standard UVOS benchmarks demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms state-of-the-art unsupervised methods. The proposed method achieves improved segmentation accuracy, robustness to motion blur, and better handling of complex scenes. The code will be publicly available. Keywords: Video Object Segmentation, Unsupervised Learning, Instance Embedding, Transfer Learning, Computer Vision.
