This paper addresses the challenges of analyzing high-dimensional data structured as multi-way arrays, or tensors, by introducing novel regularization techniques for tensor factorizations and higher-order principal components analysis (PCA). Traditional matrix-based approaches, such as standard PCA, are limited in their ability to capture complex interactions inherent in multi-modal datasets commonly found in fields such as computational biology, signal processing, and recommendation systems. To overcome these limitations, we propose a family of regularized tensor decomposition methods that impose sparsity and smoothness penalties within the factorization process. Our approach extends classical PCA to higher-order data structures, enabling efficient extraction of low-dimensional representations while mitigating overfitting and improving interpretability. Extensive experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that our regularized tensor factorization methods outperform existing techniques in terms of accuracy, stability, and feature selection capabilities. The proposed framework is flexible, allowing integration with various regularization schemes and unsupervised learning objectives. Our findings highlight the effectiveness of structured regularization in higher-order data analysis and underline its potential for advancing applications involving multi-relational data, feature extraction, and latent structure discovery in large-scale tensor datasets. Keywords: tensor factorization, higher-order PCA, regularization, sparsity, multi-way data analysis, unsupervised learning.
