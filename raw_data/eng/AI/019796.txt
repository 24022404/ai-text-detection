Low-tier (free plan) users experience several limitations, including the absence of sales forecasting, a limited number of user seats, no calendar booking functionality, lack of customization options, and no continuous executive support. Many users express dissatisfaction with Zoho’s setup process and customer support, particularly when it comes to integrating with third-party applications. By contrast, Zendesk stands out as a preferred solution for enterprise teams, offering robust multi-channel support, a shared inbox, knowledge base, and live chat features.
