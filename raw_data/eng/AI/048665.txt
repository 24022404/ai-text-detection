This paper explores the application of Neural Tangent Kernels (NTKs) in machine learning, focusing on achieving near input sparsity time in the learning process. The objective is to improve the efficiency of NTK-based models by reducing computational complexity while maintaining their expressive power. Our approach involves developing a novel algorithm that leverages the properties of NTKs to adapt to sparse input data, resulting in significant speedups in training times. The method utilizes a combination of sparse matrix operations and optimized kernel computations to achieve near input sparsity time complexity. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, showing improved performance and reduced training times compared to existing NTK-based methods. The key findings of this research contribute to the advancement of efficient machine learning algorithms, particularly in scenarios where input data is sparse or high-dimensional. Our work has important implications for applications in computer vision, natural language processing, and other fields where NTKs can be applied, highlighting the potential for significant improvements in model training efficiency and scalability. Key keywords: Neural Tangent Kernels, sparse input data, machine learning, efficient algorithms, near input sparsity time.
