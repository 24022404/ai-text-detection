This paper introduces an innovative A-expansion algorithm tailored for multi-label segmentation problems involving multiple “hedgehog” shape priors. The objective is to efficiently segment images where objects exhibit radial symmetry around multiple anchor points, a common challenge in various computer vision applications. Our method extends the traditional A-expansion framework by incorporating multiple shape priors, represented as “hedgehogs,” directly into the energy function. This allows for simultaneous optimization of shape and segmentation, overcoming limitations of previous approaches that handle only single-shape priors or require sequential processing. We demonstrate the algorithm's effectiveness on synthetic and real-world datasets, including biological microscopy images. Experimental results show a significant improvement in segmentation accuracy and computational efficiency compared to existing multi-label segmentation techniques. The proposed A-expansion algorithm provides a robust and scalable solution for segmenting images with multiple radially symmetric objects, offering potential applications in medical image analysis, object recognition, and scene understanding.
Keywords: A-expansion, multi-label segmentation, shape prior, hedgehog, image processing.
