Conditional Generative Adversarial Networks (cGANs) have demonstrated remarkable success in generating high-quality, context-aware data across various domains. However, their performance often degrades under noisy or incomplete conditional inputs, limiting their robustness in real-world applications. This paper addresses this challenge by proposing a novel framework for Robust Conditional Generative Adversarial Networks (R-cGANs). The objective is to enhance the stability and reliability of cGANs when handling imperfect or uncertain conditional data. Our approach integrates a noise-resilient encoder and a dynamic attention mechanism to adaptively refine conditional inputs during the generation process. Additionally, we introduce a hybrid loss function that balances adversarial training with reconstruction accuracy, ensuring robust performance across diverse scenarios. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that R-cGANs outperform traditional cGANs in terms of both qualitative and quantitative metrics, particularly under noisy conditions. The proposed framework achieves significant improvements in image synthesis, text-to-image generation, and data augmentation tasks. This research contributes a scalable and versatile solution for enhancing the robustness of conditional generative models, with potential applications in computer vision, natural language processing, and beyond.  

**Keywords**: Conditional Generative Adversarial Networks, Robustness, Noise Resilience, Dynamic Attention, Hybrid Loss Function, Image Synthesis.
