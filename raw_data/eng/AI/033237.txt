This paper introduces a novel framework for automatically improving teaching strategies using student feedback. The objective is to develop a system that leverages student evaluations and learning analytics to provide actionable insights for instructors, ultimately enhancing the learning experience. Our approach employs a reinforcement learning agent trained on a dataset of student feedback, course materials, and instructor actions. The agent learns to predict the impact of different teaching interventions (e.g., adjusting lecture pace, providing additional examples, changing assessment methods) on student performance and satisfaction. We evaluate our framework on real-world course data from a large university, demonstrating significant improvements in student engagement and learning outcomes compared to traditional teaching methods and baseline reinforcement learning models. The results indicate that our system can effectively personalize instruction and adapt to diverse student needs. This research contributes a practical application of reinforcement learning to the field of education, offering a scalable and data-driven approach to teacher development. Key words: Reinforcement Learning, Education, Student Feedback, Adaptive Learning, Teacher Development, Personalized Instruction.
