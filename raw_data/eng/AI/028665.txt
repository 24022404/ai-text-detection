This paper proposes Onion-Peel Networks, a novel deep learning approach for video completion tasks. The objective is to develop an effective method for filling in missing regions in videos, which is a crucial problem in various applications such as video editing, restoration, and surveillance. Our approach utilizes a multi-scale architecture, inspired by the concept of onion peels, to progressively refine the completion results. The Onion-Peel Network employs a combination of convolutional and recurrent neural networks to capture both spatial and temporal information in videos. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art techniques in terms of completion quality and efficiency. The Onion-Peel Network achieves significant improvements in handling complex scenes, large missing regions, and diverse video content. Our research contributes to the development of advanced video completion techniques, with potential applications in computer vision, video processing, and artificial intelligence. Key keywords: video completion, deep learning, Onion-Peel Networks, computer vision, video processing.
