This paper addresses the challenge of efficiently embedding point features in 3D data processing tasks, which is critical for various applications in computer vision and graphics. Traditional methods often suffer from computational inefficiencies and limitations in handling large-scale data due to their dependence on regular data structures. We propose a novel approach to overcome these limitations by introducing an Irregularly Tabulated Multilayer Perceptron (MLP).

Methods/Approach: Our approach leverages an irregular tabulation strategy within the MLP framework to accelerate the point feature embedding process. By bypassing the necessity for uniform data structures, the proposed method efficiently processes and embeds features from point clouds directly, utilizing a dynamic data handling process that adapts to the intrinsic characteristics of the data. This results in a more flexible and scalable embedding technique, tailored to overcome the innate irregularity of point cloud data.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that our Irregularly Tabulated MLP outperforms conventional MLP-based embedding methods, achieving superior speed and accuracy in feature extraction from point clouds. Comparative analysis shows a significant reduction in computational time, while maintaining or even enhancing the quality of the embeddings, especially in scenarios dealing with high-density data points.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of an Irregularly Tabulated MLP provides a transformative advancement in the field of 3D data processing, presenting a promising solution for fast and accurate point feature embedding. This method not only enhances performance in existing applications but also opens avenues for efficient real-time processing in emerging technologies such as autonomous driving and augmented reality, where rapid and reliable point feature analysis is paramount.

Keywords: Point Feature Embedding, Irregularly Tabulated MLP, 3D Data Processing, Point Clouds, Multilayer Perceptron, Computer Vision, Real-time Processing.
