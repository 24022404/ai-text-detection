This paper addresses the challenge of fast and accurate object detection, particularly for small objects, in weakly supervised learning scenarios. We propose a novel Multiple Receptive Fields and Small-Object-Focusing Weakly-Supervised Segmentation Network (MRF-SOFNet) designed to enhance detection performance while maintaining computational efficiency. The network integrates multiple receptive fields to capture diverse spatial contexts, enabling robust feature extraction across varying object scales. Additionally, a small-object-focusing mechanism is introduced to prioritize and refine the detection of smaller objects, which are often overlooked in traditional weakly supervised approaches. The proposed method leverages weakly labeled data, reducing the reliance on extensive annotated datasets, and employs an efficient architecture to ensure real-time applicability. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that MRF-SOFNet achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of detection accuracy and speed, outperforming existing weakly supervised methods. The network’s ability to generalize across different object sizes and its computational efficiency make it a promising solution for applications such as autonomous driving, surveillance, and medical imaging. This work contributes to advancing weakly supervised object detection by addressing key limitations in small-object detection and scalability.  

**Keywords**: weakly supervised learning, object detection, small-object focusing, multiple receptive fields, real-time detection, segmentation network.
