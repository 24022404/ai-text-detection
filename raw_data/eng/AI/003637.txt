To prevent errors in CD disc storage caused by long sequences of 0 bits, engineers use Run Length Limited (RLL) codes, which were invented by Immink. The purpose of these codes is to control the length of consecutive 0’s between two 1’s in the data sequence. Specifically, an (d, k)-RLL code restricts the number of consecutive 0’s to be at least d and at most k, where d and k are non-negative integers such that d is less than or equal to k. This limitation helps maintain data integrity during storage and retrieval.
