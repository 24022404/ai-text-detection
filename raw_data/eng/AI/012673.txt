To instantiate a message for a smart contract, several essential fields must be provided, as detailed in Table 4.12. These include the members who are part of the contract, the defined system thresholds that determine how decisions are made, the contract owner who has administrative rights, the expected key number which may relate to authorization or access control, and the deadline time by which specific actions should occur. Additionally, the execute message functions as the mechanism that processes incoming messages, triggering the contract’s logic accordingly.
