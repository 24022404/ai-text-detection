**Abstract**

Deep Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) have achieved significant success across various computer vision tasks, but their black-box nature often limits transparency and trust in their decisions. This paper introduces Smooth Grad-CAM++, an enhanced inference-level visualization technique designed to provide more precise and robust interpretations of CNN model predictions. The proposed method integrates the advancements of Grad-CAM++ with smoothing strategies to address limitations such as visual noise and coarse localization prevalent in conventional visualization techniques. By systematically applying input perturbations and aggregating the gradient-based explanations, Smooth Grad-CAM++ generates clearer and more informative heatmaps that highlight critical image regions contributing to model decisions. Experimental results across benchmark datasets, including ImageNet and CIFAR, demonstrate that the proposed technique outperforms existing visualization methods in terms of localization accuracy and visual clarity, facilitating better model diagnosis and trust. The findings suggest that Smooth Grad-CAM++ is a valuable tool for improving model interpretability in applications such as medical imaging, autonomous driving, and security, thereby supporting safer and more transparent deployment of deep learning systems.

*Keywords: visual explanation, deep learning interpretability, Grad-CAM++, convolutional neural networks, model transparency, heatmap localization*
