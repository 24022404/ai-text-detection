This paper addresses the challenge of automatically discovering and geotagging objects from street view imagery, a task critical for applications in urban planning, navigation, and location-based services. We propose a novel framework that integrates advanced computer vision techniques with geospatial data processing to identify and localize objects of interest in large-scale street view datasets. Our approach leverages deep learning models, specifically convolutional neural networks (CNNs), for object detection and classification, combined with a geotagging module that associates detected objects with precise geographic coordinates. The system is trained on a diverse dataset of street view images, ensuring robustness across varying environmental conditions and object types. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art accuracy in object discovery and geotagging, outperforming existing approaches by a significant margin. Additionally, the framework is computationally efficient, enabling real-time processing of large-scale datasets. The proposed solution has broad implications for smart city applications, autonomous navigation, and augmented reality systems, offering a scalable and accurate method for object localization in urban environments.  

**Keywords**: street view imagery, object detection, geotagging, computer vision, deep learning, urban mapping.
