Time series data often suffers from noise, outliers, and missing values, hindering accurate analysis and modeling. This paper introduces `tsrobprep`, a novel R package designed for robust preprocessing of time series data. The package implements a suite of robust statistical techniques, including outlier detection based on median absolute deviation, imputation of missing values using robust Kalman smoothing, and noise reduction via wavelet decomposition. We demonstrate the effectiveness of `tsrobprep` on a diverse set of real-world time series datasets, comparing its performance against existing preprocessing methods. Results show that `tsrobprep` consistently improves the quality of time series data, leading to enhanced accuracy in subsequent forecasting and anomaly detection tasks. The package is implemented in R, providing a user-friendly interface and seamless integration with existing time series analysis workflows. `tsrobprep` offers a valuable tool for researchers and practitioners seeking robust and reliable preprocessing solutions for time series data.

**Keywords:** Time series, Robust statistics, Preprocessing, Outlier detection, Missing value imputation, R package.
