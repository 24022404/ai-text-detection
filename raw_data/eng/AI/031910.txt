This paper addresses the critical issue of adversarial attacks on deep neural networks, aiming to enhance their robustness against such threats. Our objective is to develop a novel method to suppress channel-wise activations, thereby improving the resilience of neural networks to adversarial perturbations. We propose a new approach that utilizes a channel-wise activation suppressing technique, which selectively reduces the activation of sensitive channels to mitigate the impact of adversarial noise. Our method is based on a carefully designed loss function that encourages the network to produce more robust feature representations. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly improves the adversarial robustness of state-of-the-art neural networks, outperforming existing defense methods. Key findings include a substantial reduction in the success rate of adversarial attacks and improved performance on benchmark datasets. Our research contributes to the development of more secure and reliable deep learning models, with potential applications in computer vision, natural language processing, and other fields where adversarial robustness is crucial. Keywords: adversarial robustness, deep neural networks, channel-wise activation, activation suppressing, computer vision, natural language processing.
