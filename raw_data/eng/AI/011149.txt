This paper proposes an innovative approach to weakly supervised object localization by introducing an attention-based dropout layer. The objective is to improve the accuracy of object localization in images using only image-level labels, addressing the challenge of lacking precise location information. Our method employs a novel dropout technique that selectively attends to relevant regions of the image, enhancing the model's ability to focus on the object of interest. The attention-based dropout layer is integrated into a deep neural network, allowing the model to learn effective feature representations and localize objects more accurately. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in localization accuracy. The key contributions of this research include the development of a novel attention-based dropout layer and its application to weakly supervised object localization, offering a new perspective on this challenging problem. This work has important implications for various computer vision applications, including image recognition, object detection, and robotics, and highlights the potential of attention mechanisms in improving the performance of deep learning models. Key keywords: weakly supervised object localization, attention mechanism, dropout layer, deep learning, computer vision.
