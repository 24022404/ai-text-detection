This paper proposes a novel semi-supervised clustering ensemble approach that leverages voting to improve clustering accuracy and robustness. The objective is to address the challenge of clustering complex datasets with limited labeled information. Our method combines the strengths of multiple base clustering models, each trained on a subset of the data with partial supervision. The ensemble voting mechanism allows for the aggregation of cluster assignments, leading to more accurate and consistent clustering results. Experimental evaluations demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art clustering algorithms, particularly in scenarios with noisy or scarce labeled data. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of the voting-based ensemble in capturing complex cluster structures and handling high-dimensional data. This research contributes to the development of robust semi-supervised clustering methods, with potential applications in data mining, machine learning, and pattern recognition. Key keywords: semi-supervised clustering, ensemble learning, voting mechanism, cluster analysis, machine learning, data mining.
