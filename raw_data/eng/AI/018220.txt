This paper presents a novel information theoretic approach to goal-oriented visual dialog, where the objective is to generate human-like responses that align with the questioner's intent. Our method, termed "Answerer in Questioner's Mind," leverages mutual information to model the uncertainty between the questioner's goal and the answerer's response. By employing a deep learning framework, we develop an architecture that optimizes the conditional entropy between the questioner's intent and the answerer's response, resulting in more accurate and informative answers. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in goal-oriented visual dialog tasks. Our research contributes to the development of more intelligent and human-like visual dialog systems, with potential applications in areas such as human-computer interaction, robotics, and virtual assistants. Key aspects of our work include information theory, deep learning, visual dialog, and goal-oriented communication, highlighting the importance of mutual information and conditional entropy in modeling human-like interactions.
