The rapid increase in highway traffic necessitates efficient and accurate monitoring systems to ensure safety and manage congestion. This paper addresses the problem of detecting and analyzing traffic events through video surveillance using advanced deep learning techniques.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel deep learning framework that leverages Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) to process and interpret video data from highway surveillance cameras. The system is designed to automatically detect and classify various traffic events, such as accidents and bottlenecks, through real-time video analysis. The architecture incorporates transfer learning to enhance model accuracy and reduce computational overhead.

Results/Findings: The proposed system was evaluated on a comprehensive dataset of highway traffic videos. Our results demonstrate significant improvements in detection accuracy and processing speed compared to traditional methods. The deep learning model achieved an average precision of 92% and successfully identified critical traffic incidents with minimal false positives. Additionally, a comparison with state-of-the-art approaches highlights the efficiency and reliability of our method.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes to the field of intelligent transportation systems by offering a robust solution for real-time traffic surveillance. The integration of deep learning architectures not only enhances event detection capabilities but also provides scalable applications for different traffic monitoring scenarios. The system's potential for deployment in smart city infrastructure underscores its value in improving road safety and traffic management.

Keywords: video surveillance, highway traffic, deep learning, CNN, RNN, traffic event detection, intelligent transportation systems.
