This paper introduces a novel approach to graph exploration, leveraging transfer learning to enable efficient and effective navigation of complex graph structures. The objective is to develop a framework that can learn transferable graph exploration strategies, applicable to various domains and graph types. Our method employs a graph neural network-based architecture, incorporating reinforcement learning and self-supervised learning techniques to learn generalizable exploration policies. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our approach, outperforming existing methods in terms of exploration efficiency and node coverage. The key findings highlight the importance of transfer learning in graph exploration, enabling the model to adapt to new, unseen graphs with minimal additional training. This research contributes to the development of more efficient and scalable graph exploration algorithms, with potential applications in network analysis, recommendation systems, and graph-based machine learning. The proposed framework is particularly relevant to areas such as social network analysis, molecular graph exploration, and web graph crawling, where transferable graph exploration strategies can significantly improve performance and reduce computational costs. Key keywords: graph exploration, transfer learning, graph neural networks, reinforcement learning, self-supervised learning.
