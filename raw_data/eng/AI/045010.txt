This paper presents the development of a large-scale multimodal knowledge base system designed to address the challenge of answering complex visual queries. The system integrates visual and textual data to enable accurate and context-aware responses to user inquiries involving images and associated metadata. The proposed approach leverages state-of-the-art deep learning models, including convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for image analysis and transformer-based architectures for natural language understanding, to create a unified framework for multimodal data processing. A novel alignment mechanism is introduced to bridge the semantic gap between visual and textual modalities, ensuring robust query understanding and retrieval. Experimental results demonstrate the system's superior performance in handling diverse visual queries, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and response time compared to existing methods. The system's scalability is validated through deployment on a large-scale dataset comprising millions of image-text pairs. This research contributes to the advancement of multimodal AI systems by providing a scalable and efficient solution for real-world applications, such as visual search engines, augmented reality, and intelligent virtual assistants. Key innovations include the integration of multimodal alignment techniques and the development of a modular architecture that supports extensibility and adaptability. The findings highlight the potential of multimodal knowledge bases in enhancing human-computer interaction and enabling more intuitive access to visual information.  

**Keywords**: multimodal knowledge base, visual queries, deep learning, image-text alignment, transformer models, scalable AI systems.
