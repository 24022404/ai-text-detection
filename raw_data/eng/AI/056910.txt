The objective of this research is to address the challenges in image harmonization, a process that ensures seamless integration of foreground objects into background scenes, by developing an efficient and high-resolution solution. In this paper, we introduce the Spatial-Separated Curve Rendering Network (SSCRN), a novel approach designed specifically for enhanced image harmonization tasks. Our method leverages spatial separation techniques combined with advanced curve rendering to effectively balance the complexities involved in integrating visual elements across divergent image domains. The proposed network is trained on a diverse dataset, demonstrating an improvement in both efficiency and resolution of the output images compared to existing methods. Through extensive experimentation, SSCRN achieves superior performance metrics, including reduced computation time and higher fidelity in visual consistency, over current state-of-the-art models. The findings suggest that SSCRN not only enhances visual cohesion but also provides a scalable solution suited for applications in fields such as digital media, graphic design, and augmented reality. This work contributes to the domain by offering a cutting-edge model for image harmonization, characterized by its innovative use of spatial customization and curve rendering. Keywords for this study include image harmonization, spatial separation, curve rendering, high-resolution imagery, and digital integration.
