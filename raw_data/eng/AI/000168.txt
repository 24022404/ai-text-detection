This paper addresses the challenge of achieving fast and high-quality universal style transfer for both artistic and photorealistic rendering. While existing methods often struggle with balancing speed, generalization, and visual fidelity, we propose a novel approach that leverages a lightweight neural network architecture combined with adaptive feature transformation techniques. Our method introduces a multi-scale style representation framework that dynamically adjusts to diverse artistic styles and photorealistic textures, ensuring robust performance across various input domains. By optimizing the network for efficiency, we achieve real-time rendering without compromising visual quality. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of speed, style consistency, and photorealism, as validated through quantitative metrics and user studies. The proposed framework is highly versatile, enabling applications in creative design, augmented reality, and real-time video processing. This work contributes a significant advancement in universal style transfer, offering a practical solution for both artistic and photorealistic rendering tasks.  

**Keywords**: style transfer, neural networks, photorealistic rendering, artistic rendering, real-time processing, adaptive feature transformation.
