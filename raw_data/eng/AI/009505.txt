This paper addresses the challenge of efficient knowledge transfer in deep reinforcement learning, with the objective of developing a real-time policy distillation framework. Our approach leverages a novel combination of online learning and distillation techniques, enabling the transfer of knowledge from a complex teacher policy to a simpler student policy in real-time. The method utilizes a custom-designed architecture that facilitates simultaneous learning and distillation, allowing the student policy to adapt rapidly to changing environments. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in policy performance and efficiency, outperforming traditional distillation methods. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of our framework in reducing the complexity of deep reinforcement learning models while preserving their accuracy. This research contributes to the field by providing a real-time policy distillation approach, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, game playing, and autonomous systems, and is particularly relevant to keywords such as deep reinforcement learning, policy distillation, real-time learning, and knowledge transfer.
