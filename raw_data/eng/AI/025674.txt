This paper presents a novel approach to low-light image enhancement, addressing the longstanding challenge of restoring visibility in poorly illuminated scenes. Our objective is to develop a practical and efficient method for enhancing low-light images, leveraging advances in deep learning and computer vision. We propose a custom-designed neural network architecture that integrates a multi-scale feature extraction module and a progressive refinement mechanism, enabling the effective removal of noise and the recovery of detailed textures. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of peak signal-to-noise ratio, structural similarity index, and visual information fidelity. Our low-light image enhancer achieves significant improvements in image quality, making it suitable for various applications, including surveillance, autonomous driving, and low-light photography. The key contributions of this work include a unique network design, an efficient training strategy, and a comprehensive evaluation framework. By kindling the darkness, our method has the potential to unlock new possibilities in image and video processing, particularly in scenarios where lighting conditions are suboptimal. Keywords: low-light image enhancement, deep learning, computer vision, image processing, neural networks.
