This paper explores the integration of Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) with Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) regions to enhance the stability and efficiency of control policies in complex systems. The objective is to address the challenges of traditional DRL methods, which often struggle with long-term planning and stability. Our approach combines the strengths of DRL and LQR by dividing the state space into LQR regions, where the optimal control policy can be computed analytically, and using DRL to learn the switching strategy between these regions. We employ a novel algorithm that leverages the benefits of both worlds, enabling the agent to learn a stable and efficient control policy. Our results demonstrate significant improvements in performance and stability compared to traditional DRL methods, particularly in systems with nonlinear dynamics. The proposed framework has important implications for control applications, such as robotics and autonomous systems, and contributes to the development of more robust and reliable DRL algorithms. Key contributions include the introduction of LQR regions to DRL, improved stability and efficiency, and the potential for application in a wide range of control tasks. Relevant keywords: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Linear Quadratic Regulator, Control Theory, Stability, Efficiency.
