This paper presents MTStereo 2.0, an enhanced approach to stereo depth estimation leveraging Max-trees, a data structure that facilitates efficient representation and manipulation of image regions. The objective is to improve the accuracy of depth maps generated from stereo images, addressing the challenges of noise, occlusions, and textureless areas. Our method employs a novel combination of Max-trees and stereo matching algorithms to produce more reliable and detailed depth estimates. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in accuracy compared to state-of-the-art stereo matching techniques, particularly in complex scenes with varying depths and textures. The key contributions of MTStereo 2.0 include its ability to handle ambiguous regions, reduce noise, and preserve depth discontinuities. With its enhanced performance, MTStereo 2.0 has potential applications in computer vision, robotics, and 3D reconstruction, offering a robust and efficient solution for stereo depth estimation. Key keywords: stereo depth estimation, Max-trees, computer vision, 3D reconstruction, image processing.
