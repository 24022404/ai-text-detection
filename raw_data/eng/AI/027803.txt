Explainable Machine Learning (XAI) is crucial for building trust and understanding in AI systems, but often incurs significant computational overhead. This paper explores the hardware acceleration of XAI methods using Tensor Processing Units (TPUs). We focus on accelerating SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) and Integrated Gradients, two widely used XAI techniques. Our approach leverages the TPU's matrix multiplication capabilities to optimize the computationally intensive operations involved in these methods. We implemented and evaluated our accelerated XAI methods on Google Cloud TPUs, demonstrating significant performance improvements compared to CPU and GPU implementations. Results show a speedup of up to 10x for SHAP and 8x for Integrated Gradients. This acceleration enables faster and more scalable XAI, facilitating the adoption of transparent AI in resource-constrained environments. Our work demonstrates the feasibility and benefits of using TPUs to accelerate XAI, contributing to the development of more efficient and understandable AI systems.

Keywords: Explainable AI, XAI, Tensor Processing Unit, TPU, Hardware Acceleration, SHAP, Integrated Gradients, Machine Learning.
