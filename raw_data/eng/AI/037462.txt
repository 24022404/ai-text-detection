Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) have demonstrated remarkable success in generating realistic data, but their training remains challenging due to issues like mode collapse and instability. This paper introduces a novel unified training framework for GANs leveraging self-labeling and self-attention mechanisms to address these limitations. Our approach incorporates a self-labeling module that assigns pseudo-labels to generated samples, providing additional supervisory signals to the discriminator. Furthermore, a self-attention mechanism is integrated within both the generator and discriminator to capture long-range dependencies and improve feature representation. We evaluate our method on several benchmark datasets, including CIFAR-10, CelebA, and LSUN, demonstrating significant improvements in image quality and training stability compared to existing GAN training techniques. Experimental results show that our unified approach achieves state-of-the-art Inception Score and FID scores, indicating enhanced generative performance. The proposed framework offers a robust and effective strategy for training GANs, leading to more stable convergence and higher-quality generated samples. Keywords: Generative Adversarial Networks, GAN, Self-Labeling, Self-Attention, Image Generation, Unsupervised Learning.
