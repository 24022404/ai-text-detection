Accurate forecasting of seasonal time series data is crucial for effective resource allocation and strategic planning in various domains. This paper introduces a novel two-stage framework designed to improve the forecasting accuracy of time series exhibiting seasonal patterns. The first stage employs a seasonal decomposition technique based on Singular Spectrum Analysis (SSA) to extract and model the seasonal components. Subsequently, the second stage utilizes a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) network to capture the underlying trend and residual components, leveraging the strengths of deep learning in modeling complex temporal dependencies. The framework is evaluated on several real-world datasets including retail sales, electricity consumption, and weather data. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed two-stage approach consistently outperforms traditional forecasting methods such as ARIMA and Exponential Smoothing, as well as single-stage LSTM models. Specifically, the framework achieves an average reduction of 15% in Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) compared to benchmark models. The findings highlight the effectiveness of combining SSA-based seasonal decomposition with LSTM networks for enhanced time series forecasting. This framework offers a practical and accurate solution for forecasting seasonal time series data, with potential applications in various industries.

**Keywords:** Time series forecasting, seasonal decomposition, Singular Spectrum Analysis (SSA), Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM), deep learning, forecasting accuracy.
