This paper provides a comprehensive review of the early classification of time series data, an area of increasing interest due to its potential to enable timely decision-making across various domains such as healthcare, finance, and industry. The objective is to summarize current methodologies and assess their applications and effectiveness.

Methods/Approach: The paper systematically categorizes existing approaches for early time series classification, including probabilistic models, machine learning algorithms, and hybrid techniques. Recent advances and innovations are highlighted, focusing on algorithms that enhance prediction accuracy while minimizing the length of time series required for reliable early classification.

Results/Findings: The review identifies key patterns and trends in the literature, noting improvements in computational efficiency and prediction performance. Detailed comparisons of existing methods are presented, with performance metrics such as accuracy, speed, and computational cost systematically evaluated across various datasets and scenarios. The findings indicate a significant increase in the deployment of deep learning techniques, which offer promising results but also highlight challenges such as data scarcity and interpretability.

Conclusion/Implications: This study contributes by synthesizing knowledge from diverse techniques and applications, offering insights into the trade-offs and challenges associated with early time series classification. The review underscores the importance of continuing research into hybrid models and the integration of domain knowledge to enhance early classification accuracy. The implications for real-time applications and the potential for these technologies to advance predictive analytics in critical sectors are discussed.

Key Keywords: early classification, time series, machine learning, deep learning, predictive analytics, hybrid models.
