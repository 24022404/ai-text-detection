This paper introduces Ensemble Squared, a novel meta-AutoML system designed to optimize and enhance the performance of automated machine learning pipelines. The primary objective is to address the limitations of individual AutoML systems by leveraging the strengths of multiple AutoML solutions through a sophisticated ensemble strategy. Ensemble Squared employs a two-layered approach: first, it independently executes several state-of-the-art AutoML systems on a given dataset; second, it intelligently combines the resulting pipelines using a learned weighting scheme based on performance metrics and diversity measures. Experiments on a diverse set of benchmark datasets demonstrate that Ensemble Squared consistently outperforms individual AutoML systems, achieving significant improvements in predictive accuracy and robustness. Furthermore, the system's adaptive weighting mechanism allows it to dynamically adjust to the characteristics of different datasets, ensuring optimal performance across a range of problem domains. This research contributes a practical and effective meta-learning framework for AutoML, significantly reducing the need for manual intervention in model selection and hyperparameter tuning, and facilitating broader adoption of machine learning in real-world applications.

Keywords: AutoML, Meta-Learning, Ensemble Learning, Machine Learning, Model Selection, Hyperparameter Optimization.
