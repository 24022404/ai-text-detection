This paper presents a novel convolutional decoder architecture for point cloud data that leverages Adaptive Instance Normalization (AdaIN) to enhance feature representation and reconstruction quality. The objective of this research is to address the challenges of accurately generating and reconstructing structured 3D point clouds, which are crucial in applications such as scene understanding, object recognition, and augmented reality. Our proposed method introduces a convolutional approach to process unordered point cloud data, integrating AdaIN to adaptively align feature statistics at different decoding stages. This enables the network to effectively capture both global and local geometric characteristics of 3D shapes. Experimental evaluations on benchmark point cloud datasets demonstrate that our decoder outperforms existing approaches in terms of reconstruction accuracy and fidelity, as measured by common metrics such as Chamfer Distance and Earth Mover’s Distance. Furthermore, ablation studies highlight the significant contribution of AdaIN modules to improved generalization and robustness against noise and incomplete data. The proposed architecture is computationally efficient, making it suitable for real-time and resource-constrained 3D vision applications. In conclusion, our convolutional decoder with adaptive instance normalization sets a new benchmark for point cloud reconstruction, with potential impact across domains involving 3D data processing, deep learning, and computer vision. Key keywords: point cloud, 3D reconstruction, convolutional neural network, adaptive instance normalization, deep learning, shape representation.
