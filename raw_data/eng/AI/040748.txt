This paper addresses a crucial challenge in deep reinforcement learning (DRL): the dependency on online exploration to collect new experiences for efficient learning. We propose a novel off-policy DRL framework that eliminates the need for traditional exploration strategies by leveraging existing datasets of agent-environment interactions. Our approach utilizes an advanced policy evaluation and improvement mechanism that learns directly from fixed, offline trajectories, ensuring robust policy optimization without additional environment sampling. We introduce architectural and algorithmic adaptations to mitigate distributional shift and overestimation bias, which commonly hinder off-policy learning in the absence of exploration. Through comprehensive experiments on benchmark environments, our method demonstrates superior or comparable performance to state-of-the-art algorithms that rely on explicit exploration, while significantly reducing sample complexity and training time. These results highlight the viability of learning effective policies from static data, broadening the applicability of DRL to domains where exploration is costly, unsafe, or infeasible. Our contributions lay the foundation for efficient and reliable reinforcement learning in real-world scenarios using only prior data. Key keywords: deep reinforcement learning, off-policy learning, offline RL, exploration-free, policy optimization, sample efficiency.
