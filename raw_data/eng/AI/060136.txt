The increasing computational demands of deep learning models pose a significant challenge for deployment on resource-constrained mobile devices. This paper introduces MnasNet, a novel platform-aware neural architecture search approach specifically designed to optimize model performance for mobile devices. Our search algorithm incorporates a multi-objective reward function that balances model accuracy and latency, directly measuring the execution time on target mobile platforms during the search process. This allows the discovery of architectures that are not only accurate but also highly efficient in terms of real-world inference speed. We evaluate MnasNet on the ImageNet classification task and demonstrate that it achieves state-of-the-art accuracy and latency trade-offs compared to existing mobile-optimized models. Specifically, MnasNet achieves comparable accuracy to MobileNetV2 with significantly lower latency on mobile devices. The results highlight the effectiveness of platform-aware neural architecture search in discovering efficient and accurate deep learning models tailored for mobile deployment. This approach paves the way for wider adoption of advanced deep learning techniques on edge devices.

Keywords: Neural Architecture Search, Mobile Devices, Deep Learning, Platform-Aware, Latency Optimization, Image Classification, MnasNet.
