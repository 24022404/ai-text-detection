Visual attribute prediction is a critical task in computer vision, with applications ranging from image understanding to autonomous systems. This paper introduces Progressive Attention Networks (PAN), a novel deep learning framework designed to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of visual attribute prediction. The proposed approach leverages a progressive attention mechanism that dynamically refines feature representations across multiple stages, enabling the model to focus on the most relevant regions of an image. Unlike traditional methods that rely on static attention mechanisms, PAN adaptively adjusts its focus based on the complexity of the input, improving both interpretability and performance. We evaluate PAN on benchmark datasets, including CUB-200-2011 and CelebA, demonstrating significant improvements in prediction accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods. Experimental results show that PAN achieves a 5.2% higher F1-score on average, while maintaining computational efficiency. The framework’s ability to generalize across diverse datasets highlights its robustness and scalability. This research contributes to the field by introducing a dynamic attention mechanism that can be integrated into various vision tasks, offering potential applications in areas such as medical imaging, surveillance, and augmented reality. Keywords: visual attribute prediction, progressive attention, deep learning, computer vision, dynamic feature refinement.
