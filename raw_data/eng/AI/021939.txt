This paper presents a novel approach to semi-supervised volumetric medical image segmentation by introducing Momentum Contrastive Voxel-wise Representation Learning. The objective is to improve the accuracy of segmenting medical images, such as MRI and CT scans, with limited labeled data. Our method leverages contrastive learning to learn voxel-wise representations, which are then used for segmentation. We employ a momentum-based approach to maintain consistency between the query and key representations, allowing for more effective learning from unlabeled data. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art semi-supervised methods, achieving significant improvements in segmentation accuracy. The key findings include the effectiveness of our momentum contrastive framework in learning informative voxel-wise representations and the importance of incorporating both labeled and unlabeled data for robust segmentation. This research contributes to the field of medical image analysis by providing a powerful tool for semi-supervised segmentation, with potential applications in clinical diagnosis and treatment. Key keywords: semi-supervised learning, volumetric medical image segmentation, contrastive learning, voxel-wise representation learning, momentum-based approach.
