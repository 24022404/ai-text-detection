The growing intersection of blockchain technology and machine learning presents unique challenges and opportunities in analyzing data stored within blockchain networks. This paper introduces ChainNet, a novel framework designed to perform learning on blockchain graphs by leveraging their rich topological features. Our objective is to enhance the understanding and application of machine learning models in the context of blockchain data, thereby improving the ability to extract meaningful insights from these complex networks.

Methods/Approach: ChainNet employs a specialized graph neural network architecture that accounts for the unique structure and dynamic nature of blockchain systems. By integrating topological features such as transaction patterns, node connectivity, and temporal evolution, our approach provides a comprehensive learning model tailored for blockchain environments. The framework utilizes cutting-edge techniques in representation learning to construct embeddings that capture essential blockchain characteristics.

Results/Findings: Experiments conducted on real-world blockchain datasets demonstrate the superiority of ChainNet in capturing underlying patterns and predicting transactional behaviors compared to conventional models. Our results show significant improvements in metrics such as accuracy, precision, and computational efficiency. ChainNet also successfully identifies anomalous patterns indicative of fraudulent activities, underscoring its potential in enhancing security measures within blockchain ecosystems.

Conclusion/Implications: By presenting a novel methodology for learning on blockchain networks with a focus on topological features, ChainNet makes notable contributions to the fields of blockchain data analysis and graph neural networks. The implications of this research extend to improving predictive capabilities, enhancing fraud detection mechanisms, and facilitating more informed decision-making processes in blockchain-based systems. ChainNet sets a new benchmark for future research in leveraging blockchain graphs for advanced data analysis tasks.

Keywords: ChainNet, blockchain graphs, graph neural network, topological features, fraud detection, representation learning.
