This paper introduces an ante-hoc approach to induce semantic grouping of latent concepts for explanations, addressing the need for transparency and interpretability in complex models. Our objective is to develop a methodology that enables the discovery of meaningful, high-level abstractions from latent representations, facilitating a deeper understanding of model decisions. We propose a novel framework that leverages a combination of dimensionality reduction and clustering techniques to identify semantically coherent groups of latent concepts. Our approach is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its ability to uncover insightful explanations and improve model interpretability. The results show that our method outperforms existing post-hoc explanation techniques in terms of accuracy and consistency. This research contributes to the development of more transparent and trustworthy AI systems, with potential applications in areas such as explainable AI, feature attribution, and model debugging. Key keywords: explainable AI, latent concepts, semantic grouping, ante-hoc explanations, model interpretability.
