This paper proposes a novel forecasting method for grouped time series data, leveraging the k-Means algorithm to enhance prediction accuracy. The objective is to address the challenge of forecasting multiple related time series simultaneously, which is common in various fields such as finance, economics, and logistics. Our approach involves applying the k-Means clustering algorithm to group similar time series together, followed by the implementation of a forecasting model tailored to each cluster. The results show that our method outperforms traditional forecasting techniques, particularly in cases where time series exhibit similar patterns. Key findings include improved forecasting accuracy and reduced computational complexity. The proposed method has significant implications for applications where grouped time series forecasting is crucial, such as demand forecasting and resource allocation. By combining the strengths of clustering analysis and time series forecasting, this research contributes to the development of more accurate and efficient forecasting methods, with potential applications in data-driven decision-making and strategic planning. Keywords: time series forecasting, k-Means algorithm, clustering analysis, demand forecasting, data-driven decision-making.
