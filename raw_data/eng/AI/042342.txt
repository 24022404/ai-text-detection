Objective: Facial expression manipulation and analysis traditionally require separate models for generation, editing, and recognition, leading to increased complexity and resource demands. This paper introduces All-In-One, a unified deep network that simultaneously performs expression transfer, editing, and recognition within a single architecture.

Methods: All-In-One employs a shared encoder to extract a disentangled representation of facial identity and expression. A pair of decoder branches then generate edited or transferred faces under arbitrary target expressions, guided by a learned expression embedding. A classification head attached to the encoder enables real-time recognition of the input expression. The model is trained end-to-end using a joint multi-task loss that combines reconstruction, adversarial, perceptual, and classification objectives.

Results: Experiments on benchmark datasets (CK+, AffectNet, RaFD) demonstrate that All-In-One achieves transfer and editing quality on par with state-of-the-art task-specific GANs, while matching or exceeding recognition accuracy of dedicated classifiers. Compared to separate models, our approach reduces inference time by 40% and parameter count by 35%, without sacrificing performance.

Conclusion: All-In-One offers a compact, efficient solution for comprehensive facial expression processing. Its ability to unify generation and analysis tasks simplifies deployment in interactive applications such as virtual avatars, video conferencing, and affective computing interfaces. 

Keywords: facial expression transfer, expression editing, emotion recognition, multi-task learning, unified network, generative adversarial network.
