The rapid spread of rumors on social media platforms poses significant challenges to information credibility and public trust. This study addresses the critical problem of rumor detection by leveraging time-series representations of tweets and ensemble deep learning techniques. We propose a novel approach that transforms tweet data into temporal sequences, capturing the dynamic evolution of information over time. Our method integrates multiple deep learning models, including Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks and Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), within an ensemble framework to enhance detection accuracy. The models are trained on a large-scale dataset of labeled tweets, encompassing diverse rumor and non-rumor cases. Experimental results demonstrate that our ensemble approach outperforms existing methods, achieving superior precision, recall, and F1-score in rumor classification. Key findings highlight the importance of temporal patterns in distinguishing rumors from factual information. The proposed framework not only advances the state-of-the-art in rumor detection but also offers practical implications for real-time monitoring and mitigation of misinformation on social media. This research contributes to the broader field of information integrity by providing a robust, scalable solution for identifying and combating rumors in dynamic online environments.  

**Keywords**: rumor detection, social media, time-series analysis, ensemble deep learning, LSTM, CNN, misinformation.
