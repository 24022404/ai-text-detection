CityLearn provides a standardized, open-source environment for multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) research in demand response and urban energy management. Addressing the challenge of replicating and comparing results across diverse research efforts, CityLearn offers a flexible simulation platform with customizable building energy models, renewable energy integration, and realistic demand response programs. This environment facilitates the development and evaluation of MARL algorithms for optimizing energy consumption, reducing peak demand, and improving grid stability in urban settings. We present the CityLearn framework, its key features, and example MARL implementations, demonstrating its utility in addressing complex energy management challenges. Experiments showcase the platform's capability to simulate diverse scenarios and benchmark different MARL strategies. CityLearn enables researchers to accelerate progress in applying MARL to smart cities, promoting collaboration and reproducibility in the field. Keywords: Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning, Demand Response, Smart Grids, Energy Management, Simulation, Open-Source, CityLearn.
