This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing the resolution of wavelet-encoded images, a crucial task in various image processing applications. Our objective is to develop an efficient super-resolution technique that can effectively reconstruct high-quality images from their wavelet-encoded counterparts. We propose a novel approach that leverages the properties of wavelet transforms and deep learning-based methods to achieve superior image reconstruction. Our method utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture to learn the mapping between wavelet-encoded images and their high-resolution counterparts. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and visual information fidelity (VIF). The proposed technique has important implications for various applications, including image compression, denoising, and reconstruction. Key contributions of this research include the development of a wavelet-encoded image super-resolution framework, the design of a novel CNN architecture, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in reconstructing high-quality images. Relevant keywords: super-resolution, wavelet encoding, deep learning, image reconstruction, CNN architecture.
