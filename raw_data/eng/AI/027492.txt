This paper introduces a novel approach to parallelizing computation within neural networks by leveraging angle-parametrized orthogonal matrices. Our objective is to enhance training efficiency, particularly in deep learning models, by exploiting the inherent properties of orthogonal matrices for stable gradient propagation and efficient parallel processing. We present a method for decomposing orthogonal matrices into a series of Givens rotations, enabling distributed computation of matrix-vector products across multiple processing units. The backpropagation algorithm is then adapted to efficiently handle the angle-parametrized representation. Experimental results demonstrate significant speedups compared to traditional dense matrix implementations, especially when training deep neural networks on large datasets. Furthermore, the use of orthogonal matrices ensures stable training dynamics, mitigating issues such as vanishing and exploding gradients. The proposed parallelized backpropagation scheme offers a promising avenue for accelerating deep learning research and deployment, particularly for applications requiring high computational throughput. Keywords: Parallel Computing, Backpropagation, Orthogonal Matrices, Deep Learning, Givens Rotations, Angle Parameterization.
