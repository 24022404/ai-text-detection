The analysis assesses system performance across a variety of connection conditions by closely examining key metrics such as latency, requests per second (req/sec), and throughput. Latency measurements, particularly the 2.5th percentile and median values, are used to represent the typical experience of most users, while elevated percentile values often indicate potential congestion points within the system. The number of requests the system can handle per second serves as an important indicator of its concurrent processing capability, highlighting how well the system scales to accommodate multiple users or tasks simultaneously. Throughput further contributes to the evaluation by measuring the efficiency of data transmission, providing insights into the bandwidth utilized and the speed at which information is communicated. Additionally, standard deviation calculations for both latency and req/sec are considered essential, as they reflect the consistency and reliability of system performance by exposing any fluctuations or irregularities during operation.
