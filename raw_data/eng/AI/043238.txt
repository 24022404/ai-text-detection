Unsupervised Domain Adaptation (UDA) for 3D object detection aims to transfer knowledge from a labeled source domain to an unlabeled target domain. Existing methods often suffer from noisy pseudo-labels generated on the target domain, hindering performance. This paper introduces ST3D++, a novel denoised self-training framework to address this issue. ST3D++ builds upon a self-training paradigm, incorporating a dynamic pseudo-label filtering mechanism based on both prediction confidence and feature consistency to reduce noise. Furthermore, we introduce a contextual regularization technique that leverages the relationships between objects to refine predictions, especially for challenging cases. We evaluate ST3D++ on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements over state-of-the-art UDA methods for 3D object detection. Specifically, ST3D++ achieves superior performance in adapting across different LiDAR sensor configurations and environmental conditions. The results highlight the effectiveness of our denoised self-training approach in mitigating the impact of noisy labels and improving adaptation accuracy. Keywords: 3D Object Detection, Unsupervised Domain Adaptation, Self-training, Denoising, LiDAR, Point Cloud.
