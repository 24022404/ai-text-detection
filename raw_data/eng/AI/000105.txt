This paper addresses the challenge of forecasting in multivariate irregularly sampled time series with missing values, a common problem in various fields such as finance, healthcare, and environmental monitoring. Our objective is to develop a robust and accurate forecasting model that can handle irregular sampling and missing data. We propose a novel approach that combines advanced imputation techniques with state-of-the-art machine learning algorithms, specifically designed for multivariate time series forecasting. Our method utilizes a combination of interpolation and extrapolation techniques to estimate missing values, followed by a deep learning-based forecasting model that captures complex patterns and relationships between variables. The results show that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy and robustness, particularly in scenarios with high levels of missing data and irregular sampling. The key contributions of this research include the development of a flexible and scalable forecasting framework, the introduction of a new imputation technique tailored to multivariate irregularly sampled time series, and the demonstration of the effectiveness of our approach in real-world applications. Our work has important implications for fields where accurate forecasting is critical, and we highlight the potential of our method for applications such as predictive maintenance, demand forecasting, and anomaly detection, with key keywords including time series forecasting, missing values, irregular sampling, machine learning, and deep learning.
