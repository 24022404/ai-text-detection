This paper presents a novel variant of the Greedy-GQ algorithm, incorporating variance reduction techniques to enhance its finite-time performance. The objective is to improve the convergence rate and complexity of existing stochastic optimization methods. Our approach employs a greedy selection strategy combined with variance reduction, yielding a more efficient and robust algorithm. Theoretical analysis reveals that Greedy-GQ with variance reduction achieves improved complexity and finite-time guarantees, outperforming state-of-the-art methods. Key findings include a significant reduction in the number of required iterations and improved stability. The proposed algorithm has important implications for large-scale machine learning and optimization problems, offering a more efficient and scalable solution. Our research contributes to the advancement of stochastic optimization, highlighting the benefits of integrating greedy selection with variance reduction techniques. Relevant keywords: stochastic optimization, greedy algorithms, variance reduction, finite-time analysis, complexity theory, machine learning.
