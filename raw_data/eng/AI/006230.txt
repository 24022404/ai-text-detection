Object detection remains a critical challenge in computer vision, requiring accurate localization and classification of objects within complex scenes. This paper introduces RON (Reverse Connection with Objectness Prior Networks), a novel framework designed to enhance object detection performance by leveraging reverse connections and objectness priors. The proposed approach integrates a reverse connection mechanism to efficiently propagate multi-scale features, enabling the network to capture both fine-grained details and high-level semantic information. Additionally, objectness priors are incorporated to improve the detection of objects by focusing on regions with high object likelihood, reducing false positives and enhancing overall accuracy.  

Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets, such as PASCAL VOC and MS COCO, demonstrate that RON achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of both precision and recall. The framework is computationally efficient, making it suitable for real-time applications. Key contributions include the novel reverse connection architecture and the integration of objectness priors, which collectively address limitations in multi-scale feature fusion and object localization.  

The findings highlight the potential of RON for advancing object detection in diverse applications, including autonomous driving, surveillance, and robotics. This work provides a robust foundation for future research in improving detection accuracy and efficiency in complex visual environments.  

**Keywords**: Object detection, reverse connection, objectness prior, multi-scale feature fusion, computer vision, deep learning.
