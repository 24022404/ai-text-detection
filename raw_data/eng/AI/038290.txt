This paper presents a novel approach to camouflaged object detection, leveraging deep texture-aware features to enhance detection accuracy. The objective is to develop a robust model that can effectively identify camouflaged objects in complex environments. Our method employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, incorporating texture-aware modules that extract subtle texture cues from images. The results show significant improvements in detection performance, outperforming state-of-the-art methods on benchmark datasets. Key findings include the ability to detect camouflaged objects with varying textures, colors, and backgrounds. The proposed approach contributes to the field of computer vision, offering a unique solution for camouflage detection with potential applications in surveillance, wildlife monitoring, and autonomous systems. By emphasizing texture-aware features, this research provides a new perspective on object detection, highlighting the importance of texture information in identifying concealed objects. Relevant keywords: camouflaged object detection, deep learning, texture-aware features, computer vision, object detection, CNN architecture.
