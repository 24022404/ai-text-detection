This paper introduces O-CNN, a novel octree-based convolutional neural network designed for efficient 3D shape analysis. Traditional 3D CNNs suffer from high computational costs due to the cubic growth of voxel data. O-CNN addresses this challenge by leveraging octree data structures to adaptively partition 3D space, focusing computation on regions with high geometric complexity. Our approach drastically reduces memory consumption and computational complexity compared to dense voxel representations, enabling the processing of high-resolution 3D shapes. We develop specialized convolution, pooling, and unpooling operations tailored for octree structures. Experiments on several benchmark datasets for 3D shape classification and segmentation demonstrate that O-CNN achieves state-of-the-art performance with significantly improved efficiency. The octree-based approach offers a scalable and effective solution for analyzing complex 3D geometries, paving the way for broader applications in computer vision, robotics, and 3D modeling. Key words: 3D Shape Analysis, Convolutional Neural Networks, Octree, Deep Learning, 3D CNN.
