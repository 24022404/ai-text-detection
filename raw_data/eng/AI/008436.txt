This paper proposes a novel approach to image-to-image translation, leveraging a group-wise deep whitening-and-coloring transformation to achieve high-quality image synthesis. The objective is to develop an efficient and effective method for translating images from one domain to another, preserving the content and structure of the input image while adapting to the style and characteristics of the target domain. Our approach employs a deep neural network architecture that incorporates a group-wise whitening-and-coloring transformation, which normalizes and decorrelates the feature representations of the input image, followed by a coloring transformation that adapts the normalized features to the target domain. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our method over existing image-to-image translation approaches, with significant improvements in terms of visual quality, diversity, and realism. The proposed method has numerous applications in computer vision, including image editing, style transfer, and data augmentation. Key contributions of this work include the introduction of a group-wise deep whitening-and-coloring transformation, which enables efficient and effective image-to-image translation, and the demonstration of its potential in various applications. Relevant keywords: image-to-image translation, deep learning, group-wise transformation, whitening-and-coloring, computer vision.
