The objective of this research is to address the challenge of video super-resolution, specifically enhancing the resolution of low-quality video frames. We propose a novel approach, the Temporally Deformable Alignment Network (TDAN), which leverages temporal coherence in video sequences to improve alignment and reconstruction processes. The TDAN approach incorporates an innovative deformable convolutional module designed to handle motion dynamics and align features across consecutive frames effectively. By employing this temporally aware architecture, our model can dynamically adapt to temporal deformations, thereby providing a more precise alignment of features. Extensive experiments demonstrate that TDAN outperforms existing state-of-the-art video super-resolution methods. Key findings indicate that our model achieves significant improvements in visual quality and detail reconstruction, particularly in scenarios with complex motion. The TDAN provides a robust framework for enhancing video quality in fields requiring high-resolution outputs, such as surveillance, entertainment, and advanced imaging systems. In conclusion, our work contributes to the video super-resolution domain by introducing an effective temporal alignment method, paving the way for future research in optimizing video frame enhancement. Keywords: video super-resolution, temporal alignment, deformable convolution, TDAN, motion dynamics.
