This paper presents a semi-automated statistical algorithm designed to address the challenge of object separation in complex datasets, a critical task in fields such as image processing, computer vision, and data analysis. The proposed algorithm leverages statistical methods to identify and isolate objects within datasets, combining automated processes with user-guided inputs to enhance accuracy and adaptability. By integrating clustering techniques and probabilistic models, the algorithm effectively handles overlapping and densely packed objects, which are often problematic in fully automated approaches. Experimental results demonstrate the algorithm's superior performance in terms of precision and recall compared to existing methods, particularly in scenarios with high noise levels or irregular object distributions. The study highlights the algorithm's potential for applications in medical imaging, remote sensing, and industrial automation, where accurate object separation is essential. The key contribution of this work lies in its hybrid approach, which balances automation with user control, offering a flexible and robust solution for object separation tasks. Keywords: object separation, statistical algorithm, semi-automated, clustering, probabilistic models, image processing.
