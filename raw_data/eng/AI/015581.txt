This paper proposes a novel Multi-scale Interactive Network (MIN) for salient object detection, aiming to accurately identify prominent objects in images. Our approach integrates a hierarchical feature extraction mechanism with interactive learning, enabling the network to capture both local and global contextual information. The MIN model leverages a combination of convolutional neural networks and attention-based modules to selectively focus on salient regions. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art models in terms of precision, recall, and F-measure on benchmark datasets. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a multi-scale interactive architecture and a novel loss function that enhances the detection of salient objects. Our work has significant implications for computer vision applications, such as image segmentation, object recognition, and visual attention modeling. Key keywords: salient object detection, multi-scale features, interactive learning, convolutional neural networks, attention mechanisms.
