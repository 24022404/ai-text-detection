This paper explores the application of machine learning techniques to analyze machine data generated from a Computer-Assisted Telephone Interviewing (CATI) network. The objective is to identify patterns and anomalies within the network's operational data to improve efficiency and resource allocation. We employ several supervised and unsupervised learning models, including random forests, support vector machines, and clustering algorithms, to process call logs, agent performance metrics, and system resource utilization data. Our findings reveal that machine learning models can effectively predict call center performance, identify underperforming agents, and detect potential system bottlenecks with an accuracy rate exceeding 85%. Furthermore, anomaly detection techniques uncovered unexpected call patterns indicative of fraudulent activity. The results demonstrate the potential of machine learning to enhance the management and optimization of CATI networks, leading to cost savings and improved service quality. Key words: Machine Learning, CATI Network, Call Center, Anomaly Detection, Performance Prediction.
