As React applications grow in size, performance challenges often emerge from the accumulation of numerous components, expanding global state, and the inclusion of hefty third-party libraries. These factors can contribute to slower load times and increased client-side processing, which may negatively affect the user experience. To address these issues, developers should prioritize loading only the essential code at the moment it is required. By implementing techniques such as code splitting and lazy loading, it becomes possible to optimize the application’s efficiency and responsiveness.
