This paper proposes a novel method for fuzzy time series forecasting, integrating interval index numbers and membership values with support vector machines (SVMs). The objective is to enhance the accuracy of forecasting by leveraging the strengths of both fuzzy logic and machine learning. The approach involves converting time series data into fuzzy sets, represented by interval index numbers and corresponding membership values, which are then used to train an SVM model. The results show significant improvements in forecasting accuracy compared to traditional methods, with the proposed model demonstrating robust performance across various datasets. The conclusion highlights the potential of this innovative method for applications in finance, economics, and other fields where time series forecasting is crucial. Key contributions include the introduction of a new fuzzy time series representation and the successful integration of SVMs with fuzzy logic, offering a promising direction for future research in forecasting and predictive analytics, with relevant keywords including fuzzy time series, support vector machine, interval index number, membership value, and forecasting accuracy.
