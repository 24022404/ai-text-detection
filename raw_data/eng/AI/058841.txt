This paper introduces a novel approach to representation learning, leveraging adversarially-contrastive optimal transport to enhance the quality and robustness of learned representations. The objective is to develop a framework that can effectively capture complex data distributions and relationships, leading to improved performance in downstream tasks. Our method employs a unique combination of adversarial training and contrastive learning, facilitated by optimal transport theory, to identify and preserve essential data characteristics. The results demonstrate significant improvements in representation quality, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods on various benchmarks. Key findings include enhanced robustness to adversarial attacks and improved transferability across different domains. The proposed approach contributes to the advancement of representation learning, with potential applications in computer vision, natural language processing, and generative modeling. By highlighting the importance of optimal transport in adversarially-contrastive learning, this research opens up new avenues for exploring the intersection of machine learning, optimization, and geometry, with relevant keywords including optimal transport, adversarial training, contrastive learning, and representation learning.
