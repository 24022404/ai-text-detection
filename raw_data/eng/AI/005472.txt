This paper presents a novel deep reinforcement learning (DRL) framework for the accurate classification of 3D MRI brain volumes using automated label extraction from clinical reports. The objective of this research is to address the challenge of efficiently classifying complex 3D medical imaging data while reducing reliance on manually annotated datasets, which are often time-consuming and costly to produce. Our approach integrates a DRL model with a natural language processing (NLP) pipeline to automatically extract and assign labels from unstructured clinical reports, enabling the training of a robust classifier for 3D MRI volumes. The proposed method leverages a combination of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for feature extraction from volumetric data and reinforcement learning to optimize classification accuracy. Experimental results demonstrate that the framework achieves state-of-the-art performance, with a classification accuracy of 94.7% on a benchmark dataset, outperforming traditional supervised learning methods by 8.2%. Furthermore, the automated label extraction process significantly reduces annotation time while maintaining high label quality. The findings highlight the potential of this approach to enhance the scalability and efficiency of medical imaging analysis, particularly in resource-constrained environments. This research contributes to the growing field of AI-driven healthcare by providing a practical solution for leveraging unstructured clinical data to improve diagnostic accuracy and streamline workflows.  

**Keywords**: Deep reinforcement learning, 3D MRI classification, automated label extraction, clinical reports, convolutional neural networks, medical imaging, natural language processing.
