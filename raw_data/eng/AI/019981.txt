This paper addresses the challenge of learning correspondence between two time-series datasets, with the objective of establishing a robust and efficient method for temporal alignment. Our approach leverages the concept of cycle-consistency, where we formulate a novel loss function that enforces consistency across time cycles, enabling the model to learn meaningful correspondences. We employ a deep learning framework, utilizing a combination of convolutional neural networks and recurrent neural networks to capture both spatial and temporal features. Our results demonstrate significant improvements in correspondence accuracy and robustness, outperforming existing methods on various benchmark datasets. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of cycle-consistency in learning temporal correspondences, with potential applications in areas such as video analysis, motion forecasting, and time-series analysis. Our contributions include a novel cycle-consistency loss function, a deep learning architecture for temporal correspondence learning, and a comprehensive evaluation framework. Key keywords: temporal correspondence, cycle-consistency, deep learning, time-series analysis, computer vision.
