This paper presents a novel spectral approach to hyperparameter optimization, addressing the critical issue of efficiently tuning model parameters to achieve optimal performance. Our objective is to develop a method that surpasses traditional optimization techniques by leveraging spectral analysis to identify optimal hyperparameter settings. We propose a new algorithm that utilizes spectral decomposition to analyze the hyperparameter space, allowing for the identification of key patterns and relationships that inform the optimization process. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of convergence speed and solution quality. Our spectral approach enables the optimization of complex models, including deep neural networks, and has significant implications for applications in machine learning, artificial intelligence, and data science. Key contributions include the introduction of a spectral framework for hyperparameter optimization, improved optimization efficiency, and enhanced model performance. Relevant keywords: hyperparameter optimization, spectral analysis, machine learning, artificial intelligence, model optimization.
