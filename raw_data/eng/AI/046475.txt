Video semantic segmentation is a critical task in computer vision, enabling precise pixel-level understanding of dynamic scenes. However, existing methods often struggle with feature distortions caused by motion blur, occlusion, and rapid scene changes, leading to degraded segmentation accuracy. This paper introduces a novel distortion-aware feature correction framework designed to address these challenges. Our approach leverages a multi-scale feature extraction module combined with a distortion-aware attention mechanism to identify and correct feature inconsistencies across video frames. By integrating temporal information and spatial context, the proposed model dynamically refines feature representations, ensuring robust segmentation even in complex scenarios. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing approaches by a significant margin in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The framework's ability to handle real-world distortions makes it highly applicable to autonomous driving, video surveillance, and augmented reality systems. This work contributes a scalable and effective solution to video semantic segmentation, paving the way for more reliable and efficient video understanding technologies.  

**Keywords**: video semantic segmentation, feature correction, distortion-aware, attention mechanism, computer vision, dynamic scenes.
