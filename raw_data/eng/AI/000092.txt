Incremental sequence learning is a critical challenge in machine learning, particularly for applications requiring continuous adaptation to evolving data streams. This paper addresses the problem of efficiently learning and updating sequential models without retraining from scratch, which is computationally expensive and impractical for real-time systems. We propose a novel incremental learning framework that leverages lightweight updates to existing models, ensuring scalability and adaptability. Our approach combines dynamic memory mechanisms with attention-based architectures to capture temporal dependencies while minimizing resource overhead. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed method achieves competitive accuracy compared to traditional batch learning approaches, with significantly reduced training time and memory usage. The framework also exhibits robustness to concept drift, making it suitable for applications such as real-time recommendation systems, natural language processing, and time-series forecasting. This research contributes a practical solution for incremental sequence learning, enabling the deployment of adaptive models in dynamic environments. Key innovations include the integration of memory-efficient updates and the ability to handle non-stationary data streams effectively. Keywords: incremental learning, sequence modeling, dynamic memory, attention mechanisms, concept drift.
