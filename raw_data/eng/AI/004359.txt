Odoo Helpdesk offers a comprehensive suite of features to streamline support operations, beginning with account registration and system login via Gmail or password. Users can submit requests through a dedicated registration form, which automatically generates support tickets. These tickets are managed and processed by administrators, who can approve and assign them to appropriate agents for resolution. Customers receive timely notifications about any changes in their ticket status. Additionally, Odoo Helpdesk provides integrated chat capabilities, SLA management, and robust reporting tools.
