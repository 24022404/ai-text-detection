This paper investigates the application of the Sugeno integral within machine learning, specifically focusing on binary classification tasks. We introduce a novel approach that leverages the Sugeno integral to fuse multiple feature inputs, allowing for a flexible and robust classification model. Our method departs from traditional linear or additive models by incorporating the capacity of the Sugeno integral to capture non-linear interactions between features. The proposed Sugeno integral-based classifier is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating competitive performance compared to conventional machine learning algorithms like Support Vector Machines and Logistic Regression. Furthermore, we analyze the interpretability of the learned fuzzy measures, providing insights into the relative importance and synergistic effects of different features. The results indicate that the Sugeno integral offers a promising alternative for binary classification, particularly in scenarios where feature dependencies play a significant role. This research contributes to the growing field of fuzzy machine learning by providing a practical and effective method for non-linear feature fusion in classification problems.
Keywords: Sugeno Integral, Fuzzy Measures, Machine Learning, Binary Classification, Non-linear Fusion, Feature Interaction.
