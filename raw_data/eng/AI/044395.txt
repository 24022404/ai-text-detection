This paper introduces WarpedGANSpace, a novel approach to navigating the latent space of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) using non-linear Radial Basis Function (RBF) paths. The objective is to discover complex, non-linear relationships between latent variables, enabling more efficient and effective exploration of the generative model's capabilities. Our method employs a warped mapping technique to identify optimal RBF paths, allowing for more precise control over the generated outputs. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of WarpedGANSpace in producing high-quality, diverse samples that outperform traditional linear interpolation methods. The key findings highlight the importance of non-linear relationships in GAN latent space and the potential applications of WarpedGANSpace in image and video generation, data augmentation, and style transfer. By contributing a new perspective on GAN latent space navigation, this research opens up new avenues for improving the quality and versatility of generative models, with significant implications for the field of computer vision and AI. Key keywords: GANs, latent space, RBF, non-linear paths, generative models, image generation.
