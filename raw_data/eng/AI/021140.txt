This paper investigates the integration of sensing functionalities into cellular networks using NR sidelink communication. The primary objective is to enhance environmental awareness and enable novel applications such as intelligent transportation and smart city initiatives. We propose a novel framework leveraging NR sidelink to facilitate direct device-to-device communication for distributed sensing and data aggregation. Our approach explores the potential of utilizing sidelink's proximity-based services to improve the efficiency and reduce the latency of data collection from geographically dispersed sensors. We develop a resource allocation algorithm tailored for sidelink-based sensing to mitigate interference and optimize network throughput. Simulation results demonstrate that our proposed framework significantly improves the sensing data delivery ratio and reduces end-to-end latency compared to conventional cellular uplink-based sensing. The findings suggest that NR sidelink can serve as a viable and efficient technology for integrating diverse sensing applications into future cellular networks, unlocking new possibilities for real-time monitoring and control.

**Keywords:** NR Sidelink, Sensing, Cellular Networks, Device-to-Device Communication, Resource Allocation, Intelligent Transportation.
