This paper introduces BDgraph, a novel R package designed for Bayesian structure learning in graphical models. The package facilitates efficient estimation of undirected graphs, particularly Gaussian and non-paranormal graphical models, using Bayesian approaches. BDgraph implements several key algorithms, including a birth-death Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampler for posterior exploration and optimization, enabling researchers to infer network structures from observational data. The package also offers functionalities for handling high-dimensional data and incorporates priors that encourage sparsity in the resulting graphs. Performance evaluations demonstrate BDgraph's computational efficiency and accuracy in recovering true network structures compared to existing methods. BDgraph provides a user-friendly interface and comprehensive tools for statistical network analysis, offering a valuable resource for researchers in various fields seeking to understand complex dependencies among variables. The package is freely available on CRAN, promoting accessibility and reproducibility in Bayesian graphical modeling. Keywords: Bayesian networks, graphical models, structure learning, R package, Markov chain Monte Carlo, high-dimensional data.
