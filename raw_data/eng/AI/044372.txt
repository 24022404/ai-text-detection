This paper introduces DeepIGeoS, a novel deep interactive geodesic framework designed to enhance medical image segmentation. The primary objective is to address the limitations of conventional segmentation methods, which often struggle with accuracy and adaptability in complex anatomical structures. DeepIGeoS integrates deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) with interactive user guidance through geodesic distance maps, allowing for precise control and improved segmentation outcomes. The proposed approach leverages user-provided clicks or strokes to inform the network about target regions, which are then interpreted via geodesic maps that capture spatial relationships within the image. Extensive experiments on multiple medical imaging datasets, including MRI and CT scans, demonstrate that DeepIGeoS achieves superior segmentation accuracy compared to fully automatic and other interactive methods, particularly in challenging scenarios with ambiguous boundaries or low contrast. The framework also exhibits strong generalization across different imaging modalities and anatomical regions. Furthermore, the system significantly reduces the amount of user interaction required, thereby streamlining the segmentation workflow for clinicians. In conclusion, DeepIGeoS represents a substantial advancement in medical image segmentation, offering a flexible, accurate, and user-friendly tool that holds potential for broad application in clinical and research environments. Key keywords include deep learning, medical image segmentation, interactive segmentation, geodesic distance, and convolutional neural networks.
