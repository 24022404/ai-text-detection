Federated Learning (FL) has emerged as a promising paradigm for collaborative model training across distributed devices while preserving data privacy. However, integrating differential privacy (DP) into FL systems under communication constraints remains a significant challenge. This paper addresses the trade-offs between privacy guarantees, communication efficiency, and model performance in FL settings. We propose a novel framework that combines DP mechanisms with adaptive compression techniques to minimize communication overhead without compromising privacy or accuracy. Our approach leverages gradient sparsification and quantization, coupled with noise injection calibrated to meet DP requirements. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves a 20% reduction in communication costs while maintaining strong privacy guarantees (ε < 2.0) and competitive model accuracy compared to baseline methods. The findings highlight the feasibility of deploying privacy-preserving FL in resource-constrained environments, such as IoT and edge computing. This work contributes to advancing secure and efficient distributed learning systems, paving the way for broader adoption in real-world applications.  

**Keywords**: Federated Learning, Differential Privacy, Communication Efficiency, Gradient Compression, Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning.
