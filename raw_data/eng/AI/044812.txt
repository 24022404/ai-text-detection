This paper addresses the challenge of image captioning when encountering novel objects not present in the training data. The objective is to develop a system capable of generating accurate captions for images containing unseen objects. To achieve this, we propose a novel approach that utilizes online vocabulary expansion, enabling the model to dynamically adapt to new objects. Our method employs a combination of natural language processing and computer vision techniques, leveraging a neural network architecture to learn the relationships between objects, scenes, and captions. The results show significant improvements in captioning accuracy, particularly for images featuring novel objects, with our model outperforming state-of-the-art baselines. The key contribution of this research lies in its ability to expand the vocabulary of the captioning model online, allowing it to generalize to new objects and scenarios. This innovation has important implications for real-world applications, such as image search, visual question answering, and assistive technologies. Our approach highlights the potential of online learning and vocabulary expansion in image captioning, making it a valuable contribution to the field of artificial intelligence, computer vision, and natural language processing, with relevant keywords including image captioning, novel objects, online learning, vocabulary expansion, and neural networks.
