Accurate segmentation of lung computed tomography (CT) images is crucial for computer-aided diagnosis of pulmonary diseases. This paper introduces a novel knowledge-based fully convolutional network (KB-FCN) for improved lung segmentation. The KB-FCN leverages anatomical knowledge of lung structure to guide the segmentation process, incorporating prior information through a customized loss function and network architecture. The proposed method integrates a multi-scale FCN with attention mechanisms and shape constraints derived from lung anatomy. Experimental results on a publicly available lung CT dataset demonstrate that the KB-FCN achieves superior segmentation accuracy compared to state-of-the-art FCN-based methods, particularly in challenging cases with lung abnormalities. The KB-FCN exhibits improved robustness and generalization capability, offering a promising tool for automated lung disease analysis and diagnosis. This approach provides a novel framework for incorporating domain-specific knowledge into deep learning models for medical image segmentation. Keywords: Lung Segmentation, Convolutional Neural Network, Deep Learning, Computed Tomography, Medical Image Analysis, Knowledge-Based System.
