The objective of this research is to address the challenges in robust control systems through the integration of alternating optimization and quadrature methods. Traditional methods in control systems often encounter limitations when dealing with uncertainties and environmental variability. To overcome these, we propose a novel approach that leverages alternating optimization to iteratively refine control parameters, while incorporating quadrature techniques to accurately account for variation and uncertainty in system models. Our method is designed to enhance robustness by continually adjusting and predicting system behavior under different conditions. The research findings indicate significant improvements in system stability and performance when compared to conventional control methods, as demonstrated through extensive simulations and empirical testing. The combination of these methodologies ensures that the control system can withstand dynamic changes and maintain desired operational standards. This investigation contributes to the field by providing a more resilient solution to robust control and introduces potential applications in areas requiring reliable and adaptive control systems, such as autonomous vehicles and industrial automation. Key keywords include robust control, alternating optimization, quadrature methods, system stability, and adaptive control systems.
