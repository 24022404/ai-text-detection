Odoo enhances the traditional MVC architecture by introducing an "Action" layer, which directly links user interactions to particular actions, streamlining the response to user events. Its modular structure empowers developers to design independent modules, each focused on distinct functionalities, fostering scalability and maintainability. Throughout this thesis, the MVC paradigm serves as the foundation for organizing the codebase, efficiently managing the data layer, presentation tier, and the business logic, thus ensuring a well-structured application.
