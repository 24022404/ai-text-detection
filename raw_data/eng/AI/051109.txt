**Abstract**

Accurate lane topology extraction is critical for reliable autonomous vehicle navigation and advanced driver assistance systems. This paper presents DAGMapper, a novel learning-based framework designed to map road environments by discovering precise lane topology directly from sensor data. The proposed approach models the road network as a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG), enabling robust representation of complex lane shapes, splits, and merges. Leveraging a deep neural network architecture, DAGMapper processes multimodal inputs—including LiDAR point clouds and camera images—to infer lane boundaries and connections in real-time. Experimental evaluations on publicly available benchmark datasets demonstrate that DAGMapper achieves state-of-the-art accuracy in lane topology extraction and mapping, outperforming existing geometric and learning-based approaches in both urban and highway scenarios. The results indicate significant improvements in handling occlusions, lane merges, and intersections. This research contributes a scalable and efficient mapping solution that enhances the autonomy and safety of intelligent transportation systems. Keywords: lane topology, mapping, deep learning, autonomous driving, sensor fusion, graph representation, DAG.
