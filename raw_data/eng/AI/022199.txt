This paper investigates the development of an energy-efficient locomotion gait for snake robots using deep reinforcement learning (DRL). The objective is to discover a control policy that minimizes energy consumption while maintaining effective forward motion. We employ a DRL framework based on proximal policy optimization (PPO) to train a neural network controller, directly mapping robot states to joint torques. The reward function is carefully designed to encourage forward velocity and penalize energy expenditure. Simulation results demonstrate that the DRL-based controller significantly reduces energy consumption compared to traditional sinusoidal gaits, achieving a 15% improvement in energy efficiency. Furthermore, the learned gait exhibits robustness to variations in terrain friction. The proposed approach offers a promising solution for enhancing the operational endurance of snake robots in challenging environments. Keywords: Snake Robot, Reinforcement Learning, Gait Optimization, Energy Efficiency, Robotics.
