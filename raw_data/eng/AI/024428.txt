This paper addresses the challenge of modeling multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) channel modes for 6G vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communications. Our objective is to develop a deep learning approach that can learn transferable MIMO channel modes, enabling accurate prediction and efficient transmission in dynamic V2X environments. We propose a novel convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture that leverages spatial and temporal features of MIMO channels to learn robust and generalizable modes. Our approach utilizes a combination of supervised and unsupervised learning techniques to adapt to various V2X scenarios, including urban, highway, and rural settings. Simulation results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing channel modeling techniques, achieving significant improvements in transmission accuracy and reducing computational complexity. The key findings of this research highlight the potential of deep learning for MIMO channel modeling in 6G V2X communications, paving the way for enhanced reliability, safety, and efficiency in future wireless networks. Our contributions include the development of a transferable MIMO channel mode learning framework, which can be applied to various 6G V2X use cases, such as autonomous vehicles, smart cities, and industrial automation, with relevant keywords including 6G, V2X, MIMO, deep learning, channel modeling, and transfer learning.
