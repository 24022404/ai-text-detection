This paper explores the intersection of manifold learning and deep neural networks, proposing a novel framework where deep neural networks can be utilized as locality sensitive hash functions. The objective is to leverage the capabilities of deep learning in capturing complex data structures, while preserving the efficiency and scalability of locality sensitive hashing for dimensionality reduction and similarity search. Our approach employs a deep neural network architecture to learn compact, hash-based representations of high-dimensional data, enabling efficient and accurate manifold learning. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms traditional manifold learning techniques in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency, particularly in scenarios with high-dimensional and large-scale datasets. The key findings of this research highlight the potential of deep neural networks as a powerful tool for locality sensitive hashing, with significant implications for applications in data mining, computer vision, and information retrieval. By combining the strengths of deep learning and hashing, our work contributes to the development of more efficient and effective manifold learning algorithms, with potential applications in areas such as image and video analysis, natural language processing, and recommender systems. Key keywords: manifold learning, deep neural networks, locality sensitive hashing, dimensionality reduction, similarity search.
