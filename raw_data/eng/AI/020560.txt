Latent space manipulation offers powerful control over generative models, but often suffers from challenges in gradient estimation and computational efficiency. This paper introduces the Surrogate Gradient Field (SGF), a novel approach to facilitate efficient and effective latent space navigation. SGF constructs a continuous, differentiable vector field within the latent space that approximates the true gradient of a target objective function. This is achieved by training a neural network to predict the gradient based on local latent vectors and their corresponding objective values. Our method bypasses the need for backpropagation through the generative model during manipulation, resulting in significant speed improvements. We demonstrate the effectiveness of SGF across various generative tasks, including image editing and content creation, using models like GANs and Variational Autoencoders. Experimental results show that SGF achieves comparable or superior manipulation quality with orders of magnitude faster computation compared to existing gradient-based techniques. This work provides a practical and efficient framework for latent space manipulation, opening new avenues for interactive content generation and model understanding. Keywords: Latent Space Manipulation, Generative Models, Surrogate Gradient, Gradient Estimation, GAN, VAE.
