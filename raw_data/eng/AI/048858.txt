This paper presents an unsupervised approach to solving inverse problems using Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs). The objective is to develop a novel framework that can effectively recover the original input from a given set of indirect measurements, without requiring paired training data. Our approach employs a GAN-based architecture, where a generator network learns to produce solutions that are consistent with the measured data, while a discriminator network evaluates the generated solutions and guides the generator towards more accurate results. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method in solving various inverse problems, including image deblurring and signal recovery, with improved performance compared to traditional supervised methods. The key findings of this study highlight the potential of GANs in solving inverse problems, particularly in situations where labeled training data is scarce or unavailable. Our contributions include the development of a flexible and scalable framework for solving inverse problems, with potential applications in fields such as image processing, signal processing, and medical imaging. Key keywords: Generative Adversarial Networks, inverse problems, unsupervised learning, image deblurring, signal recovery.
