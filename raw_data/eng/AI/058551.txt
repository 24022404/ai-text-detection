This paper introduces MAGNet, a novel Multi-agent Graph Network designed to address the challenges of coordination and scalability in deep multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL). The objective of this research is to enhance the efficiency of decision-making processes in multi-agent systems by leveraging graph-based representations to model complex interactions among agents. MAGNet employs a graph neural network (GNN) framework to encode agent states and their relationships, enabling dynamic adaptation to evolving environments. The proposed approach integrates graph attention mechanisms to prioritize critical interactions, improving collaboration and resource allocation. Experiments were conducted on benchmark multi-agent environments, including cooperative and competitive scenarios, to evaluate the performance of MAGNet. Results demonstrate that MAGNet outperforms existing MARL methods in terms of scalability, convergence speed, and task success rates. The framework also exhibits robustness in handling environments with varying numbers of agents and sparse rewards. The key contribution of this work lies in its ability to generalize across diverse multi-agent settings while maintaining computational efficiency. MAGNet has potential applications in autonomous systems, robotics, and swarm intelligence, offering a scalable solution for real-world multi-agent coordination challenges.  

**Keywords**: Multi-agent reinforcement learning, graph neural networks, coordination, scalability, graph attention, autonomous systems.
