This paper addresses the challenge of metric learning in semi-supervised settings, where labeled data is scarce. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that leverages both labeled and unlabeled data to learn effective distance metrics. We propose a deep learning framework that resurrects traditional metric learning methods by integrating them with modern neural network architectures. Our approach utilizes a combination of convolutional neural networks and metric learning objectives to learn compact and informative representations. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our method, outperforming state-of-the-art semi-supervised metric learning approaches on several benchmark datasets. Key findings include improved clustering performance, enhanced image retrieval accuracy, and robustness to noisy labels. The proposed framework has significant implications for applications where labeled data is limited, such as image and video analysis, recommender systems, and anomaly detection. By highlighting the importance of metric learning in deep learning, this research contributes to the development of more accurate and efficient machine learning models, with relevant keywords including semi-supervised learning, metric learning, deep learning, neural networks, and representation learning.
