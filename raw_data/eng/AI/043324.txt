This paper introduces a novel approach to speech-driven facial reenactment using conditional generative adversarial networks (CGANs). The objective is to generate realistic and synchronized facial expressions from speech input, enabling applications in virtual avatars, teleconferencing, and entertainment. Our method employs a CGAN architecture conditioned on speech features, allowing for the synthesis of corresponding facial movements. Specifically, we use a deep neural network to extract acoustic features from the input speech and feed them into the generator network of the CGAN. The generator then produces a sequence of facial images, while the discriminator distinguishes between generated and real faces to enforce realistic output. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach generates high-quality facial reenactments that are temporally coherent and closely synchronized with the input speech, achieving superior performance compared to existing methods in terms of visual fidelity and lip synchronization accuracy. The proposed system enhances the realism of speech-driven animation and offers a robust solution for creating lifelike virtual characters. Keywords: Facial Reenactment, Speech Synthesis, Generative Adversarial Networks, CGAN, Deep Learning, Computer Animation.
