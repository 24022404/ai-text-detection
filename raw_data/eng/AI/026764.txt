The objective of this research is to address the challenges in neural rendering of complex textures, specifically focusing on fur, by introducing the SyntheticFur dataset. As neural rendering continues to advance, accurately capturing and representing fine details of fur remains a significant hurdle due to its intricate structure and varying light interactions. This paper presents a novel approach by developing a comprehensive SyntheticFur dataset designed to enhance the training of neural networks for realistic rendering of fur textures. The dataset is constructed using advanced simulation techniques that capture a wide range of fur types, lighting conditions, and viewpoints to ensure robustness and versatility. Our experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in the realism and accuracy of fur rendering when models are trained using the SyntheticFur dataset, outperforming existing baselines. The findings highlight the potential of this dataset to contribute significantly to fields such as virtual reality, game design, and film production where realistic rendering is crucial. By providing this dataset, we aim to facilitate further research and innovation in the domain of neural rendering, setting a new benchmark for the rendering of complex textures. Key keywords: neural rendering, SyntheticFur dataset, virtual reality, texture simulation, realistic rendering.
