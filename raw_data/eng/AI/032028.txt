The objective of this paper is to introduce a novel qualitative measurement framework for evaluating policy discrepancies in Return-Based Deep Q-Networks (DQN), a pivotal approach in reinforcement learning. Traditional DQNs primarily focus on maximizing cumulative returns, often overlooking nuanced discrepancies between proposed and optimal policies. Our proposed method augments the current evaluation techniques by incorporating qualitative metrics that offer a deeper insight into policy performance and deviations. We utilize a modified deep Q-learning algorithm tailored to assess discrepancies through a structured policy representation model, enhancing analytical capabilities beyond quantitative measurements alone. The results demonstrate that our approach effectively identifies and quantifies differences in policy execution, offering richer insights into policy efficiency and strategy alignment with optimal benchmarks. Comparisons against conventional DQN evaluations indicate a marked improvement in identifying noteworthy policy variations. This research contributes significant advancements in the understanding and refinement of deep reinforcement learning strategies, enabling more precise tuning and enhancement of policy frameworks. Potential applications span autonomous systems, game theory optimization, and strategic planning in dynamic environments. Key keywords include reinforcement learning, deep Q-network, policy discrepancy, qualitative measurement, and policy optimization.
