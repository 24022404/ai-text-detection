This paper introduces a novel dynamic filtering approach with a large sampling field to enhance the performance of convolutional neural networks (ConvNets). Current dynamic filtering methods often suffer from limited receptive fields, hindering their ability to capture long-range dependencies. To address this, we propose a dynamic filter generation module that leverages a significantly larger sampling field compared to existing techniques. This module dynamically predicts filter weights conditioned on the input, enabling adaptive feature extraction. We further introduce a spatial attention mechanism to refine the generated filters, focusing on relevant spatial locations. Experimental results on image classification and semantic segmentation benchmarks demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms state-of-the-art dynamic filtering methods and achieves competitive performance compared to other advanced ConvNet architectures. The proposed method offers a flexible and efficient way to improve ConvNet performance by dynamically adapting filter weights based on a broader contextual understanding. Keywords: Convolutional Neural Networks, Dynamic Filtering, Large Kernel, Spatial Attention, Image Classification, Semantic Segmentation.
