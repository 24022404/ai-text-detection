Semi-supervised clustering ensemble methods have gained significant attention for their ability to leverage limited labeled data while improving clustering accuracy. This paper introduces a novel semi-supervised clustering ensemble approach based on a voting mechanism, aiming to enhance the robustness and reliability of clustering results. The proposed method integrates multiple base clustering algorithms, each trained on a combination of labeled and unlabeled data, and employs a voting strategy to aggregate their outputs into a consensus solution. The approach is designed to address challenges such as label scarcity and noise in datasets, ensuring adaptability across diverse domains. Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms traditional clustering ensemble techniques, achieving higher accuracy and stability. Key contributions include the development of an efficient voting mechanism and a framework that effectively balances supervised and unsupervised learning components. The results highlight the potential of this approach for applications in data mining, pattern recognition, and knowledge discovery, where labeled data is often limited. Keywords: semi-supervised learning, clustering ensemble, voting mechanism, data mining, pattern recognition.
