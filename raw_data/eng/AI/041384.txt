This paper presents a novel approach to fast synthetic LiDAR rendering, addressing the need for efficient and accurate generation of 3D point clouds. Our objective is to develop a method that can quickly render high-quality LiDAR point clouds from 3D scenes, enabling applications such as autonomous driving, robotics, and virtual reality. We propose a spherical UV unwrapping technique for equirectangular Z-buffer images, which allows for efficient rendering of LiDAR point clouds. Our approach utilizes a unique unwrapping strategy to transform 2D equirectangular images into 3D spherical coordinates, enabling fast and accurate rendering of LiDAR data. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves significant speedups compared to existing rendering techniques, while maintaining high accuracy and fidelity. The proposed technique has important implications for various fields, including computer vision, robotics, and autonomous systems, and contributes to the development of more efficient and effective LiDAR rendering pipelines. Key contributions include fast rendering, spherical UV unwrapping, equirectangular Z-buffer images, and synthetic LiDAR generation, making this research relevant to areas such as 3D reconstruction, point cloud processing, and AI-powered computer vision.
