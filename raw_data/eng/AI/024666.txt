The COVID-19 pandemic has significantly increased the prevalence of masked faces in public spaces, posing challenges for face recognition and super-resolution tasks. This paper introduces JDSR-GAN, a novel joint and collaborative learning network designed to address the problem of masked face super-resolution. The proposed framework integrates a generative adversarial network (GAN) with a joint learning mechanism that simultaneously enhances image resolution and reconstructs occluded facial regions. By leveraging a dual-path architecture, JDSR-GAN combines global facial features with local texture details, enabling robust super-resolution even under severe occlusion. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that JDSR-GAN outperforms existing methods in terms of both quantitative metrics (e.g., PSNR and SSIM) and visual quality. The model achieves superior performance in reconstructing fine-grained facial details, such as eyes and eyebrows, while maintaining natural texture and identity consistency. This work contributes to the field of face super-resolution by providing a scalable and effective solution for handling masked faces, with potential applications in surveillance, security, and biometric systems.  

**Keywords**: masked face super-resolution, generative adversarial networks (GANs), joint learning, image reconstruction, face recognition.
