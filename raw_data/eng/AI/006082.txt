This study investigates the mining of heterogeneous multivariate time-series data to identify meaningful patterns, specifically within the realm of home health telecare. With the growing use of telecare solutions, there is an increasing need to efficiently interpret vast and varied data streams to enhance patient monitoring and care.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel framework that leverages advanced machine learning algorithms tailored for time-series analysis. Our approach involves preprocessing techniques to handle data heterogeneity, followed by feature extraction and pattern recognition methodologies designed to identify significant health indicators from the time-series data. We employ a combination of clustering, anomaly detection, and predictive modeling to extract insights pertinent to patient health status and care needs.

Results/Findings: Our experimental evaluations demonstrate the framework's capability to accurately detect and classify health-related events, outperforming traditional methods. The system was extensively tested with real-world home telecare datasets, showcasing its effectiveness in discerning critical patterns such as fluctuations in vital signs and medication adherence. The results indicate high precision and recall rates, highlighting the system's robustness and reliability in practical applications.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a significant advancement in the field of health informatics by presenting a comprehensive tool for analyzing multivariate time-series data in home telecare settings. The proposed framework not only enhances remote patient monitoring capabilities but also promotes proactive health management. Its ability to predict and alert healthcare providers to potential health issues establishes a foundation for future developments in personalized telemedicine solutions.

Keywords: time-series analysis, multivariate data, telecare, machine learning, health informatics, anomaly detection, patient monitoring.
