This paper introduces HarperValleyBank, a novel domain-specific spoken dialog corpus designed to facilitate research in task-oriented dialog systems. The corpus focuses on banking interactions, encompassing a wide range of user requests, including account inquiries, fund transfers, and bill payments. Data was collected through a combination of Wizard-of-Oz experiments and simulated dialogs, resulting in a dataset of over 10,000 dialog turns annotated with dialog acts and semantic intents. We present detailed statistics of the corpus, including vocabulary size, dialog length, and the distribution of user intents. Baseline experiments using state-of-the-art dialog models demonstrate the corpus's utility for training and evaluating spoken dialog systems. Results indicate that models trained on HarperValleyBank achieve superior performance in intent recognition and dialog state tracking compared to those trained on generic datasets. HarperValleyBank offers a valuable resource for developing and benchmarking dialog systems specifically tailored for banking applications, enabling more natural and efficient human-computer interactions. Keywords: Spoken Dialog Corpus, Banking, Task-Oriented Dialog, Natural Language Understanding, Dialog State Tracking, Wizard-of-Oz.
