This paper introduces *Luna*, a novel Linear Unified Nested Attention mechanism designed to address the computational inefficiencies of traditional attention mechanisms in large-scale machine learning models. The objective of this research is to reduce the quadratic complexity of standard attention while maintaining or improving model performance. Luna achieves this by unifying nested attention layers into a linear computational framework, enabling efficient processing of long sequences without sacrificing accuracy. The proposed approach leverages a combination of linear transformations and hierarchical attention structures to optimize memory usage and computational speed. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that Luna outperforms existing linear attention methods, achieving up to a 40% reduction in training time and a 30% improvement in memory efficiency, while maintaining competitive performance metrics. The findings highlight Luna's potential for scaling transformer-based models to larger datasets and more complex tasks. This work contributes a significant advancement in attention mechanisms, offering a practical solution for deploying efficient AI models in resource-constrained environments. Key applications include natural language processing, computer vision, and real-time systems.  

**Keywords**: attention mechanisms, linear attention, nested attention, transformer models, computational efficiency, machine learning.
