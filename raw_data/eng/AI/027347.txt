This paper introduces a novel regression framework, Hyperbolic Manifold Regression (HMR), designed to address the limitations of Euclidean-based regression methods when dealing with hierarchical or tree-like data structures. HMR leverages the unique properties of hyperbolic space to embed and regress data, effectively capturing complex relationships often distorted in Euclidean space. Our approach utilizes a Riemannian optimization scheme to minimize the regression loss directly on the hyperbolic manifold. We demonstrate the effectiveness of HMR on several benchmark datasets, including hierarchical clustering and knowledge graph embedding tasks. Results show that HMR significantly outperforms traditional Euclidean regression techniques and existing hyperbolic embedding methods in terms of prediction accuracy and representation learning. The proposed method offers a powerful tool for analyzing and predicting relationships in data with inherent hierarchical structures, opening new avenues for applications in areas such as social network analysis, bioinformatics, and natural language processing. Keywords: Hyperbolic space, Regression, Manifold learning, Riemannian optimization, Hierarchical data, Embedding.
