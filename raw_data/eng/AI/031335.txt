This paper presents SQuantizer, a novel framework designed to simultaneously achieve sparsity and low precision in neural networks, addressing the growing need for efficient and deployable deep learning models. The primary objective is to significantly reduce model size and computational complexity without compromising performance, thereby facilitating deployment on resource-constrained devices such as mobile platforms and embedded systems. SQuantizer employs a unified optimization procedure that jointly learns both the sparse (pruned) structure and low-bit quantized weights during training, ensuring seamless integration and mutual adaptation of these compression techniques. Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets and popular architectures demonstrate that SQuantizer achieves state-of-the-art compression rates and acceleration, while maintaining or even enhancing accuracy compared to separate pruning or quantization methods. The framework’s effectiveness is highlighted across image classification tasks, where models compressed with SQuantizer exhibit reduced memory footprint and inference latency. This work contributes a practical and scalable solution for real-world applications demanding efficient deep neural network deployment. Key innovations include the concurrent learning approach, compatibility with various network architectures, and streamlined training workflow. SQuantizer sets a new paradigm for optimizing deep learning models, paving the way for further advances in neural network compression, efficient AI inference, and edge computing. Keywords: model compression, sparsity, quantization, neural network optimization, edge AI, deep learning efficiency.
