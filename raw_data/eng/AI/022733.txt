This paper introduces Pix2seq, a novel language modeling framework designed for object detection tasks. The objective is to bridge the gap between computer vision and natural language processing by leveraging the strengths of both domains. Our approach utilizes a sequence-to-sequence model, where images are encoded into a sequence of tokens that can be processed by a language model. This enables the application of powerful language modeling techniques, such as transformer architectures, to object detection problems. Our results show that Pix2seq achieves state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets, outperforming traditional computer vision-based approaches. The key findings indicate that the framework's ability to capture complex relationships between objects and scenes is a major contributor to its success. The implications of this research are significant, as it opens up new avenues for applying language modeling techniques to a wide range of computer vision tasks. The contributions of Pix2seq include its unique architecture, which enables efficient and effective object detection, and its potential to be extended to other applications such as image captioning and visual question answering. Key keywords: object detection, language modeling, transformer, sequence-to-sequence, computer vision, natural language processing.
