This paper presents a cost-effective variational active entity resolution approach, addressing the challenge of efficiently resolving entities in large datasets while minimizing computational costs. Our objective is to develop a method that balances accuracy and cost, enabling effective entity resolution in resource-constrained environments. We propose a novel variational active learning framework that leverages uncertainty estimation and active sampling to selectively request labels for the most informative entities. Our approach utilizes a probabilistic model to capture entity relationships and uncertainty, allowing for efficient exploration of the entity space. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves significant cost savings while maintaining high resolution accuracy, outperforming state-of-the-art active learning and entity resolution techniques. The key contributions of this research include a scalable and cost-effective entity resolution framework, uncertainty-aware active learning, and improved handling of noisy and missing data. Our approach has important implications for real-world applications, including data integration, record linkage, and data quality management, and can be applied to various domains such as healthcare, finance, and social media analysis, with relevant keywords including entity resolution, active learning, variational inference, and cost-effective data integration.
