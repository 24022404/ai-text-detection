Explainable AI (XAI) is crucial for building trust in machine learning models, especially when dealing with high-dimensional data. This paper addresses the challenge of generating human-interpretable explanations for complex models trained on such data. We introduce a novel approach that combines feature selection with rule extraction to identify the most relevant features and represent model behavior through a concise set of decision rules. Our method employs a hybrid optimization strategy, integrating both global and local search techniques, to enhance the quality and interpretability of the extracted rules. We evaluate our approach on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its ability to provide explanations that are both accurate in representing the model's decision boundaries and understandable by human experts. Results show a significant improvement in interpretability metrics, such as rule complexity and coverage, compared to existing XAI techniques. The proposed method offers a practical solution for enhancing transparency and trust in machine learning applications involving high-dimensional data, facilitating better decision-making and model debugging. Keywords: Explainable AI, XAI, Interpretability, High-Dimensional Data, Rule Extraction, Feature Selection, Machine Learning.
