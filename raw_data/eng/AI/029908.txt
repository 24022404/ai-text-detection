The study introduces NukeBERT, a novel pre-trained language model tailored specifically for the nuclear domain, which often suffers from a scarcity of labeled data. This research addresses the pressing need for sophisticated natural language processing (NLP) tools that can effectively manage domain-specific terminologies and texts.

Methods/Approach: NukeBERT is built upon the foundational architecture of BERT, with adaptations to accommodate the specialized vocabulary and semantics inherent within nuclear literature. The model is pre-trained on a curated corpus of nuclear-related documents, enhancing its capability to comprehend and process domain-specific text. Fine-tuning techniques were applied to maximize the model's adaptability to various low-resource tasks within the field, such as information extraction, sentiment analysis, and document classification.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate that NukeBERT significantly outperforms existing baseline models in domain-specific NLP tasks, showcasing improved accuracy and efficiency. Comparative analyses illustrate NukeBERT's superior performance in understanding and processing nuclear texts, highlighting its potential to become a benchmark tool in the nuclear field.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes to the advancement of NLP in specialized fields by providing a robust language model that addresses the challenges of limited data resources. NukeBERT's introduction offers vital improvements for automated processing and analysis within the nuclear sector, suggesting broader applications to other low-resource domains. The development of such domain-specific models promises enhanced interpretability and applicability in technical fields where traditional models might falter.

Keywords: NukeBERT, pre-trained language model, nuclear domain, low resource, BERT, natural language processing, domain-specific NLP.
