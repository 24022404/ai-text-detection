Table 4.23 presents the essential structure of a JRPC request, detailing important fields like the "jsonrpc" version, which must be set to "2.0", as well as the "method" specifying the procedure to invoke, and the unique "id" used to correlate responses with requests. The "params" field accommodates any parameters necessary for the chosen method, ensuring flexibility and precise operation. Additionally, the "result" field is only included in the response when the request is successful, and its content is contingent upon the outcome defined by the particular method executed.
