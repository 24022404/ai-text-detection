Disentangling latent factors from observed data remains a significant challenge, particularly in natural data where nonlinear relationships prevail. This paper introduces a novel approach, Temporal Sparse Coding (TSC), designed to achieve nonlinear disentanglement by leveraging temporal coherence. TSC encodes time-series data into sparse latent representations, enforcing temporal smoothness on disentangled factors. We propose a neural network architecture that integrates sparse coding principles with temporal constraints, allowing the model to learn disentangled representations without explicit supervision. Experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets, including video sequences and motion capture data, demonstrate that TSC outperforms existing disentanglement methods in terms of reconstruction accuracy, factor interpretability, and downstream task performance. Furthermore, we show that TSC is robust to noise and occlusions commonly found in natural data. Our findings highlight the effectiveness of temporal sparsity as a powerful inductive bias for nonlinear disentanglement, offering a promising direction for representation learning in various domains. Keywords: Disentanglement, Sparse Coding, Temporal Modeling, Representation Learning, Nonlinear Dynamics.
