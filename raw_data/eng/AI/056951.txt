This paper presents a novel neural-based approach for answering questions about images, leveraging the synergy between visual and textual data. The objective is to address the challenge of understanding and reasoning about visual content through natural language queries. We propose a deep learning framework that integrates convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for image feature extraction and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) for language modeling, enabling the system to generate accurate and contextually relevant answers. The model is trained end-to-end on a large-scale dataset of image-question-answer triplets, optimizing for both visual comprehension and linguistic coherence. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The findings highlight the effectiveness of combining visual and textual modalities in a unified neural architecture. This research contributes to advancing multimodal AI systems, with potential applications in areas such as visual assistance, education, and human-computer interaction.  

**Keywords**: neural networks, image understanding, question answering, multimodal learning, deep learning, visual-textual integration.
