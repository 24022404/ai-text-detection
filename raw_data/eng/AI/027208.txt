Multiview object detection is a critical challenge in computer vision, particularly in scenarios requiring robust performance across varying viewpoints and lighting conditions. This paper introduces the *Shadow Transformer*, a novel architecture designed to enhance multiview detection by effectively leveraging shadow information and view-coherent data augmentation. The proposed approach addresses the limitations of existing methods, which often struggle with inconsistent feature representations across views and fail to utilize shadow cues for improved detection accuracy. The Shadow Transformer integrates a transformer-based framework with a shadow-aware attention mechanism, enabling the model to dynamically focus on relevant regions across multiple views while incorporating shadow patterns as auxiliary features. Additionally, we propose a view-coherent data augmentation strategy to simulate realistic multiview scenarios, ensuring better generalization and robustness. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing approaches in terms of detection accuracy and cross-view consistency. The results highlight the effectiveness of shadow information and view-coherent augmentation in enhancing multiview detection systems. This work contributes to advancing multiview object detection by providing a scalable and efficient solution with potential applications in surveillance, autonomous driving, and robotics.  

**Keywords**: Multiview detection, Shadow Transformer, view-coherent data augmentation, transformer models, computer vision, object detection.
