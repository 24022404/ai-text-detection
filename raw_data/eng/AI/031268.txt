Temporal multilayer graphs are widely used to model complex systems with time-varying interactions across multiple layers, such as social networks, transportation systems, and biological networks. However, missing data in such graphs poses significant challenges for accurate analysis and prediction. This paper addresses the problem of missing data estimation in temporal multilayer graphs by introducing a novel framework, the Temporal Multilayer Position-aware Graph Neural Network (TMP-GNN). The proposed approach leverages position-aware embeddings and temporal dependencies to effectively capture the dynamic and hierarchical structure of multilayer graphs. TMP-GNN integrates a graph neural network architecture with a temporal attention mechanism, enabling it to learn both spatial and temporal patterns while accounting for the positional importance of nodes across layers. Experiments on real-world datasets demonstrate that TMP-GNN outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of missing data estimation accuracy, achieving improvements of up to 15% in mean absolute error (MAE) and 12% in root mean square error (RMSE). The framework also exhibits robustness to varying levels of data sparsity. The results highlight the potential of TMP-GNN for applications in network analysis, recommendation systems, and anomaly detection, where accurate estimation of missing temporal and multilayer data is critical.  

**Keywords**: Temporal multilayer graphs, graph neural networks, missing data estimation, position-aware embeddings, temporal attention, network analysis.
