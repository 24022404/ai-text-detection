This paper presents a transfer learning approach for melanoma detection, developed for participation in the ISIC 2017 Skin Lesion Classification Challenge. Our method leverages pre-trained convolutional neural networks (CNNs), specifically VGG16 and ResNet50, fine-tuned on the ISIC 2017 dataset. The objective was to improve classification accuracy in distinguishing melanoma from benign skin lesions. We explored various fine-tuning strategies, including layer freezing and unfreezing, as well as data augmentation techniques to mitigate the limited size of the training dataset. Experimental results demonstrate that transfer learning significantly outperformed training CNNs from scratch. Specifically, our ensemble model, combining VGG16 and ResNet50 predictions, achieved a competitive performance on the ISIC 2017 challenge validation set. The findings suggest that transfer learning is an effective strategy for melanoma detection when dealing with limited medical image datasets. This research contributes to the application of deep learning in dermatological diagnosis and highlights the potential of pre-trained models in skin lesion classification.

**Keywords:** Melanoma Detection, Transfer Learning, Convolutional Neural Networks, ISIC 2017, Skin Lesion Classification, Deep Learning, Image Analysis.
