This paper proposes a novel approach to subgraph recognition using the Graph Information Bottleneck (GIB) method. The objective is to develop an efficient and effective framework for identifying subgraphs within large graph structures, which is a crucial task in various applications such as network analysis, computer vision, and bioinformatics. Our approach employs a information-theoretic framework to learn compact and informative representations of subgraphs, which enables accurate recognition and classification. The GIB method utilizes a neural network-based architecture to optimize the information bottleneck, resulting in a significant reduction in computational complexity and improvement in recognition accuracy. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our approach over existing state-of-the-art methods, achieving high accuracy and efficiency in subgraph recognition tasks. The proposed GIB method has important implications for graph-based applications, including graph neural networks, graph clustering, and graph classification, and contributes to the development of more efficient and effective graph analysis techniques. Key keywords: Graph Information Bottleneck, Subgraph Recognition, Graph Neural Networks, Information-Theoretic Framework, Network Analysis.
