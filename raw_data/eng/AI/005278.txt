A.Lampel and M.Cohn set out to design a universal test sequence for VLSI chips by concatenating de Bruijn sequences, thereby ensuring that every possible subset of t-register positions is exercised during testing. This method significantly enhances the thoroughness of the test process. Additionally, research by Zeev Barzilai and colleagues demonstrated that de Bruijn sequences could also be effectively used for VLSI self-testing, further validating their usefulness in improving chip reliability.
