This paper delves into the realm of Bayesian network learning, focusing on the utilization of continuous optimization techniques for Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs). The primary objective is to alleviate the fears associated with learning DAGs, which are crucial in representing complex probabilistic relationships. Our approach employs a novel continuous optimization framework, enabling the efficient learning of Bayesian networks from data. By leveraging advances in gradient-based optimization, we develop a method that can handle large datasets and complex network structures. The results demonstrate the efficacy of our approach, showcasing improved performance in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency compared to traditional discrete optimization methods. Our research contributes to the field by providing a robust and scalable framework for learning Bayesian networks, with potential applications in areas such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, and probabilistic modeling. Key aspects of this work include continuous optimization, Bayesian networks, DAGs, gradient-based methods, and probabilistic modeling, making it a significant step forward in the pursuit of more accurate and efficient learning of complex probabilistic relationships.
