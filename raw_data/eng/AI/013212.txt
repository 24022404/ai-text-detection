This paper proposes an autonomous spectrum management scheme for unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) networks in disaster relief operations, aiming to enhance the efficiency and reliability of communication systems in such critical scenarios. Our approach leverages artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) techniques to dynamically allocate spectrum resources among UAVs, ensuring optimal network performance and minimizing interference. The proposed scheme utilizes a novel predictive modeling algorithm to forecast spectrum demand and adaptively adjust allocation strategies. Simulation results demonstrate significant improvements in network throughput, delay, and packet loss rate compared to traditional spectrum management methods. The autonomous spectrum management scheme offers a robust and scalable solution for UAV networks in disaster relief operations, enabling rapid deployment and efficient communication in emergency response situations. Key contributions include the development of a predictive spectrum allocation framework, integration of AI/ML techniques for dynamic spectrum management, and demonstration of the scheme's effectiveness in realistic disaster relief scenarios. Relevant keywords: UAV networks, autonomous spectrum management, AI, ML, disaster relief operations, predictive modeling, dynamic spectrum allocation.
