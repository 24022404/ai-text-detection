This paper addresses the challenge of generating realistic person images with altered poses, a crucial task for applications in virtual fashion and personalized avatar creation. Traditional methods often struggle with preserving detailed textures and ensuring pose alignment, leading to distorted or unnatural results.

Methods/Approach: To tackle these challenges, we introduce a novel Progressive and Aligned Pose Attention Transfer (PAPAT) framework. PAPAT leverages a multi-stage approach to progressively refine the image generation process, incorporating a pose attention mechanism that effectively aligns the target and source poses. Our method utilizes a deep neural network architecture that integrates pose transfer and attention mechanisms to maintain high fidelity in the generated images.

Results/Findings: Extensive experiments demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms existing person image generation techniques, achieving superior results in both accuracy and visual quality. Key performance metrics indicate that PAPAT delivers enhanced texture detail preservation and improved geometric fidelity, as verified through quantitative assessments and qualitative comparisons with state-of-the-art models.

Conclusion/Implications: The PAPAT model advances the field of image synthesis by effectively addressing the limitations of previous pose transfer methods through its innovative attention-based architecture. Our findings hold potential implications for diverse applications, such as virtual try-on systems and digital content creation, offering users more realistic and customizable visualizations. Keywords: pose attention, image generation, pose transfer, neural network, person image synthesis.
