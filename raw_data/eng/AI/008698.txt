The rapid growth of graph-structured data in various domains, such as social networks, molecular structures, and recommendation systems, has necessitated the development of advanced generative models capable of capturing complex dependencies and hierarchical patterns. This paper introduces the Multiresolution Graph Variational Autoencoder (MGVAE), a novel framework designed to learn and generate graph data at multiple levels of granularity. The proposed approach leverages a hierarchical architecture to encode and decode graphs, enabling the model to capture both local and global structural properties effectively. By integrating multiresolution analysis with variational inference, MGVAE achieves superior performance in graph reconstruction and generation tasks compared to existing methods. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate significant improvements in terms of reconstruction accuracy, scalability, and the ability to generate realistic graphs. The model also exhibits robustness in handling sparse and noisy graph data. The findings highlight the potential of MGVAE for applications in drug discovery, network analysis, and other domains requiring high-fidelity graph generation. This work contributes to the advancement of graph-based generative models by introducing a scalable and interpretable framework for multiresolution graph representation learning.  

**Keywords**: Graph Variational Autoencoder, Multiresolution Analysis, Graph Generation, Hierarchical Models, Graph Representation Learning.
