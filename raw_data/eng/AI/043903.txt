Conformal prediction has emerged as a promising framework for providing statistically valid uncertainty estimates in machine learning models. However, its application in few-shot learning scenarios remains challenging due to limited labeled data. This paper introduces a novel approach to few-shot conformal prediction by leveraging auxiliary tasks to enhance model calibration and prediction accuracy. We propose a multi-task learning framework that integrates auxiliary tasks with the primary prediction task, enabling the model to generalize better from limited labeled examples. Our method employs a conformal prediction mechanism that adapts to the few-shot setting, ensuring valid prediction intervals even with minimal data. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms traditional conformal prediction methods in terms of coverage and interval width, particularly in low-data regimes. The results highlight the effectiveness of auxiliary tasks in improving model robustness and uncertainty quantification. This work contributes to the growing field of reliable machine learning by addressing the critical challenge of uncertainty estimation in few-shot learning, with potential applications in domains such as healthcare, robotics, and autonomous systems.  

**Keywords**: conformal prediction, few-shot learning, auxiliary tasks, uncertainty estimation, multi-task learning, machine learning.
