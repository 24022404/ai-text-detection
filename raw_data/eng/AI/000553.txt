Traffic prediction is a critical challenge in urban planning and intelligent transportation systems, requiring accurate and efficient models to forecast traffic patterns. This paper presents *Graph Ensemble Net*, a novel deep learning framework designed to address the complexities of traffic prediction in the Traffic4cast 2020 competition. The proposed approach integrates graph-based representations of road networks with ensemble learning techniques to capture spatial and temporal dependencies effectively. Key innovations include the design of specialized feature engineering strategies and a custom loss function tailored to the unique characteristics of traffic data. Experimental results demonstrate that *Graph Ensemble Net* outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving superior prediction accuracy across multiple metrics. The study highlights the importance of feature design and loss function optimization in enhancing model performance for traffic forecasting. This research contributes to advancing traffic prediction systems, offering practical insights for real-world applications in smart cities and transportation management.  

**Keywords**: traffic prediction, graph neural networks, ensemble learning, feature engineering, loss function design, deep learning, intelligent transportation systems.
