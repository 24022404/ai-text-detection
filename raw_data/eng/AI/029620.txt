This paper presents WiSoSuper, a comprehensive benchmark designed to evaluate the performance of super-resolution methods specifically tailored for wind and solar energy datasets. The increasing importance of renewable energy sources necessitates accurate and high-resolution data for enhanced forecasting and grid management.

Methods/Approach: WiSoSuper encompasses a diverse set of super-resolution algorithms, ranging from traditional interpolation techniques to cutting-edge deep learning models. These methods were rigorously tested on standardized datasets that represent real-world wind and solar energy scenarios. Performance metrics were carefully selected to capture both the accuracy and efficiency of the reconstruction methods.

Results/Findings: Our benchmark reveals significant disparities in performance among various super-resolution methods, highlighting the strengths and weaknesses of each. Deep learning models, particularly those based on convolutional neural networks (CNNs), achieved the best performance in terms of precision and scalability. Traditional methods, while faster, often fell short in capturing the intricate variations present in renewable energy data.

Conclusion/Implications: WiSoSuper not only provides valuable insights into the effectiveness of different super-resolution approaches for renewable energy data but also sets a foundation for future research in this domain. By identifying the most promising techniques, this benchmark aids stakeholders in selecting appropriate tools for enhancing the resolution of wind and solar datasets, ultimately contributing to more efficient energy production and management. Key contributions include the establishment of a unified evaluation framework and the promotion of innovation in super-resolution methodologies. 

Keywords: Super-Resolution, Benchmarking, Wind Energy, Solar Data, Deep Learning, CNN, Renewable Energy Forecasting.
