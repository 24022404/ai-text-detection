This paper introduces DeepIGeoS, an innovative framework designed to enhance medical image segmentation through the integration of deep learning and interactive geodesic principles. Our goal is to address the challenges of accuracy and efficiency in medical image segmentation, which is critical for diagnostic and therapeutic applications.

Methods/Approach: DeepIGeoS leverages a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture with an interactive geodesic segmentation model. The framework allows users to refine segmentation results by providing minimal guidance in the form of geodesic information, which the model uses to improve its predictions iteratively. This interactive approach ensures that the deep learning model is adaptable and can achieve high precision with less computational complexity.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that DeepIGeoS significantly improves the accuracy of segmentation across various medical imaging datasets, outperforming traditional segmentation methods and other deep learning models. Our results show that the framework efficiently incorporates user corrections, leading to more refined and clinically applicable results. The system exhibits greater robustness and adaptability in handling diverse anatomical structures.

Conclusion/Implications: DeepIGeoS offers a promising solution for enhancing medical image segmentation, combining the strengths of interactive and deep learning approaches. Its ability to effectively incorporate user input pushes the boundaries of current segmentation technologies, providing significant contributions to the field of medical imaging. The framework's adaptability and precision have potential applications in various clinical scenarios, offering healthcare professionals improved diagnostic tools and contributing to better patient outcomes.

Key Keywords: Deep learning, interactive segmentation, geodesic framework, medical imaging, CNN, image segmentation, clinical applications.
