This paper presents a novel approach to controlling dynamics on graphs using Neural Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs). The objective is to develop a framework that can effectively model and regulate complex graph-based systems, which are ubiquitous in various domains such as social networks, traffic flow, and biological systems. Our method leverages the strengths of neural networks and ODEs to learn continuous-time dynamics on graphs, enabling precise control over the system's behavior. The approach involves training a neural ODE to predict the future state of the graph-based system and then using this prediction to inform control decisions. Our results demonstrate the efficacy of this method in achieving desired outcomes, such as stabilizing or optimizing graph dynamics. The key findings highlight the potential of neural ODEs in graph control, outperforming traditional discrete-time methods. This research contributes to the growing field of graph neural networks and control theory, with implications for applications in network science, robotics, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: Neural ODEs, graph control, dynamics on graphs, neural networks, control theory.
