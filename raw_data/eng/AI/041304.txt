Adversarial learning has emerged as a powerful paradigm for training robust machine learning models, yet challenges such as instability and mode collapse persist in generative adversarial networks (GANs). This paper introduces *Generative Adversarial Forests* (GAFs), a novel framework that leverages ensemble-based decision trees to improve the conditioning and stability of adversarial learning. The proposed approach replaces traditional neural network-based generators with a forest of decision trees, enabling more interpretable and structured generation processes. GAFs employ a hybrid training mechanism that combines adversarial optimization with tree-based regularization, ensuring better convergence and reduced sensitivity to hyperparameters. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that GAFs achieve competitive performance in image generation tasks while significantly improving training stability compared to conventional GANs. Additionally, the framework exhibits enhanced robustness to adversarial attacks, making it suitable for applications in secure and reliable AI systems. The results highlight the potential of tree-based generative models as a viable alternative to neural networks in adversarial learning, offering a new direction for research in interpretable and stable generative AI.  

**Keywords**: Generative Adversarial Networks, Decision Trees, Adversarial Learning, Ensemble Methods, Interpretable AI, Robust Machine Learning.
