Video super-resolution (VSR) aims to enhance the resolution of low-resolution videos, a crucial preprocessing step for action recognition. This paper introduces a novel two-stream framework tailored to improve action recognition performance via VSR. Our method employs a spatial stream to reconstruct high-resolution video frames and a temporal stream to preserve crucial motion information. Specifically, we propose a recurrent neural network architecture with enhanced motion compensation to effectively exploit temporal dependencies. Furthermore, we introduce a joint training strategy that integrates action recognition loss to guide the VSR network towards generating features more relevant for action classification. Experiments on benchmark datasets, including UCF101 and HMDB51, demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art super-resolution performance and significantly improves the accuracy of action recognition compared to existing VSR methods. The proposed framework offers a promising direction for enhancing video analysis tasks by integrating task-specific guidance into the super-resolution process.

Keywords: Video Super-Resolution, Action Recognition, Two-Stream Network, Motion Compensation, Deep Learning.
