This paper proposes a novel pairwise teacher-student network for semi-supervised hashing, aiming to improve the efficiency and accuracy of hash function learning with limited labeled data. Our approach leverages a teacher-student framework, where a pre-trained teacher model guides a student model to learn compact and informative hash codes. The pairwise loss function is designed to preserve the semantic similarity between data points, ensuring that similar inputs are mapped to nearby hash codes. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, outperforming state-of-the-art semi-supervised hashing algorithms in terms of retrieval precision and recall. The key contributions of this work include the introduction of a pairwise teacher-student learning paradigm and the development of a robust hashing scheme that can adapt to scarce labeled data. Our approach has significant implications for large-scale image and video retrieval applications, enabling efficient and accurate search with minimal supervision. Key keywords: semi-supervised hashing, teacher-student network, pairwise loss, hash function learning, image retrieval.
