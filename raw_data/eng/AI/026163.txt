This paper addresses the challenge of learning effective representations for multiplex bipartite networks, which are prevalent in real-world applications such as recommendation systems, social networks, and biological interactions. Traditional network embedding methods often fail to capture the complex, multi-layered relationships inherent in such networks. To overcome this limitation, we propose a novel framework based on Dual Hypergraph Convolutional Networks (DHCN), which simultaneously models both the bipartite structure and the multiplex relationships across layers. Our approach leverages hypergraph convolution to encode higher-order interactions and employs a dual-network architecture to integrate information from different layers effectively. Experiments on real-world datasets demonstrate that DHCN outperforms state-of-the-art methods in tasks such as link prediction and node classification, achieving improvements of up to 15% in accuracy. The proposed framework not only provides a robust solution for multiplex bipartite network embedding but also offers insights into modeling complex relational data. This work has significant implications for applications in personalized recommendation, community detection, and multi-modal data analysis.  

**Keywords**: Multiplex networks, bipartite networks, hypergraph convolutional networks, network embedding, representation learning.
