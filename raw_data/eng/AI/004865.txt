Hierarchical disentanglement is a critical challenge in machine learning, aiming to separate and interpret latent representations of data across multiple levels of abstraction. This paper addresses the problem of evaluating and improving hierarchical disentanglement methods by introducing a comprehensive framework consisting of benchmarks, algorithms, and metrics. We propose a novel set of benchmarks designed to simulate hierarchical structures in data, enabling systematic evaluation of disentanglement techniques. Additionally, we present a new algorithm that leverages hierarchical priors and multi-scale feature extraction to achieve superior disentanglement performance. To quantify the effectiveness of these approaches, we introduce a suite of metrics tailored to assess hierarchical disentanglement, focusing on both local and global structure preservation. Experimental results demonstrate that our algorithm outperforms existing methods on the proposed benchmarks, achieving higher disentanglement scores and better interpretability. The contributions of this work include a standardized evaluation framework, an innovative algorithm, and a set of metrics that advance the state-of-the-art in hierarchical disentanglement research. These advancements have significant implications for applications in unsupervised learning, interpretable AI, and multi-level data analysis.  

**Keywords**: hierarchical disentanglement, benchmarks, algorithms, metrics, unsupervised learning, interpretable AI, multi-scale feature extraction.
