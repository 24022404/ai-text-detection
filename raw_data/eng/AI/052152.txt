The growing complexity of neural networks has made their interpretability a critical challenge, particularly in applications requiring transparency and trust. This paper introduces *LioNets*, a novel framework for local interpretation of neural networks by leveraging the penultimate layer's representations. The primary objective of LioNets is to provide intuitive, human-understandable explanations for individual predictions without compromising model performance. Our approach decodes the activations of the penultimate layer into interpretable features, enabling the generation of localized explanations that highlight the most influential factors driving a specific prediction. We employ a combination of feature attribution techniques and dimensionality reduction to ensure scalability and efficiency across diverse datasets. Experimental results demonstrate that LioNets outperforms existing interpretation methods in terms of both accuracy and interpretability, achieving superior fidelity to the original model's decision-making process. Furthermore, the framework is model-agnostic, making it applicable to a wide range of neural architectures. The implications of this work extend to domains such as healthcare, finance, and autonomous systems, where interpretable AI is essential for ethical and regulatory compliance. By bridging the gap between complex neural networks and actionable insights, LioNets represents a significant step forward in the development of transparent and trustworthy AI systems.  

**Keywords**: interpretability, neural networks, penultimate layer, local explanations, feature attribution, model-agnostic, transparent AI.
