This paper addresses the instability issues inherent in Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), a deep learning model used for dimensionality reduction and generative tasks. Our objective is to improve the stability of VAEs by re-parameterizing their latent space, thereby enhancing their performance and reliability. We propose a novel approach that utilizes a modified encoder-decoder architecture, incorporating a normalization technique to stabilize the variance of the latent variables. Our method, termed StableVAE, is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in model stability and reconstruction quality compared to traditional VAEs. The results show that StableVAE achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of reconstruction loss and latent space consistency. Our research contributes to the development of more robust and reliable VAE models, with potential applications in image and video processing, anomaly detection, and generative modeling. Key keywords: VAE, stability, deep learning, generative models, latent space regularization.
