This paper proposes a novel approach to robust subspace discovery, leveraging block-diagonal adaptive locality-constrained representation. The objective is to identify underlying subspaces in high-dimensional data, which is crucial for various applications in computer vision, machine learning, and data mining. Our method utilizes an adaptive locality-constrained representation to capture the intrinsic structure of the data, while a block-diagonal regularization term ensures the learned representation is robust to outliers and noise. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in discovering multiple subspaces, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The key findings highlight the importance of adaptive locality constraints and block-diagonal regularization in improving the representation's quality and robustness. This research contributes to the development of robust subspace discovery techniques, with potential applications in image and video analysis, clustering, and dimensionality reduction. Key keywords: subspace discovery, locality-constrained representation, block-diagonal regularization, robustness, high-dimensional data, computer vision, machine learning.
