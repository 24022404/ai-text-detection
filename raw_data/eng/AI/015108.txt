Multimodal fusion, the process of integrating information from multiple data sources, is crucial for various AI applications. However, effectively combining diverse data modalities remains a challenge due to the potential for irrelevant or redundant information to overwhelm the fusion process. This paper introduces an attention bottleneck mechanism to selectively filter and refine information during multimodal fusion. Our approach uses learnable attention weights to identify and prioritize the most relevant features from each modality before they are combined. By introducing a bottleneck that constrains the flow of information, the model is encouraged to focus on the essential aspects of each input, leading to improved fusion performance and robustness. We evaluate our method on benchmark datasets for visual question answering and multimodal sentiment analysis. Results demonstrate that our attention bottleneck significantly outperforms existing fusion techniques, achieving state-of-the-art accuracy while using fewer parameters. Furthermore, we show that the bottleneck enhances interpretability by highlighting the key features contributing to the final prediction. This work offers a novel and effective approach to multimodal fusion, with implications for a wide range of AI tasks.

**Keywords:** Multimodal Fusion, Attention Mechanism, Bottleneck, Deep Learning, Visual Question Answering, Sentiment Analysis.
