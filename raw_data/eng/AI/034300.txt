**Abstract**

Understanding complex multivariate datasets is a critical challenge in information technology, especially for domains requiring quick and reliable pattern recognition. This paper introduces a novel visualization approach based on Jumping Emerging Patterns (JEPs) to facilitate the explanation and interpretation of multivariate data. The primary objective is to address the limitations of traditional visualization tools in highlighting subtle and significant differences across classes or groups within large datasets. Our method employs an algorithmic framework to automatically detect JEPs—patterns whose support dramatically increases from one dataset segment to another—and visually represent their occurrence and impact across multiple variables. Through experiments on various real-world datasets, our approach demonstrates superior performance in both revealing critical differentiators between classes and enhancing user comprehension compared to standard visualization techniques, such as principal component analysis and heatmaps. The results highlight improvements in classification accuracy, pattern discovery speed, and subjective interpretability. This research provides an effective tool for data scientists and analysts by enabling more transparent and insightful exploration of high-dimensional data. The JEP visualization framework offers significant implications for applications in data mining, anomaly detection, and decision support systems across diverse IT sectors. Key keywords include: multivariate data, emerging patterns, visualization, pattern mining, explainable AI.
