This paper proposes a novel approach to classification and regression tasks involving functional data, leveraging Measure Inducing Classification and Regression Trees (MICRT). The objective is to develop a robust and efficient method for analyzing complex functional data, which is commonly encountered in various fields such as signal processing, time-series analysis, and medical imaging. Our approach employs a tree-based framework that incorporates measure-induced techniques to handle the unique characteristics of functional data. The MICRT method is designed to identify meaningful patterns and relationships in the data, allowing for accurate classification and regression modeling. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of MICRT in comparison to existing methods, showcasing its ability to improve prediction accuracy and reduce computational complexity. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a novel measure-induced tree-based framework, the development of efficient algorithms for functional data analysis, and the demonstration of the method's potential in real-world applications. This study highlights the importance of functional data analysis and provides a valuable tool for researchers and practitioners in the field, with relevant keywords including functional data analysis, classification and regression trees, measure-induced techniques, and machine learning.
