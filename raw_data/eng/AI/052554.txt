This paper addresses the critical challenge of uncertainty in decision-making problems modeled by Markov Decision Processes (MDPs). Specifically, we focus on enhancing robust MDP frameworks to manage uncertainties in transition probabilities effectively. Our research introduces an innovative method for constructing tight Bayesian ambiguity sets that improve the robustness of policies under model misspecification.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel approach that leverages Bayesian inference to derive ambiguity sets for MDPs. These sets are constructed by incorporating prior distribution information, allowing for a more precise representation of uncertainty. Our method ensures that the ambiguity sets are not overly conservative while maintaining robustness. Theoretical analyses are provided to demonstrate the approach's effectiveness in balancing exploration and exploitation during decision-making.

Results/Findings: Experimental results on benchmark problems reveal that our Bayesian ambiguity sets significantly enhance the performance of robust MDPs. The proposed sets lead to better decision-making outcomes compared to traditional robust methods, achieving improvements in policy performance under various levels of transition probability uncertainty. Moreover, our approach demonstrates superior adaptability to different problem settings by dynamically adjusting the size of the ambiguity sets based on available prior information.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a promising direction for improving decision-making under uncertainty in MDPs by utilizing tight Bayesian ambiguity sets. Our findings highlight the potential applications in areas such as autonomous systems, financial decision-making, and any domain requiring robust sequential decision-making under model uncertainty. The approach's capacity to enhance robustness without excessive conservatism stands to impact a wide range of real-world applications, providing a foundation for future exploration and development in this field.

Keywords: MDP, Robustness, Bayesian Ambiguity Sets, Decision-Making, Uncertainty, Sequential Decision-Making, Transition Probability.
