This paper addresses the challenge of creating adaptive assistive navigation systems that cater to individual user needs and preferences. Traditional navigation systems often lack personalization, leading to suboptimal user experiences, particularly for individuals with specific mobility or cognitive requirements. To overcome this limitation, we propose a novel framework leveraging personalized dynamics models that adapt in real-time to user behavior and environmental conditions. Our approach integrates machine learning techniques, including reinforcement learning and user-specific data modeling, to dynamically adjust navigation recommendations. The system continuously learns from user interactions, improving its predictions and adaptability over time. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in user satisfaction and navigation efficiency compared to conventional systems, with a 25% reduction in travel time and a 40% increase in user-reported comfort. The proposed framework not only enhances the usability of assistive navigation technologies but also opens new avenues for personalized human-computer interaction in smart environments. Key contributions include the development of a scalable, user-centric model and its real-world validation in diverse scenarios. This research highlights the potential of adaptive systems to transform assistive technologies, making them more inclusive and effective for a wide range of users.  

**Keywords**: assistive navigation, personalized models, adaptive systems, machine learning, reinforcement learning, human-computer interaction.
