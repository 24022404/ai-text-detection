This paper addresses the challenge of constructing multi-network models for heterogeneous multi-task learning, where multiple related tasks with diverse data types and structures need to be learned simultaneously. Our objective is to develop an automatic framework that can efficiently construct and optimize multi-network models, eliminating the need for manual design and tuning. We propose a novel approach that leverages graph-based neural networks and meta-learning to automatically construct and adapt multi-network models. Our method utilizes a hierarchical graph representation to capture task relationships and a meta-learning algorithm to optimize network architectures and weights. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in several heterogeneous multi-task learning benchmarks. The key findings of this research include improved performance, reduced manual effort, and enhanced scalability. Our contributions have significant implications for real-world applications, such as multi-modal processing, transfer learning, and few-shot learning. The proposed framework enables automatic construction of multi-network models, making it a valuable tool for researchers and practitioners in the field of multi-task learning, with relevant keywords including multi-network models, heterogeneous multi-task learning, graph-based neural networks, meta-learning, and automatic model construction.
