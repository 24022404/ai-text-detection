This paper addresses the challenge of vehicle re-identification, a crucial task in intelligent transportation systems. The objective is to develop effective methods for accurately matching vehicles across different cameras and locations, facilitating applications such as traffic management and surveillance. 

We propose a deep learning-based approach, leveraging convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and attention mechanisms to learn robust vehicle features. Our method involves a two-stage process: first, a vehicle detection algorithm is used to identify vehicles in images, and then a re-identification model is applied to extract discriminative features.

Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our approach, achieving a significant improvement in re-identification accuracy compared to existing methods. The proposed method exhibits strong generalization capabilities, performing well across various datasets and scenarios.

This research contributes to the development of intelligent transportation systems, enabling enhanced traffic monitoring and management. The novelty of our approach lies in its ability to effectively capture vehicle features, even in cases of significant appearance changes. Key contributions include the design of a robust re-identification framework and the exploration of attention mechanisms in vehicle feature learning. Relevant keywords: vehicle re-identification, deep learning, CNNs, attention mechanisms, intelligent transportation systems.
