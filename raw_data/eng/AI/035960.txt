This paper presents a novel approach to video enhancement using sparse depth information, addressing the long-standing challenge of achieving both speed and accuracy in video processing. Our objective is to develop a method that can efficiently enhance video quality while preserving crucial details. We propose a sparse depth-based video enhancement framework that leverages advanced computer vision techniques and machine learning algorithms to achieve this goal. Our approach utilizes a sparse depth map to guide the enhancement process, allowing for more accurate and efficient processing. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art techniques in terms of both speed and accuracy, with significant improvements in video quality and detail preservation. The key contributions of this research include a novel sparse depth-based enhancement algorithm and a comprehensive evaluation framework for assessing video enhancement quality. Our work has important implications for various applications, including video surveillance, film restoration, and virtual reality, where high-quality video is essential. Key keywords: video enhancement, sparse depth, computer vision, machine learning, image processing.
