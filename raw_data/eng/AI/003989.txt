Interactive image segmentation aims to extract objects of interest based on user input, but existing methods often struggle with ambiguous regions and require excessive interactions. This paper introduces a novel probabilistic attention mechanism for interactive segmentation that enhances model robustness and reduces user effort. Our approach incorporates uncertainty estimation into the attention mechanism, allowing the model to focus on informative regions while mitigating the impact of noisy or ambiguous user clicks. Specifically, we model attention weights as probability distributions, enabling the network to represent and leverage uncertainty inherent in both image features and user inputs. We evaluate our method on standard interactive segmentation benchmarks, demonstrating significant improvements in segmentation accuracy and a reduction in the number of clicks needed to achieve desired results compared to state-of-the-art techniques. The probabilistic attention mechanism provides a more robust and efficient framework for interactive segmentation, paving the way for more user-friendly and accurate image analysis tools. Keywords: interactive segmentation, attention mechanism, probabilistic modeling, uncertainty estimation, computer vision.
