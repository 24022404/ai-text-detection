This paper introduces Topological Attention, a novel approach for time series forecasting that leverages topological features to enhance predictive accuracy. The objective is to improve forecasting performance by incorporating structural information from time series data. Our method utilizes a graph-based attention mechanism to capture complex relationships between time steps, allowing the model to focus on the most relevant patterns. Experimental results demonstrate that Topological Attention outperforms state-of-the-art forecasting models, particularly in cases with non-linear relationships and multiple seasonality. The key findings highlight the importance of topological features in time series analysis and the effectiveness of attention-based models in capturing these features. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and robust time series forecasting methods, with potential applications in finance, climate modeling, and resource allocation. Key keywords: time series forecasting, topological attention, graph-based models, attention mechanisms, predictive analytics.
