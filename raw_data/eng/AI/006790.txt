**Abstract:**

This paper introduces GIMP-ML, a suite of Python-based plugins designed to seamlessly integrate state-of-the-art computer vision models into GIMP, a widely used open-source image editing software. The objective of this work is to bridge the gap between advanced machine learning techniques and practical image editing workflows, allowing users of varying expertise to enhance their creative processes with artificial intelligence (AI) tools. The GIMP-ML plugins employ popular deep learning-based models for tasks such as semantic segmentation, style transfer, image super-resolution, colorization, and inpainting. The system architecture leverages Python scripts that interact with both the GIMP environment and pre-trained machine learning models, ensuring ease of installation and compatibility across major operating systems. Experimental results demonstrate that the integration provides high-quality outputs without significant overhead in processing time, and user studies indicate improved productivity and workflow efficiency for both novice and experienced users. By making advanced computer vision capabilities accessible within a familiar interface, GIMP-ML lowers the barrier for creative professionals and hobbyists to apply AI-powered editing techniques. This work sets the foundation for future developments in user-assisted design and smart editing tools within open-source graphic applications. **Keywords:** GIMP, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Python Plugins, Image Editing, Deep Learning, Open Source, AI in Art.
