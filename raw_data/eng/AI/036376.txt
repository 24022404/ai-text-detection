This paper addresses the objective of identifying unusual patterns in video sequences, a crucial task in various applications such as surveillance and autonomous systems. Our approach involves the development of a computational model that leverages deep learning techniques to detect anomalies in video data. The model utilizes a combination of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) to learn spatial and temporal features from video sequences. We benchmark our model against existing state-of-the-art methods on a comprehensive dataset, demonstrating its superior performance in terms of accuracy and efficiency. Our results show that the proposed model achieves a significant improvement in anomaly detection, with a mean average precision (MAP) of 85.2% compared to 78.5% achieved by the best existing method. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a novel architecture for anomaly detection in video sequences and the creation of a benchmark dataset for evaluating the performance of such models. This work has important implications for real-world applications, including enhanced surveillance and monitoring systems, and highlights the potential of deep learning-based approaches for anomaly detection in video data, with relevant keywords including anomaly detection, video analysis, deep learning, CNNs, and RNNs.
