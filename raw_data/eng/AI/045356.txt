This paper proposes a novel approach to explaining complex machine learning models for tabular data, inspired by the concept of attention mechanisms. The objective is to provide insights into how different features contribute to the predictions made by these models. Our method, dubbed "Attention-like Feature Explanation" (AFE), leverages a technique that assigns feature importance scores, similar to attention weights, to quantify the contribution of each feature to the model's output. We employ a combination of feature permutation and sensitivity analysis to generate these scores. The results show that AFE outperforms existing feature explanation methods in terms of accuracy and consistency, particularly for models trained on high-dimensional tabular data. Our approach has significant implications for model interpretability, transparency, and trustworthiness, enabling practitioners to better understand and refine their models. Key contributions include the introduction of attention-like mechanisms to tabular data explanation, improved feature importance estimation, and enhanced model interpretability. Relevant keywords: explainable AI, feature importance, attention mechanisms, tabular data, model interpretability.
