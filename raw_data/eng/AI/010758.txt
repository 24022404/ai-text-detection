This paper introduces DCT-Mask, a novel approach to instance segmentation using Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) mask representation. The objective is to improve the efficiency and accuracy of instance segmentation models by leveraging the frequency domain representation of masks. Our method utilizes DCT to transform mask representations into the frequency domain, allowing for more compact and efficient encoding of mask information. We propose a mask encoding framework that combines DCT with a state-of-the-art instance segmentation architecture, enabling the model to predict masks in the frequency domain. Experimental results demonstrate that DCT-Mask achieves significant improvements in mask accuracy and reduces computational costs compared to traditional mask representation methods. The key findings of this research highlight the effectiveness of DCT-Mask in instance segmentation tasks, particularly in scenarios with complex object shapes and varying sizes. Our approach contributes to the advancement of instance segmentation techniques, with potential applications in computer vision, robotics, and autonomous systems, and is relevant to keywords such as instance segmentation, DCT, mask representation, computer vision, and deep learning.
