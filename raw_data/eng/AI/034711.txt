Road detection is a crucial task for autonomous driving and advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS). This paper presents a novel approach to road detection by fusing LiDAR point clouds and camera images using a fully convolutional neural network (FCN). Our method leverages the complementary strengths of both modalities: LiDAR provides accurate 3D spatial information, while camera images offer rich texture and color details. The proposed FCN architecture effectively integrates these features through a multi-modal fusion module, enabling robust road segmentation even in challenging scenarios with varying lighting conditions and complex road structures. We evaluate our method on a publicly available dataset and demonstrate that it outperforms state-of-the-art approaches in terms of accuracy and robustness. The results highlight the effectiveness of LiDAR-camera fusion for road detection using FCNs, offering a promising solution for enhancing the performance of autonomous navigation systems.
Keywords: Road detection, LiDAR, Camera, Sensor Fusion, Fully Convolutional Neural Network, Autonomous Driving.
