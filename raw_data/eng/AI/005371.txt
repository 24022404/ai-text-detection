This paper presents a novel approach to optimizing battery energy storage dispatch using reinforcement learning augmented with a model-based optimizer. The objective is to develop an intelligent control strategy that maximizes the economic benefits of battery energy storage systems in grid-scale applications. Our approach combines the strengths of model-based optimization and reinforcement learning to adapt to changing grid conditions and optimize energy storage dispatch. The methodology employs a deep reinforcement learning algorithm to learn an optimal policy, which is then refined by a model-based optimizer to ensure feasibility and optimality. Results show that the proposed approach outperforms traditional rule-based methods, achieving significant improvements in economic benefits and reducing energy losses. The conclusion highlights the potential of this innovative approach to transform the operation of battery energy storage systems, enabling greater grid resilience and renewable energy integration. Key contributions include the development of a novel reinforcement learning framework and the demonstration of its effectiveness in optimizing battery energy storage dispatch. Relevant keywords: reinforcement learning, model-based optimization, battery energy storage, grid-scale applications, energy management, smart grids.
