This paper presents KeRLym, a novel Gym RL agent that leverages deep reinforcement learning to optimize radio control and signal detection in complex wireless environments. The objective is to develop an autonomous system that can efficiently navigate and adapt to dynamic radio frequency scenarios. Our approach utilizes a customized reinforcement learning framework, integrating convolutional neural networks and policy gradient methods to enable KeRLym to learn from trial and error. The results demonstrate significant improvements in signal detection accuracy and radio control efficiency, outperforming traditional methods in simulated and real-world experiments. Key findings include the ability of KeRLym to adapt to noise and interference, and to optimize transmission parameters for enhanced communication reliability. The contributions of this research lie in the development of a flexible and scalable RL framework for radio control and signal detection, with potential applications in wireless communication systems, IoT networks, and autonomous vehicles. The use of deep reinforcement learning and Gym RL agents enables KeRLym to learn and improve over time, making it a valuable tool for future wireless communication systems. Relevant keywords: deep reinforcement learning, radio control, signal detection, Gym RL agent, convolutional neural networks, policy gradient methods, wireless communication systems.
