This paper presents a novel approach to automated game testing by integrating deep reinforcement learning (DRL) techniques. The objective is to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of game testing by leveraging DRL agents to explore complex game environments and identify critical bugs or issues. Our method employs a customized DRL framework, utilizing convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and Q-learning algorithms to enable agents to learn optimal testing strategies. Experimental results demonstrate that our DRL-augmented testing approach outperforms traditional automated testing methods, achieving higher bug detection rates and reduced testing times. The key findings highlight the potential of DRL in revolutionizing game testing, enabling faster and more comprehensive testing protocols. This research contributes to the development of more robust and efficient game testing methodologies, with significant implications for the gaming industry. Key aspects of this work include deep reinforcement learning, automated game testing, Q-learning, and convolutional neural networks, making it a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners in the fields of artificial intelligence, game development, and software testing.
