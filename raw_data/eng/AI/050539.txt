This paper introduces MUST-GAN, a novel Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) architecture for high-quality person image generation. The objective is to generate realistic and diverse person images with controllable attributes, without relying on paired training data. MUST-GAN achieves this through a multi-level statistics transfer mechanism, which effectively disentangles and transfers style and content information from source images to target poses. The model leverages feature statistics at multiple scales of the generator network to capture both global appearance and fine-grained details. Experimental results demonstrate that MUST-GAN significantly outperforms existing self-driven person image generation methods in terms of visual quality, identity preservation, and pose fidelity. Furthermore, we show the effectiveness of our multi-level statistics transfer in generating diverse outputs while maintaining high realism. The proposed approach offers a promising direction for generating personalized content and enabling new applications in virtual try-on and digital avatar creation. Keywords: GAN, Image Generation, Person Re-identification, Style Transfer, Unpaired Learning.
