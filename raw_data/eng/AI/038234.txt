This paper addresses the challenge of highlight detection in figure skating videos by proposing a novel approach to estimate blink probability as a key indicator of salient moments. The objective is to enhance the accuracy of identifying highlights, such as jumps and spins, by leveraging the correlation between blinks and high-intensity movements. We introduce a deep learning-based framework that combines temporal analysis of video frames with a blink detection model trained on annotated figure skating datasets. The model employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) for feature extraction and a recurrent neural network (RNN) to capture temporal dependencies, enabling robust blink probability estimation. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves superior performance compared to traditional highlight detection techniques, with a significant improvement in precision and recall. The findings suggest that blink probability serves as a reliable metric for identifying highlights in figure skating videos, offering potential applications in automated video summarization and sports analytics. This research contributes to the field of computer vision by introducing a novel, biologically inspired approach to highlight detection, paving the way for future advancements in sports video analysis.  

**Keywords**: blink probability, highlight detection, figure skating, deep learning, video analysis, sports analytics.
