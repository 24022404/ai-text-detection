This paper aims to develop a novel approach for calculating accurate and robust Shapley Values, a technique used to explain predictions made by complex machine learning models. The objective is to provide a reliable method for attributing the contribution of each feature to the predicted outcome, focusing on local important variables. Our approach employs a combination of advanced algorithms and statistical methods to address the challenges of calculating Shapley Values in high-dimensional datasets. The results show that our method outperforms existing techniques in terms of accuracy and robustness, particularly in scenarios with correlated features and non-linear relationships. The key findings highlight the importance of considering local variable interactions and the potential pitfalls of ignoring these interactions when interpreting model predictions. Our research contributes to the field of explainable AI by providing a robust and reliable technique for explaining predictions, with potential applications in areas such as feature selection, model interpretability, and decision-making under uncertainty. Key keywords: Shapley Values, explainable AI, machine learning, feature attribution, local importance.
