This paper presents a novel approach to neural network training, leveraging \ell 1-regularization and bi-fidelity data to enhance model performance and efficiency. The objective is to develop a robust and accurate neural network framework that can effectively utilize both high-fidelity and low-fidelity data sources. Our method employs \ell 1-regularization to induce sparsity in the neural network, reducing overfitting and improving generalization. The bi-fidelity data is integrated using a weighted loss function, allowing the model to balance the contributions of both data sources. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms traditional training methods, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and computational efficiency. The proposed framework has important implications for applications where high-fidelity data is scarce or expensive to obtain, such as image classification, natural language processing, and reinforcement learning. Key contributions include the development of a bi-fidelity neural network architecture and the introduction of \ell 1-regularization to improve model sparsity and performance. Relevant keywords: neural networks, \ell 1-regularization, bi-fidelity data, sparse modeling, deep learning.
