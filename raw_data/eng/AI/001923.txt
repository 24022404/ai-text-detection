This paper presents a novel approach to virtual character animation control using data-driven reinforcement learning. The objective is to develop an intelligent system that can learn to control character animations in a realistic and engaging manner. Our method utilizes a deep reinforcement learning framework, combining techniques from computer vision and machine learning to enable characters to learn from demonstration data and adapt to new scenarios. The approach involves training a neural network to predict optimal control policies from large datasets of annotated animations. Results show that our system can generate high-quality, realistic animations that surpass those created using traditional keyframe animation techniques. The findings have significant implications for the field of computer animation, enabling the creation of more realistic and interactive virtual characters for applications such as video games, film, and virtual reality. Key contributions include the development of a data-driven reinforcement learning framework for character animation control, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in generating realistic animations. Relevant keywords: reinforcement learning, virtual character animation, computer vision, machine learning, deep learning, animation control.
