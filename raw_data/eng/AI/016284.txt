Weakly Supervised Object Localization (WSOL) aims to identify object regions in images using only image-level labels, which remains a challenging task due to the lack of precise localization information. This paper introduces a novel Dual-attention Guided Dropblock Module (DADM) to enhance the localization accuracy of WSOL models. The proposed approach integrates two key components: a channel-wise attention mechanism to emphasize discriminative features and a spatial attention mechanism to focus on salient object regions. Additionally, the Dropblock module is employed to prevent overfitting by randomly dropping contiguous regions during training, thereby improving the model's generalization capability. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets, such as CUB-200-2011 and ILSVRC, demonstrate that DADM achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of localization accuracy and robustness. The results highlight the effectiveness of the dual-attention mechanism in guiding the model to focus on relevant object regions while mitigating background noise. This work contributes to advancing WSOL by providing a more reliable and interpretable framework for object localization, with potential applications in medical imaging, autonomous driving, and video surveillance.  

**Keywords**: Weakly Supervised Object Localization, Dual-attention Mechanism, Dropblock, Image Classification, Deep Learning.
