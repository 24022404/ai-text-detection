The RdB sequence stands out for its remarkable adaptability; by easing the restriction on consecutive 0’s, its rate can be increased without compromising compliance with primary system requirements. In addressing this, the chapter presents innovative constrained de Bruijn sequences that leverage a self-locating property, ensuring that only run length limitations need to be met to function effectively with the dBTS system. As a result, combining the concepts of positioning and run length limited sequences becomes a logical and efficient choice, directly inspiring the thesis’s overarching name and direction.
