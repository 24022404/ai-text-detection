Haze in images significantly degrades visual quality and impairs the performance of computer vision applications, particularly in outdoor environments. This paper presents a novel classification-driven single image dehazing approach that leverages semantic scene understanding to enhance dehazing accuracy and robustness. Unlike traditional methods that rely primarily on handcrafted priors or low-level image features, our approach integrates a deep learning-based image classifier to guide the dehazing process. Specifically, a convolutional neural network (CNN) is trained to classify scene categories and extract high-level features, informing both the estimation of transmission maps and the restoration of scene radiance. Experimental evaluations on publicly available benchmarks demonstrate that our method achieves superior haze removal and improves both perceptual image quality and quantitative metrics (PSNR, SSIM) compared to state-of-the-art dehazing techniques. Furthermore, the classification-driven strategy significantly benefits downstream computer vision tasks such as object detection and recognition in hazy conditions. The results highlight the potential of combining classification guidance with dehazing networks to advance image enhancement in challenging real-world scenarios. Keywords: image dehazing, convolutional neural network, scene classification, deep learning, image enhancement, haze removal.
