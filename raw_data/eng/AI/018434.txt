This paper introduces the Synthesize-It-Classifier, a novel generative classifier that leverages recurrent self-analysis to enhance its learning capabilities. The objective is to develop a classifier that can synthesize new data samples to improve its performance on complex classification tasks. Our approach employs a recurrent neural network architecture that enables the classifier to analyze its own performance and generate new samples to augment the training data. The results show that the Synthesize-It-Classifier outperforms traditional classifiers on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating improved accuracy and robustness. Key findings include the ability to generate high-quality synthetic data samples that effectively augment the training data, leading to improved classification performance. The Synthesize-It-Classifier has significant implications for applications where labeled data is scarce or expensive to obtain. By highlighting the novelty of our approach, which combines generative modeling with recurrent self-analysis, this research contributes to the development of more effective and efficient classification systems, with potential applications in areas such as image classification, natural language processing, and recommender systems, utilizing keywords such as generative models, recurrent neural networks, self-analysis, and synthetic data augmentation.
