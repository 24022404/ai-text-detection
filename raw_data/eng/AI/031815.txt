This paper presents a novel approach to compactly representing 3D point clouds using adversarial autoencoders. The objective is to learn a compressed representation of 3D point cloud data while preserving its essential geometric and structural information. Our method employs a variational autoencoder framework, combined with an adversarial training process, to learn a probabilistic latent space that effectively captures the underlying distribution of the point cloud data. The adversarial component regularizes the autoencoder to produce disentangled and informative representations. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of representation compactness and reconstruction accuracy. The proposed technique has significant implications for efficient storage, transmission, and processing of large-scale 3D point cloud data, with potential applications in fields such as computer vision, robotics, and virtual reality. Key contributions include the development of a compact and expressive representation of 3D point clouds, and the demonstration of the effectiveness of adversarial autoencoders in learning structured and meaningful latent spaces. Relevant keywords: 3D point clouds, adversarial autoencoders, compact representations, variational autoencoders, geometric deep learning.
