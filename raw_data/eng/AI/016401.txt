**Abstract:**  
This paper introduces RL-Duet, an innovative system for real-time music accompaniment generation leveraging deep reinforcement learning (DRL) techniques. The primary objective of RL-Duet is to create dynamic, coherent, and musically relevant accompaniment for live performers in online settings, addressing the limitations of existing rule-based and generative models that often fail to adapt to spontaneous musical changes. The proposed framework models the accompaniment generation task as a sequential decision-making problem, where a DRL agent is trained to interact with incoming live melodies and produce harmonious accompaniments by maximizing musical reward functions aligned with key, rhythm, and player preferences. The RL-Duet architecture utilizes a combination of convolutional and recurrent neural networks to capture both local and long-term musical structures in real time. Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets and live performance scenarios demonstrate that RL-Duet outperforms traditional generative and statistical models in terms of both objective metrics (harmonic consistency, rhythmic alignment) and subjective listener satisfaction. The results confirm RL-Duet’s capability to adapt to complex musical contexts and individual performer styles. This research contributes a novel, adaptable approach to interactive music generation, with potential applications in online collaboration, music education, and intelligent digital instruments.  
**Keywords:** music accompaniment, deep reinforcement learning, real-time generation, online performance, neural networks, interactive music systems
