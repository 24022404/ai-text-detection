Deep semi-supervised learning (SSL) has gained significant attention for leveraging both labeled and unlabeled data to improve classification performance. However, existing clustering-based regularization methods often struggle with scalability, robustness, and adaptability to complex datasets. This paper introduces **CycleCluster**, a novel framework designed to modernize clustering regularization for deep SSL. The proposed approach integrates cyclic consistency and adaptive clustering mechanisms to enhance feature representation and ensure robust decision boundaries. CycleCluster employs a dual-phase training process, where clustering constraints are dynamically adjusted based on the evolving feature space, enabling better alignment between labeled and unlabeled data distributions. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that CycleCluster outperforms state-of-the-art SSL methods, achieving significant improvements in classification accuracy and robustness to label scarcity. The framework also exhibits superior scalability and adaptability across diverse data domains. By addressing key limitations in traditional clustering regularization, CycleCluster offers a promising direction for advancing deep SSL in real-world applications, particularly in scenarios with limited labeled data.  

**Keywords**: Semi-supervised learning, clustering regularization, deep learning, cyclic consistency, adaptive clustering, classification.
