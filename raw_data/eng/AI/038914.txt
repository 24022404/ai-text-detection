Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) have achieved remarkable success in various domains, but their inherent complexity often leads to a lack of interpretability, hindering trust and adoption in critical applications. This survey provides a comprehensive overview of existing techniques for interpreting ANNs, categorizing them based on their approach and scope. We examine methods that focus on understanding individual neurons, connections, and layers, as well as techniques that provide global explanations of network behavior. The survey also evaluates the strengths and limitations of each approach, considering factors such as computational cost, applicability to different network architectures, and the type of insights they provide. Furthermore, we discuss the evaluation metrics used to assess the quality of interpretations and identify key challenges and open research questions in the field. This work aims to serve as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners seeking to enhance the transparency and trustworthiness of ANNs. Keywords: Artificial Neural Networks, Interpretability, Explainable AI, Deep Learning, Survey.
