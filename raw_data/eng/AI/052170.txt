This paper introduces DeepSaliency, a novel multi-task deep neural network architecture designed for accurate and efficient salient object detection. The objective is to improve the precision and robustness of identifying the most visually distinctive objects in images, a crucial task for various computer vision applications. Our model leverages a shared encoder network coupled with two task-specific decoder branches: one for pixel-level saliency prediction and another for boundary refinement. This multi-task learning approach facilitates information sharing between these related tasks, leading to enhanced performance. We trained and evaluated DeepSaliency on several benchmark datasets, including DUT-OMRON, ECSSD, and DUTS. Experimental results demonstrate that DeepSaliency achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of F-measure, precision, recall, and mean absolute error, outperforming existing single-task models. Furthermore, the model exhibits strong generalization capability across diverse image types. The proposed DeepSaliency offers a robust and effective solution for salient object detection, with potential applications in image segmentation, object recognition, and scene understanding.

Keywords: Salient Object Detection, Deep Learning, Multi-Task Learning, Computer Vision, Neural Networks, Saliency Prediction.
