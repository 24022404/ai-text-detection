This paper presents an end-to-end learning framework for 3D point cloud registration, utilizing the novel concept of virtual correspondences. The objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of aligning two unstructured 3D point clouds, a critical task in fields such as autonomous driving, robotics, and augmented reality. Unlike traditional methods that rely on hand-crafted features or explicit correspondence matching, our approach leverages deep neural networks to implicitly learn spatial relationships and generate virtual correspondences that guide the registration process. The proposed model integrates feature extraction, correspondence estimation, and transformation prediction into a unified pipeline, enabling joint optimization through supervised and unsupervised loss functions. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate significant improvements in registration accuracy, outlier resistance, and computational efficiency compared to existing classical and learning-based techniques. Our method further exhibits strong generalization across diverse point cloud scenes without requiring extensive pre-processing or manual parameter tuning. The findings highlight the effectiveness of leveraging virtual correspondences within deep learning frameworks for 3D geometric learning. This approach opens new possibilities for efficient, scalable, and reliable 3D point cloud processing in real-world applications. Key topics addressed include 3D point cloud registration, virtual correspondences, end-to-end learning, deep learning for geometric matching, and spatial transformation networks.
