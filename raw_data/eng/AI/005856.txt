This paper addresses the challenge of knowledge retention in continual learning, where neural networks forget previously learned tasks as they adapt to new ones. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that mitigates this forgetting issue, enabling more efficient and effective lifelong learning. We propose a rectification-based method that retains knowledge from previous tasks by rectifying the feature space and updating the model parameters accordingly. Our approach utilizes a combination of regularization techniques and knowledge distillation to preserve the learned knowledge. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art continual learning algorithms, achieving significant improvements in knowledge retention and adaptation to new tasks. The key findings of this study highlight the importance of rectification in continual learning, leading to improved performance and reduced forgetting. Our research contributes to the development of more robust and adaptable neural networks, with potential applications in real-world scenarios where data is continuously streaming and tasks are constantly evolving. Key keywords: continual learning, knowledge retention, rectification, neural networks, lifelong learning.
