This paper introduces a novel unsupervised approach to monocular stereo matching, addressing the challenge of depth estimation from a single moving camera without relying on ground truth depth data. Our method leverages a deep neural network trained with a photometric consistency loss and a novel smoothness constraint that encourages piecewise smooth depth maps, enhancing robustness in texture-less regions. The network learns to predict disparities between consecutive frames, effectively synthesizing stereo pairs. The approach incorporates an adversarial training scheme to further refine the generated disparities, pushing them towards more realistic and accurate values. Experiments on standard datasets demonstrate that our unsupervised method achieves competitive performance compared to existing supervised and unsupervised techniques. Qualitative results show improved depth map quality, especially in areas with low texture and occlusions. This work contributes a practical and scalable solution for 3D scene understanding using monocular video, enabling applications in robotics, autonomous navigation, and virtual reality.

**Keywords:** Monocular Stereo Matching, Unsupervised Learning, Depth Estimation, Deep Learning, Photometric Consistency, 3D Reconstruction.
