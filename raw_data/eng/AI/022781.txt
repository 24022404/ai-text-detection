This paper addresses the challenge of synthesizing realistic face photos from simple line drawings while enabling precise control over facial attributes such as age, gender, and expression. Traditional methods often struggle to generate high-quality, attribute-specific results due to the limited information provided by line drawings. To overcome this, we propose a novel deep learning-based framework that integrates a conditional generative adversarial network (cGAN) with an attribute manipulation module. The framework leverages a multi-stage pipeline, where the initial stage focuses on reconstructing the basic facial structure from the line drawing, and subsequent stages refine the output by incorporating attribute-specific features. Our approach employs a combination of perceptual loss and adversarial training to ensure realistic and visually coherent results. Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms existing techniques in terms of both image quality and attribute control accuracy. The proposed framework achieves state-of-the-art performance, with significant improvements in preserving facial details and generating diverse attribute variations. This work has practical implications for applications in digital art, animation, and virtual character design, where precise control over facial attributes is essential.  

**Keywords**: face synthesis, line drawing, attribute control, generative adversarial networks, deep learning, image generation.
