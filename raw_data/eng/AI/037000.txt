Imbalanced data poses a significant challenge in classification tasks, often leading to biased predictive models that underperform on minority classes. This paper introduces a novel deep learning approach, the Class Expert Generative Adversarial Network (CE-GAN), specifically designed to address the imbalance problem in classification datasets. The proposed method incorporates class-specific expert generators within the GAN framework, allowing for the targeted synthesis of minority class samples and the enhancement of feature representation for underrepresented categories. Experimental evaluation was conducted on several benchmark imbalanced datasets, where CE-GAN was compared with state-of-the-art oversampling and class-balancing techniques, including SMOTE, ADASYN, and cost-sensitive learning. Results demonstrate that CE-GAN achieves superior performance in terms of accuracy, F1-score, and minority class recall, effectively mitigating the bias towards majority classes without sacrificing overall model robustness. The ablation study further validates the contribution of class expert modules in improving classification fairness and generalization. These findings highlight the potential of CE-GAN for a wide range of applications involving imbalance data, such as medical diagnosis, fraud detection, and anomaly detection in information systems. Keywords: Imbalanced Data, Deep Learning, Generative Adversarial Network, Class Expert, Data Augmentation, Classification.
