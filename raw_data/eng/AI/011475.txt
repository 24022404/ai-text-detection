The objective of this research is to address the growing need for efficient, automated generation of high-definition (HD) maps, which are crucial for a variety of applications, particularly in autonomous driving and advanced navigation systems. This paper introduces HDMapGen, a novel hierarchical graph generative model designed to create detailed and accurate HD maps. Our approach leverages graph-based machine learning techniques to model the complex structures and minute details inherent in HD maps, providing a scalable solution to map generation challenges. The model is trained using a rich dataset that reflects diverse real-world environments, ensuring robustness and adaptability. Key findings demonstrate that HDMapGen significantly outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. Comparative analysis shows enhanced performance in maintaining spatial and topological fidelity compared to traditional mapping techniques. The implications of this work are significant, offering an automated, scalable tool for HD map generation that reduces the time and labor costs associated with manual map creation. These findings underscore the potential applications of HDMapGen in improving the capabilities of autonomous systems. Keywords: HD maps, generative model, hierarchical graph, autonomous driving, machine learning.
