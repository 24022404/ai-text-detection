This paper introduces GLEAN, a novel generative latent bank approach for large-factor image super-resolution. The objective is to develop an efficient and effective method for enhancing low-resolution images by a significant factor, while maintaining image quality and realism. Our approach utilizes a generative model to learn a latent space of high-resolution images, which is then used to guide the super-resolution process. The proposed method leverages a combination of generative adversarial networks and latent variable models to produce high-quality, high-resolution images. Experimental results demonstrate that GLEAN outperforms state-of-the-art super-resolution methods, particularly for large upscaling factors. The key findings include significant improvements in peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index measure (SSIM), as well as visually pleasing results. The contributions of this research lie in its ability to generate high-quality images at large scaling factors, making it a valuable tool for applications such as image and video enhancement, surveillance, and medical imaging. Key keywords: image super-resolution, generative models, latent variable models, GANs, computer vision.
