Monocular depth estimation using deep neural networks has achieved significant progress, yet the vulnerability of such models to adversarial attacks presents critical challenges for their deployment in real-world applications. This paper provides a comprehensive analysis of the robustness of state-of-the-art deep networks for monocular depth estimation under various adversarial attack scenarios. We systematically evaluate the performance degradation caused by gradient-based and black-box adversarial techniques across several benchmark datasets. To address the security gap, we propose a novel defense method combining adversarial training with feature consistency regularization, designed to enhance the model’s resilience without sacrificing estimation accuracy. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed defense method substantially mitigates the impact of adversarial perturbations, improving robustness while maintaining high-quality depth predictions. Our findings highlight key vulnerabilities of existing approaches, deliver valuable insights into attack mechanisms, and offer an effective defense strategy for secure and reliable monocular depth estimation. Keywords: deep networks, monocular depth estimation, adversarial attacks, model robustness, adversarial defense, feature consistency, neural network security.
