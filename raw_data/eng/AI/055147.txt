This paper introduces a principled learning method for Wasserstein Distributionally Robust Optimization (DRO) problems, specifically addressing local perturbations. The objective is to develop a robust optimization framework that is resilient to uncertainties in data distributions. Our approach utilizes a novel combination of Wasserstein metric-based DRO and local perturbation techniques to construct ambiguity sets that capture distributional shifts in the vicinity of the nominal distribution. The proposed method provides theoretical guarantees for out-of-sample performance and robustness. Empirical evaluations on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves superior performance compared to traditional DRO methods and other robust optimization techniques, especially when dealing with noisy or incomplete data. Furthermore, the learned solutions exhibit improved stability and generalizability across different perturbation scenarios. Key contributions include a novel ambiguity set construction, a computationally efficient optimization algorithm, and comprehensive experimental validation. Keywords: Distributionally Robust Optimization, Wasserstein Metric, Local Perturbations, Robust Optimization, Machine Learning.
