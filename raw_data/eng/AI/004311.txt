Reinforcement Learning (RL) has shown significant promise in solving complex continuous control tasks. However, its performance often degrades under model misspecification, where the learned policy fails to generalize due to discrepancies between the training environment and real-world conditions. This paper addresses the challenge of robust RL in continuous control settings by proposing a novel framework that enhances policy robustness against model uncertainties. Our approach integrates a distributionally robust optimization (DRO) technique with deep RL algorithms, enabling the agent to learn policies that are resilient to environmental variations. We evaluate the proposed method on a suite of benchmark continuous control tasks, including robotic manipulation and autonomous navigation, under varying levels of model misspecification. Experimental results demonstrate that our framework outperforms existing RL methods in terms of stability, generalization, and task performance. The key contributions of this work include a theoretically grounded robust RL formulation and a practical algorithm that bridges the gap between simulation and real-world deployment. This research advances the state-of-the-art in robust RL and provides a foundation for deploying RL-based systems in safety-critical applications where model uncertainties are prevalent.  

**Keywords**: Reinforcement Learning, Continuous Control, Model Misspecification, Robust Optimization, Deep RL, Generalization.
