Facial attribute classification using Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) has shown great promise, yet understanding the network's focus remains a challenge. This paper addresses the need for interpretable attribute classification by leveraging Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) to direct DNNs attention. We propose a novel approach that utilizes Grad-CAM generated attention maps to guide the network toward relevant facial regions for specific attribute prediction. Our method incorporates these attention maps as weighted inputs during training, encouraging the network to prioritize discriminative features. Experimental results on benchmark facial attribute datasets demonstrate that our approach improves classification accuracy, particularly for subtle and localized attributes, compared to baseline DNN models. Furthermore, visualization of the learned attention maps confirms that the network focuses on semantically meaningful facial regions associated with the predicted attributes. This work offers a more transparent and accurate facial attribute classification system, contributing to explainable AI in computer vision. Keywords: Facial Attribute Classification, Deep Neural Networks, Explainable AI, Grad-CAM, Attention Mechanism, Computer Vision.
