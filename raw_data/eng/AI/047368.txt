This paper introduces a novel approach for efficient point feature embedding using an Irregularly Tabulated Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) architecture. The primary objective of our research is to address the limitations of existing point cloud-based feature extractors, which often struggle with high computational costs and latency due to the unstructured nature of point data. Our method leverages a tabulation mechanism that selectively stores and retrieves MLP activations for irregularly distributed spatial coordinates, enabling direct and rapid feature mapping without costly repeated computations. Extensive experiments demonstrate that our Irregularly Tabulated MLP achieves significant speedups in point feature embedding tasks, with comparable or improved representation quality when tested on standard benchmarks for 3D shape recognition and scene understanding. Comparative evaluations show superior inference efficiency compared to traditional dense MLP and grid-based lookup approaches. This work contributes an effective solution for scalable and real-time point cloud processing, with broad implications for accelerated 3D reconstruction, robotics, and real-time perception systems. Key keywords: point feature embedding, MLP, tabulation, point cloud, 3D data, real-time processing, efficient neural networks.
