This paper addresses the challenge of evaluating and improving hierarchical disentanglement in representation learning. We introduce novel benchmarks specifically designed to assess the hierarchical structure learned by disentanglement algorithms. These benchmarks comprise synthetic datasets with varying degrees of hierarchical complexity and real-world datasets adapted for hierarchical analysis. Furthermore, we propose a suite of algorithms that explicitly encourage hierarchical disentanglement, leveraging techniques from information theory and tree-structured priors. We also present new metrics tailored to quantify the quality of hierarchical disentanglement, addressing limitations of existing methods. Experimental results demonstrate that our proposed algorithms outperform existing disentanglement methods on the introduced benchmarks, achieving superior hierarchical structure and improved downstream task performance. The developed benchmarks, algorithms, and metrics provide a comprehensive framework for advancing research in hierarchical disentanglement and contribute to building more interpretable and controllable AI systems. Keywords: Disentanglement, Hierarchical Representation Learning, Benchmarking, Evaluation Metrics, Representation Learning, Information Theory.
