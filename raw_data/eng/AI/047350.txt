This paper addresses the challenge of accurate object detection by introducing a novel bounding box regression approach that incorporates uncertainty estimation. The objective is to improve the precision of object localization in images by predicting not only the bounding box coordinates but also the uncertainty associated with these predictions. Our method employs a deep learning-based model that leverages the strengths of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and probabilistic regression techniques. The approach involves training a network to output a probability distribution over possible bounding box locations, allowing for the quantification of uncertainty in predictions. Experimental results demonstrate that our uncertainty-aware bounding box regression method outperforms state-of-the-art object detection algorithms, achieving significant improvements in detection accuracy and robustness. The key contributions of this research include the development of a probabilistic framework for bounding box regression and the demonstration of its effectiveness in real-world object detection tasks. Our approach has important implications for applications such as autonomous driving, surveillance, and robotics, where accurate and reliable object detection is crucial. Key keywords: object detection, bounding box regression, uncertainty estimation, deep learning, computer vision.
