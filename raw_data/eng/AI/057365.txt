Unsupervised domain adaptation (UDA) in object detection aims to transfer knowledge from a labeled source domain to an unlabeled target domain. However, performance is often hindered by the uncertainty introduced during the adaptation process, particularly in pseudo-labeling. This paper introduces an uncertainty-aware UDA framework for object detection designed to mitigate this issue. Our approach leverages a novel uncertainty estimation module that quantifies the confidence of pseudo-labels generated for target domain objects. High-uncertainty pseudo-labels are down-weighted during training, while confident pseudo-labels are emphasized to facilitate more robust adaptation. Furthermore, we introduce a cross-domain consistency regularization term that encourages the model to produce consistent predictions across domains, particularly in regions of high uncertainty. Experiments on multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms existing UDA object detection techniques. Specifically, our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on challenging domain adaptation scenarios, improving mean Average Precision (mAP) by a significant margin. The results highlight the importance of uncertainty awareness in UDA and the effectiveness of our proposed framework in leveraging uncertainty for improved object detection performance in the target domain.

Keywords: Unsupervised Domain Adaptation, Object Detection, Uncertainty Estimation, Pseudo-labeling, Cross-Domain Consistency, Deep Learning.
