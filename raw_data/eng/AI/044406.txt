Object proposal generation plays a crucial role in enhancing the accuracy and efficiency of visual tracking systems. This paper presents a novel target-specific object proposal generation method designed to address the limitations of generic proposal algorithms in dynamic tracking environments. Our approach integrates target appearance modeling with adaptive region proposal mechanisms, enabling the system to generate high-quality candidate regions closely aligned with the target in each video frame. The method employs a combination of deep feature extraction and real-time target adaptation, ensuring robustness against occlusions, background clutter, and scale variations. Experimental results on multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed method significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art object proposal techniques in terms of tracking precision, recall, and processing speed. The findings highlight the importance of leveraging target-specific information for refined proposal generation in visual tracking applications. This research contributes a scalable and effective framework applicable to real-world surveillance, autonomous navigation, and human-computer interaction systems. Key keywords: visual tracking, object proposal generation, target-specific modeling, deep learning, real-time tracking, computer vision.
