This paper introduces LEAD, a novel LiDAR extender system designed to enhance the perception capabilities of autonomous vehicles. The objective of LEAD is to address the limitations of traditional LiDAR sensors in detecting and tracking objects at long ranges and in complex environments. Our approach combines advanced signal processing techniques with machine learning algorithms to extend the effective range of LiDAR sensors, improving the accuracy and reliability of object detection and tracking. The LEAD system is evaluated through a series of experiments, demonstrating significant improvements in detection range and accuracy compared to traditional LiDAR systems. Our results show that LEAD can detect objects at ranges of up to 200 meters, with an accuracy of 95% or higher. The implications of this research are significant, enabling the development of more advanced and reliable autonomous driving systems. Key contributions of this work include the development of a novel LiDAR extender algorithm and the demonstration of its effectiveness in real-world autonomous driving scenarios. Relevant keywords: LiDAR, autonomous driving, object detection, machine learning, sensor fusion.
