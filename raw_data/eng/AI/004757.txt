Instance segmentation, a critical task in computer vision, demands precise object boundary delineation. Existing pixel-wise loss functions often struggle with accurate contour prediction, leading to suboptimal segmentation performance. This paper introduces a novel Contour Loss based on a k-step distance transformation image. The proposed loss function emphasizes contour regions by leveraging distance information, guiding the network to focus on boundary pixels during training. Specifically, the k-step distance transformation image is generated by iteratively dilating the ground truth contours. We then use this transformation as weights to penalize errors in the predicted segmentation map. Experimental results on standard datasets, including Cityscapes and COCO, demonstrate that our Contour Loss significantly improves instance segmentation accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods utilizing conventional loss functions. The improvements are particularly noticeable in the precision of object boundaries. Our approach offers a simple yet effective way to enhance instance segmentation by explicitly addressing contour prediction, contributing to more accurate and visually appealing segmentation results. Keywords: Instance Segmentation, Contour Loss, Distance Transformation, Semantic Segmentation, Deep Learning.
