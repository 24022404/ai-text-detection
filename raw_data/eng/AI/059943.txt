This paper addresses the challenge of accurate and efficient 3D face identification, a critical task in biometric security and human-computer interaction. Traditional 2D face recognition systems often struggle with variations in lighting, pose, and expression, limiting their reliability. To overcome these limitations, we propose a novel deep learning framework specifically designed for 3D face identification. Our approach leverages a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture optimized for processing 3D facial data, incorporating geometric and texture features to enhance discriminative capabilities. The model is trained on a large-scale 3D face dataset, ensuring robustness across diverse facial structures and conditions. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing 3D and 2D face recognition techniques in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. Furthermore, the proposed framework exhibits strong generalization capabilities, making it suitable for real-world applications such as access control, surveillance, and personalized user interfaces. This research contributes to advancing 3D biometric systems by introducing a scalable and effective solution for face identification, with potential implications for improving security and user experience in various domains.  

**Keywords**: 3D face identification, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, biometric security, facial recognition.
