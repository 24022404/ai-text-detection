This paper addresses the challenge of improving melanoma screening accuracy using advanced deep learning techniques. The primary objective is to explore how knowledge transfer, specifically transfer learning, can enhance the performance of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) in differentiating malignant melanoma from benign skin lesions in dermoscopic images. The proposed approach leverages pre-trained deep learning models, fine-tuned on a large-scale skin lesion dataset, to capitalize on feature representations learned from diverse visual domains. We conduct extensive experiments evaluating multiple CNN architectures, comparing models trained from scratch with those utilizing transfer learning. Results demonstrate that models employing knowledge transfer achieve superior classification accuracy, sensitivity, and specificity, significantly outperforming traditional and baseline deep learning approaches. Our findings highlight the effectiveness of transfer learning in addressing data limitations and improving diagnostic support for melanoma detection. This research contributes novel empirical evidence supporting the integration of knowledge transfer strategies in medical image analysis, with implications for the development of automated, reliable, and scalable melanoma screening tools. Key keywords: deep learning, transfer learning, convolutional neural networks, melanoma screening, medical image analysis, skin lesion classification, knowledge transfer.
