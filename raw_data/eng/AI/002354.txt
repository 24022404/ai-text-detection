This paper presents a novel approach for 3D parametric wireframe extraction using distance fields, addressing the challenge of accurately reconstructing structured 3D models from complex scenes. The proposed method leverages distance fields to represent geometric relationships between objects, enabling precise identification of edges, junctions, and connectivity in 3D space. A hybrid algorithm combining distance field computation with parametric optimization is introduced to extract wireframe structures efficiently. Experimental results demonstrate the method's robustness in handling noisy and incomplete data, outperforming traditional edge-detection and reconstruction techniques in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The approach achieves significant improvements in preserving topological consistency and geometric fidelity, making it suitable for applications in computer-aided design (CAD), robotics, and augmented reality. This work contributes a scalable and adaptable framework for 3D wireframe extraction, offering potential advancements in automated 3D modeling and scene understanding.  

**Keywords**: 3D reconstruction, wireframe extraction, distance fields, parametric optimization, geometric modeling, CAD.
