This study aims to develop a reliable approach for uncertainty estimation in cancer survival prediction, addressing the critical need for accurate and informative predictions in clinical practice. We propose a novel methodology that integrates deep learning techniques with Bayesian inference to quantify uncertainty in survival forecasts. Our approach utilizes a probabilistic framework to model the underlying distributions of survival times, enabling the estimation of uncertainty intervals for individual patients. The results show that our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art models in terms of predictive accuracy and uncertainty calibration, with significant improvements in identifying high-risk patients. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of uncertainty estimation in cancer survival prediction, enabling clinicians to make more informed decisions and providing patients with a more accurate understanding of their prognosis. This study contributes to the advancement of personalized medicine and has potential applications in clinical decision support systems, with relevant keywords including cancer survival prediction, uncertainty estimation, Bayesian deep learning, and clinical decision support.
