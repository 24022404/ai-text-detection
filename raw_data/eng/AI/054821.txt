This paper explores the application of You Only Look Once (YOLO) object detection algorithms in monitoring and analyzing marine life. The objective is to develop an efficient and accurate system for detecting and classifying marine species in underwater images and videos. Our approach utilizes a deep learning-based YOLO model, fine-tuned for marine life detection, and evaluates its performance on a dataset of underwater images. The results show significant improvements in detection accuracy and speed compared to traditional computer vision methods. Key findings include the successful detection of various marine species, such as fish, coral, and seaweed, with high precision and recall rates. The conclusion highlights the potential of YOLO-based systems for marine conservation and research, enabling automated monitoring and analysis of marine ecosystems. This research contributes to the development of innovative AI-powered tools for marine life studies, with implications for environmental monitoring, species conservation, and sustainable marine resource management. Keywords: YOLO, object detection, marine life, deep learning, underwater imaging, conservation.
