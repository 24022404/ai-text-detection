This paper addresses the challenging task of tracking individuals in crowded environments, where traditional tracking methods often fail due to occlusions and similarities in appearance. Our objective is to develop a robust tracking system that analyzes crowd dynamics based on physical characteristics, such as height, build, and gait. We propose a novel approach that combines computer vision and machine learning techniques to detect and track individuals in crowded scenes. Our method utilizes a deep learning-based framework to extract features from video footage and predict the trajectories of individuals. The results show that our system outperforms existing tracking methods in terms of accuracy and robustness, especially in scenarios with high crowd densities. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of considering physical characteristics in crowd tracking and demonstrate the potential of our approach for various applications, including surveillance, crowd management, and public safety. The contributions of this work include a new perspective on crowd tracking, an innovative application of machine learning, and a comprehensive evaluation of the proposed system. Relevant keywords: crowd tracking, computer vision, machine learning, physical characteristics, surveillance, crowd management.
