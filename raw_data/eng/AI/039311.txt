Deep metric learning aims to learn embedding spaces where similar instances are close and dissimilar instances are far apart. While effective, these models often operate as black boxes, hindering trust and interpretability. This paper introduces a novel approach for generating visual explanations for deep metric learning models. Our method leverages gradient-based techniques to identify image regions most influential in determining the similarity between two instances. Specifically, we propose a modified gradient-weighted class activation mapping (Grad-CAM) technique tailored for metric learning that highlights relevant features used for distance calculation in the embedding space. We evaluate our approach on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its ability to generate visually coherent and semantically meaningful explanations. Furthermore, we show that these explanations can be used to identify biases in the training data and improve model robustness. The generated visual explanations enhance the transparency of deep metric learning, facilitating model debugging, trust building, and identification of dataset biases. Keywords: Deep Metric Learning, Visual Explanation, Interpretability, Grad-CAM, Explainable AI.
