This paper addresses the challenge of point cloud completion, a crucial task in 3D reconstruction and computer vision. Our objective is to develop an effective method for completing incomplete point clouds, which is essential for various applications such as robotics, autonomous driving, and virtual reality. To achieve this, we propose a novel Skip-attention Network with Hierarchical Folding, a deep learning-based approach that leverages attention mechanisms and hierarchical folding techniques to capture complex geometric structures. Our method learns to complete missing regions by attending to relevant parts of the input point cloud and folding the completed regions to match the original geometry. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of completion accuracy and efficiency. The key findings of this research include the effectiveness of skip-attention mechanisms in capturing long-range dependencies and the importance of hierarchical folding in preserving geometric details. Our contributions have significant implications for 3D reconstruction, robotics, and computer vision, enabling more accurate and efficient point cloud completion. Key keywords: point cloud completion, skip-attention network, hierarchical folding, 3D reconstruction, deep learning.
