Power distribution systems face growing operational challenges as they integrate increasing amounts of distributed energy resources (DERs) and renewable generation. Effective Volt-Var Control (VVC) is essential for maintaining voltage stability, improving power quality, and minimizing losses. This paper introduces PowerGym, a novel reinforcement learning (RL) environment specifically designed for the research and development of VVC strategies in power distribution networks. Built with extensibility and realism in mind, PowerGym provides a modular simulation platform featuring detailed modeling of power flows, real-world grid configurations, and diverse control actions including on-load tap changers, capacitor banks, and distributed generation inverters. The environment supports customizable reward functions, scalable observation spaces, and seamless integration with popular RL libraries, enabling robust benchmarking and comparison of various RL algorithms. Experiments conducted using PowerGym demonstrate the effectiveness of deep reinforcement learning methods, such as Deep Q-Networks and Proximal Policy Optimization, in autonomously adapting to evolving grid conditions and outperforming traditional rule-based VVC schemes. Our results highlight improved voltage profiles, reduced power losses, and enhanced adaptability in the presence of uncertainty and fluctuating demand. PowerGym lowers the entry barrier for researchers and engineers seeking to develop, test, and deploy intelligent control strategies, and lays the foundation for further advancements in smart grid automation. Keywords: reinforcement learning, Volt-Var Control, power distribution, smart grid, deep RL, simulation environment, DER integration.
