This paper addresses the problem of selecting the most informative Gaussian experts in a mixture of experts model. The objective is to develop an efficient method for identifying the optimal subset of experts that best capture the underlying patterns in the data. We propose a novel approach based on graphical models, which enables the representation of complex relationships between experts and data. Our method utilizes a probabilistic framework to model the interactions between experts, allowing for the selection of the most relevant experts. The results show that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency. Key findings include improved expert selection, enhanced model interpretability, and increased robustness to noise. The proposed method has significant implications for applications in machine learning, data mining, and artificial intelligence, particularly in scenarios where expert selection is crucial. Our contributions include the development of a graphical model-based framework for expert selection, which can be applied to various domains, including regression, classification, and clustering tasks. Relevant keywords: Gaussian experts, graphical models, expert selection, mixture of experts, probabilistic modeling.
