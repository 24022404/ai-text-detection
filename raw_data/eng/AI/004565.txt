This paper addresses the limitations of traditional reinforcement learning methods by introducing a novel algorithm, Greedy-GQ with Variance Reduction, aimed at improving the efficiency and effectiveness of policy evaluation in reinforcement learning environments.

Methods/Approach: The proposed method leverages variance reduction techniques to enhance the Greedy-GQ algorithm, a widely recognized off-policy temporal-difference learning approach. The variance reduction is achieved through a carefully designed update rule that balances between bias and variance, leading to more stable and efficient learning. The finite-time analysis of the algorithm provides theoretical guarantees for the convergence and performance metrics, offering insights into its operational complexity.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that the Greedy-GQ with Variance Reduction significantly outperforms traditional Greedy-GQ and other baseline methods in terms of convergence speed and accuracy. The variance reduction strategy effectively reduces the complexity of the learning process, yielding faster convergence in finite-time scenarios across various reinforcement learning tasks.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes to the field by offering an advanced reinforcement learning approach that combines variance reduction techniques with established algorithms, resulting in improved learning performance and reduced computational demands. The Greedy-GQ with Variance Reduction is poised to offer practical benefits in real-world applications where efficient policy evaluation and decision-making are critical.

Keywords: Greedy-GQ, variance reduction, finite-time analysis, reinforcement learning, policy evaluation, algorithm complexity.
