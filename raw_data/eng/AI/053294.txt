Facial image manipulation has gained significant attention in recent years due to its applications in entertainment, virtual reality, and digital content creation. However, existing methods often struggle to achieve both diversity and interactivity in generated results. This paper introduces **MaskGAN**, a novel framework designed to address these challenges by enabling diverse and interactive facial image manipulation. The proposed approach leverages a conditional generative adversarial network (cGAN) enhanced with a mask-based control mechanism, allowing users to specify precise regions for manipulation while maintaining high-quality outputs. MaskGAN incorporates a multi-modal latent space to ensure diverse results, enabling the generation of multiple plausible variations for a given input. Experimental results demonstrate that MaskGAN outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of visual quality, diversity, and user control. The framework achieves a 15% improvement in perceptual quality metrics and supports real-time interaction, making it suitable for practical applications. Key contributions include the integration of mask-based guidance into the generative process and the development of a user-friendly interface for interactive editing. MaskGAN opens new possibilities for creative facial image manipulation, with potential applications in digital art, augmented reality, and personalized content generation.  

**Keywords**: Facial image manipulation, generative adversarial networks (GANs), interactive editing, mask-based control, multi-modal generation, digital content creation.
