Entity Resolution (ER) aims to identify and link records referring to the same real-world entity across different data sources. Active learning can reduce the labeling effort required for ER, but existing approaches often incur high computational costs due to the complexity of variational inference. This paper introduces a cost-effective variational active entity resolution framework that balances accuracy and computational efficiency. We propose a novel variational inference method that leverages a combination of amortized inference and targeted sampling to approximate the posterior distribution over entity matchings. This approach significantly reduces the computational burden associated with variational inference while maintaining competitive ER performance. We evaluate our framework on several real-world datasets, demonstrating its superiority in terms of F1-score and labeling cost compared to state-of-the-art active ER methods. The results highlight the potential of our approach for scaling ER to large and complex datasets with limited labeling resources. Keywords: Entity Resolution, Active Learning, Variational Inference, Cost-Effectiveness, Data Integration.
