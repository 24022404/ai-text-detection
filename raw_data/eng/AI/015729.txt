Scene text recognition often involves linking individual character predictions to form complete words, a task where traditional methods struggle with irregular layouts and complex backgrounds. This paper introduces NENET, a novel Edge Learnable Network designed specifically for robust link prediction in scene text. NENET employs a graph-based approach, representing characters as nodes and potential links between them as edges. The network utilizes a custom graph neural network architecture, enabling edges to learn and adapt based on contextual information extracted from both character features and their spatial relationships. This allows NENET to dynamically model the complex dependencies crucial for accurate link prediction. Experimental results on challenging scene text datasets demonstrate that NENET significantly outperforms existing link prediction methods, particularly in scenarios with irregular text and noisy backgrounds, achieving a 15% improvement in F1-score on average. The proposed approach offers a more flexible and accurate solution for scene text recognition by enabling edges to learn, contributing to more robust and reliable text extraction in real-world images. Keywords: Scene Text Recognition, Link Prediction, Graph Neural Network, Edge Learning, Computer Vision.
