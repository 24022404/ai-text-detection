Boosting is a powerful ensemble learning technique widely used in classification tasks, but its probabilistic outputs are often poorly calibrated, limiting its reliability in decision-making scenarios. This paper addresses the challenge of obtaining well-calibrated probabilities from boosting models. We propose a novel post-processing method that leverages temperature scaling and isotonic regression to refine the probability estimates generated by boosting algorithms. Our approach is evaluated on multiple benchmark datasets, comparing its performance against existing calibration techniques such as Platt scaling and histogram binning. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method significantly improves calibration accuracy while maintaining competitive classification performance. The findings highlight the importance of calibrated probabilities in real-world applications, such as medical diagnosis and risk assessment, where accurate uncertainty quantification is critical. This work contributes a practical and effective solution for enhancing the reliability of boosting models, making them more suitable for probabilistic decision-making tasks.  

**Keywords**: boosting, probability calibration, temperature scaling, isotonic regression, ensemble learning, classification.
