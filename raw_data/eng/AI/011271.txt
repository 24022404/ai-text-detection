This paper introduces Pareto GAN, a novel approach to extending the representational power of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to model heavy-tailed distributions. The objective is to address the limitations of traditional GANs in capturing complex, real-world data distributions that exhibit heavy tails. Our method leverages the properties of Pareto distributions to design a new generator architecture and loss function, enabling the effective modeling of such distributions. The Pareto GAN is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its ability to generate high-quality samples that better match the target distributions compared to existing GAN variants. Results show significant improvements in terms of distribution coverage and sample diversity. The proposed approach contributes to the advancement of GAN research, offering a powerful tool for modeling complex data distributions in various applications, including image and data generation, anomaly detection, and uncertainty estimation. Key contributions include the introduction of a novel generator architecture, a tailored loss function, and the demonstration of improved performance on heavy-tailed distributions, highlighting the potential of Pareto GAN in AI, machine learning, and data modeling.
