This paper addresses the challenge of improving policies in complex environments while ensuring safety and efficiency. The objective is to develop a novel approach that enables fast and safe policy improvement by promoting diverse exploration. We propose a method that integrates diverse exploration strategies with policy improvement techniques, allowing for the discovery of new and better policies while minimizing the risk of unsafe actions. Our approach utilizes a combination of reinforcement learning and probabilistic modeling to encourage exploration of under-explored regions of the state space. The results show that our method achieves significant improvements in policy performance while maintaining safety constraints, outperforming existing methods in several benchmark environments. The key contributions of this research include the development of a diverse exploration framework, the integration of safety constraints into policy improvement, and the demonstration of the approach's effectiveness in complex domains. This work has important implications for applications such as robotics, autonomous driving, and healthcare, where safe and efficient policy improvement is crucial. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, policy improvement, diverse exploration, safety constraints, probabilistic modeling.
