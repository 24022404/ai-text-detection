The increasing reliance on black-box machine learning models in critical applications has raised concerns about their robustness and vulnerability to adversarial attacks. This paper introduces *Patch Shortcuts*, an interpretable proxy model framework designed to efficiently identify vulnerabilities in black-box systems. The proposed approach leverages lightweight, interpretable models to approximate the decision boundaries of complex black-box models, enabling the discovery of adversarial examples and weaknesses without requiring direct access to the internal architecture or parameters. By employing a novel combination of gradient-free optimization and feature importance analysis, Patch Shortcuts significantly reduces computational overhead compared to traditional adversarial attack methods. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the framework achieves comparable or superior vulnerability detection rates while being up to 40% faster than state-of-the-art techniques. Furthermore, the interpretability of the proxy models provides actionable insights into the root causes of vulnerabilities, facilitating targeted improvements in model robustness. This research contributes a practical, scalable solution for assessing and enhancing the security of black-box systems, with potential applications in cybersecurity, autonomous systems, and AI-driven decision-making.  

**Keywords**: black-box models, adversarial attacks, interpretability, proxy models, vulnerability detection, robustness, machine learning security.
