This paper introduces a novel approach to reinforcement learning by incorporating relevance-guided modeling of object dynamics. Current reinforcement learning methods often struggle with complex environments involving numerous objects and intricate interactions. To address this limitation, we propose a framework that selectively models the dynamics of objects based on their relevance to the task at hand. This is achieved through an attention mechanism that dynamically identifies and prioritizes important objects, enabling the agent to focus on essential interactions and disregard irrelevant distractions. Our method employs a graph neural network to represent the relationships between objects and learn their dynamics. We evaluate our approach on several challenging simulated environments, demonstrating significant improvements in sample efficiency and task performance compared to state-of-the-art reinforcement learning algorithms. The results highlight the effectiveness of relevance-guided modeling in simplifying complex decision-making processes and facilitating efficient learning of object dynamics. This work contributes a practical and effective technique for enhancing reinforcement learning in environments with high object complexity.

**Keywords:** Reinforcement Learning, Object Dynamics, Graph Neural Networks, Relevance Modeling, Attention Mechanism, Sample Efficiency.
