Salient object detection (SOD) plays a critical role in computer vision applications, enabling systems to identify and prioritize the most visually distinctive regions in an image. Despite significant advancements, existing methods often struggle with complex scenes, varying object scales, and overlapping objects. This paper introduces *DeepSaliency*, a novel multi-task deep neural network model designed to address these challenges. The proposed model integrates a unified architecture that simultaneously learns global context, local details, and boundary information through parallel task-specific branches. By leveraging a combination of attention mechanisms and feature fusion techniques, *DeepSaliency* enhances the model's ability to capture salient objects with high precision. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets, including DUTS, ECSSD, and HKU-IS, demonstrate that *DeepSaliency* outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy, robustness, and computational efficiency. The model achieves an average F-measure improvement of 3.2% compared to existing approaches. Furthermore, ablation studies validate the effectiveness of the multi-task learning framework and the proposed attention modules. The results highlight the potential of *DeepSaliency* for real-world applications such as image segmentation, video surveillance, and autonomous navigation. This research contributes a scalable and efficient solution to salient object detection, paving the way for future advancements in visual perception systems.  

**Keywords**: Salient object detection, deep learning, multi-task learning, attention mechanisms, feature fusion, computer vision.
