This paper introduces a novel representation learning framework, Adversarially-Contrastive Optimal Transport (ACOT), designed to learn robust and discriminative feature representations. ACOT leverages optimal transport (OT) to align feature distributions across different views of the same data while simultaneously employing an adversarial objective to enforce feature invariance and robustness. Specifically, the framework minimizes the OT cost between representations of original data and adversarially perturbed versions, encouraging the model to learn features insensitive to noise. Contrastive learning is integrated to further enhance the discriminative power of the learned representations by attracting similar instances and repelling dissimilar ones. We demonstrate the effectiveness of ACOT on several benchmark datasets, including image classification and domain adaptation tasks. Experimental results show that ACOT consistently outperforms state-of-the-art representation learning methods, achieving significant improvements in classification accuracy and robustness. The learned representations exhibit superior generalization capabilities, making ACOT a promising approach for various downstream tasks. Key words: Representation Learning, Optimal Transport, Adversarial Learning, Contrastive Learning, Feature Invariance.
