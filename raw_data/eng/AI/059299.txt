Accurate traffic speed prediction is critical for intelligent transportation systems, yet remains challenging due to the complex spatio-temporal patterns and varying observation frequencies across different road segments. This paper presents a novel Multi-fold Correlation Attention Network (MCAN) designed to address the problem of predicting traffic speeds when input data arrives with heterogeneous frequencies. The proposed method integrates a multi-level attention mechanism that captures intricate spatial dependencies between road segments, while simultaneously modeling temporal correlations at both shared and segment-specific frequency levels. By employing a hierarchical feature extraction framework, MCAN effectively learns from both high-frequency and low-frequency data streams, mitigating issues arising from data imbalance and missing observations. Experiments conducted on real-world traffic datasets demonstrate that MCAN consistently outperforms existing deep learning methods in terms of prediction accuracy, particularly under non-uniform data sampling scenarios. The results highlight the model’s robustness and adaptability to practical deployment in urban environments with diverse sensor infrastructure. This research contributes a flexible and scalable solution for traffic speed forecasting, paving the way for improved urban mobility and traffic management. Keywords: traffic speed prediction, heterogeneous frequency, attention network, spatio-temporal modeling, intelligent transportation systems.
