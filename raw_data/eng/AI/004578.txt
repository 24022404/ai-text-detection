This paper introduces Luna, a novel Linear Unified Nested Attention mechanism designed to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of attention-based neural networks. The objective of this research is to address the limitations of existing attention models, which often suffer from high computational complexity and limited scalability. To achieve this, we propose a unified framework that integrates linear attention and nested attention, enabling the model to capture both local and global dependencies in input data. Our approach leverages a combination of linear transformations and nested attention layers to reduce computational costs while preserving the expressive power of traditional attention mechanisms. Experimental results demonstrate that Luna outperforms state-of-the-art attention models on several benchmark tasks, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency. The proposed mechanism has important implications for a wide range of applications, including natural language processing, computer vision, and speech recognition. Key contributions of this work include the introduction of a linear unified nested attention framework, which enables efficient and effective attention-based processing, and the demonstration of its potential to advance the field of artificial intelligence. Relevant keywords: attention mechanisms, linear attention, nested attention, neural networks, artificial intelligence, deep learning.
