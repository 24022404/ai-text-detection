The Ticket List View serves as a comprehensive display for all tickets generated by the customer, providing a clear summary that includes essential details such as ticket ID, subject, status, priority, and assignee. This view not only allows users to monitor multiple tickets at a glance, but also offers customization and filtering options to help users quickly locate the information they need. Each ticket can be further explored through the Ticket Details Form, which reveals in-depth information like ticket name, the person assigned, supporting team, creation date, current stage, and a detailed description. There is also a chatter feature embedded for real-time interaction and collaboration among users. The ticket creation form on the portal is structured to gather all the necessary information from the customer, such as their name, email, phone number, job, subject of the ticket, project, product, priority, and a detailed description or question.
