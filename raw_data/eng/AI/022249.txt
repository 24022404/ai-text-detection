The design of Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) involves complex trade-offs between performance, energy efficiency, reliability, and cost, necessitating systematic exploration of the design space. This paper introduces DISPATCH, a novel framework for automated design space exploration of CPS. DISPATCH leverages a multi-objective optimization approach combined with a modular simulation environment to evaluate and optimize system configurations across diverse hardware and software parameters. The framework integrates advanced algorithms, including evolutionary computation and machine learning, to efficiently navigate the vast design space and identify Pareto-optimal solutions. Experimental results demonstrate DISPATCH's ability to reduce design exploration time by up to 40% compared to traditional methods while achieving superior system performance metrics. Key contributions include a scalable architecture for CPS design, a robust optimization engine, and a user-friendly interface for domain experts. The implications of this work extend to applications in autonomous systems, IoT, and industrial automation, where optimized CPS designs are critical for real-world deployment. Keywords: Cyber-Physical Systems, Design Space Exploration, Multi-objective Optimization, Evolutionary Computation, Machine Learning, IoT.
