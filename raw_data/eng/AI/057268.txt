Meta-reinforcement learning (meta-RL) has emerged as a promising approach for enabling agents to adapt quickly to new tasks by leveraging prior experience. However, the lack of structured task distributions often limits the generalization and scalability of meta-RL algorithms. This paper introduces *Alchemy*, a novel structured task distribution framework designed to enhance the performance and adaptability of meta-RL systems. The framework organizes tasks into a hierarchical structure, enabling agents to learn reusable skills and transfer knowledge across related tasks efficiently. We propose a meta-RL algorithm tailored to *Alchemy*, incorporating multi-task learning and hierarchical policy optimization to improve task-solving efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate that *Alchemy* significantly outperforms existing meta-RL benchmarks in terms of task adaptation speed, generalization to unseen tasks, and overall performance. The framework also exhibits robustness across diverse environments, making it a valuable tool for real-world applications such as robotics, autonomous systems, and adaptive decision-making. This research contributes to the advancement of meta-RL by providing a structured and scalable approach to task distribution, paving the way for more efficient and generalizable reinforcement learning systems.  

**Keywords**: Meta-reinforcement learning, task distribution, hierarchical learning, multi-task learning, generalization, adaptive systems.
