The increasing complexity of time series data in cloud computing environments necessitates efficient and scalable prediction algorithms to support real-time decision-making. This paper presents a novel periodicity-based parallel time series prediction algorithm designed to address the challenges of handling large-scale, high-frequency data streams in distributed systems. The proposed approach leverages periodicity detection techniques to identify recurring patterns in time series data, enabling more accurate and efficient predictions. By integrating parallel processing frameworks, the algorithm optimizes computational resources and reduces prediction latency, making it suitable for cloud-based applications. Experimental evaluations conducted on real-world datasets demonstrate that the algorithm outperforms existing methods in terms of prediction accuracy and scalability. The results highlight its ability to handle dynamic workloads and adapt to varying data characteristics, making it a robust solution for time series forecasting in cloud environments. This research contributes to the advancement of scalable data analytics by providing a practical and innovative approach to time series prediction, with potential applications in areas such as resource allocation, anomaly detection, and predictive maintenance.  

**Keywords**: time series prediction, periodicity detection, parallel processing, cloud computing, scalability, distributed systems.
