This paper presents a novel approach for the detection of local defects using a blockwise-based methodology. The objective is to develop an efficient and accurate system for identifying defects in various materials and structures. Our approach employs a divide-and-conquer strategy, dividing the inspection area into smaller blocks and analyzing each block individually to detect local defects. We utilize a combination of image processing techniques and machine learning algorithms to classify blocks as defective or non-defective. The results show that our blockwise-based detection method outperforms traditional defect detection methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The proposed system has significant implications for quality control and inspection applications, enabling the rapid and reliable detection of local defects in a wide range of industries. Key contributions of this research include the development of a blockwise-based detection framework, the integration of image processing and machine learning techniques, and the demonstration of improved defect detection performance. Relevant keywords: defect detection, blockwise analysis, image processing, machine learning, quality control, inspection systems.
