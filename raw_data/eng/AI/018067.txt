Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) have emerged as a powerful framework for generating realistic data across various domains, including image synthesis, video generation, and data augmentation. This study conducts a comprehensive comparative analysis of state-of-the-art GAN architectures, focusing on their performance, stability, and applicability in real-world scenarios. We evaluate popular GAN variants, such as DCGAN, WGAN, and StyleGAN, using benchmark datasets like CIFAR-10 and CelebA. Our methodology involves quantitative metrics, including Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) and Inception Score (IS), alongside qualitative assessments of generated outputs. The results reveal significant differences in training stability, convergence speed, and output quality among the models. Notably, StyleGAN demonstrates superior performance in generating high-resolution images, while WGAN exhibits improved training stability. This research provides valuable insights into selecting appropriate GAN architectures for specific applications and highlights potential areas for future optimization. The findings contribute to advancing the field of generative modeling and offer practical guidance for researchers and practitioners leveraging GANs in AI-driven solutions.  

**Keywords**: Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), DCGAN, WGAN, StyleGAN, image synthesis, Fréchet Inception Distance (FID), Inception Score (IS).
