This paper addresses the challenge of extracting meaningful representations from complex time series data by introducing a novel approach to learn low-dimensional state embeddings and identify metastable clusters. Our objective is to develop a method that can effectively capture the underlying dynamics of high-dimensional systems and reveal their intrinsic patterns. We employ a combination of dimensionality reduction techniques and clustering algorithms to analyze time series data, leveraging advances in machine learning and data analysis. Our approach yields key findings, including the ability to identify metastable states and transitions between them, which are crucial for understanding complex systems. The results demonstrate improved performance compared to existing methods, particularly in terms of clustering quality and robustness to noise. The implications of this research are significant, enabling the application of our method to a wide range of fields, from physics and biology to finance and social sciences, where time series data analysis is critical. By providing a powerful tool for uncovering hidden patterns and structures in complex data, our work contributes to the advancement of data-driven research and discovery, with key keywords including time series analysis, dimensionality reduction, clustering, metastable states, and machine learning.
