Conformal prediction (CP) provides distribution-free prediction sets with guaranteed coverage, but often requires a substantial amount of data. This paper introduces a novel few-shot conformal prediction framework that leverages auxiliary tasks to improve prediction set efficiency when data is scarce. Our approach utilizes a meta-learning strategy to learn representations that are transferable across related tasks, enabling faster adaptation to new, unseen tasks with limited data. We develop a specific algorithm that combines meta-learned features with a nonconformity measure tailored for few-shot scenarios. Experimental results on image classification and regression benchmarks demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms traditional CP and existing few-shot learning techniques in terms of prediction set size while maintaining the desired coverage level. The proposed framework offers a practical solution for reliable uncertainty quantification in data-limited settings, with potential applications in areas such as medical diagnosis and robotics. Keywords: Conformal Prediction, Few-Shot Learning, Meta-Learning, Uncertainty Quantification, Auxiliary Tasks.
