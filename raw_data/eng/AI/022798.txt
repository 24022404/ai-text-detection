The accurate and real-time segmentation of robotic surgical instruments is critical for enhancing the precision and safety of minimally invasive surgeries. This paper presents an attention-guided lightweight network designed to address the challenges of computational efficiency and segmentation accuracy in real-time surgical environments. The proposed model integrates a novel attention mechanism with a streamlined convolutional architecture, enabling the network to focus on instrument-specific features while maintaining low computational overhead. We evaluate the model on a publicly available dataset of robotic surgical scenes, comparing its performance against state-of-the-art segmentation methods. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach achieves superior segmentation accuracy with significantly reduced inference time, making it suitable for real-time applications. The network’s lightweight design ensures compatibility with resource-constrained surgical systems, while the attention mechanism enhances robustness to varying lighting conditions and instrument occlusions. This research contributes a practical and efficient solution for real-time surgical instrument segmentation, with potential applications in robotic-assisted surgery and computer-aided medical interventions.  

**Keywords**: robotic surgery, real-time segmentation, attention mechanism, lightweight network, surgical instruments, computer vision.
