This survey explores the interpretability of artificial neural networks (ANNs), a pressing challenge in the field of artificial intelligence (AI), where understanding and trusting these models are critical yet complex issues. The objective of this paper is to provide a comprehensive overview of current interpretability methods and their implications in enhancing the transparency and trustworthiness of ANNs. We systematically review a variety of approaches, including feature attribution techniques, model simplifications, and visualization methods, highlighting their mechanisms, strengths, and limitations. Through comparative analysis, we identify key trends and emerging practices across different application domains, such as image recognition, natural language processing, and autonomous systems, illustrating the trade-offs between model accuracy and interpretability. Our findings reveal that while significant progress has been made, challenges remain, particularly in balancing model performance with comprehensibility. We conclude with a discussion on future directions for research in this burgeoning field, suggesting strategies for developing inherently interpretable models and enhancing existing techniques to cater to diverse industrial applications. This paper aims to serve as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners seeking to navigate the complex landscape of ANN interpretability, fostering further innovation and adoption of transparent AI systems. Keywords: Neural Networks, Interpretability, Machine Learning, Feature Attribution, AI Transparency.
