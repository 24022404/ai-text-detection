Semantic image synthesis aims to generate realistic images from semantic layouts. While existing methods have shown promise, they often struggle with generating high-quality images, particularly in complex scenes, due to the limitations in effectively incorporating semantic information into the generative process. This paper introduces a novel class-adaptive normalization technique to enhance the efficiency and fidelity of semantic image synthesis. Our approach dynamically modulates the normalization parameters based on the semantic class of each region, allowing the generator to adapt its style and texture generation accordingly. We integrate this class-adaptive normalization into a generative adversarial network (GAN) framework. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including Cityscapes and ADE20K, demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of visual quality and semantic accuracy. Furthermore, our model exhibits improved training stability and convergence speed compared to existing normalization techniques. The proposed method offers a practical solution for high-resolution semantic image synthesis, enabling more realistic and controllable image generation. Keywords: Semantic Image Synthesis, GAN, Normalization, Image Generation, Deep Learning.
