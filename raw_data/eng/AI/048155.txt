Single-stage object detectors offer efficient performance but often struggle with feature discrimination, especially for complex scenes. This paper introduces HAR-Net, a novel single-stage object detection framework designed to enhance feature representation through joint learning of hybrid attention mechanisms. HAR-Net incorporates both channel and spatial attention modules, adaptively weighting feature maps and spatial locations to emphasize salient object features while suppressing background noise. A key innovation is the joint learning strategy, where channel and spatial attention are trained in a mutually beneficial manner, allowing them to complement each other and capture richer contextual information. Experimental results on the MS COCO dataset demonstrate that HAR-Net achieves state-of-the-art performance compared to existing single-stage detectors, with a significant improvement in Average Precision (AP). The proposed hybrid attention mechanism effectively improves feature discrimination, leading to more accurate object localization and classification. HAR-Net provides a promising direction for developing high-performance single-stage detectors.

**Keywords:** Object Detection, Single-Stage Detector, Attention Mechanism, Channel Attention, Spatial Attention, Deep Learning, Computer Vision.
