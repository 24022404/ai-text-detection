Reinforcement Learning (RL) has shown remarkable success in various domains, but ensuring safety and efficiency during policy improvement remains a critical challenge. This paper addresses the problem of constrained policy optimization in RL, where the goal is to maximize performance while adhering to safety constraints. We propose a novel constrained policy improvement framework that integrates safety constraints directly into the policy update process, ensuring that the learned policies are both effective and safe. Our approach leverages a constrained optimization formulation, combined with a trust-region method to balance exploration and exploitation. We evaluate the proposed framework on a range of benchmark environments, including robotic control and autonomous navigation tasks. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves superior performance compared to existing constrained RL algorithms, with significant improvements in constraint satisfaction and sample efficiency. The findings highlight the potential of our approach for real-world applications where safety and efficiency are paramount. This work contributes to the growing body of research on safe RL by providing a robust and scalable solution for constrained policy optimization.  

**Keywords**: Reinforcement Learning, Constrained Optimization, Safe RL, Policy Improvement, Trust-Region Methods, Autonomous Systems.
