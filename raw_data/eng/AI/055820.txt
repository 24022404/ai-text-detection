This paper introduces U-GAT-IT, a novel unsupervised generative attentional network designed for image-to-image translation tasks. The objective is to develop a framework that can effectively translate images from one domain to another without requiring paired training data. U-GAT-IT employs a combination of generative adversarial networks (GANs) and attention mechanisms, along with adaptive layer-instance normalization, to achieve high-quality image translations. The approach leverages the power of self-attention to focus on relevant regions of the input image, while the adaptive normalization technique enables the model to better handle diverse image styles. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of U-GAT-IT over existing methods, with significant improvements in terms of visual quality and diversity. The proposed model has numerous applications in image synthesis, editing, and manipulation, and contributes to the advancement of unsupervised image-to-image translation techniques. Key contributions include the introduction of adaptive layer-instance normalization and the integration of attention mechanisms, which enhance the model's ability to capture complex image relationships. Relevant keywords: image-to-image translation, unsupervised learning, generative adversarial networks, attention mechanisms, adaptive normalization.
