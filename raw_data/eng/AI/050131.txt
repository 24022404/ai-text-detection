This paper introduces Bayesian Attention Belief Networks, a novel probabilistic framework that integrates attention mechanisms with Bayesian belief networks to enhance uncertainty estimation and reasoning in complex systems. The objective is to develop a more robust and interpretable model for tasks that require selective focus and probabilistic inference. Our approach combines the strengths of Bayesian networks and attention-based neural networks, allowing for flexible and efficient modeling of uncertain relationships between variables. The results show that our model outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy and calibration, particularly in scenarios with limited data or high uncertainty. Key findings include improved handling of ambiguous or missing information, as well as enhanced ability to identify relevant factors and relationships. The Bayesian Attention Belief Network has significant implications for applications in decision-making, risk analysis, and artificial intelligence, offering a powerful tool for modeling and reasoning under uncertainty. Keywords: Bayesian inference, attention mechanisms, belief networks, uncertainty estimation, probabilistic modeling, artificial intelligence.
