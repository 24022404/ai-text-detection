Within the system, a diverse range of specialized services work together to deliver a seamless user experience. The Auth service secures user registration and authentication, ensuring that only authorized individuals can access the platform, while the User service manages personal information and follow relationships. Post and Comment services facilitate content creation and community interaction, allowing users to share updates and engage in discussion threads; the Like service supports reactions on both posts and comments. Destination and Hotel Services enrich the platform with travel and hospitality features tailored for tourists. The Feed service aggregates and curates content relevant to each user. Media connects to a CDN for reliable storage and delivery of images or videos, as managed by Cloudinary. Search delivers precise, relevant results, and Chat enables private user messaging. To support performance and communication, Docker orchestrates these services, Redis offers efficient caching, and Kafka handles asynchronous event streaming and inter-service communication.
