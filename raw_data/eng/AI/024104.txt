This paper introduces PSViT, a novel vision transformer architecture that enhances the efficiency and effectiveness of visual processing. The primary objective is to address the limitations of existing vision transformers by incorporating token pooling and attention sharing mechanisms. Our approach involves a multi-stage token pooling strategy to reduce spatial redundancy and an attention sharing technique to promote feature reuse. Experimental results demonstrate that PSViT outperforms state-of-the-art vision transformers on various benchmarks, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and computational efficiency. The key findings highlight the benefits of token pooling and attention sharing in reducing the complexity of vision transformers while preserving their representational power. Our research contributes to the advancement of vision transformer architectures, offering a more efficient and scalable solution for computer vision tasks. The proposed PSViT model has potential applications in image classification, object detection, and segmentation, and paves the way for further research in vision transformer design and optimization, with relevant keywords including vision transformers, token pooling, attention sharing, and computer vision.
