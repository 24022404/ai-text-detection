This paper introduces a novel approach to enhance the transparency and efficacy of reinforcement learning (RL) agents using a deep learning framework. The objective is to develop a more interpretable and controllable RL system by integrating an action-conditional β-VAE (Beta Variational Autoencoder) model. This approach enables the agent to learn a disentangled and structured representation of the environment, conditioned on the actions taken. The proposed method combines the benefits of variational autoencoders and reinforcement learning to provide a transparent and explainable decision-making process. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of the action-conditional β-VAE in improving the agent's efficacy and transparency, outperforming traditional RL methods in complex environments. The key contributions of this research include the development of a novel deep RL framework, enhanced transparency and interpretability of agent behavior, and improved performance in challenging tasks. This work has significant implications for the development of more reliable and trustworthy autonomous systems, with potential applications in robotics, game playing, and other areas of artificial intelligence. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, β-VAE, deep learning, transparency, interpretability, autonomous systems.
