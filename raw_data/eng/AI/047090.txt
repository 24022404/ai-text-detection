Microaneurysm detection is a critical step in the early diagnosis of diabetic retinopathy, yet remains challenging due to the small size and low contrast of lesions in retinal images. This paper presents an improved approach for microaneurysm detection utilizing deep neural networks (DNNs). The proposed method leverages a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture enhanced with spatial attention mechanisms to better distinguish microaneurysms from background noise and other retinal features. A large annotated dataset of retinal fundus images was used to train and validate the model. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed system significantly outperforms conventional machine learning and baseline deep learning methods, achieving higher accuracy, sensitivity, and specificity. Ablation studies confirm the positive impact of the attention modules and optimized preprocessing on detection performance. These findings suggest that the integration of advanced deep learning techniques can substantially enhance the reliability of microaneurysm detection, contributing to improved automated screening and early intervention strategies in diabetic retinopathy. Key keywords include microaneurysm detection, deep neural networks, convolutional neural networks, retinal imaging, diabetic retinopathy, and attention mechanisms.
