Wasserstein distributionally robust optimization (WDRO) has emerged as a powerful framework for decision-making under uncertainty, offering robustness against distributional shifts. However, existing methods often struggle with computational efficiency and scalability, particularly when dealing with local perturbations in the data distribution. This paper introduces a principled learning method for WDRO that addresses these challenges by leveraging a novel optimization framework tailored for local perturbations. Our approach combines gradient-based techniques with a carefully designed regularization mechanism to ensure stability and convergence. We evaluate the proposed method on benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in computational efficiency and robustness compared to state-of-the-art WDRO methods. The results highlight the method's ability to handle complex, real-world scenarios with localized distributional changes. This work contributes to the field by providing a scalable and efficient solution for WDRO, with potential applications in finance, healthcare, and machine learning systems operating in dynamic environments.  

**Keywords**: Wasserstein distributionally robust optimization, local perturbations, gradient-based optimization, robustness, machine learning, decision-making under uncertainty.
