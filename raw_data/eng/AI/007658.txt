This paper explores the application of deep reinforcement learning (DRL) in the development of adaptive learning systems aimed at personalizing educational experiences. The primary objective is to create a system that dynamically tailors content and instructional strategies to individual learners, optimizing engagement and knowledge retention. We introduce a novel DRL framework that models the interaction between students and learning materials as a sequential decision-making process, enabling the system to adapt in real-time based on student performance and feedback. The methodology involves implementing a deep Q-network (DQN) to select optimal educational actions, such as recommending exercises or adjusting difficulty levels, by maximizing long-term learning outcomes. Experimental results on simulated and real-world datasets demonstrate substantial improvements in student progress and engagement compared to traditional rule-based and static adaptive systems. The findings highlight the advantages of DRL in capturing complex learner behaviors and supporting scalable, personalized education solutions. This work contributes a flexible and extensible model for next-generation adaptive learning platforms and suggests pathways for future integration of advanced machine learning techniques into educational technology. Keywords: deep reinforcement learning, adaptive learning systems, personalization, educational technology, student modeling, deep Q-network.
