**Abstract**

Reconstructing accurate 3D human shapes from limited input data remains a challenging problem in computer vision, with significant applications in animation, virtual reality, and medical analysis. This paper presents DC-GNet, a novel Deep Mesh Relation Capturing Graph Convolution Network designed specifically for 3D human shape reconstruction. Our approach models the human body as a graph structure, enabling the network to effectively capture complex topological relationships between mesh vertices. DC-GNet employs advanced graph convolutional layers to learn hierarchical features from sparse or noisy input, such as single-view images or partial point clouds. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that DC-GNet outperforms existing methods in terms of reconstruction accuracy, robustness to occlusions, and preservation of fine-grained surface details. Notably, our network achieves superior generalization to various poses and body shapes, addressing the limitations of previous deep learning-based reconstruction techniques. The proposed DC-GNet framework offers substantial improvements for real-time 3D human modeling and can be readily extended to other non-rigid shape reconstruction tasks. Keywords: 3D human shape reconstruction, graph convolutional network, mesh relation, deep learning, computer vision.
