The increasing necessity for real-time tool and equipment detection in various industries has necessitated the development of robust datasets and algorithms capable of operating effectively in diverse environments. This paper introduces ALET (Automated Labeling of Equipment and Tools), a comprehensive dataset explicitly designed to improve the detection of tools and equipment in natural, uncontrolled settings.

Methods/Approach: We developed a novel approach leveraging state-of-the-art machine learning techniques to create a dataset comprised of diverse images depicting tools in various contexts. The dataset encompasses a wide range of equipment categories captured under different lighting and positional conditions to ensure comprehensive training data. Alongside the dataset, we propose a baseline tool detection model utilizing convolutional neural networks (CNN) to evaluate the dataset's effectiveness.

Results/Findings: Our evaluations demonstrate that the ALET dataset significantly enhances tool detection performance over existing benchmarks, with our baseline model achieving superior accuracy in identifying and classifying tools in the wild. The model's capability to generalize across different environments and tool types underscores the dataset's quality and relevance.

Conclusion/Implications: The ALET dataset represents a significant advancement in tool detection technology, offering researchers and practitioners a valuable resource for developing and testing more efficient detection systems. Our findings highlight the potential applications of this dataset in improving automation processes in construction, manufacturing, and other sectors reliant on tool identification and management. The contributions of this paper pave the way for future innovations in automated tool recognition systems.

Key Keywords: automated labeling, tool detection, dataset, convolutional neural networks, real-time detection, equipment identification, machine learning.
