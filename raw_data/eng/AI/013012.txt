Instance segmentation is a critical task in computer vision, requiring precise delineation of object boundaries within complex scenes. This paper introduces a novel loss function, *Contour Loss*, designed to improve the accuracy of instance segmentation by leveraging k-step distance transformation images. The proposed approach addresses the limitations of traditional loss functions, which often struggle with boundary localization and pixel-level precision. The *Contour Loss* integrates distance transformation maps to emphasize boundary pixels, enabling the model to focus on fine-grained details during training. We evaluate the method on benchmark datasets, including COCO and Cityscapes, and demonstrate significant improvements in segmentation accuracy compared to state-of-the-art techniques. Experimental results show that the proposed loss function enhances boundary detection while maintaining robustness across diverse object shapes and scales. The findings highlight the potential of *Contour Loss* for advancing instance segmentation in applications such as autonomous driving, medical imaging, and robotics. This work contributes a new perspective on loss function design, emphasizing the importance of geometric transformations in improving segmentation performance.  

**Keywords**: Instance segmentation, contour loss, distance transformation, boundary detection, computer vision, deep learning.
