This paper investigates the cross-sensor portability of neural network architectures for LiDAR-based semantic labeling, aiming to address the challenge of adapting models to different LiDAR sensors. We propose a novel approach that leverages domain adaptation techniques to enable the transfer of knowledge across sensors, thereby improving the robustness and generalizability of semantic labeling models. Our method utilizes a combination of sensor-agnostic and sensor-specific features to facilitate the adaptation process. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving significant improvements in labeling accuracy when transferring models across different LiDAR sensors. The findings of this study have important implications for the development of robust and scalable LiDAR-based semantic labeling systems, particularly in applications such as autonomous driving, urban planning, and environmental monitoring. Key contributions include the introduction of a cross-sensor portability framework, the evaluation of various domain adaptation techniques, and the provision of insights into the importance of sensor-agnostic features for improving model transferability. Relevant keywords: LiDAR, semantic labeling, cross-sensor portability, domain adaptation, neural networks, deep learning.
