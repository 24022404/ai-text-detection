Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) have emerged as a transformative technology in the field of image and video synthesis, enabling the creation of realistic and high-quality media content. This paper provides a comprehensive overview of the core algorithms underpinning GANs, alongside recent advancements that have significantly improved their stability, scalability, and output fidelity. We systematically examine key innovations such as conditional GANs, progressive growing techniques, and architectures tailored for video generation. The study evaluates the performance of various GAN models on benchmark datasets, measuring factors such as image realism, temporal consistency in video synthesis, and computational efficiency. Additionally, we explore a wide range of applications, including data augmentation, artistic content creation, video prediction, and deepfake detection, highlighting the versatility of GANs across domains such as entertainment, healthcare, and security. Our analysis identifies persistent challenges in the field, such as mode collapse and training instability, and discusses emerging solutions aimed at addressing these issues. The paper concludes by outlining future research directions and potential societal impacts of GAN-based image and video synthesis. Through this comprehensive assessment, we demonstrate the pivotal role of generative adversarial networks as both a state-of-the-art synthesis tool and a catalyst for further innovation in artificial intelligence. Keywords: Generative Adversarial Networks, GANs, image synthesis, video synthesis, deep learning, neural networks, data augmentation, computer vision.
