The rapid advancement of image captioning models has significantly improved their ability to describe visual content. However, a persistent challenge lies in generating accurate captions for images containing novel objects—those not present in the training vocabulary. This paper addresses this limitation by proposing an online vocabulary expansion framework that dynamically integrates new object categories into existing captioning models. Our approach leverages a combination of pre-trained object detectors and a lightweight adaptation mechanism to seamlessly incorporate novel objects without requiring extensive retraining. We evaluate the proposed method on benchmark datasets, demonstrating its ability to generate captions with improved accuracy and relevance for images containing previously unseen objects. Experimental results show that our framework outperforms existing methods in terms of both caption quality and computational efficiency. The proposed solution offers a practical and scalable approach to enhancing image captioning systems, making them more adaptable to real-world scenarios where novel objects frequently appear. This research contributes to the broader goal of developing more robust and flexible vision-language models, with potential applications in areas such as automated content generation, assistive technologies, and multimedia indexing.  

**Keywords**: Image captioning, novel objects, vocabulary expansion, vision-language models, online adaptation.
