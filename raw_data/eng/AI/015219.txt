In today's interconnected world, analyzing complex relationships within multiplex bipartite networks is essential for understanding diverse systems such as social media interactions, biological networks, and recommendation systems. Traditional methods struggle with efficiently embedding these networks' multidiimensional data. This paper introduces a novel approach to efficiently and effectively embed multiplex bipartite networks using dual hypergraph convolutional networks.

Methods/Approach: We develop a dual hypergraph convolutional network model, leveraging its capability to capture higher-order correlations and intricate topologies present in multiplex bipartite structures. By transforming these networks into a dual hypergraph representation, our method holistically encapsulates the rich connectivity information, enabling enhanced network embedding. The model operates through two convolutional stages, each applying specialized filters to extract salient features and maintain the inherent multiplex nature of the data.

Results/Findings: Our experiments, conducted on multiple real-world datasets, demonstrate that the proposed method significantly outperforms existing network embedding techniques in terms of accuracy and efficiency. Specifically, our dual hypergraph convolutional network achieves superior performance in tasks such as link prediction and node classification, demonstrating its robust generalization capabilities. Additionally, the model is shown to be computationally efficient, making it suitable for large-scale network applications.

Conclusion/Implications: This research provides a substantial contribution to network analysis, offering an innovative solution for embedding complex multiplex bipartite networks. The dual hypergraph convolutional network method not only enhances the accuracy and effectiveness of network analysis tasks but also opens new avenues for the development of more advanced network algorithms. Potential applications span numerous fields, including social network analysis, bioinformatics, and recommendation systems, highlighting the wide-ranging impact of our approach.

Key Keywords: multiplex bipartite networks, network embedding, hypergraph convolutional networks, complex systems, link prediction, node classification.
