The integration of text within images enhances communication across diverse applications, from social media to educational materials. However, the challenge lies in determining optimal text placement to ensure readability and aesthetic harmony. This paper introduces Copyspace, a novel computational framework designed to identify suitable regions within images for text overlay. Our approach combines saliency detection, edge analysis, and color contrast assessment to evaluate potential text locations. We propose a scoring mechanism that prioritizes regions with low visual complexity, minimal object interference, and sufficient color contrast between the text and background. Comparative experiments against existing text placement methods demonstrate that Copyspace achieves superior readability and visual appeal, as validated by subjective user evaluations. Furthermore, we present an application of Copyspace in automated image captioning, highlighting its potential for enhancing accessibility and user engagement. This research contributes a robust and efficient solution for intelligent text placement in images, paving the way for improved visual communication strategies.

Keywords: Text Placement, Image Analysis, Saliency Detection, Color Contrast, Automated Captioning, Computer Vision.
