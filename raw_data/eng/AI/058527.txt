The objective of this research is to develop Impressions2Font, a novel system designed to generate fonts by specifying user-defined impressions. Traditional font design requires manual, labor-intensive processes that limit the exploration of creative typography. By leveraging advances in machine learning and natural language processing, our approach autonomously interprets descriptive impressions to create corresponding font styles. The Impressions2Font system utilizes a generative adversarial network (GAN) architecture tailored for visual style transfer, integrating a textual analysis component to process and understand user inputs. Our experiments demonstrate the system's ability to produce diverse and high-quality fonts that reflect specified impressions, significantly outperforming conventional methods in terms of design variation and user satisfaction. The results indicate potential applications in graphic design, content creation, and personalized marketing, offering users an intuitive tool to express creativity through typography. This work contributes to the field by providing a seamless and accessible font generation solution, expanding the possibilities for personalized font creation through impression-based specifications. Key keywords include font generation, generative adversarial networks, natural language processing, and graphic design.
