This study aims to develop a novel approach for detecting bid rigging in auctions using deep learning techniques, specifically convolutional neural networks (CNNs). The objective is to identify cartel participants and flag suspicious bidding patterns. Our approach involves training a CNN model on a dataset of auction records to learn patterns and anomalies indicative of collusive behavior. The model is designed to analyze bidding data and detect subtle variations that may indicate bid rigging. Our results show that the CNN-based approach outperforms traditional methods in detecting cartel participants, with a significant improvement in accuracy and recall. The proposed method has important implications for auctioneers, regulators, and law enforcement agencies seeking to prevent bid rigging and promote fair competition. By leveraging the power of deep learning, this study contributes to the development of more effective tools for detecting and preventing cartel activity, with potential applications in e-commerce, procurement, and other auction-based markets. Key keywords: bid rigging, cartel detection, convolutional neural networks, deep learning, auction analysis.
