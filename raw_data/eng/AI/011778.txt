This paper explores the interaction between deep detectors and Siamese trackers in video surveillance systems, aiming to improve object tracking accuracy and efficiency. Our approach combines the strengths of deep learning-based detectors and Siamese network-based trackers to enhance the overall performance of video surveillance systems. We propose a novel framework that leverages the detection capabilities of deep detectors to initialize and update tracks, while utilizing Siamese trackers to handle occlusions and maintain track continuity. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art tracking methods, achieving significant improvements in terms of accuracy, robustness, and real-time processing capabilities. The key contributions of this research include the development of a seamless integration mechanism between deep detectors and Siamese trackers, as well as the introduction of a new evaluation metric to assess the effectiveness of tracker-detector interactions. Our work has important implications for various applications, including security, surveillance, and autonomous systems, and highlights the potential of combining deep learning and Siamese tracking techniques to create more robust and efficient video analysis systems. Key keywords: deep detectors, Siamese trackers, video surveillance, object tracking, computer vision, AI models.
