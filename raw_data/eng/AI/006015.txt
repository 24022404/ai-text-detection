This paper proposes a novel approach to meta-learning, introducing a Bayesian model-agnostic framework that enables efficient and flexible learning across diverse tasks. The objective is to develop a meta-learning algorithm that can adapt to new tasks with limited data and generalize well across different models and domains. Our approach utilizes Bayesian inference to learn a probabilistic representation of model parameters, allowing for uncertainty-aware adaptation and improved robustness. We employ a model-agnostic meta-learning strategy, which enables our method to be applied to various deep learning architectures without requiring significant modifications. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming existing meta-learning methods in terms of accuracy and adaptability. The key findings highlight the importance of uncertainty estimation in meta-learning and the benefits of a model-agnostic approach. Our research contributes to the development of more versatile and reliable meta-learning algorithms, with potential applications in few-shot learning, transfer learning, and multi-task learning. Key keywords: Bayesian meta-learning, model-agnostic, uncertainty-aware, few-shot learning, transfer learning.
