This paper presents the development of a large-scale multimodal knowledge base system designed to answer visual queries by integrating and reasoning over textual and visual information. The objective is to enable efficient and accurate retrieval of relevant information in response to image-based queries. Our approach combines computer vision and natural language processing techniques to construct a multimodal knowledge graph, which is then used to answer visual queries. The system utilizes a novel fusion mechanism to integrate features from both modalities, allowing for more accurate and informative responses. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our system, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The proposed system has significant implications for applications such as visual question answering, image retrieval, and multimodal search. Key contributions include the development of a scalable multimodal knowledge base construction method and a novel query answering algorithm that leverages the strengths of both computer vision and natural language processing. Relevant keywords: multimodal knowledge base, visual query answering, computer vision, natural language processing, multimodal fusion, large-scale knowledge graph construction.
