This paper introduces a novel semantic segmentation approach using a highly fused convolutional network designed to improve pixel-level classification accuracy. The proposed architecture leverages multi-scale feature aggregation through dense connections and skip pathways, enabling the network to effectively capture both fine-grained details and high-level contextual information. Furthermore, we introduce a training strategy employing multiple soft cost functions, each focusing on different aspects of the segmentation task, such as boundary delineation and region consistency. These cost functions are adaptively weighted during training to optimize overall performance. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including Cityscapes and PASCAL VOC 2012, demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance, surpassing existing approaches in terms of mean Intersection over Union (mIoU) and boundary F-score. The proposed network exhibits robustness to challenging scenarios, such as occlusions and varying illumination conditions. The fusion strategy and the multi-soft cost function approach offer a promising direction for enhancing semantic segmentation accuracy and can be potentially applied to various computer vision tasks.

Keywords: Semantic Segmentation, Convolutional Neural Networks, Deep Learning, Feature Fusion, Cost Function, Pixel Classification.
