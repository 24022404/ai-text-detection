Deep neural networks (DNNs) have demonstrated remarkable success in scene understanding, crucial for automotive cyber-physical systems (CPS). However, their high computational cost hinders real-time deployment on resource-constrained platforms. This paper presents a novel approach to accelerate DNNs for efficient scene understanding in automotive CPS. We introduce a hardware-aware pruning and quantization strategy, coupled with a custom accelerator architecture optimized for sparse and low-precision operations. The pruning strategy identifies and removes redundant connections in the DNN, while quantization reduces the precision of weights and activations. The custom accelerator leverages these techniques to minimize memory access and maximize computational throughput. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate significant improvements in inference speed and energy efficiency compared to existing DNN implementations, achieving up to a 5x speedup and a 4x reduction in energy consumption with minimal accuracy loss. These findings suggest that our approach enables real-time scene understanding in automotive CPS, facilitating advanced driver-assistance systems and autonomous driving functionalities. Keywords: Deep Neural Networks, Scene Understanding, Automotive CPS, Hardware Acceleration, Pruning, Quantization, Embedded Systems.
