The 6-MdB sequence can be systematically broken down into particular Lyndon words arranged in lexicographic order, allowing for precise decomposition. By adding an appropriate prefix and suffix, as depicted in Figure 4.1 for the case where k=6 and s=2, it becomes possible to construct a (k, s)-RdB sequence with desirable properties. Notably, this method ensures that every substring of the concatenation of the prefix and suffix independently constitutes a (6,2)-RdB sequence. This approach highlights the strength and flexibility of combining Lyndon words to form robust combinatorial structures.
