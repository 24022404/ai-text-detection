This paper addresses the limitations in existing hyperspherical face recognition methods by reviving and unifying the SphereFace framework. Despite their prevalence, many hyperspherical algorithms encounter challenges in scalability and performance, especially when dealing with diverse datasets.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel approach that enhances the original SphereFace by integrating an optimized loss function and leveraging advanced deep learning techniques. Our model incorporates a refined angular margin strategy to improve classification accuracy and scalability. This approach is complemented by a robust training pipeline designed to harness large-scale datasets efficiently.

Results/Findings: The refined SphereFace framework demonstrates significant improvements across multiple face recognition benchmarks, showing enhanced accuracy and consistency compared to state-of-the-art hyperspherical methods. Experimental results highlight a substantial reduction in error rates and increased robustness in challenging environments, marking a notable advancement over previous methodologies.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes to the field by unifying and elevating hyperspherical face recognition with a more adaptable and powerful framework. The enhanced SphereFace model not only advances theoretical understanding but also offers practical improvements in applications requiring reliable identity verification. This work paves the way for future innovations in secure and efficient biometric systems.

Keywords: SphereFace, hyperspherical face recognition, deep learning, angular margin, biometric systems.
