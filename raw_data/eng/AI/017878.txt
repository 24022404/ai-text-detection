This paper proposes JDSR-GAN, a novel joint and collaborative learning network designed to address the challenging task of masked face super-resolution. The objective is to develop a robust and efficient approach that can effectively restore high-quality facial images from low-resolution masked faces. To achieve this, we employ a hybrid model that combines the strengths of generative adversarial networks (GANs) and joint learning mechanisms. Our approach utilizes a collaborative learning strategy, where multiple networks work together to refine and enhance the super-resolution process. The JDSR-GAN model is trained on a large-scale dataset of masked faces, leveraging a joint loss function that balances reconstruction accuracy and perceptual quality. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of JDSR-GAN over state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in terms of peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM). The proposed model has important implications for various applications, including surveillance, security, and facial recognition systems. Key contributions of this research include the introduction of a collaborative learning framework for masked face super-resolution and the demonstration of its effectiveness in restoring high-quality facial images. Relevant keywords: GANs, joint learning, collaborative networks, masked face super-resolution, facial image restoration, deep learning.
