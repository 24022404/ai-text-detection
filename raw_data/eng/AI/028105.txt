This paper provides a comprehensive survey and taxonomy of generative adversarial networks (GANs) applied to time series data, aiming to address the challenges of modeling and generating complex temporal patterns. We employ a systematic approach to categorize and analyze existing GAN-based architectures, evaluating their strengths and limitations in capturing long-term dependencies and non-linear relationships inherent in time series. Our results highlight the effectiveness of GANs in generating realistic synthetic time series data, outperforming traditional methods in various evaluation metrics. The key findings and contributions of this research include a novel taxonomy that facilitates the selection of suitable GAN architectures for specific time series applications, and insights into the potential of GANs for anomaly detection, forecasting, and data augmentation. This survey emphasizes the novelty and contributions of GANs in time series analysis, paving the way for future research and applications in fields such as finance, healthcare, and climate modeling, with relevant keywords including time series analysis, generative adversarial networks, deep learning, and synthetic data generation.
