This paper introduces DeepIGeoS, a novel deep interactive geodesic framework designed to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of medical image segmentation. The objective is to address the challenges of precise organ and tissue segmentation in medical imaging by leveraging the strengths of deep learning and geodesic active contours. Our approach combines a convolutional neural network (CNN) with a geodesic framework, allowing for interactive refinement of segmentation results. The CNN provides an initial segmentation, which is then refined through a geodesic active contour model that incorporates user input. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of DeepIGeoS in segmenting various organs and tissues from different medical image modalities, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The key contributions of this work include the integration of deep learning and geodesic active contours for interactive medical image segmentation, and the demonstration of its potential to improve clinical workflows. The proposed framework has significant implications for medical image analysis, enabling more accurate and efficient segmentation, and paving the way for improved diagnosis and treatment planning. Key keywords: medical image segmentation, deep learning, geodesic active contours, interactive segmentation, CNN, image analysis.
