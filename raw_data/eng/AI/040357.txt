This paper presents a novel approach to object detection using transformer-based architectures. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of object detection systems by leveraging the strengths of transformer models. Our approach employs a customized transformer encoder to process input images and detect objects of interest. The method utilizes self-attention mechanisms to weigh the importance of different regions in the image, allowing for more accurate object localization and classification. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. Compared to traditional convolutional neural network-based methods, our transformer-based object detection system exhibits improved robustness to scale and orientation variations. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a transformer-based architecture for object detection and the demonstration of its potential for real-world applications, such as autonomous driving and surveillance. Keywords: transformer, object detection, computer vision, deep learning, self-attention.
