This paper addresses the challenge of multi-label few-zero-shot learning, where a model must recognize multiple labels with limited or no training data. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that aggregates knowledge from multiple label graphs to improve the performance of few-zero-shot learning. We propose a method that constructs multiple label graphs, each representing a different relationship between labels, and then aggregates the knowledge from these graphs using a graph attention mechanism. Our approach enables the model to capture complex label relationships and adapt to new, unseen labels with minimal training data. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing few-zero-shot learning approaches, achieving significant improvements in label recognition accuracy. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a multi-label graph attention mechanism and the demonstration of its effectiveness in few-zero-shot learning scenarios. Our work has important implications for applications such as image and text classification, where multi-label recognition is crucial. Key keywords: few-zero-shot learning, multi-label classification, graph attention, label graphs, knowledge aggregation.
