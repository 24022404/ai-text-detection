Facial pose estimation is crucial for various applications, including facial animation, human-computer interaction, and augmented reality. Traditional regression-based methods often struggle with ambiguous poses and noisy labels. This paper introduces a novel deep learning approach that directly learns from label distributions rather than relying on single-point estimates. We propose a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture trained to predict a probability distribution over possible poses, effectively capturing the inherent uncertainty in pose annotations. Our method utilizes a Kullback-Leibler divergence loss to minimize the difference between predicted and ground truth distributions, generated using a Gaussian kernel. Experimental results on challenging benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art regression-based methods, particularly in handling ambiguous poses and noisy data. The learned distributions provide a more robust and accurate representation of facial pose, leading to improved performance and stability. This distribution-based learning framework offers a promising direction for enhancing facial pose estimation accuracy and robustness.

Keywords: Facial Pose Estimation, Deep Learning, Label Distribution Learning, Convolutional Neural Networks, Kullback-Leibler Divergence.
