Explaining multivariate data is crucial for understanding complex relationships and making informed decisions. However, traditional methods often struggle to provide intuitive and actionable insights. This paper introduces a novel approach, Multivariate Data Explanation by Jumping Emerging Patterns Visualization (M-JEPV), which leverages emerging pattern (EP) analysis and interactive visualization to enhance data interpretability. M-JEPV identifies significant changes in data distributions across different groups by extracting jumping emerging patterns (JEPs), which are attribute combinations exhibiting substantial frequency increases. We propose an innovative visualization technique that allows users to explore these JEPs and their relationships through an interactive graph, enabling identification of key drivers and discriminative features. The effectiveness of M-JEPV is demonstrated through experiments on real-world datasets, where it successfully reveals hidden patterns and provides valuable explanations compared to baseline methods. This approach offers a powerful tool for multivariate data analysis, facilitating knowledge discovery and decision-making in various domains.
Keywords: Multivariate Data, Emerging Patterns, Data Visualization, Explainable AI, Data Mining.
