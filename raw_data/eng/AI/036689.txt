This paper introduces a novel deep learning framework for joint semantic object prediction and spatial sound super-resolution using binaural audio. The objective is to predict the semantic categories of sound-emitting objects in a scene and enhance the spatial resolution of corresponding sound events. Our approach utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture that processes binaural recordings to simultaneously infer object classes and generate high-resolution binaural signals. The network is trained on a synthetically generated dataset consisting of diverse acoustic scenes and object arrangements. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in both semantic object prediction accuracy and spatial sound super-resolution quality, as measured by standard audio quality metrics. The proposed framework offers significant improvements over existing methods by leveraging the inherent relationship between spatial sound and object semantics. This research contributes to a more comprehensive understanding of auditory scene analysis and has potential applications in virtual reality, augmented reality, and assistive listening devices.

Keywords: Binaural sound, Spatial sound, Super-resolution, Semantic object prediction, Deep learning, Auditory scene analysis.
