This paper proposes a novel two-stream approach for action recognition-oriented video super-resolution, aiming to enhance the quality of low-resolution videos while preserving crucial action details. Our method combines a super-resolution stream, utilizing advanced convolutional neural networks to upscale video frames, with an action recognition stream, leveraging temporal convolutional networks to capture action patterns. The two streams interact through a shared attention mechanism, allowing the model to focus on action-relevant regions during the super-resolution process. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art video super-resolution methods in terms of both visual quality and action recognition accuracy. The proposed framework has significant implications for various applications, including surveillance, sports analysis, and human-computer interaction. Key contributions of this work include the introduction of a dual-stream architecture, the incorporation of action recognition guidance for super-resolution, and the achievement of superior performance in action recognition-oriented video super-resolution. Relevant keywords: video super-resolution, action recognition, two-stream architecture, convolutional neural networks, temporal convolutional networks.
