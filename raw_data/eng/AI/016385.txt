This paper presents the design and development of a robotic picking system by Team Applied Robotics, aiming to enhance efficiency and accuracy in automated warehouse management. Our objective is to create a reliable and adaptable system capable of handling a wide range of objects. We employ a combination of computer vision, machine learning algorithms, and robotic arm technology to achieve precise object detection and manipulation. The system's performance is evaluated through a series of experiments, demonstrating significant improvements in picking speed and accuracy compared to traditional methods. Our research contributes to the advancement of warehouse automation, highlighting the potential of robotic picking systems to increase productivity and reduce labor costs. Key innovations include the integration of deep learning-based object recognition and a novel gripping mechanism, allowing for gentle yet secure handling of fragile items. This study showcases the potential of robotics and AI in revolutionizing logistics and supply chain management, with applications in e-commerce, manufacturing, and beyond.
