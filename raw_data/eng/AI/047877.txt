This paper introduces KPConv, a novel convolutional operator designed for point cloud processing, which enables flexible and deformable convolutional operations. The objective is to address the challenges of applying traditional convolutional neural networks to irregular and sparse 3D point cloud data. Our approach leverages a kernel-point-based methodology, allowing the convolutional kernel to deform and adapt to the local geometry of the point cloud. The KPConv operator is designed to be flexible, efficient, and scalable, making it suitable for a wide range of point cloud-based applications. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of KPConv in achieving state-of-the-art performance on various 3D point cloud classification, segmentation, and reconstruction tasks. The key findings highlight the importance of adaptability and flexibility in convolutional operations for point cloud data. The proposed KPConv operator contributes to the advancement of 3D point cloud processing and has potential applications in fields such as computer vision, robotics, and autonomous driving. Key keywords: point clouds, convolutional neural networks, 3D processing, kernel-point-based methodology, deformable convolution.
