Single-view depth estimation is a challenging problem in computer vision. Existing convolutional neural networks often struggle to effectively integrate multi-scale information while accounting for camera-specific parameters, limiting their accuracy and generalization capability. This paper introduces Camera-Aware Multi-Scale Convolutions (CAM-Convs), a novel convolutional layer that explicitly incorporates camera intrinsic parameters into the multi-scale feature extraction process. CAM-Convs utilize a set of parallel convolutional branches with varying kernel sizes, each modulated by camera parameters to adapt to different scales and perspectives. We demonstrate that CAM-Convs enhance the network's ability to capture fine-grained details and global context relevant to depth estimation. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including NYU Depth V2 and KITTI, show that our proposed method achieves state-of-the-art performance compared to existing single-view depth estimation techniques. Specifically, CAM-Convs improve depth accuracy by effectively integrating camera-aware features, demonstrating the importance of considering camera specifics in single-view depth prediction. This approach offers a promising direction for advancing depth estimation and other vision tasks that benefit from camera parameter awareness.
Keywords: Depth Estimation, Convolutional Neural Networks, Multi-Scale Convolutions, Camera Parameters, Computer Vision.
