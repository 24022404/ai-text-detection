This paper introduces MaskGAN, a novel approach to facial image manipulation that enables diverse and interactive editing of facial features. The objective is to develop a system that allows users to manipulate facial images in a flexible and controllable manner, while maintaining realism and diversity. To achieve this, we propose a generative adversarial network (GAN) framework that incorporates a mask-based editing mechanism, enabling users to specify editing regions and control the manipulation process. Our approach combines the strengths of GANs and mask-based editing, allowing for precise control over facial feature manipulation. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of MaskGAN in generating high-quality, diverse, and realistic facial images with user-specified edits. The key findings include significant improvements in edit quality, diversity, and user control compared to existing methods. The contributions of this research lie in its ability to provide an interactive and controllable facial image manipulation system, with potential applications in fields such as computer vision, graphics, and human-computer interaction. Key keywords: facial image manipulation, generative adversarial networks, mask-based editing, interactive editing, computer vision, graphics.
