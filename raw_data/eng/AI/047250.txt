Medical image analysis is a critical domain in healthcare, facilitating accurate diagnosis, treatment planning, and disease monitoring. Recent advancements in deep learning, particularly Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), have significantly enhanced the effectiveness and efficiency of medical image interpretation across various modalities such as MRI, CT, X-ray, and ultrasound. This review paper aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the application of CNNs in medical image analysis, focusing on state-of-the-art techniques for tasks including image classification, segmentation, detection, and localization. The paper systematically examines different CNN architectures, optimization strategies, and approaches for addressing challenges such as limited annotated data, class imbalance, and variation in image quality. Key findings reveal that CNN-based models outperform traditional machine learning methods in terms of accuracy and robustness, especially when integrated with advanced techniques like transfer learning and data augmentation. Furthermore, we discuss comparative evaluations, highlight successful deployments in clinical settings, and identify existing limitations such as interpretability and generalizability. The review concludes by outlining future research directions, emphasizing the need for interpretable models and robust validation across diverse clinical environments. Keywords: medical image analysis, convolutional neural networks, deep learning, image classification, segmentation, healthcare AI.
