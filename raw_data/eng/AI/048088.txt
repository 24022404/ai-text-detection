This paper explores the concept of semantic adversarial examples, which are inputs designed to mislead machine learning models while remaining semantically similar to the original data. The objective is to investigate the vulnerability of deep learning models to such examples and develop methods to improve their robustness. We propose a novel approach that utilizes generative models to create semantic adversarial examples, allowing us to analyze the weaknesses of existing models. Our results show that state-of-the-art models are susceptible to these examples, leading to significant performance degradation. We also introduce a defense mechanism that leverages semantic information to detect and mitigate the effects of adversarial examples. The findings of this research contribute to the development of more robust machine learning models, with potential applications in areas such as computer vision, natural language processing, and autonomous systems. Key contributions include the introduction of a new type of adversarial example, the development of a generative model-based approach for creating such examples, and the proposal of a semantic-based defense mechanism. Relevant keywords: adversarial examples, deep learning, generative models, robustness, computer vision, natural language processing.
