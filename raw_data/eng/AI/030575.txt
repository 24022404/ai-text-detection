This paper presents a novel approach to facial pose estimation using deep learning techniques, focusing on learning from label distributions. The objective is to accurately estimate the pose of a face in an image, which is crucial for various applications such as facial recognition, expression analysis, and human-computer interaction. Our method utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture to learn a mapping between facial images and their corresponding pose labels, represented as distributions. The CNN is trained using a specially designed loss function that takes into account the uncertainty and ambiguity of pose labels. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. The proposed method outperforms existing techniques, particularly in cases where the pose labels are noisy or ambiguous. Our contributions include a novel loss function and a robust CNN architecture, which can be applied to various facial analysis tasks. Key findings indicate that learning from label distributions improves the accuracy and robustness of facial pose estimation, with potential applications in AI, computer vision, and machine learning. Relevant keywords: facial pose estimation, deep learning, label distributions, CNN, computer vision, machine learning.
