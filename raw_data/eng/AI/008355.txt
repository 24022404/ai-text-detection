This paper introduces ChainNet, a novel framework designed to learn on blockchain graphs by incorporating topological features. The objective is to improve the analysis and understanding of blockchain networks by leveraging their inherent structural properties. ChainNet employs a graph neural network approach, utilizing a combination of node and edge features to capture the complex relationships within blockchain graphs. The methodology involves a multi-stage process, including graph construction, feature extraction, and model training. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of ChainNet in predicting transaction outcomes and identifying key players in the network, outperforming existing methods. The key findings highlight the importance of topological features in blockchain graph analysis, enabling more accurate predictions and insights into network dynamics. ChainNet contributes to the field by providing a scalable and adaptable framework for blockchain analysis, with potential applications in fraud detection, network optimization, and cryptocurrency forecasting. Key keywords: blockchain, graph neural networks, topological features, network analysis, cryptocurrency.
