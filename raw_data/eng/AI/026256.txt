The rapid advancement of neural rendering techniques has highlighted the need for high-quality, diverse datasets to train and evaluate models effectively. This paper introduces *SyntheticFur*, a novel dataset specifically designed for neural rendering applications, focusing on realistic fur and hair simulation. The dataset comprises high-resolution, photorealistic synthetic images of various fur types, textures, and lighting conditions, generated using advanced physics-based rendering techniques. We employ a procedural modeling approach to ensure diversity and scalability, enabling the creation of thousands of unique fur samples with precise ground-truth annotations. To validate the utility of *SyntheticFur*, we benchmark several state-of-the-art neural rendering models, demonstrating significant improvements in rendering quality and realism compared to existing datasets. Our results show that models trained on *SyntheticFur* achieve superior performance in tasks such as fur reconstruction, texture synthesis, and light interaction modeling. The dataset not only addresses the limitations of current datasets but also opens new avenues for research in neural rendering, particularly in applications requiring fine-grained detail and realism. *SyntheticFur* is publicly available to foster further innovation in the field.  

**Keywords**: neural rendering, synthetic dataset, fur simulation, procedural modeling, photorealistic rendering, texture synthesis.
