This paper proposes a novel approach to action recognition using Hierarchical Multi-scale Attention Networks (HMAN). The objective is to improve the accuracy of action recognition in videos by effectively capturing spatial and temporal features at multiple scales. Our approach utilizes a hierarchical architecture that combines attention mechanisms at different scales to focus on relevant regions and frames. The network is trained using a large-scale video dataset and evaluated on several benchmark datasets. Results show that HMAN outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in recognition accuracy. The key contributions of this work include the introduction of a hierarchical attention mechanism and a multi-scale feature extraction approach, which enable the network to capture complex and nuanced actions. This research has important implications for applications such as surveillance, human-computer interaction, and robotics, where accurate action recognition is crucial. Key keywords: action recognition, attention networks, multi-scale features, hierarchical architecture, computer vision, deep learning.
