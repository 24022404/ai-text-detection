The objective of this research is to address the critical challenge of generating large-scale labeled datasets, which are essential for training machine learning models, while minimizing the manual annotation effort typically required. This paper introduces DatasetGAN, an innovative framework designed to efficiently produce accurately labeled data with minimal human intervention. Leveraging the power of generative adversarial networks (GANs), DatasetGAN synthesizes realistic samples and employs a novel annotation mechanism that reduces the dependency on manual labeling tasks. The method integrates a generator to create data samples and an auxiliary network to predict labels, thereby streamlining the annotation process. Key findings demonstrate that DatasetGAN significantly outperforms traditional data labeling approaches in terms of speed and accuracy while reducing human labor. Experimental results across various domains show enhanced model training outcomes using datasets labeled by DatasetGAN, compared with those labeled by conventional methods. The research concludes with insights into the potential applications of DatasetGAN, highlighting its capacity to democratize dataset creation for diverse machine learning tasks. This framework paves the way for more accessible AI applications where labeled data is a bottleneck, offering a scalable path to harness the full potential of AI-driven solutions. Key keywords include DatasetGAN, generative adversarial networks, labeled data, data annotation, and machine learning.
