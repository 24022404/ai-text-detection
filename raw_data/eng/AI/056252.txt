Melanoma, a deadly form of skin cancer, requires early and accurate detection to improve patient outcomes. This study addresses the challenge of automating melanoma screening by leveraging deep learning techniques, with a focus on knowledge transfer to enhance model performance. We propose a novel approach that utilizes pre-trained convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and adapts them through transfer learning for melanoma classification. The methodology involves fine-tuning state-of-the-art architectures, such as ResNet and EfficientNet, on a curated dataset of dermatoscopic images. To mitigate data scarcity and improve generalization, we incorporate data augmentation and domain-specific preprocessing techniques. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach achieves superior performance, with an accuracy of 92.5% and an AUC of 0.96, outperforming existing methods. The study highlights the effectiveness of transfer learning in medical imaging, particularly for tasks with limited labeled data. This research contributes to the field by providing a robust, scalable framework for melanoma detection, with potential applications in clinical decision support systems. The findings underscore the importance of leveraging pre-trained models and domain adaptation to advance healthcare AI solutions.  

**Keywords**: Melanoma screening, deep learning, transfer learning, convolutional neural networks, medical imaging, AI in healthcare.
