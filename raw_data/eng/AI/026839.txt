This paper introduces ResNeSt, a novel Split-Attention network architecture designed to improve the representational power of convolutional neural networks (CNNs). The objective is to enhance the ability of CNNs to model complex scenes and objects by incorporating a split-attention mechanism that allows the network to focus on different regions of the input image. Our approach involves modifying the traditional residual block to include a split-attention module, which enables the network to selectively weigh the importance of different channels and spatial locations. Experimental results demonstrate that ResNeSt achieves state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets, including ImageNet and COCO, with significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency. The key findings of this research highlight the effectiveness of split-attention mechanisms in improving the robustness and generalizability of CNNs. The ResNeSt architecture has important implications for a wide range of computer vision applications, including image classification, object detection, and segmentation, and contributes to the development of more accurate and efficient deep learning models. Key keywords: ResNeSt, Split-Attention, Convolutional Neural Networks, Computer Vision, Deep Learning.
