Object detection systems are increasingly deployed in safety-critical applications, yet their susceptibility to adversarial attacks and limited robustness under varying real-world conditions remain significant challenges. This paper investigates the impact of feature alignment strategies on both clean average precision and adversarial robustness within state-of-the-art object detection frameworks. We introduce a novel feature alignment module designed to enhance consistency between feature representations across different input variations. Our method is integrated into established object detection architectures and evaluated on standard benchmarks, with experiments conducted under both benign and adversarial scenarios. Results demonstrate that our approach yields notable improvements in clean average precision, outperforming baseline detectors by up to 4.2%. Crucially, under a range of adversarial attacks, models augmented with feature alignment exhibit a substantial increase in robustness, maintaining higher detection accuracy compared to standard counterparts. The findings suggest that feature alignment not only boosts performance in standard settings but also fortifies model reliability against adversarial perturbations. This research presents an effective pathway toward building more robust and accurate object detection systems, with practical implications for deployment in security, autonomous driving, and intelligent surveillance. Keywords: object detection, feature alignment, adversarial robustness, average precision, deep learning.
