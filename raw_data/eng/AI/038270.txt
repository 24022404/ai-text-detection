Human Activity Recognition (HAR) using wearable sensor data has emerged as a critical technology for applications in healthcare, fitness monitoring, and smart environments. This paper presents a novel machine learning-based approach for accurate and efficient HAR utilizing multi-modal sensor data collected from wearable devices. The proposed method integrates advanced feature extraction techniques with an optimized ensemble learning framework to effectively capture temporal and contextual patterns in accelerometer, gyroscope, and magnetometer signals. Extensive experiments were conducted using publicly available benchmark datasets, and the results demonstrate significant improvements in classification accuracy, robustness to sensor noise, and computational efficiency when compared to existing state-of-the-art models. Furthermore, our approach exhibits strong generalizability across diverse user populations and activity types. The findings highlight the potential of the proposed method to enable reliable real-time HAR in resource-constrained wearable platforms. This research contributes a scalable and interpretable solution, paving the way for next-generation intelligent systems in healthcare and ubiquitous computing. 

Keywords: Human Activity Recognition, wearable sensors, machine learning, ensemble learning, feature extraction, real-time monitoring, health informatics.
