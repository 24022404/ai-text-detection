Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) has shown remarkable success in solving complex decision-making tasks. However, deploying large-scale DRL models in real-time applications remains challenging due to computational constraints and latency requirements. This paper addresses the problem of real-time policy distillation, where a compact, efficient policy is derived from a computationally expensive teacher model without significant performance degradation. We propose a novel approach that leverages knowledge distillation techniques combined with adaptive sampling strategies to ensure the distilled policy maintains high accuracy while meeting real-time execution constraints. Our method employs a lightweight student network trained on a dynamically selected subset of the teacher's experiences, optimized for both speed and fidelity. Experimental results on benchmark environments demonstrate that the distilled policy achieves near-teacher-level performance with a 70% reduction in inference time, making it suitable for real-time applications. The proposed framework also exhibits robustness across diverse tasks, including robotic control and autonomous navigation. This work contributes to the advancement of efficient DRL deployment, enabling the use of sophisticated models in latency-sensitive scenarios.  

**Keywords**: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Policy Distillation, Real-time Systems, Knowledge Transfer, Efficient Inference.
