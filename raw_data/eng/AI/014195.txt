This paper introduces a novel approach to high-dimensional multivariate time series forecasting by leveraging low-rank Gaussian copula processes. Traditional methods often struggle with the computational complexity and parameter estimation challenges posed by high-dimensional data. To address these limitations, our method decomposes the complex dependencies among time series into a low-rank structure within a Gaussian copula framework, significantly reducing the number of parameters to be estimated. We employ a spectral learning algorithm to efficiently estimate the low-rank covariance matrix. Empirical evaluations on several real-world datasets, including financial and climate data, demonstrate that our proposed model achieves superior forecasting accuracy and computational efficiency compared to state-of-the-art methods such as VAR and factor models, especially when dealing with a large number of correlated time series. The findings suggest that the low-rank Gaussian copula process provides a scalable and effective tool for high-dimensional multivariate forecasting, offering valuable insights for decision-making in various domains.

Keywords: Multivariate Time Series, Forecasting, Gaussian Copula, Low-Rank Models, High-Dimensional Data, Spectral Learning.
