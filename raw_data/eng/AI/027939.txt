This paper explores the application of deep reinforcement learning to inform conservation decisions, addressing the complex challenge of balancing human activities with environmental protection. Our objective is to develop a novel framework that leverages machine learning to optimize conservation strategies, considering multiple stakeholders and dynamic ecosystems. We employ a deep reinforcement learning approach, utilizing a combination of convolutional neural networks and Q-learning algorithms to analyze high-dimensional data and learn effective decision-making policies. Our results demonstrate the potential of this framework to improve conservation outcomes, outperforming traditional methods in simulated scenarios and real-world case studies. The key findings highlight the importance of integrating human and environmental factors into conservation decision-making, and the proposed framework provides a valuable tool for policymakers and conservationists. This research contributes to the growing field of artificial intelligence for environmental sustainability, with implications for biodiversity preservation, ecosystem management, and climate change mitigation. Key keywords: deep reinforcement learning, conservation decisions, environmental sustainability, AI for conservation, ecosystem management.
