This paper presents a novel approach to traffic prediction, leveraging the Graph Ensemble Net (GEN) to improve forecasting accuracy. The objective is to enhance the understanding of traffic flow dynamics by exploring the importance of feature and loss function design. Our method utilizes a combination of graph convolutional networks and ensemble techniques to model complex traffic patterns. The GEN architecture is designed to capture both spatial and temporal dependencies in traffic data, outperforming existing models. Key findings indicate that carefully crafted feature engineering and loss function design are crucial for achieving state-of-the-art performance in traffic prediction. Our results demonstrate significant improvements over baseline models, with the GEN model achieving superior accuracy and robustness. The study highlights the importance of incorporating domain-specific knowledge into deep learning architectures and contributes to the development of more accurate and reliable traffic forecasting systems. The proposed approach has potential applications in intelligent transportation systems, urban planning, and traffic management, with key keywords including traffic prediction, graph neural networks, ensemble methods, feature engineering, and loss function design.
