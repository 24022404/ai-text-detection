In recent years, the growing concerns over data privacy and the demand for scalable machine learning models have highlighted the need for innovative solutions in deep neural network (DNN) architecture. This paper presents a novel algorithmic-cryptographic co-design framework aimed at enhancing the scalability and privacy preservation of DNNs. Our approach integrates advanced cryptographic techniques with machine learning algorithms to ensure secure data processing without compromising model performance. We implement this framework in a series of experiments using benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in scalability with minimal computational overhead. Our results reveal that the co-designed model maintains robust privacy guarantees while achieving competitive accuracy compared to traditional DNNs. This research contributes to the evolving landscape of privacy-preserving AI, offering insights into the intersection of cryptography and artificial intelligence. The proposed framework could see applications across various domains, such as healthcare and finance, where data confidentiality is paramount. Keywords: deep neural networks, privacy-preserving, cryptography, machine learning, scalable systems.
