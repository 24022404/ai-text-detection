This paper presents a novel approach for text-based person re-identification (Re-ID) by introducing a Dual-path Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) architecture integrated with a Max Gated block. The primary objective is to bridge the semantic gap between visual data and textual descriptions for enhanced cross-modal retrieval performance in surveillance and security applications. Our method leverages two parallel pathways: one for processing image data and another for textual attributes, enabling efficient feature extraction from both modalities. The Max Gated block is introduced as a key module to adaptively select and fuse salient features, effectively mitigating noise and amplifying relevant information during feature interaction. Extensive experiments are conducted on benchmark datasets for text-based person Re-ID, demonstrating that the proposed model outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of retrieval accuracy and computational efficiency. The results highlight significant improvements in cross-modal matching capability, robust feature representation, and scalability. In summary, this work contributes a powerful dual-path deep learning framework with an innovative gating mechanism, paving the way for more reliable and accurate person re-identification systems in real-world scenarios.

Keywords: person re-identification, cross-modal retrieval, text-based Re-ID, dual-path CNN, Max Gated block, deep learning, feature fusion
