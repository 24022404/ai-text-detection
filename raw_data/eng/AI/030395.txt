This paper addresses the challenge of optimizing feature weighting in multi-feature content-based image retrieval (CBIR) systems, where the accurate determination of feature importance can significantly enhance retrieval performance. The goal is to develop a robust method that automatically adjusts feature weights, improving retrieval precision and user satisfaction.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel technique that integrates indexing with pseudo-relevance feedback to automatically determine feature weights. The method involves indexing multi-feature vectors of images, followed by employing pseudo-relevance feedback to iteratively refine feature weights based on initial retrieval results. This dynamic adjustment of weights enables a more tailored retrieval process, adapting to the content characteristics and user intent.

Results/Findings: The proposed approach was evaluated using a benchmark image dataset, demonstrating substantial improvements in retrieval accuracy compared to traditional fixed-weight and static method systems. The results indicate a notable increase in precision and recall metrics, highlighting the system’s capability to adaptively prioritize the most relevant features based on the initial feedback. Furthermore, comparative analysis shows that our method outperforms standard relevance feedback techniques by effectively balancing computational complexity with retrieval efficiency.

Conclusion/Implications: The integration of indexing and pseudo-relevance feedback for automatic feature weight determination represents a significant advancement in CBIR systems. This approach not only enhances retrieval accuracy but also offers a scalable solution for large-scale image databases. The adaptability and improved performance of our method suggest potential applications in various domains requiring efficient and accurate image retrieval, such as digital libraries, medical imaging, and multimedia search engines.

Keywords: Content-Based Image Retrieval, Feature Weighting, Pseudo-Relevance Feedback, Indexing, Image Retrieval Systems, Adaptive Retrieval, Multi-feature Analysis.
