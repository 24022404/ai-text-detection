This paper introduces DRIFT, a novel approach to functional software testing leveraging deep reinforcement learning. The objective is to develop an autonomous testing framework that can efficiently identify software defects and reduce manual testing efforts. DRIFT utilizes a customized reinforcement learning model to explore the software's state space, generating optimal test cases that maximize fault detection. The approach combines techniques from artificial intelligence and software engineering to create a robust and adaptive testing environment. Experimental results demonstrate that DRIFT outperforms traditional testing methods in terms of defect detection rate and testing time. The key findings highlight the potential of deep reinforcement learning in revolutionizing software testing, enabling faster time-to-market and improved software quality. The proposed framework has significant implications for the software industry, particularly in the development of reliable and efficient testing protocols. Key contributions include the integration of deep learning and reinforcement learning for software testing, and the demonstration of DRIFT's effectiveness in real-world scenarios. Relevant keywords: deep reinforcement learning, software testing, artificial intelligence, autonomous testing, defect detection.
