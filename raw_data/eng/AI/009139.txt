Multivariate time-series anomaly detection is crucial in various applications, yet challenges remain in capturing complex inter-series relationships and temporal dependencies. This paper introduces Event2Graph, a novel event-driven bipartite graph approach for enhanced anomaly detection. The core idea involves transforming the raw time-series data into discrete event sequences, which are then modeled as a bipartite graph consisting of event nodes and variable nodes. Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) are employed to learn the structural dependencies and temporal evolution patterns within the graph. By focusing on significant event transitions rather than continuous values, Event2Graph effectively reduces noise and highlights crucial anomalies. Experimental results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that Event2Graph outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of detection accuracy, precision, and recall. The proposed method offers a new perspective on multivariate time-series analysis, providing a robust and interpretable framework for anomaly detection. Keywords: Anomaly Detection, Multivariate Time-series, Bipartite Graph, Graph Neural Networks, Event-driven.
