This study aims to address the challenge of understanding and analyzing ancient coin images, which play a significant role in archaeological research and historical studies. The research question focuses on how computer vision and machine learning techniques can be utilized to automate the identification and classification of ancient coins, thereby assisting historians and archaeologists in interpreting cultural and historical contexts.

Methods/Approach: The research employs state-of-the-art convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to process and classify images of ancient coins. A large dataset comprising various types of coins, categorized by region, period, and iconography, was assembled. Advanced image preprocessing techniques were applied to enhance the quality and features of the coin images before feeding them into the CNN models. The methodology also includes a comparative analysis with traditional image recognition frameworks to highlight the efficacy of deep learning approaches in this domain.

Results/Findings: The CNN models demonstrated a significant improvement in the accuracy of coin classification, achieving a high precision and recall rate compared to existing methods. The system was effective in identifying subtle variations in coin design, leading to the successful categorization of underrepresented and rare coin types. The comparative analysis illustrated the superiority of the proposed approach in terms of both speed and accuracy.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes to the field of digital archaeology by providing a robust tool for the automated analysis of ancient coinage. The implications of this work extend to enhancing the digitization efforts in museums and archives, enabling more comprehensive and efficient historical analyses. Additionally, by reducing the manual effort required in coin identification, the study paves the way for broader applications of machine learning in cultural heritage preservation. The novel integration of CNNs in the study of ancient coins represents a significant advancement, with potential applications in various artefacts' classification tasks.

Key Keywords: ancient coins, computer vision, convolutional neural networks, CNNs, image classification, digital archaeology, cultural heritage preservation.
