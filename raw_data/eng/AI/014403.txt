Time series forecasting is crucial in various domains, yet deep learning models often struggle with shape and temporal distortions present in time series data. This paper introduces a novel loss function, Shape and Time Distortion (STD) Loss, designed to enhance the robustness of deep learning models against these distortions. The STD Loss comprises two components: a shape loss that focuses on preserving the overall pattern of the time series and a time distortion loss that penalizes temporal misalignments. We integrate the STD Loss with existing forecasting models and evaluate their performance on benchmark datasets exhibiting varying degrees of shape and time distortions. Results demonstrate that models trained with the STD Loss consistently outperform those trained with conventional loss functions, such as Mean Squared Error (MSE), particularly when forecasting time series with significant distortions. Ablation studies further validate the contribution of each component of the STD Loss. This work offers a practical approach to improve the accuracy and reliability of deep time series forecasting models in real-world scenarios. Keywords: Time Series Forecasting, Deep Learning, Loss Function, Shape Distortion, Temporal Distortion.
