This paper addresses the challenge of off-policy reinforcement learning in environments with delayed rewards, where the reward signal is not immediately available after taking an action. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that can efficiently learn from delayed rewards and improve the agent's performance in such scenarios. We propose a new off-policy reinforcement learning algorithm that incorporates a delayed reward handler, which uses a predictive model to estimate the expected reward and updates the policy accordingly. Our method utilizes a combination of deep learning and reinforcement learning techniques to handle the delayed rewards. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing off-policy reinforcement learning methods in environments with delayed rewards, achieving significant improvements in cumulative rewards and convergence speed. The key contributions of this research include the development of a novel delayed reward handling mechanism and the demonstration of its effectiveness in off-policy reinforcement learning. Our work has important implications for real-world applications, such as robotics and recommendation systems, where delayed rewards are common. Key keywords: off-policy reinforcement learning, delayed rewards, deep learning, predictive modeling, reinforcement learning algorithms.
