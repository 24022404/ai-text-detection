This paper presents a novel neural network-based anomaly detection system designed specifically for on-device learning on edge devices. The primary objective is to address the growing need for real-time, adaptive security and fault detection in resource-constrained environments, where conventional cloud-based solutions may not be feasible due to latency, privacy, or connectivity concerns. The proposed approach utilizes a lightweight deep learning model tailored for efficient execution on edge hardware, incorporating continual learning methods to enable adaptation to evolving data patterns directly on the device. Extensive experiments were conducted on multiple benchmark datasets and real-world edge platforms to evaluate detection accuracy, computational efficiency, and memory footprint. Results demonstrate that our on-device anomaly detector achieves high detection rates with minimal false positives, while maintaining low latency and efficient resource usage compared to traditional cloud-based and static model approaches. The system also proves robust in dynamic environments by autonomously updating its model in response to new data. This work advances the field of edge artificial intelligence by enabling smart, privacy-preserving, and adaptive anomaly detection at the network edge, with potential applications in IoT security, industrial monitoring, and mobile computing. Key keywords: neural networks, anomaly detection, on-device learning, edge computing, continual learning, IoT security.
