Estimating relative camera pose between RGB-D scans is crucial for scene reconstruction and robotic mapping. This paper introduces a novel approach for extreme relative pose estimation by leveraging scene completion. We hypothesize that completing the scene from the perspective of each scan provides a more robust representation for pose estimation, especially when overlap is limited or significant viewpoint changes exist. Our method uses a deep learning-based scene completion network to generate complete scene geometries from sparse RGB-D inputs. Then, a point cloud registration algorithm aligns the completed scenes to estimate the relative pose. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing methods in scenarios with extreme viewpoint changes and limited overlap, achieving higher accuracy and robustness. The results highlight the effectiveness of scene completion as a powerful tool for enhancing relative pose estimation, paving the way for improved 3D reconstruction and simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) systems.

Keywords: Relative Pose Estimation, Scene Completion, RGB-D, Point Cloud Registration, Deep Learning, 3D Reconstruction.
