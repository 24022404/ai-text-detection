This paper introduces a novel ensemble method utilizing example-dependent and cost-sensitive decision trees to improve classification accuracy, particularly in scenarios with imbalanced datasets and varying misclassification costs. The proposed approach constructs an ensemble where each decision tree is trained on a subset of the data, weighted according to both the instance's inherent importance and the cost associated with misclassifying it. This is achieved by dynamically adjusting the sampling probabilities and splitting criteria of the decision trees based on the specific instance and its associated cost matrix. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed ensemble method outperforms traditional decision tree ensembles and existing cost-sensitive learning algorithms in terms of both accuracy and cost reduction. The key contribution lies in the adaptive, example-specific cost integration within an ensemble framework, offering a robust and efficient solution for cost-sensitive classification problems. Keywords: Ensemble Learning, Decision Trees, Cost-Sensitive Learning, Imbalanced Data, Classification.
