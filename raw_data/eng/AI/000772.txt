This research explores the development of a smartphone-based system for colorimetric detection leveraging machine learning techniques. The study addresses the need for a portable, cost-effective, and accurate diagnostic tool capable of analyzing colorimetric assays, frequently used in fields such as healthcare, environmental monitoring, and food safety.

Methods/Approach: Our approach involves integrating a smartphone camera with a machine learning model to analyze colorimetric data. We developed a mobile application that captures colorimetric images and processes them through a convolutional neural network (CNN) designed to interpret subtle color variations, providing a quantitative analysis of the assay results. The model was trained using a diverse dataset of colorimetric assays to ensure robustness and accuracy.

Results/Findings: The developed system demonstrated high accuracy in detecting and quantifying color variations correlating to specific concentrations in colorimetric assays. When tested across multiple sample types, the system achieved precision and sensitivity competitive with conventional laboratory equipment. Comparative analysis showed that our solution reduces both cost and time involved in diagnostic processes without compromising reliability.

Conclusion/Implications: This study presents a novel smartphone-based colorimetric detection system that combines the accessibility of mobile technology with the power of machine learning. Our findings indicate that this approach can serve as an effective alternative to traditional methods, particularly in remote or resource-limited settings. The system's potential applications span across various industries, offering a significant contribution to the field of on-site diagnostic technologies. Key keywords include colorimetric detection, smartphone imaging, convolutional neural network, machine learning, and portable diagnostics.
