Addressing the challenge of generating accurate image captions, particularly when novel objects not present in the training data are encountered, is a primary concern in the field of computer vision. This research aims to enhance the adaptability and accuracy of image captioning systems through online vocabulary expansion.

Methods/Approach: The proposed approach introduces an innovative model that dynamically expands its vocabulary as new object categories are encountered. This is achieved by integrating external text data in real-time to augment the system's pre-existing lexicon. The model utilizes a combination of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for feature extraction and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) for sequence generation to effectively associate visual information with linguistic representations.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate that the online vocabulary expansion algorithm significantly improves captioning quality for images with novel objects, surpassing traditional methods that rely solely on pre-trained vocabularies. Comparative analyses show enhanced performance metrics, including precision and recall, with our method achieving superior adaptability to unseen categories while maintaining fluency in generated captions.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a novel framework for real-time vocabulary adaptation in image captioning systems, thereby expanding their application scope in dynamic environments where continual learning is essential. The approach facilitates more accurate and context-aware narratives in image descriptions, promoting advancements in artificial intelligence models geared towards human-like comprehension and communication. Keywords: image captioning, novel objects, vocabulary expansion, computer vision, neural networks, dynamic adaptation.
