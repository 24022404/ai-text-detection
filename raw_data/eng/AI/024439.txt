This paper presents a novel end-to-end trainable framework for multi-instance pose estimation leveraging the power of transformer architectures. The objective is to accurately localize and predict the poses of multiple human subjects within a single image, addressing key challenges such as occlusion, varying scales, and complex interactions between instances. Our approach eliminates the need for traditional multi-stage or post-processing pipelines by unifying detection and pose estimation within a single transformer-based model. Specifically, we design a unified encoder-decoder structure that jointly learns to represent both spatial relationships and instance associations, enabling the simultaneous detection and pose regression of multiple individuals. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art accuracy, outperforming conventional convolutional neural network (CNN) based and top-down bottom-up architectures in both precision and computational efficiency. Ablation studies further showcase the robustness of our design across crowded scenes with overlapping subjects. The proposed framework significantly streamlines the multi-instance pose estimation pipeline, offering an effective solution for real-time human analysis tasks in video surveillance, human-computer interaction, and sports analytics. Keywords: multi-instance pose estimation, transformers, end-to-end learning, human pose detection, deep learning.
