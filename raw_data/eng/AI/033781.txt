Accurate 3D human pose estimation from video sequences is a challenging task with applications in areas such as motion capture, action recognition, and virtual reality. This paper introduces an enhanced approach for 3D human pose estimation from video by leveraging an attention-based neural network incorporating dilated convolutions. Our method aims to improve the accuracy and robustness of pose estimation by effectively capturing both spatial and temporal dependencies within video frames. The proposed network utilizes dilated convolutions to expand the receptive field and capture long-range contextual information, while the attention mechanism focuses on salient features relevant to pose estimation. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of pose estimation accuracy and robustness to occlusions. The proposed model's ability to capture intricate human movements from videos offers significant potential for advancements in various human-computer interaction applications. Keywords: 3D human pose estimation, video analysis, attention mechanism, dilated convolutions, neural networks.
