This paper addresses the challenge of face age progression by integrating advanced techniques for attribute manipulation to achieve highly realistic and controllable aging effects in facial images. The research aims to develop a novel model that not only progresses or regresses age features but also allows for the manipulation of facial attributes such as hairstyle, skin tone, and expression, while preserving the identity of the individual.

Methods: We propose a hybrid deep learning framework that leverages Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) combined with attribute editing techniques. Our approach involves a multi-stage model that first extracts facial features and attributes, then progressively applies transformations to simulate aging, ensuring nuanced control over specific attributes throughout the process.

Results: The proposed model demonstrates superior performance in generating age-progressed images, verified through quantitative metrics and expert assessments. Our system achieves a high level of identity preservation and demonstrates the capability to flexibly alter attributes without compromising the age progression realism. Comparative analysis with existing methods exhibits marked improvements in naturalness and detail accuracy.

Conclusion: Our research contributes to the fields of computer vision and image processing by offering an advanced tool for face age progression, which could be applied in various domains such as digital entertainment, forensics, and personalized digital healthcare. This study pushes the boundaries of attribute manipulation within age transformation frameworks, setting a new standard for realism and control.

Keywords: Face Age Progression, Attribute Manipulation, Generative Adversarial Networks, Deep Learning, Identity Preservation.
