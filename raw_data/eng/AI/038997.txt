Gastric cancer diagnosis via histopathology image analysis is crucial for effective treatment planning. However, the subtle and complex features within these images pose significant challenges for accurate classification. This paper introduces GasHis-Transformer, a novel multi-scale visual transformer architecture designed specifically for gastric histopathology image classification. Our approach employs a hierarchical transformer encoder to capture both local and global contextual information across multiple image resolutions. This multi-scale representation enables the model to effectively learn discriminative features from varying magnifications, addressing the heterogeneity present in histopathology slides. Experimental results on a large, publicly available gastric histopathology dataset demonstrate that GasHis-Transformer achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing convolutional neural network-based and transformer-based methods. The improved classification accuracy highlights the potential of GasHis-Transformer to assist pathologists in making more accurate and efficient diagnoses, leading to improved patient outcomes. Keywords: Histopathology, Gastric Cancer, Visual Transformer, Multi-scale Learning, Image Classification, Deep Learning.
