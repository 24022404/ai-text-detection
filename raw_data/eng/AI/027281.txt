This paper presents a comprehensive survey on the joint task of object detection and pose estimation using monocular vision, a crucial aspect of computer vision. The objective is to provide an in-depth analysis of the existing methods, highlighting their strengths and weaknesses, and identifying key challenges. We discuss various approaches, including deep learning-based techniques, such as convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and transformer models, which have shown promising results in detecting objects and estimating their 6D poses from a single image. Our survey reveals that most state-of-the-art methods rely on anchor-based detection, keypoint estimation, and pose refinement techniques. The results show that these methods achieve impressive performance on benchmark datasets, such as T-LESS and ObjectNet3D. We conclude that joint object detection and pose estimation is a rapidly evolving field, with significant contributions from the computer vision community. The survey highlights the importance of monocular vision in various applications, including robotics, autonomous driving, and augmented reality, and identifies potential avenues for future research, such as improving pose estimation accuracy and exploring the use of attention mechanisms and graph neural networks. Key keywords: monocular vision, object detection, pose estimation, deep learning, computer vision, 6D pose estimation, CNNs, transformer models.
