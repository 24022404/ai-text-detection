Accurate 6D pose estimation is critical for applications in robotics, augmented reality, and autonomous systems. This paper introduces a novel Multi-View Matching Network (MVMN) designed to address the challenges of estimating the 6D pose of objects in complex, cluttered environments. Unlike traditional single-view approaches, MVMN leverages multi-view data to enhance robustness and accuracy by integrating feature matching across multiple perspectives. The proposed network employs a hybrid architecture combining convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for feature extraction and a transformer-based module for cross-view feature alignment. This enables the model to effectively handle occlusions and varying lighting conditions. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that MVMN achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of both accuracy and computational efficiency. The results highlight the network's ability to generalize across diverse object categories and real-world scenarios. This work contributes a scalable and efficient solution for 6D pose estimation, with potential applications in industrial automation and interactive systems.  

**Keywords**: 6D pose estimation, multi-view matching, convolutional neural networks, transformer, robotics, augmented reality.
