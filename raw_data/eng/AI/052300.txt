This paper addresses the critical issue of bias in learned generative models, which can lead to inaccurate representations of real-world data distributions. Our objective is to develop a novel method for bias correction using likelihood-free importance weighting, enabling more accurate and reliable generative modeling. We propose a new approach that leverages importance weighting to correct for biases in learned generative models, without requiring likelihood functions or explicit density estimates. Our method utilizes a likelihood-free framework, allowing for efficient and flexible bias correction in a wide range of generative models. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in correcting biases and improving the accuracy of generative models, particularly in scenarios where likelihood functions are intractable or difficult to compute. The key contributions of this research include a novel likelihood-free importance weighting method, which provides a significant improvement over existing bias correction techniques. Our work has important implications for applications such as image and video generation, data imputation, and probabilistic modeling, where accurate and unbiased generative models are crucial. Key keywords: generative models, bias correction, likelihood-free importance weighting, probabilistic modeling, deep learning.
