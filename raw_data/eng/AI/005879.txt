This paper introduces Points2Vec, a novel unsupervised learning approach for object-level feature learning from point clouds. The objective is to enable effective representation of 3D objects without relying on labeled data. Our method leverages a vector-based embedding technique to learn compact and informative features from point cloud data. By utilizing a self-supervised framework, Points2Vec learns to identify and represent object-level structures, facilitating downstream tasks such as object recognition, segmentation, and reconstruction. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our approach, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of feature quality and robustness. The key contributions of this work include the introduction of a scalable and efficient unsupervised learning pipeline for point cloud data, and the demonstration of its potential applications in various 3D vision tasks. Our approach has significant implications for fields such as robotics, autonomous driving, and computer-aided design, where high-quality 3D representations are crucial. Key keywords: point clouds, unsupervised learning, object-level features, 3D vision, self-supervised learning, vector-based embedding.
