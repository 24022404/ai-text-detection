Declarative process modeling has gained significant attention for its ability to capture flexible and constraint-based business processes. However, existing methods for discovering declarative models from event logs often struggle with balancing accuracy and computational efficiency. This paper introduces *DisCoveR*, a novel framework designed to address these challenges by leveraging advanced algorithms and optimization techniques. The proposed approach combines constraint mining with efficient pruning strategies to reduce computational overhead while maintaining high model precision. We evaluate *DisCoveR* on real-world and synthetic datasets, demonstrating its superior performance in terms of both accuracy and runtime compared to state-of-the-art methods. Experimental results show that *DisCoveR* achieves up to 20% improvement in precision and recall while reducing discovery time by 35%. The framework’s scalability and robustness make it a practical solution for large-scale process mining tasks. This research contributes to the field by providing a more efficient and accurate tool for declarative process model discovery, with potential applications in business process management, compliance checking, and process optimization.  

**Keywords**: Declarative process models, process mining, event logs, constraint discovery, optimization, business process management.
