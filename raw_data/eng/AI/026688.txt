This paper explores the development of an adaptive teaching system that leverages student feedback to enhance the learning experience. The objective is to create a personalized education framework that adjusts its teaching strategies based on real-time student input. Our approach utilizes machine learning algorithms to analyze student feedback, identifying areas of improvement and optimizing the teaching methodology accordingly. The system integrates natural language processing to interpret and categorize student responses, enabling the adaptation of instructional materials and delivery methods. Results show significant improvements in student engagement and knowledge retention, with the adaptive system outperforming traditional teaching methods in comparative studies. The findings of this research contribute to the development of more effective and responsive educational technologies, highlighting the potential of student-centered feedback mechanisms to revolutionize the way we teach and learn. Key contributions include the design of a novel feedback-driven teaching framework and the demonstration of its efficacy in enhancing student outcomes, with implications for the broader adoption of adaptive learning systems in education.
