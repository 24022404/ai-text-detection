Abstract: The objective of this study is to introduce SSD-GAN, a novel approach leveraging Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to assess and enhance the realism of synthetic images in both spatial and spectral domains. Traditional GANs have achieved notable success in image generation, yet they often struggle to capture finer details necessary for realness verification in diverse domains. Our method integrates a dual-domain analysis to refine the fidelity of generated images by simultaneously evaluating and optimizing the spatial and spectral characteristics. We employ a feedback mechanism within the GAN framework that iteratively improves generative performance. The SSD-GAN model is benchmarked against existing state-of-the-art methods, demonstrating superior results in generating realistic images with enriched detail and color accuracy. Our experimental results exhibit a significant advancement in spectral signature maintenance, which is critical for applications such as remote sensing and medical imaging. This research contributes to the field by establishing a more comprehensive model of authenticity assessment, fostering the development of applications that require high-fidelity image synthesis. Keywords: SSD-GAN, image synthesis, spatial domain, spectral domain, GAN, realness verification, image fidelity.
