Neural rendering techniques show promise for photorealistic image synthesis but often require large, real-world datasets for training. Acquiring such datasets for complex materials like fur is challenging and expensive. This paper introduces SyntheticFur, a novel, large-scale synthetic dataset designed specifically for training neural rendering models to realistically render fur. The dataset comprises over 100,000 rendered images of diverse fur styles, colors, densities, and lighting conditions, generated using a physically based rendering pipeline. We evaluate the effectiveness of SyntheticFur by training a neural radiance field (NeRF) model and comparing its performance to models trained on limited real-world fur data. Results demonstrate that models trained on SyntheticFur exhibit superior generalization and render higher-quality fur appearance. The availability of SyntheticFur addresses a critical gap in training data for fur rendering and facilitates the development of more robust and realistic neural rendering algorithms. This work contributes a valuable resource to the research community and opens avenues for advanced fur simulation and rendering. Keywords: neural rendering, fur rendering, synthetic data, dataset, NeRF, physically based rendering.
