Specificity and false positive rate (FPR) are closely related metrics used to assess classifier performance. The ROC curve, as shown in Figure 4.4, plots the true positive rate against the FPR, providing a visual representation of a model’s discriminative ability. Figure 4.3 displays the ROC-AUC graph, with the area under the ROC curve (AUC) ranging from 0 to 1. A higher AUC value indicates a model with stronger classification performance.
