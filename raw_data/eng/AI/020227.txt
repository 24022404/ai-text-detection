Multi-view clustering aims to group data points described by multiple feature sets, or views. This paper introduces a novel Deep Embedded Multi-view Clustering framework with Collaborative Training (DEMC-CT) designed to enhance clustering performance and robustness. Our approach leverages deep neural networks to learn shared latent representations that capture complementary information across different views, mitigating the challenges posed by noisy or incomplete data. The core innovation lies in the collaborative training strategy, where each view’s learned embedding is refined by information from other views, promoting consensus and improving clustering accuracy. Specifically, we employ a deep autoencoder architecture for each view, forcing the latent representations to align with a common clustering structure through a shared clustering layer. Extensive experiments on several benchmark multi-view datasets demonstrate that DEMC-CT outperforms state-of-the-art multi-view clustering methods in terms of normalized mutual information (NMI) and accuracy. The results highlight the effectiveness of collaborative training in improving the quality and consistency of learned representations, leading to more accurate and reliable clustering results. Keywords: Multi-view Clustering, Deep Learning, Collaborative Training, Data Fusion, Unsupervised Learning.
