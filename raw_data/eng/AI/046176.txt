This paper addresses the critical challenge of detecting prohibited items that are partially or fully occluded within X-ray security inspection images—a scenario common in real-world security checkpoints. To advance research in this domain, we introduce a comprehensive X-ray Security Inspection Benchmark specifically tailored for occluded item detection. The benchmark includes a diverse dataset with varying degrees of occlusion, object types, and realistic baggage scenarios, providing a robust platform for evaluation and comparison.

Furthermore, we propose the De-occlusion Attention Module (DAM), a novel deep learning component designed to enhance feature extraction and localization of concealed objects in cluttered X-ray images. DAM selectively focuses on relevant regions while mitigating the impact of overlapping or obscuring items, effectively recovering critical information lost due to occlusion. We integrate DAM into state-of-the-art object detection frameworks and conduct extensive experiments on our benchmark dataset.

Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms traditional detection algorithms and several recent attention-based models in terms of precision and recall, especially under severe occlusion conditions. The De-occlusion Attention Module excels in distinguishing and identifying prohibited items that were previously difficult to detect, leading to higher overall security screening accuracy.

This research contributes a valuable dataset, an innovative attention-based module, and comprehensive performance baselines for the advancement of automated X-ray security systems. Our work holds significant implications for enhancing public safety in transportation hubs, leveraging computer vision, deep learning, attention mechanisms, and image de-occlusion techniques.

Keywords: X-ray security inspection, occluded object detection, benchmark dataset, de-occlusion, attention module, deep learning, computer vision, prohibited items.
