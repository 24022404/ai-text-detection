This paper introduces a novel Temporal Modulation Network (TMN) for Controllable Space-Time Video Super-Resolution (STVSR), addressing the challenge of simultaneously enhancing spatial resolution and temporal frame rates in video sequences. Traditional methods often struggle with balancing these two aspects, leading to artifacts or suboptimal results. The proposed TMN leverages a multi-scale temporal modulation mechanism that dynamically adjusts temporal features across different resolutions, enabling precise control over the interpolation and upscaling processes. By integrating a lightweight yet effective attention mechanism, the network efficiently captures spatio-temporal dependencies, ensuring high-quality reconstruction. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that TMN outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of both visual quality and quantitative metrics, achieving superior PSNR and SSIM scores. The controllability aspect allows users to fine-tune the trade-off between spatial and temporal enhancements, making it highly adaptable for various real-world applications, such as video restoration, surveillance, and medical imaging. This work contributes a significant advancement in STVSR by offering a flexible, efficient, and high-performance solution that bridges the gap between spatial and temporal video enhancement.  

**Keywords**: Space-Time Video Super-Resolution, Temporal Modulation Network, Video Enhancement, Spatio-Temporal Dependencies, Attention Mechanism, Controllable Reconstruction.
