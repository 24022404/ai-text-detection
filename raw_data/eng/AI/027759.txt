This paper introduces a novel spectral failure mode for Graph Convolutional Networks (GCNs), a type of neural network designed for graph-structured data. The objective is to identify and analyze a simple yet critical failure mode that can significantly impact the performance of GCNs. Our approach involves a spectral analysis of the graph convolutional operator, which reveals a previously overlooked vulnerability. We demonstrate that this failure mode can cause GCNs to perform poorly on certain graph structures, leading to suboptimal results. Our findings show that the failure mode is related to the eigenvalues of the graph Laplacian and can be mitigated by modifying the graph convolutional layer. The implications of this research are significant, as it highlights the importance of considering the spectral properties of graphs when designing GCNs. Our contributions include a new understanding of the limitations of GCNs and a simple modification to improve their robustness. Key keywords: Graph Convolutional Networks, spectral analysis, graph Laplacian, neural networks, graph-structured data.
