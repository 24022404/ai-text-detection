This paper presents a novel application of cycle-consistent generative adversarial networks (CycleGANs) for neural style transfer using data from the Chang'E-4 mission. The objective is to develop an effective method for transferring the style of images captured by the Chang'E-4 lunar probe to other images, while preserving the content and structure of the original images. Our approach utilizes a CycleGAN architecture, which consists of two generators and two discriminators, to learn the mapping between the style and content images. The results show that our method can successfully transfer the style of Chang'E-4 images to other images, achieving high-quality and realistic results. The evaluation metrics demonstrate the superiority of our approach compared to existing style transfer methods. This research contributes to the field of computer vision and has potential applications in areas such as image processing, robotics, and space exploration. Key keywords: CycleGAN, neural style transfer, Chang'E-4, lunar exploration, computer vision, generative models.
