This paper addresses the challenge of interpreting deep metric learning models, which are widely used for image retrieval, clustering, and classification tasks. Our objective is to develop a visual explanation framework that provides insights into the decision-making process of these models. We propose a novel approach that combines feature importance and attention mechanisms to generate visual explanations for deep metric learning. Our method utilizes a gradient-based technique to highlight the most relevant regions in the input images that contribute to the similarity or dissimilarity between images. The results show that our approach can effectively identify the discriminative features used by the model, leading to improved model interpretability and transparency. Our framework has significant implications for applications where model explainability is crucial, such as medical image analysis and autonomous vehicles. The key contributions of this research include a novel visual explanation framework, improved model interpretability, and enhanced transparency in deep metric learning models, with relevant keywords including deep learning, metric learning, visual explanation, model interpretability, and computer vision.
