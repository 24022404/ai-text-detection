This paper proposes a novel deep learning framework for detecting partial discharges on insulated overhead conductors, leveraging frequency and phase attention mechanisms. The objective is to enhance the accuracy and reliability of partial discharge detection, which is crucial for maintaining the integrity of power transmission systems. Our approach utilizes a custom-designed neural network architecture that incorporates frequency and phase attention modules to selectively focus on relevant signal features. The model is trained on a comprehensive dataset of partial discharge signals, and its performance is evaluated against traditional detection methods. The results demonstrate significant improvements in detection accuracy and robustness, particularly in noisy environments. The proposed framework contributes to the development of more efficient and reliable condition monitoring systems for power transmission infrastructure, with potential applications in predictive maintenance and fault prevention. Key innovations include the integration of frequency and phase attention mechanisms, enabling the model to adaptively weigh signal features and improve detection performance. Relevant keywords: partial discharge detection, deep learning, frequency attention, phase attention, power transmission systems, condition monitoring.
