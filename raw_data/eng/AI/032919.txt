This paper proposes ESResNet, a novel approach to environmental sound classification leveraging models pre-trained on visual domains. The objective is to improve the accuracy of sound classification by exploiting the knowledge learned from large-scale visual data. Our method utilizes a ResNet-based architecture, fine-tuned for audio classification tasks, and explores the transferability of visual features to the audio domain. Experimental results demonstrate that ESResNet outperforms state-of-the-art audio-based models, achieving a significant increase in classification accuracy. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of visual domain pre-training in capturing meaningful sound patterns. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and efficient environmental sound classification systems, with potential applications in areas such as smart homes, surveillance, and healthcare monitoring. The proposed ESResNet model showcases the potential of cross-domain knowledge transfer, paving the way for further research in multimodal learning and audio-visual feature extraction, with relevant keywords including environmental sound classification, visual domain models, ResNet, transfer learning, and audio-visual feature extraction.
