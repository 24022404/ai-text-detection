This paper introduces *Talk-to-Edit*, a novel framework for fine-grained facial editing through natural language dialog. The objective of this research is to enable precise and intuitive facial image manipulation by leveraging conversational interactions, addressing the limitations of traditional text-based or attribute-driven editing methods. We propose a multimodal approach that integrates a pre-trained language model with a generative adversarial network (GAN) to interpret user instructions and generate corresponding facial edits. The system dynamically refines edits based on iterative dialog, allowing users to achieve highly specific and personalized results. Experimental results demonstrate that *Talk-to-Edit* outperforms existing methods in terms of edit accuracy, user satisfaction, and visual quality, as validated through both quantitative metrics and user studies. The framework’s ability to handle complex, context-aware instructions highlights its potential for applications in digital content creation, virtual avatars, and personalized media. This work contributes to the advancement of interactive AI systems by bridging the gap between natural language understanding and generative image editing, offering a more intuitive and user-centric approach to facial manipulation.  

**Keywords**: facial editing, natural language processing, GAN, dialog systems, multimodal AI, image manipulation.
