This paper investigates the vulnerability of image retrieval systems to universal perturbation attacks. Image retrieval, a critical component in many applications, can be significantly compromised by subtle, image-agnostic perturbations. We introduce a novel approach for generating universal adversarial perturbations specifically tailored to disrupt image retrieval performance. Our method leverages a generative adversarial network (GAN) to craft perturbations that maximize the dissimilarity between feature embeddings of original and perturbed images within the retrieval system. The generated perturbations are then optimized through an iterative process targeting a diverse set of images to ensure universality. Experimental results on benchmark image retrieval datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves a substantial drop in retrieval accuracy across various architectures with a single, universal perturbation. These findings highlight a significant security concern for image retrieval systems and underscore the need for robust defense mechanisms. Keywords: Image Retrieval, Adversarial Attack, Universal Perturbation, Generative Adversarial Network, Security, Feature Embedding.
