This paper explores the development and application of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) for image and video synthesis, addressing the challenge of generating realistic and diverse visual content. Our approach involves designing novel GAN architectures that leverage advanced techniques such as deep learning and convolutional neural networks to improve the quality and coherence of synthesized images and videos. We evaluate the performance of our models using various metrics, including visual quality, diversity, and computational efficiency, and demonstrate their effectiveness in a range of applications, including image-to-image translation, video generation, and data augmentation. The results show that our GAN-based methods outperform existing state-of-the-art approaches in terms of visual realism and diversity, with potential applications in fields such as computer vision, robotics, and multimedia processing. Our research contributes to the advancement of GANs for image and video synthesis, highlighting the potential of these models for generating high-quality visual content and enabling new applications in areas such as AI-powered content creation, virtual reality, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: GANs, image synthesis, video synthesis, deep learning, computer vision, AI models.
