The research presented in this paper addresses the challenge of exploration inefficiencies in reinforcement learning algorithms, specifically focusing on enhancing the ε-greedy exploration strategy. Traditional approaches often suffer in environments where the optimal exploration strategy requires actions contingent on both current and future states. This study introduces a novel method for temporally-extended exploration to improve learning efficiency and policy performance.

Methods/Approach: We propose a temporally-extended ε-greedy exploration framework, leveraging action sequences rather than single actions over a fixed horizon. This approach adapts the classical ε-greedy strategy by periodically updating sequences to balance exploration and exploitation more effectively across time. We implement this exploration technique within a reinforcement learning environment and apply it to benchmark tasks with varied complexity levels.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of the proposed temporally-extended ε-greedy exploration compared to traditional ε-greedy methods. Our approach consistently yields faster convergence to optimal policies and enhances performance stability across several complex domains. Notably, our method shows significant efficiency gains in environments with delayed rewards and intricate state transitions, highlighting its advantage over short-sighted, single-action exploration strategies.

Conclusion/Implications: The temporally-extended ε-greedy exploration strategy offers a significant breakthrough in reinforcement learning by addressing the limitations of conventional exploration tactics. Our findings suggest that integrating temporal considerations into exploration mechanisms can substantially improve learning outcomes. This research opens new avenues for developing more robust and effective exploration strategies in dynamic and complex environments, with applications in automated control systems, robotics, and artificial intelligence planning systems.

Key Keywords: ε-greedy, reinforcement learning, exploration strategy, temporally-extended exploration, convergence, policy optimization.
