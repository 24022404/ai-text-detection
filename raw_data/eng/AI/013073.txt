The objective of this research is to improve real-time rendering performance of neural radiance fields, which are computationally intensive for high fidelity 3D scene representations. We introduce PlenOctrees, an innovative approach that constructs a hybrid data structure combining octrees with neural representations to efficiently render complex scenes. Our method leverages the inherent hierarchies of octrees to reduce computational complexity while maintaining the rich detail capture capabilities of neural radiance fields. The results demonstrate that PlenOctrees significantly enhances rendering speed, achieving real-time frame rates without sacrificing image quality. Benchmark comparisons with existing neural radiance field methods reveal substantial improvements in both efficiency and scalability. The implications of this study suggest potential applications in areas requiring rapid scene reconstruction and visualization, such as virtual reality, gaming, and real-time simulation environments. Our contributions lie in the novel integration of system architectures designed for optimal performance in rendering neural scene representations, marking a step forward in making high-quality real-time 3D visualization broadly accessible. Key keywords include neural radiance fields, real-time rendering, octrees, 3D reconstruction, and high-performance computing.
