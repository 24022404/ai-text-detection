Scene text recognition (STR) remains a challenging task due to the diverse appearance variations and complex backgrounds in natural scenes. This paper introduces MASTER, a novel Multi-Aspect Non-local network designed to effectively capture both local and global dependencies within text images, enhancing recognition accuracy. The proposed architecture incorporates a multi-aspect attention mechanism that aggregates contextual information from different perspectives, enabling robust feature representation. Furthermore, a non-local module is employed to model long-range dependencies between distant text regions, addressing challenges posed by irregular text shapes and orientations. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets, including ICDAR, IIIT5k, and SVT, demonstrate that MASTER achieves state-of-the-art performance, surpassing existing methods in both accuracy and robustness. These results highlight the effectiveness of our multi-aspect non-local approach for scene text recognition. Keywords: Scene Text Recognition, Non-local Network, Attention Mechanism, Deep Learning, Computer Vision.
