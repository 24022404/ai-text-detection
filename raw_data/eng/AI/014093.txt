This paper explores the application of reinforcement learning to the soccer dribbling task, with the objective of developing an autonomous agent capable of navigating complex environments while maintaining possession of the ball. Our approach utilizes a deep reinforcement learning framework, combining convolutional neural networks with policy gradient methods to learn effective dribbling policies. The results demonstrate significant improvements in the agent's ability to dribble past opponents and navigate through crowded spaces, outperforming traditional rule-based approaches. Key findings include the development of a novel reward function that encourages the agent to balance ball control with progressive movement towards the goal. The implications of this research contribute to the advancement of autonomous robotics and artificial intelligence in sports, with potential applications in areas such as robotic soccer and human-robot interaction. Our work highlights the effectiveness of reinforcement learning in complex, dynamic environments, and paves the way for future research in this field, with relevant keywords including reinforcement learning, deep learning, robotics, and autonomous systems.
