This paper investigates an enhanced approach for optimal dispatch of Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) using reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms integrated with a model-based optimizer. The objective is to address the challenges of efficient energy storage management and cost-effective grid operation under dynamic electricity markets and stochastic renewable energy generation. The proposed framework leverages a deep RL agent trained to make real-time dispatch decisions, augmented by a model-based optimization layer that incorporates system constraints and forecasts to refine action selection. Extensive simulations are conducted on realistic grid scenarios, and the performance is evaluated in terms of economic returns, state-of-charge management, and system reliability. Results demonstrate that the hybrid RL and model-based optimizer approach significantly outperforms standalone RL and conventional rule-based strategies, achieving higher operational efficiency and robust adaptation to uncertain demand and price signals. The study contributes a scalable and generalizable methodology for intelligent BESS control, with implications for improved grid stability, renewable integration, and reduced operational costs. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, battery energy storage, model-based optimization, energy dispatch, smart grid, artificial intelligence.
