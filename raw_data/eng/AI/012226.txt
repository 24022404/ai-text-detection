The proliferation of fake images generated by advanced artificial intelligence technologies has raised critical concerns for digital security and media integrity. This paper addresses the vulnerability of current fake image detection models to adversarial attacks by introducing a novel approach for crafting imperceptible adversarial examples. Our method leverages a tailored perturbation generation algorithm that produces minimal visual changes, indistinguishable to human observers, while significantly reducing the detection accuracy of state-of-the-art fake image classifiers. We systematically evaluate the proposed attack across multiple datasets and popular detection models, measuring both perturbation imperceptibility and attack success rates. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach achieves high evasion success rates (>90%) while maintaining structural similarity and perceptual invisibility. Comparative analysis against existing adversarial example generation techniques highlights our method’s superior balance between stealth and effectiveness. The findings not only expose a critical weakness in current fake image detection systems but also emphasize the urgent need for more robust defensive strategies. This research advances the understanding of adversarial robustness in AI-driven image forensics and provides practical insights for developing resilient detection models. Key keywords: adversarial examples, fake image detection, AI security, image forensics, imperceptible perturbations.
