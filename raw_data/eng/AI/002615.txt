Class imbalance is a significant challenge in deep learning that affects model performance, particularly in tasks where minority classes are underrepresented. This paper presents ReMix, a novel method for calibrated resampling tailored to address class imbalance in deep learning. The objective of ReMix is to improve model accuracy and generalization by adjusting the resampling strategy based on class representation. Our approach involves dynamically modifying the probability of selecting samples from minority and majority classes, thereby creating a balanced and effective training distribution. The key findings demonstrate that ReMix outperforms traditional resampling methods and advanced techniques by significantly enhancing the performance metrics of deep learning models, especially recall and precision in imbalanced datasets. The implications of this study highlight the potential of ReMix to be incorporated into various deep learning pipelines, offering improvements in domains such as medical diagnosis, fraud detection, and sentiment analysis where class imbalance is prevalent. Keywords associated with this study include class imbalance, deep learning, calibrated resampling, ReMix, and model generalization.
