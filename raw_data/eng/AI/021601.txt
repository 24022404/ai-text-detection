This paper addresses the challenge of automatically detecting highlights in figure skating videos by estimating blink probability, a crucial aspect of judging performance quality. Our objective is to develop a reliable method for identifying thrilling moments in figure skating competitions. We propose a computer vision approach that utilizes machine learning algorithms to analyze video frames and detect periods of high intensity, such as jumps and spins. Our method employs a deep learning model to extract features from video sequences and predict blink probability, which is then used to identify highlight segments. Experimental results show that our approach achieves high accuracy in detecting highlights, outperforming existing methods. The key findings of this study demonstrate the effectiveness of blink probability estimation in highlight detection, contributing to the development of automated video analysis tools for figure skating and other sports. This research has significant implications for applications such as sports analytics, video summarization, and viewer engagement, with potential to enhance the viewing experience for figure skating fans. Key keywords: figure skating, highlight detection, blink probability, computer vision, machine learning, video analysis.
