This paper introduces *Ask & Explore*, a novel framework for grounded question answering that facilitates curiosity-driven exploration in dynamic environments. The objective of this research is to enable autonomous agents to generate and answer contextually relevant questions, thereby enhancing their ability to explore and understand complex, unstructured environments. To achieve this, we propose a hybrid approach combining reinforcement learning with a transformer-based question generation model, which dynamically adapts to environmental cues and agent interactions. The system leverages semantic grounding techniques to ensure that questions are both meaningful and actionable within the given context. Experimental results demonstrate that *Ask & Explore* outperforms traditional exploration methods by 18% in task completion efficiency and achieves a 25% improvement in environment comprehension metrics. Furthermore, the framework exhibits robust generalization across diverse domains, including robotics and virtual simulations. The key contribution of this work lies in its ability to bridge the gap between curiosity-driven exploration and grounded question answering, offering a scalable solution for applications in autonomous systems, interactive AI, and intelligent tutoring systems. Keywords: grounded question answering, curiosity-driven exploration, reinforcement learning, transformer models, autonomous agents.
