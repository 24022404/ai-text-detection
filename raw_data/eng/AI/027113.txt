This paper presents a novel approach to satellite image classification using deep learning techniques. The objective is to accurately categorize satellite images into distinct land cover classes, such as urban, forest, and water bodies. A convolutional neural network (CNN) model is developed and trained on a large dataset of satellite images, leveraging techniques like data augmentation and transfer learning to improve performance. The results show that the proposed model achieves high accuracy and outperforms traditional machine learning methods, with a significant reduction in computational complexity. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of deep learning in extracting relevant features from satellite images, enabling accurate classification and potential applications in environmental monitoring, urban planning, and disaster response. The contributions of this research include the development of a robust and efficient satellite image classification framework, demonstrating the potential of deep learning in remote sensing applications, with relevant keywords including CNN, satellite image classification, deep learning, and remote sensing.
