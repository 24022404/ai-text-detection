This paper addresses the challenge of ensuring safety in reinforcement learning (RL) applications. While RL has achieved remarkable success across various domains, integrating safety constraints into the learning process remains a critical problem to solve. Our research introduces a novel Lyapunov-based framework to enhance the safe learning and decision-making capabilities of RL agents.

Methods: We propose a methodology that incorporates Lyapunov stability theory into reinforcement learning algorithms. By leveraging a Lyapunov function, we create a safety shield that evaluates the potential risk associated with an agent's actions. Our approach modifies the reward structure to guide agents towards policies that not only optimize performance but also maintain stability and safety within predefined constraints.

Results: Experiments conducted in simulations demonstrate that our Lyapunov-based reinforcement learning model significantly outperforms traditional RL approaches in meeting safety requirements without compromising on task efficiency. Our analysis shows improved safety assurance while maintaining competitive learning speed and performance, even in complex and dynamic environments.

Conclusion: The integration of Lyapunov stability principles into reinforcement learning frameworks presents a promising avenue for developing safe and reliable AI systems. This research contributes a substantial advancement in the pursuit of trustable AI by providing a robust approach that ensures the safety-critical aspect is naturally embedded in the learning process. Our framework holds the potential for broad applications in areas where safety is paramount, such as autonomous vehicles and robotics.

Keywords: Safe Reinforcement Learning, Lyapunov Stability, Safety Constraints, Policy Optimization, Autonomous Systems, AI Safety.
