This paper addresses the challenge of automatically assessing aesthetic attributes in images, a crucial task for applications ranging from image recommendation to content creation. We propose a novel deep learning framework that integrates global and local image features to predict aesthetic qualities. Our approach employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) to extract global features representing the overall composition and style, while simultaneously utilizing a transformer-based architecture to capture fine-grained local details and their relationships. We introduce a multi-task learning strategy to jointly predict multiple aesthetic attributes, such as symmetry, color harmony, and depth of field, enhancing the model's generalization capability. Experimental results on a large-scale aesthetic image dataset demonstrate that our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art approaches in terms of prediction accuracy and correlation with human perception. The proposed framework offers a robust and efficient solution for aesthetic assessment, with significant implications for improving image curation and personalized user experiences. Keywords: Aesthetic Assessment, Image Quality, Deep Learning, Convolutional Neural Network, Transformer, Multi-task Learning.
