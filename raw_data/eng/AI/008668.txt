This paper investigates the efficacy of leveraging just 15 keypoints for accurate human pose estimation in computer vision applications. The motivation stems from the need to optimize computational resources and simplify model complexity without significantly sacrificing performance. We present a streamlined deep learning-based approach that utilizes a reduced set of anatomically informative keypoints, carefully selected to capture essential aspects of human posture. The proposed method is evaluated on standard human pose datasets, demonstrating competitive accuracy compared to models using denser keypoint annotations while achieving notable improvements in inference speed and resource consumption. Extensive experiments reveal that the 15-keypoint configuration not only reduces the dimensionality of pose representation but also maintains robustness across varied poses and occlusion scenarios. The findings suggest that precise localization and action recognition tasks can be accomplished effectively with significantly fewer keypoints, challenging the prevailing notion that high-resolution keypoint sets are necessary for performance. This work provides practical insights for real-time applications such as motion analysis, augmented reality, and human-computer interaction, where efficiency and accuracy are paramount. Keywords: human pose estimation, keypoints, deep learning, efficiency, computer vision, dimensionality reduction, real-time applications.
