This paper addresses the challenge of text localisation in natural images by leveraging synthetic data. The objective is to develop an effective approach for detecting and localising text in images, which is a crucial task in various applications such as image understanding, robotics, and autonomous vehicles. We propose a novel method that utilises synthetic data generation to augment real-world datasets, thereby improving the performance and robustness of text localisation models. Our approach involves generating synthetic images with realistic text renderings, which are then used to fine-tune a deep learning-based text localisation model. The results show that our synthetic data-based approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods on benchmark datasets, achieving significant improvements in text localisation accuracy and recall. The key contributions of this research include the development of a synthetic data generation pipeline, a robust text localisation model, and a comprehensive evaluation framework. Our work has important implications for applications that require accurate text localisation in natural images, such as scene understanding, image retrieval, and document analysis. Key keywords: synthetic data, text localisation, natural images, deep learning, computer vision.
