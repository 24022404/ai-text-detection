Multivariate time-series anomaly detection is a critical challenge in various domains, including industrial monitoring, healthcare, and cybersecurity. Traditional methods often struggle to capture complex temporal dependencies and interactions among multiple variables. This paper introduces *Event2Graph*, a novel event-driven bipartite graph framework designed to address these limitations. The proposed approach leverages graph-based representations to model relationships between events and variables, enabling more effective anomaly detection in multivariate time-series data. The framework employs a bipartite graph structure where nodes represent events and variables, and edges encode their dynamic interactions. By incorporating temporal event sequences, *Event2Graph* captures both local and global patterns, enhancing its ability to identify anomalies. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that *Event2Graph* outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of detection accuracy and robustness. The framework also exhibits superior scalability and interpretability, making it suitable for real-world applications. This research contributes a novel graph-based perspective to time-series analysis, offering significant potential for improving anomaly detection in complex systems.  

**Keywords**: Multivariate time-series, anomaly detection, bipartite graph, event-driven modeling, temporal dependencies, graph-based learning.
