The increasing deployment of machine learning models in critical applications necessitates trustworthy explanations for their decisions. While empirical explainers, such as SHAP and LIME, offer model-agnostic explanation methods, they often suffer from computational inefficiency, limiting their applicability to complex models and large datasets. This paper introduces a novel framework for generating efficient explanations from empirical explainers, focusing on reducing the computational overhead without sacrificing explanation fidelity. Our approach leverages a combination of instance selection techniques, dimensionality reduction, and optimized sampling strategies to accelerate the explanation process. We evaluate our framework on several benchmark datasets and demonstrate significant speedups compared to traditional empirical explainers, achieving up to a 5x reduction in computation time while maintaining comparable explanation accuracy as measured by established fidelity metrics. Furthermore, we analyze the trade-offs between efficiency and accuracy, providing guidelines for selecting appropriate parameters based on specific application requirements. Our findings contribute to the practical deployment of explainable AI by enabling the efficient generation of explanations for complex models in resource-constrained environments. Keywords: Explainable AI, Empirical Explainers, SHAP, LIME, Efficiency, Instance Selection, Dimensionality Reduction, Model Explanation.
