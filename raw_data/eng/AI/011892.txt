This paper presents a novel approach to static neural compiler optimization using deep reinforcement learning. The objective is to improve the performance of neural networks by optimizing compiler settings, which is a crucial step in deploying machine learning models on various hardware platforms. Our method employs a deep reinforcement learning framework to search for optimal compiler configurations, leveraging the strengths of both static compilation and dynamic optimization. The approach utilizes a policy network to predict the best compiler settings based on the neural network architecture and hardware characteristics. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms traditional static compilation techniques, achieving significant improvements in execution time and memory usage. The key findings highlight the potential of deep reinforcement learning in compiler optimization, enabling more efficient deployment of neural networks on diverse hardware platforms. This research contributes to the development of more efficient machine learning pipelines, with potential applications in areas such as computer vision, natural language processing, and autonomous systems, and is relevant to keywords such as neural compilers, deep reinforcement learning, static optimization, and AI model deployment.
