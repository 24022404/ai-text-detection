This paper addresses the critical issue of reward poisoning attacks in reinforcement learning (RL), which can severely compromise the decision-making capabilities of intelligent systems. The aim is to develop effective defense mechanisms that can identify and mitigate such attacks, ensuring the integrity of the learning process.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel framework that integrates anomaly detection techniques with robust RL algorithms. Our approach involves monitoring the reward signals for irregular patterns indicative of malicious interference. By leveraging statistical analysis and machine learning-based classifiers, the framework adapts to a wide range of attack vectors without requiring prior knowledge of potential threats.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that the proposed defense mechanism significantly enhances the resilience of RL systems against reward poisoning attacks. Our framework achieves a detection accuracy of over 95% across various benchmark environments, outperforming existing state-of-the-art solutions. Furthermore, it maintains high performance in the presence of attacks, with minimal impact on the learning process.

Conclusion/Implications: This research provides a substantial contribution to the field of safe AI, presenting a robust solution to safeguard RL systems against reward manipulation. The proposed method not only fortifies the reliability of RL applications in critical domains such as autonomous vehicles and finance but also sets a foundation for future advancements in secure RL. Keywords include reinforcement learning, reward poisoning, anomaly detection, AI security, and algorithmic robustness.
