This paper addresses the challenge of learning and planning in complex action spaces, a crucial problem in artificial intelligence and robotics. The objective is to develop efficient algorithms that can navigate high-dimensional action spaces and make optimal decisions in real-world applications. We propose a novel approach that combines reinforcement learning with model-based planning, leveraging techniques from deep learning and graph theory. Our method learns a compact representation of the action space and uses this representation to plan optimal trajectories. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in several complex domains, including robotic manipulation and game playing, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods. The key findings of this research include improved sample efficiency, enhanced scalability, and the ability to handle complex action constraints. Our work contributes to the development of more sophisticated autonomous systems, enabling them to operate effectively in challenging environments. The proposed approach has significant implications for applications in robotics, game playing, and other areas involving complex decision-making, and paves the way for further research in learning and planning in complex action spaces, with relevant keywords including reinforcement learning, model-based planning, deep learning, and autonomous systems.
