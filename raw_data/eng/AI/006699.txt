The objective of this paper is to introduce TDIOT, a novel approach that leverages target-driven inference for enhancing deep video object tracking systems. The research addresses the critical challenge of maintaining robust tracking performance across varied and complex video scenarios. The proposed model employs an innovative inference strategy that dynamically adapts tracking parameters in response to target-specific characteristics extracted from the video. Our approach integrates deep neural networks with a sophisticated inference mechanism to optimize tracking accuracy and efficiency. Key findings reveal that TDIOT significantly outperforms traditional object tracking models, achieving higher precision and recall rates, particularly in cluttered and rapidly changing environments. The system's performance was benchmarked against state-of-the-art methods, demonstrating superior adaptability and responsiveness. The implications of this research extend to multiple applications, including surveillance, autonomous driving, and augmented reality, where real-time and reliable object tracking is paramount. By highlighting the contribution of integrating target-driven methodologies into the deep learning framework, this work paves the way for future advancements in video object tracking technologies. Keywords: deep video object tracking, target-driven inference, neural networks, real-time, surveillance, autonomous systems.
