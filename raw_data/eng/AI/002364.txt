This research addresses the growing need for efficient learning mechanisms in temporal knowledge graphs, which encapsulate dynamic relationships and temporal attributes over time. Traditional methods require extensive data for training, often rendering them impractical for time-sensitive applications. Our aim is to introduce a one-shot learning approach tailored specifically for these temporal structures.

Methods/Approach: We develop a novel model combining the principles of one-shot learning with temporal graph neural networks. This model leverages a minimal amount of temporal data while effectively generalizing to unseen scenarios. By incorporating context-aware temporal embeddings and an adaptable memory module, the approach is capable of capturing both the evolving and historical contextual nuances of the data.

Results/Findings: The proposed one-shot learning framework demonstrates superior performance on benchmark temporal knowledge graph datasets compared to conventional multi-shot techniques. We observe significant improvements in prediction accuracy and processing speed, particularly under constraints of limited temporal data. The system efficiently learns new temporal dynamics with a fraction of the data that traditional methods would require.

Conclusion/Implications: Our research contributes an innovative solution to the challenge of resource-efficient learning in temporal knowledge graphs. This one-shot framework offers potential applications in domains requiring real-time insights and adaptive modeling, such as event forecasting and dynamic network analysis. The findings underscore the feasibility of incorporating minimal data for robust temporal reasoning, setting a new direction for future research in knowledge graph management.

Keywords: One-shot Learning, Temporal Knowledge Graphs, Graph Neural Networks, Context-aware Temporal Embeddings, Dynamic Networks.
