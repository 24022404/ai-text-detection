Autonomous driving systems rely heavily on Light Detection and Ranging (LiDAR) sensors for accurate perception of the surrounding environment. However, conventional LiDAR solutions often face challenges such as limited range, reduced resolution at longer distances, and significant hardware costs, which can impede reliable real-time object detection and navigation. This paper presents LEAD (LiDAR Extender for Autonomous Driving), a novel framework designed to enhance the capabilities of existing LiDAR systems without necessitating additional expensive hardware. LEAD utilizes a hybrid approach that combines advanced signal processing with deep learning-based reconstruction algorithms to extrapolate and refine sparse or incomplete LiDAR point clouds. Experimental evaluations on publicly available autonomous driving datasets demonstrate that LEAD significantly improves object detection accuracy and extends effective perception range under various environmental conditions. Compared to baseline LiDAR configurations, our system achieves up to 30% higher detection precision at long ranges while maintaining real-time processing speeds. The proposed framework is modular and can be seamlessly integrated with current autonomous vehicle platforms, facilitating broader adoption. LEAD’s innovations offer a scalable and cost-effective solution for enhancing LiDAR performance, paving the way for safer and more reliable autonomous driving. Keywords: LiDAR, autonomous driving, point cloud reconstruction, deep learning, sensor fusion, object detection.
