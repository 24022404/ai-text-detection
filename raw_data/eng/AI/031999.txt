This paper presents a novel spectral algorithm for Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA), a widely used topic modeling technique in natural language processing and information retrieval. The objective is to improve the efficiency and scalability of LDA for large-scale datasets. Our approach utilizes a spectral method to estimate the topic distributions, leveraging the underlying structure of the document-term matrix. The algorithm is designed to reduce the computational complexity of traditional LDA methods, making it suitable for big data applications. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our spectral algorithm, achieving comparable topic quality to existing methods while significantly reducing computational time. The key contributions of this research include a fast and scalable LDA algorithm, with potential applications in text analysis, information retrieval, and machine learning. Our approach highlights the benefits of spectral methods in topic modeling, providing a new direction for future research in this area. Key keywords: Latent Dirichlet Allocation, spectral algorithm, topic modeling, natural language processing, machine learning.
