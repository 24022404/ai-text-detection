This paper addresses the challenge of multiview detection, a crucial task in computer vision, by introducing the Shadow Transformer, a novel architecture that leverages view-coherent data augmentation to enhance detection accuracy. The objective is to improve the robustness and efficiency of multiview detection systems, which are essential in various applications, including autonomous vehicles, surveillance, and robotics. Our approach employs a transformer-based model that integrates information from multiple views, along with a specially designed data augmentation strategy that ensures coherence across different views. The results show significant improvements in detection performance, outperforming state-of-the-art methods on benchmark datasets. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of the Shadow Transformer in handling complex scenes and varying lighting conditions. This research contributes to the advancement of multiview detection and has potential applications in fields requiring accurate and robust object detection, such as autonomous systems and smart cities. Key keywords: multiview detection, Shadow Transformer, view-coherent data augmentation, computer vision, object detection, transformer models.
