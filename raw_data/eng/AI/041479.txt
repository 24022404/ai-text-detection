Ensuring fairness in machine learning models is a critical challenge, particularly when dealing with sensitive attributes such as gender, race, or age. This paper addresses the problem of bias mitigation by proposing a novel approach that leverages orthogonal disentangled representations. The objective is to separate sensitive attributes from task-relevant features in the latent space, thereby reducing unfair correlations while maintaining model performance. Our method employs a deep learning framework that enforces orthogonality constraints between representations of sensitive attributes and other features, ensuring that the learned embeddings are independent. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach effectively reduces bias without compromising accuracy, outperforming existing fairness-aware methods. The results highlight the potential of orthogonal disentangled representations as a robust solution for fairness in machine learning. This work contributes to the growing body of research on ethical AI by providing a scalable and interpretable technique for bias mitigation, with implications for applications in healthcare, finance, and social systems.  

**Keywords**: Fairness, Disentangled Representations, Orthogonality, Bias Mitigation, Machine Learning, Ethical AI.
