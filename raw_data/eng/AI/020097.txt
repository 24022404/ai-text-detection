The paper addresses the challenge of enhancing the teaching process by developing a learner-aware teaching model using inverse reinforcement learning, specifically considering learner preferences and constraints. The aim is to create a teaching strategy that adapts to the individual needs of learners, thereby optimizing their educational outcomes.

Methods/Approach: Our approach leverages inverse reinforcement learning (IRL) to infer the learning preferences and constraints of individual students based on their behavior and feedback. By integrating preference modeling and constraint satisfaction within the IRL framework, we have developed an adaptive teaching system that personalizes instructional content and methods. The system utilizes a reward-based mechanism to iteratively refine teaching strategies, accounting for the dynamically changing environment and learner states.

Results/Findings: The proposed learner-aware teaching model demonstrated significant improvements in learning efficiency and engagement when compared to traditional teaching models. Empirical evaluations conducted in simulated educational environments highlighted the system's capability to tailor teaching approaches in real-time, better aligning with learner preferences and cognitive constraints. Our findings underscore the system's potential in enhancing personalized education by reducing the gap between individualized learning needs and instructional delivery.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes to the field of educational technology by introducing a novel model that adapts teaching based on learner feedback through inverse reinforcement learning with preferences and constraints. The implementation of such adaptive teaching strategies holds promise for a wide range of educational applications, potentially transforming personalized learning environments in schools, online platforms, and professional training programs. By offering a more tailored educational experience, this model can facilitate more effective and engaging learning outcomes for individuals across diverse educational settings.

Keywords: Inverse Reinforcement Learning, Learner Preferences, Educational Technology, Personalized Learning, Adaptive Teaching, Constraint Satisfaction.
