This paper presents a novel approach to conversational fashion image retrieval, enabling users to interactively refine their search queries through multiturn natural language feedback. The objective is to develop an intuitive and effective image retrieval system that incorporates user preferences and intentions expressed through conversational dialogue. Our method employs a deep learning-based framework, combining computer vision and natural language processing techniques to understand user feedback and adapt search results accordingly. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our approach, outperforming existing image retrieval systems in terms of retrieval accuracy and user satisfaction. The key findings highlight the importance of incorporating contextual understanding and user engagement in image retrieval tasks. This research contributes to the development of more interactive and user-centric image retrieval systems, with potential applications in e-commerce, fashion recommendation, and multimedia search. Key keywords: conversational image retrieval, natural language feedback, fashion image search, deep learning, multimodal interaction.
