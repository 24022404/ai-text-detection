The Jayson library plays a crucial role within the system as a reliable tool for managing JSON-RPC requests exchanged between the client and the executor component. By leveraging Jayson, the architecture empowers the executor to tackle a range of specialized tasks, such as generating cryptographic shares, decrypting sensitive data, or validating digital signatures, all while avoiding any direct interaction with the underlying database. This deliberate separation means the executor is optimized solely for its core responsibilities: it performs discrete actions dictated by client requests, rather than becoming entangled in data management or storage operations. Such an approach not only streamlines the executor’s internal logic but also promotes overall system efficiency, scalability, and easier maintenance. Ultimately, by delegating JSON-RPC communication to Jayson and deliberately isolating the executor’s functions from data persistence layers, the design establishes a clear boundary between the procedural and storage aspects of the system, ensuring robust, modular operation.
