This paper introduces CORE, a novel color regression approach for predicting multiple colors of fashion garments. The objective is to address the challenge of accurately capturing the diverse color palette present in modern clothing items. Our method leverages a deep learning-based framework, incorporating a custom-designed color embedding module to effectively learn color relationships. The approach is validated through extensive experiments on a large-scale fashion dataset, demonstrating significant improvements over existing color prediction models. Key findings include the ability to accurately predict up to five distinct colors per garment, with a mean color accuracy of 85%. The CORE system has important implications for e-commerce, fashion design, and virtual try-on applications, enabling more accurate and immersive user experiences. By advancing the state-of-the-art in color regression for fashion items, this research contributes to the development of more sophisticated and user-friendly fashion technology platforms, with relevant keywords including color regression, fashion garments, deep learning, and computer vision.
