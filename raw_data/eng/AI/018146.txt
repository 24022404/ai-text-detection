This paper introduces "Knodle," a novel framework for modular weakly supervised learning, designed to streamline the use of PyTorch in creating and managing machine learning models where labeled data is scarce. The primary objective is to address the challenges associated with weak supervision by providing a flexible, modular toolkit that simplifies model development and deployment.

Methods/Approach: Knodle leverages the capabilities of PyTorch to incorporate a modular approach that allows users to seamlessly integrate various weak supervision sources and pipeline components. The framework is designed to be user-friendly, facilitating the integration of rule-based label functions, labeling models, and noise-aware training algorithms. Its modularity ensures ease of use and customization, enabling researchers and practitioners to adapt it to a wide range of tasks and datasets.

Results/Findings: The implementation of Knodle demonstrated significant improvements in model accuracy and efficiency compared to traditional weak supervision methods. Empirical evaluations show that Knodle consistently enhances performance across various benchmarks and real-world datasets. It effectively mitigates labeling noise and improves learning efficiency, promising a substantial advancement in applications where high-quality labeled data is limited.

Conclusion/Implications: Knodle represents a crucial innovation in weakly supervised learning by offering a robust, scalable, and versatile platform that integrates seamlessly with PyTorch. The framework's modular architecture and noise-aware learning mechanisms amplify its utility in diverse machine learning applications. This advancement holds potential for significant contributions to fields like natural language processing, computer vision, and beyond, setting a new standard for future developments in weakly supervised learning. Key keywords include Knodle, weakly supervised learning, modular framework, PyTorch, machine learning, and noise-aware training.
