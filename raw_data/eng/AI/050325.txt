Abstract

Policy improvement is a central challenge in reinforcement learning and sequential decision-making, where the goal is to iteratively refine an agent’s behavior to maximize long-term rewards while minimizing potential risks. Traditional exploration strategies often trade off between fast learning and safety, potentially exposing agents to undesirable or unsafe states. This paper introduces a novel framework for diverse exploration, designed to accelerate policy improvement while maintaining safety guarantees. Our method leverages an ensemble of exploratory policies that diversify behavior by encouraging exploration across different regions of the state space, reducing the risk of premature convergence to suboptimal solutions. At the same time, safety constraints are integrated into the exploration process to prevent actions that jeopardize the agent or the environment. We evaluate the proposed approach on a suite of standard reinforcement learning benchmarks and compare it to existing exploration and safety-aware methods. Results demonstrate that our framework achieves faster policy improvement, better sample efficiency, and superior adherence to safety requirements. The integration of diversity and safety in exploration provides a robust foundation for deploying reinforcement learning agents in real-world applications, such as robotics, autonomous systems, and industrial control, where both rapid learning and operational safety are critical. Keywords: reinforcement learning, policy improvement, safe exploration, diversity, sample efficiency, robotics, autonomous systems.
