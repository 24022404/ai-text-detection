This paper proposes IT2CFNN, an innovative Interval Type-2 Correlation-Aware Fuzzy Neural Network designed to construct non-separable fuzzy rules with uncertain and adaptive shapes for nonlinear function approximation. The objective is to address the challenges of handling complex and uncertain data in nonlinear systems. IT2CFNN employs a novel approach that integrates interval type-2 fuzzy sets with correlation-aware fuzzy neural networks, enabling the creation of adaptive and uncertain fuzzy rules. The method utilizes a hybrid learning algorithm that combines the benefits of both fuzzy logic and neural networks to optimize the fuzzy rules and improve the approximation accuracy. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of IT2CFNN over existing methods in terms of approximation accuracy and robustness to noise and uncertainty. The proposed network offers significant contributions to the field of fuzzy neural networks and nonlinear function approximation, with potential applications in areas such as control systems, time-series forecasting, and data mining. Key features of IT2CFNN include its ability to handle uncertain and noisy data, adaptive shape adjustment, and improved generalization capability. Relevant keywords: fuzzy neural networks, interval type-2 fuzzy sets, correlation-aware fuzzy rules, nonlinear function approximation, uncertain data handling, adaptive fuzzy systems.
