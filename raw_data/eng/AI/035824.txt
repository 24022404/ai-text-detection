Point cloud processing is crucial for various applications, including autonomous driving and robotics, yet effectively capturing local geometric information remains a challenge. This paper introduces KPConv, a novel convolution operator designed for point clouds that offers both flexibility and deformability. KPConv leverages a set of kernel points to define the receptive field, enabling the learning of complex geometric features. The positions of these kernel points are learned during training, allowing the convolution to adapt to the local shape of the point cloud. We demonstrate the efficacy of KPConv on several benchmark datasets for 3D shape classification, semantic segmentation, and object part segmentation. Results show that KPConv achieves state-of-the-art performance while maintaining computational efficiency. The flexible and deformable nature of KPConv allows it to effectively capture intricate geometric details, leading to improved accuracy in point cloud analysis. Keywords: Point clouds, convolution, deep learning, geometric feature extraction, 3D processing.
