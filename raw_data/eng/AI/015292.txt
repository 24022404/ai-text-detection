This paper addresses the challenge of multi-relational learning at scale by proposing a novel approach based on the Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (ADMM). The objective is to develop an efficient and scalable method for learning from complex relational data, which is crucial in various applications such as knowledge graph embedding, recommender systems, and social network analysis. Our approach leverages ADMM to decompose the learning problem into smaller sub-problems, allowing for parallel and distributed computation. We evaluate our method on large-scale datasets and demonstrate its ability to achieve state-of-the-art performance while reducing computational costs. The results show significant improvements in terms of accuracy, scalability, and efficiency compared to existing methods. Our research contributes to the advancement of multi-relational learning by providing a scalable and efficient solution, with potential applications in areas such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data mining. Key keywords: multi-relational learning, ADMM, scalable learning, knowledge graph embedding, recommender systems.
