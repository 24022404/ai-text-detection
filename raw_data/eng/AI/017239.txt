**Abstract**

Dense light field reconstruction is a fundamental task in computational imaging, enabling applications such as high-quality refocusing, 3D reconstruction, and view synthesis. However, existing methods often struggle with preserving fine details and handling irregular spatial-angular sampling in the reconstructed light fields. This paper presents LapEPI-Net, a novel deep learning framework that introduces a Laplacian Pyramid Epipolar Plane Image (EPI) structure to improve dense light field reconstruction from sparse input views. The proposed approach leverages hierarchical feature extraction via the Laplacian pyramid to capture multi-scale spatial and angular information effectively. By integrating EPI-based representations with pyramid-based feature refinement, LapEPI-Net enhances the network’s ability to reconstruct accurate and detailed light fields. Experimental results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that LapEPI-Net outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of both quantitative metrics (e.g., PSNR, SSIM) and qualitative assessment, particularly in preserving texture details and geometric consistency across views. The proposed framework offers significant improvements in computational efficiency and scalability, making it suitable for real-time applications in virtual reality, augmented reality, and computational photography. Keywords: light field reconstruction, Laplacian pyramid, epipolar plane image (EPI), deep learning, multi-scale features, view synthesis.
