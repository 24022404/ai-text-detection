This paper introduces EgoFace, a novel approach for egocentric face performance capture and videorealistic reenactment. The objective is to develop a system that can accurately capture and reenact facial performances from a first-person perspective, enabling new applications in fields such as film, gaming, and social media. Our approach combines computer vision, machine learning, and computer graphics techniques to achieve high-fidelity face tracking, expression transfer, and videorealistic rendering. The EgoFace system utilizes a deep learning-based framework to capture subtle facial expressions and movements, which are then reenacted on a target face using a physics-based face model. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of EgoFace in capturing and reenacting realistic facial performances, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods. The key contributions of this research include a novel egocentric face capture approach, a robust expression transfer algorithm, and a videorealistic rendering pipeline. The EgoFace system has significant implications for applications such as virtual try-on, facial animation, and social media avatars, and paves the way for future research in egocentric face performance capture and reenactment. Key keywords: egocentric face capture, face reenactment, computer vision, machine learning, computer graphics, facial animation, virtual try-on.
