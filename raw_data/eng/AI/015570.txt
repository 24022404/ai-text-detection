This paper introduces RL-Duet, a novel system for online music accompaniment generation using deep reinforcement learning. The system aims to generate harmonically and rhythmically coherent accompaniments in real-time, responding dynamically to a live musician's performance. RL-Duet employs a deep neural network trained via reinforcement learning to learn optimal accompaniment strategies. The reward function is designed to encourage musicality by incorporating measures of harmonic consonance, rhythmic similarity, and melodic variation. We propose a unique state representation that captures relevant musical features from the performer's input, enabling the agent to make informed decisions. Experimental results demonstrate that RL-Duet generates more engaging and musically appropriate accompaniments compared to rule-based and supervised learning approaches. Subjective evaluations confirm the system's ability to enhance the musical experience for both the performer and the listener. This research contributes a practical application of reinforcement learning in the creative domain of music generation, opening new avenues for interactive musical experiences.

Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Music Generation, Accompaniment, Deep Learning, Real-time Systems, Artificial Intelligence, Interactive Music.
