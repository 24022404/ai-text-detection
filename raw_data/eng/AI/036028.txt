Object detection in computer vision has seen significant advancements, yet challenges remain in balancing accuracy and computational efficiency, particularly in single-stage detection frameworks. This paper introduces HAR-Net, a novel architecture that leverages joint learning of hybrid attention mechanisms to enhance feature representation and detection performance. The proposed approach integrates both spatial and channel attention modules within a unified framework, enabling the model to focus on salient regions and discriminative features simultaneously. HAR-Net employs a lightweight design to ensure computational efficiency while maintaining high detection accuracy. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets, such as COCO and Pascal VOC, demonstrate that HAR-Net achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing single-stage detectors in terms of both precision and inference speed. The results highlight the effectiveness of hybrid attention in improving object localization and classification tasks. This work contributes to the field by providing a scalable and efficient solution for real-time object detection, with potential applications in autonomous driving, surveillance, and robotics.  

**Keywords**: Object detection, hybrid attention, single-stage detection, spatial attention, channel attention, HAR-Net, computer vision.
