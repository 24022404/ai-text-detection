This paper addresses the challenge of automated analysis and understanding of ancient coin images, which are often degraded and exhibit significant variations in appearance. We present a novel approach combining deep learning techniques with image processing methods to enhance feature extraction and classification accuracy. Our method leverages a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, pre-trained on a large dataset of modern coin images and fine-tuned with a smaller dataset of ancient coins, augmented using synthetic data generation techniques to address data scarcity. Furthermore, we incorporate image enhancement algorithms to mitigate the effects of corrosion and wear, improving the robustness of the CNN. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in coin classification and denomination recognition, achieving a significant improvement in accuracy on a benchmark dataset of Roman coins. This research contributes to the field of numismatics by providing an effective tool for automated coin analysis, which can aid in historical research and cultural heritage preservation. Keywords: Ancient coins, image analysis, deep learning, CNN, image enhancement, numismatics, classification.
