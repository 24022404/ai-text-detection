This paper aims to enhance motion cues for face anti-spoofing by proposing a novel CNN-LSTM architecture. The objective is to effectively distinguish between genuine and spoofed faces by analyzing subtle motion patterns. Our approach combines the strengths of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for spatial feature extraction and long short-term memory (LSTM) networks for temporal sequence modeling. The proposed model is trained on a large dataset of face videos with diverse spoofing attacks, including replay and mask attacks. Experimental results demonstrate that our CNN-LSTM architecture outperforms state-of-the-art face anti-spoofing methods, achieving a significant reduction in equal error rate. The key findings highlight the importance of motion cues in face anti-spoofing and the effectiveness of the proposed architecture in capturing these cues. This research contributes to the development of more robust and reliable face recognition systems, with potential applications in security, surveillance, and authentication. Key keywords: face anti-spoofing, CNN-LSTM, motion cues, deep learning, face recognition, security.
