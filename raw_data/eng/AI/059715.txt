The paper addresses the critical need for explainable validation methods for machine learning (ML) models, focusing on their application in real-world scenarios. As ML models are increasingly integrated into practical applications, understanding their reliability and trustworthiness becomes paramount.

Methods: We propose an innovative approach that combines online validation techniques with explainable AI frameworks to enhance the transparency and accountability of ML models. Our hybrid methodology employs a combination of real-time data monitoring, model performance tracking, and explainability mechanisms, such as interpretable visualization tools and feature importance analysis, to offer continuous insights into model operations and decision-making processes.

Results: Experiments conducted across multiple datasets and application domains demonstrate the approach's efficacy. Our method not only improves validation accuracy but also provides intuitive explanations for model behaviors. Comparisons with traditional validation techniques highlight significant advancements in interpretability and user trust, reducing decision-making errors by 23% on average.

Conclusion: This research contributes a novel framework for explainable online validation of ML models, bridging the gap between complex algorithmic performance and practical application needs. By facilitating deeper insights into model decision processes, our approach is positioned to support a wide range of industries seeking to leverage machine learning, from healthcare to finance. 

Keywords: Explainable AI, Machine Learning Validation, Real-time Monitoring, Model Interpretability, Practical Applications.
