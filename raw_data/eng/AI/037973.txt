Counting objects in images is a fundamental task in computer vision with significant applications in automated analysis, scene understanding, and robotics. However, existing visual question answering (VQA) systems often underperform on complex counting questions that require reasoning beyond direct enumeration. In this paper, we present TallyQA, a novel framework specifically designed to answer complex counting questions that involve object attributes, spatial relationships, and higher-order reasoning. Our approach integrates advanced semantic parsing of natural-language questions with a multi-stage visual reasoning pipeline. We introduce a new large-scale dataset containing diverse and challenging counting queries to evaluate our system. Experimental results demonstrate that TallyQA outperforms state-of-the-art VQA models on both simple and complex counting tasks, delivering enhanced accuracy and robustness, particularly for queries demanding compositional understanding. Our contributions include the TallyQA dataset, the tailored reasoning architecture, and comprehensive analysis of system performance. These results highlight the importance of specialized frameworks for complex reasoning in VQA, opening up new possibilities for real-world applications in AI-powered analytics, autonomous monitoring, and human-computer interaction.

Keywords: visual question answering, object counting, deep learning, semantic reasoning, TallyQA, image analysis, dataset, compositional questions.
