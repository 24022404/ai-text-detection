Automated feedback systems have the potential to revolutionize education by providing timely, scalable, and personalized assessments. However, achieving near human-level accuracy in grading richly structured problems, such as essays, mathematical proofs, or programming assignments, remains a significant challenge. This paper introduces *Generative Grading*, a novel framework that leverages advanced generative models to evaluate complex, open-ended responses with high precision. Our approach combines fine-tuned transformer-based architectures with domain-specific knowledge representations to capture the nuanced structure and semantics of student submissions. We evaluate the system on diverse datasets, including programming exercises and mathematical problem-solving tasks, demonstrating its ability to achieve grading accuracy within 5% of human experts. Key innovations include a hybrid scoring mechanism that integrates both syntactic and semantic analysis, as well as a feedback generation module that provides actionable insights to learners. The results highlight the potential of Generative Grading to scale high-quality educational assessments while maintaining fairness and interpretability. This work contributes to the growing field of AI in education, offering a robust solution for automated feedback on richly structured problems.  

**Keywords**: automated grading, generative models, transformer architectures, AI in education, feedback systems, structured problem-solving.
