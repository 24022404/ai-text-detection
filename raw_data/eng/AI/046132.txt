Self-supervised learning (SSL) has emerged as a powerful paradigm for visual feature extraction, circumventing the need for large labeled datasets. This survey provides a comprehensive overview of recent advances in self-supervised visual feature learning using deep neural networks. We categorize and analyze a broad spectrum of SSL techniques, including contrastive learning, generative methods, and pretext task-based approaches. The survey explores the underlying principles, strengths, and limitations of each category, highlighting key architectural innovations and loss functions. Furthermore, we analyze the transferability of learned features to various downstream tasks such as image classification, object detection, and semantic segmentation, comparing performance across different SSL methods and datasets. Finally, we discuss open challenges and future research directions in this rapidly evolving field, including improving robustness, scalability, and theoretical understanding of self-supervised visual representations. Keywords: Self-Supervised Learning, Deep Neural Networks, Visual Feature Learning, Contrastive Learning, Pretext Tasks, Representation Learning.
