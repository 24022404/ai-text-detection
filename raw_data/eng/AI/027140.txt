This paper addresses the challenge of efficiently training large-scale neural networks with softmax output layers, a crucial component in many natural language processing and computer vision tasks. Our objective is to optimize the softmax function in TensorFlow, a popular open-source machine learning framework, to reduce computational costs and improve model performance. We propose an efficient sampled softmax approach, leveraging a combination of techniques such as importance sampling and sparse tensor operations to minimize computational overhead. Our method achieves significant speedups and memory reductions compared to traditional softmax implementations, while maintaining accuracy. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach on several benchmark datasets, including image classification and language modeling tasks. The key contributions of this work include a novel sampled softmax algorithm, optimized TensorFlow implementation, and empirical evaluations showcasing improved efficiency and scalability. This research has important implications for the development of large-scale deep learning models, particularly in applications where computational resources are limited. Key keywords: softmax, TensorFlow, sampled softmax, efficient computation, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision.
