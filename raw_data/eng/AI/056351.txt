Heterogeneous domain adaptation (HDA) addresses the challenge of transferring knowledge between domains with different feature spaces. This paper introduces a novel approach, Cross-Domain Structure Preserving Projection (CDSPP), to learn a shared subspace where structural information from both source and target domains is preserved. The objective is to mitigate the domain discrepancy and improve adaptation performance by aligning domains in a common space. CDSPP utilizes a projection learning framework that simultaneously minimizes intra-domain variance and maximizes inter-domain correlation, while preserving the intrinsic geometrical structure of each domain. This is achieved through a combination of principal component analysis and a graph embedding technique. Experiments conducted on several benchmark HDA datasets demonstrate that CDSPP significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of classification accuracy. The results highlight the effectiveness of preserving structural information during projection for robust cross-domain adaptation. This research offers a promising solution for HDA, particularly in scenarios where domain features differ significantly. Keywords: Heterogeneous Domain Adaptation, Dimensionality Reduction, Structure Preservation, Projection Learning, Machine Learning.
