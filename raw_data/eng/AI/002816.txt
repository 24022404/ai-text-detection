Zoho offers a comprehensive suite of features for managing customer support tickets, such as online data collection, an omni-channel ticketing system, and automatic ticket assignment to streamline workflow. Its SLA capabilities enable effective prioritization, while intuitive dashboards and reporting tools provide insights into key metrics and overall performance. For advanced needs, Zoho’s top-tier plans integrate AI-powered tools to enhance efficiency. However, some drawbacks exist, including complexity in setup and a learning curve for new users.
