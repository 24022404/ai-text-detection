Electronic Medical Records (EMRs) contain vast amounts of patient data that can be leveraged for predictive healthcare analytics. However, the complexity and sequential nature of EMRs pose significant challenges for accurate and interpretable modeling. This paper introduces *RetainVis*, a novel visual analytics framework that integrates interpretable and interactive Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) for analyzing EMRs. The primary objective of *RetainVis* is to provide healthcare professionals with a tool that not only delivers accurate predictions but also offers transparent insights into the model’s decision-making process.  

The framework employs a modified RNN architecture, enhanced with attention mechanisms, to capture temporal dependencies in EMR data while maintaining interpretability. A user-friendly interactive dashboard is developed to visualize the model’s predictions, highlight influential features, and allow users to explore "what-if" scenarios. Experiments on real-world EMR datasets demonstrate that *RetainVis* achieves competitive prediction accuracy for tasks such as disease progression and readmission risk, outperforming traditional black-box models. Furthermore, case studies with medical experts validate the framework’s utility in providing actionable insights and fostering trust in AI-driven predictions.  

The key contributions of this work include the integration of interpretable RNNs with interactive visualization, enabling domain experts to understand and interact with complex predictive models. *RetainVis* bridges the gap between advanced machine learning techniques and practical healthcare applications, offering a scalable solution for improving patient outcomes through data-driven decision-making.  

**Keywords**: Electronic Medical Records, Recurrent Neural Networks, Interpretability, Visual Analytics, Healthcare Predictive Modeling, Attention Mechanisms.
