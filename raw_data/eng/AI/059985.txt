Reinforcement learning (RL) research and applications have advanced rapidly, yet developing scalable and adaptable RL systems remains challenging due to limited modularity, interoperability, and support for distributed training across existing frameworks. This paper introduces PyTorchRL, a comprehensive library designed to facilitate the development, training, and deployment of reinforcement learning algorithms using the PyTorch ecosystem. PyTorchRL features a highly modular architecture, enabling users to flexibly configure environments, models, agents, and learning algorithms to suit a diverse range of RL tasks. The framework natively supports distributed training and evaluation, allowing seamless scaling to multi-core and multi-node systems. We present an extensive set of RL algorithms, including both value-based and policy-based methods, and a standardized interface that promotes code reuse and rapid experimentation. Experimental results demonstrate the efficiency, flexibility, and scalability of PyTorchRL across benchmark RL environments, with performance comparable to or exceeding existing state-of-the-art libraries. The paper highlights PyTorchRL’s ease of integration with other PyTorch-based tools, its extensibility for custom research, and its potential to accelerate advances in reinforcement learning. Key contributions include modular design, distributed training capabilities, and compatibility with popular RL and deep learning workflows. 

Keywords: reinforcement learning, PyTorch, distributed training, modular architecture, RL frameworks, deep learning, scalable algorithms
