This paper aims to enhance the interpretability of super-resolution networks by introducing local attribution maps, which provide insights into the decision-making process of these complex models. Our approach utilizes a novel attribution method to generate maps that highlight the most relevant input features contributing to the super-resolution output. We employ a deep learning framework, leveraging convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and attention mechanisms to focus on local image regions. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing attribution techniques, yielding more accurate and informative maps. Our findings have significant implications for the development of more transparent and trustworthy super-resolution models, with potential applications in image processing, computer vision, and medical imaging. Key contributions include the introduction of local attribution maps, improved model interpretability, and enhanced understanding of super-resolution networks. Relevant keywords: super-resolution, deep learning, CNNs, attention mechanisms, interpretability, attribution maps, computer vision, image processing.
