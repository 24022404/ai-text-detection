This paper introduces a novel High-order Differentiable Autoencoder (HODAE) framework for nonlinear model reduction, addressing the limitations of traditional autoencoders in capturing complex, high-dimensional data structures. The proposed approach leverages high-order differentiable functions to enhance the representation learning process, enabling more accurate and efficient dimensionality reduction for nonlinear systems. The HODAE architecture integrates advanced optimization techniques and differentiable layers to ensure robust training and improved generalization across diverse datasets. Experimental results demonstrate that HODAE outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of reconstruction accuracy, computational efficiency, and scalability, particularly for high-dimensional and nonlinear data. Key contributions include the development of a high-order differentiable framework, a scalable training algorithm, and a comprehensive evaluation on benchmark datasets. The findings highlight the potential of HODAE for applications in scientific computing, data compression, and real-time system modeling, offering a significant advancement in nonlinear model reduction techniques.  

**Keywords**: Autoencoder, Nonlinear Model Reduction, High-order Differentiability, Dimensionality Reduction, Representation Learning.
