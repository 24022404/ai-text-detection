Deep neural network (DNN) weight pruning effectively reduces model size and computational cost, enabling deployment on resource-constrained mobile devices. However, existing pruning techniques often result in irregular sparsity patterns, hindering efficient hardware acceleration and real-time execution. This paper introduces PCONV, a novel pruning strategy that enforces a predefined, structured sparsity pattern characterized by contiguous blocks of zero weights within convolution filters. Our method optimizes a sparsity mask during training to maximize accuracy under this constraint, leveraging a differentiable approximation of the L0 norm. Experiments on various DNN architectures, including MobileNetV2 and ResNet-50, demonstrate that PCONV achieves comparable or superior accuracy compared to unstructured pruning methods at similar sparsity ratios. Furthermore, the structured sparsity enables significant speedups in inference time on mobile devices due to improved memory access patterns and reduced overhead in specialized convolution kernels. PCONV offers a practical solution for deploying high-performance DNNs on mobile platforms, bridging the gap between model compression and real-time execution. Keywords: DNN, weight pruning, sparsity, mobile devices, real-time execution, structured sparsity, hardware acceleration.
