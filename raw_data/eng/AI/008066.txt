Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs) play a crucial role in modeling dynamic systems across various scientific and engineering domains. However, inferring the underlying structure of ODEs from observed data remains a significant challenge, particularly in contexts where model selection and parameter estimation must be conducted simultaneously. This paper introduces a novel data-driven framework for automatically inferring the structure of ODEs from time-series observations. Our approach leverages a combination of sparse regression techniques and machine learning algorithms to identify candidate functional forms while optimizing model parameters with high accuracy. We validate our method on both synthetic and real-world datasets, demonstrating its ability to recover accurate and interpretable equations underlying complex dynamical behavior. Comparative experiments show that our framework outperforms existing methods in terms of structure identification accuracy and computational efficiency. The proposed methodology advances the field by enabling automated and scalable discovery of ODE models, which has immediate applications in fields such as biology, engineering, and physics. Key contributions include a flexible approach to model selection, robustness to noise, and support for nonlinear and multi-dimensional systems. This work opens new possibilities for automated scientific discovery and enhances our ability to model, analyze, and predict the behavior of complex systems.  
Keywords: ordinary differential equations, model inference, sparse regression, dynamical systems, machine learning, time-series analysis, automated model discovery
