This paper aims to investigate the reliability of deep learning classifiers by evaluating their robustness and operational profiles. The objective is to develop a comprehensive framework for assessing the performance of these classifiers in real-world scenarios, where data distributions and environmental conditions may vary. Our approach involves employing robustness evaluation techniques, such as adversarial attacks and data perturbations, to test the classifiers' resilience. We also consider operational profiles, including factors like input data quality and computational resources, to simulate realistic deployment conditions. The results show that our proposed framework can effectively identify potential vulnerabilities in deep learning classifiers and provide insights into their reliability. Our findings have significant implications for the development of trustworthy AI systems, particularly in applications where reliability is critical, such as autonomous vehicles and healthcare. The key contributions of this research include a novel robustness evaluation methodology and a systematic approach to characterizing operational profiles, which can be used to improve the reliability of deep learning classifiers. Relevant keywords: deep learning, robustness evaluation, operational profiles, reliability assessment, adversarial attacks, AI trustworthiness.
