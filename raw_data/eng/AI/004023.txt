High Dynamic Range (HDR) imaging enables the capture and display of scenes with a wide range of luminance values, but accurate color reproduction remains a significant challenge due to device limitations and varying color gamuts. This paper presents a novel gamut-mapping framework specifically designed for color-accurate reproduction of HDR images across diverse display technologies. The proposed approach combines advanced color appearance modeling with adaptive gamut mapping algorithms to preserve visual fidelity and color consistency when rendering HDR content on both standard and wide-gamut displays. Experimental evaluations demonstrate that the framework consistently reduces color distortion and perceptual errors compared to existing methods, as assessed by both quantitative metrics and user studies. The solution also accommodates dynamic adaptation to different display profiles, ensuring robust performance in real-world application scenarios such as digital photography, cinematic post-production, and display calibration. The presented framework advances the state-of-the-art in HDR imaging by providing an effective, scalable method for achieving consistent and accurate color reproduction, with broad implications for imaging pipelines, color management, and visual media technologies. Keywords: HDR images, gamut mapping, color reproduction, color appearance modeling, display technology, visual fidelity, image processing.
