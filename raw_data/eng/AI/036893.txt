High-dynamic-range (HDR) imaging presents a promising avenue for improving cloud segmentation accuracy in remote sensing applications. This paper explores the use of HDR techniques to address the challenges posed by varying illumination conditions and low contrast in cloud imagery. We propose a novel approach that integrates multiple exposures captured under different lighting conditions to generate HDR images, which are then processed using a deep learning-based segmentation model. Specifically, we employ a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture optimized for cloud detection and fine-tuned on a dataset of HDR cloud images. Experimental results demonstrate that our HDR-enhanced method significantly outperforms traditional single-exposure segmentation techniques, achieving higher precision and recall in identifying cloud boundaries, particularly in areas with shadows or bright highlights. The enhanced cloud segmentation facilitates more accurate weather forecasting, climate modeling, and satellite image analysis. Keywords: HDR imaging, cloud segmentation, remote sensing, deep learning, CNN, image processing.
