Neural networks offer unparalleled performance across various tasks but often suffer from high computational costs, hindering deployment on resource-constrained devices. This paper introduces AntiDote, an Attention-based Dynamic Optimization technique designed to improve neural network runtime efficiency. AntiDote employs an attention mechanism to dynamically adjust the computational resources allocated to different parts of the network during inference. By learning to identify and prioritize salient features, AntiDote selectively prunes less important connections and operations, leading to reduced computational overhead. We evaluate AntiDote on several benchmark datasets and network architectures, demonstrating significant improvements in runtime, achieving up to 40% reduction in inference time with minimal accuracy loss compared to static pruning methods. The proposed approach offers a flexible and efficient way to optimize neural networks for deployment in resource-limited environments, advancing the practicality of deep learning in edge computing and mobile applications. Keywords: Neural Network Optimization, Dynamic Pruning, Attention Mechanism, Runtime Efficiency, Deep Learning, Edge Computing.
