This paper introduces DashNet, a novel hybrid artificial and spiking neural network designed for high-speed object tracking. The objective is to develop a robust and efficient tracking system that leverages the strengths of both artificial neural networks (ANNs) and spiking neural networks (SNNs). DashNet combines the feature extraction capabilities of ANNs with the event-driven processing of SNNs, enabling real-time object tracking with improved accuracy and reduced computational overhead. The approach utilizes a hybrid architecture that integrates a convolutional neural network (CNN) with a spiking neural network (SNN) to process visual data and generate tracking predictions. Experimental results demonstrate that DashNet outperforms state-of-the-art tracking methods in terms of speed and accuracy, particularly in scenarios with fast-moving objects. The key contributions of this research include the development of a novel hybrid neural network architecture and the demonstration of its effectiveness in high-speed object tracking applications, with potential implications for fields such as robotics, surveillance, and autonomous vehicles. Key keywords: object tracking, hybrid neural networks, spiking neural networks, artificial neural networks, computer vision, real-time processing.
