This paper introduces CAM-Convs, a novel camera-aware multi-scale convolutional approach for single-view depth estimation. The objective is to improve the accuracy of depth prediction by incorporating camera parameters and multi-scale features. Our method utilizes a hierarchical convolutional architecture that integrates camera-aware modules, allowing the model to adapt to different camera settings and scene geometries. Experimental results demonstrate that CAM-Convs outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in depth estimation accuracy and robustness. The key findings highlight the importance of camera awareness and multi-scale feature extraction in single-view depth estimation. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and reliable depth estimation systems, with potential applications in computer vision, robotics, and autonomous driving. The proposed approach is particularly relevant to applications involving monocular depth estimation, 3D reconstruction, and scene understanding, and can be extended to various fields that rely on accurate depth perception.
