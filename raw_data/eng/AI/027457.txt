This paper addresses the challenge of efficient computation and backpropagation in neural networks utilizing angle-parametrized orthogonal matrices. The objective is to develop a parallelized approach that leverages the properties of orthogonal matrices to improve computational efficiency. Our method employs a novel parametrization technique, allowing for the parallelization of matrix operations and backpropagation. The results show significant speedups in computation time compared to existing methods, with a maximum speedup of 5x achieved on large-scale neural networks. The key findings indicate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance while reducing computational overhead. The implications of this research are substantial, enabling the efficient training of large neural networks with orthogonal constraints, which is crucial for applications such as computer vision and natural language processing. Our contributions include the development of a parallelized computation framework and a novel backpropagation algorithm tailored to angle-parametrized orthogonal matrices, paving the way for future research in optimized neural network training. Key keywords: orthogonal matrices, parallel computation, backpropagation, neural networks, angle-parametrization.
