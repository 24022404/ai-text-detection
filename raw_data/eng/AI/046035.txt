The system’s security framework integrates blockchain smart contracts, Shamir secret sharing, and the Pedersen Distributed Key Generation (DKG) protocol to achieve robust and transparent protection mechanisms. By relying on blockchain smart contracts, the platform ensures that all operations remain transparent, immutable, and tamper-proof, fostering trust among participants and providing an auditable trail of actions. Shamir secret sharing further enhances security by dividing cryptographic keys into separate shares, distributed among multiple parties, so that no single individual can reconstruct the entire secret alone. Complementing these strategies, the Pedersen DKG protocol enables the distributed generation of shared cryptographic keys, ensuring that the full key is never exposed to just one party, thus minimizing risks of unauthorized access or internal breaches.
