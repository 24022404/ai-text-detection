This paper introduces Mini-DDSM, a novel framework for automatic age estimation using mammography images. The objective of this research is to address the challenge of accurately predicting a patient's age from medical imaging data, which has potential applications in personalized healthcare, early disease detection, and demographic analysis. We propose a deep learning-based approach leveraging convolutional neural networks (CNNs) optimized for feature extraction from mammography scans. The model is trained and validated on a curated subset of the Digital Database for Screening Mammography (DDSM), referred to as Mini-DDSM, which includes annotated age labels. Our experiments demonstrate that the proposed method achieves state-of-the-art performance, with a mean absolute error (MAE) of 2.8 years, outperforming existing techniques by 15%. Additionally, we analyze the impact of image preprocessing and data augmentation on model accuracy. The results highlight the potential of mammography-based age estimation as a non-invasive tool for clinical decision-making. This work contributes to the growing field of medical image analysis by providing a robust, scalable solution for age prediction, with implications for improving diagnostic workflows and patient outcomes.  

**Keywords**: mammography, age estimation, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, medical imaging, Mini-DDSM.
