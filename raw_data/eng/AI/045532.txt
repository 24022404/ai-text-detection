This paper introduces IT2CFNN, an Interval Type-2 Correlation-Aware Fuzzy Neural Network designed to address the challenges of nonlinear function approximation by constructing non-separable fuzzy rules with uncertain and adaptive shapes. Traditional fuzzy systems often struggle with capturing complex dependencies and uncertainties in high-dimensional data. To overcome these limitations, IT2CFNN integrates interval type-2 fuzzy logic with a correlation-aware mechanism, enabling the modeling of intricate relationships between input variables and enhancing adaptability to dynamic environments. The proposed approach employs a novel rule construction method that leverages non-separable fuzzy rules, allowing for more accurate representation of nonlinearities and uncertainties. Experimental results demonstrate that IT2CFNN outperforms existing methods in terms of approximation accuracy and robustness, particularly in scenarios with noisy or incomplete data. The adaptive nature of the model ensures improved performance across diverse datasets, making it a promising tool for applications in control systems, pattern recognition, and predictive modeling. This research contributes to the advancement of fuzzy neural networks by providing a scalable and efficient framework for handling complex, uncertain, and nonlinear systems.  

**Keywords**: Interval Type-2 Fuzzy Logic, Fuzzy Neural Networks, Non-Separable Fuzzy Rules, Nonlinear Function Approximation, Adaptive Systems, Uncertainty Modeling.
