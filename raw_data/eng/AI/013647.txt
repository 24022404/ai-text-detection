Unsupervised classification of sequential and networked data presents significant challenges due to the absence of labeled instances and the complex dependencies within and between data points. This paper introduces a novel unsupervised ensemble classification framework specifically designed to address these complexities. Our approach leverages a diverse set of base classifiers, each capturing different aspects of the sequential and network structures. These classifiers are then integrated using a weighted aggregation strategy, where weights are dynamically adjusted based on the local consistency and global agreement among the classifiers. We evaluate the performance of our framework on several real-world datasets, demonstrating its superiority over existing unsupervised classification methods in terms of accuracy, robustness, and scalability. Key findings reveal that the ensemble approach effectively mitigates biases inherent in individual classifiers, leading to improved clustering quality. The proposed framework offers a practical solution for analyzing unlabeled sequential and networked data in various domains, including social network analysis, anomaly detection, and time series clustering.

Keywords: Unsupervised Learning, Ensemble Classification, Sequential Data, Networked Data, Clustering, Data Mining.
