Reinforcement learning (RL) agents often struggle to generalize beyond their training environment. This paper explores a novel approach to improve generalization in RL by incorporating human instructions. We introduce a framework, “Ask Your Humans” (AYH), that leverages natural language instructions provided by humans to guide the agent's exploration and learning process. AYH allows agents to query human experts for advice during training, using the received instructions to shape its policy and value function. Experiments conducted on a suite of challenging simulated environments demonstrate that AYH significantly improves the generalization capabilities of RL agents, enabling them to adapt to unseen scenarios more effectively compared to agents trained with standard RL algorithms. Furthermore, we investigate the impact of different instruction modalities and querying strategies on overall performance. Results show that incorporating even limited human guidance can lead to substantial gains in generalization. Our findings suggest that leveraging human expertise through natural language offers a promising avenue for developing more robust and adaptable RL agents. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Generalization, Human-in-the-loop, Natural Language Instructions, Policy Shaping.
