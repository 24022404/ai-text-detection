This paper introduces a novel approach to analyzing hypergraphs by examining the local geometry of hyperedges. We define local geometric properties based on the intersections and adjacencies of hyperedges, capturing structural information beyond traditional node-centric measures. Our method provides a new characterization of hypergraph structure, enabling a more nuanced understanding of complex relationships. We apply this local geometry framework to the analysis of social networks represented as hypergraphs, where groups of individuals form hyperedges. Results demonstrate that our approach effectively identifies cohesive subgroups and reveals patterns of interaction that are not apparent using conventional graph-based methods. We show that geometric properties correlate with community structure and information flow, offering insights into group dynamics and influence within social networks. The proposed methodology provides a valuable tool for analyzing hypergraph data in various domains, including social network analysis, collaboration networks, and biological networks. Keywords: hypergraph, hyperedge, local geometry, social network analysis, community detection, network structure.
