This paper presents a novel approach to gesture recognition by exploring multi-rate and multi-modal temporal enhanced networks. The objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of gesture recognition systems by leveraging the complementary information from different modalities and temporal rates. Our approach utilizes a fusion of visual and inertial data, processed through a hierarchical network architecture that incorporates multi-rate temporal convolutions and attention mechanisms. The results show significant improvements in recognition accuracy compared to existing state-of-the-art methods, with a mean accuracy of 95.6% on a benchmark dataset. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a multi-rate temporal encoding scheme and a modal attention fusion mechanism, which enable the network to effectively capture and integrate temporal and modal variations in gesture data. This work has important implications for human-computer interaction, robotics, and healthcare applications, and demonstrates the potential of multi-modal and multi-rate temporal enhanced networks for gesture recognition. Key keywords: gesture recognition, multi-modal fusion, temporal convolutional networks, attention mechanisms, human-computer interaction.
