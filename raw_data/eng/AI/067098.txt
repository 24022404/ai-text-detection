This paper presents a comprehensive survey on the application of machine learning techniques for malicious URL detection, a crucial task in cybersecurity. The objective is to provide an overview of existing methods and approaches that utilize machine learning algorithms to identify and classify malicious URLs. Various machine learning models, including supervised and unsupervised learning, are discussed, along with their strengths and weaknesses. The survey highlights key findings, such as the importance of feature extraction and selection, and compares the performance of different models. The results show that machine learning-based approaches can effectively detect malicious URLs with high accuracy. The conclusion emphasizes the significance of this research, contributing to the development of more robust and efficient cybersecurity systems. This study provides valuable insights for researchers and practitioners, and its findings have important implications for the detection and prevention of cyber threats, particularly in the context of web security, phishing detection, and malware prevention, with relevant keywords including machine learning, URL detection, cybersecurity, and threat intelligence.
