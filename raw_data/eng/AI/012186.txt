This paper aims to provide a comprehensive tutorial on recursive models for analyzing and predicting path choice behavior, a crucial aspect of transportation research and urban planning. The objective is to introduce researchers and practitioners to the concept of recursive models and their application in understanding complex travel behavior. A recursive model approach is employed, incorporating machine learning algorithms and discrete choice theory to predict path choice decisions. The results show that recursive models outperform traditional models in capturing the dynamic nature of path choice behavior, leading to more accurate predictions and a deeper understanding of traveler decision-making processes. The conclusion highlights the significance of recursive models in transportation research, enabling the development of more efficient and responsive transportation systems. Key contributions include the introduction of a novel recursive modeling framework, the integration of machine learning techniques, and the demonstration of improved predictive accuracy. Relevant keywords: recursive models, path choice behavior, transportation research, machine learning, discrete choice theory.
