This paper proposes a novel representation learning approach for facial expression recognition, constrained by Action Unit (AU) expression knowledge. The objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of facial expression recognition systems by incorporating domain-specific knowledge into the learning process. Our method utilizes a deep neural network architecture that integrates AU-expression knowledge to guide the representation learning process. The approach is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in recognition accuracy and robustness compared to state-of-the-art methods. Key findings include the effectiveness of AU-expression knowledge in disambiguating subtle differences between facial expressions and enhancing the model's ability to generalize across varying contexts. The proposed approach contributes to the development of more accurate and reliable facial expression recognition systems, with potential applications in human-computer interaction, affective computing, and social robotics. Relevant keywords include facial expression recognition, Action Unit, representation learning, deep learning, and affective computing.
