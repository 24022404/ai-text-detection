This paper explores the application of regularized tensor factorizations and higher-order principal components analysis (PCA) for dimensionality reduction and feature extraction in multi-dimensional datasets. Traditional PCA methods are limited to two-dimensional data, whereas real-world applications often involve higher-order structures, such as time-series, spatial, or multi-modal data. To address this, we propose a novel framework that integrates regularization techniques into tensor factorization models, enabling robust decomposition and improved interpretability of high-dimensional data. Our approach leverages sparsity-inducing norms and smoothness constraints to enhance the stability and accuracy of the factorization process. Experimental results on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms conventional tensor factorization and higher-order PCA techniques in terms of reconstruction error, computational efficiency, and feature interpretability. The framework is particularly effective in applications such as image processing, recommendation systems, and network analysis. This research contributes to the advancement of tensor-based data analysis by providing a scalable and adaptable solution for extracting meaningful patterns from complex, high-dimensional datasets.  

**Keywords**: Tensor factorization, higher-order PCA, regularization, dimensionality reduction, multi-dimensional data, sparsity, interpretability.
