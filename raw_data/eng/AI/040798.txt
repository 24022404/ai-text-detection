Object detection in complex scenes remains challenging, particularly for small objects. This paper introduces a novel weakly-supervised segmentation network designed to enhance the detection of small objects while maintaining computational efficiency. Our approach utilizes multiple receptive fields to capture contextual information at various scales, enabling the network to better discern small objects from background clutter. We employ a weakly-supervised learning strategy, training the network with only image-level labels, thus reducing annotation costs. The proposed architecture incorporates a small-object-focusing module that selectively emphasizes features relevant to small objects during training. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves competitive detection accuracy, especially for small objects, while significantly reducing computational time compared to existing weakly-supervised and fully-supervised approaches. The results highlight the potential of our method for fast and accurate object detection in resource-constrained environments. Keywords: object detection, weakly-supervised learning, small object detection, receptive fields, segmentation network, fast detection.
