The rapid growth of satellite imagery data has created a pressing need for efficient and accurate classification methods to analyze large-scale geospatial information. This study addresses the challenge of satellite image classification by leveraging deep learning techniques to improve accuracy and scalability. We propose a novel convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture tailored for multi-spectral satellite imagery, incorporating attention mechanisms to enhance feature extraction and spatial context understanding. The model is trained on a diverse dataset comprising high-resolution satellite images from various geographical regions, ensuring robustness across different terrains and conditions. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming traditional machine learning methods and existing deep learning models in terms of classification accuracy and computational efficiency. The proposed system achieves an average accuracy of 94.5% on benchmark datasets, with significant improvements in handling complex land cover patterns. Furthermore, the model's lightweight design enables deployment on resource-constrained devices, making it suitable for real-time applications in environmental monitoring, urban planning, and disaster management. This research contributes to the advancement of satellite image analysis by introducing a scalable and accurate deep learning framework, paving the way for broader adoption in geospatial intelligence and remote sensing applications.  

**Keywords**: Satellite image classification, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, attention mechanisms, geospatial analysis, remote sensing.
