This paper investigates the influence of entropy on policy optimization within reinforcement learning frameworks. The study aims to understand how entropy regularization impacts the learning process and final policy performance by examining various policy optimization algorithms.

Methods/Approach: We conduct a comprehensive analysis using well-known reinforcement learning algorithms, such as Policy Gradient and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), enhanced with entropy-based techniques. Through controlled experiments, we manipulate entropy levels to assess their effects on policy exploration and stability. We introduce a novel entropy adjustment mechanism to improve adaptability during training phases.

Results/Findings: Our findings indicate that proper entropy tuning significantly enhances policy robustness and convergence speed, especially in complex environments. The novel entropy adjustment mechanism outperforms static entropy configurations, leading to superior exploration-exploitation balance and improved overall performance. Comparative analysis with existing methods shows notable advancements in both training efficiency and policy effectiveness.

Conclusion/Implications: The research underscores the critical role of entropy in achieving optimal policy performance and presents a practical entropy adjustment framework that can be integrated into existing reinforcement learning algorithms. These insights pave the way for more resilient and efficient learning systems, with potential applications in autonomous systems, robotics, and complex decision-making tasks. Key keywords include entropy regularization, policy optimization, reinforcement learning, Policy Gradient, and PPO.
