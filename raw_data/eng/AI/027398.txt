This paper addresses the challenge of fine-grained visual recognition using web images, which are often noisy and diverse. The objective is to develop a robust method that can effectively eliminate noisy samples and leverage hard examples to improve recognition accuracy. Our approach employs a novel sample selection strategy, combining a noise detection algorithm with a hard sample mining technique. The noise detection algorithm identifies and removes noisy samples, while the hard sample mining technique selectively retains informative examples that are most relevant to the recognition task. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art approaches, achieving significant improvements in recognition accuracy on several benchmark datasets. The key findings highlight the importance of sample quality and the benefits of focusing on hard examples in fine-grained visual recognition. Our research contributes to the development of more accurate and robust visual recognition systems, with potential applications in image classification, object detection, and image retrieval. Key keywords: fine-grained visual recognition, web images, noise detection, hard sample mining, deep learning, computer vision.
