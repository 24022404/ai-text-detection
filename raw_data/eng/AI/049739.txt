This paper investigates the intersection of deep graph matching algorithms and mixed-integer linear programming (MILP) to address complex combinatorial optimization challenges in graph-related problems. The objective is to enhance graph matching processes—central to numerous fields such as computer vision, bioinformatics, and social network analysis—by leveraging the strengths of both deep learning and MILP. Our approach synthesizes a deep neural network to extract meaningful graph embeddings, which are then employed to inform a MILP formulation that aims to optimize the matching accuracy and efficiency. Results demonstrate that our integrated method not only surpasses traditional graph matching techniques in terms of speed and accuracy but also provides a robust framework for handling large, complex datasets. Furthermore, the study underscores the importance of careful relaxation strategies within MILP to prevent substantial performance trade-offs. The proposed model contributes to enhancing state-of-the-art graph matching solutions, offering new insights into the potential of hybrid methodologies in computational optimization tasks. Keywords include deep graph matching, mixed-integer linear programming, combinatorial optimization, hybrid algorithms, and graph embeddings.
