The registration of noisy point clouds is a fundamental challenge in computer vision and 3D reconstruction, with applications ranging from robotics to augmented reality. This paper addresses the problem of achieving globally optimal alignment between two or more point clouds in the presence of significant noise and outliers. We propose a novel optimization framework that combines robust statistical methods with advanced geometric techniques to ensure accurate and reliable registration. Our approach leverages a combination of iterative closest point (ICP) algorithms and global optimization strategies, enhanced by a noise-resilient similarity metric. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms existing state-of-the-art techniques in terms of accuracy and robustness, particularly in scenarios with high levels of noise and partial overlap. The framework is validated on both synthetic and real-world datasets, showcasing its practical applicability. This research contributes a scalable and efficient solution to the point cloud registration problem, with potential implications for autonomous navigation, 3D modeling, and medical imaging.  

**Keywords**: point cloud registration, global optimization, noise resilience, iterative closest point (ICP), 3D reconstruction, computer vision.
