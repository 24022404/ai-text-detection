This paper presents a transfer learning approach for melanoma detection, leveraging pre-trained convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to improve the accuracy of skin lesion classification. The objective is to develop an efficient and reliable method for distinguishing between benign and malignant lesions, addressing the need for early detection and diagnosis of melanoma. Our approach utilizes a fine-tuning strategy, adapting the pre-trained CNNs to the specific task of skin lesion classification using the ISIC 2017 dataset. The results show a significant improvement in classification performance, achieving a high accuracy rate and outperforming traditional machine learning methods. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of transfer learning in medical image analysis, particularly in the context of skin lesion classification. This research contributes to the development of AI-powered diagnostic tools, enhancing the potential for early melanoma detection and treatment. The study's implications extend to the broader field of medical imaging, demonstrating the value of transfer learning in improving the accuracy and efficiency of disease diagnosis. Key keywords: transfer learning, melanoma detection, skin lesion classification, convolutional neural networks, ISIC 2017 challenge.
