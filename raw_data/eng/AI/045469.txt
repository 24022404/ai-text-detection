This research addresses the challenge of understanding complex human actions in home environments by introducing a novel framework, the Home Action Genome. The study aims to enable cooperative compositional action recognition and understanding, facilitating improved interaction with and interpretation of human activities in domestic settings.

Methods/Approach: The proposed framework leverages advanced computer vision techniques and deep learning algorithms to automatically segment and analyze compositional actions within a home. By integrating scene understanding and cooperative context modeling, the method effectively captures intricate interactions and dependencies among actions, objects, and environments. The system is evaluated using simulation environments with diverse action datasets to ensure robust performance across various scenarios.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate that the Home Action Genome significantly enhances action understanding accuracy compared to existing models, achieving superior performance in recognizing and predicting sequences of complex actions in domestic settings. The framework also shows promising results in generalization across different household environments and variations in human behavior.

Conclusion/Implications: This study contributes a valuable model for advancing home automation and intelligent systems through improved action recognition. The Home Action Genome's cooperative compositional approach paves the way for novel applications in smart home technologies, assistive living environments, and human-robot interaction systems. By accurately interpreting human actions, the framework can enhance the efficacy and user experience of intelligent home systems.

Keywords: Home Action Genome, action understanding, computer vision, deep learning, home automation, human-robot interaction, scene understanding.
