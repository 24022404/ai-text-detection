This paper introduces a novel approach to latent space manipulation, leveraging a surrogate gradient field to enable efficient and precise control over generative models. The objective is to develop a method that allows for flexible and interpretable editing of generated data, such as images or texts, by navigating the latent space. Our approach employs a surrogate model to approximate the gradient field of the latent space, facilitating the identification of meaningful directions for manipulation. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our method through extensive experiments, showcasing its ability to achieve high-quality edits while outperforming existing techniques. The key findings highlight the potential of surrogate gradient fields for latent space manipulation, enabling applications such as image editing, data augmentation, and style transfer. Our research contributes to the advancement of generative models and latent space exploration, with implications for fields like computer vision, natural language processing, and artificial intelligence. Key keywords: latent space manipulation, surrogate gradient field, generative models, image editing, data augmentation.
