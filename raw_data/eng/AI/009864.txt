Deep neural networks (DNNs) have achieved remarkable performance across various complex tasks, yet their decision-making processes remain largely opaque. This paper presents an adversarial approach to enhance the interpretability of DNN predictions by systematically generating and analyzing adversarial examples. Our method leverages adversarial perturbations—minimal, targeted changes to input data that alter model outputs—to identify critical input features influencing model decisions. We implement a novel framework that combines adversarial attack techniques with saliency mapping, enabling the extraction of human-interpretable explanations for both correct and incorrect model predictions. Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets in image classification demonstrate that our approach not only exposes vulnerabilities in model reasoning but also provides more precise and actionable insight into the internal workings of deep models compared to existing interpretability techniques. The proposed adversarial explanation framework improves transparency and supports model debugging, offering significant benefits for applications in safety-critical domains such as healthcare and autonomous systems. Key contributions include a robust evaluation of explanation fidelity and scalability to large-scale neural architectures. Our findings emphasize the dual role of adversarial methods: as both a tool for exposing model weaknesses and a mechanism for generating transparent, trustworthy explanations. Keywords: deep neural networks, adversarial examples, explainability, model interpretability, saliency mapping, transparency.
