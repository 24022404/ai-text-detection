Time series forecasting is a critical task in various domains, including finance, healthcare, and energy management. This paper presents a novel approach to time series forecasting using Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks combined with symbolic representation techniques. The objective is to enhance the accuracy and interpretability of predictions by transforming raw time series data into symbolic sequences, which capture essential patterns and trends. The proposed method leverages the LSTM's ability to model long-term dependencies and the symbolic approach's efficiency in reducing data complexity. Experiments were conducted on multiple benchmark datasets, comparing the performance of the proposed method with traditional LSTM models and other state-of-the-art forecasting techniques. Results demonstrate significant improvements in prediction accuracy, particularly for datasets with high noise and non-linear patterns. Additionally, the symbolic representation provides a more interpretable framework for understanding the underlying dynamics of the time series. The findings highlight the potential of this hybrid approach for real-world applications, offering both robust forecasting capabilities and enhanced transparency. This research contributes to the growing body of work on advanced time series analysis and underscores the value of integrating symbolic methods with deep learning architectures.  

**Keywords**: Time series forecasting, LSTM networks, symbolic representation, deep learning, interpretability, pattern recognition.
