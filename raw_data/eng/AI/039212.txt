Few-shot object detection aims to accurately identify and localize objects of novel classes using only a limited number of annotated samples, presenting significant challenges due to data scarcity and the complex variability of real-world scenes. This paper proposes a novel framework, Dense Relation Distillation with Context-aware Aggregation (DRD-CA), to address these challenges by enhancing feature representation and relational reasoning between images. Our approach introduces a dense relation distillation module that comprehensively captures interactions across multi-level features between support and query samples, facilitating robust transfer of semantic information even in low-data regimes. Further, we incorporate a context-aware aggregation mechanism that adaptively integrates contextual cues from the scene, improving object distinction and reducing false positives. Extensive experiments on standard few-shot object detection benchmarks demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art approaches in both detection accuracy and generalization ability. The DRD-CA framework thus advances the reliability and applicability of few-shot object detection, with potential extensions to related tasks in low-resource learning environments. 

Keywords: few-shot object detection, dense relation distillation, context-aware aggregation, feature learning, semantic transfer, deep learning.
