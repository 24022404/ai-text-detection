This paper introduces SketchGen, a novel approach for generating constrained CAD sketches, addressing the challenge of efficiently creating design-intent driven sketches for engineering and architectural applications. Our method leverages a conditional generative adversarial network (cGAN) architecture, incorporating geometric constraint solvers within the training loop to ensure generated sketches adhere to specified dimensional and geometric relationships. The generator network learns to produce valid sketch geometries, while the discriminator distinguishes between real and generated sketches based on both visual realism and constraint satisfaction. We demonstrate that SketchGen outperforms existing sketch generation techniques in terms of constraint adherence, geometric accuracy, and visual quality. Experiments on a diverse dataset of CAD sketches show a significant improvement in generating sketches that accurately reflect the intended design constraints. Furthermore, we present a user study that validates the utility of SketchGen in assisting designers with the initial stages of CAD modeling. Key findings highlight the potential of constrained generative models for automating and accelerating the CAD design process. Keywords: CAD Sketch Generation, Generative Adversarial Networks, Geometric Constraints, Design Automation, Computer-Aided Design.
