This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing spatial perception in auditory environments by leveraging binaural sounds for semantic object prediction and spatial sound super-resolution. The objective is to enable machines to accurately identify and localize objects in a 3D space using only binaural audio inputs, which mimic human hearing. We propose a novel deep learning framework that integrates advanced neural network architectures to simultaneously predict semantic object categories and reconstruct high-resolution spatial sound fields. The approach combines convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for feature extraction with transformer-based models to capture temporal and spatial dependencies in binaural audio data. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in both object prediction accuracy and spatial sound resolution, outperforming existing techniques by a significant margin. The framework also exhibits robustness in diverse acoustic environments, including noisy and reverberant settings. This research contributes to the development of intelligent auditory systems with applications in augmented reality, robotics, and assistive technologies for the visually impaired. Key innovations include the integration of semantic and spatial learning tasks within a unified model and the use of binaural sound for high-fidelity 3D scene understanding. Keywords: binaural sound, spatial sound super-resolution, semantic object prediction, deep learning, 3D auditory perception.
