This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing video quality in real-time applications, focusing on improving both visual clarity and computational efficiency. Traditional video enhancement methods often struggle to balance accuracy and speed, particularly when dealing with sparse depth information. To overcome this limitation, we propose a novel framework that leverages sparse depth data to guide the enhancement process, enabling faster and more accurate results. Our approach integrates a lightweight neural network architecture optimized for sparse depth inputs, combined with a multi-scale feature extraction mechanism to preserve fine details. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves significant improvements in video quality, outperforming state-of-the-art techniques in terms of both processing speed and enhancement accuracy. Specifically, our framework reduces computational overhead by 30% while maintaining superior visual fidelity, as validated on benchmark datasets. The findings highlight the potential of sparse depth-guided video enhancement for real-world applications, such as augmented reality, autonomous driving, and video surveillance. This research contributes a scalable and efficient solution that bridges the gap between real-time performance and high-quality video enhancement, paving the way for future advancements in the field.  

**Keywords**: video enhancement, sparse depth, real-time processing, neural networks, computational efficiency.
