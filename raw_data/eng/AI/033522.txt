This paper introduces Deep Ordinal Reinforcement Learning (DORL), a novel approach to address reinforcement learning problems with ordinal rewards. Unlike traditional RL methods that treat rewards as absolute values, DORL leverages the ordinal relationship between rewards to improve learning stability and sample efficiency. We propose a deep neural network architecture that learns to map states to a distribution over ordinal reward categories, enabling the agent to effectively discriminate between different levels of reward. This approach is trained end-to-end using a modified cross-entropy loss that respects the ordinal nature of the rewards. We evaluate DORL on several benchmark tasks with ordinal reward structures, demonstrating significant improvements in learning speed and final performance compared to standard deep reinforcement learning algorithms, including DQN and variants. The results highlight the effectiveness of explicitly modeling ordinal reward information for enhanced decision-making in complex environments. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Ordinal Rewards, Deep Learning, Ordinal Classification, Reward Shaping.
