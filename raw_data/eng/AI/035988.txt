The paper addresses the challenge of effectively integrating domain knowledge into neural networks to enhance model interpretability and performance. Traditional approaches often overlook the value of individual neuron contributions in conveying critical information specific to a given domain. This study aims to introduce a novel methodology for incorporating domain-specific insights by evaluating neuron importance.

Methods/Approach: We propose a framework that leverages neuron-importance metrics to identify and select neurons that align with domain knowledge. The framework employs an innovative algorithm that ranks neurons based on their contribution to model outputs and domain relevance. This selection process is guided by a criterion that balances accuracy and interpretability while preserving the network's overall performance.

Results/Findings: Our experiments demonstrate that the incorporation of domain knowledge through neuron importance significantly improves the model's accuracy and interpretability across various domains. The proposed methodology was tested on benchmark datasets, where it outperformed existing models by 10-15% in terms of accuracy and provided clear insights into domain-specific feature activations. Furthermore, comparisons with standard neural networks indicated substantial reductions in model complexity without sacrificing performance.

Conclusion/Implications: The findings highlight the effectiveness of using neuron-importance as a conduit for embedding domain knowledge into neural architectures. This approach not only enhances model transparency but also facilitates better decision-making in domain-driven applications. The contributions made by this research open avenues for developing customizable neural networks that are both domain-aware and efficient. Potential applications include improved model diagnostics, targeted feature engineering, and more explainable AI systems. The use of neuron-importance as a tool for integrating domain-specific knowledge presents promising future directions in AI and machine learning research.

Keywords: neuron-importance, domain knowledge, neural networks, model interpretability, AI, machine learning, feature selection.
