Reinforcement Learning (RL) has shown remarkable success in decision-making tasks, yet its lack of transparency and interpretability remains a significant challenge, particularly in governing agent behavior. This paper introduces a novel framework, Action-Conditional β-VAE (AC-βVAE), designed to enhance the transparency and efficacy of RL agents by disentangling latent representations of actions and states. The proposed approach integrates a variational autoencoder (VAE) conditioned on agent actions, enabling the extraction of interpretable latent factors that govern decision-making processes. By leveraging a tunable β parameter, the model balances reconstruction accuracy and disentanglement, ensuring both high performance and explainability. Experiments conducted on benchmark RL environments demonstrate that AC-βVAE outperforms traditional RL methods in terms of interpretability while maintaining competitive task performance. The framework provides actionable insights into agent behavior, making it a valuable tool for applications requiring transparent and governable AI systems, such as autonomous robotics and safety-critical domains. This research contributes to the growing field of explainable AI by offering a scalable and interpretable solution for deep RL, paving the way for more trustworthy and controllable intelligent systems.  

**Keywords**: Reinforcement Learning, Variational Autoencoder, Transparency, Explainable AI, Disentangled Representations, Action-Conditional Models.
