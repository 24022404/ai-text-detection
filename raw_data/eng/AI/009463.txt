In the evolving landscape of signal recognition, traditional approaches often struggle to effectively capture the intricacies of complex-valued data. This paper presents "Signal Transformer," a novel architecture that integrates complex-valued attention mechanisms with meta-learning to enhance signal recognition performance across diverse applications. Our method targets the challenge of learning from limited data by leveraging meta-learning principles, allowing the model to adapt quickly to new signal types with minimal supervision. We employ a complex-valued transformer architecture that effectively processes signals in their natural domain, improving the model's ability to discern subtle variations in signal patterns. Comprehensive experiments demonstrate that Signal Transformer outperforms existing methods on benchmark datasets, achieving superior accuracy and robustness in recognizing both amplitude and phase information. Through this work, we highlight the effectiveness of combining complex-valued representations with advanced attention techniques and meta-learning strategies, paving the way for future advancements in signal recognition tasks. The implications of this research extend to various fields, including telecommunications, biomedical signal processing, and audio analysis, where the need for precise signal interpretation is paramount. 

Keywords: signal recognition, complex-valued attention, meta-learning, transformer architecture, signal processing.
