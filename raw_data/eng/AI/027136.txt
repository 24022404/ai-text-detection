This paper addresses the challenging problem of extreme relative pose estimation for RGB-D scans, where scenes often contain substantial occlusions and incomplete data. The research targets enhancing the accuracy and robustness of pose estimation by leveraging scene completion techniques.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel framework that integrates scene completion with pose estimation, utilizing advanced deep learning models to infer missing information in RGB-D scans. The method employs a multi-stage process where a scene completion model initially reconstructs the 3D environment, followed by a relative pose estimation module that aligns the completed scenes. This approach reduces the reliance on dense input data and improves estimation precision.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms traditional pose estimation techniques, particularly in scenarios involving extreme occlusions and partial views. Key findings indicate a reduction in pose error by up to 30% compared to existing approaches. The robustness of the method is validated through comprehensive tests on benchmark RGB-D datasets.

Conclusion/Implications: The proposed technique of integrating scene completion with pose estimation offers a powerful tool for applications requiring accurate spatial understanding from incomplete RGB-D data. The research contributes to the field by providing a methodology that enhances pose estimation under challenging conditions and could be beneficial for robotics, augmented reality, and autonomous systems. Future work will explore the incorporation of more sophisticated scene priors and real-time applications.

Keywords: RGB-D scans, pose estimation, scene completion, deep learning, occlusions, 3D reconstruction.
