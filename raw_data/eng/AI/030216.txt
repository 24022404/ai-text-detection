Accurate and timely traffic speed prediction is crucial for intelligent transportation systems. This paper presents a novel deep learning model leveraging both bidirectional and unidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) recurrent neural networks for network-wide traffic speed prediction. The model captures both historical and future dependencies in traffic flow by employing a bidirectional LSTM to process historical data and a unidirectional LSTM to forecast future speeds. The approach utilizes network-wide traffic data as input, allowing the model to learn complex spatial-temporal correlations between different road segments. Experimental results using real-world traffic data demonstrate that the proposed model outperforms traditional time-series methods and other deep learning architectures, achieving significant improvements in prediction accuracy, particularly during peak hours and under congested conditions. The findings highlight the effectiveness of combining bidirectional and unidirectional LSTMs for capturing intricate traffic patterns, offering a promising solution for real-time traffic management and control. Keywords: Traffic Speed Prediction, Deep Learning, LSTM, Recurrent Neural Networks, Bidirectional LSTM, Intelligent Transportation Systems.
