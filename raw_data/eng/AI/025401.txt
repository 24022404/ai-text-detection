This paper introduces the Monash University, UEA, UCR Time Series Extrinsic Regression Archive, a comprehensive repository of time series datasets designed to facilitate research in extrinsic regression. The objective of this archive is to provide a unified platform for evaluating and comparing the performance of various regression models on time series data. A novel approach was employed to curate and preprocess the datasets, incorporating techniques from data mining and feature engineering to enhance their quality and relevance. The archive comprises a diverse collection of time series datasets from various domains, including finance, climate, and energy. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of the archive in evaluating state-of-the-art regression models, with key findings highlighting the importance of dataset diversity and feature extraction in achieving accurate predictions. The Monash University, UEA, UCR Time Series Extrinsic Regression Archive contributes to the field of time series analysis by providing a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners, enabling the development of more accurate and robust regression models. Key keywords: time series analysis, extrinsic regression, dataset archive, data mining, feature engineering.
