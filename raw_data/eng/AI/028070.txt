This paper explores the application of spectral learning techniques to matrices and tensors, addressing the challenge of efficiently extracting meaningful patterns and structures from high-dimensional data. The objective is to develop scalable and robust methods for decomposing and analyzing matrices and tensors, which are fundamental to various applications in machine learning, data mining, and signal processing. We propose a novel spectral learning framework that leverages eigenvalue and singular value decomposition techniques, combined with optimization algorithms, to handle large-scale datasets with improved computational efficiency. Our approach is validated through extensive experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets, demonstrating superior performance in terms of accuracy and runtime compared to traditional methods. The results highlight the framework's ability to uncover latent structures and relationships in complex data, making it particularly suitable for tasks such as dimensionality reduction, clustering, and feature extraction. The proposed method contributes to advancing spectral learning by introducing a scalable and interpretable solution for matrix and tensor analysis, with potential applications in recommender systems, computer vision, and network analysis. Key innovations include the integration of spectral techniques with modern optimization strategies, enabling efficient handling of high-dimensional data while maintaining interpretability. This work provides a foundation for future research in spectral learning and its applications across diverse domains.  

**Keywords**: spectral learning, matrix decomposition, tensor analysis, eigenvalue decomposition, singular value decomposition, dimensionality reduction, optimization algorithms.
