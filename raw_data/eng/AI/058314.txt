Short-term load forecasting is crucial for efficient power system operation and planning. Traditional load forecasting models often struggle to adapt to the dynamic nature of load patterns, leading to reduced accuracy. This paper introduces a novel reinforcement learning (RL) based dynamic model selection framework to address this challenge. The proposed approach leverages RL to intelligently select the most appropriate forecasting model from a diverse pool of candidates at each time step. The RL agent learns to optimize model selection based on historical load data and real-time environmental factors, such as weather conditions and calendar variables. Experimental results on real-world load datasets demonstrate that the proposed RL-based dynamic model selection significantly outperforms static model selection methods and benchmark forecasting models, achieving up to 15% improvement in forecasting accuracy. The findings highlight the potential of RL in enhancing the adaptability and robustness of short-term load forecasting, facilitating better grid management and resource allocation. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Load Forecasting, Dynamic Model Selection, Power Systems, Smart Grid, Time Series Analysis.
