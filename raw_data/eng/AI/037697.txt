Universal adversarial attacks pose a significant threat to the robustness of deep learning models by crafting a single, model-agnostic perturbation that can fool the network across a wide range of inputs. This survey provides a comprehensive overview of the current landscape of universal adversarial attack research. We categorize existing attack methodologies based on their underlying principles, including optimization-based, generative, and data-driven approaches. We analyze their strengths and weaknesses in terms of attack success rate, perturbation magnitude, transferability, and computational efficiency. Furthermore, we explore various defense strategies proposed to mitigate universal adversarial perturbations, examining their effectiveness and potential limitations. Finally, we discuss open challenges and future research directions in this evolving field, emphasizing the need for more robust and adaptive defense mechanisms to ensure the reliable deployment of deep learning models in real-world applications. Keywords: adversarial attacks, deep learning, robustness, universal perturbations, security, machine learning.
