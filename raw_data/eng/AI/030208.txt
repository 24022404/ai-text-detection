Salient object detection (SOD) aims to identify the most visually distinctive objects in an image. Existing methods often rely on post-processing steps involving thresholds, which can be sensitive to parameter tuning and limit performance. This paper introduces a novel approach to directly optimize the F-measure, a widely used metric for evaluating SOD, thereby circumventing the need for threshold selection. We propose a differentiable approximation of the F-measure, enabling end-to-end training of deep neural networks for SOD. Our method incorporates a dedicated loss function based on this approximation, guiding the network to produce saliency maps that inherently maximize the F-measure. Experiments on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that our threshold-free approach achieves state-of-the-art performance, significantly outperforming existing methods, especially in challenging scenarios with complex backgrounds. The results highlight the effectiveness of directly optimizing the F-measure and the potential for improved robustness and accuracy in salient object detection. Keywords: Salient Object Detection, F-measure, Threshold-free, Deep Learning, Loss Function.
