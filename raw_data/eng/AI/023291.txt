Accurate lesion segmentation is crucial for computer-aided diagnosis and treatment planning. This paper introduces a novel deep attention-guided fusion network (DAF-Net) designed to improve the precision and robustness of lesion segmentation in medical images. DAF-Net leverages a multi-scale convolutional neural network architecture, incorporating spatial and channel attention mechanisms to selectively emphasize relevant features and suppress noise. Specifically, attention modules are integrated within the network to guide the fusion of features extracted at different scales, enhancing the representation of subtle lesion boundaries. Experimental results on publicly available lesion segmentation datasets demonstrate that DAF-Net achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of Dice score, Jaccard index, and Hausdorff distance. The proposed approach exhibits improved generalization capabilities and reduced sensitivity to image variations, making it a promising tool for clinical applications. Keywords: Lesion segmentation, deep learning, attention mechanism, medical imaging, convolutional neural network, fusion network.
