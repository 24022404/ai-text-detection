Video captioning, the task of generating descriptive textual summaries for video content, remains a challenging problem in computer vision and natural language processing. This paper introduces a novel approach to enhance video captioning performance by leveraging a Dynamic Loss Network (DLN). The proposed method dynamically adjusts the loss function during training to better align with the evolving learning process, addressing the limitations of static loss functions that often lead to suboptimal model convergence. Our approach integrates a multi-modal framework combining convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for visual feature extraction and transformer-based architectures for sequence modeling. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets, including MSVD and MSR-VTT, demonstrate significant improvements in caption quality, as measured by BLEU, METEOR, and CIDEr metrics. The DLN-based model outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving a 5% increase in CIDEr score compared to existing approaches. The results highlight the effectiveness of dynamic loss adaptation in improving model robustness and generalization. This work contributes to advancing video captioning by introducing a flexible training mechanism that can be adapted to other sequence-to-sequence tasks in artificial intelligence.  

**Keywords**: Video captioning, Dynamic Loss Network, multi-modal learning, transformer models, sequence-to-sequence tasks, computer vision, natural language processing.
