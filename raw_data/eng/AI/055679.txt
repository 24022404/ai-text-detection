The paper explores advancements in Vision Transformers (ViTs), a prominent architecture in computer vision. Despite their success, ViTs often suffer from computational inefficiencies and high resource demands. This research aims to enhance ViT performance through novel token pooling and attention sharing methodologies. 

Methods/Approach: We introduce PSViT, a modified Vision Transformer model that incorporates token pooling to reduce token redundancy and attention sharing to optimize attention mechanisms across layers. The proposed methods aim to streamline computational processes while maintaining or improving model accuracy. Experiments were conducted on standard image classification datasets to evaluate PSViT against baseline ViT models.

Results/Findings: PSViT demonstrated significant improvements, offering a reduction in computational cost without sacrificing performance. The model achieved superior accuracy on multiple benchmark datasets like ImageNet, surpassing traditional ViT architectures in both efficiency and speed. Comparative analysis indicates that PSViT models require fewer resources and time, while delivering robust results.

Conclusion/Implications: The integration of token pooling and attention sharing in ViTs represents a substantial leap in model optimization. PSViT's approach provides a more resource-efficient alternative for deploying ViTs in various practical scenarios, expanding the accessibility of advanced computer vision technologies. This research highlights the potential for further innovations in ViT architecture, paving the way for future developments in efficient deep learning models.

Keywords: Vision Transformer, ViT, token pooling, attention sharing, computer vision, deep learning, model optimization.
