This paper addresses the problem of retrieving complementary items for a given fashion outfit, aiming to enhance the online shopping experience. Our approach utilizes a deep learning-based model that integrates visual and textual features to recommend matching items such as shoes, handbags, and accessories. The proposed system employs a multimodal fusion technique, combining convolutional neural networks for image processing and recurrent neural networks for text analysis. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, outperforming existing state-of-the-art approaches in terms of retrieval accuracy and user satisfaction. The key contributions of this research include the development of a novel outfit embedding framework and the introduction of a large-scale fashion dataset for complementary item retrieval. Our study has significant implications for e-commerce and fashion recommendation systems, enabling users to discover complementary items that complete their outfits. Key keywords: fashion recommendation, outfit completion, deep learning, multimodal fusion, e-commerce.
