Skeleton-based action recognition has become a prominent research area due to its effectiveness in capturing human movement for applications in surveillance, human-computer interaction, and healthcare. This paper presents a novel Attention Enhanced Graph Convolutional Long Short-Term Memory (AE-GC-LSTM) network designed to improve the accuracy and efficiency of action recognition from skeleton data. The proposed model integrates graph convolutional networks (GCNs) to effectively model the spatial relationships between joints, while LSTM layers capture temporal dynamics across frame sequences. To further enhance discriminative capability, an attention mechanism is incorporated, enabling the model to focus on critical joints and frames that are most informative for recognizing specific actions. Extensive experiments are conducted on benchmark skeleton action datasets, demonstrating that the AE-GC-LSTM consistently outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of recognition accuracy and robustness to noise. The results confirm that our attention-enhanced approach significantly improves the model’s ability to handle complex motion patterns and occlusions. This work advances the field of skeleton-based action recognition by introducing a flexible and scalable architecture that successfully combines spatial, temporal, and attention-based learning. Potential applications include security monitoring, smart fitness systems, and rehabilitation assessment.

Keywords: skeleton-based action recognition, graph convolutional network, attention mechanism, LSTM, spatio-temporal modeling, human activity recognition, deep learning.
