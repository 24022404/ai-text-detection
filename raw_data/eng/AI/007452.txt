Vertical Federated Learning (VFL) has emerged as a promising framework for collaborative machine learning while preserving data privacy across organizations that possess different features of the same user set. However, ensuring model fairness—preventing biased outcomes across sensitive user groups—remains a significant and largely unaddressed challenge in VFL due to the fragmentation of features and the heterogeneity of participants. This paper presents a novel approach for achieving model fairness in VFL by introducing a fairness-aware optimization mechanism that enables participating parties to jointly minimize prediction bias without compromising data confidentiality. We design an adaptive algorithm that incorporates fairness constraints during federated aggregation, allowing local models to adjust their learning process in response to detected disparities among demographic groups. Experimental evaluations on real-world and synthetic datasets demonstrate that our method effectively reduces group-based prediction disparities while maintaining competitive model accuracy, outperforming existing VFL baselines in both fairness and performance metrics. The proposed solution enables practical and ethical deployment of VFL in applications such as finance, healthcare, and social platforms, where equitable decision-making is critical. Our work highlights the importance of integrating fairness assurance into federated frameworks and paves the way for developing more responsible collaborative AI systems. Keywords: vertical federated learning, model fairness, bias mitigation, privacy-preserving machine learning, fairness-aware optimization.
