Pose-guided person image synthesis aims to generate realistic images of a person in a target pose, given a source image and the target pose. This paper introduces Soft-Gated Warping-GAN, a novel approach that enhances image synthesis quality by improving pose transfer and detail preservation. Our method employs a soft-gated warping module to adaptively blend warped features from the source image with the target pose representation, allowing for more accurate pose transformations while minimizing artifacts. Furthermore, we incorporate a multi-scale discriminator with attention mechanisms to enforce realistic texture generation and maintain identity consistency. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that Soft-Gated Warping-GAN significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of visual fidelity, pose accuracy, and identity preservation, as evidenced by improved quantitative metrics and qualitative evaluations. The proposed architecture effectively addresses challenges related to complex pose variations and occlusion handling, paving the way for more robust and realistic person image synthesis.

**Keywords:** Image Synthesis, Pose-Guided Generation, GAN, Warping, Attention Mechanism, Person Re-identification.
