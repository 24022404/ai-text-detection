The system is designed with scalability in mind and ensures compatibility with a wide range of social authentication providers such as Facebook, Google, and Twitter. Leveraging a decentralized architecture, it significantly boosts both request performance and the system's capacity to handle large volumes of authentication requests. As illustrated in Figure 3.1, the request flow for a user interacting with the social login system is meticulously organized into three distinct sub-processes. The architecture’s design efficiently supports future growth and integration with emerging social platforms.
