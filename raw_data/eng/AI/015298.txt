This paper introduces Language2Pose, a novel approach to human pose forecasting conditioned on natural language descriptions of future actions. The objective is to bridge the gap between language understanding and realistic human motion prediction. Our method employs a transformer-based architecture that integrates language embeddings with past pose sequences to generate plausible future poses. Specifically, we use a multi-modal attention mechanism to fuse linguistic context with motion dynamics, enabling the model to anticipate how language instructions influence body movement. We evaluate Language2Pose on a newly collected dataset of human actions paired with detailed language descriptions and demonstrate superior performance compared to state-of-the-art pose forecasting methods, particularly in scenarios involving complex and varied actions. Quantitative results show significant improvements in prediction accuracy and motion realism, as measured by standard pose error metrics. Furthermore, we present qualitative examples showcasing the model's ability to generate diverse and contextually appropriate pose sequences based on different linguistic inputs. The proposed approach holds promise for applications in human-computer interaction, robotics, and virtual reality, where natural language control of avatar movements is desired.

**Keywords:** Pose Forecasting, Natural Language Processing, Human Motion Prediction, Transformer Networks, Multi-modal Learning.
