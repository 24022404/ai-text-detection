Facial Expression Recognition (FER) is a critical task in computer vision with applications in human-computer interaction, emotion analysis, and mental health monitoring. This paper introduces a novel approach to enhance FER by integrating the Squeeze and Excitation (SE) mechanism into a Vision Transformer (ViT) architecture. The proposed method leverages the global context modeling capabilities of ViT while incorporating SE blocks to adaptively recalibrate channel-wise feature responses, improving the model's ability to capture subtle facial expression details. The model is trained and evaluated on widely used FER datasets, including FER2013 and RAF-DB. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed SE-ViT achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The integration of SE blocks not only enhances feature representation but also reduces computational overhead compared to traditional attention mechanisms. This work contributes to advancing FER by combining the strengths of transformer-based architectures and attention mechanisms, offering a scalable and efficient solution for real-world applications.  

**Keywords**: Facial Expression Recognition, Vision Transformer, Squeeze and Excitation, Attention Mechanism, Computer Vision, Deep Learning.
