Abstract: The objective of this research is to enhance the accuracy of taxi demand forecasting by developing a novel tessellation strategy based on the HEDGE algorithm. Accurate forecasting of taxi demand is crucial for optimizing fleet management and improving urban transportation systems. Our approach integrates the HEDGE algorithm, a popular machine learning ensemble technique, to systematically tessellate geographical areas into regions that better capture spatial passenger demand patterns. This method allows for a more granular and context-aware analysis of demand fluctuations across urban landscapes. Extensive experiments were conducted on real-world taxi datasets, demonstrating that our HEDGE-based tessellation strategy significantly outperforms traditional forecasting methods by effectively improving both prediction accuracy and computational efficiency. The results indicate reductions in prediction error, showcasing the adaptability and robustness of our approach across diverse urban settings. Our research contributes to the field by providing a scalable and dynamic solution for demand forecasting that can be employed by taxi service operators to enhance operational decision-making. Key implications of this work include better resource allocation and improved service levels in urban mobility systems. Keywords: taxi demand forecasting, HEDGE algorithm, tessellation strategy, urban transportation, machine learning, ensemble methods, spatial analysis.
