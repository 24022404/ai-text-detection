As deep reinforcement learning (DRL) continues to be applied in critical domains requiring high safety standards, ensuring safe exploration and operation is paramount. This paper addresses the challenge of integrating safety measures into DRL processes, specifically targeting the need for systems to learn autonomously while adhering to safety constraints.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel framework that combines deep reinforcement learning with online Gaussian Process (GP) estimation to enhance safety. By leveraging GP's ability to model uncertainties, the proposed method dynamically evaluates potential risks in real-time, allowing the DRL agent to make informed decisions that prioritize safety. The framework integrates these estimations into the policy update processes, facilitating a fine-tuned balance between exploration and exploitation in a safe manner.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate the efficacy of the proposed safety-guided DRL framework. Results indicate a significant reduction in safety violations compared to traditional DRL approaches, while maintaining competitive performance metrics across various benchmarks. The integration of online GP estimation not only enhances safety but also accelerates the learning process by providing better coverage of the state space.

Conclusion/Implications: Our research advances the field of safe reinforcement learning by providing a robust mechanism to incorporate safety constraints without compromising the agent's learning capabilities. The proposed framework opens avenues for its application in safety-critical systems such as autonomous driving and robotics, where safety cannot be compromised. By demonstrating improved safety standards and learning efficiency, this study sets a foundation for future exploration of integrating probabilistic models in DRL.

Key Keywords: Safety-guided, Deep Reinforcement Learning, Gaussian Process, Online Estimation, Safe Exploration, Autonomous Systems.
