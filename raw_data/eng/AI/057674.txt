The paper addresses the challenge of efficiently performing regression analysis on data situated in non-Euclidean spaces, specifically focusing on geodesic regression which is crucial for applications dealing with data on manifolds. Traditional methods have been computationally intensive, thus motivating the development of a faster predictive model.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel algorithm for simple geodesic regression that leverages predictive modeling techniques to significantly reduce computational overhead. By integrating a refined analytical framework with advanced optimization strategies, our approach efficiently computes regression coefficients by anticipating geodesic paths, thus minimizing iterative calculations.

Results/Findings: Our model demonstrates substantial improvements in computation speed while maintaining high accuracy, as evidenced by experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets. The method achieves better performance metrics compared to existing geodesic regression models, highlighting its potential for large-scale and real-time applications. The increased processing speed does not compromise the quality of the regression outcomes, as shown by comparative evaluations.

Conclusion/Implications: This research presents a significant advancement in the field of regression analysis on manifolds by providing a rapid and reliable alternative to conventional geodesic regression techniques. The implications of this work are profound for domains such as computer vision, medical imaging, and machine learning, where data is often inherently non-linear and resides on complex geometrical structures. Our novel approach offers a valuable tool for researchers and industry professionals needing fast and accurate geodesic regression.

Keywords: geodesic regression, non-Euclidean spaces, manifold data, computational efficiency, predictive modeling, optimization strategies.
