This paper introduces a novel Temporal Modulation Network (TMNet) for controllable space-time video super-resolution (STVSR). Addressing the limitations of existing STVSR methods in independently controlling spatial and temporal resolutions, TMNet employs a modulation mechanism to explicitly disentangle and regulate these dimensions. Our approach utilizes a recurrent architecture with modulated temporal warping, allowing for flexible upsampling ratios in both spatial and temporal domains. Specifically, the network learns temporal modulation factors conditioned on desired temporal scaling factors, which dynamically adjust the temporal warping process. This enables precise control over the generated frame rate while simultaneously enhancing spatial details. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that TMNet achieves superior performance in terms of both quantitative metrics and qualitative visual quality compared to state-of-the-art STVSR methods. Furthermore, the proposed framework offers greater flexibility and control in generating high-resolution videos with user-specified frame rates. Keywords: Video Super-Resolution, Space-Time, Temporal Modulation, Controllable Generation, Deep Learning.
