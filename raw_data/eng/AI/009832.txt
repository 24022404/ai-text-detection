This paper presents a novel approach to iris recognition for personal identification using the LAMSTAR (Large Memory Storage and Retrieval) neural network. The objective of this research is to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of biometric identification systems by leveraging the unique capabilities of LAMSTAR, which excels in pattern recognition and associative memory tasks. The proposed method involves preprocessing iris images to extract distinctive features, followed by training the LAMSTAR network to classify and match these features with high precision. Experimental evaluations were conducted on standard iris datasets, comparing the performance of the LAMSTAR-based system with traditional methods such as Support Vector Machines (SVM) and Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN). Results demonstrate that the LAMSTAR network achieves superior recognition accuracy, particularly in scenarios with noisy or low-quality images, while maintaining computational efficiency. The findings highlight the potential of LAMSTAR as a robust solution for real-world biometric applications, offering improved reliability and scalability. This research contributes to the field of biometrics by introducing a unique neural network-based approach that addresses key challenges in iris recognition, paving the way for more secure and efficient personal identification systems.  

**Keywords**: Iris recognition, LAMSTAR neural network, biometric identification, pattern recognition, personal identification, neural networks.
