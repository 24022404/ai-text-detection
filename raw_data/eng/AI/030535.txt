This paper addresses the challenge of identifying market participants from time series data, a crucial task for understanding market dynamics and making informed decisions. Our objective is to develop a novel approach to discover market participants through the adversarial calibration of multi-agent simulators. We propose a methodology that leverages machine learning and game theory to model the interactions between multiple agents in a market, allowing us to infer the presence and characteristics of various market participants. Our approach utilizes a multi-agent simulator to generate synthetic time series data, which is then used to train an adversarial model to distinguish between different market participant profiles. The results show that our method can effectively identify distinct market participant groups, outperforming traditional clustering-based approaches. The implications of this research are significant, as it enables the development of more accurate market models, improved risk management, and enhanced decision-making capabilities. Key contributions include the introduction of adversarial calibration for multi-agent simulators and the application of game theory to market participant discovery. Relevant keywords: market participant discovery, multi-agent simulators, adversarial calibration, time series analysis, game theory, machine learning.
