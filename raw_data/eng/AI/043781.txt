This paper investigates the vulnerability of Google reCAPTCHA v3, a widely used security mechanism designed to distinguish human users from bots, by leveraging reinforcement learning (RL) techniques. The objective of this research is to evaluate the robustness of reCAPTCHA v3 against advanced machine learning-based attacks and to explore potential countermeasures. We propose a novel RL-based framework that trains an agent to interact with reCAPTCHA v3, learning to mimic human behavior and bypass its detection mechanisms. The approach involves simulating user interactions, optimizing reward functions to maximize success rates, and iteratively refining the agent's strategies. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves a significant success rate in bypassing reCAPTCHA v3, highlighting potential weaknesses in its design. The findings underscore the need for more adaptive and resilient CAPTCHA systems in the face of evolving AI-driven threats. This research contributes to the broader discourse on cybersecurity by providing insights into the limitations of current bot detection technologies and proposing directions for future improvements.  

**Keywords**: reCAPTCHA v3, reinforcement learning, cybersecurity, bot detection, machine learning, human behavior simulation.
