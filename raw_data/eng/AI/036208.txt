This paper proposes a novel approach to human pose estimation by integrating knowledge-guided techniques with deep fractal neural networks. The objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of pose estimation models, particularly in scenarios with occlusions, varying lighting conditions, and diverse human poses. Our method employs a fractal neural network architecture that leverages self-similarity and scale-invariance properties to capture complex pose patterns. Additionally, we incorporate knowledge-guided modules that utilize prior knowledge of human anatomy and pose constraints to refine the estimation process. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art models, achieving significant improvements in pose estimation accuracy and efficiency. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of fractal neural networks to human pose estimation and the development of knowledge-guided techniques to enhance model performance. This work has important implications for various applications, including computer vision, robotics, and healthcare, where accurate human pose estimation is crucial. Key keywords: human pose estimation, deep learning, fractal neural networks, knowledge-guided models, computer vision.
