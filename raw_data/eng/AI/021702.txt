This paper addresses the challenge of semantic inpainting, a critical task in image processing that involves reconstructing missing or damaged regions in images while preserving contextual coherence. Traditional methods often struggle to maintain semantic consistency, especially in complex scenes with diverse textures and structures. To overcome these limitations, we propose a novel context-aware semantic inpainting framework that leverages advanced deep learning techniques. Our approach integrates a multi-scale attention mechanism with a generative adversarial network (GAN) architecture, enabling the model to focus on both local and global contextual information during the reconstruction process. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves superior performance in terms of visual quality and semantic accuracy compared to state-of-the-art techniques. Quantitative evaluations reveal significant improvements in metrics such as peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM). The proposed framework not only enhances the quality of inpainted images but also offers practical applications in areas such as image restoration, photo editing, and augmented reality. This work contributes to the field by introducing a robust, context-aware solution that bridges the gap between low-level texture synthesis and high-level semantic understanding.  

**Keywords**: Semantic inpainting, context-aware, deep learning, generative adversarial networks, image restoration, attention mechanism.
