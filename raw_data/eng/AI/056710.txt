This paper presents a novel approach to video captioning, leveraging a dynamic loss network to significantly enhance the accuracy and relevance of generated captions. The objective is to address the challenges of capturing complex video content and conveying it through concise, natural-language descriptions. Our method employs a unique loss function that adaptively adjusts during training, allowing the model to focus on the most critical aspects of video features and linguistic patterns. The dynamic loss network is integrated with a state-of-the-art video captioning framework, enabling the model to learn more effective representations of video and language. Experimental results demonstrate substantial improvements in caption quality, with our approach outperforming existing methods on standard benchmarks. The key findings highlight the importance of adaptive loss functions in video captioning, leading to more informative and contextually relevant captions. This research contributes to the advancement of video understanding and natural language processing, with potential applications in areas such as video search, recommendation systems, and accessibility services. Key keywords: video captioning, dynamic loss network, natural language processing, deep learning, computer vision.
