The placement of text on images, often referred to as copyspace, plays a critical role in visual communication, particularly in advertising, design, and multimedia applications. This paper addresses the challenge of identifying optimal copyspace regions within images where text can be overlaid without obstructing key visual elements or degrading aesthetic quality. We propose a novel deep learning-based framework that leverages convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and semantic segmentation techniques to automatically detect and recommend copyspace areas. Our approach integrates image saliency, object detection, and spatial composition analysis to ensure text placement aligns with human visual perception and design principles. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing techniques in terms of accuracy and user satisfaction, achieving a 15% improvement in copyspace identification precision. The proposed framework is scalable and adaptable to various image types, making it a valuable tool for designers and content creators. This research contributes to the field of computer vision by introducing a data-driven solution to a long-standing design challenge, with potential applications in automated graphic design, social media content generation, and personalized marketing.  

**Keywords**: copyspace, text placement, image analysis, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, semantic segmentation, visual communication.
