**Abstract:**

Semantic understanding of three-dimensional (3D) environments is crucial for autonomous driving, robotics, and intelligent transportation systems. In this paper, we present SemanticKITTI, a large-scale dataset specifically designed for semantic scene understanding using sequences of LiDAR data. The proposed dataset builds upon real-world urban and rural driving scenarios, providing dense point-wise semantic annotations across complete LiDAR sequences. Our approach includes the systematic labeling of over 43,000 scans, enabling the evaluation of methods for semantic segmentation and temporal scene consistency in challenging, dynamic environments. To benchmark performance, we implement and compare several state-of-the-art 3D deep learning models, such as PointNet, RangeNet, and multi-view projection methods, demonstrating the complexity and richness of the SemanticKITTI dataset. Experimental results highlight significant gaps in existing algorithms’ ability to capture long-term scene dynamics and rare object classes, indicating ample room for further research. SemanticKITTI addresses the limitations of prior LiDAR datasets by offering extensive temporal context, diverse driving conditions, and open-source accessibility. The dataset establishes a new standard for semantic scene understanding and provides an essential platform for the development and evaluation of robust perception algorithms for LiDAR-based applications. Key keywords: LiDAR, semantic segmentation, 3D scene understanding, autonomous driving, dataset, point cloud, temporal analysis.
