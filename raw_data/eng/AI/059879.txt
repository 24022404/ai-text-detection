This paper addresses the challenge of policy tracking in reinforcement learning using temporal-difference (TD) learning algorithms. In dynamic environments where the policy changes over time, ensuring efficient and accurate tracking is crucial for enhancing the agent's performance. We propose a novel framework leveraging the adiabatic theorem to improve policy tracking capabilities in TD-learning.

Methods/Approach: Our approach integrates the principles of the adiabatic theorem with TD-learning to create a robust policy-tracking algorithm. By employing this theoretical framework, we develop a model that adjusts smoothly with changes in the policy, ensuring more stable and reliable learning. The method involves a systematic adjustment of learning rates based on the evolving policy landscape, thus aligning with the adiabatic conditions.

Results/Findings: Experiments demonstrate that our algorithm significantly outperforms conventional TD-learning methods in environments with dynamic policies. Simulation results show improved convergence rates and more effective policy tracking, leading to enhanced overall agent performance. Comparisons with baseline approaches underline the advantage of incorporating the adiabatic theorem for maintaining policy alignment.

Conclusion/Implications: This research introduces a cutting-edge integration of the adiabatic theorem into TD-learning, providing a substantial contribution to the field of reinforcement learning. The implications extend to designing more adaptive learning systems capable of handling dynamic and uncertain environments efficiently. Our findings pave the way for future explorations in policy adaptation and reinforcement learning stability.

Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Temporal-Difference Learning, Adiabatic Theorem, Policy Tracking, Dynamic Environments, Adaptive Learning Systems.
