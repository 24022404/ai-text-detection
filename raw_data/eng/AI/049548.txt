Federated learning (FL) enables collaborative model training without direct data sharing, addressing privacy concerns. However, FL is still vulnerable to inference attacks, necessitating integration with differential privacy (DP). This paper investigates the challenges of implementing DP in communication-constrained FL environments, where bandwidth limitations restrict the amount of information exchanged. We propose a novel framework, DP-FedAvg-CC, that strategically optimizes the trade-off between privacy guarantees, model accuracy, and communication efficiency. DP-FedAvg-CC employs a client selection mechanism that prioritizes participants with high-quality updates while incorporating a communication-aware noise calibration technique to minimize the impact of DP noise under limited bandwidth. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that DP-FedAvg-CC achieves a significant improvement in model accuracy and convergence speed compared to existing DP-FL methods, while satisfying rigorous DP guarantees under stringent communication constraints. This work provides practical insights into deploying privacy-preserving FL in real-world scenarios with limited resources.

Keywords: Federated Learning, Differential Privacy, Communication Constraints, Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning, Distributed Optimization.
