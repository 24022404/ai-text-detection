In an RdB graph, connectivity is ensured by the existence of proper directed paths between any two vertices u and v, established through particular sequences of edge labels. This structural property assures that every pair of vertices can reach each other following the specified label constraints. Furthermore, Chapter 4 delves into evaluating the rate of the RdB sequence, a critical measure that helps in understanding the graph’s efficiency. The chapter also emphasizes the development of effective encoding and decoding strategies for the longest RdB sequences, optimizing performance in practical applications.
