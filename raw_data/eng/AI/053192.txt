**Abstract**

With the increasing adoption of deep learning models in automated arrhythmia detection, ensuring the robustness and security of electrocardiogram (ECG) classification systems has become critical in clinical settings. This paper presents ECGadv, a novel framework designed to generate adversarial ECG signals that can deceive state-of-the-art arrhythmia classification models. Our approach leverages gradient-based optimization techniques to craft subtle perturbations on raw ECG signals, leading to significant misclassification by popular neural network architectures while ensuring that the altered signals remain visually and clinically plausible. Experimental results demonstrate that ECGadv achieves high success rates in fooling both convolutional and recurrent neural network models across standard arrhythmia datasets, outperforming existing adversarial attack methods in terms of attack effectiveness and imperceptibility. The evaluation includes comprehensive analyses of perturbation magnitude, transferability across model types, and impacts on model confidence. Our findings highlight critical vulnerabilities in current ECG-based diagnostic systems, emphasizing the urgent need for robust defense mechanisms. ECGadv provides valuable insights for researchers and practitioners in medical AI security, adversarial machine learning, and healthcare technology regulation. 

**Keywords:** adversarial attacks, ECG signals, arrhythmia classification, deep learning security, medical AI robustness, neural networks
