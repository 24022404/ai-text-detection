Vision Transformers (ViTs) have demonstrated remarkable performance in computer vision tasks, often attributed to pretraining on large datasets and sophisticated data augmentation techniques. This paper investigates the intrinsic capabilities of ViTs, exploring their potential to achieve competitive results without relying on these common practices. We introduce a simplified training paradigm for ViTs, eliminating both pretraining and strong data augmentations, focusing instead on architectural modifications and training optimizations. Our approach involves a novel layer normalization scheme and a refined attention mechanism. Through extensive experiments on standard image classification benchmarks, including CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet, we demonstrate that our enhanced ViT models can achieve performance comparable to, and in some cases surpassing, ResNet architectures trained with standard data augmentations, without the need for pretraining. These findings suggest that the architectural advantages of ViTs are substantial and can be harnessed more effectively through targeted training strategies. This work contributes to a deeper understanding of ViT architectures and provides a pathway for training high-performing vision models with reduced computational overhead and data dependencies. Keywords: Vision Transformer, ResNet, Image Classification, Deep Learning, Self-Attention, Training Optimization.
