In this study, we address the challenge of missing data estimation in complex networks by introducing the Temporal Multilayer Position-aware Graph Neural Network (TMP-GNN). The objective of this research is to enhance the accuracy of data imputation in networks where temporal dynamics and multilayer interactions play a significant role. Our approach leverages the inherent characteristics of graph neural networks (GNNs) combined with innovative temporal and position-aware features to better understand the intricate dependencies within multilayer networks over time. We design a novel architecture that integrates node position and temporal influence, allowing for improved contextualized predictions of missing data points.

To validate our method, we conducted extensive experiments using synthetic and real-world datasets from domains such as transportation and social networks. The TMP-GNN demonstrated superior performance compared to existing models, achieving notable improvements in imputation accuracy. Our results indicate that the incorporation of temporal and position-aware layers allows the model to capture dynamic patterns effectively, leading to more reliable data completion.

The implications of this research are significant as it presents a versatile tool for data recovery in applications where timely and accurate data is crucial, such as traffic management, network analysis, and various IoT systems. The TMP-GNN framework not only contributes to the field of graph neural networks by introducing a new dimension of analysis but also opens avenues for further exploration in enhancing network-based predictive models. The novelty of our approach lies in the synergistic integration of multilayer interactions with temporal dynamics and position awareness, setting a new standard for missing data estimation in graph-based systems. Key keywords include missing data estimation, temporal graph networks, multilayer networks, position-aware GNN, and neural network imputation.
