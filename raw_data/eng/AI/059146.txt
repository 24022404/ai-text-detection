The objective of this research is to address the challenge of enhancing video resolution through both spatial and temporal dimensions, offering users control over frame enhancement with a novel approach. We propose the Temporal Modulation Network (TMN), an innovative architecture designed for controllable space-time video super-resolution. The TMN employs a dynamic temporal modulation mechanism, which intelligently adapts to varying motion patterns and resolutions within a video sequence. Our network integrates convolutional layers with transformer-based self-attention modules to capture fine-grained temporal dependencies and leverage them for enhanced spatial resolution. Through rigorous experimentation, TMN demonstrates superior performance across multiple benchmark datasets, outstripping existing state-of-the-art models in both qualitative and quantitative metrics. The results highlight TMN's capacity for improved visual clarity, preservation of motion consistency, and user-defined adjustment of space-time resolution. This contribution opens up new possibilities for applications in video editing, broadcasting, and surveillance, where high-quality video output is critical. The novelty lies in the network's ability to provide real-time user control over resolution enhancement, marking a significant step forward in the domain of video processing. Key keywords relevant to this study include video super-resolution, temporal modulation, space-time enhancement, neural networks, and controllable video processing.
