Blockchain technology provides a decentralized and immutable platform for various applications. Analyzing the underlying graph structure of blockchains, however, presents unique challenges due to its scale and evolving nature. This paper introduces ChainNet, a novel graph neural network (GNN) framework specifically designed for learning on blockchain graphs by incorporating topological features. ChainNet integrates node-level attributes with graph-level topological properties, such as cycle bases and connectivity measures, to enhance representation learning. We propose a hierarchical aggregation scheme that captures both local neighborhood information and global structural characteristics. Experimental results on real-world blockchain transaction datasets demonstrate that ChainNet significantly outperforms existing GNN models in tasks such as fraud detection and transaction classification. The incorporation of topological features allows ChainNet to better identify complex patterns and relationships within the blockchain graph. This work offers a new approach to blockchain analytics, enabling more accurate and efficient insights for security and optimization. Keywords: Blockchain, Graph Neural Networks, Topological Features, Fraud Detection, Transaction Classification.
