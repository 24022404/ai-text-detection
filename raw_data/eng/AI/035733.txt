This paper addresses the challenge of efficient option discovery in reinforcement learning, where the goal is to learn a set of temporally extended actions (options) that accelerate learning and improve performance. We introduce a novel budgeted reinforcement learning framework for option discovery, aiming to learn options that maximize cumulative reward while adhering to a computational budget. Our approach involves training an option discovery policy to select promising subgoals and associated policies within the budget constraints. We employ a meta-learning approach to efficiently explore the option space and adapt to different environments. Experimental results on a range of benchmark tasks demonstrate that our budgeted reinforcement learning framework effectively discovers high-quality options that significantly improve learning speed and final performance compared to traditional reinforcement learning methods and existing option discovery algorithms. The discovered options exhibit transferability across related tasks, highlighting the potential for efficient learning in complex and dynamic environments. This work contributes a practical and scalable approach to option discovery, enabling agents to learn more effectively with limited computational resources.

**Keywords:** Reinforcement Learning, Option Discovery, Budgeted Learning, Meta-Learning, Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning.
