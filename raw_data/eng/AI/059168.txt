Spatial attention mechanisms have shown promise in enhancing the performance of deep neural networks across various computer vision tasks by adaptively focusing on informative regions of an input image. This paper presents an empirical study evaluating the effectiveness of different spatial attention modules within diverse deep network architectures. We investigate how these mechanisms, including hard and soft attention variants, influence feature representation and overall model accuracy. Our methodology involves integrating several prominent spatial attention modules into established convolutional neural networks, such as ResNet and VGG, and evaluating their performance on image classification and object detection benchmarks. Results demonstrate that spatial attention consistently improves model performance, with specific modules exhibiting superior performance depending on the task and network architecture. Furthermore, we analyze the learned attention maps to provide insights into the regions of interest identified by each mechanism. The findings highlight the importance of selecting appropriate spatial attention modules and offer practical guidelines for their effective integration in deep learning models for enhanced visual understanding. Keywords: Spatial Attention, Deep Learning, Convolutional Neural Networks, Computer Vision, Image Classification, Object Detection.
