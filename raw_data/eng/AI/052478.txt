This paper investigates the robustness of Vision Transformers (ViT) to adversarial examples, a critical concern in deep learning-based computer vision. Our objective is to analyze the vulnerability of ViT models to carefully crafted input perturbations that can mislead their predictions. We employ a range of adversarial attack methods, including FGSM and PGD, to evaluate the robustness of ViT models against state-of-the-art convolutional neural networks (CNNs). Our results show that ViT models exhibit unique robustness properties, with some architectures demonstrating increased resilience to adversarial examples compared to their CNN counterparts. We also identify key factors influencing ViT robustness, such as patch size and attention mechanisms. Our findings have important implications for the development of secure and reliable computer vision systems, highlighting the potential of ViT models in adversarial settings. This research contributes to the growing field of adversarial robustness, with applications in areas like autonomous driving, surveillance, and healthcare, and sheds light on the importance of considering robustness in Vision Transformer design. Key keywords: Vision Transformers, adversarial examples, robustness, computer vision, deep learning.
