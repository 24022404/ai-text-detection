Abstract

Optimization methods form the backbone of modern machine learning, underpinning model training, parameter tuning, and performance enhancement across diverse applications. This paper presents a comprehensive survey of optimization techniques from a machine learning perspective, critically examining classical approaches such as gradient descent and its variants, along with recent advancements including stochastic optimization, adaptive methods, and meta-optimization strategies. We systematically categorize these methods based on their underlying principles, computational efficiency, convergence properties, and applicability to large-scale and high-dimensional problems. Special focus is given to challenges such as non-convex optimization, overfitting, and scalability. Through comparative analysis, we highlight the strengths and limitations of each approach, supported by empirical results and case studies from real-world machine learning tasks. The survey also explores emerging trends, such as distributed optimization and the integration of optimization algorithms within deep learning frameworks. Our findings provide valuable insights into selecting suitable optimization methods for specific machine learning problems and identify future research opportunities in this rapidly evolving field. Keywords: optimization, machine learning, gradient descent, stochastic methods, adaptive algorithms, deep learning, scalability.
