This paper addresses the challenge of video summarization, a critical task in managing and interpreting the growing volume of video data. Traditional methods often fail to account for user-specific requirements for summarization. We propose a novel approach that leverages meta learning to develop task-driven video summarization, allowing summaries to be tailored to specific user needs and contexts.

Methods/Approach: Our method utilizes meta learning algorithms to train a model capable of rapidly adapting to new tasks with minimal training examples. By implementing a meta-learner that optimizes for summarization objectives, the model can learn to generate concise and informative video summaries from limited input data. The framework involves a combination of convolutional neural networks for feature extraction and a recurrent network to refine the summaries based on task-specific criteria.

Results/Findings: The proposed technique demonstrates superior performance over baseline methods across various benchmarks, including user-centric and context-specific summarization tasks. Experimental results show that our model significantly reduces the summary length while maintaining critical content and exhibiting improved adaptability to differing task requirements. Comparisons with established summarization algorithms reveal noticeable enhancements in summary relevance and user satisfaction.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a meta learning-based framework that significantly enhances the flexibility and personalization of video summarization processes. By focusing on task-specific objectives, this approach offers valuable implications for fields involving large-scale video data management, such as surveillance, content curation, and personalized media consumption. Future work could explore extensions into real-time applications and integration with existing media platforms.

Keywords: meta learning, video summarization, task-driven, user-centric, convolutional neural networks, model adaptability, large-scale video data.
