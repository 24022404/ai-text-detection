This paper introduces a novel Discrete-Continuous Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (DC-ADMM) algorithm for transductive inference in higher-order Markov Random Fields (MRFs). The objective is to efficiently perform inference in MRFs containing both discrete labels and continuous features, a common challenge in various machine learning applications. Our DC-ADMM approach decomposes the original problem into a series of smaller, more manageable subproblems that are iteratively solved until convergence. Specifically, we alternate between updating discrete label assignments and continuous feature values using ADMM, exploiting the structure of higher-order potentials. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our algorithm on benchmark datasets, including image segmentation and social network analysis tasks. Experimental results show that DC-ADMM achieves comparable or superior performance in terms of accuracy and convergence speed compared to state-of-the-art methods. The proposed approach offers a scalable and efficient solution for transductive inference in complex MRF models, particularly where higher-order interactions are significant.

Keywords: ADMM, Markov Random Fields, Transductive Inference, Discrete Optimization, Continuous Optimization, Higher-Order Potentials.
