**Abstract**

The increasing complexity and volume of time-series data across diverse domains necessitate reliable and efficient algorithms for identifying optimal solutions. This paper introduces CRATOS (Cognition of Reliable Algorithm for Time-series Optimal Solution), a novel framework designed to enhance the accuracy and robustness of time-series analysis. The objective of this study is to address the challenges of non-stationarity, noise, and computational efficiency frequently encountered in time-series optimization. CRATOS integrates advanced machine learning techniques with an adaptive optimization strategy, utilizing a hybrid architecture that combines deep recurrent neural networks (RNNs) with dynamic objective function tuning. Experimental evaluations were conducted on multiple real-world and synthetic datasets, benchmarking CRATOS against state-of-the-art time-series forecasting and optimization algorithms. Results demonstrate that CRATOS consistently outperforms existing methods in terms of prediction accuracy, convergence speed, and stability under varying data conditions. The algorithm also exhibits superior generalization capabilities across different time-series applications, including financial forecasting, anomaly detection, and resource allocation. In conclusion, CRATOS provides a reliable and scalable solution for time-series optimal decision-making, with significant implications for intelligent data analytics, automated forecasting systems, and real-time optimization tasks. Key contributions include the development of a cognition-driven optimization core and demonstrable improvements in reliability and efficiency for time-series solutions.  

**Keywords:** CRATOS, time-series, optimization, machine learning, recurrent neural networks, robust algorithms, forecasting, data analytics.
