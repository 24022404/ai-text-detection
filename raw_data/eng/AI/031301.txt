Graph representation learning has gained significant attention for its ability to model complex relationships in structured data. However, detecting critical structures within graphs, such as bridges or central nodes, remains a challenging task, especially in distributed and scalable settings. This paper introduces *Ego-CNN*, a novel framework that leverages egocentric representations of graphs to identify critical structures efficiently. The proposed approach utilizes localized, ego-centric subgraphs to capture structural information, enabling distributed computation and reducing computational overhead. By employing a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture tailored for graph data, *Ego-CNN* extracts meaningful features from these subgraphs, facilitating the detection of critical structures with high accuracy. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that *Ego-CNN* outperforms existing methods in terms of both detection performance and scalability. The framework’s distributed nature makes it particularly suitable for large-scale graphs, offering significant improvements in computational efficiency. This work contributes to the field of graph analytics by providing a scalable, egocentric approach to critical structure detection, with potential applications in network analysis, social media, and infrastructure monitoring.  

**Keywords**: Graph representation learning, egocentric graphs, critical structure detection, distributed computing, convolutional neural networks (CNNs), scalability.
