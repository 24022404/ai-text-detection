This paper addresses the challenge of accurately identifying part boundaries in 3D point clouds, a critical task for applications such as 3D object segmentation, reconstruction, and robotic manipulation. Traditional methods often struggle with noisy or incomplete data, limiting their effectiveness in real-world scenarios. To overcome these limitations, we propose a novel deep learning-based approach that leverages geometric and contextual features to predict part boundaries directly from raw point cloud data. Our method employs a hierarchical neural network architecture designed to capture both local and global structural information, enabling robust boundary detection even in complex 3D shapes. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of precision and recall, achieving state-of-the-art performance. Furthermore, the proposed model is computationally efficient and scalable, making it suitable for real-time applications. The results highlight the potential of our method to advance 3D object understanding and manipulation, with implications for fields such as autonomous robotics, augmented reality, and industrial automation.  

**Keywords**: 3D point clouds, part boundary detection, deep learning, 3D segmentation, neural networks, geometric feature extraction.
