Following the COVID pandemic, Vietnam experienced a remarkable increase in domestic tourism, setting new records for monthly visitor numbers with 12 million and 12.2 million in May and June 2022, respectively. In only the first six months of 2022, the total number of domestic tourists reached 60.8 million, accompanied by revenues of 265,000 billion VND. This surge not only reflected a strong recovery but also marked an impressive growth, with figures reaching nearly 1.4 times higher than those recorded during the same period in 2019, signaling renewed vitality in Vietnam's travel industry.
