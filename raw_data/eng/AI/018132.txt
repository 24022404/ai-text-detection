This paper introduces Spectral Recurrent Neural Networks (Spectral RNNs), a novel approach to sequence prediction that leverages spectral techniques to enhance the learning and prediction capabilities of traditional RNNs. Spectral RNNs decompose the hidden state dynamics into a spectral domain, enabling efficient capture of long-range dependencies and improved generalization. Our method involves transforming the hidden state using a Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT), applying non-linearities in the spectral domain, and then reconstructing the hidden state. This spectral processing allows for targeted manipulation of frequency components, enhancing the model's ability to learn complex sequential patterns. We evaluate Spectral RNNs on several benchmark sequence prediction tasks, including synthetic datasets with long-range dependencies and real-world time series data. Results demonstrate that Spectral RNNs outperform standard RNNs and LSTMs, achieving significant improvements in prediction accuracy and convergence speed. The spectral approach also offers insights into the learned representations, providing a more interpretable model. These findings suggest that Spectral RNNs offer a promising direction for sequence modeling, with potential applications in areas such as natural language processing, financial forecasting, and signal processing.

Keywords: Sequence Prediction, Recurrent Neural Networks, Spectral Analysis, Time Series, Deep Learning.
