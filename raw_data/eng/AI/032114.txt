This paper addresses the challenges of vehicle tracking and vehicle re-identification (Re-ID) in multi-camera networks, where robustness, scalability, and computational efficiency are critical. We propose a novel framework leveraging teamed classifiers, which combines the strengths of multiple machine learning models to enhance accuracy and adaptability in dynamic environments. Our approach integrates advanced feature extraction techniques with a lightweight, modular architecture to ensure real-time performance across distributed systems. The proposed method is evaluated on benchmark datasets, demonstrating superior tracking precision and Re-ID accuracy compared to existing state-of-the-art solutions. Experimental results highlight significant improvements in handling occlusions, viewpoint variations, and lighting changes, while maintaining low computational overhead. The framework’s extensibility allows seamless integration with additional sensors or classifiers, making it suitable for large-scale urban surveillance and intelligent transportation systems. This research contributes a scalable, efficient, and robust solution for multi-camera vehicle tracking and Re-ID, with potential applications in traffic management, security, and autonomous driving.  

**Keywords**: vehicle tracking, vehicle re-identification, multi-camera networks, teamed classifiers, machine learning, real-time systems, intelligent transportation.
