This research addresses the challenge of developing an energy-efficient locomotion strategy for snake-like robots, which can adaptively navigate varied terrain while minimizing energy consumption. The study explores the potential of deep reinforcement learning to optimize snake locomotion gaits, paving the way for advances in robotic efficiency and adaptability.

Methods/Approach: Utilizing a deep reinforcement learning framework, the study employs a combination of neural network architectures and policy gradient methods to derive optimal gait patterns for snake robots. The approach is designed to iteratively refine motion strategies based on feedback from energy expenditure metrics and movement efficiency across simulated environments.

Results/Findings: The proposed methodology successfully identified energy-saving gaits, demonstrating a significant reduction in energy consumption compared to traditional locomotion strategies. Experimental results reveal that the reinforcement learning-derived gait policy results in enhanced adaptability to varied terrains, including complex and dynamic surfaces. The findings confirm the viability of deep reinforcement learning as a powerful tool for optimizing robotic locomotion beyond preconceived motion patterns.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a novel solution for energy-efficient robotic locomotion by incorporating deep reinforcement learning into gait strategy development. The innovations presented are applicable to various robotic systems requiring energy-efficient movement solutions. The broader implications of the study suggest potential applications in search and rescue missions, planetary exploration, and environmental monitoring, where energy-efficient navigation is crucial. Keywords: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Snake Locomotion, Gait Policy, Energy Efficiency, Robotic Navigation.
