This paper introduces a novel approach for learning compact and robust representations of 3D point clouds using Adversarial Autoencoders (AAEs). The objective is to generate lower-dimensional latent spaces that effectively capture the geometric and structural information present in complex 3D data. Our method employs an AAE framework where the encoder learns to map point clouds to a latent distribution enforced to be close to a prior distribution via an adversarial network, while the decoder reconstructs the original point cloud from the latent code. This regularization encourages the latent space to be continuous and well-structured. We evaluate our approach on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its ability to generate compact representations that are suitable for downstream tasks such as classification and reconstruction. Experimental results show that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of reconstruction accuracy and representation compactness compared to existing autoencoder-based methods. Furthermore, we demonstrate the robustness of the learned representations to noise and variations in point cloud density. Our approach offers a valuable tool for efficient storage, transmission, and analysis of 3D point cloud data.

Keywords: Adversarial Autoencoder, 3D Point Cloud, Representation Learning, Deep Learning, Unsupervised Learning, Dimensionality Reduction.
