Molecular optimization aims to discover molecules with improved properties for drug discovery and material science. This paper introduces a novel multimodal graph-to-graph translation framework to address the challenge of generating diverse and high-quality molecules. Our method leverages a variational autoencoder to learn a latent space representation of molecular graphs, conditioned on desired properties. We then employ a graph neural network-based decoder to translate these latent vectors into valid molecular graphs. A key innovation is the multimodal learning strategy, incorporating multiple property predictors to encourage exploration of diverse regions in the latent space, leading to a richer set of generated molecules. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of both the quality and diversity of generated molecules. Specifically, we observe significant improvements in desired property scores while maintaining high levels of molecular validity and novelty. This work contributes a powerful new approach to molecular optimization, offering the potential to accelerate the design of novel compounds with tailored properties. Keywords: Molecular Optimization, Graph Neural Networks, Multimodal Learning, Graph-to-Graph Translation, Drug Discovery.
