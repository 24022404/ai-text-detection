This paper proposes a novel approach to noise-robust image classification by integrating wavelet transforms into Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs). The objective is to enhance the robustness of image classification models against noisy data, which is a common challenge in real-world applications. Our method utilizes wavelet decomposition to extract multi-scale features from images, which are then fed into a CNN for classification. The wavelet integrated CNN (W-CNN) model is designed to leverage the denoising capabilities of wavelets and the feature learning capabilities of CNNs. Experimental results demonstrate that the W-CNN model outperforms traditional CNNs in classifying noisy images, with significant improvements in accuracy and robustness. The proposed approach has important implications for applications where image quality is compromised, such as in medical imaging, surveillance, and autonomous vehicles. Key contributions of this research include the development of a wavelet-based denoising module and its integration with CNNs, as well as the demonstration of its effectiveness in noise-robust image classification. Relevant keywords: wavelet transform, CNN, noise-robust image classification, deep learning, computer vision.
