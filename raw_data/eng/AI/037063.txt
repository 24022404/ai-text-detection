This paper introduces CrossTransformers, a novel spatially-aware few-shot transfer learning approach designed to enhance the adaptability of transformer models in computer vision tasks. The objective is to develop a method that can effectively leverage spatial relationships and limited labeled data to achieve state-of-the-art performance in various image classification and object detection tasks. Our approach utilizes a unique combination of self-attention mechanisms and spatially-aware feature extraction to enable efficient transfer learning. Experimental results demonstrate that CrossTransformers outperform existing few-shot learning methods, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and reducing the need for large amounts of labeled training data. The key findings highlight the importance of spatial awareness in transformer models and demonstrate the potential of CrossTransformers for real-world applications, such as medical image analysis and autonomous driving. Overall, this research contributes to the development of more efficient and adaptable deep learning models, with implications for the broader field of artificial intelligence and machine learning, particularly in areas like transfer learning, few-shot learning, and spatially-aware modeling.
