This paper proposes a novel approach to video summarization by transforming multi-concept attention into a concise and informative summary. The objective is to address the challenge of effectively capturing key concepts in videos and representing them in a condensed form. Our method employs a deep learning-based model that integrates multi-concept attention mechanisms to identify and weigh important frames in a video. The approach utilizes a transformer-based architecture to learn contextual relationships between frames and concepts, enabling the model to generate summaries that balance relevance and diversity. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art video summarization methods in terms of summary quality and user engagement. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a multi-concept attention framework for video summarization and the demonstration of its potential to improve summary accuracy and user experience. This work has significant implications for applications such as video analysis, content creation, and media consumption, and highlights the importance of attention mechanisms in video understanding. Key keywords: video summarization, multi-concept attention, transformer, deep learning, attention mechanisms.
