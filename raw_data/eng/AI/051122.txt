This paper introduces Detective, an attentive recurrent model specifically designed for sparse object detection tasks in computer vision. Traditional object detection frameworks often struggle with scenes where relevant objects are few and distributed irregularly, leading to diminished detection accuracy and inefficient resource utilization. Detective addresses this challenge by leveraging a recurrent neural network architecture that iteratively attends to potential object regions, guided by a novel attention mechanism tailored for sparsity. The model processes input images in sequential steps, refining its focus on promising areas while suppressing irrelevant background, thereby enhancing both precision and recall in sparse settings. Experimental evaluations on widely-used sparse object detection benchmarks demonstrate that Detective outperforms existing state-of-the-art detectors in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. Notably, the proposed model shows superior performance in scenarios with limited labeled data, highlighting its robustness and generalizability. The findings suggest that attentive recurrent strategies offer a promising direction for advanced object detection in real-world applications where target instances are infrequent and variably distributed. Keywords: sparse object detection, recurrent neural networks, attention mechanism, computer vision, deep learning.
