This paper addresses the challenge of learning fair and transferable representations in machine learning, focusing on scenarios where models need to generalize across different datasets and demographic groups. We propose a novel adversarial training framework that simultaneously optimizes for predictive accuracy, fairness, and transferability. Our method employs a domain discriminator to encourage the learned representations to be domain-invariant, an attribute discriminator to mitigate bias with respect to sensitive attributes, and a reconstruction loss to preserve information relevant for prediction. We evaluate our approach on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in fairness metrics while maintaining competitive accuracy and achieving superior transfer performance compared to existing methods. Key findings indicate that adversarial training with domain adaptation and fairness constraints effectively learns representations that are both less biased and more generalizable. The proposed framework offers a practical solution for deploying fair and robust machine learning models across diverse populations and environments. Keywords: Fair Machine Learning, Transfer Learning, Adversarial Training, Domain Adaptation, Representation Learning.
