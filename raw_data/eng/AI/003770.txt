Accurate short-term load forecasting (STLF) is essential for efficient energy management and grid reliability in modern power systems. Traditional forecasting methods often struggle to adapt to rapidly changing demand patterns and non-stationary environments. This paper presents a novel approach employing reinforcement learning (RL) for dynamic model selection in STLF tasks. The proposed framework utilizes a reinforcement learning agent to evaluate and select from a portfolio of forecasting models—including statistical techniques, machine learning algorithms, and deep learning networks—based on real-time performance and evolving system conditions. Comprehensive experiments were conducted on diverse power load datasets to assess the effectiveness of the RL-based selection strategy. Results demonstrate significant improvements in forecasting accuracy and adaptability compared to static single-model approaches and conventional ensemble methods. The system's ability to shift model preferences responsively enables robust forecasting under varying load profiles and external influences. This study highlights the potential of intelligent model governance via reinforcement learning for enhancing short-term load forecasting, contributing to smarter energy grid management. Keywords: reinforcement learning, dynamic model selection, short-term load forecasting, energy management, machine learning, adaptive systems.
