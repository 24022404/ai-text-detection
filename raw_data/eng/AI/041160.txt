This paper addresses the challenge of vehicle tracking and re-identification in multi-camera networks by proposing a novel approach that leverages teamed classifiers. The objective is to develop a robust, extensible, and fast system that can accurately track vehicles across different cameras and identify them in various environments. Our approach combines the strengths of multiple classifiers, utilizing a fusion strategy to improve overall performance. The method is based on a deep learning framework, incorporating convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) to extract features and model temporal relationships. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving state-of-the-art performance in vehicle tracking and re-identification tasks. The proposed system outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy, efficiency, and scalability, making it suitable for real-world applications. Key contributions include the development of a teamed classifier framework, the introduction of a novel feature fusion strategy, and the demonstration of the system's robustness to variations in camera settings and environmental conditions. This research has significant implications for intelligent transportation systems, surveillance, and smart cities, with potential applications in traffic management, security, and autonomous vehicles. Relevant keywords: vehicle tracking, vehicle re-identification, multi-camera networks, teamed classifiers, deep learning, CNNs, RNNs.
