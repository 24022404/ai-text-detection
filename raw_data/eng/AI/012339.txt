This paper explores the application of reinforcement learning in the R programming language, with the objective of developing efficient and adaptive algorithms for solving complex decision-making problems. We employ a model-free reinforcement learning approach, utilizing techniques such as Q-learning and Deep Q-Networks (DQN) to optimize agent performance in various environments. Our results demonstrate the effectiveness of reinforcement learning in R, achieving significant improvements in agent convergence and stability compared to traditional methods. The key findings of this research highlight the potential of reinforcement learning in R for real-world applications, including autonomous systems, game playing, and robotics. This study contributes to the existing body of research by providing a comprehensive framework for implementing reinforcement learning in R, leveraging its simplicity and flexibility to facilitate wider adoption and innovation in the field. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, R programming language, Q-learning, Deep Q-Networks, model-free learning, adaptive algorithms.
