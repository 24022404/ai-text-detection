Text generation models have made significant strides in producing coherent and contextually relevant outputs. However, achieving a balance between diversity and quality remains a critical challenge. This paper addresses the problem of jointly measuring diversity and quality in text generation models, aiming to provide a unified framework for evaluating their performance. We propose a novel evaluation metric that integrates both aspects, leveraging advanced natural language processing techniques to assess the richness of generated text while maintaining high linguistic and semantic quality. Our approach combines entropy-based diversity measures with quality metrics such as fluency, relevance, and coherence. Experiments conducted on multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of our method in capturing the trade-offs between diversity and quality, outperforming existing evaluation frameworks. The results highlight the importance of a balanced evaluation for improving text generation models, offering insights for future research and applications in areas such as conversational AI, content creation, and machine translation. This work contributes a robust and interpretable tool for researchers and practitioners to better understand and optimize text generation systems.  

**Keywords**: text generation, diversity, quality, evaluation metrics, natural language processing, AI models.
