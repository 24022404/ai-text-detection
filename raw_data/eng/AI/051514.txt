This paper presents a novel approach to optimizing molecular structures using deep reinforcement learning. The objective is to develop an efficient method for discovering molecules with desired properties, such as improved pharmacokinetics or enhanced chemical reactivity. Our approach employs a deep reinforcement learning framework, where an agent learns to modify molecular structures through a sequence of actions, with the goal of maximizing a reward function that reflects the desired properties. The method utilizes a combination of graph neural networks and reinforcement learning algorithms to efficiently explore the vast chemical space. Our results demonstrate the effectiveness of this approach in optimizing molecules for various properties, outperforming traditional methods in terms of efficiency and accuracy. The key findings include the discovery of novel molecules with improved properties and the ability to generalize to new chemical spaces. This research contributes to the field of molecular optimization, enabling the rapid discovery of new molecules with potential applications in drug development, materials science, and chemistry. Key keywords: deep reinforcement learning, molecular optimization, graph neural networks, chemical space exploration.
