Medical image segmentation is a critical task in healthcare, enabling precise diagnosis and treatment planning. However, achieving accurate segmentation often requires significant manual intervention, especially in complex cases. This paper introduces DeepIGeoS, a novel deep interactive geodesic framework designed to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of medical image segmentation. The proposed approach integrates deep learning with interactive geodesic distance maps, allowing users to provide minimal input (e.g., clicks or scribbles) to guide the segmentation process. DeepIGeoS leverages a convolutional neural network (CNN) to learn robust feature representations and incorporates geodesic distance transforms to refine boundary delineation. Experiments on multiple medical imaging datasets, including MRI and CT scans, demonstrate that DeepIGeoS achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and user interaction efficiency. The framework is particularly effective in handling challenging cases with ambiguous boundaries or low contrast. The results highlight the potential of DeepIGeoS to streamline clinical workflows, reduce manual effort, and improve segmentation outcomes. This work contributes to the growing field of interactive medical image analysis by combining the strengths of deep learning and user-guided techniques, offering a practical solution for real-world applications.  

**Keywords**: Medical image segmentation, deep learning, interactive segmentation, geodesic distance, convolutional neural networks, MRI, CT.
