Novel view synthesis from single images is a challenging task in computer vision, with applications ranging from virtual reality to autonomous systems. This paper addresses the problem of generating realistic and accurate novel views from a single input image by leveraging point cloud transformation techniques. The proposed approach begins by estimating a dense 3D point cloud representation from the input image using a deep learning-based depth estimation model. These point clouds are then transformed into the target viewpoint using a novel geometric transformation algorithm that preserves spatial consistency and minimizes artifacts. To enhance realism, a neural rendering framework is employed to synthesize high-quality textures and details for the generated views. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms existing single-image novel view synthesis techniques in terms of both visual quality and geometric accuracy. The framework achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets, with significant improvements in handling occlusions and complex scenes. This research contributes a robust and efficient solution for single-image novel view synthesis, offering potential applications in augmented reality, robotics, and 3D content creation.  

**Keywords**: novel view synthesis, point cloud transformation, single-image 3D reconstruction, neural rendering, depth estimation, computer vision.
