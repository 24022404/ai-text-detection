Object detection in complex scenes often requires understanding contextual relationships between objects and their surroundings. This paper introduces a novel Layout Transfer Network (LTN) designed to enhance context-aware object detection by leveraging spatial layout information. The proposed approach aims to address the challenge of accurately detecting objects in cluttered environments where traditional methods struggle due to overlapping instances or ambiguous visual cues. The LTN framework integrates a layout prediction module with a deep learning-based detection pipeline, enabling the model to infer and transfer spatial context from one scene to another. By learning to predict object layouts and their relationships, the network improves detection accuracy and robustness. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the LTN outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in precision and recall metrics. The results highlight the effectiveness of incorporating layout transfer mechanisms for context-aware detection tasks. This research contributes to advancing object detection techniques by introducing a scalable and interpretable approach that can be applied to various real-world applications, such as autonomous driving, surveillance, and robotics. Keywords: object detection, context awareness, layout transfer, deep learning, spatial relationships.
