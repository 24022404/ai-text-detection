This paper explores a novel symbolic approach to time series forecasting using Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks. Addressing the limitations of traditional numerical forecasting methods, our approach converts time series data into symbolic representations before feeding it into an LSTM network. This symbolic transformation aims to capture underlying patterns and reduce noise, improving forecasting accuracy. We present a new symbolic encoding method based on adaptive segmentation and demonstrate its effectiveness in capturing temporal dependencies. The performance of our symbolic LSTM model is evaluated on several real-world datasets, including stock prices and weather data, and compared against conventional LSTM models and ARIMA models. Results show that the symbolic LSTM significantly outperforms traditional methods, particularly in volatile and noisy time series. The symbolic approach enhances the LSTM's ability to generalize and provides more interpretable representations of the time series. This work contributes a new methodology for time series forecasting that leverages the strengths of both symbolic dynamics and deep learning, offering a promising avenue for improved prediction accuracy and pattern discovery.

Keywords: Time Series Forecasting, LSTM, Symbolic Dynamics, Deep Learning, Pattern Recognition, Forecasting Accuracy.
