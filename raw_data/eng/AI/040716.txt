This paper presents a novel approach to estimating continuous blood pressure from photoplethysmography (PPG) signals using a federated learning framework. The objective is to develop a non-invasive and accurate blood pressure monitoring system, addressing the limitations of traditional methods. Our approach employs a federated learning model that leverages decentralized data from multiple sources, ensuring privacy and reducing the need for data sharing. The methodology utilizes a deep learning architecture to extract relevant features from PPG signals, which are then used to estimate blood pressure. Results show that our federated learning approach outperforms traditional machine learning methods, achieving a high degree of accuracy in estimating continuous blood pressure. The key findings indicate that the proposed system can provide real-time, continuous blood pressure monitoring, enabling timely interventions and improved patient outcomes. This research contributes to the development of personalized healthcare systems, highlighting the potential of federated learning in biomedical signal processing applications, particularly in the context of wearable devices and remote health monitoring. Key keywords: federated learning, PPG, blood pressure estimation, wearable devices, continuous monitoring.
