This paper proposes a novel approach to adversarial autoencoders by incorporating constant-curvature latent manifolds, aiming to improve the representation learning capabilities of deep neural networks. Our objective is to develop a more robust and efficient autoencoder model that can learn complex data distributions while preserving the geometric structure of the input data. We employ a variational autoencoder framework with a constant-curvature latent space, which is achieved through a carefully designed manifold-based regularization term. Our approach leverages the strengths of both adversarial training and geometric deep learning to learn disentangled and informative representations. Experimental results demonstrate that our model outperforms existing autoencoder architectures in terms of reconstruction quality, generative capabilities, and robustness to adversarial attacks. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of geometric constraints in representation learning and pave the way for future applications in computer vision, robotics, and natural language processing. Our contributions include the introduction of constant-curvature latent manifolds to adversarial autoencoders, which enables more efficient and effective learning of complex data distributions. Relevant keywords: adversarial autoencoders, constant-curvature latent manifolds, geometric deep learning, representation learning, variational autoencoders.
