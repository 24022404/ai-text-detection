This paper introduces Knodle, a novel modular framework for weakly supervised learning, built on top of PyTorch. The objective is to provide a flexible and efficient approach to training machine learning models with limited or noisy supervision. Knodle achieves this by decoupling the model architecture from the weak supervision methodology, allowing for seamless integration of various weak supervision techniques. The framework utilizes a modular design, enabling users to easily swap between different weak supervision methods, such as data programming, weakly supervised object detection, and self-supervised learning. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of Knodle in improving model performance and reducing annotation costs. Key findings include significant gains in accuracy and efficiency when using Knodle with state-of-the-art weak supervision methods. The contributions of this research lie in its ability to simplify and accelerate the development of weakly supervised learning models, making it an attractive solution for real-world applications where labeled data is scarce. Keywords: weakly supervised learning, PyTorch, modular framework, data programming, self-supervised learning.
