**Abstract**

Understanding temporal dynamics in complex networks is essential for numerous information technology applications, including social network analysis, recommendation systems, and communication networks. This paper presents a novel approach for temporal network embedding based on tensor factorization. Our method aims to address the challenge of representing evolving relationships in dynamic networks by modeling the temporal interactions as multi-dimensional tensors. We employ advanced tensor decomposition techniques to factorize the network data, extracting compact and expressive node embeddings that capture both structural and temporal patterns. Experimental evaluation is conducted on several real-world temporal network datasets, where our approach demonstrates superior performance in downstream tasks such as link prediction and node classification compared to existing static and dynamic embedding methods. The results highlight the effectiveness of leveraging tensor factorization for capturing temporal evolution and uncovering latent dependencies within dynamic networks. Our study provides a scalable and flexible framework for temporal representation learning, contributing to advancements in graph analytics, temporal data mining, and network science.

**Keywords:** temporal network, network embedding, tensor factorization, dynamic networks, representation learning, link prediction, node classification
