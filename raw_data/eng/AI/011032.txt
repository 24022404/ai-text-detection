The objective of this paper is to address the challenge of effective deep learning on point cloud data, which is crucial for applications such as 3D object recognition and autonomous driving. We introduce KPConv, a novel convolutional operation designed specifically for point clouds, which is both flexible and deformable. KPConv adapts traditional convolutional kernels to the irregular and unordered nature of point clouds by employing kernel point placements that can adjust to the local geometry of the data. The approach utilizes learnable deformation parameters, allowing the kernel points to dynamically shift and better capture geometric details. Our experimental results demonstrate that KPConv significantly outperforms existing methods on standard 3D datasets, achieving state-of-the-art performance in both segmentation and classification tasks. Moreover, KPConv exhibits exceptional adaptability to varying point densities and is robust against input noise. The innovative design of KPConv provides important insights into enhancing convolutional networks for non-Euclidean data spaces. These contributions have the potential to advance numerous 3D-related technologies, facilitating improved accuracy and efficiency in real-world applications. Keywords: KPConv, point clouds, 3D object recognition, convolutional networks, learnable deformation.
