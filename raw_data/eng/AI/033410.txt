The prediction of genetic inheritance patterns is a critical challenge in bioinformatics, with significant implications for understanding hereditary diseases and personalized medicine. This study aims to develop an explainable machine learning model capable of accurately predicting genetic inheritance patterns while providing interpretable insights into the underlying biological mechanisms. We propose a novel hybrid approach that integrates decision trees with deep learning techniques, leveraging both structured genetic data and high-dimensional genomic features. The model is trained on a comprehensive dataset comprising family pedigrees, genomic sequences, and phenotypic traits. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach achieves a prediction accuracy of 92.5%, outperforming traditional methods such as logistic regression and random forests. Furthermore, the explainability component enables the identification of key genetic markers and their contributions to inheritance patterns, offering valuable insights for genetic counseling and disease prevention. This research contributes to the growing field of explainable AI in bioinformatics by bridging the gap between predictive accuracy and interpretability, paving the way for more transparent and actionable genetic analyses.  

**Keywords**: genetic inheritance, explainable AI, machine learning, bioinformatics, deep learning, decision trees, genomic data.
