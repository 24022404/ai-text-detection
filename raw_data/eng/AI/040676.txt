Semantic segmentation of LiDAR point clouds is a critical task for applications such as autonomous driving, robotics, and 3D scene understanding. However, existing methods often struggle with the challenges of sparse and irregular point distributions, leading to suboptimal performance. This paper introduces FIDNet, a novel framework for LiDAR point cloud semantic segmentation that leverages a Fully Interpolation Decoding (FID) mechanism to address these limitations. The proposed approach integrates a hierarchical encoder to extract multi-scale features and a unique interpolation-based decoder to reconstruct dense and accurate semantic predictions. By employing adaptive interpolation techniques, FIDNet effectively bridges the gap between sparse input points and dense output labels, enhancing segmentation precision. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that FIDNet achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of both accuracy and computational efficiency. The framework's ability to handle sparse data and produce high-quality segmentation maps highlights its potential for real-world applications. This research contributes a significant advancement in LiDAR point cloud processing, offering a robust and scalable solution for semantic segmentation tasks.  

**Keywords**: LiDAR, point cloud, semantic segmentation, interpolation decoding, 3D scene understanding, autonomous driving.
