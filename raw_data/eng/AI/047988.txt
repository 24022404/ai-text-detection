This paper introduces the Auto-Parsing Network (APN), a novel framework designed to enhance image captioning and visual question answering (VQA) tasks by leveraging advanced parsing techniques. The primary objective of this research is to address the limitations of existing models in accurately interpreting complex visual scenes and generating contextually relevant textual outputs. The proposed APN integrates a multi-modal parsing mechanism that dynamically decomposes visual and textual inputs into structured representations, enabling more precise alignment between visual content and language. The approach employs a hybrid architecture combining convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for image feature extraction and transformer-based models for sequential data processing. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including MS-COCO and VQA v2.0, demonstrate that APN achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in both caption quality and question-answering accuracy. Key contributions include the introduction of a parsing-driven attention mechanism and a unified framework that seamlessly bridges image understanding and language generation. The findings highlight the potential of APN in applications such as assistive technologies, automated content generation, and human-computer interaction. Keywords: image captioning, visual question answering, parsing network, multi-modal learning, transformer models, attention mechanisms.
