Abstract:

This paper presents a novel deep learning framework for semantics-aware photo adjustment, aiming to enhance image quality by intelligently adapting adjustments to the semantic content within photographs. Traditional photo editing techniques often apply global or rudimentary local enhancements without considering the underlying scene semantics, leading to suboptimal or unnatural results. To address this limitation, we propose a semantics-guided adjustment network that leverages convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to extract contextual features and segment images into meaningful regions, such as sky, vegetation, or human subjects. Each region undergoes adaptive adjustment based on its semantic category, allowing for targeted enhancement and preserving visual consistency throughout the image. Our approach is trained on a large-scale dataset annotated with both semantic maps and artist-curated adjustments, enabling the model to learn context-sensitive photo enhancement strategies. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art automatic and learning-based photo adjustment techniques in terms of both objective quality metrics and subjective user studies. The proposed deep semantics-aware adjustment not only provides visually appealing results but also offers a flexible tool for intelligent photo editing in applications such as photography, social media, and content creation. Keywords: deep learning, photo adjustment, semantic segmentation, image enhancement, convolutional neural networks, context-aware processing.
