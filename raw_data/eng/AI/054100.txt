Image classification remains a critical task in computer vision, with data augmentation playing a pivotal role in improving model generalization. This paper introduces *Attentive CutMix*, a novel data augmentation technique designed to enhance the performance of deep learning models in image classification tasks. Unlike traditional augmentation methods, Attentive CutMix leverages attention mechanisms to intelligently blend regions of interest from multiple images, ensuring that the augmented data retains semantically meaningful features. The proposed approach integrates a lightweight attention module into the CutMix framework, enabling the model to focus on salient regions during the augmentation process. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets, including CIFAR-10 and ImageNet, demonstrate that Attentive CutMix significantly improves classification accuracy compared to standard augmentation techniques. Furthermore, the method exhibits robustness across various network architectures, including ResNet and EfficientNet. The results highlight the potential of attention-driven augmentation strategies to advance the state-of-the-art in image classification, offering a practical and scalable solution for real-world applications.  

**Keywords**: Image classification, data augmentation, attention mechanisms, CutMix, deep learning, computer vision.
