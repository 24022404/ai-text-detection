This paper proposes an innovative approach to compressing Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) through online multi-granularity distillation. The objective is to reduce the computational complexity and memory requirements of GANs while preserving their generative capabilities. Our method employs a novel distillation framework that transfers knowledge from a pre-trained teacher GAN to a smaller student GAN at multiple granularities, including feature maps, intermediate representations, and output distributions. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach achieves significant compression ratios while maintaining comparable image quality and diversity. The proposed technique outperforms existing GAN compression methods, with improvements in both visual fidelity and mode coverage. This research contributes to the development of efficient and scalable GANs, enabling their deployment in resource-constrained environments and applications such as image synthesis, data augmentation, and style transfer. Key keywords: GAN compression, knowledge distillation, multi-granularity distillation, generative models, deep learning.
