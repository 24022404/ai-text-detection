This paper proposes a novel Knowledge-based Fully Convolutional Network (KFCN) designed to improve the accuracy of lung CT image segmentation. The objective is to develop an efficient and robust deep learning model that leverages prior knowledge to enhance the segmentation of lung structures from computed tomography images. Our approach integrates a knowledge-based module into a fully convolutional network, enabling the model to learn from both image features and prior domain knowledge. The KFCN model is trained and evaluated on a large dataset of lung CT images, demonstrating superior performance compared to state-of-the-art segmentation methods. The results show significant improvements in segmentation accuracy, particularly in cases with complex lung structures. The proposed KFCN model has important implications for computer-aided diagnosis and treatment of lung diseases, offering a reliable and efficient tool for clinicians. Key contributions of this research include the introduction of a knowledge-based module, improved segmentation accuracy, and the potential for application in other medical image analysis tasks. Relevant keywords: deep learning, fully convolutional network, knowledge-based systems, lung CT image segmentation, medical image analysis.
