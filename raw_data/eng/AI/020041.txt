Seasonal time series forecasting is a critical challenge in various domains, including finance, energy, and supply chain management, where accurate predictions are essential for decision-making. This paper proposes a novel two-stage framework designed to improve the accuracy and robustness of seasonal time series forecasting. In the first stage, a decomposition-based approach is employed to separate the time series into its seasonal, trend, and residual components. This step enhances the model's ability to capture underlying patterns and reduce noise. In the second stage, a hybrid machine learning model is applied, combining the strengths of traditional statistical methods and advanced neural networks to predict each component independently. The final forecast is obtained by integrating the predictions of the individual components. Experimental results on multiple real-world datasets demonstrate that the proposed framework outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of prediction accuracy and computational efficiency. The framework's adaptability to different seasonal patterns and its ability to handle non-linear relationships make it a versatile tool for practical applications. This research contributes to the field by introducing a robust, scalable, and interpretable solution for seasonal time series forecasting, with potential implications for improving operational efficiency and strategic planning in various industries.  

**Keywords**: seasonal time series, forecasting, decomposition, hybrid models, machine learning, neural networks.
