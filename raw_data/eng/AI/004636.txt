This paper introduces D3DLO, a novel deep learning approach for 3D LiDAR odometry. Addressing the challenge of accurate and robust pose estimation in complex environments, D3DLO leverages a deep neural network architecture to directly learn geometric features from raw LiDAR point clouds. Our method employs a point cloud encoder based on PointNet++ to extract multi-scale features, followed by a recurrent neural network to estimate the relative pose transformation between consecutive scans. A key innovation is the integration of a differentiable point cloud registration module within the network, enabling end-to-end training and optimization of feature extraction and pose estimation. Evaluated on the KITTI dataset, D3DLO demonstrates superior performance compared to state-of-the-art LiDAR odometry methods, particularly in challenging scenarios with sparse point clouds and significant motion blur. The results highlight the potential of deep learning to enhance the accuracy and robustness of 3D LiDAR odometry for autonomous navigation and mapping applications. Keywords: LiDAR odometry, deep learning, 3D point cloud, pose estimation, autonomous navigation.
