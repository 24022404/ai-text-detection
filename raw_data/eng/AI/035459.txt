This paper introduces *ProLab*, a novel perceptually uniform projective colour coordinate system designed to address challenges in colour representation and manipulation across digital imaging and visualization applications. The primary objective of *ProLab* is to provide a more intuitive and perceptually accurate framework for colour mapping, ensuring uniformity in human visual perception. The proposed system leverages projective geometry and psychophysical models to transform traditional colour spaces into a coordinate system that aligns more closely with human visual perception. The methodology involves the development of a mathematical model that maps RGB and CIELAB colour spaces into a projective domain, optimizing for perceptual uniformity and computational efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate that *ProLab* outperforms existing colour systems in terms of perceptual accuracy, particularly in applications such as image processing, data visualization, and 3D rendering. Comparative analyses with CIELAB and other colour spaces highlight *ProLab*'s superior performance in maintaining colour consistency across different viewing conditions and devices. The implications of this research are significant for industries relying on precise colour representation, including digital media, medical imaging, and virtual reality. *ProLab* represents a meaningful advancement in colour science, offering a robust and perceptually uniform solution for modern colour management challenges.  

**Keywords**: colour coordinate system, perceptual uniformity, projective geometry, colour mapping, CIELAB, digital imaging.
