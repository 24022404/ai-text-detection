This paper introduces VSSA-NET, a novel Vertical Spatial Sequence Attention Network designed to enhance the performance of traffic sign detection systems. The objective of this research is to address the existing challenges in accurately detecting and classifying small and occluded traffic signs within cluttered environments. The proposed VSSA-NET utilizes a unique attention mechanism that focuses on vertical spatial sequences, enabling the model to effectively capture the distinct vertical characteristics of traffic signs. This approach not only improves detection accuracy but also optimizes computational efficiency compared to traditional convolutional neural network architectures. Our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art models on benchmark datasets, demonstrating a significant increase in detection precision and recall. Moreover, VSSA-NET exhibits robust performance in various real-world scenarios, making it highly suitable for deployment in advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) and autonomous vehicles. The findings highlight the potential of integrating spatial sequence attention mechanisms in object detection tasks, offering a promising direction for future research. Key contributions include the development of a specialized attention network tailored for vertical spatial sequences and its application in enhancing traffic sign detection. Keywords: traffic sign detection, attention network, vertical spatial sequence, VSSA-NET, autonomous vehicles, ADAS.
