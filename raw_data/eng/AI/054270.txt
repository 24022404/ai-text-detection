This paper addresses the problem of identifying critical points in persistence homology, a crucial task in topological data analysis. The objective is to develop a systematic approach to determine these points, which are essential for understanding the underlying structure of complex data. We propose a novel method based on a combination of computational geometry and machine learning techniques. Our approach utilizes a graph-based algorithm to efficiently identify critical points, which are then refined using a neural network model. The results show that our method outperforms existing techniques in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach on various datasets, including those from biology, materials science, and image analysis. The key findings of this research include the development of a robust and scalable method for determining critical points, which has significant implications for the analysis of complex systems and networks. Our work contributes to the advancement of persistence homology and topological data analysis, with potential applications in fields such as computer vision, bioinformatics, and materials science. Key keywords: persistence homology, critical points, topological data analysis, computational geometry, machine learning.
