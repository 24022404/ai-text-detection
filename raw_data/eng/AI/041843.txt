This paper introduces PyKEEN 1.0, a Python library designed to facilitate the training and evaluation of knowledge graph embeddings. The primary objective of PyKEEN is to provide a unified framework for knowledge graph embedding models, enabling researchers and developers to efficiently compare and contrast different techniques. PyKEEN achieves this through a modular architecture, allowing users to easily implement and evaluate various knowledge graph embedding models, including TransE, ConvE, and RotatE. The library also includes tools for hyperparameter tuning, model selection, and evaluation metrics, making it an essential resource for the development of knowledge graph-based applications. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of PyKEEN in training and evaluating knowledge graph embeddings, with significant improvements in computational efficiency and model performance. The release of PyKEEN 1.0 contributes to the advancement of knowledge graph research, providing a widely applicable and extensible platform for the development of novel embedding models and applications. Key features of PyKEEN include support for multiple embedding models, automated hyperparameter tuning, and integration with popular deep learning frameworks. By leveraging PyKEEN, researchers can focus on developing innovative knowledge graph-based solutions, driving progress in areas such as question answering, entity disambiguation, and recommender systems.
