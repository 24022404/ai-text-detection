**Abstract**

Photorealistic style transfer aims to apply the artistic style of a reference image to a target photograph while preserving its structural and textural integrity, a challenge that often results in unnatural artifacts and loss of fine detail. This paper presents a novel approach for photorealistic style transfer based on wavelet transforms, which effectively separate image features across multiple frequency bands. Our method utilizes wavelet decomposition to deconstruct images into multi-scale representations, enabling precise manipulation of style elements while maintaining the underlying semantics and edges of the original image. The proposed system integrates wavelet-based feature extraction with an advanced neural network to achieve spatially consistent and artifact-free style transfer. Experimental results across diverse benchmark datasets demonstrate significant improvements in visual realism, edge preservation, and color consistency compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. Our approach achieves real-time performance and is flexible for various application scenarios, including image editing, film post-production, and augmented reality. The research underscores the value of incorporating multi-scale signal processing techniques, such as wavelets, into deep learning frameworks for enhanced image synthesis. Keywords: photorealistic style transfer, wavelet transforms, neural networks, image synthesis, multi-scale analysis, edge preservation.
