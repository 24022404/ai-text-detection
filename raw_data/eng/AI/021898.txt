This paper introduces FastMoE, a novel training system designed to accelerate the training process of Mixture-of-Expert (MoE) models. The objective is to address the computational inefficiencies and scalability limitations associated with traditional MoE training methods. FastMoE employs a hybrid approach, combining a sparse gating mechanism with a parallelized expert selection strategy, to reduce computational overhead and improve training speed. Experimental results demonstrate that FastMoE achieves significant speedups over existing MoE training systems, while maintaining comparable model accuracy. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of FastMoE in reducing training time by up to 3x, making it an attractive solution for large-scale MoE deployments. The contributions of this research lie in its ability to enable faster and more efficient MoE training, paving the way for wider adoption of these models in applications such as natural language processing and computer vision. Key keywords: Mixture-of-Expert, MoE, sparse gating, parallelized expert selection, scalable training systems, AI models, deep learning.
