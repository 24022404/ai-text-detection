This paper addresses the complex problem of airline crew pairing optimization, a critical task in the aviation industry that involves creating efficient and cost-effective pairings of crew members for flights. The objective is to develop a novel approach that leverages machine learning techniques to learn combinatorial patterns in crew pairing data, ultimately assisting in large-scale optimization. Our method utilizes a combination of data preprocessing, feature engineering, and advanced algorithms to identify optimal crew pairings. The results show significant improvements in solution quality and computational efficiency compared to traditional methods. Key findings include the ability to handle large-scale problem instances and the identification of meaningful patterns in crew pairing data. The conclusions highlight the potential of this approach to contribute to the development of more efficient and effective crew pairing optimization systems, with implications for the aviation industry as a whole. This research contributes to the fields of operations research, machine learning, and optimization, with relevant keywords including crew pairing optimization, machine learning, combinatorial optimization, and airline operations.
