Time series data analysis is a critical component of numerous applications in finance, healthcare, and environmental monitoring. However, preprocessing such data to ensure robustness against noise, outliers, and missing values remains a significant challenge. This paper introduces *tsrobprep*, an R package designed to address these challenges by providing a comprehensive suite of robust preprocessing techniques for time series data. The package integrates advanced methods for outlier detection, imputation, and smoothing, leveraging statistical and machine learning approaches to enhance data quality. Key features include adaptive thresholding for outlier identification, iterative imputation algorithms for handling missing data, and customizable smoothing functions tailored to diverse time series characteristics. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that *tsrobprep* outperforms existing tools in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency, particularly in scenarios with high noise levels or irregular sampling intervals. The package is open-source, user-friendly, and extensible, making it a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners. By improving the reliability of time series preprocessing, *tsrobprep* contributes to more accurate downstream analyses and decision-making processes.  

**Keywords**: time series, robust preprocessing, outlier detection, imputation, smoothing, R package.
