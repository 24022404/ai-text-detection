This paper introduces an efficient and robust algorithm for image matching based on Least Squares Normalized Cross Correlation (LSNCC). Addressing the limitations of traditional Normalized Cross Correlation (NCC) in handling varying illumination and contrast, LSNCC incorporates a least squares fitting step to normalize image patches before correlation. The method minimizes the intensity differences between the template and search image patch, making it more resilient to photometric distortions. Experiments on diverse image datasets demonstrate that LSNCC achieves superior matching accuracy compared to NCC and other correlation-based techniques, particularly in challenging scenarios with significant illumination changes and noise. Furthermore, we present an optimized implementation of LSNCC that significantly reduces computational cost, making it suitable for real-time applications. The proposed LSNCC algorithm offers a valuable tool for various computer vision tasks including object tracking, image registration, and 3D reconstruction. Keywords: Image Matching, Normalized Cross Correlation, Least Squares, Illumination Invariance, Computer Vision.
