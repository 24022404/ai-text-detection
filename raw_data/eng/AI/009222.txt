Autonomous driving systems require robust and accurate 3D object detection to ensure safe navigation in complex environments. This paper presents a novel Multi-View 3D Object Detection Network (MV3D-Net) designed to enhance the precision and efficiency of detecting objects in three-dimensional space. The proposed approach integrates data from multiple sensor modalities, including LiDAR and camera inputs, to generate a comprehensive representation of the surrounding environment. By leveraging a multi-view fusion strategy, the network effectively combines bird's-eye view and perspective view features, enabling improved object localization and classification. The architecture employs a deep learning framework with attention mechanisms to prioritize relevant spatial and contextual information, reducing computational overhead while maintaining high detection accuracy. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that MV3D-Net outperforms existing methods in terms of both detection performance and inference speed. The system achieves state-of-the-art results in 3D bounding box estimation, particularly in challenging scenarios with occlusions and varying lighting conditions. This research contributes to the advancement of autonomous driving technologies by providing a scalable and efficient solution for real-time 3D object detection, with potential applications in robotics, surveillance, and augmented reality.  

**Keywords**: 3D object detection, autonomous driving, multi-view fusion, LiDAR, deep learning, sensor fusion.
