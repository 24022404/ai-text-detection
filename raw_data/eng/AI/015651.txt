This paper introduces a novel approach to enhance the Bahdanau attention mechanism by integrating election methods and Q-learning. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of attention weight assignment in sequence-to-sequence models. Our method leverages election-based aggregation to combine multiple attention weight vectors, each derived from slightly different model configurations, thereby creating a more robust and nuanced attention distribution. Q-learning is then employed to dynamically adjust the contribution of each individual attention vector within the election process, optimizing the overall attention performance based on a defined reward function. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed model achieves significant improvements in translation quality and convergence speed compared to the standard Bahdanau attention and other attention variants. The findings highlight the potential of combining decision-making strategies from social choice theory with reinforcement learning to refine attention mechanisms in neural networks. Keywords: Bahdanau Attention, Q-Learning, Election Methods, Sequence-to-Sequence, Neural Machine Translation.
