Short-term load forecasting (STLF) is critical for efficient energy management and grid stability, yet existing models often struggle to adapt to dynamic and non-linear load patterns. This paper proposes a novel reinforcement learning (RL)-based framework for dynamic model selection in STLF, aiming to improve prediction accuracy and adaptability. The proposed approach leverages an RL agent to evaluate and select the most suitable forecasting model from a predefined set in real-time, based on evolving load characteristics and environmental factors. The framework integrates advanced machine learning models, including Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks and Gradient Boosting Machines (GBM), as candidate predictors. The RL agent is trained using a reward mechanism that prioritizes minimizing forecasting errors while ensuring computational efficiency. Experimental results on real-world energy consumption datasets demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms traditional static model selection approaches, achieving a significant reduction in mean absolute error (MAE) and root mean square error (RMSE). The study highlights the potential of RL-driven dynamic model selection in enhancing STLF accuracy and robustness, offering valuable insights for energy management systems and smart grid applications.  

**Keywords**: Short-term load forecasting, reinforcement learning, dynamic model selection, LSTM, energy management, machine learning.
