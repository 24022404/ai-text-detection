Abstract:

Ensemble deep learning has emerged as a powerful strategy to enhance the performance, robustness, and generalizability of machine learning systems by combining the predictive capabilities of multiple deep learning models. This review paper aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the key principles, methodologies, and applications of ensemble deep learning within the Information Technology domain. We systematically discuss various ensemble techniques, including bagging, boosting, stacking, and voting, and analyze their integration with popular deep neural network architectures such as convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs). The review also highlights recent advancements in ensemble methods that leverage model diversity, hybridization, and automated ensembling procedures. Empirical findings from the literature demonstrate that ensemble models often outperform single deep learning models in a range of tasks, including image classification, natural language processing, and anomaly detection. Furthermore, we identify the challenges related to computational complexity, model interpretability, and scalability in deploying ensemble deep learning systems. The paper concludes by outlining future research directions and practical recommendations for utilizing ensemble deep learning to solve complex problems in real-world IT applications. Keywords: ensemble learning, deep neural networks, bagging, boosting, stacking, model diversity, performance improvement.
