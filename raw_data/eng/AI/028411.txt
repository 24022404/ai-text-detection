This paper proposes MSDU-net, a novel deep learning architecture designed for efficient blur detection in images. The objective is to develop a robust model that can accurately identify and localize blurred regions in various types of images. To achieve this, we employ a Multi-Scale Dilated U-net (MSDU-net) approach, which combines the strengths of U-net architectures with multi-scale feature extraction and dilated convolutions. Our method leverages the capabilities of convolutional neural networks to learn rich feature representations at multiple scales, allowing for effective blur detection. Experimental results demonstrate that MSDU-net outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The proposed model achieves a significant improvement in blur detection, particularly in cases where images exhibit complex blur patterns. The contributions of this research include the introduction of a novel architecture that can be applied to various image processing tasks, such as image restoration and enhancement. Key keywords: blur detection, deep learning, U-net, multi-scale feature extraction, dilated convolutions, image processing.
