Iris recognition systems often struggle with cross-spectral and cross-resolution variations, limiting their performance in real-world scenarios. This paper introduces a novel Deep Generative Adversarial Network (GAN)-based framework to address these challenges. Our approach, Cross Spectral-Resolution GAN (CSR-GAN), learns to synthesize high-resolution iris images from low-resolution cross-spectral data, effectively bridging the domain gap. The generator network leverages a deep convolutional architecture with spectral normalization and attention mechanisms to preserve crucial iris texture details. A discriminator network enforces realism and cross-domain consistency during training. Experimental results on challenging benchmark datasets demonstrate that CSR-GAN significantly improves the accuracy of iris recognition across different spectral bands and resolutions, outperforming state-of-the-art methods. Our proposed framework enhances the robustness and applicability of iris recognition systems in diverse imaging conditions, paving the way for more secure and reliable biometric authentication. Keywords: Iris Recognition, Generative Adversarial Networks, Cross-Spectral, Cross-Resolution, Biometrics, Deep Learning.
