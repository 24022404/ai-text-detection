This paper presents a novel data-driven reinforcement learning framework for the control of virtual character animation, addressing the challenge of generating realistic and responsive movements in interactive environments. The proposed method integrates motion capture datasets with deep reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms to enable virtual characters to learn complex motor skills and adapt to dynamic tasks. By leveraging a policy optimization approach within the RL paradigm, our system maps sensory inputs to high-quality animation controls in real time. Extensive experiments were conducted using a variety of character models and action scenarios, demonstrating that our approach significantly outperforms traditional hand-crafted and physics-based controllers in terms of motion smoothness, adaptability, and computational efficiency. Results indicate improved generalization to unseen tasks, robust recovery from perturbations, and the ability to synthesize lifelike behaviors across diverse virtual worlds. This research highlights the effectiveness of combining data-driven techniques with advanced RL for character animation, offering promising applications in video games, virtual reality, and simulation environments. Key contributions include a scalable framework, enhanced motion fidelity, and practical benchmarks for future developments in data-driven animation control. Keywords: reinforcement learning, data-driven animation, virtual character, motion capture, control, deep learning.
