The disentanglement of nonlinear structures in natural data remains a significant challenge in machine learning and data representation. This paper addresses the problem of identifying and separating latent factors in complex, high-dimensional datasets, particularly those with temporal dependencies. We propose a novel approach based on Temporal Sparse Coding (TSC), which leverages sparsity constraints and temporal dynamics to achieve nonlinear disentanglement. Our method combines unsupervised learning techniques with a hierarchical framework to model both local and global dependencies in the data. Experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that the proposed approach outperforms existing methods in terms of disentanglement quality and interpretability. Key findings reveal that TSC effectively captures underlying temporal structures while maintaining robustness to noise. The results highlight the potential of this method for applications in video analysis, time-series forecasting, and multimodal data integration. This work contributes to advancing the understanding of nonlinear disentanglement and provides a scalable solution for extracting meaningful representations from complex natural data.  

**Keywords**: Nonlinear disentanglement, temporal sparse coding, unsupervised learning, latent factors, time-series analysis, high-dimensional data.
