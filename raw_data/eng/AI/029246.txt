This paper presents TempoRL, a novel reinforcement learning framework that addresses the challenge of determining optimal action timing, an aspect that is often overlooked in traditional models where actions are taken at fixed intervals. Our objective is to improve decision-making efficiency by dynamically learning when to act based on environmental context rather than adhering to a rigid schedule.

Methods/Approach: TempoRL introduces a new temporal abstraction layer integrated with deep reinforcement learning techniques, enabling agents to gauge the necessity and timeliness of actions. The model employs a neural network architecture that conditions actions not only on state observations but also on time intervals, learning through experience which timing strategies yield better outcomes.

Results/Findings: Empirical tests conducted across a variety of simulated environments demonstrate that TempoRL significantly enhances efficiency and performance when compared to traditional fixed-interval reinforcement learning algorithms. The findings reveal that our method reduces unnecessary actions, conserves computational resources, and adapts more fluidly to changes within the environment, leading to enhanced overall task performance.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of TempoRL marks a step forward in reinforcement learning by emphasizing the importance of action timing. This work opens new avenues for research in time-sensitive decision-making, offering potential applications in areas such as robotics, autonomous driving, and adaptive control systems where efficient action timing can result in more responsive and resource-efficient operations. This contribution highlights the synergy of combining dynamic temporal strategies with state-of-the-art learning techniques for improved real-world applicability.

Keywords: TempoRL, reinforcement learning, action timing, temporal abstraction, deep learning, dynamic decision making.
