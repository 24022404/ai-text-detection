This paper introduces a novel method for classifying datasets to enhance the data mining process. The objective is to improve the efficiency and accuracy of algorithm selection by providing a structured approach to dataset characterization. Our method combines statistical measures, information-theoretic features, and data complexity metrics to create a comprehensive dataset profile. These profiles are then used to train a meta-learning classifier that predicts the most suitable data mining algorithms for a given dataset. Experiments on a diverse collection of datasets demonstrate that our classification method achieves superior performance in algorithm recommendation compared to existing approaches. The results show a significant improvement in the accuracy of algorithm selection, leading to enhanced data mining outcomes. This work contributes a valuable tool for practitioners, enabling them to make informed decisions regarding algorithm choice and ultimately improving the effectiveness of data mining endeavors.
Keywords: data mining, dataset classification, meta-learning, algorithm selection, machine learning.
