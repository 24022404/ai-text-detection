This paper introduces SemanticKITTI, a novel dataset designed to facilitate semantic scene understanding of LiDAR sequences. The objective is to enable accurate and efficient interpretation of 3D point cloud data, which is crucial for various applications such as autonomous driving, robotics, and surveying. Our approach involves annotating a large-scale dataset of LiDAR sequences with semantic labels, allowing for the development and evaluation of scene understanding algorithms. The dataset consists of over 20,000 scans, each annotated with 28 semantic classes, providing a comprehensive representation of urban scenes. Our results show that state-of-the-art methods achieve significant performance gains when trained on SemanticKITTI, demonstrating its value for the development of robust scene understanding systems. The introduction of SemanticKITTI contributes to the advancement of 3D point cloud analysis, enabling improved scene understanding and object recognition capabilities. Key aspects of this work include LiDAR sequence analysis, semantic segmentation, and 3D scene understanding, making it a valuable resource for researchers and developers in the fields of computer vision, robotics, and autonomous systems.
