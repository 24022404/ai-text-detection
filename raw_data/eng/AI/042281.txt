This paper addresses the critical issue of uncertainties in Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs), a crucial aspect of machine learning that significantly impacts model reliability and trustworthiness. The objective is to investigate the fundamental sources and implications of uncertainties in ANNs, with a focus on enhancing model transparency and performance. A comprehensive approach is employed, combining theoretical analysis, simulation experiments, and empirical evaluations to identify and quantify uncertainty factors. The results show that uncertainties in ANNs can arise from various sources, including data noise, model complexity, and parameter initialization, leading to significant variations in prediction accuracy and confidence. Key findings highlight the importance of uncertainty quantification and management in ANNs, particularly in high-stakes applications such as healthcare and finance. The study contributes to the development of more robust and reliable ANN models by proposing novel uncertainty-aware training methods and evaluation metrics. Ultimately, this research aims to advance the field of artificial intelligence by promoting a deeper understanding of uncertainties in ANNs and their mitigation, with significant implications for improved model interpretability, generalizability, and decision-making capabilities. Keywords: Artificial Neural Networks, Uncertainties, Machine Learning, Model Reliability, Uncertainty Quantification.
