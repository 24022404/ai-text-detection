This paper presents a novel approach to learning deep control policies for autonomous aerial vehicles using Model Predictive Control (MPC)-guided policy search. The objective is to develop a control system that enables agile and efficient flight in complex environments. Our method combines the strengths of MPC and deep reinforcement learning to learn policies that can adapt to changing conditions and avoid obstacles. We employ a policy search algorithm guided by MPC to efficiently explore the control space and learn optimal policies. The results show that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of flight efficiency, stability, and obstacle avoidance. Our findings demonstrate the potential of MPC-guided policy search for autonomous aerial vehicles, enabling safe and efficient navigation in real-world scenarios. The contributions of this research include the development of a novel policy search algorithm and the demonstration of its effectiveness in learning deep control policies for autonomous aerial vehicles, with potential applications in areas such as package delivery, surveillance, and search and rescue. Key keywords: autonomous aerial vehicles, deep reinforcement learning, Model Predictive Control, policy search, obstacle avoidance.
