This paper addresses the problem of phase retrieval, a fundamental challenge in various fields such as optics, imaging, and signal processing. The objective is to develop an efficient algorithm for recovering the phase information of a signal from its intensity measurements. To achieve this, we propose an Expectation Consistent Signal Recovery (ECSR) algorithm based on a hypernetwork architecture. Our approach leverages the strengths of deep learning and model-based methods to improve the accuracy and robustness of phase retrieval. The ECSR algorithm iteratively refines the signal estimate by minimizing the difference between the measured intensities and the predicted ones, using a hypernetwork to model the complex relationships between the signal and its intensity measurements. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of recovery accuracy and computational efficiency. The proposed algorithm has significant implications for various applications, including coherent imaging, optical communication, and signal processing. Key contributions of this work include the development of a novel hypernetwork-based ECSR algorithm, which enables efficient and accurate phase retrieval, and the demonstration of its potential in real-world applications. Relevant keywords: phase retrieval, hypernetwork, expectation consistent signal recovery, deep learning, signal processing.
