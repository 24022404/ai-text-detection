In the realm of nonlinear function approximation, the construction of fuzzy rules that effectively handle uncertainty and adapt to complex, non-separable inputs is a significant challenge. This paper introduces the IT2CFNN, an Interval Type-2 Correlation-Aware Fuzzy Neural Network, which aims to address these shortcomings by formulating non-separable fuzzy rules with uncertain and adaptive shapes.

Methods/Approach: The proposed IT2CFNN framework leverages interval type-2 fuzzy sets to manage the inherent uncertainty in input data and employs a correlation-aware mechanism to enhance the adaptability of fuzzy rules. This approach enables the model to construct fuzzy rules that are non-separable, dynamically adjusting their shapes based on underlying data characteristics. The utilization of a neural network architecture facilitates the learning and optimization of these fuzzy rules, guided by an innovative adaptive learning algorithm.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate that the IT2CFNN significantly outperforms traditional fuzzy neural network models in terms of accuracy and robustness in nonlinear function approximation. The correlation-aware component enhances the model's flexibility, allowing it to adapt to varying degrees of data uncertainty. Comparative analyses with existing methods indicate substantial improvements in approximation precision and computational efficiency.

Conclusion/Implications: The IT2CFNN represents a notable advancement in fuzzy neural networks by introducing correlation knowledge and adaptive fuzzy rule construction to manage complex nonlinearities. This research contributes a novel tool for applications requiring precise function approximation, such as control systems, pattern recognition, and prediction models. The versatility of the IT2CFNN underscores its potential for broader application across diverse scientific and engineering domains.

Keywords: IT2CFNN, interval type-2 fuzzy sets, correlation-aware, fuzzy neural network, nonlinear function approximation, adaptive learning, non-separable fuzzy rules.
