This paper addresses the challenge of improving accuracy and reliability in multi-class classification tasks through crowdsourcing, specifically focusing on yes-no type responses. Traditional approaches often struggle with diverse crowd inputs and uncertainty in contributor expertise. Our research proposes a comprehensive probabilistic model to enhance decision-making processes within this context.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a full probabilistic framework designed to process and integrate yes-no type responses from crowdsourcing platforms. Our model accounts for varying levels of trustworthiness among contributors and probabilistically labels data into multiple classes. This is achieved by employing advanced statistical techniques, including Bayesian inference and Expectation-Maximization algorithms, which iteratively refine class probabilities based on crowdsourced inputs. 

Results/Findings: The proposed model demonstrates significant improvements in classification accuracy over existing crowdsourcing methods. Our experimentations on benchmark datasets reveal an increase in performance metrics such as precision, recall, and F1-score. Furthermore, we compare our model against traditional and state-of-the-art crowdsourcing algorithms, showcasing its robustness in handling ambiguous and noisy data inputs.

Conclusion/Implications: Our full probabilistic model significantly contributes to the field of crowdsourced multi-class classification by enhancing the reliability of yes-no type responses. The model's capability to dynamically assess contributor reliability and integrate diverse inputs makes it particularly suited for applications in large-scale data annotation and AI training datasets. The insights from this model pave the way for more accurate and efficient crowdsourced labeling systems, potentially impacting a wide range of fields from AI model development to market research. 

Keywords: Probabilistic model, crowdsourcing, multi-class classification, Bayesian inference, Expectation-Maximization, data annotation, AI training datasets.
