This paper presents a novel approach to optical flow estimation, focusing on the recovery of motion features from image sequences. The objective is to accurately estimate the motion of objects in a scene, which is crucial for various computer vision applications. Our method employs a deep learning-based framework that leverages a multi-scale architecture to extract and refine motion features. The approach utilizes a combination of convolutional neural networks and optical flow algorithms to achieve robust and efficient motion estimation. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art optical flow estimation techniques, particularly in scenarios with complex motion patterns and occlusions. The key contributions of this research include the development of a motion feature recovery module and a novel loss function that enhances the accuracy of optical flow estimation. Our approach has significant implications for applications such as object tracking, motion segmentation, and video analysis, and can be extended to other computer vision tasks that rely on accurate motion estimation. Key keywords: optical flow estimation, motion feature recovery, deep learning, computer vision, object tracking.
