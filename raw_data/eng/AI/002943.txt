This paper introduces FACIAL, a novel approach for synthesizing dynamic talking faces with implicit attribute learning. The objective is to generate realistic and expressive talking faces that capture the nuances of human communication. To achieve this, we propose a deep learning-based method that leverages a combination of generative models and attribute learning techniques. Our approach utilizes a conditional generative adversarial network (CGAN) to synthesize talking faces, while simultaneously learning implicit attributes such as lip movements, facial expressions, and head poses. The results demonstrate significant improvements in terms of visual quality, realism, and expressiveness, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods. The key findings include the ability to generate highly realistic talking faces with synchronized lip movements and facial expressions, as well as the capability to control and manipulate the generated faces using the learned attributes. The contributions of this research lie in its ability to implicitly learn and control facial attributes, enabling a wide range of applications in fields such as computer vision, human-computer interaction, and virtual reality. Key keywords: facial synthesis, talking faces, implicit attribute learning, generative models, deep learning, computer vision.
