This paper presents an automatic face recognition system that leverages Local Fourier-Bessel Features to enhance the accuracy and robustness of facial identification. The objective is to develop a reliable and efficient method for recognizing faces in various environments and conditions. Our approach utilizes a novel combination of Fourier-Bessel transforms and local feature extraction techniques to capture unique and discriminative characteristics from facial images. The system is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, yielding impressive results with significant improvements in recognition rates compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. Key findings indicate that the proposed Local Fourier-Bessel Features outperform traditional descriptors in terms of accuracy, particularly in cases with partial occlusion, pose variation, and illumination changes. The contributions of this research lie in the introduction of a new feature extraction paradigm, which has potential applications in security, surveillance, and biometric authentication. The system's novelty and effectiveness make it an attractive solution for real-world face recognition tasks, with relevant keywords including face recognition, Local Fourier-Bessel Features, biometric authentication, and computer vision.
