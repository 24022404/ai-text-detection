Face attribute editing has made significant strides in recent years, but generating high-resolution and realistic images with precise attribute control remains a challenge. This paper introduces MagGAN, a Mask-guided Generative Adversarial Network, designed for high-resolution face attribute editing. Our approach utilizes a novel mask-guided generator that incorporates attribute masks to provide explicit spatial control over attribute manipulation, enabling targeted and localized edits. We employ an adaptive discriminator that focuses on both global realism and local attribute accuracy. The MagGAN architecture allows for fine-grained control over multiple attributes simultaneously while preserving facial identity and background details. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that MagGAN outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of image quality, attribute manipulation accuracy, and identity preservation, especially at high resolutions. This research contributes a novel framework for high-fidelity face editing, offering improved control and realism for a wide range of applications, including image manipulation, virtual try-on, and personalized content generation.

Keywords: Face Attribute Editing, Generative Adversarial Network, GAN, Image Manipulation, Mask-Guidance, High-Resolution Imaging, Deep Learning.
