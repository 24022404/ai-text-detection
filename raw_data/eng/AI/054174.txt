This paper presents a comprehensive benchmarking study on word image recognition, aiming to evaluate and compare the performance of various recognition models on diverse word image datasets. The objective is to identify the most effective approaches and pinpoint areas for improvement in this crucial aspect of document image analysis. A range of state-of-the-art models, including deep learning-based architectures, are assessed using a multi-faceted evaluation methodology. The results show significant variations in model performance across different datasets, with top-performing models achieving notable accuracy improvements. Key findings highlight the importance of dataset diversity, image quality, and model generalizability. The study concludes by emphasizing the need for standardized benchmarking protocols and proposing future research directions to advance the field of word image recognition. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and robust recognition systems, with potential applications in document digitization, text retrieval, and optical character recognition (OCR), and is relevant to keywords such as word image recognition, document image analysis, deep learning, and OCR benchmarking.
