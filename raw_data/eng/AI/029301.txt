This paper addresses the challenging problem of overlapping chromosome segmentation, a crucial task in cytogenetic analysis. Our objective is to develop a robust and accurate method for segmenting overlapping chromosomes in microscopic images. To achieve this, we propose an adversarial multiscale feature learning approach that leverages the strengths of both multiscale feature extraction and adversarial training. Our method utilizes a novel architecture that combines a multiscale feature extractor with an adversarial discriminator, enabling the model to learn rich and discriminative features at multiple scales. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving a significant improvement in segmentation accuracy and robustness. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a multiscale feature learning framework and the application of adversarial training to chromosome segmentation, resulting in a more accurate and reliable method for cytogenetic analysis. This work has important implications for genetic research, cancer diagnosis, and personalized medicine, and can be applied to various biomedical image analysis tasks. Key keywords: chromosome segmentation, multiscale feature learning, adversarial training, cytogenetic analysis, biomedical image analysis.
