Instance segmentation, a fundamental task in computer vision, demands precise pixel-level classification and object delineation. This paper introduces DCT-Mask, a novel mask representation method leveraging the Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) to achieve compact and efficient instance segmentation. Unlike traditional pixel-based or contour-based mask representations, DCT-Mask represents each instance mask as a low-frequency coefficient map obtained through DCT. This compressed representation reduces memory footprint and computational overhead. We propose a lightweight network architecture that directly predicts these DCT coefficient maps. Experiments on standard datasets such as COCO demonstrate that DCT-Mask achieves competitive instance segmentation accuracy with significantly reduced mask storage requirements compared to state-of-the-art methods. Furthermore, the DCT representation facilitates efficient mask reconstruction and manipulation. Our approach provides a promising alternative for resource-constrained environments and real-time applications. Keywords: Instance Segmentation, Discrete Cosine Transform, Mask Representation, Image Compression, Computer Vision, Deep Learning.
