Semantic segmentation is a crucial task in computer vision, aiming to assign a label to each pixel of an image. This paper presents a Highly Fused Convolutional Network (HFCN) that leverages multiple soft cost functions to enhance semantic segmentation performance. Our approach integrates features at various scales while employing a novel loss function strategy that minimizes ambiguity during training. We conducted comprehensive experiments on standard benchmark datasets, demonstrating that the HFCN outperforms traditional segmentation methods and existing convolutional networks. Notably, our model achieves a significant increase in accuracy and reduced computational complexity, making it suitable for real-time applications. The findings underscore the effectiveness of the proposed architecture in capturing complex patterns and fine details in images. This research contributes to the ongoing advancements in semantic segmentation techniques and holds potential implications for various fields, including autonomous driving, medical imaging, and augmented reality. The key contributions of this work are the introduction of the HFCN framework and the elucidation of how multiple soft cost functions can effectively guide the learning process. 

Keywords: semantic segmentation, convolutional network, soft cost functions, computer vision, real-time applications.
