This paper introduces Query2box, a novel approach to reasoning over knowledge graphs in vector space using box embeddings. The objective is to enable efficient and effective querying of complex knowledge graphs by representing entities and relations as boxes in a high-dimensional vector space. Our method utilizes a bespoke box embedding model that captures the hierarchical and relational structure of the knowledge graph, allowing for robust and accurate reasoning. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of Query2box, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of query accuracy and computational efficiency. The key findings highlight the benefits of box embeddings in capturing complex relationships and hierarchies in knowledge graphs. This research contributes to the advancement of knowledge graph reasoning and has significant implications for applications in question answering, entity disambiguation, and recommender systems. By leveraging box embeddings, Query2box paves the way for more accurate and efficient knowledge graph querying, with potential applications in AI, natural language processing, and data science, leveraging key technologies such as knowledge graph embedding, vector space reasoning, and box embeddings.
