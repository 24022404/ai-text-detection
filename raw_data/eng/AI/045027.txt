This paper proposes a novel deep learning approach for lesion segmentation, leveraging an attention-guided fusion network to improve accuracy and robustness. The objective is to develop a reliable and efficient method for segmenting lesions from medical images, addressing the challenges of variability in lesion appearance and context. Our approach employs a multi-scale feature extraction mechanism, combined with a attention-guided fusion module, to selectively focus on relevant features and suppress irrelevant information. The network is trained on a large dataset of annotated medical images, and its performance is evaluated using standard metrics. The results demonstrate significant improvements in segmentation accuracy and efficiency compared to state-of-the-art methods. The proposed network has important implications for computer-aided diagnosis and treatment of diseases, enabling accurate and reliable lesion segmentation. Key contributions include the introduction of attention-guided fusion for lesion segmentation, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in improving segmentation performance. Relevant keywords: deep learning, attention mechanisms, lesion segmentation, medical image analysis, computer-aided diagnosis.
