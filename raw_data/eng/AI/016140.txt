Image Scene Graph Generation (SGG) aims to represent an image as a structured graph, enabling richer scene understanding. This paper introduces a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating SGG models, addressing limitations in existing datasets and evaluation protocols. Our benchmark incorporates a large-scale dataset with detailed annotations covering diverse scenes and relationships, alongside a standardized evaluation framework. We propose a novel evaluation metric, Graph Precision, that better captures the accuracy of generated scene graphs compared to traditional recall-based metrics. Furthermore, we analyze the performance of several state-of-the-art SGG models on our benchmark, identifying key challenges and areas for future research, particularly in handling long-tail relationship distributions and improving graph structure prediction. This benchmark provides a valuable resource for the SGG community, facilitating fair comparisons and driving advancements in scene understanding.
Keywords: Scene Graph Generation, Image Understanding, Benchmark, Evaluation Metrics, Relationship Prediction, Graph Precision.
