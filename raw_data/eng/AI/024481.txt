Transformer models have demonstrated exceptional capabilities in time series forecasting, but their computational demands and memory footprint pose significant challenges, particularly with long sequences. This paper addresses these limitations by introducing a novel approach that enhances locality and mitigates the memory bottleneck inherent in the standard attention mechanism. Our method, Localized Transformer Network (LTN), restricts the attention scope to relevant local regions while maintaining the capacity to capture long-range dependencies through a hierarchical aggregation strategy. LTN significantly reduces computational complexity from quadratic to linear with respect to sequence length. Experiments on diverse real-world time series datasets demonstrate that LTN achieves comparable or superior forecasting accuracy compared to state-of-the-art Transformer variants, while exhibiting substantially lower memory consumption and faster training times. The improved efficiency of LTN enables effective deployment on resource-constrained platforms and facilitates the analysis of ultra-long time series data. Key innovations include the localized attention mechanism, hierarchical aggregation, and a demonstrated ability to break the memory bottleneck. Keywords: Time Series Forecasting, Transformer, Attention Mechanism, Locality, Memory Bottleneck, Deep Learning.
