This paper proposes a novel approach to dictionary learning, focusing on the development of compressed dictionary learning methods. The objective is to improve the efficiency and scalability of traditional dictionary learning techniques, which often suffer from high computational costs and memory requirements. Our approach utilizes a combination of sparse coding and compression algorithms to reduce the dimensionality of the dictionary, resulting in significant improvements in processing speed and storage requirements. The compressed dictionary learning method is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its ability to achieve comparable or even superior performance to state-of-the-art methods while requiring substantially less computational resources. The key findings of this research highlight the potential of compressed dictionary learning for large-scale data analysis and processing applications, such as image and signal processing, and machine learning. The proposed method contributes to the advancement of dictionary learning techniques, enabling faster and more efficient processing of big data. Key keywords: compressed sensing, dictionary learning, sparse coding, dimensionality reduction, efficient processing.
