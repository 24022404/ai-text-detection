This paper addresses the challenge of initializing Variational Bayes (VB) for deep models, a crucial step in ensuring the convergence and accuracy of Bayesian neural networks. Our objective is to develop effective initialization strategies that improve the performance of VB in deep learning architectures. We propose a novel approach that combines techniques from both Bayesian inference and deep learning to initialize the variational parameters. Our method utilizes a hybrid scheme, leveraging the strengths of both random and informed initialization methods. The results show that our approach outperforms existing initialization methods, leading to faster convergence, improved accuracy, and enhanced robustness of the VB algorithm. Our findings have significant implications for the development of reliable and efficient Bayesian deep learning models, enabling their application in a wide range of tasks, including image classification, natural language processing, and recommender systems. Key contributions of this work include the introduction of a new initialization framework, the demonstration of its effectiveness in various deep learning scenarios, and the provision of insights into the importance of careful initialization for VB in deep models, with relevant keywords including Variational Bayes, Deep Learning, Bayesian Neural Networks, and Initialization Strategies.
