Nanopore sequencing offers long-read capabilities, but its accuracy is limited by noisy signals. Base calling, the process of translating these signals into DNA sequences, is a crucial step. This paper introduces a dynamic pooling method to enhance nanopore base calling accuracy. Unlike static pooling strategies that aggregate signals over fixed time windows, our dynamic approach adaptively adjusts the pooling duration based on signal characteristics, effectively capturing relevant information while mitigating noise. We implemented this method within a recurrent neural network (RNN) architecture. Experiments on both simulated and real nanopore data demonstrate that dynamic pooling significantly improves base calling accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods employing static pooling and other signal processing techniques. Specifically, we observed a X% reduction in error rate on a benchmark dataset. The results highlight the potential of dynamic pooling to unlock more accurate and reliable nanopore sequencing, with implications for applications ranging from genomics research to clinical diagnostics. Keywords: Nanopore sequencing, base calling, dynamic pooling, signal processing, recurrent neural network, DNA sequencing accuracy.
