This paper explores the application of data-driven reinforcement learning (RL) to enhance virtual character animation control. The objective is to develop a more realistic and adaptable animation system capable of generating diverse and dynamic motions. We propose a novel RL framework that leverages motion capture data to train virtual characters to perform complex actions. Our approach utilizes a deep neural network to approximate the optimal policy, enabling real-time control based on high-dimensional state spaces. The system is trained on a large dataset of human motion, allowing the character to learn natural and varied movements. Experimental results demonstrate that our method significantly improves the realism and responsiveness of virtual character animation compared to traditional control techniques. Specifically, the character exhibits improved balance, faster reaction times, and more fluid transitions between actions. The findings suggest that data-driven RL offers a promising avenue for creating more lifelike and engaging virtual experiences. This work contributes to the advancement of character animation in gaming, virtual reality, and robotics.
Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Virtual Character Animation, Motion Capture, Deep Learning, Artificial Intelligence.
