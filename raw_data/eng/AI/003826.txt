This paper presents FIERY, a novel framework for future instance prediction in bird's-eye view (BEV) utilizing surround monocular cameras. The growing demand for accurate scene understanding in autonomous driving motivates the challenge of forecasting the positions, shapes, and semantic categories of dynamic objects from multiple monocular camera inputs. FIERY addresses this challenge by transforming multi-view images into a unified BEV representation and leveraging a deep neural architecture to simultaneously detect and predict the short-term future trajectories of individual object instances. Our approach incorporates temporal encoding and spatial aggregation mechanisms to enhance the precision of predicted instance masks and object classes over multiple future time steps. Extensive experiments on real-world driving datasets demonstrate that FIERY outperforms existing monocular-based methods in terms of object detection accuracy, segmentation consistency, and future localization, while operating efficiently without reliance on expensive sensor modalities such as LiDAR or radar. The proposed framework offers significant implications for robust and cost-effective perception in intelligent vehicles, contributing to safer autonomous navigation in complex urban environments. Keywords: bird’s-eye view, future prediction, instance segmentation, monocular cameras, autonomous driving, deep learning.
