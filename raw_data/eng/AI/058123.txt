This paper proposes a novel convolutional decoder architecture for point cloud processing, leveraging adaptive instance normalization to enhance feature extraction and reconstruction quality. The objective is to address the challenges of point cloud decoding, where existing methods often struggle with preserving intricate details and structural information. Our approach employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) decoder, incorporating adaptive instance normalization to adaptively normalize feature maps and improve the model's ability to capture complex geometries. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art point cloud reconstruction techniques, achieving significant improvements in terms of accuracy and visual fidelity. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of adaptive instance normalization in point cloud decoding and the development of a convolutional decoder architecture that effectively preserves point cloud structures. This work has important implications for various applications, including 3D reconstruction, computer-aided design, and robotics, where high-quality point cloud processing is crucial. Key keywords: point clouds, convolutional decoder, adaptive instance normalization, 3D reconstruction, deep learning.
