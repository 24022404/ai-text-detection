This paper proposes a novel approach to sparse coding and dictionary learning, introducing Confident Kernel Sparse Coding (CKSC). The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of sparse representation models by incorporating kernel methods and confidence measures. Our approach utilizes a kernel-based framework to learn a dictionary that captures complex patterns in the data, while a confidence metric is used to selectively update the dictionary atoms. The CKSC algorithm is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating superior performance compared to state-of-the-art sparse coding methods. Results show that CKSC achieves significant improvements in reconstruction error and classification accuracy. The proposed method has important implications for applications such as image and signal processing, and can be extended to other domains including computer vision and machine learning. Key contributions of this work include the integration of kernel methods and confidence measures into sparse coding, and the development of a efficient dictionary learning algorithm. Relevant keywords: sparse coding, dictionary learning, kernel methods, confidence measures, machine learning, signal processing.
