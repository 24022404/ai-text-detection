This paper presents a novel divide-and-conquer approach to understanding deep neural networks, addressing the complexity and opacity of these models. Our objective is to develop a methodology that breaks down deep networks into manageable components, facilitating insight into their decision-making processes. We propose a hierarchical decomposition technique, leveraging techniques from graph theory and information theory to identify and isolate key functional modules within the network. Our approach enables the analysis of individual components, allowing for a more comprehensive understanding of the network's behavior and performance. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method in improving the interpretability and transparency of deep networks, outperforming existing techniques in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The implications of this research are significant, contributing to the development of more reliable, trustworthy, and explainable AI systems, with potential applications in areas such as computer vision, natural language processing, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: deep learning, neural networks, explainability, interpretability, divide-and-conquer, graph theory, information theory.
