Video classification, a fundamental task in computer vision, often suffers from processing irrelevant spatial and temporal information. To address this, we propose a novel saliency-guided video classification framework that leverages adaptively weighted learning to focus on salient regions. Our approach initially employs a spatiotemporal saliency detection network to identify informative regions within video frames. Subsequently, these saliency maps are utilized to generate adaptive weights, emphasizing salient features during the training of a video classification model. By prioritizing the learning of features extracted from salient regions, our method effectively filters out noise and irrelevant information, leading to improved classification accuracy. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our proposed framework outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in classification accuracy and robustness. The results show the effectiveness of adaptive weighting in focusing on key regions. This saliency-guided approach offers a promising direction for enhancing video classification performance in various applications.

Keywords: Video Classification, Saliency Detection, Adaptive Learning, Spatiotemporal Features, Computer Vision.
