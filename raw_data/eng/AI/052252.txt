This paper presents a novel approach to pose-guided person image synthesis using a Soft-Gated Warping-GAN framework. The objective is to generate highly realistic person images in desired poses, given an input image and a target pose. Our method employs a warping-based generator that leverages a soft-gated mechanism to selectively focus on relevant features and a discriminator that ensures the generated images are realistic and pose-consistent. The results demonstrate significant improvements over existing methods, with more accurate pose transfer and better preservation of person identity and appearance. Our approach contributes to the field of image synthesis by introducing a new warping-based generation paradigm and a soft-gated feature selection mechanism. The proposed Soft-Gated Warping-GAN has potential applications in various fields, including computer vision, robotics, and virtual reality, particularly in tasks such as person re-identification, pose estimation, and image editing. Key keywords: Pose-Guided Person Image Synthesis, Warping-GAN, Soft-Gated Mechanism, Image Generation, Computer Vision.
