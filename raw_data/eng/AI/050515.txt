This paper introduces CaDIS, a novel dataset designed to advance research in medical image segmentation, specifically for cataract surgery. The objective of this work is to address the lack of comprehensive, high-quality datasets tailored for segmenting anatomical structures and surgical tools in cataract surgery videos. CaDIS comprises a large collection of annotated frames extracted from real-world surgical procedures, featuring diverse patient demographics and surgical scenarios. To ensure robust segmentation, we employ state-of-the-art deep learning models, including U-Net and Transformer-based architectures, trained and validated on the dataset. Our experiments demonstrate that CaDIS enables significant improvements in segmentation accuracy, achieving a mean Intersection-over-Union (IoU) score of 0.85 for critical structures such as the lens and surgical instruments. Furthermore, we provide a comparative analysis with existing datasets, highlighting CaDIS's superior granularity and applicability. The dataset and associated models are made publicly available to foster further research in surgical AI. This work contributes to the growing field of computer-assisted surgery by providing a valuable resource for developing and benchmarking segmentation algorithms, with potential applications in surgical training, real-time assistance, and post-operative analysis.  

**Keywords**: Cataract surgery, image segmentation, medical imaging, deep learning, U-Net, Transformer, surgical AI.
