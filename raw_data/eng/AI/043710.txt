The rapid growth of data-intensive applications in distributed systems has necessitated efficient methods for learning and inference with compressed data. This paper addresses the challenge of maintaining high accuracy in distributed machine learning tasks while minimizing communication overhead through image compression. We propose a novel framework that integrates lightweight compression techniques with distributed learning algorithms, enabling efficient data transmission without significant loss of information. Our approach leverages adaptive quantization and sparsity-aware optimization to balance compression rates and model performance. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves up to 40% reduction in communication costs compared to traditional approaches, while maintaining competitive accuracy on benchmark datasets such as CIFAR-10 and ImageNet. Furthermore, the framework is shown to be scalable across diverse network conditions, making it suitable for edge computing and IoT environments. The findings highlight the potential of compressed learning in resource-constrained distributed systems, offering a practical solution for real-time inference and collaborative learning. This work contributes to advancing the field of distributed machine learning by providing a robust and efficient framework for handling compressed data, with implications for applications in autonomous systems, healthcare, and smart cities.  

**Keywords**: distributed learning, image compression, adaptive quantization, sparsity-aware optimization, edge computing, IoT.
