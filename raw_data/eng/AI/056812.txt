This paper presents a novel approach to view synthesis from single images by leveraging point cloud transformation. The objective is to generate photorealistic novel views of a scene from a single input image, addressing the challenge of limited viewpoint information. Our method employs a deep learning-based framework that transforms a 2D image into a 3D point cloud, which is then used to synthesize novel views. The point cloud transformation is achieved through a neural network that predicts the 3D structure of the scene, allowing for flexible and accurate view synthesis. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming existing methods in terms of visual quality and robustness. The proposed technique has significant implications for applications such as computer vision, robotics, and virtual reality, enabling the creation of immersive and interactive experiences from single images. Key contributions include the introduction of a point cloud transformation module and a novel view synthesis algorithm, which collectively enhance the state-of-the-art in image-based rendering and 3D reconstruction. Relevant keywords: view synthesis, point cloud transformation, deep learning, 3D reconstruction, computer vision, virtual reality.
