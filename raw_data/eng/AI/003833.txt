Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have emerged as a powerful tool for learning representations of graph-structured data, with applications ranging from social networks to molecular modeling. However, a critical challenge lies in effectively propagating information across nodes, especially in heterogeneous graphs where nodes and edges exhibit diverse characteristics. This paper addresses this limitation by proposing a novel approach that enhances information propagation in GNNs through heterogeneous aggregations. Our method introduces a multi-level aggregation framework that dynamically adapts to the varying properties of nodes and edges, enabling more nuanced and context-aware feature learning. We evaluate the proposed model on benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in node classification and link prediction tasks compared to state-of-the-art GNN architectures. The results highlight the model's ability to capture complex dependencies and improve generalization in heterogeneous settings. This research contributes to advancing GNNs by providing a scalable and efficient solution for handling diverse graph structures, with potential applications in recommendation systems, knowledge graphs, and bioinformatics.  

**Keywords**: Graph Neural Networks, Heterogeneous Aggregation, Information Propagation, Node Classification, Link Prediction, Graph Representation Learning.
