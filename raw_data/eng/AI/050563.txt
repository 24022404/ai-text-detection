This paper proposes a novel Text-guided Attention Model for image captioning, aiming to improve the accuracy and relevance of generated captions. Our approach leverages a deep learning framework, incorporating a text-guided attention mechanism that enables the model to focus on specific regions of the image based on the context of the caption. The model is trained on a large dataset of images and corresponding captions, utilizing a combination of computer vision and natural language processing techniques. Experimental results demonstrate that our model outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in caption quality and relevance. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a text-guided attention mechanism, which allows for more precise control over the caption generation process, and the development of a novel loss function that optimizes both image and text features. Our model has potential applications in areas such as image search, visual question answering, and human-computer interaction, and contributes to the advancement of AI models, particularly in the realm of multimodal processing and fine-tuning. Key keywords: image captioning, text-guided attention, deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing, multimodal processing.
