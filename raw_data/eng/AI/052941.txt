Accurate segmentation of overlapping chromosomes is crucial for karyotype analysis and genetic abnormality detection. This paper introduces a novel adversarial multiscale feature learning framework to address the challenges posed by chromosome clumping and variability in shape and size. Our method employs a multiscale convolutional neural network (CNN) to extract features at different resolutions, capturing both global context and fine-grained details of chromosome structures. An adversarial training strategy is then implemented, where a discriminator network distinguishes between real chromosome segmentations and those generated by the multiscale CNN. This forces the CNN to produce more realistic and accurate segmentation masks, particularly in overlapping regions. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing methods in terms of segmentation accuracy, especially in resolving overlapping chromosomes. Key improvements are observed in boundary delineation and separation of clumped chromosomes. The proposed method offers a robust and effective solution for automated chromosome segmentation, facilitating more efficient and reliable genetic analysis. Keywords: Chromosome Segmentation, Overlapping Chromosomes, Multiscale Feature Learning, Adversarial Training, Deep Learning, Karyotyping.
