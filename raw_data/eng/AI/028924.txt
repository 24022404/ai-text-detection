This paper proposes a novel approach to hypergraph learning, leveraging line expansion to enhance the representation and analysis of complex relationships within hypergraph-structured data. The objective is to develop an effective method for learning compact and informative hypergraph representations, capturing higher-order interactions and dependencies. Our approach employs a line expansion technique to transform the hypergraph into a graph, facilitating the application of established graph learning algorithms. The results demonstrate the efficacy of our method in improving hypergraph clustering, node classification, and link prediction tasks, outperforming existing state-of-the-art hypergraph learning models. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of line expansion as a means to simplify hypergraph analysis and the development of a flexible framework for hypergraph learning. This work has significant implications for various applications, including social network analysis, recommendation systems, and bioinformatics, where complex relationships and higher-order interactions are prevalent. Key keywords: hypergraph learning, line expansion, graph representation, node classification, link prediction, clustering.
