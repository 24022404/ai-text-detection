This paper presents BEV-MODNet, a novel approach for monocular camera-based moving object detection in bird's eye view, tailored for autonomous driving applications. The objective is to accurately detect and track moving objects, such as vehicles, pedestrians, and cyclists, in real-time, using a single camera. Our method leverages a deep learning-based architecture, combining convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and transformers to project 2D image features onto a 3D bird's eye view space. The results show significant improvements in detection accuracy and efficiency compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. Our approach achieves a mean average precision (mAP) of 85.6% on a benchmark dataset, outperforming other monocular camera-based methods. The key contributions of this work include a novel camera-to-BEV projection module and a multi-task learning framework that jointly optimizes object detection, tracking, and motion forecasting. The proposed BEV-MODNet has significant implications for autonomous driving, enabling more accurate and reliable perception systems. Key keywords: autonomous driving, bird's eye view, moving object detection, monocular camera, deep learning, CNNs, transformers.
