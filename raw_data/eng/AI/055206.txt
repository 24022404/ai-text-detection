This paper presents Constrained Policy Optimization, a novel approach to reinforcement learning that addresses the challenge of optimizing policies under complex constraints. The objective is to develop a framework that enables agents to learn effective policies while adhering to strict constraints, such as safety protocols or resource limitations. Our method employs a constrained optimization algorithm that integrates policy gradient methods with constraint satisfaction techniques. The results show that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of constraint satisfaction and policy performance, demonstrating its potential for real-world applications. Key findings include improved stability and efficiency in policy optimization, as well as the ability to handle complex constraint sets. The implications of this research are significant, enabling the development of more reliable and efficient autonomous systems. Our contributions include a new constrained policy optimization algorithm, experimental evaluations on benchmark domains, and insights into the trade-offs between policy performance and constraint satisfaction. Relevant keywords: reinforcement learning, constrained optimization, policy gradient methods, safety-critical systems, autonomous agents.
