This paper addresses the challenge of efficient scene understanding in automotive cyber-physical systems by accelerating deep neural networks. The objective is to develop a real-time scene understanding framework that can accurately detect and classify objects, such as pedestrians, vehicles, and road signs, while minimizing computational resources. To achieve this, we propose a novel approach that combines model pruning, knowledge distillation, and hardware-software co-design. Our method reduces the computational complexity of deep neural networks while maintaining their accuracy, resulting in a significant acceleration of scene understanding tasks. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving a speedup of up to 5x while maintaining an accuracy of over 90%. The implications of this research are significant, enabling the development of more efficient and reliable automotive cyber-physical systems that can improve road safety and enhance the driving experience. Key contributions include the development of a highly optimized deep neural network architecture, a novel knowledge distillation technique, and a hardware-software co-design framework that can be applied to a wide range of automotive applications, including autonomous driving, driver assistance systems, and smart mobility. Relevant keywords: deep neural networks, scene understanding, automotive cyber-physical systems, model pruning, knowledge distillation, hardware-software co-design, real-time processing, autonomous driving.
