Adversarial attacks pose a significant threat to the robustness of machine learning models, particularly those relying on gradient-based optimization. This paper addresses the challenge of defending against such attacks by proposing a novel minimax defense strategy. The objective is to enhance model resilience by minimizing the worst-case loss under gradient-based adversarial perturbations. Our approach integrates a minimax optimization framework into the training process, enabling the model to anticipate and counteract adversarial manipulations effectively. We evaluate the proposed method on benchmark datasets, including CIFAR-10 and MNIST, and compare its performance against state-of-the-art defense mechanisms. Results demonstrate that our minimax defense significantly improves model robustness, reducing the success rate of gradient-based attacks by up to 40% compared to existing methods. Furthermore, the approach maintains high accuracy on clean data, ensuring minimal trade-offs between robustness and performance. The implications of this research extend to applications in cybersecurity, autonomous systems, and other domains where adversarial robustness is critical. This work contributes a scalable and effective defense mechanism, advancing the field of adversarial machine learning.  

**Keywords**: adversarial attacks, minimax optimization, gradient-based defense, machine learning robustness, cybersecurity.
