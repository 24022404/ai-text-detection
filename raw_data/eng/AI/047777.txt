This paper addresses the challenge of video semantic segmentation, where distortions in video frames can significantly degrade model performance. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that corrects distortion-aware features, enhancing the accuracy of semantic segmentation in videos. We propose a distortion-aware feature correction module that leverages a combination of spatial and temporal attention mechanisms to identify and correct distorted features. Our approach utilizes a deep neural network architecture, incorporating a feature refinement stage that adaptively adjusts feature representations to mitigate the impact of distortions. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark video semantic segmentation datasets, outperforming existing methods by a significant margin. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of distortion-aware feature correction in video semantic segmentation, with potential applications in areas such as autonomous driving, surveillance, and video editing. Our contributions include a novel distortion-aware feature correction module, a refined feature refinement stage, and a comprehensive evaluation framework. Key keywords: video semantic segmentation, distortion-aware feature correction, deep neural networks, spatial and temporal attention mechanisms, feature refinement.
