Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) have achieved remarkable success in generating high-quality images, but their computational complexity and memory requirements hinder deployment on resource-constrained devices. This paper addresses the challenge of GAN compression by proposing a novel Online Multi-Granularity Distillation (OMGD) framework. The objective is to reduce the model size and computational cost while preserving the generative performance of the original GAN. Our approach introduces a multi-granularity distillation strategy that simultaneously transfers knowledge at both the feature and output levels, enabling the compressed model to learn diverse and fine-grained representations from the teacher network. Additionally, an online distillation mechanism is employed to dynamically adjust the distillation process during training, ensuring robust and stable learning. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that OMGD achieves significant compression ratios while maintaining competitive image quality compared to state-of-the-art methods. The proposed framework also shows superior generalization across various GAN architectures, making it a versatile solution for real-world applications. This work contributes to advancing efficient GAN deployment in edge computing and mobile environments, offering a balance between performance and resource efficiency.  

**Keywords**: GAN compression, knowledge distillation, multi-granularity learning, online distillation, model efficiency, generative models.
