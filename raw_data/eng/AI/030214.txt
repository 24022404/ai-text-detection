This paper proposes a novel approach for moving object detection by identifying contiguous outliers in low-rank representations. The objective is to effectively separate moving objects from the background in video sequences. Our method utilizes a robust low-rank matrix decomposition technique to model the background, and then detects moving objects by identifying contiguous outliers that do not fit the low-rank representation. The approach is validated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its effectiveness in detecting moving objects in various scenarios. Experimental results show that our method outperforms state-of-the-art approaches in terms of accuracy and robustness. The key contributions of this research include a new framework for moving object detection, improved handling of complex backgrounds, and enhanced robustness to noise and occlusions. This work has significant implications for applications such as surveillance, autonomous vehicles, and robotics, where accurate moving object detection is crucial. Key keywords: moving object detection, low-rank representation, robust matrix decomposition, computer vision, video analysis.
