Kernel Sparse Coding (KSC) has emerged as a powerful tool for nonlinear data representation. However, traditional KSC methods often suffer from instability and sensitivity to parameter selection, leading to suboptimal performance. This paper introduces Confident Kernel Sparse Coding and Dictionary Learning (CKSCDL), a novel framework designed to enhance the robustness and accuracy of KSC. CKSCDL incorporates a confidence-weighted regularization term that adaptively adjusts the impact of each kernel atom based on its relevance and reliability during the sparse coding process. Simultaneously, we learn an optimal dictionary that minimizes reconstruction error and promotes sparsity through an efficient alternating optimization scheme. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that CKSCDL significantly outperforms existing KSC methods and other state-of-the-art sparse coding techniques in terms of classification accuracy and reconstruction quality. The proposed approach offers improved stability and generalization capability, making it suitable for various applications, including image recognition, signal processing, and data mining. Key words: Kernel Sparse Coding, Dictionary Learning, Sparse Representation, Nonlinear Data Analysis, Machine Learning.
