This paper introduces KeRLym, a novel Gym Reinforcement Learning (RL) agent designed for radio control and signal detection in dynamic wireless environments. The objective is to develop an intelligent agent capable of adaptively optimizing radio parameters for enhanced signal quality and efficient resource utilization. KeRLym leverages deep reinforcement learning (DRL) techniques, specifically a deep Q-network (DQN) architecture, to learn optimal control policies from interactions with a simulated radio environment. Experimental results demonstrate that KeRLym outperforms traditional fixed parameter configurations, achieving a 15% improvement in signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) and a 10% reduction in interference. Furthermore, KeRLym exhibits robust performance across varying channel conditions and user densities. This work contributes a practical DRL-based solution for adaptive radio resource management, offering potential applications in cognitive radio networks and intelligent wireless communication systems. Keywords: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Radio Control, Signal Detection, Gym Environment, Adaptive Radio Resource Management, DQN.
