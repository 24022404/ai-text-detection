This paper addresses the challenge of training Legendre Memory Units (LMUs) efficiently by proposing a parallelization approach. The objective is to accelerate the training process of LMUs, which are neural network components designed to capture long-range dependencies in sequential data. To achieve this, we employ a distributed computing framework that splits the training data and model parameters across multiple processing units, enabling concurrent computation of gradient updates. Our method utilizes a synchronized parallelization strategy, ensuring consistency across all units. The results show a significant reduction in training time, with speedups of up to 4x on 8 processing units, while maintaining the model's accuracy. This parallelization technique contributes to the advancement of LMU training, enabling the application of these models to larger and more complex datasets. The proposed approach has implications for various applications, including natural language processing and time-series forecasting, where LMUs have shown promising results. Key contributions include the development of a scalable and efficient parallelization method for LMU training, demonstrating the potential for accelerated training of neural networks with memory-augmented components. Relevant keywords: Legendre Memory Units, parallel computing, distributed training, neural networks, long-range dependencies.
