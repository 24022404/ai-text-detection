This paper proposes a novel approach to instance segmentation, leveraging a contour loss function via k-step distance transformation image. The objective is to improve the accuracy of segmenting individual objects within an image, addressing the challenges of overlapping instances and complex boundaries. Our method employs a k-step distance transformation to generate a distance map, which is then used to compute a contour loss that guides the segmentation model to focus on object boundaries. The approach is based on a deep learning architecture, utilizing a convolutional neural network (CNN) to predict instance masks. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our contour loss function, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets. The key findings indicate that our method outperforms existing instance segmentation approaches, particularly in scenarios with dense object packing and intricate boundaries. The proposed contour loss function has significant implications for computer vision applications, including object detection, tracking, and scene understanding, with potential applications in fields such as robotics, autonomous driving, and medical imaging. Keywords: instance segmentation, contour loss, distance transformation, deep learning, computer vision.
