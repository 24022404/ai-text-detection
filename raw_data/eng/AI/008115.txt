Sleep staging is a critical task in understanding sleep patterns and diagnosing sleep disorders, yet it remains challenging due to the complexity and variability of physiological signals. This paper introduces *U-Time*, a novel fully convolutional network (FCN) architecture designed specifically for time series segmentation, with a focus on sleep staging. The proposed model leverages the strengths of FCNs to capture temporal dependencies and hierarchical features directly from raw polysomnography (PSG) data, eliminating the need for handcrafted feature engineering. U-Time employs a U-Net-inspired structure with skip connections to enhance feature representation and improve segmentation accuracy across different sleep stages. Experiments conducted on publicly available sleep datasets demonstrate that U-Time achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming traditional machine learning and existing deep learning approaches in terms of both accuracy and computational efficiency. The model’s ability to generalize across diverse datasets highlights its robustness and potential for clinical applications. This work contributes a scalable and efficient solution for automated sleep staging, paving the way for improved sleep analysis and personalized healthcare.  

**Keywords**: Sleep staging, time series segmentation, fully convolutional network, U-Net, polysomnography, deep learning.
