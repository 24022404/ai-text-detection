This paper introduces a novel high-order differentiable autoencoder (HDA) for nonlinear model reduction. The HDA leverages higher-order derivatives in the encoding and decoding processes to capture complex nonlinear relationships within high-dimensional data, surpassing the limitations of traditional linear or first-order methods. Our approach involves a tailored neural network architecture integrated with differentiable programming techniques to ensure end-to-end differentiability, enabling efficient training via gradient-based optimization. We demonstrate the effectiveness of the HDA through experiments on several benchmark problems, including fluid dynamics simulations and structural mechanics. Results indicate that the HDA achieves significantly improved accuracy and compression rates compared to standard autoencoders and proper orthogonal decomposition (POD) based reduction methods, especially in scenarios with strong nonlinearities. The proposed method offers a powerful tool for reducing the computational cost of complex simulations and facilitating real-time analysis and control. Keywords: Autoencoder, Model Reduction, Nonlinear Dynamics, Differentiable Programming, Neural Networks, Dimensionality Reduction.
