Adversarial attacks pose significant challenges to the reliability and safety of machine learning models, particularly those based on deep neural networks. This paper addresses the pressing issue of gradient-based adversarial attacks by proposing a novel minimax defense strategy designed to enhance model robustness. The proposed approach formulates the defense problem as a minimax optimization, where the model parameters are trained to minimize the worst-case loss induced by gradient-based adversarial perturbations. We implement this framework on standard image classification benchmarks and compare its performance against state-of-the-art adversarial training methods. Experimental results demonstrate that our minimax defense achieves superior robustness, substantially reducing the success rate of both white-box and black-box gradient-based attacks while maintaining commendable accuracy on clean data. Our findings highlight the effectiveness of the minimax formulation in identifying and countering strong adversarial examples. This work offers significant insights for the development of resilient AI systems and contributes a practical solution for real-world deployments where model security is paramount. Key keywords: adversarial attacks, minimax defense, gradient-based attacks, deep learning, robust machine learning, adversarial training, model security.
