This paper presents a novel approach to point cloud colorization, leveraging a densely annotated 3D shape dataset to achieve high-fidelity results. The objective is to develop an effective method for assigning realistic colors to 3D point clouds, enhancing their visual appeal and usefulness in various applications. Our approach employs a deep learning-based model, utilizing a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture to learn the complex relationships between 3D geometry and color information. The model is trained on a large-scale dataset of densely annotated 3D shapes, allowing it to capture subtle nuances in color distribution and texture. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of our method, achieving significant improvements in colorization accuracy and visual coherence compared to existing state-of-the-art techniques. The proposed approach has far-reaching implications for various fields, including computer vision, robotics, and 3D reconstruction, enabling the creation of more realistic and immersive 3D models. Key contributions include the introduction of a novel CNN-based architecture, a large-scale densely annotated 3D shape dataset, and a comprehensive evaluation framework for point cloud colorization. Relevant keywords: point cloud colorization, 3D shape dataset, deep learning, CNN, computer vision, 3D reconstruction.
