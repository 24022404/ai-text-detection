Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) have become a cornerstone of modern machine learning, yet their performance is often hindered by inherent uncertainties that arise during training and inference. This paper addresses fundamental issues related to these uncertainties, focusing on their sources, impacts, and mitigation strategies. We explore the dual nature of uncertainties—epistemic (model-related) and aleatoric (data-related)—and their influence on the reliability and robustness of ANNs. To tackle these challenges, we propose a hybrid approach combining Bayesian inference with ensemble learning techniques, enabling more accurate uncertainty quantification and improved model generalization. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method significantly reduces prediction errors and enhances confidence in model outputs compared to traditional approaches. The findings highlight the importance of addressing uncertainties in ANNs for applications requiring high reliability, such as healthcare and autonomous systems. This research contributes to advancing the field by providing a systematic framework for understanding and mitigating uncertainties, paving the way for more trustworthy and interpretable AI systems.  

**Keywords**: Artificial Neural Networks, Uncertainty Quantification, Bayesian Inference, Ensemble Learning, Robust Machine Learning.
