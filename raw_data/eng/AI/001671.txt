Image captioning models often generate similar and generic descriptions, lacking the diversity observed in human-generated captions. This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing diversity in image captioning. We propose a novel approach that incorporates a stochastic sampling strategy during the decoding phase, guided by a learned diversity metric. This metric estimates the novelty and relevance of generated captions with respect to previously produced outputs. Our method encourages the model to explore less common, yet still accurate, descriptions of an image. Experiments on benchmark datasets, including COCO and Flickr30k, demonstrate that our approach significantly improves the diversity of generated captions, as measured by metrics such as distinct-n and self-BLEU, while maintaining competitive accuracy. The results highlight the potential of incorporating diversity-aware strategies to generate more human-like and informative image descriptions. Keywords: Image captioning, diversity, stochastic sampling, neural networks, natural language generation.
