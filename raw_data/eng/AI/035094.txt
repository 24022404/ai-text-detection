This paper introduces ICON, a novel approach for learning regular maps through inverse consistency. The objective is to develop a method that can effectively learn mappings between two domains while ensuring consistency in the inverse process. To achieve this, we propose a deep learning framework that leverages inverse consistency as a regularizer to guide the learning process. Our approach utilizes a combination of forward and inverse models to learn regular maps, enabling the discovery of underlying structures in the data. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of ICON in learning high-quality maps, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of inverse consistency in regularizing the learning process, leading to improved performance and generalizability. ICON has significant implications for various applications, including computer vision, robotics, and medical imaging, where learning regular maps is crucial. Key contributions of this work include the introduction of inverse consistency as a regularizer, the development of a deep learning framework for learning regular maps, and the demonstration of state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. Relevant keywords: inverse consistency, regular maps, deep learning, computer vision, robotics, medical imaging.
