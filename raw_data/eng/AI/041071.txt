Traffic sign detection is a critical component of autonomous driving and advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS), yet it remains challenging due to variations in lighting, occlusion, and scale. This paper introduces VSSA-NET, a novel Vertical Spatial Sequence Attention Network designed to enhance traffic sign detection by leveraging vertical spatial dependencies in image sequences. The proposed network integrates a sequence attention mechanism that prioritizes vertical spatial features, enabling more accurate localization and classification of traffic signs under diverse conditions. VSSA-NET employs a lightweight encoder-decoder architecture combined with a multi-scale feature fusion module to improve detection performance across varying resolutions. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that VSSA-NET achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of precision, recall, and mean average precision (mAP). The network’s ability to handle complex scenarios, such as overlapping signs and low-visibility conditions, highlights its robustness and practicality for real-world applications. This research contributes to the field of computer vision by introducing a novel attention mechanism tailored for traffic sign detection, offering significant potential for improving the safety and reliability of autonomous systems.  

**Keywords**: Traffic sign detection, vertical spatial sequence attention, autonomous driving, computer vision, deep learning, attention mechanisms.
