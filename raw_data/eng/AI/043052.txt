This paper addresses the challenge of heterogeneous domain adaptation, where the goal is to adapt a model trained on a source domain to a target domain with different distributions and features. The objective is to develop a novel approach that preserves the structural relationships between domains, enabling effective knowledge transfer. We propose a Cross-Domain Structure Preserving Projection (CD-SPP) method, which leverages a projection-based approach to align the source and target domains while preserving the inherent structure of the data. Our method utilizes a graph-based representation to capture the relationships between domains and a projection matrix to transform the source domain data into the target domain. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of CD-SPP in adapting models across heterogeneous domains, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The key contributions of this research include a novel projection-based approach for heterogeneous domain adaptation, improved performance on benchmark datasets, and the ability to handle complex domain shifts. This work has significant implications for real-world applications, such as cross-domain image classification, sentiment analysis, and recommender systems, where domain adaptation is crucial for achieving high performance. Key keywords: heterogeneous domain adaptation, structure preserving projection, cross-domain learning, transfer learning, domain alignment.
