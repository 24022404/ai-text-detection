Single-shot object detectors have gained prominence due to their efficiency, but often struggle with detecting objects of varying scales, especially without anchor boxes. This paper introduces a Feature Selective Anchor-Free (FSAF) module to enhance the detection performance of single-shot detectors by adaptively selecting features most relevant to each object instance. The FSAF module incorporates a feature selection mechanism based on a lightweight network that predicts the optimal feature level for each object, enabling dynamic assignment of objects to appropriate feature maps. We evaluate the proposed module on benchmark datasets, including COCO, demonstrating significant improvements in detection accuracy, particularly for small objects, compared to state-of-the-art anchor-free and anchor-based methods. The results highlight the effectiveness of adaptive feature selection in improving the robustness and accuracy of single-shot object detectors. The FSAF module offers a versatile and efficient solution that can be readily integrated into existing single-shot detection frameworks, contributing to more accurate and efficient object detection in real-world applications. Keywords: Object Detection, Single-Shot Detector, Anchor-Free, Feature Selection, Deep Learning.
