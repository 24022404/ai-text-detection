Codes can be classified by their intended purpose or inherent properties into various types such as ar codes, constrained codes, error-correcting codes, and error-detecting codes. Constrained codes, in particular, are constructed to satisfy specific requirements that arise from real-world data transmission challenges. In this thesis, special attention is given to the integration of a run length limited constrained code with a positioning code. This approach leverages the unique advantages that constrained codes offer in managing transmission constraints, thus effectively addressing practical issues encountered in communication systems.
