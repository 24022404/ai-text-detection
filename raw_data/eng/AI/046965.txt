Tracking individuals in crowded scenes presents significant challenges due to occlusions, similar appearances, and complex interactions. This paper addresses these challenges by proposing a novel approach to analyze and track crowds based on physical characteristics. Our method integrates pedestrian detection with individual physical feature extraction, including height, stride length, and body mass index (BMI), to improve tracking accuracy. We leverage deep learning models for robust pedestrian detection and employ computer vision techniques to estimate physical attributes from video data. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that incorporating physical characteristics significantly enhances tracking performance, particularly in scenarios with high crowd density and frequent occlusions. Comparative analysis shows that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art tracking algorithms in terms of identity switches and trajectory accuracy. This research offers a valuable contribution to crowd analysis and surveillance applications, enabling more reliable and detailed monitoring of individuals within complex crowd dynamics. Keywords: Crowd Tracking, Pedestrian Detection, Physical Characteristics, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Surveillance.
