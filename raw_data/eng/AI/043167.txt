This paper introduces a novel approach for explaining features in tabular data using an attention-like mechanism, addressing the growing need for interpretability in machine learning models applied to structured datasets. The proposed method leverages a lightweight, attention-based framework to identify and quantify the importance of individual features, enabling transparent and intuitive explanations for model predictions. Unlike traditional feature importance techniques, this approach dynamically adapts to the context of each input, providing fine-grained insights into how specific features influence outcomes. Experiments conducted on benchmark tabular datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of the method, achieving competitive performance in both interpretability and predictive accuracy compared to state-of-the-art techniques. The results highlight the framework's ability to uncover complex feature interactions and improve trust in model decisions. This work contributes to the field of explainable AI by offering a scalable and efficient solution for feature explanation in tabular data, with potential applications in healthcare, finance, and other domains where interpretability is critical.  

**Keywords**: Tabular data, feature explanation, attention mechanism, interpretability, explainable AI, machine learning.
