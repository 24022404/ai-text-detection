This paper addresses the problem of rectifying radially-distorted scales and change of scales in computer vision. Our objective is to develop minimal solvers that can efficiently and accurately rectify distorted scales and adapt to changes in scale. We propose a novel approach that combines geometric constraints with a robust estimation framework to achieve this goal. Our method utilizes a minimal set of correspondences to estimate the distortion parameters and scale change, allowing for efficient and accurate rectification. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The key contributions of this research include the development of a minimal solver for radially-distorted scales and a change of scales, and the introduction of a robust estimation framework that can handle noisy and outlier-prone data. Our work has important implications for various computer vision applications, including 3D reconstruction, object recognition, and scene understanding. Key keywords: radial distortion, scale change, minimal solvers, computer vision, 3D reconstruction, geometric constraints.
