This paper proposes a novel Temporal Modulation Network (TMN) for controllable space-time video super-resolution, addressing the challenge of enhancing the resolution of videos while preserving their temporal consistency. Our approach combines the strengths of deep learning and traditional video processing techniques to achieve high-quality video super-resolution. The TMN model utilizes a temporal modulation module to adaptively adjust the spatial and temporal features of video frames, allowing for flexible control over the super-resolution process. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art video super-resolution algorithms in terms of peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM). The proposed TMN model also enables efficient and controllable video super-resolution, making it suitable for various applications such as video surveillance, sports broadcasting, and virtual reality. Key contributions of this work include the introduction of a temporal modulation mechanism, a novel loss function for controllable super-resolution, and a comprehensive evaluation framework for video super-resolution algorithms. Relevant keywords: video super-resolution, deep learning, temporal modulation, space-time processing, controllable super-resolution.
