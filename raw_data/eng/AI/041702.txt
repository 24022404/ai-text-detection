Labeled graphs function as more than mere visualization tools; they also act as encoders through the finite state splitting algorithm, a critical technique for mapping sequences within constrained systems. Based on their intended usage, encoders vary, but this thesis concentrates specifically on those designed to generate sequences that adhere to pre-defined constraints, as opposed to encoders that focus on accepting or recognizing input sequences. This distinction is vital for applications where sequence generation under strict rules is required. In parallel, coding theory investigates combinatorial structures, including positioning codes such as de Bruijn codes, which play a significant role in efficient data representation and transmission.
