Weak supervision has emerged as a promising paradigm for training machine learning models with limited labeled data. This paper introduces Knodle, a modular weakly supervised learning framework implemented in PyTorch. Knodle facilitates the integration of multiple, potentially noisy, weak supervision sources such as rule-based systems, knowledge bases, and crowdsourcing. The framework allows users to define custom labeling functions, combine their outputs through a trainable rule, and integrate the resulting weak labels into a standard PyTorch training pipeline. This modular design promotes flexibility and simplifies experimentation with different weak supervision strategies. We demonstrate the effectiveness of Knodle on several benchmark datasets, achieving competitive performance compared to state-of-the-art weakly supervised methods, while also reducing development time through its user-friendly API. Knodle’s modularity and PyTorch integration provide researchers and practitioners with a powerful tool for leveraging weak supervision in a variety of machine learning tasks. Keywords: Weak Supervision, Machine Learning, PyTorch, Knowledge Distillation, Noisy Labels.
