Helpdesk services are evaluated using a comprehensive set of key performance metrics, which are crucial for assessing operational efficiency and customer satisfaction. Among these, ticket backlog is monitored not just in sheer numbers but also by gauging the urgency of outstanding issues and the percentage of those that are reopened, providing insight into recurring or unresolved problems. Customer satisfaction is tracked primarily through the CSAT score, offering direct feedback from users regarding their service experiences. Metrics like average resolution time (ART) and average handle time (AHT) highlight the speed and effectiveness of issue resolution, while service level agreement (SLA) compliance measures the team’s adherence to predefined standards. Team throughput, often referred to as velocity, serves as an indicator of productivity, and the identification of workflow bottlenecks helps in streamlining processes.
