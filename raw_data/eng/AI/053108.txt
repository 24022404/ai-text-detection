This paper presents a novel approach to shape generation using spatially partitioned point clouds, addressing the challenge of efficiently creating complex 3D models from large datasets. Our method utilizes a hierarchical partitioning strategy to divide the point cloud into manageable segments, which are then used to generate detailed 3D shapes. We employ a combination of geometric and deep learning techniques to reconstruct and refine the shapes, resulting in highly accurate and detailed models. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The proposed technique has significant implications for various applications, including computer-aided design, robotics, and virtual reality. Key contributions of this research include the development of a scalable and efficient shape generation framework, enabling the creation of complex 3D models from large point cloud datasets. Relevant keywords: point clouds, shape generation, 3D reconstruction, spatial partitioning, deep learning, computer-aided design.
