This paper addresses the challenge of generating compact and efficient representations of 3D point clouds, a critical task in applications such as autonomous driving, robotics, and augmented reality. We propose a novel framework leveraging Adversarial Autoencoders (AAEs) to learn low-dimensional, semantically rich embeddings of 3D point cloud data. The proposed approach combines the reconstruction capabilities of autoencoders with the adversarial training mechanism of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to ensure that the learned representations are both compact and robust. Our method is evaluated on benchmark datasets, demonstrating superior performance in terms of reconstruction accuracy and representation quality compared to traditional autoencoders and other state-of-the-art techniques. The results highlight the effectiveness of adversarial training in capturing fine-grained geometric details while maintaining computational efficiency. This work contributes to advancing 3D data processing by providing a scalable and lightweight solution for point cloud representation, with potential applications in real-time systems and resource-constrained environments.  

**Keywords**: Adversarial Autoencoders, 3D Point Clouds, Compact Representations, Generative Adversarial Networks, 3D Reconstruction.
