Multiview detection is crucial for comprehensive scene understanding, yet it suffers from challenges such as occlusions and variations in viewpoint. This paper introduces a novel framework, the Shadow Transformer, to address these limitations. The Shadow Transformer leverages attention mechanisms to effectively aggregate features from multiple views, mitigating the impact of occlusions by emphasizing information from unobstructed viewpoints. Furthermore, we propose a view-coherent data augmentation strategy that synthesizes new multiview images, improving the model's robustness to viewpoint variations. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods in multiview object detection. Specifically, the Shadow Transformer achieves improved detection accuracy, particularly in scenarios with significant occlusions. The view-coherent data augmentation further enhances performance and generalization. Our contributions advance the field of multiview detection by providing a more robust and accurate method for aggregating information across multiple viewpoints. Keywords: Multiview Detection, Transformer, Data Augmentation, Object Detection, Computer Vision.
