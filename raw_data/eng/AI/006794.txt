Online decision trees are widely used in dynamic environments due to their adaptability and efficiency. However, their performance can degrade with complex data streams and concept drift. This paper introduces Online Local Boosting (OLB), a novel ensemble method that enhances the accuracy and stability of online decision trees. OLB maintains a pool of online decision trees, each focused on learning from different locally weighted instances based on their historical performance. The weights are dynamically adjusted using an adaptive boosting strategy, prioritizing challenging instances. The proposed method was evaluated on several synthetic and real-world datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in classification accuracy and robustness compared to state-of-the-art online decision tree algorithms and ensemble methods. OLB effectively mitigates the impact of noise and concept drift by leveraging the diversity of locally specialized trees. The results highlight OLB as a promising approach for online learning in evolving data streams, offering enhanced prediction accuracy and adaptability.

**Keywords:** Online learning, Decision trees, Ensemble methods, Boosting, Concept drift, Data streams.
