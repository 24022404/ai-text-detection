Anomaly detection is crucial for identifying rare and potentially harmful events across various domains. However, training effective anomaly detectors often suffers from the scarcity of labeled anomalous data. This paper investigates a novel approach to address this challenge by learning ensembles of anomaly detectors on synthetically generated datasets. We propose a framework that leverages generative models to create diverse and realistic synthetic anomalies, which are then used to train a collection of anomaly detection models. These models are combined into an ensemble using different aggregation techniques. We evaluate our approach on several real-world datasets, demonstrating that ensembles trained on synthetic data achieve comparable or superior performance to models trained solely on limited real anomaly data. The results show that our method significantly improves anomaly detection accuracy, particularly in scenarios with scarce labeled anomalies. This research contributes a practical and scalable solution for enhancing anomaly detection systems by effectively utilizing synthetic data. Keywords: Anomaly Detection, Ensemble Learning, Synthetic Data, Generative Models, Rare Event Detection.
