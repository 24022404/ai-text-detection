This paper introduces NeuralSampler, a novel auto-encoder architecture for learning latent representations of Euclidean point clouds and generating new samples. Addressing the challenges of unordered and unstructured point cloud data, NeuralSampler employs a permutation-invariant encoder based on graph neural networks to extract meaningful features. A novel sampling strategy in the latent space is then utilized to generate diverse and realistic point clouds. Specifically, we leverage a learned distribution over the latent space, allowing for controlled and coherent sampling of new point cloud structures. Experiments on various benchmark datasets demonstrate that NeuralSampler achieves state-of-the-art performance in point cloud reconstruction and generation, surpassing existing methods in terms of accuracy and fidelity. Furthermore, we show the ability of NeuralSampler to generate conditional samples, enabling applications in shape completion and data augmentation. Our findings highlight the effectiveness of NeuralSampler for point cloud processing and its potential for various applications in computer vision and graphics.

Keywords: Point Clouds, Auto-encoder, Graph Neural Networks, Sampling, Generative Models, 3D Reconstruction
