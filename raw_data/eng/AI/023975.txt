This paper introduces Impressions2Font, a novel system for generating fonts based on user-specified impressions. The objective is to bridge the gap between human perception of font aesthetics and the technical process of font design. Our approach utilizes a deep learning model that takes natural language inputs describing the desired font impression, such as "modern", "elegant", or "vintage", and generates a corresponding font design. The system employs a unique encoding scheme to capture the nuances of font styles and a generative adversarial network (GAN) to produce high-quality font designs. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of Impressions2Font in generating diverse and coherent fonts that match user-specified impressions. The system outperforms existing font generation methods in terms of user satisfaction and design quality. This research contributes to the field of computer graphics and human-computer interaction, enabling users to create custom fonts that reflect their personal style and preferences. Key applications include graphic design, branding, and digital publishing. Relevant keywords: font generation, deep learning, GAN, natural language processing, computer graphics, human-computer interaction.
