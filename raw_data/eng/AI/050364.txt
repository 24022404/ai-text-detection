**Abstract**

The design and generation of novel fonts is a complex task that typically requires advanced graphic skills and significant manual effort. This paper introduces Impressions2Font, a novel framework that enables users to generate custom fonts by simply specifying a set of desired impressions or descriptive keywords, such as "elegant," "playful," or "futuristic." Leveraging advances in deep learning and natural language processing, we develop a model that translates linguistic impressions into distinctive typeface designs. The system employs a multi-modal generative architecture combining a natural language encoder with a generative adversarial network (GAN) for font synthesis. Comprehensive experiments were conducted using a curated dataset of fonts with annotated impressions, demonstrating that Impressions2Font reliably generates typefaces that align with user-specified attributes. Quantitative evaluations based on user studies and automated metrics confirm the quality and perceptual fidelity of the generated fonts compared to existing methods. Our findings suggest that Impressions2Font significantly lowers the barrier for personalized font creation and can be utilized in graphic design, branding, and creative industries. This work highlights a new direction in generative design tools, bridging semantic intent and visual content in typography. **Keywords:** font generation, impression-driven design, generative adversarial networks, deep learning, human-computer interaction, typography.
