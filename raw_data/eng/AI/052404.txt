This paper addresses the challenge of efficient data harvesting from distributed wireless sensor networks (WSNs) using unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). We propose a novel path planning strategy based on deep reinforcement learning (DRL) to optimize the UAV's trajectory for maximizing data collection while minimizing energy consumption and flight time. Our approach leverages a deep Q-network (DQN) agent trained within a simulated environment that mimics realistic WSN deployment scenarios. The DQN agent learns to navigate the UAV through the network, dynamically adjusting its path to prioritize nodes with higher data volumes and lower battery levels. The performance of the proposed DRL-based path planning algorithm is evaluated through extensive simulations, demonstrating significant improvements in data harvesting rate, energy efficiency, and flight time compared to traditional path planning methods such as shortest path and boustrophedon coverage. This research offers a promising solution for autonomous and efficient data retrieval in diverse WSN applications, including environmental monitoring, precision agriculture, and disaster management. Key words: UAV, path planning, wireless sensor network, data harvesting, deep reinforcement learning, DQN.
