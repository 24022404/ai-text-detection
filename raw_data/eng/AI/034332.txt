The study aims to address the challenge of assessing aesthetic attributes in images, a key component in various applications such as photography, digital media, and online content moderation. The research seeks to provide a comprehensive model that can accurately evaluate aesthetic features and enhance the understanding of image quality from a subjective viewpoint.

Methods/Approach: We propose an innovative approach leveraging a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, designed specifically for aesthetic assessment. The model incorporates advanced feature extraction techniques to capture nuanced aesthetic attributes such as color harmony, balance, and composition. A diverse dataset of images with pre-labeled aesthetic scores is used to train and validate the model, ensuring robust performance across different styles and contexts.

Results/Findings: The proposed CNN model demonstrates superior performance in predicting aesthetic attributes, achieving significant improvements in accuracy compared to existing methods. The model's ability to generalize across various types of images is validated through extensive testing, showing notable advancements in automatic image assessment systems.

Conclusion/Implications: Our research contributes a novel CNN-based model that enhances the precision of aesthetic evaluations in image processing. This work holds important implications for the development of intelligent systems in digital media and content creation, facilitating automated feedback and improvements. Potential applications include image editing software, digital marketing platforms, and social media analytics.

Keywords: Aesthetic assessment, Convolutional neural network, Image quality, Feature extraction, Digital media.
