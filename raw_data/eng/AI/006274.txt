This paper proposes SGCN, a Sparse Graph Convolution Network designed to predict pedestrian trajectories in complex environments. The objective is to improve the accuracy of trajectory forecasting by leveraging the spatial and temporal relationships between pedestrians. Our approach utilizes a sparse graph structure to model the interactions between pedestrians, combined with convolutional neural networks to extract features from the graph. The SGCN model is trained on a large dataset of pedestrian trajectories and achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of prediction accuracy and efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate that SGCN outperforms existing methods, particularly in scenarios with high pedestrian density and complex motion patterns. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a sparse graph representation for pedestrian trajectory prediction and the development of a novel graph convolutional network architecture. This work has significant implications for applications such as autonomous vehicles, surveillance systems, and crowd management, where accurate pedestrian trajectory prediction is crucial. Key keywords: pedestrian trajectory prediction, graph convolutional networks, sparse graph representation, autonomous systems, computer vision.
