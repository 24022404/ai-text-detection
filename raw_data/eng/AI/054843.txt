Tracking individuals in crowded environments remains a significant challenge due to occlusions, dense interactions, and varying physical characteristics. This paper addresses the problem of crowd analysis by leveraging physical attributes such as height, body shape, and movement patterns to improve tracking accuracy. We propose a novel framework that integrates computer vision techniques with machine learning models to extract and analyze these characteristics in real-time. Our approach employs a multi-stage pipeline, combining object detection, feature extraction, and trajectory prediction, to enhance robustness in complex scenarios. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms existing approaches, achieving a 15% improvement in tracking accuracy under high-density conditions. The findings highlight the importance of incorporating physical characteristics for effective crowd analysis, offering potential applications in surveillance, public safety, and urban planning. This research contributes to advancing the field by introducing a scalable and adaptable solution for real-world crowd tracking challenges.  

**Keywords**: Crowd tracking, physical characteristics, computer vision, machine learning, trajectory prediction, surveillance.
