This paper explores the intersection of distributional reinforcement learning and statistical sampling, aiming to improve the efficiency and accuracy of learning in complex environments. Our objective is to develop a framework that effectively balances exploration and exploitation by leveraging statistical methods to analyze and optimize the sampling process in distributional reinforcement learning. We propose a novel approach that integrates bootstrap sampling and distributional bellman equations to estimate the return distribution, allowing for more accurate and robust learning. Our results show that this method outperforms existing approaches in various environments, demonstrating improved sample efficiency and reduced variance. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of careful sampling strategies in distributional reinforcement learning, contributing to the development of more effective and efficient reinforcement learning algorithms. This work has significant implications for applications in robotics, game playing, and other areas where reinforcement learning is applied, and contributes to the broader field of artificial intelligence and machine learning, with relevant keywords including distributional reinforcement learning, statistical sampling, bootstrap methods, and return distribution estimation.
