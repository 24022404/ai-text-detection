Time series forecasting is a critical task in various domains, yet existing methods often struggle with capturing long-term dependencies and managing computational inefficiencies. This paper addresses these challenges by enhancing the locality and breaking the memory bottleneck of Transformer models in time series forecasting. We propose a novel approach that integrates locality-sensitive mechanisms into the Transformer architecture, enabling more efficient modeling of local patterns while maintaining the ability to capture global dependencies. Additionally, we introduce a memory-efficient attention mechanism that reduces the computational overhead associated with traditional self-attention, particularly for long sequences. Our experiments demonstrate that the proposed method achieves significant improvements in forecasting accuracy across multiple benchmark datasets, outperforming state-of-the-art models in both short-term and long-term prediction tasks. The results highlight the effectiveness of our approach in addressing the limitations of existing Transformer-based methods, offering a scalable and efficient solution for time series forecasting. This work contributes to advancing the application of Transformers in time series analysis, with potential implications for domains such as finance, healthcare, and energy management.  

**Keywords**: Time series forecasting, Transformer models, locality-sensitive mechanisms, memory-efficient attention, long-term dependencies, computational efficiency.
