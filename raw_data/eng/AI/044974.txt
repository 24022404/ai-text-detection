The extraction of road curbs is a critical process in urban infrastructure management and autonomous vehicle navigation. This research focuses on developing a novel method for accurately identifying and extracting road curbs from mobile Light Detection and Ranging (LiDAR) point clouds, addressing the challenges posed by complex urban environments.

Methods/Approach: We propose a robust algorithm that utilizes a combination of point cloud segmentation and geometric feature analysis to differentiate between road surfaces and curbs. The approach includes preprocessing steps that enhance point cloud density and accuracy, followed by segmentation using spatial clustering techniques to isolate potential curb candidates. Geometric feature descriptors are then applied to precisely detect curb edges, ensuring reduced noise and improved detection rates.

Results/Findings: The proposed method was evaluated on diverse datasets collected in various urban settings, demonstrating superior performance over existing methods. Our approach achieved a curb detection accuracy of over 95%, with significant reductions in false positives due to the enhanced preprocessing and segmentation techniques. The algorithm maintained high efficiency, processing dense point clouds in real-time, which is critical for practical deployment in dynamic urban scenarios.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a significant advancement in the field of urban mapping and autonomous navigation by providing an efficient and accurate curb detection method from mobile LiDAR data. The findings have potential applications in smart city development, autonomous vehicle systems, and infrastructure monitoring. This innovative approach paves the way for further enhancements in automated mapping technologies, facilitating improved urban planning and management.

Keywords: Mobile LiDAR, Point Clouds, Road Curb Extraction, Urban Environments, Autonomous Navigation, Geometric Feature Analysis, Segmentation.
