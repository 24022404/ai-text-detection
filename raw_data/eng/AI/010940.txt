Comprehensive testing of JRPC requests will involve measuring response times, request rates, and throughput to thoroughly evaluate system performance under different loads. This approach aims to identify potential bottlenecks, assess the impact of factors such as network latency and available system resources, and gain a deeper understanding of the system’s scalability. By analyzing how well the system manages increasing numbers of users and concurrent requests, valuable insights can be obtained regarding its capacity to deliver reliable performance in real-world scenarios.
