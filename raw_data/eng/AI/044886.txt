This paper introduces a novel approach to object representation, leveraging the concept of extreme points to characterize and identify objects within complex scenes. Our objective is to develop an efficient and robust method for object detection, bypassing the need for exhaustive feature extraction and processing. We propose a unique algorithm that utilizes extreme points as a compact and informative representation of objects, enabling accurate detection and classification. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. The key findings of this research highlight the potential of extreme points as a powerful tool for object representation, with significant implications for applications in computer vision, robotics, and autonomous systems. Our contributions include a new paradigm for object detection, improved performance, and reduced computational complexity, making this approach an attractive solution for real-time object recognition and scene understanding. Key keywords: object detection, extreme points, computer vision, robotics, autonomous systems.
