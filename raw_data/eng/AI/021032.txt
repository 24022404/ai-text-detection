Certified robustness, which provides guaranteed performance bounds against adversarial perturbations, is crucial for the reliability of deep learning models. However, existing methods often suffer from scalability issues when applied to large networks. This paper introduces an automatic perturbation analysis framework designed to efficiently compute certified robustness bounds for neural networks, significantly enhancing scalability. Our approach leverages a combination of symbolic interval analysis and abstract interpretation to automatically derive and solve perturbation propagation equations. This eliminates the need for manual derivation of robustness certificates, reducing development time and potential human error. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our framework on several benchmark datasets and network architectures, achieving state-of-the-art certified robustness with significantly improved computational efficiency compared to existing methods. Furthermore, we show that our framework can be extended beyond robustness verification to other properties of neural networks. Keywords: Certified Robustness, Adversarial Perturbations, Neural Networks, Formal Verification, Abstract Interpretation, Scalability.
