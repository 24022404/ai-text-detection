This paper presents a novel pillar-based object detection approach for autonomous driving, aiming to improve the accuracy and efficiency of 3D object detection in complex driving scenarios. Our method utilizes a combination of point cloud data and 2D image features to detect objects such as cars, pedestrians, and cyclists. We propose a pillar-based representation that divides the 3D space into vertical pillars, enabling efficient and accurate object detection. Our approach is evaluated on a large-scale autonomous driving dataset, demonstrating significant improvements in detection accuracy and speed compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. The results show that our pillar-based object detection system achieves a balance between accuracy and computational efficiency, making it suitable for real-time autonomous driving applications. This research contributes to the development of more reliable and efficient autonomous driving systems, with potential applications in the fields of computer vision, robotics, and intelligent transportation systems. Key keywords: autonomous driving, object detection, 3D reconstruction, point cloud processing, computer vision.
