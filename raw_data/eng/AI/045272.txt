This paper addresses the challenge of detecting local defects in complex systems, a critical task in ensuring system reliability and performance. Traditional defect detection methods often struggle with scalability and accuracy when applied to large-scale systems with localized anomalies. To overcome these limitations, we propose a blockwise-based detection approach that partitions the system into manageable blocks, enabling efficient and precise identification of local defects. Our method leverages advanced data segmentation techniques combined with anomaly detection algorithms to analyze each block independently, ensuring localized issues are accurately pinpointed without compromising computational efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach achieves a significant improvement in detection accuracy compared to conventional methods, particularly in scenarios with high system complexity. Furthermore, the blockwise framework exhibits superior scalability, making it suitable for real-time applications in large-scale systems. The findings of this research contribute to the development of robust defect detection systems, with potential applications in industrial automation, network monitoring, and quality assurance processes. Key innovations include the integration of blockwise partitioning with anomaly detection, offering a novel solution to the limitations of existing methods. Keywords: defect detection, blockwise partitioning, anomaly detection, system reliability, scalability.
