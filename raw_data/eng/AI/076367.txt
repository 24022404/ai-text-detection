This research addresses the critical issue of roof fall hazards in underground mining, which pose significant risks to worker safety and operational efficiency. The aim is to develop an advanced method for detecting such hazards using cutting-edge artificial intelligence techniques.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel approach utilizing convolutional neural networks (CNNs) in combination with transfer learning to enhance the detection of roof fall hazards. Our model leverages pre-trained CNN architectures to effectively recognize patterns indicative of potential collapses. This approach allows for accurate hazard detection even with limited labeled data, by transferring learned features from large, generic datasets to the specific task of mining safety.

Results/Findings: The proposed model demonstrates superior detection capabilities, achieving high accuracy and precision in identifying roof fall risks. Comparative analysis with traditional methods highlights significant improvements in detection rates and reduced false positives. Our results indicate that the use of transfer learning considerably enhances the model's ability to generalize over diverse mining environments and complex datasets.

Conclusion/Implications: This study contributes to the field of mining safety by introducing an efficient, AI-driven solution for early hazard detection. The findings underline the potential of integrating modern transfer learning techniques with CNNs to improve safety measures in mining operations. The implementation of our model can lead to proactive hazard management, thereby minimizing accident rates and ensuring safer working conditions. Keywords: roof fall hazard, convolutional neural networks, transfer learning, mining safety, hazard detection, AI in mining.
