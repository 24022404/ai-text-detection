3D human shape reconstruction from single images remains a challenging task due to the inherent ambiguity and complexity of human pose and articulation. This paper introduces DC-GNet, a novel Deep Mesh Relation Capturing Graph Convolution Network designed to improve the accuracy and robustness of 3D human shape reconstruction. DC-GNet leverages a graph-based representation of the 3D human mesh, explicitly modeling relationships between mesh vertices through multiple graph convolution layers. These layers capture both local and global dependencies within the mesh, allowing the network to effectively propagate information and refine shape predictions. A novel mesh relation capturing module is introduced to enhance feature representation. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that DC-GNet achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of reconstruction accuracy and pose estimation. The proposed approach outperforms existing methods, particularly in handling challenging poses and occlusions. The key contributions of this work are the introduction of a deep graph convolutional network architecture specifically designed for 3D human shape reconstruction and the explicit modeling of mesh relationships, leading to improved accuracy and robustness. Keywords: 3D human shape reconstruction, graph convolutional network, mesh processing, deep learning, computer vision.
