Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in various generative tasks, but their significant computational demands hinder deployment on resource-constrained devices. This paper introduces a novel online multi-granularity distillation method for GAN compression, aiming to reduce model size and computational cost without sacrificing generation quality. Our approach leverages a pre-trained, high-capacity teacher GAN to guide the training of a smaller student GAN. Unlike traditional distillation methods, we perform distillation at multiple granularities, including feature-level, sample-level, and relation-level, to transfer comprehensive knowledge from the teacher to the student in an online fashion. This simultaneous training and distillation process allows the student network to dynamically learn from the teacher's evolving knowledge. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms existing GAN compression techniques in terms of Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) and Inception Score (IS), while achieving substantial reductions in model size and inference time. The proposed method offers a practical solution for deploying high-quality GANs on edge devices and in other resource-limited environments. Keywords: GAN compression, knowledge distillation, generative models, online learning, model compression, deep learning.
