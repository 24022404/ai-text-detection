This paper introduces SIDU, a novel method for explainable AI that leverages similarity, difference, and uniqueness to provide insights into complex machine learning models. The objective of SIDU is to address the lack of transparency in AI decision-making processes by developing a technique that can effectively identify and explain the contributing factors. Our approach utilizes a combination of feature attribution and model interpretability techniques to quantify the similarity and difference between input data points and their corresponding predictions. The results show that SIDU outperforms existing explainability methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency, particularly in high-dimensional datasets. Key findings indicate that SIDU can effectively identify unique patterns and relationships in the data, leading to improved model interpretability and trustworthiness. The contributions of this research lie in its ability to provide a more comprehensive understanding of AI decision-making, enabling the development of more reliable and transparent AI systems. This work has significant implications for various applications, including healthcare, finance, and autonomous systems, where explainable AI is crucial for building trust and ensuring accountability. Keywords: Explainable AI, Model Interpretability, Feature Attribution, Machine Learning, Transparency, Trustworthiness.
