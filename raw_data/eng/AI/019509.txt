The assembly quality of components on printed circuit boards (PCBs) plays a crucial role in determining the overall performance and longevity of electronic devices. Historically, manual inspection methods have been widely used to check PCB quality, but these traditional approaches often suffer from inefficiency and a high likelihood of human error. To address these challenges, there has been a significant shift toward employing automatic, machine vision-based inspection systems. This technological advancement has driven automatic visual PCB inspection to become a prominent area of research, providing opportunities to improve production system flexibility through reliable, efficient automatic recognition and inspection methods.
