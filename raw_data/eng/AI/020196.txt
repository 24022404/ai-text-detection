Deep 3D Morphable Models (3DMMs) have shown promise in representing and analyzing 3D facial shapes. However, effectively aggregating features from different layers of deep networks to enhance 3DMM fitting and reconstruction remains a challenge. This paper introduces a novel feature aggregation module designed to learn optimal combinations of multi-level features for improved 3DMM performance. Our approach utilizes a learnable attention mechanism to adaptively weight and fuse features extracted from various depths of a convolutional neural network. This allows the model to prioritize relevant features for accurate shape prediction. We evaluate our method on challenging benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in reconstruction accuracy and robustness compared to state-of-the-art 3DMM fitting techniques. Specifically, our method reduces reconstruction error by an average of 15% across various datasets. The learned feature aggregation strategy provides a more nuanced and effective representation of 3D facial shapes, leading to enhanced performance in face recognition, expression analysis, and 3D animation. Keywords: 3D Morphable Models, 3D Reconstruction, Feature Aggregation, Deep Learning, Attention Mechanism, Facial Shape Analysis.
