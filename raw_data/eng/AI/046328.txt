Snow removal from images is a challenging task due to the complex and varying nature of snow accumulation. This paper introduces DesnowNet, a novel context-aware deep convolutional neural network designed to effectively remove snow from single images. DesnowNet leverages a multi-scale contextual attention module that captures both local and global dependencies within the image, allowing the network to better distinguish between snow and underlying scene details. Furthermore, a residual learning strategy is employed to facilitate the learning process and preserve fine-grained image features. Experiments on both synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that DesnowNet outperforms existing state-of-the-art snow removal methods in terms of quantitative metrics and qualitative visual results. The proposed network effectively removes snow while preserving image details and minimizing artifacts, thereby demonstrating its potential for practical applications in computer vision systems operating in adverse weather conditions. Keywords: Snow Removal, Image Restoration, Deep Learning, Convolutional Neural Network, Contextual Attention.
