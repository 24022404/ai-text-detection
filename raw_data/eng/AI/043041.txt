Video summarization aims to generate concise and representative summaries that capture the salient content of a video. This paper introduces a novel Query-Conditioned Three-Player Adversarial Network (QC-TPAN) for improved video summarization, addressing the limitations of existing unsupervised approaches. The QC-TPAN consists of three players: a summarizer, a discriminator, and a query generator. The summarizer selects key frames, the discriminator distinguishes between real and summarized videos, and the query generator produces relevant queries that guide the summarizer toward generating summaries aligned with user interests. This query-conditioning mechanism enables the summarizer to learn contextual representations and generate summaries that are both comprehensive and query-relevant. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the QC-TPAN outperforms state-of-the-art video summarization methods in terms of both summary quality and user relevance. The proposed approach demonstrates significant improvements in capturing essential video content and adapting summaries to specific user interests, thus offering a more effective and personalized video summarization solution. Keywords: video summarization, adversarial networks, query-conditioned learning, deep learning, video analysis.
