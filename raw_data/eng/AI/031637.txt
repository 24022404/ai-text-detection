Highlight detection in figure skating videos is crucial for automated analysis and summarization, yet existing methods often overlook the impact of blinks on viewer engagement. This paper introduces a novel approach to estimate blink probability and integrate it into a highlight detection framework. We employ a deep learning model, fine-tuned on a dataset of figure skating videos, to predict blink occurrences based on facial landmarks and contextual information. The estimated blink probability is then used as a negative indicator, reducing the likelihood of selecting segments containing frequent blinks as highlights. Experimental results demonstrate that incorporating blink probability significantly improves the accuracy and perceived quality of highlight detection, outperforming baseline methods that solely rely on audio-visual features. This research contributes a valuable feature for automated sports video analysis and provides insights into the relationship between viewer attention and blinking behavior. Keywords: Figure skating, highlight detection, blink detection, deep learning, video analysis, attention modeling.
