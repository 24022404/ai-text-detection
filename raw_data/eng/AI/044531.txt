Active exploration is a critical component in autonomous systems such as robotics and artificial intelligence, where efficient information collection about the environment aids decision-making and task execution. This paper addresses the challenge of developing a robust model-based framework for active exploration that optimizes data acquisition and navigational efficiency.

Methods: We introduce a novel model-based approach that leverages probabilistic modeling and reinforcement learning to guide exploration strategies. The framework incorporates a predictive model of the environment, continuously updated with sensory data, to evaluate potential actions and their expected informative value. We have implemented this framework using a combination of Gaussian processes for environmental mapping and a policy-gradient algorithm to optimize the exploration policy.

Results: Through extensive simulations and real-world experiments, our method demonstrates significant improvements in exploration efficiency compared to baseline model-free techniques. Specifically, our approach achieves a reduction in exploration time while maintaining high accuracy in environmental understanding. Key benchmarks include performance metrics in diverse exploration scenarios, revealing the model's adaptability and generalization capabilities.

Conclusion: The proposed model-based active exploration framework offers substantial advancements in autonomous exploration tasks. It effectively balances exploration-exploitation trade-offs, providing a strategic, data-efficient solution applicable to various domains, including robotic navigation, autonomous vehicles, and environmental monitoring. The adoption of this model promises improved decision-making and task performance in complex and dynamic environments, thereby contributing to the broader field of intelligent systems.

Keywords: Active exploration, model-based approach, autonomous systems, reinforcement learning, probabilistic modeling, Gaussian processes, policy-gradient algorithm.
