This paper introduces TransGAN, a novel approach to building robust Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) using two pure transformers. The objective is to challenge the conventional wisdom that GANs require complex architectures, such as convolutional neural networks (CNNs), to generate high-quality images. Our method leverages the strengths of transformer models, typically used in natural language processing, to create a scalable and efficient GAN framework. By employing two transformers as the generator and discriminator, we demonstrate that TransGAN can produce highly realistic images while maintaining a simple and intuitive architecture. Experimental results show that TransGAN achieves competitive performance with state-of-the-art GAN models, with significant improvements in training stability and scalability. The key findings highlight the potential of transformer-based GANs for large-scale image generation tasks, enabling applications in areas such as computer vision, AI-generated content, and data augmentation. Our research contributes to the advancement of GAN architectures, emphasizing the importance of simplicity, scalability, and innovation in deep learning models, with relevant keywords including GANs, transformers, image generation, and deep learning.
