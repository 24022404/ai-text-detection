The rapid evolution of cellular networks necessitates innovative solutions to integrate sensing and communication functionalities efficiently. This paper explores the integration of sensing and communication within cellular networks using the New Radio (NR) sidelink technology. The objective is to enhance network performance by enabling simultaneous sensing and communication capabilities, thereby addressing the growing demand for low-latency, high-reliability applications such as autonomous vehicles and industrial IoT. We propose a novel framework that leverages NR sidelink for device-to-device communication, enabling seamless coordination between sensing and data transmission. The approach involves optimizing resource allocation and interference management to ensure robust performance in dynamic environments. Through extensive simulations, we demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves significant improvements in throughput, latency, and sensing accuracy compared to traditional methods. Key findings indicate a 25% reduction in latency and a 30% improvement in sensing precision under high-density network conditions. The results highlight the potential of NR sidelink as a foundational technology for future integrated sensing and communication systems. This research contributes to the advancement of 5G and beyond networks, offering practical insights for deploying efficient, dual-functional networks in real-world scenarios.  

**Keywords**: NR sidelink, sensing and communication, 5G networks, device-to-device communication, resource allocation, interference management.
