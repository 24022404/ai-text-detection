Time series classification (TSC) often defaults to either feature-based or shape-based approaches, creating a perceived dichotomy. This paper challenges this separation, arguing that effective TSC leverages both feature extraction and shape analysis. We introduce a novel hybrid framework that integrates a learned feature extraction module with a shapelet-based classification component. Our method uses convolutional neural networks to extract hierarchical features from time series data, which are then used to inform the selection and weighting of relevant shapelets. This allows the model to adaptively prioritize features and shape characteristics based on the specific dataset. Experiments on a diverse set of benchmark datasets demonstrate that our hybrid approach consistently outperforms state-of-the-art feature-based and shape-based methods when considered in isolation. Furthermore, we provide an ablation study that highlights the complementary roles of feature extraction and shape analysis in achieving superior classification accuracy. The findings suggest that a more holistic perspective, combining both features and shape, is crucial for advancing the field of TSC.

Keywords: Time Series Classification, Shapelets, Feature Extraction, Convolutional Neural Networks, Hybrid Methods, Machine Learning.
