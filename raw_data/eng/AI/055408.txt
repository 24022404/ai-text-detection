Hypergraph learning offers a powerful approach to model complex relationships beyond pairwise interactions captured by traditional graphs. However, effectively learning from hypergraphs remains a challenge. This paper introduces a novel hypergraph learning framework based on line expansion. We transform the original hypergraph into a line graph representation, where nodes represent hyperedges, and edges represent the intersection between hyperedges. This transformation allows us to leverage established graph neural network (GNN) techniques for hypergraph analysis. We propose a Line Expansion Graph Neural Network (LEGNN) model that operates on the line graph, learning representations that capture the hyperedge connectivity patterns of the original hypergraph. LEGNN incorporates an attention mechanism to weigh the importance of different intersecting hyperedges during message passing. Experiments on several benchmark hypergraph datasets demonstrate that LEGNN consistently outperforms existing hypergraph learning methods in node classification and hyperedge prediction tasks. The results show the effectiveness of the line expansion strategy and the benefits of leveraging GNNs for capturing high-order relationships in hypergraphs. Our approach provides a scalable and effective method for hypergraph learning, with implications for various domains including social network analysis, bioinformatics, and computer vision.

Keywords: Hypergraph Learning, Line Graph, Graph Neural Networks, Node Classification, Hyperedge Prediction, Attention Mechanism.
