This paper addresses the challenge of enhancing the robustness of Conditional Generative Adversarial Networks (cGANs), which are widely used for generating data conditioned on specific inputs. Traditional cGANs can be sensitive to input variations and adversarial perturbations, affecting their reliability in practical applications.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel approach for improving cGAN robustness by integrating a multi-layered adversarial training method. Our model incorporates a dual-discriminator framework aimed at enforcing stringent conditions on both global and local data distribution, thereby reducing susceptibility to adversarial attacks. Additionally, we introduce a regularization technique that enhances network stability and generalization.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in the robustness of the proposed cGAN model compared to standard cGAN architectures. The model exhibited up to a 30% increase in resistance to adversarial inputs across multiple datasets, including image and text, while maintaining high fidelity in generated outputs. Performance evaluations indicate an improved balance between accuracy and robustness, surpassing existing models in several benchmark tests.

Conclusion/Implications: Our research contributes a robust framework for conditional data generation, providing enhanced security and reliability for applications in fields such as image editing, data augmentation, and anomaly detection. The dual-discriminator and regularization strategies introduced offer a pathway for further adaptations in enhancing AI model resilience against adversarial conditions. Keywords: conditional generative adversarial networks, robustness, adversarial training, dual-discriminator, regularization, data generation.
