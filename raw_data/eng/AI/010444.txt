The objective of this research is to address the challenge of effective initialization in variational Bayes frameworks for deep models, an area critical for achieving optimal convergence and model performance. Traditional initialization techniques for variational inference often lead to poor performance or slow convergence in deep learning models due to the complex landscape of the parameter space. In this study, we propose a novel initialization strategy designed to enhance the performance of variational Bayes when applied to deep neural networks. Our method leverages a systematic approach to setting initial parameters, utilizing statistical principles to ensure better coverage and exploration of the parameter space. We evaluate our approach on a range of benchmark datasets and deep model architectures, demonstrating significant improvements in convergence speed, model robustness, and predictive accuracy compared to conventional initialization methods. The findings underscore the importance of initialization choices in variational inference for deep learning, offering a pathway to more efficient training processes. This research contributes to the field by providing a scalable and adaptable initialization technique that can be integrated into existing deep model frameworks, facilitating advancements in areas such as machine learning, probabilistic modeling, and artificial intelligence. Keywords include variational Bayes, deep learning, initialization strategy, model convergence, and probabilistic modeling.
