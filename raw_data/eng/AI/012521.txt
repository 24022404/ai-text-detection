Cervical spondylosis, a prevalent degenerative condition affecting the cervical spine, poses significant diagnostic challenges. This paper presents EasiCSDeep, an innovative deep learning-based approach for the identification of cervical spondylosis utilizing surface electromyography (sEMG) signals. The primary objective is to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of diagnosing cervical spondylosis through non-invasive means. Our approach involves the development of a robust deep neural network specifically designed to analyze sEMG data, capturing subtle changes in muscle activity associated with cervical spondylosis. The model was trained and validated using a comprehensive dataset of sEMG signals recorded from both affected individuals and healthy subjects.

The results demonstrate that EasiCSDeep achieves a high classification accuracy, significantly outperforming traditional diagnostic methods and existing models. Specifically, it offers improved sensitivity and specificity, ensuring reliable detection rates. Our findings also highlight the model’s ability to generalize over diverse patient demographics, making it applicable in various clinical settings.

In conclusion, EasiCSDeep represents a significant advancement in the field of medical diagnostics, offering a non-invasive, efficient, and accurate tool for cervical spondylosis detection. This research not only contributes to the automated diagnosis of musculoskeletal disorders but also lays the groundwork for future studies integrating deep learning with sEMG analysis. Potential applications extend to clinical diagnostics, patient monitoring, and personalized treatment planning. Keywords relevant to this study include cervical spondylosis, deep learning, surface electromyography, diagnostic model, and non-invasive diagnosis.
