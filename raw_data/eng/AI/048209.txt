This paper introduces a novel fuzzy time series forecasting method designed to improve prediction accuracy. The proposed approach integrates interval index numbers and membership values within a support vector machine (SVM) framework. By representing historical data as intervals and incorporating membership values to reflect the degree of belonging to each interval, the method captures the inherent uncertainty and vagueness present in time series data. The SVM then leverages this enriched representation to learn complex patterns and relationships. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed method achieves superior forecasting accuracy compared to traditional fuzzy time series models and standalone SVM approaches. The method shows improvements in root mean squared error and mean absolute percentage error. This research contributes a practical and effective forecasting tool with potential applications in finance, economics, and other domains where accurate time series prediction is critical. Keywords: Fuzzy Time Series, Forecasting, Interval Index Number, Membership Value, Support Vector Machine, SVM.
