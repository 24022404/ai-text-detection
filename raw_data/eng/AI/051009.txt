This paper addresses the challenge of partitioning data points into meaningful clusters by introducing a novel large-margin metric learning approach. Our method aims to learn a distance metric that maximizes the margin between different clusters while minimizing the intra-cluster variance, leading to improved partitioning accuracy. We formulate the metric learning problem as a semidefinite program with constraints that enforce large margins between clusters. We propose an efficient optimization algorithm based on alternating direction method of multipliers (ADMM) to solve the resulting optimization problem. Empirical evaluation on several benchmark datasets demonstrates that our large-margin metric learning approach outperforms state-of-the-art metric learning methods and clustering algorithms in terms of clustering accuracy and normalized mutual information. The proposed method offers a robust and effective solution for various partitioning problems, including image segmentation and document clustering, by learning a discriminative distance metric that enhances cluster separability.

**Keywords:** Metric learning, clustering, partitioning, large margin, semidefinite programming, ADMM.
