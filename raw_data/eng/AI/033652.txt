This paper proposes a novel Dual-path Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) architecture with a Max Gated block for Text-Based Person Re-identification. The objective is to improve the accuracy of person re-identification using text descriptions, addressing the challenge of varying appearances and viewpoints. Our approach combines a visual path and a textual path, leveraging the strengths of both modalities. The Max Gated block is introduced to selectively focus on relevant textual features, enhancing the model's ability to capture discriminative information. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, outperforming state-of-the-art approaches in terms of re-identification accuracy and robustness. The proposed architecture achieves a significant improvement in handling text-based person re-identification, with potential applications in surveillance, security, and multimedia analysis. Key contributions include the innovative Dual-path CNN design and the Max Gated block, which can be applied to various multi-modal learning tasks. Relevant keywords: Person Re-identification, Text-Based, Dual-path CNN, Max Gated block, Multi-modal Learning, Deep Learning.
