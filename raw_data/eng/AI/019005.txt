This paper introduces a novel approach for automatically learning part boundaries from 3D point clouds. The objective is to develop a robust method for segmenting 3D shapes into meaningful parts without relying on manual annotations. Our approach employs a deep learning framework that directly learns boundary features from raw point cloud data. Specifically, we propose a point cloud network architecture that integrates local and global context information to predict boundary probabilities for each point. We train our network on a large dataset of 3D shapes with part annotations using a combination of geometric and contextual loss functions. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in part boundary detection and segmentation. Furthermore, we show the robustness of our approach to noisy and incomplete point clouds. The learned part boundaries can be used for various applications, including shape analysis, 3D modeling, and object recognition. Keywords: 3D Point Clouds, Part Segmentation, Deep Learning, Boundary Detection, Shape Analysis.
