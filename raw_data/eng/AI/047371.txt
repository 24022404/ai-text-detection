Traffic sign detection is a crucial component of autonomous driving and advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS). Existing methods often struggle with variations in scale, illumination, and occlusion, leading to decreased performance. This paper introduces VSSA-NET, a novel Vertical Spatial Sequence Attention Network designed to enhance traffic sign detection accuracy. VSSA-NET employs a vertical spatial attention mechanism to capture long-range dependencies within the vertical dimensions of traffic signs, effectively modeling the sequential relationships between different parts of the sign. This allows the network to focus on relevant features and suppress noise. The architecture incorporates a lightweight backbone for efficient feature extraction, followed by a spatial sequence attention module and a detection head. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, including the German Traffic Sign Detection Benchmark (GTSDB) and the Chinese Traffic Sign Detection Benchmark (CCTSDB), demonstrate that VSSA-NET achieves state-of-the-art performance with a significant improvement in detection accuracy compared to existing methods, particularly in challenging conditions. The proposed network offers a robust and efficient solution for traffic sign detection, contributing to safer and more reliable autonomous driving systems. Keywords: Traffic Sign Detection, Attention Mechanism, Spatial Sequence, Deep Learning, Autonomous Driving.
