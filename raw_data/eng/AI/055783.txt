This paper presents a novel approach to road detection using LIDAR-camera fusion, leveraging fully convolutional neural networks (FCNNs) to enhance accuracy and robustness. The objective is to develop a reliable and efficient road detection system, addressing the challenges of varying lighting conditions, complex road geometries, and sensor noise. Our method combines LIDAR point cloud data with camera images, utilizing a FCNN architecture to fuse these multimodal inputs and produce a precise road mask. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in road detection accuracy, outperforming state-of-the-art methods. The proposed system achieves a high accuracy rate, with a mean intersection over union (IoU) of 0.85, and exhibits robustness to various environmental conditions. This research contributes to the development of autonomous vehicles and advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS), highlighting the potential of LIDAR-camera fusion and FCNNs for road detection. Key aspects of this work include multimodal sensor fusion, deep learning, and computer vision, with applications in autonomous driving, robotics, and intelligent transportation systems.
