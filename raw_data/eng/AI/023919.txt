Abstract:

Accurate vehicle trajectory prediction in crowded highway scenarios is critical for the safety and efficiency of autonomous driving systems and advanced driver-assistance technologies. This paper presents a novel approach that leverages bird’s eye view (BEV) representations combined with convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to predict multi-vehicle trajectories in dense traffic environments. The proposed method encodes real-time traffic information and the spatial relationships among surrounding vehicles into BEV maps, which are processed by a custom-designed CNN architecture to capture both local interactions and global traffic patterns. Extensive experiments were conducted using publicly available highway traffic datasets, evaluating the model's performance against existing recurrent neural network (RNN) and social pooling-based methods. The results demonstrate significant improvements in prediction accuracy, particularly in complex, high-density situations where conventional models often struggle. Ablation studies further highlight the effectiveness of BEV encoding in enhancing model robustness to varying traffic layouts and occlusion conditions. This research contributes a scalable, interpretable, and real-time capable trajectory prediction framework, with the potential to advance automated driving, traffic control, and safety applications. Key keywords: vehicle trajectory prediction, bird’s eye view, convolutional neural networks, crowded highway, autonomous driving, traffic modeling.
