Accurate localization in aerial imagery is a critical challenge in applications such as autonomous navigation, urban planning, and disaster response. This paper introduces LocGAN, a novel generative adversarial network (GAN)-based framework designed to enhance localization accuracy using grid maps derived from aerial imagery. The primary objective of this research is to address the limitations of existing methods, which often struggle with scalability and precision in complex environments. LocGAN leverages a dual-network architecture, combining a generator to synthesize high-resolution grid maps and a discriminator to refine localization predictions. The proposed approach integrates spatial and contextual features from aerial images, enabling robust localization even in densely populated or occluded areas. Experimental results demonstrate that LocGAN outperforms traditional methods, achieving a 15% improvement in localization accuracy across diverse datasets. Furthermore, the framework exhibits superior generalization capabilities, maintaining high performance in varying environmental conditions. The findings highlight the potential of LocGAN for real-world applications, offering a scalable and efficient solution for aerial imagery localization. This research contributes to the advancement of GAN-based techniques in geospatial analysis and provides a foundation for future developments in autonomous systems and remote sensing technologies.  

**Keywords**: Localization, Aerial Imagery, Grid Maps, Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), LocGAN, Autonomous Systems, Remote Sensing.
