This paper introduces NukeBERT, a novel pre-trained language model tailored for the low-resource nuclear domain. Addressing the challenges of limited labeled data and domain-specific vocabulary, NukeBERT is pre-trained on a comprehensive corpus of nuclear-related texts, including technical reports, research publications, and regulatory documents. We adapt the BERT architecture and employ a masked language modeling objective to learn contextualized representations of nuclear terminology. Experimental results demonstrate that NukeBERT significantly outperforms general-purpose language models and existing domain-specific models on various nuclear-related tasks, including text classification, named entity recognition, and question answering. The findings highlight the effectiveness of targeted pre-training for improving performance in specialized domains with scarce resources. NukeBERT offers a valuable tool for researchers and practitioners in the nuclear field, enabling more accurate and efficient natural language processing applications. Keywords: Nuclear Energy, Language Model, BERT, Pre-training, Low-Resource, Natural Language Processing.
