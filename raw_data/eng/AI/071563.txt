This paper presents a novel approach to single-view 3D object reconstruction, leveraging unsupervised learning from multiple images to achieve realistic and accurate results. The objective is to overcome the limitations of traditional methods, which often rely on extensive labeled datasets or complex manual processing. Our approach utilizes a deep learning framework, incorporating a generative model that learns to reconstruct 3D objects from unstructured image collections. The method involves training a neural network to predict 3D shapes from 2D images, without requiring explicit 3D supervision. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving state-of-the-art performance in reconstructing complex objects with detailed textures and structures. The key findings highlight the importance of multi-image learning and unsupervised training in improving the accuracy and robustness of 3D reconstruction. This research contributes to the development of realistic 3D object reconstruction, with potential applications in computer vision, robotics, and virtual reality. Key keywords: 3D reconstruction, unsupervised learning, single-view reconstruction, deep learning, generative models.
