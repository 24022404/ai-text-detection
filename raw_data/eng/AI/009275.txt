**Abstract:**

Face attribute editing has emerged as a significant task in computer vision, enabling precise modification of specific facial features while preserving identity and high-resolution details. This paper introduces MagGAN, a Mask-Guided Generative Adversarial Network designed for high-resolution face attribute editing. The proposed approach incorporates a novel mask-guided mechanism that facilitates localized attribute manipulation by generating and leveraging spatial masks, ensuring that modifications remain focused on target attributes without affecting other facial regions. MagGAN employs a multi-scale architecture alongside advanced adversarial and perceptual loss functions to enhance image realism and attribute consistency. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that MagGAN outperforms existing methods in both qualitative and quantitative evaluations, achieving superior results in terms of image fidelity, attribute accuracy, and the preservation of identity and background information. The model efficiently edits a wide range of facial attributes, such as age, expression, and hairstyle, supporting high-resolution outputs up to 1024×1024 pixels. Our findings highlight the effectiveness and versatility of mask-guided editing in complex face manipulation tasks, with potential applications in entertainment, forensics, digital avatars, and personalized media content. Key keywords: face attribute editing, generative adversarial network, mask guidance, high-resolution image synthesis, MagGAN, facial manipulation.
