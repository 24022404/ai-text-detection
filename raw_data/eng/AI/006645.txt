This paper introduces MASTER, a novel Multi-Aspect Non-local Network designed to advance the field of Scene Text Recognition. The objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of text recognition in complex scenes by effectively capturing long-range dependencies and multi-scale features. MASTER employs a non-local neural network architecture that incorporates multiple aspects of text representation, including visual, semantic, and spatial information. The approach leverages a combination of convolutional neural networks and attention mechanisms to learn contextual relationships between characters and words. Experimental results demonstrate that MASTER outperforms state-of-the-art methods on several benchmark datasets, achieving significant improvements in recognition accuracy and efficiency. The key contributions of this research include the innovative non-local network design and the integration of multi-aspect features, which enhance the model's ability to recognize text in challenging scenes. MASTER has potential applications in various areas, such as image and video analysis, autonomous vehicles, and smart city systems. Key keywords: Scene Text Recognition, Non-local Network, Multi-Aspect Features, Deep Learning, Computer Vision.
