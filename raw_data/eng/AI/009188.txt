The objective of this research is to address the challenge of developing robust control policies for autonomous aerial vehicles, specifically focusing on integrating machine learning with model predictive control (MPC) to enhance navigation and operation in dynamic environments. We propose a novel approach, the MPC-Guided Policy Search, which leverages deep reinforcement learning to learn control policies guided by MPC strategies. This approach ensures that learned policies can effectively generalize across various scenarios while maintaining stability and efficiency. Our method involves training deep neural networks to approximate optimal control actions, with the MPC serving as a supervisory signal that enforces feasibility and reduces exploration complexity. The experimental evaluations demonstrate significant improvements in trajectory tracking and obstacle avoidance compared to traditional MPC and reinforcement learning techniques alone. Key findings include enhanced system performance with reduced computation time and increased adaptability to real-world uncertainties. The research insights contribute to the field by presenting a hybrid control strategy that combines the decision-theoretic strengths of MPC with the adaptability of learning-based methods. Potential applications of this work extend to various sectors requiring autonomous navigation, such as delivery drones and surveillance systems. Keywords include autonomous aerial vehicles, Model Predictive Control, deep reinforcement learning, policy search, and control strategies.
