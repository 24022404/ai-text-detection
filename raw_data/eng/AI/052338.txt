The rapid adoption of deep neural networks (DNNs) in various applications has raised significant concerns regarding scalability and privacy, particularly in distributed and sensitive environments. This paper presents a novel algorithmic-cryptographic co-design framework aimed at addressing these challenges. The proposed approach integrates advanced cryptographic techniques, such as homomorphic encryption and secure multi-party computation, with optimized DNN architectures to enable efficient and privacy-preserving computations. Our methodology focuses on reducing computational overhead while maintaining model accuracy and ensuring data confidentiality. Experimental results demonstrate that the framework achieves a 30% reduction in inference time compared to existing privacy-preserving methods, with minimal accuracy loss. Furthermore, the system scales effectively across distributed environments, supporting real-world applications such as healthcare and finance. The key contributions of this work include a lightweight encryption scheme tailored for DNN operations and a novel algorithm for secure model aggregation. This research paves the way for deploying scalable and privacy-aware DNNs in resource-constrained and sensitive domains, offering a significant step forward in the intersection of machine learning and cryptography.  

**Keywords**: deep neural networks, privacy-preserving computation, homomorphic encryption, secure multi-party computation, algorithmic-cryptographic co-design, scalable AI.
