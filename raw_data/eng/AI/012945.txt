This paper addresses the challenge of ensuring reliability in transfer learning, a crucial aspect of machine learning that enables the adaptation of pre-trained models to new, unseen tasks. Our objective is to develop a framework that enhances the robustness and accuracy of transfer learning across diverse domains. We propose a novel approach that combines feature extraction, domain adaptation, and uncertainty estimation to improve the reliability of transferred models. Our method utilizes a hybrid architecture that integrates the strengths of both shallow and deep learning techniques, allowing for more effective knowledge transfer and adaptation. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art transfer learning methods, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and robustness on several benchmark datasets. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of uncertainty-aware transfer learning and provide valuable insights into the development of more reliable and generalizable machine learning models. Our contributions have significant implications for real-world applications, including computer vision, natural language processing, and autonomous systems, where reliable transfer learning is critical for achieving high performance and safety standards. Key keywords: transfer learning, reliability, uncertainty estimation, domain adaptation, machine learning, deep learning.
