This paper addresses the critical issue of dataset contamination in visual tracking, where noisy or irrelevant data can significantly degrade tracker performance. Our objective is to develop a robust and adaptive decontamination approach to enhance the quality of the training set, thereby improving the accuracy and reliability of discriminative visual trackers. We propose a unified formulation that integrates online learning, data cleansing, and robust optimization to effectively identify and remove contaminated data. Our method leverages a novel probabilistic framework to model data uncertainty and adaptively updates the training set to minimize the impact of noise and outliers. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art tracking methods, achieving significant improvements in tracking accuracy and robustness. The key contributions of this research include a flexible and efficient decontamination algorithm, a robust tracker formulation, and a comprehensive evaluation framework. Our work has important implications for various computer vision applications, including object tracking, surveillance, and autonomous systems, and highlights the importance of data quality and adaptability in visual tracking. Key keywords: visual tracking, dataset decontamination, robust optimization, online learning, computer vision.
