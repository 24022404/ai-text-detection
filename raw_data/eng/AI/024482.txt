This paper introduces a novel on-the-fly label learning framework for training robust image classification models. The approach addresses the challenge of noisy labels and limited labeled data by simultaneously learning and refining image labels during the training process. The proposed method leverages a combination of a classification network and a label correction module. Specifically, the classification network predicts class probabilities, while the label correction module estimates the reliability of existing labels and dynamically adjusts them based on the network's predictions and a consistency loss. This allows the model to iteratively improve its understanding of the data and correct erroneous labels, leading to more accurate and robust classification performance. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed framework outperforms state-of-the-art methods for training with noisy labels, achieving significant improvements in classification accuracy and robustness against label noise. Furthermore, the on-the-fly nature of the learning process makes it adaptable to new data and changing label distributions without requiring retraining from scratch. The research contributes a practical and effective solution for training high-quality classification models in real-world scenarios with imperfect or limited annotations. Keywords: image classification, noisy labels, label correction, robust learning, on-the-fly learning.
