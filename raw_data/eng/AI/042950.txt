This paper introduces MHER (Model-based Hindsight Experience Replay), a novel reinforcement learning framework designed to enhance sample efficiency and performance in sparse-reward environments. Traditional Hindsight Experience Replay (HER) methods rely on goal relabeling to improve learning in such settings but often suffer from inefficiencies due to limited exploration. To address this, MHER integrates a learned dynamics model with HER, enabling the agent to simulate and learn from synthetic trajectories. The proposed approach combines model-based planning with hindsight relabeling, allowing the agent to generate additional training data and refine its policy more effectively. Experiments conducted on benchmark robotic manipulation tasks demonstrate that MHER significantly outperforms standard HER and other baseline methods in terms of success rates and sample efficiency. The results highlight the framework's ability to leverage both real and simulated experiences to accelerate learning. MHER's contributions include a scalable and generalizable solution for sparse-reward problems, with potential applications in robotics, autonomous systems, and other domains requiring efficient reinforcement learning. Key innovations include the integration of model-based planning with hindsight relabeling and the generation of synthetic trajectories to augment training data.  

**Keywords**: Reinforcement Learning, Hindsight Experience Replay, Model-based Learning, Sparse Rewards, Robotics, Sample Efficiency.
