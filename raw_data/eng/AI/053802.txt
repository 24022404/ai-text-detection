This paper introduces a novel multi-view matching network for accurate 6D object pose estimation from RGB images. Existing methods often struggle with occlusions and texture-less objects, limiting their performance in complex scenes. To address these limitations, our approach leverages information from multiple viewpoints to establish robust correspondences between the observed image and a 3D object model. The proposed network architecture integrates a view selection module to identify the most informative viewpoints and a multi-view feature fusion module to aggregate features across different views, enabling a comprehensive understanding of the object's geometry and appearance. Extensive experiments on challenging benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in 6D pose estimation, significantly outperforming existing single-view approaches, particularly in handling occlusions and objects with weak textures. The results showcase the effectiveness of the proposed multi-view matching strategy and highlight its potential for applications in robotics, augmented reality, and autonomous navigation.

**Keywords:** 6D pose estimation, multi-view matching, deep learning, computer vision, object recognition.
