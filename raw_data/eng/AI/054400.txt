Image-to-image translation aims to map images from one domain to another without paired training data. This paper introduces U-GAT-IT, an unsupervised generative attentional network designed to improve image translation quality and robustness. U-GAT-IT incorporates a novel attention module that guides the model to focus on the most relevant regions of the input image, enhancing the translation process. Furthermore, we propose Adaptive Layer-Instance Normalization (ALIN), which dynamically adjusts normalization parameters to better handle diverse image styles and content. ALIN combines the strengths of both layer and instance normalization, adapting to dataset characteristics. Experimental results on various benchmark datasets demonstrate that U-GAT-IT outperforms state-of-the-art unsupervised image-to-image translation methods, generating higher-quality and more realistic translated images. The attention mechanism and adaptive normalization significantly improve the model's ability to handle large domain gaps and complex transformations. The proposed approach contributes to the advancement of unsupervised image translation, offering a robust framework for various applications. Keywords: Image-to-Image Translation, Unsupervised Learning, Generative Adversarial Networks, Attention Mechanism, Normalization, U-GAT-IT.
