This paper presents a novel approach to enhance the accuracy of nanopore base calling through dynamic pooling. The objective is to address the challenges posed by the inherent noise and variability in nanopore sequencing data, which can lead to errors in base calling. Our method employs a dynamic pooling strategy that adaptively adjusts the size and composition of the pooling window based on the local characteristics of the sequencing data. This approach is combined with a deep learning model to improve the accuracy of base calling. The results show that our dynamic pooling method outperforms existing base calling algorithms, achieving a significant reduction in error rates. The improved accuracy of nanopore base calling has important implications for a range of applications, including genomic research, diagnostics, and personalized medicine. Key contributions of this research include the development of a novel dynamic pooling strategy and its integration with deep learning techniques to enhance nanopore base calling accuracy. Relevant keywords: nanopore sequencing, base calling, dynamic pooling, deep learning, genomic research.
