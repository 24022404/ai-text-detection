This paper presents FastMoE, a high-performance training system designed to accelerate Mixture-of-Expert (MoE) models commonly used in large-scale deep learning applications. MoE architectures leverage the expertise of multiple sub-networks, or “experts,” enabling superior model capacity and efficiency, but often suffer from significant computation and communication overhead during distributed training. FastMoE addresses these challenges by introducing optimized scheduling algorithms, efficient memory management strategies, and streamlined expert routing mechanisms tailored for modern hardware architectures, such as GPUs and multi-node clusters. Experimental results demonstrate that FastMoE achieves substantial speed-ups—up to 2.5× faster training times—compared to conventional MoE implementations, while maintaining or improving model accuracy on standard benchmarks. Further, FastMoE provides seamless integration with popular deep learning frameworks, enabling easy adoption for both research and production environments. The proposed system advances the scalability and practicality of MoE-based AI models, paving the way for more efficient development of large-scale, expert-driven neural networks. Keywords: Mixture-of-Expert, FastMoE, distributed training, deep learning, scalability, system optimization, neural networks.
