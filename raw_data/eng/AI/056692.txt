The objective of this research is to develop a novel framework for assessing the robustness of Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) through a probabilistic lens using the Probably Approximately Correct (PAC) learning model. The challenge of ensuring robustness in DNNs is critical due to their deployment in safety-critical applications, where adversarial examples can significantly impact performance. Our approach leverages PAC learning theory to establish probabilistic guarantees about the robustness of DNN models. By integrating probabilistic techniques with robustness analysis, we create a framework that quantifies the likelihood of model misclassification in the presence of adversarial perturbations. The results demonstrate our framework's effectiveness in providing tighter robustness bounds compared to traditional deterministic approaches. Empirical evaluations on standard benchmarks reveal that our method maintains high predictive accuracy while delivering strong probabilistic confidence levels in adversarial scenarios. This work contributes to the field by offering an innovative approach for robustness assurance in DNNs, highlighting its potential to improve the reliability of AI systems in real-world applications. Key innovations include the application of PAC learning principles to robustness analysis and the provision of a scalable solution adaptable to diverse DNN architectures. Keywords: Deep Neural Networks, robustness analysis, PAC learning, adversarial robustness, probabilistic guarantees.
