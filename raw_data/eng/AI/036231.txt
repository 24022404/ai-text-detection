This paper delves into the challenge of robust object detection from unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), addressing the nuances of varying lighting conditions, angles, and backgrounds. Our objective is to develop a deep learning approach that disentangles nuisance factors, enhancing detection accuracy and reliability. We propose a novel framework that integrates a disentanglement module into a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, allowing for the separation of object representations from nuisance factors. Our approach leverages adversarial training to learn domain-invariant features, resulting in improved robustness to various environmental conditions. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our method, outperforming state-of-the-art object detection algorithms in UAV-based applications. The key findings highlight the importance of disentangling nuisance factors in achieving robust object detection, with significant implications for surveillance, monitoring, and inspection tasks. Our research contributes to the advancement of UAV-based computer vision, emphasizing the potential of deep nuisance disentanglement for enhancing object detection in complex and dynamic environments, with relevant applications in fields like aerial robotics, autonomous systems, and remote sensing, leveraging technologies such as CNNs, domain adaptation, and adversarial learning.
