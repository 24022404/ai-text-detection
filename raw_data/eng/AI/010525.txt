This paper addresses the challenge of manga colorization by proposing a novel approach that integrates painting style awareness into the process using Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs). Traditional manga colorization methods often overlook the artistic nuances and stylistic preferences inherent in manga artwork, leading to results that lack aesthetic coherence. To overcome this limitation, we introduce a GAN-based framework that learns and adapts to diverse painting styles, enabling the generation of colorized manga images that align with specific artistic conventions. Our method incorporates a style-aware loss function and a multi-scale discriminator to ensure high-quality, style-consistent outputs. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach outperforms existing methods in terms of visual quality, style fidelity, and user satisfaction. The system achieves significant improvements in preserving fine details and maintaining the artistic integrity of the original manga. This research contributes to the field of image processing by providing a robust, style-aware solution for manga colorization, with potential applications in digital art, animation, and content creation.  

**Keywords**: Manga colorization, Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), painting style awareness, image processing, artistic style transfer.
