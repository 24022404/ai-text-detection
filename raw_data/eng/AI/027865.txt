Graphical models are powerful tools for representing and analyzing complex relationships among variables in various domains, including bioinformatics, social networks, and machine learning. This paper introduces *BDgraph*, an R package designed for Bayesian structure learning in graphical models. The primary objective of *BDgraph* is to provide a robust and efficient framework for estimating the structure of undirected graphs, particularly Gaussian graphical models (GGMs) and discrete Bayesian networks, using Bayesian inference. The package implements advanced Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) algorithms, including birth-death MCMC and reversible jump MCMC, to explore the space of possible graph structures and estimate posterior probabilities. A key feature of *BDgraph* is its ability to handle high-dimensional data, making it suitable for applications where the number of variables exceeds the number of observations. The package also supports parallel computing to enhance computational efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate that *BDgraph* outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy and scalability, particularly in sparse graph settings. The paper concludes by discussing the practical implications of *BDgraph* for researchers and practitioners, highlighting its potential applications in network analysis, causal inference, and predictive modeling. *BDgraph* is open-source and available on CRAN, ensuring accessibility for the broader scientific community.  

**Keywords**: Bayesian inference, graphical models, Gaussian graphical models, structure learning, R package, high-dimensional data, MCMC algorithms.
