This paper presents Mini-DDSM, a novel approach to automatic age estimation from mammography images. The objective is to develop a reliable and efficient method for estimating a woman's age based on her mammogram, which can aid in personalized breast cancer risk assessment and screening. Our approach utilizes a deep learning-based framework, leveraging a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture to extract relevant features from the images. The Mini-DDSM model is trained and validated on a large dataset of mammography images, achieving high accuracy and outperforming existing methods. The results show a significant correlation between the estimated age and the actual age, with a mean absolute error of less than 5 years. The proposed method has important implications for breast cancer screening and risk assessment, enabling personalized and targeted interventions. The novelty of this research lies in its application of deep learning techniques to mammography images for age estimation, offering a non-invasive and cost-effective solution. Key keywords: mammography, age estimation, deep learning, CNN, breast cancer screening, personalized medicine.
