Deep neural networks (DNNs) are often over-parameterized, leading to challenges in generalization and deployment. Early stopping is a common regularization technique, but traditionally requires a validation set, which can be costly or unavailable. This paper introduces a novel channel-wise early stopping method that eliminates the need for a validation set. Our approach leverages Neural Network Kernel (NNK) theory to approximate the training dynamics of each channel in a DNN. By interpolating the NNK polytope, we estimate the generalization error for each channel without relying on external data. This allows for channel-specific stopping times, improving overall model performance. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our method on several benchmark datasets and network architectures, showing comparable or superior performance to validation-based early stopping, particularly in data-scarce scenarios. Our approach offers a practical and efficient alternative for regularizing DNNs, reducing computational overhead and improving generalization. Keywords: Deep Learning, Early Stopping, Regularization, Neural Network Kernel, Generalization, Polytope Interpolation.
