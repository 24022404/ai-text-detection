This paper presents a novel approach to object detection, introducing a Layout Transfer Network (LTN) that enables context-aware detection by transferring knowledge of object layouts across different scenes. The objective is to improve detection accuracy by incorporating spatial relationships and contextual information. Our method employs a deep learning-based approach, utilizing a combination of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and graph neural networks (GNNs) to model object interactions and scene layouts. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our LTN, achieving state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. Key findings include significant improvements in detection accuracy, particularly for objects with complex spatial relationships. The proposed LTN has important implications for various applications, including autonomous driving, surveillance, and robotics, where context-aware object detection is crucial. Our research contributes to the advancement of object detection techniques, highlighting the importance of incorporating contextual information and spatial relationships. Relevant keywords: object detection, context-aware, layout transfer, deep learning, CNNs, GNNs.
