This paper introduces WW-Nets, a novel object detection framework that leverages dual neural networks to enhance detection accuracy and efficiency. The objective is to address the limitations of existing object detection models by incorporating a secondary network that refines detection results. Our approach employs a primary network for initial object proposals and a secondary network for fine-grained classification and bounding box refinement. The WW-Nets model is trained end-to-end, allowing for seamless integration of the two networks. Experimental results demonstrate that WW-Nets outperforms state-of-the-art object detection models, achieving significant improvements in precision and recall. The proposed framework contributes to the field of computer vision by providing a robust and efficient object detection solution, with potential applications in autonomous vehicles, surveillance, and robotics. Key contributions include the introduction of a dual-network architecture, a novel loss function, and a comprehensive evaluation on benchmark datasets. Relevant keywords: object detection, dual neural networks, computer vision, deep learning, WW-Nets.
