This paper addresses the challenge of accurately estimating influence model parameters within Influencer Marketing Return on Investment (IM-RO) analysis. Current methods often fail to capture the nuanced dependencies and dynamics of influential agents within social networks, leading to suboptimal decisions in influencer marketing strategies.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel approach that integrates Bayesian methodologies with advanced machine learning techniques to estimate and refine influence model parameters. This framework leverages probabilistic inference to capture uncertainty and incorporates machine learning algorithms to enhance predictive accuracy and adaptability. Key components include the application of Bayesian networks and supervised learning models that adaptively learn from evolving data streams within the influencer ecosystem.

Results/Findings: Our approach outperforms traditional methods by achieving higher predictive accuracy and reliability in parameter estimation, as demonstrated through a series of experiments on large-scale synthetic and real-world datasets. The Bayesian techniques effectively handle parameter uncertainty, while the machine learning models provide robust performance in diverse scenarios. Comparative analysis with existing models shows significant improvements in capturing complex interaction patterns across the influencer network.

Conclusion/Implications: The integration of Bayesian and machine learning paradigms presents a robust framework for influence model parameter estimation, offering valuable insights and decision-making support for optimizing IM-RO strategies. This research contributes to the field by providing a scalable and efficient solution that can be applied across various domains where influencer dynamics play a critical role. The proposed methodology holds potential for broad applicability in marketing analytics, social media strategy optimization, and enhanced decision support systems.

Keywords: Bayesian methods, machine learning, influence model, parameter estimation, IM-RO, influencer marketing, probabilistic inference.
