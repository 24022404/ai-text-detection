Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have shown promise in node classification tasks, but their performance degrades significantly in open-world scenarios where new classes emerge continuously. This paper introduces a novel lifelong learning framework for GNNs, addressing the challenge of catastrophic forgetting and knowledge transfer in dynamic graph environments. Our approach, termed LifelongGNN, combines a graph structure regularizer with a dynamic node prototype memory to retain past knowledge while adapting to new classes. Specifically, the graph structure regularizer preserves important structural information learned from previous tasks, and the node prototype memory stores representative embeddings for each class encountered, enabling effective knowledge transfer. We evaluate LifelongGNN on several benchmark datasets simulating open-world node classification. Results demonstrate that LifelongGNN significantly outperforms existing lifelong learning methods and traditional GNNs in terms of accuracy, plasticity, and resistance to catastrophic forgetting. Key improvements are observed in the model's ability to generalize to unseen classes and maintain performance on previously learned classes. This research contributes a robust and scalable solution for GNNs in dynamic, open-world graph environments. Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Lifelong Learning, Open-World Learning, Node Classification, Catastrophic Forgetting.
