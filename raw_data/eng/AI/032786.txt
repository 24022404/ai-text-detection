This study aims to investigate the feasibility and effectiveness of using the GrowCut algorithm for vertebral body segmentation in medical imaging. Our approach utilizes a semi-automatic segmentation technique, leveraging the GrowCut method to accurately identify and isolate vertebral bodies from surrounding tissues. The workflow involves pre-processing of imaging data, initialization of seed points, and iterative refinement of segmentations. Initial results demonstrate high accuracy and efficiency in segmenting vertebral bodies, with potential applications in clinical diagnostics, surgical planning, and research studies. The use of GrowCut offers a novel and improved approach to vertebral body segmentation, overcoming limitations of traditional methods. Key contributions include the development of a practical workflow and the demonstration of GrowCut's suitability for this specific application. This research has implications for the field of medical imaging, particularly in the context of spinal analysis and treatment. Relevant keywords: GrowCut, vertebral body segmentation, medical imaging, semi-automatic segmentation, spinal analysis.
