This paper presents MnasNet, a platform-aware neural architecture search approach designed for mobile devices. The objective is to find a neural network architecture that balances accuracy and latency, given the unique constraints of mobile platforms. To achieve this, we employ a reinforcement learning-based method that incorporates platform-aware factors, such as hardware accelerators and memory constraints, into the search process. Our approach utilizes a recurrent neural network controller to generate architectures, which are then evaluated on a mobile device using a hardware simulator. The results show that MnasNet achieves state-of-the-art performance on ImageNet classification, while reducing latency by up to 30% compared to existing mobile-friendly models. The key contributions of this work include a novel platform-aware search space and a efficient search algorithm, which enables the discovery of highly efficient neural architectures for mobile devices. This research has significant implications for the development of AI-powered mobile applications, particularly in areas such as computer vision and natural language processing. Key keywords: neural architecture search, mobile devices, platform-aware, reinforcement learning, hardware accelerators.
