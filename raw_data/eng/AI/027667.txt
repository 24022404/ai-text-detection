This paper proposes a novel approach to image super-resolution using a lightweight adaptive weighted learning network. The objective is to develop an efficient and effective method for enhancing low-resolution images while minimizing computational costs. Our approach employs a compact neural network architecture that adaptively assigns weights to different features, allowing for optimal information extraction and reconstruction. The network is trained using a unique loss function that balances reconstruction accuracy and feature importance. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art models in terms of peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM), while requiring significantly fewer parameters and computations. The proposed network achieves a balance between accuracy and efficiency, making it suitable for real-time applications such as video enhancement and surveillance. Our contributions include a novel adaptive weighted learning mechanism and a lightweight network design, which collectively enable fast and high-quality image super-resolution. Key keywords: image super-resolution, lightweight neural networks, adaptive weighted learning, deep learning, computer vision.
