Graph-structured data plays a critical role in various domains, including social networks, molecular design, and recommendation systems. However, generating small, meaningful graphs that preserve structural and semantic properties remains a challenging task. This paper introduces **GraphVAE**, a novel framework based on Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) tailored for the generation of small graphs. The proposed approach leverages a graph encoder to capture latent representations of graph structures and a decoder to reconstruct and generate new graphs. Unlike existing methods, GraphVAE incorporates a hierarchical sampling strategy to ensure the generation of diverse and realistic small graphs while maintaining computational efficiency. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that GraphVAE outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of graph quality, diversity, and scalability. The framework also exhibits robustness in generating graphs with specific properties, making it suitable for applications such as drug discovery and network design. This work contributes to advancing graph generation techniques by addressing the unique challenges of small graph synthesis and providing a scalable solution for real-world applications.  

**Keywords**: Graph generation, Variational Autoencoders, small graphs, graph representation learning, generative models.
