This paper introduces a novel approach for inducing hierarchical compositional models in generative networks, addressing the challenge of learning disentangled representations. We propose a sparsification technique applied directly to the generator network, encouraging the emergence of hierarchical structures by promoting modularity and selective activation of network components. Our method utilizes a regularized training objective that penalizes the density of connections within the generator, leading to more interpretable and controllable generative processes. We evaluate our approach on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating improved disentanglement compared to existing methods. Furthermore, we show that the resulting sparse generator networks exhibit enhanced generalization capabilities and allow for intuitive manipulation of generated content by selectively activating specific modules. The induced hierarchical compositional models provide a powerful framework for understanding and controlling complex generative processes. Keywords: Generative Networks, Hierarchical Models, Compositional Models, Sparsification, Disentanglement, Representation Learning.
