This paper introduces *HarperValleyBank*, a novel domain-specific spoken dialog corpus designed to advance research in natural language understanding and dialog systems for financial services. The objective of this work is to address the lack of high-quality, domain-specific datasets that capture the nuances of customer-bank interactions, which are critical for developing robust conversational AI systems in the banking sector. The corpus comprises over 10,000 annotated spoken dialogs, collected from simulated and real-world interactions, covering a wide range of banking scenarios such as account inquiries, loan applications, and fraud detection.  

To ensure diversity and realism, the dataset incorporates variations in speaker demographics, accents, and conversational styles. A hybrid annotation approach was employed, combining manual labeling with automated tools to ensure high accuracy in intent classification, slot filling, and dialog state tracking. The dataset was evaluated using state-of-the-art dialog models, demonstrating its utility for training and benchmarking domain-specific conversational agents. Results show significant improvements in intent recognition and dialog management compared to models trained on generic datasets.  

The *HarperValleyBank* corpus represents a significant contribution to the field of spoken dialog systems, offering a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners aiming to develop AI-driven solutions tailored to the financial domain. Its release is expected to facilitate advancements in domain adaptation, intent understanding, and personalized customer service, with potential applications in banking, fintech, and beyond.  

**Keywords**: spoken dialog systems, domain-specific corpus, natural language understanding, conversational AI, banking, intent recognition, dialog management.
