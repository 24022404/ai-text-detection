The accurate prediction of healthcare costs is critical for improving resource allocation and patient care. This study addresses the challenge of identifying hidden patterns in patient multivariate time series data to enhance cost prediction models. We propose a novel approach leveraging Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) to analyze complex, high-dimensional healthcare data, including patient demographics, medical histories, and treatment timelines. The CNN architecture is designed to capture temporal dependencies and spatial correlations within the data, enabling the extraction of meaningful features for cost prediction. Our experiments are conducted on a real-world dataset comprising diverse patient records, and the results demonstrate significant improvements in prediction accuracy compared to traditional methods such as linear regression and decision trees. The proposed model achieves a 15% reduction in mean absolute error and outperforms state-of-the-art techniques in terms of both precision and computational efficiency. The findings highlight the potential of CNNs in uncovering intricate patterns in healthcare data, offering valuable insights for cost management and personalized treatment planning. This research contributes to the growing field of AI-driven healthcare analytics by providing a scalable and interpretable framework for cost prediction, with implications for improving healthcare delivery and financial decision-making.  

**Keywords**: healthcare cost prediction, convolutional neural networks, multivariate time series, patient data analysis, AI in healthcare, predictive modeling.
