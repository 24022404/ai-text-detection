This paper presents a novel approach to automatically generating building floor plan images from textual descriptions in English. The objective is to bridge the gap between natural language and computer-aided design (CAD) by developing a system that can interpret and visualize textual descriptions of building layouts. Our method utilizes a combination of natural language processing (NLP) and computer vision techniques to parse textual input and produce a corresponding floor plan image. The system employs a deep learning-based architecture to learn the mapping between textual descriptions and visual representations of building floor plans. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving a high degree of accuracy in generating floor plan images that match the input textual descriptions. The proposed system has significant implications for various applications, including architecture, real estate, and facility management, by enabling the rapid creation of building floor plans from textual descriptions. Key contributions of this research include the development of a novel text-to-image synthesis framework and the creation of a large dataset of textual descriptions and corresponding floor plan images. Relevant keywords: floor plan generation, text-to-image synthesis, natural language processing, computer vision, CAD, building information modeling (BIM).
