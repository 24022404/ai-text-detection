This research addresses the challenge of developing robust question-answering systems capable of providing accurate and contextually relevant responses based on visual information. By leveraging the vast availability of narrated video content, our study seeks to enhance the understanding and answering of questions in a multimodal context.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel approach that utilizes a large-scale dataset of narrated videos, employing advanced machine learning techniques to train and refine our question-answering model. The model integrates techniques from natural language processing and computer vision, facilitating the extraction and synthesis of information from both audio and visual modalities. Our methodology involves the use of transformer architectures and transfer learning to improve the system's ability to generalize across diverse scenarios.

Results/Findings: The proposed model demonstrates significant improvements in accuracy and contextual comprehension when benchmarked against existing question-answering systems that rely solely on text or visual content. Our findings reveal that the integration of multimodal data sources enhances the model's capacity to understand complex queries and provides more precise answers. Evaluation results highlight a marked improvement in performance metrics such as response accuracy and linguistic coherence.

Conclusion/Implications: The study presents a pioneering step towards more intelligent and contextually-aware question-answering systems by harnessing the potential of narrated video content. Our research contributes to the field by showcasing the advantages of a multimodal approach in complex AI-driven tasks. Applications of this work span various domains, including educational technology, automated video analytics, and interactive AI systems, offering substantial advancements in how machines understand and interact with the world.

Keywords: question-answering, narrated videos, machine learning, multimodal, transformer architecture, audio-visual integration, AI systems.
