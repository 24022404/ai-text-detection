This paper introduces ResNeSt, a novel convolutional neural network architecture that leverages a split-attention mechanism to enhance feature representation. ResNeSt addresses the challenge of capturing cross-channel relationships at different scales by partitioning feature maps into multiple groups and applying attention mechanisms independently within each group. This approach enables the network to learn more diverse and informative features compared to traditional convolutional architectures. The proposed architecture incorporates a modular structure, facilitating easy integration into existing network designs. Extensive experiments on benchmark image classification datasets, including ImageNet, demonstrate that ResNeSt consistently achieves superior performance compared to state-of-the-art ResNet variants and other competitive models with similar computational costs. Specifically, ResNeSt exhibits improved accuracy and robustness. The split-attention mechanism offers a flexible and efficient way to enhance feature representation in convolutional networks, offering significant potential for various computer vision tasks. Keywords: Convolutional Neural Networks, Attention Mechanism, Image Classification, Deep Learning, ResNet, Feature Extraction.
