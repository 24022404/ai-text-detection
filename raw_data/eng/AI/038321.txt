This paper introduces a novel approach to multi-view depth estimation leveraging epipolar geometry and spatio-temporal networks. The objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of depth estimation by explicitly incorporating temporal information and enforcing epipolar constraints within a deep learning framework. Our method, Epipolar Spatio-Temporal Network (ESTN), utilizes a recurrent neural network architecture to process image sequences, enabling the aggregation of information across multiple frames. Epipolar geometry is integrated into the network's loss function to ensure geometric consistency between estimated depths and camera poses. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that ESTN significantly outperforms existing multi-view stereo methods, particularly in challenging scenarios with occlusions and texture-less regions. Furthermore, ablation studies validate the contribution of both the spatio-temporal modeling and epipolar constraints to the overall performance. The proposed approach offers improved depth estimation for applications in 3D reconstruction, autonomous navigation, and virtual reality. Keywords: Depth Estimation, Multi-view Stereo, Spatio-Temporal Networks, Epipolar Geometry, Recurrent Neural Networks, 3D Reconstruction.
