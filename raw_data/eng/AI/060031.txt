This paper presents a novel approach to reversible image merging, a crucial technique in low-level machine vision. The objective is to develop an efficient method for combining multiple images into a single, informative representation while preserving the original images. Our approach utilizes a reversible transformation algorithm that enables the perfect reconstruction of the individual images from the merged image. The method is based on a deep learning architecture that learns to extract and combine relevant features from the input images. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving high-quality merged images and accurate reconstruction of the original images. The proposed technique has significant implications for various machine vision applications, including image compression, feature extraction, and object recognition. Key contributions of this work include the development of a reversible image merging framework, improved image reconstruction quality, and enhanced feature preservation. Relevant keywords: reversible image merging, low-level machine vision, deep learning, image reconstruction, feature extraction.
