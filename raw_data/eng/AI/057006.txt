This paper introduces MagGAN, a novel Mask-Guided Generative Adversarial Network designed for high-resolution face attribute editing. The objective of this research is to address the challenges of precise and realistic manipulation of facial attributes, such as age, expression, and hairstyle, while maintaining high image quality and fine-grained details. MagGAN leverages a mask-guided approach to ensure localized and controllable attribute editing, enabling users to modify specific regions of a face without affecting unrelated areas. The proposed framework integrates a dual-path architecture, combining a generator for attribute manipulation and a discriminator for adversarial training, alongside a mask prediction module to guide the editing process. Experimental results demonstrate that MagGAN achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of image quality, attribute accuracy, and editing precision, outperforming existing methods on benchmark datasets. The model’s ability to generate high-resolution images (up to 1024x1024 pixels) with realistic details highlights its practical applicability in areas such as digital entertainment, virtual reality, and facial analysis. This work contributes to the field of generative models by introducing a robust and scalable solution for fine-grained face attribute editing, paving the way for future advancements in image synthesis and manipulation.  

**Keywords**: Face attribute editing, Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), high-resolution image synthesis, mask-guided generation, image manipulation.
