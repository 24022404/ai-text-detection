This paper introduces a novel methodology for identifying key participants within financial markets using time series data. Our approach leverages multi-agent simulation calibrated through an adversarial learning framework to infer the presence and behavior of market participants. Unlike traditional methods that rely on pre-defined roles or require extensive labeled data, our method dynamically discovers participant identities by training a population of simulated agents to mimic observed market dynamics. We employ an adversarial discriminator that distinguishes between real market data and simulated data, driving the simulator to generate realistic market behaviors indicative of specific participant strategies. The results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in accurately identifying influential market participants and characterizing their trading strategies. Furthermore, we show that the discovered participant profiles can be used to improve market forecasting and risk management. This work contributes a new perspective on market microstructure analysis and offers a powerful tool for understanding complex market dynamics. Keywords: Market participant discovery, multi-agent simulation, adversarial learning, time series analysis, financial markets, agent-based modeling.
