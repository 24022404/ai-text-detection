This paper addresses the challenge of missing data in temporal multilayer position-aware graph neural networks (TMP-GNNs), which are increasingly used for dynamic network analysis. We propose a novel imputation method that leverages both temporal dependencies and node positional information within the multilayer graph structure to estimate missing node features. Our approach integrates a gated recurrent unit (GRU) to capture temporal dynamics and a position-aware attention mechanism to weigh the influence of neighboring nodes based on their structural roles within each layer. We evaluate our method on several real-world datasets with varying missing data ratios, demonstrating significant improvements in imputation accuracy compared to traditional imputation techniques and standard GNN-based methods. Specifically, our experiments show a reduction in root mean squared error (RMSE) of up to 15% for highly incomplete data. This research contributes a robust and accurate missing data estimation technique for TMP-GNNs, enhancing their applicability in domains where data incompleteness is prevalent. Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Missing Data Imputation, Temporal Networks, Multilayer Graphs, Position-aware Attention.
