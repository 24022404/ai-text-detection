This paper provides a comprehensive review of approaches and applications for early classification of time series data. The objective is to identify and summarize the existing methods, techniques, and models used for early classification, highlighting their strengths, weaknesses, and potential applications. Various approaches, including machine learning and deep learning models, are described and analyzed. The results show that early classification of time series data can be effectively achieved using techniques such as recurrent neural networks, long short-term memory networks, and random forests. The findings also emphasize the importance of feature extraction and selection in improving classification accuracy. This review contributes to the field by providing a thorough understanding of the current state of early classification of time series data and its applications in areas such as finance, healthcare, and climate monitoring. The paper highlights the novelty of using early classification for time series data and its potential to enable real-time decision-making and predictive analytics. Key keywords: time series classification, early classification, machine learning, deep learning, real-time decision-making.
