This paper aims to address the challenge of detecting colorized images, a crucial task in image forensics, by leveraging the capabilities of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs). Our approach involves designing and training a CNN model to distinguish between original and colorized images, focusing on achieving high accuracy and good generalization across various image datasets. The proposed method utilizes a combination of convolutional and pooling layers to extract relevant features from images, which are then used to classify them as either original or colorized. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, with our model outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of detection accuracy and robustness to different image manipulations. The key contributions of this research include the development of a highly accurate CNN-based detector for colorized images and the exploration of its potential applications in digital image forensics. Our work has significant implications for improving the authenticity and trustworthiness of digital images, with relevant keywords including image forensics, colorized image detection, CNNs, deep learning, and digital media authentication.
