**Abstract:**  
Heterogeneous domain adaptation addresses the challenge of transferring knowledge across domains with different feature spaces, a common obstacle in various computer vision and natural language processing applications. This paper proposes a novel Cross-Domain Structure Preserving Projection (CDSPP) framework that enables effective adaptation between heterogeneous domains by learning a unified latent space. The presented approach constructs projection matrices for each domain, which are optimized to preserve intrinsic data structures and semantic relationships across domains. By incorporating structure-preserving constraints and aligning both the local and global manifold structures, our method ensures that the learned representations maintain meaningful correspondences between source and target domains. Extensive experiments on multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate that CDSPP significantly outperforms existing heterogeneous domain adaptation techniques in terms of classification accuracy and robustness to domain discrepancies. The results verify that our structure-preserving projection not only enhances cross-domain alignment but also retains critical information necessary for downstream tasks. This work contributes an effective and scalable solution for heterogeneous domain adaptation, with potential applications in cross-modal retrieval, transfer learning, and multi-source data integration.  
**Keywords:** heterogeneous domain adaptation, cross-domain projection, structure preservation, manifold alignment, transfer learning
