This paper presents a novel approach to dynamic filtering for Convolutional Neural Networks (ConvNets) by incorporating a large sampling field. The objective is to enhance the feature extraction capabilities of ConvNets by allowing filters to adaptively sample and weigh features from a larger receptive field. Our method employs a dynamic filtering mechanism that leverages a large sampling field to capture long-range dependencies and contextual information. The approach is based on a flexible and efficient algorithm that enables the network to selectively focus on relevant features and suppress irrelevant ones. Experimental results demonstrate that our dynamic filtering approach outperforms traditional ConvNets and other state-of-the-art methods on various image classification and object detection tasks. The proposed method achieves significant improvements in terms of accuracy and robustness, making it a promising technique for computer vision applications. Key contributions of this work include the introduction of a large sampling field for dynamic filtering, the development of an efficient and flexible algorithm for feature extraction, and the demonstration of the approach's effectiveness in various vision tasks. Relevant keywords: ConvNets, dynamic filtering, large sampling field, feature extraction, computer vision, deep learning.
