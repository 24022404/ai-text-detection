Deep neural networks (DNNs) have achieved remarkable success in various fields, but their architectural design often relies on manual tuning or computationally expensive methods. This paper introduces a novel approach for automating the structure learning of DNNs using Q-learning, a reinforcement learning technique. Our method trains an agent to sequentially select architectural parameters, such as the number of layers, layer types, and connection patterns, to optimize the network's performance on a given task. The agent is rewarded based on the validation accuracy of the resulting DNN, guiding it to discover high-performing architectures. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach on image classification and natural language processing tasks, achieving competitive results compared to manually designed and evolutionarily optimized DNNs. Furthermore, the learned structures exhibit improved efficiency, requiring fewer parameters and computations. The proposed Q-learning-based structure learning offers a promising avenue for automated DNN design, reducing human effort and potentially discovering novel architectures.
**Keywords:** Deep Neural Networks, Structure Learning, Q-Learning, Reinforcement Learning, Automated Machine Learning, Neural Architecture Search.
