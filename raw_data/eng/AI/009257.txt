This paper addresses the challenge of accurate color recognition and representation in fashion garments containing multiple distinct colors, a task critical for online retail, inventory management, and visual search applications. We present CORE (Color Regression), a novel framework that leverages deep learning-based color regression to detect, quantify, and represent multiple colors within a single garment image. The proposed approach utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture trained on a curated dataset of multi-colored fashion items, enabling the model to extract localized color features and predict precise color compositions. Unlike traditional classification-based methods that assign a single dominant color label, CORE outputs continuous color distributions, capturing the full spectrum of garment colors. Experimental results demonstrate that CORE achieves superior accuracy in multi-color detection and regression compared to existing color extraction and clustering baselines, with significant improvements in metrics such as mean absolute error and color diversity recall. The system’s robustness to variations in lighting and complex patterns further underscores its value for real-world deployment. Our findings highlight CORE’s potential to enhance visual search engines, enable finer-grained product recommendations, and support advanced garment tagging in large-scale fashion databases. Keywords: color regression, fashion garments, deep learning, multi-color detection, color representation, convolutional neural networks, visual search.
