This paper presents a novel Periphery-Fovea Multi-Resolution Driving Model that leverages human attention mechanisms to enhance autonomous driving systems. The objective is to develop a more efficient and human-like driving approach by mimicking the way human drivers process visual information. Our method utilizes a multi-resolution framework, combining peripheral vision with foveal attention to focus on critical regions of the driving scene. We employ a deep learning-based architecture to predict human attention and guide the driving model accordingly. Experimental results demonstrate that our model outperforms existing driving models in terms of accuracy and efficiency, particularly in complex scenarios. The key findings highlight the importance of incorporating human attention into autonomous driving systems, leading to improved performance and safety. This research contributes to the development of more advanced and human-centered autonomous vehicles, with potential applications in various fields, including transportation and robotics. Key keywords: autonomous driving, human attention, multi-resolution model, deep learning, computer vision.
