Biomanufacturing processes are inherently complex, necessitating robust risk assessment and sensitivity analyses for Quality-by-Design (QbD) and stability control. This work introduces an interpretable framework for identifying critical process parameters (CPPs) and their impact on critical quality attributes (CQAs). The approach integrates data-driven modeling with process knowledge to quantify and visualize process risk. Specifically, we employ a combination of machine learning techniques, including decision tree learning and sensitivity analysis, to extract interpretable rules that link process parameters to product quality and stability. The proposed framework was applied to a simulated biomanufacturing case study. Results demonstrate its ability to effectively identify CPPs, quantify their influence on CQAs, and visualize the impact of process variations on product stability. The interpretable nature of the derived rules facilitates enhanced process understanding, enabling targeted process improvements and robust control strategies in biomanufacturing. This research contributes to the development of data-driven QbD methodologies and supports the implementation of enhanced process control for improved product quality and stability.

Keywords: Biomanufacturing, Quality-by-Design, Risk Assessment, Sensitivity Analysis, Machine Learning, Process Control, Stability.
