Explainable AI (XAI) has become a critical area of research as the adoption of complex machine learning models grows across various domains. This paper introduces SIDU (Similarity Difference and Uniqueness), a novel method for enhancing the interpretability of AI models by quantifying the contributions of input features to model predictions. The objective of SIDU is to provide a robust and intuitive framework for explaining model decisions, addressing the limitations of existing XAI techniques in capturing feature interactions and uniqueness. The proposed method leverages similarity metrics and difference analysis to identify the most influential features while preserving the uniqueness of their contributions. We evaluate SIDU on benchmark datasets and compare its performance with state-of-the-art XAI methods, demonstrating its effectiveness in generating more accurate and human-understandable explanations. Experimental results show that SIDU outperforms existing approaches in terms of fidelity, stability, and computational efficiency. The implications of this research extend to applications in healthcare, finance, and autonomous systems, where transparent and trustworthy AI is essential. SIDU represents a significant step forward in making AI models more interpretable and reliable, fostering greater trust and adoption in real-world scenarios.  

**Keywords**: Explainable AI, XAI, feature importance, interpretability, machine learning, SIDU, similarity metrics, uniqueness.
