Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) have achieved remarkable success across various domains, yet their decision-making processes often remain opaque, limiting their trustworthiness and interpretability. This paper addresses the critical challenge of explaining DNN predictions by proposing a novel adversarial approach. The objective is to generate interpretable explanations that highlight the most influential features contributing to a model's decision while maintaining robustness against adversarial perturbations. Our method leverages a dual-network framework, where one network generates explanations and the other adversarially evaluates their validity, ensuring both accuracy and interpretability. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets, including image classification and natural language processing tasks, demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of explanation fidelity and resilience to adversarial attacks. The results reveal that our adversarial framework not only enhances the transparency of DNN predictions but also provides insights into potential vulnerabilities in model behavior. This work contributes to the growing field of explainable AI by offering a robust and scalable solution for interpreting complex models, with implications for improving trust and accountability in AI-driven systems.  

**Keywords**: Deep Neural Networks, Explainable AI, Adversarial Learning, Interpretability, Model Transparency.
