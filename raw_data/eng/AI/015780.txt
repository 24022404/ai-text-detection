The connectivity of neural networks plays a critical role in their performance, yet understanding and optimizing this aspect remains a challenging problem. This paper addresses the challenge by exploring neural network connectivity from a topological perspective, offering a novel framework to analyze and enhance network structures. We propose a method that leverages topological data analysis (TDA) to model and interpret the connectivity patterns within neural networks, focusing on how these patterns influence learning dynamics and generalization capabilities. Our approach involves constructing topological representations of network architectures, identifying key connectivity features, and using these insights to guide the design of more efficient and robust networks. Experimental results demonstrate that our method improves network performance across various benchmarks, outperforming traditional connectivity optimization techniques. The findings highlight the potential of topological insights in advancing neural network design, providing a new direction for research in deep learning architectures. This work contributes to the growing field of interpretable AI by bridging topology and neural network theory, offering practical implications for developing more effective and interpretable models.  

**Keywords**: neural networks, topological data analysis, connectivity optimization, deep learning, interpretable AI.
