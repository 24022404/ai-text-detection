This paper introduces UltraSR, a novel approach to arbitrary-scale super-resolution (SR) leveraging implicit image functions. The objective is to overcome the limitations of existing SR methods, which often struggle with large scaling factors and complex image structures. Our approach incorporates spatial encoding as a crucial component, enabling the implicit image function to effectively capture and represent image details at various scales. We propose a modified neural network architecture that integrates spatial encoding with implicit image functions, allowing for more accurate and efficient SR. Experimental results demonstrate that UltraSR outperforms state-of-the-art SR methods, achieving significant improvements in image quality and reconstruction accuracy. The key findings highlight the importance of spatial encoding in implicit image function-based SR, enabling arbitrary-scale SR with unprecedented flexibility and accuracy. This research contributes to the advancement of image processing and computer vision, with potential applications in image restoration, enhancement, and generation. Key keywords: super-resolution, implicit image functions, spatial encoding, arbitrary-scale SR, image restoration.
