**Abstract**

The rapid expansion of e-commerce platforms has intensified the demand for effective automated product question answering (QA) systems. This paper addresses the challenge by proposing a distantly supervised approach that leverages transformer-based models for e-commerce product QA, aiming to provide accurate and context-aware responses to user queries. Our method utilizes large-scale, weakly annotated data sourced from product descriptions, customer reviews, and previously answered questions, reducing reliance on expensive manually labeled datasets. We employ a pre-trained transformer model, further fine-tuned with distantly supervised signals, to capture nuanced semantic similarities between queries and product information. Through comprehensive experiments on a benchmark e-commerce QA dataset, our approach demonstrates significant improvements in answer relevance and precision compared to baseline models relying exclusively on labeled data or traditional information retrieval techniques. The results indicate that distantly supervised transformers can effectively generalize across diverse product categories and question types, offering scalable solutions for real-world e-commerce applications. This research contributes a novel framework for integrating distant supervision with transformer architectures, highlighting its practicality for large-scale, dynamic QA environments. Keywords: e-commerce, question answering, transformers, distant supervision, natural language processing, product information retrieval.
