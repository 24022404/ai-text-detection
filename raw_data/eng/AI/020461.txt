This paper introduces Fast Predictive Simple Geodesic Regression (FPSGR), a novel approach for efficient and accurate regression analysis on Riemannian manifolds. The objective of this research is to address the computational challenges associated with traditional geodesic regression methods, which often suffer from high computational complexity and scalability issues when applied to large datasets. To overcome these limitations, we propose a fast predictive framework that leverages optimization techniques and manifold-specific approximations to reduce computational overhead while maintaining high accuracy. Our method employs a combination of gradient-based optimization and adaptive sampling strategies to achieve significant improvements in runtime performance. Experimental results on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that FPSGR outperforms existing geodesic regression methods in terms of both speed and predictive accuracy, achieving up to a 40% reduction in computation time without compromising regression quality. The proposed approach has broad implications for applications in medical imaging, computer vision, and other fields where data inherently lies on nonlinear manifolds. By providing a scalable and efficient solution, FPSGR opens new avenues for real-time analysis and large-scale data processing in manifold-based regression tasks.  

**Keywords**: Geodesic regression, Riemannian manifolds, optimization, predictive modeling, computational efficiency.
