This paper introduces a novel approach to training deep time series forecasting models, focusing on the reduction of shape and time distortion errors. The objective is to improve the accuracy and reliability of forecasts by developing a loss function that penalizes both shape and temporal distortions between predicted and actual time series. Our method employs a multi-component loss function, combining traditional metrics with newly proposed shape and time distortion measures. The approach is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in forecasting accuracy compared to state-of-the-art models. Key findings indicate that our proposed loss function leads to better preservation of temporal patterns and shapes in the forecasted series. The conclusion highlights the importance of considering both shape and time distortion in the training process, contributing to the development of more accurate and reliable deep time series forecasting models. This research has potential applications in various fields, including finance, energy, and traffic forecasting, where accurate time series predictions are crucial. Relevant keywords: time series forecasting, deep learning, loss function, shape distortion, time distortion, forecasting accuracy.
