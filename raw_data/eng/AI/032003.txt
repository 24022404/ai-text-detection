Object recognition in computer vision often relies on complex feature extraction and learning processes. This paper introduces a novel approach that represents objects as extreme points in a high-dimensional feature space, simplifying object detection and classification. By leveraging the geometric properties of extreme points, our method reduces the computational complexity associated with traditional object recognition pipelines. We propose an algorithm to efficiently identify these extreme points and demonstrate its effectiveness on standard benchmark datasets. Experimental results show that our approach achieves competitive accuracy with significantly reduced training time compared to state-of-the-art methods. Furthermore, the inherent robustness of extreme point representation makes it resilient to variations in pose, illumination, and occlusion. The proposed method offers a new perspective on object representation, enabling efficient and scalable object recognition for various applications.
**Keywords:** Object Recognition, Extreme Points, Computer Vision, Feature Representation, Machine Learning, Geometric Analysis.
