Satellite image super-resolution (SR) aims to reconstruct high-resolution (HR) images from their low-resolution (LR) counterparts, a crucial task for various remote sensing applications. This paper investigates the effectiveness of deep learning models for satellite image SR, addressing challenges such as limited training data and complex atmospheric distortions. We propose a novel deep network architecture, incorporating a multi-scale feature extraction module and an attention mechanism, designed to capture fine-grained details and enhance contextual information in satellite imagery. The network is trained using a combination of synthetic and real satellite image datasets, augmented with physically-based atmospheric degradation models. Experimental results on benchmark satellite image datasets demonstrate that our proposed method outperforms state-of-the-art SR algorithms in terms of PSNR, SSIM, and visual quality. Furthermore, ablation studies validate the contribution of each component in the proposed architecture. The results show the potential of deep learning for improving the resolution of satellite imagery, leading to enhanced accuracy in downstream applications such as land cover classification and environmental monitoring.
**Keywords:** Super-Resolution, Deep Learning, Satellite Imagery, Remote Sensing, Image Reconstruction, Attention Mechanism.
