Unsupervised domain adaptation aims to transfer knowledge from a labeled source domain to an unlabeled target domain. This work addresses the challenge of domain shift by introducing a novel domain-invariant adversarial learning approach. Our method employs a feature extractor to learn representations that are indistinguishable across domains, thereby promoting domain invariance. An adversarial network, comprising a domain discriminator, is trained to distinguish between source and target domain features, while the feature extractor is simultaneously trained to fool the discriminator. This adversarial training process encourages the feature extractor to learn domain-invariant features. Furthermore, we incorporate a classification network to leverage labeled source domain data for effective knowledge transfer. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance in unsupervised domain adaptation tasks compared to existing methods. The learned domain-invariant features facilitate improved classification accuracy in the target domain, highlighting the effectiveness of our adversarial learning strategy. Keywords: Domain Adaptation, Adversarial Learning, Domain Invariance, Unsupervised Learning, Deep Learning.
