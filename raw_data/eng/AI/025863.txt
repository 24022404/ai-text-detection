This paper provides an introduction to Conditional Random Fields (CRFs), a fundamental framework in machine learning for modeling complex relationships between variables. The objective is to explore the capabilities and applications of CRFs in structured prediction tasks, such as text processing and image segmentation. We describe the approach and methodology of CRFs, including the underlying mathematical formulation and the algorithms used for inference and training. Our results demonstrate the effectiveness of CRFs in various domains, highlighting their ability to capture intricate dependencies and improve prediction accuracy. The conclusion emphasizes the significance of CRFs in the field of artificial intelligence, particularly in natural language processing and computer vision, and discusses potential future directions for research and application. Key contributions include a comprehensive overview of CRF models, their strengths and limitations, and guidance on selecting appropriate parameters and features for real-world problems. Relevant keywords: Conditional Random Fields, machine learning, structured prediction, natural language processing, computer vision.
