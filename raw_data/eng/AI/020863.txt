This paper explores the implementation and performance of TensorFlow Lite Micro (TFLM) for embedded machine learning on TinyML systems. The objective is to demonstrate the feasibility and efficiency of deploying machine learning models on resource-constrained devices. We present a case study involving image classification using a convolutional neural network (CNN) optimized for TFLM. The model is deployed on a microcontroller unit (MCU) with limited memory and processing power. Our results show that TFLM enables real-time inference with minimal latency and power consumption, achieving an accuracy of X% on the given dataset. This research highlights the potential of TFLM to bring intelligent applications to battery-powered devices, enabling a wide range of applications in IoT, wearable technology, and edge computing. Key findings include the optimized memory footprint and computational efficiency of TFLM compared to standard TensorFlow, making it suitable for TinyML systems. Keywords: TensorFlow Lite Micro, TinyML, Embedded Machine Learning, Microcontroller, Edge Computing, CNN, Image Classification.
