This paper proposes a novel approach to interactive image segmentation by introducing probabilistic attention mechanisms. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of segmentation models by incorporating user input and uncertainty estimation. Our method employs a probabilistic attention module that learns to focus on the most relevant regions of the image, given user-provided seeds or scribbles. The approach is based on a deep learning architecture that combines the strengths of convolutional neural networks and probabilistic graphical models. Experimental results demonstrate that our model outperforms state-of-the-art interactive segmentation methods, achieving higher accuracy and requiring fewer user interactions. The key contributions of this work include the introduction of probabilistic attention for interactive segmentation, the development of a novel loss function that encourages the model to explore uncertain regions, and the demonstration of the effectiveness of our approach on a range of benchmark datasets. Our research has important implications for applications such as medical image analysis, autonomous driving, and robotic vision, where accurate and efficient segmentation is critical. Key keywords: interactive segmentation, probabilistic attention, deep learning, uncertainty estimation, computer vision.
