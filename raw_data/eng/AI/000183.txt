Person image generation aims to synthesize realistic images of people in arbitrary poses, enabling applications in virtual try-on, animation, and data augmentation. However, generating high-fidelity images under significant pose variations remains challenging due to misaligned body parts and loss of fine-grained appearance details. This paper presents a novel approach called Progressive and Aligned Pose Attention Transfer (PAPAT) for person image generation. Our method progressively transfers pose information across multiple semantic levels, while incorporating an aligned attention mechanism to accurately map body parts between the source and target poses. By leveraging deep convolutional neural networks and carefully designed attention modules, PAPAT can preserve intricate appearance attributes and achieve coherent body structure in the generated images. Extensive experiments on standard person image datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of image quality, identity preservation, and structural accuracy. The proposed framework offers a robust and scalable solution to person image generation, with potential for broader applications in computer vision and graphics. Keywords: person image generation, pose-guided synthesis, attention mechanism, deep learning, image translation.
