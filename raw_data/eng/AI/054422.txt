**Abstract:**

This paper presents a novel approach to face recognition by leveraging transformer architectures, termed as the "Face Transformer." The primary objective is to address challenges associated with intra-class variability, occlusion, and limited dataset diversity that affect the accuracy and robustness of traditional face recognition systems. The proposed method utilizes a transformer-based model, which excels at capturing long-range dependencies and complex feature representations in high-dimensional facial data. We construct a specialized attention mechanism that integrates both spatial and contextual information, enhancing the model's ability to discern subtle facial features and expressions across diverse environments. Extensive experiments are conducted on standard face recognition benchmarks, demonstrating that the Face Transformer consistently outperforms conventional convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recent state-of-the-art methods in terms of recognition accuracy, robustness to occlusion, and computational efficiency. The results highlight the model's superior generalization capability and its potential for real-world biometric applications. In conclusion, the Face Transformer offers a significant advancement in face recognition technology, providing a powerful and adaptable solution suitable for security, surveillance, and user authentication systems. Key keywords: face recognition, transformer, deep learning, attention mechanism, biometric identification.
