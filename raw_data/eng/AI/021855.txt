Reinforcement learning (RL) in continuous action spaces presents a significant challenge, particularly when actions are defined by a high-dimensional parameter vector. This paper introduces a novel hierarchical reinforcement learning approach designed to efficiently navigate parameterized action spaces. Our method decomposes the action space into a hierarchy of increasingly refined control levels. A high-level policy selects broad action categories, while lower-level policies specialize in fine-tuning specific parameters within those categories. This hierarchical structure allows for efficient exploration and exploitation by reducing the dimensionality of the search space at each level. We demonstrate the efficacy of our approach on several benchmark tasks, including robotic manipulation and continuous control problems. Experimental results show that our hierarchical RL agent achieves significantly faster learning and higher asymptotic performance compared to flat RL algorithms and existing hierarchical methods. The proposed method offers a promising avenue for tackling complex control problems with parameterized action spaces, leading to more efficient and scalable reinforcement learning.

**Keywords:** Reinforcement Learning, Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning, Parameterized Action Space, Continuous Control, Robotics, Deep Reinforcement Learning.
