This paper introduces a novel unsupervised approach for simultaneously discovering object regions and establishing correspondences between images. The method formulates image matching and object discovery as a joint optimization problem. Unlike existing methods that rely on pre-trained models or manual annotations, our approach learns both object segmentation and feature matching directly from the data. We propose a differentiable framework that iteratively refines object masks and correspondence maps by minimizing an energy function designed to promote consistency and compactness. The optimization process leverages techniques from optimal transport and robust statistics to handle outliers and occlusions. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in unsupervised object discovery and image matching. The results indicate significant improvements in the quality of discovered object masks and the accuracy of established correspondences compared to existing unsupervised approaches. This research offers a new perspective on unsupervised learning for computer vision tasks, providing a foundation for further advancements in automated object recognition and scene understanding.

Keywords: Unsupervised Learning, Image Matching, Object Discovery, Optimization, Optimal Transport, Computer Vision.
