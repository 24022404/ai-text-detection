Copy-move forgery detection (CMFD) is a critical challenge in digital image forensics, where duplicated regions within an image are manipulated to conceal or alter information. This paper presents a novel approach for CMFD based on evolving circular domains coverage, aiming to improve detection accuracy and robustness against common post-processing techniques such as noise addition, compression, and rotation. The proposed method leverages a dynamic circular domain strategy to systematically analyze and compare image regions, ensuring comprehensive coverage of potential forged areas. By employing a combination of feature extraction and similarity metrics, the algorithm identifies duplicated regions with high precision. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms existing techniques in terms of detection accuracy and computational efficiency, particularly in handling complex forgeries with geometric transformations. The study contributes to the field of digital forensics by introducing an innovative and scalable solution for CMFD, with potential applications in security, media verification, and legal investigations. Key innovations include the adaptive circular domain approach and enhanced robustness to post-processing distortions. Keywords: copy-move forgery detection, digital image forensics, evolving circular domains, image manipulation, feature extraction.
