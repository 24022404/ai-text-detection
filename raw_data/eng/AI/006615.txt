Object detection frameworks heavily rely on accurate bounding box regression, which influences the overall precision and robustness of localization tasks. Traditional loss functions for bounding box regression, such as IoU loss and Smooth L1 loss, often fail to capture critical spatial relationships between predicted and ground-truth boxes, limiting the effectiveness of deep learning models. This paper introduces the Distance-IoU (DIoU) loss function, a novel approach that integrates both the overlap area and the spatial distance between the centers of bounding boxes, directly optimizing the location accuracy during training. The proposed DIoU loss offers a more comprehensive regression metric, enabling faster convergence and improved localization performance compared to conventional methods. Extensive experiments conducted on benchmark datasets, including COCO and PASCAL VOC, demonstrate that object detectors trained with DIoU loss achieve higher mean Average Precision (mAP) and reduced training times without additional computational overhead. Analysis shows that DIoU loss enhances the learning of bounding box geometry, particularly in crowded and overlapping scenarios. The findings highlight the potential of the DIoU loss to advance state-of-the-art object detection systems and inspire further development in bounding box optimization. Keywords: object detection, bounding box regression, Distance-IoU loss, deep learning, localization accuracy, computer vision.
