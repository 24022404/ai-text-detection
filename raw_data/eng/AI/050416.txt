Algorithm 2 provides a detailed method for decoding a (k, s)-RdB sequence, utilizing the DKRR decoder as the core mechanism of the process. The decoding procedure systematically interprets the sequence structure to retrieve the original information. To affirm the optimality of the constructed sequence ck,s, a mathematical proof is introduced, establishing that its length precisely matches the upper bound U(k, s). Through step-by-step algebraic transformations, it is shown that ℓ(ck,s) = U(k, s), thereby confirming that ck,s indeed represents the longest possible (k, s)-RdB sequence achievable with the given parameters.
