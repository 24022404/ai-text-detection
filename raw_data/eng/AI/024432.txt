Object detection performance is highly influenced by the definition of positive samples during training. Standard detectors often use a fixed Intersection over Union (IoU) threshold to define positives, which can lead to sub-optimal results due to a mismatch between the IoU used for training and the IoU at which the detector performs best. This paper introduces Cascade R-CNN, a multi-stage object detection architecture designed to address this issue. Cascade R-CNN consists of a sequence of detectors trained with increasing IoU thresholds to be sequentially more selective against close false positives. These detectors are trained stage-wise, leveraging the output of one stage to train the next, leading to improved hypothesis quality at each level. This architecture enables the training of detectors optimized for higher IoU thresholds without suffering from overfitting. Experiments on the COCO dataset demonstrate that Cascade R-CNN significantly outperforms existing detectors, achieving state-of-the-art results. The proposed approach offers a robust and effective solution for high-quality object detection, providing a new perspective on the importance of adaptive training thresholds.
Keywords: Object Detection, Cascade R-CNN, Convolutional Neural Networks, High-Quality Detection, Intersection over Union (IoU), Deep Learning.
