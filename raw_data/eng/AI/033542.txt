This paper introduces U-Time, a fully convolutional network designed for time series segmentation, with a specific application to sleep staging. The objective is to accurately identify distinct sleep stages from polysomnography (PSG) recordings, a crucial task for diagnosing sleep disorders. U-Time employs a novel architecture that leverages convolutional neural networks to learn relevant features from time series data, eliminating the need for manual feature engineering. The approach is evaluated on a large dataset of PSG recordings, demonstrating superior performance compared to existing methods. Results show that U-Time achieves high accuracy and robustness in sleep stage classification, outperforming state-of-the-art techniques. The key contributions of this research include the development of a fully convolutional network for time series segmentation and its successful application to sleep staging, highlighting the potential of deep learning techniques in this field. The proposed method has significant implications for the automated analysis of sleep patterns, enabling more efficient and accurate diagnosis of sleep disorders. Relevant keywords: time series segmentation, sleep staging, fully convolutional networks, deep learning, polysomnography.
