Dynamic dispatching in real-world systems presents a complex and evolving challenge, requiring adaptive decision-making under uncertainty. This paper explores the challenges of applying Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) to dynamic dispatching problems, focusing on its potential to optimize resource allocation and task scheduling in dynamic environments. We identify key obstacles, including the high dimensionality of state-action spaces, non-stationary environments, and the difficulty of reward function design. To address these challenges, we propose a hybrid approach combining DRL with domain-specific heuristics, enabling more efficient exploration and exploitation in dynamic settings. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in reducing task completion times and improving system responsiveness compared to traditional dispatching methods. The findings highlight the potential of DRL in dynamic dispatching while underscoring the need for tailored solutions to overcome inherent limitations. This research contributes to advancing the application of DRL in real-world dynamic systems, offering insights for future developments in adaptive decision-making technologies.  

**Keywords**: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Dynamic Dispatching, Resource Allocation, Task Scheduling, Adaptive Decision-Making, Real-World Systems.
