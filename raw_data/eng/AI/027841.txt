This paper presents a novel approach to image and video processing through the development of Side Window Filtering, a technique designed to enhance visual quality by effectively reducing noise and preserving image details. The objective of this research is to address the limitations of traditional filtering methods, which often compromise between noise reduction and detail preservation. Our approach utilizes a unique windowing mechanism that adapts to the local characteristics of the image, allowing for more precise control over the filtering process. The method involves a sophisticated algorithm that analyzes the spatial and temporal relationships within the image data to optimize the filtering parameters. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of Side Window Filtering over existing methods, showing significant improvements in peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and visual information fidelity (VIF). The contributions of this research include the introduction of a new filtering paradigm, which has potential applications in various fields such as image denoising, video enhancement, and computer vision. Key aspects of this work include image processing, noise reduction, detail preservation, and adaptive filtering, making it a valuable contribution to the field of signal processing and computer vision.
