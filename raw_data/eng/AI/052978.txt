This paper aims to investigate the development of a personalized climate control system for assistant driving, focusing on mining individual preferences to enhance in-vehicle comfort. Our approach utilizes machine learning algorithms and sensor data to model driver behavior and climate settings, enabling the prediction of optimal temperature and air quality conditions. The results show a significant improvement in user satisfaction and reduced energy consumption, outperforming traditional one-size-fits-all climate control systems. Key findings include the identification of distinct climate preference patterns among drivers and the importance of incorporating real-time weather and traffic data into the prediction model. This research contributes to the advancement of intelligent transportation systems and smart vehicles, with potential applications in autonomous driving and eco-friendly transportation solutions. By leveraging data-driven insights and AI-powered climate control, our system provides a more comfortable, efficient, and personalized driving experience, highlighting the benefits of human-centered design in automotive technology. Keywords: personalized climate control, assistant driving, machine learning, intelligent transportation systems, smart vehicles, user-centric design.
