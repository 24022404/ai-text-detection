This paper addresses the challenge of automating photo adjustment by leveraging deep learning techniques to enhance image aesthetics while preserving semantic content. Traditional photo editing tools often require manual intervention, which can be time-consuming and subjective. To overcome this limitation, we propose a novel deep semantics-aware framework that integrates semantic understanding with aesthetic enhancement. Our approach employs a dual-branch neural network architecture, where one branch focuses on semantic segmentation to identify key objects and regions, while the other branch optimizes aesthetic attributes such as color balance, contrast, and lighting. The model is trained on a large-scale dataset of professionally edited images, enabling it to learn both semantic and aesthetic features simultaneously. Experimental results demonstrate that our framework outperforms existing methods in terms of visual quality and semantic consistency, as validated by both quantitative metrics and user studies. The proposed system not only automates photo adjustment but also ensures that the edited images retain their original meaning and context. This research contributes to the field of computational photography by bridging the gap between semantic understanding and aesthetic enhancement, offering a practical solution for both professional and casual users.  

**Keywords**: deep learning, photo adjustment, semantic segmentation, image aesthetics, computational photography, neural networks.
