This paper introduces a novel transfer learning approach that leverages unsupervised clustering to improve performance across different domains and tasks. We address the challenge of domain shift by learning shared cluster representations from source data and transferring these representations to target domains. Our method involves clustering source data based on feature similarity, followed by training a mapping function to align cluster assignments with task-specific labels. This learned mapping is then transferred to the target domain, enabling effective task performance even with limited labeled data. We evaluate our approach on several benchmark datasets spanning image classification and natural language processing tasks, demonstrating significant performance gains compared to existing transfer learning methods. Specifically, our results show improved accuracy and faster convergence, highlighting the effectiveness of cluster-based transfer. The proposed technique offers a practical solution for cross-domain and cross-task learning, particularly in scenarios with scarce target domain labels. Keywords: Transfer Learning, Clustering, Domain Adaptation, Representation Learning, Unsupervised Learning.
