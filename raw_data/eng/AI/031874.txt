Fashion garment color recognition is crucial for various applications, including e-commerce and virtual try-on. However, existing methods often struggle with garments featuring multiple colors or complex patterns. This paper introduces CORE (Color Regression), a novel approach that directly regresses the color composition of fashion garments. Instead of relying on segmentation or classification, CORE utilizes a convolutional neural network to predict the percentage of each color present in the garment image. We designed a custom loss function that considers both color similarity and percentage accuracy. Experiments on a newly created dataset of multi-colored fashion garments demonstrate that CORE outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of color accuracy and robustness to variations in lighting and pose. Furthermore, CORE's regression-based approach is computationally efficient, enabling real-time color analysis. This work provides a practical solution for accurate color extraction from fashion garments, with potential applications in personalized shopping and fashion recommendation systems. Keywords: Color Regression, Fashion Garments, Color Analysis, Convolutional Neural Networks, Multi-Colored Images.
