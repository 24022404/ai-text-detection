This paper presents a novel approach to real image denoising, leveraging feature attention to improve the removal of noise from images. The objective is to develop an effective method for restoring clean images from noisy observations, a crucial task in various computer vision applications. Our approach employs a deep neural network architecture that incorporates feature attention mechanisms, allowing the model to focus on relevant features and weigh their importance in the denoising process. The proposed method is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating superior performance compared to state-of-the-art denoising techniques. Key findings include significant improvements in peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM), indicating enhanced image quality and fidelity. The contributions of this research lie in the introduction of feature attention to real image denoising, enabling more accurate and efficient noise removal. This work has important implications for applications such as image processing, computer vision, and robotics, where high-quality images are essential. Relevant keywords: image denoising, feature attention, deep learning, computer vision, noise removal.
