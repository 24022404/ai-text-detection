This paper introduces the Home Action Genome, a novel framework for cooperative compositional action understanding in domestic environments. The objective is to develop a comprehensive system that can recognize and interpret complex human actions in a cooperative setting, enabling intelligent home automation and assistance. Our approach combines computer vision, machine learning, and knowledge graph embedding to model human actions as compositional sequences of sub-actions. We propose a hierarchical action representation that captures the relationships between actions, objects, and environments, allowing for more accurate and robust action understanding. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our framework in recognizing cooperative actions, outperforming state-of-the-art methods by a significant margin. The Home Action Genome has significant implications for intelligent home systems, human-robot interaction, and assistive technologies, enabling more natural and intuitive human-machine collaboration. Key contributions include a large-scale dataset of cooperative actions, a novel action representation framework, and a robust action recognition system. Relevant keywords: action understanding, computer vision, machine learning, human-robot interaction, intelligent home systems, cooperative actions.
