This paper addresses the problem of generic outlier detection in multi-armed bandit (MAB) problems, where the goal is to identify and handle anomalous arms that deviate significantly from their expected behavior. Our objective is to develop a robust and efficient method for detecting outliers in MAB settings, which is crucial for maintaining the reliability and performance of decision-making systems. We propose a novel approach that combines statistical modeling and machine learning techniques to identify outlier arms. Our method utilizes a probabilistic framework to model the behavior of each arm and detects anomalies based on their deviation from the expected distribution. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in detecting outliers and improving the overall performance of the MAB system. The key findings of this study highlight the importance of outlier detection in MAB problems and provide insights into the application of our method in real-world scenarios. Our research contributes to the development of more robust and reliable MAB systems, with potential applications in areas such as recommendation systems, online advertising, and autonomous decision-making. Key keywords: multi-armed bandit, outlier detection, statistical modeling, machine learning, decision-making systems.
