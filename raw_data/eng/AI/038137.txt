This paper introduces LRCN-RetailNet, a novel recurrent neural network architecture designed for accurate and robust people counting in retail environments. The objective is to address the challenges of varying crowd densities, occlusions, and illumination changes that significantly impact the performance of traditional counting methods. LRCN-RetailNet combines convolutional layers for feature extraction with long short-term memory (LSTM) layers to model temporal dependencies in video sequences. The architecture is trained end-to-end to directly predict people counts from video frames. Experimental results on a newly collected retail dataset, as well as publicly available benchmarks, demonstrate that LRCN-RetailNet achieves state-of-the-art accuracy in people counting, outperforming existing methods by a significant margin, particularly in crowded scenes. The proposed approach offers a practical solution for real-time occupancy monitoring and customer behavior analysis in retail settings. Keywords: People Counting, Recurrent Neural Networks, LSTM, Retail, Video Analysis, Crowd Management.
