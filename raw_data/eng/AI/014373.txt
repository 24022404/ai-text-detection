This paper addresses the challenge of ranking structured objects, such as graphs and networks, using Graph Neural Networks (GNNs). The objective is to develop an effective ranking model that captures the complex relationships and structural information inherent in these objects. To achieve this, we propose a novel GNN-based approach that learns node and graph representations, enabling the comparison and ranking of structured objects. Our method leverages a graph attention mechanism and a ranking loss function to optimize the model's performance. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art methods on several benchmark datasets. The key findings highlight the importance of incorporating structural information and attention mechanisms in ranking models. This research contributes to the advancement of GNNs and their applications in ranking and recommendation systems, with potential implications for fields such as social network analysis, recommender systems, and computer vision. Key keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Ranking, Structured Objects, Graph Attention, Recommendation Systems.
