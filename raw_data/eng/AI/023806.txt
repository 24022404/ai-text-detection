The classification of imbalanced data remains a significant challenge in the field of machine learning, often leading to biased models that perform poorly on minority classes. This paper introduces a novel approach using a Class Expert Generative Adversarial Network (CE-GAN) to address the inherent issues associated with imbalanced datasets.

Methods/Approach: Our proposed method, CE-GAN, leverages the capabilities of deep learning along with the generative adversarial network (GAN) framework to generate synthetic samples that enhance the representation of minority classes. Unlike traditional GANs, the CE-GAN is designed to incorporate class-specific experts that focus on generating data for each minority class, thereby maintaining balanced class distributions during model training.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate that CE-GAN substantially improves classification performance across various widely-used datasets, as compared to conventional methods. The system showed significant enhancement in recall and F1-score for minority classes without compromising the accuracy of majority classes. The model was also benchmarked against existing state-of-the-art techniques and consistently outperformed them in handling severe class imbalance.

Conclusion/Implications: This research introduces a bespoke deep learning framework that effectively mitigates the challenges posed by imbalanced datasets through class-specific sample generation, demonstrating a notable advancement in classification performance. The proposed CE-GAN offers significant potential for practical application in areas such as fraud detection, medical diagnosis, and any domain where class imbalance is a critical issue. Key keywords: deep learning, imbalanced data classification, GAN, class expert, synthetic sample generation, machine learning.
