Cervical spondylosis (CS) is a prevalent condition that significantly impacts quality of life, yet its early identification remains challenging. This paper introduces *EasiCSDeep*, a novel deep learning model designed to accurately identify cervical spondylosis using surface electromyography (sEMG) signals. The proposed approach leverages a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture optimized for feature extraction from sEMG data, combined with a recurrent neural network (RNN) to capture temporal dependencies in the signal. The model is trained and validated on a comprehensive dataset of sEMG recordings collected from patients with and without CS. Experimental results demonstrate that *EasiCSDeep* achieves an accuracy of 94.7%, outperforming traditional machine learning methods and existing deep learning models. The system also exhibits robustness to noise and variability in sEMG signals, making it suitable for real-world clinical applications. This research contributes to the field of medical diagnostics by providing a non-invasive, efficient, and reliable tool for early CS detection, with potential implications for improving patient outcomes and reducing healthcare costs.  

**Keywords**: Cervical spondylosis, surface electromyography (sEMG), deep learning, convolutional neural network (CNN), recurrent neural network (RNN), medical diagnostics.
