This study investigates the effect of individual rater style on the uncertainty of deep learning models in medical imaging segmentation. The objective is to understand how variations in human annotation impact the performance and reliability of artificial intelligence (AI) systems in medical image analysis. A deep learning framework is employed, utilizing a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, to segment medical images with annotations from multiple raters. The results show that individual rater styles significantly influence the uncertainty of the deep learning model, with some raters inducing higher uncertainty due to inconsistent or ambiguous annotations. The findings suggest that accounting for rater variability can improve the robustness and accuracy of AI-driven medical image segmentation. This research contributes to the development of more reliable and trustworthy AI systems in healthcare, highlighting the importance of considering human factors in the design and training of deep learning models. Key aspects of this study include deep learning uncertainty, medical imaging segmentation, rater variability, and AI model robustness.
