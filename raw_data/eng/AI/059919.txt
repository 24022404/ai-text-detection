This paper addresses the challenge of efficient k-shot learning, where models must generalize effectively from limited labeled data. We propose a novel approach leveraging regularized deep networks to enhance learning performance in low-data regimes. Our method integrates a regularization framework that mitigates overfitting while preserving the model's ability to capture essential patterns from sparse datasets. The approach combines adaptive weight regularization with a modified loss function tailored for k-shot scenarios, ensuring robust feature extraction and improved generalization. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing k-shot learning techniques in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency. Key findings reveal that the proposed regularization strategy significantly reduces model complexity without sacrificing predictive power, making it highly suitable for real-world applications with limited data availability. This research contributes to advancing k-shot learning by providing a scalable and efficient solution, with potential applications in domains such as medical imaging, natural language processing, and autonomous systems. Keywords: k-shot learning, deep networks, regularization, low-data regimes, generalization, adaptive learning.
