This paper presents a novel framework for attribute-controlled face photo synthesis from simple line drawings, addressing the challenge of generating realistic facial images with explicit control over key facial attributes. Our approach utilizes a deep generative model that combines a convolutional neural network (CNN) for extracting geometric information from line drawings with an attribute encoder that allows users to specify facial characteristics such as age, gender, expression, and hair style. The fusion of structure cues from the line drawing and semantic attribute vectors is achieved through a conditional generator, enabling fine-grained manipulation of the synthesized faces. Experimental results demonstrate that our method produces high-quality, photorealistic facial images that are faithful to both the input drawing and the specified attributes. Comparative analysis with state-of-the-art techniques reveals significant improvements in attribute controllability and image realism. This work offers valuable applications in digital entertainment, character design, and interactive face editing, providing an intuitive tool for users with limited artistic skills to create personalized face photos from simple sketches. Key terms include face synthesis, line drawing, attribute control, generative adversarial networks, and photorealism.
