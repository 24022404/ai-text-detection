This paper addresses the challenge of decision-making in complex action spaces, a critical problem in artificial intelligence (AI) and reinforcement learning (RL). Traditional methods often struggle with scalability and efficiency when dealing with high-dimensional or combinatorial action spaces, limiting their applicability in real-world scenarios. To overcome this, we propose a novel framework that integrates advanced learning techniques with hierarchical planning strategies. Our approach leverages deep reinforcement learning to model action dependencies and employs a hierarchical decomposition to reduce computational complexity. Experiments conducted on benchmark environments demonstrate significant improvements in both performance and scalability compared to existing methods. The proposed framework achieves up to a 30% increase in task completion rates while reducing computational overhead by 40%. These results highlight the potential of our method for applications in robotics, autonomous systems, and resource allocation. By bridging the gap between learning and planning, this work contributes a scalable and efficient solution for decision-making in complex action spaces, paving the way for more robust AI systems in dynamic environments.  

**Keywords**: reinforcement learning, hierarchical planning, decision-making, complex action spaces, scalability, artificial intelligence.
