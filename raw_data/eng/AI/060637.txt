This paper addresses the challenge of learning invariant representations in deep neural networks without relying on adversarial training methods. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that can learn representations which are insensitive to nuisance factors, thereby improving the robustness and generalizability of models. We propose a new framework that utilizes a combination of regularization techniques and self-supervised learning to achieve invariant representations. Our method does not require adversarial training or explicit knowledge of the nuisance factors, making it more efficient and widely applicable. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of representation quality and downstream task performance. We also show that our learned representations exhibit improved robustness to various types of perturbations and corruptions. The key contributions of this work include a novel invariant representation learning framework, improved robustness and generalizability, and state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. Our approach has significant implications for a range of applications, including computer vision, natural language processing, and reinforcement learning, where invariant representations can enhance model reliability and accuracy. Key keywords: invariant representations, deep learning, self-supervised learning, regularization techniques, robustness, generalizability.
