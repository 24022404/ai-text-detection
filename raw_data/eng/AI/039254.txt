**Abstract:**

The rapid growth of urban populations has led to significant challenges in managing traffic congestion, necessitating the development of intelligent traffic control systems. This paper presents an adaptive traffic control framework that leverages deep reinforcement learning (DRL) to dynamically optimize traffic signal timings in real-time. Our approach models the traffic environment as a Markov Decision Process, employing deep neural networks to approximate optimal policies based on continuous state and action spaces. We introduce a novel reward engineering strategy to effectively balance throughput, waiting times, and fairness across intersections. Extensive experiments are conducted using realistic traffic simulation platforms, demonstrating that the proposed DRL-based method outperforms traditional rule-based and fixed-timing controllers, as well as recent state-of-the-art adaptive systems, in metrics such as vehicular delay and intersection throughput. The results highlight the model’s robust adaptability to changing traffic patterns and scalability to large urban road networks. This research advances the application of artificial intelligence in intelligent transportation systems, offering a scalable and efficient solution for future smart cities. Keywords: adaptive traffic control, deep reinforcement learning, traffic signal optimization, intelligent transportation systems, urban mobility.
