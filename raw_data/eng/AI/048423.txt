This paper introduces LIT (Block-wise Intermediate Representation Training), a novel model compression framework designed to enhance the efficiency of deep neural networks while preserving performance. The main objective is to address the growing computational and memory demands of large-scale AI models, especially in resource-constrained environments. LIT partitions a neural network into multiple blocks and transfers knowledge by aligning intermediate representations between the teacher and student models at each block level. This block-wise strategy enables a more granular and effective distillation process compared to traditional end-to-end knowledge distillation or parameter pruning methods. Experimental evaluations across several benchmark datasets and architectures demonstrate that LIT achieves superior compression ratios, faster inference speeds, and minimal loss in predictive accuracy relative to existing techniques. The findings highlight LIT’s adaptability to various neural architectures and its effectiveness in producing lightweight models suitable for edge computing and deployment on mobile devices. This work contributes a scalable and robust solution for model compression, with significant potential for advancing real-world deployment of deep learning systems. Keywords: model compression, knowledge distillation, neural networks, intermediate representations, deep learning, resource efficiency.
