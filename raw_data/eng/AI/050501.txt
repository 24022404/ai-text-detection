This paper investigates the vulnerability of time series forecasting models to adversarial attacks, which are intentionally crafted input perturbations designed to mislead model predictions. Our objective is to develop and evaluate effective attack strategies that can compromise the accuracy and reliability of time series forecasting systems. We propose a novel approach based on gradient-based optimization, which generates adversarial examples by perturbing the input time series data. Our results show that the proposed attack method can significantly degrade the performance of popular time series forecasting models, including ARIMA, LSTM, and Prophet. We also demonstrate that our attack strategy outperforms existing methods in terms of attack success rate and perturbation magnitude. The findings of this study have important implications for the development of robust time series forecasting systems, highlighting the need for adversarial training and defense mechanisms to mitigate potential attacks. Our research contributes to the growing field of time series security and has potential applications in areas such as finance, healthcare, and IoT monitoring, where reliable time series forecasting is critical. Key keywords: time series forecasting, adversarial attacks, gradient-based optimization, robustness, security.
