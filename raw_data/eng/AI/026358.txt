User actions within a helpdesk system, including creating, updating, and deleting tickets, leaving comments, generating activities, and logging messages, are core aspects of ticket management. These operations are facilitated by the HelpdeskTicket Class, which encapsulates essential attributes that define each ticket’s data and behavior. The class designates "id" as the primary key to guarantee unique ticket identification, while many2one fields act as foreign keys, supporting relational links to other records, such as users or categories.
