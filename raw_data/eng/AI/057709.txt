This paper introduces TempoRL, a novel reinforcement learning framework addressing the challenge of determining optimal action timing within continuous-time environments. Unlike conventional RL approaches that act at fixed intervals, TempoRL learns a policy that explicitly decides when to take actions, thereby improving efficiency and performance. Our method incorporates a temporal action selection mechanism integrated directly into the RL agent's decision-making process. This mechanism leverages a learned time-to-act function, guiding the agent to strategically choose action execution moments based on the current state. We evaluated TempoRL across a suite of continuous control tasks, demonstrating significant improvements in sample efficiency and overall reward compared to state-of-the-art, fixed-rate RL algorithms. The results indicate that TempoRL is particularly effective in scenarios where precise timing is critical for achieving desired outcomes. This research offers a new perspective on RL in continuous environments, potentially enabling more intelligent and adaptable agents capable of optimizing their interaction with the world. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Continuous Time, Temporal Action Selection, Autonomous Agents, Optimal Control, Sample Efficiency.
