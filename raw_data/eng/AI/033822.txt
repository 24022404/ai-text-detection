This paper addresses the challenge of multi-view depth estimation, a critical task in 3D scene reconstruction and computer vision applications. Traditional methods often struggle with accuracy and computational efficiency when handling complex scenes with varying lighting conditions and occlusions. To overcome these limitations, we propose a novel approach using Epipolar Spatio-Temporal Networks (ESTN), which leverages both spatial and temporal information across multiple views. Our method integrates epipolar geometry constraints with deep learning techniques to enhance depth prediction accuracy while maintaining computational efficiency. The ESTN framework employs a multi-scale feature extraction mechanism and a temporal consistency module to ensure robust performance across dynamic scenes. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of both accuracy and inference speed. The proposed model shows significant improvements in handling occlusions and textureless regions, making it highly suitable for real-world applications such as autonomous driving, augmented reality, and robotics. This work contributes to the field by introducing a scalable and efficient solution for multi-view depth estimation, paving the way for more reliable 3D reconstruction systems.  

**Keywords**: Multi-view depth estimation, epipolar geometry, spatio-temporal networks, 3D reconstruction, deep learning, computer vision.
