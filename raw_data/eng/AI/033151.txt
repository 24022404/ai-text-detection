Object tracking in high-speed scenarios presents significant challenges for traditional artificial neural networks (ANNs) due to their computational demands and latency. This paper introduces DashNet, a novel hybrid neural network architecture combining the strengths of ANNs and spiking neural networks (SNNs) to achieve high-speed and efficient object tracking. DashNet leverages ANNs for robust feature extraction and SNNs for fast temporal processing and event-driven computation. Specifically, ANNs process input frames to identify potential object locations, while SNNs, configured with a dynamic threshold neuron model, rapidly refine these locations based on inter-frame motion and temporal correlations. The proposed architecture is implemented using a custom hardware accelerator to further enhance its speed. Experimental results on challenging high-speed video datasets demonstrate that DashNet achieves significantly improved tracking accuracy and reduced latency compared to state-of-the-art ANN-based trackers. The hybrid approach offers a promising direction for real-time object tracking in resource-constrained environments, enabling applications such as autonomous navigation and high-speed robotics. Keywords: Object Tracking, Spiking Neural Networks, Artificial Neural Networks, Hybrid Architecture, High-Speed Vision, Neuromorphic Computing.
