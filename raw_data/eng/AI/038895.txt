Deep neural networks (DNNs) have achieved remarkable performance in various tasks, but quantifying the uncertainty of their predictions remains a significant challenge. This paper addresses the problem of constructing prediction sets for DNNs that satisfy Probably Approximately Correct (PAC) guarantees, ensuring that the true label is contained within the predicted set with high probability. We introduce a novel calibration method that leverages conformal prediction to generate well-calibrated confidence sets, even when the underlying DNN is misspecified. Our approach uses a separate calibration dataset to estimate the empirical coverage of the prediction sets and adjusts their size to meet the desired PAC guarantee. Experimental results on image classification and natural language processing tasks demonstrate that our method produces valid confidence sets with improved efficiency compared to existing techniques. The proposed approach offers a practical and theoretically sound framework for quantifying uncertainty in DNN predictions, enhancing their reliability and trustworthiness in critical applications.

**Keywords:** Deep Neural Networks, Uncertainty Quantification, Conformal Prediction, PAC Learning, Calibration, Confidence Sets.
