Single image dehazing aims to restore clear visibility from images degraded by haze, a challenging ill-posed problem. This paper introduces a novel classification-driven approach to single image dehazing. Our method leverages a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) to classify image patches into distinct haze density levels, subsequently guiding the dehazing process. By explicitly incorporating haze density classification, we effectively estimate spatially varying atmospheric light and transmission maps. The proposed framework integrates a multi-scale feature extraction module and an attention mechanism to enhance the accuracy of haze density classification. Experimental results on both synthetic and real-world hazy images demonstrate that our approach achieves superior performance compared to state-of-the-art dehazing algorithms, particularly in preserving image details and reducing color distortion. Our method contributes a new perspective on dehazing by incorporating classification as an integral component, offering a robust and effective solution for improving image visibility.

**Keywords:** Single Image Dehazing, Image Classification, Convolutional Neural Network, Haze Density Estimation, Image Restoration, Atmospheric Light, Transmission Map.
