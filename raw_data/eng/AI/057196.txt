This paper introduces ODVICE, an innovative ontology-driven visual analytic tool designed to facilitate interactive cohort extraction. The primary objective of ODVICE is to address the challenges of identifying and extracting relevant patient cohorts from large-scale electronic health records (EHRs) by leveraging the power of ontologies and visual analytics. ODVICE employs a unique approach that combines semantic querying, data visualization, and human-in-the-loop interaction to enable users to intuitively explore and refine cohort definitions. The tool utilizes a robust ontology framework to provide a standardized and shareable representation of clinical concepts, ensuring consistency and accuracy in cohort extraction. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of ODVICE in extracting accurate and relevant cohorts, outperforming traditional methods in terms of precision and recall. The key contributions of this research include the development of a novel ontology-driven visual analytic framework, the integration of semantic querying and data visualization, and the evaluation of ODVICE in real-world clinical scenarios. ODVICE has significant implications for clinical research, personalized medicine, and healthcare quality improvement, enabling researchers and clinicians to efficiently identify and analyze patient cohorts. Key keywords: ontology-driven analytics, visual analytics, cohort extraction, electronic health records, clinical decision support.
