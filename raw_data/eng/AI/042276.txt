This paper explores the concept of diversity in deep learning models for image segmentation, with the objective of improving segmentation accuracy and robustness. We propose a novel approach that leverages ensemble methods to combine the predictions of multiple diverse models, each trained with different architectures, hyperparameters, and training data. Our method utilizes a diversity-promoting loss function to encourage the models to produce distinct predictions, which are then aggregated using a consensus-based approach. Experimental results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art image segmentation models, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and reducing overfitting. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of diversity in deep learning models for image segmentation, and our approach has potential applications in medical imaging, autonomous driving, and other computer vision tasks. The contributions of this work include the development of a novel diversity-promoting loss function and a consensus-based aggregation method, which can be applied to a wide range of image segmentation tasks. Key keywords: image segmentation, diversity, ensemble methods, deep learning, computer vision.
