This study addresses the challenge of predicting healthcare costs by analyzing multivariate time series data derived from patient records. The primary objective is to uncover hidden patterns within the data that can enhance the accuracy and reliability of cost prediction models.

Methods/Approach: We employ advanced Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) to process and learn from the complex structure of multivariate time series data. The CNN architecture is specifically designed to capture intricate temporal patterns and dependencies within the patient data. By leveraging this approach, we focus on improving model performance over traditional methods.

Results/Findings: The experimental results demonstrate that our CNN-based model significantly outperforms existing baseline models in predicting healthcare costs. The model efficiently identifies and utilizes hidden patterns within the data, leading to more precise cost forecasts. Comparative analysis shows an improvement in predictive accuracy, offering enhanced insights for healthcare budgeting and resource allocation.

Conclusion/Implications: This research highlights the potential of CNNs in the healthcare domain, particularly for multivariate time series data analysis. By demonstrating the efficacy of CNNs in predicting healthcare costs, we contribute to advancing predictive modeling techniques in healthcare finance. The study suggests broad applications for similar CNN-based approaches in other areas of healthcare data analysis, potentially aiding in decision-making processes and policy formulation.

Keywords: Convolutional Neural Networks, healthcare cost prediction, multivariate time series, patient data, pattern recognition, predictive modeling.
