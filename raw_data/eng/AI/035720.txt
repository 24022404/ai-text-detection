This paper introduces a novel approach to 3D shape reconstruction from single freehand sketches, leveraging multi-view convolutional networks. The objective is to develop a robust and efficient method for generating accurate 3D models from sparse and abstract 2D sketch input. Our method utilizes a multi-view architecture to synthesize multiple depth maps from a single sketch, which are then fused to produce the final 3D model. Specifically, we employ a convolutional neural network (CNN) trained to predict depth maps corresponding to different viewpoints around the sketched object. A volumetric fusion technique is then applied to integrate these depth maps into a coherent 3D representation. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing sketch-based reconstruction methods, achieving higher accuracy and robustness, particularly for complex shapes. The reconstructed models exhibit finer details and improved overall geometric quality. This work offers a practical solution for rapid 3D content creation, with potential applications in design, modeling, and virtual reality. Key words: 3D reconstruction, sketch-based modeling, convolutional neural networks, multi-view learning, computer vision.
