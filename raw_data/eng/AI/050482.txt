**Abstract:**

Non-stationary time series data present significant challenges in predictive modeling due to their dynamic spectral characteristics and evolving statistical properties. This paper introduces SAFE (Spectral Evolution Analysis Feature Extraction), a novel approach designed to enhance prediction accuracy for non-stationary time series. SAFE leverages spectral analysis techniques to track the evolution of frequency components over time, extracting dynamic features that capture underlying temporal patterns often missed by conventional methods. The proposed framework integrates adaptive windowing and transform-based spectral decomposition to generate robust feature representations from raw time series data. Extensive experiments were conducted on benchmark datasets across diverse application domains, such as financial forecasting and physiological signal prediction. Results demonstrate that models augmented with SAFE features achieve superior predictive performance compared to baseline statistical and deep learning approaches, particularly in highly non-stationary scenarios. The findings highlight SAFE’s effectiveness in capturing transient behaviors and sudden regime shifts, thereby improving the reliability and interpretability of time series forecasting. This work advances the state-of-the-art in non-stationary time series prediction and provides a versatile tool for data-driven analysis in domains characterized by evolving temporal dynamics.  
**Keywords:** non-stationary time series, spectral analysis, feature extraction, prediction, SAFE, time series forecasting, machine learning.
