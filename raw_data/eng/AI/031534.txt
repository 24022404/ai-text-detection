This paper presents a novel approach to multimodal image captioning, leveraging computer vision and natural language processing to analyze marketing images. The objective is to automatically generate descriptive captions for images used in marketing campaigns, enabling more effective analysis of consumer engagement and brand perception. Our approach utilizes a deep learning model that integrates visual features extracted from images with contextual information to produce accurate and informative captions. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our method, outperforming existing image captioning models in terms of caption quality and relevance. The proposed system has significant implications for marketing analysis, allowing for the automated analysis of large datasets of marketing images and providing valuable insights into consumer behavior. Key contributions include the development of a multimodal fusion framework and the introduction of a new evaluation metric for image captioning in marketing contexts. This research has the potential to enhance marketing decision-making and optimize campaign strategies, with applications in advertising, social media, and consumer research.
