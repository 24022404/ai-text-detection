Exploration strategies are critical for balancing the trade-off between exploration and exploitation in reinforcement learning (RL). This paper addresses the challenge of designing efficient exploration methods that can adapt to dynamic environments while maintaining computational simplicity. We propose a novel approach, Temporally-Extended ε-Greedy Exploration (TEE), which extends the traditional ε-greedy strategy by incorporating temporal abstraction. Unlike standard ε-greedy, which selects random actions at each step, TEE leverages temporal extensions to explore sequences of actions over extended time horizons, enabling more systematic and informed exploration. Our method integrates seamlessly with existing RL algorithms and requires minimal additional computational overhead. Experimental results on benchmark environments demonstrate that TEE significantly improves exploration efficiency, leading to faster convergence and higher cumulative rewards compared to baseline methods. The proposed approach is particularly effective in environments with sparse rewards or complex dynamics, where traditional exploration strategies often struggle. This work contributes to the advancement of RL by providing a simple yet powerful exploration technique that enhances learning performance without sacrificing computational efficiency.  

**Keywords**: Reinforcement learning, exploration strategies, ε-greedy, temporal abstraction, dynamic environments, sparse rewards.
