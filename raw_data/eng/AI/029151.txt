This paper introduces DeepSaliency, a novel multi-task deep neural network model designed for salient object detection. The objective is to accurately identify and segment salient objects within images, addressing the challenges of complex scenes and variable object sizes. Our approach leverages a dual-branch architecture, combining the strengths of both local and global feature extraction to enhance detection accuracy. The model is trained on a large-scale dataset with multiple tasks, including saliency map prediction, object boundary detection, and category recognition. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of DeepSaliency, outperforming state-of-the-art models in terms of precision, recall, and F-measure. The key contributions of this research lie in its ability to capture contextual relationships between objects and its robustness to various image conditions. With potential applications in image processing, computer vision, and artificial intelligence, DeepSaliency offers a significant advancement in salient object detection, enabling improved performance in tasks such as image segmentation, object recognition, and visual attention modeling. Key keywords: salient object detection, deep neural networks, multi-task learning, computer vision, artificial intelligence.
