This paper addresses the challenge of effective video retrieval, a task that has become increasingly important with the rapid growth of video data. We focus on enhancing retrieval accuracy by efficiently capturing and analyzing temporal dependencies within video sequences. 

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel graph-based temporal aggregation approach for video retrieval. Our method constructs a temporal graph where each node represents a frame or segment of the video, and edges capture temporal relationships. We utilize graph convolutional networks to process these temporal graphs, extracting meaningful patterns and representations that better reflect the video's dynamic content compared to traditional frame-based methods.

Results/Findings: Our experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach significantly improves retrieval accuracy over several benchmark datasets. We compare our method with existing video retrieval techniques such as CNN and RNN-based models, highlighting superior performance in both precision and recall metrics. The proposed graph-based model effectively handles complex temporal patterns, offering robustness in capturing temporal dynamics.

Conclusion/Implications: Our research provides a significant contribution to the field of video retrieval by introducing a graph-based perspective for temporal modeling. This innovative approach not only enhances retrieval performance but also paves the way for further exploration of graph-based techniques in video analysis. Potential applications include video search engines, content recommendation, and multimedia data mining. Key Keywords: Video Retrieval, Graph-Based Approach, Temporal Aggregation, Graph Convolutional Networks, Video Analysis.
