The accurate counting of people in retail environments is critical for optimizing operations, improving customer experience, and enhancing security measures. This paper introduces LRCN-RetailNet, a novel recurrent neural network (RNN) architecture designed to improve the precision of people counting in dynamic retail settings.

Methods/Approach: LRCN-RetailNet integrates Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks with Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) to capture both spatial and temporal features essential for accurate people counting. By leveraging video data from retail environments, the architecture processes sequences of frames to extract meaningful patterns associated with people movement and density. This hybrid approach allows for the effective handling of occlusions and variances in crowd density.

Results/Findings: Empirical evaluations of LRCN-RetailNet demonstrate substantial enhancements in accuracy over existing models, particularly in challenging conditions involving high-density crowds and complex backgrounds. In comparison to standard CNN and RNN models, LRCN-RetailNet achieved a significant reduction in counting errors, proving its robustness and reliability across different retail scenarios.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of LRCN-RetailNet offers significant advancements in the field of people counting technologies within retail applications. The architecture's unique combination of LSTM and CNN elements enables superior performance in real-time data processing and analysis. This research contributes to the development of smarter retail environments and paves the way for future improvements in automated people counting systems, with potential applications extending to urban planning and public event management.

Keywords: LRCN-RetailNet, recurrent neural network, people counting, retail environments, LSTM, CNN, spatial-temporal analysis.
