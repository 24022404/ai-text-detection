A hierarchical model comprising solution, criteria, and target layers is employed to allocate tickets to staff members by determining values for kv, kc, and ke. The target layer is responsible for setting the specific values of these parameters, while the criteria layer evaluates factors such as ticket running time, ticket value, and energy consumption. The solution layer then generates five different sets of kv, kc, and ke, corresponding to five staff members, illustrating the practical application of the model in ticket assignment.
