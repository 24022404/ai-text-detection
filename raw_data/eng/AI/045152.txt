Accurate taxi demand forecasting is crucial for efficient resource allocation and traffic management in urban environments. This paper introduces a novel tessellation strategy based on Hierarchical Equal Division Grid Encoding (HEDGE) to improve the accuracy of taxi demand prediction. Unlike traditional grid-based methods, HEDGE adaptively partitions the city into regions of varying sizes based on demand density, allowing for finer granularity in high-demand areas and coarser granularity in low-demand areas. We propose a deep learning model that leverages this HEDGE-based tessellation to forecast taxi demand in each region. Experimental results on a real-world taxi dataset demonstrate that our HEDGE-based approach significantly outperforms existing tessellation methods and improves overall forecasting accuracy. The adaptive nature of HEDGE also enhances the model's ability to capture spatiotemporal patterns in taxi demand. Our findings suggest that HEDGE tessellation offers a promising strategy for improving the performance of taxi demand forecasting systems, contributing to smarter and more efficient urban transportation.

Keywords: Taxi demand forecasting, tessellation, HEDGE, deep learning, spatiotemporal analysis, urban transportation.
