This paper addresses the challenge of improving teaching effectiveness in educational settings by leveraging student feedback through machine learning techniques. The objective is to develop a system that dynamically adapts teaching strategies based on real-time student performance and feedback, thereby enhancing learning outcomes. We propose a novel approach that integrates natural language processing (NLP) and reinforcement learning to analyze student feedback and optimize instructional methods. The system employs a feedback loop where student responses are continuously evaluated to refine teaching models. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method significantly improves student engagement and comprehension compared to traditional static teaching approaches. Key findings indicate a 20% increase in student performance metrics and a 15% reduction in learning gaps. The implications of this research extend to personalized education, adaptive learning systems, and the broader application of AI in educational technology. This work contributes to the growing field of intelligent tutoring systems by introducing a scalable and data-driven framework for teaching optimization.  

**Keywords**: adaptive learning, student feedback, reinforcement learning, natural language processing, intelligent tutoring systems, personalized education.
