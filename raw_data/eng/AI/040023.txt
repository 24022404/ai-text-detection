This paper introduces RSINet, a novel online visual tracking network designed to achieve robustness against significant rotation and scale variations. The primary objective is to address the limitations of existing trackers that often struggle with drastic appearance changes due to these geometric transformations. RSINet employs a unique feature extraction module that incorporates rotation-scale invariant convolutional filters, enabling the network to learn representations that are less sensitive to such variations. Furthermore, we introduce an adaptive template update strategy that dynamically adjusts the template based on the estimated rotation and scale, improving tracking accuracy over time. Experimental results on challenging benchmark datasets, including VOT2018 and TrackingNet, demonstrate that RSINet significantly outperforms state-of-the-art trackers in terms of accuracy and robustness, particularly in scenarios with severe rotation and scale changes. The proposed approach achieves a favorable balance between tracking performance and computational efficiency, making it suitable for real-time applications. Keywords: Visual Tracking, Rotation Invariance, Scale Invariance, Deep Learning, Convolutional Neural Networks.
