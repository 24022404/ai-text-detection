This paper addresses the challenges of image deblurring and super-resolution, aiming to enhance the quality of degraded images by recovering fine details and improving resolution. Traditional methods often struggle with balancing computational efficiency and reconstruction accuracy, particularly in handling diverse image structures and noise levels. To overcome these limitations, we propose a novel framework that integrates *Adaptive Sparse Domain Selection* (ASDS) and *Adaptive Regularization* (AR). The ASDS component dynamically selects the most appropriate sparse domain for different image patches, ensuring optimal representation of local structures. Meanwhile, the AR mechanism adaptively adjusts regularization parameters based on local image characteristics, effectively suppressing noise and artifacts while preserving edges and textures. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms state-of-the-art techniques in terms of both visual quality and quantitative metrics, such as PSNR and SSIM. The framework is computationally efficient, making it suitable for real-world applications in medical imaging, surveillance, and multimedia. This research contributes to the field by introducing a flexible and robust approach that adapts to varying image conditions, offering significant improvements in image restoration tasks.  

**Keywords**: Image deblurring, super-resolution, adaptive sparse domain selection, adaptive regularization, image restoration, PSNR, SSIM.
