This paper presents a novel approach to predicting genetic inheritance patterns using explainable artificial intelligence (AI) techniques. The objective is to develop a transparent and trustworthy model that can accurately forecast the likelihood of inheriting specific genetic traits. Our method employs a combination of machine learning algorithms and genomic data analysis to identify complex patterns in genetic inheritance. The results show a significant improvement in prediction accuracy compared to existing methods, with a mean accuracy of 92% in predicting single-gene disorders. The use of explainable AI techniques, such as feature attribution and model interpretability, provides valuable insights into the underlying genetic mechanisms and facilitates the identification of key genetic markers. The implications of this research are substantial, enabling personalized medicine, genetic counseling, and targeted interventions. Key contributions include the development of a novel explainable AI framework for genetic inheritance pattern prediction, improved prediction accuracy, and enhanced model interpretability. Relevant keywords: explainable AI, genetic inheritance, machine learning, genomic data analysis, predictive modeling, personalized medicine.
