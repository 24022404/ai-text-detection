This paper introduces DesnowNet, a novel context-aware deep network designed to effectively remove snow from images. The objective is to develop a robust and efficient method for restoring original scene information from snow-covered images, which is crucial for various applications such as autonomous driving, surveillance, and environmental monitoring. Our approach leverages a multi-scale convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture that incorporates contextual information to accurately detect and remove snow while preserving image details. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of DesnowNet over existing state-of-the-art methods, with significant improvements in terms of visual quality and objective metrics. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a context-aware module that captures long-range dependencies and a snow removal loss function that optimizes the restoration process. DesnowNet has potential applications in image processing, computer vision, and remote sensing, particularly in scenarios where snow removal is essential for accurate analysis and decision-making. Key keywords: snow removal, deep learning, context-aware network, image restoration, computer vision.
