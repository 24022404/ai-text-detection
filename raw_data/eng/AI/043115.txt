The rapid growth of deep learning models has led to significant challenges in deploying them on resource-constrained devices due to their high computational and memory requirements. This paper addresses the problem of network compression while maintaining model performance and robustness. We propose a novel adversarial network compression framework that leverages adversarial training to optimize the trade-off between model size, inference speed, and accuracy. Our approach integrates a lightweight discriminator network to guide the compression process, ensuring that the compressed model retains its ability to generalize effectively under adversarial conditions. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art compression ratios while preserving competitive accuracy and robustness compared to existing techniques. The proposed framework is particularly effective for edge computing and IoT applications, where efficient and reliable model deployment is critical. This work contributes a scalable and adaptive solution to the growing demand for compact yet robust deep learning models, paving the way for broader adoption in real-world scenarios.  

**Keywords**: network compression, adversarial training, deep learning, model optimization, edge computing, IoT.
