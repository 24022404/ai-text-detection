This paper introduces MeNToS, a novel approach for tracklets association in multi-object tracking, leveraging a space-time memory network. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of tracking objects across frames in video sequences. MeNToS employs a deep learning-based method, utilizing a memory-augmented neural network that captures spatial and temporal relationships between tracklets. The approach integrates a space-time memory module to store and retrieve relevant information, enabling the association of tracklets across time. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of MeNToS, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of tracking accuracy and speed. The key findings highlight the importance of incorporating spatial and temporal context in tracklets association, leading to improved performance in complex scenarios. MeNToS contributes to the field of computer vision and multi-object tracking, with potential applications in surveillance, autonomous vehicles, and robotics. Key keywords: tracklets association, space-time memory network, multi-object tracking, deep learning, computer vision.
