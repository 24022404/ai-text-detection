This paper proposes a novel Feature Selective Anchor-Free (FSAF) module designed to enhance the performance of single-shot object detection models. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of object detection by selectively focusing on the most relevant features. Our approach employs a unique anchor-free mechanism, which eliminates the need for predefined anchor boxes, thereby reducing computational complexity. The FSAF module utilizes a feature selective mechanism to adaptively weigh the importance of different features, allowing the model to concentrate on the most informative regions of the input image. Experimental results demonstrate that our FSAF module outperforms state-of-the-art anchor-based and anchor-free methods, achieving significant improvements in detection accuracy and speed. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a feature selective mechanism and an anchor-free approach, which collectively enhance the robustness and efficiency of single-shot object detection models. This work has important implications for applications such as autonomous driving, surveillance, and robotics, where fast and accurate object detection is crucial. Key keywords: single-shot object detection, anchor-free, feature selective, object detection, computer vision, deep learning.
