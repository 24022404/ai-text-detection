**Abstract**

Accurate forecasting in multivariate time series is essential for decision-making in various real-world domains such as healthcare, finance, and sensor networks. However, many practical datasets are characterized by irregular sampling intervals and substantial missing values, which pose significant challenges to conventional forecasting models designed for regularly sampled and complete data. This paper addresses the problem of forecasting in multivariate, irregularly sampled time series with missing values by proposing a novel approach that integrates temporal interpolation mechanisms with advanced machine learning architectures. Our method leverages temporal embedding layers and an attention-based recurrent neural network to effectively capture temporal dependencies and relationships among multiple variables, even in the presence of missing or irregularly spaced data points. Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our model significantly outperforms traditional imputation-based pipelines and state-of-the-art sequence models in terms of forecasting accuracy and robustness to missingness. The results highlight the ability of the proposed framework to handle complex, real-world time series scenarios without reliance on heavy pre-processing or data regularization routines. Our findings offer new insights into model architectures tailored for challenging time series data and open opportunities for reliable forecasting in domains where data irregularities and incompleteness are common. Keywords: time series forecasting, multivariate analysis, irregular sampling, missing data, attention mechanism, neural networks.
