This paper introduces a comprehensive benchmark for Image Scene Graph Generation (SGG), a crucial task in computer vision that involves generating a graphical representation of objects and their relationships within an image. The objective is to evaluate and compare the performance of various SGG models, promoting advancements in this field. Our approach involves creating a large-scale dataset with annotated scene graphs, which serves as a standard testbed for SGG algorithms. We propose a novel evaluation metric that assesses both object detection and relationship prediction accuracy. The results show that state-of-the-art SGG models achieve impressive performance on our benchmark, with significant improvements in recall and precision. Our benchmark highlights the importance of contextual understanding and relationship modeling in SGG, contributing to the development of more accurate and robust computer vision systems. The introduced benchmark has far-reaching implications for applications such as image captioning, visual question answering, and autonomous driving, where scene understanding is critical. Key keywords: Scene Graph Generation, Computer Vision, Object Detection, Relationship Prediction, Benchmarking.
