Deep Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) have achieved remarkable success in various computer vision tasks, yet their decision-making processes often remain opaque. This paper introduces *Smooth Grad-CAM++*, an enhanced inference-level visualization technique designed to improve the interpretability of CNN models. The proposed method builds upon Grad-CAM++ by incorporating a smoothing mechanism that reduces noise in the generated visual explanations, resulting in more precise and reliable heatmaps. The approach integrates gradient-weighted class activation maps with a Gaussian smoothing filter, enabling clearer identification of the regions most influential to the model's predictions. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets, including ImageNet and CIFAR-10, demonstrate that Smooth Grad-CAM++ outperforms existing visualization techniques in terms of localization accuracy and visual coherence. The results highlight its effectiveness in providing intuitive insights into model behavior, particularly for complex architectures like ResNet and VGG. This work contributes to the growing field of explainable AI by offering a robust tool for researchers and practitioners to better understand and trust deep learning models. Potential applications include medical imaging, autonomous systems, and other domains where interpretability is critical.  

**Keywords**: Explainable AI, Grad-CAM++, Convolutional Neural Networks, Visualization Techniques, Deep Learning, Interpretability.
