Text-based person re-identification (re-ID) aims to match pedestrian images with textual descriptions, a challenging task due to cross-modal variations and semantic gaps. This paper introduces a novel Dual-path Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) architecture enhanced with a Max Gated block to improve the accuracy and robustness of text-based person re-ID. The proposed model leverages two parallel CNN pathways to extract fine-grained features from both images and text descriptions. A Max Gated block is then integrated to adaptively weigh and fuse features from different layers, focusing on the most relevant information. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed Dual-path CNN with Max Gated block outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in retrieval accuracy. The model effectively captures nuanced semantic relationships between visual and textual data, providing a more discriminative feature representation for person re-identification. This work contributes a novel architecture for cross-modal learning, particularly benefiting scenarios where visual data is complemented by textual descriptions. Keywords: person re-identification, text-image retrieval, convolutional neural networks, cross-modal learning, gated networks.
