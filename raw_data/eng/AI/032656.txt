This paper presents DatasetGAN, a novel framework designed to generate high-quality labeled datasets with minimal human annotation effort. Traditional supervised learning approaches are heavily dependent on large volumes of accurately labeled data, a process that is time-consuming and resource-intensive. DatasetGAN addresses this challenge by leveraging generative adversarial networks (GANs) to synthesize realistic images while simultaneously producing detailed semantic labels. The proposed approach integrates a small set of manually annotated examples and utilizes a segmentation network trained in tandem with the GAN to propagate labels to generated samples. Experimental evaluation across multiple benchmark datasets demonstrates that DatasetGAN-generated data significantly enhances the performance of downstream tasks, such as image classification and object segmentation, reducing the need for extensive manual labeling. Furthermore, qualitative and quantitative results show that DatasetGAN maintains high fidelity and consistency in both image quality and annotation accuracy. This work highlights the potential for DatasetGAN to streamline data preparation processes in machine learning pipelines, making it a valuable tool for applications in computer vision, AI model training, and large-scale data annotation. Key keywords: dataset generation, GAN, automatic labeling, semantic segmentation, data augmentation, computer vision.
