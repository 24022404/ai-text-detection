This paper introduces FCOS3D, a novel approach to monocular 3D object detection that leverages a fully convolutional one-stage framework. The objective is to accurately detect and localize objects in 3D space from a single RGB image, addressing the challenges of depth estimation and orientation prediction. Our method employs a dense prediction strategy, utilizing a convolutional neural network to predict 3D object locations, dimensions, and orientations in a single pass. The results demonstrate significant improvements over existing two-stage and anchor-based methods, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets. Key findings include the effectiveness of our approach in handling objects with varying sizes, shapes, and orientations, as well as its robustness to occlusions and truncations. The contributions of this research lie in its ability to simplify the 3D object detection pipeline, reducing computational overhead and improving real-time capabilities. With potential applications in autonomous driving, robotics, and augmented reality, FCOS3D paves the way for more efficient and accurate 3D perception systems, leveraging advancements in computer vision, deep learning, and 3D reconstruction. Relevant keywords: monocular 3D object detection, fully convolutional networks, one-stage detection, depth estimation, orientation prediction, computer vision, deep learning.
