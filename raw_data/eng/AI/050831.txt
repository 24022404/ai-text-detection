This paper presents ADA, a novel game-theoretic approach to data augmentation for object detection. The objective is to improve the robustness and accuracy of object detection models by strategically augmenting training data. We employ a game-theoretic framework to model the interaction between the detector and the data augmentation process, allowing us to optimize augmentation strategies that maximize detection performance. Our approach utilizes a minimax algorithm to balance the trade-off between detection accuracy and augmentation complexity. Experimental results demonstrate that ADA outperforms state-of-the-art data augmentation methods, achieving significant improvements in detection accuracy and robustness to adversarial attacks. The key contributions of this work include a novel game-theoretic formulation of data augmentation and a robust optimization algorithm for object detection. Our research has important implications for computer vision applications, particularly in scenarios where data is limited or noisy. Key keywords: object detection, data augmentation, game theory, computer vision, robustness, adversarial attacks.
