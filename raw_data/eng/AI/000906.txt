This paper introduces StickyPillars, a novel approach to robust and efficient feature matching on point clouds using Graph Neural Networks (GNNs). The objective is to address the challenges of feature matching in 3D point cloud data, which is crucial for various applications such as 3D reconstruction, object recognition, and robotics. Our method leverages the power of GNNs to learn robust and discriminative features from point clouds, enabling accurate and efficient feature matching. The StickyPillars approach utilizes a graph-based architecture to model the relationships between points in the cloud, allowing for the capture of complex geometric structures and robustness to noise and variations. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of StickyPillars over state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in feature matching accuracy and efficiency. The proposed approach has important implications for various 3D vision tasks, including 3D registration, tracking, and scene understanding. Key contributions of this work include the introduction of a novel GNN-based architecture for feature matching on point clouds, and the demonstration of its robustness and efficiency in real-world applications. Relevant keywords: point clouds, feature matching, graph neural networks, 3D vision, robotics.
