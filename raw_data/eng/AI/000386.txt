Camouflaged object detection (COD) is a challenging task due to the subtle appearance differences between objects and their surroundings. This paper introduces a novel approach leveraging deep texture-aware features to enhance the detection of camouflaged objects. Our method incorporates a texture encoding module within a deep convolutional neural network to explicitly capture fine-grained textural details, which are often overlooked by standard feature extraction methods. This module learns to distinguish subtle texture variations indicative of camouflage boundaries. We further introduce a texture-aware fusion strategy to effectively integrate these texture features with semantic features from the deep network. Experimental results on several benchmark COD datasets demonstrate that our proposed method achieves state-of-the-art performance, significantly outperforming existing approaches in terms of detection accuracy and robustness. The improved performance highlights the importance of texture information for accurately identifying camouflaged objects. Keywords: Camouflaged Object Detection, Texture Features, Deep Learning, Feature Fusion, Computer Vision.
