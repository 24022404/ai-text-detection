In the context of the centralized system analyzed in the thesis, the conventional Entity-Relationship (ER) diagram is not entirely sufficient to capture the unique aspects of metadata storage leveraged by the smart contract. Rather than relying on traditional database modeling, the system adopts a distinct approach in which the smart contract serves as the primary repository for all metadata related to user identities and encryption keys (encKeys). By utilizing a key-value pair structure, the smart contract essentially operates as a decentralized database on the blockchain, enabling efficient and direct retrieval of data. This design not only supports improved access to information but also upholds critical attributes of blockchain technology, such as data integrity and immutability, especially crucial when managing sensitive metadata.
