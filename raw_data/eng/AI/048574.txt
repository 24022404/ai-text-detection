**Abstract:**

Bid rigging undermines the integrity of procurement processes and results in significant economic losses worldwide. Traditional detection methods are often labor-intensive and may not effectively identify subtle collusive behaviors within large datasets. This paper proposes a deep learning-based approach for detecting bid rigging, leveraging convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to automatically flag potential cartel participants. Our method transforms bidder transaction data into structured representations suitable for input into CNN architectures, enabling the model to capture complex patterns and anomalies associated with collusive activities. Using real-world procurement datasets and simulated cartel scenarios, we evaluate the performance of our model against conventional statistical and machine learning techniques. The results demonstrate that the proposed CNN approach achieves superior detection accuracy and demonstrates robust generalization across diverse bidding environments. Key contributions include an end-to-end automated framework for bid rigging detection, feature representation techniques tailored to procurement data, and systematic performance evaluation of deep learning models for anti-cartel enforcement. This research provides valuable insights for regulatory authorities, procurement professionals, and researchers, showcasing the potential of artificial intelligence in promoting fair and competitive markets. **Keywords:** deep learning, bid rigging detection, cartel flagging, convolutional neural networks, procurement analytics, anti-collusion, artificial intelligence.
