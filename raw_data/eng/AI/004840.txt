Model-based reinforcement learning (MBRL) offers enhanced sample efficiency compared to model-free methods, yet struggles with sparse reward environments. Hindsight Experience Replay (HER) effectively addresses sparse rewards in model-free settings by re-labeling failed trajectories with achieved goals. This paper introduces Model-based Hindsight Experience Replay (MHER), a novel algorithm that integrates HER within an MBRL framework. MHHER leverages a learned environment model to generate synthetic experiences with hindsight goals, augmenting the replay buffer and accelerating learning. We propose a modified training scheme for the environment model to ensure high-quality hindsight experiences. Experimental results across a suite of challenging robotic manipulation tasks demonstrate that MHHER significantly outperforms state-of-the-art MBRL and model-free HER baselines, particularly in environments with extremely sparse rewards. The improvement in sample efficiency and success rate highlights the synergistic benefits of combining model-based learning with hindsight experience replay. This approach offers a promising direction for tackling complex, real-world reinforcement learning problems.
Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Model-Based Reinforcement Learning, Hindsight Experience Replay, Robotics, Sparse Rewards.
