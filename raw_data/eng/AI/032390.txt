Assessing the robustness of image recognition models to variations in object properties remains a significant challenge. This paper introduces SI-Score, a novel image dataset designed for fine-grained evaluation of model sensitivity to object location, rotation, and size. SI-Score comprises synthetically generated images featuring common objects under controlled variations in these geometric attributes. We utilize SI-Score to evaluate several state-of-the-art deep learning models, revealing performance degradation under specific transformations, particularly concerning combined rotations and scale changes. Our analysis demonstrates that SI-Score facilitates precise identification of model weaknesses, offering insights beyond standard benchmark datasets. The dataset enables researchers to quantify the impact of specific geometric variations, guiding the development of more robust and generalizable vision models. SI-Score is publicly available to promote further research in robust computer vision.
Keywords: Robustness, Image Recognition, Dataset, Object Location, Object Rotation, Object Size, Deep Learning.
