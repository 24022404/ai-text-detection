The increasing adoption of deep learning classifiers in critical applications necessitates a rigorous assessment of their reliability under diverse operational conditions. This study addresses the challenge of evaluating the robustness of deep learning models by integrating robustness evaluation techniques with operational profiles. We propose a systematic framework that combines adversarial attack simulations, noise injection, and domain-specific operational scenarios to assess classifier performance in real-world settings. Our approach leverages state-of-the-art adversarial training methods and introduces a novel metric for quantifying robustness across varying operational conditions. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our framework effectively identifies vulnerabilities and provides actionable insights for improving model reliability. Compared to existing methods, our approach offers a more comprehensive evaluation, highlighting the trade-offs between accuracy and robustness. The findings underscore the importance of incorporating operational profiles into reliability assessments, enabling the development of more resilient deep learning systems. This research contributes to advancing the field of trustworthy AI by providing a practical methodology for evaluating and enhancing the reliability of deep learning classifiers in safety-critical applications.  

**Keywords**: deep learning, robustness evaluation, operational profiles, adversarial attacks, reliability assessment, trustworthy AI.
