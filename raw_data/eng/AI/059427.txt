This research addresses the challenge of localizing hue modifications within digital images, a crucial aspect for various applications such as digital forensics and image editing. Precise identification of localized color alterations can enable better image analysis and authentication.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel method named Hue Modification Localization By Pair Matching, which leverages a pairwise comparison strategy to detect discrepancies in hue across different image regions. The approach involves analyzing pixel pairs to determine variations in hue characteristics, utilizing advanced image processing techniques to assess and match hue patterns effectively.

Results/Findings: Our system was rigorously evaluated on a diverse set of images with both subtle and pronounced hue modifications. The proposed method demonstrated superiority in accuracy and efficiency over existing localization techniques, successfully identifying localized hue changes even under challenging conditions such as varied lighting and compression artifacts. Quantitative measurements showed improved precision and recall rates, confirming the robustness of our approach.

Conclusion/Implications: This research provides a significant advancement in the field of image analysis by presenting a reliable and efficient method for hue modification localization. The findings have important implications for applications requiring image authentication and integrity checks, enhancing the reliability and security of digital content. Our approach opens new possibilities for further innovations in detecting and understanding visual alterations in digital imagery.

Keywords: Hue Modification, Localization, Pair Matching, Image Processing, Digital Forensics, Image Authentication, Visual Alterations.
