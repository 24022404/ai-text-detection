This paper explores the concept of texture transfer for single image super-resolution, a technique that leverages the power of texture information to enhance image resolution. The objective is to investigate the effectiveness of this approach in improving the quality of low-resolution images. Our method utilizes a novel texture transfer algorithm that combines the benefits of deep learning-based super-resolution models with traditional texture synthesis techniques. The results show that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art super-resolution methods, particularly in scenarios where texture information is rich and diverse. Key findings include significant improvements in peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM) metrics. The implications of this research are substantial, as it demonstrates the potential for texture transfer to become a crucial component in single image super-resolution pipelines, enabling applications such as image and video enhancement, surveillance, and medical imaging. Our contributions highlight the importance of texture information in image super-resolution and pave the way for future research in this area, with relevant keywords including single image super-resolution, texture transfer, deep learning, and image enhancement.
