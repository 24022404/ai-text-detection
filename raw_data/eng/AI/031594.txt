The set W(n, s) consists of all s-RLL sequences of length n, which has been widely studied in the literature. Its cardinality is given recursively, as described in Lemma 1: if n is less than or equal to s, then every sequence of length n is included in W(n, s). For n greater than s, each sequence has a unique representation in the form 0^i1x, where x belongs to W(n−i−1, s) and i ranges from 0 to s. This representation establishes a bijection, resulting in a recursive formula.
