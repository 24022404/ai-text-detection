The production of high-quality bokeh effects in digital images is a challenging task that plays a vital role in enhancing visual aesthetics and depth perception. This paper addresses the problem of generating realistic bokeh effects by proposing a novel depth-aware blending technique that leverages smoothed images to achieve this visual enhancement.

Methods/Approach: The proposed method involves a multi-step process where input images are first smoothed using advanced image processing algorithms to segregate objects based on varying depth levels. A depth estimation module is incorporated to accurately assess the spatial hierarchy of objects within the scene. The algorithm then blends these smoothed images, applying a depth-aware fusion strategy to produce a convincing bokeh effect. This technique leverages computational photography principles, ensuring that the foreground elements remain sharp while the background is artistically blurred.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate that our depth-aware blending algorithm significantly outperforms traditional bokeh generation techniques in maintaining image clarity and depth consistency. Comparative analysis with existing methods shows notable improvements in edge preservation and the quality of the bokeh gradient when handling complex scenes with varying depth layers.

Conclusion/Implications: The research contributes to the domain of computational photography by providing a robust framework for generating enhanced bokeh effects, with potential applications in smartphone photography, digital content creation, and immersive media experiences. The technique's ability to produce aesthetically pleasing images with accurate depth representation opens new pathways for future developments in image processing and enhancement technologies.

Keywords: Bokeh effect, depth-aware blending, image smoothing, computational photography, depth estimation, image processing.
