Monocular 3D object detection is a challenging task in computer vision due to the inherent loss of depth information. This paper introduces a novel approach to monocular 3D object detection that eliminates the reliance on extrinsic camera parameters. Our method leverages geometric constraints and deep learning techniques to directly estimate 3D bounding box parameters from a single RGB image. Specifically, we propose a new architecture that decouples the estimation of object pose and dimensions, enforcing consistency through a learned geometric reasoning module. This module predicts relative spatial relationships between objects, enabling accurate 3D localization without explicit camera calibration. We evaluate our approach on the KITTI dataset, demonstrating significant improvements in 3D detection accuracy compared to existing methods, especially in scenarios with inaccurate or unavailable extrinsic parameters. The results highlight the robustness and generalizability of our extrinsic parameter-free framework, paving the way for wider deployment of 3D perception in diverse environments.

**Keywords:** Monocular 3D Object Detection, Extrinsic Parameter Free, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Geometric Reasoning.
