This paper reviews current visual tracking algorithms and analyzes their suitability for mobile robot applications. The objective is to provide a comprehensive overview of the landscape of visual trackers, focusing on those capable of real-time performance and robustness in dynamic environments. The review covers both traditional correlation filter-based methods and more recent deep learning-based approaches, evaluating their strengths and weaknesses. A comparative analysis is conducted, considering factors such as accuracy, computational cost, and robustness to occlusion, illumination changes, and motion blur. Furthermore, the paper investigates the practical implementation challenges of deploying these trackers on mobile robots, including sensor integration, computational constraints, and power efficiency. Experimental results, obtained using a simulated mobile robot platform, highlight the performance of selected trackers under different operating conditions. The findings offer valuable insights for researchers and practitioners aiming to integrate visual tracking into mobile robot navigation and perception systems, ultimately contributing to the development of more autonomous and reliable robotic platforms. Keywords: Visual tracking, mobile robot, object tracking, robot vision, real-time tracking, deep learning, correlation filter.
