This paper introduces SelfAugment, a novel approach to automatic augmentation policy generation for self-supervised learning. The objective is to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of self-supervised learning methods by leveraging automatic augmentation policies. Our approach utilizes a reinforcement learning framework to learn augmentation policies that maximize the representation quality of the model. The method involves training an agent to select and combine augmentation operations, such as rotation, scaling, and color jittering, to create a diverse set of augmented views. Experimental results demonstrate that SelfAugment outperforms existing self-supervised learning methods, achieving state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. The key findings include improved representation quality, increased robustness to domain shifts, and enhanced transferability to downstream tasks. The proposed approach has significant implications for self-supervised learning, enabling more efficient and effective pre-training of deep neural networks. Key contributions include the introduction of a reinforcement learning-based augmentation policy generation framework and the demonstration of its effectiveness in improving self-supervised learning outcomes. Relevant keywords: self-supervised learning, automatic augmentation, reinforcement learning, deep learning, representation learning.
