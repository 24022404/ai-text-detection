This paper introduces DeepPrivacy, a novel generative adversarial network (GAN) designed for face anonymization. The objective is to protect individual privacy in images and videos by automatically detecting and anonymizing faces, while preserving the original scene context. Our approach utilizes a combination of detection and generation networks to identify faces and replace them with synthetic, anonymous faces that maintain the same pose, expression, and lighting conditions. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of DeepPrivacy in anonymizing faces in various scenarios, outperforming existing methods in terms of image quality and anonymity. The proposed system has significant implications for privacy protection in surveillance, social media, and data sharing applications. By leveraging advances in GANs and face synthesis, DeepPrivacy contributes to the development of more secure and private visual data processing pipelines, with potential applications in AI, computer vision, and data privacy. Key keywords: face anonymization, generative adversarial networks, privacy protection, computer vision, AI models.
