This paper presents a novel approach for articulated shape matching, addressing the challenge of aligning non-rigid objects with varying poses. Our method leverages Laplacian eigenfunctions to establish correspondences between shapes, exploiting their isometry-invariant properties to capture intrinsic shape characteristics. We introduce an unsupervised point registration algorithm that refines initial correspondences derived from the spectral analysis. This registration process optimizes shape alignment without requiring pre-defined landmarks or manual intervention, enhancing robustness to noise and shape variations. The proposed approach demonstrates superior performance in accurately matching articulated shapes compared to existing methods, achieving higher registration accuracy and improved shape retrieval rates. Key contributions include the development of a robust spectral matching framework and an efficient unsupervised registration technique. The method has broad applications in computer graphics, animation, and medical image analysis, enabling more accurate and automated shape analysis. Keywords: Shape Matching, Articulated Shapes, Laplacian Eigenfunctions, Spectral Analysis, Point Registration, Unsupervised Learning.
