The paper addresses the challenge of short-term load forecasting in energy systems, which is crucial for effective grid management and reliable power supply. The study proposes a novel approach that leverages reinforcement learning to dynamically select models that enhance forecasting accuracy.

Methods/Approach: This research introduces a Reinforcement Learning (RL) framework that optimizes model selection in real-time, based on changing load conditions. The RL agent learns to choose among multiple predictive models, adapting to variations in data patterns. The models evaluated include traditional time series forecasting methods and contemporary machine learning techniques, enabling a hybrid approach that optimizes performance.

Results/Findings: The proposed RL-based model selection approach significantly improves forecasting accuracy when compared to static model applications. Empirical evaluations conducted on real-world load data demonstrate superior performance in terms of prediction accuracy and adaptability. The dynamic selection mechanism adapts to different scenarios and load variances, offering improvements over conventional fixed model strategies.

Conclusion/Implications: The study highlights the potential of reinforcement learning in enhancing load forecasting by intelligently and dynamically selecting the most suitable models based on current conditions. This approach not only improves accuracy but also increases the robustness and adaptability of load forecasting techniques. The implications extend to optimizing energy management and planning, supporting the integration of renewable energy sources, and contributing to more stable and efficient power systems.

Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Dynamic Model Selection, Short-Term Load Forecasting, Energy Systems, Predictive Models, Machine Learning, Grid Management.
