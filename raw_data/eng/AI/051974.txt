This paper presents a novel approach to stochastic image-to-video synthesis using conditional Invertible Neural Networks (cINNs). The objective is to generate realistic and diverse video sequences from a given image, capturing the complexity and variability of real-world scenes. Our method leverages the expressive power of cINNs to model the conditional distribution of videos given an image, allowing for efficient and high-quality synthesis. We employ a stochastic framework that combines a conditional prior with a learned likelihood model, enabling the generation of multiple plausible video sequences from a single input image. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming existing methods in terms of video quality, diversity, and realism. Our contributions include a new stochastic image-to-video synthesis framework, a conditional prior model for video generation, and a comprehensive evaluation protocol. The proposed method has potential applications in computer vision, robotics, and multimedia processing, and can be used for tasks such as video prediction, generation, and editing. Key keywords: stochastic image-to-video synthesis, conditional Invertible Neural Networks, video generation, computer vision, deep learning.
