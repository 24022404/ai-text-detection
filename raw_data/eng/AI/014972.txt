This study aims to develop an unstructured mortality prediction model utilizing free-text clinical notes, addressing the limitations of traditional structured data-based approaches. Our approach employs a deep learning-based natural language processing technique to analyze clinical notes and predict patient mortality. The model is trained on a large dataset of clinical notes and evaluated using standard metrics. Results show that our model outperforms existing methods, achieving a significant improvement in prediction accuracy. The study demonstrates the potential of leveraging unstructured clinical data to enhance mortality prediction, enabling early intervention and improved patient outcomes. This research contributes to the growing field of clinical natural language processing, highlighting the importance of incorporating free-text data into predictive models. Key findings and implications of this study can inform the development of more accurate and effective mortality prediction systems, ultimately improving healthcare quality and patient care. Relevant keywords: clinical notes, mortality prediction, natural language processing, deep learning, healthcare analytics.
