Dynamic graphs, representing entities and their relationships over time, are prevalent in various domains. While existing graph representation learning methods capture structural or attribute dynamics separately, they often overlook the intricate co-evolution between the graph structure and node attributes. This paper introduces a novel framework, Attribute-Structure Co-Evolutions Learning (ASCL), designed to simultaneously model the temporal dependencies of both graph structure and node attributes. ASCL leverages a coupled recurrent architecture to capture the mutual influence between evolving graph topology and attribute changes. Specifically, we employ separate recurrent units to model structural evolution and attribute dynamics, with cross-feeding mechanisms to facilitate information exchange. We further introduce an attention mechanism to selectively aggregate neighborhood information, adapting to varying node importance over time. Extensive experiments on several real-world dynamic graph datasets demonstrate that ASCL significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods in node classification and link prediction tasks. The results highlight the importance of modeling attribute-structure co-evolutions for effective dynamic graph representation learning. Keywords: Dynamic Graphs, Graph Neural Networks, Representation Learning, Temporal Networks, Attribute Learning, Co-evolution.
