With the rapid increase in model sizes and complexities, efficient model compression techniques become crucial for deploying deep learning models in resource-constrained environments. This paper introduces LIT, a novel Block-wise Intermediate Representation Training approach aimed at enhancing model compression without compromising performance.

Methods/Approach: The proposed method leverages intermediate representations within neural networks to strategically adjust the learning process on a block-by-block basis. LIT integrates an innovative mechanism for segmenting and reducing redundancy in model parameters, enabling smaller and more efficient model architectures. The approach is designed to maintain the fidelity of original model representations while smoothing the adaptation to compressed forms.

Results/Findings: Extensive experimentation shows that LIT achieves significant compression ratios compared to conventional methods, with a minimal loss in accuracy across a range of benchmark datasets. The technique demonstrates superior performance on convolutional and transformer-based models, highlighting its versatility and effectiveness. Comparative analysis indicates that LIT outperforms existing model compression techniques both in speed and memory efficiency.

Conclusion/Implications: By focusing on intermediate representations, LIT presents a transformative step forward in model compression for deep learning applications. The contributions of this research have substantial implications for deploying AI models in scenarios where computational and storage resources are limited. This makes LIT a valuable tool for diverse applications requiring efficient neural network deployments, including mobile and edge computing. 

Key Keywords: model compression, deep learning, intermediate representation, neural networks, resource-constrained environments, convolutional models, transformer models.
