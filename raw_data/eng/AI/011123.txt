This paper proposes a novel Dual-attention Guided Dropblock Module designed to enhance weakly supervised object localization. The objective is to improve the accuracy of object localization using only image-level annotations. Our approach combines the strengths of attention mechanisms and Dropblock regularization to guide the model's focus on relevant regions. The Dual-attention Guided Dropblock Module adaptively selects and refines feature maps, allowing for more precise object localization. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, outperforming state-of-the-art approaches in terms of localization accuracy and robustness. The key findings highlight the importance of attention-guided feature refinement and Dropblock-based regularization in weakly supervised settings. Our research contributes to the development of more accurate and efficient object localization models, with potential applications in image recognition, robotics, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: weakly supervised object localization, attention mechanisms, Dropblock regularization, deep learning, computer vision.
