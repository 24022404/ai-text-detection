The chapter opens with an introduction to coding theory in section 2.1, setting the groundwork for subsequent discussions on de Bruijn sequences, their numerous generalizations, and diverse applications, as outlined in sections 2.2 through 2.4. It further investigates Zhang et al.'s de Bruijn-based timing and synchronization system in section 2.5, highlighting that, while the underlying coding scheme functions effectively, there remains room for improvement in achieving higher rates, as well as the need for efficient encoding and decoding algorithms.
