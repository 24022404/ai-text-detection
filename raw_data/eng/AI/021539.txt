Knowledge-intensive processes (KIPs) play a crucial role in domains such as healthcare, finance, and legal decision-making, where complex reasoning and domain expertise are required. Traditional process modeling approaches struggle to capture the dynamic and context-dependent nature of these processes, limiting automation and decision support capabilities. To address this challenge, we introduce ProcK, a machine learning framework designed for modeling, optimizing, and automating knowledge-intensive processes.

ProcK leverages a hybrid learning paradigm that integrates deep learning with symbolic reasoning, enabling the system to extract, represent, and infer knowledge from structured and unstructured data sources. We propose a process-aware neural architecture that learns contextual dependencies in KIPs while ensuring interpretability through rule-based constraints. Additionally, we introduce a self-adaptive optimization module that dynamically refines process workflows based on real-time feedback and evolving knowledge bases.

Experiments on real-world KIP datasets from healthcare and legal domains demonstrate that ProcK significantly improves process efficiency, decision accuracy, and adaptability compared to conventional process mining and rule-based methods. Our findings highlight the potential of machine learning-driven approaches in transforming knowledge-intensive workflows through automation and intelligent decision support.

Keywords: Knowledge-Intensive Processes, Machine Learning, Process Automation, Hybrid AI, Symbolic Reasoning, Workflow Optimization.
