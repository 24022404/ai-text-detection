This paper proposes a novel Generator Evaluator-Selector Net (GESNet) architecture for panoptic image segmentation, enabling the simultaneous segmentation of familiar objects and the splitting of unfamiliar objects into their constituent parts. The objective is to address the limitations of existing methods in handling unknown objects and to improve overall segmentation accuracy. Our approach combines a generator network for producing segmentation masks, an evaluator network for assessing mask quality, and a selector network for determining the optimal splitting strategy for unfamiliar objects. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of GESNet in achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets, with significant improvements in segmentation accuracy and unfamiliar object splitting. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a modular architecture for panoptic segmentation and the development of a splitting mechanism for unfamiliar objects, which has potential applications in autonomous driving, robotics, and surveillance. Keywords: panoptic image segmentation, unfamiliar object splitting, generator network, evaluator network, selector network, deep learning, computer vision.
