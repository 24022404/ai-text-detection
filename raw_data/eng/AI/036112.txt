ODVICE is introduced, an Ontology-Driven Visual Analytic tool designed for interactive cohort extraction from complex datasets. Addressing the challenges of traditional query-based methods, ODVICE leverages a domain ontology to provide a semantically rich interface for users to define and refine cohorts. Our approach utilizes visual representations of ontological relationships, enabling users to explore data connections and formulate sophisticated cohort definitions without requiring specialized query languages. The system incorporates interactive filtering and drill-down capabilities, allowing for real-time cohort refinement based on user feedback. Performance evaluations demonstrate that ODVICE facilitates faster and more intuitive cohort extraction compared to standard SQL-based methods, while also improving the accuracy of cohort definitions. Key contributions include the integration of domain ontologies for semantic data exploration and a visual analytics framework that supports iterative cohort refinement. ODVICE has significant potential in domains such as healthcare, where nuanced cohort definitions are crucial for research and decision-making.
Keywords: visual analytics, cohort extraction, ontology, data exploration, interactive system.
