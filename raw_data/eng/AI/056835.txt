This study aims to develop a smartphone-based colorimetric detection system utilizing machine learning algorithms for accurate and rapid analysis of chemical samples. The approach involves capturing images of colorimetric assays using a smartphone camera and processing them through a convolutional neural network (CNN) to predict concentrations of target analytes. The system's performance is evaluated through a series of experiments, demonstrating high accuracy and precision in detecting various chemicals. Key findings indicate that the proposed method outperforms traditional spectroscopic techniques in terms of portability, cost-effectiveness, and ease of use. The research contributes to the development of point-of-care diagnostic tools, enabling users to perform on-site analysis with minimal training. The novelty of this work lies in the integration of machine learning with smartphone-based colorimetry, offering a promising solution for environmental monitoring, healthcare, and food safety applications. Relevant keywords include: machine learning, colorimetric detection, smartphone sensing, point-of-care diagnostics, and convolutional neural networks.
