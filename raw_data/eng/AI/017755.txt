Medical image segmentation is crucial for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning. However, achieving precise segmentation, particularly for complex anatomical structures, remains challenging. This paper introduces Deep Interactive Geodesic Segmentation (DeepIGeoS), a novel deep learning framework integrating interactive geodesic techniques for improved medical image segmentation. DeepIGeoS leverages a convolutional neural network (CNN) to generate a preliminary segmentation map and geodesic distance maps based on user-provided seed points. These distance maps are then used as input to refine the initial segmentation, iteratively propagating the segmentation boundaries. The interactive component allows clinicians to correct inaccuracies by adding or modifying seed points, leading to more accurate results. Experimental results on multiple medical imaging datasets, including brain MRI and cardiac CT scans, demonstrate that DeepIGeoS significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art segmentation methods, achieving higher Dice scores and lower Hausdorff distances. The interactive refinement capability of DeepIGeoS reduces the need for extensive manual correction, making it a valuable tool for clinical applications. Keywords: medical image segmentation, deep learning, geodesic distance, interactive segmentation, convolutional neural network.
