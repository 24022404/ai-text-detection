Adopting an asynchronous method for encKey generation introduces substantial advantages to system design, particularly in terms of resource optimization, scalability, performance, and the overall security and user experience. By moving away from traditional synchronous approaches, asynchronous generation allows tasks to be processed independently and concurrently, reducing bottlenecks and enabling systems to efficiently handle a greater volume of requests. A central feature of this innovation is the use of a Distributed Key Generation (DKG) protocol, which promotes both security and reliability by distributing trust among multiple parties and mitigating single points of failure. The asynchronous nature of key creation further streamlines user onboarding and interaction, as it can adapt to real-time network conditions and usage demands without causing delays or interruptions for end users. These elements together highlight a thoughtfully architected system that aims to deliver a robust and seamless Web3 experience. Furthermore, the fourth chapter delves into the social authentication system, detailing its complex structure by breaking down the architecture, identifying key components, and exploring both the practical challenges encountered during development and the solutions implemented to address them.
