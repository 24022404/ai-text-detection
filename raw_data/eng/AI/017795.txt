Accurate molecule representation is crucial for various chemical applications, including drug discovery and material design. Existing methods often overlook the impact of chemical reactions on molecular properties, leading to suboptimal representations. This paper introduces a novel chemical-reaction-aware molecule representation learning framework that explicitly incorporates reaction information into the learning process. Our approach leverages graph neural networks to encode both individual molecules and the transformations they undergo during reactions. Specifically, we design a reaction embedding module that captures the essential changes in atomic bonds and molecular structures during a chemical reaction. This module is then integrated into a molecule representation learning framework through a dedicated attention mechanism. We evaluate our method on several benchmark datasets for reaction prediction and retrosynthesis analysis. The results demonstrate that our reaction-aware approach significantly outperforms existing methods, achieving state-of-the-art performance in predicting reaction outcomes and suggesting viable retrosynthetic pathways. This work provides a valuable tool for understanding and predicting chemical reactions, with potential applications in accelerating chemical discovery.

Keywords: Molecule Representation Learning, Chemical Reactions, Graph Neural Networks, Reaction Prediction, Retrosynthesis.
