This paper addresses the challenge of efficiently performing multi-relational learning on large-scale datasets. We introduce a novel approach leveraging the Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (ADMM) to decompose the complex multi-relational learning problem into smaller, more manageable subproblems. Our method allows for distributed computation and asynchronous updates, significantly improving scalability compared to traditional approaches. We formulate the learning objective with appropriate regularization terms to prevent overfitting. Experiments conducted on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that our ADMM-based approach achieves comparable or superior predictive accuracy while exhibiting significantly reduced training time, particularly as the dataset size and number of relations increase. Furthermore, we analyze the convergence properties of our algorithm and provide insights into parameter selection. The results highlight the potential of ADMM for scaling multi-relational learning to previously intractable problem sizes. Keywords: Multi-relational learning, ADMM, Scalable learning, Distributed optimization, Knowledge graphs.
