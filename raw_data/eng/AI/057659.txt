This paper introduces Subject2Vec, a novel approach for generating vector representations of image subjects directly from sets of image patches. Addressing the challenge of capturing comprehensive subject information without relying on complete object recognition, Subject2Vec employs a hybrid generative-discriminative model. Initially, a generative component learns to reconstruct the input image patches, capturing essential features and relationships. Subsequently, a discriminative component refines these features, ensuring robustness and distinctiveness for subject classification or comparison. The model is trained end-to-end, optimizing both reconstruction accuracy and discriminative power. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that Subject2Vec achieves state-of-the-art performance in image similarity tasks and subject retrieval, outperforming existing methods that rely on global image features or object detectors. The generated subject vectors provide a compact and effective representation suitable for various downstream applications, including image clustering, content-based image retrieval, and image annotation. This work contributes a new paradigm for representing image subjects, offering improved accuracy and efficiency in visual understanding tasks. Keywords: Image representation, vector embedding, generative models, discriminative models, image patches, subject retrieval, computer vision.
