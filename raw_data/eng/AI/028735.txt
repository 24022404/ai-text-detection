This paper addresses the challenge of accurately recognizing gender and estimating age from human facial images, a task that has significant applications in areas such as targeted advertising, security, and human-computer interaction. Traditional methods often struggle with variations in lighting, pose, and expressions. Our research focuses on leveraging transfer learning with deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to enhance classification accuracy in these tasks.

Methods/Approach: We utilize a well-established deep CNN architecture as the base model, applying transfer learning techniques to fine-tune the network with domain-specific datasets. This approach leverages pre-trained knowledge from large-scale image datasets to improve recognition performance and reduce computational costs. The model's layers are selectively re-trained to adapt the feature representation specifically for gender and age tasks, balancing generalization and specialization.

Results/Findings: The results demonstrate a significant improvement in classification accuracy over existing state-of-the-art methods. The proposed model achieves superior performance on benchmark datasets, reducing error rates in both gender recognition and age estimation tasks. Comparisons with other approaches highlight the effectiveness of our transfer learning strategy, especially in smaller training datasets where feature scarcity can hinder performance.

Conclusion/Implications: This study presents a novel and effective application of transfer learning with deep CNNs for gender recognition and age estimation, offering a robust solution to the challenges faced by traditional methods. The insights gained from this research pave the way for future work in other domains where transfer learning can leverage pre-trained models for specialized tasks. Applications of this research can enhance real-time facial analysis systems, improve user-experience in interactive systems, and contribute valuable advancements to machine learning methodologies in computer vision. Key Keywords: Deep CNNs, transfer learning, gender recognition, age estimation, facial analysis, computer vision.
