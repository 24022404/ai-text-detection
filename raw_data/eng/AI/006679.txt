This study addresses the challenge of accurately analyzing and interpreting ancient coin images, which are critical for historical and archaeological research. The objective is to develop a robust computational framework capable of identifying, classifying, and extracting meaningful information from these artifacts, despite their often degraded and incomplete states. To achieve this, we propose a novel approach combining advanced image processing techniques with deep learning models, specifically tailored for the unique characteristics of ancient coin imagery. Our methodology includes preprocessing steps to enhance image quality, followed by the application of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for feature extraction and classification. Additionally, we introduce a dataset of annotated ancient coin images to facilitate training and evaluation. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach achieves significant improvements in classification accuracy and feature recognition compared to existing methods. The findings highlight the potential of AI-driven tools in numismatics, offering new insights into historical contexts and cultural heritage. This research contributes to the broader field of digital archaeology by providing a scalable and efficient solution for analyzing ancient artifacts, with implications for museums, historians, and preservationists.  

**Keywords**: ancient coins, image processing, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, digital archaeology, numismatics.
