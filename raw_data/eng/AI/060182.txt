This study aims to develop an accurate classification system for determining the primary tumor origin of lung nodules using spectral Computed Tomography (CT) scans. By leveraging transfer learning techniques, we adapt pre-trained deep learning models to analyze the spectral characteristics of lung nodules and classify their primary tumor origin. Our approach utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, fine-tuned on a dataset of spectral CT images, to extract relevant features and improve classification performance. The results show a significant improvement in accuracy compared to traditional machine learning methods, with our model achieving a high degree of precision in distinguishing between primary and metastatic lung tumors. The proposed system has important implications for clinical practice, enabling more accurate diagnosis and treatment planning for patients with lung cancer. Key contributions of this research include the application of transfer learning to spectral CT images and the development of a robust classification framework for lung nodule analysis, with potential applications in computer-aided diagnosis and personalized medicine. Relevant keywords: spectral CT, transfer learning, deep learning, lung nodule classification, primary tumor origin, computer-aided diagnosis.
