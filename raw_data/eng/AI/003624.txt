This paper proposes a novel approach to semantic segmentation using a highly fused convolutional network (HFCN) that integrates multiple soft cost functions. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of image segmentation by leveraging the strengths of both convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and soft cost functions. Our method employs a multi-scale feature fusion strategy, combining the benefits of different convolutional blocks and soft cost functions to produce a more robust and accurate segmentation model. Experimental results demonstrate that our HFCN outperforms state-of-the-art models on several benchmark datasets, achieving significant improvements in terms of mean intersection over union (mIoU) and pixel accuracy. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a highly fused convolutional network architecture and the development of multiple soft cost functions that can be easily integrated into existing CNN frameworks. Our approach has important implications for various computer vision applications, including autonomous driving, medical image analysis, and robotics, and highlights the potential of highly fused convolutional networks with multiple soft cost functions for semantic segmentation tasks, with relevant keywords including CNN, semantic segmentation, image processing, deep learning, and computer vision.
