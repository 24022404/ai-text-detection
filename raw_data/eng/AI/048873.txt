Objective: Visual object tracking systems often falter when confronted with manipulated or “fake” backgrounds, leading to drift and loss of target identity. This paper introduces a lightweight verification module—One More Check (OMC)—designed to detect and correct tracking failures caused by background alterations.  
Methods: OMC operates as a plug-and-play add-on to existing trackers. At each frame, candidate target regions are evaluated by a Siamese feature extractor that compares the current appearance to a gallery of verified templates. A similarity-based decision network then accepts or rejects the tracker’s proposal. Rejected proposals trigger a re-detection step that searches for the most likely match within a dynamically updated search region.  
Results: We integrate OMC with three state-of-the-art trackers and evaluate on standard and adversarial video benchmarks augmented with synthetic background manipulations. Across all settings, OMC yields a 7–12% gain in MOTA (Multi-Object Tracking Accuracy) and reduces ID switch rates by up to 30%, with only a 5% runtime overhead. Ablation studies confirm the effectiveness of the dynamic template gallery and adaptive search strategy.  
Conclusion: One More Check substantially enhances tracker robustness against fake-background attacks without retraining base models. Its modular design makes it broadly applicable to real-time surveillance, autonomous navigation, and augmented reality systems.  
Keywords: object tracking, fake background, verification module, Siamese network, re-detection, tracking robustness.
