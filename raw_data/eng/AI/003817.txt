Transformer models have achieved state-of-the-art results in various sequence modeling tasks. However, their computational cost and memory requirements scale quadratically with sequence length, limiting their applicability to long-range dependencies. This paper introduces Compressive Transformers, a novel architecture designed to address these limitations. Our approach incorporates a compression module that summarizes past states into a compressed memory, allowing the model to effectively process longer sequences while maintaining computational efficiency. We evaluate Compressive Transformers on several benchmark datasets for long-range sequence modeling, demonstrating significant improvements in performance and memory footprint compared to standard Transformers and other related models. Specifically, our model achieves superior results on tasks requiring the capture of dependencies spanning thousands of time steps. The results highlight the potential of Compressive Transformers to extend the applicability of Transformer architectures to previously intractable long-range sequence modeling problems. Keywords: Transformers, Long-Range Sequence Modeling, Compression, Attention Mechanisms, Deep Learning.
