This paper addresses the challenge of efficient UAV path planning for wireless data harvesting in dynamic environments. Traditional methods often fall short in optimizing for both energy consumption and data collection efficiency. Our research seeks to enhance these aspects by employing cutting-edge AI techniques.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a novel approach leveraging deep reinforcement learning (DRL) to optimize UAV paths for data harvesting tasks. By integrating environmental dynamics and constraints into a DRL framework, our solution adapts in real-time to changing conditions, enhancing decision-making processes. The model employs a reward system focused on maximizing data collection while minimizing energy expenditure, ensuring sustainable operations.

Results/Findings: The proposed DRL-based path planning algorithm demonstrates considerable improvements over conventional methods. In simulations, the system achieved up to 30% higher data collection rates and reduced energy usage by 20% compared to baseline approaches. Our method's adaptability to variable conditions was validated through extensive testing across diverse scenarios, showcasing robust performance and efficiency.

Conclusion/Implications: This research highlights the potential of deep reinforcement learning in revolutionizing UAV path planning for wireless data harvesting. Our findings suggest significant improvements in operational efficiency and resource management. The proposed approach provides a framework for integrating advanced AI techniques into UAV systems, with implications for broader applications in surveillance, environmental monitoring, and smart city infrastructures.

Keywords: UAV path planning, wireless data harvesting, deep reinforcement learning, AI, energy efficiency, dynamic environments.
