This paper introduces Gym-ANM, an open-source software package designed to facilitate the application of reinforcement learning (RL) to power system management. The objective is to provide a flexible and accessible environment for researchers and educators to explore and develop RL-based control strategies for complex power grid scenarios. Gym-ANM leverages the well-established pandapower and PYPOWER libraries to simulate realistic power system operations, while offering a Gym interface for seamless integration with various RL algorithms. The software provides a range of power system management tasks, including voltage control, congestion management, and fault recovery, as RL environments. Initial experiments demonstrate the effectiveness of Gym-ANM in training RL agents to successfully manage voltage levels and mitigate congestion in benchmark power grids. Gym-ANM enables reproducible research and accelerated learning in the domain of intelligent power system control, fostering innovation and facilitating the development of advanced grid management techniques. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Power System Management, Open-Source Software, Smart Grids, Grid Control.
