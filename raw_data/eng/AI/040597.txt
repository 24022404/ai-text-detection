This paper presents a novel algorithm for geodesic active contours, leveraging a characteristic function-based approach to enhance contour detection and image segmentation. The objective is to develop an efficient and accurate method for identifying complex object boundaries in images. Our approach utilizes a level set formulation, incorporating a characteristic function to represent the contour, and a geodesic active contour model to drive the evolution of the contour towards the desired boundary. The algorithm is tested on various images, demonstrating improved performance and robustness compared to existing methods. Key findings include enhanced accuracy in detecting intricate boundaries and reduced sensitivity to initial contour placement. The proposed algorithm contributes to the field of image segmentation by providing a more effective and efficient tool for geodesic active contour-based boundary detection, with potential applications in medical imaging, object recognition, and computer vision. Keywords: geodesic active contours, characteristic function, level set method, image segmentation, boundary detection.
