The rapid growth of financial markets has increased the demand for efficient and automated methods to discover optimal trading strategies, particularly in options trading. This paper addresses the challenge of identifying profitable options strategies under budget constraints using a novel approach based on Budgeted Reinforcement Learning (BRL). The proposed framework integrates reinforcement learning with budget-aware decision-making to optimize trading actions while adhering to predefined financial limits. We introduce a tailored algorithm that dynamically allocates resources across multiple options contracts, balancing exploration and exploitation to maximize returns. Experimental results on real-world market data demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method, outperforming traditional heuristic-based strategies in terms of profitability and risk management. The findings highlight the potential of BRL in financial decision-making, offering a scalable and adaptive solution for options discovery. This research contributes to the growing field of AI-driven finance by providing a robust framework for budget-constrained trading, with implications for both individual investors and institutional traders.  

**Keywords**: Options trading, Budgeted Reinforcement Learning, Financial decision-making, AI-driven finance, Risk management.
