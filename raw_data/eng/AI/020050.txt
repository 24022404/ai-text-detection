Keyword spotting (KWS) systems, crucial for voice-enabled devices, often struggle with robustness and efficiency. This paper introduces an innovative multi-head attention mechanism with orthogonality constraints to enhance KWS performance. The proposed Orthogonality Constrained Multi-Head Attention (OCMHA) encourages diverse feature representations across attention heads, improving the model's ability to discriminate between keywords and background noise. We enforce orthogonality between the weight matrices of different attention heads, promoting the learning of uncorrelated feature subspaces. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that OCMHA significantly outperforms conventional multi-head attention and other state-of-the-art KWS models, achieving higher accuracy with comparable model size. Furthermore, ablation studies validate the effectiveness of the orthogonality constraint in improving feature diversity and overall system performance. The results indicate that OCMHA offers a promising approach for developing more accurate and efficient KWS systems, particularly in resource-constrained environments. Keywords: Keyword Spotting, Multi-Head Attention, Orthogonality Constraints, Speech Recognition, Deep Learning.
