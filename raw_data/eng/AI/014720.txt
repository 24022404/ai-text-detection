The creation of realistic and personalized game character faces from a single portrait remains a challenging task in the field of computer graphics and artificial intelligence. This paper introduces *MeInGame*, a novel framework designed to generate high-fidelity 3D game character faces using a single input image. The proposed approach leverages advanced deep learning techniques, including generative adversarial networks (GANs) and 3D face reconstruction models, to map 2D facial features into a detailed 3D representation. The system incorporates a multi-stage pipeline that ensures accurate facial geometry, texture, and expression transfer while maintaining computational efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate that *MeInGame* outperforms existing methods in terms of visual quality, realism, and user satisfaction. The framework also supports real-time customization, enabling users to adjust facial attributes such as hairstyle, skin tone, and expressions. This research contributes to the growing demand for personalized gaming experiences and has potential applications in virtual reality, augmented reality, and digital entertainment. Key innovations include the integration of lightweight neural architectures and a user-friendly interface, making the system accessible to both developers and end-users. Keywords: 3D face reconstruction, game character generation, deep learning, GANs, personalized gaming, virtual reality.
