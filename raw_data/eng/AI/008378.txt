This paper presents a comprehensive review of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) applied to medical image analysis. The objective is to provide an overview of state-of-the-art CNN architectures and methodologies used for various medical imaging tasks, including image classification, segmentation, and detection. We explore different CNN architectures, such as VGGNet, ResNet, and U-Net, and their adaptations for analyzing medical images from modalities like X-ray, MRI, CT, and ultrasound. This review summarizes the performance of these CNN-based techniques on different medical imaging datasets, highlighting their strengths and limitations in addressing specific clinical challenges. The analysis reveals that CNNs have significantly improved the accuracy and efficiency of medical image analysis, enabling earlier and more precise diagnoses. The paper concludes by discussing current challenges and future research directions, including the need for more robust and interpretable models, as well as strategies for handling limited labeled data. This review serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners seeking to leverage CNNs for advancing medical imaging applications.

Keywords: Convolutional Neural Networks, Medical Image Analysis, Deep Learning, Image Segmentation, Image Classification, Medical Imaging, CNN.
