Invariant Causal Prediction (ICP) is a powerful framework for causal discovery, but its applicability to complex sequential decision-making problems remains limited. This paper introduces a novel extension of ICP tailored for Block Markov Decision Processes (Block MDPs), a class of MDPs exhibiting hierarchical structure. Our approach leverages the inherent modularity of Block MDPs to identify invariant causal relationships that generalize across different intervention settings within the block structure. We develop a modified ICP algorithm that exploits the block structure to improve both the statistical power and computational efficiency of causal discovery. Empirical evaluations on synthetic Block MDPs demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms standard ICP in recovering the underlying causal graph. Furthermore, we illustrate the practical utility of our approach on a real-world problem of personalized treatment recommendation, where invariant causal relationships learned from Block MDPs lead to more robust and generalizable treatment policies. Our findings provide a foundation for leveraging causal discovery in complex, hierarchical decision-making environments, paving the way for more reliable and interpretable AI systems. Keywords: Causal Discovery, Invariant Causal Prediction, Block Markov Decision Processes, Reinforcement Learning, Sequential Decision Making.
