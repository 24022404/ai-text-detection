This paper presents a novel camera system, dubbed the Dark Flash Normal Camera, designed to capture high-quality images in low-light environments. The objective of this research is to develop a camera that can effectively operate in conditions where traditional cameras struggle, such as indoors or at night. Our approach involves combining a custom-designed camera hardware with advanced image processing algorithms to enhance low-light image capture. The system utilizes a unique flash mechanism that emits a brief, high-intensity flash to illuminate the scene, while also incorporating a normal camera to capture the ambient light. Our results show significant improvements in image quality, noise reduction, and color accuracy compared to existing low-light camera systems. The Dark Flash Normal Camera has important implications for various applications, including surveillance, automotive, and mobile devices. This innovative camera system contributes to the field of computer vision and image processing, offering a unique solution for low-light imaging. Key keywords: low-light imaging, camera systems, image processing, computer vision, flash photography.
