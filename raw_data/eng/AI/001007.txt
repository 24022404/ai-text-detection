This paper presents a novel approach to Bayesian reinforcement learning, leveraging deep, sparse sampling techniques to enhance the efficiency and scalability of decision-making processes in complex, uncertain environments. Our objective is to develop a framework that integrates the benefits of Bayesian inference with the power of deep learning, enabling agents to learn optimal policies while quantifying uncertainty and adapting to changing conditions. We propose a method that utilizes sparse sampling to reduce the computational burden associated with Bayesian updates, while maintaining the accuracy and robustness of the learning process. Our results demonstrate significant improvements in learning efficiency and policy optimization, outperforming existing methods in several benchmark environments. The key contributions of this research include the development of a scalable Bayesian reinforcement learning algorithm, the introduction of deep, sparse sampling techniques for efficient Bayesian updates, and the demonstration of improved performance in complex decision-making tasks. This work has important implications for the development of autonomous systems, robotics, and other applications where uncertainty and adaptability are crucial. Key keywords: Bayesian reinforcement learning, deep learning, sparse sampling, uncertainty quantification, decision-making under uncertainty.
