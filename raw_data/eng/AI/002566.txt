This paper addresses the objective of enhancing pedestrian detection in images and videos using deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs). The purpose is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of pedestrian detection systems, which is crucial for various applications such as autonomous vehicles, surveillance, and smart cities. 

We propose a novel approach that utilizes a deep CNN architecture to learn robust features from images, allowing for accurate pedestrian detection. Our method involves training a CNN model on a large dataset of images with annotated pedestrians, enabling the network to learn effective features for distinguishing pedestrians from non-pedestrian objects.

The results show that our proposed approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets, outperforming existing pedestrian detection methods. The evaluation metrics demonstrate significant improvements in detection accuracy, precision, and recall. 

In conclusion, this research contributes to the development of more accurate and reliable pedestrian detection systems using deep CNNs. The proposed approach has potential applications in various fields, including computer vision, robotics, and transportation systems. Key keywords: pedestrian detection, deep convolutional neural networks, computer vision, object detection, autonomous vehicles.
