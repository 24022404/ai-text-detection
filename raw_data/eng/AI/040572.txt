This paper aims to enhance the understanding of complex multivariate data by introducing a novel visualization approach based on Jumping Emerging Patterns (JEPs). The objective is to provide an intuitive and insightful representation of high-dimensional data, facilitating the identification of significant relationships and patterns. Our approach employs a combination of data mining and visualization techniques to extract JEPs, which are then visualized using a customized graphical interface. The results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method in revealing hidden patterns and correlations within multivariate data, outperforming traditional visualization methods. The key findings highlight the potential of JEPs visualization in various applications, including data analysis, decision-making, and knowledge discovery. This research contributes to the field of data visualization by providing a unique and powerful tool for multivariate data explanation, with significant implications for fields such as business intelligence, healthcare, and social sciences. Key keywords: Jumping Emerging Patterns, data visualization, multivariate data analysis, data mining, knowledge discovery.
