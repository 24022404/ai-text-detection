This paper introduces a novel unsupervised learning approach for efficiently discovering and preserving similarities within high-dimensional datasets. The method leverages Modular Sparse Distributed Codes (MSDC) to represent data, facilitating dimensionality reduction and feature extraction while retaining crucial structural information. A key innovation is the introduction of Novelty-Contingent Noise (NCN), which adaptively injects noise during the learning process based on the novelty of each data point, promoting robust and generalizable representations. This dynamic noise injection mitigates overfitting and enhances the model's ability to discover subtle relationships within the data. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed MSDC-NCN framework achieves superior performance in similarity preservation compared to existing unsupervised methods, particularly in scenarios with noisy or incomplete data. The approach offers a computationally efficient alternative for unsupervised feature learning and anomaly detection, with potential applications in areas such as image recognition, natural language processing, and bioinformatics.

Keywords: Unsupervised Learning, Sparse Coding, Similarity Preservation, Noise Injection, Feature Extraction, Anomaly Detection, Modular Sparse Distributed Codes.
