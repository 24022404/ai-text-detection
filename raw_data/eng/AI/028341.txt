This paper presents a novel approach to learning invariant representations by leveraging local transformations. The objective is to develop a framework that enables deep neural networks to learn features that are robust to various transformations, such as rotations, scaling, and translations. Our method utilizes a transformation-based regularization technique, which encourages the network to learn representations that are invariant to local transformations. We employ a combination of convolutional neural networks and transformation-based modules to achieve this goal. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of robustness and generalization, particularly in scenarios where data augmentation is limited. The key findings of this study highlight the importance of incorporating local transformations into the learning process, leading to improved performance in image classification, object recognition, and other computer vision tasks. Our research contributes to the development of more robust and generalizable neural networks, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, autonomous driving, and medical imaging, and is relevant to keywords such as invariant representations, local transformations, deep learning, and computer vision.
