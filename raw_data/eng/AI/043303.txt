This paper introduces Bayesian Attention Belief Networks (BABNs), a novel probabilistic deep learning architecture that integrates attention mechanisms within a Bayesian belief network framework. BABNs address the limitations of traditional deep learning models in handling uncertainty and interpretability by propagating probabilistic beliefs through attention-weighted feature representations. The proposed architecture leverages variational inference to approximate the posterior distribution over network parameters and attention weights, enabling robust learning and uncertainty quantification. We evaluate BABNs on several benchmark datasets for classification and regression tasks, demonstrating improved predictive accuracy and calibration compared to standard attention-based neural networks and Bayesian neural networks. Furthermore, the learned attention weights provide insights into the model's decision-making process, enhancing interpretability. Results indicate that BABNs offer a powerful and interpretable approach to probabilistic deep learning, paving the way for applications in safety-critical domains requiring reliable uncertainty estimation. Keywords: Bayesian Belief Networks, Attention Mechanisms, Probabilistic Deep Learning, Variational Inference, Uncertainty Quantification, Interpretability.
