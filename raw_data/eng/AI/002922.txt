This paper introduces Side-Tuning, a novel approach for network adaptation that leverages additive side networks to efficiently fine-tune pre-trained models on new tasks. The objective is to develop a simple yet effective method for adapting deep neural networks to changing environments or new datasets, reducing the need for extensive retraining. Our approach involves adding small, learnable side networks to the pre-trained model, which enables the adaptation of the original network without modifying its architecture. Experimental results demonstrate that Side-Tuning outperforms existing fine-tuning methods, achieving state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of Side-Tuning in preserving the knowledge of the pre-trained model while adapting to new tasks. This research contributes to the field of transfer learning and network adaptation, providing a new baseline for future studies. The proposed method has significant implications for real-world applications, including computer vision, natural language processing, and robotics, where efficient adaptation to new environments is crucial. Key keywords: network adaptation, fine-tuning, transfer learning, deep neural networks, additive side networks.
