The increasing complexity of healthcare data necessitates advanced tools for efficient cohort extraction and analysis. This paper introduces **ODVICE**, an **Ontology-Driven Visual Analytic Tool** designed to facilitate **Interactive Cohort Extraction** from large-scale healthcare datasets. The primary objective of ODVICE is to enable researchers and clinicians to intuitively explore, filter, and extract patient cohorts using a combination of semantic reasoning and interactive visualization. The tool leverages domain-specific ontologies to structure and contextualize data, ensuring accurate and meaningful cohort definitions. A user-friendly visual interface allows for dynamic querying and real-time refinement of cohort criteria, while backend algorithms optimize data retrieval and processing.  

To evaluate ODVICE, we conducted experiments on a real-world electronic health record (EHR) dataset, comparing its performance against traditional query-based methods. Results demonstrate that ODVICE significantly reduces the time and effort required for cohort extraction while improving precision and recall. Furthermore, the tool's ability to integrate domain knowledge through ontologies enhances the interpretability and relevance of extracted cohorts.  

ODVICE represents a novel contribution to the field of healthcare analytics by bridging the gap between semantic reasoning and interactive visualization. Its potential applications include clinical research, personalized medicine, and population health management. By combining ontology-driven reasoning with user-centric design, ODVICE empowers users to derive actionable insights from complex healthcare data efficiently.  

**Keywords**: Ontology, Visual Analytics, Cohort Extraction, Healthcare Data, Interactive Tools, Semantic Reasoning.
