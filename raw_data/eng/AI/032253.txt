**Abstract**

The field of machine learning has witnessed significant advancements through reinforcement learning (RL), but scalability issues often hinder its application to large-scale problems. This paper investigates Evolution Strategies (ES) as a scalable alternative to traditional RL methods for training complex control policies. We present a comprehensive analysis comparing both paradigms across several benchmark tasks in simulated environments. Leveraging a population-based optimization approach, our implementation of ES demonstrates notable benefits in parallelization, computational efficiency, and straightforward hyperparameter tuning. Experimental results show that ES achieves competitive or superior performance relative to widely adopted RL algorithms such as policy gradients and Q-learning, particularly in high-dimensional and noisy environments. Furthermore, ES scales seamlessly with increasing computational resources, exhibiting near-linear improvements in training speed. Our findings reveal that ES offers a robust and flexible framework for challenging optimization tasks and can significantly reduce training times without compromising solution quality. The insights and empirical results from this study suggest that Evolution Strategies represent a compelling alternative for scalable, efficient policy optimization in artificial intelligence and robotics applications.

**Keywords:** Evolution Strategies, reinforcement learning, policy optimization, scalability, parallel computing, artificial intelligence, optimization algorithms
