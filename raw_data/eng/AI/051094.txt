Rational decision-making in reinforcement learning (RL) is crucial for developing autonomous agents that can effectively navigate complex environments. This paper introduces a set of axioms that define rational behavior for RL agents, providing a formal foundation for evaluating and designing RL algorithms. We present axioms pertaining to consistency, optimality, and adaptability, ensuring that an agent's learning process converges towards optimal policies in a stable and reliable manner. These axioms are used to analyze existing RL algorithms, identifying potential limitations and areas for improvement. Furthermore, we propose a novel RL framework that provably satisfies these axioms, leading to enhanced learning efficiency and robustness in stochastic environments. Empirical evaluations on benchmark tasks demonstrate that our axiom-driven approach outperforms traditional RL methods, achieving higher cumulative rewards and faster convergence rates. This work contributes to the theoretical understanding of rational RL and offers a practical methodology for developing more intelligent and reliable autonomous systems. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Rationality, Axioms, Autonomous Agents, Decision-Making, Optimality.
