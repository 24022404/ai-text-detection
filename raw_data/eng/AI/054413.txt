This paper introduces StrObe, a novel approach for streaming object detection from LiDAR packets, addressing the need for efficient and real-time 3D perception in autonomous systems. Our objective is to enable accurate and timely detection of objects in dynamic environments, such as autonomous vehicles and robots. We propose a lightweight and scalable method that processes LiDAR packets in a streaming fashion, leveraging a combination of point cloud processing and deep learning techniques. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets, with significant reductions in computational latency and memory usage. Key findings include the ability to detect objects at high speeds and in complex scenarios, outperforming existing methods. The StrObe system has important implications for autonomous systems, enabling more efficient and reliable 3D perception. Our contributions include the design of a novel streaming architecture, the development of efficient point cloud processing algorithms, and the demonstration of real-time object detection capabilities. Relevant keywords: LiDAR, object detection, streaming processing, autonomous systems, 3D perception, deep learning, point cloud processing.
