This paper addresses the challenge of sentiment analysis in low-resource scenarios, where the lack of labeled data hinders the performance of machine learning models. Our objective is to explore the potential of generative adversarial networks (GANs) for data augmentation in sentiment analysis. We propose a novel approach that utilizes GANs to generate synthetic data, which is then combined with the existing limited dataset to enhance model training. Our method involves training a GAN on the available labeled data, and then using the generated samples to augment the training set. The results show that our approach significantly improves the performance of sentiment analysis models, especially in cases where the original dataset is small. Our findings demonstrate the effectiveness of GAN-based data augmentation in low-resource sentiment analysis, and highlight the potential of this technique for improving the robustness and accuracy of machine learning models in similar scenarios. The key contributions of this research include the development of a GAN-based data augmentation framework for sentiment analysis, and the demonstration of its ability to improve model performance in low-resource settings. Keywords: sentiment analysis, data augmentation, generative adversarial networks, low-resource machine learning, natural language processing.
