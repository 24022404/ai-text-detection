Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) have shown promise in face generation, but controlling specific attributes remains a challenge. This paper introduces an attribute-aware face generation framework based on GANs that enables fine-grained control over facial attributes during the generation process. Our approach incorporates attribute classifiers within the GAN architecture to provide feedback on the generated faces, guiding the generator to produce images that accurately reflect the desired attribute values. Specifically, we utilize a multi-task learning setup where the discriminator not only distinguishes between real and fake images but also predicts the attributes present in the generated faces. This encourages the generator to learn a disentangled representation of facial attributes. Experiments demonstrate that our method achieves superior performance in generating faces with controlled attributes compared to existing GAN-based approaches. The generated images exhibit higher fidelity and more accurate attribute representation, as validated by quantitative metrics and qualitative evaluations. This research contributes to the field of controllable face generation and has potential applications in areas such as facial manipulation, data augmentation, and personalized avatar creation. Keywords: Generative Adversarial Networks, Face Generation, Attribute Control, Deep Learning, Image Synthesis.
