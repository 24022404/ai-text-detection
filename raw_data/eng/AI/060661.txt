This paper addresses the challenge of accelerating training processes in Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) by enhancing knowledge transfer through semantic and visual similarities. The research explores effective methodologies for reducing computational costs while maintaining or improving model accuracy.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel framework that integrates semantic similarity and visual similarity techniques to facilitate knowledge transfer in CNNs. Our approach leverages pre-trained models to identify semantic relationships between classes, while employing visual cues to enhance feature extraction and representation. The framework is designed to optimize the learning process, enabling efficient transfer across similar tasks.

Results/Findings: The proposed method demonstrates significant improvements in training efficiency and accuracy. In comparative experiments over benchmark datasets, our framework substantially reduced the training time while achieving higher accuracy than traditional CNN training methods without knowledge transfer. Moreover, the integration of semantic and visual similarities contributed to superior model generalization.

Conclusion/Implications: This study introduces an innovative approach that enhances the efficiency of CNN training through strategic knowledge transfer, leveraging semantic and visual similarities. The findings highlight the potential of the framework to reduce computational resources required for training deep learning models, with implications for applications in image classification, object detection, and other computer vision tasks. The research provides a robust foundation for future advancements in knowledge transfer methodologies in CNNs.

Keywords: Convolutional Neural Networks, Knowledge Transfer, Semantic Similarity, Visual Similarity, Training Efficiency, Model Generalization, Deep Learning.
