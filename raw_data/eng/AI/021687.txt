Side window filtering is a versatile image processing technique employed for tasks such as denoising, edge enhancement, and detail manipulation. This paper introduces an optimized approach to side window filtering, enhancing its computational efficiency and performance. The proposed method leverages integral images and adaptive window selection to reduce redundant calculations and preserve image features effectively. We present a novel algorithm that dynamically adjusts the side window size based on local image characteristics, enabling improved noise reduction in homogenous regions while preserving edges and fine details in complex areas. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach achieves a significant speedup compared to traditional side window filtering implementations, with a reduction in processing time of up to 40% on various image datasets. Furthermore, quantitative evaluations using metrics such as Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR) and Structural Similarity Index (SSIM) show that our method achieves competitive or superior performance in image denoising and detail preservation when compared to state-of-the-art filtering techniques. The proposed algorithm offers a practical solution for real-time image processing applications and provides a foundation for further research in adaptive filtering techniques.
Keywords: Side Window Filtering, Image Processing, Denoising, Edge Preservation, Adaptive Filtering, Integral Images.
