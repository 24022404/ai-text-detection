3D point clouds are vulnerable to adversarial attacks, which can significantly degrade the performance of downstream tasks. This paper introduces DUP-Net, a novel network designed to simultaneously denoise and upsample adversarially perturbed point clouds, thereby enhancing their robustness against such attacks. Our approach utilizes a two-branch architecture: a denoising branch that removes adversarial noise and an upsampling branch that increases point density, recovering lost geometric details. We employ a multi-scale feature extraction module in both branches to capture contextual information at varying resolutions. Furthermore, a novel loss function is introduced to encourage both accurate denoising and faithful upsampling. Experimental results on ModelNet40 and ShapeNet datasets demonstrate that DUP-Net significantly improves the classification accuracy of point cloud models under various adversarial attacks, outperforming existing defense methods. Specifically, DUP-Net achieves state-of-the-art defense performance while maintaining high fidelity to the original geometry. The proposed network offers a robust and effective solution for defending against adversarial attacks on 3D point clouds, contributing to more reliable point cloud processing pipelines.

Keywords: 3D Point Clouds, Adversarial Attack, Defense, Denoising, Upsampling, Deep Learning.
