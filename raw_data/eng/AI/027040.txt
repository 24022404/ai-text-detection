This paper presents a novel approach to depth prediction using fully convolutional residual networks, aiming to improve the accuracy and robustness of depth estimation in various environments. Our method leverages the strengths of residual learning and fully convolutional networks to learn complex features and spatial hierarchies, enabling more accurate depth predictions. We propose a customized network architecture that incorporates multi-scale feature extraction, residual connections, and upsampling techniques to produce high-resolution depth maps. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in depth prediction accuracy and reducing errors in challenging scenarios. The key contributions of this research include the design of a robust and efficient depth prediction model, the introduction of a novel residual learning strategy, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in various applications, including autonomous driving, robotics, and 3D reconstruction. Our work has important implications for computer vision and robotics, enabling more accurate and reliable depth perception in complex environments, with relevant keywords including depth prediction, fully convolutional networks, residual learning, and computer vision.
