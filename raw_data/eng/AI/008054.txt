This paper introduces MAGNet, a novel Multi-agent Graph Network framework designed to enhance deep multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL). The primary objective of MAGNet is to address the challenges of complex agent interactions and dynamic environments commonly encountered in cooperative and competitive multi-agent tasks. MAGNet models agents as nodes in a learnable graph structure, where edges dynamically represent interactions based on context-sensitive features. Using graph neural networks, the framework enables efficient aggregation and dissemination of information among agents, fostering improved coordination and scalable policy learning. We evaluate MAGNet on a suite of benchmark MARL environments, including both continuous and discrete domains. Experimental results demonstrate that MAGNet significantly outperforms existing baselines in terms of sample efficiency, convergence speed, and final performance, particularly in scenarios with a large number of interacting agents. Our findings underscore MAGNet’s ability to extract and utilize latent relational structures, leading to more robust and generalizable multi-agent behaviors. This work presents a substantial advancement in MARL, with potential applications in autonomous robotics, distributed control systems, and cooperative AI. Key keywords: multi-agent reinforcement learning, graph neural networks, deep learning, coordination, scalability, MAGNet.
