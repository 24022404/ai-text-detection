The paper addresses the challenge of synthesizing high-quality intermediate video frames, a critical task for enhancing video representation and smoothness in applications such as video compression, frame interpolation, and motion analysis. Traditional approaches often struggle with handling complex motion dynamics and maintaining temporal consistency.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel multi-scale video frame-synthesis network designed to efficiently generate intermediate frames while ensuring consistent temporal characteristics. The framework incorporates a transitive consistency loss function that enforces temporal coherence across synthesized frames, reducing artifacts and enhancing visual flow. Our method leverages advanced deep learning architectures to process variable frame scales, allowing for more refined control over motion details and contextual information.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods in both quantitative metrics and qualitative assessments. The proposed network exhibits significant improvements in preserving motion details and reducing visual discrepancies when compared to leading techniques, as evidenced by increased structural similarity index (SSIM) and peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) scores. The integration of the transitive consistency loss significantly contributes to these enhancements, ensuring smoother frame transitions.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of the multi-scale network with transitive consistency loss represents a significant advancement in video frame synthesis technology. This research provides a robust tool for applications requiring high-fidelity video interpolation and enhanced motion depiction. Future work may explore further optimization and integration with real-time processing systems, expanding the scope of video enhancement technologies.

Keywords: video frame synthesis, multi-scale network, transitive consistency loss, temporal coherence, deep learning, video interpolation, motion dynamics.
