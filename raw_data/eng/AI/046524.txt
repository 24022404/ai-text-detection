This paper introduces a novel method for 3D shape generation from point cloud data, leveraging spatial partitioning to enhance detail and accuracy. Our approach divides the input point cloud into spatially coherent regions, each processed independently to generate local surface patches. These patches are then seamlessly integrated to form the complete 3D shape. This spatial partitioning strategy allows for finer control over surface reconstruction, particularly in regions with high curvature or complex geometry. We employ a deep learning framework trained on a large dataset of 3D models to learn the mapping from spatially partitioned point clouds to corresponding surface representations. Experimental results demonstrate that our method significantly improves the quality of generated shapes compared to existing state-of-the-art techniques, reducing reconstruction errors and preserving intricate geometric details. Specifically, we observe a 20% improvement in Chamfer distance on complex shapes. The proposed approach offers a robust and efficient solution for 3D shape generation with applications in computer graphics, CAD modeling, and reverse engineering.

**Keywords:** Shape Generation, Point Cloud, Spatial Partitioning, 3D Reconstruction, Deep Learning.
