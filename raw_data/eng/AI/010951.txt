Abstract:

Accurate uncertainty estimation is critical in classification tasks, particularly in domains where decision confidence directly impacts safety and reliability. This paper introduces a novel logit-based uncertainty measure tailored for classification models. Unlike traditional probability-based uncertainty metrics, our approach leverages the raw logit outputs of neural networks, capturing intricate model behaviors that are often lost during softmax transformation. We formally define the proposed measure and evaluate its effectiveness on benchmark datasets using a diverse set of neural network architectures. Experimental results demonstrate that the logit-based measure provides more informative and discriminative uncertainty estimates, especially in scenarios with ambiguous or out-of-distribution data. Comparative analysis with established uncertainty metrics—such as entropy and maximum probability—reveals that our method consistently improves risk-aware decision-making and model calibration. These findings suggest that integrating logit-based uncertainty quantification can significantly enhance the reliability of classification systems in real-world applications. Key keywords: uncertainty estimation, classification, logits, neural networks, model calibration, risk assessment.
