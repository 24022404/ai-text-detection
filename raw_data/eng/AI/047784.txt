The objective of this research is to address the challenges in accurately estimating articulated 3D hand-object poses, an essential aspect in fields such as virtual reality, robotics, and human-computer interaction. Our approach, named ArtiBoost, introduces a novel framework that enhances pose estimation by leveraging online exploration and synthesis techniques. The method integrates real-time data augmentation with synthetic data generation to improve the accuracy and robustness of hand-object interaction models. Utilizing advanced machine learning algorithms, ArtiBoost dynamically adapts to diverse hand shapes and object types, offering superior generalization capabilities compared to existing methods. Our experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in pose estimation accuracy and computational efficiency, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets. The contributions of this research lie in the innovative synthesis of real and synthetic data for continuous learning, which enables enhanced model adaptability and precision in real-world scenarios. The implications of this work suggest potential advancements in the development of interactive virtual environments and assistive robotic systems. Key keywords include 3D reconstruction, hand-object interaction, pose estimation, machine learning, and data synthesis.
