E-commerce product question answering (QA) aims to provide informative answers based on user queries and product descriptions. However, acquiring labeled QA pairs for training robust models remains a challenge. This paper introduces a novel approach leveraging distantly supervised transformers for e-commerce product QA. We generate noisy QA pairs from product reviews and metadata, using a transformer-based model fine-tuned on this distantly supervised data. A key contribution is the development of a refined data filtering strategy to mitigate noise inherent in distant supervision. Our experiments on a large-scale e-commerce dataset demonstrate that our method achieves significant improvements over existing approaches, particularly in handling complex queries. The results highlight the potential of distantly supervised transformers in enhancing e-commerce product QA systems, offering a cost-effective alternative to manual data annotation. Keywords: E-commerce, Question Answering, Distant Supervision, Transformers, Product Reviews.
