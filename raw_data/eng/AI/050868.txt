This paper explores the application of Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) for the synthesis of flight controllers, addressing the challenges of designing robust and adaptive control systems for autonomous aerial vehicles. Traditional flight control methods often rely on predefined models and manual tuning, which can be time-consuming and less adaptable to dynamic environments. In this study, we propose a DRL-based framework that leverages neural networks to learn optimal control policies directly from interaction with the environment. The approach employs a combination of policy gradient methods and reward shaping to ensure stable and efficient flight control under varying conditions. Experimental results demonstrate that the synthesized controller achieves superior performance in terms of stability, adaptability, and energy efficiency compared to conventional methods. The proposed framework also exhibits robustness to environmental disturbances and system uncertainties. This research highlights the potential of DRL in advancing autonomous flight systems, offering a scalable and flexible solution for real-world applications. Key contributions include a novel reward structure tailored for flight control tasks and a comprehensive evaluation of the framework across multiple flight scenarios. The findings suggest that DRL-based controllers can significantly enhance the autonomy and reliability of unmanned aerial vehicles, paving the way for future advancements in aerospace technology.  

**Keywords**: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Flight Control, Autonomous Systems, Neural Networks, Policy Optimization, Unmanned Aerial Vehicles.
