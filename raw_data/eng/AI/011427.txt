Child trafficking is a severe global issue, and identifying locations where children are most vulnerable is critical for effective intervention. This paper introduces a novel machine learning pipeline designed to aid in the identification of schools from images potentially related to child trafficking. The pipeline integrates techniques from computer vision and machine learning to detect and analyze school-related features within images. We employ a pre-trained object detection model fine-tuned on a dataset of school buildings, playground equipment, and related objects. Detected objects are then fed into a classification model to predict the likelihood of an image containing a school environment. The performance of the pipeline was evaluated on a held-out dataset, demonstrating a significant improvement in accuracy compared to baseline methods. The proposed system offers a valuable tool for law enforcement and non-governmental organizations to prioritize investigations and allocate resources effectively, contributing to the fight against child trafficking. Keywords: Child Trafficking, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Object Detection, School Identification, Image Analysis.
