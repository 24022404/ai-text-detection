This paper addresses the challenge of coordinating multi-agent reinforcement learning by introducing Likelihood Quantile Networks (LQN), a novel approach that enables effective collaboration among agents in complex environments. Our objective is to develop a framework that balances individual agent objectives with global team goals, leading to improved overall performance. We propose a quantile-based method that models the likelihood of successful outcomes, allowing agents to adapt and coordinate their actions. Our approach leverages a combination of deep reinforcement learning and probabilistic modeling to learn coordinated policies. Experimental results demonstrate that LQN outperforms existing methods in several multi-agent scenarios, showcasing improved cooperation and adaptability. The key findings highlight the importance of quantile-based likelihood modeling in facilitating effective multi-agent coordination. This research contributes to the development of more sophisticated multi-agent reinforcement learning algorithms, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, game playing, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: multi-agent reinforcement learning, likelihood quantile networks, deep reinforcement learning, probabilistic modeling, cooperative learning.
