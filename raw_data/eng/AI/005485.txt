Abstract:

The rapid advancement of image classification technologies has led to significant improvements in a variety of real-world applications, yet ensuring robustness against noise, adversarial attacks, and domain shifts remains a critical challenge. This paper aims to address the problem of enhancing the robustness of image classification models by introducing a comprehensive framework that integrates data augmentation strategies, ensemble learning, and adversarial training. The proposed approach leverages deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and transformer-based architectures to extract resilient features, while adaptive augmentation techniques simulate diverse environmental conditions. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets—including CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet—demonstrate that our framework consistently outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of classification accuracy and robustness under noisy, adversarial, and shifted input conditions. The findings highlight significant reductions in model vulnerability and enhanced generalization to unseen data distributions. Our research contributes a systematic methodology for developing robust image classifiers and provides practical insights for deploying reliable image recognition systems in safety-critical domains such as autonomous driving and medical diagnostics. Keywords: image classification, robustness, adversarial attacks, deep learning, data augmentation, ensemble learning, transfer learning.
