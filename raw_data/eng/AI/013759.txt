Manifold learning techniques are crucial for dimensionality reduction and data representation, particularly in high-dimensional data spaces. This paper explores the potential of deep neural networks (DNNs) to act as Locality Sensitive Hash functions (LSH) within the context of manifold learning. We propose a novel DNN architecture trained to preserve local neighborhood structures inherent in the data manifold. Our approach utilizes a specifically designed loss function that encourages data points close on the manifold to map to similar binary codes in the DNN's output layer, effectively functioning as LSH. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our DNN-based LSH method achieves superior performance compared to traditional LSH techniques and other manifold learning algorithms in terms of retrieval accuracy and computational efficiency. The results suggest that DNNs can effectively capture complex manifold structures and generate locality-sensitive hashes, offering a scalable and accurate solution for similarity search and data indexing in high-dimensional spaces. Key words: Manifold Learning, Deep Neural Networks, Locality Sensitive Hashing, Dimensionality Reduction, Similarity Search.
