This paper addresses the computational bottleneck of the Softmax function in large-scale classification tasks within Tensorflow. The objective is to improve the efficiency of training deep learning models with extremely large output spaces. We propose an optimized implementation of Sampled Softmax tailored for Tensorflow, leveraging a novel sampling strategy and parallel computation techniques. Our approach reduces the computational complexity from O(N) to O(K), where N is the number of classes and K is the number of sampled classes (K << N). Experimental results on a benchmark dataset demonstrate a significant speedup in training time compared to the standard Softmax and existing Sampled Softmax implementations, while maintaining comparable accuracy. Specifically, we achieve a 2x to 5x reduction in training time with minimal impact on classification performance. This efficient Sampled Softmax implementation enables faster training of large-scale classification models in Tensorflow, facilitating research and applications in areas such as natural language processing and recommendation systems. Keywords: Sampled Softmax, Tensorflow, large-scale classification, deep learning, efficiency, optimization.
