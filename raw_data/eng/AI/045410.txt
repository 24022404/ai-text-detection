This paper proposes a novel approach to human attribute recognition by introducing a distraction-aware feature learning framework that leverages a coarse-to-fine attention mechanism. The objective is to improve the accuracy of attribute recognition by mitigating the impact of distracting factors in images. Our method employs a hierarchical attention structure that initially focuses on the entire image to identify relevant regions and then zooms in on these areas to extract fine-grained features. The results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods, achieving significant improvements in recognition accuracy. Key findings indicate that the coarse-to-fine attention mechanism effectively filters out distractions, leading to more robust and accurate attribute recognition. The contributions of this research lie in its ability to handle complex scenes with multiple distracting elements, making it a valuable tool for various applications, including surveillance, security, and human-computer interaction. The proposed framework highlights the importance of attention mechanisms in deep learning models for human attribute recognition, with relevant keywords including attention mechanism, human attribute recognition, deep learning, and computer vision.
