This paper presents Online Progressive Deep Metric Learning, a novel approach to learning effective distance metrics in an online setting. The objective is to develop a method that can efficiently adapt to streaming data and learn from limited labeled information. Our approach utilizes a progressive learning strategy, where a deep neural network is trained incrementally using a metric learning objective. The method leverages a combination of online hard negative mining and metric loss functions to learn a robust and discriminative embedding space. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art online metric learning methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The key findings highlight the importance of progressive learning and online hard negative mining in deep metric learning. This research contributes to the development of online learning methods for computer vision and machine learning applications, with potential implications for real-time image and video analysis, recommendation systems, and data streaming applications. Key keywords: deep metric learning, online learning, progressive learning, hard negative mining, computer vision, machine learning.
