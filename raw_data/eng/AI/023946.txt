This paper presents a novel approach to semantic segmentation using Boundary Neural Fields (BNFs), aiming to improve the accuracy and efficiency of image segmentation tasks. Our objective is to develop a robust and adaptive model that can effectively capture complex object boundaries and semantic contexts. We propose a new architecture that integrates neural fields with boundary-aware loss functions, enabling the model to learn precise boundary representations and semantic labels simultaneously. Our approach is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in segmentation accuracy and boundary precision compared to state-of-the-art methods. The results show that our BNF model achieves superior performance in handling complex scenes, object occlusions, and ambiguous boundaries. This research contributes to the advancement of semantic segmentation and boundary detection, with potential applications in computer vision, robotics, and autonomous systems. Key contributions include the introduction of boundary-aware neural fields, a novel loss function design, and a comprehensive evaluation framework. Relevant keywords: semantic segmentation, boundary detection, neural fields, deep learning, computer vision.
