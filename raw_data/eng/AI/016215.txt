The system is designed to empower users with the ability to manage, view, and verify their cryptographic shares and private keys independently, thus significantly enhancing both security and operational flexibility. By decentralizing the control over cryptographic assets, users are no longer reliant on a single trusted party; instead, they assume an active role in safeguarding their own sensitive information. Central to this framework is the executor, whose responsibilities are meticulously outlined in Table 4.6 and visually represented in Figure 4.5. The executor plays a pivotal role within the Distributed Key Generation (DKG) protocol, contributing to the collaborative process of key creation by securely generating and distributing shares among participants. Moreover, the executor interacts with a public smart contract through a series of clearly defined steps, each designed to uphold the transparency and correctness of cryptographic procedures. These interactions with smart contracts not only facilitate automated verification and record-keeping but also minimize the risk of human error and potential compromise. By combining autonomous user management with the rigor of protocol-based execution and blockchain-backed transparency, the system establishes a robust foundation for secure, trustless cryptographic operations that adapt to diverse application requirements.
