This paper introduces TDGIA, a novel framework designed to launch effective injection attacks on Graph Neural Networks (GNNs). The objective is to investigate the vulnerability of GNNs to targeted attacks, which can compromise their performance and reliability. Our approach leverages a combination of graph structure analysis and node injection strategies to craft malicious nodes that can mislead GNNs. We employ a reinforcement learning-based method to optimize the injection process, maximizing the attack's success rate while minimizing the number of injected nodes. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of TDGIA, outperforming existing attack methods in terms of attack success rate and node injection efficiency. Our findings highlight the potential risks associated with GNNs and the importance of developing robust defense mechanisms. The contributions of this research include a new perspective on GNN vulnerability assessment and a practical framework for evaluating the security of GNN-based systems, with implications for applications in social network analysis, recommendation systems, and graph-based AI models. Key keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Injection Attacks, Reinforcement Learning, Node Injection, GNN Security.
