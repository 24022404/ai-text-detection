This paper introduces JDSR-GAN, a novel joint and collaborative learning framework specifically designed for the super-resolution of masked face images. The proliferation of partial occlusions, particularly face masks, in real-world scenarios poses significant challenges to facial image enhancement, often leading to information loss and degraded recognition performance. To address this issue, JDSR-GAN leverages a dual-stream architecture that integrates global facial context with local masked regions, enabling more effective feature extraction and detail restoration. The network employs generative adversarial learning in conjunction with specialized attention mechanisms to reconstruct high-quality facial features from low-resolution, partially masked inputs. Extensive experiments conducted on multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate that JDSR-GAN outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of both quantitative metrics and visual fidelity, particularly under high degrees of occlusion. The proposed approach shows substantial improvements in preserving identity and facial characteristics, making it highly applicable for security surveillance, forensic analysis, and other real-world applications where faces are commonly obscured. This work advances the field of masked face super-resolution by providing an effective joint learning paradigm and sets a new benchmark for both accuracy and robustness in challenging scenarios. Key keywords: masked face super-resolution, generative adversarial network, joint learning, collaborative learning, image reconstruction, attention mechanism.
