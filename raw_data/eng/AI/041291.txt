Meta-reinforcement learning (meta-RL) enables agents to rapidly adapt to new tasks by leveraging prior experience. However, designing effective task distributions for training meta-RL agents remains a challenge. This paper introduces Alchemy, a novel framework for structured task distribution in meta-RL. Alchemy facilitates the creation of diverse and informative task distributions by composing tasks from a set of modular components. These components represent distinct aspects of the environment, such as dynamics, reward functions, or initial conditions. By systematically combining these components, Alchemy generates a task space with controllable complexity and coverage. We evaluate Alchemy on several benchmark environments, demonstrating that agents trained with Alchemy exhibit significantly improved generalization performance and faster adaptation compared to agents trained with unstructured or randomly generated task distributions. Our results highlight the importance of structured task design in meta-RL and offer a practical approach for creating effective learning curricula. Keywords: Meta-Reinforcement Learning, Task Distribution, Curriculum Learning, Generalization, Reinforcement Learning.
