In the realm of automated manufacturing, precise bin picking is challenging due to the complex interactions, varying geometries, and occlusions present in densely packed workpieces. This paper addresses these challenges by introducing a novel deep learning-based approach for workpiece region segmentation tailored to bin picking applications. The objective of our research is to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of identifying and segmenting distinct workpieces within cluttered bins, thereby facilitating reliable robotic manipulation. Our method utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, specifically optimized for spatial feature extraction and segmentation accuracy. Through extensive experimentation, we demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing segmentation techniques in terms of precision and computational efficiency. The results showcase increased system performance, achieving higher segmentation fidelity and reduced processing times, which are pivotal for real-time applications. We conclude that this method not only advances the current state of automated bin picking systems but also lays the groundwork for further integration with robotic systems, improving productivity and adaptability in industrial settings. Keywords include deep learning, bin picking, region segmentation, CNN, and robotic manipulation.
