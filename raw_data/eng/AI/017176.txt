This paper addresses the challenge of class imbalance in data classification, a common problem in many real-world applications. Our objective is to develop an effective approach to handle imbalance data using deep learning techniques. We propose a Class Expert Generative Adversarial Network (CE-GAN) model, which leverages the strengths of generative adversarial networks to generate synthetic samples for the minority class, thereby improving classification performance. Our approach utilizes a novel architecture that combines an expert generator for each class, allowing for more precise control over the generated samples. Experimental results demonstrate that our CE-GAN model outperforms state-of-the-art methods in handling class imbalance, achieving significant improvements in accuracy, precision, and recall. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a class-expert based GAN architecture and the demonstration of its effectiveness in addressing class imbalance. Our work has important implications for applications where class imbalance is a significant challenge, such as medical diagnosis, fraud detection, and text classification. Key keywords: class imbalance, generative adversarial networks, deep learning, classification, CE-GAN.
