The rapid development of generative models has led to a growing need for improved synthetic image quality. This research introduces an innovative image-based generator architecture designed specifically for refining synthetic images, addressing prevalent issues related to visual fidelity and artefacts in generated visuals.

Methods/Approach: The proposed architecture utilizes an advanced deep learning framework built upon a convolutional neural network (CNN) backbone to enhance image realism. By integrating a novel image refinement module, the architecture effectively identifies and rectifies artefacts in low-quality synthetic images. Techniques such as adversarial training and perceptual loss functions are employed to optimize refinement capabilities, ensuring higher fidelity results.

Results/Findings: Extensive experiments conducted on widely used benchmark datasets demonstrate the superiority of our approach over conventional methods. The enhanced generator achieves state-of-the-art results in image quality metrics such as Structural Similarity Index (SSIM) and Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR), while also significantly reducing visual discrepancies and artefacts in synthetic imagery.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes a significant advancement in synthetic image generation technologies, paving the way for more effective applications in fields ranging from virtual reality to digital media production. The improved accuracy and visual quality suggest potential for future developments in domains requiring high-quality image synthesis. Key innovations include enhanced architectural design for artifact correction and improved training methodologies, providing a substantial step forward in generator research.

Keywords: synthetic image refinement, generator architecture, deep learning, CNN, adversarial training, image quality enhancement.
