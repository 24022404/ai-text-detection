Accurate classification of primary tumor origin in lung nodules is crucial for effective cancer treatment planning. This study investigates the application of transfer learning to spectral computed tomography (CT) imaging for improved classification accuracy. We leverage pre-trained convolutional neural networks (CNNs) on large-scale natural image datasets and fine-tune them using a dataset of spectral CT images of lung nodules with known primary tumor origins. The proposed transfer learning approach aims to overcome the limitations of training CNNs from scratch with limited medical imaging data. Experimental results demonstrate that the transfer learning model achieves significantly higher classification accuracy compared to traditional machine learning methods and CNNs trained without transfer learning. Specifically, our model achieves an average improvement of 15% in F1-score. These findings suggest that transfer learning from pre-trained models on spectral CT imaging holds considerable promise for enhancing the diagnostic accuracy of lung nodule characterization and enabling more personalized cancer treatment strategies. Keywords: Spectral CT, Lung Nodules, Primary Tumor Origin, Transfer Learning, Convolutional Neural Networks, Medical Imaging.
