This paper addresses the challenge of accurate filling level classification in industrial applications, where variations in product appearance, lighting conditions, and container types can significantly impact performance. We propose a novel adversarial training framework to enhance the robustness and generalization ability of filling level classification models. Our approach utilizes a generator network to create adversarial examples that mimic real-world variations, thereby forcing the classifier to learn more invariant features. We evaluate our method on a diverse dataset of images with varying filling levels and container types. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed adversarial training approach significantly improves classification accuracy compared to standard training techniques, achieving state-of-the-art performance. Specifically, our method shows increased resilience to changes in lighting and perspective. This research contributes a practical and effective solution for robust filling level classification, enabling more reliable automation and quality control in manufacturing processes. Keywords: filling level classification, adversarial training, robustness, computer vision, image analysis, machine learning, deep learning.
