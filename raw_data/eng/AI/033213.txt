This paper explores the vulnerability of Google reCAPTCHA v3 to reinforcement learning (RL) based attacks. The objective is to demonstrate the feasibility of bypassing reCAPTCHA v3's bot detection mechanisms using an intelligent agent. We employ a deep Q-network (DQN) agent to learn an optimal interaction strategy with websites protected by reCAPTCHA v3. The agent is trained to mimic human-like browsing behavior, adjusting parameters such as mouse movements, click patterns, and navigation paths. Experimental results show that the DQN agent can achieve a high success rate in obtaining valid scores from reCAPTCHA v3, effectively bypassing the security system. This research highlights a critical weakness in reCAPTCHA v3, suggesting that current scoring algorithms are susceptible to sophisticated RL-based attacks. The findings emphasize the need for improved bot detection techniques that are resilient to adaptive adversarial strategies. Keywords: reCAPTCHA v3, Reinforcement Learning, Bot Detection, DQN, Security, Artificial Intelligence.
