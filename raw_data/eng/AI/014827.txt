This paper introduces NightVision, a novel approach for generating synthetic nighttime satellite imagery from readily available infrared (IR) observations. The primary objective is to overcome the limitations of traditional nighttime satellite imaging, which is often hindered by cloud cover and the high cost of specialized sensors. NightVision leverages a deep learning framework, specifically a conditional generative adversarial network (cGAN), trained to map IR data to corresponding visible light imagery. The method utilizes a custom loss function that combines pixel-wise similarity with perceptual and adversarial losses to ensure realistic and high-resolution image synthesis. Experimental results demonstrate that NightVision achieves superior performance compared to existing interpolation and statistical methods, generating visually plausible and accurate nighttime imagery even in challenging atmospheric conditions. The generated imagery holds significant potential for various applications, including urban monitoring, disaster response, and environmental studies. Key words: satellite imagery, nighttime, infrared, image synthesis, generative adversarial network, deep learning.
