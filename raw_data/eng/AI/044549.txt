Abstract:

The rapid growth of big data has posed significant challenges for efficient and accurate classification in large-scale information systems. This paper presents a novel approach to big data classification by employing augmented decision trees (ADTs), which enhance traditional decision tree algorithms with advanced feature selection, ensemble strategies, and scalable data processing techniques. The proposed model integrates distributed computing frameworks to handle high-volume, high-velocity datasets while reducing computational bottlenecks. Comprehensive experiments were conducted on several benchmark big data repositories to evaluate the effectiveness of the ADT-based classification. Comparative analyses demonstrate that our method achieves superior classification accuracy and significantly improved processing speed over conventional decision tree and random forest algorithms, particularly in scenarios with highly imbalanced or noisy data. The results highlight the model’s robustness, scalability, and adaptability in real-world big data environments. This research contributes a flexible and efficient solution for scalable data mining, data classification, and real-time analytics, making it applicable to a wide range of big data applications such as fraud detection, network intrusion analysis, and customer behavior prediction. Key findings also implicate future development of hybrid and adaptive classification systems. Keywords: big data classification, augmented decision trees, scalable algorithms, distributed computing, data mining, machine learning.
