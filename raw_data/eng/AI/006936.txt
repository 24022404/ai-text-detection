This paper addresses the critical task of estimating uncertainty in predicting cancer survival rates using machine learning models. By quantifying uncertainty, clinicians can make more informed decisions, improving patient outcomes and treatment planning.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel approach that integrates Bayesian neural networks with ensemble learning to estimate uncertainty in cancer survival predictions. This method allows for capturing both model uncertainty and data variability. We utilize a diverse set of cancer datasets to train and test our model, implementing a comprehensive cross-validation strategy to ensure robustness. Key performance metrics are analyzed, including calibration and reliability diagrams, to assess the accuracy of uncertainty estimation.

Results/Findings: Our approach demonstrates significant improvements in prediction accuracy and reliability over traditional machine learning models lacking uncertainty estimation capabilities. The results indicate that incorporating uncertainty estimation reduces prediction errors and enhances the trustworthiness of the survival predictions. Furthermore, comparative analysis with state-of-the-art techniques shows superior performance in identifying high-risk patients and predicting long-term survival outcomes.

Conclusion/Implications: The integration of uncertainty estimation in cancer survival prediction introduces a paradigm shift in predictive modeling for oncology. This research contributes a robust framework for uncertainty quantification, enabling more precise and actionable predictions. The findings have broad implications for clinical decision support systems, potentially leading to improved personalized treatment plans and patient care strategies. Future work may extend this approach to other medical prediction applications, ensuring accurate uncertainty estimation across various health domains.

Keywords: Uncertainty Estimation, Cancer Survival Prediction, Bayesian Neural Networks, Ensemble Learning, Predictive Oncology, Machine Learning.
