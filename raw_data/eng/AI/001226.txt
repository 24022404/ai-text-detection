This paper introduces a novel reinforcement learning algorithm that synergistically combines the strengths of policy gradient and Q-learning methods. The primary objective is to address the limitations of each individual approach, namely the high variance of policy gradients and the overestimation bias often observed in Q-learning. Our approach, named PGQL, leverages policy gradients for stable policy updates and utilizes a modified Q-learning framework to provide accurate value estimation. PGQL employs a shared neural network architecture for both policy and value function approximation, enabling efficient learning and knowledge transfer. Experimental results across a range of benchmark environments demonstrate that PGQL achieves superior performance compared to state-of-the-art policy gradient and Q-learning algorithms, exhibiting faster convergence, higher asymptotic reward, and improved sample efficiency. The implications of this research suggest that PGQL offers a robust and effective solution for complex reinforcement learning tasks, particularly in scenarios requiring both precise policy control and reliable value estimation. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Policy Gradient, Q-Learning, Actor-Critic, Hybrid Algorithm.
