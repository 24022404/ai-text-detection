This paper provides an in-depth tour of mobile computational photography, a field that has revolutionized the way we capture and process images using smartphones. The objective is to explore the latest advancements and techniques in mobile computational photography, including multi-frame noise reduction, super-resolution, and depth estimation. Our approach involves a comprehensive analysis of existing methods, algorithms, and technologies used in mobile devices, such as AI-powered image processing, 3D reconstruction, and machine learning-based image enhancement. The results show significant improvements in image quality, noise reduction, and depth estimation, making mobile computational photography a viable alternative to traditional digital cameras. The conclusion highlights the importance of mobile computational photography in various applications, including photography, computer vision, and augmented reality, and emphasizes its potential to enable new use cases and applications. Key contributions include a thorough review of existing techniques, identification of future research directions, and a discussion of the implications of mobile computational photography on the field of computer science, with relevant keywords including computational photography, mobile imaging, AI-powered image processing, and machine learning-based image enhancement.
