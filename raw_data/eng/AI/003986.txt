Communication channels are vulnerable to distortions caused by noise, defects, and the existence of prohibited bit patterns, which can compromise the integrity of transmitted messages, often without the receiver’s awareness. Channel coding plays a vital role in mitigating these risks by re-encoding messages after the initial stage of source coding. This process introduces redundancy and specific structures into the message, enabling the detection and correction of errors introduced during transmission. Ultimately, channel coding ensures that the channel remains effectively transparent for both the source and the destination, preserving message fidelity.
