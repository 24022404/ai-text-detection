This paper explores the enhancement of explainable machine learning models through hardware acceleration, specifically utilizing Tensor Processing Units (TPUs). The primary objective is to improve the performance and efficiency of machine learning models that provide transparent and interpretable outputs, which are becoming crucial in sensitive applications like healthcare and finance.

Methods/Approach: We developed an accelerated workflow integrating TPUs with state-of-the-art explainable machine learning algorithms. By leveraging the parallel processing capabilities and specialized architecture of TPUs, we aimed to increase the processing speed without compromising the accuracy of the explainability of the models. Techniques such as Shapley values and LIME (Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations) were adapted to work efficiently on this hardware platform.

Results/Findings: Our approach demonstrated significant improvements in both speed and scalability compared to traditional CPU and GPU implementations. The TPU-based acceleration reduced computation times by up to 65% while maintaining a high degree of interpretability and accuracy. Performance benchmarks indicate a notable enhancement in processing power, which facilitates the deployment of complex models in real-time applications.

Conclusion/Implications: The integration of TPUs for hardware acceleration in explainable machine learning offers substantial advancements in processing capabilities, making it feasible to utilize complex models in scenarios demanding real-time decision-making. This research contributes to promoting the practical adoption of machine learning models in critical sectors where explainability is paramount. The findings suggest that TPUs can play a vital role in advancing machine learning applications, thus fostering innovation in areas requiring both speed and transparency.

Keywords: Tensor Processing Units, Explainable Machine Learning, Hardware Acceleration, Model Interpretability, Shapley Values, LIME.
