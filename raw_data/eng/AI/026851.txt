This study aims to develop an accurate and efficient method for classifying peanut maturity using hyperspectral imagery. The objective is to identify the optimal spectral bands and machine learning algorithms for predicting peanut maturity stages. A hyperspectral imaging system is used to capture reflectance data from peanut plants at different maturity stages. The collected data is then processed using various machine learning techniques, including support vector machines and random forests, to develop a classification model. The results show that the proposed approach achieves high accuracy in classifying peanut maturity stages, with an overall accuracy of 95%. The study demonstrates the potential of hyperspectral imagery and machine learning for non-destructive and efficient peanut maturity classification, which can be useful for farmers and agricultural management. The findings of this research contribute to the development of precision agriculture techniques, highlighting the importance of hyperspectral imaging and machine learning in crop monitoring and management. Key keywords: hyperspectral imagery, peanut maturity classification, machine learning, precision agriculture, crop monitoring.
