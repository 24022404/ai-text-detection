Fashion outfit recommendation systems have garnered significant interest in the realm of online retail and personalized styling, with a growing demand for intelligent tools to suggest items that complement a user's selected outfit. This paper addresses the challenge of fashion outfit complementary item retrieval, aiming to automatically identify and recommend fashion products that harmonize with existing outfit choices. We propose a deep learning-based framework that combines image and textual feature extraction using a dual-encoder architecture, enabling the system to understand visual aesthetics and contextual information from fashion catalogue data. A large-scale dataset comprising diverse outfits and product metadata was constructed and used to train and evaluate our model. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms baseline models in both retrieval accuracy and user relevance, with notable improvements in handling cross-category recommendations. The findings highlight the effectiveness of integrating multimodal data for nuanced fashion understanding and personalized suggestion. This work contributes a robust solution for fashion retailers and online platforms seeking to enhance user engagement and satisfaction through seamless complementary item recommendations. Key keywords: fashion recommendation, complementary item retrieval, deep learning, multimodal, outfit matching, recommender system.
