Topological Data Analysis (TDA) provides robust descriptors of time series data, yet its computational cost can be prohibitive for large datasets. This paper addresses this challenge by introducing a novel approach to time series classification using the Sinkhorn divergence between topological signature estimates. We leverage persistent homology to extract topological features, representing time series as persistence diagrams. To efficiently compare these diagrams, we employ the Sinkhorn divergence, approximated via a sparse formulation to reduce computational complexity. Our method significantly reduces the computational burden compared to traditional bottleneck or Wasserstein distances, enabling its application to large time series datasets. Empirical evaluations on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves comparable or superior classification accuracy to existing TDA-based methods, while offering substantial speedups in computation time. This work provides a scalable and effective TDA-based framework for time series classification, opening avenues for real-time analysis and anomaly detection in various applications. Keywords: Time Series Classification, Topological Data Analysis, Persistent Homology, Sinkhorn Divergence, Computational Topology.
