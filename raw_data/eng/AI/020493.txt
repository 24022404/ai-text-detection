This paper presents a novel approach to image segmentation using a multi-threshold technique based on histogram sampling. The objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of image segmentation by automatically determining optimal thresholds. Our method employs a histogram sampling strategy to identify the most informative thresholds, which are then used to segment the image into distinct regions. The results show that our approach outperforms traditional thresholding methods, achieving higher accuracy and reduced computational complexity. The key findings indicate that the proposed technique is robust to noise and variations in image intensity, making it suitable for a wide range of applications. The contributions of this research include the development of a efficient and accurate image segmentation algorithm, with potential applications in medical imaging, object recognition, and computer vision. The use of histogram sampling and multi-threshold technique enables the segmentation of complex images, highlighting the novelty and significance of this work. Key keywords: image segmentation, multi-threshold technique, histogram sampling, computer vision, medical imaging.
