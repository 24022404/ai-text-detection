Abstract:

Scene text recognition (STR) is a crucial task in computer vision, enabling the extraction of textual information from images captured in natural environments. Traditional approaches often struggle with irregular layouts, complex backgrounds, or distortions resulting from varied perspectives. This paper introduces a novel STR framework that leverages a two-dimensional perspective, addressing the limitations of one-dimensional sequence-based models. Our method integrates a spatial attention mechanism with a convolutional neural network (CNN) backbone, effectively capturing both horizontal and vertical dependencies within a scene image. By modeling scene text as a 2D feature map rather than a simple sequence, our approach enhances robustness to spatial variations and improves recognition accuracy in challenging scenarios, such as curved, rotated, or occluded text. Extensive experiments conducted on standard scene text benchmarks demonstrate that our model surpasses existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of both accuracy and generalization across diverse datasets. The proposed framework also maintains computational efficiency, making it suitable for real-time applications. This research contributes a new perspective to STR, supporting advancements in image understanding, document analysis, and intelligent transportation systems. Key keywords include scene text recognition, two-dimensional perspective, spatial attention, convolutional neural networks, and image understanding.
