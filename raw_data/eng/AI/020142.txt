This paper introduces ProLab, a novel perceptually uniform projective colour coordinate system designed to enhance colour representation and processing in various applications. The objective of ProLab is to address the limitations of existing colour models by providing a more accurate and efficient way of capturing human colour perception. To achieve this, we employ a projective approach that combines colour science and machine learning techniques to create a uniform colour space. Our method involves a multi-stage process that includes colour data collection, dimensionality reduction, and projection onto a uniform colour manifold. The results show that ProLab outperforms existing colour models in terms of colour accuracy, robustness, and computational efficiency. The ProLab system has significant implications for various fields, including computer vision, graphics, and colour reproduction, enabling more realistic and efficient colour processing and analysis. Key contributions of this research include the development of a perceptually uniform colour space, a projective colour mapping algorithm, and a comprehensive evaluation framework for colour models. Relevant keywords: colour science, projective colour coordinate system, perceptual uniformity, colour representation, computer vision, graphics.
