This paper introduces O-CNN (Octree-based Convolutional Neural Networks), a novel framework for efficient and scalable 3D shape analysis. Traditional convolutional neural networks (CNNs) face significant challenges when applied to 3D data due to the irregular and sparse nature of 3D representations. To address this, we propose an octree-based data structure that hierarchically organizes 3D shapes, enabling efficient memory usage and computation. O-CNN leverages this structure to perform convolutional operations directly on the octree, significantly reducing computational complexity while maintaining high accuracy. Our approach is evaluated on benchmark datasets for tasks such as 3D shape classification, segmentation, and reconstruction. Experimental results demonstrate that O-CNN outperforms existing methods in terms of both computational efficiency and performance, achieving state-of-the-art accuracy with reduced memory and runtime requirements. The proposed framework not only advances the field of 3D deep learning but also opens new possibilities for real-time applications in areas such as robotics, autonomous driving, and augmented reality. Key contributions include the integration of octree structures with CNNs, a scalable training pipeline, and a versatile architecture adaptable to various 3D analysis tasks. Keywords: 3D shape analysis, convolutional neural networks, octree, deep learning, 3D reconstruction, computational efficiency.
