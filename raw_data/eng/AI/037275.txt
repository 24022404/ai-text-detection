Early classification of time series (EC-TS) aims to predict the class label of a time series instance as early and accurately as possible. This review paper surveys the existing approaches to EC-TS and their diverse applications. We categorize EC-TS methods based on their underlying techniques, including distance-based, feature-based, and model-based approaches, with a focus on recent advances utilizing deep learning. Furthermore, we examine various application domains where EC-TS has proven valuable, such as healthcare monitoring, financial forecasting, and anomaly detection in industrial processes. The review provides a comparative analysis of different EC-TS algorithms in terms of accuracy, earliness, and computational efficiency. This paper highlights the challenges and opportunities in the field, emphasizing the need for robust and interpretable EC-TS methods suitable for real-world applications with streaming data. Keywords: Time series classification, early classification, review, machine learning, streaming data, predictive monitoring.
