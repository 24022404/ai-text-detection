The project’s primary objective centers on the comprehensive deployment of a helpdesk website dedicated to ANSV’s IT support clients, ensuring a robust and user-friendly experience for both clients and internal staff. The initiative is meticulously designed to facilitate effective management of SLA (Service Level Agreement) policies directly within the website’s helpdesk system. By integrating SLA management, the platform aims to guarantee prompt and reliable responses to client requests, reinforcing trust and accountability between ANSV and its clients. Equally essential is the focus on delivering a highly usable frontend interface, which allows clients to submit support tickets with ease, track request statuses in real time, and access categorized assistance for diverse issues, thereby empowering users to interact seamlessly with the support system. On the other side, an advanced backend user interface for ANSV’s IT team is developed to offer efficient case tracking, automated ticket assignment, escalation mechanisms, and comprehensive reporting tools, which collectively streamline service delivery and performance monitoring. From a technical standpoint, an integral part of the project involves configuring Odoo15/16 services to align with organizational workflows, optimize user authentication, and maintain system scalability. To further ensure reliability and responsiveness, the project also includes the assessment and tuning of server swap space, which is crucial for maintaining optimal performance and handling fluctuating loads. Collectively, these steps aim to deliver a powerful, scalable, and integrated helpdesk solution that meets the evolving needs of ANSV’s IT support environment.
