This paper introduces a novel approach to signed graph metric learning by leveraging the Gershgorin Disc Perfect Alignment (GDPA) principle. Signed graphs, which represent both positive and negative relationships between nodes, present unique challenges for traditional metric learning techniques. Our objective is to learn a distance metric that effectively captures the underlying structure and semantics of signed graphs. We propose a GDPA-based framework that minimizes the variance within Gershgorin discs associated with each node, encouraging nodes with similar signed relationships to cluster closer together in the learned metric space. The method involves formulating a semidefinite programming (SDP) problem to optimize the metric, ensuring that the Gershgorin discs are perfectly aligned, reflecting the signed relationships. Experimental results on several real-world signed graph datasets demonstrate that our GDPA-based approach outperforms state-of-the-art signed graph embedding and metric learning algorithms in terms of link prediction and node classification accuracy. The learned metric provides a more accurate representation of signed graph data, leading to improved performance in downstream tasks. This work offers a new perspective on signed graph analysis and provides a promising direction for future research in network embedding and metric learning.

Keywords: Signed Graph, Metric Learning, Gershgorin Disc, Semidefinite Programming, Graph Embedding.
