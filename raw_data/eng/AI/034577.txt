This paper addresses the challenge of high-dimensional multivariate forecasting, where traditional methods often struggle with scalability and accuracy due to the curse of dimensionality. We propose a novel approach leveraging low-rank Gaussian copula processes to model complex dependencies across multiple time series efficiently. Our method combines the flexibility of copula-based dependency modeling with the computational efficiency of low-rank approximations, enabling accurate forecasting in high-dimensional settings. The approach is evaluated on synthetic and real-world datasets, demonstrating superior performance in terms of predictive accuracy and computational efficiency compared to state-of-the-art methods. Key findings reveal that the proposed model effectively captures both temporal and cross-dimensional dependencies, making it particularly suitable for applications in finance, climate modeling, and large-scale sensor networks. The results highlight the potential of low-rank Gaussian copula processes as a scalable and robust framework for high-dimensional forecasting, offering significant improvements over existing techniques. This research contributes to the advancement of multivariate time series analysis by providing a computationally efficient and theoretically grounded solution for real-world forecasting challenges.  

**Keywords**: multivariate forecasting, Gaussian copula, low-rank approximation, high-dimensional data, time series analysis, scalability.
