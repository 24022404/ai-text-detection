This paper addresses the challenge of distribution matching in generative models, a critical task for ensuring that generated samples accurately reflect the underlying data distribution. We introduce the "Best-of-Many-Samples" (BoMS) approach, a novel framework designed to improve the alignment between generated and real data distributions. Unlike traditional methods that rely on single-sample comparisons, BoMS leverages multiple candidate samples to identify the best match, thereby enhancing the fidelity and diversity of generated outputs. Our approach integrates a robust optimization algorithm that iteratively refines the selection process, ensuring efficient and scalable performance. Experimental results demonstrate that BoMS significantly outperforms existing distribution matching techniques across multiple benchmarks, achieving higher sample quality and better distributional alignment. The proposed method is particularly effective in applications such as image synthesis, text generation, and data augmentation, where precise distribution matching is crucial. This work contributes to the field by providing a scalable and effective solution for improving generative models, with potential implications for advancing AI-driven content creation and synthetic data generation.  

**Keywords**: distribution matching, generative models, sample quality, optimization, AI, synthetic data.
