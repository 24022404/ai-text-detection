This paper addresses the challenge of training neural networks effectively in scenarios where high-fidelity data is scarce or expensive to obtain, while low-fidelity data is abundant. We propose a novel approach that leverages \ell 1-regularization combined with bi-fidelity data to enhance model performance and generalization. The \ell 1-regularization technique is employed to promote sparsity in the network weights, reducing overfitting and improving interpretability. Simultaneously, the bi-fidelity framework integrates both low- and high-fidelity data to train the model, ensuring robust learning even with limited high-quality data. Our methodology involves a two-stage training process: first, the model is pre-trained on low-fidelity data to capture general patterns, followed by fine-tuning using high-fidelity data to refine accuracy. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach outperforms traditional methods in terms of prediction accuracy and computational efficiency, particularly in resource-constrained environments. The findings highlight the potential of combining regularization techniques with multi-fidelity data for training neural networks, offering a scalable solution for applications in scientific computing, engineering, and beyond. This work contributes to the growing body of research on efficient neural network training and provides a practical framework for leveraging heterogeneous data sources.  

**Keywords**: Neural networks, \ell 1-regularization, bi-fidelity data, sparsity, model training, machine learning.
