This paper addresses the challenge of efficient inference in probabilistic graphical models, particularly focusing on belief propagation (BP) in scenarios where computational resources are limited. We propose a novel approach, *Anytime Belief Propagation Using Sparse Domains* (ABP-SD), which dynamically reduces the complexity of BP by leveraging sparsity in variable domains. The objective is to enable real-time, resource-efficient inference without compromising accuracy. Our method introduces a domain sparsification technique that selectively prunes low-probability states during message passing, allowing the algorithm to adaptively balance computational cost and inference quality. We evaluate ABP-SD on benchmark datasets and real-world applications, demonstrating significant improvements in runtime efficiency compared to traditional BP, while maintaining competitive accuracy. The results highlight the method's ability to provide anytime solutions, making it particularly suitable for time-sensitive applications such as robotics and real-time decision-making systems. This work contributes a scalable and adaptive framework for probabilistic inference, offering a practical solution for deploying BP in resource-constrained environments.  

**Keywords**: belief propagation, probabilistic graphical models, sparse domains, anytime algorithms, real-time inference, resource efficiency.
