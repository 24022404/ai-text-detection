We introduce a novel approach for unconstrained scene generation using locally conditioned radiance fields. This method addresses the challenge of creating diverse and high-quality 3D scenes without relying on large, curated datasets or explicit object supervision. Our approach employs a generative adversarial network (GAN) architecture where the generator learns to produce radiance fields conditioned on local geometric and appearance features. These local features are extracted from a learned codebook, allowing for compositional scene creation. A key innovation is the use of a differentiable rendering pipeline that enables end-to-end training of the generator and discriminator networks. Experiments demonstrate that our method generates complex and realistic scenes with improved diversity and visual fidelity compared to existing generative radiance field techniques. Furthermore, we show that our local conditioning strategy facilitates control over scene content and structure. The generated scenes exhibit coherent geometry and appearance, demonstrating the efficacy of our approach for unconstrained 3D content creation. Key words: Radiance Fields, Generative Adversarial Networks, 3D Scene Generation, Unsupervised Learning, Differentiable Rendering.
