This paper introduces GNN3DMOT, a novel framework for 3D Multi-Object Tracking (MOT) leveraging Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and multi-feature learning. The primary objective is to address the challenges of accurately tracking multiple objects in 3D space, particularly in dynamic environments such as autonomous driving and robotics. Traditional MOT methods often struggle with occlusions, complex object interactions, and varying object appearances. To overcome these limitations, GNN3DMOT integrates a GNN-based architecture that effectively models object relationships and interactions within a graph structure. The framework incorporates multi-feature learning, combining spatial, temporal, and appearance features to enhance tracking robustness. Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets demonstrate that GNN3DMOT achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods in terms of tracking accuracy and robustness. The results highlight the effectiveness of the proposed approach in handling complex scenarios with occlusions and dynamic object movements. This research contributes to the advancement of 3D MOT by providing a scalable and efficient solution with potential applications in autonomous systems, surveillance, and augmented reality.  

**Keywords**: 3D Multi-Object Tracking, Graph Neural Networks, Multi-Feature Learning, Autonomous Systems, Object Interaction Modeling.
