This paper introduces a novel approach to point-based neural rendering, leveraging per-view optimization to enhance the quality and efficiency of 3D scene reconstruction and rendering. Traditional neural rendering techniques often struggle with scalability and computational overhead, particularly when handling complex scenes with varying viewpoints. To address these challenges, we propose a method that combines point cloud representations with neural networks, enabling real-time rendering while maintaining high fidelity. Our approach employs a per-view optimization strategy, which dynamically adjusts the rendering process for each viewpoint, ensuring optimal performance and visual quality. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in rendering accuracy and computational efficiency compared to existing methods, particularly in scenarios with sparse or incomplete input data. The proposed method also exhibits robustness across diverse datasets, showcasing its potential for applications in virtual reality, augmented reality, and 3D content generation. By integrating point-based representations with per-view optimization, this work advances the state-of-the-art in neural rendering, offering a scalable and adaptable solution for real-world applications.  

**Keywords**: neural rendering, point clouds, per-view optimization, 3D reconstruction, real-time rendering, virtual reality, augmented reality.
