This paper addresses the challenge of automatically labelling markers in optical motion capture systems, a crucial step for accurate 3D reconstruction and motion analysis. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that eliminates the need for manual labelling, reducing processing time and increasing overall system efficiency. We propose a permutation learning method that leverages deep learning techniques to learn the mapping between marker observations and their corresponding labels. Our approach utilizes a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture to extract features from marker trajectories and a permutation-invariant loss function to optimize the labelling process. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, achieving high labelling accuracy and outperforming existing state-of-the-art approaches. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a permutation learning framework for auto-labelling and the demonstration of its potential for improving the efficiency and accuracy of optical motion capture systems. This work has significant implications for various applications, including computer vision, robotics, and animation, where accurate motion capture data is essential. Key keywords: optical motion capture, auto-labelling, permutation learning, deep learning, computer vision.
