The rapid advancement of neural networks in various applications has led to an increasing demand for adaptable systems that can efficiently manage computational resources during operation. This paper presents an innovative approach to developing runtime-throttleable neural networks, designed to dynamically adjust resource consumption without significantly sacrificing performance.

Methods/Approach: Our research introduces a novel architecture that integrates throttling mechanisms directly into neural network models. We propose a framework that allows for the on-the-fly modification of network complexity based on current runtime conditions, leveraging a layer-wise adjustability technique. This approach combines elements from sparse network design and adaptive inference strategies to achieve effective throttling.

Results/Findings: We performed extensive experiments across a wide range of datasets and network architectures. The results demonstrate that our runtime-throttleable models maintain competitive accuracy levels while reducing computational costs by up to 50% compared to traditional static approaches. Our method's adaptive nature also allows for real-time balancing between power consumption and network performance in resource-constrained environments.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of runtime throttling in neural networks paves the way for more flexible and energy-efficient AI systems. By offering a spectrum of operational states, our approach supports diverse application scenarios, from mobile devices to large-scale cloud services. This adaptability not only enhances user experience by optimizing performance but also contributes to the sustainable deployment of AI technologies. Key contributions include the proposed throttleable architecture and its potential for broad application in next-generation intelligent systems.

Keywords: Runtime Throttling, Neural Networks, Adaptive Inference, Resource Efficiency, Energy-Efficient AI, Dynamic Complexity Adjustment.
