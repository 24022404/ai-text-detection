This paper introduces Ensemble Squared, a novel meta automated machine learning (AutoML) system designed to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of machine learning pipelines. The objective is to develop a robust framework that can automatically select and combine multiple base models to improve predictive performance. Ensemble Squared employs a hierarchical approach, utilizing a meta-model to optimize the selection and weighting of base models, which are themselves generated through an AutoML process. The system is evaluated on a range of benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in accuracy and reduced computational overhead compared to existing AutoML methods. Key findings highlight the effectiveness of the meta-model in identifying optimal ensemble configurations, leading to state-of-the-art performance on several tasks. The contributions of this research include the introduction of a meta AutoML framework, which has potential applications in areas such as data science, artificial intelligence, and machine learning. Ensemble Squared showcases the power of automated machine learning, meta-learning, and ensemble methods, making it an innovative solution for real-world problems. Relevant keywords: AutoML, meta-learning, ensemble methods, machine learning, artificial intelligence.
