The objective of this research is to address the challenge of determining optimal text placement on images in graphic design and visual content creation, known as copyspace identification. Current approaches often rely on manual selection or simplistic algorithms that do not consider the intricate aesthetics and compositional elements within images. Our study introduces "Copyspace," an innovative framework that utilizes advanced image processing techniques combined with machine learning models to automate this process. The method involves analyzing image content using deep neural networks to identify areas with minimal visual clutter and high text legibility. Key findings demonstrate that Copyspace significantly improves the accuracy of text placement by considering factors such as contrast, background complexity, and focal points, outperforming existing heuristic-based methods in both qualitative and quantitative evaluations. The implications of this research are profound, as it promises to streamline workflows in advertising, publishing, and digital media, enabling designers to focus more on creativity and less on technical constraints. By offering a tool that can seamlessly integrate with existing design software, Copyspace delivers a novel solution that enhances both efficiency and aesthetic quality in visual communication. Key keywords associated with this study include copyspace identification, image processing, machine learning, text placement, and design automation.
