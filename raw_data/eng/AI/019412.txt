Abstract:

Accurate classification of 3D MRI brain volumes is crucial for early diagnosis and management of neurological disorders. This study presents a novel framework that leverages deep reinforcement learning in conjunction with automated label extraction from unstructured clinical reports to classify 3D brain MRI data. The proposed method employs natural language processing techniques to automatically generate reliable training labels by parsing clinical narratives, thereby minimizing manual annotation effort. The extracted labels are integrated with a deep reinforcement learning architecture that optimizes classification performance through iterative policy refinement. Experiments conducted on a large dataset of brain MRI volumes demonstrate that our approach achieves superior accuracy compared to conventional supervised deep learning models reliant on manually labeled data. Furthermore, robustness analyses reveal the model’s strong generalization across varied patient populations and imaging protocols. Our work highlights the potential of combining automated clinical report parsing with reinforcement learning to streamline medical image analysis pipelines and reduce dependence on labor-intensive annotations. These findings pave the way for scalable, efficient, and interpretable artificial intelligence applications in clinical neuroimaging. 

Keywords: deep reinforcement learning, automated label extraction, 3D MRI, brain classification, clinical reports, natural language processing, medical image analysis.
