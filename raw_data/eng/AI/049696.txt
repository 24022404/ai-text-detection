Zero-shot object detection (ZSOD) aims to detect objects from unseen categories by transferring knowledge from seen categories. Existing ZSOD methods often suffer from limited generalization ability due to the domain gap between seen and unseen categories and the lack of diverse feature representations. This paper introduces GTNet, a Generative Transfer Network, to address these challenges. GTNet employs a generative model to synthesize realistic and diverse object features for unseen categories, bridging the domain gap and augmenting the training data. Furthermore, we propose a novel transfer learning strategy that leverages both global semantic relationships and local feature correlations to enhance the transferability of knowledge from seen to unseen categories. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that GTNet significantly outperforms state-of-the-art ZSOD methods. The proposed GTNet provides a new direction for improving ZSOD by effectively generating and transferring knowledge, paving the way for more robust and adaptable object detection systems. Keywords: Zero-Shot Object Detection, Generative Model, Transfer Learning, Object Detection, Computer Vision.
