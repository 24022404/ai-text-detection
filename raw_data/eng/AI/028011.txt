This paper addresses the challenge of facial image manipulation, focusing on achieving diversity and interactivity in the modifications while maintaining high visual fidelity. Our goal is to develop a system that allows users to customize and manipulate facial features in images seamlessly and intuitively.

Methods/Approach: We introduce MaskGAN, a novel generative adversarial network (GAN) framework designed specifically for facial image manipulation. MaskGAN utilizes a unique combination of convolutional neural networks and attention mechanisms to enable fine-grained control over facial attributes. The model is trained on a large dataset of facial images, enhancing its ability to generate diverse outcomes while maintaining realism.

Results/Findings: Our experiments demonstrate that MaskGAN outperforms existing state-of-the-art GAN models in terms of diversity and quality of facial image manipulation. Quantitative assessments show that MaskGAN achieves higher scores in both image realism and attribute control. User studies confirm that the system provides a more interactive experience, allowing for precise and intuitive adjustments to facial features.

Conclusion/Implications: MaskGAN represents a significant advancement in the field of facial image manipulation, offering a powerful tool for applications in entertainment, virtual reality, and personalized content creation. By enabling diverse and interactive modifications, our approach opens up new possibilities for user-driven creative processes. Future work may explore the integration of MaskGAN with real-time applications and further refinement of user interface elements to enhance user experience.

Keywords: MaskGAN, generative adversarial network, facial image manipulation, diversity, interactivity, neural networks, image realism, attribute control.
