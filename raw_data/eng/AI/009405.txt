This study presents a novel approach for the automatic detection of cardiac chambers from fetal echocardiography images. The goal is to enhance diagnostic accuracy and efficiency by leveraging advanced computer vision techniques, specifically tailored for the four-chamber view of fetal cardiac scans.

Methods: We propose an attention-based YOLOv4 framework, integrating attention mechanisms to improve the localization and classification performance of conventional YOLOv4 models. This approach involves refining spatial features and enhancing the detection of cardiac structures by emphasizing regions of interest pertinent to the fetal heart. We trained and validated our model on a comprehensive dataset comprising annotated echocardiography images, utilizing data augmentation and transfer learning to bolster model robustness.

Results: The attention-based YOLOv4 framework demonstrated superior performance in detecting the four primary cardiac chambers—left atrium, right atrium, left ventricle, and right ventricle—compared to baseline YOLOv4 models. The method achieved high precision and recall rates, reflected in improved detection accuracy and reduced false positives. Furthermore, a comparative analysis with existing detection algorithms highlights the advantages of our framework in terms of speed, reliability, and ease of integration into clinical workflows.

Conclusion: Our research contributes a significant advancement in the field of fetal echocardiography by introducing an efficient and accurate technique for cardiac chamber detection. The use of an attention-based mechanism distinguishes this approach, offering potential applications in automated diagnostic systems and aiding clinicians in early cardiac pathology assessment. Future work will explore expanding the model's capabilities to other echocardiographic views and integrating it into portable diagnostic devices.

Keywords: fetal echocardiography, cardiac chamber detection, attention mechanism, YOLOv4, automated diagnosis, computer vision.
