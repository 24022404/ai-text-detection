This paper introduces a novel approach to personalize climate control in vehicles using mined driver preferences, enhancing the driving experience and comfort. We propose a system that analyzes diverse data sources, including driver biometrics, environmental conditions, and historical climate control settings, to learn individual thermal comfort profiles. These profiles are then used to proactively adjust vehicle climate settings via an assistant driving system. Our methodology employs a combination of machine learning techniques, including clustering and time-series analysis, to identify patterns and predict optimal climate configurations. Experimental results demonstrate that our personalized system significantly improves driver satisfaction, reducing manual adjustments by 40% compared to traditional rule-based systems. Furthermore, the system adapts to changing environmental conditions more effectively, maintaining a comfortable cabin environment. This research contributes a practical and efficient solution for personalized climate control, paving the way for more intelligent and adaptive assistant driving technologies. Keywords: personalized climate control, assistant driving, machine learning, thermal comfort, driver preferences, automotive AI.
