Belief Propagation (BP) is a widely used algorithm for approximate inference in graphical models. However, its computational cost can be prohibitive for large and complex models. This paper introduces a novel approach to Anytime Belief Propagation using Sparse Domains (ABPSD) to address this challenge. ABPSD dynamically constructs sparse domains for variables, focusing computational effort on the most relevant configurations. The method iteratively refines these sparse domains and updates beliefs, enabling anytime behavior where progressively better approximations are obtained as computation time increases. We evaluate ABPSD on challenging inference tasks, demonstrating significant speedups and improved accuracy compared to traditional BP and other anytime variants. The results show that ABPSD offers a practical and efficient solution for approximate inference, particularly in resource-constrained environments or when timely, albeit approximate, answers are crucial. Key words: Belief Propagation, Approximate Inference, Graphical Models, Anytime Algorithm, Sparse Domains.
