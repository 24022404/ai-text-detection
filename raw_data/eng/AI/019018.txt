This paper investigates a novel approach to enhancing educational outcomes by leveraging student feedback to inform and adapt teaching strategies, a process termed “learning to teach.” The primary objective is to develop an adaptive teaching model that systematically incorporates real-time student feedback to optimize instructional methods and content delivery. We propose a machine learning-based framework where the teaching agent continuously refines its pedagogical actions in response to diverse forms of student feedback, such as quiz results, engagement metrics, and qualitative comments. The methodology employs reinforcement learning to identify the most effective teaching interventions, dynamically adjusting to student learning styles and progress patterns. Experimental evaluations were conducted in both simulated and real classroom environments, comparing the proposed approach to traditional static curriculums and rule-based adaptive systems. Results demonstrate significant improvements in student comprehension, engagement, and retention when instruction is guided by responsive feedback mechanisms. The findings underscore the potential of data-driven teaching models to personalize education at scale, fostering more effective and interactive learning experiences. This research contributes to the fields of educational technology and intelligent tutoring systems by introducing a scalable solution for feedback-informed curriculum adaptation. Key keywords include adaptive teaching, student feedback, reinforcement learning, personalized education, and intelligent tutoring systems.
