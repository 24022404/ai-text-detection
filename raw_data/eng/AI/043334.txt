This paper introduces a novel context-aware semantic inpainting approach for completing missing or corrupted regions in images. The objective is to generate realistic and semantically plausible image completions by leveraging contextual information from both local and global image regions. Our method employs a deep generative model conditioned on surrounding image features, combined with a novel attention mechanism that focuses on semantically relevant regions for coherent inpainting. We propose a two-stage training process: first, the model learns to generate plausible structures, and second, it refines the texture details guided by contextual cues. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of visual quality, semantic consistency, and quantitative metrics like Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR) and Structural Similarity Index Measure (SSIM). The generated inpaintings exhibit sharper details and better alignment with the surrounding context. The proposed method offers significant improvements in image restoration, object removal, and content creation applications. Keywords: Image Inpainting, Semantic Completion, Context-Aware, Deep Learning, Generative Models, Attention Mechanism.
