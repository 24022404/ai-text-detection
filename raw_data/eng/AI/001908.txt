Safe exploration in reinforcement learning is critical for deploying agents in real-world environments where constraint violations can lead to significant costs. This paper introduces a novel approach to safe exploration by formulating the problem as solving an early terminated Markov Decision Process (MDP). Our method allows the agent to explicitly reason about potential constraint violations and proactively terminate episodes before unsafe states are reached. We propose a modified policy iteration algorithm that incorporates safety constraints directly into the value function and policy updates, ensuring that the agent learns to avoid unsafe actions. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach on several benchmark environments, showing that our agent achieves superior safety performance compared to existing safe exploration techniques, while maintaining competitive reward acquisition. Our findings highlight the potential of early termination as a powerful tool for safe reinforcement learning and contribute a practical algorithm for its implementation. Keywords: Safe Exploration, Reinforcement Learning, Markov Decision Process, Early Termination, Constrained Optimization.
