This paper investigates the application of transfer learning with deep Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) for the tasks of gender recognition and age estimation from facial images. The primary objective is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of these tasks, particularly when training data is limited. We leverage pre-trained CNN models, specifically ResNet and VGG architectures, on large-scale image datasets like ImageNet, and then fine-tune them using facial image datasets. Our approach involves experimenting with different fine-tuning strategies and data augmentation techniques to optimize performance. Experimental results demonstrate that transfer learning significantly enhances the accuracy of both gender recognition and age estimation compared to training CNNs from scratch. Specifically, we achieve state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets, with improvements of up to 5% in gender recognition accuracy and a reduction of 10% in Mean Absolute Error (MAE) for age estimation. The findings highlight the effectiveness of transfer learning for facial attribute prediction, offering a practical solution for applications with limited data. Keywords: Transfer Learning, CNN, Gender Recognition, Age Estimation, Deep Learning, Facial Analysis.
