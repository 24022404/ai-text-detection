The objective of this research is to address the limitations of traditional anchor-based methods in object detection by introducing a Feature Selective Anchor-Free Module for Single-Shot Object Detection. Our proposed approach eliminates the dependency on pre-defined anchor boxes, which often require extensive manual tuning and computational resources. The module leverages a feature selection mechanism that dynamically identifies essential features from the input image, enhancing the detection performance. Through a novel combination of deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and a selective attention mechanism, the module efficiently captures objects of various scales without the need for anchors. Our method was evaluated on standard object detection benchmarks, showing a significant improvement in accuracy and speed compared to existing anchor-based models. The results demonstrate that the proposed method is particularly effective in detecting small and densely packed objects, a common challenge in object detection tasks. The absence of anchor boxes not only simplifies the detection pipeline but also reduces the overhead associated with anchor computation. This research contributes a viable alternative to traditional anchor-based models, with potential applications in real-time object detection systems where speed and accuracy are paramount. Keywords such as object detection, anchor-free, single-shot, feature selection, and convolutional neural networks are pivotal in highlighting the scope and innovations of this study.
