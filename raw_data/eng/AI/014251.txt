This paper presents a novel Lyapunov-based framework for enhancing the safety of Reinforcement Learning (RL) systems in uncertain and dynamic environments. The primary objective is to address the challenge of ensuring that RL agents adhere to safety constraints while optimizing performance, a critical requirement in fields such as autonomous vehicles, robotics, and healthcare. Our approach integrates Lyapunov stability theory with RL algorithms to construct safety-guaranteed policies. Specifically, we develop a method for learning Lyapunov functions online, which are then used to enforce constraint satisfaction during policy updates. We validate the proposed method on benchmark control tasks, demonstrating its ability to maintain safe operation without significantly compromising learning efficiency or reward maximization. Comparative experiments highlight improved constraint satisfaction and enhanced stability over traditional RL and other baseline safe RL approaches. The results indicate that our Lyapunov-based methodology provides a scalable and theoretically grounded mechanism for safe policy learning, offering significant potential for real-world deployment of RL systems in safety-critical domains. Key keywords: safe reinforcement learning, Lyapunov stability, constraint satisfaction, safety guarantees, policy optimization.
