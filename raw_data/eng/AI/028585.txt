Understanding the dynamics of moving vehicles is critical for advancing autonomous driving systems and improving traffic safety. This paper presents a novel deep learning framework, Deep Lidar CNN, designed to analyze and interpret the motion patterns of vehicles using Light Detection and Ranging (LiDAR) data. The proposed approach leverages a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture tailored to process sparse and high-dimensional LiDAR point clouds, enabling real-time detection and tracking of moving vehicles. The model incorporates temporal information to capture dynamic behaviors, such as acceleration, deceleration, and lane changes, with high precision. Experimental evaluations on a large-scale urban driving dataset demonstrate that Deep Lidar CNN outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency, achieving a 12% improvement in motion prediction accuracy. The results highlight the model's ability to handle complex urban environments with multiple interacting vehicles. This research contributes to the development of robust perception systems for autonomous vehicles, offering significant implications for real-world applications in intelligent transportation systems.  

**Keywords**: LiDAR, convolutional neural network, autonomous driving, vehicle dynamics, motion prediction, deep learning.
