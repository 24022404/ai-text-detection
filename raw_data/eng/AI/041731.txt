The objective of this research is to address the persistent challenge of continual area sweeping, which is a crucial task in environments such as autonomous cleaning, surveillance, and agricultural robotics. We propose a novel approach leveraging Deep Reinforcement Learning, specifically tailored to optimize continual area coverage. Our methodology, termed Deep R-Learning, incorporates state-of-the-art deep neural network architectures to enable adaptive and efficient path planning that dynamically adjusts to changing environmental conditions. Key findings demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms traditional methods and existing reinforcement learning models in terms of coverage efficiency and computational efficiency. The developed system showcases the ability to consistently maximize area coverage while minimizing energy consumption and redundancy, as evidenced through rigorous simulations and real-world testing scenarios. Concluding, this research highlights the potential of Deep R-Learning as a robust tool for enhancing the capabilities of autonomous systems involved in duties requiring extensive area coverage. The implications of this work stretch across various domains where autonomous and adaptive area sweeping is paramount, offering significant advancements in operational effectiveness. Keywords: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Continual Area Sweeping, Autonomous Path Planning, Dynamic Coverage Optimization.
