This paper proposes a general framework for Hypercomplex-valued Extreme Learning Machines (H-ELMs), addressing the limitation of traditional real-valued ELMs in handling complex-valued and hypercomplex-valued data. The objective is to develop a unified framework that can efficiently process quaternion, octonion, and other hypercomplex-valued data, enabling advanced applications in fields like signal processing, image recognition, and artificial intelligence. Our approach extends the conventional ELM architecture to accommodate hypercomplex-valued weights, biases, and inputs, leveraging the mathematical properties of hypercomplex numbers to enhance the model's representation capacity. Experimental results demonstrate the superiority of H-ELMs over traditional ELMs in various tasks, including classification, regression, and clustering, with improved accuracy and robustness. The proposed framework contributes to the development of more versatile and powerful machine learning models, with potential applications in areas like computer vision, robotics, and neuroscience, and paves the way for further research in hypercomplex-valued neural networks, quaternion neural networks, and octonion neural networks. Key keywords: Hypercomplex-valued neural networks, Extreme Learning Machines, Quaternion, Octonion, Artificial intelligence, Machine learning.
