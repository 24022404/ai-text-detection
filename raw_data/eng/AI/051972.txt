The automated detection of tools and equipment in unconstrained environments presents a significant challenge for computer vision systems. This paper introduces ALET (Automated Labeling of Equipment and Tools), a novel dataset designed to address this gap. ALET comprises a diverse collection of images depicting various tools and equipment in real-world scenarios, automatically labeled using a combination of computer vision techniques and manual verification. We establish a baseline performance for tool detection on the ALET dataset using a state-of-the-art object detection model, providing a benchmark for future research. Furthermore, we demonstrate a practical use case for ALET in automated inventory management, showcasing the potential of tool detection in improving efficiency and accuracy. The ALET dataset and baseline models offer a valuable resource for advancing research in object detection, particularly for applications requiring robust performance in uncontrolled settings. We anticipate that ALET will facilitate the development of more accurate and reliable tool detection systems with broad applicability across various industries.

**Keywords:** object detection, tool detection, dataset, computer vision, automated labeling, inventory management.
