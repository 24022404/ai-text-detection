The optimization of neural network architectures is a critical task in deep learning, where the manual design can be both challenging and time-consuming. This paper presents L^NAS, a novel approach to automate neural architecture search by leveraging continuous-action reinforcement learning (RL).

Methods/Approach: L^NAS distinguishes itself by employing a continuous-action space within the RL paradigm to effectively explore and optimize the neural architecture space. This approach enables more precise modifications to neural architectures compared to traditional discrete-action methods. The continuous-action RL framework is engineered to iteratively adjust architectural parameters and hyperparameters to discover optimal configurations that enhance model performance.

Results/Findings: Our experiments show that L^NAS achieves superior performance on standard neural architecture search benchmarks, demonstrating its effectiveness in finding highly competitive architectures. Key findings highlight the method’s ability to produce more refined and efficient networks that outperform architectures discovered by existing state-of-the-art methods, both in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency.

Conclusion/Implications: By introducing continuous-action reinforcement learning into neural architecture optimization, L^NAS offers a more nuanced and powerful mechanism for model enhancement. The proposed method not only accelerates the search process but also delivers models with improved performances suitable for a variety of applications. Our contributions have the potential to significantly influence future research in automated machine learning and model optimization. Key keywords include neural architecture search (NAS), reinforcement learning, continuous-action space, model optimization, and deep learning.
