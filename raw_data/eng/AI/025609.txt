Estimating scene illumination from a single image is a challenging problem in computer vision, critical for applications like image relighting and 3D scene understanding. This paper introduces a novel deep learning approach for simultaneous estimation of light direction and color from a single RGB image. Our method employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) trained to directly regress the parameters of a simplified illumination model, capturing both the dominant light direction as a 3D vector and the ambient light color as an RGB triplet. To overcome the lack of large-scale ground truth data, we train our network on a synthetic dataset generated with realistic rendering techniques and domain randomization. We propose a multi-stage training strategy to improve the accuracy and robustness of the estimations. Experimental results on both synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves competitive performance compared to existing methods, while being significantly faster. Furthermore, we show that our estimated illumination parameters can be effectively used for image relighting, producing visually plausible results. This work contributes to the field by providing a practical and efficient deep learning solution for single-image illumination estimation, with potential applications in augmented reality and image editing. Keywords: Illumination Estimation, Deep Regression, Convolutional Neural Networks, Single Image, Light Direction, Light Color.
