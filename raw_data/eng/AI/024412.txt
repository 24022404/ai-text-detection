This paper introduces NeuralSampler, a novel Euclidean point cloud auto-encoder and sampler that leverages deep learning techniques to efficiently process and represent 3D point cloud data. The objective is to develop a compact and informative representation of point clouds, enabling effective sampling and reconstruction. Our approach employs a neural network architecture that combines an auto-encoder with a sampling module, allowing for flexible and adaptive point cloud processing. The auto-encoder learns a compressed representation of the input point cloud, while the sampler generates new points that capture the underlying geometry and structure. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of NeuralSampler in achieving high-quality point cloud reconstruction and sampling, outperforming existing methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency. The proposed method has significant implications for various applications, including 3D modeling, computer-aided design, and robotics. Key contributions include the development of a novel neural network architecture for point cloud processing, improved sampling and reconstruction quality, and enhanced computational efficiency. Relevant keywords: point cloud processing, auto-encoder, sampler, 3D reconstruction, deep learning, neural networks.
