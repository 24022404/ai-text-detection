This research addresses the challenge of forecasting time series systems, particularly when dealing with unknown clusters of forecast tasks. The aim is to develop a method that can effectively identify and learn predictive leading indicators, thereby improving the accuracy and reliability of time series forecasts.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel model that leverages machine learning techniques to automatically detect and harness leading indicators from diverse time series data. Our approach involves a clustering algorithm that intelligently groups forecast tasks, discovering hidden patterns and relationships. This enables the model to learn tailored predictive indicators for each cluster, enhancing the precision of forecasting outcomes.

Results/Findings: The experimental evaluation demonstrates the model's superior performance in forecasting accuracy compared to traditional methods. Our results indicate a significant improvement in predictive capabilities, especially in scenarios with complex and heterogeneous time series data. The model also adapts well to varying forecast tasks by dynamically identifying relevant leading indicators within each cluster.

Conclusion/Implications: This study contributes a robust framework for enhancing time series forecasting through the identification of predictive leading indicators, especially in systems with previously unknown task clusters. The research offers practical implications in fields such as finance, supply chain management, and climate modeling, where accurate forecasts are critical. Our approach not only advances the current understanding of time series forecasting but also provides a versatile tool that can be readily applied to various domains.

Keywords: time series forecasting, leading indicators, machine learning, clustering, predictive modeling, heterogeneous data.
