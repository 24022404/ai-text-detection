This paper presents a novel approach to recover the textured 3D shape and motion of fast moving objects from blurred images. The objective is to address the challenge of estimating shape and motion from images where traditional methods fail due to motion blur. Our method, termed Shape from Blur, utilizes a deep learning-based framework that leverages the blur cues in images to estimate the 3D shape and motion of objects. We employ a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture that takes blurred images as input and outputs a 3D point cloud representation of the object, along with its motion parameters. Our results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in recovering accurate 3D shapes and motion trajectories from blurred images, outperforming traditional shape-from-motion methods. The proposed technique has significant implications for applications such as autonomous driving, robotics, and surveillance, where fast and accurate 3D reconstruction is crucial. Key contributions of this work include the introduction of a blur-based shape recovery method, a novel CNN architecture for 3D shape and motion estimation, and a comprehensive evaluation framework for assessing the performance of shape-from-blur methods. Relevant keywords: 3D reconstruction, motion estimation, deep learning, computer vision, shape from blur.
