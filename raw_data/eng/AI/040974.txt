This paper proposes a novel approach to microwave imaging of brain anomalies using a Graph Attention Network (GAN). The objective is to accurately detect and localize anomalies within the brain, such as tumors or hemorrhages, using microwave imaging techniques. Our approach employs a graph-based model that leverages attention mechanisms to focus on the most relevant features of the microwave data. The GAN is trained on a dataset of simulated and real-world microwave images, and its performance is evaluated against traditional imaging methods. Results show that the GAN achieves superior accuracy and robustness in anomaly detection, with significant improvements in sensitivity and specificity. The proposed method has important implications for the development of non-invasive and portable brain imaging systems, enabling early diagnosis and treatment of brain-related diseases. Key contributions of this research include the introduction of graph attention mechanisms to microwave imaging, and the demonstration of their potential in improving the accuracy and reliability of brain anomaly detection. Relevant keywords: Graph Attention Network, Microwave Imaging, Brain Anomaly Detection, Non-Invasive Imaging, Deep Learning.
