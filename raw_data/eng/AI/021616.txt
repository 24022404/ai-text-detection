Cloud-based machine learning (ML) offers scalability but often suffers from high costs due to resource-intensive training. This paper introduces TrimTuner, a novel sub-sampling framework designed to efficiently optimize ML job execution in the cloud. TrimTuner dynamically adjusts the training dataset size based on real-time performance feedback, minimizing resource consumption without significantly sacrificing model accuracy. Our approach employs a feedback loop integrating model performance metrics to intelligently prune redundant data points. We evaluate TrimTuner across diverse datasets and ML models, demonstrating significant cost reductions compared to full-dataset training and static sub-sampling techniques. Results show that TrimTuner achieves up to 40% cost savings while maintaining comparable or superior model accuracy in various scenarios. This adaptive sub-sampling strategy provides a practical solution for cost-effective and scalable ML training in cloud environments, offering a valuable tool for both researchers and practitioners.
Keywords: Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, Sub-sampling, Optimization, Cost Reduction, Adaptive Training.
