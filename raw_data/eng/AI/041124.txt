Reinforcement learning (RL) offers a powerful paradigm for training autonomous agents, but its application in safety-critical domains is hindered by the risk of violating constraints during exploration. This paper introduces a novel constrained policy improvement algorithm designed to enhance safety and efficiency in RL. Our method employs a Lyapunov-based approach to guarantee constraint satisfaction during policy updates, ensuring that the agent's actions remain within predefined safety bounds. We formulate a constrained optimization problem that explicitly incorporates both reward maximization and constraint adherence, allowing for efficient exploration without compromising safety. Experimental results across several benchmark environments demonstrate that our algorithm achieves significant improvements in constraint satisfaction compared to existing constrained RL methods, while also maintaining competitive reward performance. The results highlight the potential of our approach for deploying RL agents in real-world applications where safety is paramount. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Constrained Optimization, Safe Exploration, Lyapunov Stability, Policy Improvement.
