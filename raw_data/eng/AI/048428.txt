This paper addresses the challenge of threshold-free salient object detection by optimizing the F-measure, a widely used metric for evaluating detection performance. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that eliminates the need for manually setting thresholds, thereby improving the accuracy and robustness of salient object detection models. We propose a new method that leverages a loss function specifically designed to optimize the F-measure, allowing our model to learn optimal detection boundaries automatically. Our approach utilizes a deep neural network architecture, incorporating techniques such as feature fusion and attention mechanisms to enhance detection accuracy. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art threshold-free detection models, achieving significant improvements in F-measure scores. The key contributions of our research include a novel loss function, an effective detection framework, and a comprehensive evaluation protocol. Our work has important implications for various computer vision applications, including image segmentation, object recognition, and robotics, where accurate and efficient salient object detection is crucial. Key keywords: salient object detection, F-measure optimization, threshold-free detection, deep learning, computer vision.
