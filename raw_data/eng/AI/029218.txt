**Abstract:**  
This paper presents a novel approach called Convolutional Spectral Kernel Learning, targeting the enhancement of kernel-based methods for structured data analysis. The primary objective is to address the limitations of traditional kernel learning methods, which often struggle with high-dimensional data and lack the capacity to capture local and hierarchical patterns. Our method integrates convolutional operations within a spectral kernel framework, enabling efficient extraction of localized features and leveraging the strengths of both convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and spectral methods. Specifically, we propose a hybrid model where convolutional layers learn feature representations from raw input, which are then mapped via spectral kernels for non-linear classification and regression tasks. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms conventional kernel methods and standard CNN architectures on benchmark datasets regarding accuracy and computational efficiency. The improved expressiveness of the convolutional spectral kernel is also evident in tasks such as image classification, signal processing, and bioinformatics. Our findings highlight the potential of unifying spectral kernel learning with deep convolutional representations, providing a more robust and scalable framework for a wide range of machine learning applications. Key keywords: spectral kernels, convolutional neural networks, kernel learning, feature extraction, deep learning, machine learning.
