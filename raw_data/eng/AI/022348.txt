This paper addresses the challenge of dynamically controlling complex systems represented as graphs, with a focus on integrating neural ordinary differential equations (NODEs) to optimize the dynamics of graph-based processes. The study aims to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of managing graph dynamics, which are crucial in numerous applications such as network traffic control, social network analysis, and biological systems modeling.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel framework that leverages neural ordinary differential equations to model and control graph dynamics. By embedding NODEs within graph neural networks (GNNs), our approach captures the continuous-time dynamics of graph nodes and edges, allowing for a more refined control mechanism. Our model is validated through simulations on benchmark datasets, comparing its performance to traditional graph and NODE-based methods.

Results/Findings: The proposed NODE-GNN framework demonstrated superior performance in controlling the dynamics of graphs compared to existing methods. Experiments showed a significant improvement in predicting the evolution of graph states with enhanced robustness and scalability. Furthermore, our approach reduced computational complexity while maintaining high accuracy, outperforming baselines in various dynamic graph scenarios.

Conclusion/Implications: This research introduces a powerful tool for controlling and predicting dynamics within complex graph structures. The integration of NODEs with GNNs offers a novel pathway for advancing control strategies in dynamic systems, with potential applications across diverse fields such as traffic management, social networks, and bioinformatics. The framework’s ability to handle continuous-time dynamics provides a new avenue for exploring advanced AI-driven solutions in dynamic graph environments.

Keywords: neural ordinary differential equations, graph dynamics, graph neural networks, dynamic systems control, AI-driven solutions.
