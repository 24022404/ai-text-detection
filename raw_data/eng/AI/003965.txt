This paper presents a novel approach to removing night time haze and glow from images using a deep dilated convolutional network. The objective is to enhance the visibility and quality of images captured in low-light conditions, which is a challenging task due to the presence of haze and glow. Our method employs a deep learning-based approach, utilizing a dilated convolutional network to effectively remove haze and glow while preserving the original image details. The network is designed to learn the complex relationships between the hazy image, glow, and the underlying scene, allowing for accurate removal of these degradations. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in terms of image quality and visibility. The proposed method has significant implications for various applications, including surveillance, autonomous driving, and low-light photography. Key contributions of this work include the design of a deep dilated convolutional network for haze and glow removal, and the demonstration of its superiority over existing methods. Relevant keywords: night time haze removal, glow removal, deep learning, dilated convolutional network, image enhancement, low-light imaging.
