This paper proposes a novel approach to person image generation, focusing on the Progressive and Aligned Pose Attention Transfer (PAPAT) method. The objective is to generate high-quality, pose-transferred person images that maintain the identity and appearance of the original image. To achieve this, we employ a deep learning-based framework that utilizes a progressive attention mechanism to transfer pose information from a reference image to a target image. Our approach ensures alignment between the pose of the reference image and the generated image, resulting in more realistic and coherent outputs. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of PAPAT, outperforming existing methods in terms of image quality, pose accuracy, and identity preservation. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a progressive attention transfer mechanism and a pose alignment strategy, which collectively enhance the state-of-the-art in person image generation. This work has significant implications for applications such as image editing, virtual try-on, and data augmentation, with potential benefits in fields like computer vision, graphics, and artificial intelligence. Key keywords: person image generation, pose transfer, attention mechanism, deep learning, computer vision, image editing.
