The objective of this research is to investigate the isometry robustness of deep 3D point cloud models when subjected to adversarial attacks. With the increasing use of 3D point cloud models in applications such as autonomous driving and augmented reality, their susceptibility to adversarial perturbations poses a significant security threat. We explore various adversarial attack scenarios to assess their effects on these models, focusing on geometric transformations that maintain isometry. Our approach involves applying state-of-the-art deep learning architectures for point cloud processing and systematically analyzing their performance under different adversarial conditions. Key findings reveal that certain model architectures demonstrate higher resilience to these attacks, potentially due to inherent geometric invariances. Additionally, we identify specific adversarial strategies that most effectively disrupt model performance and suggest mitigation techniques to enhance robustness. This study contributes to the understanding of model vulnerabilities and the development of more robust 3D point cloud models. These insights are crucial for improving the security and reliability of applications dependent on 3D data processing. The research emphasizes the need for further exploration of adversarial defenses tailored to deep 3D point cloud models. Key terms include 3D point clouds, adversarial attacks, isometry robustness, deep learning, and model resilience.
