This paper addresses the challenge of reconstructing 3D visualizations from 2D video frames, aiming to enhance the viewing experience and provide new insights into scene understanding. Our approach utilizes a deep learning-based method, combining computer vision and 3D reconstruction techniques to generate accurate and detailed 3D models from video sequences. The proposed system employs a novel framework that integrates feature extraction, depth estimation, and mesh reconstruction to create immersive 3D environments. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, achieving significant improvements in 3D reconstruction accuracy and visual quality compared to existing state-of-the-art techniques. The contributions of this research include a robust and efficient 3D visualization pipeline, enabling applications in fields such as film production, video gaming, and virtual reality. Key aspects of this work include 3D reconstruction, depth estimation, computer vision, and deep learning, making it a valuable resource for researchers and developers in the field of video processing and 3D visualization.
