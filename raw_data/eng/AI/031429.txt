Online decision trees are widely used in streaming data applications due to their efficiency and adaptability. However, their performance can be limited by suboptimal splits and sensitivity to data distribution shifts. This paper introduces *Online Local Boosting* (OLB), a novel approach designed to enhance the predictive accuracy and robustness of online decision trees. OLB integrates localized boosting mechanisms within the tree structure, enabling adaptive refinement of decision boundaries in real-time. The proposed method leverages ensemble techniques at the node level, allowing for dynamic updates to the model as new data arrives. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that OLB significantly improves classification accuracy compared to traditional online decision trees and other state-of-the-art methods. Additionally, OLB exhibits superior adaptability to concept drift, making it particularly suitable for dynamic environments. The results highlight the potential of OLB in applications such as real-time analytics, IoT systems, and adaptive learning platforms. This work contributes a scalable and efficient solution for improving online decision tree performance, offering a practical advancement in streaming data analysis.  

**Keywords**: online decision trees, boosting, streaming data, concept drift, ensemble learning, real-time analytics.
