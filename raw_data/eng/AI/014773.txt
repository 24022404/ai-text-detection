This paper addresses the challenge of learning robust feature representations for visual data by introducing a novel Local Similarity-Aware Deep Feature Embedding framework. Traditional deep embedding methods often focus on global data relationships, potentially overlooking subtle yet crucial local similarities that contribute significantly to discriminative power. To bridge this gap, we propose an end-to-end deep learning approach that integrates local similarity metrics directly into the embedding space construction. Our method leverages adaptive neighborhood modeling and a similarity-aware loss function, enabling the network to preserve local structural information while maintaining global semantic consistency. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets in image retrieval and face verification tasks demonstrate that our approach consistently outperforms state-of-the-art embedding techniques in terms of retrieval accuracy and clustering performance. Analysis of the learned embeddings reveals enhanced separation between classes and increased cohesiveness within classes. This work highlights the importance of local similarity modeling in deep embeddings and paves the way for improved performance in various computer vision applications, including image classification, content-based retrieval, and biometric identification. Keywords: deep feature embedding, local similarity, deep learning, image retrieval, metric learning.
