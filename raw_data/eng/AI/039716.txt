This paper introduces a novel approach for generating inductive graph embeddings using locality encodings. The primary objective is to develop a graph embedding technique that can efficiently generalize to unseen graphs, a significant challenge in many existing methods. Our approach, Locality Encoding Graph Embedding (LEGE), encodes local neighborhood structures within a graph using a learnable function, creating embeddings that capture both node attributes and relational information. LEGE leverages a combination of graph neural networks and a novel locality-sensitive hashing scheme to efficiently encode and compare local graph structures. Experimental results on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that LEGE outperforms state-of-the-art inductive graph embedding techniques in node classification and link prediction tasks. Notably, LEGE exhibits superior performance when transferring to graphs with different characteristics than those seen during training. The proposed method offers a scalable and effective solution for learning transferable graph embeddings, opening avenues for various applications, including drug discovery and social network analysis.

Keywords: Graph Embedding, Inductive Learning, Graph Neural Networks, Locality Sensitive Hashing, Node Classification, Link Prediction.
