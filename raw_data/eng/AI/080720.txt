Relational recurrent neural networks (RRNNs) are presented as an innovative approach in the realm of neural network architectures, designed to effectively model complex relational data dynamics over time. The objective of this research is to address the limitations of traditional recurrent neural networks (RNNs) in capturing intricate relationships within sequential data. Our method integrates relational reasoning capabilities into the recurrent framework, enhancing the model's ability to understand and process interconnected patterns. The proposed RRNN architecture leverages the principles of both relational modeling and recurrent operations, facilitating improved temporal data processing by incorporating attention mechanisms and graph-based representations. Through rigorous experimentation, our findings demonstrate that RRNNs outperform conventional RNNs and other prevalent models on benchmark datasets, particularly in tasks requiring relational reasoning over sequences, such as social network interactions and recommendation systems. The results indicate notable improvements in accuracy and computational efficiency. Conclusively, this research contributes to the advancement of neural network models by introducing RRNNs as a versatile tool applicable to various domains requiring dynamic relational reasoning. Potential applications of RRNNs extend to areas such as temporal knowledge graphs, dynamic scene understanding, and personalized AI assistants. Key keywords include relational neural networks, recurrent neural networks, temporal data, and relational reasoning.
