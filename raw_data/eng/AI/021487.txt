Single Image Super-Resolution (SISR) aims to reconstruct high-resolution (HR) images from their low-resolution (LR) counterparts. While deep learning-based SISR methods have achieved remarkable progress, they often struggle to recover fine-grained details, resulting in overly smooth or texture-lacking reconstructions. This paper introduces a novel texture transfer approach to enhance texture fidelity in SISR. Our method leverages a pre-trained texture encoder to extract texture features from a reference HR image and subsequently transfers these features to the upscaled LR image. We propose an adaptive texture fusion module that selectively integrates texture information based on local image characteristics, preventing artifacts and preserving structural integrity. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method significantly improves perceptual quality, achieving state-of-the-art performance in terms of texture similarity metrics while maintaining competitive PSNR and SSIM scores. The results showcase the unreasonable effectiveness of texture transfer in generating visually appealing and detailed HR images from single LR inputs. Keywords: Super-resolution, Texture Transfer, Image Reconstruction, Deep Learning, Perceptual Quality.
