This paper addresses the challenge of learning stochastic transition models in complex systems, a critical task for applications such as reinforcement learning, robotics, and decision-making under uncertainty. We propose a novel approach to approximate stochastic transition models using a combination of deep neural networks and probabilistic graphical models. Our method leverages structured representations to capture dependencies between system states and actions, enabling efficient learning of transition dynamics even in high-dimensional spaces. The proposed framework is evaluated on benchmark environments and real-world datasets, demonstrating superior performance in terms of prediction accuracy and computational efficiency compared to existing methods. Results indicate that our approach achieves a 15% improvement in model fidelity and reduces training time by 20% on average. The implications of this work extend to domains requiring robust decision-making under uncertainty, such as autonomous systems and dynamic resource allocation. This research contributes a scalable and interpretable solution for learning stochastic transition models, offering a significant step forward in modeling complex systems with uncertain dynamics.  

**Keywords**: stochastic transition models, deep learning, probabilistic graphical models, reinforcement learning, decision-making under uncertainty.
