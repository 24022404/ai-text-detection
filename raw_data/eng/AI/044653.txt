Vehicle re-identification (Re-ID) is critical for maintaining consistent vehicle tracking across multiple non-overlapping camera views, particularly in surround-view systems. This paper introduces a novel approach to vehicle Re-ID tailored for surround-view camera setups, addressing challenges posed by viewpoint variations and occlusions. Our method utilizes a multi-scale feature extraction network coupled with an attention mechanism to focus on salient vehicle features robust to viewpoint changes. Specifically, we employ a Siamese network architecture trained with a combined triplet loss and cross-entropy loss to learn discriminative embeddings. We evaluate our approach on a newly collected surround-view vehicle Re-ID dataset and demonstrate significant improvements in Re-ID accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods. The results show that our approach achieves a 15% increase in Rank-1 accuracy. This research offers a robust solution for vehicle Re-ID in complex, multi-camera environments, contributing to improved vehicle tracking and scene understanding.

Keywords: Vehicle Re-ID, Surround-view Camera, Siamese Network, Attention Mechanism, Multi-scale Feature Extraction, Vehicle Tracking.
