The objective of this paper is to present SIDU, a novel method for explainable artificial intelligence (AI) that addresses the challenge of enhancing model interpretability by analyzing similarity, difference, and uniqueness in data. SIDU introduces a comprehensive approach that combines statistical analysis and machine learning techniques to dissect complex AI models into more understandable components. By evaluating the relationship between data features and AI model outputs, SIDU identifies critical attributes contributing to decision-making processes, facilitating a clearer understanding of model behavior. Our experimental results demonstrate that SIDU not only improves transparency in AI models but also outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy and interpretability across various datasets. Additionally, SIDU enables more effective model auditing and validation processes. The implications of this research are significant for industries relying on AI-driven decision-making, as SIDU offers a robust framework for ensuring accountability and fairness in automated systems. This method represents a substantial advancement in the field of explainable AI, providing tools for deeper insights into complex models while maintaining high-performance levels. Key keywords include explainable AI, model interpretability, SIDU, similarity analysis, and machine learning transparency.
