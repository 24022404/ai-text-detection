Hyperparameter optimization is a critical aspect of machine learning, significantly influencing model performance and efficiency. This paper introduces a novel spectral approach for hyperparameter optimization, aiming to improve the convergence speed and accuracy of machine learning models. Our methodology leverages spectral analysis to identify optimal hyperparameter settings, thus minimizing the computational overhead associated with traditional optimization techniques. By employing this spectral framework, we analyze parameter spaces more effectively, exploiting frequency domain transformations to gain insights into the hyperparameter landscape. Experimental results demonstrate the superior performance of our approach when compared to conventional methods such as grid search, random search, and Bayesian optimization across a variety of benchmark datasets. The findings indicate a marked improvement in model accuracy and reduced training time, highlighting the efficacy of spectral analysis in navigating complex hyperparameter spaces. This research offers significant contributions to the field by providing a robust tool for practitioners engaged in machine learning tasks, facilitating the development of more efficient and effective algorithms. Potential applications span various domains, from natural language processing to computer vision, where hyperparameter tuning is crucial. Keywords: hyperparameter optimization, spectral analysis, machine learning, parameter spaces, convergence, performance improvement.
