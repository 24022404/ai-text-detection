**Abstract**

Accurate and efficient feature matching is a critical challenge in point cloud processing for applications such as 3D reconstruction, object recognition, and autonomous navigation. This paper introduces StickyPillars, a novel framework for robust point cloud feature matching utilizing the representational power of Graph Neural Networks (GNNs). StickyPillars constructs overlapping local graph structures—referred to as "pillars"—that encapsulate spatial relationships and geometric features, enabling the model to capture both local and contextual information. Our approach leverages a specialized GNN architecture to learn discriminative descriptors and establish correspondences between point clouds, even in the presence of noise, occlusion, or partial overlaps. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that StickyPillars significantly outperforms existing point matching and registration methods in terms of accuracy, robustness to disturbances, and computational efficiency. The proposed method offers improved scalability for large-scale and real-time 3D applications, highlighting its potential for deployment in robotics, augmented reality, and mapping. Our results suggest that graph-based learning approaches like StickyPillars can advance the state-of-the-art in point cloud feature matching by delivering robust performance across diverse scenarios. Keywords: point cloud, feature matching, graph neural networks, 3D reconstruction, robust registration, computer vision.
