This paper re-examines the fundamental connection between neural network classifiers trained with the softmax cross-entropy loss and mutual information estimation. We demonstrate that, under specific conditions, the training process implicitly optimizes a lower bound on the mutual information between the input data and the predicted class labels. This perspective provides a novel interpretation of the softmax cross-entropy loss, moving beyond its traditional probabilistic formulation. Our analysis reveals that the performance of the classifier is directly linked to the tightness of this mutual information bound, suggesting avenues for improved training strategies. We introduce a modified training objective that explicitly encourages tighter mutual information estimation, leading to enhanced generalization performance on several benchmark datasets. Experimental results demonstrate that our proposed approach outperforms standard softmax cross-entropy training, especially in scenarios with limited data. Keywords: Softmax, Cross-Entropy, Mutual Information, Neural Networks, Classification, Information Theory.
