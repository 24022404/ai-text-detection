The objective of this study is to explore the dynamic interaction between deep learning-based object detectors and Siamese network-based trackers within the context of video surveillance systems. The primary focus is to address the challenge of achieving robust object detection and seamless tracking in real-time monitoring applications. Our approach leverages advanced deep neural network architectures for object detection, integrated with Siamese networks for object tracking, to form a cohesive surveillance framework. By conducting extensive benchmarking and performance evaluations, we analyze the impact of various detector-tracker combinations on system accuracy and efficiency. Our findings reveal that certain configurations significantly enhance tracking precision and detection recall rates, surpassing conventional methods in diverse surveillance scenarios. The conclusion drawn from this research highlights the crucial role of the synergistic relationship between object detectors and trackers, paving the way for next-generation surveillance technologies capable of handling complex environmental conditions. This research contributes to the field by providing new insights and methodologies for optimizing video surveillance systems, offering potential applications in areas such as smart security systems, automated monitoring, and dynamic scene analysis. Key terms include deep learning, Siamese networks, object detection, video surveillance, and real-time tracking.
