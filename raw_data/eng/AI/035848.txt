Object detection in sparse and complex environments remains a challenging task in computer vision, particularly when dealing with limited or incomplete data. This paper introduces *Detective*, a novel attentive recurrent model designed to address the limitations of existing object detection methods in sparse scenarios. The proposed model leverages a combination of recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and attention mechanisms to enhance feature extraction and localization accuracy, even in the presence of sparse or occluded objects. By iteratively refining predictions through recurrent processing and focusing on salient regions via attention, *Detective* achieves robust performance in detecting objects with minimal visible features.  

Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that *Detective* outperforms state-of-the-art methods in sparse object detection tasks, achieving higher precision and recall rates. The model’s ability to adaptively focus on relevant regions and refine predictions over time highlights its effectiveness in challenging environments. Furthermore, *Detective* exhibits improved computational efficiency compared to traditional approaches, making it suitable for real-time applications.  

The findings of this research contribute to advancing object detection technologies, particularly in scenarios where data sparsity and occlusion are prevalent. Potential applications include autonomous driving, surveillance, and robotics, where accurate detection in complex environments is critical. *Detective* represents a significant step forward in addressing the challenges of sparse object detection, offering a scalable and efficient solution for real-world deployment.  

**Keywords**: object detection, recurrent neural networks, attention mechanisms, sparse data, computer vision, autonomous systems.
