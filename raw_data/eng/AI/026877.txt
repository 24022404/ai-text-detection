Seasonal time series forecasting is critical for accurate prediction of patterns in various domains such as finance, weather, and supply chain management. This research addresses the challenge of enhancing prediction accuracy through a novel two-stage framework. 

Methods: Our proposed framework employs a hybrid approach, combining decomposition and machine learning models to capture both trend-seasonal components and anomalous patterns effectively. In the first stage, time series decomposition is applied to isolate seasonal and trend components. In the second stage, advanced machine learning algorithms, including recurrent neural networks and gradient boosting, are used to model the residual patterns. This sequential strategy allows for efficient and accurate prediction across diverse data sets.

Results: The two-stage framework was evaluated using multiple benchmark time series data sets, demonstrating superior performance over traditional single-model approaches. Results indicate a significant reduction in prediction errors and enhanced adaptability to different types of seasonal patterns. Furthermore, comparative analysis with state-of-the-art models reveals the proposed framework's robustness and scalability in varied forecasting scenarios.

Conclusion: The study highlights the efficacy of a combined decomposition and machine learning method for improving the accuracy of seasonal time series forecasting. The proposed framework not only advances current forecasting techniques but also offers practical applications in fields requiring reliable long-term forecasting. Future work will explore dynamic adjustments of the framework parameters to further optimize performance. Key Keywords: Seasonal Time Series, Forecasting, Time Series Decomposition, Machine Learning, Recurrent Neural Networks, Gradient Boosting.
