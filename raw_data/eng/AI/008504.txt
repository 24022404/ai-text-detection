This paper proposes a novel approach to achieving fairness in machine learning by learning orthogonal disentangled representations. The objective is to address the issue of bias in AI systems by separating sensitive attributes from the rest of the data, thereby ensuring that predictions are fair and unbiased. Our method utilizes a multi-task learning framework to learn orthogonal representations that disentangle sensitive attributes from other factors, resulting in fair and accurate predictions. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing fairness methods, achieving state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of disentangled representations in fairness and the effectiveness of our approach in real-world applications. This work contributes to the development of fair AI systems, with potential applications in areas such as computer vision, natural language processing, and decision-making systems, and is relevant to keywords such as fairness, disentangled representations, orthogonal learning, and bias mitigation.
