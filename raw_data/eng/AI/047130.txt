The paper addresses the challenge of generating realistic person images under different poses, which is vital for applications in virtual reality, fashion, and digital content creation. Existing methods struggle with preserving fine details and achieving coherent pose transformations.

Methods/Approach: We propose a novel Progressive Pose Attention Transfer (PPAT) model that enhances the quality and coherence of person image generation. Our approach employs a multi-stage attention mechanism, progressively transferring pose information while maintaining local and global image consistency. The model incorporates a unique pose attention layer that captures spatial and contextual representa­tions, facilitating seamless image transformations. 

Results/Findings: Experiments on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that PPAT outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in generating high-fidelity images with significant improvements in detail preservation and pose accuracy. Comparative analyses show our model's superiority in handling complex poses and maintaining sharp image features, evidenced by quantitative evaluations and visual assessments.

Conclusion/Implications: The proposed PPAT framework presents significant advancements in person image generation, with potential applications in personalized avatar creation, computer-generated imagery, and virtual fashion prototyping. Our approach sets a new standard for handling variable pose generation with enhanced attention mechanisms, paving the way for future research in high-quality image synthesis.

Keywords: Person Image Generation, Pose Transformation, Attention Mechanism, High-Fidelity Synthesis, Virtual Reality, Digital Content Creation.
