This paper introduces GABO, a novel approach to graph augmentations using bi-level optimization. The objective is to improve the performance of graph neural networks (GNNs) by learning optimal graph augmentations that enhance node and edge representations. GABO employs a bi-level optimization framework, where the outer loop optimizes the graph augmentation policy and the inner loop updates the GNN parameters. The method utilizes a reinforcement learning-based approach to search for the optimal augmentation strategy. Experimental results demonstrate that GABO outperforms existing graph augmentation methods, achieving state-of-the-art performance on several benchmark datasets. The key findings highlight the importance of bi-level optimization in graph augmentations, leading to improved robustness and generalizability of GNNs. This research contributes to the development of more effective graph learning models, with potential applications in node classification, link prediction, and graph generation tasks. Key keywords: graph neural networks, graph augmentations, bi-level optimization, reinforcement learning, node classification.
