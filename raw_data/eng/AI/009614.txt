This paper addresses the challenge of accommodating picky customers in complex decision-making scenarios through the lens of multi-objective reinforcement learning. The objective is to develop a framework that balances competing objectives while minimizing regret and exploration complexity. We propose a novel approach that integrates regret-bound analysis with exploration strategies to efficiently navigate high-dimensional state and action spaces. Our method leverages a combination of model-based and model-free techniques to adapt to changing customer preferences and optimize multiple objectives simultaneously. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in cumulative regret and sample efficiency compared to existing methods. The key findings highlight the importance of balancing exploration and exploitation in multi-objective settings, with implications for real-world applications such as recommendation systems and autonomous decision-making. Our contributions include a new regret bound analysis and an exploration complexity reduction technique, making multi-objective reinforcement learning more accessible and effective for accommodating picky customers. Key keywords: multi-objective reinforcement learning, regret bound, exploration complexity, model-based reinforcement learning, model-free reinforcement learning.
