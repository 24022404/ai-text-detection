The homepage is designed to leverage the existing logic from the profileModule, utilizing the initModules function to dynamically extract and inject the required saga and reducer into the Redux store, depending on the exports of each specific screen module. This structure streamlines integration and fosters modularity throughout the application. However, the explanation also points out a notable gap in the tourism sector: while there are countless commercial tourism websites, platforms that function as genuine social networks are rare. As a result, users often struggle to access comprehensive information about destinations, which limits engagement and community-driven discovery.
