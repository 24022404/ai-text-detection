The calibration of modern neural networks plays a critical role in their reliability and interpretability, particularly as these models are increasingly deployed in safety-critical applications. This paper aims to systematically revisit calibration techniques to enhance the trustworthiness of predictions by contemporary neural architectures. 

Methods: We explore advanced calibration methods tailored for present-day deep learning models, emphasizing techniques that can be seamlessly integrated into training regimes. The research investigates the efficacy of temperature scaling, label smoothing, and Bayesian approaches. Additionally, the study introduces a novel calibration algorithm designed to minimize prediction biases and improve uncertainty quantification without significant computational overhead. 

Results: Experiments were conducted on various state-of-the-art neural networks, including ResNets, Transformers, and Convolutional Neural Networks, using diverse datasets covering both classification and regression tasks. Our findings demonstrate that the proposed algorithm outperforms existing methods in reducing both Expected Calibration Error (ECE) and Brier Score across multiple benchmarks. It also shows significant improvement in models' generalization and robustness under out-of-distribution scenarios.

Conclusion: This research provides a comprehensive framework for enhancing the calibration of modern neural networks, highlighting the necessity of considering both model architecture and dataset characteristics. The proposed method contributes to the field by offering a scalable solution potentially applicable to a wide range of AI applications where prediction reliability is paramount. Future work will explore real-time calibration techniques and their implications for adaptive AI systems. 

Keywords: Neural Networks, Calibration, Temperature Scaling, Prediction Uncertainty, Model Reliability, Deep Learning, Modern Architectures.
