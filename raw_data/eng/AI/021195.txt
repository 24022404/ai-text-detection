This paper introduces a novel method for extracting 3D parametric wireframes from point cloud data using distance fields. The primary objective is to develop a robust and efficient algorithm capable of reconstructing accurate wireframe models from noisy and incomplete point clouds. Our approach leverages the properties of signed distance fields (SDFs) to implicitly represent the underlying geometry. We then introduce a new optimization framework that iteratively refines the wireframe parameters by minimizing the discrepancy between the wireframe and the SDF representation. This framework incorporates geometric constraints to ensure the extracted wireframe adheres to the underlying shape. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves superior accuracy and robustness compared to state-of-the-art wireframe extraction techniques, particularly in the presence of noise and occlusions. The extracted parametric wireframes can be directly used for various applications, including CAD modeling, reverse engineering, and shape analysis. Key innovations include the SDF-based representation and the constrained optimization framework.

**Keywords:** 3D Reconstruction, Wireframe Extraction, Point Cloud Processing, Distance Fields, Parametric Modeling, Optimization.
