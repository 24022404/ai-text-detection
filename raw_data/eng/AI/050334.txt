**Abstract:**  
Incremental sequence learning addresses the challenge of continuously acquiring knowledge from evolving sequential data without forgetting previously learned information. This paper proposes a novel framework for incremental sequence learning that enables adaptive model updates as new data arrives, minimizing catastrophic forgetting and enhancing generalization. Leveraging a combination of recurrent neural networks and memory replay mechanisms, our approach dynamically integrates new sequences while preserving the integrity of prior learning. Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets, including text and time-series prediction tasks, demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms standard sequential learning and static models in terms of both accuracy and retention of prior knowledge. Key advantages include efficient utilization of computational resources and scalability to large, streaming datasets. The findings highlight the applicability of incremental sequence learning to real-world scenarios such as continuous language modeling, online forecasting, and adaptive control systems. This research contributes a robust solution to incremental learning within sequential domains and provides insights for future developments in lifelong learning, neural architectures, and efficient data adaptation.  
**Keywords:** incremental learning, sequence modeling, recurrent neural networks, memory replay, catastrophic forgetting, continual learning, adaptive systems.
