Visual tracking is a critical component in the field of robotics, particularly for mobile robots that rely on real-time perception and navigation. This paper provides a comprehensive review of state-of-the-art visual trackers, analyzing their methodologies, strengths, and limitations. We explore various approaches, including traditional feature-based methods and modern deep learning-based techniques, with a focus on their applicability to mobile robot systems. Key performance metrics such as accuracy, robustness, and computational efficiency are evaluated across different environments and use cases. The findings highlight the trade-offs between model complexity and real-time performance, emphasizing the need for lightweight yet effective solutions in resource-constrained mobile platforms. Additionally, we discuss the integration of visual trackers with other sensor modalities to enhance situational awareness and decision-making in dynamic environments. This review contributes to the field by identifying gaps in current technologies and proposing future research directions to improve the reliability and adaptability of visual tracking systems for mobile robotics.  

**Keywords**: visual tracking, mobile robots, deep learning, real-time perception, sensor fusion, robotics navigation.
