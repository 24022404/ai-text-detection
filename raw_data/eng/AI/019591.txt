This paper presents a novel approach for the automatic rendering of building floor plan images from textual descriptions in English. The objective is to bridge the gap between natural language descriptions and visual representations, enabling the generation of accurate and detailed floor plans without manual intervention. To achieve this, we propose a hybrid framework that combines natural language processing (NLP) techniques with computer vision algorithms. The system first parses the textual input to extract spatial and structural information, such as room dimensions, layouts, and relationships between spaces. This data is then processed by a generative model to produce a 2D floor plan image. Our approach leverages advanced deep learning architectures, including transformer-based models for text understanding and convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for image synthesis. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves high accuracy in generating floor plans that align with the input descriptions, outperforming existing methods in terms of both precision and visual quality. The system also supports iterative refinement, allowing users to modify descriptions and observe real-time updates to the rendered floor plan. This research contributes to the fields of architectural design, real estate, and smart home automation by providing a scalable and efficient solution for converting textual data into visual representations. Key innovations include the integration of NLP and computer vision, as well as the ability to handle complex and varied descriptions. The implications of this work extend to applications such as automated building design, virtual reality environments, and accessibility tools for visually impaired users.  

**Keywords**: floor plan generation, natural language processing, computer vision, deep learning, architectural design, image synthesis.
