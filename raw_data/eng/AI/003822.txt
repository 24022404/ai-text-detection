Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) has emerged as a transformative paradigm for addressing complex decision-making problems across diverse domains. This paper provides a comprehensive overview of modern DRL algorithms, highlighting their theoretical foundations, practical implementations, and empirical performance. We examine key advancements beyond traditional methods, including actor-critic approaches, policy gradient methods, and value-based techniques, with a focus on innovations that enhance stability, sample efficiency, and generalization. Specifically, we investigate algorithms such as Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), Soft Actor-Critic (SAC), and Distributional Reinforcement Learning, analyzing their strengths and weaknesses in various benchmark environments. Furthermore, we explore recent trends in DRL, including hierarchical reinforcement learning, meta-learning for RL, and techniques for addressing partial observability. Through extensive experimental evaluations, we demonstrate the performance improvements achieved by modern DRL algorithms compared to classical approaches. Our findings offer valuable insights into the current state-of-the-art in DRL and provide a roadmap for future research directions, ultimately contributing to the development of more robust and efficient intelligent agents. Keywords: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Actor-Critic, Policy Gradient, Proximal Policy Optimization, Soft Actor-Critic, Reinforcement Learning.
