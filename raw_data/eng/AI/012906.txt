This survey aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the current state of interpretability in Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs), addressing the growing need to understand and trust complex neural network models. To achieve this, we employ a systematic approach, reviewing and categorizing existing techniques and methods that enhance interpretability, including feature importance, layer-wise relevance propagation, and model explainability. Our analysis reveals key findings on the strengths and limitations of various interpretability methods, highlighting their performance, applicability, and potential drawbacks. The results of this survey demonstrate the importance of interpretability in ANNs, particularly in high-stakes applications such as healthcare, finance, and autonomous systems. This research contributes to the development of more transparent and reliable neural network models, paving the way for widespread adoption in critical domains. Key contributions include a taxonomy of interpretability techniques, an evaluation framework for assessing model explainability, and recommendations for future research directions. Relevant keywords: Artificial Neural Networks, Interpretability, Explainability, Model Transparency, Deep Learning.
