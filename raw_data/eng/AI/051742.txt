The increasing deployment of machine learning (ML) models in real-world applications demands robust online validation mechanisms that not only monitor model performance but also provide transparent, interpretable insights. This paper presents a novel framework for explainable online validation of ML models, enabling continuous performance assessment while delivering actionable explanations for model predictions and validation outcomes. Our approach integrates lightweight explainability algorithms with streaming validation techniques, allowing end-users and practitioners to understand fluctuations in model accuracy, detect concept drift, and identify potential causes of misclassification in real time. We evaluate the proposed framework on multiple practical use cases, including fraud detection and predictive maintenance, demonstrating its effectiveness in maintaining high model reliability and interpretability under changing data distributions. Experimental results indicate that the system can identify performance degradation swiftly and provide meaningful, user-friendly explanations without significant computational overhead. The findings highlight the importance of explainable validation in operational settings, facilitating trust and informed decision-making for stakeholders. Key contributions include a unified method for real-time validation with explainability, empirical validation across domains, and guidelines for practical integration in existing ML pipelines. Keywords: machine learning, explainability, online validation, real-time monitoring, concept drift, trustworthy AI, practical applications.
