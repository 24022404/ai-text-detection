This paper introduces Likelihood Quantile Networks (LQNs), a novel framework for enhancing coordination in multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) settings. The objective of this research is to address the challenges posed by non-stationarity and partial observability in cooperative multi-agent environments, where effective coordination is critical for achieving optimal collective performance. We propose LQNs as an extension to traditional quantile-based value estimation, incorporating likelihood measures to better capture the uncertainty and distribution of possible returns. Our method employs a quantile regression approach combined with a likelihood weighting scheme, enabling agents to share richer information about value distributions during decentralized policy learning. Experimental results on several benchmark MARL environments, including cooperative navigation and matrix games, demonstrate that LQNs significantly improve coordination efficiency, convergence speed, and overall team performance when compared to established baselines. The findings indicate that leveraging likelihood-based quantile information leads to more robust joint policies and greater resilience to dynamic environmental changes. This work contributes to the field of MARL by providing a scalable and generalizable approach for quantile-based value distribution learning, with potential applications in autonomous robotics, smart grid optimization, and distributed sensor networks. Key keywords: multi-agent reinforcement learning, quantile networks, coordination, likelihood estimation, value distribution learning.
