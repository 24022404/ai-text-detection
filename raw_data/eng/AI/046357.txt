Accurate forest biomass estimation is crucial for carbon cycle monitoring and sustainable forest management. This paper introduces a novel approach for constructing high-resolution forest biomass prediction maps from radar backscatter data, leveraging a sequential regression model enhanced by a conditional generative adversarial network (cGAN). Our method first employs a regression model to predict initial biomass estimates from radar backscatter features. Subsequently, a cGAN is trained to refine these initial estimates, incorporating spatial context and reducing prediction errors. The cGAN is conditioned on the initial biomass map and radar backscatter, enabling it to generate more realistic and accurate biomass predictions. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly improves the accuracy and spatial consistency of forest biomass maps compared to traditional regression methods and other deep learning techniques. Key contributions include the integration of sequential regression with cGANs for biomass estimation and improved mapping accuracy with enhanced spatial details. This method offers a robust and efficient solution for large-scale forest biomass mapping, enabling more informed decision-making in forestry and climate change mitigation. Keywords: Forest biomass, Radar backscatter, Generative Adversarial Network, Sequential Regression, Remote Sensing, Mapping.
