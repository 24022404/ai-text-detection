This paper proposes a novel two-stage framework for seasonal time series forecasting, addressing the challenges of accurately predicting complex seasonal patterns. The objective is to develop a robust and adaptable approach that can handle multiple seasonality and non-linear trends. Our method combines a decomposition technique to separate seasonal components from the original time series, with a machine learning model to forecast the future values. The framework utilizes a clustering algorithm to identify similar seasonal patterns, and a long short-term memory (LSTM) network to predict the future values. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming traditional forecasting methods such as ARIMA and SARIMA. The proposed framework achieves significant improvements in forecasting accuracy, particularly for time series with multiple seasonality. This research contributes to the development of more accurate and reliable seasonal time series forecasting methods, with potential applications in fields such as finance, economics, and environmental monitoring. Key keywords: seasonal time series forecasting, two-stage framework, machine learning, LSTM, clustering algorithm.
