Abstract

Explainable image recognition has become increasingly critical as deep learning models are applied in high-stakes domains. This paper introduces SCOUTER, a novel Slot Attention-based classifier designed to enhance both the accuracy and interpretability of image recognition systems. SCOUTER leverages the Slot Attention mechanism to decompose input images into distinct semantic parts, or "slots," which are then individually processed and classified. By associating classification decisions with discrete visual components, our approach provides transparent reasoning for model predictions. We evaluate SCOUTER on multiple benchmark datasets, comparing its performance with traditional convolutional neural networks and recent attention-based models. The results demonstrate that SCOUTER maintains competitive classification accuracy while significantly improving model interpretability, as evidenced by comprehensive visualizations and quantitative explainability metrics. Furthermore, ablation studies reveal the effectiveness of the Slot Attention architecture in disentangling object features relevant for classification tasks. SCOUTER offers a meaningful advancement for interpretable artificial intelligence (AI) in image recognition, enabling users to gain insights into the decision-making process of complex models. Keywords: Explainable AI, Slot Attention, Image Recognition, Classifier, Model Interpretability, Deep Learning.
