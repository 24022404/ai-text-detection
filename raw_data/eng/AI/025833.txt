This paper presents a novel approach to iris recognition, leveraging deep generative adversarial networks (GANs) to address the challenges of cross-spectral and cross-resolution iris imaging. The objective is to develop a robust and accurate iris recognition system that can operate effectively across different spectral bands and image resolutions. Our approach employs a GAN-based framework to generate synthetic iris images that can bridge the gap between different spectral and resolution domains. The method utilizes a pair of generators and discriminators to learn the underlying patterns and features of iris images, enabling the synthesis of high-quality images that can be used for recognition. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving state-of-the-art performance in cross-spectral and cross-resolution iris recognition. The proposed system has significant implications for various applications, including security, surveillance, and border control. Key contributions of this research include the development of a novel GAN-based architecture for cross-spectral and cross-resolution iris recognition, and the demonstration of its potential to improve the accuracy and robustness of iris recognition systems. Relevant keywords: iris recognition, GANs, cross-spectral, cross-resolution, deep learning, biometrics.
