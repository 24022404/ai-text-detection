This paper introduces HyperColor, a novel approach for automatically generating colored 3D models, specifically targeting efficient game scene population. Addressing the limitations of manual texturing and traditional procedural methods, HyperColor leverages a hypernetwork architecture to synthesize diverse and visually appealing textures directly onto 3D models. Our method employs a compact hypernetwork conditioned on model geometry and a small set of style parameters, which then generates the weights for a texture-generating network. This approach enables the creation of numerous unique textures with minimal computational overhead. We demonstrate the effectiveness of HyperColor by generating a large dataset of auto-colored 3D models and integrating them into complex game environments. Experimental results show that HyperColor achieves significant improvements in texture diversity and visual quality compared to existing automated texturing techniques, while also offering fine-grained control over style variations. The proposed method facilitates rapid prototyping and content creation for game developers, offering a scalable solution for populating virtual worlds. Keywords: 3D Models, Texture Synthesis, Hypernetworks, Game Development, Procedural Generation, Auto-coloring.
