This paper addresses the problem of registering two or more point clouds in the presence of significant noise and outliers. The objective is to achieve accurate and robust alignment without relying on good initial estimates. We present a novel algorithm based on a branch-and-bound (BnB) framework to find the globally optimal rigid transformation. Our approach formulates point cloud registration as a mixed-integer program (MIP), which can be solved to global optimality. A key contribution is the development of tight relaxations and effective branching strategies to accelerate the convergence of the BnB algorithm. We evaluate the performance of our method on synthetic and real-world datasets with varying levels of noise and outlier contamination. Experimental results demonstrate that our algorithm achieves superior registration accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods, particularly in challenging scenarios with high noise levels. The proposed method offers a robust and reliable solution for point cloud registration in applications such as 3D reconstruction, robotics, and computer vision.

Keywords: Point Cloud Registration, Global Optimization, Branch-and-Bound, Noisy Data, 3D Reconstruction, Mixed-Integer Programming.
