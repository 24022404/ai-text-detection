Breast cancer diagnosis using ultrasound imaging is a critical area of research, with the potential to improve early detection and patient outcomes. This study addresses the challenge of achieving high diagnostic accuracy and reliability in AI-based breast ultrasound analysis by proposing a novel multi-AI combination approach. Unlike traditional single-model systems, our method integrates multiple AI models, each specialized in different aspects of ultrasound image interpretation, to enhance overall diagnostic performance. The proposed framework employs a fusion mechanism that combines outputs from convolutional neural networks (CNNs), recurrent neural networks (RNNs), and transformer-based architectures, leveraging their complementary strengths. Experimental results on a large-scale dataset demonstrate that the multi-AI combination achieves a 12% improvement in diagnostic accuracy compared to state-of-the-art single-model approaches, with a significant reduction in false positives and false negatives. Additionally, the system exhibits robust performance across diverse patient demographics and imaging conditions. This research contributes a reliable and scalable AI solution for breast ultrasound diagnosis, offering potential applications in clinical settings to support radiologists and improve diagnostic workflows. Key innovations include the integration of diverse AI architectures and a dynamic decision-making framework that adapts to varying diagnostic complexities. Keywords: breast ultrasound, AI diagnosis, multi-AI combination, convolutional neural networks, transformer models, medical imaging.
