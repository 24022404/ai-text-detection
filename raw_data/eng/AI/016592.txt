This paper introduces YuruGAN, a novel approach for generating Yuru-Chara mascots using Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) trained on small datasets. Addressing the challenge of limited data availability for Yuru-Chara design, YuruGAN incorporates a clustering technique to augment the training process. This method identifies and groups similar mascot features, enabling the GAN to learn more effectively from fewer examples. Our experiments demonstrate that YuruGAN generates diverse and recognizable Yuru-Chara designs with improved visual quality compared to standard GAN architectures trained directly on the same small dataset. The generated mascots exhibit characteristic Yuru-Chara features, such as round shapes and simplistic designs. The results suggest that YuruGAN provides a practical solution for automated mascot creation, even with limited training data, offering potential applications in regional branding and character design. Keywords: GAN, Yuru-Chara, mascot generation, small dataset, clustering, generative model.
