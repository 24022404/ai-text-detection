This paper presents PlenOctrees, a novel data structure for real-time rendering of neural radiance fields (NRFs). The objective is to address the challenge of efficiently rendering complex, dynamic scenes while maintaining high visual fidelity. Our approach combines the benefits of octree-based scene representation with the capabilities of neural networks to achieve fast and accurate rendering. We propose a hierarchical, octree-based encoding of NRFs, enabling efficient traversal and rendering of complex scenes. Our results demonstrate significant improvements in rendering speed and quality compared to existing methods, with frame rates exceeding 30 FPS for complex scenes. The PlenOctrees method has important implications for applications such as virtual reality, video games, and cinematic rendering, where real-time performance and high image quality are crucial. By leveraging the strengths of both octree-based rendering and neural networks, our work contributes to the development of more efficient and effective rendering techniques for complex, dynamic scenes, with key applications in computer graphics, machine learning, and real-time rendering.
