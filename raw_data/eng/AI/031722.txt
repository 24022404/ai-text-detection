This paper introduces RetainVis, a visual analytics system that leverages interpretable and interactive recurrent neural networks (RNNs) to analyze electronic medical records (EMRs). The objective is to improve the understanding and interpretation of clinical predictions made by RNNs, enabling healthcare professionals to make more informed decisions. Our approach combines a novel RNN architecture with interactive visualization techniques, allowing users to explore and understand the contributions of individual input features to the predicted outcomes. The results demonstrate the effectiveness of RetainVis in identifying meaningful patterns and relationships in EMRs, outperforming traditional machine learning methods in terms of accuracy and interpretability. Our system provides a unique framework for visualizing and interacting with RNNs, facilitating the discovery of new insights and improving the trustworthiness of clinical predictions. The contributions of this research include the development of a novel RNN architecture, an interactive visualization framework, and a comprehensive evaluation methodology, highlighting the potential of RetainVis to enhance the analysis and interpretation of EMRs in clinical practice, with key applications in healthcare analytics, medical informatics, and artificial intelligence.
