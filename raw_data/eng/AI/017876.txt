The study focuses on enhancing the accuracy of filling level classification in containers, a crucial task in various industrial applications. Incorrect classification can lead to inefficient resource management and operational challenges. This research aims to improve the precision of fill level detection by leveraging adversarial training techniques.

Methods/Approach: We developed a classification model incorporating adversarial training, which involves training the model with both genuine and adversarially generated examples to strengthen its robustness against perturbations. This approach ensures that the model can accurately discern fill levels under various conditions that might lead traditional models to errors. The model architecture is based on a convolutional neural network (CNN) to capture spatial features efficiently.

Results/Findings: Our experiments demonstrate that adversarial training significantly improves the model's performance, achieving a higher accuracy rate in fill level classification compared to standard training methods. The model exhibited increased resilience to common adversarial attacks and noise, resulting in more reliable predictions when tested on challenging datasets. Comparative analyses highlight an improvement of over 10% in classification accuracy compared to baseline models.

Conclusion/Implications: The introduction of adversarial training in filling level classification marks a novel enhancement that addresses the limitations of traditional techniques. This research contributes to increased operational efficiency in industrial environments and suggests a broader application of adversarial learning techniques for robust classification tasks. Future work will explore the application of this approach in other domains and the integration with real-time monitoring systems.

Keywords: Filling level classification, adversarial training, convolutional neural network, model robustness, industrial applications.
