This paper presents a novel approach to safety-guided deep reinforcement learning, leveraging online Gaussian process estimation to ensure safe and efficient exploration in complex environments. The objective is to develop an agent that can learn optimal policies while avoiding unsafe states and actions. Our method employs a Gaussian process to model the uncertainty of the environment and estimate the safety of unseen states, allowing the agent to adaptively balance exploration and exploitation. The results demonstrate that our approach significantly improves the safety and performance of deep reinforcement learning agents in various tasks, outperforming existing methods in terms of cumulative reward and safety violation rate. The key contribution of this work is the integration of online Gaussian process estimation with deep reinforcement learning, enabling the agent to learn safe and optimal policies in real-time. This research has important implications for the application of reinforcement learning in safety-critical domains, such as robotics and autonomous systems, and highlights the potential of Gaussian process-based methods for improving the safety and efficiency of deep learning algorithms. Key keywords: deep reinforcement learning, Gaussian process, safety-guided learning, online estimation, autonomous systems.
