Volumetric medical image segmentation is a critical task in healthcare, enabling precise diagnosis and treatment planning. However, the scarcity of annotated data poses a significant challenge for supervised learning approaches. This paper introduces a novel framework, Momentum Contrastive Voxel-wise Representation Learning (MCVRL), designed to address the limitations of semi-supervised learning in volumetric medical image segmentation. The proposed method leverages momentum contrastive learning to enhance voxel-wise feature representations by effectively utilizing both labeled and unlabeled data. MCVRL employs a dual-branch architecture, where one branch is updated via exponential moving average to ensure stable and consistent feature learning. Additionally, a voxel-wise contrastive loss is introduced to maximize the similarity between corresponding voxels across augmented views while minimizing similarity with non-corresponding voxels. Experiments on benchmark datasets, including BraTS and LiTS, demonstrate that MCVRL outperforms state-of-the-art semi-supervised methods, achieving significant improvements in segmentation accuracy and robustness. The framework’s ability to generalize across diverse medical imaging modalities highlights its potential for real-world clinical applications. This research contributes a scalable and efficient solution to semi-supervised volumetric segmentation, paving the way for more accurate and accessible medical image analysis.  

**Keywords**: Volumetric medical image segmentation, semi-supervised learning, momentum contrastive learning, voxel-wise representation, deep learning, medical imaging.
