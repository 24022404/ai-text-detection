This paper presents a novel approach to trajectory optimization by integrating reinforcement learning with classical control techniques, specifically through the development of a Neural Network Iterative Linear Quadratic Regulator (iLQR). The objective is to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of trajectory planning in dynamic systems.

Methods/Approach: Our approach leverages a reinforcement learning framework to train neural networks that approximate the dynamics and value functions used in iLQR. By combining the learning capacity of neural networks with the optimal control capabilities of iLQR, the proposed architecture aims to improve trajectory optimization processes. The system is evaluated using a benchmark set of dynamic control tasks to validate its effectiveness.

Results/Findings: The experimental results demonstrate that the Neural Network iLQR architecture achieves significant improvements in both computational efficiency and trajectory precision compared to traditional iLQR and other reinforcement learning methods. The proposed method shows enhanced adaptability to a variety of dynamic environments and outperforms existing models in terms of speed and accuracy.

Conclusion/Implications: This research contributes to advancements in trajectory optimization by merging neural networks with classical control methodologies, offering a robust and adaptable solution for complex dynamic systems. The Neural Network iLQR architecture has potential applications in autonomous vehicles, robotics, and any domain requiring efficient path planning and dynamic adaptation. The work highlights the promising synergy between reinforcement learning and control theory, paving the way for further innovations in intelligent trajectory optimization.

Keywords: Neural Network iLQR, trajectory optimization, reinforcement learning, control theory, dynamic systems, autonomous vehicles, robotics, path planning.
