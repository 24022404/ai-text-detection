This paper addresses the challenge of ensuring robustness in deep neural networks (DNNs) applied to video data, a critical concern given their vulnerability to adversarial attacks and noisy real-world conditions. We introduce a novel framework that provides provable robustness guarantees for video-based DNNs by leveraging temporal smoothing techniques and spectral normalization. Our approach constructs a robust certificate based on the Lipschitz constant of the network, effectively bounding the perturbation required to change the network's prediction within a temporal window. We demonstrate the efficacy of our method through extensive experiments on benchmark video datasets, including UCF101 and HMDB51, showing significant improvements in certified robustness compared to existing state-of-the-art methods, while maintaining competitive accuracy on clean video data. The results indicate that our framework enhances the reliability of DNNs for video analysis in safety-critical applications. Keywords: Deep Neural Networks, Robustness, Video Analysis, Adversarial Attacks, Temporal Smoothing, Lipschitz Constant, Certification.
