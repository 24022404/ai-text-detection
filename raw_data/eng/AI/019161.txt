3D object detection in point clouds is crucial for autonomous driving and robotics. Voting-based methods demonstrate promise in noisy outdoor environments but often suffer from redundancy in proposal generation, leading to computational inefficiency and reduced accuracy. This paper introduces a novel back-tracing representative point selection (BRPS) module to refine the voting process and improve detection performance. Our method first identifies a set of representative points that effectively capture the geometric characteristics of objects. Then, we back-trace from the initially voted points to these representative points, effectively pruning redundant proposals and aggregating votes more precisely. Experimental results on the KITTI dataset demonstrate that BRPS significantly improves both the precision and recall of existing voting-based detectors, achieving state-of-the-art performance with a notable reduction in computational cost. The proposed approach offers a robust and efficient solution for 3D object detection in challenging point cloud scenarios.
Keywords: 3D Object Detection, Point Clouds, Voting, Representative Points, Autonomous Driving.
