Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have shown promise in node classification tasks, yet inconsistent experimental setups hinder fair comparisons between different architectures. This paper introduces a comprehensive pipeline designed to standardize the evaluation process for GNNs. Our pipeline incorporates a unified data processing module, automated hyperparameter optimization based on a held-out validation set, and rigorous statistical analysis of performance metrics. We evaluate several popular GNN models using this pipeline on benchmark datasets, revealing significant performance variations compared to previously reported results. Specifically, we demonstrate that careful hyperparameter tuning and data splitting strategies can substantially impact the relative ranking of different GNN architectures. The standardized pipeline enables more reliable and reproducible evaluation, facilitating the development of more effective and robust GNNs. We provide the pipeline as an open-source tool to encourage widespread adoption within the GNN research community. Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, Node Classification, Benchmarking, Reproducibility, Hyperparameter Optimization, Fair Comparison.
