This paper addresses the challenge of efficient distributed learning and inference in resource-constrained environments, focusing on the utilization of compressed images. The objective is to develop a framework that enables the accurate analysis of visual data while minimizing the requirements for storage, transmission, and computational resources. Our approach involves a novel combination of image compression techniques and distributed machine learning algorithms, allowing for the parallel processing of compressed images across multiple devices. The results demonstrate significant reductions in communication overhead and processing time, while maintaining competitive accuracy levels compared to traditional methods. Key findings include the successful application of our framework to various image classification tasks, achieving up to 90% reduction in data transmission and 75% decrease in processing time. The proposed method has important implications for edge computing, IoT applications, and real-time image analysis, highlighting the potential for widespread adoption in scenarios where resources are limited. Our contributions include the design of a scalable and efficient distributed learning framework, leveraging compressed images to facilitate fast and accurate inference, with relevant keywords including distributed learning, image compression, edge computing, and resource-constrained environments.
