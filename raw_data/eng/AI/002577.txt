Bin picking, a fundamental task in robotic automation, relies heavily on accurate workpiece segmentation to enable reliable grasping. This paper introduces a novel deep learning framework for robust and precise workpiece region segmentation within cluttered bin environments. Our approach leverages a Mask R-CNN architecture, enhanced with a custom loss function that emphasizes boundary delineation and reduces over-segmentation errors common in dense object arrangements. We further incorporate a multi-view aggregation strategy to fuse information from multiple camera perspectives, mitigating occlusions and improving overall segmentation accuracy. Experimental results on a diverse dataset of industrial parts demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance, surpassing existing approaches in terms of segmentation precision, recall, and F1-score. The proposed framework significantly improves the reliability of bin picking applications by providing accurate 3D workpiece masks, enabling more efficient and robust robotic grasping. Keywords: Bin Picking, Deep Learning, Semantic Segmentation, Mask R-CNN, Robotic Automation, Multi-View Vision.
