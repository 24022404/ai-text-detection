This paper explores the concept of transformations between deep neural networks, aiming to facilitate the conversion of knowledge and representations across different network architectures. Our objective is to develop a framework that enables the efficient transfer of learned features and patterns from one neural network to another, potentially enhancing performance, reducing training time, and promoting model interpretability. We propose a novel approach based on a combination of feature alignment and weight transformation techniques, allowing for seamless transitions between disparate network structures. Our method is evaluated on various benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency compared to traditional training methods. The results show that our transformation framework can effectively adapt convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to new tasks and datasets, outperforming state-of-the-art transfer learning techniques. This research contributes to the field of deep learning by introducing a new paradigm for neural network transformations, with potential applications in areas such as computer vision, natural language processing, and robotics. Key keywords: deep neural networks, transformations, transfer learning, feature alignment, weight transformation, CNNs, model adaptability.
