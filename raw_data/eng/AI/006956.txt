Knowledge distillation (KD) is a widely used technique to transfer knowledge from a large teacher model to a smaller student model. However, conventional KD methods require multiple training generations, first training the teacher and then the student. This paper introduces Snapshot Distillation, a novel approach that performs teacher-student optimization within a single training generation. We leverage cyclical learning rate schedules to generate multiple high-performing teacher snapshots during a single training run. These snapshots are then used to distill knowledge into the student model, effectively creating a diverse ensemble of teachers at no additional training cost. Our method significantly reduces the training overhead associated with traditional KD while achieving comparable or superior student performance. Experiments on image classification tasks demonstrate that Snapshot Distillation outperforms existing KD techniques, especially when computational resources are limited. The proposed method offers a practical and efficient way to improve the performance of student models with minimal training effort. Keywords: Knowledge Distillation, Teacher-Student Learning, Model Compression, Deep Learning, Snapshot Ensembling, Cyclical Learning Rates.
