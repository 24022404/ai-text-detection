Hyperparameter optimization is crucial for achieving state-of-the-art performance in machine learning models. This paper introduces a novel spectral approach to hyperparameter optimization, leveraging the eigendecomposition of the performance landscape to efficiently identify optimal hyperparameter configurations. Our method constructs a low-dimensional representation of the hyperparameter space based on the eigenvectors corresponding to the largest eigenvalues, thereby capturing the most significant performance variations. We then employ gradient-based optimization within this reduced space, significantly reducing the computational cost associated with traditional search methods. Experiments on several benchmark datasets and models, including deep neural networks, demonstrate that our spectral approach achieves comparable or superior performance to established techniques like Bayesian optimization and random search, while requiring significantly fewer evaluations. Furthermore, the spectral analysis provides valuable insights into the sensitivity and interdependencies of hyperparameters. Keywording: Hyperparameter optimization, spectral analysis, machine learning, eigendecomposition, dimensionality reduction, neural networks.
