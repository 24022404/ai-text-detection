This paper presents a novel approach to video classification by leveraging saliency-guided, adaptively weighted learning. The objective of this research is to address the challenge of effectively capturing and utilizing the most informative spatial-temporal regions within video data for improved classification accuracy. To this end, we propose a framework that integrates a saliency detection module with a deep neural network, allowing the system to dynamically focus on and assign higher importance to salient regions within both individual frames and video sequences. The adaptively weighted learning mechanism automatically adjusts the contribution of salient versus non-salient regions during training, ensuring that critical visual cues are emphasized without manual intervention. Experimental evaluations on benchmark video datasets demonstrate that our method consistently outperforms state-of-the-art video classification models in terms of accuracy and robustness, particularly in scenarios with cluttered or dynamic backgrounds. The results indicate significant improvements in recognizing actions and events compared to conventional attention-based or uniform processing techniques. In conclusion, this work highlights the effectiveness of saliency-guided adaptive weighting for enhancing video classification performance and offers a promising direction for future research in intelligent video understanding. Key keywords include video classification, saliency detection, adaptive weighting, deep learning, and spatial-temporal attention.
