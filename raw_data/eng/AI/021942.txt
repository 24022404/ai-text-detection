This paper presents a novel adaptive mixed norm approach for optical flow estimation, aimed at enhancing motion detection precision in dynamic and complex visual scenes. Traditional optical flow methods often struggle with varying noise levels and motion discontinuities, resulting in suboptimal performance in real-world applications. To address these challenges, we propose a flexible framework that dynamically combines multiple norm-based regularization terms, allowing the model to adapt to local image characteristics and varying motion patterns. The proposed algorithm selects optimal weighting factors for different norms, such as L1 and L2, based on local image features, ensuring robust estimation across smooth regions and motion boundaries. Extensive experiments on standard benchmark datasets demonstrate that our adaptive mixed norm method outperforms conventional single-norm approaches in terms of accuracy, noise-resilience, and preservation of motion discontinuities. The outcomes highlight significant improvements in both dense and sparse optical flow fields, particularly in scenes with illumination changes, occlusions, and textured backgrounds. This research contributes a flexible and effective tool for a broad range of computer vision applications, including video analysis, object tracking, and autonomous navigation. Key innovations include adaptive norm selection and improved regularization for enhanced optical flow accuracy. Keywords: optical flow, mixed norm, adaptive regularization, motion estimation, computer vision.
