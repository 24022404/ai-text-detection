Computed tomography (CT) image quality is severely degraded by artifacts in sparse-view scenarios, limiting its clinical applicability. This paper introduces a novel adversarial learning framework for reducing artifacts in sparse-view cone-beam CT (CBCT) reconstruction. Our approach combines a deep convolutional neural network (CNN) generator with an adversarial discriminator to distinguish between reconstructed images and ground truth images. The generator network learns to map artifact-corrupted sparse-view reconstructions to high-quality images, while the discriminator network guides the generator to produce more realistic and artifact-free results. A novel sparse-view data augmentation strategy further enhances the robustness of the model. Experimental results on both simulated and real CBCT datasets demonstrate that our method significantly reduces artifacts and improves image quality compared to traditional and other deep learning-based reconstruction techniques. The proposed method achieves superior performance in terms of PSNR, SSIM, and visual assessment. The findings suggest the potential for reduced radiation dose and improved diagnostic accuracy in CBCT imaging. Keywords: CBCT, sparse-view reconstruction, adversarial learning, artifact reduction, deep learning.
