HDMapGen introduces a novel hierarchical graph generative model for the automated creation of high-definition (HD) maps, a critical component for autonomous driving and smart city applications. The objective of this research is to address the challenges of generating accurate, scalable, and semantically rich HD maps, which are traditionally labor-intensive and costly to produce. Our approach leverages a hierarchical graph structure to model the spatial and semantic relationships between map elements, such as lanes, intersections, and traffic signs. By integrating graph neural networks (GNNs) with a probabilistic generative framework, HDMapGen captures the complex dependencies and variability inherent in real-world road networks. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed model outperforms existing methods in terms of map accuracy, scalability, and computational efficiency, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets. The generated maps exhibit high fidelity to real-world environments, enabling improved navigation and decision-making for autonomous systems. This work contributes a scalable and efficient solution for HD map generation, with potential applications in autonomous vehicle development, urban planning, and geospatial analysis. Key innovations include the hierarchical graph representation and the integration of probabilistic modeling with deep learning, offering a robust framework for future advancements in map generation technologies.  

**Keywords**: HD maps, graph neural networks, generative models, autonomous driving, smart cities, probabilistic modeling.
