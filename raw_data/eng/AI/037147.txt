This paper presents a robust method for image stitching aimed at enhancing the accuracy and efficiency of panoramic image generation. Addressing the challenge of aligning multiple images seamlessly, this research introduces a novel approach that outperforms existing methods in terms of quality and computational speed. Our method employs a combination of feature detection algorithms with enhanced robustness to variations in lighting and perspective, integrated with an optimized transformation model that ensures precise alignment of image edges. The proposed framework is validated through comprehensive experiments on diverse datasets, showcasing superior performance in maintaining image quality and reducing edge artifacts compared to conventional techniques. Key findings demonstrate significant improvements in the consistency and visual appeal of stitched images, even in complex scenarios with dynamic scenes. This research contributes to advancements in image processing technology, with potential applications spanning digital photography, virtual reality, and surveillance systems. Keywords include image stitching, feature detection, image alignment, panoramic generation, and transformation model.
