This paper addresses the challenge of efficiently extracting features from large-scale industrial time series data. The objective is to develop a scalable and parallel approach to handle the complexities of big data in industrial applications. A distributed and parallel framework is proposed, leveraging advanced computing architectures to accelerate feature extraction processes. The approach utilizes a combination of data partitioning, parallel processing, and synchronization techniques to ensure efficient and accurate feature extraction. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in processing time and scalability, outperforming traditional sequential methods. The proposed framework enables the extraction of meaningful features from industrial big data, facilitating informed decision-making and improved process control. Key contributions include a novel distributed feature extraction algorithm, optimized for industrial time series data, and a scalable architecture for parallel processing. This research has significant implications for industrial big data applications, including predictive maintenance, quality control, and process optimization, with relevant keywords: time series analysis, parallel computing, big data, industrial applications, feature extraction, distributed systems.
