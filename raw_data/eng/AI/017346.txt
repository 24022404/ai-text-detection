This paper addresses the challenge of accurately tracking pedestrian heads in dense crowd environments, a critical task for applications such as public safety monitoring, crowd management, and intelligent surveillance systems. Traditional tracking methods often struggle in scenarios characterized by heavy occlusion, frequent interactions, and dynamic movement patterns. To overcome these obstacles, we propose a robust tracking framework that combines advanced deep learning-based object detection with trajectory prediction models. The approach incorporates a convolutional neural network (CNN) for precise head detection, followed by a multi-object tracking algorithm that employs appearance and motion cues to maintain consistent identities across frames, even under severe occlusion. Extensive experiments on benchmark crowd datasets demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art techniques in terms of accuracy, robustness, and real-time performance. The results highlight improved head localization and identity preservation in complex, high-density scenes. Our system also exhibits strong generalization to different crowd scenarios and varying lighting conditions. The key contributions of this work include the development of a scalable pedestrian head tracking framework and new insights into feature integration for reliable tracking in dense crowds. The proposed solution has the potential to enhance automated crowd analysis, anomaly detection, and urban planning tools. 

Keywords: pedestrian head tracking, dense crowd, object detection, multi-object tracking, convolutional neural networks, surveillance, crowd analysis.
