Semantic scene understanding is a critical challenge in autonomous driving and robotics, requiring precise interpretation of 3D environments from sensor data. This paper introduces *SemanticKITTI*, a large-scale dataset designed to advance research in semantic segmentation and scene understanding using LiDAR sequences. The dataset extends the popular KITTI Vision Benchmark Suite by providing dense point-wise annotations for 28 semantic classes across 22 sequences, capturing diverse urban driving scenarios. To address the challenges of dynamic environments, *SemanticKITTI* includes temporal information, enabling the development of models that leverage sequential data for improved accuracy. We propose a novel benchmark for semantic segmentation of LiDAR point clouds, evaluating state-of-the-art methods on this dataset. Our experiments demonstrate that incorporating temporal context significantly enhances segmentation performance, particularly for dynamic objects. The results highlight the dataset's potential to drive innovation in 3D perception and autonomous navigation. *SemanticKITTI* is publicly available, providing a valuable resource for the research community to develop and evaluate advanced algorithms for semantic scene understanding in real-world environments.  

**Keywords**: LiDAR, semantic segmentation, 3D perception, autonomous driving, point cloud, temporal modeling.
