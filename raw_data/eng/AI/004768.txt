This paper introduces a novel approach to time series data analysis using Symbolic Analysis-based Reduced Order Markov Modeling (SA-ROM). The primary objective is to develop a computationally efficient method for capturing the essential dynamics of complex time series while minimizing model complexity. Our approach leverages symbolic analysis to discretize continuous data into a sequence of symbols, facilitating the construction of a reduced-order Markov model. The method involves optimizing the symbol set and model order to balance accuracy and computational cost. Results demonstrate that SA-ROM effectively captures the underlying dynamics of various time series datasets, including those exhibiting non-linear and non-stationary behavior, while significantly reducing the number of states compared to traditional Markov models. The performance is evaluated using metrics such as prediction accuracy and model size. The findings suggest that SA-ROM provides a powerful tool for time series analysis, offering advantages in terms of computational efficiency and interpretability, with potential applications in areas such as anomaly detection and forecasting.

Keywords: Time series analysis, Markov modeling, Symbolic analysis, Reduced order modeling, Data discretization, Model reduction.
