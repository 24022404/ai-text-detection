This paper introduces NukeBERT, a pre-trained language model tailored for the low-resource nuclear domain. The objective is to address the scarcity of domain-specific language models in the nuclear field, which hinders efficient information retrieval, text analysis, and knowledge discovery. To achieve this, we employ a transformer-based architecture and fine-tune it on a curated dataset of nuclear-related texts. Our approach leverages a combination of masked language modeling and next sentence prediction to learn domain-specific representations. The results show that NukeBERT outperforms general-purpose language models on nuclear domain-specific tasks, such as text classification and named entity recognition. The model demonstrates improved performance on low-resource scenarios, making it a valuable resource for the nuclear community. The introduction of NukeBERT contributes to the advancement of natural language processing in the nuclear domain, enabling more accurate and efficient analysis of nuclear-related texts. Key contributions include the development of a domain-specific pre-trained language model, improved performance on low-resource tasks, and the potential for applications in nuclear information retrieval, safety analysis, and knowledge management. Relevant keywords: NukeBERT, pre-trained language model, nuclear domain, low-resource languages, transformer, fine-tuning, text analysis.
