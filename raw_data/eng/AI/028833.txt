This paper introduces a novel approach for designing robust controllers using an alternating optimization framework coupled with quadrature methods. The objective is to synthesize controllers that guarantee stability and performance despite uncertainties in the system's dynamics. The proposed method iteratively optimizes controller parameters and quadrature nodes, leveraging the latter to efficiently approximate uncertain system responses. Specifically, we employ an alternating scheme that alternates between optimizing controller parameters for fixed quadrature nodes and updating the quadrature nodes for a fixed controller, leading to a solution that satisfies robustness criteria. Numerical experiments demonstrate that this approach yields controllers with improved robustness margins compared to traditional methods, particularly for systems with significant parametric uncertainty. Furthermore, the quadrature-based approach offers computational advantages for complex systems, enabling the efficient design of high-performance robust controllers. Keywords: Robust Control, Alternating Optimization, Quadrature, Uncertainty, Control Design.
