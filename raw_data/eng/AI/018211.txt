Age estimation from facial images in unconstrained environments remains a challenging task due to variations in lighting, pose, and occlusions. This paper presents a novel approach for fine-grained age estimation using Attention-based Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks. The proposed method leverages the temporal dependencies in facial features across different age groups, enabling more accurate predictions in real-world scenarios. Our model integrates an attention mechanism to focus on the most relevant facial regions, enhancing its ability to capture subtle age-related patterns. We evaluate the approach on publicly available datasets, including Adience and IMDB-WIKI, and demonstrate superior performance compared to state-of-the-art methods, achieving a mean absolute error (MAE) of 3.2 years. The results highlight the effectiveness of combining attention mechanisms with LSTM networks for age estimation, particularly in challenging, in-the-wild conditions. This research contributes to advancing facial analysis technologies, with potential applications in security, healthcare, and personalized services.  

**Keywords**: Age estimation, Attention mechanism, LSTM networks, Facial analysis, In-the-wild datasets.
