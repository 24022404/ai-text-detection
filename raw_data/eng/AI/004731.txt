This paper addresses the challenge of noise reduction in burst photography, a critical issue in low-light imaging and high-speed photography. The objective is to develop a robust deep learning-based framework capable of effectively denoising image bursts while preserving fine details and textures. We propose a novel approach leveraging a multi-scale convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture, specifically designed to process temporal and spatial information from burst sequences. The model integrates attention mechanisms to prioritize relevant features across frames and employs a self-supervised training strategy to enhance generalization. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art denoising techniques in terms of both quantitative metrics (e.g., PSNR and SSIM) and visual quality, particularly in scenarios with extreme noise levels. The proposed framework is computationally efficient, making it suitable for real-time applications on mobile devices. This research contributes to advancing burst photography by providing a scalable and adaptive solution for noise reduction, with potential applications in computational photography, medical imaging, and autonomous systems. Keywords: burst denoising, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, low-light imaging, attention mechanisms.
