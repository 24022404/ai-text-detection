This paper addresses the complex task of single object localization using deep learning frameworks. Traditional methods often face challenges in efficiency and integration with neuromorphic systems. We propose a novel approach that leverages Deep Spiking Convolutional Neural Networks (DSCNN) coupled with a Deep Continuous Local Learning mechanism to enhance object localization capabilities.

Methods/Approach: The proposed DSCNN architecture combines the temporal processing advantages of spiking neural networks with the spatial feature extraction strengths of convolutional layers. The network is trained using a Deep Continuous Local Learning strategy, which facilitates more effective learning by continuously updating local synaptic weights. This method mimics biological learning processes, providing robustness and adaptability in localization tasks.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that our model significantly outperforms traditional convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and other spiking neural network models in terms of accuracy and processing speed for object localization. The integration of spiking dynamics contributes to lower energy consumption, making it particularly suitable for real-time applications on resource-constrained neuromorphic hardware.

Conclusion/Implications: The DSCNN model offers a transformative approach to single object localization by integrating spiking neural dynamics with deep learning frameworks. This study contributes to the advancement of energy-efficient, biologically inspired computing solutions, with potential applications in autonomous systems, robotics, and real-time surveillance technologies. Our findings suggest that the use of continuous local learning principles in deep spiking networks could catalyze further innovations in neuromorphic computing.

Keywords: Deep Spiking Convolutional Neural Network, Object Localization, Deep Continuous Local Learning, Neuromorphic Computing, Bio-Inspired AI, Energy Efficiency, Real-Time Processing.
