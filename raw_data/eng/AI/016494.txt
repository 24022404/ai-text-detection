This paper explores the application of metareasoning in modular software systems, focusing on the development of an on-the-fly configuration approach using reinforcement learning. The objective is to optimize system performance by dynamically adapting to changing operational conditions. Our approach leverages rich contextual representations to enhance the decision-making process of the reinforcement learning algorithm. We utilize a modular architecture that enables seamless integration of new components and facilitates efficient exploration of the configuration space. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method in achieving significant improvements in system efficiency and adaptability. The key findings highlight the importance of incorporating contextual information into the reinforcement learning framework, allowing for more informed decision-making and improved overall system performance. This research contributes to the advancement of metareasoning in software systems, offering a novel solution for optimizing modular systems using reinforcement learning with rich contextual representations, and has potential applications in areas such as autonomous systems, edge computing, and IoT devices, with relevant keywords including metareasoning, modular software systems, reinforcement learning, contextual representations, and adaptive configuration.
