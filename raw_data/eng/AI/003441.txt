This paper presents GNN3DMOT, a novel approach to 3D multi-object tracking utilizing graph neural networks (GNNs) with multi-feature learning. The objective is to accurately track multiple objects in 3D space by leveraging the strengths of GNNs in modeling complex relationships between objects. Our approach employs a GNN-based architecture that integrates multiple features, including spatial, temporal, and appearance information, to enhance tracking performance. The method is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in tracking accuracy and robustness compared to state-of-the-art methods. Key findings include the effectiveness of multi-feature learning in handling occlusions and cluttered scenes. The proposed GNN3DMOT framework contributes to the field of 3D multi-object tracking by providing a robust and efficient solution, with potential applications in autonomous driving, robotics, and surveillance. By combining graph neural networks with multi-feature learning, our research highlights the importance of exploiting complex object relationships and diverse feature representations in 3D tracking tasks, advancing the state-of-the-art in this area. Keywords: Graph Neural Networks, 3D Multi-Object Tracking, Multi-Feature Learning, Autonomous Driving, Robotics.
