High Definition (HD) maps are crucial for autonomous driving, yet their creation and maintenance are labor-intensive. This paper introduces HDMapGen, a novel hierarchical graph generative model designed to automatically generate HD maps from raw sensor data. HDMapGen utilizes a two-level graph structure to represent map elements and their relationships: a global graph captures the overall road network topology, while local subgraphs detail individual lane geometries and attributes. Our model employs a graph neural network (GNN) to learn latent representations of map elements and generate new map structures in a hierarchical fashion. Experiments on a real-world driving dataset demonstrate that HDMapGen achieves state-of-the-art performance in generating accurate and consistent HD maps, outperforming existing methods in terms of geometric precision and topological correctness. The generated maps exhibit improved fidelity in representing complex road structures, including intersections and lane merges. The research contributes a scalable and efficient approach to HD map generation, reducing reliance on manual annotation and enabling rapid map updates. Key words: HD map generation, graph neural networks, autonomous driving, hierarchical modeling, generative models.
