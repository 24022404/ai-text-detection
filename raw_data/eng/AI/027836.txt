This paper presents a novel approach to learning point embeddings for efficient 3D data processing. The objective is to develop a robust and scalable method for representing complex 3D point clouds in a compact and meaningful way. Our approach utilizes a deep learning-based framework that leverages self-supervised learning techniques to learn informative point embeddings. The method involves a multi-stage process, including point cloud encoding, feature extraction, and embedding refinement. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in various 3D data processing tasks, such as point cloud classification, segmentation, and reconstruction. Compared to existing methods, our approach achieves significant improvements in terms of accuracy, efficiency, and robustness. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a novel point embedding architecture and the development of a large-scale 3D point cloud dataset for benchmarking and evaluation. Our work has important implications for a range of applications, including computer vision, robotics, and 3D modeling, and highlights the potential of deep learning-based methods for 3D data processing. Key keywords: 3D point clouds, point embedding, deep learning, self-supervised learning, computer vision, robotics.
