This paper addresses the challenge of enabling autonomous agents to learn cooperative behaviors in multi-agent systems through policy search methods. The objective is to develop a framework that allows agents to efficiently adapt their policies to achieve shared goals without explicit communication or centralized control. We propose a novel decentralized reinforcement learning approach that leverages policy gradient methods to optimize cooperative strategies in dynamic and uncertain environments. Our method incorporates a reward-sharing mechanism to align individual agent objectives with the collective goal, ensuring scalable and robust cooperation. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach outperforms traditional centralized and independent learning methods in terms of convergence speed and task performance across various cooperative scenarios, including resource allocation and collaborative navigation. The findings highlight the potential of decentralized policy search for applications in robotics, swarm intelligence, and multi-agent systems, offering a scalable solution for real-world cooperative tasks. Key contributions include a flexible framework for cooperative learning, improved scalability, and empirical validation of its effectiveness in complex environments. Keywords: multi-agent systems, reinforcement learning, policy search, cooperation, decentralized control.
