Seasonal time series forecasting is crucial in various domains such as finance, retail, and climate analysis, where accurate prediction of periodic trends can drive effective decision-making. This paper proposes a novel two-stage framework designed to enhance the accuracy and robustness of seasonal time series forecasting. In the first stage, the framework employs advanced decomposition techniques to separate the input data into trend, seasonal, and residual components, thereby isolating the unique patterns present in each element. In the second stage, specialized predictive models are applied to each component, with particular emphasis on capturing seasonal fluctuations through tailored algorithms, before recombining the forecasts to generate the final prediction. Experimental results on multiple real-world datasets demonstrate that the proposed approach outperforms traditional single-stage and benchmark methods, achieving lower forecasting error and improved adaptability to complex seasonal variations. The findings suggest that the two-stage framework offers significant advantages in modeling both regular and irregular seasonal patterns, making it a valuable tool for practitioners and researchers in time series analysis. Keywords: seasonal time series, forecasting, decomposition, predictive modeling, two-stage framework, trend analysis.
