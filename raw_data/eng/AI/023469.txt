Sparse training has emerged as a promising approach to reduce the computational cost and memory footprint of deep neural networks. This paper introduces a novel sparse training method, Boosting Pruning Plasticity with Neuroregeneration (BPPN), designed to enhance network plasticity during training while maintaining high accuracy. BPPN leverages a dynamic pruning strategy that boosts the regrowth potential of pruned connections, effectively simulating neuroregeneration. By periodically re-evaluating and reactivating pruned connections based on their potential impact on network performance, BPPN encourages exploration of the sparse connectivity landscape. Experiments on benchmark datasets, including CIFAR-10 and ImageNet, demonstrate that BPPN achieves significantly higher accuracy than existing sparse training methods at comparable sparsity levels. Specifically, BPPN improves the accuracy of ResNet-50 on ImageNet by 2-3% compared to state-of-the-art methods at 90% sparsity. This neuroregeneration-inspired approach facilitates the discovery of more efficient and robust sparse networks, offering a promising avenue for deploying deep learning models on resource-constrained devices. Keywords: Sparse Training, Pruning, Neuroregeneration, Deep Learning, Neural Networks, Plasticity.
