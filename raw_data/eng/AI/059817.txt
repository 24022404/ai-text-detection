Interactive Video Object Segmentation (iVOS) facilitates precise object delineation through user-guided interactions. Current iVOS methods often require users to provide initial masks or subsequent corrections on specific video frames. This paper introduces a novel approach to optimize the selection of frames for user interaction, thereby minimizing user effort and improving segmentation accuracy in challenging, unconstrained video scenarios. We propose a reinforcement learning framework to train an agent that learns to strategically recommend frames for user input based on the predicted segmentation uncertainty and object motion dynamics. The agent is trained to maximize segmentation performance while minimizing the number of user interactions. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method significantly reduces the annotation cost and enhances segmentation accuracy compared to existing iVOS techniques and frame selection strategies. Our approach showcases superior performance, especially in complex scenarios with significant object deformation and occlusion. This work contributes a practical solution for efficient and accurate iVOS, making it more accessible for real-world applications.

**Keywords:** Interactive Video Object Segmentation, Reinforcement Learning, Frame Recommendation, User Interaction, Video Annotation.
