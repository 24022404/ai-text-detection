This paper introduces a novel Bayesian approach for analyzing high-dimensional time series data using an Orthogonal Stochastic Linear Mixing Model (OSLMM). The objective is to accurately infer the underlying latent factors driving the observed complex dynamics, even when the number of observed series is large. Our method leverages orthogonal constraints within a hierarchical Bayesian framework to enhance model identifiability and reduce computational complexity. We employ efficient Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling techniques to perform posterior inference on model parameters and latent factors. We demonstrate the efficacy of our approach through simulations and real-world financial time series data, showing significant improvements in factor recovery and forecasting accuracy compared to existing methods, such as Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and traditional factor models. The OSLMM framework provides a robust and scalable solution for high-dimensional time series analysis, offering valuable insights into complex systems.
Keywords: Bayesian Inference, Time Series, High-Dimensional Data, Factor Model, MCMC, Orthogonal Stochastic Linear Mixing Model.
