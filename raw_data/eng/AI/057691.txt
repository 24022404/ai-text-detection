Action Unit (AU) intensity estimation, a crucial task in facial expression analysis, often suffers from limited labeled data. This paper introduces a novel transfer learning approach to heatmap regression for AU intensity estimation, leveraging knowledge from related tasks to improve performance. We propose a two-stage transfer learning framework: first, pre-training a convolutional neural network on a large-scale facial landmark detection dataset; second, fine-tuning the pre-trained model for AU intensity estimation using heatmap regression. Specifically, we generate heatmaps representing the spatial distribution of AU-related facial landmarks and train the network to predict these heatmaps. This allows the model to learn subtle facial deformations associated with varying AU intensities. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our transfer learning approach significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods, especially when training data is scarce. The learned heatmaps provide a visually interpretable representation of AU activations. Our approach offers a robust and efficient solution for AU intensity estimation, paving the way for improved facial expression recognition systems.
Keywords: Action Unit, Heatmap Regression, Transfer Learning, Facial Expression Analysis, Intensity Estimation, Convolutional Neural Network.
