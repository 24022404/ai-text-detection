The existence condition for universal cycles in this particular context remains an open problem, largely because it is impossible to explicitly define the transition graph for n k. This difficulty arises from the unique property that every k-set is allowed to appear in any of the k! possible orders, yet can only occur once throughout the cycle. Fan Chung and Graham have put forth a conjecture that addresses this intriguing challenge, and there is currently a prize on offer for whoever is able to produce the first definitive solution to this longstanding mathematical question.
