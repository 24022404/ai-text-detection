Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) have emerged as a promising solution for wireless data harvesting in remote or inaccessible areas. However, optimizing UAV path planning to maximize data collection efficiency while minimizing energy consumption remains a significant challenge. This paper proposes a novel deep reinforcement learning (DRL)-based approach to address this problem. The objective is to develop an intelligent path planning algorithm that dynamically adapts to environmental constraints and data source distributions. The proposed method leverages a DRL framework, integrating a deep Q-network (DQN) to learn optimal flight trajectories by balancing exploration and exploitation. The UAV is trained to navigate complex environments, avoid obstacles, and prioritize high-data-yield regions. Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed approach outperforms traditional heuristic methods in terms of data collection efficiency and energy utilization. Key findings reveal a 25% improvement in data harvesting rates and a 15% reduction in energy consumption compared to baseline techniques. The study highlights the potential of DRL in enhancing UAV autonomy and operational efficiency for wireless data harvesting applications. This research contributes to the growing field of intelligent UAV systems, offering a scalable and adaptive solution for real-world deployment in IoT, environmental monitoring, and disaster recovery scenarios.  

**Keywords**: UAV path planning, wireless data harvesting, deep reinforcement learning, DQN, energy efficiency, autonomous systems.
