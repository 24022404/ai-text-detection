Abstract:

Image retrieval has become a fundamental task in computer vision, with large-scale datasets presenting new challenges in efficiency, accuracy, and scalability. This paper presents a comprehensive benchmark on a range of practical techniques—commonly referred to as "tricks"—employed to enhance the performance of large-scale image retrieval systems. We systematically investigate and evaluate the effectiveness of strategies such as feature normalization, advanced data augmentation, query expansion, re-ranking, and efficient indexing across multiple state-of-the-art deep learning-based retrieval models. Using several publicly available, large-scale image datasets, we conduct extensive experiments to assess the impact of individual and combined tricks on retrieval accuracy, speed, and memory consumption. Results reveal significant improvements in retrieval performance when specific combinations of these techniques are applied, with some tricks offering notable gains in both precision and computational efficiency. Our findings provide actionable insights and best-practice recommendations for practitioners aiming to build or improve large-scale image retrieval solutions. By releasing our benchmarking framework and analysis, we aim to foster reproducibility, facilitate further research, and support the development of more robust and effective retrieval systems. Keywords: image retrieval, benchmarking, feature normalization, query expansion, deep learning, large-scale datasets, system optimization.
