This paper introduces a novel approach to adversarial autoencoders (AAEs) by incorporating constant-curvature latent manifolds, aiming to improve data representation and generation. AAEs typically struggle with disentangling complex data distributions, leading to suboptimal generative performance. We propose embedding the latent space within manifolds of constant positive (spherical) or negative (hyperbolic) curvature, leveraging their inherent geometric properties to encourage structured representations. Our method utilizes Riemannian optimization techniques to train the encoder and decoder networks while enforcing the manifold constraint. We evaluate our approach on benchmark datasets, demonstrating that constant-curvature AAEs achieve superior performance in terms of reconstruction quality, sample diversity, and latent space traversal compared to traditional Euclidean AAEs and other manifold learning techniques. The results indicate that the proposed method effectively captures the underlying data structure, leading to more coherent and meaningful data generation. This work contributes a geometrically informed framework for unsupervised representation learning with potential applications in data generation, dimensionality reduction, and anomaly detection.

**Keywords:** Adversarial Autoencoders, Constant Curvature, Latent Manifolds, Riemannian Optimization, Generative Models, Representation Learning.
