This paper addresses the challenge of safe exploration in complex environments by introducing a novel approach to solving early terminated Markov Decision Processes (MDPs). The objective is to develop an efficient and reliable method for balancing exploration and safety in sequential decision-making problems. Our approach involves formulating the safe exploration problem as an MDP with early termination, which allows us to leverage the benefits of model-based reinforcement learning while minimizing the risk of unsafe actions. We propose a new algorithm that solves this MDP by iteratively improving the policy and updating the model parameters. Our results show that the proposed method outperforms existing safe exploration algorithms in terms of cumulative reward and safety constraints. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of early termination in MDPs and demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in real-world applications, such as robotics and autonomous systems. The contributions of this work include a novel formulation of the safe exploration problem, a new algorithm for solving early terminated MDPs, and experimental evaluations demonstrating improved performance and safety. Key keywords: safe exploration, Markov Decision Processes, early termination, reinforcement learning, sequential decision-making.
