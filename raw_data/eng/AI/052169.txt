This paper introduces a novel approach to weakly supervised top-down salient object detection by integrating a backtracking mechanism with Spatial Pyramid Pooling (SPP). Current weakly supervised methods often struggle with accurately localizing salient objects due to the limited supervision. To address this, we propose a Backtracking SPP-based image classifier that iteratively refines the attention maps generated from image-level labels. Our method utilizes SPP to capture multi-scale contextual information, enhancing the initial coarse localization. The backtracking mechanism then selectively revisits and refines regions based on a confidence score, effectively mitigating noise and improving the precision of salient object boundaries. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance compared to existing weakly supervised methods. Specifically, our classifier exhibits improved Intersection-over-Union (IoU) scores and reduced background noise, resulting in more accurate salient object detection. The proposed method offers a promising direction for weakly supervised learning by leveraging backtracking to enhance the quality of attention maps and improve localization accuracy. Keywords: Salient Object Detection, Weakly Supervised Learning, Spatial Pyramid Pooling, Backtracking, Attention Maps, Image Classification.
