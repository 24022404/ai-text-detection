This paper introduces ArtiBoost, a novel approach to boost articulated 3D hand-object pose estimation by leveraging online exploration and synthesis. The objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of pose estimation in complex hand-object interactions. Our method employs a unique online learning strategy, which explores the interaction space and synthesizes new training data to augment existing datasets. We utilize a deep neural network architecture, incorporating a transformer-based module to model the intricate relationships between hand and object poses. Experimental results demonstrate that ArtiBoost significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving superior accuracy and generalizability across various hand-object scenarios. The key contributions of this research lie in its ability to adapt to new, unseen interactions and its capacity to generate high-quality synthetic data, thereby reducing the need for extensive manual annotation. ArtiBoost has significant implications for applications in human-computer interaction, robotics, and computer vision, particularly in areas such as gesture recognition, grasping, and manipulation. Key keywords: 3D pose estimation, hand-object interaction, online learning, synthesis, transformer, computer vision, human-computer interaction.
