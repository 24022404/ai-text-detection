Program synthesis, the task of automatically generating programs from specifications, offers a promising avenue for explainable AI. This paper explores the use of program synthesis to generate black-box explanations for complex systems. Our method leverages input-output examples of a target system to synthesize concise programs that mimic its behavior, thereby providing interpretable summaries. We introduce a novel approach that combines search-based program synthesis with techniques for abstracting system behavior, allowing us to generate programs that capture key functionalities without overfitting to the provided examples. We evaluate our approach on several benchmark domains, demonstrating that the synthesized programs effectively approximate the target system’s behavior while remaining significantly simpler and more interpretable. Results show that these “black-box explanations” achieve high fidelity in predicting system outputs and offer valuable insights into the system's internal logic. This approach facilitates understanding and debugging of complex systems and can be applied in scenarios where access to the system’s internal state is limited. Keywords: program synthesis, explainable AI, black-box explanation, program abstraction, interpretable models.
