This tutorial provides a comprehensive guide to recursive models for analyzing and predicting path choice behavior. It introduces the theoretical foundations of recursive utility and cumulative prospect theory, demonstrating how these frameworks can be applied to model route selection and travel decisions. The tutorial presents a step-by-step approach to model specification, estimation, and validation, utilizing readily available software packages and illustrative examples. The focus is on practical implementation, providing researchers and practitioners with the tools necessary to understand and apply these sophisticated models. The tutorial highlights the advantages of recursive models in capturing dynamic decision-making processes, incorporating factors such as learning, adaptation, and risk preferences. This resource aims to bridge the gap between theoretical concepts and real-world applications, fostering a deeper understanding of path choice dynamics and improving the accuracy of behavioral predictions in transportation planning and related fields.

**Keywords:** Recursive Utility, Cumulative Prospect Theory, Path Choice, Behavioral Modeling, Transportation Planning, Decision Making.
