This paper presents a novel approach to learning answer generation from large-scale narrated videos, enabling machines to comprehend and respond to questions about visual content. Our objective is to develop an AI model that can effectively extract relevant information from millions of videos and provide accurate answers to user queries. We propose a multimodal learning framework that leverages both visual and auditory features from videos to train a question-answering system. Our method utilizes a transformer-based architecture to fuse video and audio embeddings, allowing the model to capture complex relationships between visual and narrative elements. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our approach, achieving state-of-the-art performance on several video question-answering benchmarks. Our research contributes to the development of more sophisticated video understanding and question-answering systems, with potential applications in areas such as video search, recommendation, and accessibility. Key aspects of our work include multimodal learning, video question-answering, and large-scale video analysis, making it a significant step forward in the field of AI-powered video understanding.
