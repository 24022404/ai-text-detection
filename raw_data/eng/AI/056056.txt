Pose-guided person image synthesis is a challenging task in computer vision, aiming to generate realistic images of a person in a target pose while preserving their appearance. This paper introduces Soft-Gated Warping-GAN, a novel generative adversarial network (GAN) framework designed to address key limitations in existing methods. The proposed approach leverages a soft-gated warping mechanism to dynamically align source and target poses, ensuring high-fidelity image synthesis with minimal artifacts. The framework integrates a multi-scale discriminator to enhance texture details and spatial consistency, while a pose-aware feature extraction module captures fine-grained pose and appearance information. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that Soft-Gated Warping-GAN outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of visual quality, structural accuracy, and pose alignment. The model achieves significant improvements in both quantitative metrics and qualitative evaluations, showcasing its ability to handle complex pose variations and occlusions. This work contributes to advancing pose-guided image synthesis, with potential applications in virtual try-on, animation, and augmented reality.  

**Keywords**: Pose-guided image synthesis, GAN, soft-gated warping, person image generation, computer vision, deep learning.
