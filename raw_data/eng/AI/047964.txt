This paper presents a novel approach to human pose estimation by jointly training a convolutional neural network (CNN) and a graphical model. Our objective is to leverage the strengths of both deep learning for feature extraction and structured prediction for enforcing anatomical constraints. The CNN is designed to learn robust image features, while the graphical model encodes relationships between body joints. These two components are integrated into a unified architecture and trained end-to-end using a carefully designed loss function that considers both feature learning and structural consistency. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our joint training approach outperforms existing methods that train these components separately. Specifically, we achieve significant improvements in pose estimation accuracy, particularly in challenging scenarios with occlusions and pose variations. The proposed method provides a more coherent and accurate pose estimation by effectively combining image-based cues and structural priors. Keywords: Human Pose Estimation, Convolutional Neural Network, Graphical Model, Joint Training, Computer Vision.
