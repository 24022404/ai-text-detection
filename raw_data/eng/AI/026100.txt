Image harmonization aims to seamlessly blend foreground objects into background images by adjusting visual attributes such as color, tone, and texture. Existing methods often struggle with balancing computational efficiency and high-resolution output, particularly when handling complex spatial relationships. This paper introduces the Spatial-Separated Curve Rendering Network (SSCRN), a novel deep learning framework designed to address these challenges. SSCRN leverages a spatially separated architecture that decouples global and local feature processing, enabling efficient handling of high-resolution images without compromising quality. The network employs curve-based rendering techniques to model fine-grained color transformations, ensuring smooth and realistic harmonization. Experimental results demonstrate that SSCRN outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of both visual quality and computational efficiency, achieving significant improvements in processing speed while maintaining high-resolution output. The proposed approach is particularly effective for applications requiring real-time image editing and large-scale image processing. This work contributes a scalable and innovative solution to the field of image harmonization, with potential applications in computer graphics, augmented reality, and digital content creation.  

**Keywords**: image harmonization, deep learning, high-resolution rendering, curve-based rendering, spatial separation, computational efficiency.
