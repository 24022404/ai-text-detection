This paper presents a novel approach to gesture recognition in RGB videos by leveraging human body keypoints and dynamic time warping (DTW). The objective is to develop an accurate and efficient system for recognizing human gestures from video sequences. Our method utilizes a deep learning-based approach to detect human body keypoints, which are then used to extract meaningful features. These features are subsequently compared using DTW to measure the similarity between different gesture sequences. The results show that our approach achieves high accuracy in recognizing various gestures, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a new feature extraction technique and the application of DTW to gesture recognition, which enables the system to handle variations in gesture speed and duration. This study has significant implications for human-computer interaction, surveillance, and healthcare applications, where gesture recognition can be used to enhance user experience, detect anomalies, and monitor patient activity. The proposed system is robust, efficient, and can be applied to various domains, making it a valuable contribution to the field of computer vision and machine learning, with relevant keywords including gesture recognition, human body keypoints, dynamic time warping, deep learning, and computer vision.
