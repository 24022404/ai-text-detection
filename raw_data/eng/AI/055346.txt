This paper introduces a novel deep learning approach for feature embedding that leverages local similarity information to improve representation learning. The objective is to learn feature embeddings that effectively capture the underlying structure of data by explicitly considering the relationships between neighboring data points. We propose a local similarity-aware embedding network (LSAEN) that incorporates a similarity regularization term into the loss function. This term encourages the network to produce embeddings where similar data points in the original feature space are also close together in the embedded space. The LSAEN architecture consists of a deep neural network followed by a similarity preservation layer that enforces the local similarity constraints. We evaluate the performance of LSAEN on several benchmark datasets for tasks such as clustering and classification. Experimental results demonstrate that LSAEN consistently outperforms existing feature embedding methods, achieving significant improvements in accuracy and F1-score. The proposed approach offers a promising direction for learning more discriminative and robust feature representations, with potential applications in various computer vision and machine learning tasks. Keywords: Feature Embedding, Deep Learning, Similarity Learning, Representation Learning, Clustering, Classification.
