This paper presents a novel approach for generating realistic bokeh effects in digital images by leveraging depth-aware blending of smoothed images. The objective is to enhance the aesthetic quality of photographs by simulating the shallow depth-of-field effect typically achieved with high-aperture cameras. Our method employs a depth estimation model to segment the foreground and background regions, followed by a smoothing algorithm applied selectively to the background. The smoothed background is then blended with the sharp foreground using depth-aware weighting, ensuring seamless transitions and natural-looking bokeh effects. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach outperforms existing techniques in terms of visual quality and computational efficiency. Comparisons with state-of-the-art methods highlight improvements in edge preservation and depth consistency. The key contribution of this work lies in its ability to generate high-quality bokeh effects without requiring specialized hardware, making it accessible for integration into mobile and consumer-grade imaging applications. This research advances the field of computational photography by providing a practical and effective solution for bokeh effect generation, with potential applications in smartphone cameras, image editing software, and augmented reality systems.  

**Keywords**: bokeh effect, depth-aware blending, image smoothing, computational photography, depth estimation, shallow depth-of-field.
