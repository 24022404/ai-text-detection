The rapid advancement of autonomous driving technologies necessitates precise and reliable 3D object detection to ensure safety and operational efficacy. This paper presents a Multi-View 3D Object Detection Network designed specifically for autonomous vehicles. The objective is to enhance the detection and localization precision of objects from various perspectives within a highly dynamic environment. Our approach integrates multi-view data fusion with an innovative neural network architecture, leveraging both PointCloud and camera data. This method allows for comprehensive spatial understanding and a robust detection pipeline. The results demonstrate a significant improvement in detection accuracy and computational efficiency compared to existing single-view methods. Key findings indicate that the proposed network excels in complex scenarios, such as crowded urban environments and variable lighting conditions, showing superior performance metrics in comparison to state-of-the-art benchmarks. The implications of this work underscore its potential contributions to improving the perception systems of autonomous vehicles, facilitating safer navigation and decision-making. Moving forward, the integration of this network could lead to advancements in real-time deployments, highlighting its scalability and effectiveness within the autonomous driving sector. Keywords: 3D Object Detection, Multi-View, Autonomous Driving, Neural Network, PointCloud Data, Camera Fusion.
