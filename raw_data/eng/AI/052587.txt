This paper introduces an end-to-end trainable multi-instance pose estimation framework leveraging transformer networks. Addressing the challenges of simultaneously detecting and estimating the poses of multiple objects in complex scenes, our approach eliminates the need for separate detection and pose estimation stages. We propose a novel transformer-based architecture that directly predicts object bounding boxes and corresponding keypoint locations in a single forward pass. The model utilizes self-attention mechanisms to capture contextual relationships between objects and keypoints, enhancing robustness to occlusions and variations in appearance. Experimental results on challenging benchmark datasets demonstrate significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency compared to existing multi-stage methods. Specifically, our model achieves state-of-the-art performance while reducing computational complexity. The end-to-end training paradigm simplifies the pipeline and allows for joint optimization of all components. This work contributes a streamlined and effective solution for multi-instance pose estimation, paving the way for applications in robotics, augmented reality, and human-computer interaction. Keywords: Pose Estimation, Transformers, Multi-Instance Learning, End-to-End Training, Computer Vision.
