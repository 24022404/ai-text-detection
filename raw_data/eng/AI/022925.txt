This paper presents a novel approach to sign language recognition, leveraging the strengths of Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN) and BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers) to accurately identify and interpret sign language poses. The objective is to develop a robust and efficient system capable of recognizing sign language from pose-based inputs, addressing the limitations of existing methods. Our approach utilizes GCN to model the spatial relationships between body joints and BERT to capture the contextual dependencies between signs. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, achieving a significant improvement in recognition accuracy compared to state-of-the-art approaches. The proposed system has important implications for enhancing communication between sign language users and non-signers, with potential applications in fields such as education, healthcare, and social services. Key contributions include the introduction of a novel GCN-BERT architecture for pose-based sign language recognition and the demonstration of its superiority over existing methods. Relevant keywords: sign language recognition, GCN, BERT, pose estimation, AI-powered communication systems.
