This paper addresses the challenge of effectively fusing multimodal data, such as text, images, and audio, to improve the performance of deep learning models. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that efficiently integrates multiple modalities while minimizing computational overhead. We propose Attention Bottlenecks, a lightweight and flexible framework that leverages attention mechanisms to selectively focus on relevant modalities and filter out redundant information. Our approach is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating significant improvements in multimodal fusion tasks, such as visual question answering and sentiment analysis. The results show that Attention Bottlenecks achieve state-of-the-art performance while reducing computational costs by up to 30%. Our research contributes to the development of more efficient and effective multimodal fusion methods, with potential applications in areas like human-computer interaction, multimedia analysis, and decision support systems. Key keywords: multimodal fusion, attention mechanisms, deep learning, efficient computing, multimodal processing.
