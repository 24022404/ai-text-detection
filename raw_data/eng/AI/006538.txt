Reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms often struggle with sparse reward environments and noisy observations. This paper introduces Adaptive Symmetric Reward Noising (ASRN), a novel technique designed to improve exploration and robustness in RL. ASRN dynamically adjusts the magnitude of symmetric noise added to the reward signal based on the agent's learning progress. Specifically, noise is increased during periods of stagnation to encourage exploration and reduced as the agent demonstrates proficiency to refine policy convergence. We evaluate ASRN across a suite of benchmark RL environments, including those with sparse and deceptive rewards. Results demonstrate that ASRN consistently outperforms standard RL algorithms and fixed reward noising strategies in terms of sample efficiency, final performance, and robustness to environmental noise. The adaptive noise schedule enables the agent to escape local optima and discover more effective policies. ASRN offers a simple yet powerful approach to enhance exploration and learning stability in challenging RL scenarios. Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Exploration, Reward Shaping, Noise Injection, Adaptive Learning.
