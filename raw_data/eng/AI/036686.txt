Image super-resolution (SR) aims to reconstruct high-resolution (HR) images from their low-resolution (LR) counterparts. Multi-reference super-resolution (MRSR) leverages information from multiple reference images to enhance the reconstruction quality. This paper introduces a novel attention-based multi-reference learning framework for image SR, designed to effectively fuse relevant information from multiple references while mitigating noise. Our approach employs an attention mechanism to adaptively weight and aggregate features extracted from both the LR input and the reference images. Specifically, a cross-attention module captures inter-image dependencies, enabling the network to selectively focus on the most informative regions of the reference images. Furthermore, a self-attention mechanism refines the fused features, enhancing their representational power. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art MRSR algorithms, achieving superior PSNR and SSIM scores with improved visual quality. The proposed framework offers a robust and efficient solution for leveraging multi-reference information in image super-resolution, paving the way for enhanced image restoration applications. Keywords: Super-Resolution, Multi-Reference Learning, Attention Mechanism, Image Reconstruction, Deep Learning.
