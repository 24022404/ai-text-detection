The paper presents D3DLO, a novel deep learning framework designed to enhance 3D LiDAR odometry by improving accuracy and robustness in real-time applications. Traditional methods often struggle with dynamic environments and sensor noise, necessitating an advanced approach to effectively harness LiDAR capabilities.

Methods: D3DLO employs a deep neural network architecture that integrates convolutional and recurrent neural network layers to efficiently process and interpret sequential LiDAR point cloud data. The model is trained to accurately estimate relative motion by capturing complex spatial and temporal features. Data augmentation techniques and a robust loss function are used to improve generalization across diverse environments and conditions.

Results: Experimental evaluations demonstrate that D3DLO outperforms existing LiDAR odometry methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency, particularly in challenging scenarios involving complex and dynamic surroundings. Performance benchmarks indicate significant improvements in trajectory estimation and reduction in drift, with results validated on multiple real-world datasets.

Conclusion: The introduction of D3DLO provides a substantial leap forward in the domain of 3D LiDAR-based navigation systems. Its ability to accurately perform odometry in real-time supports autonomous vehicle applications and contributes to improving robust mapping and localization. The framework's adaptability to various environments highlights its potential for widespread adoption in the field of autonomous systems.

Keywords: 3D LiDAR, Odometry, Deep Learning, Neural Networks, Autonomous Vehicles, Real-Time Systems, Point Cloud Processing, Robust Localization.
