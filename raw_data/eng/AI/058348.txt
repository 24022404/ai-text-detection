Research into constructing universal cycles for particular classes of sets has focused on the application of generalized FKM and greedy algorithms. Moreno demonstrated that such methods are effective in generating universal cycles for rotations of the lexicographically largest i-necklace. Building on this, Au showed that the same approach successfully produces all aperiodic k-ary strings of length n. Further expanding these findings, Joe Sawada and colleagues generalized the results to broader families of k-ary strings of length n that adhere to specific closure conditions.
