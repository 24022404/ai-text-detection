This paper introduces a novel unified message passing model for semi-supervised classification, termed Masked Label Prediction (MLP). The objective is to address the challenge of limited labeled data in classification tasks by leveraging both labeled and unlabeled samples. Our approach combines the strengths of graph neural networks and masked language modeling to predict missing labels. The MLP model uses a message passing mechanism to propagate information between nodes, while masking a portion of the labels to encourage the model to learn from unlabeled data. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of MLP, outperforming state-of-the-art semi-supervised classification methods on several benchmark datasets. The key findings highlight the importance of unified message passing and masked label prediction in improving classification accuracy. This research contributes to the development of more efficient and accurate semi-supervised learning methods, with potential applications in areas such as natural language processing, computer vision, and graph-structured data analysis. Key keywords: semi-supervised classification, graph neural networks, masked language modeling, message passing, unified model.
