This paper addresses the challenge of domain generalization and adaptation, where models trained on a specific domain often fail to perform well on new, unseen domains. Our objective is to develop a novel approach that enables deep learning models to generalize across domains by aligning and restoring features. We propose a feature alignment and restoration framework that leverages adversarial training and self-supervised learning to reduce domain shift and promote domain-invariant representations. Our method involves a dual-branch architecture that aligns features across domains and restores the original feature distribution, resulting in improved generalization and adaptation performance. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, outperforming state-of-the-art domain generalization and adaptation methods on benchmark datasets. The key contributions of this research include a novel feature alignment and restoration technique, improved domain generalization and adaptation capabilities, and enhanced model robustness. Our work has significant implications for real-world applications, such as computer vision, natural language processing, and robotics, where domain shift is a common challenge. Key keywords: domain generalization, domain adaptation, feature alignment, feature restoration, deep learning, adversarial training, self-supervised learning.
