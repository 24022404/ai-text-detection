Abstract:

Dynamic graphs, characterized by their evolving structures and changing node relationships, present unique challenges for traditional graph representation and learning approaches. This paper introduces Dynamic Graph Convolutional Networks (DGCN), an innovative neural network architecture designed to effectively capture both the spatial and temporal dependencies inherent in dynamic graph data. Our approach extends standard graph convolutional networks by integrating temporal modules that adaptively learn node and edge evolution over time, enabling the model to maintain high representational fidelity in non-static environments. We implement a temporal attention mechanism and a dynamic neighborhood aggregation strategy, allowing DGCN to selectively focus on relevant temporal patterns and evolving topological features. Experimental evaluations on benchmark dynamic graph datasets demonstrate that DGCN significantly outperforms existing static and dynamic graph neural network models in tasks such as node classification, link prediction, and anomaly detection. The results highlight improved accuracy, robustness to rapid structural changes, and faster adaptation to new patterns. Our findings suggest that DGCN offers substantial advancements for applications in recommendation systems, social network analysis, and real-time traffic prediction. Keywords: dynamic graphs, graph convolutional networks, temporal modeling, deep learning, network analysis.
