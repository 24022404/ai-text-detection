This paper addresses the challenge of efficient dictionary learning in high-dimensional data spaces, a critical task in signal processing and machine learning. Traditional dictionary learning methods often suffer from high computational complexity and memory requirements, limiting their scalability. To overcome these limitations, we propose a novel compressed dictionary learning framework that leverages sparse coding and dimensionality reduction techniques. Our approach integrates a compression step into the learning process, significantly reducing storage and computational overhead while maintaining reconstruction accuracy. We evaluate the proposed method on benchmark datasets, comparing it with state-of-the-art dictionary learning algorithms. Experimental results demonstrate that our framework achieves comparable or superior performance in terms of reconstruction quality, while reducing memory usage by up to 40% and computational time by 30%. The proposed method is particularly effective for applications involving large-scale datasets, such as image processing and natural language processing. This work contributes to the field by introducing a scalable and resource-efficient solution for dictionary learning, with potential applications in real-time systems and edge computing environments.  

**Keywords**: dictionary learning, sparse coding, dimensionality reduction, signal processing, machine learning, scalability.
