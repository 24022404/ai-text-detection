This paper introduces SliderGAN, a novel approach for synthesizing expressive face images with fine-grained control over facial expressions. Our method leverages the power of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) in conjunction with a 3D blendshape model to achieve precise manipulation of facial expressions. Specifically, SliderGAN learns a mapping between a latent space and the parameters of a 3D blendshape model, allowing users to intuitively control expressions by “sliding” these parameters. This approach facilitates the generation of high-resolution facial images with nuanced expressions that are difficult to achieve with existing GAN-based methods. Experimental results demonstrate that SliderGAN outperforms state-of-the-art face image synthesis techniques in terms of expression control, image quality, and identity preservation. Furthermore, we show the utility of SliderGAN for applications such as facial expression editing and animation. Our work offers a new avenue for generating realistic and controllable facial imagery, opening up opportunities in various fields, including virtual reality, animation, and human-computer interaction.

**Keywords:** Face Image Synthesis, GAN, 3D Blendshape, Facial Expression, Image Generation, Deep Learning.
