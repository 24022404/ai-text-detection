Generative adversarial networks (GANs) are known for their instability during training, often leading to mode collapse or vanishing gradients. This paper introduces Generative Adversarial Forests (GAF), a novel approach to improve the conditioning of adversarial learning. GAF replaces the traditional generator network with an ensemble of decision trees trained adversarially. This ensemble leverages the diverse representational power of decision trees to capture complex data distributions while mitigating the instability issues associated with deep neural networks. We employ a modified adversarial training procedure adapted for decision tree ensembles. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that GAF achieves superior performance compared to traditional GANs and other adversarial training methods, exhibiting improved stability and reduced mode collapse. The GAF architecture shows a promising direction toward more robust and reliable generative models, particularly in scenarios where training data is limited or the data distribution is complex. Keywords: Generative Adversarial Networks, Decision Trees, Ensemble Learning, Adversarial Learning, Generative Models, Mode Collapse.
