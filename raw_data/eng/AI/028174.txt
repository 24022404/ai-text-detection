This paper presents a novel approach to synthesizing flight controllers using deep reinforcement learning. The objective is to design a controller that can efficiently navigate complex flight scenarios while ensuring stability and safety. Our method employs a model-free, deep reinforcement learning algorithm to learn optimal control policies from raw sensor data. The approach utilizes a customized reward function that prioritizes smooth flight trajectories, minimal control effort, and robustness to disturbances. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our approach, with the synthesized controller outperforming traditional baseline controllers in terms of flight stability, tracking accuracy, and adaptability to changing environmental conditions. The key findings highlight the potential of deep reinforcement learning in automating the design of high-performance flight controllers, with significant implications for the development of autonomous aerial vehicles. Our contributions include the introduction of a novel reward function and the demonstration of improved controller performance in realistic flight simulations, making this work relevant to areas such as autonomous systems, robotics, and aerial robotics, with key keywords including deep reinforcement learning, flight control, autonomous aerial vehicles, and model-free control.
