The paper addresses the challenge of developing a large-scale multimodal knowledge base system capable of answering visual queries. The primary aim is to bridge the gap between visual content understanding and natural language processing to enhance information retrieval efficiency and accuracy.

Methods/Approach: Leveraging advancements in artificial intelligence and deep learning, the proposed system integrates multimodal data processing techniques, employing both convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for visual data and transformer-based models for textual data. A novel multimodal fusion architecture is introduced, enabling seamless integration and processing of diverse data types. An innovative indexing mechanism is also developed to facilitate rapid access and retrieval of relevant information within the knowledge base.

Results/Findings: Experimental evaluations demonstrate the system's superior performance in interpreting and responding to complex visual queries compared to existing methods. Key metrics, such as accuracy, response time, and user satisfaction, reveal significant improvements, showcasing the system's ability to handle large datasets effectively and generate precise answers.

Conclusion/Implications: The research presents important contributions to the field of knowledge management by offering a robust framework to answer visual queries. The system's novelty lies in its multimodal integration and efficient query processing capabilities, which holds significant potential for applications in various domains, including education, digital libraries, and intelligent virtual assistants. This approach sets a new benchmark for future research in visual query answering systems.

Keywords: Multimodal knowledge base, visual queries, CNN, transformer, AI, data processing, information retrieval, digital libraries, virtual assistants.
