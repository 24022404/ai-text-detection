This paper introduces DISPATCH, a novel framework for design space exploration of cyber-physical systems (CPS). The objective is to efficiently navigate the complex design space of CPS, which integrates physical and computational components, to optimize system performance, safety, and reliability. DISPATCH employs a hybrid approach, combining machine learning, optimization techniques, and model-based design to explore the vast design space of CPS. The results show that DISPATCH can effectively identify optimal design configurations, reducing the time and effort required for manual design exploration. Key findings include significant improvements in system performance and a reduction in design errors. The conclusion highlights the importance of DISPATCH in enabling the efficient design of complex CPS, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, autonomous vehicles, and smart cities. By leveraging DISPATCH, designers and engineers can create more efficient, reliable, and safe CPS, paving the way for widespread adoption of these systems in various industries. Keywords: cyber-physical systems, design space exploration, machine learning, optimization, model-based design.
