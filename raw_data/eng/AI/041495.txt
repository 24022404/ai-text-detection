This research addresses the existing challenge of accurately classifying 3D MRI brain volumes by utilizing the vast amount of unstructured clinical data in medical reports. We propose a novel approach that combines deep reinforcement learning with automated label extraction to improve classification accuracy and efficiency.

Methods/Approach: Our method leverages a deep reinforcement learning model, which is trained to classify 3D MRI brain volumes through automated label extraction from clinical reports. The reinforcement learning algorithm dynamically optimizes decision-making processes by interacting with a high-dimensional input environment. The automated label extraction mechanism processes clinical reports to identify relevant classification labels without manual intervention, thus enhancing the scalability and reliability of the classification process.

Results/Findings: The model demonstrated superior performance compared to existing classification methods, achieving higher accuracy in label classification of 3D MRI volumes. Our approach successfully extracted and applied clinically relevant labels from reports, leading to more robust and clinically aligned classification outcomes. Experimental results showed significant improvements in classification accuracy and efficiency, reducing the need for manual labeling in large datasets.

Conclusion/Implications: This study highlights the potential for deep reinforcement learning integrated with automated label extraction to revolutionize brain MRI classification tasks. Our method offers a scalable and accurate solution for handling complex medical image data, potentially transforming diagnostic processes in clinical settings. Future applications could extend to other medical imaging modalities, enhancing the utility and adoption of AI-driven healthcare solutions.

Keywords: Deep reinforcement learning, automated label extraction, 3D MRI classification, clinical reports, medical image analysis, brain volume classification.
