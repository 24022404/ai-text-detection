The burgeoning field of fashion technology demands innovative solutions for accurately predicting and displaying colors on various garments, especially with the multiplicity of colors involved. This study introduces CORE (Color Regression for Multiple Colors Fashion Garments), aimed at enhancing digital color accuracy and representation for fashion garments that incorporate multiple hues.

Methods/Approach: CORE leverages novel regression algorithms that focus on color calibration and transformation processes tailored to the fashion industry. By optimizing machine learning algorithms, our approach accurately identifies and predicts the appearance of multiple colors on fabric surfaces, adapting to different lighting conditions and material textures. The model was trained and validated using a diverse dataset of fashion images to ensure robustness and applicability.

Results/Findings: The proposed system demonstrates a significant improvement over existing color prediction methodologies, achieving higher correlation coefficients and lower error margins in color approximation. Through comprehensive testing, CORE successfully maintained color integrity across a spectrum of garment types and settings, showcasing superior performance over traditional methods.

Conclusion/Implications: CORE represents a substantial advancement in digital fashion technology, offering designers and retailers improved tools for accurate color visualization in e-commerce platforms and virtual fittings. This research contributes to the future of digital garment design and merchandising, potentially impacting how consumers interact with online fashion retailers and enhancing the virtual shopping experience. Key keywords: color regression, fashion technology, machine learning, digital color accuracy, multicolor garments.
