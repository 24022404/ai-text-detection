This paper proposes a novel Multi-View Matching Network for 6D pose estimation, addressing the challenge of accurately estimating the position and orientation of objects in 3D space. Our approach leverages a deep learning framework that integrates multiple views of an object to improve pose estimation accuracy. The network employs a combination of convolutional neural networks and graph-based matching algorithms to establish correspondences between different views, enabling robust and efficient 6D pose estimation. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, outperforming state-of-the-art approaches in terms of accuracy and robustness. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a multi-view matching mechanism and a novel loss function that enhances the network's ability to generalize across various objects and scenes. This work has significant implications for applications such as robotics, augmented reality, and computer vision, where accurate 6D pose estimation is crucial. Key keywords: 6D pose estimation, multi-view matching, deep learning, computer vision, robotics.
