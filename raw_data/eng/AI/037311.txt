Entity resolution (ER), the process of identifying and merging records that refer to the same real-world entity across diverse datasets, is a fundamental challenge in data integration. However, achieving high-accuracy ER typically requires extensive manual labeling, leading to significant costs. This paper presents a novel, cost-effective framework for variational active entity resolution that strategically reduces labeling overhead while maintaining robust performance. Our approach integrates variational inference with active learning to intelligently select the most informative record pairs for labeling, maximizing model improvement per labeling cost. We propose an adaptive query mechanism that estimates uncertainty and expected utility, ensuring optimal allocation of annotation resources. Comprehensive experiments on multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms existing active and passive ER techniques in terms of precision, recall, and overall cost-efficiency. The results highlight the framework's scalability and effectiveness, especially in scenarios with limited budgets. This research advances the state of active learning for entity resolution and offers practical contributions for organizations seeking accurate, scalable, and budget-friendly data cleaning solutions. Keywords: entity resolution, active learning, variational inference, data integration, cost-efficient annotation, uncertainty sampling.
