This paper presents Talk-to-Edit, an innovative framework for fine-grained facial editing through conversational interaction. This research addresses the need for intuitive and precise control in facial image manipulation, leveraging natural language dialogue to guide the editing process.

Methods/Approach: The proposed system integrates advanced natural language processing with state-of-the-art computer vision techniques to enable real-time facial alterations. Utilizing a robust dialogue management system, users communicate desired changes through simple conversation. This input is processed by a combination of a transformer-based language model and a generative adversarial network (GAN) tailored for image editing, ensuring seamless and realistic modifications.

Results/Findings: Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of Talk-to-Edit in producing precise and fine-grained changes on facial images, outperforming existing methods in both user satisfaction and accuracy of edits. The system successfully handles a wide variety of requests, including adjustments to facial expressions, features, and accessory inclusion, showcasing its adaptability and user-friendliness.

Conclusion/Implications: Talk-to-Edit offers a novel approach to interacting with image editing software, emphasizing the potential of language-driven interfaces for creative applications. By reducing the barrier to entry for users unfamiliar with traditional editing tools, this work opens new avenues for personalization in digital media industries and sets a foundation for further exploration in voice-driven edits. Potential applications extend into fields such as virtual reality, gaming, and personalized digital assistants, highlighting the broader relevance and impact of this technology.

Key Keywords: Fine-grained editing, natural language processing, facial image manipulation, dialogue-based system, generative adversarial networks, real-time interaction.
