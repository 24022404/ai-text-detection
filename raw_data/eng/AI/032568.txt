This paper introduces a novel approach to learner-aware teaching by leveraging inverse reinforcement learning (IRL) to model and adapt to learner preferences and constraints. The objective is to design teaching strategies that are not only effective but also personalized, ensuring optimal learning outcomes by aligning instructional methods with individual learner characteristics. We propose a framework that integrates IRL with preference elicitation and constraint satisfaction techniques, enabling the system to infer learner goals, preferences, and limitations from observed behavior. Our method employs a combination of reward modeling and optimization algorithms to generate adaptive teaching policies. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach outperforms traditional teaching methods in terms of learner engagement and knowledge retention, particularly in scenarios with diverse learner profiles. The findings highlight the potential of this framework to revolutionize personalized education, offering scalable and efficient solutions for adaptive learning systems. Key contributions include the integration of IRL with preference-based learning and the development of a constraint-aware teaching model. This research has broad implications for educational technology, intelligent tutoring systems, and AI-driven personalized learning platforms.  

**Keywords**: Inverse Reinforcement Learning, Learner-aware Teaching, Preference Modeling, Constraint Satisfaction, Personalized Education, Adaptive Learning Systems.
