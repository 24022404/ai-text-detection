This paper explores a novel smartphone-based colorimetric detection system leveraging machine learning for rapid and cost-effective chemical analysis. The objective is to develop a portable and accessible alternative to traditional laboratory-based spectrophotometry. The system utilizes the smartphone's built-in camera to capture images of colorimetric reactions, and employs machine learning algorithms, specifically a convolutional neural network (CNN), to correlate color changes with analyte concentrations. We trained and validated the CNN model on a dataset of synthetically generated color gradients and real-world measurements of varying concentrations of target analytes. The system achieved a detection accuracy of 95% and a limit of detection comparable to benchtop spectrophotometers for selected analytes. Results demonstrate the feasibility of using smartphones as portable colorimetric readers, offering significant advantages in terms of cost, portability, and ease of use. This approach facilitates on-site environmental monitoring, point-of-care diagnostics, and food safety analysis in resource-limited settings. Keywords: smartphone, colorimetric detection, machine learning, convolutional neural network, chemical analysis, portable diagnostics.
