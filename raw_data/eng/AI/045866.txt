This paper proposes a novel Visibility Constrained Generative Model (VCGM) for depth-based 3D facial pose tracking, addressing the challenge of accurately estimating facial poses under varying visibility conditions. Our approach combines a generative model with a depth-based tracking framework, leveraging the strengths of both to improve pose estimation accuracy. The VCGM utilizes a deep learning-based architecture to learn a probabilistic representation of 3D facial structures, incorporating visibility constraints to handle occlusions and missing data. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, outperforming state-of-the-art approaches in terms of pose estimation accuracy and robustness to visibility variations. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a visibility-aware generative model for 3D facial pose tracking and the development of a robust depth-based tracking framework. Our work has significant implications for applications such as facial recognition, human-computer interaction, and virtual reality, where accurate 3D facial pose tracking is crucial. Key keywords: 3D facial pose tracking, depth-based tracking, generative models, visibility constraints, computer vision, machine learning.
