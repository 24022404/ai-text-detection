This paper introduces a novel framework for rational reinforcement learning, addressing the need for more efficient and effective decision-making in complex environments. Our objective is to establish a set of axioms that provide a foundation for rational behavior in reinforcement learning agents. We propose a new approach based on a set of intuitive axioms that ensure the agent's behavior is consistent with rational decision-making principles. Our method involves the development of a new algorithm that incorporates these axioms, allowing the agent to learn optimal policies in a wide range of environments. The results show that our approach outperforms existing methods in terms of convergence rate and overall performance, demonstrating the potential for significant improvements in reinforcement learning. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a novel set of axioms for rational reinforcement learning, the development of a new algorithm that incorporates these axioms, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in a variety of environments. Our work has important implications for the development of more advanced reinforcement learning systems, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, game playing, and autonomous decision-making, and is relevant to keywords such as reinforcement learning, rational decision-making, artificial intelligence, and machine learning.
