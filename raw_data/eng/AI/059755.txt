This paper presents a novel approach to synthesizing realistic face photos from simple line drawings, with a focus on attribute-controlled generation. The objective is to develop a system that can produce high-quality, photorealistic face images from basic line drawings, while allowing for control over specific attributes such as facial expression, hair style, and accessories. Our method employs a deep learning-based framework, utilizing a generative adversarial network (GAN) architecture to learn the mapping between line drawings and face photos. The results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in generating realistic face images with desired attributes, outperforming existing methods in terms of image quality and attribute control. Our research contributes to the field of computer vision and graphics, with potential applications in fields such as entertainment, education, and social media. Key contributions include the development of a novel attribute-controlled face synthesis model, and the demonstration of its ability to generate high-quality face images from simple line drawings. Relevant keywords: face synthesis, line drawing, attribute control, GAN, computer vision, graphics.
