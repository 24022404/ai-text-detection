This paper introduces SSTVOS, a novel approach to video object segmentation leveraging sparse spatiotemporal transformers. The objective is to accurately segment objects of interest in videos, addressing the challenges of spatial and temporal complexity. Our method employs a transformer-based architecture that efficiently models long-range dependencies and spatiotemporal relationships, while sparse attention mechanisms reduce computational costs. Experimental results demonstrate that SSTVOS outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency, achieving significant improvements on benchmark datasets. The key findings highlight the effectiveness of our approach in capturing nuanced object boundaries and handling complex scenes. This research contributes to the advancement of video object segmentation, with potential applications in areas such as autonomous driving, surveillance, and video editing. The novelty of SSTVOS lies in its ability to balance accuracy and efficiency, making it a promising solution for real-world applications. Key keywords: video object segmentation, spatiotemporal transformers, sparse attention, computer vision, deep learning.
