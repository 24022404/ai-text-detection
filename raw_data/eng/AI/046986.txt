This paper addresses the challenge of audio classification by introducing a novel approach based on shift-invariance sparse coding. The objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of audio classification systems by effectively capturing shift-invariant features from audio signals. Our method employs a sparse coding technique that incorporates shift-invariance, enabling the model to learn features that are invariant to time shifts and robust to variations in audio data. The approach is evaluated on several audio classification tasks, including music genre classification and speech recognition, and the results demonstrate significant improvements in performance compared to existing methods. Key findings include the ability of the proposed method to learn compact and informative representations of audio data, leading to improved classification accuracy and reduced computational complexity. The contributions of this research include the development of a shift-invariance sparse coding framework for audio classification, which has potential applications in music information retrieval, speech processing, and audio event detection. Relevant keywords for this research include sparse coding, shift-invariance, audio classification, music genre classification, and speech recognition.
