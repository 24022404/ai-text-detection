This paper introduces RLCache, a novel approach to automated cache management leveraging reinforcement learning (RL) techniques. The objective is to optimize cache performance in dynamic environments, where traditional static cache management strategies often fall short. RLCache utilizes a deep RL model to learn adaptive cache replacement policies, maximizing hit rates and minimizing latency. Our approach employs a customized Q-learning algorithm, incorporating cache state and request patterns as inputs to inform decision-making. Experimental results demonstrate that RLCache outperforms state-of-the-art cache management algorithms, achieving significant improvements in cache hit rates and reduced average response times. The key contributions of this research include the development of a scalable and efficient RL-based cache management framework, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in real-world scenarios. By applying RL to cache management, RLCache offers a flexible and autonomous solution, enabling efficient data retrieval and storage in various applications, including cloud computing, edge networks, and content delivery systems. Key keywords: reinforcement learning, cache management, Q-learning, automated systems, cloud computing.
