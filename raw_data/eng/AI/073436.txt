The rapid growth of big data has necessitated the development of efficient and scalable classification techniques to handle large-scale datasets. This paper addresses the challenge of improving classification accuracy and computational efficiency in big data environments using augmented decision trees. We propose a novel approach that integrates ensemble learning and feature augmentation techniques to enhance the performance of traditional decision tree algorithms. Our method leverages distributed computing frameworks to ensure scalability and adaptability to high-dimensional data. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed approach achieves superior classification accuracy compared to conventional decision trees and other state-of-the-art methods, while maintaining computational efficiency. The findings highlight the potential of augmented decision trees in real-world applications, such as fraud detection, recommendation systems, and healthcare analytics. This research contributes to the advancement of big data classification by offering a robust and scalable solution that bridges the gap between accuracy and efficiency.  

**Keywords**: big data, decision trees, classification, ensemble learning, feature augmentation, distributed computing.
