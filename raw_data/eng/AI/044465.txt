In real-world applications, machine learning models often face challenges due to adversarial and interventional shifts, where data distributions change due to malicious manipulations or external interventions. This paper addresses the critical problem of ensuring model robustness and generalization under such dynamic and adversarial conditions. We propose a novel framework that integrates adversarial training with causal inference techniques to mitigate the impact of these shifts. Our approach leverages a combination of adversarial robustness mechanisms and interventional data augmentation to enhance model resilience. Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms existing approaches in maintaining accuracy and stability under adversarial and interventional perturbations. The results highlight the importance of combining adversarial and causal perspectives to build more reliable machine learning systems. This work contributes to advancing the field by providing a robust framework for learning under complex distributional shifts, with potential applications in cybersecurity, healthcare, and autonomous systems.  

**Keywords**: adversarial robustness, causal inference, distributional shifts, machine learning, interventional data augmentation.
