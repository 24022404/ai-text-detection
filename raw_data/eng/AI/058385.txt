Understanding the inner workings of deep neural networks remains a significant challenge. This paper introduces a novel divide-and-conquer approach to dissect complex deep networks into more manageable sub-networks, enabling a more granular understanding of their functionality. Our method involves systematically partitioning the network based on layer connectivity and analyzing the isolated behavior of each sub-network through targeted input perturbation and output analysis. The approach is applied to various convolutional and recurrent architectures trained on image classification and natural language processing tasks. Results indicate that specific sub-networks are responsible for distinct feature extraction and decision-making processes. Furthermore, comparative analysis reveals that the performance bottlenecks can be localized to certain sub-networks, suggesting targeted optimization strategies. This research provides a practical methodology for demystifying deep networks, paving the way for improved interpretability, robustness, and efficient network design.

Keywords: Deep Networks, Interpretability, Divide-and-Conquer, Neural Networks, Feature Extraction, Sub-networks.
