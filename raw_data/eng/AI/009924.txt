UltraSR provides a novel approach to arbitrary-scale image super-resolution by integrating spatial encoding into implicit image functions, addressing a critical gap in existing methodologies. The objective of this research is to enhance the adaptability and accuracy of super-resolution techniques over a wide range of scaling factors. Our method introduces a spatial encoding mechanism that encapsulates the positional context of pixels, significantly improving the precision of reconstructed images. Leveraging advanced machine learning architectures, UltraSR demonstrates a flexible and scalable model that surpasses traditional fixed-scale super-resolution algorithms. Initial evaluations showcase substantial improvements in image quality metrics, including PSNR and SSIM, across diverse datasets and varying resolution requirements. Compared to existing approaches, UltraSR exhibits superior performance in preserving texture and structural details at arbitrary scales. The implications of this work suggest significant advancements in fields requiring dynamic resolution adjustments, such as digital media, remote sensing, and medical imaging. Through UltraSR, spatial encoding emerges as a pivotal component, facilitating enhanced performance and opening new avenues for future research in super-resolution technologies. Key keywords include super-resolution, spatial encoding, implicit image functions, and arbitrary-scale.
