This paper addresses the challenge of content-based image retrieval (CBIR) for 3D brain MRI, particularly focusing on disease-oriented image embedding with enhanced scanner standardization. In the realm of medical imaging, variabilities in scanner protocols significantly impede efficient and accurate image retrieval systems. Our objective is to develop an approach that bridges these inconsistencies while enabling reliable retrieval of disease-specific brain MRI data. We propose a pseudo-scanner standardization technique that harmonizes image quality across different MRI scanners, thus establishing a unified platform for analysis. This is coupled with an innovative embedding model specifically designed for disease orientation, tailored to capture nuanced pathological features in 3D brain MRIs.

Our methodology involves a comprehensive dataset comprising diverse MRI scans subjected to our standardization process. The embedding model utilizes advanced machine learning techniques to extract and encode disease-relevant features, ensuring robust comparison across varied datasets. Through extensive experimental evaluations, our approach demonstrates superior performance in retrieval accuracy compared to traditional CBIR methods. In particular, our system shows strong capabilities in recognizing and retrieving complex brain pathologies, despite differences in scanner specifications.

In conclusion, the proposed disease-oriented image embedding with pseudo-scanner standardization significantly improves CBIR systems for 3D brain MRI. This research not only enhances the accessibility and usability of MRI databases for clinical and research applications but also paves the way for further advancements in machine learning-driven medical image analysis. Keywords: CBIR, 3D brain MRI, disease-oriented embedding, pseudo-scanner standardization, medical image retrieval.
