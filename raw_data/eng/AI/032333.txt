This paper addresses the challenge of causal discovery in the presence of incomplete data, a prevalent issue in various fields, including healthcare and social sciences. The primary objective is to develop an innovative approach that accurately identifies causal relationships despite data limitations. We present a novel framework that combines an encoder for effective data representation with reinforcement learning (RL) techniques to sequentially refine causal inference. The encoder captures underlying patterns from incomplete datasets, while the RL component systematically explores and optimizes causal hypotheses. Our experiments demonstrate that this integrated approach significantly outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy and robustness, particularly in scenarios with varying degrees of data incompleteness. Furthermore, the system exhibits a remarkable ability to adaptively learn from incomplete observations, which enhances its utility in real-world applications. The findings indicate that our method not only provides improved causal insight but also offers substantial implications for fields reliant on reliable causal inference. Keywords: causal discovery, incomplete data, encoder, reinforcement learning, causal inference.
