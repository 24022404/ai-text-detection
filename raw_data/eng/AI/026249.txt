Monocular depth estimation is a critical task in computer vision, enabling numerous applications ranging from autonomous navigation to augmented reality using a single RGB image. This paper presents a novel deep multi-scale architecture designed to improve the accuracy and robustness of monocular depth prediction. Our approach leverages multi-scale feature extraction within a convolutional neural network (CNN) framework, enabling the model to capture both global scene context and fine-scale details. By integrating hierarchical feature maps through skip connections and adaptive fusion layers, the proposed model achieves superior spatial depth consistency without sacrificing computational efficiency. Experimental results on standard benchmarks demonstrate that our architecture consistently outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of both quantitative metrics and visual quality. Notably, our approach shows marked improvements in handling complex scenes and preserving depth discontinuities. The proposed system offers a practical solution for real-time depth estimation on edge devices. Our findings highlight the effectiveness of multi-scale architectures in advancing monocular depth estimation and open new avenues for future research in 3D scene understanding and real-world perception systems. 

Keywords: monocular depth estimation, deep learning, multi-scale architectures, convolutional neural networks, 3D reconstruction, computer vision
