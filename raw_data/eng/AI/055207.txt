This paper introduces FCOS3D, a novel fully convolutional one-stage framework for monocular 3D object detection, aiming to bridge the gap between accuracy and real-time performance in autonomous driving and robotics. The proposed approach leverages a dense prediction paradigm, eliminating the need for anchor generation or proposal-based methods, which often introduce computational complexity and hyperparameter sensitivity. FCOS3D extends the popular fully convolutional object detection strategy to the 3D domain by incorporating specialized regression heads to estimate object location, orientation, dimensions, and depth directly from single RGB images. Extensive experiments on the challenging KITTI dataset demonstrate that FCOS3D achieves competitive accuracy compared to existing multi-stage and anchor-based methods while significantly improving inference speed. Notably, the approach enables efficient, end-to-end training and robust generalization to various road scenarios. These findings indicate that fully convolutional architectures can serve as an effective and scalable alternative for 3D perception tasks from monocular inputs. FCOS3D offers practical benefits for real-time scene understanding in intelligent vehicles and surveillance systems. Key contributions include a simplified architecture, reduced computational overhead, and enhanced 3D detection accuracy without reliance on depth sensors or multiple camera setups. Keywords: monocular 3D object detection, fully convolutional network, one-stage detector, autonomous driving, real-time perception.
