This paper proposes SSA-CNN, a novel Semantic Self-Attention Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) designed to enhance pedestrian detection accuracy. The objective is to improve the robustness and efficiency of pedestrian detection systems, particularly in complex and dynamic environments. Our approach integrates a self-attention mechanism into a CNN architecture, allowing the model to focus on relevant semantic features and contextual information. The SSA-CNN model is trained on a large-scale pedestrian dataset and evaluated on benchmark datasets. Results show that our model outperforms state-of-the-art pedestrian detection methods, achieving significant improvements in detection accuracy and speed. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a semantic self-attention mechanism, which enables the model to capture long-range dependencies and contextual relationships, and the demonstration of its effectiveness in pedestrian detection tasks. This work has important implications for autonomous vehicles, surveillance systems, and other applications where accurate pedestrian detection is critical. Key keywords: pedestrian detection, self-attention, CNN, deep learning, computer vision.
