Keyword spotting (KWS) is a critical task in speech recognition systems, enabling efficient detection of specific words or phrases within audio streams. This paper introduces a novel approach to enhance KWS performance by incorporating orthogonality constraints into multi-head attention mechanisms. The proposed method, Orthogonality Constrained Multi-Head Attention (OC-MHA), aims to improve the diversity and interpretability of attention heads, addressing the redundancy issue commonly observed in traditional multi-head attention architectures.  

The OC-MHA framework enforces orthogonality among attention heads during training, ensuring that each head captures distinct and complementary features from the input data. This is achieved through a regularization term added to the loss function, which minimizes the correlation between attention head outputs. Experiments were conducted on benchmark KWS datasets, comparing the proposed method against standard multi-head attention and other state-of-the-art approaches. Results demonstrate that OC-MHA achieves superior accuracy and robustness, particularly in noisy environments, while maintaining computational efficiency.  

The findings highlight the importance of attention head diversity in KWS tasks and provide a scalable solution for improving speech recognition systems. The proposed method not only advances the state-of-the-art in KWS but also offers broader implications for other sequence modeling tasks, such as natural language processing and time-series analysis.  

**Keywords**: Keyword Spotting, Multi-Head Attention, Orthogonality Constraints, Speech Recognition, Attention Mechanisms.
