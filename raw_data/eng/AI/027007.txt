This paper introduces Canonical Autocorrelation Analysis, a novel approach to analyzing complex time-series data. The objective is to identify underlying patterns and relationships in autocorrelated data, which is crucial in various fields such as signal processing, finance, and climate science. Our method leverages a combination of canonical correlation analysis and autocorrelation techniques to extract meaningful features from the data. The approach involves applying a canonical correlation framework to the autocorrelation matrices of the input data, resulting in a more robust and efficient analysis. Our results show that Canonical Autocorrelation Analysis outperforms traditional methods in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency, particularly in the presence of noise and non-stationarity. The key findings of this research have important implications for the development of more accurate predictive models and improved signal processing techniques. This study contributes to the advancement of time-series analysis and has potential applications in fields such as machine learning, data mining, and statistical modeling, with relevant keywords including autocorrelation, canonical correlation, time-series analysis, and signal processing.
