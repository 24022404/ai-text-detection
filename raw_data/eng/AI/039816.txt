Pedestrian detection remains a critical challenge in computer vision, particularly under complex urban environments with varying occlusions and backgrounds. This paper introduces SSA-CNN, a novel Semantic Self-Attention Convolutional Neural Network designed to enhance pedestrian detection accuracy and robustness. The proposed approach integrates a semantic self-attention mechanism within the CNN architecture, enabling the network to dynamically focus on salient image regions pertinent to pedestrian features while simultaneously suppressing background noise and occlusions. Extensive experiments were conducted on widely used pedestrian detection benchmarks, demonstrating that SSA-CNN surpasses existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of both precision and recall. Results indicate significant improvements in detecting small-scale and heavily occluded pedestrians without a substantial increase in computational complexity. The study further analyzes the impact of semantic self-attention modules on feature representation and detection performance. Overall, SSA-CNN offers a scalable and effective solution for real-time pedestrian detection in intelligent transportation systems, surveillance, and autonomous driving applications. Key contributions include the introduction of a semantic self-attention mechanism for feature enhancement, empirical verification of improved detection metrics, and practical insights into deploying SSA-CNN in real-world scenarios. Keywords: pedestrian detection, convolutional neural network, semantic self-attention, computer vision, real-time detection.
