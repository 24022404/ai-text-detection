This paper introduces *DeepV2D*, a novel deep learning framework for video-to-depth estimation that leverages differentiable Structure from Motion (SfM) to achieve highly accurate depth reconstruction from monocular video sequences. The primary objective of this work is to address the challenges of depth estimation in dynamic scenes, where traditional methods often struggle with occlusions, motion blur, and varying lighting conditions. *DeepV2D* integrates a differentiable SfM module within a neural network architecture, enabling end-to-end training and optimization of both depth and camera pose estimation. The proposed approach combines the strengths of geometric reconstruction and data-driven learning, resulting in robust and scalable depth prediction.  

Experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that *DeepV2D* outperforms existing methods in terms of depth accuracy and computational efficiency. The framework achieves state-of-the-art performance on both indoor and outdoor scenes, with significant improvements in handling complex motion patterns and textureless regions. Additionally, the differentiable nature of the SfM module allows for seamless integration with other deep learning pipelines, making it adaptable to a wide range of applications, including autonomous navigation, augmented reality, and 3D scene understanding.  

The key contributions of this work include the development of a differentiable SfM-based depth estimation framework, a novel loss function that jointly optimizes depth and pose, and extensive empirical validation of the approach across diverse datasets. *DeepV2D* represents a significant advancement in video-to-depth estimation, offering a scalable and accurate solution for real-world scenarios.  

**Keywords**: Depth estimation, Structure from Motion (SfM), differentiable optimization, monocular video, 3D reconstruction, deep learning.
