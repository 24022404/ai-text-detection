This paper presents a novel approach to video scene graph generation, focusing on target adaptive context aggregation. The objective is to improve the accuracy of scene graph generation by effectively capturing contextual relationships between objects in videos. Our method employs a graph-based neural network that adaptively aggregates context from target objects, enabling more precise representation of complex scenes. The approach utilizes a multi-scale attention mechanism to weigh the importance of different contextual information. Experimental results demonstrate that our target adaptive context aggregation method outperforms existing state-of-the-art models, achieving significant improvements in scene graph generation accuracy. The key contributions of this research include the introduction of a dynamic context aggregation mechanism and the application of a graph-based neural network to video scene graph generation. This work has important implications for various applications, including video understanding, object detection, and robotic vision. Key keywords: video scene graph generation, target adaptive context aggregation, graph-based neural networks, multi-scale attention, computer vision.
