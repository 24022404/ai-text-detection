This paper presents a novel Lyapunov-based approach to safe reinforcement learning, addressing the critical issue of ensuring safety constraints in complex control systems. Our objective is to develop a framework that guarantees the satisfaction of safety specifications while optimizing performance in uncertain environments. We propose a method that integrates Lyapunov functions with reinforcement learning algorithms, enabling the derivation of safe and optimal policies. Our approach utilizes a model-based technique to construct Lyapunov functions, which are then used to modify the reward signal and guide the learning process towards safe exploration. The results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method in various control tasks, showcasing improved safety and performance compared to existing safe reinforcement learning methods. The key contributions of this work include the development of a Lyapunov-based safety framework, the integration of model-based and model-free techniques, and the demonstration of safe and optimal control in complex systems. Our research has significant implications for the application of reinforcement learning in safety-critical domains, such as robotics and autonomous systems, and highlights the potential of Lyapunov-based methods for ensuring safety and reliability in AI systems. Key keywords: safe reinforcement learning, Lyapunov functions, model-based control, uncertainty, optimal control, robotics, autonomous systems.
