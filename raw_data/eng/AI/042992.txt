This paper introduces a novel framework for robust vehicle tracking and re-identification (Re-ID) in multi-camera networks, addressing the challenges of occlusion, varying illumination, and viewpoint changes. Our approach employs a team of diverse classifiers, each specialized in different feature representations (e.g., color histograms, texture descriptors, deep features extracted from convolutional neural networks). These classifiers are dynamically weighted and combined based on their individual performance and agreement levels, enabling robust tracking even under adverse conditions. We propose an extensible architecture that allows for the seamless integration of new classifiers and feature extraction methods. Furthermore, we introduce algorithmic optimizations to achieve real-time performance, making the system suitable for large-scale deployments. Experimental results on challenging benchmark datasets demonstrate that our teamed classifier approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods in both tracking accuracy and Re-ID precision, while maintaining a significantly faster processing speed. The proposed framework offers a practical and scalable solution for intelligent transportation systems and video surveillance applications.

**Keywords:** Vehicle Tracking, Vehicle Re-Identification, Multi-Camera Networks, Classifier Fusion, Deep Learning, Real-Time Performance.
