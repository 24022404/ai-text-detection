This paper presents an innovative approach to real-time segmentation of robotic surgical instruments using a lightweight deep learning network. The objective is to accurately identify and distinguish surgical instruments in robotic-assisted surgeries, enhancing operational efficiency and safety. Our method employs an attention-guided mechanism to focus on relevant features, reducing computational complexity while maintaining precision. The proposed network architecture leverages a compact design, facilitating real-time performance on standard computing hardware. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in segmentation accuracy and speed compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. The key findings indicate that our approach achieves a balance between model complexity and performance, making it suitable for real-time applications in robotic surgery. This research contributes to the development of more efficient and reliable computer-assisted surgical systems, with potential applications in medical robotics, surgical navigation, and healthcare technology. Key keywords: real-time segmentation, robotic surgical instruments, attention mechanisms, lightweight networks, deep learning, medical robotics.
