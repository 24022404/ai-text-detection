Temporal Action Localization (TAL) is a critical task in video analysis, aiming to identify and localize action instances within untrimmed videos. Despite recent advancements, achieving robust and accurate localization remains challenging due to the complexity of temporal dynamics and the variability of action features. This paper addresses the problem by exploring stronger feature representations to enhance the performance of TAL systems. We propose a novel approach that integrates multi-scale temporal feature extraction with attention mechanisms to capture both local and global contextual information effectively. Our method leverages advanced deep learning architectures, including transformer-based models, to improve feature discriminability and temporal coherence. Experimental results on benchmark datasets, such as THUMOS14 and ActivityNet, demonstrate significant improvements in action localization accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods. The proposed approach achieves superior performance in mean Average Precision (mAP), particularly in detecting actions with varying durations and complex temporal structures. This research contributes to the field by introducing a more effective feature representation framework, which can be applied to various video analysis tasks. The findings highlight the importance of feature quality in TAL and provide insights for future developments in video understanding and action recognition.  

**Keywords**: Temporal Action Localization, Video Analysis, Feature Representation, Transformer Models, Attention Mechanisms, Deep Learning.
