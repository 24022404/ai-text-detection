This paper presents an end-to-end framework for 3D point cloud registration, leveraging virtual correspondences to enhance alignment accuracy and efficiency. The registration task, critical in applications such as robotics, autonomous driving, and augmented reality, often faces challenges due to noise, partial overlaps, and computational complexity. To address these issues, we propose a novel approach that integrates deep learning with virtual correspondences, enabling robust feature extraction and precise point cloud alignment. Our method employs a neural network architecture designed to learn both local and global geometric features, followed by a correspondence generation module that predicts virtual matches between point clouds. These virtual correspondences are then used to optimize the transformation matrix, ensuring accurate registration. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of both accuracy and computational efficiency. The proposed framework achieves significant improvements in handling noisy and partially overlapping point clouds, making it a promising solution for real-world applications. This research contributes to advancing 3D point cloud registration by introducing a scalable and effective end-to-end learning pipeline, with potential implications for robotics, computer vision, and beyond.  

**Keywords**: 3D point cloud, registration, virtual correspondences, deep learning, feature extraction, transformation optimization.
