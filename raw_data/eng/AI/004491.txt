This paper introduces Convolutional Spectral Kernel Learning (CSKL), a novel approach for kernel learning that leverages the power of convolutional operations in the spectral domain. CSKL aims to enhance the efficiency and scalability of kernel methods when dealing with high-dimensional data. Our method employs a learned convolutional filter to transform spectral representations of data, effectively reducing dimensionality and extracting relevant features. This transformation allows for the construction of more compact and discriminative kernel matrices. We demonstrate the effectiveness of CSKL through experiments on several benchmark datasets, comparing its performance against traditional kernel methods and other state-of-the-art dimensionality reduction techniques. The results show that CSKL achieves competitive or superior accuracy with significantly reduced computational cost, especially for large datasets. The proposed approach offers a promising avenue for scaling kernel methods to tackle complex learning tasks in various domains. Keywords: Kernel Learning, Convolutional Neural Networks, Spectral Analysis, Dimensionality Reduction, Machine Learning.
