This paper proposes a novel self-supervised learning approach that leverages local attention-aware features to improve representation learning. The objective is to develop an efficient and effective method for learning meaningful representations from unlabeled data. Our approach utilizes a local attention mechanism to focus on relevant regions of the input data, allowing the model to learn more informative features. We employ a contrastive learning framework to train the model, which enables it to distinguish between similar and dissimilar samples. Our results show that the proposed method outperforms existing self-supervised learning approaches on several benchmark datasets, achieving state-of-the-art performance on image classification and object detection tasks. The key findings of this research highlight the importance of local attention-aware features in self-supervised learning, enabling the model to capture nuanced patterns and relationships in the data. This work contributes to the development of more efficient and effective self-supervised learning methods, with potential applications in computer vision, natural language processing, and other areas of artificial intelligence. Key keywords: self-supervised learning, local attention, feature learning, contrastive learning, deep learning.
