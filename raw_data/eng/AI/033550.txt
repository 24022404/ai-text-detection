This paper addresses the challenge of multi-agent coordination by introducing a novel graph-driven communication framework. The objective is to enable effective collaboration among agents in complex, dynamic environments. Our approach leverages graph neural networks to learn communication protocols that facilitate the exchange of relevant information between agents. The methodology involves training agents to transmit and receive messages based on their local observations and graph-based representations of the environment. Experimental results demonstrate that our framework achieves significant improvements in coordination tasks, such as cooperative navigation and resource allocation, compared to existing methods. The key findings highlight the importance of structured communication in multi-agent systems and the potential of graph-driven approaches to enhance cooperation and decision-making. This research contributes to the development of more efficient and scalable multi-agent coordination algorithms, with potential applications in areas like robotics, smart cities, and autonomous systems. Key keywords: multi-agent systems, graph neural networks, communication protocols, cooperative learning, distributed AI.
