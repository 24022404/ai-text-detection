This paper addresses the challenge of interactive segmentation, a key task in computer vision that enables users to efficiently guide the delineation of objects within images with minimal input. We propose a novel approach, Probabilistic Attention, which enhances interactive segmentation models by integrating uncertainty modeling directly within the attention mechanism of deep neural networks. Our method assigns dynamic attention weights based on the probabilistic estimation of user intent and image content, effectively balancing between spatial cues and ambiguous regions that require refinement. Through comprehensive experiments on benchmark interactive segmentation datasets, our approach demonstrates superior accuracy and faster convergence compared to traditional attention-based and deterministic models, particularly in scenarios with sparse user guidance. The results highlight improved boundary delineation and robustness to varying image complexities. This work establishes Probabilistic Attention as a powerful and flexible tool for interactive segmentation, with potential applications in medical imaging, photo editing, and autonomous vision systems. Keywords: interactive segmentation, probabilistic attention, deep learning, uncertainty modeling, computer vision.
