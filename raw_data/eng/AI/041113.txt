This paper addresses the challenge of fashion outfit complementary item retrieval, a critical task in enhancing personalized styling and user experience in the fashion industry. The objective is to develop an intelligent system capable of suggesting complementary fashion items based on a given outfit, thereby assisting in the creation of aesthetically cohesive ensembles. Our approach leverages a deep learning model incorporating convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and attention mechanisms to analyze visual and contextual attributes of fashion items. By training our model on a comprehensive fashion dataset, we utilize embeddings to capture nuanced relationships between items, refining the retrieval accuracy. Experimental results indicate that our model outperforms traditional heuristic-based methods, demonstrating notable improvements in both precision and recall metrics. Furthermore, the proposed system exhibits robustness across diverse fashion categories, suggesting its scalability and adaptability to various user preferences and styles. The findings underscore the potential of applying advanced machine learning techniques to solve complex problems in fashion technology. This research contributes to the field by offering a novel framework for enhancing fashion recommendation systems, paving the way for more sophisticated, AI-driven fashion retail solutions. Keywords: fashion retrieval, complementary item recommendation, deep learning, CNN, attention mechanism, personalized styling.
