This paper addresses the challenge of efficient resampling in large-scale 3D point clouds, a critical task for applications such as computer vision, robotics, and virtual reality. We propose a novel graph-based framework that leverages the structural relationships within point clouds to enable fast and accurate resampling. Our method constructs a k-nearest neighbor graph to represent point connectivity, facilitating the identification of representative subsets while preserving essential geometric features. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing resampling techniques in terms of computational speed and fidelity to the original structure. Furthermore, comparative analyses show that the proposed method maintains superior surface representation even at extreme downsampling ratios. These findings highlight the potential of incorporating graph signal processing concepts into 3D point cloud manipulation. Our research contributes a scalable and adaptable solution for real-time processing and efficient data reduction in 3D sensing pipelines. Potential applications include autonomous navigation, 3D reconstruction, and immersive visualization systems. Keywords: 3D point cloud, resampling, graph signal processing, fast algorithms, data reduction, surface reconstruction.
