This paper addresses the challenge of online learning in environments where feedback is provided through an uncertain graph structure. We introduce a novel online learning algorithm, UFG-OL (Uncertain Feedback Graph Online Learning), designed to effectively leverage partial and noisy feedback signals. UFG-OL incorporates a graph learning component that dynamically estimates the underlying feedback graph based on observed feedback patterns and a confidence-weighted exploration strategy to mitigate the impact of uncertainty. The algorithm employs a regularized online optimization framework to balance exploration and exploitation. Experimental results on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that UFG-OL significantly outperforms existing online learning algorithms that assume complete or fixed feedback graphs, particularly in scenarios with sparse and unreliable feedback. The findings highlight the practical benefits of explicitly modeling feedback uncertainty in online learning systems, paving the way for improved performance in applications such as recommendation systems, active learning, and network control. Keywords: Online Learning, Feedback Graphs, Uncertainty, Graph Learning, Exploration-Exploitation.
