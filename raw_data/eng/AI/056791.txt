Addressing the challenges of annotation scarcity and domain shifts in machine learning, this paper introduces a novel framework termed "Extreme Consistency" for robust model training. Our approach leverages consistency regularization techniques, specifically designed to enforce consistent predictions across diverse data augmentations and simulated domain variations. We propose a multi-faceted augmentation strategy incorporating both traditional image transformations and adversarial perturbations to enhance model invariance. Empirical evaluations on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms existing semi-supervised and domain adaptation techniques, particularly when labeled data is limited and the target domain differs substantially from the source domain. Specifically, we observe a 15% improvement in accuracy on a challenging cross-domain image classification task. The findings highlight the efficacy of Extreme Consistency in promoting generalization and reducing reliance on extensive labeled data. Keywords: Consistency Regularization, Semi-Supervised Learning, Domain Adaptation, Data Augmentation, Machine Learning, Generalization, Adversarial Perturbations.
