The paper introduces SliderGAN, an innovative approach aimed at synthesizing expressive face images by manipulating 3D blendshape parameters. This research seeks to improve the realism and diversity of facial expressions in generated images, addressing limitations in current face synthesis methods that often fail to capture nuanced expressions.

Methods: SliderGAN utilizes a Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) framework integrated with a novel parameter-sliding mechanism. By adjusting 3D blendshape parameters, the model dynamically transforms facial expressions, offering fine-grained control over the synthesis process. The system leverages 3D face modeling techniques to guide the GAN, ensuring consistency and precision in expression rendering.

Results: The results demonstrate that SliderGAN can produce highly realistic and diverse facial expressions that outperform existing state-of-the-art GAN models. Quantitative evaluations reveal superior performance in terms of expression accuracy and diversity, verified by both objective metrics and subjective human assessments. Additionally, comparisons with conventional methods highlight the enhanced capability of SliderGAN to synthesize expressions beyond standard emotional categories.

Conclusion: SliderGAN represents a significant advancement in expressive face image synthesis, providing a versatile tool for applications in animation, virtual reality, and human-computer interaction. The study's contributions include a novel integration of 3D blendshape parameters within a GAN framework, paving the way for more natural and expressive digital avatars in interactive environments. Keywords: SliderGAN, GAN, expressive face synthesis, 3D blendshape, facial expression modeling.
