The classification of imbalanced datasets remains a significant challenge in machine learning, as traditional algorithms often exhibit bias toward the majority class, leading to suboptimal performance. This paper addresses this issue by proposing a novel approach using a Class Expert Generative Adversarial Network (CE-GAN) for effective imbalance data classification. The objective is to enhance the classification accuracy of minority classes by generating synthetic samples that better represent their distribution. The CE-GAN framework introduces a class-specific generator and discriminator architecture, enabling the model to focus on learning the unique characteristics of each class. The proposed method is evaluated on multiple benchmark datasets with varying degrees of class imbalance. Experimental results demonstrate that CE-GAN outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of precision, recall, and F1-score, particularly for minority classes. Furthermore, the model exhibits robustness across different imbalance ratios, making it suitable for real-world applications such as fraud detection, medical diagnosis, and anomaly detection. The key contribution of this research lies in the integration of class-specific expertise within the GAN framework, offering a scalable and adaptable solution for imbalance data classification. This work advances the field of deep learning by providing a novel approach to address class imbalance, with potential implications for improving decision-making systems in diverse domains.  

**Keywords**: Imbalance data classification, Generative Adversarial Network (GAN), Deep learning, Class-specific learning, Synthetic data generation, Minority class enhancement.
