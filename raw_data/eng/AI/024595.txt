This paper addresses the challenge of few-shot extrapolation in reinforcement learning (RL), where an agent must generalize to unseen environments given limited experience. Current RL algorithms often struggle to effectively extrapolate beyond the training distribution, particularly when faced with structured environmental variations. To address this, we propose a novel approach, Structured MaxEnt RL, that leverages a structured exploration strategy guided by a learned prior over environment parameters. Our method optimizes for maximum entropy within the constraints imposed by the structured prior, encouraging exploration of diverse, yet plausible, environment configurations. We evaluate our approach on a suite of simulated environments exhibiting structured variations, demonstrating significantly improved few-shot extrapolation performance compared to state-of-the-art RL algorithms and standard maximum entropy RL. The results highlight the importance of structured exploration in enabling effective generalization. Our findings suggest that incorporating environment structure into exploration can lead to more robust and sample-efficient RL agents capable of adapting to novel scenarios.
Keywords: Reinforcement Learning, Few-Shot Learning, Extrapolation, Maximum Entropy, Structured Exploration, Generalization.
