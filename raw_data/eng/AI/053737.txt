The increasing demand for secure and reliable biometric systems has driven the exploration of electroencephalogram (EEG) signals as a unique and robust biometric modality. However, EEG-based systems face challenges such as signal variability and vulnerability to adversarial attacks. This paper investigates the application of adversarial deep learning techniques to enhance the robustness and accuracy of EEG biometric systems. We propose a novel adversarial training framework that integrates generative adversarial networks (GANs) with deep neural networks (DNNs) to improve feature extraction and classification of EEG signals. Our approach involves generating synthetic EEG data to augment training datasets and simulating adversarial attacks to strengthen the system's resilience. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves a 15% improvement in classification accuracy compared to traditional methods and exhibits superior resistance to adversarial perturbations. Furthermore, the system maintains high performance across diverse datasets, showcasing its generalizability. This research contributes to advancing EEG biometrics by addressing critical vulnerabilities and improving system reliability, with potential applications in secure authentication and brain-computer interfaces.  

**Keywords**: EEG biometrics, adversarial deep learning, generative adversarial networks (GANs), deep neural networks (DNNs), secure authentication, brain-computer interfaces.
