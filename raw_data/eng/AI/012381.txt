This paper presents an innovative approach to multi-instance pose estimation by leveraging the capabilities of transformer models in an end-to-end trainable framework. The objective of this research is to address the challenges associated with accurately estimating the poses of multiple entities within complex scenes using a streamlined methodology. Our approach utilizes transformer networks, known for their effectiveness in handling sequential data, to effectively model spatial relationships and dependencies across multiple instances within an image. The proposed model capitalizes on transformers' self-attention mechanisms to ensure precise pose estimation without the need for predefined anchor points or complex post-processing steps. Experimental evaluations demonstrate significant improvements in pose estimation accuracy when compared to traditional convolutional neural network-based methods. Moreover, our approach showcases robust performance across diverse datasets, highlighting its adaptability to varied environments. The findings suggest that our model not only enhances pose estimation tasks but also simplifies the computational pipeline, making it suitable for real-time applications. By integrating transformers in this domain, we offer a novel perspective that could influence future research directions in computer vision tasks reliant on multi-instance feature extraction and processing. Key keywords associated with this research include transformers, pose estimation, end-to-end training, and computer vision.
