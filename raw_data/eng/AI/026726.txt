This paper introduces a comprehensive dataset designed to advance research in sheep identity recognition, age estimation, and weight prediction, addressing critical challenges in livestock management and precision agriculture. The dataset comprises high-resolution images, biometric features, and metadata collected from a diverse population of sheep under controlled and real-world conditions. To ensure robust performance, we employed a combination of deep learning-based computer vision techniques, including convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and transfer learning, for feature extraction and model training. Additionally, regression models were integrated to estimate age and weight based on visual and biometric data. Experimental results demonstrate high accuracy in sheep identity recognition (98.7%), age estimation (mean absolute error of 1.2 months), and weight prediction (mean absolute error of 2.1 kg), outperforming existing methods. The dataset and proposed framework provide a foundation for developing automated livestock monitoring systems, reducing manual labor, and improving decision-making in agricultural practices. This work contributes to the growing field of precision livestock farming by offering a novel, scalable solution for individual animal tracking and health management.  

**Keywords**: Sheep identification, age estimation, weight prediction, precision agriculture, deep learning, livestock management.
