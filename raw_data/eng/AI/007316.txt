**Abstract**

This paper presents a novel approach to image colorization, termed Pixelated Semantic Colorization, which leverages semantic understanding to enhance the realism and detail of colorized grayscale images. Traditional colorization methods often suffer from context ambiguity, resulting in unrealistic or oversimplified color assignments. To address this, we integrate a deep learning framework that combines pixel-level classification with semantic segmentation, enabling our model to better interpret scene content and assign contextually appropriate colors. The proposed system utilizes a dual-stream convolutional neural network (CNN): one stream predicts color distributions based on local pixel information, while the other extracts global semantic cues to guide colorization. Extensive experiments on established datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art colorization techniques in both qualitative and quantitative metrics, particularly in challenging scenes with diverse object categories. Visual inspections and user studies confirm improved color consistency and vividness, especially for semantically distinct objects. The Pixelated Semantic Colorization framework offers significant advancements for applications such as image restoration, media content creation, and digital archiving. Keywords: image colorization, semantic segmentation, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, pixel-level classification.
