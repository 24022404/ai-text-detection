Neural Architecture Search (NAS) has emerged as a powerful approach for automatically discovering high-performing neural network architectures, yet it often suffers from prohibitively high computational costs due to the exploration of vast and complex search spaces. This paper introduces a novel framework, Smooth Variational Graph Embeddings (SVGE), designed to enable more efficient and effective NAS by leveraging smooth, probabilistic representations of neural architectures. Our approach models candidate architectures as graphs and learns their embeddings through a variational framework, ensuring both continuous representation and smoothness in the latent space. This facilitates scalable architecture comparisons and robust performance prediction. Experimental results on standard NAS benchmarks demonstrate that SVGE not only accelerates the search process compared to traditional discrete and non-smooth embedding techniques, but also achieves competitive or superior architecture discovery. Furthermore, ablation studies highlight the benefits of embedding smoothness in preserving structural information and enhancing generalization. The proposed SVGE method paves the way for more accessible and reliable neural architecture search, with potential applications in automated machine learning, efficient deep learning model design, and adaptive systems. Keywords: Neural Architecture Search, Graph Embedding, Variational Methods, Deep Learning, Automated Machine Learning.
