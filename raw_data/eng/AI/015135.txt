Class imbalance presents a significant challenge in deep learning, often leading to biased models with diminished performance on minority classes. This paper introduces ReMix, a novel calibrated resampling framework designed to address class imbalance in deep neural networks. ReMix dynamically adjusts the frequency and weight of samples from underrepresented and majority classes during the training process through a data-adaptive calibration mechanism. Our approach is integrated with common deep learning architectures and is compatible with both image and tabular datasets. Experimental evaluations on benchmark datasets demonstrate that ReMix significantly improves classification accuracy, especially on minority classes, compared to traditional resampling and cost-sensitive methods. Additionally, ReMix enhances model calibration, resulting in more reliable confidence estimates for all classes. The proposed method introduces minimal computational overhead and integrates seamlessly into standard deep learning workflows. These findings suggest that ReMix provides an effective and scalable solution for practitioners dealing with class imbalance, with broad applicability across domains such as medical imaging, fraud detection, and natural language processing. 

Keywords: class imbalance, deep learning, resampling, model calibration, minority class, imbalanced data, ReMix.
