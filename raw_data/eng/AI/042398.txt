This paper introduces SSTVOS, a novel framework employing Sparse Spatiotemporal Transformers for Video Object Segmentation. The objective of this research is to accurately segment objects in video sequences while addressing the computational challenges of modeling long-range spatial and temporal dependencies. Our approach leverages sparse attention mechanisms within transformer architectures to efficiently capture both intra-frame and inter-frame object relationships. Unlike traditional dense transformers, SSTVOS reduces redundant computations by selectively attending to salient regions across space and time, significantly improving scalability and speed. Experimental evaluations on multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate that SSTVOS achieves competitive segmentation accuracy, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods in both efficiency and overall performance. The results show marked improvements in handling object occlusions, fast motions, and complex backgrounds. In conclusion, SSTVOS offers an effective and resource-efficient solution for video object segmentation, paving the way for real-time applications in video analytics, autonomous systems, and human-computer interaction. Keywords: video object segmentation, sparse transformers, spatiotemporal modeling, attention mechanism, deep learning, computer vision.
