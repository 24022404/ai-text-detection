This paper addresses the challenge of optimizing reinforcement learning algorithms by proposing a novel approach that combines the strengths of policy gradient methods and Q-learning. The aim is to enhance learning efficiency and stability in environments characterized by high dimensionality and continuous action spaces.

Methods/Approach: We introduce a hybrid reinforcement learning framework that integrates policy gradient techniques with Q-learning. The proposed method utilizes the policy gradient to effectively handle continuous action policies while leveraging Q-learning to evaluate actions based on accumulated experiences, thus improving the quality of the policy updates. A detailed algorithm is devised to seamlessly combine the updates and promote cooperation between these two distinct strategies.

Results/Findings: Empirical evaluation on benchmark environments demonstrates that our combined approach significantly outperforms traditional policy gradient and Q-learning methods in terms of convergence speed and overall performance. The new technique shows marked improvements in achieving higher rewards and reduces the variance in policy updates, leading to more stable training processes. Additionally, our method was able to adapt efficiently to changes in dynamic environments, indicating its robustness.

Conclusion/Implications: The research highlights the potential of combining policy gradient and Q-learning as a powerful reinforcement learning tool for complex tasks. This innovative approach not only enhances the learning capabilities but also presents a scalable solution applicable to a wide range of AI-driven applications such as robotics and autonomous systems. This integration opens up new avenues for further optimizing reinforcement learning algorithms, providing a substantial contribution to the field.

Keywords: reinforcement learning, policy gradient, Q-learning, continuous action spaces, algorithm, AI, machine learning, convergence, dynamic environments.
