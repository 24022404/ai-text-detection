Root canal therapy (RCT) is a critical dental procedure requiring precise evaluation to ensure successful outcomes. However, manual assessment is time-consuming and prone to variability. This paper introduces an Anatomy-Guided Parallel Bottleneck Transformer Network (AGPBT-Net), a novel deep learning framework designed to automate the evaluation of RCT. The proposed model leverages a parallel bottleneck architecture integrated with transformer mechanisms to enhance feature extraction and spatial understanding of dental anatomy. By incorporating anatomical guidance, AGPBT-Net ensures accurate segmentation and analysis of root canal structures from 3D dental imaging data. The network is trained and validated on a comprehensive dataset of dental scans, achieving state-of-the-art performance in terms of segmentation accuracy and diagnostic precision. Experimental results demonstrate that AGPBT-Net outperforms existing methods, with a 12% improvement in Dice similarity coefficient (DSC) and a 15% reduction in false-positive rates. The model’s robustness and generalizability are further validated across diverse clinical scenarios. This research contributes a scalable and efficient solution for automated RCT evaluation, with potential applications in dental diagnostics and treatment planning. The integration of anatomical guidance and transformer-based architectures represents a significant advancement in medical imaging analysis, paving the way for future innovations in AI-driven healthcare.  

**Keywords**: Root canal therapy, transformer networks, 3D medical imaging, deep learning, anatomical guidance, automated diagnostics.
