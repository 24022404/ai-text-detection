This paper addresses the critical issue of reward poisoning attacks in reinforcement learning, where an adversary manipulates the reward signal to compromise the learning process. Our objective is to develop effective defense mechanisms against such attacks, ensuring the integrity and reliability of reinforcement learning systems. We propose a novel approach that combines anomaly detection and robust reward estimation to identify and mitigate the effects of reward poisoning. Our method utilizes a statistical framework to detect deviations in the reward signal and adaptively adjusts the learning process to prevent exploitation. Experimental results demonstrate the efficacy of our defense strategy, showing significant improvements in resilience against various types of reward poisoning attacks. Our research contributes to the development of secure reinforcement learning systems, with potential applications in areas such as robotics, autonomous vehicles, and recommender systems. Key aspects of our work include reinforcement learning, reward poisoning attacks, anomaly detection, and robustness, highlighting the importance of defense mechanisms in ensuring the trustworthiness of AI systems.
